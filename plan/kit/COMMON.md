## Rules for every page (all briefs)
- **Nothing invented.** A fact is astronomy, the tradition as the guide lists it, your pages' data, or Joana's notebook exactly as the guide states it. For anything else (a date in history, a name, a study, a rule from an old book), check it and cite the exact source; if you can't confirm it, leave it out. No percentages except the notebook's: write *about 1 person in 12*.
- **Checking outside facts:** only English Wikipedia opens on this network (the page fetcher is blocked). Use `bash /home/user/astrostudy/plan/kit/wiki.sh "Article title"`: it prints the article's text and then its full numbered references. Cite the primary source as the article's reference list gives it, and the article itself with its address. A fact seen only in a search snippet stays out. No address on a source you couldn't open.
- **No variants.** Not one sentence may fit another page of your batch with the names swapped. Never reuse an opening, a closing or a stock paragraph. Each page answers its own title with something particular to it.
- **No birth time, no reading** (*There's always a birth time. Or no reading.*). Never write that Joana reads part of a chart without the time ("the sign but not the house", "the planets but not the angles"). Say what depends on the time, and that this is why she doesn't read a chart without it. Houses: Placidus, Joana's house system; link the birth-time article (`/blog/unknown-birth-time/`).
- **Weather, not a forecast.** Never predict health, death, pregnancy, money, legal outcomes or break-ups. The 5th, 6th, 8th and 12th houses never predict children, illness, death or confinement: link the ethics page the guide's Voice section names.
- **Joana's voice.** First person, warm, plain and exact, British English. Gentle with hard placements: no doom, no verdict, never "bad aspect"; "malefic" or "unlucky" are a tradition's words, said as such. No famous people as examples. Readers are adults; the site never reads children's charts. "Modern tropical astrology", never "modern astrology" alone. Both rulers for Scorpio, Aquarius and Pisces ("I read both"); both nodes, mean and true ("I look at both").
- **Joana's notebook** (`/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/`): only in the guide's words (Facts section). Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time; overall about a third held; the Sun sign, rising sign, Venus and Mercury are among the placements everyone reads first; never a figure of its own for anything else.
- **French.** *vous*. Joana writes in the feminine (*je suis venue*); never assume the reader's gender. **No gender for Mercure, Mars, Jupiter, Saturne, Uranus, Neptune, Pluton**: no le/la/un/une/du/au/son/sa/ce/cette before them, no il/elle for them (no "-t-il" questions: use "Est-ce que…"), no agreeing adjective or participle; repeat the name or turn the sentence round. Le Soleil, la Lune, Vénus, Chiron, Lilith and the nodes keep their usual gender. Houses: *maison 7*. Natural French, adapted rather than translated word for word.
- **Files.** Write only your own pages: `content/en/astrology/<id>.md` and `content/fr/astrology/<id>.md`. Do NOT commit, push or build the site. Run `node tools/fr-typography.mjs content/fr/astrology/<id>.md` on each of your French files, one file at a time, never with a glob. Keep scratch scripts in a folder of your own under `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/c1ed0d9e-5110-517b-927d-f2b2c3cb2e15/scratchpad/`.
- **Front matter.** Copy the models' shape exactly. `slug` in the French file only (from your data's `slugFr`); `date: 2026-11-25` and `reviewed: 2026-11-25`; no `draft` line; no `terms` line unless the brief says so; `family`, `weight` and `glyph` from your data (✦ when there's no glyph); definition of 40–60 words; description of 110–160 characters; 3–4 FAQ whose answers have no links; facts and sources as the guide's section says.
- **Categories and tags.** Never write them by hand: once a page is written in both languages, run `node tools/plan/tags.mjs <id> --write`. It adds the `categories:` and `tags:` lines after `family:`, worked out from the plan; the check reports a page without them.
- **Search words, from the owner (24 November): good interlinking, good metadata, SEO keywords, no redundant words or sayings, in my voice.** The words people type for the page (*Saturn transiting the 7th house*, *composite Venus in Scorpio*, *Mars on the 10th house cusp*, and their French) sit in the title, the h1, the start of the description and the first sentence of the definition, each once and naturally, never stuffed. FAQ questions are the ones people type. Every sentence adds something: no sentence that restates the one before, no filler (*it's important to note*, *in conclusion*, *whether you're…*, *journey*, *dive into*, *unlock*, *delve*, *tapestry*, *navigate*, *embrace*, *resonate*), no stock opening or closing. Joana's sayings (*weather, not a forecast*, *a timetable, not a wall*, *no birth time, no reading*) at most once a page, and only where they carry the point.
- **Links.** `node tools/plan/links-for.mjs <id>` lists the pages each page must link: link every one, in both languages, with the page's title in the page's language as link text and the English path in both (`[Bélier](/astrology/aries/)`). A planned page not yet written shows as plain text until it is. Then link the closest pages the text names (the same pair as a natal aspect, in synastry or by transit; the planet in that house or sign; the technique's own page; the blog article whose subject the text touches), and the pages under "Worth linking" where the text calls for them: aim for 8 to 15 links in all, each where it helps the reader. At most one link to a reading page (`/birth-chart/` or `/synastry/`). No links in definition, description or FAQ answers.
- **Check.** After every five pages, `node tools/plan/check-entries.mjs <those ids>`, and fix every error. At the end it must report 0 errors for all your ids.
- **When you finish**, reply in at most three lines: how many pages you wrote, the check's last line, and anything you left out or that blocked you.
