# AGS library: techniques (knowledge/vocabulary/techniques), compact
Each technique: its canonical function, domains, what it is not (notInherent) and its constraints. Meanings on the site must agree with these.

## annual_profections (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Advances the natal Ascendant one house per year to identify the life topics and planetary ruler receiving symbolic emphasis during a given annual cycle.
- **coreDomains**: hellenistic_astrology; annual_focus; house_emphasis; timing; planetary_ruler
- **notInherent**: event_prediction; replacement_for_other_techniques; guaranteed_results; isolated_interpretation; fixed_destiny
- **constraints**: Interpret with the natal chart and the year's time lord.; Identifies emphasis rather than outcomes.; Originates in traditional astrology.; Benefits from corroboration with other timing methods.

## composite (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Represents the symbolic chart of a relationship created from the midpoints between two natal charts, describing the character, purpose, and dynamics of the relationship itself.
- **coreDomains**: relationship_identity; shared_purpose; partnership; collective_dynamics; development
- **notInherent**: marriage_guarantee; relationship_lifespan; replacement_for_synastry; inevitable_events; objective_entity
- **constraints**: Should be interpreted together with synastry and natal charts.; Describes the relationship as a symbolic whole.; Does not predict duration or success.; Emphasizes shared dynamics rather than individual psychology.

## davison (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Creates a relationship chart from the actual midpoint in time and space between two births, symbolically describing the partnership through an alternative relationship-chart method.
- **coreDomains**: relationship_chart; shared_experience; partnership; symbolic_time; comparison
- **notInherent**: universally_superior_method; guaranteed_events; marriage_prediction; replacement_for_composite; objective_destiny
- **constraints**: Different schools prefer Composite or Davison techniques.; Interpret within broader relationship analysis.; Describes symbolic patterns rather than certainties.; Avoid treating it as inherently more accurate.

## eclipses (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Describes a solar or lunar eclipse that falls on a natal point, or opposite it, as a period of symbolic emphasis for what that point and its house represent.
- **coreDomains**: timing; emphasis; activation; change_of_focus; life_context
- **notInherent**: omens; catastrophe; guaranteed_events; fixed_fate; isolated_interpretation
- **constraints**: Counts only within about three degrees of a natal point, on either end of the eclipse's axis.; Interpreted through the natal point and its house, never on its own.; A penumbral lunar eclipse weighs less than a total, annular or partial one.; Symbolic emphasis, never an omen.

## lunar_return (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Represents the monthly chart cast for the Moon's return to its natal longitude, symbolically emphasizing emotional focus, habits, and experiential themes for the coming lunar month.
- **coreDomains**: monthly_cycle; emotions; habits; inner_focus; timing
- **notInherent**: monthly_predictions; guaranteed_events; replacement_for_transits; fixed_outcomes; isolated_reading
- **constraints**: Interpret relative to the natal chart.; Focuses on symbolic monthly emphasis.; Should not be read as deterministic.; Integrates with broader timing techniques.

## lunations (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Describes new and full moons as short monthly markers that can bring a period already active in the chart into focus when they fall on the same natal point.
- **coreDomains**: monthly_rhythm; triggers; focus; timing
- **notInherent**: independent_significance; guaranteed_events; fixed_fate
- **constraints**: Used only as triggers inside a window already active by transit, progression, solar arc or eclipse.; One-degree orb; a full moon counts on either end of its axis.; Never read on its own as a forecast.

## planetary_returns (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Describes a planet's return to its own natal position (Jupiter about every twelve years, Saturn about every twenty-nine and a half) as the close of one cycle of that planet's function and the opening of the next.
- **coreDomains**: life_cycles; developmental_cycles; renewal; timing; maturation
- **notInherent**: crisis; guaranteed_events; fixed_fate; age_based_destiny
- **constraints**: Read through the returning planet's natal sign, house and aspects.; Saturn's return has its own entry (techniques/saturn_return).; Developmental themes take precedence over event claims.

## saturn_return (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Marks the period when transiting Saturn returns to its natal position, emphasizing review, consolidation, accountability, maturation, and the restructuring of life according to the natal Saturn's developmental themes rather than introducing new symbolic meanings.
- **coreDomains**: developmental_cycles; responsibility; structure; maturation; life_review
- **notInherent**: guaranteed_crisis; automatic_loss_or_success; inevitable_major_life_events; punishment_or_bad_luck; same_experience_for_everyone
- **constraints**: Describes a developmental season rather than a fixed outcome or prediction.; Interpretation must be anchored in the natal Saturn's sign, house, aspects, and overall chart context.; Intensity varies between individuals and according to concurrent astrological factors.; Should describe themes of responsibility and restructuring without implying unavoidable external events.

## secondary_progressions (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Applies the symbolic day-for-a-year progression to the natal chart to describe the gradual internal evolution of personality, perception, emotional development, and life orientation as they unfold over time.
- **coreDomains**: inner_development; psychological_growth; life_cycles; symbolic_time; personal_evolution
- **notInherent**: literal_planetary_motion; guaranteed_external_events; replacement_for_transits; rapid_or_daily_change; fixed_predictions
- **constraints**: Primarily describes internal developmental processes rather than external circumstances.; Should be interpreted together with the natal chart and other timing techniques such as transits.; Progressions unfold gradually and emphasize symbolic maturation rather than sudden change.; Interpretations should distinguish symbolic progression from astronomical movement.

## solar_arc_directions (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Moves every natal point forward by the arc the progressed Sun has travelled since birth, about one degree a year, to describe the slow unfolding of the whole natal chart, timed when a directed point meets a natal point.
- **coreDomains**: symbolic_time; life_development; timing; activation; maturation
- **notInherent**: literal_planetary_motion; guaranteed_events; fixed_fate; isolated_interpretation; exact_predictions
- **constraints**: Must always be interpreted alongside the natal chart.; Orbs are narrow: about one degree, which is about a year either side of exact.; A direction gains weight when a transit, a progression or an eclipse meets the same natal point at the same time.; Developmental themes take precedence over event claims.

## solar_return (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Represents the annual chart cast for the moment the Sun returns to its exact natal longitude, symbolically describing the principal themes, areas of emphasis, and developmental focus of the coming solar year in relation to the natal chart.
- **coreDomains**: annual_cycle; yearly_emphasis; life_focus; symbolic_context; personal_development
- **notInherent**: guaranteed_events; exact_predictions; replacement_for_the_natal_chart; isolated_interpretation; fixed_destiny_for_the_year
- **constraints**: Must always be interpreted in relation to the natal chart rather than independently.; Describes symbolic emphasis and developmental themes, not certain future events.; Interpretation should integrate planetary placements, angles, and aspects within the return chart.; The chart indicates qualitative focus for the year rather than measurable outcomes.

## synastry (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Compares two natal charts to describe patterns of interaction, compatibility, challenge, and mutual growth through planetary relationships between individuals.
- **coreDomains**: relationships; interpersonal_dynamics; compatibility; growth; communication
- **notInherent**: soulmate_proof; relationship_success_guarantee; marriage_prediction; good_or_bad_chart; fixed_destiny
- **constraints**: Neither harmonious nor challenging aspects determine relationship success alone.; Each natal chart remains primary.; Context, maturity, and free choice matter.; Should describe interaction patterns rather than outcomes.

## transits (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Describes the ongoing relationship between current planetary positions and the natal chart, highlighting periods when existing natal potentials, developmental themes, and psychological processes are symbolically emphasized.
- **coreDomains**: timing; developmental_cycles; activation; life_context; symbolic_change
- **notInherent**: guaranteed_events; isolated_interpretation; fixed_fate; single_cause_explanations; exact_predictions
- **constraints**: Must always be interpreted alongside the natal chart.; Transits describe symbolic timing rather than inevitable outcomes.; Multiple simultaneous transits modify interpretation.; Psychological and developmental themes take precedence over event claims.

## zodiacal_releasing (identity.json)
- **canonicalFunction**: Uses a sequence of symbolic periods derived from the Lot of Spirit or related lots to organize long-term chapters of development and changing areas of emphasis across life.
- **coreDomains**: hellenistic_astrology; life_chapters; timing; development; symbolic_periods
- **notInherent**: precise_event_prediction; guaranteed_fame; fixed_destiny; isolated_interpretation; certainty
- **constraints**: Interpretation varies across traditional practitioners.; Should be combined with the natal chart and complementary timing techniques.; Describes changing emphasis rather than inevitable events.; Avoid deterministic conclusions.
