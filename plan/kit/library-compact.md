# AGS library (knowledge/vocabulary), compact
The owner's definitions: each body is a function, a sign a manner, a house a context, an aspect a relation. Meanings on the site must agree with these, and never say what `notInherent`, `boundaries` or `forbiddenInferences` exclude.
