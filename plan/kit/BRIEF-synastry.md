## The page
- Headings and anchors: English `## What flows {#what-flows}`, `## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}`, `## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}`; French `## Ce qui circule {#ce-qui-circule}`, `## Là où ça frotte {#la-ou-ca-frotte}`, `## Comment le vivre {#comment-le-vivre}`. Questions come from the `faq` front matter.
- h1: *Sun square Moon between two charts* · *Soleil carré Lune entre deux thèmes*; `family: "combinations"`; 550 to 750 words of text per language.
- **Read it both ways round**: what it is like to carry each side. This is the heart of every page.
- **How common**, once, with the orb's number (never a percentage): a personal pair: *about 1 pair of people in 7 has it, one way round or the other, within 6.8°*; Sun, Mercury and Venus together: how far apart the birthdays usually are; `onlyBetweenTwo`: no one has it inside their own chart (Mercury within about 28° of the Sun, Venus about 47°, the two never more than about 75° apart); two slow bodies: **who has it**, one or two examples from `examples` in plain sentences, and Joana's practice: her synastry readings count only contacts with at least one personal body (the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven) and read a contact of two slow bodies through the houses, as a difference between two eras more than between two people.
- The birth time: both charts need one; say where it decides the contact (the Moon moves about 13° a day). Link `natalPage` when it isn't null. Consent where it fits: [why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).
- Any adult pair: couples, friends, family, coworkers. Never "soulmate", "doomed", "karmic debt"; never a prediction of marriage, break-up, conflict, violence or a sexual outcome. A square is friction, never failure; an opposition is a seesaw, never enmity.
