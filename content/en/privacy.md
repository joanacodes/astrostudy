## What I collect, and why

When you write through the form or by email, I receive:

- **your name and email address** — to reply to you;
- **your message** — so I know what you’re asking;
- **the reading you’re interested in**;
- **birth details, if you choose to send them** — date, time and place of birth, yours and, for synastry, the other person’s. They’re only used to prepare your reading.

I use them to answer you and, if we agree on a reading, to prepare and deliver it. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, and then the contract itself. Birth details are never used for anything else: never published, never sold, never used for marketing.

## Someone else’s details

For a synastry reading you may send another person’s birth details. Please do so only in a way you’d be comfortable telling them about — and for co-worker readings, only with their agreement. I use their details for your reading alone.

## Who else touches it

- **FormSubmit** ([formsubmit.co](https://formsubmit.co)) receives what you type in the form and forwards it to my inbox. Their own privacy terms, on their site, explain what they keep.
- **My email provider** stores our messages.
- **{{< host >}}** hosts this website and, like any web host, keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and secure.

These services may store or process data outside the European Union — {{< host >}}, for example, is based in the United States. Each explains its own safeguards on its site.

## No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no advertising trackers. The fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. The moving meadow behind the pages is drawn by your own browser; nothing about it is sent anywhere. The sky on the home page comes from [FreeAstroAPI](https://freeastroapi.com), asked by this site’s own server a few times a day — never by your browser, and nothing about you is sent to them. Your browser then moves the planets on to the minute.

## How long I keep things

Messages that don’t lead to a reading are deleted within twelve months. For readings, I keep what I need for as long as you might come back to me with questions about it, and no longer than two years — unless you ask me to delete it sooner, which you can do at any time.

## Your rights

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit or stop its use, or send it to you in a form you can take elsewhere — and, as French law allows, tell me what should happen to it after your death. Just write to {{< email >}}. If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your data, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)), or to the authority where you live — in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)) — but I’d be grateful for the chance to put it right first.
