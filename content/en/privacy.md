## What I collect, and why

**When you write to me** through the form or by email, I receive your name, your email address and your message — to answer you.

**When you order a reading**, I receive, with your payment:

- **your name and email address** — to deliver your reading and keep in touch about it;
- **the reading and the option you chose**;
- **your birth details** — date, time and place of birth — and, for a synastry, the other person’s first name and birth details;
- **anything you add for me** in the order.

In the order form, birth details reach me only with a payment. They’re used to prepare and deliver your reading, and nothing else: never published, never sold, never used for marketing. The legal basis is the contract between us — and, before it, steps taken at your request.

## Someone else’s details

For a synastry reading, the order asks for another person’s birth details. Give them only if they know you’re asking for this reading — the order form asks you to confirm it — and for co-worker readings, only with their agreement. I use their details for your reading alone.

## The blind chart calls

For the free [blind chart calls](/challenge/), Zoom’s registration form asks for your name, your email address, your date, time and place of birth, how much astrology you know, how you heard about the calls, and your agreement to their rules. I use them to draw your chart, write your portrait for the call and send you the link, and for nothing else. The legal basis is your consent, given in that form: you can withdraw it at any time, by cancelling or by writing to me.

The calls aren’t recorded. Afterwards I publish only how many people picked their own portrait. The registrations, and any note that links a person to a birth date, are deleted within 60 days of the call.

## No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no advertising trackers. The fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. The moving meadow behind the pages is drawn by your own browser; nothing about it is sent anywhere.{{% live-sky %}} The positions of the planets on these pages come from [FreeAstroAPI](https://freeastroapi.com), asked by this site itself a few times a day — never by your browser, and nothing about you is sent to them. Your browser then moves the planets on to the minute.{{% /live-sky %}}

## How long I keep things

Messages that don’t lead to a reading are deleted within twelve months. For readings, I keep what I need for as long as you might come back to me with questions about it, and no longer than two years — unless you ask me to delete it sooner, which you can do at any time.

## Your rights

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit or stop its use, or send it to you in a form you can take elsewhere — and, as French law allows, tell me what should happen to it after your death. Just write to {{< email >}}. If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your data, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)), or to the authority where you live — in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)) — but I’d be grateful for the chance to put it right first.
