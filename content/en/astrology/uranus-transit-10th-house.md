Uranus reaching the top of your chart is change arriving where it shows most: your work, your role, the direction people know you for. From the Midheaven on, the stay lasts nearly eight years as a rule.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reforms: it looks at a structure as a whole and asks what it could become. The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is public life, the role you play, the responsibility you carry, your reputation and what you'd like to leave behind. Under Uranus, a direction that once made sense can start to feel like someone else's plan. You may want to change course, work more independently, take your skills somewhere unexpected, or reshape the role you already have from the inside. The pull is towards work that fits who you've become, even if it fits no ready-made title.

In the natural order the 10th belongs to Capricorn, and so to Saturn, the planet that keeps shapes. Uranus, the planet that breaks them open, crossing Saturn's house looks like the old and the new negotiating in public. Its first act here is to cross your Midheaven ([transiting Uranus conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)), which Placidus, my house system, takes as the door into the 10th. With your Sun in the 10th from birth, the pass can change not only what you do but what you're known for.

None of this predicts a promotion, a dismissal or a change of career; visibility isn't success, and a reputation isn't a verdict. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) describes the weather around your working life, as your chart already sets it out, and the freedom you're asking of it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Placidus charts drawn for a London birth keep Uranus in the 10th for nearly eight years in the middle, just above its all-house figure there, and the 10th is one of the more even houses: most stays fall between just under five years and about ten. For a New York birth the middle is the same and the band a little narrower, from nearly six years to just under ten.

The count begins on the first day Uranus touches your Midheaven, retrograde returns over it included, and ends with its last day in the 10th. The Midheaven goes round all twelve signs in a day, so its degree comes from the minute you were born, which is why that minute comes before any reading I give ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Uranus arrives at the Midheaven from your 9th, where beliefs were being remade ([Uranus transiting the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-9th-house/)), and later moves on among the friends, groups and shared plans of your 11th ([Uranus transiting the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-11th-house/)).

The years to 2033 belong to Midheavens in Gemini: Uranus is in that sign from July 2025 to May 2033. From August 2032 to August 2039 it's the turn of Midheavens in Cancer, and from August 2039 of Leo ones. A Midheaven in Taurus was crossed between May 2018 and April 2026, so that 10th may already be well into its stay.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Draft the role you'd want before leaving the one you have. Uranus in the 10th rewards a clear picture of the new direction more than a dramatic exit from the old one.

Test an independent idea in public at a small scale: a side project, a proposal, a piece of work signed with your own name. Being seen is how the 10th learns what works.

Keep the responsibilities that still matter to you while you change the rest. Freedom at work is easier to win from a position people already trust.
