A composite Sun in Aries puts the centre of your relationship's chart in the first sign of the zodiac. It describes the bond the two of you make rather than either of you, and that bond is never more itself than when it's beginning something.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) moves every planet to the halfway point between its places in two birth charts, by the shorter way round. For the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), that point sits close to the date halfway between your two birthdays, counted the short way round the year: when that date falls between about 21 March and 19 April, the composite Sun is usually in [Aries](/astrology/aries/). Born on 5 March and on 5 May, a Pisces and a Taurus share a composite Sun at about 15° Aries. In the site's count of two million random pairs born between 1930 and 2009, about 1 pair in 12 has it in this sign.

Read as the relationship's own Sun, it puts the bond's identity in the act of starting. Such a pair tends to feel most like itself in the first week of a project, on the first morning in a new city, at the moment one of you says *let's* and the other is already reaching for a coat. Vitality, for this bond, is a matter of momentum. Ask what the relationship is for and the answer tends to be a verb: going, trying, building the next thing. It also speaks plainly: what you want together is usually said out loud, and early.

Tradition places the Sun's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Aries and gives the sign to [Mars](/astrology/mars/). In a composite, Mars is a midpoint as well, and its sign and house show where the bond spends the energy this Sun gives it. An exalted composite Sun isn't a better relationship; it's one with a strong, simple sense of what it's for. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) of a birth chart describes one person's self, while this one belongs to neither of you: two unhurried people can make a bond that dares more than either would alone.

Where it struggles is the stretch where nothing starts. A relationship that knows itself by its beginnings can lose sight of itself in upkeep: the third year of a renovation, a long wait for news. That quiet isn't an ending, and a composite has no say over how long two people stay together. It's a bond that still has to learn it exists between projects too. Its neighbours find their centre elsewhere: the [composite Sun in Pisces](/astrology/composite-sun-in-pisces/) in a feeling the two of you share without needing to name it, the [composite Sun in Taurus](/astrology/composite-sun-in-taurus/) in what you keep and tend.

This page describes a technique I don't use myself, so take it as a description rather than a reading: astrologers who use composites set them beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), never alone. And since it's made from two people's birth data, it's one more thing to start together, with both of you knowing and agreeing ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
