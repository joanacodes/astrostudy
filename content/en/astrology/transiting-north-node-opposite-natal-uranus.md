Nine years or so after the North Node crosses your Uranus, the South Node reaches it. For a month or so, your oldest ways of standing apart are lit from the familiar end of the axis: the freedoms you fought for, and the ones that have quietly turned into habit.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part of a chart that refuses what doesn't fit: independence, difference, reform. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) is capacity already consolidated, ready to hand and sometimes overused; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), across the chart, is capacity still being practised. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two ends through polarity and awareness, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your Uranus stands at the familiar pole, in plain view of the less familiar one.

What shows is independence as a reflex. Leaving before you can be asked to stay. Saying no to a rule on principle, before reading it. Keeping a distance that once protected you and now mostly keeps you apart. None of it is a fault: your capacity to go your own way is real and stays yours. The North Node, opposite, often arrives through other people, as an arrangement you could join without losing yourself. Nothing here foretells a sudden break, in work, love or anywhere else.

For a generation, the dates are shared. From July 2026 to March 2028 the South Node crosses Leo, and with it the Uranus of everyone born with [Uranus in Leo](/astrology/uranus-in-leo/), from August 1955 to August 1962. The North Node crossed the same degrees while it was in Leo, from May 2017 to November 2018, and will again from November 2035 to May 2037. For many born in 1957 and 1958, whose Uranus sits near 10° Leo, the total solar eclipse of 2 August 2027, at 9°55′ Leo, falls on their Uranus about two months before the South Node does. Each person's houses, which need a birth time, say where it lands ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My orbs open 1½° before exact and close 1° after, and the true node usually takes about a month to pass between them. When an eclipse season falls on the transit, the node hovers near your Uranus while the Sun is close, and the pass lasts three months or more; about one pass in 25 crosses the degree three times. Eclipses that land within about 3° count as contacts of their own ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Once a round makes four or five by 80. The first came at 11.1 for someone born on 1 January 1975 and at 17.6 for 1 January 1985. With [Uranus opposite North Node](/astrology/uranus-opposite-north-node/) in your chart, Uranus sits on your natal South Node, and the transit comes back at each nodal return.

On either side, about three years away, lie [Transiting North Node trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-uranus/), then [Transiting North Node square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-uranus/) and [Transiting North Node sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-uranus/), and nine years away [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-uranus/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Catch the reflex. The next time you feel the urge to leave or refuse, wait a day before you do.

Stay in one arrangement a little longer than is comfortable, and see what it gives back.

Ask what freedom means to you now, not what it meant when you first claimed it. The answer may have changed.
