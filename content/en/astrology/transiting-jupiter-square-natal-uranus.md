Twice in each Jupiter lap, the planet of growth squares the degree your Uranus held at birth. The part of you that wants more and the part that wants out start pushing each other, and for a few days, sometimes for months, everything seems ready to be changed at once.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is your need to do things your own way: to question, to experiment, to reform what doesn't work. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) wants growth and a principle that explains it. Under a [square](/astrology/square/), constructive friction between them, growth and change stop agreeing on the route. The result is usually restlessness: a bigger change than you'd planned, wanted sooner than is wise, or a new idea that won't sit alongside a conviction you've held for years.

Both planets have a weak spot here, and the square presses on it. Jupiter can mistake a belief for a certainty; Uranus can mistake being different for being right, or change a system before understanding what in it still works. That is why I read this square as a test rather than a verdict: the change worth making is the one that survives your own questions.

None of it predicts a break-up, a resignation or a sudden loss, and I don't read [transits](/astrology/transits/) for those ([How I read](/how-i-read/)). Where the restlessness gathers depends on your Uranus's house and the house Jupiter is crossing, which only the birth time can show ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Jupiter square Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-square-uranus/), you'll meet one of these squares close to every [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Jupiter's straight passes through this square take about eleven days, counting from 1½° before exact to 1° after. Loops are the exception, three in ten: Jupiter then crosses three times and the square stays some eight months. Expect thirteen or fourteen by 80 if you were born between 1930 and 2009.

Born on 1 January 1965, people met the first at about six months; born on 1 January 1945, not until about six years. After that the squares fall a quarter of the way round from each crossing of your Uranus, roughly three years either side. The first of a pair tends to test an idea that has already started; the second, three years before Jupiter comes back, asks which changes you'll still want by then.

Of Jupiter's five contacts with your Uranus, this is the one that makes change justify itself. [Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-uranus/) introduces the idea, [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-uranus/) smooth its way, and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-uranus/) holds it up against other people. [Transiting Uranus square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-jupiter/) is the long version, many months near exact.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change one thing at a time, and keep a way back. If the new arrangement holds for a month, you can go further; if it doesn't, you've lost little.

Put the new idea and the old conviction side by side and ask what would change your mind about each. Whichever survives the question deserves the room.

Notice whether you want change or just movement. A long walk, a trip, a new project in a quiet corner of life can absorb restlessness that would otherwise land on everything you've built.
