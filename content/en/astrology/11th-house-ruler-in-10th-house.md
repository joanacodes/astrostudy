In the old courts, the king had his council: the advisers and allies who stood just behind the throne. Place the ruler of your 11th house in the 10th and your own council works close to your public role, and the people you choose tend to stand beside you at work.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is the life you lead in public: your work, your standing, the reputation you earn and the mark you'd like to leave. It opens at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the top of the chart. The [11th](/astrology/11th-house/) follows it, and holds the people you're allied with and the aims you keep for the long run. With the 11th's ruler placed in the 10th ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), those allies come to stand where your work is seen.

Tradition tied the two houses closely. Because the 11th comes after the 10th, the house of the king, it was given the king's advisers, his council and his allies, and a share of worldly eminence. In a modern life I read that as a working world full of friends: peers who recommend you, a professional body you help to run, a cause you end up representing in public, a team whose loyalty outlasts any one job.

Your career often moves through people. A friend's introduction opens a door, a network you built for its own sake turns out to be where the work is, a group's goal becomes your job. What you hope for has a public face as well: a role, a project with your name on it, a change you'd like to make in your field. The risk is friendships that live and die with your title, and the best allies are the ones who would still ring if you changed careers.

Counted by sign, neighbouring houses make no aspect ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)), so in the old geometry the 10th and the 11th don't see each other. In [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), though, a house rarely matches a sign exactly, and the ruler of the 11th often stands in the 10th while still in the sign on the 11th cusp, which is its own sign ([domicile](/astrology/domicile/)): at home by sign, a few degrees short of its house. Then your public role and your circle sit closer than the house count suggests.

A Capricorn 11th belongs to Saturn, which builds a professional network slowly and keeps it for life. Saturn is a ruler I give weight to here: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), it was among the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time. When the 11th cusp falls in Scorpio, there are two planets to follow, Mars and Pluto, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 10th fights for its allies in public, while Pluto, wherever it stands, changes the groups you lead or is changed by them.

None of this promises success or fame: it shows where your allies are, not how far you'll rise. The Midheaven moves quickly with the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A ruler just short of it, about to culminate, would have crossed into the 9th with a later birth, and your friendships would spread abroad and into ideas ([Ruler of the 11th in the 9th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)); one near the 11th cusp would have been at home in the 11th with an earlier birth ([Ruler of the 11th in the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)).
