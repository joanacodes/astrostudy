Their names appear side by side on the company website, the campaign leaflet or the sign above the shop. Composite Mars in the 10th house belongs to couples who strive in public.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

At the top of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) stands the composite [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), and the house that begins there, [the 10th](/astrology/10th-house/), covers career, public role, reputation and what you are known for. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) there spends the relationship's energy on achievement in view of others. The house is angular, and Capricorn, its natural sign, is the one tradition gives Mars as its exaltation; the house shares that sign's taste for disciplined, visible work.

This couple works in public. It runs a business together, leads a campaign, builds a practice or a restaurant that carries both names, or pushes each other's careers forward with real energy. People see it as effective, a pair that gets things done, and its ambitions are said out loud. Desire is bound up with respect: admiration for what the other achieves is part of the attraction. Close to the composite Midheaven itself, Mars is at its most prominent, and the pair's drive is what people mention first when your names come up. Opposite lies the 4th, the house of home, which this placement can leave quieter than either of you would like. [Mars in the 10th house](/astrology/mars-in-10th-house/), natally, is ambition that shows; [their Mars in your 10th house](/astrology/mars-in-10th-house-synastry/) can push your public life forward or put it under pressure; in the composite, ambition belongs to the relationship.

The difficulty is rivalry between two careers. Whose job decides where you live, who takes the promotion, who steps back for a season: these questions can turn the bond into a contest, and the couple's public image may come to outweigh the private life behind it. Achievement isn't status, and a shared reputation isn't a relationship. What helps is taking turns at the front, celebrating each other's wins without keeping a tally, and guarding time that no one outside ever sees.

Here the two house methods meet: in either one, the composite Midheaven is the point halfway between your two Midheavens. It still needs both exact birth times, because four minutes either way move each Midheaven by about a degree ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)). The house's far edge, the 11th cusp, depends on the method chosen.

Public rivalry is friction. Being made afraid in private, whatever the couple looks like in public, is another matter and no chart question: call 999 in a UK emergency, or Samaritans on 116 123.

Composites aren't part of the work I do, so take this as the method's reading. Its users never take it without the partners' natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) at hand; no promotion, fame or fortune can be read in it. [Composite Mars in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-9th-house/) chases meaning; [composite Mars in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-11th-house/) works with friends and groups; the 10th wants a summit with a name on it. A chart that maps a public life waits for both people's consent ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
