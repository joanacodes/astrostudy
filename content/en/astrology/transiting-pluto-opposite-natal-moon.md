Pluto opposite your Moon arrives through other people. For two to three years the planet of transformation faces your Moon from across the chart, and what you need in order to feel safe is mirrored, sometimes intensely, in the people you're closest to.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) brings hidden motives and attachments to the surface so that what can't go on can be reorganised. Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds your needs and your ways of attaching. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates them through polarity: under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) you see your own needs most clearly in someone else's reaction to them. A partner who wants more closeness, or less. A parent's expectations, met again as an adult. A friendship where one of you has always done the looking after.

Being seen like this can feel like exposure. It isn't humiliation: needs that someone else can see are needs that can finally be talked about. Attachment isn't dependency, and caring for others isn't the same as giving yourself up; the opposition is often where that line gets drawn again, from both sides.

Pluto is intensity and change, never control presented as fate. If someone close to you uses that closeness to control where you go, whom you see or what you spend, that is not a season to sit out until Pluto moves on: talk to someone you trust, or to someone trained to help ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

With your Moon in the 10th and Pluto crossing the 4th ([Pluto transiting the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-4th-house/)), the feelings you carry into your working life meet the home and family you go back to. The houses, and the Moon's exact degree, come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Moon opposite Pluto](/astrology/moon-opposite-pluto/), you met this transit in your first years or not at all, and what comes later is Pluto's square to its own place ([Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/)), which reaches your Moon by square too.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs of my timing, the opposition lasts about two years and eight months, and about twenty-two months in the quickest passes. Pluto backs up over the exact degree each year, so there are three exact hits, or five in about two passes out of five.

Across Pluto's circle from the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-moon/)), the opposition is just as rare: more than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet it by 80, and nobody meets it twice, since Pluto would have to go all the way round. The squares ([Transiting Pluto square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-moon/)) sit halfway between the two, the trines ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-moon/)) 60° either side of the opposition, and the sextiles ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-moon/)) 60° either side of the conjunction.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it opposes Moons in Leo.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say what you need before it hardens into a demand. Under an opposition, a need left unspoken tends to come back as a quarrel about something else.

Notice your old defences when someone else's feelings run high: going quiet, taking charge, leaving the room. Ask whether the moment really needs them.

Allow some distance inside closeness. Time apart, a room of your own, an evening with friends: these keep a bond breathing while Pluto presses on it.
