Nine years or so after the North Node crossed your Saturn, the South Node arrives on it. For a month or so, the structures you have leaned on longest sit at the familiar end of the axis, and the less practised direction looks at them from the other side of the chart.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is where a chart has built its frame: commitments, the rules you live by, the work you can be counted on to do. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) consolidates: capacity already established, quick to reach for, sometimes reached for too often. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), opposite, is capacity still being practised. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) works through polarity and awareness, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your Saturn is seen from the far end: what you carry because it matters, and what you carry because you always have.

It tends to show as duty on autopilot. The role in the family you took on early. A standard you hold yourself to without remembering why. The way you take charge before anyone has asked. These structures are real achievements, and nothing asks you to pull them down; the month asks which of them are still load-bearing. Across the chart, the North Node points to the sign and house opposite your Saturn, where a lighter, less guarded way of working waits to be practised.

With Saturn in your 3rd, the South Node crosses your 3rd while the North Node crosses your 9th ([North Node transiting the 9th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-9th-house/)): the everyday duties you manage so well, the messages answered and errands run, meet an invitation to look further, towards a course, a journey or a view of your own. That reading rests on your houses, which rest on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the South Node usually spends about a month over your Saturn. In an eclipse season it can stay three months or more, lingering near the degree while the Sun is close; about one pass in 25 brings three exact hits. This is the second of the two stretches of each round in which an eclipse can reach your Saturn: in the year or so around this transit, one may fall on it from the South Node end, and I count it within about 3° ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

It comes once a round, four or five times by 80. The first came at 11.7 for someone born on 1 January 1955 and at 9.8 for 1 January 1945; for someone born on 1 January 1985, at three weeks old. From July 2026 to March 2028 the South Node crosses Leo, and with it the Saturn of everyone born with [Saturn in Leo](/astrology/saturn-in-leo/), in the years Saturn spent there: August 1946 to May 1949, September 1975 to July 1978, July 2005 to September 2007. With [Saturn opposite North Node](/astrology/saturn-opposite-north-node/) in your chart, your Saturn sits on your natal South Node, and each nodal return brings this transit round again.

About three years either side of it come the trines ([Transiting North Node trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-saturn/)); further out lie the squares ([Transiting North Node square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-saturn/)) and the sextiles ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-saturn/)), and nine years away, the North Node's own crossing ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-saturn/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

List what you do for others without being asked. Keep what you would choose again today, and let one item go.

Practise the far end in small, low-stakes ways: whatever the sign and house opposite your Saturn ask for, an hour at a time.

Look back nine years, to the North Node's crossing of your Saturn. What you began then is often what this month asks you to consolidate, or to release.
