Three years after Jupiter crosses the top of your chart, and three years before it gets back there, it stands at right angles to your Midheaven. A square to one end of the meridian is a square to the other, so your public role and your private base feel the same push at once.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) enlarges whatever it touches and looks for its meaning; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where your capacities take a visible form: a role, the obligations you carry, the contribution you're known for. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through constructive friction. In this [transit](/astrology/transits/), growth tends to arrive as more than your present arrangement holds: a bigger brief, a second commitment, an idea of your work that has outgrown the job that hosts it.

Because the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) sits exactly opposite, the square presses on your private foundations too. Evenings, family plans and the need for rest put in their claims just as work asks for more, and the adjustment is usually about proportions rather than choosing a side.

In Placidus, the first square of a round usually comes while Jupiter crosses your 12th or 1st house, near your Ascendant, so the push starts with you: a personal wish to grow that the role no longer fits ([Jupiter transiting the 12th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-12th-house/)). The second usually comes from your 6th or 7th, near your Descendant, through the daily workload or the people you work with. Either way, more responsibility isn't more worth, and this square is a good time to remember it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the square holds your Midheaven for about eleven days in seven passes out of ten. In the other three, [Jupiter retrograde](/astrology/jupiter-retrograde/) brings it back across, three exact hits in about eight months.

Your birth time has to be right to the minute, and one mistake is worth checking before any other. A birth in summer time, entered without the hour's shift, moves the Midheaven by about fifteen degrees, which would put this square months away from its real dates. It's one of the reasons the time comes before the reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have had thirteen or fourteen of these squares. Each round runs from [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-midheaven/) through [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-midheaven/) to the first square at about three years; [transiting Jupiter trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-midheaven/) eases things a year later, and after [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-midheaven/) at six, the second square comes at about nine.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List what you've taken on since Jupiter last crossed your Midheaven, and cross out what no longer fits where you're heading. This square asks for editing more than for effort.

Keep one fixed block of time at home and treat it like a meeting. With the IC squared as well, rest booked in advance is easier to keep than rest you hope to find.

Negotiate the size of a role before you turn it down. Often the growth is right and only the scale is wrong.
