Venus in the 4th house keeps what it values most out of public view: at home, among the people you call yours, in the private self underneath. The planet of value and relating sits at the very bottom of the chart. I read it as a gift for making a home people are glad to return to, and a peace under your roof that is worth keeping honest.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) holds home, roots and family: where you come from, what you build in order to belong, the self you are when the door is shut. It is angular, and in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, it begins at the Imum Coeli, the point opposite the Midheaven. Its natural sign is Cancer. With [Venus](/astrology/venus/) here, pleasure and harmony are found mostly in private: a kitchen table, a garden, a room arranged just so, an evening with the people who know you best.

Venus is never far from the Sun, so Venus in the 4th usually goes with a birth in the night, around midnight, Venus itself far below your feet. The Imum Coeli is a sensitive point for timing. A birth a few minutes later can carry Venus across it into [Venus in the 3rd house](/astrology/venus-in-3rd-house/); an earlier one can leave it in [Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house/). Without an exact time I can't place it at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, a 4th-house Venus wants to make a home with someone, in whatever form home takes for you. You tend to show affection by hosting, cooking and creating comfort, and you may feel closest to a partner on an ordinary evening in. Family matters, and its welcome of a partner, or the lack of one, can weigh more than you expect. The house across is the 10th, the house of public life, so this Venus often saves its tenderness for private hours. The risk is protecting the peace at home so carefully that a needed argument never happens.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Venus values a setting that feels like a refuge: a workplace with warmth in it, or the freedom to work from home. Interiors, architecture, property, hospitality, gardening, family businesses, local history and archives can suit it. You may be the colleague who makes the office kitchen pleasant, or who keeps a small team feeling like a household. Work that takes you away from home for long stretches can cost this Venus more than it seems from outside.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Home can turn into a hiding place. When the world feels harsh, you may retreat behind your own door and stay there, letting invitations lapse. Nostalgia can paint the family past more kindly than it was, or keep you loyal to an old home's way of loving. And the wish for peace under your roof can mean swallowing disagreements that would have been better aired.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let your home hold disagreements as well as comfort: a household where people can argue kindly is more peaceful in the long run. Invite people in, rather than only retreating. Make one corner, one ritual, one object entirely your own, chosen for your taste and not the family's. And if your roots were difficult, the home this Venus describes can be one you build now, not only the one you came from.

Venus held about a third of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), along with the other placements everyone reads first. In the 4th house it speaks of a private life I usually know least about at a first reading, so I ask before I assume anything.
