With Aquarius on the 3rd house cusp, the everyday mind runs on fixed air: talk, messages, errands and the first things you learned all take the manner of the sign of the group. Conversation is an exchange between equals, and you'd rather see how something works than be told that it does.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is the near world, what you say, read and run out for in an ordinary week, and tradition called it the Goddess. With [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) on its cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), that near world works like a network. You tend to talk to the neighbour, the cousin and the colleague from another department in the same open way, keep several threads going at once, and know who to ask about almost anything. At school you may have been the pupil who wanted to know why the rule was the rule. Brothers and sisters, where there are any, often feel more like friends or fellow members of a club than like family duties, and if you grew up the odd one out among them, that belongs to this cusp too. The fixed side of the sign shows in your opinions: slow to form, kept for years, and then sometimes dropped all at once for a better idea.

Uranus and Saturn share the rule of your everyday talk, and I read both. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) shows where your thinking takes sudden turns and where new ways of talking to people catch on with you first; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) shows where you learn patiently, check your facts and keep your word. In 3 London charts in 5, and nearly 4 New York charts in 5, Capricorn opens the 2nd as well, so Saturn rules what you earn as well as how you think and speak, and words, teaching or explaining can become part of a living. The house each ruler stands in is where your talk and your errands end up serving: as ruler of the 3rd in the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Over a whole day of births, Placidus, the house system I use, puts Aquarius on the 3rd cusp about once in 14 at London and once in 13 at New York. That's commoner than on the 2nd: two cusps below the eastern horizon, this one moves through the sign less hurriedly than the cusps beside the Ascendant. Pisces usually waits on the IC below, in nearly 3 London charts in 4 and 4 New York charts in 5, so an airy, talkative near world sits over a softer, more private home. In the rest, the 3rd is wide enough, at about 39° in London, to hold the whole of Pisces ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and Neptune and Jupiter then lend your daily mind their imagination. Scorpio rises with this cusp in just over half the London charts and Sagittarius in the others, while at New York Sagittarius rises in 7 in 10. Aquarius can also miss the cusps altogether: in about 1 London chart in 7, it has none.

Born later that day, you would have had Aquarius on the 2nd cusp ([Aquarius on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/)); earlier, on the IC ([Aquarius on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/)). The clock decides, and without it I don't read the chart at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
