Of Neptune's contacts with your Sun, the sextile asks the least and offers quietly. For about two and a half years, a more imaginative or more generous version of your purpose is within reach, and nothing will hand it to you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) opens: imagination, compassion, the sense of belonging to something larger. Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) gives your life a centre and a direction. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, joins two functions through opportunity, and opportunity asks to be taken up. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), your identity stays clear while it gains a softer edge: a creative project you'd put aside, a cause that asks for your time, a way of working that serves more than your own ambition. The sextile doesn't make you take any of it up; it makes the first step easier than usual.

Your Sun's house and the house Neptune is crossing show where the opening lies. With your Sun in the 5th and Neptune moving through your 3rd ([Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/)), what you write, learn or say can carry what you love doing; with the Sun in the 11th and Neptune in the 9th, a shared ideal and a wider belief find each other. Your birth time fixes both houses, so that's where I begin ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Neptune's blind spot here is assuming the ideal will look after itself. A sextile works when the dream gets an appointment in the diary.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Neptune keeps the sextile for about two years and seven months, or twenty-two months in the quickest passes. Every pass brings more than one exact hit: three as a rule, five in about a quarter of passes.

Before 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 meet this sextile once and some twice, while about 1 in 6 never do. Neptune reaches one sextile after the square ([transiting Neptune square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-sun/)) and before the conjunction ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-sun/)), the other after the conjunction; the trine ([transiting Neptune trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-sun/)) and the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-sun/)) lie further round. Which of them a life holds depends on how far Neptune stood from your Sun at birth. Born with [Sun sextile Neptune](/astrology/sun-sextile-neptune/), you began life under one of the two sextiles; Neptune never returns to it, though it may reach the other.

Until March 2039, the sextile from Aries goes to Gemini birthdays, about 21 May to 20 June, and Aquarius ones, about 20 January to 18 February, the earlier birthdays in each sign first ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to the creative or caring project that keeps coming back to mind. A sextile turns into something only when it's used.

Give it a form with edges: a course with a start date, a weekly hour, a piece of work with a deadline. Neptune's gifts last longer in a container.

Share the idea with one person who will ask about it later. An opening is easier to keep when somebody else knows it's there.
