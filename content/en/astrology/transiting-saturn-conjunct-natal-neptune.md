Every 29½ years or so, Saturn passes over the degree Neptune held when you were born. It isn't the [Saturn–Neptune conjunction of 2026](/astrology/saturn-neptune-conjunction-of-2026/), when the two planets met in the sky: this meeting happens in your chart alone, and asks a dream to show what it looks like on an ordinary weekday.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) tests what will hold over time; [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines, forgives, and loosens boundaries that are no longer needed. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses them, so for the length of this [transit](/astrology/transits/) imagination and reality-testing work as one process. A hope you've carried for years can get its first practical shape: a course enrolled in, a practice with a time and a place, a vocation you finally put a name to. Some ideals shrink on the way, to the size you can actually live, and I read that shrinking as definition rather than loss.

Neptune's blind spot is taking what you imagine for what you know, and Saturn is very good at telling the two apart. People often describe a sobering: a person, a plan or a belief seen more plainly than before. Disillusion ends an illusion, not the dream behind it. If what comes up feels heavier than disappointment, and stays, please take it to a doctor or a therapist ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)); a transit can name a season, not treat it.

Your Neptune's house shows where the dream lives, and the crossing gives it Saturn's attention there. With Neptune in your 5th, it may be an art you've played at for years; in your 11th, a cause or a community you'd like to build. Houses need your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In a birth chart, [Saturn conjunct Neptune](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-neptune/) is this meeting fixed for life; as a transit, it's a season, and it passes.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune barely moves while Saturn goes by, so Saturn's pace sets the length: about nine months within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after. A little more often than not the crossing comes in three exact passes as Saturn loops back, stretching it to about nine and a half months; one clean pass can take three weeks.

It comes about every 29½ years, two or three times by 80, and nobody misses it. Neptune needs about fourteen years to cross a sign, so everyone born in the same year meets it within a year or so of the same age: about 3 if you were born in 1985, then about 33 and 62; about 28 if you were born in 1955, then about 57. People born between 1984 and 1998, with Neptune in Capricorn, had their latest one while Saturn crossed Capricorn, from December 2017 to 2020; those born between 1998 and 2012 had theirs during Saturn's years in Aquarius, until March 2023.

The crossing opens Saturn's round with your Neptune. [Transiting Saturn sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-neptune/) follows about five years later, [transiting Saturn square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-neptune/) about seven, [transiting Saturn trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-neptune/) about ten, and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-neptune/) marks the halfway point, about fifteen years on.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write the dream down with one concrete step and a date beside it. Saturn on Neptune rewards an ideal that agrees to be tried.

Give a practice a fixed place: the same hour, the same room, every week. Imagination tends to turn up more readily when it knows where to find you.

Check one belief against the facts, kindly. What survives the check is worth building on; what doesn't was costing you more than it gave.
