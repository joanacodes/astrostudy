A third of the way round from your Mars, in either direction, Uranus trines it: once some 28 years before crossing it and once some 28 years after. For about twenty months each time, effort runs smoothly in new directions.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is drive and execution, the push that turns an intention into a result. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) adds invention and autonomy. With a [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, two functions back each other without friction, so effort and originality travel together: a new method works the first time, an independent project gets moving without a struggle, and a change of plan doesn't cost you your momentum.

This is the ease [Mars trine Uranus](/astrology/mars-trine-uranus/) gives some people from birth: acting on a new idea without first arguing with yourself. Under the transit you may find yourself training differently, reorganising how you work, or taking on alone something you'd once have done only with permission.

What a trine asks of Mars is a target. Without one, the extra drive turns into restlessness, a dozen starts and no finish, and the twenty months go by.

Independence is the theme Uranus brings to Mars, and under a trine it rarely costs you allies. Going freelance, running a project your own way or changing how a team works meets friendlier conditions now than under the conjunction or the squares, where the same wish runs into more resistance.

With Mars in your 6th and Uranus crossing your 2nd ([Uranus transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-2nd-house/)), changing how you work day to day changes how you value your effort: a method of your own, hours that suit you, work you're proud to put your name to. Neither house exists without your birth time, and I don't read the trine without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, my orbs for timing, the trine stays about twenty months, and only the shortest tenth of passes are under a year. In more than nine passes out of ten there are three exact hits, which gives the momentum three pushes rather than one.

The first trine comes about a third of the way round from [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-mars/), after [transiting Uranus sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-mars/) and [transiting Uranus square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-mars/); [transiting Uranus opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-mars/) follows some fourteen years later, and the second trine some fourteen years after that. By 80, two trines is the norm and one the least.

Born with [Mars conjunct Uranus](/astrology/mars-conjunct-uranus/), you'll meet both trines at about 28 and 56, when Uranus also trines its own birth place; for you, this [transit](/astrology/transits/) and that milestone arrive together.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Start the project you've been circling. The trine lowers the cost of beginning, and it won't keep it low forever.

Change one working method for good. A trine is when a better way of doing a task sticks, so swap the old one rather than keeping both.

Lend your momentum. A colleague's or a friend's project can ride on the ease you have now, and effort shared under this trine tends to go further.
