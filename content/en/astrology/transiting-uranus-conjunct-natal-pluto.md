Which side of 1965–66 you were born on, the years Uranus and Pluto stood together in Virgo, decides whether transiting Uranus reaches your natal Pluto in your youth, late in life or not at all. When it does, the urge to break free and the pressure to renew at the root share one degree for about twenty months.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the function of depth and transformation: what you hold on to hardest, and the power to let it go and begin again. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) frees and reforms. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) co-activates two functions into one process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) liberation and renewal stop being separate wishes.

It tends to work at the roots: a pattern you've lived inside for years, a fear, a loyalty, a way of holding power or of giving it away, comes into view and loosens. Change that had been building out of sight can surface quickly once it starts. Pluto here is renewal, not destruction, and the transit names no ending.

Pluto can confuse control with safety; Uranus can assume that disruption produces progress. Together they push for a dramatic break where a slow release would serve better, so under this conjunction I'd look for the one grip you can loosen rather than everything at once.

With Pluto in your 8th, Uranus crosses it there ([Uranus transiting the 8th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-8th-house/)): trust, intimacy and what you share with others are where the old pattern gives. Tradition gave the 8th to death; I read it as shared bonds and deep change, and I never read a chart for a death ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). That house, like every house, needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Change at the roots rarely finishes in one pass, and this transit has three: Uranus holds the conjunction within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, for about twenty months, eleven when the pass is short, touching your Pluto three times in more than nine passes out of ten. It comes once at most.

Born before the mid-1960s, you had Uranus trailing your Pluto, and it caught up early: in the site's count at about 20 for someone born at the start of 1935, about 13 for 1945 and about 6 for 1955, which for most of that generation means a transit of youth, read only in hindsight. Born in 1963–68 with [Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/), you had it at birth or in your first year.

Born after the meeting, you started life with Uranus already past Pluto, and it needs most of its round to come back: about 79 years for someone born at the start of 1975, about 74 for 1985, about 70 for 1995 and about 65 for 2005. For most births from mid-1967 to early 1972 it falls after 80, which is why about 1 person in 17 of the births from 1930 to 2009 never meets it ([generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/)).

For births before 1965 the conjunction opened the round and the other steps followed it; for births after 1966, [transiting Uranus sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-pluto/), [transiting Uranus square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-pluto/), [transiting Uranus trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-pluto/) and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-pluto/) all come before it, the opposition in the twenties, thirties or early forties.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the old pattern in one sentence. Uranus brings it into view; writing it down keeps it there long enough to change.

Loosen one grip at a time. Pluto renews best gradually, even when Uranus is in a hurry.

Get support for the deep work. A trusted person, or a therapist if the pattern weighs heavily, will help you more in these months than any chart.
