Earth meets earth here. The Sun in Taurus wants to make something good and keep it; the Moon in Virgo settles once that thing works properly, down to the last detail. I think of it as a workshop pair, born under a Moon already past its first quarter.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Four signs apart and both [earth signs](/astrology/earth-signs/), the two lights make a trine by sign: they share an element and a taste for what is real, useful and well made. The modes differ, fixed for Taurus and mutable for Virgo. The [Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/) wants to make something solid and enjoy it; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) keeps adjusting it until it's right. Most days the two cooperate with very little fuss: your purpose gives the Moon something worth refining, and the Moon's care keeps the Sun's work in good order.

Tradition adds one wrinkle. Your Sun's ruler, [Venus](/astrology/venus/), finds Virgo, your Moon's sign, the hardest place to be, since tradition puts its fall there: pleasure and the sense of worth are least at ease where the critic lives. I read that as a pair that can enjoy its work only once it's done properly, and can spoil a good evening by noticing the one thing out of place. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of Virgo, is the planet to look at for how your feelings get sorted, named and filed. Neither light has a dignity of its own here.

The Moon was more than a quarter of the way round from the Sun and less than halfway, between 91° and 149°; in days, that is from about day seven and a half of the lunar month to day twelve. An astronomer would call that a waxing gibbous Moon; the eight-phase count, whose gibbous phase begins only at 135°, files seven of these births in eight under the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) and the rest under the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/). If the gap was close to 120°, the trine is exact as well as by sign: a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the two ways of counting.

Roughly 1 person in 139 is born with both lights in earth like this. Born on a day when the Moon was changing sign, when it left Leo for Virgo or Virgo for Libra, you need the time to know which pair is yours, this one, the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-leo/) or the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-libra/), each of those Moon signs lasting only about two and a half days ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign needs that time too; until it's known the chart has no outline, only two lights floating free, and the details a Virgo Moon will rightly ask about come later still.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You show love through usefulness and constancy: the repaired shelf, the meal planned around someone's tastes, a steady presence year after year. Partners usually feel very well looked after. What may need practice is enjoyment without improvement: an evening, a present or a holiday taken as it comes, without adjusting anything. Let a partner do things their way now and then, and let the Taurus Sun's pleasure in being cared for win over the Virgo Moon's urge to correct.

## At work {#at-work}

Craft suits this pair: work in which patience and precision both count, and the result can be seen, used and improved. You tend to be the one who does the job properly and keeps doing it properly, which colleagues come to rely on. Chaotic workplaces and rushed jobs bother both lights, the Sun because nothing lasts and the Moon because nothing is right. Choose places where quality is valued, and learn to say when good enough really is enough.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two earth signs can make a life that is very well run and not much enjoyed. The Virgo Moon finds what needs fixing; the Taurus Sun holds on to routines that work; together they can turn every free afternoon into maintenance. Worry can become tidying, and tidying a reason not to rest. What helps is protecting Venus's share on purpose: book something purely for enjoyment, a meal, a walk, an evening of music, and leave it exactly as it is.
