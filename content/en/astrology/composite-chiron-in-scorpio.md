Some pairs share a bank account and the keys to everything, yet never say what frightens them; others confide all their fears and won't share a drawer. Composite Chiron in Scorpio makes trust the relationship's tender spot, and describes the careful way two people decide, together, what can be opened.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks the place in a chart where something keeps catching, and where a way of working round it slowly becomes expertise. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), drawn from the midpoints of two birth charts, that place belongs to the relationship. [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), fixed water, puts it in the bond's depths: trust, intimacy, what is pooled and what is guarded, who holds the power when things get hard. The pair may find exposure frightening even with each other, test each other's loyalty, or keep one part of the bond sealed off, as though opening it would cost too much.

The sign has two rulers, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) in modern tropical astrology and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) in the tradition, and I read both: in a composite each is a midpoint too, and where they fall shows where the bond does its work on trust. That work belongs to the pair. Two people who trust easily elsewhere can find that, together, everything feels weighty; two guarded people can find in each other the one place to set the armour down. The adapting is gradual and chosen: confidences offered a little at a time, agreements about what stays between the two of you, crises met side by side and talked over afterwards.

In two million pairs of birth dates drawn from 1930 to 2009, about 1 pair in 22 had it, few by the standards of composite Chiron. Its same-age pairs come from the births of November 1946 to November 1948 and of December 1996 to September 1999. Most others are a few years apart: born in June 1994 and June 2003, two people have Chirons at about 5° Virgo and 17° Capricorn, and halfway between lies about 11° Scorpio. A handful arrive across a whole generation, from two Chirons nearly opposite. Born in July 1955 and April 1992, two people have theirs at about 3° Aquarius and 3° Leo, and the composite lands at about 3° Scorpio or 3° Taurus, depending on the days.

[Chiron in Scorpio](/astrology/chiron-in-scorpio/) in one birth chart concerns a single person's guard. The composite's guard is shared, and each of you may meet it as the other's secrecy, or as the other's probing.

Its hard side is control. A bond tender about trust can turn watchful, keep a record of old wounds, or use what it knows as leverage in an argument. Saying what you're afraid of, plainly and early, does more than any test, and so does keeping the small promises that are easy to keep. On either side of it, [composite Chiron in Libra](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-libra/) is tender about fairness and [composite Chiron in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-sagittarius/) about meaning; Scorpio is tender about what lies underneath.

As with all the composites here, I'm describing a technique I don't use: its practitioners read this Chiron beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it predicts no betrayal, no inheritance and no ending. A relationship chart about trust also needs some: two people who both know it is being drawn ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
