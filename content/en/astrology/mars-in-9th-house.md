Some people argue about the bill or the parking space. Mars in the 9th house argues about what's true, what's right and what makes a life worth living, and it would rather cross the world than settle for a small one.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the will to act, and to stand up for something. [The 9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is the far horizon: long journeys, higher study, belief and the search for meaning. With Mars here, your energy goes into what you believe and what you want to understand. You'll defend a principle, take on a difficult subject, set off for somewhere unfamiliar, or argue a point late into the night. Learning is an adventure for you, and adventure a way of learning.

Cadent, with Sagittarius as its natural sign, the 9th was the God in the old books, and the Sun has its joy there. Opposite it is the 3rd, the everyday mind: the 3rd argues about this week, the 9th about the meaning of things.

When you were born, Mars had crossed the meridian, the high point of its daily arc, not long before. A little earlier it would have stood in the 10th, just short of the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/); in Placidus, which I use, only the birth time can say which ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Its neighbours read differently: [Mars in the 8th house](/astrology/mars-in-8th-house/) goes deep, [Mars in the 10th house](/astrology/mars-in-10th-house/) goes public.

This is the house of belief, my own included, so a word on how I keep mine honest. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Mars's house went down like everything else, tick or cross, and Mars didn't turn out to be one of the three that held far more often; across all I checked, roughly a third held. I'd rather tell you that than sell you a certainty.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In close relationships, this Mars needs someone who can think and travel with it. Shared beliefs matter, or at least a shared respect for argument: a partner who can disagree with you about big things without the relationship shaking is worth a great deal. Differences of culture or background can attract you, and plans to go somewhere together, or to learn something side by side, keep the bond alive. The risk is turning love into a debate you have to win, or a mission to convert. The skill is a curiosity about what your partner believes that's as fierce as your attachment to your own.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, Mars in the 9th is drawn to teaching, research, publishing, the law, travel, campaigning, any field where you can fight for ideas or carry them across borders. You're often an energetic teacher or advocate, persuasive because you believe what you're saying. Sport can carry this Mars far too: competing abroad, coaching, the discipline of training for a distant goal. Your energy drops fast in work that feels meaningless, or in places where questions aren't welcome.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Preaching. Treating your beliefs as the only reasonable ones, and other people's doubts as obstacles. Impatience with anyone who doesn't share your sense of urgency about a cause. And a restlessness that keeps you leaving for the next horizon before you've learned what this one had to teach.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Learn the strongest version of the argument against you, and state it fairly before you answer it. Study something hard, formally, with a teacher who'll push back. Travel with a purpose, and come home to tell what you learned. Pick causes you can act on, not only argue for. And keep open a question you're not sure about; this Mars grows when it lets itself be uncertain.
