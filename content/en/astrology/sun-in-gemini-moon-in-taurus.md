The Moon sits one sign behind the Sun here, still in steady Taurus while the Sun has moved on into Gemini. With this pair the mind travels light and fast and the heart keeps to a well-worn path, and you were born in the quiet days just before a new moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Gemini follows Taurus in the zodiac, yet the two share neither element nor mode: mutable air after fixed earth. Counted from the Sun, the Moon is one sign back, a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign, neighbours who keep adjusting to each other. The [Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/) learns who it is through exchange, by asking, comparing, trying another angle; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) settles through the body and through repetition: the same walk, the same chair, food made slowly. One light likes its options open, the other likes them settled, and a good week for you has plenty of both.

Tradition is generous to the Moon here and indifferent to the Sun: Taurus is the sign of the Moon's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), the place it rests most easily, while Gemini gives the Sun nothing either way. Your needs stand on firmer ground than your plans, and you can change jobs and opinions with real agility as long as the evenings stay the same. The rulers, [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) and [Venus](/astrology/venus/), never stray far from the Sun, and with a Gemini Sun either can stand in Taurus with your Moon: Mercury there slows your thinking to the heart's pace, Venus there adds pleasure to its comforts.

You were born in the last days of a lunar month. The Moon was 1° to 60° behind the Sun, twenty-four and a half to nearly twenty-nine and a half days after the previous new moon. All but about one birth in eight is a [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) birth, the phase I read as finishing and clearing ground, and the earliest fall in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/). An odd place for a Taurus Moon, which likes to keep things: the phase asks it to let go. An early Gemini Sun and a late Taurus Moon may even make a conjunction that straddles two signs, an [out-of-sign aspect](/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says more.

This pair turns up about once in every 139 births. Taurus holds the Moon for some sixty hours, after Aries and before Gemini; if your birthday fell on either crossing, only the time decides between this page, the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-aries/) and the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-gemini/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign is set by the same time, and until I know it, the two lights are only two lights, not yet a chart.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You can talk with a partner about anything, and you also need them to be there, physically and reliably. The Gemini Sun brings conversation and humour; the Taurus Moon needs touch, routine and the sense that the bond isn't going anywhere. What unsettles you is a relationship that is all talk and no presence, or one so settled it has stopped talking. A weekly ritual, the same meal or the same walk, with room for whatever the conversation brings, keeps both lights fed.

## At work {#at-work}

Your best work often joins a lively mind to a steady hand: writing that gets finished, teaching that builds over years, trade, editing, any job where words and things meet. The Sun wants variety and contacts; the Moon wants a desk of its own, a predictable schedule and something solid by evening. A stable base with a stream of new questions coming through it suits the pair.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The mind can race ahead of feelings that move slowly. You may talk yourself into a change your Taurus Moon isn't ready for and feel oddly unsettled for months, or talk endlessly about a change while the Moon quietly makes sure it never happens. Because the Moon is exalted, its comfort habits are strong, and they can become a place to hide from a decision. What helps is giving each light its own clock: decide in conversation, then let the body take its time, a trial run, a visit, a month of the new routine, before anything is final.
