Who does the washing-up, who remembers the bins, who looks after whom when one of you is run down: the daily round can carry more feeling than any grand gesture. Composite Chiron in the 6th house locates a relationship's sore spot right there, in the round, and describes the working rhythm that makes it bearable.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) holds the ordinary work of life: chores, routines, jobs done day after day, service, the habits that keep a household going. Its old name, Bad Fortune, sounds harsher than the house is, and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) has its joy here, at work. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) reads it as the relationship's daily running, and a [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) there turns the daily round into the sore point. Chores may be quietly unequal, a routine may suit one of you and wear the other down, or the pair may feel it only earns its keep by being useful.

Ask who does what in an ordinary week and the answer is usually more lopsided than either of you thought; that lopsidedness is the composite's subject, not a fault in either person. A fair rota does more good here than any grand gesture: a division of tasks both of you agreed and can renegotiate, a pace the slower of you can keep, care given without keeping score. Colleagues who share this composite often build working methods that others end up borrowing.

Tradition also tied the 6th to illness. A composite predicts no illness for either of you, and no chart can ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); what this Chiron describes is the work of daily life and how two people share it.

Two birth times are the price of entry, and then a method: the midpoints of your two charts' cusps, or houses built from the composite Midheaven at the place where you live. The second inherits the limits of the house system it is drawn in. [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, can't be calculated beyond the polar circles, so a pair living that far north or south gets no composite houses by that route ([houses at high latitudes](/astrology/houses-at-high-latitudes/)). Without both birth times, neither route gets anywhere ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Work and usefulness are one person's question in a natal [Chiron in the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-in-6th-house/), and in synastry [their Chiron in your 6th house](/astrology/chiron-in-6th-house-synastry/) threads the other's experience through your working week. The composite's routine is the pair's own, slowness and busyness together.

Love can shrink into a list of tasks; that is the risk. It helps to notice the care hidden in the chores, and to say thank you for the dull things as readily as for the grand ones.

Before it, [composite Chiron in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-5th-house/) is tender about play; after it, [composite Chiron in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-7th-house/) about the partnership itself. I describe composites without using them in readings; their practitioners read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), as a picture of a shared routine rather than a forecast.
