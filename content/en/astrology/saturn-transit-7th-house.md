Saturn's years in your 7th house are the years when commitment gets looked at closely: the partner, the business associate, the agreements that hold two people together. They begin at your Descendant, directly across from your Ascendant.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds what can carry weight over time; the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is partnership, commitment, contracts and the people you meet as equals. Its passage there tends to sort the bonds that are built to last from the ones that were only convenient. What's unequal asks to be renegotiated, what's solid shows it, and you may find yourself taking on a commitment, formalising one, or redrawing its terms so that both people can live inside it.

Tradition placed Saturn's exaltation in Libra, the 7th's sign in the natural order, as if the planet of structure worked best where fairness is the point. I find that a good key to this transit: the aim isn't to tighten a bond but to make it fair, and fairness needs a structure both people can see.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 7th opens at the Descendant, so Saturn begins by standing opposite your rising degree ([transiting Saturn opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-ascendant/)). With your Moon in the 7th, the pass asks what you need from the people closest to you, and whether you've been saying it.

A [transit](/astrology/transits/) doesn't end or begin a relationship. It brings out what your chart already holds about partnership and gives it a frame, and tradition's open rivals and contracts get no verdict from me either: I don't read the outcome of a dispute, a case or a marriage.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

At London's latitude Saturn's stays in the 7th sit a little below the middle of the range, about 2 years and 8 months in Placidus charts drawn there, with most between about 2 years and just over 4. New York's latitude gives about 2 years and 10 months, from 2 years to just under 4. The 1st, across the chart, is always the same size as your 7th, yet it can hold Saturn up to about 5 years: the 7th stops sooner.

Your Descendant comes from your birth time and nowhere else, so without the time there's no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows Saturn's years in your 6th, the house of daily work ([Saturn transiting the 6th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-6th-house/)), and gives way to its years in your 8th, where what a bond shares and owes is weighed ([Saturn transiting the 8th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-8th-house/)).

Whatever your rising sign, the Descendant sits in the sign across from it. With Libra rising it's in Aries, and Saturn is crossing it now: February 2026 to April 2028, after a first visit in 2025. For Scorpio rising, the Taurus Descendant has its turn from April 2028 to May 2030; for Sagittarius rising, Gemini from June 2030 to July 2032; for Capricorn rising, Cancer from July 2032 to May 2035; for Aquarius rising, Leo from August 2034 to July 2037; for Pisces rising, Virgo from October 2036 to September 2039; and for Aries rising, Libra, the sign of Saturn's exaltation, from September 2039. With Virgo rising, the Pisces Descendant had Saturn from March 2023 to February 2026, and Taurus to Leo rising come after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name what's unequal, kindly and early. Saturn in the 7th tends to make imbalance hard to ignore, and a conversation held in good time costs less than one held in a crisis.

Put agreements in writing, in love as well as in work, if that suits you both. Saturn likes structures that each person can see and point to.

Keep the commitments you'd choose again, and give the person across the table the seriousness you ask of them. The 7th is a mirror, and under Saturn it reflects how you hold your own side of a bond.
