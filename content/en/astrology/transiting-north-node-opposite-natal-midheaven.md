Halfway round from its crossing of your Midheaven, the North Node reaches the bottom of your chart, the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), and the South Node moves up to the top. Both points come from your birth time, the IC being the degree opposite your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Through those weeks, the direction you are still learning points to your foundations, while your public role shows you what you already do well.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The IC roots the chart: continuity, support, belonging, the private ground you recover on. It isn't only home or family, and it isn't only the past. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), which marks your least practised capacity, sits on it under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), and an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) pairs that end with the other: the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) on your Midheaven, where your role, standing and contribution are.

The growth on offer is foundational: building support you can lean on, letting rest restore you rather than merely pause you, belonging somewhere for its own sake. The South Node, meanwhile, lights up the public competence you've consolidated, the role that runs smoothly because you've done it for years. It stays yours; the month asks what it is resting on.

Nothing here forecasts a move, the loss of a job or a family event. A root is something you tend, not an address, and the people in your family have charts of their own.

In Placidus, the system I use, the IC is the cusp of the 4th house, so this crossing is also the North Node's move from your 4th into your 3rd ([North Node transiting the 4th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-4th-house/)), while the South Node moves from your 10th into your 9th. In equal or Whole Sign houses the IC can fall inside the 3rd or the 5th, and the crossing and the change of house no longer coincide.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, a crossing of your IC takes the North Node about a month, and a single exact hit is the rule, nineteen times in twenty. A tenth of passes are done in 25 days or less. Three exact hits, when they happen, take about 100 days; that's one pass in twenty-five.

The longest passes, a tenth of them past 100 days, belong to eclipse seasons, and they can carry an eclipse onto the axis. A Midheaven at 29° Taurus shows how close the two can come. From 18 March to 30 June 2031 the North Node holds its IC within orb, with a single exact hit on 4 April; on 21 May, while the South Node sits about a degree from the Midheaven, the annular solar eclipse falls at 0°04′ Gemini, about a degree past it ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Four or five of these crossings come by 80, one in each round, and nobody misses them. Counted from this point, the trine to your Midheaven is about three years away in either direction ([Transiting North Node trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-midheaven/)), the square about four and a half ([Transiting North Node square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-midheaven/)) and the sextile about six ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-midheaven/)); the conjunction, nine years off either way, puts the North Node on the Midheaven itself ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)). Of those five aspects, this one and the conjunction are the two my forecast counts, because one of the nodes then stands on the angle itself.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Repair one support you've let slide: a routine of sleep, a friendship, a corner of the home that works.

Let the public role run on what you already know for a month, and give the spare attention to what holds you up.

Spend time where you feel you belong, and notice what it gives back.

Write down what you'd want to keep whatever your role becomes. That list is your foundation.
