Vesta is the one asteroid you can sometimes see without a telescope: at its brightest it reaches magnitude 5.1, a faint point under a dark sky. Heinrich Olbers found it on 29 March 1807 and left the naming to Carl Friedrich Gauss, who chose the Roman goddess of the hearth.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Vesta was the virgin goddess of the hearth, home and family, and she was rarely shown in human form: in her temple in the Forum, the goddess was the fire. Her priestesses, the Vestal Virgins, were bound to keep it alight. The same temple held the Palladium, the image of [Pallas](/astrology/pallas/) Athena that Romans said Aeneas had brought to Italy, and Vesta was a sister of [Juno](/astrology/juno/), of Ceres and of Jupiter. Astrologers who use the asteroid read the fire: focus, devotion, a commitment to a task or a practice that keeps going whoever is watching.

My readings don't include Vesta. No asteroid is part of the system I built, so what follows is how astrologers who use them read it:

- **Its sign:** the style of your focus, whether intense, steady, curious or quietly devoted.
- **Its house:** what you guard and tend, the area of life you'd keep going through anything.
- **Its aspects:** whatever competes with that focus, or feeds it.

Beside [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), which is time and the work time asks for, Vesta is read as the reason you keep at it. With the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), devotion becomes part of who you are; with the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), the hearth turns literal, and home itself is what you tend.

## Vesta through the signs {#through-the-signs}

Vesta is the quickest of the four first asteroids, 3.6 years round the Sun, so its sign changes more often than theirs and is still shared with many people born near you. I have no ephemeris for it, so there are no dates here, and its house will say more about you than its sign.

- **Vesta in Aries:** devotion that starts things; you focus best when you lead the way.
- **Vesta in Taurus:** slow, patient devotion to a craft, to land or to the body; the fire that lasts.
- **Vesta in Gemini:** focus shared among ideas, with a devotion to learning and to words.
- **Vesta in Cancer:** devotion to home and to the people under your roof, the hearth taken literally.
- **Vesta in Leo:** devotion to creative work, a flame you'd like others to see.
- **Vesta in Virgo:** focus through method and service, and devotion to doing a thing properly.
- **Vesta in Libra:** devotion to a partner or to fairness, with focus that wants company.
- **Vesta in Scorpio:** intense, private focus that goes deep and doesn't explain itself.
- **Vesta in Sagittarius:** devotion to a faith, a teaching or a search for meaning.
- **Vesta in Capricorn:** focus on long work and responsibility; a career followed as a vocation.
- **Vesta in Aquarius:** devotion to a group or an ideal, with attention on what comes next.
- **Vesta in Pisces:** devotion through compassion, art or spiritual practice, in need of quiet.

## Vesta in the houses {#in-the-houses}

Only the [time of birth](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) places Vesta in a house, and I work in Placidus. The 12th, which tradition gave to confinement, reads here as chosen solitude and retreat. If solitude ever stops feeling chosen, the right door is a professional's, not a chart's ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

1. **1st house:** people see your dedication before they see much else.
2. **2nd house:** devotion to building security, and resources carefully kept.
3. **3rd house:** focus on study and writing, and on the daily round close to home.
4. **4th house:** the hearth itself: home kept as a sanctuary.
5. **5th house:** devotion to creative work and to what you love doing for its own sake.
6. **6th house:** devotion to daily work and service, and routines that protect a craft.
7. **7th house:** devotion inside a partnership, or a partner who respects the space you need.
8. **8th house:** private commitments, and focus on what is shared and rarely shown.
9. **9th house:** devotion to study, belief or teaching.
10. **10th house:** a vocation followed with a single focus, sometimes in public view.
11. **11th house:** devotion to a group, a cause or a community you serve.
12. **12th house:** a private practice, and retreat as a source of strength.

## Vesta in synastry {#in-synastry}

Asteroids are no part of my [synastry readings](/synastry/), which count only contacts involving at least one personal body. Astrologers who compare two Vestas, or one person's Vesta with the other's Sun or Venus, read it both ways. The Vesta person may treat the other's work or presence as something to protect; the other may feel respected, or held at the edge of a space they're not invited into. Two charts are compared only with two people's consent: [why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## Transits and the Vesta return {#vesta-return}

Every 3.6 years or so, Vesta returns to its place in your chart. My forecasts work with the transits of Jupiter to Pluto, Chiron and the true node, and Vesta isn't among them. Astrologers who follow it read Vesta crossing a personal planet as a time to narrow your focus and protect it. Read that as weather, not a forecast: a season for concentration, nothing more. However many asteroids you add, the planets underneath stay the same: [How to use asteroids without drowning the chart](/astrology/how-to-use-asteroids-without-drowning-the-chart/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Vesta is about virginity.”** The Vestals' vows belonged to Rome; in a chart, Vesta is read as focus and devotion, never as a statement about anyone's body.
- **“A strong Vesta means you'll end up alone.”** It can describe a need for solitude you choose, which is not loneliness, and never a verdict.
- **“Vesta is just another asteroid.”** It's the brightest of them all and the second largest, after Ceres.
- **“Vesta's fire means anger.”** Hers is the hearth's fire, kept and tended, not a blaze.
