The way you talk becomes part of the way you're known. Words are ruled from the house of public life here, and what you say, write or teach tends to build your reputation.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is the house of small, daily communication: errands, messages, lessons, the people near you. Its ruler ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)) carries all of that up into [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), the most visible of the angular houses: your career, your standing, the direction others can see.

So communication tends to be part of the job, or the job itself. Writing, teaching, speaking, selling, translating, editing, broadcasting, coordinating: any work where information has to move from one person to another is natural ground. Even in other trades, you may be the one who writes the report, chairs the meeting or explains the change to everyone else. A reputation tends to form around your words, which is a gift and a responsibility: a careless sentence of yours travels further than most people's. The 10th is also the house of authority, so you may end up explaining the rules to others, or questioning them out loud.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 10th begins at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), and a planet in it was climbing towards the top of the sky when you were born, east of the meridian. The ruler of a cadent house, one of the quietest, is lifted into the house where things are seen ([angularity](/astrology/angularity/)): ordinary skills, the knack of explaining, an ease with the phone or the page, become public assets.

Learning follows the same route. You may learn most on the job and in front of others, through training, mentors and the courses a career asks for, and a profession can grow out of what began as a love of reading or writing. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 3rd from the 10th, makes the communicator's path almost literal; Venus, ruling Taurus or Libra, smooths it with charm and diplomacy; Saturn, ruling Capricorn on the cusp, or Aquarius, where Uranus is the other ruler, sits in the house of its own natural sign and builds an expert's voice over years.

The people of the 3rd can follow you into public life: a sibling in the same field, a family business run with brothers or sisters, a name made locally among neighbours before it travelled. That's how those ties figure in your working life as you live it, never a forecast for theirs.

Pisces on the 3rd cusp gives it Jupiter and Neptune at once, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 10th can make teaching, publishing or advice a public calling, while Neptune lends the voice its imagination from another house.

Its neighbours tell other stories with the same ruler: in the 9th, study and belief ([Ruler of the 3rd in the 9th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)); in the 11th, friends and networks ([Ruler of the 3rd in the 11th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)). Which one is yours depends on the Midheaven's exact degree, which a birth time rounded to the hour can't give ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
