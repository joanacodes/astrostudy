With Mercury opposite Chiron, the tender spot sits at the opposite end of the chart from your own voice. It is often other people, and what they say, that bring it into view.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the planet that gives thoughts their words and listens for other people's: it interprets, explains and exchanges. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the place where something stays tender, and where, in time, you build the skill to work around it and to help others with the same. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) faces them across the chart, so that each end makes the other easier to see.

Often it plays out between people. A passing remark lands on an old sensitivity; a teacher, a partner or a colleague says something that leaves you feeling unheard, even when they didn't mean it. The other end of the axis shows up too: you may become the one who explains for others, turning their difficulty into words they can use. Neither end is the whole story. The opposition asks you to keep both in view: to understand other people's sore spots, and to let yours be understood in turn.

That two-way understanding is the gift. This opposition often makes you unusually careful with words, because they know from both sides how much a sentence can carry.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Opposite signs share a modality and complete each other, and this aspect puts Mercury in one and Chiron in the other. Chiron's sign names the tender theme, worth in Taurus or being understood in Gemini, say, and Mercury's sign gives the voice that faces it. The houses usually face each other too: Mercury in the 4th opposite Chiron in the 10th, for instance, might set the way you talk at home against an old sensitivity about being judged in public. A reading of this opposition is a conversation rather than an instruction, and you decide how much of the tender end to look at ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

About 1 person in 33 is born with it, within 6°. The nearer to exact, the more everyday remarks reach the tender spot; at the edge of the orb, only the people closest to you tend to.

The other aspects of the pair bring the tender spot nearer to your own voice:

- **[Mercury conjunct Chiron](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-chiron/):** the sensitivity and the voice are one, with nobody else needed to set it off.
- **[Mercury sextile Chiron](/astrology/mercury-sextile-chiron/):** a friendly opening, where your experience is offered rather than provoked.
- **[Mercury square Chiron](/astrology/mercury-square-chiron/):** words catch on the sore spot as you speak, without anyone else's help.
- **[Mercury trine Chiron](/astrology/mercury-trine-chiron/):** speaking about old difficulties comes easily, and other people's words rarely catch.

Set against another person's chart, [Mercury opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-chiron-synastry/) sets what one person says against what the other keeps tender. It can wound without meaning to, or become the most understanding conversation either of you has known; the difference is usually care.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-mercury/) tends to bring the sensitivity through someone else: a remark, a review, a conversation that touches the old place. Chiron may hold the opposition for months or, where it moves slowly, come back to it more than once. Many astrologers see in it a chance to answer differently than you once did.

Weather, not a forecast: the transit describes a tender season in your exchanges, never what anyone will say.
