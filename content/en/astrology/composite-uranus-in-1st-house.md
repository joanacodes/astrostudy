The 1st house is the face a relationship shows before anyone knows the two people in it. With composite Uranus there, the face is an unusual one: people notice the arrangement first, and the individuals after.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Few houses depend so much on how [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is drawn. The 1st begins at the composite [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), and the two methods disagree about where that is: one takes the midpoint of your two natal Ascendants, the other rebuilds the Ascendant from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live. The Ascendant also changes sign about every two hours on average, so an hour's error in one birth time can move a natal Ascendant by around half a sign, and the composite one by roughly half that. I never read a birth chart without its time (*no birth time, no reading*); a composite house needs two times, and a method on top ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is angular, the natural house of Aries, and the house of appearance, presence and self-definition. With [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) there, the relationship defines itself rather than borrowing a model: an age gap nobody can ignore, roles reversed in public, two homes, a friendship that looks like a partnership or a partnership that looks like a friendship. It tends to change its image every few years, and people who know you often describe you as a pair before they describe either of you. It also takes initiatives as a pair: the first in its circle to try a new way of living, the first in a group to break with a custom. The third thing matters here: two people who are conventional on their own can, together, make a bond that strangers find startling.

The pages around this one show what is particular to it. [Uranus in the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-in-1st-house/) of a birth chart describes one person who comes across as unusual; [their Uranus in your 1st house](/astrology/uranus-in-1st-house-synastry/) describes someone whose presence stirs your sense of yourself. In the composite, the unusual presence belongs to the pair. Its sign, shared by most couples of your age, colours it, and [composite Uranus in Aries](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-aries/), the sign that matches this house, is one that few living couples have.

The trap is having to explain yourselves. A bond that is always noticed can start performing its difference, or defending it, and the image can change faster than the life underneath. What helps is to let the unusual be ordinary between you, and to decide together how much of your arrangement anyone else needs to understand.

[Composite Uranus in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-12th-house/) keeps its freedom out of view; [composite Uranus in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-2nd-house/) puts it into what the pair owns and values. Composites have no place in my own readings, but the technique is widely used, and those who use it read this house beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), with nothing predicted about how others will treat you or how long you'll last, and only for two people who both agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
