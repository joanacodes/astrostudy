Some couples are noticed as a pair before anyone has learned their names: something about the two of them together seems unusual, intense, hard to place. That is composite Lilith in the 1st house: a relationship's untamed side worn on its surface, where other people meet it first.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In a birth chart, [the 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) belongs to the body and the manner, the self a person leads with. An angular house, with Aries as its natural sign, it opens at the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/). Carried over into [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), built from the midpoints of two birth charts, it becomes the relationship's bearing: the impression the two of you make together. With [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) here, the part I read as untamed or hidden doesn't stay hidden: people sense it in the way you are together, in a frankness, a closeness or an oddness they can't name, and some are drawn to it while others are put out.

It needn't come from either person. A pair made of two quiet people can still be the most conspicuous one in the room, and each may be startled by the reactions the two of you provoke together. What this Lilith refuses is the performance: holding hands at the right moments, explaining yourselves, being the kind of couple, or the kind of friends, that a family or a workplace finds easy to file.

No house rests more visibly on the birth times. The composite Ascendant is either the midpoint of your two Ascendants, or the degree that rises, at the latitude where you live, with the composite Midheaven on the meridian, and the two can be several degrees apart. An Ascendant also moves about a degree every four minutes on average, so a quarter of an hour's error in one birth time moves the composite Ascendant by about two degrees. A Lilith within a few degrees of it can sit in the 1st house by one reckoning and the 12th by another ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one birth chart, [Lilith in the 1st house](/astrology/lilith-in-1st-house/) is a person whose hidden side shows in their presence. In synastry, [their Lilith in your 1st house](/astrology/lilith-in-1st-house-synastry/) is someone else's untamed side meeting the way you show up. The composite's 1st belongs to neither: it is the face of the bond. Just behind it, [composite Lilith in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-12th-house/) keeps the same refusal entirely out of sight; just after it, [composite Lilith in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-2nd-house/) carries it into what the pair values and holds.

The hard part is being read before you have spoken. A pair like this can be judged on its surface, cast as rebels or as a scandal it isn't, and then start playing up to the role or hiding from it. It helps to decide together how much of yourselves you show, and where, and to let the curious look without owing them an explanation.

I don't draw composites in my readings; the astrologers who do read this Lilith beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and none of them can tell from it how others will treat you, or how long you will last. Since it describes what a pair shows of its untamed side, it is for two people who both want it looked at ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
