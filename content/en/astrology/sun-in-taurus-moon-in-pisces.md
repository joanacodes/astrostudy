Venus rules your Sun's sign, and Pisces, where your Moon is, happens to be the sign where tradition exalts Venus. One planet is honoured on both sides of this pair, and I read its thread as pleasure and beauty, felt first in the body and then in the imagination. You were born after the last quarter, with the Moon thinning in the morning sky.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Earth and water, fixed and mutable, the Moon two signs behind the Sun: a sextile by sign, help that works when you take it up. The [Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/) knows itself through what it can touch, keep and enjoy; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) takes in the moods of the people around it and settles through music, imagination, solitude and kindness. The Sun gives the Moon solid ground to come back to; the Moon gives the Sun a reason to look past what it already owns.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is close to a Taurus Sun by nature, and with the Sun early in Taurus it can even stand in Pisces, its exaltation, in your Moon's sign. Your Moon has two rulers, and I read both: [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), which modern astrologers gave to Pisces, for where you dream and lose yourself in what you love; and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), its ruler before Neptune was found in 1846, for where all that feeling finds a meaning. Neither light has a dignity in its sign: the honour belongs to Venus.

You arrived between the last quarter and the next new moon: the Moon was 31° to 90° behind the Sun, twenty-two to twenty-seven days into the month. Seven of these births in eight are [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) births and the rest [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) ones; close to 60° behind, the lights make a waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). I read the last quarter as a time for deciding what to keep and what to release, the very question a Taurus Sun finds hard and a Pisces Moon finds easy. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sets out where each phase begins.

About 1 person in 138 has this pair. The Moon spends two and a half days or so in Pisces, last of the signs, before Aries opens the round again; if it entered from Aquarius or left for Aries on your birth date, the time tells whether this is your page or the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-aquarius/) or the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-aries/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same time gives the rising sign; the three make an outline, and the shading comes from what else the time reveals, the houses and angles.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love tenderly and steadily: the Taurus Sun with presence, touch and comfort, the Pisces Moon with intuition, forgiveness and a sense of what someone needs before they ask. The risk lies in loyalty and compassion working together. The Sun doesn't like to leave, the Moon doesn't like to hurt, and between them they can keep you giving to a bond that no longer gives back. A partner who says plainly what they feel, so you don't have to absorb it and guess, lets both lights rest.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that joins craft to imagination suits you: music, food, gardens, design, or caring work done with patience. The Sun wants something tangible to show; the Moon wants the work to mean something to someone. Noisy, aggressive workplaces drain the Pisces Moon, and a role without a visible result leaves the Taurus Sun unsure what it's for. A calm place, a steady rhythm and some solitude each day suit both.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The pair can take in a great deal and let very little go. The Pisces Moon absorbs other people's moods; the Taurus Sun holds on to whatever it has taken in, so a sadness picked up on Monday may still be sitting with you on Friday. What helps is giving the Moon a way to release and the Sun a way to ground the release: a walk in the rain, an hour in the garden, music played rather than only heard, anything that lets the day's moods pass through the body and out.
