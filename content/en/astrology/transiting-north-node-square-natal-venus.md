When the nodes square your Venus, neither end of the axis touches it; the axis runs past at right angles, a quarter of the zodiac clear on both sides. For about a month, what you love sits across the path, and the path asks to go round it, or through it, kindly.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs what you value and what you enjoy, and how you meet other people halfway. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) both stand at right angles to it now, one on each side, and a [square](/astrology/square/) works through friction that wants an adjustment. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), a pleasure or an attachment sits between your past habits and your next step.

That can show as a relationship that suits the old you better than the new one, a comfort that keeps you from a step, or a step that seems to ask you to give up something you love. Harmony isn't the same as avoiding conflict, and the square doesn't ask you to choose between your affections and your growth; it asks what each would need to make room for the other. The answer is often smaller than the worry: a conversation, a change of rhythm, a pleasure moved rather than cancelled. None of this forecasts a parting, and no transit could.

With your [Venus in the 4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house/), the friction tends to sit at home: the warmth of family habits, the place you've made comfortable, the people you'd rather not disappoint. If you're tempted to look up someone else's chart to see what the square means for them, ask them first ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)). And your 4th, like the rest of your houses, exists only once the birth time is known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A transit lives, for me, inside 1½° before exact and 1° after; the true node usually crosses that span in about a month. The Sun sets the pace. Midway between eclipse seasons, with the Sun 90° from the nodes, the node runs backwards at about twice its average speed, and the square may be done within three and a half weeks, as a tenth of passes are. Inside a season it nearly stops, and a tenth run to three and a half months or more, about one in twenty-five with three exact contacts.

The node needs about four years and eight months to turn a quarter of the zodiac, so the two squares of a round sit on either side of [Transiting North Node opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-venus/), each flanked by [Transiting North Node sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-venus/) and [Transiting North Node trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-venus/), with [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-venus/) at the round's start and end. Nine squares by 80 for most people, eight for the rest. With [Venus square North Node](/astrology/venus-square-north-node/) at birth, this square is guaranteed at each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) and each [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the pleasure or the bond that seems to stand in the way, and ask what it would need in order to come along.

Move a comfort rather than cancel it: a different evening, a different place, the same warmth.

Say the awkward thing kindly and early. A square between two people is easier to share than to guess at.
