Some people hear your bluntest refusal and are, if anything, relieved by it. With Neptune trine Lilith in synastry, one person's Neptune and the other's Black Moon Lilith are spaced a trine apart, and what the other learned to hide is met without a flinch, by someone whose own dreaming gets braver in return.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the imaginative, compassionate side of a chart, at home with ambiguity and drawn to what lies past the ordinary. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's apogee averaged into a slowly turning point, stands for many astrologers for instinctive autonomy, a person's own sense of power, and whatever they set outside the frame; the traditions vary, so this is a symbol, not a law. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) is facilitative flow: two functions strengthen each other without either losing its shape. Among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), that flow runs between two people, and here it runs both ways.

If the Lilith is yours, you don't have to soften yourself for this person. Your temper, your hunger, your refusal to play along: they take it in their stride, often with a kind of quiet delight. Around them, the part of you that was too much becomes simply part of you.

Carrying the Neptune, you'll often find their directness steadies your imagination. Your dreams get a spine; you say what you hoped for instead of hinting at it, because they plainly prefer the truth. You came to accept them, and find you've been made bolder yourself.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Ease can keep this acceptance in a private world. A pair with the trine may welcome each other's wildness in long talks, shared art or a running joke, and never test it on anything practical: money, family, a decision with a deadline. An easy angle is still an unused one until someone leans on it: if the welcome is never used, the Lilith person's refusals stay poetic and the Neptune person's courage stays in the imagination.

Elsewhere in the pair:

- **[Neptune conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** fascination at close range, where the picture can swallow the person.
- **[Neptune sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** the same welcome, smaller and waiting for a yes.
- **[Neptune square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-lilith-synastry/):** kindness and refusal catching on each other, then learning to see clearly.
- **[Neptune opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** dreaming and refusing stand at either end and pull.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Put the welcome to work. Bring the untamed side into one ordinary decision, and see that it is still welcome there. If the Neptune is yours, say aloud what their directness has given you; if the Lilith is yours, say what it was like not to be flinched at. Old friends with this trine often become each other's honest witnesses; a couple, a home where both can be blunt and kind; colleagues, a team in which a dissenting voice is heard as useful.

Someone born in mid-1985 has it both ways round with people born from May to July 1985, each one's Neptune trine the other's Lilith. Their Lilith, early in Taurus, also trines the Neptune of anyone born from November 1984 to January 1987, and of anyone born from January 1930 to September 1931, when Neptune was in Virgo. Contacts shared this widely never enter my synastry count, which keeps to the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant and the Midheaven; I read this one in the houses, as an exchange between eras rather than between temperaments.

The hour of birth leaves the trine alone, both points being slow, while the houses need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). All of these windows rest on mean Lilith ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). My notebook had Lilith among [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), with Saturn and Chiron, 75 to 90% of the time; that was one person's chart, read by sign and house, so this trine inherits no figure from it.

[Neptune trine Lilith](/astrology/neptune-trine-lilith/), in a single chart, is someone whose wildness found a home in their own imagination. Here the home is offered by another person, and I look at it only at both people's request ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
