Venus opposite Neptune puts what you value and what you long for on opposite sides of the sky. It's a polarity rather than a quarrel: the chosen and the imagined face each other, and a good part of life goes into learning to hold both in view.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a chart that evaluates: what you're drawn to, what you prefer, what feels worth keeping, and how you balance give and take. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the part that imagines and dissolves: compassion, inspiration, the sense of something larger, and the loosening of edges that needn't be there. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity: each becomes clearer by contrast with the other, and the task is to integrate them, not to pick one.

In plain words, one end of you weighs and chooses, and the other wants more than any choice can hold. You might love a real person and still feel the pull of who they might be. Oppositions are often lived through other people first: a partner may seem to carry the dream for you, or to need your compassion so much that your own preferences go quiet. Giving yourself to compassion is not the same as disappearing into it, and seeing someone at their best is not the same as being fooled. As awareness grows, the two ends inform each other: taste that keeps a touch of the boundless, and imagination that knows what it actually likes.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Opposite signs share a modality and pair fire with air or earth with water. For births in the 1930s, with Neptune in Virgo, Venus opposite it sat in Pisces, its sign of exaltation, facing the sign of its fall: a feeling for the boundless across from a need to sort and refine. With Neptune in Scorpio, Venus opposite it was in Taurus, its own sign: a love of the solid facing a longing for depth. The houses set it across an axis of life: Venus in the 1st opposite Neptune in the 7th is the classic shape, the ideal arriving through partners; Venus in the 4th opposite Neptune in the 10th, a love of home facing a calling that's hard to name.

About 1 person in 30 is born with Venus and Neptune within 6° of an opposition, half as many as have the trine or the square. The geometry has a second consequence. Venus never strays more than about 47° from the Sun, so with Venus opposite Neptune the Sun is roughly opposite Neptune too, which is when Neptune turns retrograde: everyone born with this opposition was born during a [Neptune retrograde](/astrology/neptune-retrograde/). I read that as imagination working inward before it's shared ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

The pair's other angles, seen from this one:

- **[Venus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/venus-conjunct-neptune/):** the two on one spot; the dream lives inside what you choose rather than across from it.
- **[Venus sextile Neptune](/astrology/venus-sextile-neptune/):** imagination as a friendly neighbour, on hand when you call on it.
- **[Venus square Neptune](/astrology/venus-square-neptune/):** the ideal cutting across what you prefer, and clarity won by adjusting.
- **[Venus trine Neptune](/astrology/venus-trine-neptune/):** the two in one element, generous with each other and easy to take for granted.

Between two charts, [Venus opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-neptune-synastry/) often feels like recognition at a distance: one person's taste faces the other's dream. Each may see in the other what they long for, so I read it from both sides, and slowly.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Neptune opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-venus/) comes to some generations and not to others, depending on how far your Venus sits from Neptune; when it comes, it stays about two years, in several passes, and can blur and then clarify what you want from the people you love. Born with the opposition, you meet a related season at about 41: the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) to its own birth place is, by the same geometry, a square to your Venus.

About once a year, Venus in the sky opposes your natal Neptune for a day or two, more often when it turns retrograde nearby: days when the gap between the ideal and the actual is easier to see.

Weather, not a forecast: these transits change the light on what you love, not the facts of it.
