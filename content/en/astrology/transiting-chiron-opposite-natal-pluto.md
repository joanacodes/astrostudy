Between 2018 and 2039 this opposition passes like a slow wave through three generations: people born with Pluto in Libra, in their forties, then those with Pluto in Scorpio, around 40, then those with Pluto in Sagittarius, in their thirties. For most of them it is the only one they will meet by 80.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) holds your relationship to power and depth: where you hold on, where you let go and begin again. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) stands for the tender place itself and what living with it has taught you. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions across from each other, and what sits across from you is usually other people, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) questions of power and vulnerability tend to arrive through a relationship.

Someone's intensity may land right on an old sore spot: a manager's need for control, a partner's jealousy, a friend's crisis that asks more than you can give. Or you notice that you hold power over someone else's tender place, and have to decide how to use it. Pluto's blind spot here is assuming every unequal exchange is a deliberate power play. Some are; many are two people protecting old hurts at once. The opposition asks you to tell the two apart, and often to negotiate: what is theirs to carry, what is yours, and what can be shared.

No transit says that a bond will break or that anyone will be harmed, and I never read one that way ([disclaimers](/disclaimers/#claims)).

Your Pluto's house and the house opposite, where Chiron is, mark the two sides of the table. With [Pluto in the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-in-7th-house/), Chiron crossing your 1st brings the power questions you know from close partnership up against an old ache about being yourself in plain view ([Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/)). Your houses need the minute you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Natal aspects carry the contact further: if your Mars is opposite your Pluto, Chiron now sits on your Mars, and the same months bring [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-mars/). Born with [Pluto opposite Chiron](/astrology/pluto-opposite-chiron/), between 1959 and 1970, you had this transit at birth.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured with my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, it lasts about ten months in most cases, three exact hits as Chiron loops. Chiron moves slowly in Aries, Taurus and Gemini, so the current wave is likely to take the long shapes, a few of them stretching to five hits over about twenty-one months. Single crossings, a third of all passes, take about three weeks.

The first can come in the first weeks of life or as late as about 49: about 19 for people born in 1935, about 11 for 1945, about 4 for 1955, at birth for 1965, then about 45 for 1975, about 42 for 1985, about 38 for 1995 and about 32½ for 2005. The jump after 1965 comes from Chiron's orbit: the later groups were born just after it had passed the point opposite their Pluto, and had to wait for it to come almost all the way round. A second opposition comes only to those whose first came before about 30.

It stands halfway through the round, opposite the point where [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-pluto/) began it; [transiting Chiron sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-pluto/), [transiting Chiron square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-pluto/) and [transiting Chiron trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-pluto/) lead up to it, and follow it in mirror order.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Before reacting to someone's intensity, ask what old hurt of theirs it might be guarding. Then ask the same of your own reaction.

Negotiate out loud: what you'll carry, what you won't, what you can share.

If you hold power over someone's tender place, use it gently, and give it back as soon as you can.
