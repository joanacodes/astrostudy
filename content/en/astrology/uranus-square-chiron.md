Uranus square Chiron is among the rarer aspects of the count, about 1 birth in 39: two runs of under two years each, from 1942 to 1944 and from 1996 to 1998. It describes friction between the urge to break with the expected and a place that stays tender.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables liberation, differentiation and change: it shakes loose what has set too hard and insists on a path of its own. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the place that stays sore, a limit you keep meeting, and the skill you build by working around it. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through constructive friction: neither gives way, and the rub drives adaptation.

In daily life, change tends to press on the sore spot. A sudden shift of plans unsettles the arrangements you built around a limit; the urge to be different rubs against the very place you'd rather keep covered. That is uncomfortable, and it isn't failure or punishment. Each round of friction asks for a better adaptation than the last, and many people with this square become skilled at rebuilding after disruption, theirs and other people's. Nothing in it says the tender place was caused by a particular hurt, and nothing says it must stay as it is.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 39 born between 1930 and 2009 has this square within 6°, in six short stretches: October 1942 to March 1943, August to November 1943, March to August 1944, November 1996 to March 1997, August to November 1997, and April to September 1998. The runs were brief because Chiron was moving quickly, through signs it crosses in two or three years: Leo and Virgo in the 1940s, Scorpio in the 1990s.

The signs set a tone. In 1943 and 1944 Uranus in Gemini squared Chiron in Virgo, two mutable signs; around 1997 Uranus in Aquarius squared Chiron in Scorpio, two fixed ones. The houses show where the friction lives: Uranus in the 10th square Chiron in the 1st, for instance, could set a working life that keeps changing course against a sensitivity about how you come across. That needs a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Chiron](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-chiron/):** absent from the count.
- **[Uranus sextile Chiron](/astrology/uranus-sextile-chiron/):** born 1938 to 1941 or 1999 to 2001; experiment opening a door for the tender place.
- **[Uranus trine Chiron](/astrology/uranus-trine-chiron/):** born 1945 to 1946 or 1994 to 1996; adapting comes easily.
- **[Uranus opposite Chiron](/astrology/uranus-opposite-chiron/):** born on and off from 1951 to 1990; the two face each other.

[Uranus square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-chiron-synastry/) can feel like being unsettled exactly where you are sore, or like unsettling someone there. It deserves a gentle reading, from both sides, and only with both people's consent.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-uranus/) lasts from months to a couple of years, depending on how fast Chiron is moving through the sign. Transiting Uranus squares natal Chiron twice in its 84-year round, for some months each time, usually in several passes.

Born with the square, you meet it again at the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, when Chiron comes back to its birth place and so squares your natal Uranus once more. Weather, not a forecast: a season that asks what the tender place needs now, not a warning of what will happen.
