For you, security tends to mean a roof. The ruler of your money lives at the bottom of the chart, in the 4th house, and what you earn tends to come home to the people and the place you belong to.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Here the ruler of your [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/), the planet of the sign on its cusp ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), has taken your money and your sense of worth home, into the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/): the home you grew up in and the one you make, family and belonging, the past you carry. Tradition gave the 4th land and houses as well, which makes this one of the most literal pairings in a chart.

Horary astrology drew the line neatly. The 2nd was the house of moveable possessions, what you can carry, spend or sell, and the 4th of immovable ones: houses, gardens, land, whatever comes out of the ground. With the ruler of your moveable goods in the house of the immovable ones, loose resources tend to become fixed: savings into a deposit, earnings into a kitchen, wages into a family's security.

Home is often where your money goes, and sometimes where it comes from: a family business, work done from home, a house that is also a workplace. What you value was shaped early, around the family table, by what was said about money and what was left unsaid, and much of the adult work is sorting which of those values are really yours. I read that gently. The 4th describes your experience of home, not your parents' finances, and nothing here says what anyone will leave you.

Each planet furnishes this home in its own way. Where Cancer occupies the 2nd cusp, the Moon is at home in the house the natural zodiac gives its sign, and money becomes a way of keeping your people fed and safe. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn 2nd, builds a home the slow way and pays it down year by year. Venus here puts what you earn into making a place beautiful.

The 4th is an angular house, so this ruler carries weight even out of sight. The planet of your money was nearing the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart, when you were born; Placidus, the house system I use, opens the 4th there, and the IC moves with the minutes ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Just before that cusp, in the 3rd, your money would run through words and trade ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 3rd house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)); further round, in the 5th, through what you create and enjoy ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 5th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)).

Aquarius on the 2nd cusp hands your money to two rulers, Saturn and Uranus, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 4th wants property and permanence, while Uranus, wherever it sits, may keep you renting, moving or living differently from the family pattern.

No chart can tell you when to buy a house or what it will be worth ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). This placement describes what security means to you, and that, for you, it has an address.
