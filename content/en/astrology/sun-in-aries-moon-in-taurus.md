Tradition gives each light one sign where it is most honoured: Aries for the Sun, Taurus for the Moon. Born with this pair, you have both, one beside the other, in the first days of a lunar month. To me it describes a self that likes to begin, resting on needs that like to stay.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

The signs are neighbours, and neighbouring signs share nothing: Aries is cardinal fire, Taurus fixed earth. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) comes alive in the first step; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) calms down through the senses, small rituals and comforts it already trusts. So the self goes out early and the needs want the same chair by the window at the end of the day, and both are entirely sincere.

In Ptolemy's scheme each light has one sign of [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), and these are the two: no other pairing of Sun and Moon signs places both in theirs. I take that as ease on both sides rather than any kind of rank: purpose meets little resistance from its sign, and so does need. The rulers make a well-known couple, [Mars](/astrology/mars/) for Aries and [Venus](/astrology/venus/) for Taurus: the effort you make, and what you make it for. How those two sit in your chart, by sign, house and aspect, says a great deal about how this pair gets along day to day.

You arrived soon after a new moon: the Moon had moved between 1° and 59° past the Sun, which is up to about five days. Sorted into eight phases, as many astrologers sort the month, seven such births in eight land in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase and one in eight in the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/). The Moon has only just left the Sun behind and already stands in the next sign, the one that makes things last. Where the boundaries lie, and why they matter, is the subject of [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/).

About 1 person in 145 has this pair. The Moon changes sign roughly every two and a half days, and a birthday on which it did needs the time to choose between this page and the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-aries/) or the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-gemini/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Your rising sign needs that time as well, and it's the third line of a first sketch the two lights only begin.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You tend to choose quickly and stay a long time. The Sun makes the first move; the Moon needs the bond to grow familiar before it feels safe, and then it holds. A partner finds frankness and loyalty in the same person, which is rarer than it sounds. What catches you out is the gap between the two speeds. You may announce a change with Aries confidence, then feel your own Taurus Moon dig in once it arrives at home. Give yourself the time to adjust that you'd give anyone you love.

## At work {#at-work}

You can open a project and stay with it, which not every Aries Sun can. The launch draws the Sun; the Moon is calmest once effort turns into something solid that stays put, a workshop, a client list, a set of tools you know by heart. Constant reorganisation costs the Moon more than it costs the Sun, so the role that suits the pair best lets you start new things from a stable base, and come back to it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Beginning and keeping pull different ways. The Sun wants the new thing now; the Moon wants the familiar thing tonight. You can feel restless when life is calm and unsettled when it changes, sometimes in the same week. And with both lights exalted, you may trust both impulses equally and expect them to agree. They rarely will on their own. Give each one its territory, new starts at work or in a project, steady rituals at home, and the two exaltations stop competing for the same evening.
