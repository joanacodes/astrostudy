The 6th house is the house of ordinary days: the work you do, the routines that hold you up, the way you look after your body. Tradition gave it illness and toil; I read it as daily work and daily care.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 6th house as the shape of an ordinary day. It covers the work you do day to day — the tasks rather than the title — your habits and routines, the way you organise yourself, the colleagues you work alongside, and the care you give your body: sleep, food, movement, rest. Modern astrologers often add the animals you look after.

Tradition gave the 6th illness and called it Bad Fortune. I don't read illness in a chart: no chart can diagnose or predict a health problem, and anything about your health belongs with a doctor ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). What I read here is how you work and how you care for yourself.

- **A cadent house.** It follows a succedent one, and cadent houses are read as places of adjusting and maintaining: the 6th keeps things running. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) and [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/): method, detail, usefulness.
- **Bad Fortune.** Tradition gave it that name and said [Mars](/astrology/mars/) rejoices here ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): the planet of effort, at home in the house of toil.
- **Its opposite.** The [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/), which tradition called Bad Spirit, where Saturn has its joy: the 6th is the daily round you can see, the 12th what goes on out of sight.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/), just before it, is play; the 6th is the work that follows. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), just after it, turns the chart towards other people: after looking after yourself comes the person across the table.

Like every house, the 6th is placed by your birth time: without it, I can read your planets in their signs, but not which of them shape your days ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 6th house {#planets}

A planet in the 6th house works for its living: it shows in your job, your habits and the way you look after yourself.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), against about a third for most placements. If Saturn, [Chiron in the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-in-6th-house/) or [Lilith in the 6th house](/astrology/lilith-in-6th-house/) sits here, the way you work — and whether you know when to stop — is where I'd spend the most time in a reading.

- **[Sun in the 6th house](/astrology/sun-in-6th-house/):** you shine through useful, well-made daily work.
- **[Moon in the 6th house](/astrology/moon-in-6th-house/):** a day that runs well is how you feel safe; you look after others almost without thinking.
- **[Mercury in the 6th house](/astrology/mercury-in-6th-house/):** in the house of its own sign: lists, methods and details; you think by doing.
- **[Venus in the 6th house](/astrology/venus-in-6th-house/):** you need a pleasant place to work and easy colleagues; you bring grace to ordinary tasks.
- **[Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/):** in its joy: a hard worker with energy for every task, and little patience for slowness.
- **[Jupiter in the 6th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-6th-house/):** generous at work and quick to help — and inclined to take on too much.
- **[Saturn in the 6th house](/astrology/saturn-in-6th-house/):** a strong work ethic that needs limits as much as discipline.
- **[Uranus in the 6th house](/astrology/uranus-in-6th-house/):** irregular routines; you need freedom in how and when you work.
- **[Neptune in the 6th house](/astrology/neptune-in-6th-house/):** work as service or calling; routines that slip unless they mean something.
- **[Pluto in the 6th house](/astrology/pluto-in-6th-house/):** all or nothing about work; you change the way things are done.

No planet here? An [empty 6th house](/astrology/empty-houses/) doesn't mean you don't work or don't look after yourself. It's read through the sign on its cusp, the place of its ruler, and Mercury.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the cusp of the 6th house gives your days their style: how you work, which routines suit you, how you look after yourself.

- **[Aries on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-6th-house-cusp/):** you work fast and in bursts; routine bores you unless you set it yourself.
- **[Taurus on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a steady rhythm; comfort and good food keep you going.
- **[Gemini on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-6th-house-cusp/):** variety, several tasks at once, and talk while you work.
- **[Cancer on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/):** you make a workplace feel like home; care comes first.
- **[Leo on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** you need to be proud of your work, and appreciated for it.
- **[Virgo on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** the house's natural sign: method and precision; you improve how things are done.
- **[Libra on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-6th-house-cusp/):** harmony with colleagues and a pleasant place to work.
- **[Scorpio on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/):** deep focus; you work best alone, and to the bottom of things.
- **[Sagittarius on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** you need meaning and room to move; repetition wears you down.
- **[Capricorn on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-6th-house-cusp/):** discipline and long hours; you like a structure that holds.
- **[Aquarius on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** your own methods; teamwork, as long as you stay free.
- **[Pisces on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/):** flexible days; work that helps, and boundaries to keep.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 6th house is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp. Where it sits shows where your daily work and routines go: the part of life your effort serves. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the cusp, there's a modern and a traditional ruler to read ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** you're known for how you work; effort is part of who you are.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** daily work as a livelihood; skills that pay.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** work with words, messages or short trips.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** work from home, or routines built around home and family.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** work that feels like play, or a craft you love.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** routines matter twice over; the work itself is the point.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** one-to-one work: clients, partners, the people you serve.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** work with other people's money, with crises, or with research.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** work tied to teaching, travel, publishing or far-off places.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** daily work and career closely linked; a name for competence.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** work in teams, groups and networks.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** work behind the scenes, in institutions or in quiet service.

## Transits through the 6th house {#transits}

The Moon crosses your 6th house every month, the Sun once a year, Mercury, Venus and Mars for a few weeks: short spells when the to-do list takes over. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 6th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-6th-house/):** about a year of more work, more willingness, and better habits within reach.
- **[Saturn transiting the 6th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-6th-house/):** about two and a half years of reorganising your days: discipline, limits, routines rebuilt to last.
- **[Uranus transiting the 6th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-6th-house/):** around seven years of changes in how you work, and a need for freedom in your routine.
- **[Neptune transiting the 6th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-6th-house/):** around fourteen years when routines dissolve unless they mean something, and work leans towards service.
- **[Pluto transiting the 6th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-6th-house/):** a long, deep change in your relationship with work.
- **[Chiron transiting the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-6th-house/):** an old hurt about being useful, or being enough, comes up to be tended.
- **[North Node transiting the 6th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-6th-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards better routines and useful work.

I read these as weather, not a forecast. None of them says anything about your health: they describe what work and routine may feel like for a while, and a doctor is the person to ask about your body.
