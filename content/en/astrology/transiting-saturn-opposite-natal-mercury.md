About fifteen years after Saturn crosses your Mercury, halfway round its cycle, it stands opposite. For some months your way of thinking and speaking is set against a structure that isn't yours: an editor's red pen, an examiner's criteria, a client's terms, the rulebook of a new job.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) processes what you take in and turns it into words, plans and decisions. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) sets the frame: standards, responsibilities, what has to hold over time. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, so that each shows up clearly against the other. Here your ideas are seen from the outside, by people who measure them, and you see their standards more sharply than before.

That can feel like being marked. A proposal comes back with questions, a draft with cuts, and a decision you took quickly needs explaining to someone who will hold you to it. Some people give way too fast under this transit, others contest every point; both miss what it's for, which is finding out which standards you genuinely share and saying so plainly. Analysis isn't overthinking, but rehearsing a hard conversation for weeks comes close: this opposition is better met by having the conversation.

Contracts, negotiations and paperwork often come up, and I give no verdicts on them. I don't read the outcome of an agreement, an exam or a dispute ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)).

The two houses on the axis show what is being weighed. With your Mercury in the 1st and Saturn crossing your 7th ([Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/)), your own way of putting things meets a partner's or an associate's terms. A birth time is what tells me Mercury is in your 1st and not your 12th; without it, the axis can't be drawn and I give no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use for timing, Saturn usually spends about nine months on this opposition. When it turns round nearby, as it does in a little over half of its [transits](/astrology/transits/), it touches the exact point three times in about nine and a half months; a straight crossing can take as little as three weeks. Saturn moves slowest at its [stations](/astrology/stations/), the days when it seems to stand still before reversing, and a station within a degree or two of the point is what draws the transit out.

The opposition comes once a round, so people meet it two or three times by 80, and nobody gets there without one. Because Mercury is never more than about 28° from the Sun, it falls within two or three years of [transiting Saturn opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-sun/), sometimes in the same season.

Each round begins with [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-mercury/). [Transiting Saturn sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-mercury/), [transiting Saturn square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-mercury/) and [transiting Saturn trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-mercury/) each come twice, once on either side of the opposition, which comes only once, at the far end. Born with [Mercury opposite Saturn](/astrology/mercury-opposite-saturn/), you know the feeling of a marker reading over your shoulder from the inside, and this transit turns it up for a season.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask for the criteria in writing. Knowing exactly what you're measured against takes half the sting out of being measured.

Answer after a night's sleep. Saturn opposite Mercury rewards the considered reply over the quick one.

Separate the work from your worth. A cut paragraph, a refused idea or a corrected figure is information about the work, and the opposition is far easier to live with once you treat it that way.

Then decide which rules you'll keep. Some of the standards you meet now will become yours; say which, and let the rest go.
