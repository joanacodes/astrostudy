Some people make the part of you that you learned to hide seem suddenly ordinary, or suddenly very visible. Uranus conjunct Lilith in synastry puts one person's Uranus within 4° of the other's Black Moon Lilith, so the first person's appetite for difference sits right on top of the second person's refusals.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the urge to differ and to free what has been held too tightly; it reforms, experiments and claims autonomy. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's apogee, is a symbol rather than a body, and traditions weight it differently; I read it as instinctive autonomy, a personal boundary, and whatever was set aside early as too much. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) runs two functions together, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one lays one person's licence to be different on the other's untamed side.

The meeting can feel like permission. Beside someone whose Uranus sits on your Lilith, what you were told was too loud, too odd or too independent reads as interesting rather than as a problem, and some people stop editing themselves.

Carrying the Uranus, you may find the other person's hidden edge fascinating and say so; their refusals look to you like originality waiting for an outlet.

Carrying the Lilith, you are seen at exactly the place you usually guard. That can be a relief, or it can feel like being outed before you were ready.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Fusion blurs whose freedom it is. The Uranus person can turn the other's untamed side into a cause of their own and push for it to be shown on a timetable that isn't theirs; the Lilith person can borrow the other's licence instead of choosing their own moments, then feel exposed when the other moves on. A boundary isn't a rule waiting to be broken, and difference isn't automatically truth.

- **[Uranus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** an outlet offered at a distance, taken up when the other chooses.
- **[Uranus square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-lilith-synastry/):** restlessness pressing on a guarded line, and the line holding.
- **[Uranus trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-lilith-synastry/):** two appetites for doing things one's own way, egging each other on.
- **[Uranus opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** the openly different one and the one who refuses, face to face.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask before you celebrate. The Uranus person can ask how much of this the other wants seen, and by whom; the Lilith person can say where the line is, and move it when they choose. Between partners, that may be what is shown in front of family; at work, how much of a colleague's unusual streak gets credit.

Someone born in mid-1965, with Uranus in Virgo, has it with anyone born from January to March 1962, when mean Lilith crossed the same degrees; the window returns about every nine years, from September to November 1979, from July to September 1988, from May to August 1997. Turned round, the Uranus of anyone born, on and off, from March 1994 to January 1997 sits on that 1965 Lilith. Whoever shares those months shares the contact, so it stays outside my synastry count, which tallies only contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. I read it through the houses, as two eras' ideas of what may be shown more than two people's.

These dates use mean Lilith; true Lilith can sit tens of degrees away and take the contact in or out of orb ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). The hour barely moves either point, but it sets the houses, so both birth times come first ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Saturn, Lilith and Chiron held 75 to 90% of the time, each checked within one chart; that figure says nothing about a contact between two.

Inside one chart, [Uranus conjunct Lilith](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-lilith/) makes a person's originality and their untamed side the same thing. Between two charts it touches what someone learned to hide, so I read it only when both people want it read ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
