Long before it has a word for something, water knows how it feels. Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces are the water signs, and in a chart they mark the places where you take life in through feeling first.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Water closes each run of four signs, after fire, earth and air, so [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) and [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) sit a [trine](/astrology/trine/) apart. Water's way is feeling: it reads the room, holds on to memory and attachment, senses undercurrents, and needs trust before it opens. Each water sign follows one of the [air signs](/astrology/air-signs/), Gemini, Libra and Aquarius: what air talked over, water now lives.

The three move differently ([the three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/)):

- **Cancer**, cardinal, reaches out. The Sun is there from about 21 June to 22 July, from the June solstice: looking after people, making a place safe.
- **Scorpio**, fixed, holds on. About 23 October to 21 November, in deep autumn: depth, loyalty, the truth under the surface.
- **Pisces**, mutable, lets go. About 19 February to 20 March, as winter ends: compassion, imagination, the softening of edges.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Water is where two of the three outer planets arrived. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules Cancer alone, but Scorpio has Pluto and Mars, and Pisces Neptune and Jupiter: two of the three water signs have a modern ruler and a traditional one, more than any other element ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)).

In Dorotheus's scheme the water triplicity answers to Venus by day and Mars by night, with the Moon as partner: once again Ptolemy's three night planets ([triplicity rulers](/astrology/triplicity-rulers/)). With earth, water makes up the receptive, feminine half of the zodiac. Jupiter is exalted in Cancer and Venus in Pisces. Three planets have their fall in water, Mars in Cancer, the Moon in Scorpio and Mercury in Pisces: action, comfort and plain words each have to find another way in.

Water's natural houses are the [4th](/astrology/4th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house/) and [12th](/astrology/12th-house/): home and roots, intimacy and what's shared, and the part of life that happens out of sight. They're the most private houses of a chart, and water suits them.

Across the zodiac, every water sign faces an earth sign: Cancer and Capricorn, Scorpio and Taurus, Pisces and Virgo. Those are three of the [six sign axes](/astrology/six-sign-axes/); the other three join fire to air. Feeling on one side, form on the other, and each pair has what the other lacks.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

Planets in water signs show where you're most permeable: where moods reach you, where you remember, where you need closeness to feel safe. Several of them often go with a private, intuitive, loyal temperament. None doesn't mean no feeling, only that it may come through thought or action before it's recognised ([element balance and missing elements](/astrology/element-balance-and-missing-elements/)). Water placements are also where I choose my words most carefully: what people feel deeply, they usually feel privately, and naming it is something to offer gently rather than announce.

Cancer's ruler is the quickest body in the chart. The Moon spends only two and a half days or so in each sign, and when it enters or leaves a water sign on the day of a birth, the time has to decide which ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). And with Scorpio or Pisces on a house cusp, I read both rulers and tell you what each one says ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Water signs are too sensitive.”** Sensitivity is a way of taking things in, not a weakness. It notices what others miss.
- **“Water signs are psychic.”** Water reads moods and undercurrents well, which can feel uncanny. It's close attention, and like all attention it can be wrong.
- **“Water signs are moody.”** Feeling deeply isn't the same as changing all the time. Scorpio is the fixed water sign, and its feelings stay.
- **“Water signs can't make decisions.”** Cancer is cardinal and takes the first step to look after someone. Water decides through feeling, which is still deciding.
