Jupiter conjunct Neptune joins the planet of meaning to the planet of imagination. The two meet about every thirteen years, so the people born in a few months around each meeting share it; through your houses, it becomes your own way of believing in something larger.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) synthesises meaning: it learns, believes, and looks for the principle that makes sense of experience. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and dissolves unnecessary boundaries: it inspires, feels for others, perceives symbols and longs for something beyond the everyday. Joined in a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), they become a single process with two natures intact: belief turns imaginative, and imagination goes looking for meaning.

In plain words, your sense of what life could be is wide and generous. You may be drawn to causes, art, spiritual questions or the care of others, to anything that makes the world feel larger and kinder. That's a real gift for vision, compassion and inspiration. Its tender spot is scale: an ideal can grow faster than the means to live it, and compassion can forget your own limits. Idealisation isn't delusion, and compassion isn't self-erasure; the conjunction matures as it learns both.

Both planets rule Pisces, Jupiter in tradition and Neptune in modern tropical astrology; I read the two rulers side by side, and this meeting brings the two faces of that sign's faith and imagination together.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Roughly one birth in 27 between 1930 and 2009 carries it within 6°, spread over seven windows: August to October 1932 and April to May 1933; August to October 1945; December 1957 to April 1958 and August to October 1958; December 1970 to October 1971; December 1983 to February 1984 and July to October 1984; December 1996 to February 1997; and April to December 2009.

Born in one of those windows, you share it with everyone who arrived that season, so it describes a cohort first, as the slower planets' patterns do ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). Your houses make it personal. A 9th-house conjunction could mean a search for meaning through study, travel or faith; a 12th-house one, a private, inner sense of the sacred; a 6th-house one, compassion put into daily work. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Jupiter and Neptune at their other angles:

- **[Jupiter sextile Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-neptune/):** an opening for inspiration to become useful, for those born in 1955–56 or 1968–69, among others.
- **[Jupiter square Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-square-neptune/):** ideals and principles rubbing, for the births of 1954–55 or 1999–2000, for example.
- **[Jupiter trine Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-trine-neptune/):** meaning and imagination flowing together with little effort.
- **[Jupiter opposite Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-neptune/):** the two facing each other, asking for a balance between belief and vision.

[Jupiter conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-neptune-synastry/) puts one person's sense of meaning on the other's imagination: inspiring, sometimes idealising, and kindest when both keep their feet on the ground.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-neptune/) comes round with each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/), about every 12 years, for a few days or weeks, since your Neptune sits beside your Jupiter. [Transiting Neptune conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) is rare: Neptune is so slow, about 165 years round, that it catches up with your Jupiter only in early childhood, when Jupiter was slightly ahead at birth, and otherwise never. What this cohort does meet is the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/), around 41, when transiting Neptune squares both planets at once.

Weather, not a forecast: seasons in which ideals and imagination ask for room, and checking what is real stays part of the work.
