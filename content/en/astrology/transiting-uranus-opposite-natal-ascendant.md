The point opposite your Ascendant is your Descendant, where the chart turns towards other people one to one. Transiting Uranus opposite your Ascendant is Uranus arriving there, about forty-two years after it crossed your rising degree, at the start of its years in your 7th house.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) frees: it asks for enough room to test other arrangements. Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is how you start from yourself; the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), opposite, is how you turn towards someone else. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates them through polarity: two ends of one axis, each clearer for the other. With Uranus on your Descendant, your independence and your partnerships come into view together: a partner, a colleague or a friend changes the terms, or you want to stay close without being held in place. Sometimes it's a new kind of bond altogether: a collaboration or a friendship built on more independence than the ones before it.

Autonomy isn't isolation, and that distinction does most of the work here. Reciprocity isn't agreement either: two people can give each other more room and still want different things, and under this opposition the difference is often put into words for the first time. The question this [transit](/astrology/transits/) puts is how much freedom a bond can hold, yours and the other person's, and the answers are as varied as the relationships. A separation, a wedding, a new partner: I don't read any of them from this transit ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 7th house begins at the Descendant, so the opposition is the first day of Uranus's stay there: about seven years as a rule in charts drawn for London, from under five to about twelve ([Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/)). Born with Uranus close to your Ascendant ([Uranus in the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-in-1st-house/)), you meet this transit at your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 42, when Uranus reaches the point opposite both its own birth position and your rising degree.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus holds your Descendant for about twenty months, or as little as eleven, and crosses it three times in about thirteen passes out of fourteen.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have met it once, and about 1 in 33 never do: they were born with Uranus a little past their Descendant, and it needs nearly its whole round of 84 years to get back there. [Transiting Uranus trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-ascendant/) comes about fourteen years before and after it, [transiting Uranus square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-ascendant/) about twenty-one, and [transiting Uranus sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-ascendant/) about twenty-eight, on the way to and from [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-ascendant/).

[Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/) has its turn while Uranus crosses Gemini, until May 2033, and Capricorn rising while it crosses Cancer, between 2032 and 2039. Your rising degree, and so your birth time, sets the months ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say what you need in a form the other person can answer: a request, not a verdict.

Change the agreement before you look for the door. Many bonds can make more room than anyone has asked of them.

Keep something that is yours alone, a day, a project, a long walk, so that closeness doesn't have to carry all of your need for room.
