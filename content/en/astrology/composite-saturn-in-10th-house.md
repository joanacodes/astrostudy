Two names on the door of the practice, the pair everyone in the village asks when something needs organising, a joint reputation built over twenty years. Composite Saturn in the 10th house hands a relationship a public role, and the job of carrying it well.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is a chart's public face: its work in the world, its reputation, the role others see it in. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it's how the relationship stands in public, and what it is known for. With [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) here, it's known for being reliable. The pair may work together, run something together, or simply be the two people a community turns to when a job needs doing properly. Ambitions are pursued on a long timescale, and the bond's standing is built slowly, through promises kept where others can see them.

The 10th opens at the composite [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the midpoint of your two Midheavens, and that is the one cusp every method of composite houses agrees on; the cusp that closes the house is where they part. Both birth times are still needed, because each natal Midheaven moves with the hour, and a Saturn near the far edge can slip into the 11th with the method chosen ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use for birth charts, the Midheaven always opens the 10th, as in every quadrant system.

Capricorn, Saturn's own sign, is the 10th's natural sign, and the house rewards what Saturn does best: patience, responsibility, the long view. Compare a single chart, where [Saturn in the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-in-10th-house/) tends to mean a career built late and on solid ground, or a synastry, where [their Saturn in your 10th house](/astrology/saturn-in-10th-house-synastry/) is one person's sense of duty bearing on the other's standing: in the composite, the reputation belongs to the pair, and may differ from what either of you is known for alone. Legacy belongs here as well. What the two of you will be remembered for, in a workplace, a street or a family, tends to be the steady thing rather than the spectacular one.

Picture the chart's top floor: [composite Saturn in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-9th-house/) is the study where the bond works out what it believes, [composite Saturn in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-11th-house/) the room where its friends gather, and the 10th the balcony everyone can see from the street.

Where it can go wrong is a bond that lives inside its role. Public duties can swallow private time, the pair's image can matter more than how it actually feels, and success, or the lack of it, can start to stand for the worth of the bond itself. It helps to keep part of life entirely out of view, to decide together what success means to the two of you rather than borrowing a definition, and to mark achievements privately as well as in public. A composite never promises the success it describes a pair working towards.

Since the 10th is the house of what is said in public, I'll say it plainly: I describe composites, but my readings don't use them. Astrologers who do would set the composite 10th beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and read no career forecast from it.
