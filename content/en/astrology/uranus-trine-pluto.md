Uranus trine Pluto is missing from every birth between 1930 and 2009: in those eighty years, the two planets never came within 6° of a trine. It belongs to people born before or after the count, and it still reaches everyone else, through transits and between charts.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables reform, liberation and new solutions: it unsettles what has grown rigid. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) enables transformation, regeneration and empowerment: it lets the obsolete go and brings up a deeper strength. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates the two through flow, each reinforcing the other while keeping its own nature.

For this pair, the trine would mean change that reaches the roots without a fight. A reform would carry real renewal with it; a deep letting-go would welcome a new form rather than resist it. That's a rare combination, since these two planets, read on their own, both tend to arrive with some intensity. The trine's ease isn't inevitability, though. Change taken for granted can stall, and a quiet confidence in transformation can be left with nothing to transform.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The count found no birth with it, and its own periods show why. From 1930 to 1936, Uranus was 90° behind Pluto and closing; it caught up in the conjunction of 1963 to 1968, pulled ahead through the sextile of the 1990s, and in 2009 was already back at a square. The trine lies before the first of those squares and after the second, outside the eighty years on both sides. So among adults born from 1930 onwards, nobody has it. Many of them have no major Uranus–Pluto aspect at all: births from 1936 to 1941, from 1947 to 1963, from 1968 to 1991 and from 2001 to 2009 fall between the pair's aspects.

What the pair's other aspects describe instead:

- **[Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/):** the meeting of the 1960s, in Virgo.
- **[Uranus sextile Pluto](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto/):** the two openings of the 1940s and the 1990s, about 1 birth in 7 in the count.
- **[Uranus square Pluto](/astrology/uranus-square-pluto/):** the friction of the early 1930s, back in orb in 2009.
- **[Uranus opposite Pluto](/astrology/uranus-opposite-pluto/):** like the trine, absent from the count.

Between two charts, [Uranus trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-pluto-synastry/) is possible for people born some years apart: one person's Uranus trine the other's Pluto, as between someone born in the early 1930s with Uranus in Aries and someone born with Pluto in Leo, from the end of the 1930s to the 1950s. One brings the appetite for change, the other the depth, and I read it from both sides. No birth chart from those eighty years holds this trine, but a relationship can.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-pluto/) comes twice in Uranus's 84-year round; at about seven years a sign it lasts months, often in three passes. For those born with the sextile, in the 1940s or the 1990s, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) at about 42 is also this trine, since the point opposite natal Uranus lies 120° from natal Pluto.

[Transiting Pluto trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-uranus/) is slow, Pluto taking 12 to 31 years over a sign. For the generation born with Uranus and Pluto together in Virgo, Pluto's years in Capricorn brought it to their natal Uranus. Which parts of life such a season touches depends on the houses involved, and those rest on a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Weather, not a forecast: a season when changing and letting go come unusually easily, with nothing decided in advance.
