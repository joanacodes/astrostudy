Pluto sextile your Venus is a long chance to keep what you love and let go of what you only put up with. For two to three years the planet of depth stands 60° from your Venus, and deepening a bond, lightening a crowded life or closing something gracefully comes more easily, if you decide to.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) renews by releasing what can't go on. Your [Venus](/astrology/venus/) values, prefers, and weighs what is given and what comes back. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through opportunity and cooperation, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) what you value can be deepened or simplified with less friction than usual: a friendship becomes closer, a cupboard emptier, a taste you had outgrown is put down without regret. The sextile offers; it doesn't choose for you.

Some preferences were adopted to keep the peace or to fit in: a style worn for someone else, an agreeableness that cost more than it gave, a pleasure dropped because somebody frowned on it. Those were adaptations rather than your nature, and this sextile makes it easier to find out which tastes are really yours. Harmony isn't conflict avoidance, either. Saying what you prefer, kindly, is part of what keeps a bond honest.

Pluto's other contacts with Venus ask harder questions of what you value: the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-venus/)) whether it is honest, the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-venus/)) whether it is shared fairly, the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-venus/)) whether it is still yours at all. The trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-venus/)) and this sextile ask more gently, and the sextile waits for your answer. In a birth chart, [Venus sextile Pluto](/astrology/venus-sextile-pluto/) keeps that door ajar for life.

With Venus in the 4th and Pluto crossing the 2nd ([Pluto transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-2nd-house/)), sorting what you own and value makes home feel more like yours; it says nothing about money, gained or lost. A 4th-house Venus and a 2nd-house Pluto can only be found with the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My orbs for timing, 1½° before exact and 1° after, give this sextile about two years and eight months, and about twenty-two months in the quickest passes. Pluto turns back over the point each year, so it is crossed three times, or five in about two passes out of five.

Nearly one person in four born between 1930 and 2009 never meets a Pluto sextile to Venus by 80. Most of the others meet it once, some twice, one from each side of the circle, and a very few three times.

Pluto, in Aquarius until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), sextiles Venuses in Aries and Sagittarius, the signs on either side. Since Venus keeps within about 47° of the Sun, a Venus in Aries goes with a birthday between about early February and early June, and a Venus in Sagittarius with one between about early October and early February.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sort by love rather than by guilt: keep what you would choose again today.

Say one true preference aloud each week, to someone who matters to you. Small honesty is how this sextile does its work.

Try mending before ending. A sextile is the easiest season for the frank conversation a tired bond needs.
