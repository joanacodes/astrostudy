The plan was settled on Sunday; by Wednesday one of you has a better idea. Sun square Uranus between two charts has the Uranus of one person at roughly a right angle to the other's Sun, most often in two signs of one modality, and the need for change keeps meeting a direction side-on.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises a person's identity and purpose, and gives a life its line. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and reforms: it wants autonomy, experiment, the better system. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through constructive friction, the kind that forces adaptation, and here, as in all [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the need for change and the need for a line live in different people.

Out of this rub comes reform that lasts, because it had to convince somebody first. The Sun person, pushed off a path they'd stopped questioning, often ends up with a plan that's better for having been challenged. The Uranus person, meeting someone who asks why, learns to time a change and argue for it instead of simply making it.

The modality shapes the argument. Two cardinal signs both want to start something, just not the same thing; two fixed signs turn it into a standoff between keeping and changing; two mutable signs revise each other's revisions.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

For the Sun person: interruptions. A direction you were proud of gets redesigned, sometimes without warning, and the other's need for space can feel like a comment on you. You may find yourself defending plans you weren't even attached to, just to keep something steady.

For the Uranus person: a feeling of being organised. The other's plans seem to expect you to fit around them, and your need for autonomy flares exactly when they take the lead. You can end up cast as the one who disrupts, when you were only trying to improve things.

Innovation isn't chaos, and reform isn't destruction; the friction is ordinary and useful. It gets stuck when change arrives as a surprise rather than a proposal, and when a plan becomes a matter of pride.

Uranus's sign is shared across about seven years of births, so it says more about the Uranus person's generation than about them. The Sun person's birthday is what puts the square between these two particular people.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree on what's fixed and what's open. The Sun person can name the one or two things that have to stay put; the Uranus person can give notice of changes and propose them as experiments with a review date. Couples often need separate interests as much as shared ones; friends, plans with a flexible day built in; families, holidays where one tradition is up for change each year; coworkers, a way of trying new methods that the project's owner signs off.

About 1 pair of people in 7 has this square, one way round or the other, within 6.8°. Both birth times come first. A few hours seldom move the Sun far enough to change the square, but the houses tell me which area of each life takes the interruptions: a shared home, a career, a circle of friends ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

If yours is one of the other angles, the change behaves differently: [Sun sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus-synastry/) and [Sun trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus-synastry/) offer it rather than force it, [Sun conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) lands it on the identity itself, and [Sun opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus-synastry/) holds it up like a mirror. In one chart, [Sun square Uranus](/astrology/sun-square-uranus/) is a person whose own plans and own restlessness rub; here the plan and the restlessness belong to two people, which makes the tension easier to see and harder to ignore.
