Opposite your rising degree sits the Descendant, the point where the zodiac was setting when you were born. When Pluto reaches it, the depth that Pluto brings arrives from across the room: through the people you face one to one, for two to three years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) works by empowerment, growth in the kind of agency that is really yours, and by clearing out arrangements that no longer hold. Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is the stance you begin from; the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) opposite it is where you meet a counterpart, someone with a centre of their own. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates two ends of one axis, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) what you learn about your own footing tends to come through someone else: a partner who asks for more of you, a collaborator with real stakes in the outcome, a rival who shows you what you want, a client or an adviser whose judgement weighs more than usual.

Pluto's question here is who sets the terms. It can show up as a negotiation, as a bond that grows more serious, as a pattern of giving way you finally notice, or as your own strength making someone else uneasy. Two mistakes come easily: reading every intense connection as the true one, and reading every uneven exchange as someone trying to dominate you. Neither is a rule. What tends to come out of these years is a way of relating where both people can be powerful without either having to shrink.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 7th house begins at the Descendant, so this crossing opens Pluto's long stay there ([Pluto transiting the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-7th-house/)). Both ends of the [horizon axis](/astrology/horizon-axis/) come from the minute and place of your birth, and a time remembered as "late afternoon" can put this transit years away from where it falls; I don't read a chart without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Control and abuse are never written in a chart, and nothing in this transit asks you to accept them. If someone in your life frightens you, that's not a season to wait out: it's for people trained to help ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/); help lines are in my [disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within the orbs I use, 1½° before exact to 1° after, Pluto holds your Descendant for about two years and eight months, and for about twenty-two months when it is moving quickly. Its yearly loop brings three exact crossings in about three passes out of five and five in the rest; a few of the five-crossing passes last more than four and a half years.

More than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet it by 80, and nobody meets it more than twice. The Descendant is a single point, and Pluto travels less than half the zodiac in a lifetime: it reaches your Descendant only if that point lies in the stretch Pluto covers after you are born. The conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)) is just as rare. The square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-ascendant/)) comes to about seven people in eight, and the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-ascendant/)) and the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-ascendant/)) to about three in four.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until January 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), the Descendants it crosses belong to Leo rising: the first degrees of Leo in the late 2020s, the middle in the 2030s, the last as Pluto leaves. Virgo rising follows once Pluto is in Pisces.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Before you agree to anything big with someone, write down what you want from it in your own words. It tells you, later, whose terms you signed up to.

Treat intensity as information, not proof. A strong pull towards someone says the bond matters; it doesn't say on its own that the bond is right.

Practise letting other people be powerful without making yourself smaller, and being powerful without asking them to be smaller. That balance is what this opposition is teaching.
