Venus trine Mars is usually looked up for love, and it does say something about love. But it reaches further: it's what happens in a chart when the part of you that values and the part of you that acts are on the same side.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a chart that weighs what's worth having: what attracts you, what you prefer, what you find beautiful, and how you keep an exchange fair. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the part that acts: it starts things, asserts what you want, puts in the effort and holds a line. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates them through flow: valuing doesn't turn into acting, or acting into valuing, but each makes the other easier.

In plain words, what you want and the way you go after it line up. You tend to pursue things you actually like rather than what you think you ought to want, and you can ask for something without spoiling the relationship you're asking in. Effort feels worth making because it's aimed at what you value, and pleasure doesn't drain your drive. I read it as warmth with a spine: affection that can act, and drive that stays kind.

What the trine adds to this pair is a shared temperament. A trine usually joins two signs of the same element (see [the four elements](/astrology/four-elements/)), so your Venus and Mars are likely to be both in fire, both in earth, both in air or both in water: you want things in the same register you act in. In fire, what draws you is what feels alive, and you go straight for it; in earth, it's what's real, and you build towards it patiently; in air, ideas and company draw you, and you act by talking; in water, closeness draws you, and you move by feel.

Ease has its own quiet trap. Because desire and action rarely argue in you, you may never have had to work out what you want, or to push when someone doesn't respond in kind. The trine doesn't guarantee anything: it's a resource, at its best when you notice it and use it on purpose, in a relationship, in work you care about, anywhere liking and doing need to cooperate.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, Venus trine Mars tends to show as easy traffic between wanting and doing, styled by the signs and set by the houses: Venus in the 7th trine Mars in the 11th, for instance, might let the warmth you bring to a partner and the energy you bring to friends feed each other. About 1 person in 19 is born with Venus and Mars within 6° of a trine. Within a degree or two, that ease between wanting and doing becomes a theme of the chart; near the edge of the orb, it's more of a background warmth.

Venus is the faster of the two on most days: it crosses a sign in about three to four weeks, Mars in about six to seven. So it's usually Venus that closes or widens the gap, and many astrologers feel a trine that's still closing as stronger than one that's opening ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)). Each also turns retrograde from time to time, Venus about every 18 months and Mars about every two years. Born during either, you may know what you want well before you act on it: I read that as the same ease working inward first, a rhythm rather than a delay ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

Venus and Mars can meet in every aspect, and each one wires value and action differently:

- **[Venus conjunct Mars](/astrology/venus-conjunct-mars/):** value and action fused in one gesture; the work is learning which of the two is speaking.
- **[Venus sextile Mars](/astrology/venus-sextile-mars/):** the two cooperate once you make the first move; an opening that asks to be taken.
- **[Venus square Mars](/astrology/venus-square-mars/):** what you value and how you assert yourself rub against each other, and the friction keeps you learning.
- **[Venus opposite Mars](/astrology/venus-opposite-mars/):** the two face each other; you often meet one of them in other people first, and learn to hold both.

Between two charts, [Venus trine Mars in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-mars-synastry/) is one person's way of valuing meeting the other's way of acting, at ease. I read it in both directions: the person who brings the Venus and the person who brings the Mars don't feel the same ease.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Venus and Mars are both quick, so their trines to each other in [transits](/astrology/transits/) are short. When Venus in the sky trines your natal Mars, it lasts two or three days, about twice a year: a good moment to ask for something, or to put your effort into something you love. When Mars trines your natal Venus, it stays a few days longer, since Mars is the slower: energy that goes easily towards what, and whom, you value. In a year when either one turns retrograde near the spot, the same trine can come back two or three times.

Weather, not a forecast: a Venus–Mars trine in transit is a mild tailwind for warmth and effort. It doesn't decide whom you'll meet or what you'll get; it makes the asking a little easier.
