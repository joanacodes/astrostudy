Leo is in its eclipse years: a total solar eclipse at 20°02′ Leo on 12 August 2026, another at 9°55′ on 2 August 2027. Then the sign rests until 2036, which brings three eclipses in Leo within a single year.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Leo](/astrology/leo/) is fixed fire, ruled by the Sun: expression, warmth, creative work and the wish to be seen for who you are. Its eclipses fall on the Leo–Aquarius axis, the one and the many, and they alternate with Aquarius’s as the Moon’s nodes move. At a [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) in Leo, the Sun is covered by the Moon in the very sign it rules; at a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/), the full Moon in Leo faces the Sun in Aquarius, as on 31 January 2037 at 11°59′.

For a chart, the AGS rule decides: an eclipse counts within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of its axis, read through that point and its house, and a penumbral lunar eclipse, like those of February 2009 and February 2017, weighs less. So the eclipse of 2 August 2027 would touch something of yours near 10° Leo or 10° Aquarius, and leave the rest of the chart alone. When natal nodes sit near such a degree, I check the mean node and the true node alike. It is emphasis, never an omen.

Sixteen in my list, from 2000 to 2040:

- **2000:** 21 January 2000, total lunar, 0°28′ · 31 July 2000, partial solar, 8°11′
- **2008–2009:** 1 August 2008, total solar, 9°32′ · 9 February 2009, penumbral lunar, 20°52′
- **2017–2019:** 11 February 2017, penumbral lunar, 22°34′ · 21 August 2017, total solar, 28°52′ · 31 January 2018, total lunar, 11°38′ · 11 August 2018, partial solar, 18°41′ · 21 January 2019, total lunar, 0°49′
- **2026–2027:** 12 August 2026, total solar, 20°02′ · 2 August 2027, total solar, 9°55′
- **2036–2038:** 11 February 2036, total lunar, 22°47′ · 23 July 2036, partial solar, 1°09′ · 21 August 2036, partial solar, 29°13′ · 31 January 2037, total lunar, 11°59′ · 21 January 2038, penumbral lunar, 1°05′

The most recent, 12 August 2026, and the next, 2 August 2027, are both total solar eclipses. Nineteen years separate several pairs: 1 August 2008 at 9°32′ and 2 August 2027 at 9°55′, 21 August 2017 at 28°52′ and 21 August 2036 at 29°13′. On either side, Cancer and Virgo have pages of their own ([Eclipse in Cancer](/astrology/eclipse-in-cancer/), [Eclipse in Virgo](/astrology/eclipse-in-virgo/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

I need [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) to know which house Leo opens, because the cusps move through the day; that is why there is no reading without it. Each line, in Placidus, my house system, holds only if a Leo eclipse meets a natal point in that house, and the Aquarius side of the axis reaches the house opposite.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/leo-rising/):** Leo rising: the warmth you show on arrival; how you want to be seen, brought into focus.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/leo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** generosity and what you value; the worth you put on your talents.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** expressive words; something you say with your whole heart.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a place of warmth; your pride in where you come from.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** Leo in its natural house: creativity, romance, play; a piece of work that carries your name.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** pride in daily work; how your efforts get seen.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Leo on the Descendant, with Aquarius rising: a partner who shines; the admiration that runs between you.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/leo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** loyalty and intimacy; what you share once pride is set aside. The 8th was the old house of death; I read intimacy and trust in it, and no date of anyone’s death ever comes from a chart I read ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
9. **[9th house](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** big-hearted beliefs; a journey or a teaching that warms you.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** visibility at work; a role where your light is noticed.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/leo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends who cheer you on; your place in a group.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/leo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** hidden warmth; the creative life you keep to yourself.
