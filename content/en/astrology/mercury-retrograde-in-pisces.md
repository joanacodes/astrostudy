Mercury turns back in Pisces between early February and early April, in the sign where tradition counts it weakest twice over, by detriment and by fall. That reputation makes this retrograde sound murkier than I find it: what slows is the mind’s sorting, and what comes forward is what it sensed and couldn’t yet say.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) is mutable water, ruled by Jupiter in the tradition and by Neptune for modern astrologers; I read both, the faith and the dream. Mercury, the planet of lists and labels, works there through images, music and intuition. When [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) reaches Pisces, the planet appears from Earth to drift to a halt and slip backwards, and what it goes over are impressions rather than facts: a dream you half remember, a feeling about someone you never put into words, a story that lives more in pictures than in sentences.

Seventeen retrogrades touch Pisces from 2000 to 2040, and seven of them stay wholly inside it, as many as in any sign. The most recent did: from 26 February to 20 March 2026, Mercury backed from 22°33′ to 8°29′ Pisces. The next, from 9 February to 3 March 2027, stations at 5°58′ Pisces and turns direct at 20°55′ Aquarius ([Mercury retrograde in Aquarius](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-aquarius/)). In 2025, from 15 March to 7 April, it had come in from Aries ([Mercury retrograde in Aries](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-aries/)), from 9°35′ Aries to 26°49′ Pisces, so Pisces has three years running: 2025, 2026 and 2027.

A birth during one of these weeks puts Mercury retrograde in Pisces into the chart for good, a way of thinking that listens inward before it speaks ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house these weeks touch depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): the houses are drawn from it, and without it I don’t draw them, or read the chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, I look at the house Pisces opens and at the degrees of the stations, then at the houses of your natal Jupiter and Neptune, since I read both rulers.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/pisces-rising/):** Pisces rising: a receptive manner that takes on the colour of the room; what you absorbed from others, sorted from what’s yours.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/pisces-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** values that money can’t measure; what you hold dear, put into words.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/pisces-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** imaginative talk and an intuitive bond with the people around you; a hunch about someone close, said aloud.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a refuge; an old family atmosphere, remembered more clearly.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/pisces-on-5th-house-cusp/):** idealised romance and creative work in music, film or poetry; a piece begun in a daydream, taken up again.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work that helps or imagines; gentle structure brought back to the day.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Pisces on the Descendant, with Virgo rising: a gentle, dreamy partnership; the relationship seen as it is, and put into words.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/):** intimacy as merging, and shared matters with soft edges; the edges drawn again, kindly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/):** a poetic or spiritual faith; a belief reconsidered through the images it gives you.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a working life in the arts, in care, or in anything hard to define; how you describe what you do, rewritten.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/pisces-on-11th-house-cusp/):** kindred spirits and groups with an ideal; a shared dream, put into a plan.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/pisces-on-12th-house-cusp/):** Pisces in its natural house: solitude, imagination and the unseen; time alone for what the busy mind kept missing.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write it down before you explain it: in Pisces, what Mercury is working on comes out more easily in a notebook than in a debate. If a feeling about someone arrives without its reasons, give it a few days to find words rather than acting on the hunch alone.

Clarity in Pisces arrives sideways, through a picture or a song, and these weeks leave room for it to come.
