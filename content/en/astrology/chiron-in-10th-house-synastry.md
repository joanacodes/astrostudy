Being seen at work, judged in public, given authority or refused it: few people climb through their 10th house without a bruise or two. When someone's Chiron falls there, the know-how they gained around an old hurt meets your public life.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) describes the limitation a person learned to work with rather than against, and the know-how they now pass on. [The 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is your public direction: the role you play in the world, the reputation you build, the authority you earn or resist, what you'd like to have done. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their practical wisdom meets your ambitions.

With them, being visible may get easier. They tend to know what it costs to be judged in public, so they help you take up a role without pretending to be flawless: the presentation rehearsed until it's yours, the gap in a CV explained without apology, the career change made late and on your own terms. Their praise is specific, and their warnings usually worth hearing.

For them, your standing can touch a sore place. Your ambition, your title or simply the ease with which you're seen may stir what they once had to work around in their own public life, and they can feel proud of you and exposed beside you at the same time. Angular and in full public view, the 10th has [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) for its natural sign. Right at its start, their Chiron meets your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the point where the meridian cut the zodiac at the hour of your birth, and that contact gets as much of my attention as the house.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Mentoring can become management. They may guard your reputation more fiercely than you'd like, warn you off risks they once paid for, or treat your career as a second chance for their own. If one of you manages the other, an old sensitivity about authority can turn ordinary feedback into something heavier.

From their side, your visibility can feel like a spotlight that catches them too. Public criticism of you may hurt them more than it hurts you, and a promotion you take in your stride may stir questions they thought they'd settled.

No overlay says whether a career will rise or stall, yours or theirs. This one describes how two people handle being seen.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let them advise and keep the decisions yours; give them credit out loud when their help made the difference. A couple building two careers can agree whose turn it is to take the risk; a mentor at work does best opening doors rather than choosing which ones you walk through; a friend can be the audience for your rehearsal; adult relatives may need to talk through old family expectations about status and what people will think.

Should your Chiron fall in their 10th house, the same applies with the roles exchanged: their public life presses on your old hurt, and the mentoring is yours to offer. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 10th begins at your Midheaven, and only your birth time can tell whether their Chiron sits just before it, in your 9th, or just after; their own date places Chiron firmly enough. I start a reading of the pair once both times are known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A reputation is public; a chart is not, and this one waits for both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
