Mars is exalted in Capricorn, yet in the dates I use it never makes a whole retrograde loop there: the loop of 2018 grazed the sign’s last degrees, and the one of 2033 covers its first twelve and a half. Both come in summer, and both are among the shortest Mars makes.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) is cardinal earth, ruled by Saturn, and tradition exalts Mars there: effort becomes discipline, aimed at a goal years away. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when Mars seems from Earth to halt and climb back down the slope for nine or ten weeks, the review lands on that discipline: the long plan you have been carrying out without checking it, the responsibility you took on because nobody else would, the ambition that was once yours and may now be a habit. Anger here is often held in check by duty, and these weeks can show what it was guarding. Desire wants something lasting; the question is whether it is still the same thing.

In 2018 Mars stationed at 9°13′ Aquarius ([Mars retrograde in Aquarius](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-aquarius/)) on 26 June and turned direct at 28°36′ Capricorn on 27 August, so only the sign’s last degree and a half or so saw it. In 2033 it stations at 12°30′ Capricorn on 26 May and backs into Sagittarius ([Mars retrograde in Sagittarius](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-sagittarius/)), turning direct at 29°47′ on 1 August, with Mars opposite the Sun, then in Cancer, around the end of June. Between 12°30′ and 28°36′, the middle of Capricorn meets no Mars retrograde in these years.

If Mars was retrograde in Capricorn when you were born, that inward-gathering, exalted drive is a placement for life, and I read it apart ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Capricorn occupies depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and I don’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, both loops cross a sign line, so each can begin in one house and end in the next.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/capricorn-rising/):** Capricorn rising: a composed, serious first impression; the effort to look in control, set down for a while.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** security built step by step; how much of your effort goes into staying safe, reconsidered.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** measured words; an instruction you gave a sibling or neighbour, softened.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/):** duty at home; the family responsibility you carry, shared out.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-5th-house-cusp/):** creative work taken seriously; play you turned into a project, allowed to be play again.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a demanding daily routine; overtime treated as normal, cut back. Tradition gave this house illness too, and there I read the routine, never your health ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
7. **[7th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn on the Descendant, with Cancer on the Ascendant: a partner who sets the rules; who carries the responsibility between you, rebalanced.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-8th-house-cusp/):** trust built slowly; the guard you keep in closeness, reconsidered.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs earned the hard way; a long course of study, its purpose re-examined.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn’s natural house: career and standing; a summit you have climbed towards for years, checked against what you want now.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-11th-house-cusp/):** long-term plans with friends; your role as the one who organises everything, eased.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-12th-house-cusp/):** duty done unseen; private ambitions you never admit, acknowledged.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Capricorn builds for the long term, so these weeks suit an audit rather than a demolition: list what you are working towards, cross out what you no longer want, and keep the rest. Saturn rules Capricorn, and the house of your natal Saturn shows where responsibility shapes the review; your natal Mars shows where the drive starts.

None of this predicts a promotion or a setback. It describes a summer in which the most disciplined drive in the zodiac checks what its discipline is for.
