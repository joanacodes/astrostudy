An open book with one chapter locked: that is how this pair often reads. The Gemini Moon settles by naming feelings and sharing them, lightly and often; the Scorpio Sun keeps its core for the few who have earned it. You were born a few days after a full moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Scorpio is fixed water and Gemini mutable air, five signs apart counting back from the Sun: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, a pairing that has to keep adjusting. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) goes deep into one thing at a time and stays there; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) feels better after a conversation, a change of subject, a question answered. Curiosity is the bridge between them: the Moon's questions often lead the Sun to exactly the depths it wants.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Gemini, so the way your Moon finds words, quickly or carefully, aloud or on paper, follows Mercury's sign and house. In the old books Scorpio belongs to [Mars](/astrology/mars/), which shows how your Sun pursues what it wants to know; modern astrologers give it [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), which shows what it needs to uncover and change. I read both. Neither light has a dignity in its sign, so the interest lies in the conversation between Mercury's lightness and Pluto's weight.

The Moon had passed its full, 180° to 239° from the Sun, in a lunar month 15 to 19½ days old. Almost every birth with this pair belongs to the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase, the few days after the exact full; the latest, about one in eight, fall in the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/). The quincunx itself is exact around 210°. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows where these two phases begin and end.

About 1 person in 149 is born with the Moon in Gemini and the Sun in Scorpio. The Moon moves through Gemini in two and a half days or so; born on the day it arrived from Taurus or left for Cancer, you may have the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-taurus/) or the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-cancer/) instead ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign comes from the same time, and it often decides how much of the locked chapter anyone gets to see.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, the talk comes easily and the trust comes slowly. The Gemini Moon wants conversation, news and play; the Scorpio Sun wants depth, loyalty and honesty about the hard things. A partner who only ever gets the conversation may feel they never quite reach you, and one who only meets the depth may miss your lightness. Letting someone have both, the jokes and the locked chapter, is how this pair comes to be fully known.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that needs questions and patience suits you: research, journalism, investigation, editing, psychology, any role where you keep asking until the real answer appears. The Gemini Moon gathers information quickly and talks to everyone; the Scorpio Sun knows which piece matters. You can be the colleague who seems to chat with the whole building and turns out to know exactly what is going on.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Talk can protect the core so well that nobody reaches it, you included. You may describe a feeling cleverly and never let it touch the depth where it would actually change something, or keep a serious hurt hidden behind light conversation for years. Now and then, let a conversation with someone you trust go all the way down, and leave a silence in it long enough for what is underneath to come up.
