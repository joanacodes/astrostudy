In Sun square North Node in synastry, one person's Sun stands 90° from the other's North Node, which puts it 90° from their South Node as well. It sits at the bend of the other person's path, halfway between where they come from and where they're heading, and belongs to neither end.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the organised centre a person acts from: identity, purpose, the self they express. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity someone is developing through practice, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite, the capacity they already rely on. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates functions through constructive friction. Of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) a Sun can make to the North Node, it's the only one that meets both nodes the same way: there are two such points, one on each side of the axis, and a Sun on either squares both ends at once.

What the friction makes possible is growth that has to include somebody. Around this person, the North Node person can't treat their development as a private project: the Sun person's way of being asks to be taken into account, neither as part of the old life nor as part of the plan. That's uncomfortable, and it tends to make the growth sturdier, because it has been tested against a real life beside it.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

With the North Node on your side, the other person can feel like a detour. Their rhythms, their needs, the way they take up space don't belong to your familiar ground, and they don't belong to the direction you've set yourself either. Around them you may feel asked to choose between old and new before you're ready, or pulled off a course you'd only just found.

Holding the Sun, you may feel like a side issue in the other's story. Your way of being gets treated as an interruption to their plans, or a question they'll answer later. It can sting, and it isn't a verdict on you: you've simply landed at the one point of their path that neither end covers.

Neither of you is in the way. A square is friction, not failure.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Talk about direction openly. If the North Node is yours, say where you're heading and why; if the Sun is yours, ask where you fit, and say what you need from the route. Then build one that includes both. Between co-workers, it's the colleague who keeps asking how a new approach affects their team: tiresome, and usually what makes the approach work. In families, couples and friendships, it's often the person whose presence turns a plan into something livable. Nor is it [Sun square Saturn in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-saturn-synastry/), where the friction is with limits and structure: here it's with a direction.

In my count it links one pair of people in every 7 or so, within 6.8°, whichever of the two brings the Sun. I check it against both the mean and the true node, which can disagree by a degree or two near the edge of the orb. And as with any synastry, I need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): they rarely make or unmake the square, but they place it in the houses of both charts, which show what part of each life the path has to bend around.

The same square in one chart is [Sun square North Node](/astrology/sun-square-north-node/). Between two, [Sun conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) and [Sun opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-north-node-synastry/) land on the ends this square misses, while [Sun sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-north-node-synastry/) and [Sun trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-north-node-synastry/) lean softly towards one end or the other.
