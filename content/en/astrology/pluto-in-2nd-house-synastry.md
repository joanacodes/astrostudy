Possessions, earnings, self-esteem: when their Pluto falls in your 2nd house, all three can come up for review. It's rarely a quiet audit: another person's depth reaches the house of your resources and asks what truly holds you up.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) goes to the root: it clears away what has stopped being viable and rebuilds on firmer ground. [The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) covers your income and belongings, the abilities that keep you afloat, and the value you put on yourself. Through this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their depth reaches your sense of security.

With them, you may let go of possessions that have stopped meaning anything, change the way you earn, or rebuild your self-worth on something sturdier than approval. Questions you'd rather avoid, such as what you'd keep if you had to choose, or what you're really paid for, become hard to dodge.

What you hold dear fascinates them. Your steadiness, or the things you guard, can draw them in; they may want to understand your values, back your plans, or find their own sense of worth shaken and remade by yours. The 2nd belongs to the succedent houses, which gather and keep. [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), its natural sign, faces Scorpio across the zodiac, the sign modern tropical astrology gives to Pluto, so this overlay sets depth against the wish to keep things as they are.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Money can turn into a language of power between you: who earns more, who decides, whose name is on what. Possessiveness may surface, about things or about each other, and arguments over value can feel more intense than the sums involved.

From their side, your attachment to what you have can look like a wall, and they may push to change what you'd rather keep, or feel judged by your caution.

This overlay never says whether you'll gain or lose; no chart can ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). It describes how depth and power move through what two people value.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep money transparent and decisions shared. Agree who pays for what, keep some resources each, and talk about worth before you talk about price. In a couple, a joint budget works best when neither of you holds all the information; between friends, lending is simpler with a date to repay; between colleagues, fees and ownership deserve writing down; between adult relatives, family belongings and old money matters go better discussed openly than assumed.

From the other side, this is your Pluto in their 2nd house, your depth reaching their resources. A generation's relationship to money and ownership is part of what a slow planet marks. Close in age, you share Pluto's sign, and if your own Pluto sits in your 2nd ([Pluto in the 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-in-2nd-house/)), this overlay echoes it; with a decade or two between you, two eras' ideas of security meet over your accounts.
