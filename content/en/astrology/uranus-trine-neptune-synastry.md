Some generations hand each other exactly what they need. In Uranus trine Neptune in synastry, about 120°, within 3.4°, link one person's Uranus to the other's Neptune: new ideas from one era, running the same way as the ideals of another.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) introduces the untried: new methods, new arrangements, freedom from what has gone stale. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) keeps hope and imagination alive: the vision of what life could be, and a compassion wider than one's own circle. Joined by a [trine](/astrology/trine/), the two make each other's work easier, each keeping its own character; in the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), that help crosses from one person to the other.

With the Uranus on your side, you find that the other person's ideals give your changes a direction you trust. You may find yourself building the practical version of something they have long hoped for.

If the Neptune is yours, this person's ideas look like your hopes made workable. Change, around them, feels less like a loss and more like a way forward.

A Virgo Uranus with a Capricorn Neptune, both earth: practical improvements, beside an ideal of institutions that last.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

What never rubs is rarely tested. The Uranus person's changes may fit the ideal so well that no one asks whether the ideal still holds; the Neptune person's vision may be served so smoothly that it never meets a hard question. Two generations agreeing easily can leave out everyone who sees things differently. Ease isn't proof, and the trine still has to be put to work.

- **[Uranus conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** change and ideal at a single point, close enough to blur.
- **[Uranus sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** two signs nearer, an offer of help rather than a current.
- **[Uranus square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune-synastry/):** a sign nearer, where reform and ideal rub.
- **[Uranus opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** two signs further, change and ideal facing each other.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Point the alliance at something difficult. Pick a problem the shared vision hasn't solved; the Uranus person can draft the change, the Neptune person keep asking what it is for, and a third view now and then helps both. A grandparent and a grown grandchild, a parent and an adult child, an older mentor and a younger colleague: the contact often spans decades, and it works best when the ease is put to use rather than simply enjoyed.

The count's examples span decades. A mid-1965 birth with Uranus in Virgo has the trine with people born from November 1988 to January 1991, when Neptune was in Capricorn; for a mid-1945 birth with Uranus in Gemini, the partners were born from December 2003 to March 2006, under Neptune in Aquarius, close to sixty years later. Everyone born in those spans has the same contact with the same people, so I leave it out of my synastry count, which only includes contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Read through the houses, it describes how easily one era's inventions carry another era's hopes, more than anything between two individuals.

Birth hours don't move either planet; they place the houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Uranus in the Neptune person's 10th house, the alliance shows in working life: a career reshaped towards an ideal ([Their Uranus in your 10th house](/astrology/uranus-in-10th-house-synastry/)).

Births of 1936 to 1946 hold [Uranus trine Neptune](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune/) within one chart, on and off. Between two charts the ease is shared across an age gap, and the reading waits for both people's request ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
