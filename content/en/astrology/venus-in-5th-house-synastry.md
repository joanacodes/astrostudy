Their Venus in your 5th house is the overlay most often read as romance, and it does tend to make things delightful. Its real territory, though, is pleasure itself: play, art, flirting, games, the things done for joy, which friends share as well as lovers.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is your playground: making things, games, romance, the self that comes out when nobody is keeping score. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is appreciation and attraction, what a person enjoys and how they show it. In a birth chart, tradition said Venus rejoices in the 5th, and the old name of the house was Good Fortune. When it's someone else's Venus that lands there, as in this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), the old idea still describes the mood: pleasure that feels at home.

You may feel more playful, more attractive and more creative around them. They enjoy what you make and tell you so, choose the outing that turns into the best day of the month, and laugh easily at what you find funny. If there's attraction, it tends to be warm and light rather than heavy.

Your pleasures delight them: your hobbies, your humour, the way you throw yourself into something you love. They feel they can play with you, a freedom many adults rarely get. The house is succedent, and its natural sign is [Leo](/astrology/leo/), the Sun's own: warm, generous, a little theatrical.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Pleasure can become the whole relationship. When everything between you is about fun, ordinary life, the chores, the worries, the bills, can feel like an intrusion, and one of you may start avoiding the dull days. Attraction can also be read into play: friends and colleagues with this overlay sometimes need to say clearly what their delight means and what it doesn't.

They may come to want only the sparkling side of you, or feel that you treat their affection as entertainment. If they love an audience and you're tired, or you love one and they're not in the mood, the stage can feel lonely.

Tradition read the 5th for children too. This overlay doesn't predict a pregnancy or a family, for anyone ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)); what it describes is the joy two adults take in each other.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep playing, and let the play include ordinary days. Make something together: a garden, a song, a trip with no purpose at all. In a couple, dates that keep surprising you both, and honest talk when real life needs room. Between friends, a shared hobby or a standing night out. Between colleagues, creative projects go well, with clear limits on flirting at work. Among adult relatives, the one you go to concerts or matches with.

When the overlay runs the other way, your Venus in their 5th house, their creativity and humour are what delight you. Around it, [their Venus in your 4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house-synastry/) is warmth at home, and [their Venus in your 6th house](/astrology/venus-in-6th-house-synastry/) affection in the daily round.

Because Venus keeps within about 47° of the Sun, their Venus usually lands within a house or two of their Sun in your chart, and their birth date places both. Your birth time is the part I can't do without: in Placidus, the house system I use, it sets the cusps of your 5th, as it does every cusp ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If the attraction is new, curiosity about each other beats a chart for a few months ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#we-just-met)).
