Neptune reaches the top of your chart once at most, and only half of people see it before 80. For about two and a half years your public direction takes on Neptune's colours: vocation, ideals, and other people's projections.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where your abilities become visible: the role you take on in the world, the contribution people know you by. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) adds longing, compassion and a sense that work should serve something beyond itself. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) sets the two working together, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your public direction softens at the edges and asks for a reason: work done for a living starts wanting to be work done for something, and the picture others hold of you can say more about their hopes than about your job.

The Midheaven blind spot Neptune finds most easily is assuming that responsibility requires self-erasure, giving yourself to a cause, a team or a vision until no outline is left. I read the crossing as a question about vocation, never a forecast for a career: it doesn't say whether a post begins or ends, only what kind of meaning you'll look for in whatever you do. I hold it with some care, too: my notebook didn't score the Midheaven on its own, and the angle it does report, the rising sign, held about a third of the time ([The invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)).

The Midheaven is the 10th house cusp in Placidus. Before the crossing, Neptune has spent years in your 9th, among beliefs and far horizons ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)); after it, a long stay in your 10th begins ([Neptune transiting the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-10th-house/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before your Midheaven to 1° past it, Neptune spends about two and a half years on the point, twenty-two months in the shortest passes; most bring three exact hits, about one in four brings five.

Who meets it depends on the hour of birth. Born in the twelve hours after Neptune crossed the meridian high in your sky, you have Neptune in the western half of your chart, houses 4 to 9, and its slow transit is heading for your Midheaven: the sooner after that crossing you were born, the sooner it arrives, and nearly twelve hours after means close to 80. Born in the other twelve hours, Neptune reaches your IC first ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-midheaven/)), and the Midheaven lies beyond 80. Among births from 1930 to 2009 that splits people in half.

Four minutes of clock time move the Midheaven by about a degree. An hour's error moves it some 15°, which at Neptune's pace is the better part of seven years ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Two steps usually lie between a natal Neptune in the west and this crossing, the square from near your Descendant ([transiting Neptune square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-midheaven/)) and the sextile from around your 8th ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-midheaven/)); the trines ([transiting Neptune trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-midheaven/)) belong to the lower stretches of the round. Neptune in Aries until March 2039 is crossing Aries Midheavens ([Aries on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/)), the earliest degrees first.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Separate the role from the reason. Write down what you do and, apart from it, why it matters to you; under Neptune the two blur, and the second list is the one worth acting on.

Let projections pass through. Admiration or disappointment aimed at your public role is often about other people's hopes, and you don't have to become either picture.

Give an ideal a working form inside your field: a project that serves something you care about, a skill offered where it helps, a standard you keep even when no one is looking.
