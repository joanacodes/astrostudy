Mercury square North Node puts your way of thinking at right angles to the line your growth runs along. Because the two nodes face each other, Mercury squares both at once: it stands beside the path rather than on it, and its job is to keep both ends talking.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the part of a chart that processes what you take in: it notices, makes sense of things and puts them into words for other people. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a direction rather than a planet: a less familiar capacity that grows with practice, with the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite as the ground you start from. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates Mercury to that line through friction: neither replaces the other, and each keeps pressing on the other.

In plain words, your way of thinking doesn't sit neatly at either end. Your habits of thought were built on the South Node's familiar ground, the North Node asks for something you haven't practised yet, and Mercury, standing between them, finds that its words fit neither place quite well. It can feel like translating all the time, the new for the part of you that liked the old, and back again. That's the work this square gives you: try, listen to the result, adjust. The North Node grows by practice, and Mercury is what reads the results.

I'm gentle with this square because its friction is useful: a mind that keeps being asked to adjust is rarely tempted to mistake its first answer for the last.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as a mind that sits in the middle of your growth rather than at either end. The nodes always fall in opposite signs of the same modality, and the square usually puts Mercury in that modality too: with the North Node in Gemini and the South Node in Sagittarius, Mercury would usually be in Virgo or Pisces, all three mutable. The houses show where it plays out. With the nodes across the 1st and 7th and Mercury in the 4th or 10th, what you think about home, or about work, might become the go-between for your own way and partnership.

About 1 person in 15 is born with Mercury within 6° of a square to the North Node. Within a degree or two, how you think and where you're growing are hard to pull apart; nearer 6°, the square is more of a background habit of checking the method.

Mercury is by far the quicker of the two, two or three weeks in a sign against the node's 18 months or so, backwards, so the square is set mostly by your birth date. I check it against both nodes, mean and true ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), which differ by a degree or two at most. The houses, which make it yours, need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Mercury makes every aspect to the nodes in turn, and each one gives thinking a different job on the path:

- **[Mercury conjunct North Node](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-north-node/):** the mind on the path itself; curiosity leads the way you're growing.
- **[Mercury sextile North Node](/astrology/mercury-sextile-north-node/):** an opening towards the new direction, taken one conversation at a time.
- **[Mercury trine North Node](/astrology/mercury-trine-north-node/):** thinking that flows towards growth, as long as understanding turns into practice.
- **[Mercury opposite North Node](/astrology/mercury-opposite-north-node/):** Mercury on the South Node; a fluent, familiar mind asked to reach across the chart.

Between two charts, [Mercury square North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-north-node-synastry/) sets one person's way of thinking across the other's direction of growth: conversations that unsettle as much as they stretch. The one being stretched rarely feels it the way the one stretching does, so I read it from each chart.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), Mercury in the sky squares your natal nodes at least twice a year, a day or so each time: a conversation or a piece of news that sits awkwardly between old habits and new aims. The slower version is [Transiting North Node square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-mercury/): the node stays near the square for weeks, and comes back to it about every nine years.

Born with the square, you meet that transit at telling moments, close to your [nodal returns](/astrology/nodal-return/) and [nodal reversals](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), at about 18½, 28, 37, 46 and 56. Weather, not a forecast: a season in which the way you think is asked to make room for where you're going, never an event.
