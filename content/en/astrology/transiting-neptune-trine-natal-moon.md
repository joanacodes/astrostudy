Neptune's trine to the Moon is a quiet visit to your memory as much as to your feelings. Over some two and a half years, memories and present needs can be looked at with more tenderness than usual, and very little pushes you to do it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) widens: imagination, compassion, a sense of what lies beyond the everyday. Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds your feelings, your needs and your emotional memory, what soothed you once and still does. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates them through flow, and here flow means tenderness that doesn't wash anything away: an old hurt can be seen from further off, a family story can be told more kindly, and caring for yourself takes less effort. Memory, for the Moon, is the feeling a thing left in you more than the facts of it, and that is the layer this trine reaches.

Unlike the natal [Moon trine Neptune](/astrology/moon-trine-neptune/), which describes a lifelong ease between feeling and imagination, this transit has a start and an end, and that's what makes it useful: you know when to lean on it. With Neptune, ease can slide into drifting; the trine is at its best when the softness is given somewhere to go. A trine doesn't insist, and two and a half years of easier feeling can go by unremarked unless you use them.

With your Moon in the 5th and Neptune crossing your 9th, creative pleasure and a wider search for meaning ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)) feed each other; with the Moon in the 8th and Neptune in the 12th, quiet time with yourself can make closeness easier. Both houses, and your Moon's exact degree, need your birth time; without it, I hold off ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the [transit](/astrology/transits/) lasts about two and a half years, around twenty-two months in the tightest passes; three exact hits are the norm, and roughly one pass in four has five, when Neptune's retrograde loops overlap. Each year Neptune is retrograde for about five months, so a middle hit comes while it moves back: a natural pause for looking over what the first pass stirred.

By 80, about 1 in 6 people born between 1930 and 2009 have never had it; most have had it once, and a few twice. Of Neptune's two easy contacts with your Moon, the trine lies nearer the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-moon/)), the sextile ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-moon/)) nearer the conjunction ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-moon/)); the square ([transiting Neptune square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-moon/)) sits between them.

Until 2039, Leo and Sagittarius Moons are the ones in this trine's path ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Revisit something you've avoided remembering, gently and with company if you like: a place, a photograph, a song.

Make something from what you feel: a meal, a garden, a letter you may never send.

Use the ease to change one small habit of self-care; a trine lowers the effort it takes.
