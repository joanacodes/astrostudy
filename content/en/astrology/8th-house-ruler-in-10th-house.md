Some careers are built on talent, some on charm; yours tends to be built on being trusted. Here the planet that rules your 8th has climbed near the top of the chart, into the 10th, the house of work, standing and reputation.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your work and how you're known for it belong to the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/); trust, joint resources, debts and the times that change people, to the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/). Through this ruler, those private matters move into the open, as a profession or a reputation ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Trust is the foundation of the career here. You may work with other people's money, risks or troubles, in roles where discretion is the job, and a reputation for keeping confidences can matter more than any title. Visibility cuts both ways: the 10th shows what you do, while the 8th's matters are ones people prefer kept quiet, so your best work may be the kind nobody sees credited. Your working life may be marked by turning points as well, a career that ended and began again, a role taken on in a crisis, and you tend to be good in rooms where something has gone wrong. Power is the thing to handle with care: the 8th is where control and trust meet, and a public role that holds both asks for scruples.

Counted round the wheel, the 10th comes two houses after the 8th, a sextile, and the link tends to work: depth finds a use. The 8th's turning points can happen in view, too: a public change of direction, a role taken over when someone left, a company restructured on your watch. You tend to handle such transitions well, because upheaval doesn't frighten you the way it frightens most.

Which planet it is changes the work. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn 8th, takes responsibility for what others entrust and builds a slow, solid reputation; the Moon, ruling a Cancer 8th, turns a career towards caring for people in difficulty; Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 8th, suits audit, analysis and the paperwork of other people's affairs. Scorpio on the 8th cusp gives the house to Mars and Pluto together ([Scorpio on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 10th makes you decisive in a crisis, and visible for it, while Pluto's house shows where your work touches power.

Neither money nor success is promised: the 10th isn't fame, and the 8th isn't a fortune ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). The placement describes the kind of work in which you're trusted.

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), which opens the 10th in Placidus, the house system I use, turns quickly with the clock. Ten minutes later, the ruler could have crossed it into the 9th, where the 8th's matters become beliefs ([Ruler of the 8th in the 9th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)); ten minutes earlier, it could sit in the 11th, where they're shared with friends and groups ([Ruler of the 8th in the 11th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)). The birth time settles it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Being trusted is where this career starts, and keeping that trust is what it's made of.
