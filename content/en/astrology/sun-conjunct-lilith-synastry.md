Sun conjunct Lilith in synastry sets one person's Sun on the other's Black Moon Lilith: the centre of one life lands on the part of the other that learned to stay out of sight. It's a contact about being looked at, and the two people almost never feel it the same way.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is what a person organises themselves around: identity, vitality, purpose, the self they show. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the spot where the Moon's orbit carries it farthest from Earth, is read very differently from one astrologer to the next. I read it as the side of a person that refused to be tamed: instinct, autonomy, whatever got pushed aside, and their own sense of power. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) binds two functions into one process; between two charts that process has two owners, and like all [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) it reads differently from each end.

If the Lilith is yours, the other person's way of being falls exactly on what you were once told was too much: the appetite, the bluntness, the flat refusal. Around them it's hard to keep that part folded away, and often you stop wanting to. Standing in the light of someone who is simply being themselves can feel like permission, and on other days like being caught without your coat.

If the Sun is yours, the other person's untamed side tends to fascinate you, and to feel oddly close to home: what they hide sits right where you are most yourself. You may be the first person in years to look at it without flinching. That's the gift: recognition for a part of someone that usually gets none.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A conjunction blurs edges, and what blurs here is whose story the hidden part belongs to. Seen this clearly, the Lilith person's refusal can start to orbit the Sun person: shown in their company and nowhere else, or reshaped to fit how they describe it. The Sun person, in turn, can come to wear the other's wildness as part of their own identity, admired, talked about, defended on the other's behalf, until it's no longer quite the other's to show or keep. Seeing someone isn't defining them.

It can also go fast. What Lilith marks was put away for a reason, and being seen before trust has grown is a sign to slow down, not a sign that anything is wrong between you.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the person whose Lilith it is set the pace. If the Sun is yours, notice what you see and ask before you name it; if the Lilith is yours, you can enjoy being met and still decide what stays private. In a couple or a friendship this often becomes unusual honesty; between adult relatives, the one person in the family who never asked you to be smaller; between co-workers, keep it to what you've both chosen to bring to work. Because it reaches what someone keeps private, I read it only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Within 8°, the orb I give this pair, about 1 pair of people in 12 has it, one way round or the other. Both charts need a birth time, and I don't read a synastry without both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The time rarely makes or unmakes this contact, since the Sun moves about a degree a day and mean Lilith spends some nine months in a sign; it decides the houses, and so where in each life the contact is felt.

In my notebook, Saturn, Lilith and Chiron held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)): Lilith in single charts, not between two, so there's no figure for this conjunction. It's why Lilith always gets its own part when I read two charts.

Inside one birth chart, the same pair is [Sun conjunct Lilith](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith/). The other contacts of the pair hold the two at other distances: [Sun sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith-synastry/) offers the hidden part a friendly opening, [Sun square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-lilith-synastry/) makes it catch, [Sun trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith-synastry/) lets it relax, and [Sun opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith-synastry/) holds it up across the room like a mirror.
