Mercury in Sagittarius wants to know what the facts mean before it wants all of them. It thinks in principles and stories, travels by idea, and says what it sees, often more frankly than the room expected.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire ruled by Jupiter and the sign of the whole picture, [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), the collector of particulars, finds itself among meanings, beliefs and long views. Tradition calls this Mercury's [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), because Sagittarius sits opposite Gemini, one of Mercury's own signs. I read the detriment as a change of method, not a lesser mind: the planet of detail, placed in the sign of meaning, gathers the meaning first and lets the details catch up. [My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) can't settle whether the tradition's weakening shows, since it counts Mercury as a whole, which held about a third of the time, so when a reading rests on this Mercury, I say so.

This Mercury thinks by synthesis. It finds the principle under ten examples, the story that ties a life's work together, the question behind the question. It learns quickly when the subject matters to it and slowly when it can't see the point. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the sign's ruler, sets the direction: wherever it sits in your chart is where this mind goes looking for meaning.

Mercury usually takes about 20 days over Sagittarius, and up to 67 in a year when it turns retrograde there, circling back as if to check the map. For about 1 person in 11 born between 1930 and 2009, this is the Mercury they have.

Next door, [Mercury in Scorpio](/astrology/mercury-in-scorpio/) digs and [Mercury in Capricorn](/astrology/mercury-in-capricorn/) builds, while Mercury in Sagittarius looks up and asks where it's all going. The Sun can be a sign away at most, and each choice changes the voice. A deep, private Scorpio Sun with this open Mercury makes someone who says the true thing and keeps the personal one. A Capricorn Sun gives the big ideas a builder to carry them out. If the Sun is in Sagittarius as well, your sense of direction and your thinking share one horizon.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You say it as you see it, and you mean it kindly, even when it doesn't land that way. Enthusiasm is how you love: the book you press into someone's hands, the trip you plan, the late conversation about what life is for. You need a partner you can argue big ideas with, who won't take a frank remark for an attack, and you tend to forgive and forget quickly. The skill to learn is that some truths need a gentler road, and that a partner's feelings are part of the facts.

## At work {#at-work}

Teaching, publishing, journalism, translation, travel, strategy, coaching: work where vision and explanation count for more than the small print. You're good at seeing where a project is heading and at telling people why it matters. The details may need a system or a colleague, the dates, the figures, the clause on page twelve. With that in place, this Mercury is often the one who gives a team its sense of purpose.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Generalising. One example becomes a rule, a hunch is spoken as a certainty, and reasoning that's mostly right gets presented as entirely right. The detail that slips can undo a good argument. Frankness can wound, and a sermon can be under way before you notice it started. And a mind fixed on the horizon can miss what's on the table in front of it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep a checklist for the details, and use it before you send. Ask *how do I know this?* about your strongest opinions. Teach, if you can: explaining something to a beginner forces precision. Before saying the frank thing, ask whether it's also the useful thing.
