Behind every front door there's a version of you that visitors don't meet. With Lilith in the 4th house, that version is kept closer than most. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point of the Moon's orbit farthest from Earth, sat at the base of your chart when you were born, in [the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) of home, family and roots.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The 4th is the angular house at the bottom of the chart, and Cancer is its natural sign. It covers home, family, roots, the private life behind the door and the foundations you stand on. With Lilith here, the hidden part is close to the root. It may be something the family didn't talk about, a way of being that had no room at home, or simply the self you are when nobody is watching.

This Lilith's refusal is a refusal to inherit everything. Some of what you were handed you keep; some you quietly decline to pass on — a rule, a silence, a way of treating people. Its boundary is the front door: who comes in, and what they're allowed to see once inside.

In Placidus, the system I use, the 4th begins at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest of the four angles, facing the Midheaven across the chart. That takes an accurate birth time ([what the time decides](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)): with Lilith close to that cusp, a slightly different time could make it [Lilith in the 3rd house](/astrology/lilith-in-3rd-house/) instead.

Because Lilith held so often in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), 75 to 90% of the time with Saturn and Chiron, I take this part of a reading slowly. Home is tender ground.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

The question this Lilith asks in love is who gets the key. You may be warm in public and very slow to let anyone into your private life, or the reverse: generous with your home and guarded about what it means to you. The hidden part wants a partner who can live with the real you at home, not the visiting version, and who doesn't try to rebuild your family's patterns under a new roof.

The flirting, playful side of love belongs more to [Lilith in the 5th house](/astrology/lilith-in-5th-house/). The 4th is about who sits at your kitchen table at midnight.

## At work {#at-work}

At work this placement often shows as a firm line between public and private: colleagues can know you for years without learning much about your life. You may need a base that feels like yours — a desk, a workshop, a room at home — and do better in work that lets you lay your own foundations. The hidden part resents jobs that expect your home life to bend around them.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The family rule about what's acceptable can follow you long after you've left the house. You may still hear it when you decorate, cook, choose a partner or raise your voice. Or you may have built a home so unlike the one you grew up in that the difference has become another kind of rule.

Feeling like a stranger at your own family's table is common with this placement, and it isn't a judgement on anyone. It's a sign that the hidden part needed a different kind of home.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make one place where the hidden part lives openly — a room, a garden, a corner, a home you've chosen — and let the people you trust see it. Decide, on purpose, which family traditions you keep and which stop with you: that isn't disloyalty, it's authorship.

If there are things the family didn't talk about, you're allowed to talk about them now, with someone safe. A root you know about holds you better than one you've never looked at.
