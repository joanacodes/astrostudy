Some of what one generation dreamed of is exactly what the next sets out to rebuild from the root. Neptune conjunct Pluto in synastry puts one person's Neptune within 4° of the other's Pluto, and in every pair my data holds, the Neptune belongs to the elder.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and dissolves: it holds ideals, compassion and a sense of something larger, and softens boundaries that no longer serve. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms: it clears away what has become unsustainable, renews from the root and asks for authentic power. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into one process without erasing either; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one joins an elder's dream to a younger person's depth.

Signs give it a subject. Neptune in Virgo for births of the mid-1930s and Pluto in Virgo for births of the mid-1960s, for instance: an ideal of useful, careful service meets a drive to overhaul how work and method are done.

If the Neptune is yours, you may see your own ideals come back to you with an intensity you never gave them: taken seriously, pushed further, sometimes changed beyond recognition.

If the Pluto is yours, the other meets your need for deep change with faith rather than fear. That can steady you, or soften something you needed to face squarely.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Fusion blurs compassion with control. The Neptune person can idealise the other's intensity, or forgive what deserved a clear no; the Pluto person can try to make the elder's dream real by force of will, or strip it bare to see what lies underneath. Intensity isn't truth, and resonance isn't evidence, yet both are easy to mistake when two functions this deep share a degree.

- **[Neptune sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto-synastry/):** the one Neptune–Pluto contact that people of nearly the same age share.
- **[Neptune square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-pluto-synastry/):** the ideal and the clearing-out at right angles, a decade or two apart in age.
- **[Neptune trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-pluto-synastry/):** depth giving ideals weight, between people a generation apart.
- **[Neptune opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** dream and renewal facing each other across more than fifty years.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep the facts on the table and the hopes in view. The Neptune person can say which ideal matters most and why; the Pluto person can say what must change and what they would keep. A parent and an adult child, a teacher and a former student, colleagues a generation apart, a couple with a wide age gap: each can let the dream guide the renovation without letting either run the other.

Neptune and Pluto are the two slowest bodies I read, which makes this the slowest contact on the site: one birthday shares it with nearly five years of births. Someone born in mid-1965, with Pluto in Virgo, has it with anyone born, on and off, from November 1932 to August 1937, whose Neptune was in Virgo; someone born in mid-1955, with Neptune in Libra, with anyone born, on and off, from December 1979 to August 1984. In every example the Neptune person is the elder, by about 22 to 31 years. A contact shared that widely says almost nothing about two individuals, so it never enters my synastry count, kept for the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant and the Midheaven. In the houses, it reads as one era's dream meeting the next era's overhaul.

Neither planet moves more than a sliver in a day; what the hours decide is the houses, whether the meeting falls in the Pluto person's 11th house, among friends and causes, or their 2nd, among values ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The count of births from 1930 to 2009 shows [Neptune conjunct Pluto](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-pluto/) in no single chart; between two charts it joins two generations, and both people need to want it read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
