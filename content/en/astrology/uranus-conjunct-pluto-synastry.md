One generation's urge to break a pattern meets another's urge to renew it at the root in Uranus conjunct Pluto in synastry. The Uranus in one chart lies within 4° of the Pluto in the other, so the change one era reached for lands on the depth another era works from.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) breaks patterns: it differentiates, experiments, and reforms systems grown too tight. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms from underneath: it clears out what no longer serves, renews what is left, and returns a strength that runs deeper. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) welds two functions into one process, and in the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the welding happens between two people, change from one, depth from the other.

If the Uranus is yours, your reforms touch something in this person that wants to go further than reform: not a new arrangement but a renewal. You may find your quick changes deepened, or slowed, by what they reach.

If the Pluto is yours, this person's experiments land exactly where you do your slow, deep work. It can feel like a release, a pattern finally broken, or like being hurried past something that needed time.

What the meeting can make is change that holds because it goes all the way down.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Fused this closely, disruption and regeneration are easy to confuse. The Uranus person can assume that breaking something is the same as renewing it; the Pluto person can take intensity for truth and hold on to control in the name of depth. Transformation is not destruction, and freedom is not the absence of responsibility; the pair goes wrong when either forgets it.

- **[Uranus sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto-synastry/):** an opening between reform and renewal, taken up or not.
- **[Uranus square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-pluto-synastry/):** change and depth pushing against each other at right angles.
- **[Uranus trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-pluto-synastry/):** the two urges flowing together across a wide age gap.
- **[Uranus opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** breaking free and renewing at the two ends of one line.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Separate what needs changing from what needs transforming. The Uranus person can propose the experiment; the Pluto person can say what lies underneath it and how long it will take. Agree which changes are quick and which are slow. A parent and an adult child rethinking a family pattern, colleagues of different ages reorganising a team, friends a decade apart: each can turn the meeting into change that lasts.

Who holds which planet depends on the 1960s, when the two last met in Virgo. Before then Uranus trailed Pluto, so the younger person holds the Uranus: someone born in mid-1955, Uranus in Cancer, has it with anyone born between May 1934 and September 1938, as Pluto crossed the last degrees of Cancer. After the meeting Uranus ran ahead, so the older person does: someone born in mid-1985, Pluto in Scorpio, has it with anyone born between April and October 1976, Uranus in Scorpio. Whole years of births share each side, so my synastry count, kept for contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven, leaves it out; through the houses I read two eras meeting at depth, more than two people.

The hour moves neither planet but sets every house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If the Pluto falls in the Uranus person's 4th house, the meeting reaches home, roots and family patterns ([Their Pluto in your 4th house](/astrology/pluto-in-4th-house-synastry/)).

Most people born between August 1963 and 1968 carry [Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/) in their own chart. Between two charts, the change and the depth belong to different people, and I look at it only when both of them want me to ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
