Every family has a few things nobody says at the table. The other person's least tamed side walks, with their Lilith, into your 4th house: your home, your roots and the private self behind your own front door.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is the part of a person that didn't fit the mould they were handed, and has stopped apologising for it, or is learning to. [The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home in every sense, the childhood house and the one you make now, family, belonging, and the past you carry. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their refusal to fit meets your roots.

With them, you may notice what you've kept quiet at home for years: an opinion, a way of living, a version of yourself your family never quite met. Their irreverence about house rules can be contagious; a room gets rearranged, a tradition gets dropped, a role you were handed as a child stops being worn.

For them, your home can be provocation or shelter. Your family's customs may stir their own history of not fitting, and they may bristle at a ritual everyone else takes for granted; or they may feel, unexpectedly, at home in a place where nobody asks them to tidy themselves up. The 4th is angular, the root of the chart, and [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), its natural sign, guards what it loves. Close to the cusp, their Lilith also meets your [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), and that contact gets its own weight in my reading.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Loyalty and freedom can pull in opposite directions. They may question your family's ways out loud, at the table, and you may feel torn between defending people you love and agreeing with the question. Sharing a home, house rules become the battleground: who decides, and whose habits count.

From their side, your attachment to tradition can feel like a cage they once climbed out of. They may also hide how much they long for the belonging they mock.

The 4th shows how home feels from inside; it doesn't say who anyone's parents are or how a family will fare. This overlay describes two people's ways of belonging.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep what you love about home, and let go only of what was never yours. Ask about each other's family customs before judging them. A couple setting up house can write their own rules rather than inherit both families'; a friend can be the guest who makes your home feel less formal; colleagues rarely meet this overlay, except where work happens at someone's kitchen table; among adult relatives, the one who never fitted sometimes becomes the one who frees everyone else.

From the other side, it's your Lilith in their 4th house: your untamed part walks into their home. [Their Lilith in your 3rd house](/astrology/lilith-in-3rd-house-synastry/) loosens your tongue; [their Lilith in your 5th house](/astrology/lilith-in-5th-house-synastry/), your sense of play.

Mean Lilith, the version I read, goes round the zodiac in about 8 years and 10 months, so adults born about nine years apart, an elder and a younger sibling for instance, have their Liliths close together, and this overlay then echoes a placement you carry yourself. Use the same mean Lilith on both sides ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). Your IC, which only your birth time can fix, opens the 4th in Placidus, the house system I use, so a few minutes can move their Lilith between your 3rd and your 4th; I need their time as well before I read the pair ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A home is private ground, read only with both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
