The middle of Sagittarius puts together two planets the old tables keep apart: the Moon, which rules its face, and Mars, which rules it in the modern scheme and has its fall in the Moon's own sign. It is also the decan the red star Antares has only recently entered.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

After Mercury ([Sagittarius, first decan](/astrology/sagittarius-first-decan/)), the [Chaldean order](/astrology/chaldean-order/) reaches the Moon, so the middle face of [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) is the Moon's ([faces](/astrology/faces/)); the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/), from 10° to 20°, finds in its face its only dignity in the sign. The modern decans give the middle third to Aries, the next fire sign, and so to Mars. Saturn takes the face after this one ([Sagittarius, third decan](/astrology/sagittarius-third-decan/)).

On paper the pair is uneasy. Mars has its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Cancer, the Moon's own sign, so the old tables put the planet of effort at its least comfortable where the planet of needs is at home. Together on these degrees I read them as a working arrangement: the Moon asks what you need to keep going, Mars how far you'll go for it, and Sagittarius wants the answer to lead somewhere.

## Its image {#its-image}

Cosmas of Maiuma gives these degrees to Kore, the Maiden: the name Persephone bore as a goddess of growing things, in the myth where she spends part of every year below the earth and comes back. [Firmicus Maternus](/astrology/firmicus-maternus/) wrote Sagon, and Joseph Scaliger, twelve centuries later, Sagen. I won't tie the Maiden to the Moon's face more tightly than the lists do, and the lists say nothing.

The sky here is busier than the books. [Antares](/astrology/antares/), the red heart of the Scorpion, crossed into the decan around 2017 and stands at 10°07′; [Ras Algethi](/astrology/ras-algethi/), the head of the Kneeler we now call Hercules, is at 16°31′; [Sabik](/astrology/sabik/), in the Serpent-bearer, at 18°20′. Antares lies within 5° of the Sun's path, while Ras Algethi stands 37° north of it and meets the planets only by degree.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun is in these degrees from about 2 to 11 December, the heart of the [Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/) season. With the Moon's face and Mars's decan, I read the shade here as enthusiasm that needs a reason to keep going: the sign's long view, fuelled by what matters to you and by the will to act on it.

A decan refines a Sun sign; it doesn't stand on its own. The Sun sign was among the placements that held about a third of the time when I tested my own chart and others, and whenever a reading leans on something that held less often, I tell you so ([How I read](/how-i-read/)).

[Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/) in its second decan takes about 50 minutes to rise at the latitude of Paris, 51 at London's: noticeably quicker than any decan of Libra or Scorpio, and the signs after it rise faster still.

[Pluto in Sagittarius](/astrology/pluto-in-sagittarius/) spent early 1999 to 2004 in these degrees; Uranus had passed through between late 1983 and 1986, and Neptune between late 1974 and 1980.
