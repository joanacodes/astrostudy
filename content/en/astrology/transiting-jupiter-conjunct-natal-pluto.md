Every twelve years or so, the planet that widens things sits for a few days on the planet that goes deepest. Whatever your Pluto has been working on out of sight gets light and room, and sometimes, at last, a name.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is where a chart transforms: it reorganises what has become unsustainable, lets go of what is finished, and gives back a deeper kind of strength. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands and looks for meaning. Meeting in a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), the two act as one: growth goes to the root, and depth finds room to grow.

Often a slow, private change becomes visible or finds its reason in these weeks: a habit you've been dismantling, a loss you've been working through, a strength you hadn't claimed. The question underneath is what has become unsustainable, and what you would need, in yourself or around you, to change it; Jupiter's room tends to make the answer easier to see. Pluto's blind spots grow too. Intensity can pass for truth, control for safety, secrecy for depth. Expansion isn't excess, and empowerment isn't power over anyone: those are the two misreadings I take care to avoid.

Some people fear Pluto's transits because of the planet's associations with loss. I don't read any [transit](/astrology/transits/) for death, illness or loss of any kind ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). Pluto's house says where the change has been happening, and the conjunction always falls in Jupiter's year in that house; both depend on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Jupiter conjunct Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-pluto/) in your chart, the conjunction comes back near each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, the conjunction to your Pluto is a matter of about eleven days if Jupiter keeps moving forward. If Jupiter stations close by, as it does about three times in ten, the meeting repeats: three exact hits over about eight months. Six or seven: that's how many fall before 80 in births from 1930 to 2009.

How long did people wait for the first? It depended on where Jupiter stood. A sign behind Pluto in January 1955, it got there in under a year, at about 0.8; just past Pluto in January 1995, it needed almost twelve, about 11.9.

Six years on, [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-pluto/) shows the same change from the outside, often through another person. In between, [transiting Jupiter square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-pluto/) asks it to prove its strength, while [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-pluto/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-pluto/) give it easier ground.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name what you've been changing quietly, and give it one step in the open: a conversation, a decision said aloud, a piece of work shown.

Let go of one thing that has grown too big to carry. Jupiter on Pluto is good at showing what has outlived its use, and lighter is often stronger.

Use the strength for your own life. If you find yourself wanting to win, convert or control, that is the transit's shadow; if you find yourself able to stand your ground calmly, that is its gift.
