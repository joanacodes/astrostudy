Capricorn is the sign that turns a plan into a building. With Neptune there, a relationship's dream wants foundations: the two of you idealise what you can make together and keep standing.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), each planet stands halfway between its places in two birth charts, and the chart as a whole describes the relationship as a third thing, with a character of its own. Composite [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where that character dreams: what it imagines, what it would give itself to, where the two people's edges blur. [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), cardinal earth ruled by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), gives the dream a structure. Such a bond tends to idealise what it builds and keeps: a household run with care, a project or business shared over years, a commitment honoured on hard days as well as easy ones. Its compassion takes the form of responsibility, and its imagination likes a plan with dates on it. Friends often see such a couple as reliable in an almost old-fashioned way: the pair that turns up when called, keeps its word and remembers what was promised.

Saturn, the ruler of Capricorn, is the planet to read beside it: in a composite, Saturn is the bond's frame, and with Neptune in Capricorn the frame and the dream are meant to fit each other. Where they do, the pair can make an ideal last for decades; where they don't, one partner may hold the plans while the other holds the longing, and each feels the other has the easier part.

Two people born between January 1984 and November 1998 both have [Neptune in Capricorn](/astrology/neptune-in-capricorn/), and their composite does too. Capricorn is also where nearly every pair lands that joins the Sagittarius years, 1970 to 1984, to the Aquarius years from 1998: a partner born in 1978 and one born in 2000 meet at about 11° Capricorn, a sign that appears in neither birth chart. Over two million random pairs of 1930–2009 births, it turns up for about 1 pair in 6. Next door, [composite Neptune in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-sagittarius/) looks for meaning in distance and [composite Neptune in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-aquarius/) in a wider community; Capricorn builds it in time.

The difficulty is a dream postponed until everything is in place. A bond that idealises building can turn every wish into a five-year plan, measure itself by what it has achieved, or let duty crowd out tenderness until the structure stands and nobody remembers what it was for. Achievement isn't the same as meaning; for this pair, an unplanned weekend can protect the dream better than another milestone.

The house is where a generation's sign becomes one couple's own, and it needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). I describe composites without drawing them in my readings; those who practise the method read this Neptune in the light of both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and only with both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)). Like Capricorn itself, it deals in what can be built, never in guarantees: it promises neither success nor failure.
