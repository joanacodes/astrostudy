Jupiter opposite Neptune sets what you believe across the chart from what you long for. It comes round every 12 or 13 years, when Jupiter stands on the far side of the zodiac from Neptune, and it asks for a balance rather than a choice.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the function that makes sense of experience: it builds principles, learns, and widens what you think is possible. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the function that reaches past what can be defined: imagination, compassion, inspiration, the sense of belonging to something larger. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them as a polarity. They aren't enemies; they stand at the two ends of one line, and each makes the other visible.

In plain words, a framework and a feeling face each other. One side of you wants to understand, to hold a philosophy you could explain; the other wants to be moved, to dissolve into a piece of music or into someone else's need. Often you meet one end in other people: a teacher whose certainty you admire, a friend whose compassion has no edges, a cause that seems to ask for everything. The opposition's work is awareness: noticing which end you're standing at, and walking back towards the middle.

The tension here depends on context, not on the aspect's nature. When the two ends talk to each other, this opposition can give unusual range: principles with tenderness in them, and dreams with a spine.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 32 born between 1930 and 2009 has the opposition within 6°, half as many as have the trine, because Jupiter reaches the point opposite Neptune only once in each lap. The windows were 1939–40, 1951–52, 1964–65, 1977, 1989–90 and 2002–03, a few months each, sometimes split in two by Jupiter's retrograde. Everyone born in them shares it, so on its own it describes a year-group.

What's personal is the axis it runs along. The two signs of an opposition usually share a modality and set fire across from air or earth across from water, so each end speaks a language the other completes. It also crosses two opposite houses. Jupiter in the 10th with Neptune in the 4th might set a public philosophy against a private, porous home life; Jupiter in the 3rd with Neptune in the 9th, everyday learning against a longing for faraway meaning. Which pair of houses the axis falls across depends on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects hold the two at other distances:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-neptune/):** the two ends folded into one point, so dream and belief arrive as a single thing.
- **[Jupiter sextile Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-neptune/):** a small, friendly distance; they help each other once you ask.
- **[Jupiter square Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-square-neptune/):** neither face to face nor side by side, they cross, and the friction keeps asking which ideals hold.
- **[Jupiter trine Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-trine-neptune/):** they flow together without anyone else having to carry one end.

Between two charts, [Jupiter opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-neptune-synastry/) sets one person's convictions facing the other's imagination. It can widen and soften both at once; each person holds one end, so each side gets its own reading.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Jupiter opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-neptune/) comes once in each of Jupiter's laps, for a few weeks, or longer, in several passes, when Jupiter turns retrograde near the point.

[Transiting Neptune opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-jupiter/) behaves differently if you were born with the opposition. The point opposite your Jupiter lies close to where Neptune stood at your birth, so the transit was already there when you arrived; Neptune drifts off it in your first years and won't be back for some 165 years. For everyone else it comes at an age set by their birth year, and Neptune, needing some 14 years to cross a sign, holds it near exact for a year or two.

To me, transits like these are weather, not a forecast: a time when what you hold true and what you dream of ask to be weighed again. The weighing stays yours.
