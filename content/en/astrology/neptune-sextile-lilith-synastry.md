Not every invitation to be more yourself arrives as a challenge. In synastry, Neptune sextile Lilith puts one person's Neptune and the other's Black Moon Lilith about 60° apart, and the invitation comes softly: one person's imagination opens a door, and the other can walk through it, or not.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where a person dreams up forms and feels for others, loosening boundaries that only ever kept people apart. Many astrologers tie [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's apogee taken as an average position, to instinctive autonomy, to authenticity, and to what someone pushed aside to fit in; its weight varies from school to school, and I hold it as a symbol. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) is a matter of opportunity: either function can wake the other, provided somebody moves first. Among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), either person can take it.

If the Lilith is yours, this person tends to make room for your untamed side in a shape that feels safe: a character in a game, a song written together, a late conversation where nothing is held against you. You don't have to confess anything. The refusal you learned to hide can come out sideways, in play, and be welcomed there.

If the Neptune is yours, their bluntness gives your dreaming something to push against. Your imagination grows braver around them, less vague and more pointed, because they won't pretend to feel what they don't. You bring the welcome; they bring the nerve.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Ease can keep the hidden part in costume. A pair with this sextile may let the untamed side out only in play, on a stage or in a story, and never across the kitchen table on an ordinary Tuesday. The Neptune person can come to prefer the version with music; the Lilith person can accept the invitation once and then wait to be asked again, so nothing grows. A sextile is an opportunity, not a result, and an unused one simply goes quiet.

Its four neighbours in the same pair:

- **[Neptune conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** the hidden part and the imagination meet head on, and blur.
- **[Neptune square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-lilith-synastry/):** an ideal and a refusal collide at right angles, which forces a conversation.
- **[Neptune trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-lilith-synastry/):** acceptance that turns up without being invited.
- **[Neptune opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** one person reaches to merge while the other keeps their distance.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take the invitation on purpose, and give the play a date: a project, a trip, a habit you share. If the Lilith is yours, try carrying one refusal out of the story and into ordinary life, and see how it's received. If the Neptune is yours, keep welcoming it when it arrives without the music. Friends can use this sextile for a shared craft; adult siblings, for a family story told more honestly; colleagues, for work that needs one blunt voice.

Someone born in mid-1965 has their Neptune, in Scorpio, 60° from the Lilith of people born between February and April 1965, a few months older, whose Lilith was in Capricorn. With so narrow an orb the windows are short, and they come twice in each round of Lilith, with people born in January or February 1971 for instance. Their own Lilith, at the end of Capricorn, stands 60° from the Neptune of anyone born from October 1969 to December 1970. Since it comes with the windows rather than with the people, I leave it out of the aspects a synastry reading counts, all of which involve the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. I look for it in the houses, where it tells of two periods of history brushing past each other rather than of two characters.

Both points crawl, so the hour of birth neither makes nor unmakes this sextile; the houses it joins, in both charts, can't be found without both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The windows come from mean Lilith ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

[Neptune sextile Lilith](/astrology/neptune-sextile-lilith/), in one chart, is a person's own door between dreaming and refusing. Between two charts one person holds the door and the other walks through, and I read that only with both of them ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
