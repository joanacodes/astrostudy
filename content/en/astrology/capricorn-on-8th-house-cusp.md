The house of what you share with others begins in Capricorn when the sea-goat holds your 8th house cusp. Trust here is a long contract: slow to sign, and honoured.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) holds the life you share and the debts that come with it: intimacy, trust, joint money, loans, the closeness that changes you ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) on its cusp brings structure to all of it. I read it as the joint account with a spreadsheet behind it, the loan you'd rather not take, the mortgage treated as a twenty-year project, and the friend people call when something has to be sorted out properly. Intimacy tends to deepen year by year; you open up in stages, and once you have, you stay, and desire tends to grow with familiarity rather than fade with it. When a shared venture ends, you're the one who makes sure the accounts are settled fairly. What comes harder is depending on someone: being looked after can feel like a debt you'd rather not run up.

Tradition gave this house death and legacies, and with Saturn in charge of it that can sound grim. It isn't, in my reading. I don't read death or inheritance in any chart ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and what this cusp describes is how carefully you hold what's shared, now.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) governs this 8th house from wherever it sits: its house shows where shared money and intimate trust are put to the test, and its sign how you meet it, in the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Because Saturn held 75 to 90% of the time when I checked placements in my notebook ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), Saturn's house carries much of the weight here. Aquarius, the other sign Saturn rules, which Uranus shares, opens the 9th or the Midheaven in all these charts, so the planet of what you owe also speaks for your beliefs or your public work.

Count the charts with this cusp in Placidus, the house system I use, and you find roughly one in 10, at London and New York both. Just over the western horizon, where this cusp sits, Capricorn takes its time going down, as long as Cancer takes to climb in the east. This 8th is a narrow house, about 17° at London, so in nearly 9 London charts in 10 Capricorn reaches a second cusp ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)): the Descendant, when Cancer rises, and then the people you commit to and what you share with them come under one ruler; or the 9th, when Gemini rises, joining what you share to what you believe. Either way, the talkative manner of Gemini or the tender one of Cancer tends to hide how private you are about what you share. Across the chart, Cancer opens the 2nd: what's yours is handled by feeling, what's shared by rule.

Shift the birth a little earlier and Capricorn would open the 9th instead ([Capricorn on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-9th-house-cusp/)); a little later, the Descendant ([Capricorn on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/)). Only the minute of birth separates these, and I wait for it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
