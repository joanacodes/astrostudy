Some pairs show affection by passing things along: the article sent with one line of comment, the book returned with notes in the margin, the friend introduced because the two of them will get on. Composite Venus in Gemini makes that traffic the relationship's pleasure.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Composite Venus is found by halving the arc between two natal Venuses, and in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) it describes what the relationship values and enjoys, and how it gives and receives. [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), mutable air ruled by [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), grants [Venus](/astrology/venus/) neither dignity nor debility; it lends a manner of comparing and exchanging. This bond likes what it can discuss, swap and try in several versions, and it tends to show care through attention to what the other person said last week.

Two quiet people, each with a taste for one book read slowly, can make a bond that keeps a dozen threads going: the group chat, the shared playlist, the second-hand market visited for the conversation as much as the finds. Someone born with [Venus in Gemini](/astrology/venus-in-gemini/) brings that curiosity to every friendship; in a composite, it describes what the two of you enjoy between you, and it can surprise you both. Because Mercury rules the sign, this Venus also takes its lead from composite Mercury: where the relationship's mind wanders, its pleasures tend to follow. Of the twelve signs, Gemini is the one composite Venus reaches most often, by a whisker: about 1 pair in 11, level in round numbers with Aries and Virgo.

A composite can also do something no sky does. A birth chart never lets Venus get more than about 47° from the Sun, and the arithmetic of midpoints leaves composite Venus only two places to go: within 47° of the composite Sun, where about 9 pairs in 10 find it, or within 47° of the point opposite, never anywhere in between. For a Gemini Venus, the near zone puts the composite Sun somewhere from Aries to Leo; the far zone puts it around Sagittarius, and in about 1 pair in 22 it sits within 8° of exactly opposite. Gemini likes a comparison, so here is one: the near zone is the rule a real sky keeps, and the far one is a by-product of the method, not a hidden quarrel.

The risk is breadth without a favourite. A relationship that enjoys everything a little can go years without saying what it would hate to lose, and when one of you needs a single plain sign of being chosen, a stream of links and jokes may not count as one. Variety isn't indecision, but it helps to narrow it on purpose now and then: if the two of you could keep only three of your shared pleasures, which would they be? The answer is often a surprise, and worth saying aloud.

Just before it, [composite Venus in Taurus](/astrology/composite-venus-in-taurus/) keeps one pleasure for years; just after, [composite Venus in Cancer](/astrology/composite-venus-in-cancer/) gathers its pleasures round a home. I describe the composite rather than practise it, and those who do practise it read it alongside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), never to tell two people whether to stay together. Two people's birth data go into it, so both people should know it's being drawn ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
