Mercury’s retrogrades in Libra come between early September and early November, usually three years running and then not for three or four. They turn back through the sign of the scales, and what goes back on the scales is mostly what two people have said to each other.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Libra](/astrology/libra/) is cardinal air, ruled by Venus, and Mercury there weighs one view against another and looks for the fair, tactful word. During [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/), when the planet seems from Earth to stall and back through the sign, the weighing starts again: an agreement two of you reached, a compromise that seemed even, the thing you left unsaid to keep the peace. Mercury holds no dignity in Libra, and rather than a verdict, I read these weeks as a second hearing: balance being checked, not something broken.

Between 2000 and 2040, eighteen retrogrades touch Libra, in runs of three years in 2000–2002, 2007–2009, 2020–2022, 2027–2029 and 2033–2035. The most recent, from 10 September to 2 October 2022, stationed at 8°55′ Libra and ended at 24°12′ Virgo ([Mercury retrograde in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-virgo/)). The next opens a new run: from 7 to 28 October 2027, starting at 4°55′ Scorpio ([Mercury retrograde in Scorpio](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-scorpio/)) and ending at 19°18′ Libra. From 19 September to 11 October 2028 Mercury stays wholly in Libra, from 18°35′ to 3°28′, and in September 2029 it backs out into Virgo once more.

Mercury retrograde in Libra in a birth chart is another matter, a mind that weighs both sides inside before it speaks, and I write about it in [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house it crosses depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); I can’t draw the houses without it, and I don’t read a chart without it either. In Placidus, which I use, a retrograde of about 15° can easily reach across a cusp, and Libra’s often start in Scorpio or end in Virgo, so the degrees of the stations matter.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/libra-rising/):** Libra rising: your gracious way of meeting people; the peace you keep, and what it costs you, looked at again.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/libra-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** a sense of worth tied to fairness and taste; what counts as a fair share, weighed again.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/libra-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** tactful speech and the go-between’s role; a message you softened, reread for what it really said.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/libra-on-4th-house-cusp/):** harmony at home; a household compromise that seemed fair, talked through again.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/libra-on-5th-house-cusp/):** courtship and creative duets; what was said in the early days of a romance, remembered and reconsidered.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/libra-on-6th-house-cusp/):** working in pairs; who does which share of the daily work, rebalanced.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Libra in its natural house, the Descendant itself, with Aries rising: partnership and agreements; the terms between you and someone close, gone over together.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/libra-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what’s shared, negotiated rather than seized; an exchange that felt uneven, reopened.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/libra-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs formed by hearing both sides; an argument you thought settled, heard from the other side.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/):** mediating, advising or representing others; a public position you took for someone, reconsidered.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/libra-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friendships that feel like partnerships; a group decision, revisited until everyone has had a fair say.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/libra-on-12th-house-cusp/):** time alone to hear your own opinion, apart from everyone else’s.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Hear both sides, including your own: Libra’s retrograde suits the second conversation, the one in which each person says what they meant the first time. Venus rules Libra, and the house Venus occupies in your birth chart shows where your sense of fairness is most at stake.

A reopened question doesn’t mean the first answer was wrong; it means the balance can be checked. The scales don’t have to stop moving for you to choose, only to be read honestly.
