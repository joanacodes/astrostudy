Mercury’s retrogrades in Sagittarius are the quickest of the year, about 20 days each, and the widest, about 16°. They come between early November and early January, always three years in a row and then not for three or four.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) is mutable fire, ruled by Jupiter, and it is Mercury’s detriment, opposite Gemini: the planet of detail in the sign of the big picture. When [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) comes here, the planet only seems from Earth to turn round, but the big picture is what gets reread: the plan for next year, the belief you were sure of, the meaning you drew from something. The details that slipped past on the way forward tend to come back on the way round, small print included.

Every one of the eighteen retrogrades that reach Sagittarius from 2000 to 2040 lasts within a few hours of 20 days. The most recent, from 9 to 29 November 2025, stationed at 6°51′ Sagittarius and backed into Scorpio ([Mercury retrograde in Scorpio](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-scorpio/)), to 20°42′. Sagittarius then waits four years, until 6 December 2030, when Mercury stations at 1°51′ Capricorn ([Mercury retrograde in Capricorn](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-capricorn/)) and backs to 15°33′ Sagittarius by 25 December. The last wholly inside the sign ran from 26 November to 15 December 2024, from 22°40′ to 6°23′; the next will run from 28 November to 18 December 2037.

Mercury already retrograde in Sagittarius at your birth is something else, a mind that tests its big ideas inside before sharing them, a way of thinking for life rather than a season ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house it crosses depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): the cusps move every few minutes, and without the time there’s nothing to place it in, which is why I don’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, 16° is more than half of an average house, so the station degrees decide whether one house is involved or two.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/):** Sagittarius rising: your open, frank manner; a promise made with enthusiasm, revisited with care.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** a sense of worth tied to freedom; a generous plan, looked at in detail.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** big ideas in everyday talk; the frank remark, the story told on the move, heard back.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home full of books, guests and opinions; plans for hosting or moving, reconsidered.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** love as adventure and play far from home; a romantic plan or a trip for fun, rethought.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work that needs meaning; the routine you resisted, given a reason to exist.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius on the Descendant, with Gemini rising: partners who widen your world; the freedom you each need, talked about again.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** honesty about what’s shared; a generous arrangement, its details checked.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius in its natural house: study, travel and belief; a journey, a course or a conviction, planned again with the details in view.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** teaching, publishing or spreading ideas at work; a message you put out, revised.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends from everywhere and causes on a grand scale; the group’s big hope, matched to its next step.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a private faith; questions of meaning, turned over alone.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep the vision and check the map: these weeks suit going over the itinerary, the reading list or the plan for the year more than abandoning it. Jupiter rules Sagittarius, so the house of your natal Jupiter shows where the big picture is drawn in your chart.

If a conviction wobbles, let it; a belief that has survived its own questions is the stronger for it. And when the end-of-year rush meets this retrograde, as it often does, give the details an extra glance and the spirit of things the benefit of the doubt.
