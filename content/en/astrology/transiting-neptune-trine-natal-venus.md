A trine from Neptune to your Venus is the kind of transit people tend to notice only afterwards. For about two and a half years kindness, taste and imagination run the same way, and the ease is real; so is the risk of letting it drift by.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) chooses, enjoys and harmonises: it knows what it finds beautiful and how it likes to be loved. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) loosens edges that aren't needed and brings compassion and imagination. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates two functions through flow while each keeps its own nature, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) imagination feeds your taste without clouding it, and tenderness comes without costing you your judgement.

It is often the season when art gets easier to make and to take in. A piece of music finally opens up, a room or a garden starts to look the way you'd pictured it, you find words for a feeling you've carried a long time. In relationships it can show as patience, as forgiveness that comes without strain, or as a gentler way of asking for what you want. Ease isn't inevitability, though: the trine makes these things possible, and a flow left to itself only carries you where you were already going.

Of Neptune's contacts to Venus it's the least likely to feel confusing, and still I don't read it for love arriving, for money, or for anything else it can't promise.

With Venus in your 3rd, the trine often comes while Neptune crosses your 7th or your 11th: a partner's softness, or a friend's, can change how you talk about what you love ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)). Only your birth time can say whether your Venus is in the 3rd at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Someone born with [Venus trine Neptune](/astrology/venus-trine-neptune/) carries this flow as a habit of the heart; under the transit, anyone can borrow it for a while.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune stays within orb of the trine, 1½° before exact to 1° after in my count, for about two years and seven months, seldom under twenty-two. That is long enough for Venus in the sky to turn retrograde at least once ([Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/)), while Neptune crosses the exact trine three times in three passes out of four and five times in the rest.

Your Venus has two trine points, a third of the zodiac apart on one side and two thirds on the other. Most people meet one before 80 and some meet both; about 1 person in 6 born between 1930 and 2009 meets neither, because their eighty years of Neptune's motion fall entirely in the wide gap between the two.

A trine always sits about fourteen years from a square, [transiting Neptune square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-venus/); about twenty-seven years from [transiting Neptune sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-venus/) on one side and from [transiting Neptune opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-venus/) on the other; and about fifty-five from [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-venus/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Use it on purpose. Book the lessons, finish the piece, plan the visit to the place that moves you: a trine smooths the road, and you still choose where it goes.

Let kindness be specific. Forgive the thing you've been carrying, write the note, offer the help; the ease of the season does the rest.

Notice what you take for granted. The people and pleasures that ask nothing of you in these years are often the ones worth thanking.
