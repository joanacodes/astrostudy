A belief that has carried you a long way starts to creak. Twice in most lives Uranus squares the degree your Jupiter held at birth, and for about twenty months your sense of what makes life meaningful is asked to make room for something it didn't plan on.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) tests inherited assumptions and tries alternatives. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the part of you that builds meaning: principles, beliefs, the picture of the world you grow by. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates two functions through constructive friction, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) sets the urge to reform against the framework that has been doing your understanding for you.

The friction usually starts in the frame rather than in the facts. A school of thought you trusted begins to explain less; a teacher or a cause you followed looks smaller up close; a plan for growth, a degree, a move abroad, a long project, no longer fits the person who made it. Meanwhile the new idea pulling at you can arrive with Jupiter's enthusiasm and Uranus's urgency at once, and look like the whole answer.

I read this square as an update, not a conversion. Uranus can mistake difference for accuracy, and Jupiter can take size for wisdom; together they make a leap look like growth. The useful work is slower: keep the principles that still hold, and revise the ones that have stopped describing your life.

With Jupiter in your 6th and Uranus crossing your 9th ([Uranus transiting the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-9th-house/)), a new outlook, from study, travel or another culture, rubs against the way your days and your work are organised. Those houses rest on your birth time, and a chart without one is a chart I don't read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A belief takes time to revise, and this square gives it about twenty months within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after; eleven at the shortest. More than nine times in ten Uranus meets it three times, and I read the three hits as stages of one argument: the question raised, reconsidered while Uranus is retrograde, then settled on the last pass.

The two squares stand on opposite sides of Jupiter, so they come about forty years apart, give or take a few with Uranus's uneven pace. In the site's count, someone born at the start of 1945 met the first before the age of 3 and the second at about 42; someone born at the start of 1965 met the first at about 34, and meets the second near 78. Nobody born between 1930 and 2009 misses both.

[Jupiter square Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-square-uranus/) at birth fixes the calendar: Uranus starts out already on one square, and the other lies 180° further on, the distance it covers by about 42, the age of your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/). For everyone else, the squares divide the round in two. Between them come [transiting Uranus trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Uranus opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-jupiter/) and a second trine; on the other side of the circle, the sextiles and [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-jupiter/), with [transiting Uranus sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-jupiter/) the gentlest of the five.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the belief that's under strain. Friction is easier to work with once you can say which principle it's rubbing on, and it's rarely all of them.

Try the new idea at a small scale first: a term rather than a degree, a visit rather than a move. Jupiter likes to go big, and a square rewards the version you can still adjust.

Keep talking to someone who disagrees with you. A square grows through resistance, and a good argument does more for your outlook than a fresh set of certainties.
