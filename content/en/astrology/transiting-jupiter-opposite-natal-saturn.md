About six years after crossing your natal Saturn, Jupiter arrives on the far side of the chart from it. What you have built, and keep, finds itself across the table from what you could grow into, and very often the growth arrives in somebody else's hands.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in your chart stands for the structures you've built and the responsibilities you keep, along with the fear of wasting effort that made you careful. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) brings the wider view: opportunity, belief, a reason to go further. Across an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), neither cancels the other: they face each other until you see what each one is for.

In practice the growth tends to come from outside. Someone's offer, a partner's ambition, a belief you meet in another person: each one appears across from what you're already committed to, and asks whether it still fits. It isn't a contest between security and adventure, whatever it feels like. An opposition is about balance, and the useful question is what your structure would need in order to hold a little more.

The weighing takes place in your Saturn's house, with Jupiter passing through the one across from it. With Saturn in your 7th, where commitments to a partner take their time, the opposition comes while [Jupiter transiting the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-1st-house/) gives your own growth room just as a long commitment asks to be kept. Your birth time is what fixes both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Taken from 1½° before exact to 1° after, as I take every Jupiter [transit](/astrology/transits/), a straight pass across the point opposite your Saturn is done in about eleven days. In roughly three cases out of ten Jupiter is near a station, and three exact passes spread the opposition over some eight months. By the age of 80, each birth from 1930 to 2009 has seen six or seven.

Its first visit follows the reverse of the pattern on [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-saturn/). Born a few years after a great conjunction, people met it early: at about 2.8 for a birth on 1 January 1965, about 3.5 for 1 January 1985. Born a few years before one, they waited: about 8.8 for 1 January 1935, about 9.9 for 1 January 1975.

Two links in the chart change its timing. With [Jupiter opposite Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-saturn/) at birth, it falls near each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/); with Jupiter conjunct Saturn, it arrives with [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-jupiter/), the halfway point of the Jupiter cycle. In the round it shares with your Saturn, the opposition sits between [transiting Jupiter square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-saturn/) on either side, with [transiting Jupiter trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-saturn/) closer in and [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-saturn/) further out.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Weigh before you choose. Write down what the structure gives you and what the offer would give you, and notice how much of each you could keep.

Talk to the person across the table. An opposition often works through someone else, and their ambition is easier to balance with yours once both are said out loud.

And ask what your commitment needs in order to grow, before deciding whether to leave it. Many oppositions end with the same structure, rebuilt a size larger.
