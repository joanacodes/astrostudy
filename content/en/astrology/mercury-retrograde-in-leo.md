Mercury turns back in Leo between early July and early September, for 23 to 24 days at a time. The sign of the Sun speaks with conviction and likes a story well told; in these weeks the story goes back to the drafting table.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Leo](/astrology/leo/) is fixed fire, the only sign the Sun rules, and Mercury there puts things into words with warmth and wants a voice of its own, though it holds no dignity in the sign. During [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/), when the planet seems from Earth to hang back and reverse through Leo, that voice rehearses instead of performing: the speech rewritten, the creative piece taken back for another pass, the way you’ve been telling your own story heard with a little more honesty. What slows is the moment of showing; what gets the attention is the work behind it.

Fourteen retrogrades reach Leo between 2000 and 2040, sometimes three years running, as from 2004 to 2006 and from 2017 to 2019. The most recent, from 18 July to 11 August 2025, stayed wholly in the sign, from 15°34′ back to 4°14′. Then comes the longest pause in Leo’s list, five years without one, until Mercury backs through Leo alone again from 29 July to 22 August 2031, from 26°25′ to 14°17′. The year after, from 10 July to 3 August 2032, it starts at 7°32′ Leo and ends in Cancer ([Mercury retrograde in Cancer](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-cancer/)); in 2024 it had come in from Virgo ([Mercury retrograde in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-virgo/)), from 4°06′ Virgo to 21°24′ Leo.

Mercury already retrograde in Leo on the day you were born is a different matter, a voice that drafts inside before it performs, and I read it as such ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which part of your life these weeks reach depends on the house Leo occupies, and that takes [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): the cusps turn with the sky through the day, and without the time I don’t read a chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, 10° to 13° of retrograde can straddle a cusp, so I check both station degrees against yours.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/leo-rising/):** Leo rising: how you present yourself; an image or an introduction that wants updating.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/leo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you’re proud to have earned and to own; whether it still says who you are.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** your storytelling voice in daily talk; a story you tell often, retold more truthfully.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home you’re proud of and gather people in; plans for a family occasion, rethought.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** Leo in its natural house: creative work, play and romance; a piece taken back for another draft, or words of love you’d like to say better.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** pride in daily work; the credit given or not given, talked through again.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Leo on the Descendant, with Aquarius rising: a warm, confident partner; how the two of you celebrate each other, put into words again.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/leo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** wholehearted intimacy; a need that pride kept quiet, given a second chance to be said.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** convictions shared with enthusiasm; a belief you’ve championed, put into fresh words.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** leadership and recognition at work; a presentation or a public statement, reworked before it’s final.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/leo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** loyal friends and the groups you lead; the role you play among them, reconsidered with their help.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/leo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** creativity kept private; work you’ve never shown, taken out again, for you alone.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Rehearse rather than retire: a retrograde in Leo suits revising the performance more than cancelling it. The Sun rules Leo, so the house of your natal Sun shows where the wish to be seen tends to lead the review.

If you’ve been waiting for applause to know that a piece of work is good, these weeks leave room to judge it yourself, so that when you step back into the light, it’s with words you’ve tested on yourself first.
