One light wants roots, the other a horizon. This pair needs a life solid enough to stand on and meaningful enough to be worth it, and notices quickly when it has only one of the two. The birth came in the days just after a full moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Earth and fire, fixed and mutable, with five signs between them counting back from the Sun: these two share no quality at all, and by sign they form a quincunx, not a clash and not a flow but a standing need to make room for each other. The [Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/) trusts what it can touch, keep and enjoy; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) needs space, honesty and something to look forward to, and settles once a feeling fits a bigger story. The Sun builds the house; the Moon keeps asking what the house is for, and both questions deserve an answer.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Taurus and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules Sagittarius. Venus shows what you value and enjoy, Jupiter where you look for meaning and room to grow, and their signs and houses show whether the two agree. When Jupiter gives the Moon a real horizon, a course, a journey, a belief worked out for yourself, the Taurus Sun can stay put without feeling stuck.

A full moon had just happened when you were born, no more than about five days earlier: the Moon stood 181° to 239° on from the Sun, fifteen to nineteen and a half days into the lunar month. Seven births in eight with this pair still count as [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) births in the eight-phase scheme, because that phase runs on for 45° after the exact full; the rest are [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) births. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows how the two are read.

Of every 143 people or so, one has this pair. On average the Moon needs two and a half days to cross Sagittarius, so for the Moon's sign the hour of birth matters only on the days it crosses a border, in from Scorpio or out to Capricorn; on those days it decides between this page, the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-scorpio/) and the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-capricorn/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). For the rising sign the hour matters every day, and without it the two lights are a sketch with no setting.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You want a partner who is both a home and an adventure. The Taurus Sun needs constancy, touch and someone who stays; the Sagittarius Moon needs honesty, humour and room to roam, in ideas if not in miles. When a bond is all comfort, the Moon gets restless; when it's all freedom, the Sun stops trusting it. A shared plan, the trip saved for, the house with a door open to the world, gives both lights what they want.

## At work {#at-work}

You do well where solidity and meaning meet: building something that lasts and that you believe in. The Sun wants tangible results and a steady pace; the Moon wants to learn, to teach, to see the bigger picture. A secure job with no larger purpose leaves the Moon restless, and a cause without structure unsettles the Sun. Look for work that pays its way and also points somewhere.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The pair can swing between staying and leaving. The Taurus Sun holds on to security; the Sagittarius Moon dreams of the open road, and the quincunx means neither simply wins. You may stay put out of habit and resent it, or make a big move and then miss what you left. What helps is building the horizon into the routine: a fixed day for learning, a trip planned months ahead, a belief examined rather than inherited, so the Moon gets its space without the Sun losing its ground.
