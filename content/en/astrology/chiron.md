Chiron was found in 1977, and astrologers took it up within a few years: a small body on a strange, stretched orbit, named after the one centaur who was a healer and a teacher — and who carried a wound that would not close. That story is how it's read.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Chiron shows the wound a person keeps coming back to: the thing that still stings when someone touches it, sometimes decades later. It's also, often, where that person is best at caring for others, because they know exactly how that hurt feels.

It's the placement I read most carefully. Naming a wound accurately is a form of intimacy, and it has to be offered, never imposed. It's one reason I [never read a chart behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## Chiron through the signs {#through-the-signs}

Because its orbit is so uneven, Chiron's sign is shared by everyone born over a span of anywhere from under two years to about eight. Its sign colours the wound — around confidence in Aries, worth in Taurus, being understood in Gemini, belonging in Cancer — but the house and the aspects make it personal.

## Chiron in the houses {#in-the-houses}

1. **[1st house](/astrology/chiron-in-1st-house/):** feeling not quite right as you are; later, a way of putting others at ease.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/chiron-in-2nd-house/):** a sore spot about value — your own, or what you earn.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/chiron-in-3rd-house/):** not being heard, or misunderstood early; later, a gift for explaining.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/chiron-in-4th-house/):** a wound around belonging; often a welcoming home made later.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/chiron-in-5th-house/):** play or self-expression that was mocked; later, encouraging other people's.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/chiron-in-6th-house/):** feeling useful only when helping; learning to work without wearing out.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/chiron-in-7th-house/):** hurt in or by close relationships; later, a rare way of listening to a partner.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/chiron-in-8th-house/):** deep wariness in closeness; a capacity to sit with people in their hardest moments.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/chiron-in-9th-house/):** a wound around meaning or faith; later, teaching what you had to find alone.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/chiron-in-10th-house/):** feeling exposed or judged in public; often work that helps people in the same place.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/chiron-in-11th-house/):** feeling on the outside of groups; later, gathering other outsiders.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/chiron-in-12th-house/):** a hurt that's hard to name; a quiet compassion others feel without knowing why.

## Chiron in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), when one person's Chiron touches the other's planets, each can reach the other's tender spot — to hurt it or to help it heal. Chiron contacts with the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) and [Venus](/astrology/venus/) are often described as recognition: *you see the part of me I hide*. They ask for gentleness on both sides.

## Transits and the Chiron return {#chiron-return}

Chiron's uneven orbit means its transits arrive at different ages for different generations, except one: the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, when it comes back to its birth position. People often describe it as the point where an old hurt is finally seen for what it was.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

When I checked charts placement by placement — mine, then my family's, friends', and strangers' who told me nothing first — Chiron, [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) and [Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) held 75 to 90% of the time, while most placements held about a third. It's a notebook, not a study: a count by hand, mostly on people I knew. But it's why Chiron gets more time than the Sun in my readings. ## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Chiron is a planet.”** It's a centaur, a small icy body. It's read like a planet; it isn't one.
- **“Chiron means you're broken.”** It means you have a tender place, like everyone. Its placement says where, not how much.
- **“A Chiron transit makes you ill.”** A chart can't tell you anything about your health, and I don't read it for that.
