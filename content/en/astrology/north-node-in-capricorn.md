The North Node in Capricorn asks you to take your place in the wider world, on your own authority. When the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is in [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), the growth is slow and solid: goals that take years, responsibility you choose, a structure you can stand on.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Capricorn is cardinal earth: it begins by building, and it plans for the long term. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) lies opposite in [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/): the warmth of the familiar circle, a gift for care, a fine sense of what people feel, and a strong pull towards home. All of that is worth keeping. The Capricorn end asks you to carry it out of the house, into a role where you answer for something larger than your own circle, and where your authority doesn't depend on anyone's approval.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules Capricorn. Its sign and house show where the structure gets built, and what kind of patience the building asks of you.

The true node spent March 1935 to September 1936 in Capricorn, then October 1953 to April 1955, April 1972 to October 1973, November 1990 to August 1992 and August 2009 to March 2011; it comes back in March 2028 and stays until September 2029. Because the last of those stays began in August 2009, inside the count, about 1 person in 12 born from 1930 to 2009 has it. Before Capricorn it was in Aquarius, after it in Sagittarius, so births near those handovers may read better with [North Node in Aquarius](/astrology/north-node-in-aquarius/) or [North Node in Sagittarius](/astrology/north-node-in-sagittarius/). I look at the mean node as well as the true one; the gap between them never exceeds a degree or two, but at a sign's border that's enough to differ, and I'll say so. The sign is a generation's; the house, for which I need [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), is yours.

As for what my [testing notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) shows: the rising sign is its one angle, and about a third held; the three to stand out were Saturn, Chiron and Lilith.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Looking after someone, and being looked after, the Cancer end knows well; it can also slide into fixed roles, the one who soothes and the one who's soothed. The Capricorn end asks for a partnership of two adults: shared plans, kept promises, each person standing on their own feet. Commitment, here, is something you build on purpose.

It can also mean letting a partner see your ambition without apologising for it.

## At work {#at-work}

Work is where this node does much of its practising. You may be wonderful at making a workplace feel like a family, and slower to claim the title, the pay rise, the decision. The Capricorn end asks you to step into authority: to apply for the role that stretches you, to set goals years ahead, and to become someone others can rely on in public as well as in private.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

When the world asks too much, the South Node calls you home. Feelings decide, plans soften, and the long-term goal waits for a better mood. You may also fear that ambition will make you cold.

It won't, though the overcorrection can look like it: working all hours, locking feelings away, treating yourself more harshly than you'd treat anyone else. That's Capricorn learnt too fast. Structure is meant to hold you up, not hold you down.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Set one goal that will take years, and break it into the first season's steps. Take on one public responsibility: a role, a committee, a project with your name on it. Keep regular hours; Capricorn grows through rhythm.

The node comes back to Capricorn from March 2028 to September 2029: the [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) for everyone born with it there, at about 18½ for births in 2009–11, 37 for 1990–92 and 56 for 1972–73. It's a season for reviewing the structure you've built, not an event to wait for.

And bring the Cancer end with you. People follow someone who cares, and your care is part of your authority.
