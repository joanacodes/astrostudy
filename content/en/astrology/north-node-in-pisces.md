The North Node in Pisces asks the person who fixes everything to let something be. For anyone born with the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) in [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), growth is a softening: trust, compassion, imagination, and room for what can't be planned.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The last sign of the zodiac, Pisces is mutable water: porous, compassionate, at home with what has no clear edges. Across from it, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) holds the ground you know: competence, precision, a gift for improving things, an eye that finds the flaw before anyone else does. It's a useful eye. The Pisces end asks you to close it now and then, and to trust that life holds together even when you aren't holding it.

Pisces keeps an old ruler and a new one, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern ruler, spends about fourteen years in a sign, so its house matters most: where imagination and compassion want room. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the ruler tradition gave it, shows in its sign and house where trust and a wider sense of meaning can grow.

The true node was in Pisces from December 1931 to June 1933, July 1950 to March 1952, April 1969 to November 1970, November 1987 to May 1989 and June 2006 to December 2007, and most recently from January 2025 to July 2026. For births between 1930 and 2009, that comes to about 1 person in 10. It came in from Aries and went on to Aquarius, so for a birth near a change, [North Node in Aries](/astrology/north-node-in-aries/) or [North Node in Aquarius](/astrology/north-node-in-aquarius/) may suit better. The mean and true nodes, a degree or two between them at most, can sit either side of that line; I look at both and say which way each one falls. The sign belongs to everyone born within that year and a half, and it's the house, which [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) decides, that tells your version.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Care, from the Virgo end, means improving: advice, corrections, the helpful list. The Pisces end loves the person as they are, rough edges included, and lets itself be loved the same way. Compassion does more here than critique. So does tenderness without a purpose: an afternoon with no plan, a feeling shared without being solved.

It's also about trust. You may never have all the evidence that someone loves you; this node asks you to feel your way rather than check.

## At work {#at-work}

In the workplace you may be the most reliable person in the building, the one who catches every mistake. The North Node asks you to let imagination in: creative work, caring work, anything that runs on intuition as well as method. It may also mean letting a project breathe before it's perfect, delegating without checking twice, and remembering why the work matters as well as how it's done.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Worry is the old companion. Under pressure, the Virgo end tightens: more lists, more checking, more criticism of yourself and everyone near you, in the belief that if you stop fixing, it will all fall apart.

When the Pisces end opens, the swing can go too far the other way: all structure dropped at once, and the ground gone with it. Trust grows better slowly. Loosen one grip at a time.

Try not to over-analyse this page, either. My [testing notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) reports one angle, the rising sign, and about a third held; Saturn, Chiron and Lilith were the placements that stood out.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give yourself time without a purpose: music, water, a long walk with no destination, art you make badly on purpose. If prayer or meditation belongs in your life, make room for it. Practise saying *that's good enough*, and leaving it there.

And let the Virgo end serve the dream instead of guarding against it. Your skill is what gives imagination a shape.
