Saturn's years in your 6th house are spent where life is most ordinary: the tasks, the routines, the work you do every day and the way you keep your days going. It's the house where Saturn's habits feel most at home, and where they're most likely to overdo it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the part of a chart that organises, limits and maintains; the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is daily work, routine, craft and care. These are years of rebuilding. Methods get overhauled, workloads get weighed, and routines that never quite worked get replaced by ones that will. Responsibility at work often grows, and with it the chance to become genuinely good at something by doing it every day.

The risk is that structure tips into control. Saturn's tension is between the order that helps and the order that only reassures, and in the 6th that can look like perfectionism, or like carrying tasks nobody else is allowed to touch. Discipline is not the same as self-criticism, and service is not servitude: the work of these years is to build routines that serve you, rather than the other way round.

With your Mercury in the 6th, the house's natural ruler, Saturn's pass tends to tidy your methods: the lists, the systems, the way you plan a week.

The old name for the 6th was Bad Fortune, and books once read illness there. I don't. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) can't diagnose or foretell anything about your body ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and if your health worries you, a doctor is the person to see. What this one brings out is what your chart already holds about how you work.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Few houses vary as much. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, Saturn's stays in the 6th run from about 22 months to nearly 5 years, around a middle of about 2 years and 8 months, a little shorter than the all-house middle of just under 3. Move the birthplace south to New York's latitude and the middle rises to about 2 years and 10 months while the long end comes down to about 4 years.

Where your 6th begins and how far it reaches both come from your birth time, the first thing I ask for ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Saturn reaches your 6th from the 5th ([Saturn transiting the 5th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-5th-house/)), and leaves it at your Descendant for its years with partners ([Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/)).

The months Saturn enters each sign are enough to find yours: Aries in February 2026, for good after a first visit in 2025; Taurus in April 2028; Gemini in June 2030; Cancer in July 2032; Leo in August 2034; Virgo, the 6th's natural sign, in October 2036; Libra in September 2039. Two of those entries are followed by a step back: Saturn returns to Cancer after its first weeks in Leo and only leaves it in May 2035, and returns to Leo after touching Virgo, leaving it in July 2037.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Rebuild one routine at a time, starting with the one that holds the others up. The 6th under Saturn suits the slow, boring fix better than the dramatic overhaul.

Give some tasks away. Delegation isn't Saturn's strength, and a 6th house full of jobs only you can do is a structure that can't bend.

Put rest inside the routine, not at the end of it. Maintenance includes yours, and a schedule with no slack in it isn't discipline, it's brittleness.
