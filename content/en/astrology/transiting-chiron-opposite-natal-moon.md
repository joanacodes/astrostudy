Opposite your Moon, Chiron shows an old sensitivity about needs in someone else first: the person you're looking after, the friend whose loneliness you recognise, the partner who needs comforting in exactly the way you once did. Once or twice in a life, for weeks or months, you meet your own tender place from the outside.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is how you attach, need and care: the comfort you seek and the comfort you give. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is a sensitivity that stayed, and the skill you have from living with it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, puts your needs and that old sensitivity on either side of you, so what you'd rather not see in yourself appears first across the room. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), you may find yourself very good at looking after someone whose needs rhyme with your old ones, and quite bad at saying what you need yourself. Or someone close presses, without meaning to, on the old place, and you react with a strength that surprises you both.

People who are good at caring often confuse it with being cared for, and this is the confusion the opposition brings to light. What tends to come out of it is a fairer balance: needs said out loud on both sides, help that goes in both directions, and an old reaction recognised as old before it runs the evening.

The houses show which relationships carry it, and they rest on your birth time. With the Moon in the 10th, Chiron faces it from your 4th ([Chiron transiting the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-4th-house/)): the needs you've been meeting through your public life meet their mirror at home and in your family history. If you were born with [Moon opposite Chiron](/astrology/moon-opposite-chiron/), the people you care for have long mirrored your own needs; the transit repeats the pattern your chart began with, and Chiron's return around 50 brings it once more.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Using my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition usually lasts about ten months, with three exact crossings in nearly two passes out of three. About one pass in three is a single crossing over in some three weeks, and about one in twenty crosses five times, over twenty-one months or so.

Most people meet it twice by 80, half a century apart, and everyone meets it at least once. Its partner at the other end of the circle, the conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-moon/)), is just as rare and puts the sensitivity on your Moon rather than across from it; the sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-moon/)), square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-moon/)) and trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-moon/)) come about three times each.

At the end of 2026, Chiron is opposite Moons in the last degrees of Libra; from 2027 to 2033, as it crosses Taurus, Moons in Scorpio, the sign tradition calls the Moon's fall. A Moon at the very end of Libra or the start of Scorpio is in it now, through Chiron's loop of 2026 and early 2027. Which month counts most depends on the Moon's exact degree, and the Moon moves a degree about every two hours, so the birth time decides it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

When someone else's need moves you strongly, ask what it's telling you about your own.

Tell the person you look after most what comforts you, in words. People can only give back care they know the shape of.

If someone presses on the old place, wait a day before you answer. The reaction is often older than the remark.
