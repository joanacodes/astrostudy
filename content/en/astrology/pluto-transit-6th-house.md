Most of a life happens on ordinary weekdays, and the 6th house is where those days are organised. Pluto spends about two decades there, rebuilding the routines from the bottom up.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) strips things to what is essential and shows where power really sits. The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the working day: the tasks you do, the people you work with or for, the routines that keep you going, and the daily care you give your body and your surroundings. With Pluto here, routines that once held you up can start to feel like a cage, and you may find yourself rebuilding the week around what matters. Work can become a place of deeper commitment, or of a clearer refusal: a job done half-heartedly gets harder to bear. Power at work, in a hierarchy, between colleagues, in the quiet contract over who does the unseen tasks, tends to come into focus. The wish to get every detail right can sharpen too, and part of the work of these years is learning when good enough is enough.

A natal Mars in the 6th, the house where tradition says Mars rejoices, is met along the way and asked to work with more strategy and less strain.

To the tradition, the 6th was Bad Fortune, and illness fell to it. I don't read illness or health in any chart ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and nothing in this transit forecasts a diagnosis. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) shows a season in your habits and your work, the part of the 6th that is in your hands.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, Pluto keeps to the 6th for about 20 years in the middle, with most stays between about 18 and 25. That is close to its middle across all twelve houses, also about 20 years, but far more regular than the full range there, about 10 to 37. At New York's latitude the middle runs a little longer, about 22 years, and most stays fall between about 18 and 24.

When the stay begins and ends depends on the cusps of your 6th, and they depend on the time you were born. Without it I can't place them, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Pluto comes to the 6th from the house of pleasure and play ([Pluto transiting the 5th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-5th-house/)), and its next house is the 7th, where the work turns to partnership ([Pluto transiting the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-7th-house/)).

As for the sky, Pluto has been in Aquarius since March 2023, continuously since November 2024, and my count leaves it there in December 2039. With your 6th cusp in Aquarius, the stay is now or near, and the cusp's degree gives the year; with it in Capricorn, the stay fell between January 2008 and November 2024; with it in Pisces, the stay belongs to the years after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Rebuild one routine at a time. Pluto in the 6th rewards a slow overhaul more than a sudden one, and twenty years is plenty of time.

Notice the invisible work: who does it, who gets the credit, who decides. Naming it is usually the first step towards a fairer share.

When a habit has become a way of avoiding something, ask what it protects before you drop it. Pluto tends to find out either way, and knowing first is gentler.

Keep looking after your body in ordinary ways, and take any worry about your health to a doctor, not to a chart.
