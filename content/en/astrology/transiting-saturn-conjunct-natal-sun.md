Saturn needs about 29½ years to come back round to the degree your Sun held on the day you were born, so this crossing comes two or three times in a lifetime. When it does, it stays for months, and it asks the question the Sun always carries: who are you, and what are you building?

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) establishes structure, responsibility and limits, and integrates things over the long run; the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity, vitality and purpose. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes them act together, so for a while your sense of self and your sense of duty become one question. Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), it adds nothing to your chart: it asks what your Sun already describes to take a lasting form.

In practice it often feels like being taken seriously, by others and by yourself, with the weight that comes with it. Plans that rested on enthusiasm are asked for foundations, and roles you took on to please someone feel heavier than the ones that are really yours. Energy can run lower than usual. Tradition read Saturn on the Sun as hard on vitality; I read tiredness as a reason to pace yourself, never as a diagnosis ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

Your Sun's house says where the consolidation happens. With the Sun in your 10th, it falls inside [Saturn transiting the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-10th-house/), and the question becomes the shape of your working life; that house needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you were born with [Sun conjunct Saturn](/astrology/sun-conjunct-saturn/), this crossing is your [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/), and the two seasons are one.

Saturn was one of the three placements that held far more often than the rest in my notebook, but that count was about birth charts. It says nothing about transits, and I give this one no figure ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Saturn moves slowly, so the crossing is a season, not a week. With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, it usually lasts about nine months. A little over half the time it comes as a loop: Saturn crosses your Sun, turns back over it during [Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/) and crosses it a third time, about nine and a half months in all. When Saturn passes at full speed, it can be over in three weeks.

When yours comes depends on how far Saturn stood behind your Sun when you were born, at about two and a half years a sign: three signs behind, and the first crossing came at about seven or eight. Saturn is in Aries from February 2026 to April 2028, after a first visit in 2025, so Aries birthdays are meeting it now, the early ones in 2026 and 2027, the late ones in 2027 and early 2028. Taurus birthdays follow until mid-2030. Tradition exalted the Sun in Aries and gave Saturn its fall there, an old picture of two paces that have to learn each other.

Between two crossings, Saturn takes nearly thirty years to work round your Sun: the first [transiting Saturn sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-sun/) comes about five years on, the first [transiting Saturn square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-sun/) at about seven, the first [transiting Saturn trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-sun/) at about ten, and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-sun/) around fifteen. By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have had the crossing itself two or three times.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one commitment that matches who you are and give it a structure: a schedule, a deadline, a person to answer to. Saturn on the Sun favours the long version of a plan.

Put down what you carry for someone else's approval. This season shows quickly which duties are yours, because those are the ones that still make sense when you're tired.

Rest on purpose. Lower energy isn't failure, and a pace you can keep for nine months is worth more than a sprint you can't.
