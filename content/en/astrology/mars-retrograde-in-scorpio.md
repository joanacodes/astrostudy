Scorpio is one of the two signs Mars rules in the tradition, so when Mars turns back here it turns back on home ground: the deep, quiet half of its territory rather than the open field of Aries. In the dates I use it happens twice, in 2016 and in 2031, both times between late March and the end of June.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) is fixed water, ruled by Pluto in modern tropical astrology and by Mars in the tradition; I read both. Mars there is strategic and persistent, committing fully or not at all. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when Mars appears from Earth to stop and sink back through the sign for some eleven weeks, that concentration turns on what it has been holding: the grievance never released, the resolve kept long after its reason faded, the desire guarded so closely that even you stopped looking at it. Anger that runs deep can go quiet rather than loud, and these weeks may bring a buried one up to be understood.

The most recent began in Sagittarius ([Mars retrograde in Sagittarius](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-sagittarius/)) at 8°54′ on 17 April 2016, crossed back into Scorpio and turned direct at 23°03′ Scorpio on 29 June. The next stays wholly inside the sign, from 21°38′ on 29 March 2031 to 4°26′ on 13 June: 76 days over about 17°, with Mars opposite the Sun, then in Taurus, in early May. On the other side, the 2014 loop kept to Libra ([Mars retrograde in Libra](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-libra/)) and stopped at 27°32′, short of Scorpio.

A Mars that was retrograde in Scorpio when you were born belongs to your own chart and calls for its own reading ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house Scorpio occupies, and so where this spring review digs, comes from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); I won’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2016 loop may begin in the house Sagittarius holds and end in the Scorpio one.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/scorpio-rising/):** Scorpio rising: an intense, watchful presence; the guard you keep up before acting, lowered a little.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you hold on to, and why; something you defend fiercely, its meaning re-examined.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** words chosen with care; a conversation you ended abruptly, reopened.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/):** family loyalties; an old hurt at home, approached with less armour.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/):** passionate love and absorbing creative work; an attraction pursued in private, looked at in daylight.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work you give your whole self to; a tug of war with a colleague over control, examined.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio on the Descendant, opposite a Taurus Ascendant: a partnership of strong wills; who holds the power between you, talked about plainly.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio’s natural house: intimacy, trust and what is shared, which tradition linked to death and which I read only in life ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); a desire kept secret, admitted to yourself.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/):** convictions held with force; a belief you fought for, traced back to where it began.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio on the Midheaven: ambition played as strategy; a long campaign at work, reconsidered.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a small circle you would defend to the end; a test of loyalty among friends, set aside.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-12th-house-cusp/):** anger turned inward; an old hurt kept in the dark, brought gently into the light.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Scorpio goes to the bottom of things, so I find these weeks suit one honest look rather than many anxious ones: write the grievance down, read it again a week later, and keep only what is still true. The house of your natal Mars shows where your drive lives, and the house of your natal Pluto where the deeper review tends to lead.

None of this foretells a betrayal or a reckoning. It describes a spring in which the sign that holds on hardest is asked what it can put down.
