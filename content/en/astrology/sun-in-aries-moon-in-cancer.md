Both lights are strong here: the Sun at its exaltation in Aries, the Moon in Cancer, the sign it rules. Both signs are cardinal, each ready to make the first move, and they move in different directions, the Sun out into the world and the Moon back towards the people it keeps safe.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aries is cardinal fire and Cancer cardinal water, three signs apart: a square by sign. They share a way of moving, by initiative, and disagree about where to go. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) builds a self by setting off; the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/) feels secure once there's a home base and someone to look after. The courage is real on both sides. One kind goes out to meet things; the other stands in the doorway.

Tradition dignifies both lights, the Sun by exaltation and the Moon by domicile, which gives each a clear and confident voice. The rulers complicate things. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules Aries and has its fall in Cancer, so the planet that drives your Sun is least at ease in your Moon's sign: initiative, on Cancer's ground, has to turn protective before it feels right. The Moon answers to no ruler but itself, which is why, with this pair, I read its own house and aspects closely.

At this birth the Moon stood 60° to 120° ahead of the Sun, five to ten days into the lunar month. Counted in eight phases, half of these births belong to the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) and half to the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/), and the nearer the Moon was to 90° ahead, the nearer you are to a waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). Where the line between those two phases falls is set out in [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/).

Some 1 person in 141 is born with the pair. Because the Moon needs only about two and a half days to cross a sign, a birthday that saw it leave Gemini or reach Leo has to be checked against the clock: earlier hours may give the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-gemini/), later ones the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-leo/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same clock gives your rising sign, and the three together make a first draft of a chart, the kind I'd never hand anyone as finished.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You protect and you tell the truth, and both arrive quickly. A partner may get a frank challenge at breakfast and a carefully cooked supper at night, and both are meant as care. The Moon keeps a long record, which the quick-forgiving Aries Sun may not expect of itself: an old hurt can resurface well after you've declared it finished. Say both halves out loud, the Aries half (*here's what I want*) and the Cancer half (*I need to know we're all right*), so a partner can answer the one that's really asking.

## At work {#at-work}

You tend to start things for someone's sake: the project that shields a team, the service that looks after people better, the business built to keep a family secure. That gives your drive a reason and your care some backbone. Cold competition, and a workplace where nobody knows anyone's name, drain the Cancer Moon even while the Aries Sun enjoys the contest. A team that feels like a crew suits you better than a ladder.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Leaving and returning. The Sun wants to be out on the next venture; the Moon wants to be home when the people it loves need it, and you can feel guilty in both places at once. Under strain, the Aries side may push away exactly the closeness the Cancer side is longing for. Plan both on purpose: a real departure, a real return and a small ritual between them, so that going out never feels like abandoning anyone and coming home never feels like giving up.
