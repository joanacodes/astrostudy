Pluto in the 2nd house puts the planet of deep change among the things you call yours: your money and possessions, your talents, your sense of what you are worth. I read it as security rebuilt from the ground up, sometimes more than once, until it rests on something nobody can take away.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds what is yours: money and possessions, but also your skills, your values and your sense of worth. It is succedent, one of the houses that gather and keep what the angular houses begin, and its natural sign is Taurus, the sign opposite Pluto's own in modern tropical astrology. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) in this house makes security a deep matter. What you own can carry great emotional weight, and the turning points of a life tend to involve resources: building them, losing faith in them, rebuilding them on truer ground.

This describes an attitude, never an outcome. A chart can't say what you will earn, inherit or lose, and I don't read one for that ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). What a 2nd-house Pluto shows is how much security matters to you, and how thoroughly you rethink it when it is shaken.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the size of each house changes with the time and place of birth, and a Pluto near the edge of the 2nd can fall in the 1st or the 3rd after a small change of time. So I read it only from an accurate [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/). Next door, [Pluto in the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-in-1st-house/) transforms the self you show, and [Pluto in the 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-in-3rd-house/) the way you think and speak.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In relationships, a 2nd-house Pluto cares a great deal about what is mine, what is yours and what is ours. Independence, especially with money, can matter more than you show, and depending on a partner may feel like a risk. Gifts and belongings can carry deep meaning, which makes generosity powerful and losses painful. The tender point is possessiveness, of things and sometimes of people. What helps is clarity: agreements about money made early, and a sense of worth that doesn't rise and fall with a bank balance.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Pluto is drawn to resources and what can be made of them: turning little into something, reviving a struggling business, looking after what other people value, crafts that make lasting things from raw materials. You often have staying power, and a strong instinct for what deserves your effort. Values guide your choice of work more than you may admit, and you are at your best where what you do builds something you can stand behind.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard part is making security everything. A 2nd-house Pluto can hold on to money, things or a job out of fear rather than need, or swing between holding tight and letting everything go at once. Self-worth can get tangled up with what you own or earn, so that a setback feels like a judgement on you. And control over resources can slide into control over people, often without your meaning it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Separating worth from net worth, on paper if need be: a list of what you value in yourself that nobody could take. Letting go of one belonging you keep only out of fear. Making money decisions with someone you trust, rather than alone at night. Building security in layers, a skill, a habit, a friendship, so that no single loss can carry it all away. At its best, a 2nd-house Pluto knows what is truly valuable, because it has had to find out.
