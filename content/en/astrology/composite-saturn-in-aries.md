Some relationships commit with a speech; this one commits by doing. Composite Saturn in Aries gives the bond's structure, the part that holds it together over the years, the manner of the first sign: direct, self-starting, and in no hurry to ask permission.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is the relationship's frame: what it takes seriously, the responsibilities it carries, the rules it lives by, and the places where it feels slow or tested. [Aries](/astrology/aries/), cardinal fire ruled by Mars, gives that frame a habit of acting first. A pair like this tends to show commitment in deeds rather than declarations: one of you books the removal van while the other is still weighing it up, and the bond counts the van as the promise. The rules it keeps are often rules about independence, the right of each person to act alone, to run their own projects and to have their own say.

Tradition calls Aries Saturn's [fall](/astrology/fall/), the sign opposite its exaltation in Libra, where the planet of caution meets the sign of the first move. In a relationship I read that as a tension to manage, not a flaw: the pair has two speeds, careful and impatient, and has to learn which one a given decision needs. Starting things together is where the lesson sits. Some pairs freeze at the starting line, each waiting for the other to move; others start, stop and start again, as though a beginning could be made perfect by repeating it.

A composite belongs to the relationship rather than to either person, so this Saturn can describe two people who are, on their own, anything but hasty. The midpoint does it. Born in June 1965, one of you has Saturn at about 17° Pisces; born in June 1970, the other has it at about 17° Taurus; halfway between lies 17° Aries, a Saturn neither of you was born with. Two people born between March 1967 and April 1969 both have Saturn in Aries, and so does their composite, as it does for nearly every pair born together between April 1937 and March 1940 or between April 1996 and February 1999. In two million pairs drawn from the births of 1930 to 2009, it came up for about 1 pair in 10: with Pisces, the commonest composite Saturn sign. For a pair of one generation, the sign mostly describes the years you were born in; what makes the composite Saturn yours is its house, which needs both birth times, and its contacts with the composite's other planets.

In one birth chart, [Saturn in Aries](/astrology/saturn-in-aries/) describes a person learning to back their own initiative. Here the bond has to learn it, and each of you may feel that lesson as the other's impatience, or the other's hesitation. Next door, [composite Saturn in Pisces](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-pisces/) builds its structure out of care and shared imagination, and [composite Saturn in Taurus](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-taurus/) out of upkeep and repetition; in Aries, the structure is made of first steps.

The difficulty is usually autonomy turning into separate lives. A relationship that guards each person's right to act alone can end with two people acting alone, side by side, and calling it respect. It helps to make one beginning a shared one on purpose: a project with a date for its first step and a name beside each task. Leadership is the other sore point. Two people who each expect to go first, or who each wait for the other, need a plain rule about who starts what, and this Saturn keeps such rules well once they exist.

Composites aren't part of my readings, so what you're reading is the technique's account of an Aries Saturn, which the astrologers who use it set beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). None of it says how long the two of you will last, or whether you should: Saturn describes how a relationship builds, never whether it's allowed to. A composite also reads two people's charts at once, so it waits for two yeses, not one ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
