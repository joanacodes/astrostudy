Almost everyone meets this square, and the age is written in the birth year: about 23 for someone born at the start of 1975, about 32 for 1985, about 40 for 1995. For about two and a half years, the urge to change things and the pull of an ideal rub against each other.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reforms and experiments; it needs room to test another way. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves edges and longs for something shared and whole. A [square](/astrology/square/) puts them in a friction that asks for adaptation, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the wish to break free and the wish to belong, the plan for a better system and the dream behind it, keep catching on each other.

It can show as disillusion with a movement, a technology or a community you had believed in; as confusion about which change you actually want; or as restlessness inside an ideal that has stopped moving. Uranus tends to assume that disruption brings progress, and Neptune that a shared feeling proves something, and the square tests both. What usually comes out of it is change with continuity: a reform you can explain, carried by people who agree on what it's for.

With Uranus in your 1st, the square often comes while Neptune crosses your 10th or your 4th: your public direction ([Neptune transiting the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-10th-house/)), or your sense of home, blurs just as you feel the need to reinvent yourself. Every house in that sentence hangs on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Uranus square Neptune](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune/), as at the start of 1955, you had this square in your first months; the other square point lies some eighty years of Neptune's travel away.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The square spans about two years and seven months within my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, and never much less than twenty-two; three exact contacts in most passes, five in about a quarter, give it a slow pulse.

Nearly everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets it before 80, a very few twice, and the age climbs with the birth year: in the first months for the start of 1955, about 11 for 1965, 23 for 1975, 32 for 1985, 40½ for 1995 and 47½ for 2005. The two oldest groups in the count meet it late, at about 61 for the start of 1935 and 70 for 1945.

Born between 1990 and 1996, with Uranus and Neptune together in your chart, you meet this square between about 37 and 43, in the same years as your [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) and close to your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/): three outer-planet seasons overlapping.

For someone born at the start of 1975, [transiting Neptune sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-uranus/) came at about 9, this square at about 23 and [transiting Neptune trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-uranus/) at about 36; [transiting Neptune opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-uranus/) follows decades later, and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-uranus/) never.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Test the vision on a small scale before you give it your life: one project, one season, one group.

Ask what a change keeps as well as what it breaks. Under this square, the reforms that last are the ones that know what still works.

Belong without dissolving. You can join something larger and keep the independence that made you want to change it.
