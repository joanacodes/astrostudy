Jupiter sextile Uranus links your wish to grow with your wish for something new. Change tends to open doors for you rather than slam them, and the doors are there to be walked through. It belongs to a few months of births at a time, so your houses are what make it yours.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the search for a larger picture: learning, principles, the sense of what is possible. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the drive to differentiate and reform: new methods, independent thinking, the break that lets something better in. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperation: the two can set each other going, provided someone engages.

In plain words, novelty tends to be useful to you. A new technology, a move, an unusual course or an unexpected acquaintance can widen your life rather than unsettle it, and you often sense which changes are worth making. Nothing forces the issue: the sextile makes offers, and you decide which of them deserve a yes.

Opportunity requires engagement. Left alone, this aspect can become a fondness for new ideas that never leave the page; used, it becomes a knack for growing by adapting early.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Within 6°, it concerns about 1 person in 17 born from 1930 to 2009, in short spells: January to May 1930; February to April 1939; July to September 1943; May 1952 to March 1953; December 1956 to March 1957 and August to October 1957; June to August 1966 and January to April 1967; March to April 1971 and November 1971 to January 1972; August to October 1980 and March to July 1981; April 1985 to February 1986; October to December 1994; March to May 1999; and February to December 2008.

Each spell's births share it, so it reads first as a cohort's openness to the new; where it sits among your houses, and what it touches among your personal planets, make it particular to you ([generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/)). With Jupiter in the 9th and Uranus in the 11th, for instance, study or travel might open up through friends and networks; with Jupiter in the 5th and Uranus in the 7th, creative growth might come through an unconventional partnership. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The rest of the Jupiter–Uranus family:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus/):** the meeting itself, about every fourteen years, as in 1954–55 or 1982–83.
- **[Jupiter square Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-square-uranus/):** the friction version, for those born in 1951–52 or 1986–87, among others.
- **[Jupiter trine Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-trine-uranus/):** an easier, wider channel, for the births of 1945–46 or 2005–06, for example.
- **[Jupiter opposite Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-uranus/):** a polarity between belief and change, for those born in 1933–34, 1962–63 or 2003–04.

[Jupiter sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-uranus-synastry/) links one person's sense of possibility to the other's taste for the new: a good combination for travel, study or projects that need fresh thinking.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-jupiter/) is slow: needing some seven years per sign, Uranus stays near the sextile for many months, often in three passes, a long stretch in which a change of direction can widen your world. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-uranus/) passes in days or weeks, and returns every few years.

Weather, not a forecast: an open window rather than an event, and the choice to climb through it is yours.
