Twice in Uranus's round, about twenty years before it reaches your Moon and about twenty years after, the planet of change squares the place where you look for rest. For about twenty months each time, comfort and freedom want different things from the same evenings.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) keeps you steady: attachment, habit, the felt sense of being safe, and the speed at which you respond when things change. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) liberates and reforms, and wants room to try another arrangement. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, joins two functions through the kind of friction that forces adaptation, so the very place you go to recover becomes the place that asks for change.

The restlessness tends to be felt at home rather than out in the world. The flat feels small, the routine that got you through last year feels like a rut, and a quiet evening can bring on the urge to rearrange something. Feelings arrive fast and leave fast; you may want closeness and room within the same hour, and so may the people you live with.

I read it as a redesign. Very little of what this square unsettles needs throwing out: it needs different hours, a different room, a different way of being looked after. If the unsettled feeling stops being a season and becomes every night, talk to someone trained to help ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)).

With your Moon in the 2nd and Uranus crossing your 5th ([Uranus transiting the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-5th-house/)), the comfort you've built from what's steady and owned meets an appetite for play, risk and surprise. Those houses and the Moon's exact degree all come from your birth time; without it I can't even time the square, so I don't read it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The usual length, with my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after, is about twenty months; a few passes take only eleven. Three exact crossings are what most people get, in more than nine passes out of ten, which gives the square a beginning, a middle and an end.

One square comes about twenty years before Uranus crosses your Moon ([transiting Uranus conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-moon/)) and the other about twenty years after, so some 42 years separate them, with [transiting Uranus opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-moon/) halfway. The friendlier angles of the round, [transiting Uranus sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-moon/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-moon/), sit about seven years either side of each square. Most people have lived through both squares by 80; the rest, through one.

Until 2033, while Uranus crosses Gemini, the Moons this [transit](/astrology/transits/) reaches are in Virgo and Pisces. Born with [Moon square Uranus](/astrology/moon-square-uranus/), you know this friction from the inside; the transit is the same rub with a start date and an end date.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change the arrangement, not the attachment. A different routine with the same people often answers this square better than a different life.

Protect one quiet hour a day. The square makes rest feel restless, and a small, fixed, unbroken hour gives your feelings somewhere to land.

Name the mood before you act on it. Under this square a feeling can arrive with an urgent plan attached; saying what you feel, out loud, usually separates the two.
