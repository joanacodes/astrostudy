No telescope will find the Part of the Father. It's a degree worked out from your chart and set aside for one subject, and my readings do without it, even when your father is what we're talking about.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

A lot measures the distance between two points of a chart and counts it again from a third, nearly always the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), so that a relationship in the sky becomes a place in your chart ([doctrine of the lots](/astrology/doctrine-of-the-lots/)). The best known, the [Part of Fortune](/astrology/part-of-fortune/), concerns your circumstances. Others were made for people: a partner in the [Part of Marriage](/astrology/part-of-marriage/), offspring in the [Part of Children](/astrology/part-of-children/), and each parent, the father here and the [Part of the Mother](/astrology/part-of-the-mother/) beside it.

Not every astrologer thought a father needed a lot. Ptolemy, in the 2nd century, gave the parents a chapter of their own in his *Tetrabiblos* (book III, chapter 4), placed first among the matters that precede a birth, since your parents were there before you were. Yet in the same book he set aside any reasoning "by means of lots and numbers of which no reasonable explanation can be given" (book III, chapter 3). The Part of the Father belongs to the other side of that quarrel: the astrologers who went on building a lot for each question they put to a chart, until there were dozens.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Like every lot, it's built from two points of the chart and the Ascendant, and for many lots the two points change places when the birth happens at night ([sect: day and night charts](/astrology/sect-day-and-night-charts/)). Two things follow, whichever points are used:

- **The time decides the degree.** Every four minutes of birth time carry the Ascendant, and the lot with it, about a degree further; get the hour wrong and the lot can land in another sign, so [without an exact birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides) there's no lot to speak of.
- **Day or night decides the version.** Born close to sunrise or sunset, a few minutes settle which of the two counts applies.

You won't find a formula on this page. I only print a rule I've been able to check against its source, and I haven't managed it for this one; a lot built from the wrong two points is simply another degree. If a program offers you a Part of the Father, its documentation should name the two points; check them, and the night rule, before you trust the degree.

The older books also gave the father a house, though they never settled whether the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) belonged to the father or the mother, and horary astrologers count on from a parent's house to ask about that parent's world ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)). Even for those who used it, the lot was one pointer among several, never the whole answer.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I don't calculate this lot. The system behind my readings draws two lots, Fortune and Spirit, and uses them for timing, so the only fathers in my readings are the ones you bring into the conversation.

Fathers still come up, often, and when one does I read the adult in front of me. Your chart describes your life, so what it can show is fathering as you lived it: what was expected of you, how protection and authority felt, what you took away and what you're still putting down. For that I look at your own placements: the 4th house for roots, [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) for rules and responsibility, the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) for how you've come to recognise yourself.

Three lines I keep:

- **His life is in his chart.** A father has a birth chart of his own, and only that chart, read with his agreement, says anything about him ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
- **Adults only.** I read the grown-up's side of the story. I don't read children's charts, or a child's bond with a parent from either side ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).
- **No forecasts about him.** Nothing in your chart can say whether he'll fall ill, how long he'll live or whether the two of you will make peace ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The Part of the Father shows what your father is like.”** It's a degree in your chart, not in his. At most it speaks of fathering as part of your own life.
- **“It can tell you who your father is.”** No chart can identify a person. Questions of parentage belong to records, to the people who know, and to a DNA test.
- **“A hard contact to it means a bad father.”** A chart passes no verdict on anyone, least of all on someone who isn't there to answer.
- **“If you never knew your father, the lot is empty.”** Every chart has this degree, whatever your family looked like: it's arithmetic, and it doesn't know who was there. That's one more reason I read the life you've lived rather than a point.
