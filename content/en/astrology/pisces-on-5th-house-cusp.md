Pisces on the 5th house cusp gives your pleasures to the sign of imagination and devotion. What you do for joy tends to be something you can lose yourself in, and when you fall for someone, you often fall for everything they might become as well.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is the part of life you don't have to justify; the old books called it Good Fortune and gave Venus its joy there ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) is the sign where Venus is exalted, so this cusp brings the favourite sign of the planet of pleasure into its favourite house. Joy here is immersion: a novel finished at three in the morning, an afternoon in a dark cinema, a song played until it's worn through, a part in a play that lets you be someone else for a while. You may make things more easily for someone else than for yourself, and a present you made by hand can mean more to you than anything bought. In love, you tend to give generously and imagine generously, and the work is to see the person in front of you as well as the one you dreamed of.

I read both rulers here: Neptune dreams your pleasures up, Jupiter enlarges them. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows where romance and art take on a haze of longing, and where you may idealise; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where enjoyment grows big-hearted, and where a hobby becomes a kind of faith. When Libra rises with this cusp, in nearly half the London charts and 3 in 10 at New York, [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your chart, so the planet exalted in Pisces governs you while Pisces governs your pleasures. Follow the two rulers by house to see where your joy spills over, as ruler of the 5th in the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Births with this cusp run at about 1 in 11 at London and 1 in 12 at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. The 5th is narrow in these charts, about 24° to 28° at London, and Leo, the 5th's own natural sign, sits on the Midheaven in about 6 charts in 7: what you do for joy and what you're known for are close neighbours, and the Sun rules your public life. Aquarius usually holds the IC just below, putting Uranus and Saturn in charge of home. In about 1 London chart in 12, Pisces runs on to the 6th cusp as well ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), and play and daily work answer to the same two planets. At London, Pisces is also among the signs most often left out altogether, intercepted in about 1 chart in 6 ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Children belonged to the 5th in the old books. Whether or when anyone has a child is beyond any chart ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and the charts I read belong to adults ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)); what this cusp describes is your own play, love and making. The time of birth places it: a little later and Pisces would sit on your IC ([Pisces on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/)), a little earlier on the 6th ([Pisces on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/)), and a guess at the hour won't settle which ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
