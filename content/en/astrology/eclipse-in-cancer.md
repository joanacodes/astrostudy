Three times in my list, an eclipse falls on 21 June in the first degree of Cancer: in 2001, 2020 and 2039. Cancer is the sign of home and care, and no sign has more solar eclipses between 2000 and 2040.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) is cardinal water, ruled by the Moon: protection, belonging, the people and places you look after. Its eclipses fall on the Cancer–Capricorn axis, home and the world outside, care and duty, and they alternate with Capricorn’s as the Moon’s nodes move. A [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) in Cancer brings the Sun and Moon together in the sign, as on 21 June 2020 at 0°21′; a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) puts the full Moon in Cancer, its own sign, with the Sun in Capricorn, as on 31 December 2028 at 10°34′.

The AGS rule I follow keeps this precise: an eclipse counts within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of the axis, and then I read it through that point and its house. A penumbral lunar eclipse, like that of 10 January 2020 at 19°53′ Cancer, weighs less. Natal nodes are points like any other here, and I look at both, mean and true. What I describe is emphasis, never an omen.

Nineteen from 2000 to 2040:

- **2000–2001:** 1 July 2000, partial solar, 10°14′ · 9 January 2001, total lunar, 19°36′ · 21 June 2001, total solar, 0°10′ · 30 December 2001, penumbral lunar, 8°41′
- **2009–2011:** 22 July 2009, total solar, 29°26′ · 31 December 2009, partial lunar, 10°21′ · 11 July 2010, total solar, 19°23′ · 1 July 2011, partial solar, 9°11′
- **2018–2020:** 13 July 2018, partial solar, 20°41′ · 2 July 2019, total solar, 10°37′ · 10 January 2020, penumbral lunar, 19°53′ · 21 June 2020, annular solar, 0°21′
- **2028–2029:** 12 January 2028, partial lunar, 21°33′ · 22 July 2028, total solar, 29°50′ · 31 December 2028, total lunar, 10°34′ · 11 July 2029, partial solar, 19°37′
- **2037–2039:** 13 July 2037, total solar, 21°04′ · 2 July 2038, annular solar, 10°47′ · 21 June 2039, annular solar, 0°12′

The last was the annular eclipse of 21 June 2020; the next, the partial lunar eclipse of 12 January 2028. The nineteen-year echoes are strong in this sign: 22 July 2009 at 29°26′ and 22 July 2028 at 29°50′, 31 December 2009 and 31 December 2028, and the June eclipses of the first degree. Next door are the eclipses of Gemini and of Leo ([Eclipse in Gemini](/astrology/eclipse-in-gemini/), [Eclipse in Leo](/astrology/eclipse-in-leo/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) is what puts Cancer on one of your houses; the houses come from it, and without it I don’t read the chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, read a line only if a Cancer eclipse lands on a natal point in that house, remembering that the Capricorn degrees opposite reach the house across.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** Cancer rising: the protective way you meet people; how much of your care shows on the surface.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/cancer-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what makes you feel secure; the things you keep close for the safety they give.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** family talk and close neighbours; a message that brings someone nearer.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** Cancer in its natural house: home, family, roots; who and what you call home.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/):** tender pleasures and work made from the heart. Tradition gave this house children; this site reads no child’s chart, and I read an adult’s joys here ([children’s charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).
6. **[6th house](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/):** care in daily work; the people you look after through your job.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Cancer on the Descendant, with Capricorn rising: a partner who feels like home; who looks after whom.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/):** trust of the heart; the family side of what’s held in common.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/):** belonging far away; a home found in another country or another faith.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a public role that cares; your work as a kind of looking-after.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends as family; a circle that shelters you.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/cancer-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private memory; old family feeling, tended quietly.
