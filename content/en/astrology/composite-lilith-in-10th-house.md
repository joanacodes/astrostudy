Two people who are discreet at work can find that, the moment they do something together in public, it gets talked about, and is done in a way nobody quite approved in advance. With composite Lilith in the 10th house, the relationship's untamed side lives where everyone can see it: in its public life.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Vocation, standing and reputation belong to [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), the angular house at the top of a chart, whose natural sign is Capricorn. It opens at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), where the zodiac crosses the meridian, which is not quite the point overhead. In a relationship's own chart, [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the 10th is the pair's standing in the world and what it builds there. Since I read [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) as what a chart won't have tamed, a Lilith here gives the pair a public face that refuses to be polished into what others expect. The pair may run a business, a cause or a household that others watch with a mixture of admiration and disapproval; it may decline the public rituals of coupledom altogether; it rarely asks permission.

That edge belongs to the bond, not to either person's career. Two private people can make a relationship with a public streak, and each may be surprised at how visible the two of you become when you act together. What it hides, sometimes, is how much it cares what people think; what it won't tame is its way of doing things in front of them.

Here the two ways of drawing composite houses meet. Whether the houses come from the midpoints of your two charts' cusps or are built from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, the 10th begins at the same degree: halfway between your two Midheavens. So a Lilith just above or below it falls on the same side whichever method you choose, as long as both birth times are right. They matter a great deal: a Midheaven moves about a degree every four minutes on average ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A person's hidden side going public is what [Lilith in the 10th house](/astrology/lilith-in-10th-house/) describes in one chart, and [their Lilith in your 10th house](/astrology/lilith-in-10th-house-synastry/) is someone else's refusal to bow meeting your ambitions. The composite's 10th is the pair's public life, nobody else's. One house back, [composite Lilith in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-9th-house/) keeps its convictions to itself; one house on, [composite Lilith in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-11th-house/) takes its refusal among friends.

The hard part is being judged in public for being yourselves, and the temptation either to provoke or to disappear. A pair like this can spend energy fighting its reputation, or can split its life into a tidy public version and a real private one. It helps to decide together what you show the world, and then to let the work you do, not the commentary on it, speak for the bond.

Composites aren't something I draw: what I describe is how the technique's users read this Lilith, together with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It foretells no success, scandal or ending. And since it concerns how two people stand before the world, both of them should agree to it being drawn ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
