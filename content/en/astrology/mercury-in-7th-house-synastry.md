Their Mercury in your 7th house sits across the table from you. This is the overlay of dialogue between equals: two minds that think best in conversation with each other, and sometimes in argument.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) belongs to the person facing you: partners, close collaborators, anyone you meet one to one, and, in the old books, the rival you can see coming. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) reasons, negotiates and exchanges. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their thinking arrives as a voice opposite yours.

For you, they can be the person you think things through with. You bring half an idea and leave with a whole one; you test a decision against their questions; you discover that disagreeing with them clarifies what you actually believe. Agreements between you, from who cooks to who signs what, tend to be talked out rather than assumed.

For them, you're the natural other side of the conversation. They may feel they can reason with you as with few people, and your replies sharpen their arguments. The 7th is angular, and [Libra](/astrology/libra/), its natural sign, is ruled by Venus: fairness, balance, the pleasure of company.

Early in your 7th, their Mercury is also conjunct your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the degree that was setting in the west when you were born, and the contact strengthens everything above.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Dialogue can harden into debate. With two minds this keen on the exchange, a conversation can turn into a contest someone has to win, and a decision can stall while both sides are weighed one more time. You may feel you have to defend every choice to them.

From their side, the risk is a mirror that only talks back. If you answer each idea with its opposite, they may feel you never simply agree; if you defer, they may feel alone in the conversation they wanted to share.

Tradition gave the 7th contracts and open enemies. This overlay describes how the two of you negotiate, not how a contract or a dispute between you will end: I don't forecast legal outcomes for anyone.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take turns, and let some questions stay open. Write down what you agree, especially where money or work is involved, and agree how you'll disagree before you need to. In a couple, a weekly talk where each of you brings one subject keeps things from piling up; between friends, the one you argue with for hours and still ring the next day; between colleagues or business partners, clear roles in a negotiation and a contract read by both; between adult siblings deciding something together, one meeting with everything on the table.

Seen from their side, this is your Mercury in their 7th house: you become the voice across their table. The houses beside it shift the subject. [Their Mercury in your 6th house](/astrology/mercury-in-6th-house-synastry/) talks about the work in hand, and [their Mercury in your 8th house](/astrology/mercury-in-8th-house-synastry/) about what you share and owe.

The Descendant is simply the far end of your horizon, opposite the Ascendant, so it shifts as fast as the Ascendant does, and a small change in your birth time can carry a Mercury near it from one house to the next, in Placidus, the house system I use, as in the other quadrant systems. Their Mercury needs their time only when it sits right on that line; I need both before I read the two of you ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). This is the house of partners, and I read it only with both partners' knowledge ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
