Some people find their circle at school or at work. With Uranus in the 11th house, the circle is usually chosen later and on purpose: friends who share a hope, groups built around an idea, people who let you stay entirely yourself.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is friends, groups and hopes: the people you build a future with, and the future you'd like to build. Succedent, it was the Good Spirit of the Hellenistic astrologers, who placed Jupiter's joy in it. Its natural sign is Aquarius, which modern tropical astrology gives to [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) and the tradition to Saturn, so Uranus here sits in the house that echoes its own sign. Dignities belong to signs rather than houses, but the fit is easy to feel: innovation, autonomy and systems thinking at work in your friendships and causes.

Placidus, my house system, shows Uranus climbing in the east when you were born, a few hours after rising. That position comes from the hour, not the year, so your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) is what puts Uranus in this house rather than the 10th or the 12th. [Uranus in the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-in-10th-house/) takes the same independence into public life, and [Uranus in the 12th house](/astrology/uranus-in-12th-house/) into private.

Because Uranus's sign is shared across about seven years of births, the 11th house adds something the sign can't: a personal stake in the future your generation imagines. You may be the friend who turns a shared hope into a plan.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, friendship comes first: you may need a partner who is also a companion in your causes and your circle, and who doesn't need to be your whole world. You may keep friends from very different groups, and want a relationship that fits around them rather than replacing them. The 5th house opposite is romance; the 11th reminds this placement that the best romance often grows out of shared hopes. Jealousy of your friendships is the thing most likely to make you feel caged.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, Uranus in the 11th house thrives in networks, collectives, charities, campaigns, online communities, research groups, anywhere people pool ideas for a common future. You may be the one who connects people who would never have met otherwise. Committees that resist change frustrate you, and so do groups that demand conformity as the price of belonging. Work feels most meaningful when it serves something larger than the job. You may also be good at designing how a group works, its rules, its meetings, its way of deciding, so that more voices count.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard part is belonging without losing yourself. You may join groups enthusiastically and leave them abruptly when they start to feel like a mould, or keep friendships at a comfortable distance. Ideals can also crowd out the people who hold them, and a crowd of acquaintances can feel easier than one close friend who knows you well. Autonomy is not isolation: a circle can welcome your difference if you let it see you.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose a few friendships to invest in over years, not only the causes that bring you together. Notice when you're leaving a group because it's wrong and when it's simply because it has become familiar. Saturn, the traditional ruler of Aquarius, deserves a look next to Uranus. Anyone born from 1995 to 2003 has Uranus in Aquarius as well, so sign and house say the same thing ([Uranus in Aquarius](/astrology/uranus-in-aquarius/)). Fitting house or not, [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) made no exception: I checked Uranus like every placement, it wasn't one of the three that held far more often, and about a third held in total.
