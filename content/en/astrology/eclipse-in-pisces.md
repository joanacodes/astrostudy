Pisces closes the zodiac, and two of its eclipses sit right at the end of it: total solar eclipses at 29°27′ Pisces on 20 March 2015 and at 29°52′ on 20 March 2034, nineteen years apart to the day. Pisces softens edges, and its eclipses fall on the axis it shares with Virgo.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) is mutable water with two rulers, Jupiter in the tradition and Neptune in modern tropical astrology, which I read together. It is the sign of compassion, imagination and letting go, and its eclipses fall on the Pisces–Virgo axis, the whole and the detail, faith and method, alternating with Virgo’s as the Moon’s nodes move. In 2016 the Sun and Moon met in Pisces for a [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) on 9 March, at 18°55′; on 7 September 2025 a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) caught the full Moon at 15°24′ Pisces, with the Sun in Virgo.

I count an eclipse only by the AGS rule: within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of the axis, read through that point and its house, with a penumbral lunar eclipse weighing less. If your natal nodes are involved, I look at the mean node and at the true one. That gives emphasis, never an omen.

Thirteen from 2000 to 2040:

- **2006–2007:** 7 September 2006, partial lunar, 15°05′ · 19 March 2007, partial solar, 28°06′ · 28 August 2007, total lunar, 4°46′
- **2015–2017:** 20 March 2015, total solar, 29°27′ · 9 March 2016, total solar, 18°55′ · 16 September 2016, penumbral lunar, 24°13′ · 26 February 2017, annular solar, 8°11′
- **2024–2026:** 18 September 2024, partial lunar, 25°46′ · 7 September 2025, total lunar, 15°24′ · 28 August 2026, partial lunar, 4°51′
- **2034–2036:** 20 March 2034, total solar, 29°52′ · 9 March 2035, annular solar, 19°12′ · 27 February 2036, partial solar, 8°09′

The most recent is the partial lunar eclipse of 28 August 2026, nineteen years to the day after a total one at 4°46′; the next is the total solar eclipse of 20 March 2034. For the eclipses just before and after it in the zodiac, go to Aquarius and Aries ([Eclipse in Aquarius](/astrology/eclipse-in-aquarius/), [Eclipse in Aries](/astrology/eclipse-in-aries/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

I can only place Pisces in one of your houses with [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); the houses are built on it, and without it I don’t read a chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, each line holds when a Pisces eclipse meets a natal point in that house; the Virgo degrees opposite reach the house across.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/pisces-rising/):** Pisces rising: a gentle, receptive presence; how much of other people’s moods you take on.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/pisces-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you give away; generosity weighed with some care for yourself.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/pisces-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** intuitive words; something sensed before it was said.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a refuge; the people you have taken in.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/pisces-on-5th-house-cusp/):** imagination and romance; a creative dream given shape.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/):** caring work; service that asks a great deal of you.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Pisces on the Descendant, with Virgo rising: compassion in partnership; seeing a partner as they are.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/):** deep trust; what merges, and what stays your own.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/):** faith and far shores; a spiritual path or a journey of meaning.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a calling to help or inspire; your work as a form of service.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/pisces-on-11th-house-cusp/):** compassionate friendships; a group that shares an ideal.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/pisces-on-12th-house-cusp/):** Pisces in its natural [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/): solitude, retreat, inner life. Hidden troubles and confinement were this house’s lot in the tradition; an eclipse here, for me, underlines rest and reflection, and a person’s state of mind is never something I read from the sky ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).
