Uranus reaches your natal Moon once in a life at most, and usually passes over it twice more before moving on. For about twenty months, what makes you feel safe has to make room for what makes you feel free.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feeling and need: the habits that steady you, the comfort you reach for, the people and places that feel like home. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) brings experiment and differentiation, the wish for room to live by arrangements of your own. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions for a while, so here the urge to change doesn't arrive from outside. It starts in what you need.

In practice, comforts you relied on can start to feel tight. A routine that held you for years begins to look like a habit kept for its own sake; needs you'd never named, for solitude, for a different kind of company, for another rhythm to the week, become hard to ignore. Moods can turn faster than usual. None of this means something is going wrong: the Moon is adapting, which is one of the things it's for.

The question of where and how you live often comes up, as a feeling long before it's a plan, and I don't predict a move. If the restlessness turns into anxiety that won't ease, see your doctor or a therapist; a chart can describe a season, never treat one ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

The house holding your Moon is the house Uranus is crossing. With the Moon in your 4th, the conjunction comes somewhere in Uranus's years there ([Uranus transiting the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-4th-house/)), and the arrangement of home and family is what gets rethought. The Moon also moves about 13° a day, so an unknown birth time leaves its degree uncertain across most of that span: about three years of Uranus's travel. Without the time I can't say when this [transit](/astrology/transits/) comes, and I don't read it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured from 1½° before exact to 1° after, my orbs for timing, this conjunction runs to about twenty months, some twenty new moons; the shortest passes take about eleven. Three exact crossings are usual, because Uranus’s yearly retrograde takes it back over the degree before it moves on for good; only about one pass in fourteen has a single one.

Uranus’s other contacts with your Moon fall at set distances from this one. Fourteen years or so either side lie the sextiles ([transiting Uranus sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-moon/)); about twenty years either side, the squares ([transiting Uranus square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-moon/)); about 28, the trines ([transiting Uranus trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-moon/)). The opposition ([transiting Uranus opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-moon/)) is the farthest point, some 42 years away.

To find your age for it, count forwards through the zodiac from your natal Uranus to your Moon, allowing about seven years per sign. About 1 person in 33 born between 1930 and 2009 never meets it before 80, mostly people whose Moon sat a little behind Uranus at birth, so that Uranus had only just passed it. Born with [Moon conjunct Uranus](/astrology/moon-conjunct-uranus/) and the Moon slightly ahead, you met this transit in your first years instead.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep one anchor. Change what needs changing, but leave one ritual, a meal, a walk, a weekly call, exactly as it was, so the rest has something to move around.

Try new comforts in small doses. A week somewhere different, a new evening routine, another way of spending time with the people you love: test before you rebuild.

Tell the people you live with what you need before you take it. Said early, a wish for more room is a request; taken suddenly, it can feel to them like a door closing.
