I'd rather you checked me than trusted me. A careful practice is visible from the outside, and so is a scam: not in what someone claims to see, but in what they ask of you. Everything below can be checked before you pay anyone, me included. [Questions to ask an astrologer](/astrology/questions-to-ask-an-astrologer/) gives you the questions to put; this page is about reading the answers.

## Step by step {#step-by-step}

1. **Start with what they refuse.** A careful astrologer tells you what they won't read before you've asked. My list is on [How I read](/how-i-read/): health diagnoses, death, pregnancy, ongoing legal proceedings, investments, curses to lift, and anyone under 18. A list like that shows someone has thought about the harm a reading can do. If an astrologer will take any question from anyone, there's nothing they've decided not to sell you.
2. **Look at what they ask for.** Your date, time and place of birth, with a reason for the time. Your word that you're 18 or over. For a synastry, the other person's knowledge, and for a colleague, their agreement as well ([Preparing a synastry together](/astrology/preparing-a-synastry-together/)). An astrologer who's happy to read a partner's chart in secret, or a child's, is careless with what isn't theirs, and has no reason to be more careful with what's yours.
3. **Check what's promised.** The honest answer is: no outcome. My readings are offered for reflection and entertainment, and no outcome is promised. A guarantee that someone will come back, a case be won or money arrive is a sales line, because no chart can promise any of it; the rituals sold to deliver it are [Paid “spiritual work”](/astrology/paid-spiritual-work/).
4. **Watch the money.** Know the price, and what you'll receive for it, before you pay, and expect both to stay where they were. Pressure to pay more is the sign: for the rest of the message, a deeper reading, a protection, a ritual. So is a reading sold by the minute that never quite gets to the point ([Fake psychics](/astrology/fake-psychics/)).
5. **Notice what they do with fear.** A careful reading can be hard to hear, but it never ends with a danger only the reader can remove. I hold to this: there are no bad charts and no doomed placements. A dark warning followed by a price is a technique, not a reading.
6. **Notice what they ask you to hide.** Being told to keep it from your partner, your family or your friends protects the seller, never you. A careful astrologer loses nothing if you show the reading to everyone you know, so show it to someone.
7. **Walk away from curse removal, every time.** No chart shows a curse, and no one can lift one for a fee. It's one of the subjects I refuse outright, together with any paid "work" to free you from one, because that offer is the very definition of a scam ([Curse removal scams](/astrology/curse-removal-scams/)).
8. **If you've already paid, stop there.** Not one more payment, whether it's to finish the work, undo it or protect you from it. Keep every message, receipt and bank record, tell someone you trust, ask your bank what can be done, and report it: [How to report a scam](/astrology/how-to-report-a-scam/) goes through how.

## Common mistakes {#common-mistakes}

- **Being won over by one striking line.** A detail that lands can feel like proof. But general statements feel personal when they're addressed to you ([The Barnum (Forer) effect](/astrology/barnum-forer-effect/)), and an attentive listener learns a great deal from how you answer ([Cold reading](/astrology/cold-reading/)). Judge the method, not the moment.
- **Treating a gift as a qualification.** I don't claim one: there's nothing in a reading you couldn't learn yourself, with time. A gift nobody can check is no reason to pay.
- **Paying once more to make it stop.** No fee lifts a curse, so no payment is ever the last one asked for. Stopping is the one step that ends the payments.
- **Keeping quiet out of embarrassment.** These offers can find anyone at a low moment, after a death, a break-up or a diagnosis, and being reached at such a moment says nothing about anyone's intelligence. Telling one person you trust is the quickest way back to solid ground.
- **Blaming the person who was taken in.** Whether it's you or someone you love, the fault lies with the method and with whoever used it. Shame keeps people paying in silence; kindness gets them talking.
