Sun sextile Lilith links the two a sixth of the zodiac apart, close enough to work together. It describes an instinct for your own terms that your sense of purpose can draw on, and that tends to stay quiet until you invite it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your direction: the purpose you organise yourself around, and the self you express through it. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the mean far point of the Moon's orbit, is read by astrologers in very different ways. In my reading, it's the refusing part of you: instinct, autonomy, what was pushed out of sight, and a clear sense of your own boundaries. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers their connection as an opportunity: the link is there, and it works when you use it.

In plain words, you have access to a streak of independence that doesn't need a fight to exist. When your purpose calls for it, you can say no without apologising, trust a gut feeling over a polite consensus, or take a path you'd been told wasn't for you. Without that invitation, the streak tends to stay in the background, a private voice you hear and don't always follow. The opportunity is real, but potential isn't a promise: it asks to be taken up. Readings of Lilith differ more than those of almost any other point, so I always say plainly which one is mine ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as quiet self-possession: knowing your own terms and keeping them without making a scene. Usually two signs separate them, of complementary elements. With Lilith in Scorpio, a Sun in Virgo or Capricorn can put deep, private wants to work in practical ways, rather than keeping them out of sight. Its houses name the two areas of life the opening joins.

About 1 person in 11 is born within 8° of a Sun–Lilith sextile, whose two dates in each 13½-month round fall a little over two months before and after the Sun meets Lilith. The closer to 60°, the easier your own terms are to reach. The count uses mean Lilith; the true one wobbles enough that its sextiles to your Sun can come and go.

The pair's other aspects give the refused part a different place:

- **[Sun conjunct Lilith](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith/):** nothing to open; it sits at the centre and shows whether invited or not.
- **[Sun square Lilith](/astrology/sun-square-lilith/):** the same instinct as friction, pushing from the side when you'd rather keep the peace.
- **[Sun trine Lilith](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith/):** the easier of the two soft links; your own terms come naturally, unasked.
- **[Sun opposite Lilith](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith/):** the refused part is met across the room, in other people first.

Between two charts, [Sun sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith-synastry/) lets one person's purpose meet the other's independence as a friendly offer: each can encourage the other to be less polite and more true.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Mean Lilith crosses a sign in about nine months, so its sextile to your Sun comes and goes within a few weeks, a little under three of them close to exact. And twice a year the Sun itself sextiles your natal Lilith, briefly, for a day or so. Weather, not a forecast: short openings in which your own terms are easier to say out loud, and nothing more is promised.
