Change, for this pair, looks like a better kitchen rota. Composite Uranus in Virgo describes a relationship that reforms itself through method and daily practice, and it is one of the places where two ways of drawing a relationship chart nearly agree.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the function of reform and independence; in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) it is the relationship's own appetite for a better arrangement. [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), mutable earth ruled by [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), turns that appetite to method, routine and use. With no dignity for Uranus in Virgo, the ruler does more of the work: composite Mercury shows how the pair thinks a problem through before taking it apart.

As a third thing, the bond's freedom is practical. It invents its own way of dividing the work of a life: who cooks on which days, how the paperwork is shared, which tasks rotate and which stay with whoever does them best, and it is glad to drop a habit the moment a better one appears. It reforms by small improvements rather than grand gestures, and its independence shows in the details other couples take for granted. [Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/uranus-in-virgo/) marks a generation that changed things through work and tools; in a composite, the method is shared, and so is the pleasure of getting it right.

About 1 pair in 12 of the two million random pairs in my count has it, and Uranus crossed Virgo only once in their birth years, from November 1961 to June 1969, so couples of the same age who share it are in their late fifties or early sixties; the next Virgo generation starts in October 2045. With an age gap, the composite settles near where Uranus stood halfway between the births. A pair born in September 1958 and September 1972, with Uranus at about 14° Leo and 16° Libra, gets about 15° Virgo, and [the Davison chart](/astrology/davison-chart/), cast for September 1965, halfway between them, puts Uranus at about 15° Virgo too. For a planet this slow, the two relationship charts usually agree within a few degrees, until the births are four decades apart.

The trap is fixing what isn't broken. A relationship that enjoys improving can take apart an arrangement before it understands why the old one worked, and one of you may hear each new system as a comment on how you were doing it. Change one thing at a time, try it for a month, and keep what earned its place. A composite in Virgo describes how a shared life is run, not anyone's health, and it predicts nothing about either of you.

With a generation sharing the sign, the house is the pair's own part, and it needs both birth times: [composite Uranus in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-6th-house/), Virgo's natural house, puts the reform into routines and shared work. [Composite Uranus in Leo](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-leo/) wants its originality seen; [composite Uranus in Libra](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-libra/) renegotiates the terms of the bond. The composite is a technique I explain rather than practise. It belongs beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and is drawn only once both people have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
