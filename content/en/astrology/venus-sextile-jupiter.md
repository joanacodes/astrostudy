Venus sextile Jupiter is a friendly angle between the planet of what you enjoy and the planet of what helps you grow. It doesn't hand out good fortune; it leaves doors ajar, and the pleasure is in walking through them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs value and attraction: what you like, whom you like, what feels fair. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens: it seeks meaning, learns, and makes room for more. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the two through opportunity and cooperation: each can wake the other, as long as you take part.

In plain words, your pleasures tend to lead somewhere. A class you take for fun becomes a field of interest; a friend's invitation becomes a trip that changes your view; a taste for something beautiful becomes knowing why it's beautiful. Generosity runs the same road the other way: being open-handed with others tends to widen your own world too. None of this happens by itself. This sextile waits for a yes, and specifically a yes to the slightly larger version of what you already enjoy.

Jupiter isn't luck in my reading: an opening isn't a promise, and this aspect is at its best when you treat it as an invitation to learn.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The two signs are usually two apart, one fire and one air or one earth and one water, so taste and growth come at things from neighbouring temperaments. With Venus in Libra and Jupiter in Sagittarius, Jupiter's own sign, fairness and a wider view feed each other: you warm to people who think big, and they value your sense of balance. With Venus in Taurus and Jupiter in Cancer, where Jupiter is exalted, comfort and care grow together. The houses are often two apart: Venus in the 5th with Jupiter in the 7th might let play and pleasure open the way to partnership.

About 1 person in 14 is born with Venus and Jupiter within 6° of a sextile. Venus is the one moving, weeks to a sign against Jupiter's year, so the aspect depends on your birth date, not on Jupiter's sign alone. Within a degree or two, saying yes to growth is part of your style; near 6°, you have to notice the door first.

Taste and growth strike other bargains at the pair's other angles:

- **[Venus conjunct Jupiter](/astrology/venus-conjunct-jupiter/):** the two benefics in one sign; generosity of taste, with "how much?" as its only question.
- **[Venus square Jupiter](/astrology/venus-square-jupiter/):** liking and enlarging at cross purposes; promises that learn their true size.
- **[Venus trine Jupiter](/astrology/venus-trine-jupiter/):** taste and meaning in one element, flowing so easily they're easy to take for granted.
- **[Venus opposite Jupiter](/astrology/venus-opposite-jupiter/):** pleasure close at hand and meaning far off, at two ends of the chart, learning to take turns.

Between two charts, [Venus sextile Jupiter in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-jupiter-synastry/) joins one person's taste to the other's openness: the friend who introduces you to a world, and enjoys your company in it. Who opens the world and who enjoys it can switch over the years; I read both charts, and only when both people have agreed to a reading ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-venus/) comes about two years and again about ten years after each pass of Jupiter over your Venus, and lasts for weeks, longer when Jupiter's yearly retrograde brings it back: a season when saying yes to something you'd enjoy tends to widen your world.

Weather, not a forecast: a door ajar, and the choice of walking through it stays yours.
