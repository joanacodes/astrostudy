A sextile to the Midheaven is a quiet sort of invitation, a door into your public direction that opens if you push it. It is measured from the degree at the top of your birth chart, which only a recorded time and place can give.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) frames how your capacities become visible: roles, responsibilities, contributions. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the experience of living with a limit, together with the wish to pass on what it taught you. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) chances come to use that experience in public: an invitation to mentor someone, to write or speak about a difficulty you know well, to take on a role in which your history counts as an asset rather than something to explain.

Each round brings two sextiles, two houses or so either side of the top of your chart. One comes as Chiron climbs towards your Midheaven and often prepares the ground for the crossing itself ([transiting Chiron conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)); the other comes as Chiron moves down from it towards your Ascendant, when what that crossing taught you can be put to use. Either way, the sextile to your Midheaven is also a trine to your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), so home and private life tend to back the step rather than compete with it.

A sextile is an offer, not a promotion. The Midheaven's blind spot is confusing recognition with legitimacy, and this transit works whether or not anyone applauds. Nothing here predicts a job, a title or a change of career.

The Midheaven depends on the place of birth as well as the minute: the same moment gives Midheavens about three degrees apart in London and in Edinburgh, enough to shift this sextile by most of a year while Chiron is slow. I need both before I read anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile typically lasts about ten months, with three exact contacts in nearly two passes out of three. When Chiron crosses only once, about one pass in three, it lasts some three weeks, and in one pass in twenty it stretches to about twenty-one months with five.

Over a lifetime to 80, the count is usually three. With a Gemini, Cancer or Leo Midheaven, these years bring the sextile on Chiron's climb, from Aries, Taurus and Gemini in turn; with an Aquarius, Pisces or Aries Midheaven, they bring it on the way down from the top.

The later sextile leads on to [transiting Chiron square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-midheaven/) and then [transiting Chiron trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-midheaven/); halfway round, Chiron reaches your IC ([transiting Chiron opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-midheaven/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to one invitation that draws on what you know from experience, even a small one: a talk, an answer to a stranger's question, an hour of mentoring.

Ask for the kind of recognition you would readily give someone else in your place.

Let home back you. Tell the people you live with what you are taking on.
