Venus in Leo gives affection the way the Sun gives light: openly, generously, and hoping someone will notice. It's the planet of what you value in the sign of warmth and pride, under a ruler that never strays far from it. I read it as a big-hearted way of loving that thrives on being received.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) holds your sense of what deserves love, and of how to offer it. [Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire ruled by the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), gives it warmth, loyalty and a sense of occasion. What moves you is wholeheartedness: the friend who laughs fully, the gesture made without counting, the work someone has put their whole self into. Once you love something, you keep that fire lit for years.

Venus has no dignity in Leo, but its ruler is unusually close. The two are never more than about 47° apart, which puts your Sun in Leo too, or somewhere from Gemini round to Libra. The Sun's sign and house describe the sense of self and purpose this Venus warms, and I read them side by side.

In Leo, Venus normally spends about 25 days, or up to 60 in a year when it is retrograde there, and about 1 person in 12 born 1930–2009 carries it. What it wants, more than anything, is for its affection to be received as the gift it is. That isn't vanity. It's reciprocity in a fiery key: warmth given, and warmth hoped for in return.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, this Venus is generous and demonstrative. You remember to celebrate, you say it out loud, you make a fuss on birthdays, and you'd rather risk too much warmth than too little. Loyalty is close to sacred: when you choose someone, you stand by them in public. Reciprocity, here, means being appreciated in return: a thank-you, a compliment, a partner who is glad to be seen with you. It's how a Leo Venus knows its gift has landed. What hurts most is indifference. Being taken for granted can wound this Venus more deeply than any quarrel.

## At work {#at-work}

A Leo Venus works best with creativity, recognition and some warmth in the room. You often shine at whatever involves an audience or an occasion: presenting, hosting, performing, designing something that makes people stop and look. Leading generously suits it; you're the kind of manager who praises in public. A workplace where effort goes unseen, or where nobody is allowed to enjoy the job, drains it, and this Venus tends to give its best where it feels valued.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Pride can stand in the way of asking. You may wait for a grand gesture rather than request a small one, then feel unloved when it doesn't come. Generosity can become a way of being needed, or of staying at the centre. And when admiration dries up, it's easy to doubt the love itself, rather than notice that some people show it in quieter ways.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask for appreciation in plain words, and give it just as plainly. Make something with no audience at all, a song for the kitchen, a sketch nobody sees, and notice that it still counts. Borrow from the neighbours: a little of the privacy of [Venus in Cancer](/astrology/venus-in-cancer/), so that not every feeling needs a stage, and a little of the modesty of [Venus in Virgo](/astrology/venus-in-virgo/), the kindness nobody sees.

In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), both Venus and the Sun sign landed among the placements that held about one time in three. A Leo Venus is read with its ruling Sun, so that's two lightly held placements leaning on each other, and I let the rest of your chart have its say too.
