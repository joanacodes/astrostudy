One couple splits every bill to the penny and gives lavishly to strangers; another shares everything and still refuses a parent's help with the deposit. From outside, neither makes sense. Composite Lilith in the 2nd house puts a relationship's untamed side in what it values, and in its refusal to let anyone else put a price on it.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) follows the house of the self: it covers what is had and held, belongings, money, skills that could earn a living, and the sense of worth underneath them. It is succedent, and Taurus is its natural sign. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), a chart for the relationship made of midpoints, it describes what the pair holds and what it thinks it is worth. With [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) there, the point I read as hidden or untamed, those values go quiet or go their own way: a bond that won't be bought, won't be paid in gratitude, and keeps its real sense of its own worth to itself.

Whatever each of you is like with money alone, the pair may handle it in a way neither family would recognise, and keep that way private. What it guards is less the money than the principle: whose gift comes with strings, what the relationship owes to whom, which things it will never sell. Outsiders who try to value the bond for it, by its income, its home or its prospects, tend to meet a polite, closed door.

The house itself depends on choices a birth chart never asks for. Both birth times are needed, and one of two methods: the midpoints of your two charts' cusps, or houses built from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live. Inside either method sits a third choice, the house system. The cusp midpoints inherit whatever system each birth chart was drawn in, so the two must match; for birth charts I use Placidus ([which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)). A Lilith near either cusp of the 2nd can move house when any of these changes ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In a single chart, [Lilith in the 2nd house](/astrology/lilith-in-2nd-house/) is a person's sense of worth that learned to keep quiet. Read between two charts, [their Lilith in your 2nd house](/astrology/lilith-in-2nd-house-synastry/) is someone's untamed side meeting what you allow yourself to want. The composite's 2nd describes neither of you: it is the bond's own purse and its own scale of value. Next to it, [composite Lilith in the 1st house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-1st-house/) shows its refusal on its face, and [composite Lilith in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-3rd-house/) keeps it in its daily talk.

Its difficulty is that what it refuses to price, it can also refuse to discuss: who pays for what, what each person brings, what the relationship is worth to each of you. It helps to talk about values rather than sums, and to agree aloud on what is shared, what is separate, and what is simply not for sale.

This is a technique I describe rather than draw in my readings; its users read it with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It forecasts no fortune and no loss. It also looks at what two people hold most privately, so it should only be drawn when both of them agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
