The 11th house is about the people you choose and the future you hope for, two things that change over a lifetime. Pluto stays here long enough to change both.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) goes deep and stays there, and it tends to sort what is essential from what is merely familiar. The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is your friendships, the groups and networks you belong to, the causes you support, and your hopes for the years ahead. Tradition called it the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy there. With Pluto passing through, friendship tends to be measured by depth rather than number. Some circles may thin; one or two people may become far more important than before. Groups bring questions of power with them, about who leads, who belongs and what it costs to stay, and you may see those dynamics more clearly than you'd like. Hopes get the same treatment: wishes carried out of habit give way to aims you would genuinely work for. Even your idea of the future may change scale, from a private wish list to a project you would want to share.

A natal Jupiter in the 11th, met along the way, is asked which of its hopes are worth the effort they would take.

None of this is fate. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) doesn't decide who stays in your life or which plans succeed; it describes a season in which you look at your friendships and hopes more honestly, over many years.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 11th has one of the widest ranges for Pluto. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the middle is about 18 years, a little under the 20 or so Pluto spends in a house across all twelve, yet most stays fall anywhere between about 8 and 48, much wider than the usual 10 to 37. At New York's latitude the middle is about 19 years, and most stays fall between about 10 and 42.

The size of your 11th, and so the length of this stay, follows from your birth time; without it I couldn't tell you when the stay starts or ends, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Pluto comes to the 11th from the house of vocation and public life ([Pluto transiting the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-10th-house/)) and moves on to the most hidden of the houses ([Pluto transiting the 12th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-12th-house/)).

There's a particular fit in the sky now. Pluto is in Aquarius, the sign that matches the 11th in the natural order, from March 2023, settled since November 2024, and still there at the end of my count in December 2039. An 11th house cusp in Aquarius places the crossing now or ahead, the degree giving the year. A cusp in Capricorn had it from January 2008 to November 2024, and a cusp in Pisces waits until after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Invest in the few friendships that can hold a real conversation. Pluto in the 11th rewards depth in a small circle more than reach in a large one.

Before you commit to a group, look at how it handles power and disagreement. The groups worth staying in are the ones where you can say no.

Write down what you hope for in ten years' time, and look at it again each year. This stay is long enough to show which hopes keep their pull.
