Some charts argue with themselves about where to go. This one seldom does: a Sagittarius Sun picks the far horizon, an Aries Moon gets you out of the door, and both are fire. To me the pair is a far aim and a quick first step that agree, and seldom stop to rest.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Count four signs on from Sagittarius and you reach Aries: the same element, a trine by sign, mutable fire for the Sun and cardinal fire for the Moon. The [Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/) builds its sense of self around a conviction and something ahead to aim at; the [Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/) regains its balance through motion and a clean, direct response: the walk out of the room, the message sent, the problem tackled first thing. When you feel something, you're usually halfway to acting on it, and the action tends to serve the larger aim.

Neither light has a dignity here; what ties them is their rulers. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), for your Sun, widens the frame and asks what an experience means; [Mars](/astrology/mars/), for your Moon, supplies effort and nerve. I read Jupiter's sign and house as the territory your Sun wants to explore, and Mars's as the place your feelings go to burn off; when the two point the same way, this pair moves with very little friction. Both houses need your birth time.

You arrived in the week after a half moon. The Moon had moved 91° to 149° ahead of the Sun, seven and a half to twelve days after the new moon, and it was up in the evening sky, more than half lit. About seven of these births in eight fall in the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) as astrologers who count eight phases divide the month, the rest in the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/). Around 120°, nearly ten days in, the trine by sign becomes an exact waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). I read a first quarter birth in two fire signs as a need that has already decided to act, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains how the month is cut.

A Sagittarius Sun paired with an Aries Moon belongs to about 1 person in 148. If the Moon left Pisces or reached Taurus on your birthday, the hour you were born decides between this page, the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-pisces/) and the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-taurus/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It decides the rising sign as well, the third line of the sketch the two lights begin, and the houses of Jupiter and Mars: one more reason I won't begin a reading without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love candidly and out loud, and you'd rather settle a quarrel tonight than let it sleep. The Aries Moon needs honesty it can answer at once; the Sagittarius Sun wants a companion for the road, someone who'll say yes to the trip and argue about the route. Both lights cool quickly once a thing is said, which makes you easy to make up with and, sometimes, hard to keep up with. A partner who needs a day to think isn't holding anything back. Ask, then wait for the answer: patience is the gift this pair has to practise giving.

## At work {#at-work}

Give this pair a mission and a start date. You shine where a large purpose needs someone to go first: a new office to open, a field project, a campaign, a subject nobody has mapped yet. The Moon brings urgency and nerve, the Sun the reason to bother, and colleagues often catch your momentum. Long holding patterns are harder: committees, a project frozen for a year. Where you can, keep a front-line part in any role, something you can act on this week while the larger aim takes its time.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two fires leave little in the chart to slow them, so the risk is pace rather than conflict. You can be three steps into a plan before you've asked what you need, and halfway to the next horizon before the last one has paid you back. Tiredness gets ignored until it insists, and other people's feelings, slower than yours, can be overtaken in the rush. What helps is a pause you schedule on purpose, a long walk with no destination, an evening with nothing to start, and one person you trust to say *not yet* when you're sure it's *now*.
