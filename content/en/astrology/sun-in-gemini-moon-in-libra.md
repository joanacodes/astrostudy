Both of your lights are in air, and both like company: the Gemini Sun for the conversation, the Libra Moon for the harmony. With this pair, talking things through is how you live and also how you keep the peace. The first quarter had passed a few days before your birth, and the evening Moon was more than half lit.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Two air signs four apart, one mutable and one cardinal: a trine by sign, the easy relation of signs that share an element. The [Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/) finds itself through exchange, questions and variety; the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) settles when things are even between people, the conversation fair and the room at peace. Purpose and need agree on a great deal here. Both value talk, both value other people, and a good evening with friends can do as much for your sense of direction as for your mood.

Your Sun answers to [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) and your Moon to [Venus](/astrology/venus/), and the two planets often travel together near the Sun, Mercury within about 28° of it and Venus within about 47°. When they meet or aspect each other in your chart, words and charm tend to work as one; when they don't, you may find yourself saying the clever thing when the kind one was wanted. Tradition gives neither light any special standing in Gemini or Libra.

By the eighth to twelfth day of the lunar month the Moon had moved 91° to 148° ahead of the Sun: past the half moon, and swelling. Nearly all of these births are [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) births in the eight-phase count; a few late ones already count as [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) births. That's an interesting mix: a trine of signs, which flows, born in a phase I read as friction that pushes you to act. The ease is real, and it still asks you to do something with it. Near 120° ahead, the lights make a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the boundaries.

This pairing falls to about 1 person in 140. Crossing Libra takes about two and a half days; born on the day it left Virgo or reached Scorpio, the time would have to settle it between this page, the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-virgo/) and the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-scorpio/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Your rising sign comes from that same time, and so do the houses that show where all this conversation actually happens.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Your ideal partner is also your favourite person to talk to, and you tend to be a generous, attentive one yourself. The Gemini Sun keeps things lively; the Libra Moon makes sure each of you is heard. Arguments, for this pair, are often conducted as discussions, reasonable, witty and thorough. The risk is that the real feeling never gets said, because saying it might tip the balance. A partner who can hear *I'm hurt* without opening a debate gives you room to say it.

## At work {#at-work}

Mediating, negotiating, teaching, writing, hosting, designing anything people use together: you're often good wherever ideas need to be shared fairly. The Sun wants a steady supply of new subjects; the Moon wants a civil, pleasant place to work. Open hostility drains you, and you may end up as the team's unofficial peacekeeper without ever having applied for the job. Teams where disagreement is allowed, and handled well, suit you best.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in air, feelings can stay at the level of conversation. You can argue every side of a question, and argue it well, and still leave your own position out. Pleasantness may then harden into a habit that hides disagreement until it breaks through all at once. What helps is writing down what you think before a conversation starts, and treating your own view as one of the voices a fair discussion has to include.
