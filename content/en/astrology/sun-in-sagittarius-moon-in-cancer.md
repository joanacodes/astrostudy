Tradition gives Jupiter its exaltation in Cancer, and Jupiter rules Sagittarius: with this pair, the planet your Sun answers to is honoured in your Moon's sign. You were born just after a full moon. I read the pair as a travelling life with a home port, a wide self that grows by looking after people.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cancer lies seven signs on from Sagittarius, five counting back: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, mutable fire for the Sun and cardinal water for the Moon, nothing shared, so the two adjust to each other rather than blend. The [Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/) looks far afield for a meaning large enough to live by; the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/) keeps its circle close and its door open to that circle: the spare room made up, the phone answered at midnight, the family recipe kept going. You may spend the year planning a journey and the evening checking that everyone at home is all right.

The dignities are what make this pair unusual. The Moon rules Cancer, so it is in its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), with no other planet to consult: its own house and aspects carry it. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), your Sun's ruler, has its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Cancer, the sign where tradition honours it most. So purpose and need share a meeting point: what you look for far away tends to grow fastest once it's brought home and used to care for someone. Jupiter spends about a year in each sign, so its house, from your birth time, is what makes it personal.

You were born after the Moon had passed full. It was 181° to 239° ahead of the Sun, fifteen to nineteen and a half days into the month, rising after sunset and still bright. About seven births in eight fall in the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase, the three or four days after the opposition, and the rest in the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/); around 210° the quincunx by sign is exact, waning. I read a birth just past the full as needs that have seen the whole picture and now want to bring it home, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the turn.

About 1 person in 150 is born with these lights. On a day when the Moon moved from Gemini into Cancer, or on into Leo, your birth time chooses between this page, the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-gemini/) and the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-leo/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also gives the rising sign, which with your Sun and Moon makes a first sketch rather than a portrait, and the houses where home and horizon sit.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love protectively, with an open door. The Cancer Moon remembers what a partner needs before it's asked for; the Sagittarius Sun brings humour, plans and the conviction that life should be shared widely. The difficulty is distance, inner or outer: the Sun can go off into an idea, or a country, while the Moon at home wonders whether it still counts. Saying where you are, in both senses, keeps the bond safe. A partner who travels with you and also tends the home you come back to fits both lights.

## At work {#at-work}

You tend to do well where growth and care meet: teaching, hospitality, family businesses that spread, organisations that welcome strangers, any work that makes people feel at home somewhere new. The Moon notices who is struggling; the Sun knows why the work matters. Cold, purely transactional workplaces cost you, and so do jobs that never let you look beyond the walls. A role with both a team to look after and a horizon to aim at uses this pair well.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The quincunx asks the lights to adjust, and they adjust in opposite directions: the Sun by leaving, the Moon by drawing closer. Going can make you feel guilty, and staying can make you feel boxed in. Old hurts, which the Cancer Moon keeps, may be waved away by a Sun that prefers to move on, and then come back at the worst moment. What helps is rituals of return: a homecoming meal after every trip, a call home from wherever you are, a feeling named before the next plan is made.
