Those born between October 1971 and August 1984 carry Pluto in Libra. With the planet of deep change on the scales, I read a generation that remakes the terms of relationship itself: what is fair between two people, and what each owes the other.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms by going to the root, and empowers what is real once the false has gone. [Libra](/astrology/libra/), cardinal air ruled by Venus, weighs, balances and connects; whatever sits in it works through other people and through the question of what is fair. Relating is where the depth goes. This generation doesn't take the old terms of partnership for granted: who decides, who gives way, what equality between two people looks like from one day to the next. Cardinal signs begin things, and here the beginning is a negotiation, often reopened.

Libra gives Pluto no dignity: the tradition was unaware of Pluto, and the modern scheme assigns it to Scorpio. Scorpio comes straight after Libra, and this Pluto often reads like Scorpio's depth spoken in the language of fairness. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules the scales, so I read Venus alongside it: where Venus sits shows the kind of partner a person is drawn to, and the terms they will insist on.

Pluto was in Libra from October 1971 to August 1984, and returns from November 2217 to October 2230. It crossed back and forth with [Pluto in Virgo](/astrology/pluto-in-virgo/) until July 1972, and with [Pluto in Scorpio](/astrology/pluto-in-scorpio/) from November 1983, so births at either end are settled only by the chart. Among births from 1930 to 2009, about 1 person in 7 has it. Shared by a whole generation, the sign describes the terms everyone negotiates; the house describes where you negotiate them, and only the [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) can show it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Partnership is where Pluto in Libra does its deepest work. Relationships are taken seriously as partnerships between equals, and the balance of power between two people is noticed, talked about and, when it tips, renegotiated. There is a gift for seeing the other person's side, sometimes before one's own. The tender point is harmony bought at the price of truth: keeping the peace so carefully that resentment builds out of sight, until the relationship has to be remade rather than adjusted. What this placement learns, often over years, is that a disagreement said gracefully is a form of respect.

## At work {#at-work}

Fairness has to be built somewhere, and this Pluto likes to be the builder: mediation, law, design, diplomacy, any role that brings people to an agreement they can live with. It can transform how a team shares decisions, or how a business treats the people on the other side of the deal. It often works best in partnership, and needs partners who play fair. Places where power is hidden, or the rules are bent quietly, can feel intolerable to it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard part with Pluto in Libra is the struggle for power that hides behind politeness. Charm can become a way of steering people, and fairness can turn into scorekeeping, every favour counted. Decisions may be put off for fear of choosing wrongly and upsetting the balance. And there can be a deep fear of being alone, strong enough to keep a relationship going long after its terms stopped being fair.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Saying the unbalanced thing out loud, *this doesn't feel fair to me*, before it becomes a grievance. Making agreements explicit, and revisiting them as life changes. Choosing once, and trusting that a choice can be adjusted. Spending time alone on purpose, so that partnership is chosen rather than needed. I checked Pluto like every other placement in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), with the consent of the people I tested, and it wasn't one of the three that held far more often; overall, about a third held. With a Libra Pluto I take that as a reason to weigh the sign gently, and to let the house and the aspects tip the scales.
