Once in about eighteen and a half years, the North Node spends the better part of a year and a half in your 1st house, and the South Node spends the same months in your 7th. One end of the axis points at you, the other at the person facing you, and the question running between them is who gets to decide.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the direction a chart grows in: what doesn't come naturally yet and gets easier with use. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is the self you lead with, your manner and the way you begin things. With the node here, the growth on offer is initiative: deciding before you've polled the room, starting something on your own say-so, letting your wishes show first.

The other end matters as much. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) is crossing your 7th, the house of partners, and it describes the familiar move of these months: adjusting, accommodating, waiting to see what the other person wants. That skill is real and stays yours. The work is noticing when it has become the only thing you do.

Because the nodes move backwards, this stay ends where Jupiter's and Saturn's begin. In Placidus, the house system I use, the North Node leaves your 1st by crossing your Ascendant while the South Node crosses your Descendant ([transiting North Node conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)), and those last weeks are often when the theme is plainest. A planet you were born with in the 1st is met on the way, its later degrees first, for about a month.

Like every [transit](/astrology/transits/), this one stirs what your chart already holds. It doesn't hand you a new personality, and the talk of a fated turning point that some astrologers attach to the nodes isn't mine: I read direction and habit here, never fate.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the true node usually spends about 17 months in the 1st, a shade under the year and a half of the average house, and most stays run from about 11 months to about 2 years and 10 months. That long end is as far as the node's stays reach anywhere: so far north, a sign that rises slowly can make a 1st house very broad. At New York's latitude the middle is the same, about 17 months, and the long end comes down to about 2 years and 5 months.

I count with the true node, which wobbles, so a dip back over a cusp within 120 days is still the same stay. I look at both nodes, mean and true, a degree or two apart at most ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), and near a cusp they can give slightly different dates.

Your Ascendant comes from your birth time, and so does the width of your 1st, which is why I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node reaches the 1st from your 2nd ([North Node transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-2nd-house/)) and goes on into your 12th ([North Node transiting the 12th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-12th-house/)).

Your rising sign tells you when the stay ends, since the node crosses your Ascendant while it is in that sign. It is in Aquarius from July 2026 to March 2028, so Aquarius rising has its crossing in that window. Capricorn rising follows between March 2028 and September 2029, then Sagittarius rising to March 2031, Scorpio rising to December 2032, Libra rising to June 2034, Virgo rising to November 2035, Leo rising to May 2037 and Cancer rising to February 2039; Gemini rising comes after that. With Pisces rising, the crossing came between January 2025 and July 2026, and the node is now in your 12th.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make small decisions alone and keep them: which task comes first, where to walk, what to wear. Initiative is built by repetition, not by one brave gesture.

Say what you want before you ask what the other person wants. You can still meet them halfway; the difference is that your half is on the table.

Keep the South Node's gifts in use. Tact, listening and a sense of fairness don't stop counting because the axis points the other way. Born with the North Node in your 1st ([North Node in the 1st house](/astrology/north-node-in-1st-house/))? Then this stay brings your [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/), a good moment to see how far that direction has taken you.
