Twelve houses fall into three families of four, and the family tells you something before the house does. It all depends on one question: where does the house stand in relation to the four angles of the chart?

## What it is {#what-it-is}

The four angles, the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) and the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), divide a chart into quarters. In Placidus, the house system I use, and in the other quadrant systems, each angle opens a house, and each quarter holds three houses, one of each kind ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)):

- **Angular houses**, the [1st](/astrology/1st-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house/) and [10th](/astrology/10th-house/), begin at the angles. Greek astrologers called the angles *kentra*, pivots.
- **Succedent houses**, the [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house/) and [11th](/astrology/11th-house/), come next. Their Greek name, *epanaphora*, means rising after.
- **Cadent houses**, the [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house/), [9th](/astrology/9th-house/) and [12th](/astrology/12th-house/), close each quarter. The Greek *apoklima* means a decline, and *cadent* comes from the Latin for falling.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

The old names describe a movement you can watch. As the Earth turns, the whole sky wheels round once a day, and every planet passes through all twelve houses, in the opposite direction to their numbers: up from the Ascendant through the 12th, 11th and 10th to the Midheaven, down through the 9th, 8th and 7th to the Descendant, then on under the horizon. A planet in an angular house is about to reach its angle: in the 1st it's about to rise, in the 10th about to culminate. The succedent house behind it is next in line. A planet in a cadent house has just passed an angle and is moving away: in the 12th it has just risen, in the 9th it has just culminated.

Tradition drew its ranking from that: angular houses strongest, succedent next, cadent weakest. Modern astrologers often read the three alongside the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/), because the natural signs fall the same way. The cardinal signs, Aries, Cancer, Libra and Capricorn, sit naturally on the angular houses, the fixed signs on the succedent ones, the mutable signs on the cadent ones. Angular houses start things, succedent houses keep and build, cadent houses adjust and pass on.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I read the kind of house as pace and visibility rather than strength. A planet in an angular house tends to show: people see it in you, and it shapes what you do; right on the angle, it speaks loudest ([angular planets](/astrology/angular-planets/)). A succedent planet works by accumulation, in what you build up, keep and share. A cadent planet works by adjusting: learning, preparing, looking after, thinking things over. Tradition gave the 6th and the 12th hard names, but a planet there describes how you live that part of life, not a sentence passed on it.

The kinds depend on the cusps, and the cusps on the birth time. The angles turn through the whole zodiac in a day, so a few minutes can carry a planet from a cadent house into an angular one, from the 12th across the Ascendant into the 1st ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). They also assume a quadrant system: in Whole Sign houses, the Midheaven can fall inside the 9th or the 11th, and the scheme shifts with it ([which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Cadent houses are weak houses.”** Tradition ranked them last, but a planet there still works, through learning, adjusting and steady effort that doesn't always show.
- **“An empty angular house means nothing happens there.”** Every house is read through the sign on its cusp and that sign's ruler, whatever it holds ([empty houses](/astrology/empty-houses/)).
- **“Angular planets are always good news.”** They're loud, not lucky: a planet near an angle shows more, whatever it describes.
- **“A planet in the 12th is hidden for life.”** It works more quietly, in private or behind the scenes. Quiet isn't absent.
