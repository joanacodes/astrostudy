There are two doors here, and one of them is easier to walk through. In Mars sextile North Node in synastry, the other's North Node sits about 60° from the Mars of one person, and their South Node twice as far: a modest opening towards growth, and a smooth road back to the familiar.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) gets a person moving: first steps, effort, competition, holding their ground. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is where someone is still a beginner; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, is where they've long since stopped needing instructions. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers the two an opening that someone has to use, and as [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) go, this one gives the Node person a gentle invitation forward, and a comfortable way back.

Seen from the node, this person's suggestions tend to land well: the course, the trip, the new role they mention can make a first step towards the less familiar feel possible. Seen from Mars, your energy fits easily with who they already are, which makes you easy company; the invitation forward is yours to offer, never to enforce.

With the North Node in Pisces, for instance, the opening might be towards letting go of control now and then, while the South Node in Virgo, trined by the Mars, is the careful managing both of you slip into without trying.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The 120° side of this contact, the one touching the South Node, is the smoother of the two, and smooth wins when nobody chooses. The pair can spend years doing, very happily, what the Node person already does well, while the opening towards growth stays an occasional conversation. Established strengths aren't the enemy, but leaning on them alone narrows a person's range.

On the Mars side, it's tempting to applaud the familiar version of the other person, since it's the one that matches your drive best. On the Node side, it's tempting to wait for the push instead of choosing the new door yourself.

The same two at the pair's other angles:

- **[Mars conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the drive lands on the growing edge itself, with more push.
- **[Mars square North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-north-node-synastry/):** a sign further round, effort sits across both ends of the axis.
- **[Mars trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-north-node-synastry/):** the smooth side faces forward this time, towards what's being learned.
- **[Mars opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** the drive settles on the familiar end and stays there.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Choose the new door on purpose, now and then. Agree on one small step towards the less familiar and put a date on it, then enjoy the familiar the rest of the time without guilt. The Mars person can offer, once and clearly, and let the Node person decide. Friends can trade "the usual" for a new plan once a month; colleagues can give one task the unfamiliar treatment; a parent and adult child can try a different way of spending time together.

About 1 pair of people in 13 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 3.6°, counted with the true node. With an orb this tight, the small gap between the two ways of calculating the node, a couple of degrees at most, can decide whether the sextile is there at all, more than the hour of birth can; I look at both and say which shows it. On the node's side, people born within a few months of each other share nearly the same position, the node needing about 18 months for a sign; the Mars person's birthday makes it a contact between two. The houses, as always, wait on both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Someone with [Mars sextile North Node](/astrology/mars-sextile-north-node/) in their own chart chooses between the two doors alone. Shared, it becomes a choice two people make together, which is easier to keep honest.
