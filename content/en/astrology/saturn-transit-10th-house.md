Saturn's years in your 10th house are the ones it spends at home: in the house of Capricorn, its own sign, at the top of your chart. Work, responsibility and reputation come under the planet that rules them in the natural order, for about three years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds slowly and for the long term; the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is public life: career, responsibility, authority, reputation, the legacy you're working towards. Here the planet and the house speak the same language. I read the stay as a season of responsibility at work and an honest look at what you've built: what holds, what was built on someone else's plan, and what you now want to build on your own.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 10th opens at the Midheaven, and Saturn's crossing of it ([transiting Saturn conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)) is often the most visible part of these years: authority becomes something you carry, and the work asks more of you, in plain view.

If your natal Saturn sits in the 10th, this stay includes your [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/): the review comes back to the house where your chart first placed it, at the top of the sky.

What Saturn shows here is the season you're in at work, not what will happen to your career. Achievement isn't status and authority isn't power: like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), this one brings out what your chart already holds and gives it a structure, and it promises neither a promotion nor a fall.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 10th is the only house where the middle of Saturn's stays reaches a full three years in Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, and its range is narrow: most stays last from 2 years to about 3 years and 8 months. At New York's latitude the 10th is steadier still, between just over 2 years and 3, around a middle of just under 3.

A Midheaven is only as exact as the birth time behind it, and a reading built on a guessed one isn't a reading I'll give ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows Saturn's testing of your beliefs in the 9th ([Saturn transiting the 9th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-9th-house/)) and is followed by its years among friends and groups in your 11th ([Saturn transiting the 11th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-11th-house/)).

Your Midheaven's sign gives the window. For a Midheaven in Aries the crossing falls between February 2026 and April 2028; in Taurus, between April 2028 and May 2030; in Gemini, between June 2030 and July 2032; in Cancer, between July 2032 and May 2035; in Leo, between August 2034 and July 2037; in Virgo, between October 2036 and September 2039; and in Libra from September 2039. A Pisces Midheaven was crossed between March 2023 and February 2026, while Capricorn, the 10th's own sign, comes after 2039 with Scorpio, Sagittarius and Aquarius.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take on the responsibility that matches the work you want to be known for, and say no to the kind that only adds weight. Saturn in its own house tends to hand out more than one person should carry.

Build on a long horizon. Plans measured in years, not quarters, suit this stay, and the results it supports are the slow, durable kind.

Share authority as you earn it. Saturn struggles to delegate, and the 10th is where that habit shows most; a role you can hand parts of to others is a role that can last.
