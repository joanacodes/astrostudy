Most people remember a group they weren't let into: a table at school, a circle of friends that closed, a team that chose everyone else first. When Chiron crosses your 11th house, that memory has a few years to be understood.

## What changes {#what-changes}

With [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/), the hurt is rarely the whole story: there is also the way you learn to work around it, and often a knack for guiding others through the same place. The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is where you stand with other people in the plural: the friends you chose, groups, teams, networks, causes, and the hopes you share with them. The old texts called it the Good Spirit, the house where [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rejoices; its natural sign is [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), which I read with both its rulers, Uranus and Saturn. With Chiron passing through, an old hurt about belonging can resurface: being left out, picked last, dropped by friends, or feeling at the edge of every group even when you're inside it. It can return through ordinary things: a group chat that goes quiet, an invitation to a reunion, a cause you care about whose regulars never quite include you. You may find out which circles you join out of fear of exclusion, and which actually fit. Chiron doesn't promise you'll never feel on the outside again; it helps you build a place to stand anyway. Often the person who knows this hurt becomes the one who gathers other outsiders, a gift [Chiron in the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-in-11th-house/) carries from birth.

Born with Jupiter in the 11th? When Chiron reaches it, you may notice how much of your generosity goes to groups that never quite let you in.

Chiron here forecasts no lost friendships and no fallings-out. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) works on your sense of belonging, not on other people's choices.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, Chiron's middle stay in the 11th is about 3 years, shorter than its middle of about 4 across all twelve houses, yet the long end reaches about 12. New York's figures barely differ: a middle of about 3 years, and a long end of about 11.

Your 11th lies between your Midheaven and your 12th, and both its cusps come from your birth time; I don't read a chart without that time, and this stay couldn't be dated without it either ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). On one side of this stay lies Chiron's time in your public life ([Chiron transiting the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-10th-house/)); on the other, the quiet of the house of solitude ([Chiron transiting the 12th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-12th-house/)).

If Taurus is on your 11th cusp, yours may be one of the long stays: Chiron is in Taurus from June 2026 to May 2034, and at London's latitude an 11th house there is often among the widest, up to 48°, while one in Sagittarius tends to be narrow and quickly crossed. For the neighbouring signs the dates are April 2018 to April 2027 for Aries, July 2033 to April 2039 for Gemini, and from July 2038 for Cancer, where Chiron stays beyond the end of my count. Where your cusp falls within its sign narrows the stay to a year or so.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look at the groups you belong to and ask which you'd join today. Leaving one, gently, is allowed.

Invite one person you like to something small. Belonging is often built two people at a time.

Find a group organised around something you love rather than around who's in it; shared work turns outsiders into insiders quickly.

Notice who stands at the edge of your own circles, and make room for them.
