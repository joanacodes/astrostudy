Out of the open country of Sagittarius, the progressed Moon starts the long climb of Capricorn, the sign tradition calls the Moon’s detriment. For two years or so, comfort tends to come from structure: a plan that holds, a responsibility carried well, the steadiness of knowing what you are building.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The progressed Moon](/astrology/progressed-moon/) sets the emotional key of each chapter of your [secondary progressions](/astrology/secondary-progressions/). In [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), cardinal earth ruled by Saturn, its needs become serious in the good sense: to take responsibility, to work towards something that will last, to feel safe because the ground has been tested. Feelings are often shown in deeds rather than words, care as reliability, love as turning up, and the satisfaction of a task finished well can do more for your mood than any amount of reassurance. It can look like taking on a role others depend on, setting a long goal, or putting part of your life on a firmer footing. Ambition may grow too, and that is no betrayal of feeling: in Capricorn, wanting to achieve something can be a way of caring for the person you will be later. Respect matters in both directions, and being taken seriously can comfort more than being cosseted.

Tradition calls Capricorn the Moon’s detriment, opposite its own sign, Cancer: feelings there have to fit inside limits. I read that as warmth that shows in what you do, never as coldness. The chapter’s risk is carrying everything alone; its gift is learning that a structure can hold feelings too, not only tasks.

Where the chapter’s work gathers depends on the house the progressed Moon crosses, which [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) decides; in Placidus, the system I use, I can’t place it without an exact time, so I don’t read the chart. Saturn rules Capricorn, and the house your natal Saturn occupies shows where these years ask for patience, and where they repay it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

For births from 1930 to 2009, the progressed Moon took between two and two and a half years to cross Capricorn, usually about two years and four months; the detriment doesn’t make it linger, however long the climb may feel. How long yours lasts was settled in the first days of your life, by the pace of the real Moon.

It comes after the [progressed Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-sagittarius/) and before the [progressed Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-aquarius/): the vision, then the structure, then the question of whom the structure is for. Capricorn is the tenth of the twelve signs, so the round of about 27 years has only Aquarius and Pisces left before it begins again in Aries. With a natal [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/), you have lived with this need for footing all your life; the chapter gives it a season of its own.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Build something, and let it be enough. A Capricorn chapter is usually steadied by one long project, a skill, a role, a home, a body of work, rather than by many short ones, and by the patience to let it grow at its own pace.

Rest on purpose: schedule it as seriously as the work, because this chapter rarely offers it unasked. Ask for help before you need it; carrying a load well includes sharing it. If you look after others, at work or at home, count yourself among the people you look after: Saturn’s sign keeps careful accounts, and that includes the rest you are owed. And when Aquarius arrives, it will ask who your structures serve, so it helps to have built them with other people in mind.
