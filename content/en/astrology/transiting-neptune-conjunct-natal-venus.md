Once in a life at most, Neptune settles on your Venus for about two and a half years. Tradition exalts Venus in Pisces, the sign modern tropical astrology gives to Neptune, so these two planets have long been thought to understand each other: one chooses what it loves, the other longs for more than can be chosen.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs and chooses: what attracts you, what feels fair between two people, what you'd rather not live without. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines past the edges of things and feels its way into other lives. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes one process of two functions, so for the length of this [transit](/astrology/transits/) choosing and longing work together: you value something for what it is and for what it stirs in you, and the two are hard to tell apart.

The change often shows in taste before it shows in love. Music, colour, the sea, a film or a few lines of poetry can move you more than usual, and you may go looking for a beauty you can't explain. Affection turns more forgiving; you give before you're asked. Neptune's gifts come with a soft edge, and this one is no exception: for these years it's easier to love the version of a person, or of a plan, that you've imagined. Discernment belongs to Venus and stays yours. The transit asks you to use it gently, not to set it down.

It says nothing about whom you'll meet, whether a bond lasts, or what anything is worth in money, and I don't read those in any transit ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). What it describes is the way you love and value while it lasts.

Where it lands is set by the house of your Venus, and the house needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With Venus in your 2nd, Neptune crosses it during its years in that house ([Neptune transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-2nd-house/)): the things you keep start to matter for what they mean rather than for what they are, and it helps to notice which is which.

If Venus and Neptune were already together when you were born ([Venus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/venus-conjunct-neptune/)), this transit came in your first years, or never will: it depends on which of the two stood ahead.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, one pass over your Venus lasts about two years and seven months, seldom less than twenty-two, while Venus in the sky laps the whole zodiac more than twice. Neptune's yearly retrograde loop takes it across the exact degree three times in three passes out of four, and five times in the rest ([Neptune retrograde](/astrology/neptune-retrograde/)).

Half of the people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet it before 80. In eighty years Neptune covers a little under half the zodiac, and Venus never strays more than about 47° from the Sun, so the day of the year you were born counts as much as the year: among people born the same year, a Venus one sign further on meets the conjunction about fourteen years later, and a Venus just behind Neptune never meets it.

Neptune is in Aries from January 2026 until March 2039, so in those years it reaches, degree by degree, everyone born with Venus in Aries ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)).

After the conjunction, Neptune reaches [transiting Neptune sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-venus/) about twenty-seven years later, [transiting Neptune square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-venus/) about forty-one and [transiting Neptune trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-venus/) about fifty-five. [Transiting Neptune opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-venus/) would take some eighty-two, so no one meets it and the conjunction before 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the longing something to do. Sing, paint, plant, dance, write: inspiration is what this conjunction hands out freely, and what you make with it is the part you keep.

Show a new love, of a person or of a plan, to someone who cares for you and isn't dazzled. The questions they ask are worth more, in these years, than their approval.

Let a change of taste prove itself over a few seasons. Two and a half years leaves time to see which pulls were real and which were mist.
