Mars conjunct Uranus puts the urge to act and the urge to change in the same place in your chart. When the two meet, effort tends to arrive as a break with routine: a new method, a sudden start, a way of doing things that's entirely your own.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the part of you that takes the initiative: it asserts, competes, makes the effort and draws a line. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees: it experiments, innovates and reforms what has grown too tight. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) they work as one, each keeping its nature, so acting and changing come in the same movement.

In plain words, you're at your most energetic when something needs doing differently. A fixed routine can drain you, while a problem nobody has solved yet wakes you up. You probably prefer your own method and your own timing, and others may find your decisions sudden even when they've been building for weeks. That isn't rebellion for its own sake. Lived well, it's courage at the service of change: the person who tries the new way first and shows that it works.

Its lesson is direction. Freedom to act is one thing and knowing what the change is for is another; the conjunction grows when the two meet.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The sign colours the meeting: in Aries, Mars's own sign, the push is direct and quick; in Aquarius, Uranus's sign in modern tropical astrology, the effort tends to serve ideas, groups and systems. The house shows where you insist on your own way of doing things: in the 6th, how you work day to day; in the 10th, how you go about a career. The houses depend on your birth time; the conjunction barely does, since Mars moves too little in a day to change it much ([What changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

With an orb of 6°, about 1 person in 28 is born with this conjunction. Within a degree or two, the need to act in your own way is hard to miss; towards 6°, it shows more when a routine starts to feel like a cage.

Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, so its sign is shared across years of births. Mars, six or seven weeks to a sign, catches up with Uranus about every two years and passes it within a few weeks: that's what makes the conjunction personal rather than a generation's.

Mars and Uranus in their other aspects:

- **[Mars sextile Uranus](/astrology/mars-sextile-uranus/):** change and effort cooperate at a distance, offering a new method when you reach for one.
- **[Mars square Uranus](/astrology/mars-square-uranus/):** the two pull at a right angle, and the friction teaches you to aim the urge for change.
- **[Mars trine Uranus](/astrology/mars-trine-uranus/):** original initiative comes easily, in one element, without the conjunction's all-at-once charge.
- **[Mars opposite Uranus](/astrology/mars-opposite-uranus/):** the need for freedom seems to sit across the chart, often in other people, until you claim it.

Between two charts, [Mars conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) puts one person's drive on the other's urge to change: lively, unsettling at times, and read from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

How this conjunction meets you in transit depends on which planet led at your birth. If Mars was a few degrees ahead, [Transiting Uranus conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-mars/) came in your first years, as Uranus caught up; if Mars was behind, Uranus won't reach it again until the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/), at about 84. For everyone else the transit comes at most once in a life, within reach for a year or so and often exact three times. Around 42, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) faces your Uranus and your Mars together.

When Mars in the sky reaches the pair, about every two years, it touches both within days: a short burst of the same restlessness, or of the same knack for a fresh start.

Weather rather than a forecast, these transits stir the wish to do things differently and leave the choosing to you.
