Chiron on your Moon reaches the part of you that keeps you steady: your habits of comfort, your sense of belonging, the way you look after yourself when nobody's watching. Once or twice in a life, an old sensitivity there comes close enough to touch.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feeling and need: attachment, memory, felt security, the habits that keep you on an even keel. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a sensitivity stayed and you adapted around it. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the way you comfort yourself and the way you've coped with an old ache become hard to tell apart. A habit that kept you steady for years, staying busy, never asking, being the one who doesn't need much, can show what it costs. A memory of not being looked after quite as you needed can come back, not to reopen anything but to be seen with older eyes.

The skill comes into view as well. People with a tender Moon often know how to sit with someone through a bad hour, because they remember what they needed. Asking for support isn't dependence, and this transit tends to make that easier to believe: the care you've always offered others can, for a while, be turned towards you.

With the Moon in the 4th, the conjunction falls during Chiron's stay in that house ([Chiron transiting the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-4th-house/)), and home, family history and the old question of where you belong are where it's felt. Born with [Moon conjunct Chiron](/astrology/moon-conjunct-chiron/), you had this transit in your first weeks, and Chiron brings it round again at your [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50.

If old feelings arrive with more weight than you can carry, tell a therapist or a doctor rather than a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/); my [disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help) list help lines that answer day and night).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the conjunction usually lasts about ten months: in nearly two passes out of three, Chiron loops back and crosses your Moon three times. About one pass in three crosses once, in some three weeks, and about one in twenty five times, over twenty-one months or so.

The Moon covers about thirteen degrees a day, so an hour of birth time moves it about half a degree. With Chiron gaining four or five degrees a year through Taurus, that half degree is a month or more of difference in when this begins and ends, and on some days the birth time decides the Moon's sign as well ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)).

There is one conjunction in each round of Chiron's, about fifty years, so by 80 most people have had two and everyone at least one. The sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-moon/)), square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-moon/)) and trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-moon/)) come about three times each, the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-moon/)) once or twice.

From 2027 to 2033 Chiron crosses Taurus, the sign tradition gives the Moon as its exaltation, and Taurus Moons meet the conjunction in those years, the early degrees first. A Moon in the last degrees of Aries has it now, at the end of 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick one habit of comfort that has started to cost more than it gives, and try a gentler one beside it.

Let someone look after you in a small, concrete way: a meal, a lift, an hour of company.

Write down what you needed then and didn't get. Then ask what you need now, which may be something else.
