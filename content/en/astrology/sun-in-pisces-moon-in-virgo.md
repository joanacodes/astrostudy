A Pisces Sun takes in the whole of something at once, its mood, its sorrow, its promise; a Virgo Moon relaxes only when the parts have names and places. You were born with your two lights opposite each other, the Moon full or two or three nights either side of it. To me they are the whole and its details, each asking to be trusted by the other.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water facing mutable earth: an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) by sign, and both signs bend, so the pull runs back and forth rather than locking. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) finds itself in compassion, in imagination and in a sense that everything is connected; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) steadies when it can tend to one useful thing at a time, done well and done by hand. You may grasp the whole of a friend's grief in a moment and then need to do something small and exact before you can bear it.

Mercury has its home and its exaltation in Virgo; in Pisces it meets both detriment and fall. Your lights occupy the two signs that mark its best and its hardest places. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your Moon, yet it travels on your Sun's side of the sky, within about 28° of it, in Aquarius, Pisces or Aries, facing the Moon it serves. Behind your Sun I read both rulers of Pisces: [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the older, which tradition puts in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Virgo, so the planet behind your Sun's sign is uneasy in your Moon's; and [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), added in modern tropical astrology, for the places where you feel joined to everything around you. Neither light gains a dignity.

Between the lights lay 151° to 209°, from two and a half days or so before a full moon to as long after, with the exact [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/) in the middle of that span. The opposition splits these births evenly: the earlier half fall in the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) phase, the later half in the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase itself. The full moon at the centre was, in most of these births, a [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/). As I read it, being born so near the full lets the whole and the detail see each other clearly, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says which half is yours.

This full-moon pair turns up about once in every 149 births. If the Moon changed sign on your birthday, from Leo to Virgo or from Virgo to Libra, three pages are possible, this one between the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-leo/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-libra/), and only the hour narrows them to one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon) says why). So close to a full moon the hour does double work, deciding the phase as well as the sign, and it gives the houses, which tell me where in your life the whole and the detail meet.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with imagination and with practical care: you sense a partner's mood before they name it, then quietly deal with whatever is weighing on them. The Pisces Sun forgives and believes in people; the Virgo Moon shows love through small, careful acts of service. The risk is looking after a partner's whole life while your own needs wait at the back of the queue. Let someone do a small, useful thing for you, and let it be done their way.

## At work {#at-work}

Healing and craft both suit you: nursing, pharmacy, therapy, music, editing, restoration, any job where a large compassion needs exact hands. The Pisces Sun brings a sense of what matters; the Virgo Moon adds method and patience with detail. Chaotic places tire the Moon and cold, purely technical ones starve the Sun, so look for work that is both humane and well run.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Opposite signs can take turns running the show: one week you're full of feeling and imagination, the next you're sorting everything in sight and the feeling waits. Neither is wrong, but swinging between them is tiring. A full moon birth asks to see both at once, so try holding them together: let the feeling be large, and give it one small, practical task. A walk by water with a list in your pocket is this pair's idea of balance.
