Sun sextile North Node puts the Sun a sixth of the zodiac from the North Node, and so a third of the zodiac from the South Node, which always sits exactly opposite. It describes a sense of self with an open door towards growth, and solid ground behind it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your purpose and vitality: what you're for, and the energy you bring to it. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a capacity that's less familiar and worth building through practice; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, holds what you already do well. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the Sun to the North Node through opportunity, and the trine to the South Node lets the familiar flow along with it.

In plain words, when you decide to try the thing that doesn't come naturally yet, your sense of who you are tends to back you rather than resist. You can practise something new without first giving up something old: the strengths you came with keep working, and they make the early attempts less lonely. As with every sextile, the opening waits. Nobody grows towards their North Node by accident, and potential isn't a promise; but here the first step costs less than it might. I read it, like every node contact, as a direction to try on, never a verdict ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as a fairly relaxed relationship with change and learning, and as a talent for using old skills to support new ones. With the North Node in Aries, a Sun in Gemini or Aquarius can bring words and ideas to the new habit of acting on your own say-so; with the North Node in Virgo, a Sun in Cancer or Scorpio brings feeling to the work of turning good intentions into daily habits. The houses show which two areas of life the opening links, and the South Node's house, where the trine falls, shows which old strengths are easiest to bring along.

About 1 person in 12 is born with the Sun within 8° of a sextile to the North Node. The Sun reaches it twice in each round of about 346 days: some two months before it meets the North Node, and some two months after. The closer to 60°, the clearer the door. A birthday with this sextile falls about two months before or after an eclipse season: too far from the nodes for an eclipse that day.

The pair's other aspects set who you are at other angles to the path:

- **[Sun conjunct North Node](/astrology/sun-conjunct-north-node/):** no opening to take; who you are and where you're growing are one.
- **[Sun square North Node](/astrology/sun-square-north-node/):** the Sun across the path, square to both nodes, asking to be included.
- **[Sun trine North Node](/astrology/sun-trine-north-node/):** the stronger link to the North Node, with a sextile to the South Node instead.
- **[Sun opposite North Node](/astrology/sun-opposite-north-node/):** the Sun on the South Node, the new direction facing you.

Between two charts, [Sun sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-north-node-synastry/) is one person's Sun making the other's next step look possible: encouragement that doesn't push.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-sun/) comes twice in each 18.6-year round of the nodes, and lasts a month or two: a short opening to practise something new. The Sun in the sky also sextiles your natal North Node twice a year, a day or so at a time. Weather, not a forecast: an opening, never an obligation.
