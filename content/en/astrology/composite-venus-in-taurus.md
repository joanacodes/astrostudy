A dish that comes out every Sunday, a walk taken on the same route for ten years, a sofa re-covered rather than replaced: with composite Venus in Taurus, the pleasures a relationship cares about most are the ones it keeps. Of anything it enjoys, it asks how to make it last.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), your two natal Venuses are reduced to one point, halfway between them by the shorter arc, and that point speaks for what the relationship values, enjoys and holds on to, and for the way it gives and receives. Here it stands in [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), fixed earth, one of the two signs [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules, its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/) in tradition. That makes composite Venus the ruler of its own sign, and the trail of rulers stops with it: what this relationship likes is read from Venus's own house and aspects, with no other planet to consult.

That steadiness is the bond's own. Two people who change jobs, cities and hobbies on their own can find that together they keep things: a friendship of twenty years held together by one standing dinner, a couple whose favourite shop, song and seat at the cinema never change. A natal [Venus in Taurus](/astrology/venus-in-taurus/) is one person's loyalty to what they love; the composite's is a loyalty the two of you built and neither of you owns. Its give-and-take runs at the same pace: a favour is returned in kind over the following months rather than on the day, and both of you can count on it. Venus never lingers in Taurus, which makes it one of its rarer signs at birth, about 1 person in 14; as a composite, about 1 pair in 12 shares it.

The composite Sun is usually not far off. In about 9 pairs in 10, composite Venus sits within 47° of it, as Venus does in a birth chart, which puts the [composite Sun in Taurus](/astrology/composite-sun-in-taurus/) or within two signs of it, from Pisces to Cancer. For the remaining pairs, born months apart, the two midpoints can split across the circle, and about 1 pair in 22 has the composite Sun near Scorpio, facing this Venus. That's a quirk of the calculation, not a tension hidden in the two of you.

Continuity has a cost when it outlives the pleasure. A ritual can go on because stopping it would feel like a statement: the Sunday dish nobody has chosen for years, the holiday cottage that has become more upkeep than holiday. Keeping something isn't the same as still wanting it, and a bond this good at maintenance can forget to ask. What helps is a review that changes nothing by default: name the habits you'd both choose again, and rest one of the others for a season to find out whether it's missed.

Before it, [composite Venus in Aries](/astrology/composite-venus-in-aries/) values the first step; after it, [composite Venus in Gemini](/astrology/composite-venus-in-gemini/) values what can be exchanged and compared. A bond that keeps its word would want this kept too: a composite is drawn only with both people knowing and agreeing ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
