Mercury never strays more than about 28° from the Sun, so the node, sliding backwards, reaches your Mercury within a year and a half of reaching your Sun. When it arrives, the way you take in and pass on information sits on the point of growth for about a month, and the South Node faces it from across the chart.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) processes: it takes in information, sorts it, makes sense of it and passes it on, in words, notes, questions and arguments. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) stands for a skill that only repetition builds. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two work as one, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) learning something and growing amount to the same activity.

What tends to come forward is a way of thinking you've practised less: asking where you used to explain, writing where you used to talk, checking where you used to answer at once, or the reverse of any of these. A subject you avoided because it didn't come naturally may be the one that asks for your attention, and it will sound unconvincing in your own mouth until it has been used for a while. Meanwhile the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) stands on the point opposite your Mercury, on the explanations you've always given and the arguments you know by heart. They still work; the month shows where they answer before you've listened.

Which comes first depends on your chart. The node moves backwards, so if your Mercury sits at a later degree than your Sun, it is reached first and [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-sun/) follows; if it sits earlier, the Sun's turn comes first.

Mercury rules Gemini, the natural sign of the 3rd, and with your [Mercury in the 3rd house](/astrology/mercury-in-3rd-house/) this transit belongs to the node's stay in that house ([North Node transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-3rd-house/)): letters, neighbours, brothers and sisters, the courses and conversations of every day. Mercury moves so little in a day that your birth date nearly fixes the timing; it's the house that needs your birth time, and I don't read the transit without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My forecast opens the window 1½° before exact and closes it 1° after. The node usually needs about a month to cross it, 32 days when it passes Mercury's degree once, as it does in nineteen passes out of twenty. When an eclipse season arrives in the middle, the true node all but stops, since its pace depends on where the Sun stands; then the conjunction stretches to three and a half months or more, as one pass in ten does, and about one in twenty-five crosses three times. The season of February 2027 is one: a Mercury born near 20° Aquarius has the node on it from late December 2026 to late March 2027, and the annular eclipse of 6 February falls at 17°37′ Aquarius, close enough to count ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Each round of the nodes brings the conjunction once, so most people have four by 80, and those who met the first before about five and a half have five. After it, the node moves on to [Transiting North Node sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-mercury/) and [Transiting North Node square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-mercury/) within five years, to [Transiting North Node trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-mercury/) at about six and to [Transiting North Node opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-mercury/) at about nine, when the South Node takes its turn on your Mercury. Born with [Mercury conjunct North Node](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-north-node/), you meet this transit each time the nodes come home to their birth degrees.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Learn one thing the slow way: a language, an instrument, a skill you've always called "not for me". Twenty minutes a day for the month is enough to feel the difference.

Ask the question you usually answer for other people, and let someone else do the explaining.

Write by hand for a change: a letter, notes from a class, the draft of something you would normally just say.
