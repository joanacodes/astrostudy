Mercury conjunct Venus is one of only two major aspects these planets can make. Both stay close to the Sun, so when they meet, they usually meet in or beside your Sun sign, and the way you think takes on the way you value.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) makes meaning out of information: it notices, reasons, learns and puts things into words. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part that weighs value: what attracts you, what you prefer, what feels fair in an exchange, what you find beautiful. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) they act as a single unit, each still itself, so thinking and valuing happen in the same gesture.

In plain words, you tend to think in terms of what matters to you, and to speak with an ear for how it will land. A sentence isn't finished until it sounds right; an argument isn't won if the other person leaves feeling smaller. You may notice the shape of an idea as much as its content, and learn best from what you find appealing. None of that is vanity or flattery. It's judgement at work inside language.

The one habit to watch is a fondness for harmony. When valuing and speaking are fused, the pleasant word can arrive before the necessary one. But harmony isn't the same as avoiding conflict, and this pairing is at its best when it puts its grace in the service of something true: a hard thing said kindly, so that it can be heard.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

With Mercury always within about 28° of the Sun, this conjunction usually sits in your Sun sign or the one beside it, colouring the core of the chart rather than a corner. The sign gives it a style: in Gemini, quick and playful with words; in Taurus, slow, sensory and careful; in Libra, attentive to the balance of every exchange. The house shows where you use it most, whether that's teaching, writing, selling, or keeping a family talking.

About 1 person in 10 is born with Mercury and Venus within 6° of each other. Mercury is usually the faster, so it does most of the closing and widening; when it turns retrograde, three or four times a year, it can hover near Venus for weeks. In my notebook, Mercury held about a third of the time, and so did Venus, like most placements ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)); I never build a reading on this conjunction alone.

Mercury and Venus have only one other major aspect, [Mercury sextile Venus](/astrology/mercury-sextile-venus/): the two on either side of the Sun, about 60° apart, cooperating instead of merging. A square, a trine or an opposition between them can't happen.

Between two charts, [Mercury conjunct Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-venus-synastry/) lays one person's way of thinking on the other's way of valuing, and conversation tends to feel appreciative. I read it in both directions: the one whose ideas are admired and the one doing the admiring don't feel the same thing.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Both planets are quick, so their transits to each other are short. Mercury in the sky crosses your natal Venus roughly once a year and stays a day or two, up to three times when one of its retrograde loops falls there. Venus crosses your natal Mercury roughly once a year as well, lingering two or three days, and longer when it turns retrograde. Weather, not a forecast: a few good days for the letter, the apology or the compliment you've been meaning to write.
