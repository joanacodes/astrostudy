Leo on the 3rd house cusp gives the sign of the heart to the house of everyday talk: messages, lessons, the street you live on, the brothers and sisters you grew up with. What you say, you tend to say warmly, and you like it to land.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

A cusp lends its sign's manner to the whole house ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Leo](/astrology/leo/) makes the [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) expressive. I read it as a mind that learns by telling: you understand a thing properly once you've explained it to someone, and you remember what came with a story attached. Your messages tend to have a voice, recognisably yours, whether it's a report at work or a note left on the table. The 3rd's natural sign is Gemini, quick and changeable; Leo, a fixed sign, gives the house more constancy, so an opinion formed in conversation tends to stay formed, and a story you've told your way goes on being told your way. With brothers and sisters, cousins and neighbours, it often shows as loyalty: you're the one who speaks up for them in public. Pride is the tender spot. Being corrected in front of others can sting more than the correction deserves, and what helps is being heard properly, even once.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules your 3rd house, and wherever it sits is where your words and lessons go to work. As ruler of the 3rd, it can be found in the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). At London, about 9 charts in 10 with this cusp have [Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/), so the Moon rules the chart while the Sun rules the everyday mind. The two lights meet here a second time: tradition called the 3rd the Goddess and gave the Moon its joy there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). In a chart ruled by the Moon, I read that as feeling and speech travelling together: you talk most freely where you already feel at home.

Leo opens the 3rd in about 1 birth in 11, at London and New York alike, when the houses are drawn in Placidus, my house system. That's close to the average of 1 in 12, and the two cities agree because the 3rd lies between the horizon, where the north slows Leo down, and the IC, where latitude counts for nothing. The house itself is narrow at London, about 21° to 27°, so Leo often opens a neighbour as well: the 2nd in about 2 London charts in 5, tying your words to your earnings, or the 4th in about 1 in 4, tying them to home ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). And in some charts Leo has no cusp anywhere: it's [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) about once in 7 London births, and once in 11 at New York.

Which of those neighbours Leo opens, if any, comes down to a few minutes either way, since all three cusps turn with the sky. [Leo on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-2nd-house-cusp/) and [Leo on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/) describe the charts drawn a little later and a little earlier than yours, and only the recorded time tells me which is yours ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The 3rd, like every house that touches family, describes how those ties feel to you, not who your brothers and sisters are. With Leo on its cusp, it describes a voice that wants to warm the room it speaks in.
