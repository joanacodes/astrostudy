Your Moon is at home in Cancer, the sign it rules, and your Sun is in Scorpio, a second water sign: this is a pair that feels a great deal and shelters what it feels. You were born several days after a full moon, as the Moon began to wane.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cancer is four signs back from Scorpio, a [trine](/astrology/trine/) by sign, and both are water: fixed for the Sun, cardinal for the Moon. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) wants to understand what lies beneath and to change what needs changing at the root; the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/) settles in a safe place among its own people, with its memories near. Purpose and need speak the same emotional language, and most of the time they agree about who matters.

The Moon is in its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), so its own house and aspects describe your needs with no other ruler in between. For the Sun's sign I read both rulers, [Mars](/astrology/mars/) from the tradition and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) from modern astrology. Mars has its fall in Cancer, your Moon's sign, which I read gently: the part of your Scorpio Sun that pushes and fights tends to lay down its arms at home. Pluto, by house, shows where the Sun lets old things go; Mars, where it still has to stand its ground.

With the Moon 211° to 270° ahead of the Sun, the full moon was a few days behind you, and the new moon 17 to 22 days back. The [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) takes the larger share of these births; a small share at the start of the range, roughly one birth in eight, is still in the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase, and at 240° the trine of signs becomes a waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/) by degree. The phase's name means sowing far and wide, and I read a birth in it as one that passes on what it has learnt; here that is often an understanding of feelings, offered as care. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sets the phases in their order.

About 1 person in 145 has the Moon at home in Cancer behind a Scorpio Sun. A Moon that entered Cancer, or left it, on the day you were born leaves the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-gemini/) and the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-leo/) in play until the time is known ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). For a Moon this centred on home, its house is the first thing that time would show me, along with the rising sign.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Your love is protective, and long. The Cancer Moon keeps track of what each of its people needs and sees to it; the Scorpio Sun gives its loyalty whole, once it trusts. You need a home that is safe and a partner who is honest, and you notice at once when either is missing. A hurt from someone close can go very deep; saying so early, while the bond is still warm, keeps it from hardening into a wall.

## At work {#at-work}

You often do well where care and depth meet: counselling, care work, social work, family businesses, research into people's lives and histories, any role that asks you to hold difficult things safely. The Cancer Moon wants colleagues who feel like kin; the Scorpio Sun wants work that matters. Your loyalty to colleagues runs deep, and you protect anyone in your charge.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two water signs can hold on to feelings long after they have done their work. Old hurts, old loyalties and old fears may stay in the house with you, and on a bad day the Scorpio Sun's privacy and the Cancer Moon's shell can keep everyone out together. Give the past a smaller room: keep what still nourishes you, set a date to clear out what doesn't, and tell one person when the shell is closing, before it closes.
