Libra on the 2nd house cusp hands your resources to the sign of the scales. The 2nd is where a chart keeps account of what you have and what you think you're worth, and Libra keeps that account by comparison: this against that, my share against yours.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

A sign on a cusp sets the manner of the house that follows it ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Libra](/astrology/libra/) brings to the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) the habit of weighing. A price feels right to you when it's fair to both sides, so you may be the one who splits the bill to the penny, checks what others charge before naming your own rate, and turns down a bargain that leaves someone else short. Earning tends to involve another person: a client, a partner, a negotiation both sides walk away from content. What you buy is often chosen for proportion, the thing that fits the room or the life. Your sense of worth works the same way, and that's where the practice lies: a worth measured only against other people moves with every comparison. The steadier measure is one you set yourself.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra, and so it rules your 2nd house. It has a claim on this house in any chart, since the 2nd's natural sign is Taurus, Venus's other home. With Libra on the cusp, it's the Venus of exchange and agreement that keeps your accounts rather than the Venus that holds on, and the house it occupies is where your earning and spending play out: the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Taurus opens your 9th house in about 9 London charts in 10 with this cusp, and more at New York, so Venus usually rules that house too: what you value and what you believe answer to one planet. Opposite, Aries opens the 8th, and Mars looks after what you share and owe while Venus looks after what's yours.

About 1 birth in 10 has Libra on this cusp, at London as at New York, counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use. North of the equator Libra rises slowly, nearly three hours at London, so it lingers on the cusps either side of the Ascendant, and the 2nd is one of them. Of the London charts with this cusp, about 4 in 5 have Virgo rising, with Mercury as chart ruler. In the rest, Libra is rising as well and opens both the 1st and the 2nd ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), which makes Venus your chart ruler too. Libra is never intercepted in the 2nd. When it opens no house at all, in about 1 London birth in 6 and 1 New York birth in 8, it lies further round the chart, somewhere between the 3rd and the 9th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

A birth a little later could have lifted Libra to the Ascendant ([Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/)); a little earlier, it could have sat lower down ([Libra on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-3rd-house-cusp/)). Only the birth time settles which, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What the 2nd won't do is name a figure. I read it for habits and values, and no chart can choose an investment or promise a salary ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). With Libra here, the habit is looking for the fair price, and the value is fairness itself.
