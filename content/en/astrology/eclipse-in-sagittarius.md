Sagittarius and Capricorn share the largest count in my list: twenty eclipses each between 2000 and 2040. Sagittarius looks for meaning and room to move, and its eclipses fall on the axis it shares with Gemini, the big picture and the small print.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) is mutable fire, ruled by Jupiter: exploration, learning, faith, the wish to see further. Its eclipses sit on the Sagittarius–Gemini axis, and the moving nodes make them alternate with Gemini’s. The Sun and Moon stood together in Sagittarius for the total [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) of 4 December 2021, at 12°21′; for the [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) of 26 May 2040 the full Moon will be here, at 5°49′, and the Sun in Gemini.

For a chart, though, the sign is only half of it. Under the AGS rule I use, an eclipse counts within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of its axis, and is read through that point and its house; five of Sagittarius’s twenty are penumbral, and those weigh less. For the nodes I compare the mean and the true positions. Emphasis, then, and never an omen.

Twenty from 2000 to 2040:

- **2001–2003:** 14 December 2001, annular solar, 22°56′ · 26 May 2002, penumbral lunar, 5°10′ · 4 December 2002, total solar, 11°57′ · 23 November 2003, total solar, 1°13′
- **2011–2013:** 15 June 2011, total lunar, 24°22′ · 25 November 2011, partial solar, 2°37′ · 4 June 2012, partial lunar, 14°08′ · 25 May 2013, penumbral lunar, 3°58′
- **2020–2021:** 5 June 2020, penumbral lunar, 15°41′ · 14 December 2020, total solar, 23°08′ · 26 May 2021, total lunar, 5°28′ · 4 December 2021, total solar, 12°21′
- **2029–2031:** 5 December 2029, partial solar, 13°45′ · 15 June 2030, partial lunar, 24°38′ · 25 November 2030, total solar, 3°02′ · 5 June 2031, penumbral lunar, 14°30′
- **2038–2040:** 17 June 2038, penumbral lunar, 26°10′ · 6 June 2039, partial lunar, 15°59′ · 15 December 2039, total solar, 23°32′ · 26 May 2040, total lunar, 5°49′

The most recent, a total solar eclipse on 4 December 2021, came nineteen years to the day after the total eclipse of 4 December 2002; the next, on 5 December 2029, is partial. Scorpio comes before it and Capricorn after; both are covered separately ([Eclipse in Scorpio](/astrology/eclipse-in-scorpio/), [Eclipse in Capricorn](/astrology/eclipse-in-capricorn/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Placing Sagittarius among your houses needs [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): without it the cusps can’t be drawn, and I don’t read the chart. Each line follows Placidus, my house system, and applies when a Sagittarius eclipse meets a natal point in that house; the Gemini degrees opposite reach the house across the chart.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/):** Sagittarius rising: your frank, open manner; the way you head out into the world.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you value enough to spend on learning or travel; your sense of worth, widened.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** big ideas in everyday talk; a conversation that opens your view.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a base for adventure; the beliefs you grew up with.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** play, romance and adventurous pleasures. The tradition gave this house children as well; my reading keeps to grown-up joys, and no pregnancy is ever predicted from it ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)).
6. **[6th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work with meaning; daily tasks weighed against a larger purpose.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius on the Descendant, with Gemini rising: a partner who widens your world; freedom within the bond.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** shared beliefs and shared means; trust that lets each of you grow.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius in its natural house: study, travel, faith; a teaching or a journey that changes your outlook.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a vocation with a vision; the ideals behind your work.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends from far away; a group gathered around a cause.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a private search for meaning; what you believe when no one is listening.
