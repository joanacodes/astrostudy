The 7th house is where other people stand in your chart, and with Neptune there, partners tend to arrive in a glow, half person and half dream. Your tenderness towards them can be extraordinary.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is partners and close one-to-one bonds: love, marriage, working partnerships, even open rivals, and what you look for in the person facing you. It's angular, opened by the Descendant, the degree setting in the west when you were born, and its natural sign is Libra, ruled by Venus. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) here brings imagination and compassion to the person across the table. You may sense a partner's inner life quickly, forgive easily, and hope for a meeting that feels meant. In this house, the other person can become a screen for the ideal. Opposite sits the 1st house, the house of the self, and with Neptune in the 7th the balance tips towards the other: you may understand a partner better than you understand yourself, and know what they need before you know what you want. Venus, the natural ruler of the 7th, is worth reading beside it, since what you find beautiful in a person shapes the dream.

Your generation shares Neptune's sign; what's yours is that Neptune falls in the house of the other. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 7th begins at the Descendant, so a Neptune close to that angle may belong to [Neptune in the 6th house](/astrology/neptune-in-6th-house/) instead, depending on a few minutes ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). One house on, [Neptune in the 8th house](/astrology/neptune-in-8th-house/) takes the same longing into intimacy and trust.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

This is the heart of the placement. Love often begins with recognition: the feeling of having known someone for ever. You may give a partner the benefit of every doubt, love their potential, want to rescue or to be rescued. None of that is a flaw; it's the ideal looking for a home. What it needs is time and daylight. Get to know someone across seasons, in bad moods and good, before deciding who they are. And if you compare charts with a partner, do it together ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

My [notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) made no exception for love: Neptune's house got its tick or cross like any other placement. When the results came in, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith were the three that held far more often. Neptune wasn't one of them, and overall about a third held.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Neptune does well in one-to-one roles: counselling, mediation, coaching, care, representing artists or clients, any job built on understanding one person at a time. You can sense what a client needs before they say it. Business partnerships can be idealised too, so put shared ventures in writing and read the agreement twice: it protects the vision as well as the people. Open rivals belong to this house too, and a 7th-house Neptune can find it hard to see an opponent clearly, softening them into a friend or imagining more hostility than is there.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard part is the moment the glow fades and the person remains. Nobody can live up to an ideal for ever, and disappointment can arrive simply because someone turned out to be human. You may stay too long in a bond you hoped would change, or blur your own needs into theirs. And people who want rescuing can find you very easily.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let the ideal describe how you love, not who someone must be. Keep your own outline inside a relationship: friends, work, a life that's yours. Say what you need plainly, even when it breaks the mood. And trust the love that survives ordinary days; in the 7th house, that's Neptune's dream come true.
