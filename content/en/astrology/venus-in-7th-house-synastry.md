Their Venus in your 7th house sits where you look for a partner. Whoever the other person is to you, a lover, a friend, a business associate, being one to one with them tends to feel easy, fair and pleasant, which is why this overlay has such a reputation.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

Equals meet in [the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/): partners, close collaborators, the person across the table. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) seeks reciprocity and harmony, and knows what it finds lovely. With their Venus in this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), what they value meets your idea of a good companion.

You may find that they embody qualities you've always wanted in the person across the table: a certain grace, a fairness, an ease. Agreements come without struggle, compromises feel like gifts rather than losses, and being a pair, of whatever kind, seems natural with them. Even the practical side of a partnership, sharing a flat, splitting a bill, running a project, tends to go smoothly.

In their eyes you can look like a good partner, someone they enjoy facing and choosing. They value the balance between you and may work to keep it. The 7th is angular, and Venus rules [Libra](/astrology/libra/), its natural sign, so the overlay speaks the house's own language of balance.

Close to your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the western horizon of your chart, their Venus also makes a conjunction with it, and the sense of meeting your counterpart grows stronger.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Harmony can become a rule. When keeping things pleasant matters this much, disagreements go unsaid, decisions get made to avoid friction, and one of you may quietly give way more often. Harmony isn't the same as avoiding conflict, and this overlay can blur the two.

They may feel loved as an ideal rather than as themselves: the right partner rather than a real person with bad days. And if they're the one always smoothing things over, the balance they value can tilt without anyone saying so.

This overlay doesn't predict marriage, or how long anything will last. The 7th describes how you meet one to one, and a synastry describes how two people interact, never the outcome.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Disagree on purpose, kindly and early: a pair that can argue well keeps its harmony honest. Let them be ordinary. In a couple, talk about commitment in your own words rather than the chart's; between friends, even a best friend with whom everything is easy deserves a hard conversation now and then; between business partners, put the fair arrangement in writing, however friendly it feels; between adult siblings who work as a pair, make sure both voices count.

Seen from their chart, this is your Venus in their 7th house, and you'd be the one who sees a good partner in them. Either side of it, [their Venus in your 6th house](/astrology/venus-in-6th-house-synastry/) is affection at work and in routine, and [their Venus in your 8th house](/astrology/venus-in-8th-house-synastry/) the deeper intimacy of what you share.

Your Descendant faces your Ascendant across the chart and moves with every minute of your birth time; in Placidus, the house system I use, it is the 7th's cusp, and a Venus a degree or two away can fall on either side of it. Their own birth date places their Venus well enough unless it sits that close ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A partner's chart, like yours, is read only with consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
