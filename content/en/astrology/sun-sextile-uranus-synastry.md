A suggestion at the right moment, taken or left without fuss: that's the texture of Sun sextile Uranus between two charts. Here the Uranus of one person and the Sun of the other are some 60° apart, two signs away, and new options keep turning up on the Sun person's path without anyone forcing them.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is a person's sense of who they are and where they're going. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) innovates and frees: it differentiates, experiments and reforms what no longer works. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) puts the two in cooperative contact, an opening rather than a push. As [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) go, it's the gentlest way of being shaken up.

With your Sun in this pair, you collect options. Your aims stay yours, but around this person they gain a few paths you hadn't considered, and because nothing is pressed on you, experimenting feels safe. You can try a new approach for a month and drop it without anyone taking offence.

With your Uranus in it, the other's goals give your inventiveness somewhere to go. You're heard, and your independence is never in question, since the Sun person isn't trying to hold on to you. Collaborating without merging suits you.

The elements set the pace. Between fire and air, the ideas come quickly and out loud; between earth and water, quietly: a better way of running the household, or permission to feel differently about an old routine.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An easy angle can let talk stand in for change. The pair discuss the course, the move, the new system, enjoy the discussion, and leave everything as it was. An opening is not yet a change; someone has to act. After a while the Uranus person may stop suggesting, feeling their ideas are admired and shelved, and the Sun person may not notice the silence.

There's a quieter friction too. What the Uranus person offers as a possibility, the Sun person can hear as restlessness, as if the present weren't good enough.

Uranus's sign is the part of this contact that belongs to everyone born within about seven years of the Uranus person. The Sun, two signs on, is the part that is yours as a pair.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Act on one idea. The Sun person can pick a single suggestion and give it a real trial, with a date. The Uranus person can say which of their ideas actually matter to them, so they don't all sound equally casual. A couple might try one new thing each season; friends, a trip or a class neither would have booked alone; a family, a tradition updated at a relative's suggestion; coworkers, a small pilot instead of a long debate.

About 1 pair of people in 10 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 4.8°. Both birth times are part of what I ask for before I begin. The hour can matter at the very edge of an orb this narrow, a day moving the Sun by a degree or so; it always matters for the houses, which show whether the new options open at work, at home or among friends ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The other angles of the same two bodies:

- **[Sun conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus-synastry/):** the change settles right on the Sun person's sense of self.
- **[Sun square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-uranus-synastry/):** it cuts across their direction and asks to be negotiated.
- **[Sun trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus-synastry/):** it comes in without waiting for an invitation.
- **[Sun opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus-synastry/):** it stands facing the Sun person, as someone else's freedom.

In one chart, [Sun sextile Uranus](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus/) is a person's own knack for change; between two people, the knack belongs to the other, and you borrow it.
