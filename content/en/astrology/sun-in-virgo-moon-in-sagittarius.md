A Virgo Sun wants the detail right; a Sagittarius Moon needs the big picture, and room to roam inside it. Your birth came around the first quarter, with the half moon high at dusk. The two rulers of this pair each find the other's sign the hardest place to be, and I read it as the editor and the explorer in one person.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Earth and fire meet here at a square by sign, both of them mutable, with the Moon three signs on. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) adapts by narrowing down and finding the exact method; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) adapts by widening out, and settles once a feeling fits a larger story and there's space ahead. You may plan a journey to the last connection and then long to tear up the plan.

The rulers cross in an unusual way. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of your Sun's sign, is exalted in Virgo and has its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Sagittarius, your Moon's sign; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), ruler of your Moon's sign, has its detriment in Virgo by the older scheme. Each planet is least at home where the other rules. I read that as a mind that distrusts grand theories and a heart that distrusts small print, and the pair's work is letting each correct the other. Mercury, within 28° or so of the Sun, sits in Leo, Virgo or Libra; Jupiter's sign and house show where the Moon looks for meaning.

This is a birth from the first quarter's neighbourhood. On the day, the Moon was high in the sky at sunset and close to half lit, having moved 61° to 120° from the Sun in the five to ten days since the new moon. The [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) and the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) take these births in equal numbers, and close to 90° the square is exact, a waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). I read a first quarter birth as friction resolved by doing, which suits a Moon that would rather move than brood; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the turn at 90°.

The pair comes to about 1 person in 140. On a birthday when the Moon moved from Scorpio into Sagittarius, or on into Capricorn, the hour chooses between this page, the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-scorpio/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-capricorn/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)), and on the day of the quarter it also decides which phase is yours. The rising sign rests on it too, and so do the houses, which show where in life this square does its work.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you're honest, funny and practical, often in the same sentence. The Sagittarius Moon needs freedom, candour and a partner who laughs; the Virgo Sun shows love through careful help and remembered details. The square can make you seem to want two things at once, a partner who keeps the itinerary and one who'll abandon it with you. Telling a partner which of the two is asking today saves a lot of guesswork.

## At work {#at-work}

Teaching, publishing, research, translation, travel and its logistics: work that joins a wide horizon to careful detail suits you. The Moon contributes an appetite for ideas and a sense of what things mean, the Sun method and accuracy, so you can sell an idea and check its footnotes. Narrow, repetitive work starves the Moon; vague, overblown projects irritate the Sun. Look for roles where the big picture is yours to shape and the details yours to get right.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Under strain, the pair swings. The Virgo Sun checks everything twice; the Sagittarius Moon wants out, promises more than any week can hold, and then the Sun worries about the promise. Restlessness and worry can feed each other. What helps is one larger aim chosen on purpose, broken into steps small enough for the Virgo Sun to trust, and an escape built into the week, a long walk, a day trip, a new subject, before the Moon goes looking for one.
