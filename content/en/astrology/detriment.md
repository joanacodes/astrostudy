Every planet that has a home also has a sign that faces it across the zodiac. A planet standing there is in detriment, the first of the two debilities on an old table. The word sounds harsher than the thing it describes.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Detriment is the sign opposite a planet's [domicile](/astrology/domicile/). The Sun, at home in Leo, is in detriment in Aquarius; the Moon, at home in Cancer, in Capricorn. Mercury is in detriment in Sagittarius and Pisces, Venus in Aries and Scorpio, Mars in Libra and Taurus, Jupiter in Gemini and Virgo, and Saturn in Cancer and Leo. In the modern scheme, Uranus is in detriment in Leo, Neptune in Virgo and Pluto in Taurus; the tradition never knew those three and says nothing about them ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)).

The older word for any such weakness is a debility. A table of dignities lists the five dignities first, from domicile down to the [faces](/astrology/faces/), and then the two debilities: detriment, opposite the planet's own sign, and [fall](/astrology/fall/), opposite its exaltation.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Look at whose signs the detriments are, and a pattern appears. Every planet in detriment stands in a sign ruled by its opposite number.

- **Venus and Mars** swap: Venus is in detriment in Aries and Scorpio, the signs of Mars, and Mars in Libra and Taurus, the signs of Venus.
- **Mercury and Jupiter** swap: Mercury's detriments, Sagittarius and Pisces, are Jupiter's signs; Jupiter's, Gemini and Virgo, are Mercury's.
- **Saturn and the lights** swap: Saturn is in detriment in Cancer and Leo, the signs of the Moon and the Sun, and the lights are in detriment in Saturn's Capricorn and Aquarius.

So detriment sets each planet down in the manner of the planet most unlike it. The function that values and attracts works in signs built for acting and asserting, and the other way round. The function that gathers detail works in signs built for widening the view, and the one that widens the view in signs built for detail. The functions of vitality and feeling work in signs built for structure and limits.

Two planets can stand in each other's signs and both be in detriment at once. With the Sun in Aquarius and Saturn in Leo, each is in the other's sign, a [mutual reception](/astrology/mutual-reception/), but each also faces its own home, and an exchange between two planets in detriment is reckoned of little help to either.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In my readings a sign is only ever a manner, and a manner never takes a function away: a planet in detriment is still entirely itself. What it lacks is a manner made for it, so it works the way your other hand writes: it gets there, it takes attention, and what it produces is often more deliberate than what comes easily.

[Venus in Scorpio](/astrology/venus-in-scorpio/), for instance, values what lies beneath the surface and won't settle for the pleasant version of things. That can take longer to trust than Venus in Taurus, which enjoys and keeps what it already has. [Saturn in Cancer](/astrology/saturn-in-cancer/) builds its structures out of care: responsibility at home may come early, and the shelter it learns to make is solid. Neither is a lesser Venus or a lesser Saturn.

I watch for what detriment teaches. A function that had to adapt to an unfamiliar manner often ends up with a skill other people never needed to learn. That's the spirit in which I read every hard placement: no doom, no verdict, only parts of a chart that ask for more patience than others ([The three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#gently)).

For the outer planets, detriment is shared by a generation. Everyone born while Uranus was in Leo has it there ([Uranus in Leo](/astrology/uranus-in-leo/)), and what makes it personal is the house it falls in, which needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“A planet in detriment doesn't work.”** It works fully, in a manner that isn't its own. Effort isn't failure.
- **“Detriment and fall are the same thing.”** Detriment faces the planet's own sign; fall faces its exaltation. Mercury in Pisces happens to be both.
- **“A [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) means you're cold.”** It means feeling is handled with structure and restraint. Restraint isn't absence, and reserve isn't coldness.
- **“Detriment is something to fix.”** It's a placement to understand. What it asks for is time and practice, not repair.
