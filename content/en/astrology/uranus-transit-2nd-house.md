Uranus in your 2nd house brings the planet of change to what you live on: how you earn, what you keep, what you think things are worth, and what you think you're worth. For about seven years, sometimes nearer twelve, security gets weighed against freedom.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) frees and reforms; it wants room to rearrange whatever has stopped fitting. The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is resources and values: income, possessions, the skills you could live from, and the self-worth underneath them. During Uranus's stay, the way you support yourself can start to feel too narrow for the person you've become. Some people change how they earn, others what they spend on, others what they'd call security at all, swapping a fixed arrangement for a freer one, or the other way round once freedom has proved precarious.

With Saturn in your 2nd from birth, the pass can loosen a caution about money that was learned early, and show which parts of it still protect you.

None of this is a statement about your bank account. I don't read gains, losses or windfalls in a chart, and a [transit](/astrology/transits/) can't tell you what to invest in or when ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). What it shows is the weather around your values: which are yours, which you inherited, and which you're ready to drop.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn for a London birth, Uranus's stays in the 2nd centre on just under seven years, below its middle for all houses there of about 7 years and 8 months, and most run from nearly five years to nearly twelve. For a New York birth the centre is about seven years and the long end nearly eleven.

Counted from the day Uranus first crosses your 2nd cusp to its last day in the house, retrograde loops included, the length depends on how wide your 2nd is, and that width, like the cusp's degree, comes from your birth time and the latitude you were born at. A chart without a birth time has no cusps at all, which is the reason I won't read one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay takes over from Uranus's years in your 1st, spent on how you present yourself ([Uranus transiting the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-transit-1st-house/)), and hands on to its years in your 3rd, where the change moves into how you think and talk ([Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/)).

If your 2nd house cusp is in Gemini, the stay begins somewhere between July 2025 and May 2033, the span Uranus spends in that sign, and in April 2026 it settled there for good. Cancer cusps follow, between August 2032 and August 2039, and Leo cusps from August 2039. A Taurus cusp has just had its turn: Uranus crossed Taurus, the sign the natural zodiac pairs with the 2nd, from May 2018 to April 2026, so that stay may still be running.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tell freedom from risk before acting on either. Uranus in the 2nd can make a safe income feel like a cage, and from the inside a cage and a floor look alike: check which one you're standing on.

Experiment at a size you can afford. One new source of income alongside the old, one possession let go before the rest, a trial month of spending differently: small tests show what a big leap would only gamble.

Notice whose values you've been living by. This transit is good at showing which of your rules about money came from your family or your first job, and which you'd choose today.
