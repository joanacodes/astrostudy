Twice in each twelve-year round, Jupiter in the sky makes a sextile to the degree your Saturn held at birth. The planet that widens and the planet that builds slowly are on speaking terms for a few days, and something you hope for can be given a shape that lasts.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your natal [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) marks what you have learned to carry: duties, standards, a sense of how long real things take, and the need for an order that can survive time. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) brings growth, meaning and the wish to go further. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through cooperation, and only through cooperation: it offers, and leaves the taking to you.

These are good weeks for putting a structure under a hope. A timetable for the course you keep meaning to take, a proper agreement for work you've been doing on goodwill, a first conversation with someone who knows how it's done: small, practical moves that suit the slow builder in you. The sextile rarely announces itself. It shows as a door that is easier to open than usual, and most people walk past.

Which part of life it concerns depends on two houses: the one your Saturn occupies, and the one Jupiter is crossing at the time, often two houses away. Both come from the birth time, and without it there's nothing I can place ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If your chart has [Jupiter sextile Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-saturn/), one of these transits falls near every [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/), since Jupiter coming home also stands about 60° from your Saturn.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured between 1½° before exact and 1° after, the orbs I time [transits](/astrology/transits/) with, a sextile to your Saturn that Jupiter crosses once is over within about eleven days. Roughly three times in ten Jupiter is near one of its stations, and the window opens three times over some eight months. With two sextiles in every round, people born between 1930 and 2009 meet thirteen or fourteen by 80.

The first can come within weeks of birth or not until nearly eight: at about 1.1 for a birth on 1 January 1995, at about 7.1 for 1 January 1965. The difference is where Jupiter stood at birth, and how far it had to travel to reach a point 60° from your Saturn.

The sextile is the lightest of Jupiter's contacts with your Saturn. [Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-saturn/) widens the structure itself, [transiting Jupiter square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-saturn/) tests it, [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-saturn/) weighs it against other people's plans and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-saturn/) lets it run with little effort. This one only offers. [Transiting Saturn sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-jupiter/) offers the same cooperation from the other side, and much more slowly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick one long-term thing and give it a form this month: a schedule, a written agreement, a set number of hours a week, a first lesson booked.

Ask someone who has done it before. Saturn learns best from people further along the same road, and a sextile makes the asking easier than it usually is.

Don't wait to be pushed. If you're used to working only under pressure, notice that there isn't any, and start anyway: that is the whole skill this transit asks for.
