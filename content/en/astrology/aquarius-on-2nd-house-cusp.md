Aquarius on the 2nd house cusp hands your money, your belongings and your sense of worth to the sign of fixed air. You tend to judge a thing by what it frees you to do rather than by what it says about you, and you keep your own count of what you're worth.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

With [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) on its cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is run on principles. I read it as money treated as a tool for independence: earnings that come through what you know, a network, a shared project or a trade your family had no word for, and spending that buys time, company or a way out of someone else's rules. You may lend, pool or share more easily than you collect. Prices interest you less than fairness, and you notice at once when a deal suits one side only. Self-worth follows the same line: it rests on being your own person, and it can wobble when a job asks you to fit a mould. Being fixed, Aquarius also keeps the values it has reasoned out for years.

Two planets rule this cusp, and I read both. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern ruler, marks where your way of earning changes course, sometimes overnight; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional one, marks where security is built slowly and the accounts get kept. Saturn gets my longer look, because it was one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). Follow each planet to its house and you find where the earning and the valuing get done, as ruler of the 2nd in the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

This is one of the sign's rarer cusps: in Placidus, the house system I use, about 1 London birth in 17 has it, and 1 New York birth in 15. Seen from northern latitudes, Aquarius clears the eastern horizon in about 69 minutes at London, so the cusps close to the Ascendant cross it quickly, and more quickly the further north you go. The 2nd house is wide in these charts, about 47° at London and 41° at New York, and at London more than half of them hold the whole of Pisces inside it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)): Neptune and Jupiter, the rulers of Pisces, then speak for your resources as well. Capricorn rises with this cusp in 7 New York charts in 10 and about 2 London charts in 5, and Saturn then rules your whole chart as well as your money ([chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/)); in the rest Sagittarius rises, and Capricorn is the sign left without a cusp, inside your 1st house. Aquarius itself opens no house at all in about 1 London birth in 7, and 1 New York birth in 11.

A later birth on the same day would have brought the sign up to the Ascendant ([Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/)); an earlier one, down to the 3rd cusp ([Aquarius on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/)). Only the birth time settles which, and it's why I don't read a chart without one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What this cusp never gives me is a figure. It shows the terms you set with money, not what will reach your account, and choosing investments is beyond any chart ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).
