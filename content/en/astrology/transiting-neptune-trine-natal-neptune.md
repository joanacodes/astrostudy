Neptune needs some fifty-five years to travel a third of the way round from its birth place. The trine it then makes to itself reaches everyone in the early to mid-fifties, about fourteen years after the Neptune square.

## What changes {#what-changes}

By the fifties, the [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) in your chart has a history: the ideals it gave you, the ones you kept, the ones you let go of at the square. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets two functions run together without either losing its shape, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the ideals you were born with and the planet's present step move the same way, usually from a sign of the same element. What the square loosened around forty can settle into something gentler and more lived-in: compassion with experience behind it, imagination that has learnt what it can carry.

Flow isn't passivity. A trine supports a direction you choose, and without one its two and a half years can pass with nothing to show but a pleasant mood. The trap is to let effort go in the name of acceptance, as if surrender and helplessness were the same thing; this transit rewards a light, steady effort more than none.

The house transiting Neptune crosses, about four houses on from your natal Neptune, shows where the current runs. With Neptune born in your 5th, the trine usually comes from around your 9th, and what you create can open onto study, belief or far places ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)). Without a birth time those houses can't be placed, so there is no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The trine opens, in my orbs of 1½° before exact to 1° after, somewhere between 51.7 and 55.2 for births from 1930 to 2009, and the later the birth, the earlier it comes: 55.1 for births at the start of 1935, 1945 and 1955, 54.3 for 1965 and 1975, 53.4 for 1985, 52.5 for 1995 and 2005. It stays about two and a half years, the quickest passes about twenty-two months; most passes hold three exact hits, and about a quarter hold five. Born at the start of 1950, you met five, from 55.3 to 56.9.

Everyone meets it once. Two steps of the cycle lie behind it, the sextile of the mid-twenties ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-neptune/)) and the square of the early forties ([transiting Neptune square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-neptune/), the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/)); one lies ahead, [transiting Neptune opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-neptune/), at about 80. For most births from the mid-1940s to the mid-1980s, when Neptune ran a sixth of the zodiac ahead of Pluto, [transiting Neptune opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-pluto/) lands a year or so before or after this trine: imagination flowing from one side while something long held faces you from the other.

People born with [Neptune in Sagittarius](/astrology/neptune-in-sagittarius/), from 1970 to 1984, are the ones meeting it now, at about 54: Neptune is in Aries, a fire sign like theirs, until March 2039 ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Teach what the square taught you. By the fifties, the softening of the early forties has often become something you can pass on, to a colleague, a student or a friend starting out.

Put a date on one thing you have wanted to make or learn. A trine doesn't press, and a whole season of it can go by without a single step.

Let ease be enough now and then. Not every Neptune transit is a test, and rest you enjoy without guilt is among the things this one supports.
