Sagittarius on the 11th house cusp means your circle is drawn with a wide compass. The people you count as yours rarely come from one street, one job or one way of thinking.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is about belonging by choice: the friends you pick, the teams and networks you join, the causes you sign up to, the plans you make with others ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) on its cusp makes that circle wide and mixed. I read it as the friend from a language class, the colleague who moved abroad and still writes, the stranger you argued with after a talk and now meet every year. You tend to join a group for what it believes and drift away when it stops believing it, and you're the one who introduces people who ought to know each other. Hopes run large: a fairer world, a long journey, a project bigger than one life. When such a plan stalls, it's usually because it was built for a bigger life than the present one allows, and what helps is a first step, not a smaller dream. The risk is spreading yourself across too many causes, or gathering an audience when what you wanted was friends.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules this 11th house, and tradition gave it its joy here, in the house the old astrologers named the Good Spirit: the planet thought most at home among the people you choose is the one in charge. Its house shows where your friendships and causes grow, its sign how you give to them: the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Pisces, where Jupiter shares the rule with Neptune, sits in your 1st or 2nd house in every London chart, most often on the 2nd cusp: the people you belong with and what you earn then come under one planet.

About one birth in 11 has this cusp, at London and New York alike, in Placidus, the house system I use: a little above an even share, as on every cusp of the chart's eastern half, where Sagittarius moves slowly. [Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/) goes with it in about 2 London charts in 3, Aquarius in the rest; at New York the two split evenly. Saturn then rules your chart, sometimes with Uranus, and since Saturn also rules Aquarius, the 11th's natural sign, this house has an old claim on your chart ruler too: a measured front, a generous circle behind it. At London the 11th spans under 20° here, less than the sign itself, so Sagittarius often reaches a second cusp. In more than 4 London charts in 10 it opens the 12th as well, joining your friendships to your solitude ([Sagittarius on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-12th-house-cusp/)); in nearly 4 in 10 it climbs to the Midheaven, joining them to your work ([Sagittarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/); [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)).

A few hours either way moves all of this: before your birth, Sagittarius stood on the 12th cusp; after it, on the 10th. The minute is what tells these charts apart, and I won't work from a guess ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
