In my list, Venus turned back in Virgo only twice between 2000 and 2040, in the summers of 2007 and 2015, and each time it left the sign within its first degrees, backing on into Leo. Since 2023 the summer series begins in Leo, so Virgo’s last Venus retrograde of the list is already behind us.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) is mutable earth, ruled by Mercury, a sign that sorts, adjusts and wants things to work properly. Tradition counts it as Venus’s fall, the sign opposite its exaltation in Pisces: affection there is shown by small, useful attentions rather than grand gestures, and preferences are examined before they’re trusted. When [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) began in Virgo, the planet seemed from Earth to stop and turn back there, and the review fell on the details: what you do for the people you love, the standards you hold them and yourself to, the gap between being useful and being appreciated.

Both of Virgo’s retrogrades in my list started in the opening degrees and ended in Leo ([Venus retrograde in Leo](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-leo/)). The first ran from 27 July to 8 September 2007, from 2°57′ Virgo back to 16°35′ Leo; the second, the most recent, from 25 July to 6 September 2015, from 0°46′ Virgo back to 14°23′ Leo. Eight years on, in 2023, the station had slipped to 28°36′ Leo, and no later retrograde in my list reaches Virgo. On the other side, the autumn series turns direct in Libra, never lower than 20°27′ Libra, in 2034 ([Venus retrograde in Libra](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-libra/)).

If your own chart has Venus retrograde in Virgo, the placement is yours, a careful way of loving that checks before it trusts, and I read it apart from any transit ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Only [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) can place Virgo in one of your houses; the cusps depend on it, which is why I don’t read a chart that lacks it. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2007 and 2015 retrogrades began in the house with Virgo and spent most of their weeks in the house where Leo sits, so for these two I look at both.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/virgo-rising/):** Virgo rising: a neat, attentive manner; the care you take over appearances, and whether it still pleases you.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/virgo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you think is worth paying for; quality chosen over quantity, checked against what you enjoy.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/virgo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** helpful words; the advice you give the people you love, and whether they asked for it.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a well-kept home; the chores done out of love, and who notices them.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/virgo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** careful pleasures; a craft or a romance where you notice every detail, enjoyed rather than corrected.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/virgo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** Virgo in its natural house: service and daily work; the help you give colleagues, valued fairly. Tradition also gave this house illness; I read it for ordinary days and leave health to those trained in it ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
7. **[7th house](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Virgo on the Descendant, with Pisces rising: a partner who shows love by helping; who does what for whom, rebalanced.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/virgo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** trust built on reliability; the practical side of what you share, gone over kindly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/virgo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs tested against practice; a course chosen for its usefulness, reconsidered for its pleasure.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a reputation for doing things well; the work you’re valued for, and the work you value.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/virgo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** the friend who helps; favours given and received in a group, looked at for balance.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/virgo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** quiet acts of care nobody sees; what you do for others in private, and what you’d like done for you.

## What helps {#what-helps}

As Virgo’s ruler, your natal Mercury shows by its house where you tend to sort and grade what you love. Virgo’s Venus improves what it loves.

A retrograde here, as I read it, is a reminder to let some things be loved as they are, and that reminder doesn’t need a retrograde to come round again before you take it.
