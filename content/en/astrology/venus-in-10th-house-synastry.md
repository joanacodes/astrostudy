Their Venus in your 10th house is drawn to the person the world sees. Your work, your standing, the way you carry responsibility in public: this is where their affection and taste settle, and with them around you may find it a little easier to be proud of yourself.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

Your public life lives in [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/): your work and role, your reputation, the authority you carry, what you'd like to build. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) values and appreciates, and tends to make things pleasant. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), it brings their appreciation to your public self.

You may feel valued for your achievements, not just liked. They speak well of you to others, take pleasure in your successes, and may help with grace where you'd use force: a kind word to the right person, the right jacket for the interview, the gentle suggestion that softens your pitch. Being seen with them can feel like an asset.

Your public role attracts them. They enjoy your ambition, admire how you work and may be proud to be associated with you. Saturn rules [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), the 10th's natural sign, and lends this angular, very visible house a taste for loyalty and the long term.

Conjunct your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), their Venus sits at the very top of your chart, and their regard becomes part of how the world sees you.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Being admired for your role can blur into being loved for it. You may wonder whether their warmth would survive a change of career, a quiet year or a failure, and image can start to matter more than it should: how you look together at events, what people say.

They may feel like an accessory to your public life, brought out for dinners and left out of decisions, or sense that your work always comes first. Mixing affection and work can also put them in an awkward position with colleagues.

Nothing here promises promotion, fame or a match that suits your career. The 10th describes your public direction, and this overlay, the worth the two of you give it.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep a private life that doesn't depend on your public one, and let them see you without the title. Share credit and visibility fairly. With a partner, decide together how much of your life together is on show. With a friend, this is the one who celebrates every success and stays for the setbacks. At work, warmth between colleagues is a pleasure, though romance across a reporting line needs care and openness. In a family business, appreciation flows more easily when the roles are clear.

Swap places, your Venus in their 10th house, and it's their work and standing you admire.
