Aries is the first sign of the zodiac. The Sun enters it at the March equinox, the start of spring in the northern hemisphere, and the whole zodiac is counted from that point. It's the sign of beginnings, and wherever it falls in a chart, I read it as the place where you go first.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Aries somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it brings the same style: direct, quick, brave, a little impatient. Aries asks *what do I want, and can I go and get it now?* It's a [fire sign](/astrology/fire-signs/), like Leo and Sagittarius, so it runs on enthusiasm; and it's cardinal, one of the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/), so it starts things rather than keeps them going. I read the signs as a sequence, each one answering the last: after [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), where everything blurs and dissolves, Aries draws a line and starts again — and [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), next, makes something lasting out of what Aries began.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/)** — born between about 21 March and 19 April. You find out who you are by trying things, and courage is the muscle you keep training.
- **[Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/)** — feelings arrive fast and leave just as fast. You need to react before you can reflect, and you settle by moving, not by being soothed.
- **[Aries rising](/astrology/aries-rising/)** — you meet the world head on, and people notice your energy before anything else. Mars becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/). A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of the three is the whole of you. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So I read Aries through the whole chart: the planets in it, the house it opens, and where Mars sits.

## Its ruler: Mars {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, the one that carries the sign's business into the rest of the chart. For Aries that's [Mars](/astrology/mars/), so wherever your Mars sits, by sign and house, shows where your Aries energy actually goes. With Aries on the cusp of the 10th house and Mars in the 4th, say, the drive to make your mark draws its fuel from home and family. Tradition gave Mars a second sign, Scorpio, which modern astrologers hand to Pluto: more on [traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/).

Three more planets have a particular bond with Aries:

- The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is [exalted](/astrology/exaltation/) here, the old books' word for a guest of honour: strong and well received. The will to be yourself is at its simplest in Aries.
- [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is in its [fall](/astrology/fall/): patience is hard to find in a sign that wants things now. I read it as a pace to learn, not a flaw.
- [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite its own sign, Libra: love here is frank and impatient rather than diplomatic. It works differently, not worse.

## Planets in Aries {#planets-in-the-sign}

A planet in Aries takes on the sign's speed and honesty; the house it sits in shows where that plays out.

- **[Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/):** the Sun at its exaltation — a clear, simple will to begin, and to lead the way.
- **[Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/):** feelings that flare and pass quickly; quick to forgive, too, and in need of room to react.
- **[Mercury in Aries](/astrology/mercury-in-aries/):** a mind that goes straight to the point; you say it first and refine it later.
- **[Venus in Aries](/astrology/venus-in-aries/):** love that makes the first move; attraction is instant, and slow courtship is a skill you learn.
- **[Mars in Aries](/astrology/mars-in-aries/):** Mars at home — energy direct and at full strength, quick to start and quick to flare.
- **[Jupiter in Aries](/astrology/jupiter-in-aries/):** faith in going first; you grow by taking chances, and by encouraging others to take theirs.
- **[Saturn in Aries](/astrology/saturn-in-aries/):** confidence built slowly; trusting your own push, and acting without waiting for permission, come with time.
- **[Uranus in Aries](/astrology/uranus-in-aries/):** a generation's urge to break away and start afresh, shared by everyone born over about seven years; its house makes it personal.
- **[Neptune in Aries](/astrology/neptune-in-aries/):** ideals that want to act, not just dream; shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so I read it through its house.
- **[Pluto in Aries](/astrology/pluto-in-aries/):** a generation's deep need to start over and reclaim the will to act; shared over twelve years or more, made personal by its house.

## Aries on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Aries on the cusp — the starting line — of one of its twelve houses. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Aries opens a house, that's where you act first and ask questions later, and Mars, wherever it sits, says where the story goes next. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aries-rising/):** Aries rising — you meet life head on, frank and quick, and people see you as the one who gets things moving.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aries-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** you want to earn your own way, on your own initiative; money decisions come fast, and waiting before you spend is the skill to learn.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aries-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** plain, direct words; you think out loud, enjoy a good argument, and would rather say it than hint.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a base to set out from; you want your own say under your own roof.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aries-on-5th-house-cusp/):** play as a challenge — sport, competition, a crush pursued openly; creativity that comes in bursts.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aries-on-6th-house-cusp/):** you work fast, and best on your own initiative; routines you choose suit you better than routines you're handed.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aries-on-7th-house-cusp/):** drawn to frank, independent partners; a relationship that can argue and make up keeps you interested.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aries-on-8th-house-cusp/):** you meet crises head on; in intimacy and shared money, you want everything in the open, and quickly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aries-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs you've had to fight for or find yourself; you learn by doing, and travel on impulse.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/):** ambition that wants to lead, or to start its own thing; a public image as the one who begins.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aries-on-11th-house-cusp/):** in a group, you're the one who gets it moving; friendships built on shared projects and a little friendly rivalry.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aries-on-12th-house-cusp/):** drive kept private; you act best alone, and anger may be the feeling you find hardest to show.

## Aries in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Aries planets bring directness and initiative: the person who has them often makes the first move, says what they want, and expects the same frankness back. To the other person that can feel like fresh air or like pressure, depending on where those planets land in their chart and what they touch. Across the zodiac sits [Libra](/astrology/libra/), the opposite sign: Aries asks *what do I want?*, Libra *what do we want?* When one person's Aries planets face the other's Libra ones, each holds what the other is missing, and the pull runs both ways — attraction, and the odd stand-off. As for comparing Sun signs, [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind, so I never judge a pair by it.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Aries are aggressive.”** Aries is direct, and that isn't the same thing. The sign describes how someone moves — fast, frankly — not how kindly they treat people.
- **“Aries only think of themselves.”** Aries knows what it wants, which isn't the same as not caring what others want. The courage that goes first can go first for someone else.
- **“The stars have shifted, so you're really a Pisces.”** Western astrology uses the [tropical zodiac](/astrology/tropical-zodiac/), tied to the seasons: Aries begins at the March equinox, whichever constellation lies behind the Sun. And [Ophiuchus](/astrology/ophiuchus-and-the-13th-sign/) is a constellation, not a missing sign.
- **“Born on the cusp, you're a bit of Pisces too.”** At the minute you were born, the Sun was in one sign or the other; within a day of a change, your birth time settles which. More on being [born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/).
