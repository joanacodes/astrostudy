Pluto opposite your Mars sets another force across from yours. For two to three years the planet of depth and power faces your Mars from the far side of the chart, and other people's drive, ambition or anger becomes the measure against which you find out what your own strength is for.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) concentrates power and brings it into view. Your [Mars](/astrology/mars/) asserts, competes and defends. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates them through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) you tend to meet your drive in someone else's: a rival at work, a partner whose will is as strong as yours, a negotiation where both sides have something to lose. The opposition shows your force by contrast.

Empowerment isn't domination, and competition isn't hostility. The useful outcome of this opposition is strength that can be shared: two people pulling hard, in the same direction or in different ones, without either needing to flatten the other. What tends to change is proportion, knowing how much force a situation really needs. The other person's strength can turn out to be an ally, too: some of the best work of this transit is done alongside the very person whose will first felt like an obstacle.

Pluto is never violence or abuse presented as fate. If someone's force or temper frightens you, the transit is not the reason, and it is no reason to stay: tell someone you trust, and get help from people trained to give it. No transit forecasts an injury or an accident, either ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

Those born with [Mars opposite Pluto](/astrology/mars-opposite-pluto/) tend to meet strong wills early and often; the transit brings everyone else the same encounter for a season.

With Mars in the 1st and Pluto crossing the 7th ([Pluto transiting the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-7th-house/)), the force across from you often belongs to a partner or a close collaborator. The Ascendant and the Descendant that frame this pair are set by the hour of birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Allowing 1½° before exact and 1° after, as I do in timing, the opposition runs for about two years and eight months: about twenty-two months in the quickest passes, nearly four years in the slowest. Three passes in five bring three exact hits; the rest bring five.

Of Pluto's five contacts with Mars, the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-mars/)) is the one most people meet, about seven in eight, followed by the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-mars/)) and the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-mars/)), each reaching about three in four or more. The conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-mars/)) and this opposition come to fewer than half: more than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet the opposition by 80.

Leo, across the zodiac from Aquarius, is where Mars has to be for this opposition until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Decide what you are actually competing for. Under this opposition it is easy to win a contest you never wanted.

When two strong wills share a project, make the terms explicit: who decides what, and how disagreements get settled.

Step away from a confrontation that turns frightening. Strength includes leaving, and leaving early.
