*So are you two together, or what?* Some pairs get asked that for years, and enjoy not answering. Composite Lilith in Gemini puts a relationship's untamed side in its conversation: what it says only to itself, and its refusal to fit a single word.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) marks what a chart keeps hidden or won't have tamed, and in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), drawn from the midpoints of two birth charts, it describes that part of the relationship itself. [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), mutable air ruled by [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), sets it in words and questions. A bond with this Lilith often has a private language, nicknames, running jokes, messages nobody else could decode, and a curiosity others find a little improper: the questions it asks about people, ideas or ways of living that the families on both sides would rather leave alone. It resists labels too. Friends, partners, colleagues, accomplices: outsiders keep trying to sum it up, and it keeps slipping the description.

None of this needs to belong to either of you. Two quiet people can make a bond that never stops talking; two talkers can make one with a single subject it never raises. That missing subject is where this Lilith hides things: under wit, under speed, under a conversation that covers everything except the plain sentence.

About 1 pair in 12 has it, as for every sign. Two people born during the same Gemini stay of mean Lilith, February to November 1986 or January to September 1995, for example, share it. The geometry has a twist here. Every pair of positions has two [midpoints](/astrology/midpoints/), opposite each other, and the composite takes the nearer; when two natal Liliths are almost opposite, which happens to people born about four and a half years apart, both are nearly as near. Born on 15 January 1993, one person has Lilith at about 10° Pisces; born on 15 June 1997, the other has it at about 10° Virgo, and their composite Lilith sits at about 10° Gemini. Had the second person been born on 1 July instead, it would sit at about 11° Sagittarius. When the two Liliths are within about a tenth of a degree of exactly opposite, even the birth times can decide which side wins.

On a single person, [Lilith in Gemini](/astrology/lilith-in-gemini/) is a curiosity that went further than it was allowed to. Here the curiosity is shared, and each of you may notice it first in the other's questions. Next door, [composite Lilith in Taurus](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-taurus/) refuses without a word, and [composite Lilith in Cancer](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-cancer/) shelters what it hides rather than talking around it.

Its hard part is that cleverness makes a good hiding place. A pair like this can discuss everything except the one thing, or tell the story of the relationship so well that the story replaces it. What helps is unglamorous: saying the plain sentence now and then, in the plainest words, and letting the private language carry the dull facts as well as the jokes.

I don't draw composite charts; this is the technique's own reading, which its users weigh against both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It predicts no secret and no ending. And as with any chart of two people, it's for a pair who both said yes to looking ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
