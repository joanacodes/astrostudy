Belief gets practised rather than preached once the ruler of your 9th house lands in the 6th, among the tasks, habits and routines of an ordinary day. What you study tends to turn into a skill.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Whatever sign is on the cusp of your [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), its ruler carries higher study, long travel, philosophy and faith to the house where it sits ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). In the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/), life is counted in weekdays: tasks rather than titles, the routines that hold you up, the colleagues beside you, the way you look after your body.

Horary astrology's old lists show what this joins. They put universities and every student of higher learning in the 9th, from doctors and lawyers to priests and astrologers, and put employees and the workplace in the 6th. With the ruler of one living in the other, study and work run together: the apprenticeship, the evening degree, the qualification earned on the job, the language learned because the work required it. Many people with this placement work in teaching, training, travel or research, and many more bring a philosophy to an ordinary job, so the way they file, cook or care is itself a statement of belief.

Belief, here, is something you do: the morning meditation, the weekly class, the standard you keep when nobody checks. It suits a long apprenticeship to anything. What it needs is a sense of proportion. A belief turned into a set of rules can become a list of things you've failed to do, and serving a cause can slide into overwork. Tradition also called the 6th Bad Fortune and gave it illness. I don't look for illness in a chart, and no chart can diagnose one: questions about your health are for a doctor ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

Mercury gives the most literal version. With Virgo on the 9th cusp, Mercury rules it, and in the 6th it sits in the house the natural zodiac gives to Virgo, so method is how you reach meaning: notes, checklists, careful reading, a faith in getting the details right ([Mercury in the 6th house](/astrology/mercury-in-6th-house/)). The Moon, ruling a Cancer 9th from here, finds comfort in daily rituals that came from far away, the recipe or the prayer carried over from another country.

A ruler in the 6th had already crossed the Descendant when you were born: it had set a short while before and was heading down towards the lowest point of the chart. Had you been born a little earlier, it would still stand above the Descendant, in the 7th, where meaning comes through a partner ([Ruler of the 9th in the 7th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)). Later, the 5th takes it, and study turns into play ([Ruler of the 9th in the 5th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)). Placidus, the house system I use, works those cusps out from the birth time, and a time rounded to the hour can put the ruler in the wrong house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Scorpio on the 9th cusp puts the house in the hands of Mars, its traditional ruler, and of Pluto, its modern one ([Scorpio on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/)); I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Mars in the 6th works at its convictions like a trade, with energy, and with some impatience for colleagues who don't. Pluto, from its own house, deepens and sometimes overturns what you think your work is for.
