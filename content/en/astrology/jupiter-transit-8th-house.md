The 8th house is where a bond gets serious, and Jupiter's year there widens what you share: trust, intimacy, the money and the obligations you hold with other people. It's a year for depth more than for display.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens and makes sense of things; the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is what happens after you let someone in: shared resources, intimacy, trust, what you owe and are owed, and the changes that closeness brings. In Jupiter's year there, trust can grow where it's earned, sharing can feel more generous in both directions, and you may be more willing to look at what lies underneath a bond, including the parts you usually leave alone.

It's also a house of transitions, and Jupiter's way of meeting change is to look for what it means. Where something in your shared life is shifting, the year tends to help you understand it rather than only endure it.

With your Pluto or your Mars in the 8th from birth, the two rulers of Scorpio, the house's sign in the natural order (I read both), Jupiter's pass can bring depth and nerve to a conversation you've been putting off.

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance, and many books still open with them. I don't read death in a transit, for anyone ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and Jupiter here promises no legacy and no sum of money. As transits do, this [transit](/astrology/transits/) brings out what your chart already holds: here, the way you share, not what you'll receive.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Here's a curiosity of the count. Your 2nd and your 8th face each other across the chart and are always the same size, yet in Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude Jupiter's stays in them come out a little different: about a year in the 8th, most often between about 11 months and nearly 2 years, against a little over a year in the 2nd, whose long end stops at about 22 months. At New York's latitude the 8th's long end is about 22 months too.

Where your 8th begins comes from your birth time, which is why I ask for it before anything else ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Jupiter comes into the 8th from your 7th, where the year was about meeting people ([Jupiter transiting the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-7th-house/)), and goes on to your 9th, where what you've lived through looks for a meaning ([Jupiter transiting the 9th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-9th-house/)).

Find the sign on your 8th house cusp in its element. Water, the 8th's element in the natural order: Scorpio from September 2029 to October 2030, Pisces from April 2033 to April 2034, Cancer from May 2037 to June 2038. Fire: Leo from June 2026 to July 2027, Sagittarius from October 2030 to November 2031, Aries from April 2034 to April 2035. Earth: Virgo from July 2027 to August 2028, Capricorn from November 2031 to November 2032, Taurus from April 2035 to May 2036. Air: Libra from August 2028 to September 2029, Aquarius from April 2032 to December 2033, with some back and forth in 2032, and Gemini from May 2036 to May 2037.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Talk about what's shared with the people you share it with: money, time, care, plans. Jupiter in the 8th makes these conversations easier to start, and honesty is what turns closeness into trust.

Look at what you owe and what you're owed, in both senses. Some debts are on paper; others are favours, silences, old promises. A year like this is good for settling the ones you can.

Let trust grow where it's earned, and only there. Depth is a gift, not an obligation, and the 8th is the house where you learn the difference.
