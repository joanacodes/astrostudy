For a few months, twice in each round of Saturn, the planet of discipline and the part of you that dreams keep the same pace. Saturn's trine to your natal Neptune is rarely dramatic: it's the season when the hour you set aside for something you love actually gets kept.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) structures and sustains; [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines, feels with others and reaches for meaning beyond the everyday. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets them reinforce each other without either giving up its nature. During this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the gap between what inspires you and the way you spend your days tends to narrow: a corner of a room becomes a studio, a choir rehearsal becomes fixed, a meditation practice finds its morning, care for someone takes a shape you can sustain.

What I watch for is drift. Flow isn't passivity, and a trine between these two can be so comfortable that it turns into pleasant daydreaming with a timetable. It helps most when you ask something specific of it: a piece finished, a practice kept for the whole stretch, a cause given its weekly hours.

The house of your Neptune says what kind of dream is involved: in the 12th, a quiet inner life; in the 3rd, words and images. The house Saturn is crossing at the time shows where the steadiness comes from. Both rest on your birth time, the minute a chart is built on ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Natal [Saturn trine Neptune](/astrology/saturn-trine-neptune/) makes this cooperation a lifelong habit; the transit lends it for a season.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Saturn keeps this trine for about nine months, counted with my orbs from 1½° before exact to 1° after. A little over half the time, its retrograde loops across the degree for three exact hits, in about nine and a half months; with a single hit, the window can be as short as three weeks.

The trines come in pairs about ten years apart, one before and one after Saturn opposes your Neptune ([transiting Saturn opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-neptune/)); some twenty years then pass, across Saturn's crossing of it ([transiting Saturn conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-neptune/)), before the next pair. Most people have five by 80, some six. Neptune moves about two degrees a year, so people born in the same year share the ages: the first came at about 13 for those born in 1985, about 17 for 1945, and within two years of birth for 1965 and 1975, when the trine was forming in the sky. Saturn's crossing of Aries, until April 2028, trines all of Sagittarius: with Neptune in Sagittarius, as for most people born between 1970 and 1984, your trine falls now, at about 51 if you were born in 1975.

[Transiting Saturn square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-neptune/) comes two or three years before the first trine of a pair and after the second; [transiting Saturn sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-neptune/) lies about five years further out.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Set up the place before the inspiration: the desk cleared, the instrument out of its case, the cushion by the window. The trine makes the setting up easy; imagination tends to do the rest.

Choose one compassionate commitment and give it a schedule you can keep for years rather than weeks.

Show someone what you've been making or practising. A trine's gains last when they leave the privacy of a dream and meet an ordinary day.
