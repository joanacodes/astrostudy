Pluto sextile Chiron gives the place in you that stays tender a quiet access to depth and renewal. Nothing forces the change; the sextile only makes it available. Three groups of births carry it in the count, the first over several years in the early 1930s.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms: it clears away what is no longer alive, regenerates, and returns a sturdier kind of strength. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where something stays uneven, together with the adaptations you build there: a different tool, a gentler pace, experience you can pass on to someone facing the same thing. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity: depth is offered to the tender place, and the tender place gives depth a purpose.

In plain words, you can renew the way you live with a difficulty, if you choose to. Letting go of an old workaround, one that cost more than it helped, often opens a better one. Pluto's part is the honesty: seeing which habits around the sore place still help, and which only guard an old fear. You may be good at helping others through deep changes without pushing them, because you know change can be gentle. The opening is real, and it waits: it works when it's used.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Within 6°, about 1 person in 18 born from 1930 to 2009 has this sextile, in three groups. The first was born from March 1930 to May 1933 and from December 1933 to February 1934; the second in three short spells, January–February 1945, August–December 1945 and May–August 1946; the third in two, October 1994 to February 1995 and July to October 1995. The first group leads up to the pair's meeting of 1940–42, the second follows it, and the third leads up to the meeting of 1999–2000.

The lengths tell Chiron's story. In the early 1930s it was moving slowly enough for the sextile to hold for over three years; in the mid-1940s and mid-1990s, near the quick part of its orbit, it passed through in a few months.

Each group shares its sextile, which becomes personal through its houses, usually two apart. Chiron in the 2nd with Pluto in the 4th, for instance, might renew an old doubt about your worth by going back to your roots. Without the hour of birth, that stays a guess ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The rest of the pair, as the count found it:

- **[Pluto conjunct Chiron](/astrology/pluto-conjunct-chiron/):** the meeting these spells lead up to and away from, depth and the tender place as one.
- **[Pluto square Chiron](/astrology/pluto-square-chiron/):** clearing away and accommodating at odds with each other.
- **[Pluto trine Chiron](/astrology/pluto-trine-chiron/):** renewal and adaptation in easy step.
- **[Pluto opposite Chiron](/astrology/pluto-opposite-chiron/):** the tender place and the depths across the chart from each other.

Between two charts, [Pluto sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-sextile-chiron-synastry/) can be a quietly helpful contact: one person's depth gives the other room to change around a sore spot, at its own pace.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-pluto/) comes twice in each Chiron round of about 50 years, at ages that differ from one generation to another. Born with the sextile, you meet it once more at the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, when Chiron is back on its birth degree. Pluto's own sextile to natal Chiron, when it comes, is a long, slow affair.

Weather, as ever, not a forecast: a season when an old way of coping is easier to let go of, if you want to.
