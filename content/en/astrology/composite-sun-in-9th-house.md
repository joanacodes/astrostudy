Of all the houses, the 9th is the one tradition gave the Sun as its joy. A composite Sun there puts the relationship's centre in the house of the far horizon: travel, study, belief, and the long search for what things mean.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In a birth chart, a Sun in the 9th tells you roughly when you were born: early in the afternoon, just after the Sun had passed the top of the sky. A composite Sun in [the 9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) tells no such thing, since a chart made of midpoints has no hour of the day; [the Davison chart](/astrology/davison-chart/), cast for a real moment halfway between two births, is the relationship chart whose Sun's house does mark a time. The houses of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) come instead from both birth times and a choice of method, midpoints of your natal cusps or a composite Midheaven at the place where you live ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and a [Sun](/astrology/sun/) near a cusp may sit in different houses by each.

A shared worldview is what the 9th gives this Sun to work with. A relationship like this is often organised around something the two of you are trying to understand: a language you've learned side by side since the class where you met, a faith or philosophy shaped to fit you both, a subject you teach together, the years spent living in a country neither of you was born in. It's revived by discovery, and its purpose tends to be understanding, of the world and of each other.

The old name of this cadent house, whose natural sign is Sagittarius, was the God, and tradition gave it to the Sun as its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), a sense that the Sun is at ease in this part of life. Meaning is one person's search in the [Sun in the 9th house](/astrology/sun-in-9th-house/), and a gift from one to the other in [their Sun in your 9th house](/astrology/sun-in-9th-house-synastry/), where someone widens your horizons. In the composite, the horizon belongs to both, and people who never wanted to leave home can start planning, together, how to.

The far horizon can make the near one look dull. A bond centred on the next journey or the next idea can treat ordinary life as an interval, and a worldview built together can harden into certainty, with both of you agreeing too quickly. Truth-seeking isn't the same as having the answer; keeping a few questions open between you does this bond more good than settling them. Nothing here forecasts where the two of you will live, or how long the relationship will last.

The [composite Sun in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-8th-house/) before it centres a bond on trust and depth; the [composite Sun in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-10th-house/) after it, on its place in the world. My own readings leave composites aside, so this page reports the technique as its users explain it, always with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) in view. A bond that cares about truth would want this chart made openly, with both people knowing ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
