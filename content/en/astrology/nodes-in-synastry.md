Between two charts, the lunar nodes are where one person's planets meet the other's sense of direction. When someone's planet lands on your North Node or your South Node, the contact tends to feel either like something you already know by heart or like a push towards something you're still learning.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Every chart has a pair of nodes, always exactly opposite each other: the [nodal axis](/astrology/nodal-axis/). In [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), two charts are laid side by side and their contacts read one by one ([synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/)). A node contact is one person's planet or angle in aspect to the other's axis: conjunct the North Node, and so opposite the South; conjunct the South Node, and so opposite the North; or square to both, across the middle of the axis.

I read the two ends the same way in synastry as in one chart. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) describes capacities a person consolidated early and can rely on; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), a less familiar direction they grow into through practice. So a node contact concerns the node person's development first. The other person brings a function, their Sun's sense of purpose, their Venus's way of valuing, their Mars's initiative, to the place where the node person is either at ease or at work.

That makes these contacts lopsided by nature. Their Moon on your North Node says something about your growth and their way of caring; it says nothing about their nodes.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

My synastry orbs follow the wider of the two bodies, so with a node the planet's own orb applies: 8° for the Sun and the Moon, 6° for Mercury, Venus and Mars, in full for a conjunction or an opposition and a little narrower for the other aspects. Counted on 3,000,000 random pairs of people born between 1930 and 2009, with the true node, those orbs give:

- **The Sun:** within 8° of the other's North Node in about 1 pair in 12, either way round, and of the South Node in about 1 pair in 11.
- **The Moon:** the same figures.
- **Mercury, Venus and Mars:** within 6° of either node in about 1 pair in 15, for each planet and each end.

These contacts are ordinary, then, not the rare mark of a special bond.

Contacts between the two axes are another matter. The nodes take about 18.6 years to go round the zodiac, so everyone born within the same year and a half or so shares the same nodal signs. Two people born about nine years apart have reversed axes, the same geometry as the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/) in a single life; born about 18½ years apart, their axes line up again. The same goes for the nodes with Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Chiron or Lilith. In my synastry readings I count only contacts with at least one personal body: the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. The others I read through the houses they fall in, as two eras meeting more than two people.

The mean and the true node sit a degree or two apart at most, enough to make or unmake a contact at the edge of its orb, so I look at both ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). Both birth times matter too. The Moon covers about 13° a day, the angles a degree every four minutes, and the houses where one person's nodes land in the other's chart ([house overlays](/astrology/house-overlays/)) need both times: [their North Node in your 1st house](/astrology/north-node-in-1st-house-synastry/) is a different contact from the same node in your 12th.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I read each node contact in both directions, alongside the rest of the two charts. [Sun conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) puts one person's centre exactly where the other is learning; [Mars square North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-north-node-synastry/) puts one person's drive across the other's axis, pressing on old habits and new practice at once. Neither tells you whether a relationship will last.

What tends to help, in a couple, a friendship, a working relationship or a family: the node person sets the pace of their own growth, and the planet person offers rather than steers. Ease at the South Node is a resource, the comfort of being understood without explanation; it only turns into a rut when nothing new is ever asked of either of you. Those are the four relationships my [synastry readings](/synastry/) are built for, always between adults.

A synastry involves someone else's chart, and so their consent: [why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“A node contact proves you knew each other in a past life.”** No chart can show a past life. I read the familiarity of a South Node contact in this one: two ways of being that fit without effort.
- **“A planet on your North Node means you're meant to be together.”** The North Node describes development, not destiny. The contact shows how someone meets your growth, not where the relationship is going.
- **“South Node contacts keep you stuck.”** Familiar ground is useful ground. It holds you back only if neither of you ever steps off it.
- **“Matching nodes mean a soul connection.”** Everyone born in the same year and a half or so has the same nodal signs; a matching axis is shared by a whole cohort.
