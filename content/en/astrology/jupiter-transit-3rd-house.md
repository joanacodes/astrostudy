Jupiter's year in your 3rd house brings its growth down to the scale of an ordinary week: the messages and errands, the conversation on the way somewhere, the thing you look up and then can't stop reading about. Big questions, for once, arrive through small doors.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) synthesises: it takes experience and makes it mean something. The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is where information comes and goes, in talk, reading, short trips and the first kind of learning, and where you meet the people close at hand, siblings and neighbours included. Tradition called it the Goddess, the house where the Moon rejoices. With Jupiter crossing it, I read a year of appetite for the near world: a course you finally take, a subject that keeps pulling you back, a conversation that changes how you see something you thought you knew.

There's a tension built into it, and it's a useful one. Jupiter likes the whole picture; the 3rd house deals in pieces, many of them, quickly. So the year can scatter you across ten interests, or it can be the year the ten interests turn out to be one. What it asks is that you let information become understanding.

Planets you were born with in the 3rd meet Jupiter first. A natal Mercury there, in the house of Gemini in the natural order, tends to fill those weeks with words: writing, explaining, being asked to say what you think.

As with any [transit](/astrology/transits/), nothing here comes from outside your chart. Jupiter brings out the curiosity it already holds and gives it more room for a while.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In charts drawn at London's latitude, in Placidus, a Jupiter stay in the 3rd usually lasts a little over a year, most often between about 11 months and 22; across all twelve houses the middle is about a year and the long end nearly 2, so the 3rd's stays vary a little less than those of the 1st or the 7th. New York's latitude gives the 3rd the same figures. The stay ends when Jupiter reaches your Imum Coeli, the cusp of the 4th, and both of its edges come from your birth time: without the time there are no cusps to read, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Before this comes [Jupiter transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-2nd-house/); after it, [Jupiter transiting the 4th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-4th-house/).

Year by year, the sign on the cusp of your 3rd tells you when. Jupiter is in Leo from June 2026; in July 2027 it moves into Virgo, in August 2028 into Libra, in September 2029 into Scorpio, in October 2030 into Sagittarius and in November 2031 into Capricorn. In 2032 it touches Aquarius in April, falls back, and leaves Capricorn for good in November; in 2033 it touches Pisces in April and leaves Aquarius in December. April 2034 brings Aries, April 2035 Taurus, May 2036 Gemini, the 3rd house's own sign in the natural order, May 2037 Cancer and June 2038 Leo again. Then add the degree: Jupiter enters your 3rd when it reaches the exact degree of the cusp.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick one line of enquiry and follow it all the way. A year of Jupiter in the 3rd is generous with beginnings, and the growth is in the pieces you connect, not in the number you collect.

Write things down. Notes, letters, a page a day: the 3rd house thinks by putting things into words, and Jupiter helps the words go somewhere.

If there's a sibling, a cousin or a neighbour you've lost touch with, this is a natural year to try again, on terms that suit you both. And when an idea arrives with great enthusiasm, give it a week before you sign up for all of it: by then you'll know which part was yours.
