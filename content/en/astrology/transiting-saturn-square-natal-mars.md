About seven years after Saturn crosses your Mars, and again about seven years before it returns, the two make a square. It can feel like a handbrake left on: you want to move, and something insists on a slower, more careful way.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) wants to act now: to start, assert, compete, defend. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) wants things done properly and in their own time. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates them through constructive friction, the kind that makes both adapt. Friction isn't failure, and the square is no punishment for wanting things.

In practice it can look like delays, paperwork, a body that asks for more recovery than you'd like, an authority that says not yet. Impatience tends to rise, and assertion can tip into irritability. Assertion isn't aggression, though, and this square asks for the first without the second: say plainly what you want, then build the route to it rather than charging the wall. On the way out from the conjunction, the square checks whether what you started is built to last or only to start; on the way back, it asks which fights you're still having out of habit.

Some people fear an accident or an injury under this square. I don't read transits for that, and none is foretold here ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); what the square does describe is effort that needs pacing.

With your Mars in the 10th, effort aimed at work and standing meets resistance, and Saturn is usually crossing your 1st or your 7th ([Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/)), so your own limits or an associate's terms set the pace. Those houses come from your birth time, and I don't read the transit without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If your chart has [Mars square Saturn](/astrology/mars-square-saturn/), this friction is an old acquaintance, and you probably learned long ago how to pace a fight.

With Saturn in Aries until April 2028, the squares falling now are to Mars in Cancer, where tradition puts Mars in its fall, and to Mars in Capricorn, where it puts Mars in its exaltation: the same transit, met from Mars's weakest and strongest signs.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, the square usually holds for about nine months. In a little over half of these [transits](/astrology/transits/) Saturn's retrograde loop brings it over the exact square three times, across about nine and a half months; a single crossing at full speed can be over in three weeks. Either way it outlasts any Mars-sized burst of effort, which is rather the point.

Twice a round gives five or six squares by 80, for everyone. The first sits between [transiting Saturn sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-mars/) and [transiting Saturn trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-mars/), about seven years after [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-mars/); the second follows [transiting Saturn opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-mars/) and comes about seven years before the conjunction returns.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Find the slower route that still gets there. Under this square, the long way round is often the one that arrives.

Learn the rules you keep running into. Knowing exactly what's in the way turns a wall into a set of steps.

Spend frustration on effort you choose. A hard training session, a heavy job finished, a difficult conversation held calmly: friction needs somewhere to go.
