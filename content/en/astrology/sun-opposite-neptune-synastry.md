One of you draws lines; the other sees past them. Across two charts, one person's Sun and the other's Neptune sit six signs apart, facing each other across the zodiac, and a clear outline faces a wide horizon.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity, purpose and the outline a person shows the world. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination, compassion and the dissolving of boundaries that aren't needed. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) holds them in polarity, so that each throws the other into relief and the meeting point has to be found. Here, more than in the pair's other [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the two ends are easy to see.

Holding the Sun, you meet an openness you may not have. The other's imagination and compassion soften your edges and show you what your certainty leaves out. You may also find yourself becoming the definite one, deciding for two.

Holding Neptune, you meet a definiteness you can lean on. The Sun person's clarity gives your ideals somewhere to land, and you may admire it the way people admire a skill they lack. With them, a vague hope can turn into a plan.

What flows is a kind of completion: between them, the pair can both imagine and decide.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Defining and dissolving pull against each other. The more the Sun person decides, the more the Neptune person yields, and each can start to resent the role. From the Sun end, the other can look hard to read, never quite saying what they want; from the Neptune end, the Sun person can look rigid, deaf to nuance. Each is seeing the end they don't hold. Compassion isn't self-erasure, so the Neptune person's giving needs a limit; a clear outline isn't a wall, so the Sun person's certainty needs some give.

Neptune's sign, which lasts about fourteen years, is shared with the Neptune person's whole generation. What ties the opposition to this pair is the Sun, sitting six signs away because of one particular birthday.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Visit each other's end, in turn. The Neptune person can state a preference and hold it; the Sun person can leave a decision open for a while, and live with not knowing yet. In a couple, alternate who chooses; between friends, one plans and the other dreams, then swap; in a family, the practical sibling and the dreamer can trust each other's half; at work, the strategist and the visionary can treat each other as necessary rather than difficult.

About 1 pair of people in 11 has this opposition, one way round or the other, within 8°. Both birth times are my starting point. In a few hours the Sun covers only a fraction of a degree, too little to change the opposition, but the times set which two areas of life the ends fall into, and so where the seesaw is felt ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The neighbouring angles: [Sun conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-neptune-synastry/) merges the outline and the ideal, [Sun square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-neptune-synastry/) makes them rub, and [Sun trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-neptune-synastry/) and [Sun sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-neptune-synastry/) let them cooperate. In one chart, [Sun opposite Neptune](/astrology/sun-opposite-neptune/) is a single person learning where they end and others begin. Between two, each holds one side of that line, which is why I take it on only when both people are part of the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
