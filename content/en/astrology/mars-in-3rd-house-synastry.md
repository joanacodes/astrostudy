Their Mars in your 3rd house brings another person's drive into the comings and goings of your days: conversations, messages, errands, the way your mind works on the move. Talk between you rarely stays idle for long; it turns into a plan, a debate or a dare.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is effort and assertion, the push behind anything you set out to do. [The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is where you think out loud, learn by doing and move around your neighbourhood; it also holds brothers, sisters, cousins and neighbours, as you experience those bonds. Their Mars in this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) puts force behind your everyday words.

You may speak up faster with them: make the call, send the message, say what you think while the meeting is still going. They sharpen your arguments by pushing back on them, and learning something practical can suddenly feel urgent. Errands get done; short trips happen on a whim.

For them, your quickness is a sparring partner. They enjoy testing ideas on you, and their plans get going in conversation, often halfway through a sentence. Like every cadent house, the 3rd adjusts and passes things on, and [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), its natural sign, adds curiosity and speed; its old name is the Goddess, and the Moon was said to rejoice there. Across the wheel lies the 9th, the house of beliefs, and the 3rd is where a conviction gets tried out first, over coffee or in a message. Late in your 3rd, their Mars draws near your [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the base of your chart, and the talk starts to reach home ground.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Words can come out sharper than either of you means them. You may feel interrupted, talked over or argued at, or find that every chat becomes a debate with a winner. Messages fly too fast: a line typed in irritation lands before anyone can take it back.

From their side, your words cut back. They may feel corrected on details, or find that your quickness slips out from under every challenge they make, so they push harder to be heard, and a friendly argument climbs a notch each time.

If they're your brother, your sister or your neighbour, the overlay describes how the two of you talk and get around each other now, as adults. It doesn't settle who started what, years ago.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give arguments an off switch. One subject at a time, a pause before replying to anything heated, and the right to say "let me think about it": simple rules let the spark stay a spark. In a couple, keep serious disagreements out of text messages; between friends, banter works as long as either of you can call time; between colleagues, argue with the proposal rather than the person; between adult siblings, notice the old roles of who explains and who interrupts.

From the other chair, it's your Mars that falls in their 3rd house, and your words they hear as the quick, sharp ones. [Their Mars in your 2nd house](/astrology/mars-in-2nd-house-synastry/) pushes at what you value; [their Mars in your 4th house](/astrology/mars-in-4th-house-synastry/) carries that energy home.

Their Mars spends six or seven weeks in each sign, so between the morning and the evening of their birthday it hardly moves. The house it lands in is a question for your birth time, which sets every cusp in Placidus, the house system I use. I still don't read the two of you without both times, because their houses hold the other half ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). And if they would rather not take part, a reading of your own chart is the better place to start ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#instead)).
