Chiron on your Venus reaches what you value and how you let yourself be valued: your taste, your pleasures, the way you give and receive. Once or twice in a life, an old sore spot there comes close enough to see, along with what you've learned by living around it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) evaluates and prefers: what draws you, what you find beautiful, what you're glad to give and to be given. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a sensitivity that stayed and the adjustments you made around it. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), Chiron sits on your Venus's own degree, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your likings and that old sensitivity act as one. A taste you kept quiet about after someone laughed at it can come back to mind. So can the belief that being liked has to be earned, or that a pleasure must be paid for with a little guilt. Pleasure isn't indulgence, and this transit often makes that easier to believe.

The skill is there as well. People with a tender Venus tend to notice who in a room feels out of place, and how to make them welcome, because they remember the feeling. The conjunction asks you to turn some of that generosity inward: to choose what you like without checking first, and to receive kindness without rushing to repay it.

It brings no romance and ends none. Venus covers relating far beyond love, and this conjunction describes how you value and let yourself be valued, which any life can hold. With Venus in the 2nd, Chiron crosses it during its stay in that house ([Chiron transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-2nd-house/)): what you think you're worth and what you allow yourself to have come up together, though nothing here forecasts your income. Placing the house needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Venus conjunct Chiron](/astrology/venus-conjunct-chiron/) in your chart, this tenderness in your likings goes back to the start; the transit lights it from outside for a while.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted with my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, nearly two passes in three take Chiron over your Venus three times, over about ten and a half months. About one in three is a single crossing of some three weeks; about one in twenty crosses five times, over twenty-one months or so.

Venus can sit up to two signs from your Sun, so this conjunction can come years before or after Chiron's conjunction with your Sun ([transiting Chiron conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-sun/)): while Chiron is slow, up to about ten years apart. Over eighty years, Chiron's round of about fifty brings it to your Venus once or twice, twice for most people. Its other contacts come at other distances: the sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-venus/)) as a practical opening, the square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-venus/)) as friction in the give and take, the trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-venus/)) as ease, about three times each, and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-venus/)) through someone close, once or twice.

From 2027 to 2033 Chiron crosses Taurus, Venus's own sign, and conjoins any Venus there: one that belongs to someone born between about early March and early July.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Enjoy one thing you like without explaining why you like it.

Put something you find beautiful where you'll see it every day, whatever anyone else makes of it.

Choose one thing for yourself this week, a meal, a colour, an evening, without asking anyone's opinion first.
