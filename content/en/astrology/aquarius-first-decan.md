Aquarius opens under Venus, with the sign's two rulers, Saturn and Uranus, sharing the decan in the modern scheme. One old list gives these ten degrees to Justice herself, and Pluto is walking through them now.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

Venus takes the first face of [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), following the Sun's face at the end of Capricorn ([Capricorn, third decan](/astrology/capricorn-third-decan/)) in the [Chaldean order](/astrology/chaldean-order/). The run of faces here copies Cancer's: Venus, then Mercury ([Aquarius, second decan](/astrology/aquarius-second-decan/)), then the Moon ([faces](/astrology/faces/)). For [Venus in Aquarius](/astrology/venus-in-aquarius/), then, the opening ten degrees are a face of its own.

In the modern scheme a sign's opening belongs to its ruler, and Aquarius has two, Saturn in the tradition and Uranus in modern tropical astrology. I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and here they sit beside Venus: the planet of what we value, next to the planet of structure and the planet of change. I read the decan as fairness worked out for a group: what is valued, how it is shared, and what has to change for it to be shared well.

## Its image {#its-image}

Cosmas of Maiuma gives these degrees to Dike, Justice: the Greek goddess of fair judgement, shown as a young woman carrying a balance and wearing a laurel wreath, whose scales were once thought to be the constellation Libra. On a decan whose face belongs to Venus, the ruler of Libra, that is a striking pair, and I take it as one of the old lists' happier accidents.

Wallis Budge's Egyptian name here is Sasa-sert, the second of three neighbouring names built on sert. [Firmicus Maternus](/astrology/firmicus-maternus/) gave two names, Oro or Asoer, and Joseph Scaliger's list later fused them into one, Oroasoer. The Hermetic lists call the decan Isi in Greek and Ularis in Latin; Athanasius Kircher, in 1652, Brondeus.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) in its first ten days, about 20 to 29 January, falls in this decan, and a birthday on 19 or 20 January may still belong to Capricorn. Under Venus's face and the sign's two rulers, I read the shade as ideas about fairness that want company: friendship, causes, a group held together by something it values. Venus itself, placed here, holds its own face and answers to the sign's two rulers, and I read it as affection that likes room: friendship first, closeness by agreement.

[Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/) in its first decan takes only about 26 minutes to rise in London and 28 in Paris. The bright star [Altair](/astrology/altair/), the Eagle, sits at 2°08′ Aquarius, where a planet or an angle can share its degree, though the star stands some 29° north of the Sun's path, so the two meet only by number.

[Uranus in Aquarius](/astrology/uranus-in-aquarius/), at home in the modern scheme, crossed these degrees between 1995 and 1998, and Neptune followed from 1998 to early 2003. Pluto entered them in 2023 and, after going back and forth over the border with Capricorn in 2023 and 2024 ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)), is crossing them now, until about 2030 ([Pluto in Aquarius](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius/)). For anyone with planets here, that's weather, not a forecast.
