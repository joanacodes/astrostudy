In forty years of Venus’s retrogrades, from 2000 to 2040, Aquarius has a single brush with one: on 24 December 2005, Venus stopped at 1°28′ Aquarius and then backed out of the sign. Of the signs Venus turns back in at all, it is the only one with just one retrograde in my list.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) is fixed air with two rulers, Saturn in the tradition and Uranus in modern tropical astrology, and I read both. Venus there values freedom within closeness, friendship as much as romance, and tastes that are its own rather than the crowd’s. When [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) begins in Aquarius, the planet seems from Earth to halt there and back towards Capricorn, and the review is of independence in love: how much room you want, which friendships carry real affection, which of your unusual preferences you would keep if nobody noticed them.

The one instance in my list ran from 24 December 2005 to 3 February 2006. It stationed at 1°28′ Aquarius, slipped back into Capricorn early on and spent most of its weeks there, turning direct at 16°01′ Capricorn ([Venus retrograde in Capricorn](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-capricorn/)). Eight years later the station had moved to 28°58′ Capricorn, and the winter series has stayed out of Aquarius since. On the other side, the spring retrogrades still turn direct more than twenty degrees into Pisces in 2033 ([Venus retrograde in Pisces](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-pisces/)). So no other Venus retrograde reaches Aquarius before the end of 2040.

As a birth placement, Venus retrograde in Aquarius is affection that keeps its independence while it decides, and I read it for itself ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house with Aquarius on it changes from chart to chart with the time of birth, so this list needs [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) before it means anything; without it I don’t read the chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2005 retrograde began in the Aquarius house and soon moved on into the Capricorn one.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aquarius-rising/):** Aquarius rising: a friendly, independent manner; the distance you keep at first, and what it protects.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** unconventional values; what you spend on causes, gadgets or ideas, weighed against what you enjoy.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** frank, curious exchanges; a talk among friends that changed how you see love.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an unusual home; the freedom each person under the roof needs, renegotiated.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romance as friendship; an affection that began in a group, looked at again.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work among like minds; a colleague who became a friend, valued for it.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius on the Descendant, with Leo rising: a partner who is also a friend; closeness and independence, rebalanced.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what’s held in common, held loosely; what you share in a group or a partnership, and on what terms.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** ideals and beliefs about the future; a cause you loved, revisited with clearer eyes.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** work that serves a wider purpose; the public cause you stand for, valued again.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius in its natural house: friends, groups and shared hopes; which friendships still carry affection.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private ideals; a hope for the future you keep to yourself, given room.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look at both rulers: the house of your natal Saturn shows where you hold to your commitments, the house of your natal Uranus where you insist on freedom, and Venus in Aquarius lives between the two.

With no retrograde due here before 2041, those two houses, and the weeks when Venus moves forward through Aquarius, are where these questions come up instead.
