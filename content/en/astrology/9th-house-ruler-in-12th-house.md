Out of sight, in the 12th house, is where the ruler of your 9th house lives. Belief turns inward: the search for meaning is lived in solitude, in retreat, or in work that nobody sees.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Study, travel, philosophy and faith, the business of the [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), go wherever its ruler goes ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Here that's the [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/), the last before the Ascendant: solitude and rest, retreat and recovery, quiet work behind the scenes, care given or received without being seen, and the inner life.

Horary astrology's lists draw a line that fits this placement closely. They gave the 12th captivity and every confinement that is imposed on a person, and gave the 9th the monastery, because a religious retreat is chosen. With the 9th's ruler in the 12th, the two meet in the retreat you choose: the silent week, the long walk alone, the year spent studying in an archive or a library where nobody knows your name.

Faith, with this placement, is a private matter. What you believe may be hard to put into words, and you may not want to try: it lives in practice, in prayer or meditation, in dreams you take seriously, in a sense of meaning that doesn't need an audience. Travel can be a kind of disappearance, to places where you're anonymous and can think. Some people with this placement do quiet work for a cause, in care, research or translation, and never sign it. Isolation is the hazard. A belief nobody else ever hears can't be tested, and solitude works best as a room you can leave.

Tradition was hard on the 12th, and gave it hospitals, prisons and hidden enemies. Nothing here predicts confinement or illness, and no placement in the 12th is a diagnosis ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/) explains why, and where a chart's usefulness stops).

Pisces belongs to the 12th in the natural zodiac. When it's on your 9th cusp ([Pisces on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/)), the 9th has two rulers, Jupiter and Neptune; I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and either one in the 12th stands on the ground of its own sign. Neptune here makes the inner life wide and porous, the faith of a mystic or an artist ([Neptune in the 12th house](/astrology/neptune-in-12th-house/)). Jupiter here tends to be generous out of sight, with the kind of help that's never announced.

Placidus, the house system I use, gives the 12th the first third of the climb after rising, so a ruler there had come up over the eastern horizon shortly before you were born. The Ascendant moves quickly enough that a small error in the time can change this ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A few minutes earlier, the ruler would not yet have risen, and in the 1st your beliefs would be worn openly, in your manner ([Ruler of the 9th in the 1st house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/)). Let the clock run on a little and it rises into the 11th, where belief gathers friends and causes ([Ruler of the 9th in the 11th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)).
