Your Moon had already slipped into Libra, the sign that opens at the September equinox, while your Sun was still in Virgo: a pair that knows what is right and needs it to be fair. The lunar month was only days old, and I read the pair as precision that wants to be kind.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Earth and air, mutable and cardinal, with the Moon a single sign further on than the Sun: neighbours with little in common but the border. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) organises itself around discernment, getting things exactly right; the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) settles when there is balance and good feeling between you and the people around you. So you tend to see the flaw and to feel the cost of naming it in the same moment.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Virgo and is exalted there; [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra. Venus can roam up to 47° or so from the Sun, which for a Virgo Sun means anywhere from Cancer to Scorpio, and two of those signs tell a story of their own: in Libra, Venus is at home with your Moon, and in Virgo, beside your Sun, it is in its fall. Where your Venus sits says how easily the Moon's need for harmony is met. Neither light carries a dignity in these signs, though Libra is, by tradition, where the Sun has its fall and Saturn its exaltation.

Only days, at most five, separated your birth from the new moon before it: the Moon stood 0° to 60° ahead of the Sun. The [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase holds almost all of them, roughly nine in ten, the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) the rest, and that new moon was usually a [new moon in Virgo](/astrology/new-moon-in-virgo/). In the first hours of the range the two lights may sit a few degrees apart across the Virgo–Libra border, a conjunction that ignores the sign line ([out-of-sign aspects](/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/)) and still counts as a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) describes the new moon phase in full.

About 1 person in 141 has these lights. Should your birthday be one of the days the Moon entered or left Libra, the hour picks between this page, the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-virgo/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-scorpio/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Born around 22 September, at the equinox, your Sun's sign depends on it as well, and the rising sign always does.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you're considerate, fair and quietly romantic. The Libra Moon wants harmony, courtesy and a partner who meets you halfway; the Virgo Sun shows love by noticing what would make a shared life run better. You may hold back an honest observation to keep an evening pleasant, then turn it over for days. Say it gently, and sooner: the Libra Moon cares how a thing is said and the Virgo Sun cares that it is true, and both can be honoured at once.

## At work {#at-work}

Work where getting it right and getting along both count suits the pair: design, editing, mediation, any role that improves something shared. The Sun brings precision, the Moon tact and a sense of proportion, so you can fix a problem without making anyone feel small. Workplaces full of friction wear on the Moon, and careless ones on the Sun; a fair manager and a well-run office are worth more to you than they look.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The Sun sees what's wrong and the Moon can't bear the quarrel that naming it might start. You may swallow the correction, let it ferment into worry, then deliver it at the wrong moment, too sharply. Weighing every side can also keep a decision open long after it should have closed. What helps is a rule of timing: say the true thing, kindly, while it's still small, and decide by a date, even when the scales won't sit perfectly still.
