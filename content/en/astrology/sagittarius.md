Sagittarius is the ninth sign of the zodiac, a mutable fire sign, drawn as an archer: a centaur, half human and half horse, bow drawn. I read it as the sign of the long view — the arrow aimed somewhere past what you can see from here.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Wherever Sagittarius falls in your chart, it asks *what does this mean, and where does it lead?* It's a [fire sign](/astrology/fire-signs/), so it acts on enthusiasm, and a mutable one, so it adapts and moves on (the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/) explain the difference). [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), just before it, goes down into the depths; Sagittarius comes up for air and looks at the horizon. [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), next, will want to build something on what it found.

The three placements people ask about first:

- **[Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/):** you come alive by searching for meaning — through travel, study, belief or simply a good argument. You need a direction more than a destination.
- **[Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/):** you feel safe when you have room: space, plans, a horizon. Humour is how you comfort yourself and others, and feeling hemmed in is what unsettles you most.
- **[Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/):** you meet the world with openness and frankness, and people find you easy to talk to. Jupiter becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/), so where it sits matters a great deal. Knowing your rising sign takes an accurate [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/).

The Sun sign and the rising sign are the placements everyone reads first. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) they held about a third of the time, so I read Sagittarius as one voice in a chart, never the whole of it.

## Its ruler: Jupiter {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, and where that planet sits in your chart shows where the sign's energy goes. Sagittarius is ruled by [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), which tradition also gave to Pisces: in Pisces, Jupiter's faith turns inwards, towards compassion; in Sagittarius it turns outwards, towards travel, teaching and big ideas. If Sagittarius is on your 6th house cusp and Jupiter sits in your 9th, say, your daily work keeps pulling you towards study or somewhere far away.

Tradition exalts no planet in Sagittarius, and none is in fall here. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite its home in Gemini: the planet of details in the sign of the big picture. That isn't a weak mind. It's a mind that thinks in stories and principles rather than lists, and has to make a little more effort with the small print.

## Planets in Sagittarius {#planets-in-the-sign}

Every planet passes through Sagittarius sooner or later: the Moon for about two and a half days a month, the Sun for a month each year, Jupiter for about a year in every twelve. In Sagittarius, each one wants to go further:

- **[Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/):** a self that grows by exploring — places, ideas, beliefs — and by telling the truth as it sees it.
- **[Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/):** comfort in freedom, plans and laughter; restless when life gets too small.
- **[Mercury in Sagittarius](/astrology/mercury-in-sagittarius/):** Mercury in detriment: big-picture thinking, frank speech and a gift for teaching, while the details can slip past.
- **[Venus in Sagittarius](/astrology/venus-in-sagittarius/):** love as a shared adventure; you need a partner who is also a friend and a fellow traveller, and room to breathe.
- **[Mars in Sagittarius](/astrology/mars-in-sagittarius/):** drive fuelled by enthusiasm and causes; you act fast for what you believe in, and lose interest in what you don't.
- **[Jupiter in Sagittarius](/astrology/jupiter-in-sagittarius/):** Jupiter at home: optimism, generosity and faith in the future, with a risk of promising more than one person can give.
- **[Saturn in Sagittarius](/astrology/saturn-in-sagittarius/):** shared by everyone born over about two and a half years: convictions earned the hard way, after doubting the ones you were handed.
- **[Uranus in Sagittarius](/astrology/uranus-in-sagittarius/):** shared by everyone born over about seven years: restless, radical ideas about belief, travel and learning. Its house shows where.
- **[Neptune in Sagittarius](/astrology/neptune-in-sagittarius/):** shared by everyone born over about fourteen years: a longing for faith, for distant places and for a bigger meaning. Its house makes it personal.
- **[Pluto in Sagittarius](/astrology/pluto-in-sagittarius/):** shared by everyone born over many years: beliefs and truths transformed, sometimes from the ground up. Its house says where.

## Sagittarius on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

In most charts Sagittarius opens one of the twelve houses (when it doesn't, it's [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/), held inside a house), and that part of life is where you want space, meaning and room to grow. Which house it is depends on your birth time and place.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/):** Sagittarius rising — an open, frank, restless presence; you meet people as if the conversation had already started.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money as freedom rather than security; generous and optimistic with it, sometimes too much so; you may earn through teaching, travel or ideas.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** frank speech and big ideas; you learn on the move and talk in stories.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home full of books, guests and opinions; roots in beliefs more than in one place.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** love as adventure; play that happens outdoors or far from home; creativity that tells stories.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work that needs meaning and room to move; routines you resist unless they serve something bigger.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius on the Descendant, which means Gemini rising: partners who widen your world, and a need for freedom inside commitment.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** intimacy met with honesty and humour; shared money handled generously, sometimes loosely; crises faced with faith.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius in its natural house: study, travel and belief come naturally, and you stay a student all your life.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career in teaching, publishing, law, travel or anything that spreads ideas, and a public image of an explorer.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends from everywhere, groups with a cause, and hopes on a grand scale.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a private faith; optimism kept in reserve for hard times, and solitude as a journey inwards.

## Sagittarius in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Sagittarius placements bring enthusiasm, honesty and a wish to grow together, along with a need for freedom that a partner can mistake for distance. Its opposite sign, [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), is the pull: Gemini gathers facts and voices, Sagittarius looks for the meaning that ties them together, and between them the conversation never quite ends. Two Sun signs can't tell you how two people will get on — [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind there is — so I read where each person's planets fall in the other's chart.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Sagittarians can't commit.”** Sagittarius needs room to grow; given that, its commitment can be wholehearted. Venus, Saturn and the 7th house say far more about commitment than a Sun sign does.
- **“Sagittarius is the lucky sign.”** Jupiter's sign is linked with optimism and generosity, not with guaranteed good fortune, and a chart can't predict luck.
- **“You're really an Ophiuchus.”** The Sun does cross the constellation Ophiuchus, between Scorpius and Sagittarius, but the signs are twelve equal parts of the [tropical zodiac](/astrology/tropical-zodiac/), tied to the seasons. [Ophiuchus](/astrology/ophiuchus-and-the-13th-sign/) is a constellation, not a thirteenth sign.
- **“Born on 21 or 22 December, you're half Capricorn.”** At the minute you were born the Sun was in one sign; near a change, your birth time settles which ([born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/)).
