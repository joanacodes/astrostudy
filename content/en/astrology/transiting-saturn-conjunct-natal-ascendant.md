After three years or so behind your horizon, in your 12th house, Saturn comes up over the eastern edge of your chart, onto the degree that was rising when you were born. It happens about every 29½ years, and it changes the way you walk into a room before it changes much else.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds structure, sets limits and works on the long term; the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is your orientation, the stance you take at the start of anything. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses them, so for the months of this [transit](/astrology/transits/) Saturn shapes your first move: you weigh a beginning before you make it, say yes less often and mean it more, and others may read you as more serious than before. A quick rising sign gets a pause; a cautious one gets firmer footing.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant is the cusp of the 1st house, so this crossing opens [Saturn transiting the 1st house](/astrology/saturn-transit-1st-house/), a stay of a little under three years. The months on the degree itself are the threshold: what was being quietly closed during [Saturn transiting the 12th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-12th-house/) gives way to a simpler, more definite way of presenting yourself.

People sometimes worry about the body here, because the old books read it from the Ascendant, and Saturn's reputation for age and heaviness doesn't help. A chart can't see your body or forecast its health ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). What this crossing describes is a manner, and a manner can be chosen. If you were born with Saturn close to your Ascendant, the crossing and your [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/) fall together, one season instead of two.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Saturn takes about nine months over your rising degree. A little over half of these crossings come as three exact hits, over about nine and a half months, as Saturn backs over the degree and crosses it again; the rest touch it once, and the quickest are done in three weeks.

Those dates are only as good as your birth time. Your Ascendant moves about a degree every four minutes and Saturn about a degree a month, so on average each minute of error moves the window by about a week, and a time rounded to the hour can put it more than half a year out. No birth time, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, people meet this crossing two or three times. After the 1st house, Saturn works its way down through your 2nd and 3rd towards the bottom of the chart, and the other angles to your rising degree follow in turn: [transiting Saturn sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-ascendant/) around your 3rd house, [transiting Saturn square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-ascendant/) near your IC, [transiting Saturn trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-ascendant/) around your 5th, and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-ascendant/) on your Descendant, about fifteen years after this crossing.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose your entrances. Say yes to fewer new things in these months, and arrive at each one prepared.

Let your manner match your intentions: notice the first thing you do when you meet someone, and decide whether you'd keep it.

Don't rush to put back on a persona the 12th-house years retired. A plainer outline usually suits this crossing better.
