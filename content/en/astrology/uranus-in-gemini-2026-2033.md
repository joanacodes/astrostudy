Uranus spends about seven years in each sign. It first reached Gemini in July 2025, stepped back into Taurus that November and settled in Gemini on 26 April 2026. It stays until May 2033, with one short visit to Cancer at the end of 2032.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) goes round the zodiac in about 84 years, which gives it about seven in each sign. Its entry into [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) and its exit both take two steps, because its yearly retrograde straddles the border. In the dates this site keeps:

- **7 July 2025:** first crossing into Gemini.
- **8 November 2025:** back into Taurus.
- **26 April 2026:** into Gemini for good.
- **3 August 2032:** into Cancer, then back into Gemini on 12 December 2032.
- **22 May 2033:** gone for good.

Eighty-four years is the length of a long life, so the previous stay, from August 1941 to June 1949, is within living memory: people born then now see Uranus come back to its birth place, at about 84 ([Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/)). Before that, Uranus crossed Gemini from June 1858 to March 1866, and from June 1774 to April 1782, a stay that took in 1781, the year William Herschel found the planet. The natal placement has its own page ([Uranus in Gemini](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini/)); here it is a transit, and it runs through every chart.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Each year Uranus moves about four degrees on, then spends about 150 days retrograde, so it crosses most degrees of Gemini three times. Its loops, in the stations this site keeps:

- **2026–27:** back from 5°41′ to 1°40′ Gemini.
- **2027–28:** from 9°57′ to 5°55′.
- **2028–29:** from 14°14′ to 10°12′.
- **2029–30:** from 18°33′ to 14°30′.
- **2030–31:** from 22°53′ to 18°50′.
- **2031–32:** from 27°15′ to 23°11′.
- **2032–33:** from 1°39′ Cancer to 27°35′ Gemini.

A natal planet in Gemini is met around the loop that covers its degree, for about twenty months with the orbs I use for timing ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). The house that holds Gemini receives Uranus for the whole stay; [Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/) shows what that can look like. Gemini is ruled by [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), and Mercury's place in your birth chart shows where that house's matters tend to play out ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Neither can be worked out without your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Three groups also meet it through their own Uranus, at an age its cycle sets:

- **Born February 1981 to December 1988,** with Uranus in Sagittarius: the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 44 or 45.
- **Born March 2003 to March 2011,** mostly with Uranus in Pisces: the first square, [transiting Uranus square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-uranus/), at about 21 to 23.
- **Born August 1941 to June 1949,** with Uranus in Gemini: the return, at about 84.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In the system I built for my readings, Uranus enables innovation, differentiation and systemic change; Gemini is a manner, not a subject: comparing, linking, adapting, keeping information moving, trying more than one approach at once. So in the house Uranus crosses, I read seven years in which that part of your life is reorganised in that manner. Options get compared, separate things get connected, and arrangements that only worked by habit are tested against alternatives. The same system names one of Uranus's blind spots: changing a system before understanding what still works. In that house, I ask what to keep as well as what to try.

Uranus is one of several slow planets changing sign in these years. Pluto settled in Aquarius in November 2024, Neptune in Aries in January 2026 ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)), and Saturn joins Uranus in Gemini on 1 June 2030. Which of these movements touches your natal planets depends on your birth year as much as on your birth time: [generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/) sets out the slow planets' signs year by year. A sign shared by seven years of births describes a sky, not the people born under it ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Uranus in Gemini means chaos.”** Uranus is innovation and reform, not chaos. In a chart, it reworks one house and what it meets there.
- **“It started in 2025.”** That was the first crossing. Uranus went back into Taurus in November 2025 and settled in Gemini on 26 April 2026.
- **“Only Geminis will feel it.”** Gemini falls somewhere in every chart. A Gemini Sun meets Uranus only around the loop that reaches its degree.
- **“It will bring back what happened in the 1940s.”** Earlier stays are history, not a template, and I don't read the past forward into forecasts.
