Your rising degree is set by the minute of birth, and so is every square to it. When Chiron squares your Ascendant it squares your Descendant too, so the whole horizon of the chart, you on one side and the people you meet on the other, rubs against an old sore spot coming from below or from above.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is the orientation you begin from, and the Descendant opposite it is the frame in which you meet someone else. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) holds an old sore spot and the workarounds you built for it. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through constructive friction, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the friction usually falls on a habit of approach: a way of arriving that worked for years starts to cost more than it gives.

Where Chiron stands when it squares the horizon changes the story. From below, around your 3rd or 4th house, the pressure tends to come from your roots: the version of you that your family recognised, the manner you learned at home to stay safe, now rubbing against how you would like to meet the world ([Chiron transiting the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-4th-house/)). From above, around your 9th or 10th, it comes from public life: what a role or a set of beliefs expects of your manner, against the way you actually arrive ([Chiron transiting the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-10th-house/)).

One of the Ascendant's blind spots is using the same orientation everywhere, and a square tends to find the setting where it no longer fits. Friction isn't failure. What usually comes of it is a second way of arriving, kept for the rooms that need it. Partners and close friends feel the adjustment, since the Descendant is squared as well, and sometimes they are the ones who ask for it.

Here the birth time matters twice: it fixes your rising degree, and it decides which house Chiron occupies when the square forms. Without it neither can be placed, which is why a chart that lacks one stays unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

These squares keep Chiron's usual timetable: about ten months each within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, with three exact hits in nearly two cases out of three. A single crossing, about one pass in three, can be over in three weeks; one in twenty takes five hits and about twenty-one months.

With two squares to a round of about 50 years, three by 80 is the usual count, and nobody has fewer than two. Chiron in Aries, until April 2027, squares Cancer rising from above and Capricorn rising from below; Chiron in Taurus, from 2026 to 2034, squares Leo rising from above and Aquarius rising from below; from 2033, Chiron in Gemini squares Virgo rising from above and Pisces rising from below.

The first square of a round comes after [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Chiron sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-ascendant/), with Chiron below the horizon; the second comes after [transiting Chiron trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-ascendant/), above it, on the way back to the next crossing.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice the room where your usual way of arriving stops working, and try a second way there only.

Ask which parts of your manner came from home and which you chose. Keep the chosen ones.

Tell a partner or a close friend what you are adjusting. They feel this square too.
