Uranus square Neptune belongs to births of the 1950s, when Uranus stood mostly in Cancer and Neptune in Libra, a quarter of the circle apart. Here invention and imagination meet at right angles: a friction a generation shares, and each chart lives out in its own houses.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables differentiation, liberation and the reform of systems: it wants clear lines and the freedom to redraw them. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination, compassion and a softening of boundaries that no longer serve: it wants to include, to idealise, to sense the whole. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through constructive friction, the kind that forces adaptation without making either function less itself.

In plain words, the urge to break away and the urge to belong to something larger keep catching on each other. You change a system and feel the dream go out of it; you hold an ideal and find it needs a reform you'd rather not make. Restlessness and disenchantment both visit. Yet each planet keeps the other honest: Uranus asks the ideal to become workable, and Neptune asks the innovation what it's for. Over time, that is the square's gift: ideals tested by change, and changes that keep their meaning.

Cancer and Neptune's Libra are both cardinal signs, signs that start things. As a shared theme I read the square as friction between new ways of living at home and ideals of partnership and fairness. What it does in one life depends on the chart around it.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 16 born between 1930 and 2009 has this square within 6°. Every one of them was born from July to October 1951, from June to December 1952, from May 1953 to July 1957, or from January to May 1958; nobody else in those eighty years has it. The closer your birth to an exact square, the louder the friction.

Being a generation's aspect, it becomes yours through your houses ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). Uranus in the 4th square Neptune in the 7th, for example, could set a home life that needs room to change against an ideal of partnership that asks for harmony. A contact with your Sun, Moon or an angle makes it personal too. The houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Uranus moves faster, about 84 years round the zodiac to Neptune's 165.

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune/):** born from 1990 to 1996; invention and imagination fused.
- **[Uranus sextile Neptune](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune/):** born from 1963 to 1970; an opening between the two, waiting to be used.
- **[Uranus trine Neptune](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune/):** born from 1936 to 1946; the two in easy agreement.
- **[Uranus opposite Neptune](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune/):** absent from every birth between 1930 and 2009.

[Uranus square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune-synastry/) sets one person's need for change across the other's ideals. Each feels the friction from a different side, so I read it in both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-neptune/) comes twice in Uranus's 84-year round and lasts months, often in three passes. [Transiting Neptune square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-uranus/) takes far longer: Neptune spends about 14 years in a sign and can stay within reach of the exact square for a couple of years.

For many people born with the square, both midlife turning points came together under the conjunction of the 1990s. Uranus and Neptune, meeting in Capricorn, stood opposite natal Uranus in Cancer and square natal Neptune in Libra: the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) and the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) as a single season. Weather, not a forecast: transits like these ask an old ideal and an old reform to meet again, and leave the outcome to you.
