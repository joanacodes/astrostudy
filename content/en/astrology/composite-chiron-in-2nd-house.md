Who pays for what, whose things fill the flat, whether the relationship is worth the time it takes: every pair settles these questions, and some settle them more painfully than others. Composite Chiron in the 2nd house makes value the bond's tender spot, and describes the arrangements two people build so that it stops hurting.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds what a chart has and what it values: money, belongings, skills that could earn a living, the sense of being worth something. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) it becomes the relationship's own purse and its own self-esteem. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) there marks a limit the bond keeps meeting around value. One of you earns far more; one saves and the other spends; one brought a house to the relationship and the other a suitcase; or the pair quietly doubts that what it has together is worth as much as what each had alone.

The sore spot is the relationship's, even when the money is very real. Separate money can run smoothly for years; it is the shared household budget, or the present that cost too much, where the tension gathers. Arrangements are its answer: a system for joint and separate money that both of you chose, agreements written down before they're needed, a habit of counting what the bond produces that has no price, the skills taught to each other, the home kept going. Pairs like this are often good at helping friends talk about money without shame.

The 2nd is a succedent house with Taurus as its natural sign, and its cusp is one of the in-between cusps that house systems disagree about most. A composite adds a second choice on top: the midpoints of your two charts' cusps, or houses built from the composite Midheaven where you live ([Which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)). Both methods need both birth times, and a Chiron near either edge of the 2nd can change house from one to the other ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

One person's knot between earning and self-worth is what a natal [Chiron in the 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-in-2nd-house/) describes, and [their Chiron in your 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-in-2nd-house-synastry/) is how, in synastry, the other's old sore spot meets your resources. The composite's knot is tied by both hands at once.

Its difficulty is letting money stand in for worth: keeping score in pounds, or proving the bond's value by what it owns. A composite says nothing about whether two people will be comfortable or short; this Chiron describes how they feel about what they have. Talking about money when nothing is wrong helps far more than talking about it in a crisis.

Next to it, [composite Chiron in the 1st house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-1st-house/) is tender about how the bond looks, and [composite Chiron in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-3rd-house/) about its everyday exchanges. Composites stay outside my readings; their users read this Chiron beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and anything touching two people's money is looked at only when both have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
