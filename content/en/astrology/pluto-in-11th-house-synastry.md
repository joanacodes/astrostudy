Circles shift around someone whose Pluto falls in your 11th house. Friendships deepen or fall away, the groups you belong to come into question, and the hopes you share with others start to be taken seriously.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) gets to the heart of a matter and rebuilds what no longer holds. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is your life among many: chosen friends, networks and groups, causes, and the future you would like to help build. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their depth meets the company you keep.

With them, you may find out which friendships are real, leave a group that no longer fits, or commit to a cause you had only supported from a distance. Your hopes for the future can turn into plans with weight.

Your circle draws them in. They may want to belong to it deeply, or to change the group from the inside, and your friends and causes can matter to them as much as you do. The 11th is a succedent house, where shared achievement is kept alive; its old name was Good Spirit, and Jupiter had its joy there. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), its natural sign, was ruled by Saturn before Uranus was given it; I read both, the patience and the change that any group needs.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Groups have their power dynamics, and this overlay can bring them into view: loyalty tests, factions, the question of who speaks for the circle. A friendship with them may feel intense in a way friendships rarely do, and others around you may notice.

From their side, they can feel outside your group however close they are to you, and be tempted to reshape it so that they belong.

No overlay predicts a falling-out or the end of a friendship. Pluto describes depth and renewal in what you share with many, and community is not conformity.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let friendships breathe. Keep some friends who are only yours and some who are shared, and introduce them to your circle at a pace that suits everyone. In a couple, shared causes can bind you if each keeps their own voice; between friends, intensity is a gift when it doesn't require loyalty tests; between colleagues in a network, clear roles keep a group's changes constructive; between adult relatives, a family's shared hopes can be renewed if everyone has a say.

Reading from their chart, you'd find your Pluto in their 11th house, your depth among their friends. [Their Pluto in your 10th house](/astrology/pluto-in-10th-house-synastry/) reaches your career and standing; [their Pluto in your 12th house](/astrology/pluto-in-12th-house-synastry/) goes into your inner life.

This is where Pluto's generational nature shows most plainly. Its sign marks what a whole cohort sets out to transform, so if you're close in age, Pluto carries the same project in both your charts ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)); with a wide gap, two generations' causes meet in your circle, and each can learn what the other was fighting for. Pluto's degree can be read straight from their birth date. Your 11th, in Placidus, the house system I use, lies in the stretch of sky between your Midheaven and your Ascendant, which your birth time decides ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
