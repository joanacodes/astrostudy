Venus in the 7th house sits across the chart from you, in the house of the person facing you. The planet of attraction and reciprocity lives here among partners, close allies and even open rivals. I read it as a gift for partnership and fair exchange, and a habit of keeping the peace that needs your own voice in it.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is the house of the other person: the partner, the spouse, the business associate, even the open rival, and the qualities you hope to find in whoever sits across the table. Angular, it starts at the Descendant, which in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the system I use, is the degree sinking below the western horizon as you were born. Its natural sign is Libra, one of Venus's own. [Venus](/astrology/venus/), the planet of reciprocity, is at home here: relating is where you tend to come alive, and fairness between two people matters to you as much as warmth.

With the Sun never far from Venus, this placement usually means a birth around sunset. Near the Descendant, minutes count. A slightly later birth would put Venus below the horizon, in [Venus in the 6th house](/astrology/venus-in-6th-house/), and an earlier one could place it higher, in [Venus in the 8th house](/astrology/venus-in-8th-house/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

One distinction is worth making. This page is about your own Venus in your 7th house. Someone else's Venus falling in your 7th belongs to synastry ([Their Venus in your 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house-synastry/)), and that is a reading I do only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Partnership is close to the centre of this placement. You tend to enjoy being half of a pair, to bring courtesy and consideration into a couple, and to look for grace, fairness and good company in the other person. Often you recognise your own Venus most clearly in the people you choose, as if part of your taste lived across the table. Reciprocity is everything: a relationship has to feel fair to feel right. The house facing the 7th is the 1st, your own self, and the lesson runs between the two: loving someone without disappearing into them, and keeping the peace without giving up your side of it.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, a 7th-house Venus thrives in partnership: a business partner, a client relationship, a team of two. Negotiation, mediation, consulting and agency work, anything where you work closely with one person at a time, can suit it. You often know how to turn a rival into an ally, or at least into a respectful opponent. The risk is giving away too much in a deal to keep the relationship sweet.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Peace can be bought too cheaply. Disagreement can feel like a threat to the bond, so you smooth it over, agree too soon, or let a partner decide. You might define yourself through your relationships, and feel unsure who you are between them. And because fairness matters so much to you, an imbalance you've never mentioned can sour things slowly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Bring your own preferences into every partnership, early and plainly. Treat a fair argument as part of harmony, not its failure. Keep friendships, interests and plans that are yours alone, so that a couple is made of two whole people. And practise being single well, for an evening or for a season: this Venus loves more freely when it knows it can stand on its own.

Venus held about a third of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). In the 7th, where it describes what you look for in others, I take extra care: a chart can describe your taste in partners, but it can never tell you who, or when.
