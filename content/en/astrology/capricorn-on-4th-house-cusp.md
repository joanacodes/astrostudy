Capricorn on the 4th house cusp puts the sea-goat at the root of your chart. Home is something you hold up, and the private self underneath tends to stand very straight.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In Placidus, the house system I use, the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) opens the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/): home, family, belonging, and the self you show only behind your own front door ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) at that point makes home a structure to keep up. I read it as the person who knows where the documents are, who fixes what's broken before anyone asks, who keeps the family's dates and traditions. Rituals matter here, the Sunday meal, the decorations that come out on the same date every year, kept because someone has to keep them. Belonging comes through responsibility, given or carried, and the private self is often more serious than the public one. On family, the cusp speaks only of the inside view, often a home with duties in it or a sense of having to grow up early, never a judgement of the people who brought you up. With the years, many people with this IC build the steady base they once had to hold up for others, and find they can rest in it.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules this IC. The house it occupies shows where your sense of home gets built, its sign how: the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In my notebook, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), and Saturn's house is where I start with this cusp. Aquarius, Saturn's other sign, which it shares with Uranus, opens the 5th in most of these charts: home and pleasure then have one ruler, and the fun tends to have a structure, often enjoyed at home.

Every chart with a Capricorn IC at London or New York has [Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/) and Cancer on the Midheaven ([Cancer on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/)), so Aries sets on the Descendant. All four angles fall in cardinal signs, the signs that start things: Venus rules the way you meet people, Mars the people you face, the Moon your public life and Saturn your private one. People meet your Libran charm first, and only later learn how much structure stands behind it. About 1 birth in 11 has this IC, and London and New York give the same figure, since the bottom of the chart follows the hour rather than the latitude: Capricorn begins at the December solstice, and the meridian spends some two hours and eight minutes crossing it. The 4th is about 35° wide at London, leaving Capricorn a single cusp, and roughly one London chart in six holds Aquarius whole inside it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

An earlier birth would have put Capricorn on the 5th cusp ([Capricorn on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-5th-house-cusp/)), a later one on the 3rd ([Capricorn on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-3rd-house-cusp/)). The IC changes sign every two hours or so, so I need the exact time before your roots come into a reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
