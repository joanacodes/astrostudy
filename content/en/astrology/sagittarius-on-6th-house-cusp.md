With Sagittarius on the 6th house cusp, the far-sighted sign runs the near things: the to-do list, the commute, the Tuesday afternoon. Your days need a reason as much as a schedule.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) holds the working week as it's lived: the tasks, the habits, the people at the next desk, the way you look after your body ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). Its natural sign is Virgo, the sign of method; [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) on its cusp brings the sign of meaning instead. I read it as a working life that has to point somewhere: a job that teaches you, crosses borders or lets you explain why, and a dislike of being watched over while you do it. A team spread over several places suits it, and so does a manager who explains the why before the how. You're often the colleague who keeps spirits up and remembers what the task is for. The catch is Jupiter's yes: more projects than days, and promises made on a good morning that the rest of the week has to keep.

The tradition that named this house Bad Fortune also gave it illness. That's not something I look for: nothing on this cusp foretells a health problem, and questions about your health go to a doctor ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). What the cusp describes is your own way of keeping well, and with Sagittarius that tends to work when it feels like freedom: a long walk, a game outdoors, a bike ride to somewhere new, rather than a regime counted in repetitions.

With Sagittarius here, your working days take their orders from [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/): where it sits is where they lead, and its sign is how they run, in the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Pisces, Jupiter's second sign, co-ruled by Neptune, opens your 9th or your 10th in all these charts, and at London it sits on the Midheaven itself in nearly 3 charts in 4. Jupiter then speaks for the daily round and the public direction alike, and I read Neptune at the top as well.

Sagittarius turns up here less often than on most cusps, in about 1 London birth in 13 and 1 New York birth in 12, in Placidus, the house system I use. This cusp lies a little under the western horizon, where signs go down, and Sagittarius sets quickly in the north, in the time Gemini takes to rise opposite, so the cusp passes through it fast. Close to 9 London charts in 10 have [Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/) with it, and the rest Leo: with the Moon as chart ruler, your moods set the pace of a day, and Jupiter asks that the day mean something. About one London chart in nine holds Capricorn entire within the 6th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and Saturn adds a taste for structure to all that freedom.

Your birth time is what separates this chart from its neighbours: earlier, Sagittarius would be on the Descendant ([Sagittarius on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/)); later, on the 5th cusp ([Sagittarius on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-5th-house-cusp/)). The minute decides, and I won't read a chart until I have it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
