Capricorn builds structures and Virgo makes them work, so with your Sun and Moon in these two earth signs, competence comes in two layers. You were born under a disseminating Moon, the stretch of the month that asks for what has been learned to be passed on. I read the pair as mastery built patiently and handed on.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Virgo lies four signs before Capricorn: an earth trine by sign, cardinal for the Sun and mutable for the Moon. The [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) plans a long climb and takes responsibility for it; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) feels safest with something to improve in its hands: the recipe tried a third time, the report cleared of its last error, the shelf rebuilt to hold more. Capricorn sets the sequence, Virgo refines each step, and between them very little gets left half-done.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of Virgo, is also exalted there, which puts your Moon's ruler at full strength in your Moon's own sign: sorting, analysing and naming are this Moon's native tools. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), ruling your Sun's sign, has no dignity in Virgo, nor Mercury in Capricorn, and the lights have none in these signs. I read Saturn and Mercury together here: where and how they meet in your chart shows whether method and duty pull in harness.

You were born well after the full moon. The Moon had moved 210° to 270° ahead of the Sun, seventeen to twenty-two days after the new moon, waning and rising later each night. Seven in eight of these births belong to the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/), the rest to the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase, and around 240° the trine by sign becomes a waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). The phase takes its name from the Latin for seed, and I read a birth in it as a skill that wants students: a fair portrait of a Virgo Moon that enjoys showing how it's done. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the phase.

About 1 person in 146 has this pair. If your birthday saw the Moon move from Leo into Virgo, or from Virgo into Libra, the time of birth decides between this page, the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-leo/) and the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-libra/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also gives the rising sign, the third line in the first sketch, and the houses where Saturn and Mercury do their work.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love, for this pair, is practical and attentive. The Virgo Moon notices what a partner needs and quietly provides it; the Capricorn Sun commits for the long term and keeps its word. What can be harder is closeness with no schedule: the lazy morning, the feeling without a solution, being looked after when you're usually the one doing the looking after. A partner who accepts your care and insists on giving some back, without letting you correct how they do it, softens both lights.

## At work {#at-work}

This is a pair for crafts, systems and anything where quality is the point: engineering, editing, accounting, archives, operations, skilled trades, any field where an error matters and someone has to catch it. The Sun brings the long plan and the authority, the Moon precision and a feel for what isn't quite right yet. You may become the person everyone asks, and the one who trains the next. Watch for picking up everyone's loose ends because you'd tie them better.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With two earth signs in easy agreement, work can swallow everything else. The Sun's sense of duty and the Moon's need to be useful reinforce each other until rest feels like idleness and a feeling becomes one more task to sort. An inner inspector may judge your efforts more harshly than anyone else would. What helps is pleasure with no purpose, a walk with no destination, an afternoon in which nothing improves, and naming a feeling before reaching for the list.
