Scorpio comes just before Sagittarius, so your Moon sits one step behind your Sun, in the sign the Sun crossed in the weeks before your birthday. You were born in the last dark days of a lunar month. I picture the pair as open sky above deep water: a frank, wide self carried by feelings that go all the way down.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Neighbouring signs share nothing, and these two show it: mutable fire for the Sun, fixed water for the Moon. The [Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/) looks outward and ahead, for the meaning that would make sense of everything; the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) steadies itself by going all the way down: the conversation at two in the morning, the question asked until the real answer comes, the bond that has been through a hard year and held. So you may be the most open person in the room and also the one who has noticed what nobody is saying.

Tradition puts the Moon in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Scorpio, opposite its exaltation in Taurus. I read the fall as the effort comfort takes here, never as a lesser heart: this Moon has to know what is true before it can rest. Scorpio has two rulers and I read both: [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), for modern astrologers, shows where this Moon renews itself after a loss; [Mars](/astrology/mars/), in the older books, where it stands guard and draws its lines. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), ruler of your Sun's sign, has no dignity in Scorpio. Where Jupiter widens, Pluto and Mars concentrate, and the pair asks you to do both.

This is a birth from the end of the month. Your Moon was 300° to 359° ahead of the Sun, which is up to 60° behind it, twenty-four and a half to twenty-nine and a half days after the last new moon: a thin crescent rising before the Sun, or lost in the dawn. Most of these births fall in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) as eight phases count it, and about one in eight in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/). I read the balsamic phase as an ending that clears the ground, and a Scorpio Moon knows about endings: it lets go deeply or not at all. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains why the coming new moon matters.

About 1 person in 149 shares this pair. If the Moon moved from Libra into Scorpio, or on into Sagittarius, on the day you were born, the time decides between this page, the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-libra/) and the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-sagittarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It sets the rising sign too, the third part of a first sketch, and the houses of Pluto, Mars and Jupiter.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You bring warmth and humour to the start of a relationship and depth to its long middle. The Sagittarius Sun makes friends easily; the Scorpio Moon keeps its deepest feelings for the one or two people who have shown they can hold them. That can confuse others, who meet the easy charm and are surprised by the intensity underneath. Loyalty, once given, runs deep. What helps is saying the deep thing out loud, early, instead of waiting to see whether a partner has guessed it.

## At work {#at-work}

Research, investigation, psychology, philosophy, crisis work, any field that asks why things really happen and is willing to look: this pair has the Sun's breadth and the Moon's stamina for difficult material. You can explain what you've found in a way that gives people hope without lying to them. Shallow cheer and office politics weary the Moon; narrow specialism with no larger point tires the Sun. Choose work that lets you dig, and then tell people what it means.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The Sun wants to move on; the Moon is not finished. A hurt can be joked away in public and kept for years in private, and the gap between the two can leave you feeling unknown even by people close to you. The Sun's frankness may also land on a Scorpio wound and press harder than it means to. What helps is letting endings complete: a conversation taken all the way through, a grief given its time, a journal where the Moon can be as deep as it likes before the Sun goes out again.
