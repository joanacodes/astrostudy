The planet of harmony runs through this whole pair: Venus rules Libra, where your Sun is, and has its exaltation in Pisces, where your Moon is. On the Libra side it measures and judges fairly; on the Pisces side it gives without measuring. You were born in the days before a full moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Libra is cardinal air, Pisces mutable water, five signs on: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, a pairing with nothing structural in common, so each has to keep making room for the other. The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) finds its centre in proportion and fair judgement, in knowing where one person ends and another begins; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) absorbs the feelings around it and answers with a compassion that rarely measures. You may weigh everything at work and absorb everything at home.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra and has its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Pisces: one planet links your two lights, and where it sits by sign and house shows how your sense of fairness and your tenderness get along. Pisces has two rulers and I read both: the modern one, [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), marks where your Moon feels most with other people; the traditional one, [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), where that feeling grows into faith, study or generosity. Libra is the Sun’s fall, and Pisces gives the Moon no dignity of its own.

Your Moon was 120° to 179° ahead of the Sun, 10 to nearly 15 days into the lunar month, and growing towards a full moon that came within about five days of your birth, usually a full moon in Aries. The [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) holds most of these births; the earliest, about one in eight, are still [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) births. Halfway through, at 150°, the angle between the lights is an exact quincunx too. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the gibbous phase and the one before it.

About 1 person in 144 has a Libra Sun with a Pisces Moon. If the Moon reached Pisces from Aquarius, or left it for Aries, on the day of your birth, the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-aquarius/) and the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-aries/) are both still possible until I know the time ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Knowing it would also give the rising sign, which often decides how much of this soft Moon the world gets to see.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love tenderly and fairly at once: the Libra Sun keeps the relationship balanced and gracious, and the Pisces Moon gives comfort freely and senses a partner's mood before it's spoken. The risk is giving so much that your own needs fall off the scales: you weigh the other person's needs so carefully, and feel them so keenly, that yours never get weighed at all. A partner who asks, and listens, keeps both lights in view.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that joins fairness to compassion suits you: care, counselling, the arts, mediation, advocacy, anything where people need to be treated justly and gently at once. The Libra Sun brings judgement; the Pisces Moon brings imagination and empathy. Harsh, competitive places drain you faster than hard work does. Protect some quiet in the day, and some limits on whose problems you take home.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Libra measures and Pisces doesn't, and inside you the two can blur: a boundary feels unfair, a refusal feels unkind, and you give past the point where it helps anyone. The other side of it is the Libra Sun's guilt when the Moon simply needs to withdraw. Let fairness include you: decide in advance what you can give this week, and take the Pisces Moon's need for solitude as seriously as anyone else's need for you.
