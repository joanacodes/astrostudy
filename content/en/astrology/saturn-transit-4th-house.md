Saturn's years in your 4th house are the ones it spends underneath everything else: home, family, and the foundations the rest of your life stands on. They begin when it crosses your Imum Coeli, the lowest point of your chart.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds what's meant to last and checks what isn't; the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, roots, family and the private self. With Saturn crossing it, I read a season of repair. Responsibilities at home can grow, a living arrangement can need rethinking, and the past, family patterns included, can ask to be looked at with adult eyes. The work is often invisible from outside, and it's some of the most durable Saturn does.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the IC is the cusp of the 4th, and Saturn on it is Saturn opposite your Midheaven ([transiting Saturn opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-midheaven/)): a time when what you build in public tends to depend on what you repair at home. Cancer, the 4th's natural sign, is where tradition put Saturn in detriment, the planet of rules in the house of feeling. I read that friction as useful: care that gets a structure, and a home that's built rather than only inherited.

With your Venus in the 4th, home's comforts get a hard, useful look: which ones still hold you, which ones only decorate.

Older traditions read a parent in the 4th, without ever settling which one. Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), this one brings out what your chart already holds about home; it says nothing certain about anyone's parents, and it doesn't decide whether you move.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Most of Saturn's stays in the 4th, in Placidus charts drawn for a London birth, last between about 22 months and just under 4 years, with just under three as the middle; for a New York birth, about 23 months to nearly 4. The 4th is one of the houses whose size varies least at those latitudes, with the 3rd, the 9th and the 10th, and its stays don't stretch as far as those of the 1st or the 12th.

Your IC hangs on the minute of birth, so a chart without the time is one I leave unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows Saturn's years in your 3rd, the house of the near world ([Saturn transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-3rd-house/)), and gives way, when Saturn leaves the 4th, to its years in your 5th, where the work turns to joy ([Saturn transiting the 5th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-5th-house/)).

Your IC is always opposite your Midheaven, so take them as a pair. Midheaven in Libra, IC in Aries: February 2026 to April 2028. Midheaven in Scorpio, IC in Taurus: April 2028 to May 2030. Midheaven in Sagittarius, IC in Gemini: June 2030 to July 2032. Midheaven in Capricorn, IC in Cancer, the 4th's own sign: July 2032 to May 2035. Midheaven in Aquarius, IC in Leo: August 2034 to July 2037. Midheaven in Pisces, IC in Virgo: October 2036 to September 2039. Midheaven in Aries, IC in Libra: from September 2039. An IC in Pisces was crossed between March 2023 and February 2026, and Scorpio to Aquarius come after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Repair what needs repairing, at home and underneath it: the boiler, the boundary, the old arrangement nobody has looked at in years.

Take on the responsibilities at home that are truly yours, and hand back the ones that aren't. Saturn finds it hard to delegate, and in the 4th that can mean carrying a whole household alone.

Build the home you'd choose, not only the one you were given. Saturn is slow in the 4th, and what it helps you build there is meant to hold.
