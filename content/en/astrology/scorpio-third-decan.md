The end of Scorpio is ruled by two planets the sign doesn't make comfortable: Venus, in detriment here, and the Moon, in fall. In its last ten degrees, the old tables and the modern ones give each of them a share of the rule all the same.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

After the Sun's face ([Scorpio, second decan](/astrology/scorpio-second-decan/)), the Chaldean order moves on to Venus, and the last face of [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) is Venus's ([faces](/astrology/faces/)). It's an odd gift. Venus is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Scorpio, opposite its home in Taurus, so [Venus in Scorpio](/astrology/venus-in-scorpio/) between 20° and 30° sits at once in the sign that suits it least and in a face of its own.

The modern decans give the last third to Cancer, the remaining water sign, and so to the Moon, which is in [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Scorpio ([Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/)). Both rulers of this decan, then, are planets Scorpio makes work harder: the planet of what we value and the planet of what we need. I find that fitting rather than unlucky. Scorpio's own rulers, Mars and Pluto, still govern the sign around them, and I read both.

## Its image {#its-image}

Wallis Budge matched these degrees with the Egyptian decan Sapt-khennu. [Firmicus Maternus](/astrology/firmicus-maternus/) gives Sentineu or Aterceni, and Athanasius Kircher, in 1652, Panotragus. The list of Cosmas of Maiuma does something unusual here: it names the decan Kairos, the right moment, a name it has already given to the middle of Libra ([Libra, second decan](/astrology/libra-second-decan/)). I don't know whether that's a copyist's slip or a choice.

The stars give a clearer picture. [Unukalhai](/astrology/unukalhai/), at 22°26′ Scorpio, is the brightest star of the Serpent that the Serpent-bearer holds; its Arabic name means the serpent's neck, its Latin one, Cor Serpentis, the serpent's heart. It stands about 25° north of the Sun's path, so the planets share its degree without coming near it.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun crosses these degrees from about 13 to 21 November, the last stretch of a [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/). Born on 21 or 22 November, the Sun may already have reached Sagittarius, and only the time of birth settles it ([Sagittarius, first decan](/astrology/sagittarius-first-decan/)). I read this decan's shade as Scorpio's intensity turned towards what it loves and what it needs: loyalty, and a slow, deep kind of care. Venus or the Moon placed here are the planets I look at twice: each is uneasy in the sign, and each is in charge of the decan.

[Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/) in its last decan takes about 54 minutes to cross the horizon at the latitude of Paris and 56 at London's, a little less than the decans before it, because the signs are starting to rise faster. A birth time rounded to the hour can still land the Ascendant here when it belongs in Sagittarius ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Uranus in Scorpio](/astrology/uranus-in-scorpio/) crossed these degrees between early 1979 and late 1981, and Pluto between 1991 and late 1995, its last years in the sign. Neptune had been through between early 1965 and 1970. Each marks a generation; the house each one falls in is what makes it personal.
