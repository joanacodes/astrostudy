One person's drive cuts straight across the other's path. In Mars square North Node in synastry, the Mars of one person makes a right angle with the other's North Node and, by the same geometry, with their South Node: it stands at the crossroads, as far from the road behind as from the road ahead.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is wanting turned into motion: a start, a push, a contest, a position held. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the less travelled way of doing things, which only becomes natural with repetition; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, is the well-worn way, reliable and quick to hand. A [square](/astrology/square/) makes two things catch until they adapt; as [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) go, it's the one that lays a person's energy across both ends of someone else's [nodal axis](/astrology/nodal-axis/) at once.

What the friction can teach is deliberate choice. The Mars person's plans keep raising a question for the Node person: is this the old way or the new one? Over time, the Node person gets better at telling the two apart and choosing on purpose, rather than by habit. The Mars person, meanwhile, learns that their drive affects someone else's development, and becomes more careful about where they aim it.

The modality is often shared by all three points. With the nodes across Gemini and Sagittarius, a Mars in Virgo or Pisces brings mutable, adaptable energy right between everyday learning and the wider view.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

From the Node side, the other's energy can feel like an interruption: just as you lean into something new, their plans pull you sideways, or back into an old role you were leaving. From the Mars side, you may feel your aims are always being measured against someone else's growth, and resent having to defer to a direction you can't see.

Both experiences are accurate; they're the same crossing seen from its two sides. Friction of this kind asks for choices, never for one of you to step aside for good.

Seen from the crossing, the other angles:

- **[Mars conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the drive lands on the growing end, pushing forward.
- **[Mars sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** a sign closer, an opening rather than a crossing.
- **[Mars trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-north-node-synastry/):** a sign further, effort flows with the growth.
- **[Mars opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** the drive sits on the familiar end, facing the new.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make the choice explicit, one situation at a time. The Node person can say which end they're working on right now, the practised strength or the new one; the Mars person can ask before steering. Agree in advance which projects belong to whose priorities. Colleagues can map who leads where; in a couple, alternate whose aims set the calendar; between adult siblings, take turns deciding how a shared family matter is handled.

By the true node, about 1 pair of people in 9 has this square, one way round or the other, within 5.1°; the mean node, which I also read, can only tip a borderline case ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The node's degree belongs to a few months of births, since it takes about 18 months to back through a sign; Mars, by birthday, turns that shared position into this pair's crossing. The houses show which areas of life the crossing runs through, and they need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one person's chart, [Mars square North Node](/astrology/mars-square-north-node/) makes every effort a small vote between old and new. Between two, the vote is a conversation, which is harder and often clearer.
