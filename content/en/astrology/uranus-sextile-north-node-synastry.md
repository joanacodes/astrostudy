Sometimes the most useful thing a person does is show you a side door. Uranus sextile North Node in synastry sets one person's Uranus about 60° from the other's North Node, within 2.4°, and 120° from their South Node, so the first person's experiments open a way forward that also flatters what the second already knows.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) tries alternatives and breaks with routine; it is the function of breakthroughs, reform and room to differ. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a capacity a person is still building, awkward at first and steadier with practice; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite it holds the skills they consolidated long ago. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) puts two functions within reach of each other, an opportunity that is real only once taken; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it hands one person's experiments to the other as an opening.

Here the geometry is generous at both ends. The sextile to the North Node makes the new reachable, and the trine to the South Node makes the familiar feel fresh, so the node person can step forward without disowning who they were.

On the North Node side, you get ideas, tools and examples at just the right distance: close enough to try, far enough to stay yours.

On the Uranus side, you have the pleasure of watching your unusual methods help, without having to run the result.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The easier half can win. With a trine to the South Node and only a sextile to the North, the Uranus person's influence may mostly refresh old strengths: new paint on familiar habits, and no step taken. The opportunity has to be engaged, and practice is still practice. The Uranus person, for their part, can lose interest if the opening isn't used quickly.

- **[Uranus conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the experiment sits on the path itself and presses harder.
- **[Uranus square North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-north-node-synastry/):** Uranus stands across the path, halfway between its ends.
- **[Uranus trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-north-node-synastry/):** the two nodes swap roles, and the easy line runs to the new.
- **[Uranus opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** Uranus on the familiar end, unsettling what was settled.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use the opening on purpose. The node person can pick one small, unfamiliar thing to practise with the other's help, and repeat it; the Uranus person can offer tools rather than verdicts. Colleagues might try a new process together; a couple might take up a skill neither has; friends might travel in a way neither would alone.

Someone born in mid-1985, with Uranus in Sagittarius, has it with anyone born from December 1989 to April 1990, when the North Node was in Aquarius, or from March to July 1996, with the node in Libra. Anyone born, on and off, from July 1952 to April 1954 has Uranus in Cancer 60° from that 1985 North Node in Taurus. The narrow orb trims each window to a few months, yet each still holds months of births, so the contact never enters my synastry count; that count is for the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant and the Midheaven. I read it through the houses, as an era of experiment lending another era its tools.

I date it with the true node and check the mean one too: they are a degree or two apart at most, which matters with an orb this narrow ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The hour moves neither body enough to count; it places the houses, say the Uranus in the node person's 3rd house, among new ways of learning, and that needs both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Uranus sextile North Node](/astrology/uranus-sextile-north-node/) inside a single chart turns experiment into a person's own route to growth. Between two charts the route is offered, and the offer, like the reading, needs a yes from both ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
