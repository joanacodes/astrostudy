Cancer on the 8th house cusp gives the house of what's shared, owed and held in trust to the sign that guards its own. Closeness, joint money and the bonds that change you all go through the same door here, and the door opens from the inside.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the cusp of the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is the way you let someone in, and what you do with what you then share ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) lets people in slowly and keeps them. You tend to need to feel safe before closeness of any kind, and once you do, the bond is held like a household: joint money is family money, a debt is a promise, and a partner's trouble becomes yours. Betrayals are remembered for a long time; so are kindnesses. In a crisis you are often the one who feeds people, makes the calls and keeps the house running, and that care is how you come through it yourself. Heirlooms, family stories and old photographs can matter to you more than their price, because the 8th also covers what passes between generations. None of this is clinging: Cancer protects what it shares, and protecting is not controlling.

Sagittarius rises with this cusp in nearly 9 London charts in 10, and Scorpio in the rest ([Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/)), so Jupiter usually rules your chart while the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules the 8th: an open, generous manner in front, a guarded heart in matters of trust. The Moon's house shows where questions of sharing and closeness come up: the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Opposite, Capricorn opens your 2nd, so Saturn rules what's yours and the Moon what you share: your own money tends to be handled with caution, money in common with feeling.

Counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use, about 1 birth in 13 has Cancer on the 8th at London and 1 in 12 at New York: a little under two hours of each day, because opposite, the 2nd cusp is crossing Capricorn, one of the quick risers of the north. The 8th is wide in these charts, about 42° to 47° at London, so Cancer never opens the 7th or the 9th as well, and in about half the London charts, 3 in 10 at New York, the whole of Leo lies inside the 8th too ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The Sun then shares the house with the Moon, and pride and warmth join care in what you hold with others. [Cancer on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/) comes with Capricorn rising and [Cancer on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/) usually with Scorpio, so neither describes this chart.

Tradition hung death and inheritance on this house. I don't look for death in any chart, and none can say what someone will inherit ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); with Cancer here, I read how you trust, share and protect. All of it waits on the birth time, which is what places the 8th at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
