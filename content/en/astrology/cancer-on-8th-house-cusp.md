Cancer on the 8th house cusp sets a guard on the house of what's shared, owed and held in trust. Closeness, joint money and the bonds that change you all go through the same door here, and the door opens from the inside.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) cusp sets the terms on which someone gets in, and what becomes of what you then share ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) lets people in slowly and keeps them. You tend to need to feel safe before closeness of any kind, and once you do, the bond is held like a household: joint money is family money, a debt is a promise, and a partner's trouble becomes yours. Betrayals are remembered for a long time; so are kindnesses. In a crisis you are often the one who feeds people, makes the calls and keeps the house running, and that care is how you come through it yourself. Heirlooms, family stories and old photographs can matter to you more than their price, because the 8th also covers what passes between generations. None of this is clinging: Cancer protects what it shares, and protecting is not controlling.

Sagittarius rises with this cusp in nearly 9 London charts in 10, and Scorpio in the rest ([Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/)), so Jupiter usually rules your chart while the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules the 8th: an open, generous manner in front, a guarded heart in matters of trust. The Moon's house is where you learn whom to trust and what to share: the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Opposite, Capricorn opens your 2nd, so Saturn rules what's yours and the Moon what you share: your own money tends to be handled with caution, money in common with feeling. The Midheaven is usually Libra at London, in about 7 charts in 10, and Virgo otherwise, so Venus or Mercury rules your public life; at New York the two are about even.

Because the 2nd cusp, facing it, is crossing Capricorn, one of the quick risers of the north, Cancer holds the 8th for a little under two hours of each day: with Placidus cusps, Cancer is on the 8th in about 1 London chart in 13, and in 1 New York chart in 12. Your 8th is broad, about 42° to 47° at London, wide enough to hold all of Leo as well in about half the London charts and 3 in 10 at New York ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The Sun then shares the house with the Moon, and pride and warmth join care in what you hold with others. Neither neighbouring page fits a chart like this: [Cancer on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/) needs Capricorn rising, and [Cancer on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/) nearly always Scorpio.

Tradition hung death and inheritance on this house. I don't look for death in any chart, and none can say what someone will inherit ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); with Cancer here, I read how you trust, share and protect. All of it waits on the birth time, which is what places the 8th at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
