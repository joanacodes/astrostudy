With the Moon in the 6th house, comfort comes from a day that works. The kettle on at the same time, the list ticked off, the dog walked: routine is how this Moon keeps its balance.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) holds the working day and the habits around it: the tasks you actually do, your routines, the colleagues beside you, the animals you look after and how you look after your body. Like the other cadent houses, it is about adjusting and maintaining, and Virgo is its natural sign. Tradition was blunt about it, calling it Bad Fortune, and put the joy of Mars here, among the chores. With the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) here, habit becomes a form of regulation. The small repeated patterns of the day are how you keep yourself steady, and when they go, you feel it. You also tend to look after people through the ordinary round: the lunch made, the shift covered, the reminder sent.

Illness was part of this house for the old astrologers. A chart can't tell you anything certain about your health, let alone diagnose it; those questions are for a doctor (see [can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). Instead, with the Moon here, I look at how you tend your days and yourself, and how your feelings show in the way you work.

At your birth the Moon was below the western horizon, a little while after moonset. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 6th ends at the Descendant, so a Moon near it sits in the 6th or the 7th depending on a few minutes, and those two houses ask quite different things of a Moon ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)). Before it comes the [Moon in the 5th house](/astrology/moon-in-5th-house/), which steadies itself through play; after it, the [Moon in the 7th house](/astrology/moon-in-7th-house/), which steadies itself through a partner.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You often show love by noticing what someone needs on an ordinary day and quietly seeing to it: a coat by the door when rain is forecast, their favourite tea restocked. What moves you in return is attentiveness of the same small kind. Your moods tend to show in your habits more than in your words, so a partner who learns to read the signs, the skipped breakfast, the suddenly spotless kitchen, can reach you before you've said a thing. Modern astrologers add the animals you care for to this house, and with the Moon here, looking after one can be as steadying as any human bond.

## At work {#at-work}

You tend to be good at the work that keeps things going: caring roles, service, organising, the jobs where people depend on someone turning up and doing it well. Your mood often shows in how you work, quick and focused on a good day, scattered when something is wrong at home. A friendly, predictable team suits you; a disorganised one costs you more than the tasks themselves. You're often the colleague who knows how everyone takes their coffee, and which of them is having a hard week.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

When the routine breaks, the ground goes with it: a holiday, a move or a new rota can unsettle you more than you expect. Feelings can turn into busyness, so you clean, sort and fix instead of noticing what's wrong. You may look after everyone's day except your own. And because practical care comes so naturally, you can end up carrying more of it than anyone realises, yourself included.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Build routines that include you: a fixed time to eat properly, to walk, to stop. When a change is coming, keep one or two small habits intact to carry you through it. Let your work and your mood be separate things now and then, since a bad day isn't a bad job. Anything about your health goes to a doctor, not to a chart; for the rest, give yourself the attentive daily care you give others. And let someone else make the tea sometimes.

[My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) was a routine of its own: chart after chart, a tick or a cross by every placement. The Moon's house took its turn with the rest; the three that pulled well ahead of everything were Saturn, Chiron and Lilith, and in total about a third held. I read a 6th-house Moon with that in mind, carefully, alongside the rest of your chart.
