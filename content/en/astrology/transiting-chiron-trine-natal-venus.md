A trine is the angle you can sleep through. When Chiron makes one to your Venus, an old tenderness about being liked, or about what you find beautiful, becomes easier to touch, and it's possible to let the whole season go by without using it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) chooses: whom you're drawn to, what pleases you, how you like to give and be given to. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a sensitivity that stays and the know-how that grows up around it. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets two functions help each other while each keeps its own nature, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the sore spot and your pleasure stop getting in each other's way.

In practice it can be small and very welcome. A pleasure you dropped after someone laughed at it, singing, dancing, dressing the way you like, comes back within reach. Care offered by a friend is easier to accept without the usual joke or apology. And the thing that once made you feel unattractive or out of place turns out to be what makes you good at welcoming others: you know exactly who in the room needs a word. Chiron's people are often better at helping than at being helped; this trine tips the balance, briefly, towards receiving.

Ease is not inevitability. The trine supports what you start, and it starts nothing by itself. It doesn't promise a romance, a reconciliation or a lucky meeting, and I never read it that way.

With [Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house/), the house of play and creation, Chiron is often crossing your 9th ([Chiron transiting the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-9th-house/)): something you study or come to believe makes room for a pleasure you'd set aside. Those houses rest on your birth time, the reason I don't read this trine without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Venus trine Chiron](/astrology/venus-trine-chiron/), you'll recognise the ease: the sky is repeating, for a while, an angle your chart already holds.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

I count the trine from 1½° before exact to 1° after. Within them, a quick single crossing is over in about three weeks, which happens in about one trine in three and is easy to miss. The usual pattern is longer: Chiron's retrograde loop brings it over the exact trine three times, nearly two passes out of three, over about ten and a half months. Now and then, about once in twenty, it crosses five times and the easy angle lasts about twenty-one months. Which shape you get depends on Chiron's speed in that part of the zodiac, and its stretched orbit makes the speed vary widely.

Most people meet this trine three times by 80, a few twice, a few four times, and nobody misses it. There are two trines in each round: the first after [transiting Chiron square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-venus/) and before [transiting Chiron opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-venus/), the second after the opposition, on the way back through the second square and [transiting Chiron sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-venus/) to [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-venus/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take the help that's offered, and leave out the apology.

Go back to one pleasure you gave up because of an old embarrassment, and do it badly, for fun.

Tell someone what they once did that helped you. It's the kind of exchange this trine makes easy.

Make or arrange something beautiful with no audience in mind.
