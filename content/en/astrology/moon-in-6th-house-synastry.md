Their Moon in your 6th house shows up in small, practical kindnesses. Another person's feelings settle into your routines and working days, and care between you tends to look like a sandwich made, an errand run, a task quietly taken off your list.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) covers your ordinary days: the work you actually do, your habits and routines, the people beside you, and the way you look after your body through sleep, food, movement and rest. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is a person's need for comfort and the habits that keep them steady, so it is at home among routines. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their need for steadiness meets the rhythm of your days.

For you, the effect is often being looked after without any fuss. They notice when you've skipped lunch, remember the appointment you forgot, or simply make the working day kinder by being in it. You may find your routines steadier, and some of them more pleasant, because they're shared.

For them, your daily rhythm can be deeply reassuring. The way you organise a morning, keep a kitchen running or get through a list soothes them, and helping you can be how they feel close. As a cadent house, the 6th keeps things maintained and adjusted; it corresponds to [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) in the natural zodiac, and tradition put the joy of Mars here.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Care that lives in routines can slide into management. Their concern for how you eat, sleep or work may start to feel like supervision, and small daily requests can carry feelings that would be better said outright. On difficult days their mood may colour your working hours, and yours theirs.

From their side, practical care is easily taken for granted. They may feel that what they do every day goes unseen, or that your routines leave little room for how they feel, as if the list mattered more than the person helping with it.

Tradition called the 6th Bad Fortune and gave it illness. Another person's Moon here says nothing about your health, and no chart can ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); what it describes is how care and habit pass between you.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make the care visible and the jobs fair. Thank each other for the small things, and say plainly which help you want and which you'd rather do yourself. In a couple, a few shared rituals, the morning coffee, the Sunday shop, can hold a great deal of affection; between friends, an exercise or cooking habit done together suits this overlay well; between colleagues, it's the person who makes the office feel humane, and who shouldn't become everyone's carer; between adult relatives, practical help works best when it's asked for.

Seen from their chart, the same overlay puts your Moon in their 6th house; then helping is how you show care, and it's worth checking that it's still welcome. Near the end of your 6th, their Moon approaches your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), and everyday care then starts to feel like partnership. [Their Moon in your 5th house](/astrology/moon-in-5th-house-synastry/) is play; [their Moon in your 7th house](/astrology/moon-in-7th-house-synastry/) is partnership itself.

Their Moon gains about a degree every two hours, so close to the end of your 6th, the hour of their birth can decide whether it sits in your 6th or on your Descendant. Your own time sets that line, and in Placidus, the house system I use, every other cusp with it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)).
