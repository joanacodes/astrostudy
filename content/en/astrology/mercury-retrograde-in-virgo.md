In Virgo, Mercury is in its own sign and its exaltation at once, so its retrograde there is Mercury’s craft turning back on itself: checking, sorting, correcting. It comes between early August and early October, and in the dates I use it often comes three years in a row.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), mutable earth, is where tradition places Mercury at its most precise: Gemini gathers, Virgo sorts. When [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) arrives, the planet appears from Earth to stop and back through the sign, and that precision turns from the next task to the last one: the report you filed, the instructions you wrote, the system you set up and now see could work better. It’s a season of editing more than writing, of catching the small error before it becomes a habit.

Sixteen retrogrades between 2000 and 2040 back through Virgo, three years in a row in 2002–2004, 2009–2011, 2022–2024 and 2035–2037. The most recent ran from 5 to 28 August 2024, from 4°06′ Virgo back to 21°24′ Leo ([Mercury retrograde in Leo](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-leo/)). The next begins on 2 September 2029 at 1°49′ Libra ([Mercury retrograde in Libra](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-libra/)) and ends at 17°26′ Virgo on 25 September; a year later, from 16 August to 8 September 2030, Mercury stays wholly in Virgo, from 14°29′ to 1°06′. In 2015 it turned direct at 0°53′ Libra, within a degree of Virgo but not in it.

Mercury born retrograde in Virgo is read differently, as a careful mind that checks its own work for life; [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/) says how.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house it crosses in your chart depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): the cusps turn with the hours, and without the time I can’t place them, which is why I don’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, a retrograde of 12½° to 15° crosses a cusp more often than the narrow ones of May and June, so I look at both station degrees.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/virgo-rising/):** Virgo rising: your careful, capable manner; the way you present your work, double-checked.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/virgo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** a practical sense of worth; your records and lists, and what you know about where things stand, brought up to date.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/virgo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** precise words and learning by analysis; a fact you were sure of, checked again.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an orderly home; the household systems everyone relies on, overhauled one shelf at a time.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/virgo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** creative craft and small attentions; a piece you’d call finished, refined once more.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/virgo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** Virgo in its natural house: daily work and method; a routine improved rather than replaced. I read the 6th for working days, never for illness ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
7. **[7th house](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Virgo on the Descendant, with Pisces rising: a partnership that works day to day; who does what, talked through again.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/virgo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** careful trust; the details of what’s shared, gone through slowly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/virgo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs examined in detail; a course of study or a travel plan, checked before it’s set.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a reputation for doing things properly; a professional standard you set yourself, reconsidered.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/virgo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** the one who organises the group; shared plans, put in order again with everyone’s help.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/virgo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** the quiet sorting you do in private; time to set the checking mind down.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose what to correct: a retrograde in a sign this exacting can find something to fix everywhere, and not all of it needs fixing. Mercury rules Virgo, so the house of your natal Mercury shows where the review tends to go.

Edit, check and file, then let good enough be good enough. Precision is a gift; a retrograde in Virgo asks you to aim it.
