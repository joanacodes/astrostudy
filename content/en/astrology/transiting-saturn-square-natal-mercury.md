Twice in each of Saturn's rounds, seven years or so from a crossing on either side, Saturn stands at right angles to your Mercury. The way you think and speak meets resistance in these seasons, and finds out what it's made of.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) sets standards, limits and deadlines; [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) thinks, learns, argues and speaks. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction that pushes development, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) ideas meet resistance: a proposal sent back, a course harder than it looked, a conversation with someone in authority that needs more preparation than you'd like. The friction is real, and so is what it builds: arguments made properly, words chosen with care.

Doubt tends to get louder. Analysis can tip into going round in circles, and criticism, from others or from yourself, lands harder than usual. Saturn's square takes nothing from your mind; it asks your thinking to carry more weight. What usually helps is structure, a deadline, a list, a draft, rather than more thinking.

Saturn also stands for authority, and Mercury for your voice, so speaking up to a manager, a teacher, an institution or an elder of the family can feel harder than usual. Preparation helps: notes, a clear request, a time agreed in advance. What you learn to say plainly under this square tends to stay sayable afterwards.

From your 7th, for instance, the resistance comes through partners, clients and agreements, terms to read slowly and discuss plainly ([Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/)). This square doesn't predict the outcome of an exam, a contract or a dispute, and I never read it for one ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). With [Mercury square Saturn](/astrology/mercury-square-saturn/) in your chart, you'll know this friction already; each square in transit is a chance to use the voice it has given you.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the resistance usually lasts about nine months, and a little over half the time it comes back three times in a loop of about nine and a half ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)). Now and then Saturn makes the square at full speed, and the whole thing takes three weeks. Your birth time decides the house it plays out in ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Five or six of these squares come by 80 for people born between 1930 and 2009, two in each round, and together with [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-mercury/) and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-mercury/) they split the round into quarters. Before the first square comes [transiting Saturn sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-mercury/); after it, [transiting Saturn trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-mercury/); the second square repeats the pattern in reverse.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put the argument on paper before you make it out loud. Saturn's squares reward the version of an idea that has been written, checked and cut.

Give each worry a slot: a list, a time to look at it, a decision about the next step. Thinking in circles eases when it has edges.

Ask for specific feedback, and keep only what's useful. Criticism under this square can be sharp, and the skill it builds is telling the fair part from the rest.
