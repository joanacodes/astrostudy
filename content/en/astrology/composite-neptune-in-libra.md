Libra weighs two things until they balance, which is also how a composite chart is made. With Neptune in Libra, the relationship idealises the very thing it is: a partnership of equals.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

A [composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) sets every planet at the point of equal distance between two birth charts, and astrologers read the result as the pair's own chart rather than either partner's. Its [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows what the bond idealises, where its compassion and its imagination meet. [Libra](/astrology/libra/), cardinal air ruled by [Venus](/astrology/venus/), is the sign that most loves that whole idea of a midpoint, and with Neptune here the pair's dream is the partnership itself: two equals, fair to each other, making something gracious together. Such couples often have a gift for tact, for beauty in the spaces they share and for making peace, with friends and family as much as between themselves; they can be the pair others turn to when a quarrel needs a go-between. In daily life it can show as rooms arranged to suit both people's tastes, decisions left open until both agree, and a courtesy between the two that years together don't wear away.

Two people born between October 1942 and August 1957 were both born with [Neptune in Libra](/astrology/neptune-in-libra/), and their composite shares it. So do many pairs from either side of that generation: with one partner born in June 1940, Neptune at about 23° Virgo, and the other in June 1958, at about 2° Scorpio, the midpoint lands at about 12° Libra. Pair people born between 1930 and 2009 at random, two million times over, and about 1 pair in 6 gets it. For any of these couples, the sign speaks first of the years they were born in; the house, read from both birth times, is where the dream finds its own address.

In one birth chart, Neptune in Libra describes a generation's ideal of perfect love; in a composite, the ideal belongs to the bond, and it can sit between two people who would each describe love quite differently. Next door, [composite Neptune in Virgo](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-virgo/) gives the bond a useful ideal and [composite Neptune in Scorpio](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-scorpio/) a deep one; Libra's is a fair one.

The difficulty is a peace kept by leaving things unsaid. A bond that idealises harmony can treat every disagreement as a failure of the dream, keep score so carefully that fairness turns into accountancy, or show the world such a graceful face that neither partner admits when something hurts. Harmony isn't the absence of conflict; for this pair, an honest quarrel, kindly conducted, can be the most Libran thing they do.

This reading of Libra belongs to the composite technique, which I describe here but don't practise. Those who do practise it read it with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and none of it says whether two people will stay together. In Libra above all, a reading of two people is fair only when both have said yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
