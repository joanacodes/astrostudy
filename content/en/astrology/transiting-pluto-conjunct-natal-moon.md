When Pluto reaches the degree of your Moon, the planet of renewal settles for two to three years on the part of you that feels, needs and attaches. Nothing stands between them: whatever Pluto reworks, it reworks in the place where you look for comfort.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) regenerates: it clears out what no longer works so that something sounder can grow. Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates your emotional life, your attachments and your sense of being safe, mostly through habit and memory. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one process, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) goes straight to the root of how you look after yourself. A routine that always calmed you can stop working. An attachment you never questioned can ask to be looked at. Memories return with their feelings still attached.

When the ground moves like this, the reflex is to tighten your grip on everything. Control can feel like safety, and it isn't the same thing. What the conjunction tends to build, slowly, is a security that doesn't depend on everything staying put. However strong the feelings, you remain the person feeling them.

The other Pluto contacts reach the Moon from some distance: the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-moon/)) and the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-moon/)) open doors you may or may not use, the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-moon/)) presses on habits, the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-moon/)) arrives through the people closest to you. The conjunction leaves no distance at all. Born with [Moon conjunct Pluto](/astrology/moon-conjunct-pluto/), you met this transit as a small child or never, and with the Moon moving so fast, a few hours either side of the exact conjunction decided which.

The Moon in your chart is your own way of feeling and caring, not your mother, and this transit predicts nothing about anyone's health or life ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). Should what surfaces feel heavy, or your mood stay low for weeks, please talk to a doctor or a therapist; care comes first, and no chart should delay it ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

With your Moon in the 4th, the conjunction comes during Pluto's long stay in the house of home and roots ([Pluto transiting the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-4th-house/)) and works on what home has meant since childhood. The house needs your birth time, and so does the Moon's exact degree: the Moon moves about half a degree an hour, and with Pluto crossing Aquarius at about a degree and a half a year, one hour's difference moves this transit by about four months. With so much riding on the hour, I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

I count a transit from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and on that count Pluto spends about two years and eight months on your Moon, the quickest passes about twenty-two months and the slowest nearly four years. About two passes in five bring five exact hits rather than three, because Pluto backs over the degree twice before it moves on.

Pluto needs some 248 years for one round, so a lifetime sees only a stretch of it, and whether your Moon lies on that stretch depends on where it was at birth. More than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet this conjunction by 80.

While Pluto crosses Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it reaches Moons in that sign, the early degrees first: 5° in 2026, 20° in 2036 and the last degrees in the early 2040s.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep one daily ritual that feeds you, and keep it on the hard days too.

Let what comes up be as old as it is. Some of it belongs to who you were at seven, and it can be listened to without being obeyed.

Choose who hears it. A conjunction to the Moon asks for a small circle: one or two people, or a therapist, rather than everyone at once.
