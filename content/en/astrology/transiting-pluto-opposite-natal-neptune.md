No one born between 1930 and 2009 will live to see Pluto opposite their natal Neptune. The two planets meet in every one of those lives, but in other ways, and the reason this meeting never comes says a good deal about Pluto.

## What changes {#what-changes}

In a life, nothing: the transit doesn't arrive. In principle, an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, would relate [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the function of ideals and compassion, to [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the function of transformation, through polarity: your ideals facing a deep change that reaches you through other people. It is a real [transit](/astrology/transits/), and worth knowing to be out of reach, because a forecast that names it for you has mixed up the planets.

What these generations meet instead is a sequence that stops short of it. Pluto crosses your Neptune between about 22 and 30 ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-neptune/)), squares it in the sixties or seventies for about 2 people in 3 ([Transiting Pluto square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-neptune/)), and trines it near 80 for about 1 in 6 ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-neptune/)). The sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-neptune/)) is the one nearly every life holds, in the cradle, in the fifties or both. Each is a step in the long exchange between your ideals and your capacity for renewal; the opposition would be its far end, and no life reaches it.

The opposition between these two planets does reach every one of these lives, the other way round. [Transiting Neptune opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-pluto/) comes once to everyone born from 1930 to 2009, between about 51 and 62, because Neptune, already ahead of Pluto at birth, keeps moving away from it at a steady pace. [Neptune opposite Pluto](/astrology/neptune-opposite-pluto/) exists in no chart of those years.

Your natal Neptune's house still matters, because it is where the transits that do come will land, and placing it needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

If it came, it would last like any Pluto transit within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after: about two years and eight months, with three exact hits in about three passes out of five and five in the rest. The question is how long it would take to arrive.

In every chart from 1930 to 2009, Pluto stood some 45° to 65° behind Neptune, so to reach the point opposite your Neptune it must travel some 225° to 245°. Pluto's orbit is so stretched that in 80 years it covers anywhere from about 110° to about 170°, fastest around its closest approach to the Sun, in September 1989. The earliest births counted would have to live to about 112 to meet it; for births of 1945, the age is about 125, and for everyone born from about 1960 on, more than 140.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Check which planet is moving. If a list of your transits seems to show this one, look again: the form of this opposition that does reach you is transiting Neptune opposite natal Pluto, in your fifties.

Read the conjunction you lived in your twenties. It is the Pluto–Neptune transit everyone has, and the one that says most about how your ideals were remade.

Look ahead to the square if it is still to come. For people born from 1957 to 1970, it arrives while Pluto crosses Aquarius, between about 65 and 72.
