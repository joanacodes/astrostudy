Composite Pluto in Scorpio sets the relationship's planet of deep change in its own modern sign. Pluto crossed Scorpio faster than any other sign, from November 1983 to November 1995, and pairs born in those years, now in their thirties and early forties, are the ones who mostly carry it.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), fixed water, works beneath the surface: it investigates, consolidates and recovers. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is at home in this sign, and in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) it makes closeness and truth the way the bond changes. Astrologers read a pair that goes to the bottom of things together, the family secret finally discussed, the debt paid off side by side, the grief shared rather than borne alone, and that comes back from each hard season altered but intact. What it builds, it builds to last, and it doesn't do anything halfway.

Its trap is mistaking intensity for truth. A pair like this can come to trust only what hurts: the conversation at three in the morning counts, the easy Sunday doesn't, and calm starts to look like a lie waiting to be uncovered. Depth doesn't need drama to be real. Scorpio has two rulers, Pluto in modern tropical astrology and Mars in the tradition, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). With Pluto in its own sign, composite [Mars](/astrology/mars/) becomes the other key: it shows how the relationship acts on what it feels, and how it fights.

Pluto moves fastest near the Sun, which is why its Scorpio stay lasted only about twelve years. Two people born within it share [Pluto in Scorpio](/astrology/pluto-in-scorpio/) in their own charts, and their composite has it as well. An age gap reaches it from outside: a parent born in June 1975, with Pluto at about 6° Libra, and a child born in June 2000, with Pluto at about 11° Sagittarius, have a composite Pluto at about 9° Scorpio, a sign neither was born with. Across random pairs of birth dates from 1930 to 2009, about 1 in 6 lands here.

So the sign says a good deal about the years you were born in and little about the two of you in particular. The house, which needs both birth times and depends on how the composite is drawn, carries the personal reading; with [composite Pluto in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-8th-house/) as well, Pluto would sit in its own modern sign and in Scorpio's own house at once. [Composite Pluto in Libra](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-libra/), on one side, belongs mostly to pairs a little older; [composite Pluto in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-sagittarius/), on the other, to pairs born between 1995 and 2008.

Depth never excuses fear. If either of you is frightened of the other's reactions, or has their messages, money or movements checked, no placement explains or softens that: ring 999 in a UK emergency, or 988 by call or text in the US and Canada; the rest are gathered on my Disclaimers page ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)). A bond this private also deserves a private chart, drawn only when both of you want to look ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
