Their North Node in your 4th house sets the direction the other person is growing in at the bottom of your chart: your home, your roots, your private life. Their South Node sits at the top, in your 10th, among your work and standing, and the distance between your front door and your office is what this overlay is about.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is the ground you stand on: home, family, belonging, the self only close people see. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows a capacity someone is still practising. Through this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), the practice is belonging, being at home, unhurried and off duty, and they learn it near you.

For you, they may want to be in your kitchen more than in your diary. They ask where you grew up, notice how you've arranged a room, sit down to a meal without looking at their phone. Seen through their interest, your private life can feel worth more than you'd usually allow.

For them, your home shows what an inner life with roots can look like. Letting feelings and belonging count as much as achievement is their stretch; it comes slowly, but it comes. The 4th is the most private of the angular houses, and [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), its natural sign, is ruled by the Moon, the body whose crossings of the Sun's path make the nodes. Close to your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), their North Node meets the root of your chart and their South Node your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), so their line runs straight down your meridian.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Their [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) makes your public life the place where they're competent and at ease: your career, your reputation, your ambitions. They may give you sharp professional advice, introduce you to the right people, and seem oddly unsettled on your sofa.

From their side, the pull towards home can feel like idleness. When they slip back into strategy, planning your next career move over dinner, it's the old ground calling, not a lack of care; still, you may have wanted company and got a consultant.

The 4th describes how home and belonging feel from inside, not anyone's parents or family history, and this overlay doesn't say whether you'll ever share a roof. It shows a direction they're practising and the ground they already know.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Invite them in, in the plainest sense. In a couple, let ordinary evenings count as much as plans; between friends, cook for them rather than meeting in town; between colleagues, this may be the one who asks about your weekend and means it, so answer; between adult relatives, let them belong without having to perform.

Swap the charts and it becomes your North Node in their 4th house, with their home as the place where you learn to settle. [Their North Node in your 3rd house](/astrology/north-node-in-3rd-house-synastry/) sits a step back, in daily talk; [their North Node in your 5th house](/astrology/north-node-in-5th-house-synastry/) a step on, in play and pleasure.

The IC goes round the whole zodiac in a day, and in Placidus, the house system I use, it is the cusp of your 4th, so without your birth time there is no knowing where your home ground begins ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Their node moves slowly enough for their birth date to place it, about 18 months to a sign. If it lies a degree or two from your IC, the mean node and the true node may split between your 3rd and 4th, sending the South Node to your 9th or your 10th with them; I look at both, and their own IC needs their time just as yours needs yours. Home is private ground, so this is for two adults who have both agreed to have it read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
