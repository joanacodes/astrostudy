Once in every round of the nodes, the point your chart grows towards slides back over the degree of your Saturn. For a month or so, the less familiar direction and your sense of duty stand on the same spot, and the question is what you are willing to build there.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the part of a chart that structures: commitments, limits, discipline, the long view. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity you develop by practising it, awkward before it is easy. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into one process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) growth arrives in Saturn's clothes: as a responsibility, a skill that needs hours, a commitment you could take on or decline.

What tends to show is a duty that is also a door. The course that takes two years, the role nobody else wants, the boundary you have never set: each is slower than the habit across the chart, where the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) now sits opposite your Saturn and the familiar way to cope is ready to hand. Saturn's blind spot is believing that control guarantees safety; the node's is expecting the new to feel rewarding at once. This month tends to correct both, quietly. Saturn is a timetable, not a wall: what comes slowly here comes to stay.

Saturn is one of the three placements that held best in my notebook, 75 to 90% of the time ([Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#saturn)), so I read its house closely before I say anything about this month. With Saturn in your 10th, the conjunction falls during the node's stay there ([North Node transiting the 10th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-10th-house/)), and the work is a public role taken on deliberately. Placing that house takes your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My orbs run from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and the true node usually takes a month or so to cross them. If the conjunction falls in an eclipse season, the node barely moves in the weeks the Sun spends near it, and the pass lasts three months or more; in about one pass in 25 it steps forward over the degree and back again, for three exact hits. Eclipses keep within about 17° of the nodes, so the year or so either side of this transit, and of the opposition nine years later, are the only stretches of the round in which one can land on your Saturn.

How old you are the first time depends on the node's place at your birth. For someone born on 1 January 1965 the first exact hit came at 5.8, for 1 January 1975 at 7.3; after that it comes back every 18.6 years, and by 80 your Saturn has had four or five of these crossings. From July 2026 to March 2028 the node crosses Aquarius, so it is the turn of people born with [Saturn in Aquarius](/astrology/saturn-in-aquarius/), in the years Saturn spent there, January 1962 to December 1964 and February 1991 to January 1994, each when the node reaches their own degree. With [Saturn conjunct North Node](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-north-node/) in your chart, every nodal return repeats it.

The round continues: about three years later the node sextiles your Saturn ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-saturn/)), then squares it ([Transiting North Node square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-saturn/)) and trines it ([Transiting North Node trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-saturn/)), and about nine years on the South Node arrives ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-saturn/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one commitment that serves the direction you are growing in, and give it a timetable: a weekly hour, a date to review it.

Find someone who has built what you want to build, and ask how they paced it. Saturn learns well from people further along.

Keep the South Node's skills as tools, not refuges. They will steady the new structure while it sets.
