Secondary progressions turn a day of the sky into a year of your life. Change the exchange rate and you get other progressions: a day for a month, or a month for a year. Tertiary and minor progressions are two of them, and they run the same sky much faster.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

All progressions read the sky of the days after your birth as a symbolic clock. What differs is the rate. [Secondary progressions](/astrology/secondary-progressions/), by far the most used, take a day for a year. Tertiary progressions take one day after birth for one lunar month of life. Minor progressions, also called quaternary, take one lunar month after birth for one year of life.

A lunar month can mean the Moon's return to the same place in the zodiac, about 27⅓ days, the interval a [lunar return](/astrology/lunar-return/) marks, or the time from one new moon to the next, about 29½. Either way, the clocks run fast. In a tertiary progression, a year of life uses only about thirteen days of sky; in a minor progression, about four weeks.

The family is wider still. Some astrologers use rates borrowed from the planets, a day of the ephemeris for twelve years in a Jupiter progression, a month for thirty years in a Saturn one, and [converse progressions](/astrology/converse-progressions/) run any of these clocks backwards from birth. [Solar arc directions](/astrology/solar-arc-directions/) and [primary directions](/astrology/primary-directions/) belong to the same broad search for a measure of time hidden in the birth chart.

## How it is done {#how-it-is-done}

A program does the counting: choose tertiary or minor progressions, give the date, and it casts the chart for the matching moment after birth. The arithmetic shows how quickly things move:

- **Tertiary:** the progressed Sun moves about 12 to 13° a year, so it changes sign every two and a half years or so; the progressed Moon goes round the whole zodiac in about two years.
- **Minor:** the progressed Sun moves about 27 to 29° a year, close to a sign; the progressed Moon goes round the zodiac once a year, which puts it on every natal planet in turn.
- **Secondary, for comparison:** the [progressed Sun](/astrology/progressed-sun/) moves about a degree a year, and the progressed Moon takes about 27 years to go round ([the progressed Moon](/astrology/progressed-moon/)).

Then you look for the same things as in any progression: changes of sign and house, and aspects to natal points within a narrow orb, a degree either side. Which lunar month a program uses changes the rates a little, so check its settings before comparing two charts.

Like every progression, these start from the natal positions, and the Moon's degree and the angles depend on the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

Astrologers read them much as they read secondary progressions: as development from within, timed, while outside circumstances belong to transits. The difference is the scale. With the usual degree of orb, a contact from the secondary progressed Sun lasts about two years; from the tertiary Sun, about two months; from the minor Sun, three or four weeks.

The natal chart decides what any of it can mean. A progressed contact between two planets brings out what their natal relationship already holds, and one between planets with no natal aspect weighs less than one that repeats a natal link.

I don't use either. My forecasts move the chart with secondary progressions and solar arcs, and read the shorter seasons through the [transits](/astrology/transits/) of Jupiter to Pluto, of Chiron and of the true node ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## Its limits {#its-limits}

The faster the clock, the more contacts it makes. A minor progressed Moon crosses every natal planet each year and a tertiary one every two years or so, so there is always something to point at. Read alone, that busyness can make any month look meaningful after the fact.

They are also symbolic twice over. The positions are real, but the rate is a convention, and different conventions give different charts for the same day of your life. Nothing in the sky moves when a tertiary Sun changes sign.
