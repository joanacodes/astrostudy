Pluto settles for two years or more on the degree of your natal Saturn, the part of you that builds, carries and endures, and the structures your life rests on are examined from underneath.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) establishes structure: responsibility, boundaries, discipline, the long work of making something last. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms, clearing out what has become unsustainable. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one process, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) works on the frame itself: the duties you took on, the rules you set yourself, the role that has shaped your days. Some of it turns out to be load-bearing and is reinforced. Some was built for a life you no longer lead, and comes down slowly.

These two planets share a blind spot: Saturn believes control guarantees safety, and Pluto confuses control with safety. Tightening every rule when the ground shifts is the reflex; the work is telling a structure that protects you from one that only keeps you busy.

Born with [Saturn conjunct Pluto](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-pluto/) after the exact meeting, in August 1947 or November 1982, you had this transit in early childhood, with Saturn just ahead of Pluto; born before it, you never will.

Your natal Saturn's house says where the rebuilding happens: in my notebook, Saturn was one of the three placements that held most often, 75 to 90% of the time ([Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). With Saturn in your 10th, the career you built is reworked during Pluto's long stay there ([Pluto transiting the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-10th-house/)); with Saturn in the 4th, the duties of home and family. Without your birth time, Saturn has no house, and this part of the reading is lost with it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

None of this forecasts a loss, an illness or an ending. If the weight of a stretch like this stays with you for weeks, a doctor or a therapist is the right help ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/); [disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I time by, Pluto spends about two years and eight months on your Saturn, most passes between twenty-two months and three and three-quarter years. Three exact hits in about three passes out of five, five in the rest: Pluto turns back over the degree before it lets it go.

Three people in five born between 1930 and 2009 reach 80 without it, and no one has it twice. Saturn changes sign every two and a half years, so the age swings with the birth date: for births on 1 January, 7.9 in 1985, 35 in 1955, 53.2 in 1995 and 77.4 in 1965, while 1935, 1945, 1975 and 2005 don't see it by 80.

While Pluto crosses Aquarius, until early 2044, it reaches Saturns in Aquarius: people born from 1962 to 1964 meet it in their sixties and seventies, and those born from 1991 to 1994 between about 31 and 49 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)).

Pluto's other contacts with Saturn are commoner: about 6 people in 7 meet the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-saturn/)), about 3 in 4 the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-saturn/)) and nearly 4 in 5 the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-saturn/)), while the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-saturn/)) misses just over half.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List what you carry: every duty, rule and commitment, and beside each one, whether you would take it on again today.

Rebuild one structure at a time. A working life or a household can be redesigned room by room rather than in one demolition.

Hand something over. Saturn finds delegating hard, and this season goes better when not every beam rests on you.
