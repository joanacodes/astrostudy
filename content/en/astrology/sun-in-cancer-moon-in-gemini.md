Memory is your Sun's territory and conversation your Moon's: you care for people by keeping them talking, and keeping them in touch. The lunar month was in its last dark days when you were born.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and mutable air sit next to each other in the zodiac, the Moon one sign back from the Sun: a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign, a relation of small, constant adjustments. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) organises a life around home, family in the widest sense, and the past it carries; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) steadies itself by putting feelings into words, asking questions and keeping the news flowing. One light keeps the memories, the other tells them, and family stories tend to live on through you.

In this pair the chain of command runs through the Moon: it rules Cancer, so it has the last word on your Sun, and it speaks from Gemini, curious, sociable and quick to change. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of Gemini, completes the chain, and never far from the Sun, it keeps to Gemini, Cancer or Leo when the Sun is in Cancer. In Gemini it shares the Moon's sign and the talk flows easily; in Cancer it thinks through memory and feeling, with the Sun. Neither Cancer nor Gemini adds a dignity to its light, so the chain from Sun to Moon to Mercury does the work.

These are the last days of the lunar month: the Moon still had 1° to 59° to go before catching the Sun, a fine crescent before dawn or nothing at all. In the eight-phase count nearly every one of these births is [balsamic](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), the phase of endings and of making room; just the earliest are [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) births. The new moon that followed would usually fall in Cancer, the Sun's sign: a [new moon in Cancer](/astrology/new-moon-in-cancer/). A Moon in Gemini's final degrees and a Sun in Cancer's first can even meet in a conjunction across the sign line. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says more about the closing phases.

In the count, this pair comes up roughly once in 139 births. The Moon lingers in Gemini about two and a half days; on the day it crossed over from Taurus, or on to Cancer, the birth time is what separates this page from the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-taurus/) and the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-cancer/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). That time also places the rising sign and the houses: the setting this whole family of feelings lives in.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love by staying close in conversation: the call on the way home, the message at lunchtime, the long talk at the kitchen table. The Cancer Sun looks for someone to shelter and come home to; the Gemini Moon, for someone to swap the day's small news with, and to listen. Worry can come out as questions, many of them, which a partner may hear as pressure. Saying the feeling behind the questions, *I miss you*, *I'm anxious about this*, usually settles both of you faster.

## At work {#at-work}

Communication in the service of a community suits you: teaching, local journalism, customer care, family history, running a club or a staff newsletter, anywhere people need to feel informed and included. The Sun cares most that the work keeps a group together; the Moon wants variety, contact and something new to learn each week. Isolation drains both lights, and so does a workplace where nobody talks to anybody.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Care can scatter. The Gemini Moon checks on everyone and the Cancer Sun feels responsible for all of them, and you may end the day having touched base with twenty people without having been looked after yourself. Anxiety, too, can be talked round and round rather than soothed. What helps is choosing depth over breadth now and then: one long, unhurried conversation with one person who knows you, and a quiet hour that belongs to nobody but you.
