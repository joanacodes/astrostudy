The Sun has moved on into Cancer, where belonging and memory matter most, while the Moon is still a sign behind in quick, talkative Gemini. With this pair you look after people by staying in touch with them, and you were born in the dark days just before a new moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and mutable air sit next to each other in the zodiac, the Moon one sign back from the Sun: a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign, a relation of small, constant adjustments. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) organises a life around home, family in the widest sense, and the past it carries; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) steadies itself by putting feelings into words, asking questions and keeping the news flowing. One light keeps the memories, the other tells them, and family stories tend to live on through you.

Because the Moon rules Cancer, your Moon is also the ruler of your Sun, and it stands in Gemini: your purpose takes its cues from a curious, sociable, quick-changing Moon. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of Gemini, completes the chain, and with a Cancer Sun it can only be in Gemini, Cancer or Leo. In Gemini it shares the Moon's sign and the talk flows easily; in Cancer it thinks through memory and feeling, with the Sun. Tradition gives the lights no dignity here, so the chain from Sun to Moon to Mercury does the work.

The Moon was 1° to 59° behind the Sun, in the last five days of the lunar month: a thin crescent before sunrise, or no Moon at all. All but about one birth in eight is [balsamic](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), the phase I read as finishing and making room, and the earliest belong to the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/). The new moon that followed would usually fall in Cancer, the Sun's sign: a [new moon in Cancer](/astrology/new-moon-in-cancer/). With the Sun early in Cancer and the Moon late in Gemini, the lights can also form an out-of-sign conjunction across the border. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says more about the closing phases.

Roughly one person in 139 is born with it. The Moon passes through Gemini in about two and a half days, and on the day it arrived from Taurus or left for Cancer, the birth time is what separates this page from the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-taurus/) and the double Cancer of the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-cancer/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also gives the rising sign and the houses, the setting this whole family of feelings lives in.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love by staying close in conversation: the call on the way home, the message at lunchtime, the long talk at the kitchen table. The Cancer Sun wants a partner it can shelter and come home to; the Gemini Moon wants one who talks, listens and shares the small news of the day. Worry can come out as questions, many of them, which a partner may hear as pressure. Saying the feeling behind the questions, *I miss you*, *I'm anxious about this*, usually settles both of you faster.

## At work {#at-work}

You often do well where communication serves a community: teaching, local journalism, customer care, family history, running a club or a staff newsletter, anywhere people need to feel informed and included. The Sun wants the work to protect someone or keep a group together; the Moon wants variety, contact and something new to learn each week. Isolation drains both lights, and so does a workplace where nobody talks to anybody.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Care can scatter. The Gemini Moon checks on everyone and the Cancer Sun feels responsible for all of them, and you may end the day having touched base with twenty people without having been looked after yourself. Anxiety, too, can be talked round and round rather than soothed. What helps is choosing depth over breadth now and then: one long, unhurried conversation with one person who knows you, and a quiet hour that belongs to nobody but you.
