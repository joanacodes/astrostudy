Halve a quintile and you have the decile: two planets 36° apart, a tenth of the circle. I read it as potential on a small scale, a particular ability that two planets can sharpen into a speciality.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Thirty-six degrees is one sign and six. Unless the first planet sits in the last six degrees of its sign, a decile joins neighbouring signs; only from those last degrees does it skip one.

What I read in it is creative potential refined into something specific. Where the quintile organises material into a pattern, the decile narrows and polishes: a technique, a particular way of using two planets together that grows more precise with practice. It focuses ability rather than spreading it: one thing, done with increasing care. It doesn't guarantee talent, and it confers no superiority. Blocked, it hardens into rigid specialisation; developed, it becomes a fine, specific skill.

Its parent is the [quintile](/astrology/quintile/), one of Johannes Kepler's three additions to Ptolemy's aspects in *Harmonices Mundi* (Linz, 1619); the decile is that 72° halved, which is why it's also called the semi-quintile. [Harmonic charts](/astrology/harmonic-charts/) are built on dividing the circle by five, seven, nine or eleven; the decile divides it by ten, twice five, and in the fifth-harmonic chart it shows up as an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/).

It needs a tight orb, a degree or so (see [orbs](/astrology/orbs/)), since the undecile at 32°44′ and the novile at 40° sit close on either side. It falls 6° past the semi-sextile and 24° short of the [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), and its multiples run through the major aspects' territory: two make the quintile, three the tredecile, between the [square](/astrology/square/) and the [trine](/astrology/trine/), and five the opposition.

## Between which planets {#between-which-planets}

Deciles don't get a page for each pair of planets; I read them by speed, and by what each of the two planets is good at.

For two personal planets, a decile tends to show as a particular knack: Mercury and Venus, say, refining a way with words, or the Moon and Mars a way of acting on instinct with precision. With a slow planet, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune or Pluto, the speciality takes years to refine, and that's the decile I'd pay attention to. Between two slow planets it marks a generation, and the houses show where that refinement happens in your life.

Among the minor aspects, its neighbours are the [quadnovile](/astrology/quadnovile/), at 160°, the far end of the ninths, and the [tredecile](/astrology/tredecile/), at 108°, three deciles, where refining turns into reorganising.

## The decile in synastry {#in-synastry}

In synastry, a decile often shows as one person helping the other refine something specific: a technique, a habit of attention, a way of working that gets sharper in the other's company. It's small beside a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) or a square between you, and it promises no shared talent. Tight and with personal planets, I mention it in [synastry readings](/synastry/), from both sides.

## The decile in transits {#in-transits}

Transiting deciles slip by: a day or two from the Sun, hours from the Moon, kept to a degree, and I'd only mention one from a slow planet. When one does hold it, it can coincide with a stretch of refining a skill you already have, the kind of improvement you notice only afterwards. Weather, not a forecast: a decile transit describes conditions for refinement, never the result.
