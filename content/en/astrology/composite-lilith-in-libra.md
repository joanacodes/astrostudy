*It's fine, really.* In some relationships that sentence carries more weight than any argument. Composite Lilith in Libra puts a bond's untamed part on the scales: the grievances it keeps quiet to keep the peace, and its fierce refusal of anything unfair.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is made of midpoints, and its [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point I read as what is hidden or untamed, describes what the relationship keeps out of view and won't let anyone tame. [Libra](/astrology/libra/), cardinal air ruled by [Venus](/astrology/venus/), makes that a matter of balance. Such a bond looks harmonious, and often is, but it keeps a private ledger: who gave way last time, whose family got Christmas, whose plans were quietly dropped. It refuses unfairness from outside just as firmly, and will close ranks against a friend who treats either person lightly.

The ledger belongs to the pair. Frankness may come easily to each of you elsewhere, and still the relationship turns oddly diplomatic about its own complaints, until each of you wonders where the other's bluntness went. Its idea of what is fair stays untamed, and may not be anyone else's: a split of chores, money or time that outsiders find strange and the two of you find exact.

Libra is a fitting place to see how two relationship charts can weigh the same pair differently. [The Davison chart](/astrology/davison-chart/) is cast for the moment halfway between two births, so its Lilith is wherever mean Lilith really stood then. The composite takes the nearer midpoint of the two natal Liliths. Because mean Lilith moves at a steady pace, the two agree for people born less than about four and a half years apart, and are exactly opposite for people born between about four and a half and thirteen years apart. For a pair born in March 1981 and November 1988, with natal Liliths at about 9° Scorpio and 21° Virgo, the composite Lilith sits at about 14½° Libra and the Davison Lilith at about 14½° Aries.

Counted over all pairs of birth dates from 1930 to 2009, Libra comes up for about 1 pair in 12, and two people born in the same months of a Libra stay have it: April to December 1980, February to November 1989, December 1997 to September 1998. [Lilith in Libra](/astrology/lilith-in-libra/), in one person's chart, is their own weight kept off the scales; in the composite, the weight being kept off is the bond's. [Composite Lilith in Virgo](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-virgo/) keeps its judgements to itself, and [composite Lilith in Scorpio](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-scorpio/) refuses the polite surface altogether.

The hard part is peace bought on credit. Grievances kept quiet don't vanish; they collect interest, and a bond that always looks balanced can surprise everyone, itself included, when the account is finally settled. It helps to open the ledger while it's thin: a regular, unhurried conversation about what feels uneven, in which neither of you has to be the one who complains.

Neither the composite nor the Davison is part of my readings; I describe both as techniques, which their users read with the two birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). They predict no ending and no wedding, and they look closely at what two people keep private, so they are for pairs who both agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
