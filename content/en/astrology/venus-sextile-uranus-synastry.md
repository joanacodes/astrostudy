Not every new idea is a good one, and it helps to know someone with an eye. With Venus sextile Uranus in synastry, about 60° separate one person's Venus from the other's Uranus: the second keeps trying things, the first can tell which ones are worth keeping, and both enjoy the arrangement.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the taste-maker in a chart: attraction, enjoyment, the choices that show what someone loves. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the inventor: it tests alternatives, drops habits that have gone stale and wants room to be distinct. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) leaves an opening between two functions that only works when someone walks through it, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the opening lies between two people.

If the Venus is yours, you become this person's first audience and best editor. Out of ten experiments you can tell which two are lovely, and you enjoy saying so. Some of them end up shaping your own taste, by invitation rather than by takeover.

If the Uranus is yours, you get a sounding board that's warm rather than wary. Your odd proposals are met with interest and sorted kindly, and the best ones become sociable: an experiment turns into something you can share with other people, which is not always where your ideas end up.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opening still needs a step through it. The Uranus person may keep their ideas back unless asked, or race on to the next one before the last has been enjoyed, mistaking novelty for invention. The Venus person may say yes to everything just to keep things pleasant, editing out their own preferences to avoid a disagreement; but harmony isn't the same as never saying "not that one". Left alone, the sextile becomes pleasant talk about things you might do one day.

The pair's other contacts change how the new arrives. In [Venus conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) taste and novelty fuse, so nobody is editing anyone; [Venus square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-uranus-synastry/) makes the new collide with what one person already loves; [Venus trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-uranus-synastry/) lets each roam without asking; and [Venus opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-uranus-synastry/) puts togetherness and independence in different hands.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Try one experiment at a time, and give it a season. The Venus person can be candid about which suggestions they love and which leave them cold; the Uranus person can stay with a good idea long enough for it to become shared. At work, that might be two colleagues piloting a new tool before it reaches the team; in a family, a grown child showing a parent a new way to keep in touch; between friends, a book club with rules nobody else would choose.

Whichever of the two has the Venus, this sextile to Uranus comes within 3.6° for about 1 pair of people in 13. Uranus advances only a little over four degrees a year, so anyone born close to the Uranus person in age brings much the same Uranus: it belongs to a cohort. Venus is the narrow part. With an orb of 3.6°, a week's difference in the Venus person's birthday could take the sextile away, and that birthday is what makes this contact theirs. Neither planet does much between morning and evening, so the hour won't usually decide it; it decides the houses, which can only be read with both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Someone born with [Venus sextile Uranus](/astrology/venus-sextile-uranus/) updates their own tastes as they go. Between two people, one proposes and the other picks, and neither can do the other's half.
