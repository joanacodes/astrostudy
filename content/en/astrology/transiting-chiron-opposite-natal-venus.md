Across the chart from your Venus, Chiron shows an old sore spot about worth in someone else's face. It might be a partner who needs more reassurance than you know how to give, or a friend whose way of hiding is uncomfortably familiar. Once or twice in a life, the question of how you value and are valued gets asked by other people.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how you relate: what you like, what you offer and whether the exchange feels fair. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/), named after the centaur healer who could not heal himself, is read as a hurt that stays tender and the skill you build by working around it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions face to face, and it tends to make you aware of a polarity by showing it to you in someone else.

So under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the sore spot travels. You may find yourself caring for a person whose hurt rhymes with an old one of yours, and doing it so well that your own needs go unmentioned. Or someone you admire presses, without meaning to, on the place where you still doubt you're worth choosing. Venus likes harmony, and harmony is not the same as avoiding friction: keeping the peace at your own expense is the habit this opposition shows up. The aim is a balance in which both people's tender spots count.

With [Venus in the 4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house/), where comfort and private life sit, Chiron is crossing your 10th ([Chiron transiting the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-10th-house/)): the ease you have at home faces a sore spot about being seen at work or in public. That placement, like every house, comes from the time on your birth certificate; without it I can't say where the opposition falls, and I don't read it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If your chart holds [Venus opposite Chiron](/astrology/venus-opposite-chiron/), care given and care received is an old subject for you, and the transit hands it to the people around you for a while.

No transit can say whether a relationship begins, holds or ends, and I don't read one for that ([disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted with my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition usually lasts about ten months, the length it takes when Chiron's retrograde loop carries it across the exact point three times. That happens in nearly two passes out of three. About one pass in three is a single crossing of about three weeks, and about one in twenty crosses five times, over some twenty-one months.

There is one opposition in each of Chiron's rounds of about 50 years. By 80, most people have met it twice and everyone at least once; the second comes only to those whose first came early enough, before about 30. It is the halfway point of the round: after [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-venus/), [transiting Chiron sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-venus/), the first [transiting Chiron square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-venus/) and the first [transiting Chiron trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-venus/), and before the same aspects in reverse order.

Over the coming years, Chiron in Aries opposes Venus in Libra until April 2027; in Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, Venus in Scorpio; in Gemini, from July 2033 to April 2039, Venus in Sagittarius.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice what you admire or resent in the people closest to you. Either can point to the tender place this opposition is lighting.

Ask what someone needs instead of guessing, and then say what you need.

Take turns. If you are always the one who soothes, let yourself be soothed once.

When the question is someone else's hurt, offer company rather than a cure.
