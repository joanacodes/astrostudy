When you were born, Saturn was just under the eastern horizon, somewhere between a few minutes and a couple of hours from rising. That puts it in the 1st house, the house of the self: the planet of time standing in the doorway you walk through first.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Your body, your manner, your first reaction, the impression you make before anyone knows you: [the 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is what people meet first. It's angular, one of the four houses that begin at an angle, so whatever sits there shows. With [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) here, the planet of structure and responsibility becomes part of how you meet the world: composed, watchful, careful in new rooms, and often serious earlier than the children around you seemed to be.

The 1st house's natural sign is Aries, where Saturn is in its fall, and the echo is easy to hear: the house of first moves, holding the planet that would rather wait. Spontaneity may come late. What comes early is a feeling that you have to get yourself right, your look, your manner, your timing, before you're allowed to relax into them.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant opens the 1st house, so the closer Saturn sits to it, the louder it speaks. A few minutes of birth time can move Saturn from the 12th into the 1st, or from the Ascendant's degree to the middle of the house, which is why I won't read it without an accurate time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Saturn's sign, which you share with your whole cohort, says how the lesson goes; this house says it's about you, in plain view.

Its neighbours work elsewhere: [Saturn in the 12th house](/astrology/saturn-in-12th-house/) labours out of sight, [Saturn in the 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-in-2nd-house/) builds what you own. In the 1st, the work is on the self that other people see.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

People often meet your composure first and your warmth later. You may come across as reserved, even stern, when you're simply being careful, and it can take a while for someone close to see how much you feel. The 1st faces the 7th, the house of partners, so this Saturn looks across at others with a quiet question: will they stay once they see the real me? The answer gets better with practice, and with partners patient enough to wait for the second impression.

## At work {#at-work}

You look as though you can carry responsibility, so you're handed it, sometimes before you feel ready. You lead by example rather than by speeches, and people trust your steadiness when things go wrong. Authority suits you more as the years go by. The risk is taking on too much because you look capable, and never letting anyone see you struggle.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Self-criticism, mostly about the self that shows: how you look, how you sound, whether you're doing it right. A heaviness that can make you feel old when you're young. A first reaction of caution that keeps you out of things you'd enjoy. At its hardest, Saturn here can feel like being permanently on duty as yourself.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let people see effort, not only results; it makes you more approachable, not less. Treat your body as a companion with its own pace rather than a project to correct. Laugh at your own seriousness now and then. And give it time: this placement tends to sit more comfortably with every decade, as the outside catches up with the gravity you started with. In my tests Saturn was one of the few placements I couldn't argue with ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), and in the 1st house it's where a reading of mine slows down first.
