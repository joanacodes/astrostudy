Opposite your Midheaven lies the IC, the lowest point of your birth chart and its foundation. When Uranus reaches it, about forty-two years after crossing your Midheaven, the ground you build on is where change starts, and your public role has to answer from the other end.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) liberates and reforms. The [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) roots you: support, continuity and belonging, the base your public life rests on, while the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), opposite, is that public life. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, holds two functions across the chart from each other, and each shows up more clearly by contrast. With Uranus on your IC, your foundations ask for room to change, and your role in the world is seen in their light: a home arranged differently, a family pattern you stop repeating, a sense of belonging that no longer depends on one place. Some people feel it first as restlessness at home, a room or a routine that no longer fits, before they can say what they want instead.

The IC isn't only a house or a family. It's whatever sustains you, and roots can be rearranged without being pulled up. It doesn't describe only your childhood either: it's what holds you up now as much as where you came from. I don't read a change of address, or any family event, from this [transit](/astrology/transits/) ([how I read](/how-i-read/)); it describes the climate under your feet, not what you'll do about it.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 4th house starts at the IC, and this opposition opens Uranus's years there, about eight as a rule ([Uranus transiting the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-4th-house/)). Whatever you were born with in the 4th, Uranus meets next, one planet at a time in the order of their degrees: with Venus there, the change reaches the comforts and pleasures that make a place yours.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus sits on your IC for about twenty months, sometimes only eleven; it usually gets there in three exact passes, about thirteen times in fourteen.

By 80, it has come once to most people born between 1930 and 2009, and about 1 in 33 reach that age without it. [Transiting Uranus conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-midheaven/) lies about forty-two years away; [transiting Uranus trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-midheaven/) falls about fourteen years either side of this opposition, [transiting Uranus square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-midheaven/) about twenty-one, and [transiting Uranus sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-midheaven/) about twenty-eight.

Uranus in Gemini, until May 2033, opposes Midheavens in Sagittarius, which put the IC in Gemini; from 2032 to 2039, in Cancer, it opposes Capricorn Midheavens. The exact months come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look at what holds you up before you change it: the routines, people and places that sustain you, and which of them still do.

Let public decisions wait while your base is being rearranged. A choice about work made on moving ground is worth revisiting once the ground settles.

Change the arrangement, not necessarily the address. Belonging can be renewed in the place you already are.
