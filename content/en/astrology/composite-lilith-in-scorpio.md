Small talk dies quickly around some couples, and some friendships too. Composite Lilith in Scorpio keeps a relationship's depth to itself: the truths it holds between two people, its fierce loyalty, and its refusal to pretend, to anyone, that things are lighter than they are.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

I read [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) as what a chart hides or won't let be tamed. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), every body sits at the midpoint of its two natal places, and Lilith there describes that part of the relationship itself. [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) is fixed water, ruled by [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and, in the older scheme, by [Mars](/astrology/mars/); I read both. Pluto gives this Lilith its depth and its interest in what lies underneath, Mars its readiness to defend the bond. Such a relationship tends to know things about the two of you that nobody else does, to keep its loyalties and its angers private, and to refuse the pleasant surface others expect of it.

The intensity need not be either person's. It can surprise two easy-going people to find that, together, they insist on the truth underneath everything, and each may find the other unexpectedly searching, or guarded. Kept from view: how much the bond feels, and sometimes how much power each of you holds in it. Beyond taming: its refusal of pretence, since a relationship like this would rather have the difficult conversation than act out a fine one.

A curious case belongs here. Mean Lilith goes round the zodiac in about 8 years and 10 months, so two people born almost exactly one cycle apart have their Liliths on the same degree. A birth on 1 June 1981 gives Lilith at about 17° Scorpio, a birth on 1 April 1990 about 16½° Scorpio, and the composite Lilith of the two falls within a degree of both. The bond's untamed part then lies exactly where each person keeps their own, which can make the relationship the place where both are finally unguarded, or where both are most on guard. More ordinary ways in: two people born in the same Scorpio stay, February to November 1972 or November 1989 to July 1990, for example. All told, about 1 pair in 12 has it.

A person with [Lilith in Scorpio](/astrology/lilith-in-scorpio/) learns to keep an intensity below the surface; a pair with it in the composite keeps it there together. Its neighbours handle truth differently: [composite Lilith in Libra](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-libra/) swallows its grievances for the sake of peace, and [composite Lilith in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-sagittarius/) says its truths out loud, sometimes too loud.

The difficulty is that privacy can harden into secrecy, and loyalty into tests of loyalty. A bond with this Lilith may quietly measure who holds more power, or keep a hurt underground until it changes the climate. What helps is a distinction made out loud: what belongs to the two of you, kept from the world, and what neither keeps from the other. Small truths told often stop large ones from piling up.

I don't use composites in my readings; this is the technique's own logic, which its users always set beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it predicts no betrayal and no ending. Reading what a pair keeps hidden without one of them knowing would be exactly the intrusion this Lilith refuses ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
