Small talk dies quickly around some couples, and some friendships too. Composite Lilith in Scorpio describes a relationship whose untamed part is its depth: the truths it keeps between two people, its fierce loyalty, and a refusal to pretend, to anyone, that things are lighter than they are.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

I read [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) as what a chart hides or won't let be tamed. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), every body sits at the midpoint of its two natal places, and Lilith there describes that part of the relationship itself. [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) is fixed water, ruled by [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and, in the older scheme, by [Mars](/astrology/mars/); I read both. Pluto gives this Lilith its depth and its interest in what lies underneath, Mars its readiness to defend the bond. Such a relationship tends to know things about the two of you that nobody else does, to keep its loyalties and its angers private, and to refuse the pleasant surface others expect of it.

The intensity need not be either person's. Two easy-going people can make a bond that insists on the truth underneath everything, and each may find the other unexpectedly searching, or guarded. What it hides is how much it feels, and sometimes how much power each of you has in it. What it won't tame is its refusal of pretence: a relationship like this would rather have the difficult conversation than act out a fine one.

A curious case belongs here. Mean Lilith goes round the zodiac in about 8 years and 10 months, so two people born almost exactly one cycle apart have their Liliths on the same degree. Born on 1 June 1981, one person has Lilith at about 17° Scorpio; born on 1 April 1990, the other has it at about 16½° Scorpio; their composite Lilith sits within a degree of both. The bond's untamed part then lies exactly where each person keeps their own, which can make the relationship the place where both are finally unguarded, or where both are most on guard. More ordinary ways in: two people born in the same Scorpio stay, February to November 1972 or November 1989 to July 1990, for example. All told, about 1 pair in 12 has it.

In a single chart, [Lilith in Scorpio](/astrology/lilith-in-scorpio/) is an intensity a person learned to keep below the surface. In the composite it is the bond's, felt by each of you as the other's depth. On either side, [composite Lilith in Libra](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-libra/) keeps its grievances quiet for the sake of peace, and [composite Lilith in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-sagittarius/) tells its truths far more loudly.

The difficulty is that privacy can harden into secrecy, and loyalty into tests of loyalty. A bond with this Lilith may quietly measure who holds more power, or keep a hurt underground until it changes the climate. What helps is a distinction made out loud: what belongs to the two of you, kept from the world, and what neither keeps from the other. Small truths told often stop large ones from piling up.

I don't use composites in my readings; this is the technique's own logic, which its users always set beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it predicts no betrayal and no ending. Reading what a pair keeps hidden without one of them knowing would be exactly the intrusion this Lilith refuses ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
