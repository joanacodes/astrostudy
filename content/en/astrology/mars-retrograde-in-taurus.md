Mars backs through Taurus only twice in the dates I use, both times in autumn and thirty-two years apart: from October to December 2005, and from October to December 2037. Between them the sign has no Mars retrograde at all, so each is a long and rare season for the drive it slows.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) is fixed earth, ruled by Venus, and Mars is in detriment there, across the zodiac from Scorpio, which tradition gives to Mars. The planet’s urge to strike out meets a sign that would rather build, keep and continue. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when the red planet seems from Earth to stop and drift back for ten weeks, the review lands on effort that has become habit: the load you shoulder because you always have, the position you dug in on, the want you have pursued quietly for years. In Taurus I read anger as slow to gather and slow to leave, and an old grievance can come up for another look. Desire slows to the pace of the senses.

The 2005 loop stayed inside the sign, from 23°22′ Taurus on 1 October to 8°14′ on 10 December: 69 days over about 15°. Halfway, in early November, Mars faced the Sun, then in Scorpio. The 2037 loop runs from 12 October to 23 December and begins at 6°15′ Gemini ([Mars retrograde in Gemini](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-gemini/)); Mars crosses back into Taurus during it and turns direct at 20°07′. On the other side, the 2020 loop kept to Aries ([Mars retrograde in Aries](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-aries/)).

A Mars that was already retrograde in Taurus at your birth is a lifelong placement rather than a season, with its own inward rhythm ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Only [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) can place Taurus among your houses, and that is why I won’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2037 loop may start in the house Gemini occupies and finish in the Taurus one.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/taurus-rising/):** Taurus rising: a calm, solid way of meeting the world; the patience people count on in you, checked for whether it still serves you.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/taurus-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** possessions and self-worth; effort spent guarding what you have, weighed against what you want to grow.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/taurus-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** familiar streets and routines; a point you hold firm on, heard again from the other side.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home you worked hard for; the family role you never put down, picked up and examined.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/taurus-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasure and handmade creative work; a slow-burning attraction, or a piece you keep reshaping.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a steady working routine; the extra weight you carry simply because you can, reconsidered.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Scorpio rising, Taurus sets on the Descendant: a partner who stands firm; a long standoff over who gives way, reopened gently.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/taurus-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what two people share and owe each other, a house tradition also tied to death; I read trust and intimacy there, never anyone’s end ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
9. **[9th house](/astrology/taurus-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs rooted in experience; a conviction held for years, tested before you defend it again.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Taurus on the Midheaven: a reputation built brick by brick; the pace of your ambition, reset.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/taurus-on-11th-house-cusp/):** loyal friends and long-running groups; a commitment kept out of habit, rethought with them.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/taurus-on-12th-house-cusp/):** resentment stored out of sight; a grudge never voiced, looked at in private.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Taurus works through the body and the hands, so I find these weeks go better with slow, physical effort: digging a garden, kneading bread, a long walk, anything that lets drive move without needing an opponent. Venus rules Taurus, and the house your natal Venus occupies shows where comfort and value can soften the review; your natal Mars shows where the drive itself lives.

If a stubborn quarrel resurfaces, it isn’t the sky’s doing: it is an old disagreement asking for a different answer. Nothing here decides an outcome. It describes a long, slow autumn in which effort asks what it is for.
