Across the chart from the 11th house sits the 5th, and with the 11th's ruler there, the crowd comes out to play. The friends you choose are the ones you make things with, laugh with, and now and then fall for.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) gathers what you do for the pleasure of it: creativity, games, romance, the self that comes out when nothing is at stake. The [11th](/astrology/11th-house/) faces it: your circle, your causes, the plans you make with others for the years ahead. The 11th's ruler has crossed the whole wheel to get here ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), taking that business to the house opposite.

Friendship and play are hard to tell apart. Your circles tend to gather around something you love doing: a band, a choir, a five-a-side team, a writing group, a weekly game that has outlived three flats. What you create, you often create with friends or for them, and a friend's applause can mean more than a stranger's prize. Love often grows out of friendship, and lovers sometimes stay friends long after; the line between the two can blur, which is lovely until somebody is hurt by not knowing which side of it they're on.

Tradition put its two kindest houses on this axis. The 5th was Good Fortune, where Venus rejoiced, and the 11th the Good Spirit, Jupiter's joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). Counted by sign they stand in opposition, the aspect of two sides facing each other, and I read the ruler's crossing as a seesaw between the many and the few: the group's plans and your own pleasures, the meeting and the date. When Taurus or Libra opens your 11th, Venus rules it, and from the 5th it sits in its own joy: friendships that feel like a pleasure in themselves, with the taste and warmth to match.

Other planets change the game. Mars, ruling an Aries 11th from here, makes play competitive and loyalty fierce. If Aquarius opens the house, Saturn and Uranus both have a say, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 5th turns the group into a place of craft and long practice, rehearsals and all, while Uranus, wherever it stands, wants your friendships to leave room for experiment.

Children were among the 5th's subjects in tradition too, and nothing here says whether you'll have any, or when ([Can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)). My reading of the house stays with your own grown-up life: the things you make, the pleasures you share and the people you fall for.

The 5th and 11th cusps lie exactly opposite and move together with the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A ten-minute error can change the house here. Later, and a ruler on the 5th cusp drops back into the 4th, where friends come home rather than out to play ([Ruler of the 11th in the 4th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)); earlier, and one at the far side of the 5th reaches the 6th, among colleagues and daily routines ([Ruler of the 11th in the 6th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)). And if the ruler of your 5th sits in your 11th as well, the two houses have swapped rulers, and the traffic runs both ways ([Ruler of the 5th in the 11th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)).
