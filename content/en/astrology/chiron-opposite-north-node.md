Chiron opposite North Node puts Chiron on the South Node, the familiar end of the nodal axis. Working around a difficulty is something you've done for a long time and do well; the growth across the chart asks for a capacity that isn't a work-around.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the adaptive function: it finds new methods, tools and paces around a limitation that persists. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) holds consolidated capacities, established and easy to reach; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), always opposite, a complementary capacity you develop by practice. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, and here Chiron stands on the familiar side: your adaptations belong to your established repertoire.

In plain words, coping is a skill you trust. You may be the person others turn to when something can't be fixed, because you know how to live around it. The North Node asks for something less practised, which its sign names: perhaps meeting a situation head-on, or letting support in, rather than finding one more way round.

The South Node is no lesser end. The opposition asks you to keep your hard-won adaptations and to practise, alongside them, the response they can't give you.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Chiron usually shares a sign with the South Node, with the North Node opposite. With Chiron and the South Node in Pisces and the North Node in Virgo, adapting through compassion and imagination is old ground, and growth asks for practical method and daily, useful habits. Because the node moves backwards towards a forward-moving Chiron, the opposition lasts only a few months of births each time.

With a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), the houses show where each end lives. Chiron on the South Node in the 7th, for instance, could make adapting yourself to partners familiar ground, with the North Node in the 1st asking you to put yourself forward.

About 1 person in 29 born between 1930 and 2009 has Chiron within 6° of an opposition to the North Node, counted with the true node and checked against the mean. Chiron's placement held up well in my notebook, one of the three at 75 to 90%, but the nodes were never part of it ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#chiron)), so this axis is a reading I offer rather than a count.

Beside the opposition, the pair's other aspects:

- **[Chiron conjunct North Node](/astrology/chiron-conjunct-north-node/):** the reverse, with adaptation as the new direction instead of the old ground.
- **[Chiron sextile North Node](/astrology/chiron-sextile-north-node/):** adaptations that sit well with the familiar and open towards the new.
- **[Chiron square North Node](/astrology/chiron-square-north-node/):** the difficulty across the path, owned by neither end.
- **[Chiron trine North Node](/astrology/chiron-trine-north-node/):** adapting that flows straight into growth.

In [Chiron opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-opposite-north-node-synastry/), the coping one person knows by heart lands on the other's familiar ground, the South Node: a meeting that can comfort, or keep both of you circling the same patch. I take my time with it, from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), the North Node opposes your natal Chiron once in each 18.6-year round, which means the transiting South Node is crossing it, for a month or two, with eclipses nearby. Born with the opposition, that happens at each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/); at each [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), at about 9, 28 and 46, it's the North Node that crosses your Chiron, a season when the direction of growth passes right over the old adaptation.

The [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, brings Chiron back to the South Node of your chart.

Weather, not a forecast: these seasons ask whether the old way round is still the best way. Only you can answer.
