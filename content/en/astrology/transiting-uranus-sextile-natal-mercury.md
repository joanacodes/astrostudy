Fourteen years or so before Uranus reaches your Mercury, and again fourteen years after, it passes a sextile to it. Each time the mind gets about twenty months of easy access to the new, which is worth more if you plan to use it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is cognition and exchange: noticing, reasoning, learning, and passing it on. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) contributes experiment and originality. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, opens a door between two functions, and someone has to walk through it. So this one doesn't hand you ideas; it makes the ones you go looking for easier to find and easier to share.

Other people and tools usually carry it: a class that changes how you see a subject, a colleague with a quicker method, a piece of software, a language, a format for your writing that suits you better. Conversations go somewhere new. You may notice you're more willing to say an unusual thing in a meeting, and that it lands. A sextile usually links signs of friendly elements, fire with air, earth with water, so the new idea tends to arrive in a form your own way of thinking can use at once.

Because a sextile asks for a step, it rewards plans. Twenty months is long enough to learn something properly, if you start in the first few.

With Mercury in your 9th and Uranus crossing your 11th ([Uranus transiting the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-11th-house/)), a group, a network or a circle of friends feeds what you study: a reading group, a forum, a society for the subject you love. Your 9th and your 11th both hang on the hour you were born, and without it I don't read the sextile ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Twenty months is the usual stretch within my orbs, which open 1½° before exact and close 1° after; eleven is about the shortest. In more than nine passes out of ten Uranus crosses the exact sextile three times, so a course started at the first hit can be well under way by the last.

The two sextiles are the closest pair in Uranus's round with your Mercury: 28 years apart, with [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-mercury/) halfway between them. Further round come [transiting Uranus square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-mercury/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-mercury/), each twice, and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-mercury/), once, at the far point. Most people meet the pair of sextiles by 80; for the rest, the second arrives later than that.

Mercury makes the reverse contact, sextile your natal Uranus, a few times a year for a day or two; when Uranus is the one moving, the same link between curiosity and invention stays open for twenty months. Born with [Mercury sextile Uranus](/astrology/mercury-sextile-uranus/), you have it open already, and the [transit](/astrology/transits/) widens it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sign up. Enrol in the course, join the group, book the lesson: a sextile turns intention into ability only once it has a start date.

Write to the person whose ideas interest you. Uranus sextile Mercury favours exchanges that begin with a question.

Try a new format for your thinking: a different notebook, a new tool, drawing instead of writing. A change of medium is often where the new idea comes from.
