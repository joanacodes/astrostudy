A square to your Ascendant is a square to your Descendant too: Jupiter, 90° from both ends of your horizon, presses at once on how you meet the world and how you meet other people. It comes twice in each twelve-year round, once from the lower half of your chart and once from the upper.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) enlarges and looks for meaning; the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is your orientation, the posture you start from. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction that asks for adjustment, so growth stops fitting your usual way in. You may be offered a bigger part than your manner is used to, or find that the way you start things no longer matches what you're trying to start. Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), it works on what your chart already holds: it doesn't make you someone else, it asks your first move to stretch.

The first square comes about three years after Jupiter crossed your Ascendant, usually while it's low in your chart, in your 3rd or 4th house in Placidus, so the pressure starts from home, family or daily exchanges ([Jupiter transiting the 4th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-4th-house/)). The second comes about nine years after, usually from your 9th or 10th: the push comes from work, study or a public role that wants you to show up differently.

It's easy to confuse this square with Jupiter crossing your Midheaven or IC, but the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is rarely exactly 90° from the Ascendant. At London's latitude it can sit anywhere from about 54° to 126° away, so the two moments can fall more than a year apart. The gap depends on your birth time and place, which is why I don't read a chart without them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, a square from Jupiter holds your Ascendant for about eleven days in seven passes out of ten. The other three are loops ([Jupiter retrograde](/astrology/jupiter-retrograde/)), with three exact hits over about eight months: long enough for the friction to become a theme rather than a week.

By 80, everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has met it thirteen or fourteen times. [Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) opens each round; [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-ascendant/) and this square follow within three years, [transiting Jupiter trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-ascendant/) eases the pressure a year later, and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-ascendant/) marks the halfway point, before a second square arrives from the top of the chart.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Fit your entrance to what you've taken on. If a new role needs a different manner, practise it in small rooms first rather than forcing it in the big one.

Choose at the door. A square from Jupiter brings more invitations than your way of being can carry, and saying no early is kinder than saying yes and arriving half there.

Ask someone who knows you how you've been coming across lately. The square reaches your Descendant too, so the people closest to you often see the strain, or the growth, before you do.
