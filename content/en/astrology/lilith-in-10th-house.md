The top of a chart is its most public point, and Lilith in the 10th house puts the part you learned to hide right there. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point of the Moon's orbit farthest from Earth, was in [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) when you were born, the house of career, reputation and what the world knows you for.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The 10th, at the top of the chart, is the last of the four angular houses, with Capricorn as its natural sign. It covers your public life: your work in the world, your reputation, the authority you carry and the mark you want to leave. With Lilith here, the hidden part goes public, whether you planned it or not. It may be a direction you weren't expected to take, a way of leading that didn't look like the ones you were shown, or simply a visibility you were taught to avoid: being noticed, being named, being in charge.

What this Lilith refuses is a public life built on someone else's plan. It won't climb a ladder just because it was there, or wear a reputation that was handed to it. Its boundary is authorship: what you're known for should be something you chose.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 10th begins at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), where the zodiac crosses the meridian, which isn't the point straight overhead. It turns quickly with the clock, so this placement needs an accurate birth time ([what the time decides](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)). The 10th sits beside the 9th at the top of the wheel: where [Lilith in the 9th house](/astrology/lilith-in-9th-house/) looks for meaning, the 10th has to stand up in public with whatever it found.

Lilith is one of [the three placements that held best](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) in my notebook, 75 to 90% of the time, and at the top of a chart it's rarely quiet. I go slowly here, asking what visibility has cost you and what you'd like it to bring.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

A partner of this Lilith has to live with its visibility, or with its refusal of it. You may need someone who's glad to see you take up public space, and who doesn't mistake your ambition for a verdict on the relationship. The hidden part bristles at being expected to dim a career for the sake of a couple's image.

The friends and groups around your public life are the subject of [Lilith in the 11th house](/astrology/lilith-in-11th-house/). Here, it's the one person who has to share a life with your reputation.

## At work {#at-work}

This is the placement of an unconventional career, or of a conventional one done in an unconventional way. You may become known for exactly the quality you were once told to tone down. Authority sits uneasily on you when it's borrowed, and well when it's earned your own way. The hidden part refuses to be the face of decisions it didn't make.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

A reputation can arrive before you're ready for it, and not always the one you'd choose: difficult, intense, too much. Or you keep yourself so far from the top of anything that nobody can call you that, and your ability stays out of sight. Both leave you working against your own visibility.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose the public role on purpose, and say out loud what you want to be known for. Let your work carry your name and your voice; a career that sounds like you is easier to stand behind on the hard days.

Ask for authority rather than waiting to be handed it, and once you have it, use it the way you once wished someone had used theirs with you. That's the leadership this Lilith was keeping in reserve.
