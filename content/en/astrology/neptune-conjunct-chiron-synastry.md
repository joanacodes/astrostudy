Some people seem to feel your old sore point before you've said a word about it. Neptune conjunct Chiron in synastry puts one person's Neptune on the other's Chiron, within 4°, so the first person's compassion and imagination land exactly where the second has learned to live with a lasting limit.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) permeates: it imagines, empathises and lets boundaries soften where they do no good. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts: it develops ways of functioning around a limitation that persists, and often turns them into practical wisdom. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) lets two functions work as one, each still itself; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one sets one person's tenderness directly on the other's most careful place.

At its best it is understanding without interrogation. The Neptune person senses what the limit asks of the other and makes room for it; the Chiron person, met with that softness, can stop performing competence for a while and simply be helped.

If the Neptune is yours, the other's tender place may move you more than you can explain, and you may want to ease it.

If the Chiron is yours, you may feel understood without having to describe the details, or idealised, cast as someone wiser or more wounded than you feel.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Merging blurs empathy with knowledge. The Neptune person can feel the other's difficulty so strongly that they assume they know what helps, or wish the limit away with kindness and overlook the practical adaptations it needs. The Chiron person can mistake being soothed for being supported, or keep the soothing coming by never mentioning what is hard. Compassion isn't unlimited availability, and a limit isn't an identity.

- **[Neptune sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-chiron-synastry/):** imagination offering the tender place a kinder frame, if it is taken.
- **[Neptune square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-chiron-synastry/):** kindness and a practical limit at cross purposes, worked out by talking.
- **[Neptune trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-chiron-synastry/):** compassion and adaptation in one element, helping without pressing.
- **[Neptune opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-chiron-synastry/):** an ideal and a limit at two ends of an axis, easy to idealise across.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask what helps instead of guessing. The Chiron person can say plainly what the limit needs and what it doesn't; the Neptune person can offer comfort and practical help separately, so neither replaces the other. Partners may need to talk about care at home; friends, about when to listen and when to act; at work, a colleague's sympathy can become a useful adjustment once it is named.

Someone born in mid-1965, with Neptune in Scorpio, has it with anyone born, on and off, from November 1947 to September 1948, and again from December 1997 to October 1998, both times with Chiron in Scorpio. Turned round, the Neptune of anyone born, on and off, from February 1998 to January 2002 sits on the Chiron of someone born in mid-1955. At that scale the contact belongs to no one in particular: my synastry count includes only contacts that reach the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven, and this one I read through the houses, as one era's compassion meeting another era's adaptations.

Chiron is one of the three placements [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) singled out: Saturn, Lilith and Chiron held 75 to 90% of the time. Those were single charts, read placement by placement, so the figure belongs to a person's Chiron, not to this contact. The hour leaves the conjunction where it is and decides its house, the Chiron person's 7th, in partnership, or their 11th, among friends ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

People born from late 1944 to late 1945 carry [Neptune conjunct Chiron](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-chiron/) in one chart. Between two charts it is someone else's softness at your sore spot, which I read only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
