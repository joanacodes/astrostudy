Mercury’s retrogrades in Aquarius fall in the first weeks of the year, between early January and early March, often three years running. They turn back through the sign of ideas for the future, so it’s often the future you’d planned that gets the second look.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

In [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, Mercury answers to two rulers at once, the rule-maker Saturn of the tradition and the inventor Uranus of modern astrologers, and I read both. Mercury there thinks in systems and patterns and likes the idea nobody has had yet. When [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) comes to this sign, the planet seems from Earth to pause and drift back through it, and the new idea waits while the system is checked: the plan you sketched for the group, the network you built, the theory you’ve been sure of for a while. Modern astrologers tie Aquarius to technology, so this is the retrograde blamed for crashed screens, though I know of no study showing that devices fail more in these weeks ([Does Mercury retrograde break things?](/astrology/does-mercury-retrograde-break-things/)).

Seventeen retrogrades reach Aquarius from 2000 to 2040, three years running in 2007–2009, 2014–2016, 2020–2022 and 2027–2029. The most recent, from 14 January to 4 February 2022, began at 10°20′ Aquarius and turned direct at 24°22′ Capricorn ([Mercury retrograde in Capricorn](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-capricorn/)). The next arrives from Pisces ([Mercury retrograde in Pisces](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-pisces/)): from 9 February to 3 March 2027, stationing at 5°58′ Pisces and ending at 20°55′ Aquarius. From 24 January to 14 February 2028 it stays wholly in Aquarius, from 19°41′ to 3°59′, and the run ends with January 2029’s, which starts in Aquarius and finishes in Capricorn.

When Mercury retrograde in Aquarius appears in a birth chart, [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/) is the page for it: an original mind that tests its ideas inside first, a rhythm of thought rather than a season.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which part of your chart it crosses depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), since the cusps turn with the hours; without the time there are no houses, and I don’t read a chart without them. In Placidus, my house system, the wholly Aquarian retrograde of 2028, from 19°41′ to 3°59′, crosses a cusp only if one falls in that stretch; those that begin in Pisces or end in Capricorn can reach the house next door.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aquarius-rising/):** Aquarius rising: your friendly, independent manner; the way you explain yourself to a group, reworded.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** value placed on freedom and ideas; an unusual plan for your resources, thought through again.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** inventive talk and a questioning mind; an argument you challenged, heard out this time.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an unconventional home; the household’s arrangements for everyone’s independence, renegotiated.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** love that starts as friendship, and creative experiments; an experiment you shelved, taken up again.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** new methods at work; a system you meant to improve, reviewed before the change.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius on the Descendant, with Leo rising: a partner who is also a friend; the room each of you needs, discussed again.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** space within intimacy; shared arrangements made your own way, looked at together.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** progressive beliefs and ideas ahead of their time; a theory revisited before you champion it further.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** reforming a field, or work with technology or causes; a public project’s design, reconsidered.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius in its natural house: friends, groups and shared causes; the group’s direction, talked through again.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a private independence; ideas you work on behind the scenes, sorted alone.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Test the idea on people before you test it on the world: Aquarius thinks for the group, and these weeks suit hearing what the group thinks back. Read both rulers: the house of your natal Saturn shows where the system wants checking, that of your natal Uranus where the new idea is waiting.

An idea that still excites you after a few weeks of questions is one you can take forward on firmer ground.
