After the shell of Cancer, the progressed Moon steps into Leo, the Sun’s sign, and the heart wants to be seen. For two years or a little more, what restores you tends to be warmth, play and making something of your own, and having someone notice.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The progressed Moon](/astrology/progressed-moon/), the quickest point of [secondary progressions](/astrology/secondary-progressions/), describes what you need through a chapter of your life. In [Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire ruled by the Sun, those needs turn outward and warm: to express what you feel rather than keep it in, to be recognised for what you make, to give generously and have that generosity received. Fire makes feelings bright and quick to show; fixity makes them loyal and steady once they are given. The Moon has no dignity in Leo, and the chapter is less about attention than about coherence: feeling that the person others see is the person you are. Even if your Sun sits somewhere quieter, a Leo chapter lends you some of the sign’s warmth; the art is to accept the loan rather than apologise for it.

Play matters more than usual. A creative project, a hobby taken seriously, time with people who make you laugh: in Leo, these are how the Moon refuels, not distractions from the real work. Recognition helps, though it doesn’t have to be the point, and the chapter sits best when your own pleasure in what you make comes first. If appreciation is slow to come, the hurt can feel like pride wounded; I read that as information about what matters to you, never as a verdict on your worth.

Where all this shows depends on the house the progressed Moon crosses, and the houses come from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): in Placidus, the system I use, they can’t be drawn without it, and neither can the reading. The Sun rules Leo, so the house of your natal Sun adds where the wish to shine tends to go.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Leo is one of the two signs, with Virgo, where the longest crossings in my count stretch past two and a half years: for births from 1930 to 2009, the progressed Moon took from two years to about two years and seven months to cross it, usually about two years and four months. Your own length reflects how fast the real Moon was moving in the days after you were born, a day for each year.

It arrives from the [progressed Moon in Cancer](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-cancer/) and hands over to the [progressed Moon in Virgo](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-virgo/): from the home to the stage, then to the workshop. Each sign comes round about every 27 years, so a Leo chapter met at one age has a different stage to stand on the next time. With a natal [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/), the chapter doubles the sign’s warmth; with a natal Moon in Aquarius, across the zodiac, it can feel like a pull between needing room and needing to be seen, which is worth knowing before it starts.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make something and show it to someone. A Leo chapter tends to brighten when there is a piece of work, however small, that carries your mark: a song, a garden, a table laid for friends.

Give warmth freely, and let it come back; if pride makes it hard to ask for attention, ask anyway, plainly. Keep the playful part of the week as safe as the serious part, and if you lead something in these years, lead it warmly: Leo’s authority comes from the heart rather than from rank. And when Virgo comes next, it will ask how to make what you made in these years work day to day, which is easier if you have made something worth refining.
