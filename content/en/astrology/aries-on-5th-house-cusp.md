With Aries on the 5th house cusp, the house of what you do for joy opens in the sign of beginnings. Play, pleasure, the things you make for the sake of making them, the way you fall for someone: all of it starts with a first move.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is the part of life that needs no justifying, and the sign on its cusp is the way you enjoy it ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Aries](/astrology/aries/) gives that joy a starting line. You tend to like games with a score in them, hobbies you can get better at, the first draft more than the polishing, and the moment a plan becomes real. In love, you'd usually rather say it than wait to be guessed. Flirting can look like a dare between equals, and a love that stops being a little playful tends to lose you. Aries shares its element with Leo, the 5th's natural sign, so fire is at home here. Tradition also put the joy of Venus in this house, and Aries is the sign where Venus is in detriment: pleasure here is taken directly, without much ceremony. Libra, on the opposite cusp, opens your 11th, so the pleasures you take for yourself are brisk and the ones you share with a crowd more considered.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules your 5th. Scorpio usually rises with this cusp, in about 3 London charts in 4 and about half at New York, and Scorpio has two rulers, Pluto and, by tradition, Mars. I read both, so Mars is then a ruler of your whole chart as well as of your play. How you meet the world and what you do for joy answer to the same planet; when one is held back, the other often feels it too. Mars's house shows the part of life your play pours into: the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

About 1 birth in 11 has Aries on the 5th house cusp at London's latitude, and 1 in 12 at New York's: one of the cusps where Aries is commoner further north. On the western side of the chart, between the IC and the Descendant, Aries takes its time, the more so the further you are from the equator. In about a third of these London charts it opens a second house too, usually the 6th ([Aries on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-6th-house-cusp/)), sometimes the 4th ([Aries on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/)), and one ruler then speaks for both ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). In other charts Aries opens no house at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)): about 1 London birth in 6. All of these are Placidus cusps, the system I use, worked out from the birth time. No birth time, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The old books also hung children on this house. What I read here is your own play and your own loves, never a forecast about a family ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and a child's chart isn't one I read ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). With Aries on the cusp, the question this house asks you is simpler: what did you last start purely because you wanted to?
