Two years after Jupiter tops your chart, and again two years before it gets back there, it stands 60° from your Midheaven. Both times it opens a side door into your working life rather than the front one.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens what you understand and finds a use for it; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) frames how your capacities become visible: role, contribution, responsibility. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) links them through cooperation and asks for engagement, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) tends to show up as an offer you have to answer: a project that fits your direction, a contact who knows the next person, a chance to show what you can do on a smaller stage first.

Because the Midheaven and the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) face each other, Jupiter is 120° from your IC at the same time. In practice, home and private life tend to back the opening rather than compete with it: a household that makes room, quiet evenings that make the extra work possible, family who are glad to see you take it on.

In Placidus, the first sextile of a round usually catches Jupiter in your 11th house, where tradition gave it its joy, or in your 12th, so the opening often comes through friends, a network or people working behind the scenes. The second usually comes from your 7th or 8th, through a partner, a client or a shared venture ([Jupiter transiting the 8th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-8th-house/)).

Either way, the work it favours is often less visible than the crossing of the Midheaven itself: preparation, a sideline, help given to someone else's project. I count that as growth in your public role too, because the Midheaven isn't only what people see; it's what you're building towards.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile is a matter of about eleven days in seven cases out of ten. In the other three it falls inside [Jupiter retrograde](/astrology/jupiter-retrograde/) and is exact three times in about eight months, which gives a slower offer time to take shape.

Those dates hang on your Midheaven, and so on a birth time you can trust. If yours is missing or only remembered, start with the birth record itself ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#finding-your-time)); I don't read until we have it.

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have had it thirteen or fourteen times. The first of each round follows [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-midheaven/) by about two years and comes a year before [transiting Jupiter square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-midheaven/). The second follows [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-midheaven/) by about four years and the round's last [transiting Jupiter trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-midheaven/) by about two, as Jupiter heads back towards the top of your chart.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Answer the offer within the season. A sextile from Jupiter rarely waits, and a reply sent after it has passed has to start the conversation again.

Make one move towards the role you want, smaller than you think it should be: a proposal, a sample of your work, a conversation with the person who decides.

Let the household in on it. With Jupiter trine your IC, the people at home are often readier to help than you expect, and telling them early turns goodwill into a plan.
