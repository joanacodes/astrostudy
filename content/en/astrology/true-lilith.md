If your chart lists a Lilith followed by the word *true* or *osculating*, it is showing you a different point from the [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) I write about on this site. Both follow the far end of the Moon's orbit. True Lilith follows it moment by moment, and that makes it restless.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/)'s orbit is an ellipse, only slightly stretched: about 363,300 km from Earth at its nearest and 405,500 km at its farthest. The far end of that ellipse, the apogee, turns slowly round the zodiac, once every 8.85 years. Lilith is the name astrologers give it, and there is more than one way to place it ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

True Lilith takes the Moon's position and motion at a single moment, works out the ellipse those would trace if nothing but the Earth were pulling, and places Lilith at that ellipse's far end. *Osculating*, its other name, is the astronomers' word for such an orbit fitted to one instant. The Sun's pull, left out of that instant's sum, is never left out of the Moon's real path, so the fitted ellipse is different the next day, and its far end with it.

Unicode even gives it a character of its own, named True Black Moon, next to the ⚸ used for Lilith. Programs don't all use it, so read the label, not the glyph.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Because the Moon's orbit is so nearly a circle, a small change in its shape can swing the direction of its far point a long way. That's why true Lilith wanders so far from mean Lilith, tens of degrees at times. Mean Lilith moves forwards about 40° a year, roughly 3° a month, and never turns back. True Lilith swings round that steady point, runs ahead of it, falls behind it, and often stops and moves backwards for a while: a retrograde that belongs to the calculation, since there is nothing in the sky at either point.

That restlessness changes what the point can do in a chart:

- **Its sign is less settled.** Mean Lilith stays about nine months in a sign. True Lilith can leave a sign, come back and leave again, so two people born a few weeks apart may have it in different signs while their mean Liliths sit together.
- **Its transits are jumpier.** A true Lilith crossing a natal planet can do it several times over a few months, where mean Lilith crosses once.
- **Its returns blur.** Mean Lilith comes back to its birth place about every nine years, on a date you can name. True Lilith arrives in several passes, so its return is a stretch of time rather than a day.

A third calculation, [interpolated Lilith](/astrology/interpolated-lilith/), takes the Moon's actual farthest points, month by month, and smooths the path between them, avoiding true Lilith's widest swings. The same choice exists, on a smaller scale, for the lunar nodes ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), where the gap is a degree or two at most.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I read mean Lilith. It moves at a steady pace, it is the one most astrologers use, and everything I write about Lilith here, in a sign like [Lilith in Aries](/astrology/lilith-in-aries/) or in a house, is written for it. If your true Lilith falls in another sign, that explains why another site or app may describe you differently; I still read the mean one, and I don't read the two side by side as if they were one point seen twice.

Lilith matters in my readings more than most points. In my notebook it sat with Saturn and Chiron at the top, [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) 75 to 90% of the time. I have no separate count for true Lilith, which is one more reason to stay with the point my experience is built on. And whichever Lilith you choose, its house needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“True Lilith is where the Moon is farthest from Earth that month.”** It's the far point of the ellipse fitted to one instant. The Moon's actual farthest points are what interpolated Lilith follows.
- **“True Lilith moves steadily, like the mean.”** It lurches ahead, falls behind and sometimes turns back.
- **“If my true Lilith is in another sign, my Lilith reading is wrong.”** Each describes its own point. Check which one the text you're reading was written for.
- **“The ⚸ symbol always means mean Lilith.”** Many programs use it for every Lilith. The words beside it decide.
