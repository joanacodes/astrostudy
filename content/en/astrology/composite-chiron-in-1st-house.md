Before anyone knows either of you, they see the two of you together: how you come into a room, what you seem to be to each other. Composite Chiron in the 1st house puts the relationship's tender spot in that first impression, and describes the manner the bond works out for itself.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is the chart's front door: appearance, manner, the way something meets the world. Read in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it is the relationship's own manner, and [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) there makes that manner the bond's sore point. The pair may feel conspicuous together, be misread at first sight, taken for colleagues or siblings or strangers, or simply be unsure how to be a *we* in public. Sometimes one person feels the other's way of showing up as a comment on their own.

Two people at ease in their own skin can find that, as a pair, they never quite know how to stand in a doorway; the composite reads it as the relationship's manner, not a flaw in either of you. You can see the adapting from outside: a way of introducing yourselves that you chose, small signs of being together that suit you rather than anyone's script, a readiness to explain the relationship once and then stop explaining. Other unusual pairs tend to relax in your company: you know how a first look can sting.

This is an angular house, and its cusp is the composite [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), which is exactly where the two ways of drawing composite houses part company. One takes the midpoint of your two natal Ascendants. The other takes the composite Midheaven, the midpoint of your two Midheavens, and works out the Ascendant that goes with it at the latitude where you live. Their Ascendants can differ by several degrees, enough to move a Chiron between the 12th house and the 1st, and astrologers are divided on which to use. Either way, both birth times are needed, since an Ascendant moves about a degree every four minutes on average ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The house is also what makes this Chiron belong to the two of you. Its sign is shared by everyone born within a few years of you; its house is not. A natal [Chiron in the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-in-1st-house/) concerns one person's sense of being seen as they are, and in synastry, [their Chiron in your 1st house](/astrology/chiron-in-1st-house-synastry/) is one person's sore spot meeting the other's manner. Here the tender place is the bond's shared face.

Letting other people's first impressions decide what the relationship is: that is the trap here. A bond sore about how it looks can hide, over-explain, or perform a version of itself that it thinks will pass. It helps to agree, between you, how you want to be introduced, and to let the rest go.

Before it, [composite Chiron in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-12th-house/) keeps its tender place out of sight; after it, [composite Chiron in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-2nd-house/) finds it among what the pair owns and values. Composites aren't part of my own readings: the astrologers who use them set this Chiron beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it says nothing about how long two people will be together.
