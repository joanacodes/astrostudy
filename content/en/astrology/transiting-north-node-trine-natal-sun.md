When the North Node trines your Sun, the year's eclipse seasons fall about four months from your birthday on one side and about two on the other, the mirror image of the sextile. The direction of growth stands 120° from who you are, the South Node 60°, and for a month or so the new comes almost as easily as the old.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your sense of self and direction: what you are for. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the capacity still under construction, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) the one you have built already. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates through flow, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the newer capacity sits so comfortably with who you are that practising it hardly feels like effort.

Trines join signs of the same element, and here it is the North Node that shares your Sun's. With the node in Aquarius until March 2028, Gemini Suns, born about 21 May to 20 June, and Libra Suns, born about 23 September to 22 October, have an air node trine an air Sun, while the South Node in Leo sextiles them from fire. The direction of growth is in your own element, which is why it can feel native rather than foreign. From March 2028, with the node in Capricorn, the trine moves on to Taurus and Virgo Suns.

Ease has a cost of its own. What the node needs is permission to practise without demanding instant competence, and a trine can make practice seem unnecessary, as if being suited to something were the same as having done it. The month goes furthest when you use the ease to do the thing, not only to feel ready for it.

Your Sun's house shows where it happens. With the [Sun in the 11th house](/astrology/sun-in-11th-house/), a group, a friendship or a shared plan tends to carry the new step. The house needs a recorded birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)); your Sun's degree, and so the dates, come from the birth date. If your chart holds [Sun trine North Node](/astrology/sun-trine-north-node/), the transit restates a flow you were born with.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine usually lasts about a month, and nineteen passes in twenty cross it once. In the year of the trine the Sun reaches the North Node about four months from your birthday and the South Node about two months from it on the other side ([eclipse seasons](/astrology/eclipse-seasons/)). An exact trine that falls in either season catches the true node at its slowest, and the pass can run past three and a half months, as one in ten do, or cross three times, as one in twenty-five do. The eclipses themselves stand 120° or 60° from your Sun and don't count on your chart.

The trines come in pairs: about six and twelve and a half years after the North Node crosses your Sun ([transiting North Node conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-sun/)), then twelve and a half years without one until the next round's pair, eight or nine by 80 in all. The first follows [transiting North Node sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-sun/) and [transiting North Node square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-sun/); the second comes after [transiting North Node opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-sun/), halfway round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Do the new thing this month rather than planning it. Starting is easy now, so start.

Learn alongside someone who already does it well. The South Node's sextile puts familiar help within reach.

Notice what feels natural that didn't a few years ago. That is growth the node has already given you.
