If the planet of your rising sign sits in the 5th house, the self you lead with comes fully alive when it's enjoying itself. Your chart ruler lives among play, pleasure, romance and the things you make.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The ruler of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/), has carried the story of the self into the [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), which covers what you do for joy: making things for the pleasure of it, the games and hobbies you lose hours in, flirting and falling in love, the risks you take for fun, and the way you show yourself when no one's keeping score.

Delight is how this self grows. You tend to find out who you are by doing what you love, and a life with no room for it can leave you feeling half there. Pleasure isn't a distraction from your direction; with your ruler here it often is the direction, or at least the compass. People see you most clearly when you're absorbed in something: performing, building, painting, courting, coaching a team, cooking for twelve. One question keeps it honest: who is the play for? Making things you'd make even if nobody were watching keeps the joy your own.

Good Fortune was the 5th's old name, and Venus, the planet of pleasure, was said to rejoice there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)); with Taurus or Libra rising, a Venus in the 5th rules your chart from the house where it was happiest. Counted by sign, the 5th sign from your Ascendant is a trine away, in the same element as your rising sign, and astrologers who group the houses the way the signs are grouped put the 1st, 5th and 9th together as the fire houses. Self and pleasure, in this chart, speak the same language.

The planet shows what kind of joy. The Sun, ruling Leo rising from the 5th, sits in the house matched with its own sign and wants to shine at whatever it loves. Saturn, ruling Capricorn rising, takes its pleasures seriously: the long-practised craft, the instrument played for twenty years. Mars from here, ruling Aries rising, plays to win.

Older astrology also read children from the 5th. A chart can't say whether children will be part of your life, or when ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I only read the charts of adults who've asked for it ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). What I read here is your own play, love and making.

The houses either side show what the 5th is not. A house earlier, your life would gather at home, among roots and family ([Ruler of the 1st in the 4th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)); a house later, in the daily round of work and usefulness ([Ruler of the 1st in the 6th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)). The 5th is the free time between the two, and in Placidus, the house system I use, a ruler there had set in the west some hours before your birth and was sinking under the earth. The birth time decides both which planet rules your chart and whether it plays in the 5th or stands a house either side ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

With Scorpio rising, Mars and Pluto both rule the chart, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 5th puts your energy into play and passion, while Pluto, from wherever it stands, deepens another part of life, and the reading holds the two together.
