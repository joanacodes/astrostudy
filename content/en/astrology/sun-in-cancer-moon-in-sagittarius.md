The ruler of your Moon's sign, Jupiter, is exalted in your Sun's sign, so this unlikely pair has a thread of welcome running between them. The Cancer Sun keeps the hearth; the Sagittarius Moon needs to know there's a wider world and a truth worth telling. Your Moon was nearly full, a few nights short of it.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and mutable fire, the Moon five signs on: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, a relation of steady give and take between signs with nothing in common. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) puts down roots, keeps faith with family and memory, and protects its people; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) settles through candour, humour, learning and a belief worked out for itself. One light wants to be careful with feelings, the other wants to say the honest thing, and much of your life goes into finding words that are both kind and true.

The rulers are generous to each other. The Moon rules Cancer, and here it stands in the sign of [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), while Jupiter has its exaltation in Cancer, your Sun's sign. I read that exchange as care and faith that can feed each other: home becomes a place for learning and welcome, and belief becomes a way of looking after people. Jupiter's sign and house show where this works best for you. As for dignities, neither Cancer nor Sagittarius gives its light any.

Between the tenth and fifteenth days of the lunar month, the Moon stood 120° to 179° from the Sun and rose in the afternoon, nearly round. The eight-phase count gives the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) all but a few early births, which go to the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/); if the gap was close to 150°, the quincunx is exact by degree too. The gibbous phase, as I read it, is for fine-tuning before something is shown, and this pair fine-tunes its beliefs. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says more about it.

Some 1 person in 143 has this pair. Sagittarius holds the Moon for about two and a half days, between Scorpio and Capricorn, so on a changeover day the birth time picks one of three pages: this one, the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-scorpio/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-capricorn/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The houses, and the rising sign that anchors them, also depend on that time; they show where the hearth and the horizon each have their place.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You offer a partner both a home and a wider world: warmth, loyalty and good food, and also humour, ideas and plans to go somewhere. The Cancer Sun needs closeness and continuity; the Sagittarius Moon needs truthfulness and some freedom. The tension shows when an honest remark lands on a tender spot, or when a need for space is taken as a lack of care. Saying both halves, *I love being home with you* and *I need this trip*, helps a partner hear them as one person.

## At work {#at-work}

Teaching, publishing, hospitality, community and faith work, international families or organisations, anything that joins care to a larger meaning suits you. The Sun wants to protect and nurture; the Moon wants to learn, explain and widen people's horizons. Newcomers often find in you a generous mentor, and colleagues someone who speaks up when something isn't right. Petty, closed workplaces frustrate the Moon, and cold ones leave the Sun homesick.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Candour and care can collide. The Sagittarius Moon says the true thing quickly; the Cancer Sun is hurt when others do the same to it, and may then retreat while the Moon is already planning an escape. You can feel torn between the people who rely on you and the life you'd like to explore. What helps is building both into the year on purpose, a home base that's truly yours and regular journeys, of the body or the mind, and choosing words that are kind as well as honest.
