Jupiter, ruler of your Moon's sign, has its exaltation in your Sun's, and that single link runs a thread of welcome through an otherwise unlikely pair. The Cancer Sun keeps the hearth; the Sagittarius Moon needs to know there's a wider world and a truth worth telling. Your Moon was nearly full, a few nights short of it.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and mutable fire, the Moon five signs on: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, which asks for steady give and take between signs with nothing in common. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) puts down roots, keeps faith with family and memory, and protects its people; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) settles through candour, humour, learning and a belief worked out for itself. One light wants to be careful with feelings, the other wants to say the honest thing, and much of your life goes into finding words that are both kind and true.

The rulers are generous to each other. The Moon rules Cancer, and here it stands in the sign of [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), while Jupiter has its exaltation in Cancer, your Sun's sign. I read that exchange as care and faith that can feed each other: home becomes a place for learning and welcome, and belief becomes a way of looking after people. Look to Jupiter's sign and house to see where this works best for you. The lights themselves have no dignity in these signs; the bond runs through Jupiter.

Between the tenth and fifteenth days after the new moon, the Moon you were born under rose in the afternoon, nearly round, its lead over the Sun somewhere from 120° to 179°. In the eight-phase count, all but a few early births here are [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) births, the rest [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) ones; a lead close to 150° makes the quincunx exact by degree as well. The gibbous phase, as I read it, is for fine-tuning before something is shown, and this pair fine-tunes its beliefs. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says more about it.

Some 1 person in 143 has this pair. In Sagittarius the Moon stays about two and a half days, entering from Scorpio and moving on to Capricorn; when the change fell on your birthday, the birth time may move you one page over, to the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-scorpio/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-capricorn/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The houses, and the rising sign that anchors them, also depend on that time; they show where the hearth and the horizon each have their place.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You offer a partner both a home and a wider world: warmth, loyalty and good food, and also humour, ideas and plans to go somewhere. The Cancer Sun needs closeness and continuity; the Sagittarius Moon needs truthfulness and some freedom. The tension shows when an honest remark lands on a tender spot, or when a need for space is taken as a lack of care. Saying both halves, *I love being home with you* and *I need this trip*, helps a partner hear them as one person.

## At work {#at-work}

Teaching, publishing, hospitality, community and faith work, international families or organisations, anything that joins care to a larger meaning suits you. Nurture comes from the Sun, and from the Moon the wish to learn, explain and widen people's horizons. Newcomers often find in you a generous mentor, and colleagues someone who speaks up when something isn't right. Petty, closed workplaces frustrate the Moon, and cold ones leave the Sun homesick.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Candour and care can collide. The Sagittarius Moon says the true thing quickly; the Cancer Sun is hurt when others do the same to it, and may then retreat while the Moon is already planning an escape. You can feel torn between the people who rely on you and the life you'd like to explore. What helps is building both into the year on purpose, a home base that's truly yours and regular journeys, of the body or the mind, and choosing words that are kind as well as honest.
