Plenty of people's best-loved interests began with someone saying "come along". Venus sextile Jupiter in synastry often works like that: one person's Venus sits about 60° from the other's Jupiter, and the second keeps opening doors to things the first turns out to love.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) names what a person finds worth enjoying: taste, attraction, pleasure, a fair give and take. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is their reach: learning, meaning, principles, the wish to make room for more. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) links them through opportunity, which only works when taken up, and in the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the opportunity passes from one person to the other.

On the Venus side, this person keeps showing you things you didn't know you'd like: a writer, a place, a subject, a group of people. Their suggestions fit your taste closely enough to be tempting and widely enough to change it.

On the Jupiter side, your enthusiasms find someone who enjoys them, which is its own reward. Their pleasure in what you share encourages you to share more, and their sense of what's beautiful or fair gives your bigger ideas a pleasing shape.

The signs involved are usually neighbours but one, in elements that cooperate. One person's Venus in Capricorn with the other's Jupiter in Pisces, a sign tradition gives to Jupiter: a taste for things built to last meets a broad, compassionate outlook, so the Jupiter person widens what the Venus person allows themselves to enjoy, and the Venus person gives the other's ideals a practical form. In a friendship this might be a book swapped every month; at work, a manager who keeps offering just the right training; in a family, an adult sibling who drags you to the concert you end up loving.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An invitation is easy to decline politely, and this sextile can go years without anyone saying yes. Its narrow orb, 3.6°, keeps it quiet even when present: real, but easy to miss, and opportunity is never guaranteed success.

There's a subtler risk in the exchange itself. The Jupiter person can keep offering what they love rather than asking what the Venus person likes, and the Venus person can accept out of courtesy. Or the giving runs one way only, one person always opening doors, the other always walking through, when Venus is also about reciprocity.

The other angles of the pair handle taste and growth differently:

- **[Venus conjunct Jupiter in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-jupiter-synastry/):** the two in one place, generous at full volume.
- **[Venus square Jupiter in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-jupiter-synastry/):** a sign further, where the question is how much.
- **[Venus trine Jupiter in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-jupiter-synastry/):** the same goodwill, flowing without waiting for invitations.
- **[Venus opposite Jupiter in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-jupiter-synastry/):** pleasure and meaning at the two ends of a line.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Say yes to one thing, and then say what you liked about it. The Jupiter person can ask before recommending; the Venus person can open a door or two in return, so the exchange goes both ways. In a couple, take turns choosing the next new thing to try.

About 1 pair of people in 13 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 3.6°. Venus moves little more than a degree a day and Jupiter far less, so the hour of birth matters only at the sextile's very edge; the houses where the invitations land need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Its effects are mostly seen in hindsight: the person whose suggestions, added up, changed your shelves, your holidays, your work.

In one person's chart, [Venus sextile Jupiter](/astrology/venus-sextile-jupiter/) turns enjoyment into learning without outside help. Between two charts, someone else holds the door.
