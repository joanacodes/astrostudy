Lilith opposite North Node in synastry stretches the whole width of the zodiac, within 3°, from one person's Black Moon Lilith to the other's North Node, which puts that Lilith on top of their South Node: opposite one node means conjunct the other. The wildness of one lands on the oldest, most practised ground of the other. It can feel like recognition, or like going round an old loop together.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's mean apogee, is linked in many readings with the self that won't be ruled, with boundaries and with what was set aside; the schools read it so differently that I keep it symbolic. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) collects what a person already does by reflex, and the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) across from it, what they're still learning. Under an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), two functions stare at each other across a line, and each makes the other plain; and as one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one puts the Lilith at the familiar end of the other's axis.

Holding the Lilith, you may feel that the other person knows your wildness already. Your refusals and appetites don't shock them; they recognise them, perhaps because they resemble their own old ways of standing apart. There's ease in that, and a strange sense of being expected.

Carrying the North Node, you find the other's untamed side oddly familiar, like a version of your own oldest habits. Time together can feel like home. The capacity you're learning sits across the chart from their Lilith, so they're less likely to pull you towards it than to keep you company where you've always been.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Nobody here is anybody's enemy; the risk is a seesaw stuck at its familiar end. The pair may go round the same old loop, the node person's habitual refusals echoed and approved by the other's, while the less practised direction waits. The Lilith person may also feel uneasy when the other starts to change, as if a shared wildness were being left behind.

- **[Lilith conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** Lilith placed on the road ahead rather than on what's already known.
- **[Lilith sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** a nudge from two signs off, friendly to the other's old ground.
- **[Lilith square North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-square-north-node-synastry/):** the instinct thrown across the axis, between the two ends.
- **[Lilith trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-trine-north-node-synastry/):** frankness carried towards the other's growth by an easy current.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Name the loop when you see it: "we're doing the old thing again". Then use the familiarity as a base. Whoever carries the North Node can take one step a season towards the less practised capacity, with the other's blessing; whoever carries the Lilith can cheer that step rather than read it as rejection. The same holds for long friendships, siblings, couples and colleagues of many years: shared ground makes a good runway.

The dates use mean Lilith and the true node, with the mean node checked. For a birth in mid-1985, the North Node is in Taurus, so the South Node is in Scorpio, and anyone born from March to May 1990 has Lilith in Scorpio, right on that South Node and opposite the North Node. Mean Lilith returns to Scorpio in under nine years, so births in May and June 1981 and from January to March 1999 carry the same opposition. Reversed, the picture belongs to people born from March to July 1995, who have their North Node in Scorpio, and their South Node in Taurus on the 1985 Lilith.

Contacts like this one, shared by whole runs of births, have no place in my count of a synastry, where every contact involves the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Its houses, one at each end, show an era's wildness giving another era's routines a lived-in look. Both birth times are still needed for those houses, though an hour moves neither point by anything that matters here ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Lilith opposite North Node](/astrology/lilith-opposite-north-node/), inside a single chart, describes someone for whom saying no is long practice. Here one person's no meets the other's oldest habits, and the reading waits until both of them want it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
