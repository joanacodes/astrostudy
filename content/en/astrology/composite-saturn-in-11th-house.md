The same four friends at the same table every New Year's Eve, a residents' association the two of you have chaired in turns, a hope for the future written down ten years ago and still on the list. With composite Saturn in the 11th house, a relationship keeps a few long friendships and takes a long view.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds the people a chart gathers by choice, friends, colleagues, groups, and the hopes it shares with them. Transposed to [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it becomes the relationship's social world and its plans for the future. With [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) here, both are built to last. The pair tends to keep a small circle for decades, to take on real responsibility in groups, the treasurer, the organiser, the people who stay behind to stack the chairs, and to pursue shared hopes on a long timetable, one step a year if need be.

The 5th, across the chart, is the pleasure a pair takes in itself; the 11th is the pleasure it takes in other people. With Saturn on this side, the bond's loyalty reaches outwards, and it may find that friendship asks as much patience of it as love does.

Tradition called the 11th the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy here ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)); it's a succedent house, and its natural sign is Aquarius, one of Saturn's two signs in the old scheme. Both its cusps fall in the stretch between the Midheaven and the Ascendant, where the two ways of drawing composite houses, averaged cusps or houses computed from the composite Midheaven for where you live, can drift apart. Whichever is used, nothing can be drawn until both of you know your birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A circle of friends can recognise three different Saturns. There's the friend whose own chart has [Saturn in the 11th house](/astrology/saturn-in-11th-house/), loyal to a chosen few and dutiful in groups; there's the friend whose Saturn falls in yours, [their Saturn in your 11th house](/astrology/saturn-in-11th-house-synastry/), steadying your hopes or doubting them; and there's the pair whose composite Saturn sits here, two people their friends rely on as a unit, whatever each is like alone.

Seen by the public in [composite Saturn in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-10th-house/) and by no one but itself in [composite Saturn in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-12th-house/), the bond with Saturn in the 11th is seen by its friends, and that's where it keeps its promises.

Over time, the circle can close. New people stop getting in, old friendships are kept up from duty rather than pleasure, and the pair's hopes for the future harden into a plan nobody dares revise. Group responsibilities can also pile up until the two of you spend more evenings at meetings than with each other. It helps to let one or two new people in every few years, to hand on a role before it turns into a burden, and to revisit the shared hopes now and then: a plan made ten years ago deserves a fresh look.

I don't read composites. The astrologers who do read this house next to both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), as one view of a relationship among several.
