Most signs have one ruling planet, and nobody argues about it. Three have two, for a reason that's simply history: in a century and a half, the sky astrologers could see grew three planets larger.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Each sign has a ruling planet, the one whose home, or [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), it is; wherever that planet sits in a chart is where the sign's business goes. The traditional scheme, the one Ptolemy sets out, uses the seven moving lights visible without a telescope, and it's built with care. The two lights take one sign each, side by side: the Moon Cancer, the Sun Leo. The five planets then take a sign on each side of them, outwards in the old order of the planets: Mercury Gemini and Virgo, next to the lights; Venus Taurus and Libra; Mars Aries and Scorpio; Jupiter Pisces and Sagittarius; and Saturn, the slowest, Aquarius and Capricorn, opposite the lights.

The modern scheme keeps all of that and adds three. William Herschel discovered Uranus in 1781, Neptune was found in 1846 and Pluto in 1930, and modern astrologers came to give them Aquarius, Pisces and Scorpio. So [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) has Pluto and Mars, [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) has Uranus and Saturn, and [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) has Neptune and Jupiter. The other nine signs have the same ruler in both schemes.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Notice where the change landed: one air sign and two water signs. No fire or earth sign gained a modern ruler, so the [fire signs](/astrology/fire-signs/) keep the rulers they've always had, Mars, the Sun and Jupiter, and the earth signs keep Venus, Mercury and Saturn.

The two rulers of a shared sign work at very different speeds. Pluto can spend anywhere from 12 to 31 years in a sign, Neptune about 14 and Uranus about 7, so everyone born over several years shares their sign, and what makes them personal is the house they fall in. Mars, Jupiter and Saturn move far faster, from about six weeks to two and a half years in a sign, and speak more directly to one person.

Weakness follows rulership. Detriment is the sign opposite a domicile, so a planet that gains a sign gains a detriment as well: modern astrologers put Uranus in detriment in Leo, opposite Aquarius, beside Saturn's traditional detriment there. Exaltation is another matter: tradition gives the outer planets none, and the scheme of exaltations stays as the old books left it.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I read both. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on a house cusp, two planets carry that house's story, and they often sit in different parts of the chart. With Pisces on the cusp of the 10th, say, Jupiter might show where your public life opens out and Neptune the ideal you're chasing in it ([Pisces on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/)). I tell you what each one says, and where they disagree. When the sign is your rising sign, both are your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/).

The rulers are also why the exact sign matters for anyone born near a change. One day's difference can hand you a different pair: the traditional ruler stays Saturn from Capricorn into Aquarius, while from Aquarius into Pisces both rulers change ([born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/)). That's part of [how I read](/how-i-read/): the chart as it is, with every planet that speaks for a house.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The modern rulers replaced the old ones.”** They were added. Traditional astrologers still use Mars, Saturn and Jupiter alone, and many modern ones keep them as co-rulers.
- **“Traditional rulership is out of date.”** It's older, not wrong. The traditional ruler describes something the outer planet can't: the faster, more personal thread of the sign.
- **“Every planet rules two signs.”** The Sun and Moon rule one each, and so, in the modern scheme, do Uranus, Neptune and Pluto. Only Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn rule two, and three of them now share one of theirs.
- **“Two rulers make a confused sign.”** They describe two layers, the generation you belong to and the person you are. Scorpio, Aquarius and Pisces simply have more to say.
