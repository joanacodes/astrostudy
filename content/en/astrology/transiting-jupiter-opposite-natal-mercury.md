Halfway round its twelve-year circuit of your chart, Jupiter faces your Mercury. What you know in detail and the larger view someone else holds end up in the same room, and the conversation between them is the transit.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is your way of noticing, reasoning and saying; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the search for meaning and the beliefs that come of it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the larger view tends to arrive in someone else's voice: their theory, their plan, their objection to yours.

It can be a teacher who widens what you thought you knew, a critic who makes your argument better, a conversation with someone from a very different world, or somebody else's big project that needs your precision. Polarity isn't contradiction. The usual gift is a sharper sense of what you actually think, found by hearing what you don't.

The two planets have faced each other in tradition for a long time. Mercury's signs, Gemini and Virgo, lie opposite Jupiter's, Sagittarius and Pisces, and each planet is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in the other's: the gatherer of facts and the maker of meaning at the two ends of one axis.

Which house Jupiter is crossing is set by your Mercury's: always the one directly across the chart, in Placidus, the house system I use. With Mercury in the 3rd, the opposition falls during [Jupiter transiting the 9th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-9th-house/), in the natural houses of Gemini and Sagittarius, which tradition named the Goddess and the God: lessons and local talk on one side, philosophy, travel and long study on the other. Neither house exists without your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and neither does my reading. Born with [Mercury opposite Jupiter](/astrology/mercury-opposite-jupiter/), you'll recognise the feel of it: the debate you were born into, replayed in the sky each time Jupiter returns to its birth place.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Jupiter usually holds the opposition to your Mercury for about eleven days. Roughly three times in ten it does so inside a retrograde loop: three exact passes and about eight months.

Your Mercury sits within about 28° of your Sun, so this opposition comes within about a year of [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-sun/), and the two seasons often read as one: your ideas and your sense of direction meeting other people's at once.

Six or seven times by 80, everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has Jupiter across from their Mercury, once a round. It comes about six years after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-mercury/), when the subject that opened then has gone public enough to be questioned. Before it, Jupiter has invited your Mercury to speak ([transiting Jupiter sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-mercury/)), stretched its attention ([transiting Jupiter square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-mercury/)) and given it an easy tongue ([transiting Jupiter trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-mercury/)); the same three come back, in reverse, as the round closes.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Listen to the end before you reply. The opposition often brings the view you most need in the voice you least expected.

Write your position down, then the best case against it. Mercury does its finest work under this transit when it has to be fair to Jupiter.

Let a disagreement stay a conversation. Polarity is information, not a contest to win.
