A band formed at sixty, a front door painted a colour the whole street remembers, an exhibition of things made at the kitchen table. Composite Uranus in Leo is a relationship's originality wanting to be seen, in a sign that pulls Uranus between being different and being admired.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the planet that breaks with habit, becomes in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) the relationship's own break with habit. [Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire ruled by the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), gives that break warmth, staying power and a wish to be seen. In modern tropical astrology Leo is Uranus's detriment, the sign opposite its own Aquarius: Uranus reforms on behalf of the group, Leo expresses the self. In a relationship chart that tension is not a weakness but a question the pair keeps answering: to stand apart, or to shine?

Detriment or not, it is no rarer than its neighbours: about 1 pair in 12 of the two million random pairs in the count. Two people of the same age get their generation's sign, and Uranus crossed Leo from August 1955 to August 1962, so same-age couples with it are now in their mid-sixties to early seventies; the next Leo generation begins in August 2039. An age gap can produce it too: someone born at the start of 1950, with Uranus at about 3° Cancer, and someone born at the start of 1966, at about 20° Virgo, meet at about 11° Leo. For them it's a sign neither brought, and so the relationship's own.

The bond's originality is creative and visible. It tends to make things together, a garden, a show, a renovation nobody else would attempt, and it likes its independence noticed: the couple who mark an anniversary with a performance, the friendship that runs a festival stall in matching costumes. Freedom here is the freedom of each person to shine without the other dimming, and of the bond to have a style of its own. Where [Uranus in Leo](/astrology/uranus-in-leo/) gave a generation the urge to create and stand out loudly, the composite asks two people to stand out together, and the composite Sun, Leo's ruler, shows what all that creating is for.

The trap is difference performed for an audience. When independence needs applause, the relationship can pick the unusual option because it looks bold rather than because it suits you, and start to confuse being different with being right. What helps is to make some things only for the pleasure of making them, and to give each person a stage of their own as well as the shared one.

As the sign is a generation's, look to the house for what is yours; it needs both birth times. [Composite Uranus in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-5th-house/), Leo's natural house, puts the invention into play and romance. [Composite Uranus in Cancer](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-cancer/) reinvents home; [composite Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-virgo/), the details of daily life. I don't use composites in my own work, so read this as the technique explained: it sits alongside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), tells you nothing about how long you'll last, and is drawn only with both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
