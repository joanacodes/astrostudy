The second decan of Virgo runs from 10° to 20° of the sign, the Sun's days in early September. It holds Mercury's exaltation degree, and all three exact meetings of Uranus and Pluto in the 1960s.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

The Chaldean order follows the Sun with [Venus](/astrology/venus/), so Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) give the middle of [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) to the planet tradition puts in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) here, opposite its exaltation in Pisces. By element, the modern decans give the middle of an earth sign to the ruler of the next earth sign, Capricorn, and that is [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/).

Under Mercury, then, the decan answers to Venus or to Saturn: what is worth keeping, in one list, and what will hold up over time, in the other. With a planet here, I read your Mercury first, since it rules the sign, and then whichever of Venus and Saturn is the more prominent in your chart.

[Venus in Virgo](/astrology/venus-in-virgo/) between 10° and 20° is in fall by sign and in its own face: love shown through attention and practical care, with the face as a small foothold in a sign that doesn't otherwise favour Venus.

Mercury has its own mark in the decan. The old tables put its exaltation degree at the 15th of Virgo ([exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/)), so [Mercury in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-in-virgo/) between 14° and 15° stands in its own sign, on its own exaltation degree, in a decan whose rulers are other planets.

## Its image {#its-image}

Budge's pair of names closes here: Themat-khert follows the Themat-ḥert of the decan before ([Virgo, first decan](/astrology/virgo-first-decan/)). The Greco-Egyptian lists write Uste-bikot, the Greek Hermetic list Brysous; Firmicus Maternus has Tophicus, or Tropicus, Joseph Scaliger Thopitus, and the *Testament of Solomon* Ieropaêl.

Cosmas of Maiuma set the Moirai over these degrees, the Fates of Greek myth, and Athanasius Kircher Piosiris. Fates are the one picture I keep firmly out of a reading: a chart shows how you tend to move, never a thread already spun ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun stands here for births from about 3 to 12 September ([Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/)). In 2026 it reached 10° Virgo on 2 September at 10:53 UT and 20° on 12 September at 18:08 UT, and it crosses Mercury's exaltation degree on about 6 and 7 September.

Uranus and Pluto met in this decan three times: on 9 October 1965 at 17°10′ Virgo, on 4 April 1966 at 16°27′ and on 30 June 1966 at 16°06′ ([Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/)). Uranus held these degrees from August 1964 to September 1966, Pluto from July 1963 to November 1966, so many people born in those years carry both here. Neptune passed before them, for births from June 1934 to October 1937, and Saturn in 1949–50, 1978–79 and 2008–09.

The total eclipse of the Moon on 3 March 2026 fell at 12°51′ Virgo ([Eclipse in Virgo](/astrology/eclipse-in-virgo/)), and Jupiter crosses the decan from 11 September to 29 October 2027, then again from April to June 2028.

Saturn, the decan's ruler by element, is one of the three placements that held most often in my tests, 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). Where your Saturn stands is worth a close look whenever this decan holds something of yours. Ten degrees on, Mercury takes the face ([Virgo, third decan](/astrology/virgo-third-decan/)).
