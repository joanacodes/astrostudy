All three exact meetings of Uranus and Pluto in the 1960s happened in the middle of Virgo, in degrees that also hold Mercury's exaltation. They mark the second decan of Virgo, 10° to 20° of the sign, and the Sun is there in early September.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) follows the Sun in the Chaldean order, and so the middle face of [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) in Lilly's table of [faces](/astrology/faces/) goes to a planet in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) here, opposite its exaltation in Pisces. The decan by element belongs to [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), ruler of Capricorn, the earth sign after Virgo.

Under Mercury, then, the decan answers to Venus or to Saturn: what is worth keeping, in one list, and what will hold up over time, in the other. Your Mercury, ruler of the sign, gets my first look, and then whichever of Venus and Saturn is the more prominent in your chart.

[Venus in Virgo](/astrology/venus-in-virgo/) between 10° and 20° is in fall by sign and in its own face: love shown through attention and practical care, with the face as a small foothold in a sign that doesn't otherwise favour Venus.

The 15th degree of Virgo carries a further honour for Mercury: the old tables count it as Mercury's exaltation degree ([exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/)). A [Mercury in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-in-virgo/) between 14° and 15° combines home sign and exaltation degree in one place, while the decan itself answers to Venus and Saturn.

## Its image {#its-image}

Themat-khert, as Budge gives it, answers the Themat-ḥert of the decan before ([Virgo, first decan](/astrology/virgo-first-decan/)). Uste-bikot is the Greco-Egyptian name and Brysous the Greek Hermetic one. Firmicus Maternus's Tophicus, or Tropicus, and Joseph Scaliger's Thopitus are near neighbours, and the *Testament of Solomon* adds Ieropaêl.

The Moirai, the Fates of Greek myth, are Cosmas of Maiuma's choice here, and Piosiris is Athanasius Kircher's. Fates are the one picture I keep firmly out of a reading: a chart shows how you tend to move, never a thread already spun ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun is in this decan for births from about 3 to 12 September ([Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/)), and on Mercury's exaltation degree for those of about 6 and 7 September.

Uranus and Pluto met in this decan three times: on 9 October 1965 at 17°10′ Virgo, on 4 April 1966 at 16°27′ and on 30 June 1966 at 16°06′ ([Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/)). Uranus was in these degrees from August 1964 to September 1966 and Pluto from July 1963 to November 1966, so many people born in those years carry both here. Neptune had crossed them earlier, for births from June 1934 to October 1937, and Saturn followed in 1949–50, 1978–79 and 2008–09.

The total eclipse of the Moon on 3 March 2026 fell at 12°51′ Virgo ([Eclipse in Virgo](/astrology/eclipse-in-virgo/)), and Jupiter crosses the decan from 11 September to 29 October 2027, then again from April to June 2028.

Saturn, the decan's ruler by element, is one of the three placements that held most often in my tests, 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). Where your Saturn stands is worth a close look whenever this decan holds something of yours. Mercury's own face comes next, in the last ten degrees of the sign ([Virgo, third decan](/astrology/virgo-third-decan/)).
