Your Moon was still in Virgo, one sign behind the Sun, in the last days before a new moon. I read the pair as an eye for proportion and an eye for detail in one person: the Libra Sun wants a thing to be fair and pleasing, and the Virgo Moon can't rest until it also works.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Virgo is the sign just before Libra: neighbours, a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign, sharing neither element nor mode, mutable earth beside cardinal air. Neighbouring signs tend to know each other's habits without sharing them. The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) looks for the balanced, gracious solution; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) settles by sorting, mending and improving, by making things work properly. Together they are hard to satisfy, and very good at whatever they take on.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your Sun's sign, and Venus has its fall in Virgo, where tradition says it loses its footing; [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of your Moon's sign, is exalted there as well. I read that as taste and precision in a slightly uneasy partnership: pleasure that wants a reason, and analysis that is entirely at home. Apart from the Sun's [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Libra, neither light has a dignity or a debility here, and the signs and houses of Venus and Mercury show whether the two cooperate in your chart or keep correcting each other.

The Moon had come 301° to 359° round from the Sun, which left it 1° to 59° behind, in a lunar month 24½ to 29½ days old. These are mostly [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) births, under the last thin crescent before dawn, with a small share, about one in eight, still in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/). The next new moon was days away, usually a [new moon in Libra](/astrology/new-moon-in-libra/), and at the very end of the range a Moon in Virgo's last degrees can be conjunct a Sun just into Libra. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the balsamic phase and its edges.

Roughly one birth in 145 has the Moon a sign behind a Libra Sun. On a day when the Moon was crossing from Leo into Virgo, or from Virgo into Libra, only the birth time can tell whether you have this page, the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-leo/) or the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-libra/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It is the same time that gives the rising sign and the houses, which show the part of life where this Moon goes on sorting things.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You show love by noticing: the train time remembered, the gift that fits exactly, the small thing fixed before anyone asked. The Libra Sun wants the relationship to be fair and graceful; the Virgo Moon wants it to work, day to day. You may see what isn't right between you before your partner does, and offer an improvement when what you meant was closeness. Saying the feeling first, *I miss you*, *I'm worried*, before the suggestion changes how it lands.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that has to be both well judged and exact suits you: design within technical limits, contracts, quality control, accounts, restoration, any craft where proportion and accuracy both count. The Libra Sun sees how the whole should look; the Virgo Moon checks every part. Colleagues may trust you to catch what's wrong before it goes out. The risk is fixing everyone else's work long after your own is done.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two standards can run at once, fair and correct, and it may feel as if there is never time to meet both. The result can be a quiet, constant self-review, and difficulty letting anything be finished. Decide in advance what good enough looks like, by both measures, and stop there. Then let one thing a week be merely pleasant, left unimproved, with no purpose but your enjoyment of it.
