Board games end in rematches, the tennis gets serious, and the dance class turns into a project. Composite Mars in the 5th house is a relationship that spends its drive on play, pleasure, and making things for no reason but delight.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) gathers play, romance, creativity and whatever is done for pleasure. Its old name, Good Fortune, and the joy of Venus placed there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)) make it a lively setting for Mars, the planet of pursuit. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [Mars](/astrology/mars/) in the 5th puts the relationship's energy into what it enjoys. Leo is its natural sign, and the house is succedent.

Such a pair plays hard. It joins the five-a-side team, enters the pub quiz to win, takes up salsa and practises on the kitchen floor, writes songs or builds things purely for the fun of it. Romance stays active: this bond courts, surprises and pursues long after the first months. Competition is the spice, and rematches get demanded. Making something together, a podcast, a garden, a band of two, gives this Mars its best outlet, because the rivalry then goes into the work. [Mars in the 5th house](/astrology/mars-in-5th-house/) gives one person a playful, bold streak; [their Mars in your 5th house](/astrology/mars-in-5th-house-synastry/) is someone who makes you want to play or to show off; the composite makes play the relationship's way of spending energy.

Disagreements can start in play: a game that stops being fun, a loser who sulks, a moment in the spotlight one of you thinks the other stole. Drama comes easily, and so does boredom when life leaves no room for fun. Play isn't immaturity, but rivalry needs limits. What helps is keeping score only where both of you enjoy it, taking turns at the centre of attention, and protecting time for pleasure even in busy seasons.

Children belonged to the 5th in traditional astrology. This placement says nothing about whether you'll have children or when, a question I never put to a chart ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I read no child's chart, ever ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). In an adult relationship, the 5th describes what the two of you create and enjoy.

Rivalry in a game is friction. If play ever turns into intimidation, or one of you is afraid, set the chart aside and find help: 116 123 for Samaritans across the UK and Ireland, 988 in the US and Canada, 999 in a UK emergency.

Composite houses need both exact birth times, and they depend on whether the astrologer averages the two charts' cusps or derives them from the composite Midheaven and the place you share ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). I keep composites out of my practice; those who use them never read this one apart from your own charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). Next to it, [composite Mars in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-4th-house/) pours its energy into the home, [composite Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-6th-house/) into daily work; the 5th spends it on joy, and like any composite it needs both people's consent before it's drawn ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
