A lot of friendships begin as a conversation nobody wants to end. When the 11th's ruler lives in the 3rd, your circle is made of talk: the neighbour you chat with at the gate, the evening class, the message thread that has run for years, the brother or sister who turned into a friend once you were both grown.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) covers the mind you use every day and the world a short walk away: how you talk, write and learn, the errands and short journeys of an ordinary week, brothers, sisters, cousins and neighbours. The ruler of your [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), the house of the people you've picked and the causes you'd turn out for, has gone to live there ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Your friendships happen in conversation and nearby, and much of what you hope for gets worked out in words.

You tend to make friends wherever there's talk: at the school gate, in a language class, at the residents' meeting, under a post you couldn't leave alone. The groups that suit you have something to discuss, a reading circle, a debating society, a local campaign whose newsletter somebody has to write, and that somebody is often you. Brothers, sisters and cousins can become friends in adult life, and friends a kind of sibling, with the teasing and the shorthand that come with it; that's your side of those bonds, and your siblings' own lives stay in their charts. Plans become real once you've said them aloud or written them to someone. The risk is a future that stays at the talking stage, discussed in detail and never begun.

Four signs separate the 3rd from the 11th, the distance of a trine. Both belong to the houses some astrologers group as the air houses, with the 7th between them: the places of exchange, where you meet people as equals at every distance, from the neighbour to the partner to the crowd. The trine, to me, is an easy current between your circle and your everyday thinking. You think better among friends, and friends often come to you for the way you put things.

The 3rd was the Goddess in the old names, the house where the Moon rejoiced ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With [Cancer on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/), the Moon rules your friendships, and from the 3rd it keeps them on a daily footing: the regular call, the neighbour who checks in, the friend you message about nothing at all. If Gemini opens the 11th, Mercury takes the minutes and remembers everyone's news. If the cusp is in Scorpio, the 11th has Mars for its traditional ruler and Pluto for its modern one, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 3rd argues hard for a cause, while Pluto, from its own house, brings the rare friendship deep enough to change your mind.

All of this rests on two cusps that the clock places ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With a slightly later birth, a ruler near the start of the 3rd would have turned back into the 2nd, where friends and money share a thread ([Ruler of the 11th in the 2nd house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)). With an earlier one, a ruler close to the IC would have sunk into the 4th, and friends would become part of the household ([Ruler of the 11th in the 4th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)). In [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the system I read in, a few minutes are enough to move a planet across that line.
