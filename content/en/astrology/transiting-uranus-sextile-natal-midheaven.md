A sextile from Uranus to your Midheaven rarely makes noise. For about twenty months, a new way of doing your work, or of being seen in it, is available, and it stays available only as long as you're the one who picks it up.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) innovates; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) frames your visible contribution, the role and responsibilities you carry where others can see them. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through opportunity: this [transit](/astrology/transits/) makes room for a change in your public direction without forcing one. A project you'd been sketching, a skill kept on the side, a more independent way of working: the sextile lowers the cost of trying, and leaves the trying to you.

The same Uranus trines your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), so a change in public life has the support of your base rather than a quarrel with it: the people and places that sustain you can make room for what you're attempting. If home has been asking for a change of its own, this is the aspect under which the two can be arranged together.

The sextile before Uranus reaches your Midheaven comes, as a rule, from your 8th house in Placidus, the house system I use, sometimes from the 7th: a close collaboration, or a commitment shared with someone, opens the way, often through another person's trust in your work ([Uranus transiting the 8th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-8th-house/)). The one after, about fourteen years past the crossing, usually comes from your 11th or 12th: friends and shared plans, or work done out of sight ([Uranus transiting the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-11th-house/)). The Midheaven's degree and these houses all come from your birth time, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile is in reach for about twenty months, eleven in the shortest passes, and in about thirteen passes out of fourteen Uranus touches the exact degree three times.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have had it twice, and all of them once. The two sextiles stand either side of [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-midheaven/), about fourteen years from it, with [transiting Uranus square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-midheaven/) about seven years further out; [transiting Uranus trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-midheaven/) and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-midheaven/) belong to the far side of the round.

In these years, Uranus in Gemini sextiles Midheavens in Aries, from your 11th or 12th, and in Leo, from your 8th ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)); in Cancer, from 2032 to 2039, it reaches Taurus and Virgo Midheavens the same way.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to the proposal that doesn't fit your job description, and decide afterwards whether it fits you. Sextiles open side doors more often than the front one.

Give the idea a trial with a start and an end: a pilot, a season, a commission. Twenty months is enough to learn whether it holds.

Tell the people you work with what you're trying. A sextile works through cooperation, and a door is easier to go through with someone holding it open.
