The 8th house begins where a bond gets serious. After the 7th, where you meet someone, the 8th is what happens when you let them in: trust, intimacy, money in common, and everything that's harder to take back once it's shared.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance, and many books still open with that. I don't read death in a chart, for anyone ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/) says why), so I read the 8th in an adult's life, where it's busy with the living: intimacy, sexual and otherwise; trust, and what it costs to give it; what you share with other people, and what you owe them.

Counted from the 7th, the 8th is your partner's 2nd house — their money — which is why it also covers joint accounts, loans, debts and anything held in common ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)). It's the house of what's owed in both senses: money, and the debts people carry between them. It never says what you'll gain or lose; it shows how you handle sharing.

- **A succedent house.** It follows an angle, and succedent houses are where things settle and build up: the 7th makes the bond, the 8th holds what the bond creates. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), with [Mars](/astrology/mars/) as Scorpio's traditional ruler: depth, intensity, and the nerve it takes to stay close.
- **Its opposite.** The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/): the 2nd is what's yours, the 8th what you share or owe.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), just before it, is the person across the table; the 8th is what you build, risk and owe once they're on your side of it. The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), just after it, is where what you've been through goes looking for a meaning.

Like every house, the 8th is placed by your birth time: without it, I can read your planets in their signs, but not which of them fall here ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 8th house {#planets}

A planet in the 8th house goes deep. It tends to work in private, show itself to very few people, and describe what happens to you when you let someone close.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith are the placements I read most carefully: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) they held 75 to 90% of the time, against about a third for most placements. With Saturn, [Chiron in the 8th house](/astrology/chiron-in-8th-house/) or [Lilith in the 8th house](/astrology/lilith-in-8th-house/) here, a reading of mine would slow down around trust: who earned yours, and at what cost.

- **[Sun in the 8th house](/astrology/sun-in-8th-house/):** you grow through intimacy, trust and what you share; you come alive in depth, not on the surface.
- **[Moon in the 8th house](/astrology/moon-in-8th-house/):** you need emotional closeness to feel safe, and you feel other people's moods strongly.
- **[Mercury in the 8th house](/astrology/mercury-in-8th-house/):** a mind for what's hidden — research, psychology, the question nobody asks.
- **[Venus in the 8th house](/astrology/venus-in-8th-house/):** love that wants depth and loyalty; money and affection can get tangled.
- **[Mars in the 8th house](/astrology/mars-in-8th-house/):** strong desire and a strong will; you fight for what's shared, and learn where your power ends.
- **[Jupiter in the 8th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-8th-house/):** generous with what's shared; hard times tend to leave you wiser rather than smaller.
- **[Saturn in the 8th house](/astrology/saturn-in-8th-house/):** trust given slowly — and kept; debts and promises taken seriously.
- **[Uranus in the 8th house](/astrology/uranus-in-8th-house/):** you need freedom even in deep closeness; sudden changes in what's shared.
- **[Neptune in the 8th house](/astrology/neptune-in-8th-house/):** boundaries blur in intimacy and in shared money; deep empathy, and the need to know what's yours.
- **[Pluto in the 8th house](/astrology/pluto-in-8th-house/):** in the house of its own sign: intensity, privacy, and the power to rebuild yourself after a crisis.

No planet here? An [empty 8th house](/astrology/empty-houses/) doesn't mean a shallow life, or one without intimacy. It's read through the sign on its cusp and the place of its ruler, the next two sections.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the cusp of the 8th house describes how you approach what's shared: how you trust, how you handle intimacy and joint money, and what you do when a bond asks for more than you meant to give.

- **[Aries on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-8th-house-cusp/):** you share quickly and meet a crisis head-on, but keep some independence.
- **[Taurus on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what's shared has to feel solid; you like knowing what's whose.
- **[Gemini on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-8th-house-cusp/):** you need to talk before you can trust; depth comes one question at a time.
- **[Cancer on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/):** intimacy needs safety first; once you trust, you protect what's shared like family.
- **[Leo on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** you give generously and want to be cherished; pride can hide what you need.
- **[Virgo on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** careful with debts and contracts; trust grows through small, reliable gestures.
- **[Libra on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what's shared should be fair; intimacy and joint money work best as an equal deal.
- **[Scorpio on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/):** the house's natural sign: private, intense, all or nothing; you trust slowly and completely.
- **[Sagittarius on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** you meet depth with openness and humour; hard times turn into questions of meaning.
- **[Capricorn on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-8th-house-cusp/):** responsible with what's owed; intimacy comes with time and proven trust.
- **[Aquarius on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** you need space inside intimacy, and dislike feeling owned.
- **[Pisces on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/):** boundaries in closeness and money can blur; you learn where you end and others begin.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 8th house is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp, and where it sits shows where the 8th's questions — trust, sharing, debt — come up in your life. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the cusp, there's a modern and a traditional ruler, and I read both ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** intensity shows in how you meet the world; depth is part of who you are.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** what you share and what you own are tied together; clear lines help.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** a researcher's mind, and the conversations others avoid.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** trust, sharing and what's owed play out at home and in the family.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** intensity in love and creation; passion that wants depth, not just fun.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** depth in daily work: crises, research, or other people's money.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** partnership is where trust and shared money are tested and built.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** the ruler at home: private, resilient, at ease with depth.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** beliefs shaped by what you've been through; drawn to what lies underneath.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** work with other people's resources, crises or secrets; a role that asks for discretion.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** friendships that go deep; resources or projects shared with a group.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** you work things through in private; trust given quietly.

## Transits through the 8th house {#transits}

The Moon passes through your 8th house every month, the Sun once a year, Mercury, Venus and Mars for a few weeks at a time: a question of trust or money comes up, then moves on. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 8th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-8th-house/):** about a year when trust comes more easily and sharing feels generous, both ways.
- **[Saturn transiting the 8th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-8th-house/):** about two and a half years of taking stock of what you owe and are owed, and of whom you really trust.
- **[Uranus transiting the 8th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-8th-house/):** around seven years of sudden changes in what's shared, and of needing freedom inside intimacy.
- **[Neptune transiting the 8th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-8th-house/):** around fourteen years when boundaries in money and closeness soften: a time for compassion, and for clear accounts.
- **[Pluto transiting the 8th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-8th-house/):** a long, slow change in how you handle power, trust and letting go.
- **[Chiron transiting the 8th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-8th-house/):** an old wound around trust comes up to be tended; its pace is uneven, from under two years to about eight in a sign.
- **[North Node transiting the 8th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-8th-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards sharing, while the South Node crosses your 2nd.

I read these as weather, not a forecast: none of them predicts a death, an inheritance or a sum of money, only what sharing and trust may feel like for a while.
