Some relationships grow up fast: a business started together, a parent to care for, a mortgage at twenty-five. In those bonds, composite Chiron in Capricorn marks authority and responsibility as the sore places, and the structure two people build for them, slowly, as the answer.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The weight is the point here. [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), cardinal earth ruled by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), is the sign of structure, rules, time and standing, and [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a chart keeps meeting a limit and turns the meeting into skill. Placed in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the chart of midpoints that describes a relationship rather than either person, it makes authority the bond's sore place: who is in charge, what the pair owes and to whom, whether families, institutions or the outside world take the relationship seriously.

Two easy-going people can find their relationship strangely dutiful, as if it had signed a contract neither of them read; two serious people can find it the one place they put the load down. It copes by building: rules the pair writes for itself, long plans broken into steps, authority earned by keeping promises rather than claimed. Such pairs are often a steady guide for others through official forms, hard decisions or a first job, having had to find their own way through them.

Composite Chiron falls in Capricorn for about 1 pair in 13, in two million pairs of birth dates drawn from 1930 to 2009. Those born together between February 1951 and January 1955, or between December 2001 and December 2005, have it from their generation. A decade's gap can produce it. Chiron stood at about 5° Sagittarius in June 1949 and about 28° Aquarius in June 1959, so two people born then share a composite Chiron at about 16° Capricorn. With a wider gap, it can hang on a hair. Someone born in July 1971 has Chiron near 14° Aries; someone born in January 1996, near 14° Libra; their two midpoints, 14° Capricorn and 14° Cancer, are almost equally near, and the days of birth decide which the composite takes.

Where [Chiron in Capricorn](/astrology/chiron-in-capricorn/) marks one person's dealings with authority, the composite makes authority a matter between the two of you, and the world outside, a bank, a landlord, a family, may test it before either of you does.

Overload is the danger: too much taken on, achievements made the measure, rules hardened until there is no room for play. The load gets lighter once it is shared out explicitly, with milestones marked and some time kept that has no purpose at all. On one side, [composite Chiron in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-sagittarius/) is tender about what the bond believes; on the other, [composite Chiron in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-aquarius/) about how different it is allowed to be.

Composites stay outside my readings, so this is the technique speaking, as its users read it with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It keeps no timetable, and never says whether two people will marry, succeed or stay. Before anyone draws a chart of two people, both of them should know about it, one of the few things I ask before a couple reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
