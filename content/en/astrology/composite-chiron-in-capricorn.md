Some relationships grow up fast: a business started together, a parent to care for, a mortgage at twenty-five. Composite Chiron in Capricorn makes authority and responsibility the bond's tender spot, and describes the structure two people build for it, slowly and on their own terms.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The weight is the point here. [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), cardinal earth ruled by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), is the sign of structure, rules, time and standing, and [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a chart keeps meeting a limit and turns the meeting into skill. Placed in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the chart of midpoints that describes a relationship rather than either person, it makes authority the bond's sore place: who is in charge, what the pair owes and to whom, whether families, institutions or the outside world take the relationship seriously.

The heaviness may belong to the bond alone. Two easy-going people can find their relationship strangely dutiful, as if it had signed a contract neither of them read; two serious people can find it the one place they put the load down. The adapting is architectural: rules the pair writes for itself, long plans broken into steps, authority earned by keeping promises rather than claimed. Such pairs are often a steady guide for others through official forms, hard decisions or a first job, having had to find their own way through them.

Composite Chiron falls in Capricorn for about 1 pair in 13, in two million pairs of birth dates drawn from 1930 to 2009. Those born together between February 1951 and January 1955, or between December 2001 and December 2005, have it from their generation. A decade's gap can produce it: born in June 1949 and June 1959, two people have Chirons at about 5° Sagittarius and 28° Aquarius, and their composite's sits at about 16° Capricorn. With a wider gap, it can hang on a hair. Born in July 1971 and January 1996, two people have Chirons at about 14° Aries and 14° Libra, nearly opposite, and depending on the days the composite takes the midpoint at 14° Capricorn or the one at 14° Cancer.

In a single birth chart, [Chiron in Capricorn](/astrology/chiron-in-capricorn/) describes one person's relationship with authority. In the composite it is a shared relationship with it, and each of you may feel the lesson as the other's control, or as the other's reluctance to take charge.

Its hard side is the load. A bond sore about responsibility can take on too much, measure itself by achievements, or let the rules harden until there is no room for play. It helps to share the load out explicitly, to mark the milestones you reach, and to keep some time that has no purpose at all. On one side, [composite Chiron in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-sagittarius/) is tender about what the bond believes; on the other, [composite Chiron in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-aquarius/) about how different it is allowed to be.

I set out the technique without practising it: composites stay outside my readings, and those who use them read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It never says whether two people will marry, succeed or stay. Before anyone draws a chart of two people, both of them should know about it, one of the few things I ask before a couple reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
