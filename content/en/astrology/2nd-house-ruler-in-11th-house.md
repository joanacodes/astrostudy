Your money tends to have a social life. With the ruler of the 2nd in the 11th house, what you earn and what you value run through friends, networks, groups and the plans you share with them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your money and your sense of worth take their direction from the ruler of the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and this one lives in the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), the world of belonging: the friends you chose, teams, networks and communities, the people who help without having to, and the hopes you'd like to see through.

Resources, for you, tend to move through circles of people. Work may come by recommendation, a contract through a friend of a friend, an income from an organisation or a cause you believe in. Money goes out the same way: to the group, the club, the fundraiser, the round of drinks, the friend who needs a hand. What you value is shaped by the people you stand with, and a shared future can matter more to you than a private hoard. The care it asks for is keeping your own accounts clear inside the circle: lending to friends, pooling for a project or giving to a cause works best once you've decided what you can spare.

Benefactors, friends and hopes were the 11th's business in the old books, which named it the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With Sagittarius on the 2nd cusp, Jupiter rules your money from that house: open-handed, sociable, inclined to share and to be shared with. With Pisces there, Jupiter shares the house with Neptune, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Neptune, from its own house, can make a shared ideal the thing your money serves. With Aquarius on the cusp, Saturn and Uranus rule your 2nd from the house the natural zodiac gives their sign, and money becomes a means to independence or to a common cause: Saturn keeps careful books for the group, the treasurer who reads the small print of a shared project, while Uranus prefers money that buys freedom from any single employer. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 2nd from the 11th, earns through contacts and conversation: the introducer, the one who always knows who's hiring.

The planet ruling your money had come up over the eastern horizon a few hours before you did and was still climbing: the 11th, in Placidus, the house system I use. A later birth would carry it up into the 10th, where your money would follow your career and standing ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 10th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)); an earlier one would leave it lower, in the 12th, where money goes to rest, retreat and quiet kindness ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 12th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)). Only the birth time settles which ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Patrons, investors and lucky networks aren't promised by any placement, and no chart can say which shared venture will pay ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). This one describes how your resources travel: in company, towards a future you share.
