Jupiter square Uranus puts your frame of meaning at right angles to your urge for change. The two keep testing each other, which is uncomfortable at times and very good for keeping your beliefs alive. It returns to the sky about every seven years, so it marks a few months of births each time.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is your frame of meaning: what you believe, what you learn, how far you think things can grow. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the urge to differentiate and reform: to break with what no longer fits and try what hasn't been tried. A [square](/astrology/square/) sets them rubbing, and the rub is productive: neither lets the other rest, and both have to adapt.

In plain words, you can't hold an opinion for long without wanting to test it, and you can't change something without asking what it means. You may leap at a new direction and then question it, or hold a belief tightly until it suddenly gives way to a better one. Over time this square tends to produce a mind that updates honestly, and a sense of freedom that has thought about what it's for.

Rubbing isn't failing. The risk lies in taking on too much that is new at once; the gift lies in reforming your own views before anyone else has to ask.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In the eighty years from 1930 to 2009, about 1 birth in 17 has it within 6°, in spells about seven years apart: August 1930 to June 1931; January–February and September–October 1938; August–October 1944 and May–July 1945; April 1951 to February 1952; October–December 1958; May–July 1965 and December 1965 to March 1966; December 1972 to February 1973; July–August 1979 and April 1980; May 1986 to March 1987; December 1992 to February 1993 and August–October 1993; April–June 2000; and December 2006 to November 2007.

It says as much about a birth year as about you, since a social planet and a generational one make it ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). In the houses, it becomes particular. Jupiter in the 10th square Uranus in the 1st, say, might set your public ambitions against your need to be entirely your own person; Jupiter in the 2nd square Uranus in the 11th, the resources you build for yourself set against causes you share with friends. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Measured against the square:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus/):** the meeting itself, every fourteen years or so, as in 1968–69 or 1997.
- **[Jupiter sextile Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-uranus/):** an opening rather than a rub, for the births of 1956–57 or 1980–81, among others.
- **[Jupiter trine Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-trine-uranus/):** the two pulling the same way, for those born in 1959–60 or 1977–78, for example.
- **[Jupiter opposite Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-uranus/):** a polarity, for the births of 1948, 1975–76 or 1989–90.

[Jupiter square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-uranus-synastry/) sets one person's beliefs across the other's urge for change: stimulating arguments, sometimes tiring ones, and good for both when neither needs to win.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-jupiter/) is the long one: many months near exact, often in three passes, a season in which what you believe is asked to make room for something new. [Transiting Jupiter square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-uranus/) comes and goes in days or weeks, twice in each of Jupiter's twelve-year rounds.

Weather, not a forecast: stretches in which an old certainty may be asked to update, and how it does is up to you.
