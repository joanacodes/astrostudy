Half the guests at their parties met through them, and the cricket team, the residents' association or the campaign would struggle without them. Composite Mars in the 11th house runs a relationship's energy through friends and groups.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) gathers the people chosen beyond family, friends, groups and networks, together with the hopes held in common with them. Good Spirit was its old name, and Jupiter, tradition said, rejoiced there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). The pair's [Mars](/astrology/mars/), placed here in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), points the relationship's energy outwards, at its circle. It is a succedent house, with Aquarius as its natural sign. Opposite it lies the 5th, so this placement often has to choose between the private fun of two and the larger life of the group.

This couple organises. It captains the cricket team, runs the residents' association, hosts the book group, raises money for a cause you both believe in, and keeps a wide, loyal circle that knows it can call on you. Competition happens within groups, a debate at a meeting, a friendly rivalry with another couple, and the pair often ends up leading. Desire grows out of friendship and shared ideals, and needs some freedom to stay alive. Hopes matter here too: the bond tends to plan ten years ahead, together and out loud. Natal [Mars in the 11th house](/astrology/mars-in-11th-house/) spends one person's energy on friends and causes; [their Mars in your 11th house](/astrology/mars-in-11th-house-synastry/) opens new circles to you, or stirs up old ones; the composite makes the group part of the relationship's own life.

Arguments are about friends and time: whose friends come first, why every weekend is booked, why one of you said yes to another committee. Disputes inside a group can spill into the relationship, and loyalty to the group can compete with loyalty to each other. Community isn't conformity, and a full calendar isn't intimacy. What helps is choosing causes together, letting each of you keep friends of your own, and guarding evenings that belong to the two of you alone.

If one of you is ever cut off from friends, or frightened into giving them up, that is not a placement to interpret but a reason to get help: dial 999 in a UK emergency; Shout's text line, 85258, is free and always open.

Exact birth times for both of you are a must here: the 11th's cusps are either averages of your two charts' or houses set up from the composite Midheaven where the two of you now live, and the two can put a Mars near the edge in different houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Composite charts are something I describe rather than cast; their users read this Mars with each birth chart and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) beside it, and never as a forecast of who stays in your life. [Composite Mars in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-10th-house/) aims for public standing; [composite Mars in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-12th-house/) labours unseen; the 11th works through the crowd. A chart that reaches into your circle starts with both of you agreeing, not one alone ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
