Some pairs keep each other's secrets for decades and pool what they have without counting. With composite Venus in the 8th house, a relationship values trust and intimacy above any easy pleasure.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is succedent, Scorpio is its natural sign, and it holds intimacy, trust, shared resources and the obligations two people take on together. Venus is in detriment in that sign, a reminder that ease isn't what this house rewards. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) its cusps need both birth times and a choice of method, and a [Venus](/astrology/venus/) near either edge may belong to the 7th or the 9th instead ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Death and inheritance were the old books' additions to this house; neither appears in my reading of any chart ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and a composite forecasts no money, debt or legacy.

Where the 7th house is the agreement, the 8th is what the agreement creates: the things now jointly owned, owed and trusted. Venus here makes depth the bond's pleasure. It values confidences kept for years, help offered before it's asked for, and the slow building of trust until two people can lean on it with their full weight. Closeness, physical and otherwise, matters to it. What the pair holds jointly, a home, a project, obligations taken on together, is held with care, as a matter of trust rather than accounts. The way one person shares and trusts is what a natal [Venus in the 8th house](/astrology/venus-in-8th-house/) describes; [their Venus in your 8th house](/astrology/venus-in-8th-house-synastry/) names a closeness felt with someone in particular; in the composite, the sharing belongs to the bond.

For about 9 pairs in 10, the composite Sun lies within 47° of this Venus, often in this house or the one beside it. Born months apart, a pair can see the two midpoints end up facing each other across the chart, and roughly 1 pair in 22 then has the composite Sun near the point opposite this Venus, around the 2nd house of possessions ([composite Sun in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-2nd-house/)). The calculation does that, not the bond.

Merging has a cost. A bond that values sharing this much can lose track of where one person ends and the other begins, or keep an unspoken ledger of what was given in a crisis. Intimacy isn't obligation, and holding some things jointly needn't mean holding everything jointly. Pooling can be lovely and still leave each person a corner of their own. It helps to agree plainly what is shared and what stays each person's own, and to say thank you for help as readily as it was given.

[Composite Venus in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-7th-house/) cares for the partnership others see; [composite Venus in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-9th-house/) looks out at the wider world. I don't draw composites; this is the technique as written up by those who do, read in the light of the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and of each person's birth chart. Trust is the point of this house, and it starts before the chart does: both people are asked first ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
