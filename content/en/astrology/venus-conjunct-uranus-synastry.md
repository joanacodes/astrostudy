Some people change what you think you like. Venus conjunct Uranus in synastry lays one person's Venus over the other's Uranus, within 6°, and the first person's taste tends to get rearranged by the second's originality. I read it from each side: having your preferences shuffled is not the same as doing the shuffling.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) decides what a person finds lovely and whom they warm to, and keeps an eye on whether the give and take is fair. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part that differentiates: it questions habits, tries new arrangements and needs room to be unlike anyone else. Joined in a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), these two work as a single impulse; here, as in all [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the impulse is divided between two people: one brings the liking, the other the newness.

If the Venus is yours, this person tends to rearrange your taste. Music you'd have skipped, a way of dressing, a kind of friendship you didn't know existed: you find yourself enjoying it, sometimes before you can say why. Your pleasures get wider, and less predictable.

If the Uranus is yours, the very things that usually make you the odd one out are what this person enjoys. You're appreciated for your difference rather than forgiven for it, which can make you freer here than almost anywhere. Ideas you'd normally keep to yourself get tried out loud.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

At 0°, attraction and novelty run together, and it can be hard to tell which one you're drawn to. If the Venus is yours, your taste may start to follow the other's latest experiment, until a settled preference of your own feels dull, or even disloyal. If the Uranus is yours, being enjoyed for surprising people can quietly become a duty to keep surprising them; and when the Venus person asks to keep something as it was, it's easy to hear resistance in what is only a slower pace. Treating someone else's speed of adjusting as opposition is one of Uranus's oldest blind spots.

Fusion still leaves two people. The other angles give the same pair more distance:

- **[Venus sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-uranus-synastry/):** new things offered as invitations, accepted one at a time.
- **[Venus square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-uranus-synastry/):** cherished habits and fresh arrangements competing for the same room.
- **[Venus trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-uranus-synastry/):** freedom given and taken without much negotiation.
- **[Venus opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-uranus-synastry/):** closeness and independence parked at opposite ends of the room.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep a few tastes that are yours alone, on purpose. The Venus person can name the pleasures that aren't up for renewal; the Uranus person can ask before reinventing something the other loves, and let some experiments be private. Between friends, that might mean one trip planned the usual way for every three done on a whim; at work, a designer and an engineer agreeing which features stay familiar; in a couple or a family, a tradition kept even while everything around it changes.

About 1 pair of people in 15 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 6°. Uranus stays around seven years in each sign and takes some 84 years to come full circle, so nearly everyone born a year either side of the Uranus person has Uranus within a few degrees of theirs: that half of the contact describes a generation. Venus, a few weeks in each sign, is the half that makes it belong to these two people. Neither moves much in the hours of one day, so the birth time rarely makes or breaks the conjunction; it settles which houses it falls in, the part of each life being refreshed, and I need both times for that ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Venus conjunct Uranus](/astrology/venus-conjunct-uranus/), one person carries both the taste and the urge to change it. Between two charts, one supplies the novelty and the other the delight, and each can ask the other for a pause.
