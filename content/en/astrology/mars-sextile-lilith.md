Mars sextile Lilith gives your drive a side door into the untamed part of you. It opens when you decide to use it, which makes this the most voluntary of the pair's aspects: nothing pushes you through, and nothing stops you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is effort with a direction: the urge to start, compete, go after something and stand at the edge of your own territory. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), a calculated point at the Moon's far end, is read as the instinct that won't be domesticated, the material you were taught to leave out, and your sense of personal power. Readings of it vary widely, and I present mine as symbolism. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the two through opportunity: they cooperate, provided someone takes the opening.

In plain words, your untamed side is available to your drive without running it. You can act unconventionally when a situation calls for it, push back on a rule that doesn't fit, take the route nobody suggested, then come back to the ordinary road without fuss. Because nothing forces the issue, the sextile is easy to leave unused, and it rewards people who notice the door and walk through it.

It often shows in small, practical refusals: declining the role you were handed in a team, training or working in your own way, negotiating rather than complying. None of it needs drama, and that is its strength.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The two bodies usually sit in signs that get on by element, fire with air, earth with water, so drive and instinct speak languages that understand each other. Mars's sign sets the style of action; Lilith's sign, shared by everyone born within the same nine months or so, colours what in you resists taming. The houses show where the door is. Mars in the 3rd sextile Lilith in the 5th, for instance, might let the untamed side into how you play and create, reached through everyday errands and conversations.

About 1 person in 15 is born with Mars within 6° of a sextile to mean Lilith. Tight, the opening gets used almost by reflex; wide, it waits until you look for it. Mars, by far the quicker, carries the aspect in and out of orb, while Lilith holds its place for months.

My notebook gives the two ends different weight. Lilith stood out, holding 75 to 90% of the time along with Saturn and Chiron, while about a third of all placements held; Mars, checked like every other, was not among the three ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). So in a reading, Lilith's end of this sextile is the one I look at longest.

Among the pair's other aspects:

- **[Mars conjunct Lilith](/astrology/mars-conjunct-lilith/):** drive and the untamed side fused; acting is refusing, and refusing is acting.
- **[Mars square Lilith](/astrology/mars-square-lilith/):** they rub, and the effort goes into holding your ground.
- **[Mars trine Lilith](/astrology/mars-trine-lilith/):** a wider, easier channel; acting on instinct seldom needs a decision.
- **[Mars opposite Lilith](/astrology/mars-opposite-lilith/):** a polarity in which someone else often carries the untamed half before you claim it.

In [Mars sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-lilith-synastry/), one person's drive gives the other's untamed side room to move: an encouragement to take the less expected route together.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), the version worth noticing belongs to Lilith. Mean Lilith never turns back, so when it reaches 60° from your natal Mars it goes through in a single pass of a few weeks, without the second and third passes a retrograde planet makes. In those weeks the side door tends to be easier to find, and a refusal you'd been rehearsing finds its moment.

These are openings rather than events: weather, not a forecast, and a door that stays ajar for a while, to use or to leave.
