Pisces on the 12th house cusp returns the last sign of the zodiac to the last house, where it has always belonged. Your inner life tends to be wide and deep, and time alone is less a retreat from life than the place where you come back to yourself.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/) is solitude, rest, recovery, the care given quietly and the work done unseen; the old books called it the Bad Spirit ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). It is [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/)'s own house in the natural zodiac, and with the sign on its cusp, both of its natural rulers rule it in your chart too. Your private life is likely to be rich in images: dreams you remember, music that takes you somewhere else, a habit of soaking up the mood of a room and needing time alone to let it go again. Quiet, water, prayer, a long walk or a closed door can bring you back to yourself. You may give a great deal where nobody sees it, and find meaning in work done behind the scenes, among people who are being looked after.

Here the 12th keeps its natural rulers, Neptune and Jupiter, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows where your inner life opens into imagination or faith, and where it can blur into escape; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where retreat restores you and where compassion grows wide. In every London chart with this cusp the 8th opens in Sagittarius, often the 9th too, so Jupiter governs your intimacy as well as your solitude, and closeness and time alone tend to feed each other. The house each one occupies is where you go to dissolve and come back, as ruler of the 12th in the [1st](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Just 1 London birth in 20, and 1 New York birth in 17, has Pisces on the 12th cusp in Placidus, the house system I use, exactly as rare as on the 2nd, the cusp on the other side of the Ascendant. What makes these charts unusual is the width of the 12th: at London it spans about 60°, two whole signs, so in almost every London chart the whole of Aries lies inside it as well and [Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/) follows ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Mars then has a say in your private life too: you tend to start things alone and in silence, and to fight for others quietly. At New York the house is narrower, about 45° to 48°, and Aries rises in a little over 2 charts in 5. Capricorn always holds the Midheaven, with Aquarius on the 11th. The 12th can also hold Pisces without opening in it: in about 1 London chart in 30 its cusp falls in Aquarius and the whole of Pisces lies inside ([Aquarius on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-12th-house-cusp/)).

Tradition put confinement and hidden enemies in the 12th. I read neither into a chart, and needing a great deal of solitude says nothing about your health ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)). Minutes matter on this side of the chart: a little earlier and you would have [Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/), a little later [Pisces on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-11th-house-cusp/), and only the recorded time can tell ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
