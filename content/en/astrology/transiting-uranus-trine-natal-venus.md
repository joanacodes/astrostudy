Twice in a long life, some 28 years before or after Uranus crosses your Venus, the two form a trine. For some twenty months each time, love and freedom stop negotiating and start cooperating.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) holds attraction, preference and reciprocity, and the pleasure you take in what's beautiful. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) brings a taste for the unusual and the freedom to follow it. Under a [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, two functions run together without friction, so what you love and how freely you love it agree for a while: new tastes feel like yours at once, and the people close to you can change without it feeling like a loss.

There's a reason [Venus trine Uranus](/astrology/venus-trine-uranus/) is counted among the easier birth placements: love and freedom rarely get on this well. The transit brings that agreement for twenty months, whatever angle the two made at your birth.

It often shows in small, welcome changes: an arrangement at home that gives both people more room, a friendship that deepens because nobody has to pretend, a craft taken up for pleasure that becomes a real part of the week. Because nothing pushes, the ease is easy to waste.

Venus also keeps the accounts of a relationship, what's given and what's received, and under this trine those accounts are easier to talk about. Asking for more freedom, or offering it, tends to sound generous rather than threatening, which makes these months a good time to renegotiate the terms of your time together.

With Venus in your 3rd and Uranus crossing your 7th ([Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/)), a partner or a collaborator brings fresh air into your everyday pleasures: new places to go, new things to talk about, a lighter way of being together. Those two houses only exist once your birth time is known, and without it there's nothing here for me to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My orbs give this trine about twenty months, from 1½° before exact to 1° after; about one pass in ten is shorter, at eleven months or less. Three exact hits come in more than nine passes out of ten.

Uranus reaches the first trine about 28 years after crossing your Venus ([transiting Uranus conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-venus/)) and the second about 28 years before crossing it again. Between the two lies [transiting Uranus opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-venus/); nearer the crossing come [transiting Uranus sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-venus/) and [transiting Uranus square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-venus/). Both trines fit into most lives that reach 80, and every such life has at least one, which makes this one of the Uranus [transits](/astrology/transits/) to Venus that nearly everyone gets to use.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make or learn something beautiful. A class in drawing, dancing or music suits this trine better than shopping for new things.

Give room as well as asking for it. The trine makes it easier to let someone you love change, and that is often what keeps a bond fresh.

Say yes to the unlikely friendship. Under this trine, closeness with people quite unlike you comes more easily than usual.
