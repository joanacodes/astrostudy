Uranus trine Neptune belongs to births from the late 1930s to the mid-1940s, first with Uranus in Taurus and Neptune in Virgo, then with Uranus in Gemini and Neptune in Libra. A third of the circle lay between the two planets, and for a generation, invention and imagination ran in the same direction.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, autonomy and change in the way whole systems run. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination, compassion and the perception of what lies beyond the literal. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates them through flow: each reinforces the other, and neither loses its nature.

Lived, it's the sense that a new idea and a larger ideal belong together. Change arrives with a vision attached, and the vision finds its way into practical changes without much forcing. People with this trine often take it for granted that things can be done differently, and better, for others as well as for themselves.

Ease is not inevitability, though, and flow is not passivity. A trine can sit unused, a quiet confidence nobody asks to do anything; or an ideal can feel so natural that it's mistaken for one already reached. It gives most when it's handed real work. The two halves of the period colour it: in earth, the vision tends to take the shape of things built and kept; in air, of ideas, words and agreements between people.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 13 born between 1930 and 2009 has this trine within 6°: on and off from June 1936 to March 1946, without a break from March 1940 to June 1943. Before 1940 it came for a few months each year; after mid-1943, in three shorter stretches. Outside those months, no birth of the eighty years carries it.

In the count, this trine is where a long approach begins. Uranus, about twice as fast, was gaining on Neptune, and the gap went on closing through the square of the 1950s and the sextile of the 1960s to the meeting of the 1990s. Many people born from 1941 to 1946 also carry [Uranus sextile Pluto](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto/).

So widely shared, the trine becomes personal through the houses ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). Uranus in the 11th trine Neptune in the 3rd, for instance, could let new friendships and groups feed a way with words that moves people. Contacts with your Sun, Moon or angles turn it up, and the houses rest on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune/):** born from 1990 to 1996; the end of the approach, the two fused.
- **[Uranus sextile Neptune](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune/):** born from 1963 to 1970; a smaller opening between the two.
- **[Uranus square Neptune](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune/):** born from 1951 to 1958; the same two urges at cross purposes.
- **[Uranus opposite Neptune](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune/):** nobody in the eighty years of the count.

[Uranus trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune-synastry/) lets one person's originality and the other's imagination encourage each other, like a shared language for what might be possible. Each person brings a different half, so I read it from each side in turn.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-neptune/) comes twice in Uranus's 84-year round and lasts months, with Uranus often passing the point three times. [Transiting Neptune trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-uranus/) unfolds over years: Neptune spends about 14 years in a sign.

Born with the trine, you meet it again at the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/), at about 84, when transiting Uranus comes back to its birth place and so back into a trine with your Neptune. For the earliest births of this generation, that return falls in the 2020s. Weather, not a forecast: a long, mild season for looking again at what you once imagined.
