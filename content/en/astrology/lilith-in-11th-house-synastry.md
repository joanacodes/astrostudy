Some people never quite joined the group, and stopped minding long ago. With their Lilith in your 11th house, that outsider's ease comes to sit among your friends, your circles and your hopes.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is, for me, the part of a person that didn't fit what was expected and kept its shape anyway. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) covers the people you choose to stand with: friends, groups, teams, networks, causes, and the future you'd like to build with them. Read as a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their refusal to blend in lands in the middle of your sense of belonging.

With them, you may notice which of your circles you belong to out of habit. They tend to ask why you still go along with the group's opinions, bring you to gatherings where odd is ordinary, and make a hope you'd filed as unrealistic sound like a plan. Natal [Lilith in the 11th house](/astrology/lilith-in-11th-house/) is about finding your own kind; with this overlay, someone else's search for theirs runs through your friendships, and some people start something new together.

For them, your friendships can feel like a club that once kept them out, or the first group that ever let them in unaltered. A succedent house, the 11th keeps alive what the 10th achieved by sharing it; [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), its natural sign, has two rulers, Saturn in the old texts and Uranus in modern tropical astrology, and I read both. Tradition was fond of this house, naming it the Good Spirit and letting Jupiter rejoice there.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Belonging can tear. They may not get on with your friends, say so plainly, or embarrass you in a group whose good opinion you value, and you may feel you have to choose between them and your circle. A shared cause can turn into a contest over who is the truer rebel.

From their side, your group can look like the crowd that once mocked them, and your loyalty to it like a demand that they conform. They may keep one foot out of any circle you invite them into.

No overlay makes or breaks a friendship; this one shows how two people's need to belong, and to stay themselves, meet.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the bond between the two of you stand on its own before you fold it into a crowd, and keep a circle each. In a couple, decide which gatherings you go to together and which alone; between friends, a cause you both care about can carry the friendship further than a social calendar; colleagues in a team can make use of the one who questions the consensus; adult relatives can stop measuring each other by the family's friends.

Swap the charts and it is your Lilith in their 11th house, your outsider's ease among their circles. Next door, [their Lilith in your 10th house](/astrology/lilith-in-10th-house-synastry/) stands in your public life; [their Lilith in your 12th house](/astrology/lilith-in-12th-house-synastry/) reaches what you keep out of sight.

Mean Lilith, the one I read on both sides ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)), spends about nine months in a sign, so friends from the same school year often share its sign, and a classmate whose Lilith falls in your 11th usually has it within a sign or so of your own. Which of your houses that is depends on your birth time, in Placidus, the house system I use, and the pair waits until I have both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A friendship gets the same courtesy as any other bond: both people ask first ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
