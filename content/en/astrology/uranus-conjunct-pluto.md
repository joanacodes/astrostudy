Uranus conjunct Pluto is a meeting that comes round only every century and more. The two planets last met in the 1960s, in Virgo, and most people born between 1963 and 1968 carry it. On its own it describes a generation more than a person; through your houses, it becomes yours.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part of a chart that differentiates and frees: it questions the way things have always been done, experiments, and reforms what has grown too tight. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the part that transforms: it lets go of what has outlived its use, regenerates, and hands back a deeper kind of strength. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two work as one while each keeps its identity: the urge to break a pattern and the urge to renew it at the root arrive together.

In plain words, Uranus on its own wants something different; Pluto on its own wants something renewed. Met in one place, they want both at once: change that goes all the way down, and renewal that refuses to be a copy of the old. That's intense, and it isn't destruction. I read it as the capacity to rebuild something from the inside, and as an impatience with fixes that only change the surface.

The meeting took place in [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), the sign of sorting, improving and being useful, so a generation was born with [Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/uranus-in-virgo/) and [Pluto in Virgo](/astrology/pluto-in-virgo/) side by side. As a shared theme, I read it as wanting to change, and renew, the way everyday things get done: the methods, tools and routines that hold daily life together. A generation's theme isn't anyone's portrait, though. What it does in your life depends on where it lands in your chart.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 17 born between 1930 and 2009 has this conjunction, within 6°. That figure hides how it works. It isn't scattered across the years like an aspect of the fast planets: everyone born between August 1963 and December 1967 has it, and again between March and August 1968, when Uranus's retrograde brought the two back within range for a few months. Nobody else born in those eighty years does. The meeting was exact three times, once in 1965 and twice in 1966; the two had last met around 1850, and won't meet again until after 2100.

Five years of births sharing one aspect is what makes Uranus conjunct Pluto a generation's aspect more than a personal one ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). The sign and the aspect belong to everyone born in those years; what's yours is the house the pair falls in, and whether it touches your Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars or an angle. In the 4th house it might ask for a different kind of home from the one you grew up in; in the 10th, for work that changes the way a job is done; on the Ascendant, for a way of meeting the world that's entirely your own. Houses and angles need a birth time, which is why, for this conjunction, a chart without the time says almost nothing personal ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The orb still matters. Within 3° or so, as for births from the autumn of 1964 to the autumn of 1966, the meeting is loud; at 5° or 6°, at either end of the window, it's quieter. Uranus is the faster of the two, about 84 years to go round the zodiac against Pluto's 248, so it does the overtaking. Born before the first exact meeting, in October 1965, you have Uranus still catching up with Pluto; born after the last, in the summer of 1966, you have it pulling away; in between, the two crossed back and forth ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)).

Uranus and Pluto take generations to change aspect, so each of their other aspects belongs to the people born in a few given years:

- **[Uranus sextile Pluto](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto/):** born in the 1940s or the 1990s; change and renewal cooperate, as long as someone takes the opening.
- **[Uranus square Pluto](/astrology/uranus-square-pluto/):** born in the early 1930s, and again between 2009 and 2018; the two urges push against each other, a friction that keeps asking for reform.
- **[Uranus trine Pluto](/astrology/uranus-trine-pluto/):** nobody born between 1930 and 2009 has it; the two urges flow together for those born around 1920 or in the later 2020s.
- **[Uranus opposite Pluto](/astrology/uranus-opposite-pluto/):** nobody born between 1930 and 2009 either; a polarity between the two urges, for those born around 1900 or in the later 2040s.

Between two charts, [Uranus conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto-synastry/) sets one person's urge to break the pattern on the other's urge to renew it. Each feels the other's push differently, so I read it in both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In transits, the same meeting happens when one of the two, moving in today's sky, reaches the other's place in your chart. [Transiting Uranus conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-pluto/) is the quicker of the two: Uranus crosses a sign in about seven years, so it spends a year and a half or so within a degree of your Pluto, usually in three passes as it turns retrograde. [Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-uranus/) is slower still: Pluto takes 12 to 31 years to cross a sign, and stays near your Uranus for about two years, often longer. When either comes depends on your birth year: Uranus reaches natal Pluto at very different ages from one generation to the next, and Pluto, far slower, reaches natal Uranus only for some generations.

Born with the conjunction, you meet the two transits differently. Pluto, already beside your Uranus at birth, leaves the pair behind in your first years and doesn't come back: it needs about 248 years to go round. Uranus does come back, at about 84, as it completes its own round, so for this generation the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/) is also a return to the meeting point. On the way, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) at about 42 was a transit to both planets at once.

Weather, not a forecast: these transits describe a long season in which the urge to change and the urge to renew press on the same part of your life. They don't say what will change.
