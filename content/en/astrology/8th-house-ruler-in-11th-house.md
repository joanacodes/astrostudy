Friendship, for you, is rarely casual. The 8th's ruler has settled among friends, groups and hopes, in the 11th, and the people you call friends tend to be the ones who've seen you through something.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Friends, groups, causes and the hopes you share with them fill the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/); the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) holds what's shared, owed and trusted, and the closeness that changes people. Depth, with this ruler, spreads across a circle ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Friendships here go deep or not at all. You tend to keep a small number of people very close, the ones who stay when things are hard, and they can usually count on you in return. Friends may also be the people you share the 8th's heavier business with: the one who co-signs a lease, the one who knows where the papers are. Groups can be places of real trust as well: a support group, a mutual aid network, a team that has come through a crisis together. Resources are often pooled, a cooperative, a crowdfunded project, a shared house, a cause you fund together, and you may end up keeping the group's accounts or its confidences. The work is to let some friendships be light, and to keep shared money in a group as clearly agreed as any other.

Round the wheel, the 11th stands at a square to the 8th, a working tension: friendship asks for lightness, the 8th for depth, and your friendships tend to end up unusually honest as a result.

Jupiter, ruling a Sagittarius 8th, is at ease here, in the house of its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/): generous with friends, trusting of groups, sometimes too quick to underwrite a shared plan. When Pisces holds the 8th cusp, Jupiter shares the rule with Neptune ([Pisces on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/)); I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and Neptune's house shows where the boundaries between your resources and the group's can blur. Venus, ruling a Taurus 8th, makes you the friend who shares comfort and keeps loyalties; the Moon, ruling a Cancer 8th, makes a group feel like family.

Hopes, the 11th's other subject, are shaped by what you've been through. A turning point often changes what you want from the future, and the friends who stayed through it tend to be part of the new plan.

No chart says whether a shared venture will pay, or what friends will give or owe ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)); I read how trust and sharing work in your circle.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 11th sits between the Midheaven and the 12th, and its cusps follow the birth time. Earlier, the ruler would be in the 12th, where these matters are lived out of sight ([Ruler of the 8th in the 12th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)); later, in the 10th, where they become your work ([Ruler of the 8th in the 10th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)). I place it only from a known time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Your friends are likely to be few, close and tested. That's a wealth worth having, and not one anyone can count.
