Both lights are in earth, and the Moon sits in the sign tradition calls its detriment: a pair that feels safest when the plan is sound and the work is done. The Moon was growing past its first quarter at your birth. Method and responsibility in easy agreement is how I read the pair, sometimes so easy that feelings wait their turn too long.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Virgo is mutable earth and Capricorn cardinal earth, four signs apart: a trine by sign, two [earth signs](/astrology/earth-signs/) agreeing about what is real and what lasts. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) wants to get things exactly right; the [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) steadies itself through structure and responsibility, and lets feelings in once the situation is secure. The Sun refines and the Moon organises, so between them plans tend to turn into working systems.

Tradition puts the Moon in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Capricorn, opposite Cancer, its own sign. I read that as needs handled by taking charge rather than by asking, which is not the same as having fewer of them. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules Capricorn, and I lean on its placement here more than on most, because in my own testing it was one of [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) far more often than the rest. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler and exaltation of Virgo, completes the picture from Leo, Virgo or Libra, always close to the Sun.

Your birth came after the first quarter, with the Moon already up in the afternoon sky, 91° to 149° east of the Sun, 7½ to 12 days on from the new moon. Astrologers who follow Rudhyar count about seven of these births in eight as [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) births and the rest as [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) ones, and near 120° the lights form a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). I read the first quarter as friction that pushes into action, which an earth pair tends to turn into building; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains why the sky and the astrologers name this stretch differently.

About 1 person in 139 shares these lights. If the Moon came over from Sagittarius or went on into Aquarius on your birthday, only the hour can say whether this page is yours, or the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-sagittarius/), or the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-aquarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The house Saturn occupies, the part of the chart I read most carefully with this Moon, is set by the same hour, as is the rising sign.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love dependably: the lift home, the bill paid, the promise kept to the letter. The Capricorn Moon shows care through reliability and the Virgo Sun through attention, so partners rarely doubt your commitment, though they may sometimes wonder what you feel. Letting someone see you tired, or unsure, is the harder gift. Your jokes may carry the tender things; say a few of them straight as well.

## At work {#at-work}

Responsibility comes to you early and tends to stay. The Sun brings precision, the Moon a long view and the patience to build, and together they make you good at running things that must not fail, from a household budget to a hospital rota to a building schedule. Its price is that work may turn into the only place feelings are allowed, and then only in the form of more work.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Earth and earth, method and duty, with very little friction between them: the pair can build a life that works and forget to enjoy it. Feelings are scheduled for later, and later keeps moving. Self-reliance can harden into refusing help that is warmly offered. What helps is putting rest and pleasure in the diary as firmly as deadlines, letting a job done differently stay done, and celebrating the finished thing out loud, which a Capricorn Moon rarely does unprompted.
