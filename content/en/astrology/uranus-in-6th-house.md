A timetable someone else wrote rarely survives contact with a 6th-house Uranus. The house of daily work and habits, with the planet of reform in it, describes days that run on your own method, and a knack for fixing the way things are done.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the shape of an ordinary day: the work you do, the routines that hold you up, the people you work alongside and the care you give your body. It's cadent, its natural sign is Virgo, ruled by Mercury, and the Hellenistic astrologers called it Bad Fortune and placed Mars's joy there. With [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) here, reform and experiment work on method: you improve procedures, change tools, and need some say in how and when your work gets done.

Tradition gave this house illness. No chart can predict your health, and I don't read one for it ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). In an adult's life I read the 6th as daily work and the habits you live by, and Uranus here as a need for flexible routines you've chosen yourself.

In Placidus, the house system I use, a 6th-house Uranus had set in the west not long before you were born. The house turns with the Earth, so your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) places it, while the sign belongs to your generation. Before it comes [Uranus in the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-in-5th-house/), experiment in play; after it, [Uranus in the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-in-7th-house/), experiment in partnership. Opposite lies the 12th, the house of what's done out of sight, which is often where a 6th-house Uranus goes to recover from too much structure.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In a shared life, this placement shows up in the daily arrangements: who does what, when, and how. You may need a partner who doesn't mind unusual hours or a household run on your own system, and who'll renegotiate chores without a fight. Small acts of practical help are often how you show love. Friction tends to come from imposed routines rather than from the big questions, so agree on the everyday early, and leave room to change it.

## At work {#at-work}

Work is a 6th-house Uranus's home ground: flexible hours, freelance or remote work, roles in technology, process improvement or research, any job where you're trusted to change how things are done. You may notice the flaw in a workflow and fix it before anyone asks. Micromanagement is the fastest way to lose your goodwill; colleagues who share information and let you experiment get your best. You often do well as the person who brings a new tool into a team and teaches the others to use it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard part is routines that never stick. You may start every new system with enthusiasm and drop it within weeks, or resist any schedule, even one that would help, because someone else set it. Restlessness in daily work can make a good job feel like a cage, and too many changes at once can leave colleagues tired of your improvements. Reform is not destruction: the goal is a better routine, not no routine at all.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Design your own week, with variety built in, and keep the few habits that genuinely support you. Change one procedure at a time and see whether it works before changing the next. Look at Mercury, natural ruler of this house, for how you organise your thinking about work; [Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/uranus-in-virgo/), the generation born from 1961 to 1969, carries the same theme by sign. My [notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) gave Uranus's house its tick or cross like any placement's; Uranus was not among the three that held far more often, and in all, roughly a third held.
