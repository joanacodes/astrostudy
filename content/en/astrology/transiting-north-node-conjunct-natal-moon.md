Of everything the North Node can cross in a chart, the Moon is the one it comes from: the node is where the Moon's own path cuts the Sun's, heading north. When that crossing point slides back to the degree your Moon held at birth, the Moon's present path meets the Sun's on that very degree, and for a month or so your needs stand on the line of growth.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) keeps you regulated: what you need to feel safe, the habits that settle you, the way you respond when things change. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) describes a capacity still being learned, one that improves each time it is used. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) growing and looking after yourself become one task.

In practice it tends to show as a need you've had little practice in meeting: rest taken before you've earned it, help asked for, a feeling said aloud before it has been tidied into a plan. These can feel awkward, and awkward isn't wrong; the node points at what is less familiar, not at what is better. Your oldest ways of soothing yourself sit at the other end, under the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), which now holds the degree opposite your Moon. Keep them; what this month questions is only their monopoly.

In those weeks, when the Moon in the sky comes back to your Moon's degree, as it does every 27 days or so, it arrives just as it crosses the Sun's path heading north, so a [lunar return](/astrology/lunar-return/) drawn then puts the Moon on the North Node.

With your [Moon in the 4th house](/astrology/moon-in-4th-house/), the conjunction falls during the node's stay there ([North Node transiting the 4th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-4th-house/)), and the new kind of care is asked for at home, among your own people. That house comes from your birth time, and so does the Moon's degree: the Moon covers about 13° in a day, which the node takes some eight months to cross, so without the time I couldn't place this month in its year ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The orbs I use run from 1½° before exact to 1° after. Within them, the true node usually spends about a month on your Moon and crosses it once. Its pace follows the Sun: the node all but stands still in the middle of each eclipse season, when the Sun lines up with it, and runs backwards fastest in between. So one pass in ten is over in about three and a half weeks, one in ten lasts three and a half months or more, and about one in twenty-five brings three exact hits over some three and a half months. Most of the long passes are ones an eclipse season catches. In June 2029, for instance, the node stands almost still near 5° Capricorn while the total lunar eclipse of 26 June darkens the Moon at 4°49′ Capricorn: a Moon born at that degree has both at once ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Once in each round of the nodes, about every 18.6 years, it comes back: four or five visits by 80, with no exceptions. In between, the node sextiles your Moon about three years later ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-moon/)), squares it at about four and a half ([Transiting North Node square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-moon/)) and trines it at about six ([Transiting North Node trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-moon/)); at about nine, the South Node reaches your Moon ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-moon/)). Born with [Moon conjunct North Node](/astrology/moon-conjunct-north-node/), you have this transit whenever the node completes a lap and stands again where it stood at your birth.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name one need you keep putting off, and meet it once this month on purpose, without waiting to deserve it.

Ask for the kind of support you usually give. Being looked after is a skill as well, and this is a month for practising it.

Keep the old comforts within reach, and notice which ones you reach for without choosing.

Before a decision, write down what you feel about it, then what you think. The order is the practice.
