Venus trine Uranus lets your affections and your appetite for the new get along without arguing. Change, in love and in taste, tends to feel like fresh air rather than a storm, and freedom is something you give as easily as you take.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) values: attraction, preference, the balance of a bond. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) renews: it frees, tries things out and renews what has gone stale. Joined by a [trine](/astrology/trine/), they work through flow: affection gets lighter and originality warmer, with neither taking the other's place.

In plain words, you can be yourself in love without a fight about it. Friends can become something more, or partners stay friends; tastes change as you do, and the people around you mostly enjoy the change. You give others room without feeling abandoned, and take room without making anyone feel left behind. In art and style, it often shows as a light, original touch: the unexpected detail that works.

The ease has a blind spot. Because freedom comes so naturally to this trine, you may not notice when someone close needs more steadiness than you do. Its lightness serves you best used deliberately, including the choice to stay.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Trines tend to link signs of one element, and since Uranus moves so slowly, the element comes from your generation's Uranus sign while the trine itself comes from your birth date. In air, love tends to speak the language of friendship, ideas and freedom; in earth, originality turns practical, with new ways of making and keeping beautiful things; in fire, love likes adventure; in water, intuition leads to unusual kinds of closeness. The houses show where the ease gets used: Venus in the 6th trine Uranus in the 10th might let a pleasant daily routine and an unconventional career feed each other.

About 1 person in 16 is born with Venus and Uranus within 6° of a trine. The closer it is, the more others notice the lightness; nearer 6°, it's a quiet readiness for something new. Uranus was checked like every placement in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), and it wasn't among the three that held far more often; with a generational planet I lean on its aspects to the personal ones, and this trine is one of those.

Compared with the pair's other angles:

- **[Venus conjunct Uranus](/astrology/venus-conjunct-uranus/):** originality and attraction as one; love that likes surprise and needs air.
- **[Venus sextile Uranus](/astrology/venus-sextile-uranus/):** an opening for the new in love and friendship, which waits for your yes.
- **[Venus square Uranus](/astrology/venus-square-uranus/):** attachment and novelty in friction; bonds that learn flexibility the hard way.
- **[Venus opposite Uranus](/astrology/venus-opposite-uranus/):** the two across the chart; closeness here, independence there, and a life built for both.

Between two charts, [Venus trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-uranus-synastry/) has one person's affection relaxing alongside the other's originality: a friendship-like bond that keeps both people interested. Who feels freer depends on whose Uranus it is, so I look at it from each chart.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), some 28 years earlier or later than Uranus's pass over your Venus, comes [Transiting Uranus trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-venus/), lasting about a year: a year when your tastes and bonds can renew themselves without fuss. Born with the trine, you'll find that Uranus reaches your Venus at the two ages when it trines its own birth place, around 28 and 56: once by trine, once by conjunction.

Weather, not a forecast: a fair wind for change in love and taste, with no destination attached.
