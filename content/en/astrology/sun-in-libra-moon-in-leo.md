Your Moon lives in the Sun's own sign and your Sun in the sign of its fall, so the warmth and pride tradition gives the Sun at home have passed to your feelings. I read a self that shares the light around, fairly, and a heart that needs someone to notice who was holding the lamp.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Leo is two signs behind Libra, a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) by sign, and air and fire suit each other: ideas give warmth somewhere to go. Cardinal Libra starts things for the sake of a fair outcome; fixed Leo keeps its warmth going, steadily, for the people it loves. The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) aims for proportion, everyone given a fair share of attention; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) needs a share of its own, to be seen and appreciated by the people who matter. The two agree more often than they clash, as long as you count yourself among the people who get a turn.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo, so your Moon takes its lead from your Sun, and your Sun is in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Libra, under [Venus](/astrology/venus/). I read that chain gently: the part of you that needs recognition follows a self that would rather consult than shine alone. By the old rules, Leo neither strengthens nor weakens the Moon. The Sun's house says a great deal about where this Moon looks for warmth, and Venus, by sign and house, shows what the whole pair finds beautiful enough to work for.

The Moon was 271° to 330° ahead of the Sun, which is 30° to 89° behind it: the lunar month was 22 to 27 days old, and the Moon a waning half or crescent in the morning sky. The [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) holds the bulk of these births, and the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) the last few, about an eighth. Near 300° the lights form a waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). I think of the last quarter as the month's editing phase, and with a Leo Moon the edit often concerns what you'll go on performing and what you'll keep for the few. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sets out the eight phases in order.

The Moon in the Sun's own sign, the Sun in its fall: about 1 person in 143 is born this way. Births on the day the Moon left Cancer, or the day it moved on into Virgo, may belong to the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-cancer/) or the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-virgo/) instead, and the clock decides ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It decides the rising sign as well. A Leo Moon wants to be seen, and the rising sign shows how you are seen first, so I wouldn't describe this pair without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You're a generous, attentive partner: the Libra Sun keeps the relationship fair and pleasant, the Leo Moon brings warmth, loyalty and a sense of occasion. What the Moon needs back is to be chosen, and told so. You may hold back from asking, because the Sun feels it's unfair to want more than your share of attention. Asking isn't unfair. A partner who says, specifically, what they love about you gives this Moon its light, and leaves the Sun free to share the rest.

## At work {#at-work}

You're often the one who makes a team work: giving credit fairly, bringing out the best in people, making each client feel like the only one. Creative or public work suits the Leo Moon, especially when your name is on it; collaborative work suits the Libra Sun. The difficulty comes when your contribution disappears into the group's success and nobody notices. Keep a note of what you did, and make sure someone who matters sees it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

You may share the spotlight so fairly that your own need for it goes unmet, and then feel a hurt you think you have no right to. Pride can stop you saying so, and the Libra Sun's courtesy can keep you smiling through it. Your waning Moon knows how to review and let go, so use it: once a season, ask what you still do for applause and what you do for love, keep the second, and tell one person plainly when you need to hear that you did well.
