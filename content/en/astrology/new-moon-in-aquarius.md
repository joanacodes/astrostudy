From 2026 to 2028, the new moon in Aquarius is an annular eclipse three years running: the Moon passes in front of the Sun but is too small to cover it, and a ring of light stays round its edge. In any year, it starts a lunar month in the sign of friends, groups and the future.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) begins each lunar month with the Sun and Moon on one degree. In [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, the beginning is an idea more than an impulse: a better way of doing something, a group you'd like to belong to, a plan that includes more people than you. Aquarius has two rulers, [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in modern tropical astrology and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in the tradition, and I read both: Uranus's house shows where you want to change the system, Saturn's where you'll have to build the new one. Tradition puts the Sun in its detriment here, opposite its home in Leo. I read that as a self that shines through the group rather than in the spotlight, which suits a month that starts with other people.

An ordinary Aquarius new moon is a marker of a few days. It counts in your chart when a planet or angle of yours lies within about a degree of it, and then only as a trigger for what a transit, a progression or a solar arc already has in hand. As weather, it's a month's tone in one house, not a forecast.

The eclipses are another matter. Three of these new moons are annular [solar eclipses](/astrology/solar-eclipses/): 17 February 2026 at 28°50′, 6 February 2027 at 15:56 UT, at 17°38′, and 26 January 2028 at 6°11′. The same years bring solar eclipses in Leo, at the other end of the axis, on 12 August 2026 and 2 August 2027 ([eclipse in Aquarius](/astrology/eclipse-in-aquarius/)). An eclipse carries more weight: its orb widens to about 3°, and it counts at the Leo end of the axis as well as the Aquarius one. I read it through the natal point it meets and that point's house. It marks emphasis, never an omen. After the series, the new moons of 13 February 2029 (25°00′) and 2 February 2030 (13°51′) are ordinary ones.

With the Sun in Aquarius the same month, the [full moon in Leo](/astrology/full-moon-in-leo/) falls two weeks before or after: in 2027, on 22 January, two weeks ahead of the eclipse. It follows the [new moon in Capricorn](/astrology/new-moon-in-capricorn/) by a month, and the [new moon in Pisces](/astrology/new-moon-in-pisces/) follows it a month later.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Houses depend on the time you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and mine are Placidus. Usually the new moon falls in the house with Aquarius on its cusp. The eclipse series walks backwards through the sign, about eleven degrees a year, from 28°50′ to 6°11′, so with a cusp in mid-Aquarius it can begin in one house and end in the one before.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Aquarius rising: a new start in how you present yourself, more yourself and less what's expected, the odd one out by choice.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** money your own way: an unconventional way of earning, values that put people before possessions.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** new ideas in circulation: a newsletter, an online course, talk with neighbours that turns into a local project.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** home rethought: a shared house, a family arrangement that breaks with habit, more independence under the same roof.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** pleasure among friends: a creative collaboration, an unusual hobby, a love that leaves both people room.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** the working day reorganised: new tools, a flexible routine, a team that runs better because everyone has a say.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** a partnership on equal and unusual terms: a friend who becomes a partner, an agreement that leaves you both free.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** shared money and trust reconsidered: a pooled fund for a cause, an intimacy that respects independence.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** ideas that change your view of the world: a new field of study, science, a belief brought up to date.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** a public role as a reformer: change at work, or a career built around a cause.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** Aquarius's own house: a new circle of friends, a network or movement you join, a hope you start planning for.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** time away from the crowd to hear your own thoughts, help given behind the scenes, an idea that grows in private.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **For the eclipses, widen the net.** A planet or angle within about 3° of 28°50′, 17°38′ or 6°11′, in Aquarius or in Leo, meets one of them. Ask what else is active at that point; the eclipse emphasises, it doesn't decide.
- **For 2029 and 2030, keep to a degree.** Those are ordinary new moons.
- **Read both rulers together.** Uranus shows where you want things to change, Saturn what it will take to make the change last.
- **Begin in company.** Aquarius starts best with other people, on terms that leave everyone free.
