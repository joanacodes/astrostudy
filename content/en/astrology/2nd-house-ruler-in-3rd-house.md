Here, what you earn and what you value travel by word of mouth. The ruler of the 2nd sits in the 3rd house, among talk, errands, lessons and the people close by, and your resources tend to move rather than sit still.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

From the [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/), the planet ruling the sign on your [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) cusp ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)) ties money, belongings, the skills you could live from and your sense of worth to the traffic of daily life: messages, conversations, reading, short trips, first lessons, and the siblings, cousins and neighbours you grew up alongside.

The skills of your everyday mind tend to be the ones you live from or lean on: explaining, writing, selling, teaching, translating, driving, knowing whom to ask. Money comes and goes in many small exchanges rather than one large one, and you're often good at the paperwork of it, the invoices, the bills, the small agreements of a week, which horary astrology also gave to the 3rd. What you value is shaped by what you learn, and you spend readily on books, courses and the means of getting about. The thing to watch is depth: a busy flow of small sums and small ideas can hide what you'd really like to build.

The planet sets the tone. With Cancer on the 2nd cusp, the Moon rules your money from the 3rd, the house of its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): moods and money move together, and so do conversation and comfort. With Gemini or Virgo there, [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) handles your money from Gemini's own house in the [natural zodiac](/astrology/natural-zodiac/), and trade, in every sense, comes easily. With Scorpio on the cusp, Mars and Pluto share the house, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 3rd negotiates fast and sells hard, while Pluto, from its own house, wants to know what lies under the price.

The 3rd also holds brothers and sisters, cousins and neighbours, and with your 2nd house ruler there, money and belongings can run through those ties: shared costs, things lent and given back, a family business, habits about money picked up at the kitchen table. That describes your side of those exchanges and what you value in them, never your siblings' finances.

When you were born, the planet that governs your money had already passed beneath your feet, the lowest point of the sky, and was heading back towards the east; that's the 3rd in Placidus, the house system I use. The cusps either side move with the clock ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): a little further from that lowest point, back in the 2nd, the ruler would stay in the house it rules ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)); past it, in the 4th, money would tie itself to home, family and property ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 4th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)).

What your words or your trade will earn is not written here. The placement shows where your money and your sense of worth go to work: into exchange, learning and the life of the neighbourhood.
