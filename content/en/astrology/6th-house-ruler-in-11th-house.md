With the 6th's ruler in the 11th, the working day is a team effort. Colleagues turn into friends, groups need organising, and service often goes to a cause you share with others.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your friends, your circles and networks, the future you're planning together: that is the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/). Put the ruler of your [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) cusp there ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)) and the ordinary working day stops being a solo affair: chores, schedules and routines happen with a group, and often for one.

I read it as work that belongs to a collective: the association you help run, the union branch, the open-source project, the volunteer rota, the team that becomes a second circle of friends. Perhaps you hold a group's practical side together: the minutes, the bookings, the spreadsheet everyone relies on. The risk is carrying a group's chores unthanked, or finding that your friendships live only at work and fade when the job changes.

The old names make a pointed pair here: the 6th was Bad Fortune, the 11th Good Spirit, home of friends and benefactors. When Sagittarius or Pisces opens the 6th, the ruler is Jupiter, which finds its joy in the 11th ([Jupiter in the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house/); [planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): help offered freely, and a talent for making a group feel looked after. By sign, though, the two houses are in aversion, five signs apart ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)), and I read that as a group that doesn't always see the daily effort behind it. Name your work when it matters.

The 11th is also the house of hopes, so daily work tends to attach to a future you share with others: the campaign that runs for years, the community garden, the cooperative you're building together. Routine feels lighter when you can see where the group is heading.

If the cusp is Pisces rather than Sagittarius, Neptune shares the rule with Jupiter ([Pisces on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/); [traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and both are part of my reading: Neptune, from its own house, pulls the work towards charities, causes and people who need help. A Gemini or Virgo 6th gives the job to Mercury, which keeps a network informed; a Capricorn 6th gives it to Saturn, the one who makes the committee meet on time.

When you were born, this ruler stood partway up the eastern sky. If it sat close to the 11th's upper cusp, a later birth time would have carried it into the 10th, where the daily round turns into a reputation ([Ruler of the 6th in the 10th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)); close to the 12th cusp, an earlier one would have kept it in the 12th, where the work happens out of view ([Ruler of the 6th in the 12th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)). The recorded time is what tells me ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A group can ask a great deal of whoever keeps it going. If volunteering or team work starts to wear you out, say so to a doctor or a therapist ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)), and let the group share the load. No chart, this one included, can tell you whether you'll fall ill, whatever the 6th's old reputation ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
