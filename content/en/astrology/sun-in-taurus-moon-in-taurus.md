Here the Sun and the Moon share Taurus: what you're building and what comforts you are made of the same slow, solid material. You came into the world close to a new moon, and I read the pair as one deep root rather than two separate stems.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Tradition treats the two lights unequally in this sign. Taurus is the Moon's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), where it is most at ease, while the Sun has no dignity there at all: needs honoured by their sign, and a purpose that has to find its own footing. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules both. Venus can't wander far from the Sun, 47° at the very most, so the ruler of both your lights is always close to them and sometimes in their very sign; I read its sign and house before almost anything else.

Same element, same modality: fixed earth on both sides, so there's no inner debate about pace. The [Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/) grows by tending what it already has; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) settles through the senses, rituals and what is familiar. In practice, what you're working towards and what calms you down tend to be the same things: a home kept up, a craft improved, people you've known for years.

No more than 29° separated the two lights, so the new moon was at most two and a half days away, past or still to come. The eight phases split these births almost exactly in two, a fraction more in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase and the rest in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) that closes the previous month. Close to exact, the lights make a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/), and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains what changes on either side of the new moon.

About 1 person in 144 is a double Taurus in this sense. The Moon stays in Taurus for about as long as a long weekend, two and a half days, so on the day it came in from Aries or went on to Gemini, the birth time settles whether you belong here or with the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-aries/) or the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-gemini/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign, also a matter of the time, may bring a very different note to all this earth, one more reason a double Taurus is never the whole chart.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love steadily and expect the same. Affection, for a double Taurus, is shown in the body and over time: meals, touch, a drawer kept for someone's things, years of turning up. Trust grows slowly and then holds very firmly, and a partner who keeps small promises will matter more to you than one who makes big ones. What can be hard is change inside the bond: a partner who wants to move house or rethink the routine may meet a resistance you hardly notice in yourself. Warning helps. Ask to hear about a change well before it happens, and give the same courtesy back.

## At work {#at-work}

Patience is your great asset at work. You can stay with a task long after others have tired of it, refine it until it's right and build something that lasts: a business, a body of work, a reputation for being reliable. You do best at your own tempo, with results you can see and touch. Sudden reorganisations cost you a great deal, and work that never shows a result can slowly drain both lights at once.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in fixed earth, nothing in the pair pushes for change. That gives you a steadiness others lean on, and it can also keep you in a job, a house or a habit long after it stopped serving you, because staying put feels like being yourself twice over. Irritation tends to build slowly and silently, then becomes very hard to shift. Movement usually has to come from elsewhere: a faster planet or the rising sign, a friend who loves novelty, and your own deliberate habit of asking, every so often, whether what you're keeping still deserves its place.
