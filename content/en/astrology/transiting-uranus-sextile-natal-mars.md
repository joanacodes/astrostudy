Uranus makes two sextiles to your Mars in its 84-year round, each about fourteen years from the conjunction. Each time, for about twenty months, doing something new and doing something at all stop feeling like two separate efforts.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is agency: initiative, effort, execution, the courage to begin. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) brings invention and independence. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, lets two functions cooperate as soon as one of them moves, and here the one that has to move is you.

It tends to look practical: a better way to do your job, a project you finally run yourself, a physical skill learned from scratch, a team that works differently and suits you. Effort feels less like pushing and more like steering. Mars tends to win by sheer effort; Uranus suggests a cleverer route. Under this sextile the cleverer route is usually there to be found, and taking it leaves energy for something else.

Because the sextile only opens the door, plans matter. Twenty months is long enough to start, fail once and start again better.

With Mars in your 10th and Uranus crossing your 12th ([Uranus transiting the 12th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-12th-house/)), work done quietly, out of sight, gives your public effort a new method: research, training, a project developed privately before it's shown. Only the birth time puts Mars in the 10th and Uranus in the 12th, so without it this reading doesn't begin ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Two natal aspects change the timetable. With [Mars trine Uranus](/astrology/mars-trine-uranus/) in your birth chart, one of these sextiles arrives with your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), around 40; with [Mars sextile Uranus](/astrology/mars-sextile-uranus/), one was already there when you were born.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each sextile lasts about twenty months within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and one pass in ten or so is eleven months or shorter. More than nine in ten bring three exact hits, so there's time to start, pause and start again.

The two sextiles bracket [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-mars/), fourteen years or so either side. Beyond them, further round, lie [transiting Uranus square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-mars/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-mars/), twice each, and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-mars/), once. Eighty years is enough for both sextiles in most lives, and for one in all of them, which makes this [transit](/astrology/transits/) a season almost everyone can plan for.

The sky of the next few years gives it to Mars in Aries, the sign Mars rules, and in Leo: Uranus is in Gemini until 2033, sextile both. Mars changes sign every six or seven weeks, so two people born in the same season can fall on different sides of that line.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take the first step this month. Under a sextile, the earliest move is the one that sets the rest going.

Team up with people who work differently. A collaborator with methods you don't have takes this transit further than effort alone.

Learn a new physical skill. Mars drives the body as well as the will, and learning a movement from scratch is one of the most direct ways to use this opening.
