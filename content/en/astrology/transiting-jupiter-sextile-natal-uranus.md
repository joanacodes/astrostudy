An easy angle between today's Jupiter and your birth Uranus opens twice in every Jupiter lap. For a few days, trying something new costs less than usual, and the people who could help you try it are easier to find.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) wants room to do things differently: new methods, independent choices, reforms of systems that have stopped serving you. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is how you learn, and what you find worth learning. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) joins them through cooperation, which makes it the gentlest of the five contacts between them: nothing breaks, nothing is forced, and change arrives as a door you're free to walk through.

Uranus thinks in networks and systems as much as in individuals, so the opening often comes through other people: a group with the skill you lack, a new circle that does things another way, a colleague who has already tried the experiment you're considering. It can also be as simple as the week you finally learn the tool you've been avoiding. Of the five contacts, this is the easiest one for picking up a skill: Jupiter brings the curiosity, Uranus the wish to do it your own way, and nothing in the angle pushes back.

The sextile relies on engagement: of all [transits](/astrology/transits/), it's the one whose weeks most often pass without anything changing, and that's the only way to waste it. To know which part of life the opening touches, I need your Uranus's house, and so your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Anyone born with [Jupiter sextile Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-uranus/) has this cooperation in their chart already, and the sky repeats it near each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Inside orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after, Jupiter needs about eleven days to get past a sextile to your Uranus. Seven sextiles in ten are single passes like that; the other three loop, with three exact hits over some eight months. Two per lap gives thirteen or fourteen before 80 for births from 1930 to 2009.

The first came between a few weeks and about eight years after birth: at about 1.5 for 1 January 1965, at about 6.4 for 1 January 1945. Born between December 1988 and March 1995, with Uranus in Capricorn, you meet the next two while Jupiter crosses Scorpio, from September 2029 to October 2030, and Pisces, from April 2033 to April 2034.

Where [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-uranus/) starts a change and [transiting Jupiter square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-uranus/) makes it prove itself, the sextile simply makes trying easy. [Transiting Jupiter trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-uranus/) does the same with even less effort, and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-uranus/) brings other people's plans into it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Run one experiment with an end date. A new way of working for two weeks, a different route through a familiar task: something you can measure and drop if it fails.

Go where people already do it differently. A meeting, a class, an online group: Uranus learns fastest by contact with the people who changed first.

Change one rule you've outgrown at home or at work, and tell whoever it affects. Small reforms made in these weeks tend to stick, because nobody had to fight for them.
