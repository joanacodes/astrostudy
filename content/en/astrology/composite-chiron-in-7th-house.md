*What are we to each other?* Some pairs answer that once and never ask again; for others it stays an open, tender question for years. Composite Chiron in the 7th house belongs to the second kind: the partnership's own terms are the sore point, and the agreements two people write for themselves are the way through.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is the house of the other: partners, contracts, the person across the table, open rivals too. Libra is its natural sign, and its door is the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the western angle, which makes it one of the four angular houses. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) the relationship already is a partnership, so the 7th describes how it holds its own terms and how it meets other partnerships as a pair. With [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) there, the terms are where it snags: the shape of the commitment, who owes what, whether the outside world recognises the arrangement, how the pair gets on with other couples or business partners.

Two people who are excellent partners elsewhere, at work, in friendship, in an earlier marriage, can find that this particular partnership resists the usual forms. That is the bond's own tenderness, not a failing in either of you. Its adapting is contractual in the kindest sense: agreements made explicit, revisited when life changes, worded in the pair's own language rather than borrowed from a template. Pairs who do this well are often good at helping other partnerships, couples or co-founders, find terms that fit them.

Its cusp, the composite Descendant, lies opposite the composite Ascendant and moves with it. In one method it is the midpoint of your two Descendants; in the other it is the point setting opposite the Ascendant that goes with the composite Midheaven where you live. The two can differ by several degrees, and a Chiron close to that line can fall in the 6th or in the 7th depending on the choice. Both methods need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one birth chart, [Chiron in the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-in-7th-house/) describes a person's tender spot in one-to-one bonds; in synastry, [their Chiron in your 7th house](/astrology/chiron-in-7th-house-synastry/) brings the other's old hurt into the partnership. The composite's 7th is the partnership itself, and its tenderness belongs to the agreement, not to either of the two who made it.

The danger is defining the relationship by what it lacks: the ring, the title, the business plan other pairs seem to have. A composite says nothing about whether two people will marry, sign or separate. Take the time to decide what your commitment consists of, in your own words, and to say it to each other now and then.

Read beside its neighbours, the pattern is plain: [composite Chiron in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-6th-house/) carries the daily round, [composite Chiron in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-8th-house/) what is pooled and trusted, and the 7th the agreement between them. The composite is a technique I describe, not one I use; those who use it read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). A chart of a partnership is a kind of agreement too, and it needs both partners' knowledge ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
