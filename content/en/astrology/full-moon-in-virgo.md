The full moon in Virgo is the last full moon before the March equinox: the Moon in Virgo, the Sun opposite in Pisces, at the tail end of the northern winter. The list faces the dream, the detail faces the whole, and in 2026 and 2027 it is a lunar eclipse.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

When the Moon is full, it stands opposite the Sun with its whole face lit; this one stands in [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) while the Sun crosses [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/). The Moon in Virgo brings the practical side of the month to a head: the tasks and routines that make a day work, and the urge to put them right. Across the axis, the Sun in Pisces asks for rest, imagination and a looser hold. The Virgo–Pisces axis runs between the detail and the whole, and a [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) here often catches you fixing everything except what matters, or drifting while the small things pile up. Both halves are needed. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Virgo; Pisces has two rulers, Neptune and Jupiter, and I read both.

Read it as a marker. It matters in a chart when one of the two lights falls within a degree or so of a natal planet or angle; even so, it only brings forward what slower timing (transits, progressions, solar arcs) is already working on. On its own, it says nothing about what will happen.

The most recent, on 3 March 2026 at 12°54′ Virgo, was a total [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/). The next, on 20 February 2027 at 23:24 UT, at 2°05′, is a penumbral one: the Moon only grazes the Earth's outer shadow and barely darkens, and in a chart it weighs less than a total eclipse. Ordinary full moons follow on 11 March 2028 at 20°59′, 28 February 2029 at 10°24′ and 19 March 2030 at 29°10′, with the Sun on the last degree of Pisces.

Both eclipses came two weeks after an annular solar eclipse in Aquarius ([eclipse in Virgo](/astrology/eclipse-in-virgo/)). For an eclipse the orb grows to about 3°, at the Virgo end or the Pisces end, and the natal point it meets is read through its house, never as an omen.

The [new moon in Pisces](/astrology/new-moon-in-pisces/) falls in the same solar month: on 8 March 2027, two weeks after the penumbral eclipse. A month either side fall the [full moon in Leo](/astrology/full-moon-in-leo/) and the [full moon in Libra](/astrology/full-moon-in-libra/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The Moon's house is the one holding its Virgo degree; the Sun's is opposite, where Pisces is. Both depend on when you were born, which is why [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) matters here. My houses are Placidus. Read the line for the house with Virgo on its cusp:

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Virgo rising: you want order and competence in how you come across, while a partner asks for patience and gentleness.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** the household budget, sorted to the last line, set against shared money where things have grown vague.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** facts checked and messages answered, while a belief or a far-off plan asks you to trust what you can't see yet.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** the repairs and routines of home come to a head, while your public life calls for vision rather than detail.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** a craft or hobby you want to get right, set against a group's ideals that ask you to let go of perfect.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** Virgo's own house: work and daily habits peak, while the 12th asks for rest; both are part of looking after yourself.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Virgo on the Descendant: a partner's practical demands, or criticisms, while you need room to dream.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** shared accounts and trust looked at closely, while your own sense of worth asks for compassion.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** study by the book, a journey planned in detail, while daily life fills with intuition and images.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Virgo on the Midheaven: your competence becomes visible at work, while home asks for softness and rest.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** the useful role you play among friends, set against a creative dream of your own.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** private worries you try to put in order, while the working day asks for trust rather than control.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Check both degrees.** Allow a degree around each position above, Virgo end or Pisces end; about three for the eclipse of 2027, the lightest kind there is.
- **One list, one letting go.** Virgo's side: one thing to put in order this month. Pisces's side: one thing you stop trying to control. Mercury, Neptune and Jupiter, by house, show where each belongs.
- **Keep the body out of it.** If the lists are really about a worry for your health, that belongs with a doctor, not a chart ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
