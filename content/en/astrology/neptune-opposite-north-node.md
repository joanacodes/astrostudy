Neptune opposite the North Node sets the planet of imagination down on the South Node, in the part of the chart you already know by heart. Dreaming, feeling with others, letting edges blur: that's home ground. The road you're asked to grow along runs away from it, across the chart.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and softens: compassion, inspiration, a feel for what lies beyond the visible, and the ability to let needless boundaries go. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) holds what came easily and has had long practice; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), exactly opposite, a capacity that only grows by doing. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, and here Neptune's gifts sit at the familiar end, with the less practised direction facing them.

In plain words, you know how to feel your way into other people's lives, how to imagine, how to make room, and perhaps how to disappear a little when the world gets hard. Those are real gifts; they're also where you go back to under pressure. Whatever the North Node's sign and house describe, growth here may ask for something with a clearer outline: a decision, a structure, a self that stays visible in the room. The opposition doesn't ask you to give Neptune up. It asks you to carry its compassion over to the other end, so that the new direction is walked with a soft heart rather than instead of one.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Within 6° of the true node, the opposition belongs to about 1 person in 29 born from 1930 to 2009. The node slips back through a sign in about 18 months and Neptune takes about 14 years, so the two face each other only for some months at a time: an aspect of your chart, not of your generation. I check the mean node too, since it can tip a chart at the edge of the orb either way ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

The houses show the two sides. With Neptune and the South Node in the 4th and the North Node in the 10th, a rich private, imaginative life might be the familiar ground, and growth might point towards a public role with a definite shape. Reading the houses this way depends on the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Neptune's other positions around the nodal axis:

- **[Neptune conjunct North Node](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-north-node/):** the reverse arrangement, with Neptune as the new direction rather than the old ground.
- **[Neptune sextile North Node](/astrology/neptune-sextile-north-node/):** imagination close beside the path, offering openings.
- **[Neptune square North Node](/astrology/neptune-square-north-node/):** Neptune halfway between the nodes, crossing the road.
- **[Neptune trine North Node](/astrology/neptune-trine-north-node/):** imagination flowing towards growth, sextile the South Node.

Between two charts, [Neptune opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-north-node-synastry/) places one person's Neptune on the other's South Node: a soft, familiar dreaminess that can feel like home, and can also keep both people in old patterns, so it is worth noticing which.

## In transits {#in-transits}

The moving nodes line up with your natal Neptune twice in every 18.6-year round. At the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), at about 9, 28 and 46 if you have this opposition, the transiting North Node passes over Neptune: a few weeks in which the old gift is invited onto the new road. When the nodes are home again, at the [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) (about 18½, 37 and 56), the South Node returns to Neptune while the North Node stands across from it, which is [transiting North Node opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-neptune/), close for a month or so. Transiting Neptune opposite your natal node moves far more slowly, lingering within a degree for a year or so.

Weather, not a forecast: brief seasons when the familiar refuge and the less familiar road come into view together.
