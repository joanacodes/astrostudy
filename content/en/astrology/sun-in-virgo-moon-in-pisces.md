On the axis of Virgo and Pisces, two planets trade places: Mercury, at home and exalted in Virgo, is in detriment and fall in Pisces, while Venus, exalted in Pisces, falls in Virgo. Your Sun and Moon sit at either end of that axis, close to a full moon. Order facing the ocean is how I read the pair, each needing what the other has.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

These are opposite signs, both mutable, earth across from water: they complete each other more than they clash, and both bend easily. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) knows itself through discernment and useful work; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) has porous edges, absorbs the atmosphere around it, and steadies itself through quiet, imagination and kindness. One light draws distinctions and the other dissolves them. You may sort out a crisis with great precision by day and lie awake at night carrying everyone's feelings about it.

Pisces has two rulers, and I read both: [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern one, for where you most easily merge with others, and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the traditional one, for the studies and beliefs through which your compassion grows wiser. The crossing on this axis is unusually neat. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), your Sun's ruler, is exalted in Virgo and in both detriment and fall in Pisces; Jupiter, your Moon's older ruler, has its detriment in Virgo; and [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is exalted in Pisces and falls in Virgo. To me it describes a mind that must learn to trust what it can't measure, and a heart that does better with a little structure.

Whatever the year, the full moon that falls during Virgo's month is a [full moon in Pisces](/astrology/full-moon-in-pisces/), and your birth came within a day or two of one, with the Moon 150° to 209° from the Sun. The births divide almost exactly between the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) before the opposition and the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase after it, and a few hours either way can change which is yours; near 180°, the chart holds a [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains what changes as the Moon turns full.

Among every 144 people, about one has these lights. A birthday on which the Moon left Aquarius, or reached Aries, can belong to any of three pages, this one, the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-aquarius/) or the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-aries/), and the hour picks ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign and the houses wait on that hour as well, and on an axis like this one they show where in life the sorting and the dissolving take place.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love devotedly and practically: the soup made, the appointment remembered, the long listening late at night. The Pisces Moon feels a partner's moods almost as its own, and the Virgo Sun turns that feeling into help. The risk is rescuing, falling for someone who needs fixing, or staying long after kindness stopped coming back. A partner who is gentle and also clear about what they need lets you give without being drained.

## At work {#at-work}

Caring work done with real skill suits the pair: nursing or teaching, therapy or social work, music or illustration, any job where compassion needs method to be of use. The Sun brings precision and the Moon imagination and empathy, so you can be both the one who notices and the one who comforts. Noise and harshness drain the Moon, chaos frustrates the Sun, and a quiet corner of every working day, one no task is allowed into, keeps both going.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The Virgo Sun tries to fix what the Pisces Moon absorbs, and there is always more to absorb. You can end the day worried about details and flooded with feelings at once, then blame yourself for both. Boundaries can feel unkind, so they tend to arrive late. What helps is giving this mutable pair a shape: set hours for helping, a ritual for coming home to yourself, and a creative practice where the Moon's imagination can flow and the Sun's craft can hold it.
