Leo is the sign of being seen, and a double Leo arrives when the Moon can barely be seen at all: a day or two either side of a new moon, both lights side by side in the Sun's own sign. I read the pair as a single flame, what you aim for and what comforts you lit from the same source.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Both lights burn in fixed fire. The [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) organises your sense of self around making things from the heart and being recognised for them accurately; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) needs warmth to come back, to be chosen and noticed by the people who matter. Here the two needs are really one. When the people you love see what you made, purpose and comfort are fed in the same moment, and when nobody does, both go hungry together.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules [Leo](/astrology/leo/), so in this chart it rules both lights and answers to nobody: the Moon reports to the Sun, and the Sun to itself. The Sun is in its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/); the Moon holds no dignity of its own in Leo, only the standing it borrows from its ruler. Where another pair sends me on to a second planet, a double Leo sends me back to the Sun again, to its house and to its aspects, which the Moon, so close by, very often shares.

Anywhere from 30° behind the Sun to 29° ahead of it: that is where your Moon could be, which puts the birth no more than about 2½ days from a new moon. Half these births fall in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), as the old month closes, and half in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase that opens the next; the new moon on either side of your birth was usually a [new moon in Leo](/astrology/new-moon-in-leo/), and with the lights close together the chart has a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). Which side of the exact new moon you came is a matter of hours, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says what each side means.

Roughly one person in 142 is a double Leo. Most birthdays settle it, since the Moon usually keeps one sign from midnight to midnight; on the day it came in from Cancer or moved on to Virgo, the hour decides between a double Leo, the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-cancer/) and the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-virgo/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). When both lights share a sign, it's tempting to read that sign twice and call it a portrait. I hold back: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), about a third of the Sun signs I checked held up, and the Moon, ticked or crossed like every placement, didn't join the three that held far more often.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love wholeheartedly and loyally, and you need the same wholeheartedness back. A partner's attention warms both your lights at once, which is why a careless word can chill both at once too. You celebrate the people you love in public and want to be someone's particular person in private. What keeps the bond alive is play, kept up on purpose, and a partner who tells you, specifically, what your warmth has given them.

## At work {#at-work}

Creative authority suits a double Leo: work that carries your judgement and your name, a design, a performance, a kitchen, a classroom, a business with your stamp on it. You give generously to the projects you believe in and to the people working beside you, and you can carry a team on warmth alone for a surprisingly long time. A role where your taste is overruled as a matter of course drains purpose and comfort together.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With one light ruling both, there's no second voice inside the pair. When recognition fails to come, your sense of purpose and your sense of safety can falter in the same hour, and pride may keep you from saying so. You might turn the brightness up, or dim it and wait for someone to notice. What helps comes from outside the two lights: the slower planets, the rising sign, a creative habit kept for no audience, and a few friends who see you on a dim day and stay.
