Sun trine Lilith in synastry joins one person's Sun and the other's Black Moon Lilith at 120°, a third of the way round the zodiac. Of the five ways this pair can meet, it's the one where the untamed side relaxes: in the other person's light, it doesn't have to fight to exist.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) gives a person their centre: the coherent self they live from and express, their vitality, what they're for. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is interpreted in very different ways; in my readings it's the untamed side, the instinct, desire and refusal a person learned to keep under wraps, and their sense of their own power. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets two functions reinforce each other without either giving way, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) it's the one I'd call easy, as long as easy isn't mistaken for automatic.

When the Lilith is yours, this person tends to take your "too much" in their stride. You can be frank, hungry, contrary, and nothing much happens: no flinch, no lecture. You stop explaining. For someone used to editing themselves, that alone can feel like a long exhale.

When the Sun is yours, you tend to find their untamed side natural rather than alarming, even restful. Their ease with what others hide rubs off: in their company you may find your no cleaner, or your wants more openly said.

The shared element usually sets the tone. In earth, a Sun in Taurus with a Lilith in Virgo or Capricorn, appetites and refusals get treated as plain facts rather than confessions; in air, a Sun in Gemini with a Lilith in Libra or Aquarius, the things nobody was allowed to say become conversation.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Lilith marks what wasn't accepted somewhere. When it's accepted this easily between two people, the hiding the Lilith person still does elsewhere can go unnoticed by both: with each other they're themselves, at work or with family they still fold small. Ease can also file the edge off. The untamed side becomes "your wild streak", an affectionate label that's a little less than the real thing. And because so little has had to be fought for, the first serious no aimed at the Sun person can come as a shock.

Flow isn't passivity. A trine can sit unused for years, pleasant and unexamined.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use the ease on purpose. While things are good, ask each other what you'd never show anyone else, and what it would take to show a little more of it outside the pair. If the Sun is yours, take the other's refusals as seriously as you enjoy their frankness. Co-workers with this trine are often the two who don't have to pretend in meetings; adult relatives, the ones who never needed to perform for each other. Couples and friends can let it carry the harder conversations instead of steering round them.

Within 6.8°, about 1 pair of people in 7 has this trine, with the Sun in either chart. Each chart needs its birth time, and without both I don't read the synastry ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Neither the Sun nor Lilith moves far in a day, so the time seldom decides the trine; what it settles is the houses, which tell you where in each life the ease shows.

Lived inside a single chart, this angle is [Sun trine Lilith](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith/). Beside it, between two charts, sit its soft sibling [Sun sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith-synastry/), which waits to be used; [Sun conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith-synastry/), which exposes; and the two that rub, [Sun square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-lilith-synastry/) and [Sun opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith-synastry/).
