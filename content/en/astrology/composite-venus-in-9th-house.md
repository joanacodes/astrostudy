A pair who met abroad and still talk to each other in a third language; two friends who have argued about the same philosophers for thirty years; a couple whose holidays are planned around a museum. Composite Venus in the 9th house prizes whatever takes a relationship past the familiar.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is where the question of place matters most. Composite houses can be drawn with no reference to place at all, from the midpoints of each pair of natal cusps, or around the composite Midheaven for the town the two of you live in now; for a 9th-house pair that town may be far from where either was born, and a move to another latitude would shift the result. Both need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), so [Venus](/astrology/venus/) can only be placed in the 9th of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) once both are known.

The 9th is cadent and belongs naturally to Sagittarius; travel, higher learning, beliefs, teaching and worldview are its matters, and its old name was the God, with the Sun taking its joy there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With Venus here, the bond's pleasure lies in widening its view: a course taken together, a long walk on an old pilgrim route, friends from other countries at the table, an outlook worked out over years of argument. The difference between two backgrounds can itself be what the relationship loves. [Venus in the 9th house](/astrology/venus-in-9th-house/), at birth, draws one person towards the foreign and the distant; [their Venus in your 9th house](/astrology/venus-in-9th-house-synastry/) makes distant things feel close; the composite's horizon belongs to the pair.

Composite Venus keeps within 47° of the composite Sun in about 9 pairs in 10, as it does at birth. The rest come from pairs born months apart, whose two midpoints may fall in opposite halves of the circle: for about 1 pair in 22, that puts the composite Sun nearly opposite this Venus, back near the 3rd house of everyday surroundings ([composite Sun in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-3rd-house/)). That comes from the arithmetic, not from a pull between home and away.

A shared view of the world can come to feel like the relationship's foundation, so that one person changing their mind feels like a threat to both. Beliefs aren't dogma and learning isn't superiority, yet a bond that loves its outlook can defend it a little too well, against friends or against one of its own two members. It helps to leave room for each of you to change your mind, and to treat disagreement about big questions as part of the pleasure rather than a crack in it.

[Composite Venus in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-8th-house/) turns inward, [composite Venus in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-10th-house/) outward into public life. The composite is a technique I present rather than practise, and its users keep the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and each natal chart beside it. Whatever two people explore together, this chart should be one they both chose to look at ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
