Pluto moves so slowly that people born in the same year as you have it within a few degrees of yours. When Jupiter comes round to face that degree, about every twelve years, your whole year group meets this transit at about the same age, and your own chart decides where in life it lands.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens: it reaches for meaning, for principles, for what could come next. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) goes to the root: it clears out what has worn through and hands strength back to whoever does the work. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) puts the two on either side of the chart, so for a while the wide view and the deep hold face each other. Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), it adds nothing to your chart; it shows what your Pluto already holds, from further away.

What you grip hardest tends to come up for review: a loyalty, a fear, a need to keep control, a strength you keep out of sight. From across the chart, Jupiter asks how large that really is, and what it's for. Oppositions often work through other people, so the question may arrive with a face: someone more open-handed than you, or someone who sees your depth and names it. Pluto has a reputation for endings, but this transit has nothing to say about the end of a life ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).

Pluto's house shows where you hold on, and the house Jupiter crosses opposite it shows where the wider view comes from. With Pluto in your 4th, for instance, the opposition comes during [Jupiter transiting the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-10th-house/): a larger public role weighed against family loyalties and what you keep at home. Both houses come from your birth time, so without it I'd have no way to say where this happens, and I don't read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

If [Jupiter opposite Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-pluto/) is part of your chart, every [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) brings this opposition with it. With the two conjunct at your birth, it comes halfway between returns instead, when Jupiter faces its own birth degree.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Most of these oppositions are short. With the orbs I use, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, seven in ten last about eleven days, Jupiter passing the far side of your Pluto at its ordinary pace. The other three come in a loop. Jupiter spends about four months of each year retrograde ([Jupiter retrograde](/astrology/jupiter-retrograde/)), and when that stretch falls across the point, the opposition forms three times over about eight months.

The first one comes before the age of 12 for everyone, at an age set by where Jupiter stood when you were born: about 9 for someone born in 1965, about 3 for someone born in 1985. Jupiter's twelve-year lap brings it back after that. By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have had it six or seven times, and none of them misses it.

Each opposition marks the halfway point of Jupiter's round with your Pluto. About six years earlier, Jupiter crossed it ([transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-pluto/)), and the angles in between, [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-pluto/), [transiting Jupiter square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-pluto/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-pluto/), come once on the way out and once on the way back. Of the five, the opposition is the one most likely to arrive through someone else.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take one thing you hold on to tightly and look at it from the far side: a rule you live by, a grudge, a fear of losing control. Jupiter's question isn't whether to let go. It's whether the thing still fits the life you have now.

If a person is carrying the other end, ask what they can teach you before deciding what they want from you. Someone whose openness unsettles you, or whose depth makes your hopes look thin, is showing you the half of the axis you use less.

Give a hidden strength a wider use. What you learned the hard way, the patience you built where things were difficult: Pluto's resources tend to grow when Jupiter finds them more room.
