Twice in its round of about 84 years, once on the way to your Sun and once on the way past it, Uranus stands at right angles to the degree your Sun held at birth. Each time, the self you've built spends about twenty months rubbing against the self you haven't tried yet.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and reforms: it wants enough freedom to test other arrangements. Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the centre the rest of your life is organised around, your identity, your purpose and the energy you put behind them. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates two functions through constructive friction, so for these months the centre is asked to move a little, and everything arranged around it feels the pull.

It rarely looks like a crisis. More often it's a life that works and fits a little less well each month: the role you're known for, the plan you made ten years ago, the version of yourself your friends expect. Some people change direction under this square. More of them change the terms, keeping the work and redesigning the hours, keeping the city and moving across it. Energy tends to come in bursts rather than as a steady supply, and the season goes better when you plan around that than when you fight it.

The trap is pulling down what still works because one part of it doesn't. A Uranus square to the Sun asks for reform, which is slower and more careful than escape.

The houses show where. With your Sun in the 10th and Uranus crossing your 1st ([Uranus transiting the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-transit-1st-house/)), a freer way of presenting yourself rubs against the work you're known for, and one of the two has to give a little. Both houses are drawn from your birth time; without it I don't read this [transit](/astrology/transits/), or any other ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus holds the square for about twenty months, long enough for a birthday or two to pass inside it; the quickest passes take about eleven. Uranus spends about five months of each year retrograde, so in more than nine passes out of ten it crosses the exact square three times: forward, back, then forward again for good.

The two squares fall on either side of [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-sun/), about twenty years before it and about twenty years after, so roughly forty-two years separate them. [Transiting Uranus sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-sun/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-sun/) are the easier steps of the same round; [transiting Uranus opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-sun/) lies halfway between the two squares. By 80, everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has met at least one square, and most have met both.

Born with [Sun square Uranus](/astrology/sun-square-uranus/), your timetable was set from the start: one of the two squares was already there at birth, and the other arrives with your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), between about 39 and 44½, when Uranus, facing its own birth place, squares your Sun from the other side.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List what still works before you change anything. A square to the Sun can make a whole life look wrong when only one part of it is.

Change one structure at a time. A new role, a new schedule or a new place each takes a while to settle, and twenty months is long enough to do them in sequence.

Give the restlessness something to build as well as something to leave. Under this square, the urge to differ is at its strongest, and its most useful, when it has a project of its own.
