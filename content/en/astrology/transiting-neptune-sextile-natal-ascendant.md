Every Ascendant has two sextiles, a third of the zodiac apart, and Neptune's slow round reaches one of them in most lives, both in some and neither in about 1 in 6. Each is a quiet opening in the way you meet the world.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) sets the angle you come at things from: how you walk into a room, how you begin. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) lends imagination, tenderness and a feel for atmosphere. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates the two through an opening that waits for you to use it, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) a gentler, more imaginative way of presenting yourself becomes easy to try: a softer first approach, a creative side you let people see sooner, more patience with first impressions.

Which sextile you're under shows in where the opening arrives. From above the horizon, Neptune is usually crossing your 11th house, and the opening comes through friends, groups and shared hopes ([Neptune transiting the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-11th-house/)); from below, around your 3rd, through conversation, study, writing or the streets near home ([Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/)). Those are rough places: in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/) the houses aren't 30° each, and only your birth time can say ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A sextile doesn't act on its own. Neptune's blind spot here would be expecting a pleasant atmosphere to carry you; the transit offers the climate, and showing that side of yourself remains your step. The Ascendant's own blind spot, making too much of first impressions, eases under it too: you can let people take longer to see you, and take longer to see them.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The sextile lasts about two and a half years within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after; the shortest passes take twenty-two months. Three exact hits are usual, and about a quarter of passes bring five.

The two sextiles lie 120° apart, and Neptune covers almost half the zodiac in 80 years, so the count for births from 1930 to 2009 finds most people meeting one, some both, and about 1 in 6 neither. Born with Neptune near the top of your chart, you meet both, with [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) between them. Born with Neptune low, around your 3rd or 4th house, past one sextile and too far from the other, you are among the 1 in 6.

Its neighbours on the same round are the conjunction and [transiting Neptune opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-ascendant/), each met by half of people; [transiting Neptune square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-ascendant/), met by everyone; and [transiting Neptune trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-ascendant/), as common as this sextile. Until March 2039 Neptune is in Aries, sextile to [Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/) from the 11th-house side and to [Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/) from the 3rd.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Show the side of you that usually waits: the drawing, the music, the kindness you keep for close friends. A sextile from Neptune makes that first showing easier.

Say yes to one invitation that fits the house it comes through: a group, a class, a conversation you would usually skip.

Keep a note of what you begin in these years: a group joined, a class started, a new way of introducing yourself. Sextile seasons are easy to forget, and the note shows what the opening was for.
