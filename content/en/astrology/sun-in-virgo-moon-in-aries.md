A Virgo Sun likes to check before it moves; an Aries Moon moves first and checks afterwards, if at all. The full moon had only just passed when you arrived, and I think of the pair as a craftsperson with a starting pistol: careful method, quick feeling, and a lifelong negotiation about which goes first.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Earth and fire, mutable and cardinal, and five signs back from the Sun to the Moon: by sign a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/), two manners that share nothing and must keep adjusting. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) organises a life around discernment and good method, and feels most itself when something is done exactly; the [Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/) needs to answer a feeling at once, and calms down once it has. Under pressure you may act on a feeling first and examine it afterwards, then wonder which of the two was really you. Both are.

Tradition gives neither light a dignity in these signs, with one twist: Aries, where your Moon is, is the Sun's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), so your needs stand on ground that honours the Sun while the Sun itself works without rank. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Virgo and is exalted there too, and it stays within some 28° of the Sun, in Leo, Virgo or Libra. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules Aries. Mercury for how you think things through, Mars for how you discharge a feeling: the conversation between those two planets is the one I listen to first.

The Moon had come round to the far side of the sky and a little beyond, 181° to 239° from the Sun, 15 to 19½ days after the new moon, the full moon at most five days gone. In Rudhyar's count about seven of these births in eight are still [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) births and the rest [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) ones, and just after the opposition the chart may carry a [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/). I read a full moon birth as a self that learns its own outline from the people across the table; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) has the detail.

Roughly 1 birth in 145 carries this pair. When your birthday was also the day the Moon left Pisces or reached Taurus, the time decides between this page, the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-pisces/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-taurus/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). A Virgo Sun will want the error bars on that, so here they are: without the time I can't place the rising sign or the houses either, which is why I don't read the chart until the time is found.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You show love by noticing and by acting. The Virgo Sun pays exact attention, the errand run, the problem solved; the Aries Moon defends, speaks up and wants things settled today. What you need back is honesty without delay, and a partner who doesn't take a quick flare for a verdict. Because the Moon recovers fast and the Sun keeps a careful record, you may forgive quickly and still remember every detail, so tell people which of the two they're getting.

## At work {#at-work}

You're often the person called when something breaks. The Aries Moon rises to urgency and the Virgo Sun brings the method that fixes things properly, so emergencies, launches and troubleshooting can bring out your best. Slow committees and vague instructions frustrate both lights for different reasons, the Moon because nothing moves and the Sun because nothing is clear. Work that lets you act fast on a sound process suits the pair.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The friction is between speed and care. The Moon wants to answer now and the Sun wants to answer correctly; in a hurry you may manage neither, then turn your own high standards on yourself for the result. Irritation can also gather round other people's sloppiness when the real feeling underneath is tiredness. What helps is a short pause built into the reflex, a breath, a walk, a draft left overnight, and permission to be quick and good enough when the moment calls for it.
