Someone else usually opens the door. With the ruler of your 9th house in the 7th, the house of the person across the table, meaning tends to arrive through a partner from another place, a teacher who became an equal, a rival who made you think.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is where you look for meaning, through travel, higher study, philosophy and faith. Its ruler carries that search to the house it occupies ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), which begins at the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), is the house of the people you meet one to one: partners in love and in work, close collaborators, and the open rivals tradition placed there too. Counted by sign, the 7th and the 9th are two signs apart, a sextile, which tradition read as an easy, cooperative link.

Meaning is a two-person project here. People with this placement often fall for someone from another country, another faith or another field, or find that a partner opened a door they'd never have tried alone. The relationship itself can be an education, two people trading books, languages and certainties for years. In work, it can describe a practice built with a partner, or one-to-one work in teaching, coaching, interpreting or advising. The difficulty is handing the search over entirely. A partner can show you a horizon but can't believe on your behalf, and a bond built on one person's convictions leaves the other with less room than they need.

The 7th is also the house of contracts and of the adversary you can see coming, and the old horary lists put lawyers, with the other learned professions, in the 9th. With this ruler, agreements, negotiations and disputes often turn on principles, and some people find their way into law, mediation or diplomacy. A chart can't tell you how a case will end, or whether a partnership will last ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)); it describes what you bring to the table.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 7th opens at the Descendant, the degree setting in the west, and a ruler inside it was still above the horizon at your birth, moving down towards the point where it would set. An exact time decides whether it had already crossed ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A little later, below the Descendant in the 6th, your beliefs would be put to work, day by day ([Ruler of the 9th in the 6th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)). A little earlier, higher up in the 8th, they would be tested in intimacy and in what you share ([Ruler of the 9th in the 8th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)).

Saturn is one of the two rulers of an Aquarius 9th, with Uranus ([Aquarius on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). In the 7th, Saturn sits in the house the natural zodiac gives to Libra, its sign of exaltation, and takes commitments seriously: the partner chosen slowly, the promise kept, the teacher whose standards you still hear ([Saturn in the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-in-7th-house/)). Uranus in the 7th wants a partnership with air in it, and often a partner who thinks very differently from you.
