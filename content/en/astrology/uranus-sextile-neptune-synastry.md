Some generations could help each other more than they realise. Uranus sextile Neptune in synastry places one person's Uranus about 60° from the other's Neptune, within 2.4°, the narrowest orb I use: reform on one side, an ideal on the other, and an opening between them.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) works on systems: it tests alternatives, frees what is stuck, reforms what no longer fits. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) works on meaning: imagination, compassion, the sense that something larger is at stake. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates two functions through an opportunity that needs engaging, and spread over two charts, as the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) are, the opportunity runs between two generations.

If the Uranus is yours, you can see how the other's ideals could be made to work: a structure, a method, a new arrangement. They give your reforms a purpose you didn't have to invent.

If the Neptune is yours, this person can turn what you imagine into something that runs. In return, your sense of what matters keeps their changes from becoming change for its own sake.

A Virgo Uranus with a Scorpio Neptune: reforms made by improving how things function, beside ideals that look for depth and truth.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opening between generations is easy to leave unused. Each can assume the other won't understand: the Uranus person that the ideal is vague, the Neptune person that the reform is soulless. Or both wait, one for inspiration, the other for an invitation. The sextile offers the help; it doesn't deliver it.

- **[Uranus conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** change and ideal fused on one degree, with the blur that brings.
- **[Uranus square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune-synastry/):** a sign further, where reform and ideal rub instead of helping.
- **[Uranus trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune-synastry/):** two signs further, where they run together without effort.
- **[Uranus opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** across the zodiac, at either end of a long line.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give the opening a project. Choose a cause both care about: the Neptune person says why it matters, the Uranus person how it could work. Volunteers of different ages in one group, colleagues a few years apart, adult siblings, a parent and an adult child: each can turn the angle into something built.

Two people born a few years apart can have it: someone born in mid-1965, Uranus in Virgo, with anyone born between September 1961 and November 1962, Neptune in Scorpio. A generation on, the Sagittarius Uranus of a mid-1985 birth sextiles the Aquarius Neptune of anyone born between January 2004 and April 2005. Shared by every birth in those spans, it is not a contact my synastry count includes; the count keeps to the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant and the Midheaven. I read it through the houses, as one era's methods meeting another era's hopes, more than two people.

No birth hour moves Uranus or Neptune enough to matter; it only sets the houses, and those decide where the help would land ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Neptune in the Uranus person's 9th house, the exchange is likely to be about beliefs, study and far horizons ([Their Neptune in your 9th house](/astrology/neptune-in-9th-house-synastry/)).

People born with [Uranus sextile Neptune](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune/), on and off from 1963 to 1970, carry the same opening inside one chart. Between two charts it becomes a collaboration, and one I read only with both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
