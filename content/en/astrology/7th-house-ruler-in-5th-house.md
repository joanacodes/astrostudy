Romance, play and the things you make for the joy of it fill the house where the ruler of your partnerships stands. A committed bond, for you, has to keep something of a love affair about it: someone you'd still choose on an ordinary Tuesday.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is what you do for delight: creativity, play, flirtation, the self you show when there's nothing at stake. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) asks for commitment between equals: partners, associates, the rival you face head-on. The 7th's ruler takes partnership into the 5th's territory ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

I read it as love that keeps courting. Romance doesn't stop when commitment starts: you want dates, surprises, a partner who still makes you laugh, and you notice quickly when a relationship turns into administration. Many people with this placement meet partners through what they love doing, a choir, a dance floor, a stage, a team, and creative partnerships suit you: two people writing, playing or building something neither would make alone. Even rivalry tends to become a game, played hard and, at its best, with good grace. The task is to let a bond grow past the thrill of its beginning, and to find the play in the long middle rather than looking for it elsewhere.

Counted round the wheel, the 5th comes two houses before the 7th, a sextile, an open and friendly link between pleasure and partnership. Tradition called the 5th Good Fortune and gave Venus its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) there, so if Venus rules your 7th, with Taurus or Libra setting, the planet of love stands where the old astrologers said it was glad to be, and affection itself becomes the shared project.

Other rulers set other tones. With Aquarius rising, Leo sets and the Sun rules your 7th: you want admiration that runs both ways. Mercury, given the 7th by Gemini or Virgo, keeps the flirting verbal and the jokes running. Two rulers come into play with [Aquarius on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/), Saturn and Uranus, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 5th takes pleasure seriously and courts slowly, and Uranus, whatever its house, asks that the bond leave room to surprise you.

Children were the 5th's in tradition, too. This placement doesn't say whether a couple will have them, or when ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/); what I read is what two adults create and enjoy together.

Placidus, the house system I use, puts the 5th below the western horizon, and a few minutes either way can carry the ruler across a cusp. One way lies the 4th, where partnership settles into home ([Ruler of the 7th in the 4th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)); the other, the 6th, where it lives in daily work and routine ([Ruler of the 7th in the 6th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)). Without a time I can't tell which side of either line yours is on ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What doesn't change is the pull towards delight. With this ruler, a partnership is worth keeping when it's also worth enjoying.
