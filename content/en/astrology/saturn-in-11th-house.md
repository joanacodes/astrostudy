Some people collect friends. With Saturn in the 11th house you choose them, slowly, and keep them for decades. Groups are where you end up taking responsibility, and hopes are something you plan rather than simply wish for.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Friendship, groups and hopes share one house in a chart, [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/): the circles you move in, the people who back you, and the future you're quietly planning. It's a succedent house, where what you achieve is kept alive with others, and tradition called it the Good Spirit, one of the fortunate houses, where [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) has its joy. Its natural sign is Aquarius, one of [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/)'s homes in the tradition, so the planet is in familiar territory: rules that serve the group, loyalty that lasts, a long view of the future.

In practice that often means friendships made slowly, sometimes with people older than you, and kept for life. You may be the one who organises, takes the minutes, remembers the birthdays, or feels slightly outside the circle you care most about. Hopes tend to come with timelines attached.

In Placidus, the system I divide the houses by, the 11th lies above the eastern horizon, between the 12th and the Midheaven, so Saturn here had risen a few hours before you were born and was still climbing. An hour's doubt about the time can move it into the 10th or the 12th ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Opposite, the 5th holds your private pleasures; with Saturn in the 11th, it's the shared future that gets the seriousness. One house before, [Saturn in the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-in-10th-house/) carries public responsibility, and the next house round, [Saturn in the 12th house](/astrology/saturn-in-12th-house/), works alone; here, Saturn works with others.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love usually grows out of friendship for you. You may need to like and trust someone as a friend before anything else is possible, and a partner who is also your closest friend suits this Saturn well. You may be slow to join a partner's social world, and loyal to it once you do. Old friends may know you better than anyone, and a partner who welcomes that is a gift. And let your friends look after you too, not only the other way round.

## At work {#at-work}

Teams, networks, associations, committees, causes: you're the reliable member who keeps them running, and over time often the one who leads them. Long-term plans suit you better than quick wins, and colleagues often become friends only after years, then for good. The risk is ending up doing most of the work in every group you join, because you're the one who noticed it needed doing.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Feeling like the outsider in a group you belong to. A fear of rejection that keeps you from joining at all. Doing all the work and resenting it quietly. Groups that change their rules without asking can feel like a betrayal, even when nothing personal was meant. And a cynicism about hopes, born of having seen too many plans fail for lack of anyone to carry them.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tend one or two friendships deliberately, with regular time that doesn't need a reason. Choose groups with a purpose you believe in, and share the load out loud. Give hopes a timeline, but leave room for surprise. Let a new friendship be light for a while before it has to be loyal. I tested charts, mine first and then those of family, friends and strangers who agreed to it ([the invoice method](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)), and Saturn kept coming back right far more often than most. With an 11th-house Saturn I'd say the same of friendship: test it slowly, and keep what holds.
