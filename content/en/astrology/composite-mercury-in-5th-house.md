Some pairs can't pass a bad pun without topping it. Composite Mercury in the 5th house describes a relationship whose mind is at its best when it's playing: with words, with games, with ideas that are fun before they're useful.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), each planet sits halfway between its places in your two birth charts, and composite [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the thinking the relationship does as a pair. [The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is the house of play, creativity, pleasure and romance. Succedent, with Leo as its natural sign, it was called Good Fortune in tradition, and Venus, not Mercury, has its joy there.

With the bond's mind in this house, talk turns into play. Wordplay, running jokes, games at the kitchen table, quizzes and crosswords, the long message thread that is mostly nonsense: this is how the relationship thinks out loud. It often makes things together in words, a blog, a play, a podcast nobody else listens to, and it flirts with language, whatever kind of bond it is. A natal [Mercury in the 5th house](/astrology/mercury-in-5th-house/) is one person's playful mind; [their Mercury in your 5th house](/astrology/mercury-in-5th-house-synastry/) is one person's wit delighting the other. The composite describes the game you play together, which neither of you could play alone.

The 5th was also the house of children in the old books, and some astrologers still read it that way. I don't, here or anywhere: no chart, composite or natal, can say whether a pregnancy or a family will come ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and this site never reads a child's chart ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). Between adults, the 5th describes what the bond does for joy.

Composite Mercury stays close to the composite Sun in about 17 pairs out of 18, often in the same house or the next. In about 1 pair in 26 the composite Sun sits opposite instead, around the 11th, the house of friends and groups: play facing company, by the arithmetic of midpoints.

The house needs both birth times, and a method: the 5th's cusp is either the midpoint of your two natal 5th cusps or a product of the composite Midheaven at the place where you live, and the two can disagree. If one of you doesn't know a birth time, the house can't be known either; the birth-time article lists the places to look ([finding your time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#finding-your-time)).

The difficulty is the joke that stands in for the conversation. A bond this playful can deflect every serious subject with a quip, and laughter can become a way of not saying the hard thing. Wit can also turn competitive, each of you angling for the last word. It helps to keep some conversations joke-free, and to laugh afterwards rather than instead.

[Composite Mercury in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-4th-house/) keeps its talk at home; [composite Mercury in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-6th-house/) puts it to work. Composite charts aren't among the readings I offer; astrologers who use them read them with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and only for two adults who both agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
