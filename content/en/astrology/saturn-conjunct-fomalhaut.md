Saturn takes about two and a half years to cross a sign, so when it reaches Fomalhaut's degree in early Pisces it stays for months, and everyone born in those months shares the contact. Between 1930 and 2009 that happened three times: in 1935, in 1964–65 and, briefly, in 1994.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In my readings [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) describes what you build to stand up over time: commitments kept, rules you set yourself, the patience to do a thing properly even when nobody is checking. [Fomalhaut](/astrology/fomalhaut/) brings a royal title that doesn't survive much checking. The title entered astrology books through R. H. Allen's *Star Names* of 1899, with its four royal stars of the Persians, while the Zoroastrian commander of the southern sky it was meant to be is now more often identified with Antares. If Saturn has a question for any star, it is what the claim rests on, and here the answer is: not much.

The three stretches ran very differently ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)). In 1935 Saturn crossed the degree on 12 March and moved on; in the autumn it came back and stood still on 8 November, about half a degree short of the star, before setting off forward again, so births from mid-October to the end of November 1935 have Saturn almost motionless beside it. In 1964 it crossed on 1 May, turned back over the degree on 2 August, and made its last crossing on 22 January 1965. In 1994 there was a single crossing, on 2 March; later that year Saturn's retrograde loop brought it back towards the star, but it turned forward again on 9 November, nearly two degrees short.

Each stretch puts Saturn in the first degrees of Pisces ([Saturn in Pisces](/astrology/saturn-in-pisces/)), the Saturn sign of a whole generation each time; the star narrows it to a few months of births. Pisces is where Saturn's structures get built around compassion, and where the hard lesson is often where you end and other people begin.

The cycle also links the three groups. Anyone born under the 1935 pass found Saturn back on the star at their [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/) in 1964–65, those born then met it again in 1994, and the next return came in 2023, with crossings on 17 April and 23 August that year and on 12 January 2024.

In the sky, Saturn never came near. Fomalhaut lies 21° south of the ecliptic and Saturn keeps within a few degrees of it, so on each pass the planet went by about 19° to 20° north of the star.

Saturn held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook, one of three placements, with Chiron and Lilith, that held far more often than the rest; stars were never tested ([The three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). So what I'd stand behind in this contact is Saturn: its sign, its house, its aspects, and a lesson that takes its time. A timetable, not a wall.

The [Sun conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/sun-conjunct-fomalhaut/) comes with a birthday, the [Moon conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/moon-conjunct-fomalhaut/) with an hour, [Venus conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/venus-conjunct-fomalhaut/) and [Mars conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/mars-conjunct-fomalhaut/) with a few days; Saturn's comes with a season. Fomalhaut itself I leave out of every chart I read, and the method behind star contacts is in [conjunctions to fixed stars](/astrology/conjunctions-to-fixed-stars/).
