A synastry lays two charts side by side, so it's the one reading that is never only yours. The rule is short: the other person knows you're asking for it, and a colleague agrees. This page is about going further than the rule, and preparing the reading with them rather than for them.

## Step by step {#step-by-step}

1. **Ask them first.** Before anyone's birth details go into the order form, the other person must know you're asking for this reading, and a colleague must also agree to share theirs. The form asks you to confirm it. It isn't paperwork: their chart describes things they never chose to show you, the wound they keep circling among them ([Why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)). Asking often turns into a good conversation of its own.
2. **Check that you're both adults.** Both people must be 18 or over, in every kind of synastry, family included. I don't read about anyone younger, even inside a family reading ([Children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).
3. **Choose the bond together.** Couple, friends, co-workers or family: the method is the same, the questions aren't, and each bond has its own reading ([Synastry readings](/synastry/)). For a couple, I suggest waiting until you've been together at least a few months. A co-worker reading is for working better together, never for hiring, firing or promotion.
4. **Find both birth times.** Both are needed. The time fixes the houses, in each chart and in where each of you lands in the other's, so without both times there is no reading. Look for each one in a record rather than in memory: [How to find your birth time](/astrology/how-to-find-your-birth-time/) and [What changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) explain where, and why.
5. **Write your questions side by side.** The order form has an optional space for what you'd like the reading to look at. Fill it in together, in words you'd both sign: why some conversations flow and others knot, what each of you needs on a hard day, where you rub. A question that casts one of you as the problem isn't one to send: nobody is the villain of a chart.
6. **Know what the reading will look at.** It takes each chart on its own first, then the space between them: the contacts between your planets ([Synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/)), where each of you lands in the other's houses ([House overlays](/astrology/house-overlays/)), how you talk, comfort and repair, and the sensitive contacts of Saturn, Chiron and Lilith. It's written about both of you, and never against either.
7. **Read it together, at your own pace.** Sit down with it when you both have time. If something old comes up, stop there and come back to it another day. What you do with it afterwards is for the two of you to decide.

## Common mistakes {#common-mistakes}

- **Keeping it a surprise.** Even as a present, a synastry needs the other person to know before their details are sent: [Giving a reading as a gift](/astrology/giving-a-reading-as-a-gift/).
- **Using it as a test.** A synastry won't tell you whether a friend is a "real" friend, or a partner the right one. People show you that themselves, in time.
- **Waiting for a score.** There's no percentage. Every pair has easy contacts and hard ones; the reading says which are which, and that's far more use than a number ([Sun-sign compatibility, and why it isn't enough](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/)).
- **Hoping it will decide for you.** I won't tell either of you to stay, leave, forgive or cut anyone off. That decision belongs to the two of you.
- **Going to someone who doesn't ask.** An astrologer who never checks that the other person knows is showing you how they treat charts that aren't theirs ([How to tell a careful astrologer from a scam](/astrology/how-to-tell-a-careful-astrologer-from-a-scam/)).
- **Reading through fear.** If you don't feel safe with the other person, a reading isn't the place to start: please reach out now to someone who can help, and in an emergency, call the emergency services.
