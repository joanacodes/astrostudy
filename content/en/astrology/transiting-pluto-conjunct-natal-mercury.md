When Pluto reaches your Mercury, thinking goes to the root. For two to three years the planet of depth sits on the degree that takes in, sorts and speaks, and a subject, a question or a conversation can grip you the way few things have before.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) wants access to what lies underneath: hidden motives, buried assumptions, the real reason. Your [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) processes information, makes meaning and puts it into words. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two work as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your mind tends to dig. Small talk can feel thin. You may want to research one thing properly instead of skimming twenty, and an opinion you've held for years can be pulled up to see what it rests on. Words carry more weight, yours included: what you say in these years tends to be said with intent.

A mind that goes to the root can start treating whatever is hidden as truer than whatever is in plain sight. Reading between the lines is a skill; reading between every line is a habit, and sometimes the plain meaning was the real one. Reasoning isn't certainty either. The deepest thought of the season is worth checking like any other.

Born with [Mercury conjunct Pluto](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-pluto/), you know this depth from the inside, and you met the transit in your first years if Mercury stood just ahead of Pluto, or never.

With Mercury in the 3rd, the conjunction falls during Pluto's long stay in the house of words, learning and daily exchange ([Pluto transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-3rd-house/)), and conversations with siblings or neighbours can change in tone. The house comes from your birth time, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My timing allows from 1½° before exact to 1° after. Within that window Pluto stays on Mercury for about two years and eight months, and nearly four years in the longest passes. In three passes out of five it crosses the exact degree three times; in the others, five times, because it backs over the point twice.

Fewer than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 meet this conjunction by 80, and slightly fewer still meet the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-mercury/)). Most meet a square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-mercury/)) instead, and many the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-mercury/)) or the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-mercury/)).

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044, it reaches Mercurys in Aquarius. Mercury is never more than about 28° from the Sun, so a Mercury in Aquarius belongs to a birthday between about late December and mid-March.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write before you speak. The thinking of this conjunction often needs a draft, and a sentence that is right on the third try lands better than one said in the heat of the first.

Check what you find. Under Pluto a discovery can feel like a revelation; sources, dates and a second opinion keep it a discovery.

Give conclusions time. Pluto crosses this degree more than once, and an idea that still holds by the last pass has earned its place.
