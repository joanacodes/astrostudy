One of you wants to know where you'll both be next year; the other would like next year left open. In Sun opposite Uranus between two charts, the Uranus of one person stands across the zodiac from the other's Sun, six signs on, and each tends to hold the end the other lets go of.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is a self with a line through it: identity, vitality, a direction. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and liberates, reforming whatever has stopped working. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, each making the other easier to see. Among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it's one where two people can learn the most about themselves from the other's end.

Holding the Sun, you watch the other live a freedom you rarely give yourself. They show you what could be loosened, which habits are chosen and which merely inherited, and some of that is a relief. Around them your sense of direction grows clearer, if only because it's being questioned.

Holding Uranus, you find in the other a fixed point that makes your own movement visible. Their self-assurance is something to admire and to push off from, and with them your need for autonomy gets an answer instead of an argument.

What flows is complementarity: one keeps a centre, the other keeps the doors open, and a shared life needs both.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Then the see-saw starts. Requests for commitment, plans and consistency bring requests for room; room taken brings a tighter hold. Over time one becomes "the steady one" and the other "the free one", though each has both needs. Distance can then be read as rejection, and a request for plans as a cage, when neither is meant that way.

The other angles change the pressure: [Sun conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) puts the urge for freedom right on the Sun, [Sun square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-uranus-synastry/) turns it into interruptions, and [Sun trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus-synastry/) and [Sun sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus-synastry/) let it in as an ally.

Everyone born in the same seven years or so has Uranus where the Uranus person has it. What turns that common placement into this opposition is a birthday six signs away.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Each of you can carry a little of the other's end. The Sun person can try an experiment with no plan behind it; the Uranus person can keep one promise that matters to the other, however small. Agreed routines and agreed freedoms work better than either alone. A couple might keep one evening together and one apart each week; friends who live far apart, a yearly meeting; a family, room for the relative who moved away and the one who stayed; coworkers, a clear split between maintaining the system and redesigning it.

About 1 pair of people in 12 has this opposition, one way round or the other, within 8°. Without both birth times there is no reading. The hour rarely decides the opposition itself, the Sun gaining only about a degree in a day; what the hour settles is where the two ends land among the houses: whether the pull runs between home and career, say, or between one's own projects and the partnership ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one chart, [Sun opposite Uranus](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus/) describes someone who often meets their own freer side in other people first. Between two charts, that other person is right there, and the balance becomes something you can work out together.
