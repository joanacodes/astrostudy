Facts are easy to collect and harder to add up, and the North Node in Sagittarius is about the adding up. [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) widens whatever it touches, and the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) there points to a bigger view, a belief of your own, and the nerve to follow it.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

As mutable fire, Sagittarius is restless and generous, always looking past the horizon for what things mean. Across the chart, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) is the ground you know by heart: curiosity, quick wits, a talent for information and for seeing every side. You can argue any case. The harder part is saying which one you hold. The Sagittarius end asks you to stop gathering for a moment, look at what you've gathered, and decide.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the ruler of Sagittarius, shows by sign and house where your sense of meaning grows, and what kind of understanding you're reaching for.

The true node was in Sagittarius from September 1936 to March 1938, April 1955 to October 1956, October 1973 to July 1975, August 1992 to January 1994 and March 2011 to August 2012, and will be again from September 2029 to March 2031. Of everyone born between 1930 and 2009, about 1 person in 13 has it. It came over from Capricorn and went on to Scorpio, so a birthday near a change may call for [North Node in Capricorn](/astrology/north-node-in-capricorn/) or [North Node in Scorpio](/astrology/north-node-in-scorpio/) instead. There, too, the mean and true nodes can split across the boundary, a degree or two apart at most; I look at both, and I'll name the disagreement if your chart has one. Everyone born in the same eighteen months shares the sign. Its house, set by [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), is where it lands in your life.

My [testing notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), put in a sentence: its one angle, the rising sign, held about one time in three, and Saturn, Chiron and Lilith were the ones to stand out.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Between partners, the Gemini end talks, teases, analyses every message and keeps its options open. The Sagittarius end asks what the two of you believe in, and where you're going together. Honesty matters more here than cleverness: saying what you really think, kindly and plainly, and trusting your instinct about a person rather than collecting evidence for months.

A partner who shares your hunger for the wider world, a journey, a shared cause, a long conversation about what life is for, feeds this node.

## At work {#at-work}

Knowing a little about everything, you may be the colleague who can always find the answer. The North Node asks for the role where you choose the direction: strategy, teaching, publishing, training, work across borders or cultures. Committing to one path, a qualification, a long project, a field you go deep into, is part of the practice. So is giving an opinion in a meeting without three caveats in front of it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Indecision dressed up as open-mindedness is the old trap. One more article, one more opinion, one more option kept open, and the choice never quite gets made.

When a conviction does arrive, it can arrive too hard. A belief newly found can turn into a sermon, and a beginner's certainty is louder than an expert's. Let it settle. The Sagittarius end grows wise by living what it believes, not by announcing it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you believe about one thing that matters to you, in a sentence, and live by it for a season before you revise it. Take the course, plan the long trip, read the big book rather than the summary. Spend time with people whose world is different from yours.

And keep the Gemini end as your fact-checker. Curiosity is how a belief stays honest.
