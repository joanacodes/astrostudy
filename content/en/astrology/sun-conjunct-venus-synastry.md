When one person's Venus falls on the other's Sun, what one of you values lands squarely on who the other is. It's one of the warmest contacts two charts can make, and one of the easiest to mistake for romance: Venus is about valuing, and this contact belongs to friends, colleagues and relatives just as much as to partners.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is where a person's sense of self lives: identity, vitality, the wish to be recognised for who they are. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) evaluates: it decides what's worth having, what draws someone, what makes an exchange fair. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) binds two functions into a single active unit, each keeping its own job, and as in all [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), each job here belongs to a different person.

The two usually share a sign, so what the Venus person likes has the colour of who the Sun person is. A Libra Venus on a Libra Sun: a shared regard for grace and fairness. A Taurus Venus on a Taurus Sun: the same appetite for things that are good, solid and made to last.

If it's your Sun, you'll sense that they enjoy exactly what you are. Their liking doesn't feel like a reward for good behaviour; it attaches to your manner, your choices, the way you do things, and being valued like that tends to make you more fully yourself. If it's your Venus, their company is a pleasure in itself. Your sense of what's worth having recognises something in who they are, and appreciating them takes no effort at all.

The gift is recognition, in the Sun's own sense of the word: someone sees what you are and values it. Friends who love the same films, colleagues who hold the same standard of good work, siblings who still enjoy each other: any of them can know this contact.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

What blurs at this conjunction is approval and identity. The Sun person can start leaning on the Venus person's liking to feel like themselves, so that a cool day from the other lands harder than it should. The Venus person can shape their own taste around the Sun person, or keep the peace to keep the warmth, never saying what they don't like. As I read Venus, harmony is not the same as avoiding conflict, and appreciation that can't include a "not that" is only half of it.

Then there's reciprocity, which sits inside Venus's own definition: the appreciation mostly runs from Venus to Sun, and it's worth checking that something comes back. Elsewhere on the circle, the same two functions meet differently:

- **[Sun sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-venus-synastry/):** about two months between birthdays; pleasures offered sideways, taken up or not.
- **[Sun square Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-venus-synastry/):** liking with a condition attached; taste and identity at an angle.
- **[Sun trine Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-venus-synastry/):** appreciation that flows without a word, in signs of one element.
- **[Sun opposite Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-venus-synastry/):** the scales, with what one values facing who the other is.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the liking include an honest dislike now and then: "I love this, and I don't love that" keeps appreciation true. The Sun person can return it in words, saying what they value in the other, so the warmth runs both ways. Between colleagues, that can mean asking for real criticism of work you both admire; in a couple or a family, choosing the next outing in turn.

About 1 pair of people in 12 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 8°. Since Venus keeps within about 47° of its own Sun, the two people's birthdays are usually about a month apart, and rarely more than seven or eight weeks, whatever the years.

Here the birth time weighs little on the aspect itself, unless it sits right at the limit of the orb; it decides the houses, and with them the areas of life where the appreciation shows, and I give no reading without both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). On its own, in one chart, [Sun conjunct Venus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-venus/) puts values at the centre of a single self. Between two people the valuing comes from outside, so I read it with both of them or not at all ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
