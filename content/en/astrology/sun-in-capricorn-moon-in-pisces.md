A Capricorn Sun builds; a Pisces Moon dreams, and feels for everyone in the room. You were born under a young crescent a few evenings after the new moon, and I read the pair as steady hands around a tender imagination, each the better for the other.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Pisces lies two signs on from Capricorn: a sextile by sign, mutable water beside cardinal earth. The [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) takes responsibility and works to a plan; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) restores itself through what can't be measured: the hour by the water, the story reread for the comfort of knowing how it ends, the kindness done without anyone knowing. A sextile offers cooperation if you take it up, and this one is easy to take up: the Sun gives compassion a timetable, and the Moon gives ambition a reason that isn't status.

I read both of Pisces's rulers here. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern one, shows where your edges soften most and where imagination runs deepest; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the traditional one, shows where feeling finds meaning, and Jupiter has its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Capricorn, your Sun's sign. So the traditional ruler of your Moon is weakest on the Sun's ground: meaning has to make room for itself inside a practical life. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), for its part, has no dignity in Pisces, and neither light has one here. Neptune spends about fourteen years in a sign; its house, from your birth time, is the personal part.

This is a crescent birth. Your Moon had moved 31° to 89° ahead of the Sun, two and a half to seven days after the new moon: a growing sliver low in the west after sunset. The [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) takes almost nine of these births in ten as eight phases count them, the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase the rest, and near 60° the sextile by sign becomes an exact waxing [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). A crescent birth, as I read it, is a need finding its first form, and a Pisces Moon's first form is usually care. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the crescent.

About 1 person in 145 was born with a Capricorn Sun and a Pisces Moon. If the Moon left Aquarius or reached Aries on your birthday, the time is what separates this page from the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-aquarius/) and the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-aries/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign comes from the same time, and with the two lights it gives only the first sketch of the chart.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love protectively and with great tenderness. The Pisces Moon senses what a partner feels before it's said; the Capricorn Sun makes sure the practical side is handled, so care arrives as both comfort and help. The risk is carrying too much: other people's moods through the Moon, other people's responsibilities through the Sun. A partner who notices when you're depleted, and who is steady enough that you don't have to absorb their weather, lets both lights rest.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that is both structured and humane suits this pair: care services run well, music or art practised with discipline, charities, teaching, any institution that looks after people. The Sun brings method, stamina and the capacity to lead; the Moon brings empathy, imagination and a sense of what a person or a room needs. Harsh, purely competitive places drain the Moon, and chaos frustrates the Sun. Protect some quiet in every working day.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two kinds of giving can leave little for yourself. The Sun shoulders duties, the Moon absorbs feelings, and boundaries come late because each light expects the other to set them. Tiredness can show up as a low mood you can't place. What helps is shape and retreat together: a routine that includes solitude, a clear no now and then, and work that ends at a set hour, so that the Moon's tenderness has somewhere safe to come back to.
