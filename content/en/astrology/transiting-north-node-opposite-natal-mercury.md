Here it is the South Node that arrives: as the North Node opposes your Mercury, its partner crosses the degree itself. For about a month your most practised ways of thinking are the ones in the light, and the less familiar view stands across the chart, often in someone else's voice.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is your thinking and its traffic: what you notice, how you reason, how you explain. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), crossing it now, consolidates; it marks skills so practised that they run on their own. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) faces it, and an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two ends face to face, so that each grows clearer against the other. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the habits of your mind become visible from the outside.

Mental habits are useful; you couldn't get through a day without them. The month shows which have hardened into scripts: the joke that always comes, the argument you win without listening, the way of explaining yourself that people have stopped hearing. Across the chart another view is on offer, and it tends to come from someone who thinks nothing like you. Hearing it out to the end is most of the work.

With your [Mercury in the 4th house](/astrology/mercury-in-4th-house/), the South Node crosses your 4th and the North Node your 10th ([North Node transiting the 10th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-10th-house/)): the family's ways of talking, the old arguments at the kitchen table, meet the voice you need at work and in public. Which pair of houses the axis crosses is set by your birth time, so that is where any reading of this transit has to start ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The South Node's pass over your Mercury, inside my window of 1½° before exact and 1° after, usually lasts about a month, and a tenth are over within three and a half weeks. The true node keeps the Sun's time rather than its own: it nearly halts when the Sun reaches the nodes, so in an eclipse season a single pass can stretch to three and a half months or more, as a tenth of them do, and about one in twenty-five touches Mercury's degree three times. June 2029 shows the long kind. A Mercury born near 5° Cancer has the South Node on it from early May to mid-August, and at the total lunar eclipse of 26 June the Sun stands at 4°49′ Cancer, with the eclipsed Moon opposite ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Halfway through each round, nine years or so after the North Node itself crossed your Mercury ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-mercury/)), it is the South Node's turn. Between the two lie [Transiting North Node sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-mercury/), [Transiting North Node square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-mercury/) and [Transiting North Node trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-mercury/), which the node makes on its way round. By 80 everyone has had four of these oppositions, and some five. Born with [Mercury opposite North Node](/astrology/mercury-opposite-north-node/), you will find this transit and your nodal return falling in the same weeks.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Listen to one argument all the way through before answering it, ideally from someone you usually disagree with.

Retire a stock phrase. Notice the sentence you say most often about yourself, and find another way to say it.

Read off your usual shelf for a month: a field, a genre or a writer you would normally pass by.
