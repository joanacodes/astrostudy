A Leo Moon wants to make something and have it seen; a Pisces Sun is often happiest when its work speaks and its name stays quiet. You were born a few days before a full moon, the Moon nearly round in the evening sky. I read the pair as a gentle maker who still needs, now and then, applause from someone who matters.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and fixed fire, five signs apart: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, with no element, mode or polarity in common, so each keeps adjusting to the other. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) builds its sense of self from imagination and compassion, and doesn't much mind who gets the credit; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) is warmed by generosity that comes back to it, by being chosen, thanked and occasionally made a fuss of. You may give without a name for months and then feel hurt that nobody noticed, which is not a contradiction but two lights asking for different things.

Leo belongs to the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), which here stands in Pisces, the sign least concerned with being seen, so the light your Moon looks to for direction is itself content to work unnoticed. Pisces brings Neptune and Jupiter into the picture, and I read both: [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) for where your imagination asks to be given a form, [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) for where your generosity finds a larger purpose. Neither light holds a dignity; the link that counts is the Moon living in the Sun's own sign.

The Moon was 122° to 179° ahead of the Sun and still growing, ten to almost fifteen days after the new moon. The [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) phase is where seven in eight of these births fall, the earliest being [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) births, and the exact quincunx at 150° lies inside the range; the full moon that followed, within about five days, was usually a [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/). I read the gibbous phase as refining before revealing, and a Leo Moon does exactly that: it wants what it shows to be ready, and lovely. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) covers the days before the full.

Births with this pair run at about 1 in 149. When the Moon crossed from Cancer into Leo, or from Leo into Virgo, on your birthday, the hour alone separates this page from the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-cancer/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-virgo/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)); a sign holds the Moon for only about two and a half days. The hour also gives the houses, and with a Leo Moon, the house of your Sun tells me where your heart wants to be recognised.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love romantically and generously, and you need to be adored back in plain, visible ways. The Pisces Sun gives, forgives and senses a partner's moods; the Leo Moon needs to be chosen, praised, celebrated on the right day. The risk is giving so selflessly that the Moon's need goes unspoken, then feeling unseen. Ask for the gesture you want: a partner can't applaud what they don't know you need.

## At work {#at-work}

Performing arts, music, writing, creative direction, teaching, hospitality: work that lets imagination take the stage suits you. The Pisces Sun brings the vision and the empathy; the Leo Moon brings warmth and presence. Anonymous work drains the Moon over time, so make sure what you create carries your name somewhere, even if the Sun would rather it didn't.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The quincunx keeps you adjusting between modesty and the wish to shine. You may hide your best work until it's perfect, which it never quite is, or feel ashamed of needing praise at all. The gibbous phase, to me, is about finishing and then showing: set a date, show the work, accept the thanks. Being seen is a need for this Moon, not a vanity.
