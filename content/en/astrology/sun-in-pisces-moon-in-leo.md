A Leo Moon wants to make something and have it seen; a Pisces Sun is often happiest when its work speaks and its name stays quiet. Your Moon was nearly full when you were born, still filling out, with a few nights to go. I see a gentle maker here, one who needs applause now and then, from someone who matters.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water, fixed fire, five signs between them: by sign a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/), the two signs sharing nothing, neither element, mode nor polarity, so neither light can take the other for granted. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) builds its sense of self from imagination and compassion, and doesn't much mind who gets the credit; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) is warmed by generosity that comes back to it, by being chosen, thanked and occasionally made a fuss of. You may give without a name for months and then feel hurt that nobody noticed, which is not a contradiction but two lights asking for different things.

Leo's ruler is the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) itself, and yours stands in Pisces, a sign that cares little for the spotlight, so the light your Moon looks to for direction is content to work unnoticed. Pisces brings Neptune and Jupiter into the picture, and I read both: [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) where your imagination wants a form to live in, [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) where your generosity finds a larger purpose. Neither light holds a dignity; what ties them is the Moon living in the Sun's own sign.

The Moon led the Sun by 122° to 179° and was still filling, from day ten of the lunar month to the last hours before full. The [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) phase holds all but an eighth of these births; the earliest, before 135°, still belong to the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/). The exact quincunx, 150°, falls within the range, and the next full moon, no more than five days away, would as a rule be a [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/). The gibbous phase, as I understand it, polishes before it shows, and a Leo Moon wants the same: whatever it offers should be ready, and lovely. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) places you on one side of 135° or the other.

Roughly 1 person in 149 is born with a Pisces Sun and a Leo Moon. The Moon, as [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon) explains, keeps to a sign for two and a half days or so. If it entered Leo from Cancer, or left Leo for Virgo, on your day of birth, three neighbouring pages, this one, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-cancer/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-virgo/), stay open until your birth hour closes two of them. The houses wait on the hour as well, and because the Sun rules your Moon's sign, the house your Sun stands in shows me where your heart most wants recognition.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love romantically and generously, and you need to be adored back in plain, visible ways. Your Sun forgives quickly and reads a partner's moods before they're spoken; your Moon wants to be picked out, praised and celebrated on the right day, out loud. The risk is giving so selflessly that the Moon's need goes unspoken, then feeling unseen. Ask for the gesture you want: a partner can't applaud what they don't know you need.

## At work {#at-work}

Performing arts, music, writing, creative direction, teaching, hospitality: you do well where imagination gets a stage. Pisces gives you the vision and the empathy, Leo the warmth and the presence. Anonymous work drains the Moon over time, so make sure what you create carries your name somewhere, even if the Sun would rather it didn't.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The quincunx keeps you adjusting between modesty and the wish to shine. You may hide your best work until it's perfect, which it never quite is, or feel ashamed of needing praise at all. Here the phase's lesson is to finish and then show: set a date, show the work, accept the thanks. Being seen feeds this Moon; wanting it is nothing to apologise for.
