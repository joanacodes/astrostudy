Chiron opposite your Sun works from across the table. Once or twice in a life, someone in front of you, a partner, a colleague, a person you're helping, brings an old tender subject about being yourself into the light, and you see it in them before you see it in you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is identity and agency: the self you put forward and hope will be recognised. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a limitation you adapted to, and the know-how that came from it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates two functions through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) awareness tends to arrive from outside. You may find yourself coaching someone through the very doubt you carried at their age, and doing it better than you ever did it for yourself. Or a person whose opinion matters to you fails to see you the way you need, and an old ache about recognition answers before you do.

The opposition shows both ends at once, which is its use. The helper and the helped are often the same person at different hours, and people who are good at caring are usually slower at letting themselves be cared for. These months make that imbalance visible. What tends to come out of them is a self that needs other people's reactions a little less, and can take their help a little more.

The houses show which relationships carry it, and they rest on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Sun in the 1st, Chiron opposes it from your 7th ([Chiron transiting the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-7th-house/)): partners, clients and close collaborators become the mirror. If your chart holds [Sun opposite Chiron](/astrology/sun-opposite-chiron/), this transit was there at your birth and comes back at your Chiron return, around 50.

None of this forecasts how anyone will treat you, or whether a bond lasts. It describes what you may notice in yourself through them.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, half of all passes last more than about ten months. One in ten is over in under three weeks, a single crossing at Chiron's quicker pace; one in ten runs past twenty-one months, when its loops are slow and overlapping. In between, the common shape is three exact crossings over about ten and a half months.

There is one opposition point, so each round of Chiron's, about fifty years, brings one. If the first comes in your twenties or earlier, the second arrives before 80; most people meet two, everyone at least one. The conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-sun/)) keeps the same count, while the sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-sun/)), square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-sun/)) and trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-sun/)), with two points each, come about three times.

At the end of 2026, Chiron faces the Suns of birthdays around 16 to 22 October. Through Taurus, from 2027 to 2033, it moves along the Scorpio birthdays, four or five days a year, from late October to the third week of November; Sagittarius Suns take their turn while it crosses Gemini.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice whose reactions you've been organising your plans around, and ask whether they still deserve that weight.

When you help someone with a sore spot like yours, listen to the advice you give them, and try a little of it yourself.

Ask one person you trust what they see in you that you don't, and write the answer down.
