When Neptune reaches the point opposite your Jupiter, what you believe meets, face to face, what other people dream of. It lasts about two and a half years, comes once at most, and passes a little under half of all lives by.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is your sense of meaning: the principles, beliefs and hopes that make experience add up. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the longing for something larger, and the readiness to merge with it. Across an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) two functions face each other and become aware of each other, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your beliefs tend to meet their counterpart in other people: a teacher who seems to know, a movement that seems to have the answer, a partner whose faith is more vivid than yours.

The pull can be generous and widening. It can also lead you to hand your own judgement to someone else's vision, or to idealise a person for what they seem to stand for. A belief isn't a dogma and a teacher isn't the truth: the opposition asks you to take in what others see without giving up the right to weigh it. What you admire in a guide often tells you what you're ready to grow into yourself.

I don't read it as a warning against anyone, or as a forecast of a meeting, a loss or a gain.

With Jupiter in your 9th, the opposition comes from your 3rd, always the house facing it ([Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/)): what you hear and read every day pulls on what you believe about the world. That pair of houses, like every house, is drawn from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Jupiter opposite Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-neptune/), as at the start of 1965, you had this transit in your first months, and it won't come back in a lifetime.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, the opposition fills about two years and seven months, twenty-two at the least, and reaches exactness three times in most passes, five in about one in four.

A little under half of all births between 1930 and 2009 never bring it before 80; for the rest it comes once, at ages as varied as Jupiter's place: in the first months for the start of 1965, about 27 for 2005, 40 for 1955, 58½ for 1995 and 78 for 1945, and not before 80 for 1935, 1975 or 1985.

For someone born at the start of 2005, [transiting Neptune trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-jupiter/) came in the first months, this opposition comes at about 27 and [transiting Neptune square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-jupiter/) at about 67½. For them [transiting Neptune sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) lie beyond 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Learn from teachers without handing them your judgement. Ask, compare, and keep the right to disagree.

Notice what you admire in the people you idealise, and practise it yourself.

Let a cause earn your loyalty over time. Under this opposition the vision arrives fast; the evidence takes longer.
