Uranus sextile Pluto came twice in the eighty years of the count: in the 1940s, on the way to the two planets' meeting of the 1960s, and in the 1990s, on the way out of it. Two generations share it, and through your houses it becomes an opening of your own.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, liberation and the reform of systems that have grown too tight. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) enables transformation and regeneration: it lets what is obsolete go and returns a deeper kind of strength. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperation, a link that activates when something engages it and stays quiet when nothing does.

In plain words, the wish to try something new and the capacity to let something old end can help each other. A change you make reaches further down than you expected; a long letting-go finds a new form instead of leaving a gap. Neither planet is destruction, and this aspect shows it well: reform that respects the roots, renewal that doesn't need a clean sweep. But the opening asks for you. Potential is not the same as a result.

The signs give each generation its own accent. In the 1940s, Uranus in Gemini and Pluto in Leo joined air and fire: change through ideas and words, renewal through self-expression. In the 1990s, Uranus in Capricorn and Pluto in Scorpio joined earth and water, and then, from the middle of the decade, Uranus in Aquarius and Pluto in Sagittarius joined air and fire again.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 7 born between 1930 and 2009 has this sextile within 6°, the most of any Uranus–Pluto aspect in the count. The first run lasted from May 1941 to February 1947, without a break from March 1942 to May 1946; the second from April 1991 to February 2001, without a break from January 1993 to April 1999. The rest of the eighty years has none.

The second run lasted longer. Pluto had passed the nearest point of its orbit to the Sun in 1989 and was moving at its quickest, so Uranus, the faster of the two, took longer to pull away. Many people born from 1991 to 1996 also carry [Uranus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune/).

What makes it yours is the houses ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). Uranus in the 5th sextile Pluto in the 3rd, for example, could let a creative independence renew the way you think and speak. The houses come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/):** born from 1963 to 1968, in Virgo; the two urges fused.
- **[Uranus square Pluto](/astrology/uranus-square-pluto/):** born from 1930 to 1936, and for a few months of 2009; the same urges in friction.
- **[Uranus trine Pluto](/astrology/uranus-trine-pluto/):** no birth from 1930 to 2009.
- **[Uranus opposite Pluto](/astrology/uranus-opposite-pluto/):** no birth in those years either.

In [Uranus sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto-synastry/), one person's appetite for change gives the other's depth an outlet, and the other's depth gives that change some weight. I read it both ways.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-pluto/) comes twice in Uranus's 84-year round; it lasts months, often in three passes as Uranus turns retrograde. [Transiting Pluto sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-uranus/) depends on Pluto's uneven pace, 12 to 31 years in a sign, and can stay within orb for years.

Weather, not a forecast: these are seasons when a door opens between changing something and letting something go, and you decide whether to walk through.
