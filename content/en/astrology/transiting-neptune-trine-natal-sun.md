Neptune's trine to your Sun is the easiest contact the two can make. For a little over two and a half years, what you aim for and what you imagine stop competing; the only risk is that the ease goes unused.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) widens and inspires; your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) directs, the centre your purpose and vitality come from. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets two functions move together and leaves each its own shape, which matters with Neptune: under a trine the self isn't dissolved, it's enlarged. During this [transit](/astrology/transits/), work that serves something beyond you, art, care or a spiritual practice, fits into your days without a fight, and your direction may ask less of you in effort and more in attention. Purpose isn't status, and the trine makes it easier to notice which of your aims were only ever about status, and to let them go without a fight. Recognition isn't fame either: this transit can bring a quieter kind, being known for kindness or for work that helps.

Neptune is in Aries, a fire sign, from 2026 to 2039, so its trines reach Suns in the other two: [Leo](/astrology/leo/), birthdays from about 23 July to 22 August, and [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), from about 22 November to 21 December, the early degrees of each first.

With your Sun in the 9th and Neptune crossing your 5th ([Neptune transiting the 5th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-5th-house/)), study or travel and creative play feed each other; with the Sun in the 2nd and Neptune in the 6th, what you value and how you spend your working days line up. Your birth time places both houses, and a guessed time would place them wrongly ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine lasts about two years and seven months, around twenty-two months when the loops are tight. Three exact hits are usual, in about three passes out of four; the rest have five, and those five-hit passes are the shorter ones, about twenty-two months from first touch to last.

Most people born between 1930 and 2009 see it once in 80 years, a few see it twice, and about 1 in 6 not at all. Approaching your Sun, Neptune makes the trine first, ahead of the square ([transiting Neptune square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-sun/)), the sextile ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-sun/)) and the conjunction ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-sun/)); past the Sun it comes after the second square, with the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-sun/)) next. With [Sun trine Neptune](/astrology/sun-trine-neptune/) in your birth chart, the other trine lies at least a third of the circle away, more than fifty years of Neptune's motion.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put the ease to work on something you've been postponing: a creative project, a cause, a slower way of working.

Make gentleness a method rather than a mood, and give it hours in the week.

Don't wait to be pushed. A trine from Neptune won't insist, and two and a half years can slip by unnoticed.
