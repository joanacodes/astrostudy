Aquarius on the 9th house cusp gives the sign of the future the house of belief, study and the far horizon. You tend to trust the conclusions you've reasoned your way to, and to look for meaning in ideas that are still arriving rather than in the ones already settled.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is where you go, in miles or in thought, to understand more, and tradition called it the God. With [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) on its cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), belief is something you build with other people and test in argument. You may have left behind what you were taught and kept whatever survived your questions, and a doctrine that forbids questions loses you quickly. Learning often happens outside the lecture hall: in a reading group, through a network of people who know more than you, or by teaching yourself late at night. Abroad, you're drawn less to sights than to people: the friend in another city, the project that needs hands, the conference where strangers argue as equals. When you teach or write, you'd rather start a discussion than deliver a sermon.

I read both rulers of your beliefs, and they pull in different directions. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) marks where your views change in one leap, often after a journey, a book or a conversation that turned something over; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) marks where beliefs are built slowly, the long course of study, the conviction earned over years. In nearly 9 London charts in 10, Cancer rises with this cusp and Capricorn sits on the Descendant, so Saturn rules your partners as well as your beliefs, and a close bond is often where your views get tested and widened; the Moon then rules the chart. Look for each ruler's house to see which part of life feeds your convictions, as ruler of the 9th in the [1st](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Roughly 1 birth in 11 has Aquarius here, at London and New York alike, in the Placidus houses I use. Pisces usually follows on the Midheaven, in about 3 London charts in 4 and 4 New York charts in 5, which hands your public direction to Neptune and to Jupiter, the 9th house's own natural ruler. In about 1 London chart in 4, Aquarius reaches the Midheaven itself and opens both the 9th and the 10th, and in about 2 in 5 it opens the 8th as well ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); either way the same two rulers speak for more than one part of your life. Every London chart with this cusp has a pair of intercepted signs somewhere, and in about 1 London chart in 7, Aquarius is one of them, with no cusp at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

A cusp this high in the chart still changes sign every couple of hours: a little later in the day, Aquarius would open your 8th instead ([Aquarius on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-8th-house-cusp/)); a little earlier, your Midheaven ([Aquarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)). The time of birth decides, and I don't read a chart without one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
