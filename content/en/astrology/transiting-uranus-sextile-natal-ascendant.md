Uranus sextiles your Ascendant about fourteen years before it crosses it, and again about fourteen years after. Each time, for a year and a half or so, a different way of meeting the world is within reach, and the door opens only as far as you push it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates: it questions inherited arrangements and tries out new ones. Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is your orientation, the first move you make, before thought catches up, and the manner people meet first. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, joins them through opportunity, and opportunity needs a taker. So this [transit](/astrology/transits/) doesn't change how you come across; it makes changing it cheaper. A new style, a new way of introducing yourself or of starting work, a habit of hanging back you could drop: each costs less effort than usual.

Your Descendant lies opposite the Ascendant, so the same Uranus trines it. A change of manner made now tends to sit comfortably with the people you meet one to one, rather than asking them to choose between the old you and the new.

The two sextiles arrive from opposite sides of the horizon. The one before the crossing usually arrives while Uranus is in your 10th or 11th house in Placidus, the house system I use: a group, a network or a public role calls for a version of you that hasn't had much use ([Uranus transiting the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-11th-house/)). The one after usually comes from your 2nd or 3rd, once Uranus has crossed your rising degree: everyday exchanges, learning and the people nearby give the new manner somewhere to practise ([Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus spends about twenty months within reach of the sextile, sometimes only eleven. In about thirteen passes out of fourteen, Uranus touches the exact degree three times, its retrograde months taking it back over the sextile in between.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have met it twice, and everyone at least once. It is the nearest step on either side of [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-ascendant/). [Transiting Uranus square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-ascendant/) comes about seven years further from the crossing, [transiting Uranus trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-ascendant/) seven years beyond that, and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-ascendant/) marks the halfway point of the round.

Your rising degree sets the dates, and it moves fast: about one degree for every four minutes of clock time, on average, while Uranus covers about four degrees a year. Fifteen minutes' error in the birth time can shift this transit by most of a year, one reason I insist on the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In these years, Uranus in Gemini sextiles [Aries rising](/astrology/aries-rising/) from below the horizon, some fourteen years after crossing it between 2010 and 2019, and [Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/) from above, ahead of a crossing that waits until after 2039 ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)). Taurus and Virgo rising follow while Uranus is in Cancer, from 2032 to 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick one visible thing to change and try it while the window is open: how you introduce yourself, how you dress for a first meeting, how you begin a piece of work. Twenty months is long enough to see whether it fits.

Say yes to the invitation that asks for a less familiar version of you. A sextile offers; the rest is yours to do.

Notice what changed without effort. Easy transits are the ones people forget to count, and a manner that settled in quietly is still a change you made.
