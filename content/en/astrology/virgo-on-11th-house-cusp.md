Friendships, networks, the causes you join and the long-term aims you share with others: that's the 11th house. With Virgo on the 11th house cusp, you show up for all of it with something useful in hand.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) gives the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) its practical turn ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). I read friendship here as help freely given: you're the one who turns up with tools, remembers the deadline, keeps the club's accounts or the committee's minutes. Friendships often grow around shared work or a shared skill, and a group appeals most when it has something to do. Your aims tend to come broken into steps: not "one day", but a list, a timetable, the first small task. Tradition gave the 11th to Jupiter's joy, and Virgo, where Jupiter is in detriment, trades the house's expansiveness for usefulness: hopes sized to be reachable. The risk is being valued for what you do more than for who you are, or quietly carrying the group's work while privately criticising its disorder. Opposite, Pisces opens the 5th, so your pleasures tend to be looser and dreamier than your friendships, and that's often where you rest from being useful.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your 11th house, and the house it occupies shows where friendships and plans take shape: Mercury as ruler of the 11th may be found in the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). More than 4 London charts in 5 with this cusp have Leo on the Midheaven ([Leo on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/)), so the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules your public life while Mercury rules your circles. The two can never be more than about 28° apart, which keeps your work and your friendships close in the chart: colleagues become friends, and friends bring work. Gemini, Mercury's other sign, opens the 9th or the 8th in most London charts, adding study and travel, or shared resources, to the houses Mercury speaks for.

In Placidus houses, as I draw them, about one London birth in eleven has Virgo opening the 11th, and one New York birth in twelve. Virgo climbs slowly in the east, and the cusps above the Ascendant hold it longer than the western ones do: about 131 minutes a day on this one at London. Scorpio or Libra rises in these charts, about evenly at London: with Libra, Venus rules the chart and new people come easily; with Scorpio, Mars and Pluto rule it (I read both), and friendships take longer to form but run deep. The 11th is narrow in these charts, 24° to 28° at London, so now and then Virgo runs on to the 12th cusp as well, in about 1 London chart in 11 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); on other days Virgo opens nothing at all, an [intercepted sign](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) in about 1 London birth in 6.

If your recorded time is a little out, you may belong on a neighbouring page: [Virgo on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-12th-house-cusp/) lies just before yours in the day, [Virgo on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/) just after. A few minutes decide it, so I work only from a recorded time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
