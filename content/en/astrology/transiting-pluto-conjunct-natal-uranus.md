When Pluto reaches the degree of your natal Uranus, the planet of renewal merges with the part of you that wants to be free and different. Only people born after the mid-1960s ever meet it, and the later the birth, the later it comes.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees: it is where you need room to live your own way, question what you inherited and try something new. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms at depth, eliminating structures that have become obsolete. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into one process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your freedom itself is reworked. Independence you once claimed by breaking away may need to become the independence of building something that lasts; a cause you followed by instinct may need a reason you can stand behind.

Uranus's blind spots sharpen here: confusing novelty with innovation, and assuming that disruption automatically produces progress. Pluto adds its own, confusing intensity with truth, and together they can make a dramatic break feel like the only honest move. Reform is not destruction, though, and autonomy is not isolation. People who come through this well usually change the system they are in rather than walking out of every system.

Born with [Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/), between 1963 and 1968, you met this transit as a baby, unless Pluto had already passed your Uranus when you were born. The two planets were exactly aligned three times, at about 16° to 17° Virgo: in October 1965, April 1966 and July 1966.

Your Uranus's house says what kind of freedom is at stake. With Uranus in your 11th, it is the friendships, groups and causes you belong to, during Pluto's long stay in that house ([Pluto transiting the 11th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-11th-house/)); with Uranus in the 2nd, the way you support yourself and what you own asks to change form. Any planet close to your Uranus at birth is reached in the same few years. Which house your Uranus falls in depends on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Pluto stays on your Uranus for about two years and eight months, twenty-two months in the quickest passes. Three exact hits in about three passes out of five, five in the rest.

Who meets it goes back to that meeting in Virgo. Uranus, round the zodiac in 84 years, has pulled ahead of Pluto ever since, a little further each year, so the later you were born, the further Pluto has to travel to reach your Uranus. For births on 1 January, it came within orb at 8.9 in 1975, 16 in 1985 and 25.1 in 1995, and comes at 39.3 for 2005; the last births counted, in 2009, meet it at about 53. Everyone born from 1930 to the mid-1960s, with Uranus behind Pluto, never meets it: a little under half of the births counted.

Pluto's years in Aquarius, until early 2044, bring it to everyone born with Uranus in Aquarius, from 1996 to 2004, between about 26 and 40 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)).

The same geometry sets the other contacts. For births of the mid-1960s, Pluto's square to natal Uranus ([Transiting Pluto square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-uranus/)) came at about 36, alongside their [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/), and its trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-uranus/)) at about 49. The sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-uranus/)) reaches about 5 people in 6, the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-uranus/)) about 2 in 5.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tell freedom from escape. Ask of each change you want whether it moves you towards something or only away.

Reform one system you depend on from the inside: a way of working, a household rule, a group you belong to. This transit rewards changing something you are willing to stay with.

Keep what still works. Uranus is quick to change a system before understanding it, and Pluto's slowness gives you time to understand first.
