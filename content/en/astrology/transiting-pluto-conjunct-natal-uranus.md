Pluto's arrival on your natal Uranus merges the planet of renewal with the part of you that wants to be free and different. Only people born after the mid-1960s ever meet it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees: it is where you need room to live your own way, question what you inherited and try something new. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms at depth, eliminating structures that have become obsolete. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into one process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your freedom itself is reworked. Independence you once claimed by breaking away may need to become the independence of building something that lasts; a cause you followed by instinct may need a reason you can stand behind.

Uranus's blind spots sharpen here: confusing novelty with innovation, and assuming that disruption automatically produces progress. Pluto adds its own, confusing intensity with truth, and together they can make a dramatic break feel like the only honest move. Reform is not destruction, though, and autonomy is not isolation.

Born with [Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/), between 1963 and 1968, you met this transit as a baby, unless Pluto had already passed your Uranus when you were born. The two planets were exactly aligned three times, in Virgo: in October 1965, April 1966 and July 1966.

Your Uranus's house says what kind of freedom is at stake. With Uranus in your 11th, it is the friendships, groups and causes you belong to, during Pluto's long stay in that house ([Pluto transiting the 11th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-11th-house/)); with Uranus in the 2nd, the way you support yourself and what you own asks to change form. It is the birth time that puts Uranus in one house rather than another ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Pluto stays on your Uranus for about two years and eight months, counting from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use; the quickest passes take about twenty-two months. It touches the exact degree three times in most passes and five times in about two in five.

Who meets it goes back to that meeting in Virgo. Uranus, round the zodiac in 84 years, has pulled ahead of Pluto ever since, a little further each year, so the later you were born, the further Pluto has to travel to reach your Uranus. Someone born on 1 January 1975 entered the orb at 8.9, and someone born on 1 January 1995 at 25.1; on 1 January 2005, the age is 39.3, and the last births counted, in 2009, meet it at about 53. Everyone born from 1930 to the mid-1960s, with Uranus behind Pluto, never meets it: a little under half of the births counted.

Right now it belongs to people born with Uranus in Aquarius, from 1996 to 2004: Pluto, in Aquarius until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), reaches them between about 26 and 40.

The same geometry sets the other contacts. Pluto's square to natal Uranus ([Transiting Pluto square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-uranus/)) arrived at about 36 for births of the mid-1960s, with the [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/), and its trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-uranus/)) at about 49. As for the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-uranus/)), it reaches about 5 people in 6, and the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-uranus/)) about 2 in 5.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tell freedom from escape. Ask of each change you want whether it moves you towards something or only away.

Reform one system you depend on from the inside, at work, at home or in a group: this transit rewards changing something you are willing to stay with.

Keep what still works. Uranus is quick to change a system before understanding it, and Pluto's slowness gives you time to understand first.
