Their Mars in your 11th house brings another person's drive into your friendships, your groups and the plans you hold for the future. Around them, hopes have a way of turning into projects, and a circle of friends into a team with something to do.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is initiative and effort aimed at a goal. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds the people you choose to belong with: friends, networks, communities, the collaborators behind a shared aim, and the shape you hope the years ahead will take. Their Mars, in this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), gets your collective life moving.

With them, you may finally act on the plan the group keeps discussing: organise the event, start the club, join the campaign. They recruit you, and you may find yourself braver among people, standing up for a friend or a cause with an energy that surprises you.

For them, your circles are a place to put their drive to use. They may feel effective among your friends, take on a role in your groups, or champion a hope you've told them about. Succedent houses build on the angle before them, and the 11th gathers allies around what the 10th set in motion; its natural sign is [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), ruled by Saturn in tradition and by Uranus in modern astrology, and I read both. Tradition knew it as the Good Spirit, and the planet said to rejoice there is Jupiter, not Mars: their drive works in a house tradition kept for friends and benefactors.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A group can only move as fast as its slowest agreement. Their push may clash with the pace of your friends, or volunteer you for more than you meant to take on, and a friendship can slide into rivalry over who leads the team. You might also feel that your hopes are being turned into their projects.

From their side, your circles may not move when they push. They can feel like the outsider urging everyone on, or sense your friends closing ranks against their energy.

Community isn't conformity, and friendship isn't a contest of influence. This overlay shows where two people's drive and belonging meet, not who will be popular or who will be left out.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give their energy a real role, and keep yours. A clear part in the group's plan, a task they can lead, lets their drive serve the circle instead of steering it. In a couple, share some friends and keep some apart; between friends, a common cause or a team sport can carry this overlay at its best; between colleagues, it suits building a network or running a project with many hands; between adult relatives, they may be the one who organises the family gathering, provided the others get a say.

If the roles were reversed, with your Mars in their 11th house, you'd be the one energising their friendships and testing their groups' patience. [Their Mars in your 10th house](/astrology/mars-in-10th-house-synastry/) works on your public role; [their Mars in your 12th house](/astrology/mars-in-12th-house-synastry/) slips into your solitude and the hours no one sees.

Your birth time places the cusp of your 11th, and in Placidus, the house system I use, that cusp is one of the in-between ones, worked out from your time and the latitude where you were born. Mars moves only a degree or two in a few days, so anyone born the same week as them would put it in much the same place in your chart; it's your clock, not theirs, that settles this house, unless their Mars sits on the cusp itself. Both times are still needed before I sit down with the two charts ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A friend keeps a say in whether their chart is read at all ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
