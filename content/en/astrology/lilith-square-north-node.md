Lilith square North Node sets the untamed part of you across the line of the nodes, at right angles to the familiar end and the new one alike. Neither your old habits nor your new direction quite has room for it, and the friction keeps asking for a path wide enough to include it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's mean apogee, is linked by many astrologers to instinct, autonomy, boundaries and what was kept out of view; it's read differently from one tradition to another, and I treat it as a symbol. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a capacity you develop by practice, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) the strengths you already have, and the two always face each other. A [square](/astrology/square/) works by constructive friction, and a point square one node is square the other too: Lilith sits halfway along the axis, outside both ends.

In plain words, your instinct doesn't fit the old way or the new one. Fall back on what you know, and the part of you that refuses to be managed objects; push towards your growth, and it objects to that too, whenever the growth asks you to be tamer. It can feel like being told off from both sides.

Friction isn't failure. Both poles stay in view, and so does the untamed part: integration here means growth that has room for your frankness, not growth that requires you to file it down.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The three usually share a modality, so the instinct moves in the same manner as the nodes and wants something else: in fixed signs it digs in, in cardinal signs it starts initiatives of its own, in mutable ones it argues and shifts. In the sky, this is a moment when the Moon's farthest point from Earth falls where its path rises highest above the Sun's, or dips lowest below it.

With Lilith moving forward and the nodes back, this square comes round twice every six years or so, each time for a couple of months of births. The houses show where the friction lives, given a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): Lilith in the 2nd with the nodes across the 5th and 11th, for instance, might set what you want for yourself across both your creative joys and the groups you belong to.

About 1 person in 15 has this square within 6°, with the mean Lilith and the true node. If your software shows the true Lilith instead, the picture may change completely ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

The pair's other aspects, set against the square:

- **[Lilith conjunct North Node](/astrology/lilith-conjunct-north-node/):** the instinct on the path itself, part of the growth.
- **[Lilith sextile North Node](/astrology/lilith-sextile-north-node/):** an easy angle, where the instinct scouts for the new.
- **[Lilith trine North Node](/astrology/lilith-trine-north-node/):** the instinct flowing into growth without a fight.
- **[Lilith opposite North Node](/astrology/lilith-opposite-north-node/):** refusal settled at the familiar end, facing the new.

Between two charts, [Lilith square North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-square-north-node-synastry/) can set one person's untamed side across the other's direction of growth: provoking, sometimes usefully. It goes well with honesty and consent, and I look at it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), born with the square, Lilith squares your natal nodes twice in each of its rounds of about nine years: at every Lilith return, and again about halfway between, from the other side. The [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) and the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/) restore the square from the node's side.

The two also form squares in the sky about every three years, an echo of your birth angle.

Weather, not a forecast: these seasons ask, again, whether your path has room for all of you. How wide you make it is up to you.
