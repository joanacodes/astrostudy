The Descendant is the point opposite your rising degree, the edge of the chart where other people come in, and it is fixed by the same minute of birth. When Chiron reaches it, an old sore spot tends to show up in company: in a partner's way of seeing you, in a colleague who presses on it without knowing, or in someone whose hurt you recognise at once.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) orients you towards experience; the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), exactly opposite, orients you towards a counterpart, the person across the table. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) carries an old sore spot, and the slowly learned ways of taking part despite it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates the two ends of one axis through polarity, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) Chiron sits on the other person's side of your chart, and most of what you learn about the sore spot, you learn in relationship.

It can run in several directions. You may meet someone who carries a hurt like yours and find you understand them faster than anyone has understood you. A partner's needs may press exactly where you are tender, without either of you meaning harm. Or you notice that you keep choosing people who need looking after, so that your own sore spot never comes up. The Descendant's blind spot is confusing reciprocity with agreement: the work here is a give-and-take in which both tender places count, not one person keeping the peace.

Nothing here predicts that a relationship begins, holds or ends. And if you are tempted to look up a partner's chart to make sense of the sore spot between you, ask them first: I never read anyone's chart behind their back ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Descendant is the cusp of the 7th, so this crossing ends Chiron's time in your 6th, the house of daily work and routine ([Chiron transiting the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-6th-house/)), and opens its stay in your 7th ([Chiron transiting the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-7th-house/)). Those houses, and the date of the crossing, come from your birth time; without it I don't read the chart, partnership included ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Chiron usually takes about ten months over your Descendant, crossing it three times in nearly two passes out of three, so that for a while it moves between your 6th and your 7th. A single crossing, about one pass in three, takes some three weeks; five crossings, one pass in twenty, about twenty-one months.

With Libra rising, Chiron in Aries sits on your Descendant until April 2027; with Scorpio rising, Chiron in Taurus reaches it between 2026 and 2034; with Sagittarius rising, Chiron in Gemini, from 2033. These are slow years for Chiron, so the longer shapes are the likely ones. With Pisces or Aries rising, the Descendant lies in Virgo or Libra, where Chiron hurries.

Most people meet this crossing twice by 80 and some once, the second about fifty years after the first. Halfway round from [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-ascendant/), it is reached through [transiting Chiron sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-ascendant/), [transiting Chiron square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Chiron trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-ascendant/), and left the same way.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tell one close person plainly what still hurts, without dressing it up as a joke.

Notice whom you rush to look after, and let one of them look after you for a change.

Agree one small exchange with a partner or a friend: something they do for you, something you do for them.
