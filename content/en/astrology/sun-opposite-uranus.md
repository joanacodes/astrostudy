Sun opposite Uranus puts the two at opposite ends of your chart. It belongs to someone who wants to be a definite self and also to stay free to become someone else, and who often meets that second wish in other people before claiming it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the part of a chart that holds you together: your identity, your vitality, the purpose you organise your days around. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part that differentiates and frees: it experiments, reforms, and needs room to do things another way. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them as a polarity. Each end makes you aware of the other, and neither is complete alone.

In plain words, you want to know who you are, and you want to stay free to change your mind about it. The steady part of you builds a life with a recognisable shape; the Uranian part keeps opening windows in it. With an opposition, that other end often arrives through someone else: a partner who needs more room than you'd planned for, a friend whose sudden changes unsettle you, or people who find you hard to pin down. The work isn't choosing an end but letting both be yours: a self that stays itself while it changes. Autonomy isn't isolation, and the best version of this aspect stays close to people while keeping some air.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as a life with a strong outline and sudden turns: stretches of building, then a need to rearrange. A Sun in Leo facing Uranus in Aquarius, for instance, wants to shine personally and to belong to something larger: two signs that move alike and look at life from opposite ends. The houses show where the seesaw stands. With the Sun in the 1st and Uranus in the 7th, partnership is where the need for room appears; with the Sun in the 10th and Uranus in the 4th, a public direction meets a home that keeps rearranging itself.

About 1 person in 24 is born with the Sun and Uranus within 8° of an exact opposition. The Sun does the moving, about a degree a day against Uranus's four or so a year, so the aspect comes from your birthday: each year the Sun reaches the point opposite Uranus about four days later than the year before, and stays in orb for about two weeks. The closer to exact, the louder the polarity.

At this aspect Uranus is always [retrograde](/astrology/uranus-retrograde/): seen from Earth, a body beyond us appears to move backwards while the Earth overtakes it, around its opposition to the Sun. I read that as change that brews inside before it shows, [a rhythm, not a delay](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

The Sun makes Uranus's other aspects as it goes round each year:

- **[Sun conjunct Uranus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus/):** Uranus beside the Sun; being different is part of who you are rather than someone you meet across the room.
- **[Sun sextile Uranus](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus/):** change on offer rather than pressing, taken when it serves your purpose.
- **[Sun square Uranus](/astrology/sun-square-uranus/):** the same two needs at right angles, inside your own life, asking you to reform your plans.
- **[Sun trine Uranus](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus/):** being yourself and doing things differently feel like one movement.

Between two charts, [Sun opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus-synastry/) sets one person's steady centre across the other's need for room, felt differently on each side, so I read it both ways.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Once a year the Sun in the sky opposes your natal Uranus for a day or two: a short stretch when other people's plans, or your own restlessness, ask you to loosen something. The rarer transit is [transiting Uranus opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-sun/). Uranus takes about 84 years to go round, so it comes once in a long life, if at all, and stays within a degree or so for about a year, sometimes crossing the exact point three times.

Born with the opposition, your timetable is unusual. Uranus begins your life within orb of the point opposite your Sun and leaves it in your first years. At about 42, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) everyone meets brings it onto your Sun itself: for you, it is also [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-sun/). Weather, not a forecast: these transits describe seasons when freedom asks for room, never what will change.
