Their Sun in your 7th house puts someone's sense of purpose exactly where your chart turns to face another person. The 7th is the house a synastry looks at first, and this overlay shows up between spouses, business partners, close friends and well-matched rivals alike.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is where you meet someone face to face: partners in love and in business, anyone you treat as an equal, and the open rival who stands in front of you rather than behind. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the core of a person's identity, the part of them that wants to be recognised for who they are. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), that wish arrives in the very place you go looking for a counterpart.

For you, they tend to matter early: a partner, an equal, someone whose direction you take seriously because it seems to answer something you were waiting for. The 7th often holds qualities you more readily notice in another person than in yourself, so they may look like the embodiment of what you want, or of what you've never quite allowed yourself. The house is angular, so what it holds shows, and [Libra](/astrology/libra/) corresponds to it in the natural zodiac: measure, a sense of justice, a taste for company.

For them, partnership with you can be where their purpose comes into focus. They feel met as an equal, and their sense of who they are sharpens for having you opposite. If their Sun sits right on your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the point that was setting on the western horizon at your birth, it meets the frame through which you see every other person, and I read that contact together with the overlay.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A light this well placed is easy to hand your own direction to. You might let their purpose become the purpose of the pair, or admire in them a strength you could be building in yourself, and then feel smaller in their company without knowing why. And because the 7th is also the house of open rivals, the same person can become the one you measure yourself against, at work or in a friendship.

From their side, being someone's counterpart can turn into carrying the light for two. They may feel the bond depends on them staying sure of themselves, and find it hard to admit doubt to the person who sees them as the strong one.

None of this is a verdict on a relationship. An overlay shows how two people meet; whether, when or how they commit belongs to them.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep two lights in the room. In a couple, that means each of you naming what you're aiming for on your own, not only together; between business partners, writing down who decides what, since contracts belong to this house; between friends, taking turns at leading; between rivals, keeping the respect that made the rivalry worth having.

From the other chart, the same overlay reads as your Sun in their 7th house; it's worth checking whether it runs both ways: when each Sun falls in the other's 7th, both people may feel met, and both may lean too hard. [Their Sun in your 6th house](/astrology/sun-in-6th-house-synastry/) is the working day before it; [their Sun in your 8th house](/astrology/sun-in-8th-house-synastry/) is the trust and intimacy after it.

Your Descendant lies exactly opposite your Ascendant and moves as fast, so in Placidus, the house system I use, the 7th needs your birth time. Their birth date fixes their Sun to within a degree, which settles the matter unless that degree is on one of your cusps; even so, my rule holds for both of you: no birth time, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A couple reading starts with both people knowing it's happening ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
