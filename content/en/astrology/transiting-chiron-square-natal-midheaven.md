Some directions are chosen before you know your own limits. When Chiron squares your Midheaven, a path in public life and an old sore spot rub at right angles, and the question becomes whether the role can bend to the person you have become.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is your public direction: the role you carry, the responsibilities you accept, what you aim to contribute. Its other end, the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), is the foundation it stands on. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is what a persistent limit has taught you about what you can and can't sustain. A [square](/astrology/square/) brings them together through friction that asks for adjustment, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the adjustment is usually to the shape of a role rather than to the person in it.

The Midheaven's blind spot is assuming that responsibility means self-erasure, and the square tends to show its cost: a pace that ignores what you know about yourself, an ambition borrowed from someone else's idea of success, a duty taken on to prove an old hurt wrong. None of this foretells a lost job or a stalled career. Friction like this usually ends with a direction that fits better: a role reshaped, a responsibility handed back, a goal restated in your own terms.

The two squares of a round press from different sides. The first comes after Chiron has crossed your Midheaven, as it nears your Ascendant, and the pressure tends to come from how you present yourself and what you want for your own life. The second comes on the climb back, near your Descendant, and the pressure comes from partners or from the daily round of work ([Chiron transiting the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-7th-house/)). Your IC is squared as well, so home rarely stays out of it.

In Placidus, the quadrant system I use, the Midheaven and the Ascendant are seldom exactly 90° apart, so this square and Chiron's crossing of your horizon are separate seasons, sometimes years apart. Only the minute and place of birth can tell them apart, which is why I don't read a chart without them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Most of these squares last about ten months within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, with Chiron touching the exact angle three times; that is the pattern of nearly two passes in three. A third are over in about three weeks with a single touch, and one in twenty runs to five touches over some twenty-one months.

Three is the count most people reach by 80, and two the fewest. With a Capricorn Midheaven, Chiron in Aries squares it from near your Ascendant until April 2027; with a Cancer Midheaven, from near your Descendant. Chiron in Taurus does the same for Aquarius and Leo Midheavens between 2026 and 2034, and Chiron in Gemini, from 2033, for Pisces and Virgo.

The first square follows [transiting Chiron sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-midheaven/) on Chiron's way down from the top. Between it and the second come [transiting Chiron trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-midheaven/), the crossing of your IC ([transiting Chiron opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-midheaven/)) and the second trine, and the round closes at [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-midheaven/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

List what your role asks of you that the sore spot can't sustain, and renegotiate one item on the list.

Hand back one responsibility you took on to prove something.

Tell someone at home what is shifting at work. The IC is part of this square too.
