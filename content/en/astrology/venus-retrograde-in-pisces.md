Venus’s spring retrograde begins in Aries and ends in Pisces, so Pisces receives its closing weeks and its turning point. Since 2009, Venus has turned direct in the sign of its exaltation every time, a little deeper at each eight-year return.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) is mutable water with two rulers, Jupiter in the tradition and Neptune in modern tropical astrology; I read both. Tradition makes it Venus’s exaltation, where valuing is tender, imaginative and quick to forgive. When [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) backs into Pisces, the planet seems from Earth to drift back through the last degrees of the zodiac before it turns, and what slows is that tenderness: the people you’ve idealised and the ones you’ve forgiven, the longing for a love that asks nothing, the line between compassion and giving yourself away.

In my list, four retrogrades between 2000 and 2040 end in Pisces, eight years apart, each stationing in Aries ([Venus retrograde in Aries](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-aries/)) and turning direct a little over 2° deeper into Pisces than the last: 29°12′ on 17 April 2009, 26°54′ on 15 April 2017, 24°37′ on 13 April 2025, 22°21′ on 10 April 2033. The most recent began on 2 March 2025, when Venus stationed at 10°50′ Aries; the next begins on 27 February 2033 at 8°31′ Aries. Before them, the 2001 retrograde stayed in Aries and never reached Pisces, and none of the series reaches back to Aquarius ([Venus retrograde in Aquarius](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-aquarius/)).

Venus retrograde in Pisces in a birth chart is compassion that turns inward before it flows out, and I read it as a placement of its own ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Pisces lands on a house of your chart that only [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) can show, since the cusps are worked out from it; that’s the reason I read no chart without the time. In Placidus, the house system I use, these retrogrades begin in the house where Aries sits and finish in the Pisces house, so the turning point, the weeks around the station direct, belongs to Pisces.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/pisces-rising/):** Pisces rising: a gentle, receptive manner; how easily you take on other people’s moods, noticed kindly.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/pisces-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you give away; generosity with your time and means, weighed with some care for yourself.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/pisces-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** soft, intuitive words; what you said to spare someone’s feelings, looked at again.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a refuge; the people you’ve taken in, and the peace you need.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/pisces-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romantic ideals and imaginative play; a love or a creative dream you idealised, seen as it was.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/):** caring work; service given beyond what’s asked, valued fairly, by you as well.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Pisces on the Descendant, with Virgo rising: a partner you idealise or try to rescue; compassion and clear sight, brought together.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/):** deep merging; trust given without conditions, and whether it is still mutual.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/):** faith and far shores; a spiritual path or a dream of elsewhere that once moved you.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a calling to help or inspire; what your work gives others, and what it gives you.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/pisces-on-11th-house-cusp/):** compassionate friendships; the friend you always forgive, and the one who forgives you.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/pisces-on-12th-house-cusp/):** Pisces in its natural house: solitude, retreat and quiet compassion; old tendernesses, let go gently. Tradition tied this house to confinement; I read it for inner life and leave mental health to those trained for it ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Read both rulers: the house of your natal Jupiter shows where you give generously, the house of your natal Neptune where you dream and idealise, and between them they show where these weeks take your tenderness.

Compassion includes you, and a kindness kept for yourself is part of what Venus in Pisces values. Nothing in these weeks decides anything about a relationship.
