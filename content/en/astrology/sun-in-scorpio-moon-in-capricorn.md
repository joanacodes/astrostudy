Your Sun in Scorpio sees what lies under appearances; your Moon in Capricorn rests easier once there is a plan for what it has seen. I read the pair as depth with a strategy, a self that understands and needs that organise, born a few days into the lunar month.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Capricorn sits two signs on from Scorpio, so the lights are in [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) by sign, and earth gives water its banks. Fixed Scorpio holds on; cardinal Capricorn starts and organises. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) wants the truth, and the power to change things at the root; the [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) calms down when it has a ladder to climb, a job it is trusted with and ground that will not shift. You may be the person who has worked out the real problem and already has a long plan for it.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/), the traditional ruler of Scorpio, is exalted in Capricorn: the old ruler of your Sun's sign is at its strongest in your Moon's. I read that as drive with staying power, effort that knows how to wait. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the modern ruler, I read as well, since I read both for every Scorpio: it shows what you are prepared to transform. The Moon itself is in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Capricorn, a Moon whose tenderness comes out as duty more than softness, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules its sign. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) Saturn did what most placements did not: with Chiron and Lilith it held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), where the notebook as a whole managed about a third. With the Moon in Saturn's own sign, then, Saturn's house and aspects get a larger share of my reading.

In the days around your birth a young crescent followed the Sun down in the evening sky: the Moon led the Sun by 31° to 89°, and the lunar month was only a few days old. Only the earliest eighth of these births come before the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) and still count as [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) births, and near 60° the gap becomes a [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/), an opening that rewards effort. A Capricorn Moon takes to the crescent's urge to begin: it likes to start building early. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) places the crescent in the whole cycle.

Roughly 1 person in 144 has this sextile, with the Moon in the sign of Mars's exaltation. Some birthdays straddle the Moon's move into or out of Capricorn; for those, the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-sagittarius/) and the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-aquarius/) stay on the table until the birth time settles it ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Saturn's house, which matters a great deal for this Moon, waits on that time as well, and so does the rising sign.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Your love is steady, loyal and quietly intense. The Scorpio Sun wants depth and complete trust; the Capricorn Moon proves its care in deeds: the promise kept, the problem quietly dealt with. Saying what you feel may come last, after the planning and the providing. A partner who sees the feeling inside the reliability, and tells you so, lets both lights relax.

## At work {#at-work}

Long, serious work suits you well: strategy, management, investigation, engineering, any field where you need to understand a system deeply and then run it well. Mars exalted in your Moon's sign gives your effort endurance; the Scorpio Sun gives it focus. You may take on heavy responsibility early. Watch that work does not become the only place your feelings are allowed.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two self-reliant signs can make you very hard to help. The Scorpio Sun guards what is at its centre and the Capricorn Moon prefers to cope alone, so a difficult time may be managed in silence until it is over, or until it isn't. Let one person in before the crisis is solved: tell them what is happening while it is happening, and let them carry one practical thing for you.
