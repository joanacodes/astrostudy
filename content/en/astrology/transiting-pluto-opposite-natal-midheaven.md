Facing the top of your chart, at its foot, is the IC. When Pluto reaches it, the reworking of your direction in the world begins underground, in what that direction stands on, and for two to three years the change shows at home before it shows anywhere else.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) regenerates from the roots: what has become unsustainable comes up so that it can be reorganised. The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is your direction and public standing; the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) opposite it is the foundation that sustains them, your sense of belonging, continuity and support. The [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, works from the far end of the meridian, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your public life is reshaped from below. The reasons behind your ambitions tend to change before the ambitions do: security, a parent's hopes, a promise you made yourself at twenty can stop explaining why you do what you do. Home and private life are reshaped, and that changes what your public life can carry.

Transformation isn't destruction. A way of coping you built long ago can feel like part of who you are, and Pluto here asks whether it still is; what replaces it tends to be chosen rather than inherited. Older books sometimes tie this crossing to a loss in the family. I don't: no transit can say when anyone will die, and I never read a chart for that ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).

Pluto's long stay in your 4th house starts here, since in Placidus, the house system I use, the IC is that house's cusp ([Pluto transiting the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-4th-house/)). The Midheaven and IC are the two ends of the [meridian axis](/astrology/meridian-axis/), set by the minute of your birth: a degree of IC is about four minutes on the clock and about eight months of Pluto in Aquarius, so a time ten minutes out can move this transit by well over a year ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A typical pass across your IC takes about two years and eight months by my orbs, which run from 1½° before exact to 1° after; the quickest take about twenty-two months. In about three passes out of five Pluto crosses the exact degree three times, over two years and seven months or so; the rest cross it five times, over about three years and seven months, and the slowest of those take more than four and a half years.

By 80, fewer than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 have met it, almost always once. It's the other end of the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)), and just as rare: the two ends of the meridian lie half the zodiac apart, and Pluto covers less than that in a lifetime, so it almost never reaches both. Seven people in eight meet its square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-midheaven/)); about three in four meet the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-midheaven/)), and as many the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-midheaven/)).

Until January 2044 Pluto is in Aquarius ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), the sign on the IC of a Leo Midheaven, and it moves on about a degree and a half a year: a Midheaven at 10° Leo meets this transit around 2028 to 2031, one at 20° around 2035 to 2037. After 2044, from Pisces, the same crossing reaches Virgo Midheavens.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tend the base before the façade. The home, the routine and the relationships that restore you are where this transit does its work.

Ask what you're working for, and let the answer change before you change the work. A new reason often reshapes a role more gently than a new role would.

Keep one small public commitment steady while the ground shifts. A regular piece of work you can count on gives the deeper change somewhere to stand.
