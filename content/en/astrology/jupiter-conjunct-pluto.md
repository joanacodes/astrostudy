When Jupiter catches up with Pluto, the urge to grow and the urge to transform start from the same degree. The meeting recurs every 12 or 13 years; the people born during one share it, and your houses decide what it does in your own life.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In a chart, [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the function of expansion: it learns, finds meaning and opens possibilities. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the function that goes to the root: it lets go of what has outlived its use, regenerates, and gives back a deeper strength. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two into one process while each keeps its own identity.

In plain words, you grow by transforming, and you transform by understanding. Simply adding more rarely satisfies this conjunction: it wants the wider picture and the deeper one at once, and explanations that stay on the surface make it restless. A conviction formed here is usually hard-won and thorough, because it has been taken apart and rebuilt before you trust it.

Expansion isn't excess, and empowerment isn't domination: those are the two misreadings I keep away from. Lived well, this conjunction is the capacity to rebuild a belief from its foundations when it no longer holds, and to help other people through the same work.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Counted within 6°, the conjunction belongs to about 1 person in 31 born from 1930 to 2009. It came in seven meetings: September 1930 to June 1931, July to September 1943, September 1955 to July 1956, September to November 1968 and again April to July 1969, September to December 1981, October 1994 to January 1995, and November 2007 to January 2008. Some windows lasted most of a year and others two or three months, depending on whether Jupiter's yearly retrograde fell near Pluto. Everyone born inside one shares it.

Sharing the meeting with everyone born in the same months, you share its sign too; what's yours is the house it falls in, and whether it lands on a personal planet or an angle. In the 2nd house this conjunction might ask you to transform what you value rather than pile it up; in the 9th, to found a philosophy afresh; in the 11th, to change a group from the inside. Placing it takes a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Jupiter, at about a year per sign against Pluto's 12 to 31, is the one that catches up. Born before the exact meeting, you have the conjunction still forming; born after, already loosening ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)).

Jupiter's other aspects to Pluto keep the two apart in different ways:

- **[Jupiter sextile Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-pluto/):** growth and depth two signs apart, cooperating when you take the opening.
- **[Jupiter square Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-square-pluto/):** "more" and "deeper" at right angles, a friction that tests every plan.
- **[Jupiter trine Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-trine-pluto/):** renewal pours into growth without effort, which makes it easy to overlook.
- **[Jupiter opposite Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-pluto/):** the two face each other, and the transforming end is often met in someone else.

Between two charts, [Jupiter conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-pluto-synastry/) puts one person's Jupiter on the other's Pluto: encouragement that goes deep, or depth that suddenly has room to grow. It runs strong both ways, so I read it from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-pluto/) comes about every 12 years, as Jupiter passes your Pluto: a few weeks within a degree or so, or three passes spread over several months if Jupiter turns retrograde there. [Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) is rarer and slower: Pluto takes 12 to 31 years to cross a sign and can stay within reach of your Jupiter for a year or two. Born with the conjunction, you had that one at the start. Pluto was already beside your Jupiter, drifts away in your first years and doesn't return within a lifetime.

Weather, not a forecast: each describes a season when growth asks to go deeper and depth asks for room. Neither tells you what will change.
