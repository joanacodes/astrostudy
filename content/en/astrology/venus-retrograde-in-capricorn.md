Venus turns back in Capricorn in the middle of winter, once every eight years, and its six weeks straddle the turn of the year. Capricorn builds things to last, and this retrograde asks what you have been building with the people you love.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) is cardinal earth, ruled by Saturn: structure, responsibility, endurance, the long view. Venus there values what proves itself over time, and shows love through reliability and effort more than display. When [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) arrives in Capricorn, the planet appears from Earth to stop and move back through the sign for about 41 days, and what slows is the long-term side of valuing: the commitments you’ve kept, the bonds you’ve invested in, the gap between what you owe and what you want.

In the dates I work from, every winter retrograde between 2000 and 2040 ends in Capricorn, and each returns eight years later about 2½° lower, the biggest step of Venus’s five places. The first began just inside Aquarius, at 1°28′, on 24 December 2005 and turned direct at 16°01′ Capricorn on 3 February 2006 ([Venus retrograde in Aquarius](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-aquarius/)). The other four stay wholly in Capricorn: from 28°58′ to 13°33′ over the winter of 2013–14; from 26°29′ to 11°04′, the most recent; from 24°01′ to 8°37′, the next; and from 21°31′ to 6°08′ over the winter of 2037–38. That last station direct is the nearest any Venus retrograde in my list comes to Sagittarius ([Venus retrograde in Sagittarius](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-sagittarius/)).

If Venus was retrograde in Capricorn when you were born, I read it as love that proves itself slowly, in its own good time ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Where Capricorn falls among your houses is something only [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) can tell; it fixes every cusp, and I don’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, these narrow retrogrades often stay within the house Capricorn occupies, apart from the 2005 one, which began in the Aquarius house.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/capricorn-rising/):** Capricorn rising: a composed, capable manner; the reserve you show at first, and whether it still protects what you value.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you’ve worked for; the things and skills you’ve built up, valued for the effort they hold.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** measured words; the compliment you keep until it’s earned, given a little sooner.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/):** duty and love at home; the responsibilities you carry for family, shared out again.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasure taken seriously; a romance or a craft you’ve worked at for years, enjoyed for its own sake.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-6th-house-cusp/):** diligence; the work you do because it’s expected, told apart from the work you do because you care.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn on the Descendant, with Cancer rising: a partner who is a rock; commitment and support, valued again.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-8th-house-cusp/):** long-term promises; what you’ve committed to share, and on what terms.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-9th-house-cusp/):** traditions and teachers; a belief you’ve lived by for years, looked at fondly.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn in its natural house: career and standing; what your ambitions mean to you once the effort is counted.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-11th-house-cusp/):** dependable old friends; the networks you’ve built, and the friendships beneath them.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private loyalties; obligations you carry silently, set down for a moment.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Saturn rules Capricorn, and Saturn was one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), so the house of your natal Saturn deserves a close look during these weeks: it shows where your commitments are drawn. Saturn, for me, describes a timetable rather than a barrier.

Nothing here forbids a commitment or ends one. It gives you a season to see which of your commitments you would choose again.
