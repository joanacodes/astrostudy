Glyphs are what make a chart look like a secret code, and they're the easiest part of astrology to learn. About thirty of them matter, and most draw their own meaning once you look at them for a moment.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

A glyph is a symbol that stands for one thing in a chart: a planet, a sign, an aspect or a point. Glyphs are what let a whole sky fit inside one wheel ([Chart wheel, and how to read it](/astrology/chart-wheel-and-how-to-read-it/)) and a year of positions onto a few pages of an ephemeris. Their history, including the symbols coined for the outer planets after their discovery, has a page of its own ([The origin of the glyphs](/astrology/origin-of-the-glyphs/)).

Start with the ten planets, two lights among them:

- ☉ **Sun:** a circle with a point at its centre.
- ☽ **Moon:** a crescent.
- ☿ **Mercury:** a circle over a cross, crowned with a crescent.
- ♀ **Venus:** a circle above a cross.
- ♂ **Mars:** a circle with an arrow pointing up and to the right.
- ♃ **Jupiter:** a crescent joined to a cross.
- ♄ **Saturn:** the sickle of Kronos.
- ♅ **Uranus:** an H over a small circle, for Herschel, who found it.
- ♆ **Neptune:** the trident of the sea god.
- ♇ **Pluto:** the letters P and L joined.

Uranus and Pluto each have a second symbol in use, so if a chart shows something unfamiliar where you expect them, check its legend.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

The twelve signs are easiest to learn by element, since each group shares a temperament:

- **Fire:** ♈ Aries, the ram's horns; ♌ Leo, the lion's mane or tail; ♐ Sagittarius, the archer's arrow.
- **Earth:** ♉ Taurus, the bull's head and horns; ♍ Virgo, the maiden; ♑ Capricorn, the sea-goat, half goat, half fish.
- **Air:** ♊ Gemini, the twins side by side; ♎ Libra, the scales; ♒ Aquarius, the water-bearer's waves.
- **Water:** ♋ Cancer, the crab's two claws; ♏ Scorpio, the scorpion with its sting; ♓ Pisces, two fish tied together.

The five major aspects are drawn as shapes: ☌ the conjunction, a small circle with a line rising from it; ⚹ the sextile, a six-pointed star; □ the square; △ the trine; ☍ the opposition, two small circles joined by a line.

Then the points: ⚷ Chiron, a key, or the letters O and K; ⚸ Lilith, a dark crescent over a cross; ☊ the North Node, an arch with a small loop at each end, and ☋ the South Node, the same turned upside down; ⊗ the Part of Fortune, a circle crossed by an X. The angles are written as letters: AC for the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), DC for the Descendant, MC for the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), IC for the Imum Coeli.

Put together, a line such as ☉ 16°♏53′ reads *the Sun at 16 degrees 53 minutes of Scorpio* ([degrees, minutes and seconds](/astrology/degrees-minutes-and-seconds/)), and ☉ □ ♄ reads *Sun square Saturn*.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

If you learn only three glyphs before your reading, make them ♄, ⚷ and ⚸: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) they were the placements that held 75 to 90% of the time, and they're where I spend the most time in a chart. Find them on your wheel, note their signs and houses, and you'll follow that part of a reading easily.

Three pairs cause most of the mix-ups. ♀ and ♂ differ only in what's attached to the circle: a cross below, or an arrow above. ♍ and ♏ differ in the tail: Virgo's loops back on itself, Scorpio's ends in a sting. ☊ and ☋ are the same shape, one the right way up and one upside down, and since the nodes always sit exactly opposite each other, the sign on the other side of the wheel settles it.

One glyph needs a label more than any other. ⚸ is used for more than one Lilith, and when I write about Lilith I mean mean Lilith, the Black Moon ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). Check what your chart's ⚸ stands for before you read anything into it.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Glyphs are secret, occult signs.”** They're shorthand, like the symbols on a map, and every one of them is on this page.
- **“♀ and ♂ make Venus and Mars about women and men.”** In a chart they mean Venus and Mars, and neither is about gender: Venus weighs value and attraction, Mars starts action and effort.
- **“Every program draws the same symbols.”** Mostly, but Uranus and Pluto have alternative glyphs, and ⚸ can mean different Liliths.
- **“You must memorise them all before reading a chart.”** Start with the planets and the signs. The aspects and points come quickly once those are familiar.
