Mercury rules both of your lights: the sign of your Sun and the sign of your Moon answer to the same planet. The half moon of the last quarter rises near midnight, and your birth came close to one. To me the pair is a mind that sorts and a mind that roams, sharing one desk.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Your Sun's sign and your Moon's sit three signs apart and share a mode: mutable earth and mutable air, two ways of adapting that square off against each other. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) adapts by refining, narrowing things down until they are right. The [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) adapts by widening, and settles once a feeling has words, a conversation, a change of subject. One wants the single correct answer; the other is comforted by having several possible ones.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules both signs and is exalted in Virgo as well, so one planet speaks for your purpose and your comfort. Mercury is always found close to the Sun, 28° away at most, so with yours in Virgo it is in Leo, Virgo or Libra and never with your Moon in Gemini. Its sign and house tell me how this pair thinks; its aspects, whether thinking soothes you or keeps you awake. Tradition also places Jupiter's detriment in both your signs, Gemini outright and Virgo through Jupiter's older rule of Pisces: a pair that trusts the particular over the grand theory.

Between day 19½ and day 24½ of the lunar month, a half moon rose around midnight, or one nearly there: your Moon trailed the Sun by 61° to 119°. The [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) and the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) share these births half and half, so the hour can move you from one to the other; close to 90° behind, the chart holds a waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). I read the last quarter as friction met from the inside, settled by changing your own priorities, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains both sides.

Of every 139 people, one or so has this pair. A birthday on which the Moon was passing out of Taurus or into Cancer leaves two candidates besides this page, the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-taurus/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-cancer/), and the time settles it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Like the rising sign, Mercury's house, which counts twice here, is known only once the time is.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Conversation is how you love: long talks, shared jokes, practical help offered as a question, *shall I sort that out for you?* A partner who talks back, and who lets your care be useful, suits both lights. What can go wrong is analysis standing in for tenderness: you may explain a feeling to someone instead of letting them comfort it. Now and then, say the simple thing and stop there.

## At work {#at-work}

Work made of information suits you: editing, teaching, research, translation, any job where accuracy and range both count. The Gemini Moon brings speed, curiosity and a gift for explaining; the Virgo Sun brings method and an eye for the error. You can hold many threads and still notice which one is loose. Monotony bores the Moon, sloppiness bothers the Sun, and too many open tabs, on a screen or in your head, tire both.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

A pair run by one planet can't easily get away from its own mind. At night the Virgo Sun reviews what went wrong and the Gemini Moon rehearses what might be said tomorrow, while the square keeps the two talking past each other, and worry can pass for thinking. What helps is giving the mind a body to report to: a walk before a decision, a page written and then closed, and a rule that nothing gets solved after ten at night.
