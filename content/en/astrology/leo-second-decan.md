In the middle of Leo the two lists of decan rulers agree on Jupiter, and a night birth adds a third tie between the planet and these degrees. The decan runs from 10° to 20° of the sign, and the Sun crosses it in the first half of August.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

After Saturn comes [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) in Lilly's table of [faces](/astrology/faces/), and so the middle of [Leo](/astrology/leo/) is Jupiter's face. The element scheme lands on the same planet by another road, through Sagittarius, the fire sign after Leo. Only Leo and Aries have two decans on which the lists agree: here Jupiter, and in Leo's last ten degrees Mars.

There is a third tie. In the traditional triplicities, Jupiter rules the fire signs by night ([triplicity rulers](/astrology/triplicity-rulers/)), so a [Jupiter in Leo](/astrology/jupiter-in-leo/) between 10° and 20° in a night chart holds the triplicity and the face, three points and one in the old scoring, in a sign that is neither its home nor its exaltation. Whether a chart is a day or a night one depends on the Sun being above or below the horizon at birth ([Sect: day and night charts](/astrology/sect-day-and-night-charts/)).

Under the Sun, which rules the sign, the decan's colour is Jupiter's: growth and meaning, the wish to make what you create add up to something larger. For a planet here, the Sun's sign and house come first in my reading, then Jupiter's.

## Its image {#its-image}

Ḥā-tchat begins a new pair in Budge's list, completed by Peḥui-tchat in the next decan. Ha-tet, the Greco-Egyptian form, stays close to it. The Greek Hermetic list has Ipi. Firmicus Maternus gives just Sic, Joseph Scaliger the longer Sithacer, and the *Testament of Solomon* two names, Leroel and Kumeatêl.

Isis turns up twice in the lists for the late-summer signs: Cosmas of Maiuma set her over these degrees, while Athanasius Kircher, who chose Peroeus here, put her at the start of Virgo ([Virgo, first decan](/astrology/virgo-first-decan/)). Neither list agrees with the other, and I don't ask them to ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Birthdays from about 2 to 12 August fall in this decan ([Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/)), and around 5 and 6 August the Sun passes [Acubens](/astrology/acubens/), α Cancri, a faint star of the Crab that stands at 14°00′ Leo in 2026.

The day-or-night question above is one more thing only the birth time settles: the same Jupiter, born at noon or at midnight, stands differently in this decan, which is the kind of detail I never guess ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

For Pluto, this is the decan of births from June 1946 to August 1951; its first visits came late in 1944 and its last in mid-1952 ([Pluto in Leo](/astrology/pluto-in-leo/)). Uranus's stay ran from July 1958 to October 1959; Saturn's three came in 1947–48, 1976–77 and 2005–07, with a return in 2035–36. Jupiter itself crossed its own decan from 15 August to 2 October 2026, and returns, retrograde, from 27 February to 28 May 2027.

On either side the rulers change: Saturn has the face before, Mars both lists after ([Leo, first decan](/astrology/leo-first-decan/), [Leo, third decan](/astrology/leo-third-decan/)).
