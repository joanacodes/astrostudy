The second decan of Leo runs from 10° to 20° of the sign, the first half of August for the Sun. Jupiter rules it in both lists, and in a chart drawn for a night birth it rules the fire triplicity as well.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) follow Saturn with [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), so the middle of [Leo](/astrology/leo/) is Jupiter's face. The modern decans reach the same planet by element: the middle ten degrees of a fire sign go to the ruler of the next fire sign, Sagittarius, and that is Jupiter again. Only Leo and Aries have two decans on which the lists agree: here Jupiter, and in Leo's last ten degrees Mars.

There is a third tie. In the traditional triplicities, Jupiter rules the fire signs by night ([triplicity rulers](/astrology/triplicity-rulers/)), so a [Jupiter in Leo](/astrology/jupiter-in-leo/) between 10° and 20° in a night chart holds the triplicity and the face, three points and one in the old scoring, in a sign that is neither its home nor its exaltation. Whether a chart is a day or a night one depends on the Sun being above or below the horizon at birth ([Sect: day and night charts](/astrology/sect-day-and-night-charts/)).

Under the Sun, which rules the sign, the decan's colour is Jupiter's: growth and meaning, the wish to make what you create add up to something larger. With a planet here, I read the Sun's sign and house first, then Jupiter's.

## Its image {#its-image}

Budge's list starts a new pair of names here, Ḥā-tchat, followed by Peḥui-tchat in the next decan. The Greco-Egyptian lists write Ha-tet, the Greek Hermetic list Ipi; Firmicus Maternus gives just Sic, and Joseph Scaliger Sithacer. The *Testament of Solomon* has Leroel, or Kumeatêl.

Cosmas of Maiuma set Isis over these degrees, and Athanasius Kircher Peroeus. Isis turns up twice in the lists for the late-summer signs: here for Cosmas, and at the start of Virgo for Kircher ([Virgo, first decan](/astrology/virgo-first-decan/)). Neither list agrees with the other, and I don't ask them to ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun stands here for births from about 2 to 12 August ([Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/)). In 2026 it reached 10° Leo on 2 August at 06:23 UT and 20° on 12 August at 16:47 UT, and on about 5 and 6 August it passes [Acubens](/astrology/acubens/), α Cancri, a faint star of the Crab at 14°00′ Leo in 2026.

The day-or-night question above is one more thing only the birth time settles: the same Jupiter, born at noon or at midnight, stands differently in this decan, which is the kind of detail I never guess ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Pluto held these degrees for births from June 1946 to August 1951, with visits from late 1944 and until mid-1952 ([Pluto in Leo](/astrology/pluto-in-leo/)). Uranus followed from July 1958 to October 1959, Saturn in 1947–48, 1976–77 and 2005–07, and Saturn comes back in 2035–36. Jupiter itself crossed its own decan from 15 August to 2 October 2026, and returns, retrograde, from 27 February to 28 May 2027.

On either side the rulers change: Saturn has the face before, Mars both lists after ([Leo, first decan](/astrology/leo-first-decan/), [Leo, third decan](/astrology/leo-third-decan/)).
