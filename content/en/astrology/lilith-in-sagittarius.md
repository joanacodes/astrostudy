Somewhere there was a horizon you weren't supposed to go past. Lilith in Sagittarius is the part of you that went anyway, or badly wanted to. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point of the Moon's orbit farthest from Earth, was in the sign of the archer when you were born, and it marks a hunger for more world than you were given.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) is mutable fire: it explores, widens the view and looks for what things mean. In this sign Lilith usually hides a hunger for more than was on offer — more world, more questions, more room to find out for yourself. The family map may have ended at the edge of town, or the answers came ready-made and doubting them was disloyal. So the wish to go went quiet, or went into books.

Its refusal is a refusal of small worlds. Fence it in and it looks for the gate; hand it an answer it can't check and it goes to see for itself. It often tells the truth with a laugh, which can sound careless and isn't: that's how this Lilith keeps its freedom and its honesty at once. Its boundaries are measured in room.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), Sagittarius' ruler, shows by sign and house where that hunger for meaning gets fed, or starved.

Mean Lilith ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)) has only just left Sagittarius: its latest stay ran from December 2025 to September 2026. If yours is in Sagittarius, those months brought your Lilith return, as mean Lilith passed back over its birth degree. Before that it was there from June 1937 to March 1938, May 1946 to January 1947, March to December 1955, January to October 1964, November 1972 to August 1973, September 1981 to June 1982, July 1990 to April 1991, June 1999 to February 2000 and April 2008 to January 2009. A birth in July 1990 or April 1991 sits on the seam: check whether your chart shows [Lilith in Scorpio](/astrology/lilith-in-scorpio/) or [Lilith in Capricorn](/astrology/lilith-in-capricorn/) instead. The house, which [needs your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-it-doesnt), is where your own horizon lies.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, Lilith in Sagittarius needs room to roam, in body or in mind, and a partner who travels with it rather than waiting at home disapproving. It's drawn to people from other places and other ways of thinking, and it cools when a relationship starts shrinking the world instead of widening it. The hidden part may test a bond by heading off — a trip, a course, a new idea — to see whether the other person follows, lets go, or reaches for the reins.

What it wants is a fellow traveller: someone who can hear *I need to go and see* without hearing *I'm leaving you*.

## At work {#at-work}

You may need meaning more than a title. This Lilith does well where there's something to learn, teach, explore or argue about, and badly where the mission statement is something you're expected to believe without asking. It's the person who says out loud that the strategy has no clothes, and it resents being told to keep its doubts to itself.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Leaving can become a habit. If every bond or job that asks for depth starts to feel like a fence, you may keep moving before the question is answered: the next country, the next course, the next big idea. Or the hunger was talked out of you so early that you live smaller than you want to, and call it being sensible.

The truth said with a laugh can land harder than you meant, too. Being free to say it isn't the same as being heard.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Go somewhere, on purpose: a place, a subject, a conversation with someone who thinks differently. It doesn't have to be far to count. Write down what you actually believe, as opposed to what you were handed, and test it the way I tested astrology, with a tick or a cross beside each claim. Lilith, for the record, was one of three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in [that notebook](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/).

And when you tell the truth, aim it. The archer is a symbol of direction as much as of distance.
