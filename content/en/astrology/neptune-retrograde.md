Neptune moves so slowly that each year's loop barely clears the last. It backs up less than three degrees in five months, and the next loop begins before this one's ground is left behind.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) spends roughly fourteen years in each sign, about 165 years on the whole zodiac, and Earth passes it every 367 to 368 days. Each time, Neptune seems to back up for 158 to 160 days, and each loop starts two or three days later in the calendar than the one before. Halfway through, it stands opposite the Sun, although even then it is too faint to see without binoculars or a telescope.

The loops from 2025 to 2028, each starting in early July:

- **4 July to 10 December 2025**, from 2°10′ Aries back to 29°22′ Pisces;
- **7 July to 12 December 2026**, from 4°25′ to 1°36′ Aries;
- **9 July to 15 December 2027**, from 6°39′ to 3°51′ Aries;
- **11 July to 16 December 2028**, from 8°54′ to 6°05′ Aries.

The loop of 2025 was Neptune's last to reach back into Pisces; from 2026 its loops stay in Aries until 2038, when one turns back from 1°22′ Taurus ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)). The 2026 loop stopped at 1°36′ Aries, less than a degree from the spot where Saturn had met Neptune that February ([The Saturn–Neptune conjunction of 2026](/astrology/saturn-neptune-conjunction-of-2026/)).

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Neptune's loops hardly change length: 160 days until 2009, 159 from 2010 to 2025, 158 from 2026. What makes them unusual is how they sit together. Each year's station retrograde comes only about two and a quarter degrees further on than the last, while each loop covers almost three, so one year's loop overlaps the next.

A point in a chart that falls in that overlap gets crossed five times in two years: three times in one loop, twice more in the next. With the orbs my forecast uses, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, about three in four of Neptune's transits to a natal point make three exact passes and one in four makes five, and a typical one lasts about two years and seven months from first touch to last. The [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/), which comes around the age of 41, arrives at that pace.

Its neighbours: [Uranus retrograde](/astrology/uranus-retrograde/) is slightly shorter and moves on further each year, so its loops sit almost end to end; [Pluto retrograde](/astrology/pluto-retrograde/), the next one out, lasts slightly longer and makes the smallest loops of all.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

What Neptune does, in my readings, is imagine, feel with others and perceive in symbols; deception and escapism are no part of the function. Close to three people in seven are born with it retrograde, and I read that as imagination kept close: ideals examined before they are trusted, a sensitivity to other people that checks it isn't losing itself, a sense of meaning that grows in private. I describe that inward pace at more length in [Natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

Neptune spends about fourteen years in a sign and its retrograde covers five months of births in each of them, so I read it through the house it occupies and the aspects it makes to personal planets. The house depends on the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

As a [transit](/astrology/transits/), Neptune retrograde lingers on two or three degrees of one house for five months, often over a point it will cross again the following year. I read it as a season for checking an ideal against what is real: a hope looked at again in plain light, a sense of something that still needs facts before it becomes a decision. It describes; it doesn't decide.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Neptune retrograde makes everyone confused.”** Confusion isn't part of Neptune's function. The loop describes a season of re-examining ideals, which tends to clear things.
- **“Secrets come out when Neptune turns direct.”** No transit uncovers anyone's secrets. What people tell each other stays their choice.
- **“It's too slow to matter.”** Its slowness is what makes it felt: a point in its path can be crossed five times.
- **“Born with Neptune retrograde, you can't trust your intuition.”** It describes intuition that checks itself before acting, which is no bad habit.
