Halfway round its 84-year circuit from your Moon, Uranus faces it from the other side of the zodiac. For about twenty months, the change that touches your sense of home tends to start in someone else.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is attachment and felt security: who and what you lean on, and how you respond when they move. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates: it pulls the parts of a shared life far enough apart for each to breathe. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, works through polarity, so your needs are seen against someone else's freedom, and theirs against yours.

Often it's the people closest to you who change first. A partner takes up something that needs evenings; a grown child, a parent or a housemate wants a different arrangement; the household's rhythm stops matching yours. Or the roles swap, and you're the one wanting room while others want things as they were. Either way, the opposition brings out needs that ran unspoken for years.

Whether anyone moves out, in or on is not something I read from a [transit](/astrology/transits/). What this one shows is a balance being renegotiated: closeness on one side, independence on the other, and an arrangement that can hold both.

With the Moon in your 4th and Uranus crossing your 10th ([Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/)), changes in your working life pull on home, and home asks to be fitted around a path that no longer keeps the old hours. That axis and the Moon's exact degree both come from your birth time, and without it I don't read the opposition ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The natal [Moon opposite Uranus](/astrology/moon-opposite-uranus/) sets closeness and independence at the two ends of one axis for life. The transit sets them there for twenty months, usually through one relationship or one household in particular.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use for timing, Uranus usually holds the opposition for about twenty months; only the quickest passes, around eleven months, are much shorter. More than nine passes in ten cross the exact point three times, so the same question tends to be asked three times, a few months apart.

Counted from [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-moon/), it comes about 42 years on. That puts it between the two trines ([transiting Uranus trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-moon/)), fourteen years from each, and well away from the squares ([transiting Uranus square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-moon/)) and the sextiles ([transiting Uranus sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-moon/)), which lie nearer the conjunction. Uranus doesn't quite finish its round in 80 years, and for about 1 person in 33 born between 1930 and 2009, the point opposite the Moon lies in the stretch it hasn't reached.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give other people's changes time to land. What looks like someone drifting away is often someone making room for themselves, and it reads differently three months on.

Ask for what you need, out loud. Under this opposition, unspoken needs get lost in other people's rearrangements.

Keep a corner that's yours. A room, a ritual or an hour that doesn't depend on anyone else's plans steadies the Moon while everything around it shifts.
