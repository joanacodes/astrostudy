Two eras meet in Uranus conjunct Neptune in synastry. One person's Uranus lies within 4° of the other's Neptune, so the way one generation changes things lands on the ideals of another; neither planet is personal, and I read the meeting that way.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and reforms: it questions inherited arrangements, experiments, and frees what has grown too tight. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines: it senses meanings larger than one life, extends compassion, and dissolves boundaries that no longer need to be there. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the fusion is split between two people: the change from one chart, the ideal from the other.

If the Uranus is yours, the other person's ideals look like material: something to rebuild, make practical, take further. You may be the one who turns their vision into a working system, or the one who questions it.

If the Neptune is yours, the other person's experiments land on what you hold worth believing in. It can feel like your ideal finally getting a method, or like being told it's out of date.

What the meeting can make is an ideal with a working edge, and a reform with a reason beyond itself.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Fused, change and ideal blur. The Uranus person can take novelty for progress and redesign what the Neptune person cherished before understanding it; the Neptune person can take a stirring feeling for a reason and follow the new thing uncritically, or dismiss it as cold. Neither instinct is wrong; each needs the other's check.

- **[Uranus sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** a narrow, friendly angle where vision and reform trade help.
- **[Uranus square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune-synastry/):** ideals and experiments at right angles, each testing the other.
- **[Uranus trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune-synastry/):** new ideas and old ideals running the same way.
- **[Uranus opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** change at one end, ideal at the other, with decades between.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask what the ideal is for before changing it, and how the change serves it afterwards. The Neptune person can say what they hope a system will make possible; the Uranus person, what they would change and why. A parent and an adult child, an older and a younger colleague, a mentor and a former student: the pairing is mostly between ages, and it works when each treats the other's era as information rather than error.

For births before the 1990s, the Uranus belongs to the younger person: Uranus moves faster and trailed Neptune round the zodiac until the two met in 1993, so it reached each Neptune degree years later. Someone born in mid-1985, Uranus in Sagittarius, has it with anyone born between November 1975 and January 1979, Neptune in Sagittarius; someone born in mid-1965, Neptune in Scorpio, with anyone born between September 1978 and November 1979, Uranus in Scorpio. Years of births share each side, so I don't count this contact in a synastry, where my count keeps to contacts touching the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Through the houses, it is the gap between two eras' visions of change, more than between two people.

The hour of birth doesn't move either planet, but it decides every house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If the Uranus falls in the Neptune person's 11th house, the meeting is likely to happen among friends, groups and shared causes ([Their Uranus in your 11th house](/astrology/uranus-in-11th-house-synastry/)).

Born with [Uranus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune/), as nearly everyone born between 1990 and 1996 was, a person carries invention and imagination together. Between two charts they come from different people, often different decades, and both people have to want the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
