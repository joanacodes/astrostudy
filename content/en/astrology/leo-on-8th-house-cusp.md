The 8th house holds what you share and what you owe: joint money, debts, trust, the closeness that changes you. Leo on the 8th house cusp brings to all of it a big heart, and a proud one.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on a cusp gives its house a manner ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Leo](/astrology/leo/) makes the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) wholehearted. I read it as trust given entirely or not at all, a fixed sign's loyalty brought to the most private bonds. In intimacy you want to be cherished, and you cherish back, warmly and with some ceremony. With shared money you tend to be generous: you cover the bill, lend without keeping score, pick up the joint expense nobody else noticed. The difficulty runs the other way. Owing, borrowing, needing: these come hard to a proud heart, and you may hide a need until it's urgent. The 8th is also where closeness changes people, and with this cusp the change usually comes when you let someone see what you need rather than what you give.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules your 8th house, and its place shows where shared resources and deep trust come into your life: as ruler of the 8th, in the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). The house is wide in these charts, about 46° to 48° at London, and in more than half of them the whole of Virgo lies inside it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)): Mercury then shares the Sun's work, and the small print of joint accounts gets read after all. In a similar share of London charts, Cancer sits wholly inside the 7th next door, so the Moon's sign and the Sun's lie side by side, one within the house of partners, the other opening the house of what partners share.

About 1 birth in 17 has Leo on the 8th house cusp at London, and 1 in 15 at New York, counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use. The 8th cusp sits a little above the western horizon, and Leo, which climbs so slowly in the east, drops through the western cusps quickly: about an hour and twenty-five minutes a day on this one at London. Sagittarius or Capricorn rises in these charts, and Leo never opens the 7th or the 9th as well. Leo itself goes without any cusp in about one London chart in seven.

On either side in time lie [Leo on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/), for a birth a little earlier, and [Leo on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/), for one a little later. The minute decides between them, which is why I want it before I read anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance. I read it as intimacy, trust and what's held in common, and no chart can say when a life will end or what anyone will inherit ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
