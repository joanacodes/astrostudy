What you share with others, and what you owe them, is the 8th house's business: money held jointly, debts, trust, intimacy. Leo on the 8th house cusp brings to all of it a big heart, and a proud one.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Whatever sign opens a house colours everything in it ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Leo](/astrology/leo/) makes the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) wholehearted. I read it as trust given entirely or not at all, a fixed sign's loyalty brought to the most private bonds. In intimacy you want to be cherished, and you cherish back, warmly and with some ceremony. With shared money you tend to be generous: you cover the bill, lend without keeping score, pick up the joint expense nobody else noticed. The difficulty runs the other way. Owing, borrowing, needing: these come hard to a proud heart, and you may hide a need until it's urgent. The 8th is also where closeness changes people, and with this cusp the change usually comes when you let someone see what you need rather than what you give.

Follow the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) to see where shared resources and deep trust come into your life; as ruler of the 8th, it can occupy the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). The house is wide in these charts, about 46° to 48° at London, and in more than half of them the whole of Virgo lies inside it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)): Mercury then shares the Sun's work, and the small print of joint accounts gets read after all. In a similar share of London charts, Cancer sits wholly inside the 7th next door, so the Moon's sign and the Sun's lie side by side, one within the house of partners, the other opening the house of what partners share.

At London, 1 birth in 17 or so has Leo opening the 8th; at New York, 1 in 15. Those are Placidus cusps, the ones I read. The 8th cusp sits a little above the western horizon, and Leo, which climbs so slowly in the east, drops through the western cusps quickly: about an hour and twenty-five minutes a day on this one at London. Sagittarius or Capricorn rises in these charts. With Capricorn, in about 2 London charts in 5, Saturn rules the chart, and tradition puts Saturn in detriment in Leo: I often read a reserved first impression, with the warmth kept for the few you let close. Leo never opens the 7th or the 9th as well here, and across all births it goes without any cusp in about one London chart in seven.

On either side in time lie [Leo on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/), for a birth a little earlier, and [Leo on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/), for one a little later. The minute decides between them, which is why I want it before I read anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The old astrologers looked to the 8th for death and for legacies. I read it as intimacy, trust and what's held in common, and no chart can say when a life will end or what anyone will inherit ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
