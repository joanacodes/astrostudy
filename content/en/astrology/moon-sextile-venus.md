Moon sextile Venus is the knack of making someone feel at home, written as an angle. It links your needs and your affections through an easy, friendly channel that does its best work when you put it to use: an invitation, a kind word, the kettle on.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds your needs and attachments: what you reach for to feel safe, and how you care for others. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) holds your values and affinities: what you're drawn to, what you prefer, and the give-and-take that keeps a bond fair. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity: cooperation that's there for the asking.

In plain words, affection comes naturally once there's an opening. You know how to welcome people, and how to let them welcome you; you can ask for comfort without making it heavy, and offer it without making it a debt. Reciprocity is the quiet strength here: care moves both ways. But the sextile won't send the message or set the table for you. Left unused, it's a warmth that people sense and you never quite act on.

Two signs apart, the link joins elements that get along. With the Moon in Cancer and Venus in Virgo, care shows as useful kindness, the errand run, the thing mended; with the Moon in Sagittarius and Venus in Aquarius, a need for room is met by friendships that leave it.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Most often it shows as hospitality, friendships that steady you, and a talent for small comforts. The houses show where you practise it. With the Moon in the 11th and Venus in the 1st, friends can come to feel like family, and you meet strangers with an open face.

Within 8°, it belongs to about 1 person in 11, so it's common; what makes it yours is the pair of signs and houses it links. Near exact, that warmth is one of the first things the chart says about you; at the wide end, it's a pleasant undertone. Venus turns retrograde about every 18 months, for about six weeks, and if you were born in one of those spells you may show affection more quietly, or a little later, once you're sure of your own feelings: an inward rhythm, not a coldness ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

Compared with this sextile, the pair's other aspects either fuse the two, flow, or pull:

- **[Moon conjunct Venus](/astrology/moon-conjunct-venus/):** comfort and pleasure fused into one gesture, close to a new moon; the sextile keeps them separate enough to trade.
- **[Moon square Venus](/astrology/moon-square-venus/):** the same two at cross purposes, so what soothes you and what you enjoy have to negotiate.
- **[Moon trine Venus](/astrology/moon-trine-venus/):** a wider, unprompted ease, affection that flows without waiting for an invitation.
- **[Moon opposite Venus](/astrology/moon-opposite-venus/):** need and affection across the chart, often found in a partner before you find them in yourself.

Between two charts, [Moon sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/moon-sextile-venus-synastry/) lets one person's affection meet the other's need for comfort in a friendly, unforced way, as long as someone takes the first step. I look at both sides, since the one who welcomes and the one welcomed don't always swap roles.

## In transits {#in-transits}

The Moon sextiles your natal Venus on two days each month, and about twice a year Venus in the sky returns the favour to your natal Moon for a couple of days: good moments to invite someone round, or to say yes to an invitation.

It's weather, not a forecast: an easy breeze for warmth and company, which still needs you to open the door.
