Both of Neptune's trines to your Midheaven rise from below the horizon, from the half of the chart that works out of sight: one from around your 2nd house, one from around your 6th. Most people meet one, some both, about 1 in 6 neither.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where what you can do takes a visible shape: a role, a contribution, the work you're known for. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) adds imagination, compassion and a sense of calling. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, gives the two a common current, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the ideal and the job pull the same way for a while: a creative side finds room at work, care becomes part of how you do things, and what you do in public feels more like your own.

Each trine has its own root. The one from the 2nd house grows out of what you value and the skills you have built ([Neptune transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-2nd-house/)); the one from the 6th grows out of the daily craft and its routines ([Neptune transiting the 6th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-6th-house/)). Neither says anything about income or health: they describe where the ease comes from, not what it pays or costs.

The Midheaven's blind spot under a trine is taking recognition for legitimacy. Warmth from others for what you do is pleasant, but it isn't the measure of the work, and a trine doesn't push: its two and a half years can slip by unused.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, this trine lasts about two and a half years a pass, twenty-two months in the briefest, usually with three exact hits and with five in about a quarter of passes.

The two trines sit 120° apart, so someone who meets both has about 55 years between them, with [transiting Neptune opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-midheaven/) at the IC in the middle. In the count for births from 1930 to 2009, most people meet one by 80, some both and about 1 in 6 neither, mostly people whose natal Neptune lay in the western stretch from the late 6th to the 8th house, just past one trine.

A birth time recorded in summer time has to be corrected by an hour before the chart is drawn; leave that out and the Midheaven moves about 15°, taking both trines with it ([Time zones and daylight saving in charts](/astrology/time-zones-and-daylight-saving-in-charts/), [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

From the 2nd-house side, the trine follows the square near your Ascendant ([transiting Neptune square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-midheaven/)) on Neptune's way down to the IC; from the 6th, it comes after the IC and before the square near your Descendant, which leads up to [transiting Neptune sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-midheaven/) and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-midheaven/). Neptune in Aries, until March 2039, trines Sagittarius Midheavens from the 2nd-house side and Leo ones from the 6th ([Leo on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/), [Sagittarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let the work carry something you love. This trine makes it easy to bring a craft, a cause or a kindness into what you are paid or known for.

Keep the measure your own. Praise may come more easily now; judge the work by whether it does what you meant it to.

Give the ease a routine to live in: an hour a day for the creative side, a weekly practice. From the 6th-house side especially, the trine works through habits.
