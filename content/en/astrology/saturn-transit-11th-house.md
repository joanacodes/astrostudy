Saturn's years in your 11th house sort the crowd. Friends, groups and the hopes you share with them come under a slow, honest light, and what you build with other people gets a structure that can last.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) tests what will hold and builds what can; the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is community, friendship, networks and the goals you pursue with others. Saturn's stay here tends to show which friendships carry weight and which were only habit, which groups you belong to and which you only attend. Some circles may thin out while others deepen, and you may take on a role, a commitment or a long project with people you trust.

Before Uranus was discovered, Aquarius belonged to Saturn, and Aquarius is the 11th's sign in the natural order, so this house is old Saturn country even though modern astrology hands the sign to Uranus; I read both. It shows in the transit's manner: it asks your hopes to become plans, with dates, roles and a first step.

With your Sun in the 11th, Saturn's pass asks what part you want to play among others, and whether you've been playing someone else's.

Belonging isn't conformity, and friendship isn't popularity: what Saturn builds here is fewer, truer ties. Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), it brings out what your chart already holds about the people around you, and it doesn't decide who comes into your life or who leaves it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 11th matches the all-house figures at London's latitude almost exactly: just under three years in the middle of Placidus charts drawn there, with most stays between about 23 months and 4 years. At New York's latitude the short end lengthens to just over 2 years and the long end shortens to nearly 4.

Every cusp in a reading rests on the birth time, the 11th's included, and I'd rather give no reading than one with the houses guessed ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows Saturn's years at the top of your chart, in the 10th ([Saturn transiting the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-10th-house/)), and leads into its quiet years in the 12th ([Saturn transiting the 12th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-12th-house/)).

Find the sign on your 11th cusp, and its ruler if that helps you remember. Saturn is now in Mars's sign, Aries, from February 2026 to April 2028; then in Venus's Taurus from April 2028 to May 2030, Mercury's Gemini from June 2030 to July 2032, the Moon's Cancer from July 2032 to May 2035, the Sun's Leo from August 2034 to July 2037, Mercury's Virgo from October 2036 to September 2039 and Venus's Libra from September 2039. Aquarius, the 11th's natural sign, comes after 2039, as do Scorpio, Sagittarius and Capricorn, and Pisces had its turn from March 2023 to February 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep the friendships that hold, and let the others thin out without a verdict on anyone. Saturn in the 11th sorts, and sorting isn't the same as rejecting.

Take on a role in a group only if you mean to keep it. Commitments made here tend to be noticed, and kept ones become the backbone of a circle.

Give a shared hope a plan: a date, a budget, a first meeting. The 11th is the house of the future, and Saturn's gift to it is the step that makes the future start.
