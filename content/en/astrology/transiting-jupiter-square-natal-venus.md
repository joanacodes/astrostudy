Two planets that like a yes don't always agree on how much. When Jupiter squares your Venus, the appetite for more pleasure, more company and more beauty runs into the question of what you actually value.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) decides what you value and enjoy, and keeps the give and take of a bond fair. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) wants more of whatever it touches. At 90°, a [square](/astrology/square/) makes them push on each other, and the push here is between liking and valuing: all you could have, and the part of it that's worth having.

It tends to show as too much of good things: one more evening out, one more thing bought because it pleased you for a moment, generosity in a relationship that leaves the other person owing more than they wanted to. Taste can inflate too: the bigger version, the finer one, the one you'd have to stretch for. Beauty isn't vanity, and wanting lovely things is fine; the square only asks whether this one is really yours. Reciprocity can tilt as well. None of this foretells a loss, of money or of love. The square's work is discernment, which is Venus at its best: choosing what you'll say yes to, and meaning it.

Tradition has a sign where both planets struggle: Virgo, where Venus has its [fall](/astrology/fall/) and Jupiter is in detriment. The old picture fits this square well, with pleasure and abundance both asked to count carefully.

Where the strain shows depends on the house Jupiter is crossing. From your 2nd ([Jupiter transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-2nd-house/)), it tends to sit in what you own, spend on and keep; your Venus's house says what the appetite is really for. Both houses come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), so I read neither without it. If [Venus square Jupiter](/astrology/venus-square-jupiter/) is in your chart, this [transit](/astrology/transits/) is an old acquaintance: one of its two squares arrives with every Jupiter return.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured with my orbs, which begin 1½° before exact and end 1° after, each square holds your Venus for about eleven days. When it sits inside a retrograde loop, about three times in ten, Jupiter makes the square exact three times in about eight months.

Jupiter reaches this angle twice a round: once while the warmth of [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-venus/) is still settling into your habits, and once after [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-venus/) has shown you someone else's terms. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-venus/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-venus/) give the same two planets an easier exchange. By 80, everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has had thirteen or fourteen of these squares, each a chance to learn the difference between what you like and what you value.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Before you buy, book or promise, ask whether you'd still want it in three months. The square makes the moment's pleasure loud.

Give with an eye on balance. Generosity that leaves someone uncomfortable isn't the kind Venus is for.

Choose one pleasure and do it properly, rather than five in a hurry. Discernment is the skill this angle trains, and it is easier to practise on a short list.
