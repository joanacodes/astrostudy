The North Node's months in your 10th house close at the very top of your chart, the Midheaven, and the South Node spends them in your 4th, at the bottom. For about nineteen months the vertical line of the chart tilts upwards: out of the house, into the world.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the less practised direction, and the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is your public life: work, standing, reputation, what you want to build where others can see it. With the node here, the growth is taking up room in that world: accepting responsibility, putting your name to something, letting your work be judged and learning from the judgement.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in your 4th describes what feels safe: home, family, the private self, the work nobody sees. That ground stays yours, and you'll still go back to it. These months ask whether it has become a place to hide.

Capricorn, the 10th's natural sign, belongs to Saturn, and tradition gave Mars its exaltation there: ambition with patience. Born with Saturn in the 10th? When the node passes over it, a long-held aim tends to feel within reach of real effort.

A [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this one brings out what your chart already holds about vocation. It doesn't appoint you to anything, and I don't read a career outcome or a destiny in it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the true node's stays in the 10th last about 19 months in the middle and rarely fall short of a year, the same short end as the 4th across the chart; most are over by about 2 years. At New York's latitude the middle is the same, and the range runs from about 13 months to just under 2 years.

Counted with the true node, a slip back over the cusp that reverses within 120 days belongs to the same stay. The mean node, the other one I look at ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), is never far: a degree or two at most.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the stay ends at your Midheaven ([transiting North Node conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)), while the South Node crosses your IC. Your Midheaven needs your birth time, like every angle, which is why I don't read without one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node arrives from your 11th ([North Node transiting the 11th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-11th-house/)) and leaves for your 9th ([North Node transiting the 9th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-9th-house/)).

The year your Midheaven is reached depends on its sign. An Aquarius Midheaven is crossed between July 2026 and March 2028; a Capricorn one, in the 10th's own sign, between March 2028 and September 2029; then Sagittarius up to March 2031, Scorpio up to December 2032, Libra up to June 2034, Virgo up to November 2035, Leo up to May 2037 and Cancer up to February 2039. With a Pisces Midheaven, the stay closed between January 2025 and July 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take one visible step: the application, the proposal, the conversation with whoever decides.

Accept the responsibility that fits you, and say no to the kind that is only someone else's weight.

Keep home in the timetable. The South Node in the 4th is where you recover, and a public life lasts longer with a private one under it.
