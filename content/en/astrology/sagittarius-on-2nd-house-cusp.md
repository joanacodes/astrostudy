With Sagittarius on the 2nd house cusp, the house of possessions and self-worth opens in the sign of the long view. Money here is less a cushion than a ticket, and the question it keeps asking is what your resources will let you learn, see or try next.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds what you earn and keep, and the worth you feel underneath it; [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) on its cusp gives that house the manner of an explorer ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). I read it as money valued for the room it makes: the course paid for before the sofa, savings that turn into a year abroad, work taken for less because it teaches you something. Self-worth follows the same line. You tend to feel worth more when you're learning or on the move, and smaller when life narrows to a routine that leads nowhere. The generosity is real, and so is the risk that comes with it: Sagittarius trusts that more will come, and the 2nd is where that trust meets the bank statement.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules Sagittarius, so it rules this house, and the place it occupies in your chart is where money is made, spent or given away; its sign says in what spirit. Jupiter changes sign roughly once a year, so many people share its sign with you; the house is the personal part. Where the money goes, Jupiter shows: the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/) house. Jupiter has a second sign, Pisces, which it shares with Neptune, and in nearly all these charts Pisces opens your 4th house or your 5th. Your income then answers to the same planet as your home or your pleasures, and I read Neptune there too.

Few of Sagittarius's cusps are commoner: it opens the 2nd in about 1 birth in 10, a figure London and New York share, in Placidus, the house system I use. Sagittarius closes the slow-rising half of the zodiac in the northern hemisphere, so the cusps under the rising degree linger in it far longer than in a quick sign like Capricorn. It settles the east of the chart as well. At both latitudes, every chart with this cusp has [Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/): Pluto and Mars speak for the chart and Jupiter for the purse: a guarded front with an open hand behind it. In these charts the 2nd spans some 35° to 42° at London, too wide for Sagittarius to reach a second cusp, and roughly 3 London charts in 10 hold all of Capricorn inside it too, an [intercepted sign](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) that gives Saturn a quieter say in what you hold on to.

The sky keeps turning after the minute you were born. Later the same day, Sagittarius climbs to the Ascendant ([Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/)); earlier, it sat on the next cusp down ([Sagittarius on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/)). Only the birth time says which of these is yours, and I need it before I begin ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Jupiter's name for plenty tempts people to read this cusp as a promise of wealth. It isn't one. The 2nd shows how you earn, spend and value, never the sums, and picking investments is beyond any chart ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). What it can show is why money that only sits still rarely feels like security to you.
