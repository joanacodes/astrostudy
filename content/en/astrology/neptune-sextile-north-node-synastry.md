Help with growing doesn't always come as a push. With Neptune sextile North Node in synastry, the help comes as an offer from one person's Neptune, some 60° away from the other's North Node: a picture of where they might go, held out to be taken or left.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) gives a person their feel for the possible: images, ideals, compassion, a trust in what can't yet be measured. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) stands for a capacity still being learned, less automatic than what came first; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite keeps what came first. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them by opportunity; and because the two nodes sit 180° apart, a Neptune 60° from one stands 120° from the other, so this contact touches both ends. Among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), that makes it a bridge one person lends to the other.

For the one whose North Node it is, the new direction feels less solitary around this person. They suggest rather than steer, they can picture you doing the unfamiliar thing, and they're happy with your old strengths too, since their Neptune trines your South Node. With them, you don't have to choose between who you've been and who you're becoming.

If the Neptune is yours, your dreaming finds a use. You can hand over a vision, a story, a sense of what's possible, and watch someone turn it into practice. It's the gentlest kind of influence, and it works best when you're content to suggest.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The trine to the South Node is the soft trap. Imagination flows downhill, and here downhill is the familiar: shared memories, old routines, the comfortable version of the other person. A pair can spend years enjoying the easy line while the sextile, the opening towards the new, sits unused. Neither person is holding the other back; the old ground is simply more pleasant to dream in.

How the same two meet at other angles:

- **[Neptune conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the dream sits right on the path, harder to tell apart from it.
- **[Neptune square North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-north-node-synastry/):** imagination crosses the road at right angles, a detour asking to be folded in.
- **[Neptune trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-north-node-synastry/):** a wider, steadier current towards the new, with the sextile turned back to the old.
- **[Neptune opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** Neptune resting on old ground, with the growth across the chart.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take the offer on purpose. Once in a while, check whether your time together feeds the new direction or only the old one, and steer a little if it's always the old. If the North Node is yours, tell the other what you're practising, so their imagination has something to land on. Friends can turn this sextile into a shared project; siblings, into encouragement free of family roles; colleagues, into a vision someone else gets to build.

A 1975 birth makes the point: Neptune there, in Sagittarius, is 60° from the North Node of people born from December 1977 to February 1978, and of people born from April to August 1990. Reverse the roles and the 1975 North Node finds Neptune 60° away in everyone born from November 1997 to February 1999, and in people born from August 1942 to October 1943: one contact reaching a parent's generation and a child's. That reach is why it stays off my list of synastry contacts, each of which touches the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven, and read it in the houses, where generations hand each other something.

The hour of birth won't change the sextile; for the houses, both times are needed ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The orb is only 2.4°, so the node matters: I look at both nodes here, mean and true; these months come from the true one ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

[Neptune sextile North Node](/astrology/neptune-sextile-north-node/), in one chart, lets a person's own imagination help their growth. Lent across two charts, it belongs to a pair, and I read it with both of them or not at all ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
