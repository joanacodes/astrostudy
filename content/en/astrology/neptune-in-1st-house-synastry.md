Someone looks at you as if they had been expecting you. With their Neptune in your 1st house, another person's imagination settles on the most visible part of you, the face and manner you lead with, and the way you come across softens at the edges, in their eyes and sometimes in yours.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is imagination and compassion in a chart: sensing more than is said, idealising, letting boundaries thin. [The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is your body, your manner and your first moves, the self that walks in before anyone knows you. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their dreaming meets the way you appear.

For you, it can feel like being seen in a kind light. You may move more gracefully around them, fret less about your looks, try a softer style, or let a creative streak show in your clothes and gestures. Their gaze tends to forgive rather than inspect.

For them, you can be something like a muse. Your way of arriving answers a picture they already carried, and their imagination gathers around your presence: they may want to film you, write about you, or simply watch you begin things. The 1st is angular, so what lands there shows quickly, and [Aries](/astrology/aries/), its natural sign, opens the zodiac: fog is an unusual companion for a first step. A few degrees from your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), their Neptune also conjoins the rising degree itself, the loudest part of the overlay.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A soft lens blurs as well as flatters. You may lose track of which version of you they see, and start shaping yourself to fit it: the calmer you, the more mysterious you, the one who never catches a cold. Your first moves can grow hesitant around them, as if acting plainly might break a spell.

From their side, the image can stand between them and you. On an ordinary Tuesday, short of sleep and patience, you may disappoint a picture you never agreed to pose for, and they may feel a loss that has nothing to do with anything you did.

Neither of you is pretending. Neptune here describes imagination working on a first impression, not deceit, and an image can live beside the real person for years if both of you keep looking.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let them see the unfinished versions: early mornings, bad moods, your face without effort. Ask what they see in you, and say which parts are true. In a couple, that keeps tenderness from becoming a role; between friends, it lets the one who idealises you know you too; between colleagues, it stops your manner from turning into a story told about you; between adult relatives, it can retire an old family picture of who you were.

Seen from their chart, the same contact is your Neptune in their 1st house, with your imagination resting on their face and manner. [Their Neptune in your 12th house](/astrology/neptune-in-12th-house-synastry/) works on what you keep private; [their Neptune in your 2nd house](/astrology/neptune-in-2nd-house-synastry/) on what you value and earn.

Neptune spends about fourteen years in a sign, so everyone born within a few years of them carries it near the same degree. Close in age, their Neptune lies near yours, and if your own sits in your 1st ([Neptune in the 1st house](/astrology/neptune-in-1st-house/)), much of this soft focus belongs to your shared generation; with a decade or more between you, it's the dream of their era meeting your face. Their birth date places Neptune. Your birth time draws the Ascendant, which moves about fifteen degrees an hour on average, and in Placidus, the house system I use, that decides between your 12th and your 1st ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Both times, and both people's yes, come before I read the pair ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
