Some pairs can tell you exactly what they'd save from a fire, what they'd never sell and what they could happily do without. Composite Mercury in the 2nd house gives a relationship that kind of clarity: a shared mind that keeps returning to what the two of you value and hold.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Composite [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the relationship's own thinking, the midpoint of your two natal Mercurys in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), and its house says what that thinking is busy with. [The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/), succedent, with Taurus as its natural sign, covers resources, possessions, skills and values: what a life stands on.

Here the bond talks about what it has and what it's for. Lists matter (what to repair, what to replace, what to give away), and so do the skills the two of you bring together: one cooks, one fixes, both teach. The relationship often has a practical intelligence about shared means, from a joint project's budget to whose turn it is to pay for the train. It also puts its values into words more readily than most: what we won't do, what we always make time for. Compare the natal [Mercury in the 2nd house](/astrology/mercury-in-2nd-house/), which weighs one person's worth and means, and the overlay [their Mercury in your 2nd house](/astrology/mercury-in-2nd-house-synastry/), where one person's ideas change what the other values. In the composite, the conversation is about what belongs to both, and it can make two careless people careful.

None of it forecasts money. Resources aren't wealth, and no chart, composite or natal, can say what a pair will earn, save or lose ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). What this placement describes is a habit of thought: the relationship weighs, compares and keeps track, as Mercury does, and the 2nd gives it things to weigh.

Composite Mercury normally keeps within about 28° of the composite Sun, so the two often share a house or sit in neighbouring ones. In about 1 pair in 26, though, the midpoints land nearly opposite, and the composite Sun ends up around the 8th, the house of what's shared and owed: a coincidence of arithmetic.

The 2nd's cusp isn't an angle, which matters for the method. Taken from the midpoints of your natal cusps, it is simply halfway between your two 2nd-house cusps; built from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, it comes out of a house system's own sums. A Mercury near that line can sit in the 1st by one method and in the 2nd by the other, and both methods need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The risk is talk about money standing in for talk about worth. A bond that tracks resources carefully can end up keeping score, or arguing about a purchase when the real disagreement is about what each of you values. Keeping the two conversations apart helps: the practical one with a notepad, the other without.

[Composite Mercury in the 1st house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-1st-house/) is known for its talk; [composite Mercury in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-3rd-house/) lives in its daily messages. This is a description of a technique I leave out of my own readings. Those who use it read it with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and like any chart of two people it starts with both of them agreeing ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
