When Jupiter crosses the Imum Coeli, the point at the very bottom of your chart, it begins about a year in your 4th house. Its growth goes underground for a while: into home, family and the private self, the part of your life most people never see.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens what you understand and helps it mean something; the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, roots and belonging, the ground you stand on and the self you keep behind your own front door. For a year, Jupiter makes that ground more generous. You may want more room where you live, more people round the table or fewer, more time inside your own life, or a better understanding of where you come from and what you carry from it.

The IC is where your chart touches the ground. In Placidus, the house system I use, it's the cusp of the 4th, and Jupiter standing on it is also Jupiter opposite your Midheaven ([transiting Jupiter opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-midheaven/)), as if the public side of your life stepped back to let the private one grow. There's a fitting echo in the natural order, too: Cancer, the 4th house's sign, is where tradition placed Jupiter's exaltation, as if care and belonging were where its generosity feels most at home.

If you were born with the Moon in your 4th, the house's natural ruler, Jupiter's pass over it is often the warmest part of the year: feelings about home come closer to the surface.

Like every [transit](/astrology/transits/), it brings out what your chart already holds; it doesn't decide whether you move, or what happens in your family.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 4th tends to keep Jupiter a little over a year, the range running from about 11 months to 22 in Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, with New York's giving the same. What stretches a stay is Jupiter's retrograde loop, about four months of every year. When a loop falls across your IC, Jupiter crosses the point three times, and the stay counts from the first crossing.

When it starts depends on your IC, which hangs on the minute you were born just as the Ascendant does, and how long it lasts on the size of your 4th: both need your birth time, the reason I never read a chart without one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The year before, the growth was in talk and learning ([Jupiter transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-3rd-house/)); the year after, it turns to play and pleasure ([Jupiter transiting the 5th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-5th-house/)).

Your IC is always the sign opposite your Midheaven: with the Midheaven in Aquarius, for instance, the IC is in Leo, which Jupiter crosses from June 2026 to July 2027. An IC in Virgo comes next (July 2027 to August 2028), then Libra (to September 2029), Scorpio (to October 2030), Sagittarius (to November 2031) and Capricorn (to November 2032). Aquarius runs from April 2032 to December 2033, as Jupiter moves back and forth over the line, Pisces from April 2033 to April 2034, Aries to April 2035, Taurus to May 2036 and Gemini to May 2037, and an IC in Cancer, the sign of Jupiter's exaltation, waits for May 2037 to June 2038.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make room at home in whatever sense you need: a cleared corner, an open door, or a quieter house. Jupiter in the 4th widens the private world, and you get to choose what fills it.

Ask about your family's story, from the people willing to tell it. Understanding where you come from is one of the gentler forms of growth this house offers, and it doesn't need anyone else to change.

Root something that will outlast the year: a habit at home, a garden, a ritual that makes a place yours. If a move is already on your mind, let the year ask what you need from a home before it asks where.
