Halfway round each of its laps of your chart, Jupiter faces the degree your Neptune held at birth. Your ideals meet somebody else's large vision, and the work is to see both clearly without growing cold towards either.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where you imagine, idealise and feel with others; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), where you believe and grow. In an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), one end of the axis is often held by another person, and here it's usually Jupiter's: a teacher whose certainty is inspiring, a partner with a vision for both of you, a group with a cause that asks for your heart.

The gift is real. Other people's beliefs can widen your own dreams, and compassion flows easily across an opposition. The risk is one of Neptune's blind spots: feeling deeply with someone is not the same as knowing them, and this transit blurs the two. The useful question is always what you know, as well as what you feel.

Which part of life is involved depends on your Neptune's house; Jupiter will be moving through its opposite number. With Neptune in your 1st, where the way you come across can be hard to pin down, the opposition comes during [Jupiter transiting the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-7th-house/): a partner's or adviser's large view faces your own still-forming sense of self. Placing either house takes your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A chart with [Jupiter opposite Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-neptune/) has this tension built in, and the sky renews it close to every [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/).

A season like this is also when people are most open to anyone selling a spiritual shortcut. No one can sell the sense of meaning this opposition stirs, and if someone offers, read [Paid “spiritual work”](/astrology/paid-spiritual-work/) first.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Jupiter faces your Neptune for about eleven days when it passes straight on, counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after. If one of its stations falls close by, as in three [transits](/astrology/transits/) out of ten, it faces it three times over about eight months. Six or seven oppositions mark the years to 80 for anyone born from 1930 to 2009.

How far Jupiter had to travel set the age. From Pisces, where it stood in January 1975, the point opposite that year's Neptune was a quarter of the zodiac away: about 2.4 years. From Cancer, in January 1955, it was three quarters away: about 9.2.

From here, the rest of the round looks like a preparation and an aftermath. [Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-neptune/), six years earlier, planted the dream; [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-neptune/), [transiting Jupiter square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-neptune/) shaped it on the way here, and on the way back they help you bring it home.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look at the person as well as the vision. Ask what they actually do, how they treat people, what they want from you; admiration survives the questions if it deserves to.

Decide in advance what you can offer a cause or a person, and say it out loud. Compassion with a shape lasts longer than compassion without one.

Keep your own dream alive beside theirs. An opposition asks for balance, and a vision you borrow is easier to hold when you still have one of your own.
