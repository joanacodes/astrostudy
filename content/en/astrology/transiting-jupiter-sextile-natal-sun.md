Twice in every twelve years, Jupiter stands two signs from your Sun: two years after it has crossed your Sun, and two years before it crosses again. Of everything Jupiter does to your Sun, the sextile is the quietest, and the one that most depends on your noticing.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens whatever it touches and looks for what things mean; your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the part of you that settles on a direction and puts yourself into it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates the two through cooperation: Jupiter offers, and the Sun has to answer. It forces nothing on you, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) slips by unnoticed in a great many lives.

What it offers tends to suit who you already are. It can be an invitation that uses a skill you'd stopped counting, a class that starts at the right moment, a project where your confidence meets somebody else's need. A sextile links signs whose elements get on, so the opening usually comes from a part of life that speaks your Sun's language rather than one that asks you to become someone else.

Which house Jupiter is crossing says what kind of door it is. From your 11th, the house of Jupiter's own joy, the sextile tends to come through friends, groups and plans made with others ([Jupiter transiting the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-11th-house/)); your Sun's house says what the opening is for. Both depend on your birth time, and without them I wouldn't read it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Sun sextile Jupiter](/astrology/sun-sextile-jupiter/), you'll have one of the two sextiles at each Jupiter return, from the side it held when you were born.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, a sextile from Jupiter usually holds your Sun for about eleven days. Roughly three passes in ten are stretched by a retrograde loop to about eight months and three exact hits.

Your Sun sign is enough to see this one coming, and everyone knows their Sun sign. The sextile comes from the signs two places either side of yours, and Jupiter spends about a year in each. It is in Leo from June 2026 to July 2027, and in that time it sextiles every Sun in Gemini and in Libra; your Sun's degree gives the day.

Everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has thirteen or fourteen of these small doors for their sense of purpose by 80, two in every round. The first comes about two years after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-sun/), when what began at the crossing is ready for a practical first step; the second about two years before the next crossing, when what you've grown into can be put to use. Between them, Jupiter presses on your direction at [transiting Jupiter square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-sun/), lets it run at [transiting Jupiter trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-sun/) and holds it up to other people at [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-sun/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes in a way that costs you some effort: fill in the form, send the message, turn up. A sextile favours whoever moves first, and there's rarely a second invitation before it ends.

Offer something too. Jupiter grows by sharing, and the Sun by being seen doing what it believes in, so the best use of these days often looks like generosity with your own skills.

Keep it small enough to start this week. Remaking your life is the conjunction's work; the sextile is for the step the conjunction made possible.
