Some pairs are at their best with a problem nobody has solved yet. Mars sextile Uranus in synastry sets one person's Mars about 60° from the other's Uranus, and between them a new idea rarely waits long for someone to test it.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is initiative: the willingness to start, to push, to compete, to see a thing through. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is invention: differentiation, experiment, reform, the freedom to rearrange what's there. Under a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), two functions have an opening between them, real but waiting to be used. Between two charts, as with all [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), each person holds one of those functions and offers it to the other.

One person's Mars in Cancer with the other's Uranus in Virgo, water and earth: effort that wants to protect and provide meets a knack for practical reform. The Mars person wants the household to run better for everyone; the Uranus person has three ideas for how, and one of them works. The pair's gift is speed of learning: an idea is tried, the result is in by Friday, and the next version is better.

If the Uranus is yours, you finally have someone who'll test your ideas rather than just admire them, which is rarer than it sounds. If the Mars is yours, you're handed new methods just when the old ones stall, and your effort may go further with less strain.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Experiments are easy to start here, and just as easy to waste. They begin cheerfully and stop halfway; the Uranus person moves on to the next idea, the Mars person to the next task, and the improvements never quite settle. Novelty isn't the same as innovation, and a pair this comfortable with trying things can confuse the two.

Freedom can go unshared, too. If the Uranus person always proposes and the Mars person always carries out, the Mars person can feel like a test bench, and the Uranus person never tries their own ideas with their own hands.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Pick one experiment and finish it. If the Uranus is yours, try carrying out one of your own ideas; if the Mars is yours, suggest a change now and then rather than only making them. In a couple, it might be a new way of sharing the chores that actually stays; between friends, a shared hobby that keeps evolving; in a family, a fresh approach to an old tradition; at work, the improvement that gets written down so others can use it.

Inside its 3.6° orb, the sextile joins about 1 pair of people in 13, one way round or the other. Mars turns retrograde about every two years, for about two and a half months, and lingers over the same few degrees while it does, so more birth dates than usual share its position then; the hour still decides only the houses, and those I always need ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Where it's Mercury rather than Mars, [Mercury sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-uranus-synastry/) trades ideas in words instead of in action.

In a single chart, [Mars sextile Uranus](/astrology/mars-sextile-uranus/) is one person's own effort and inventiveness on friendly terms. Between two charts, [Mars conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) puts the effort right on the idea, [Mars square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-uranus-synastry/) makes them catch on each other, [Mars trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-uranus-synastry/) gives the same ease a deeper, one-element footing, and [Mars opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-uranus-synastry/) pulls effort and freedom to opposite ends.
