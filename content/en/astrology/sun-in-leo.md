Every other Sun sign hands part of its business to another planet. With the Sun in Leo, there's no one to hand it to: the Sun was in its own sign when you were born, and it describes a self that has to be its own reference point, which is both the gift and the difficulty.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity, vitality, purpose and what I think of as a chart's creative centre, the place your own way of making things comes from. [Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire, is the one sign the Sun rules, so here the function works in its native manner: warm, steady, and loyal to whatever it has decided is its own. Tradition calls this the Sun's [domicile](/astrology/domicile/). I read it as ease of expression, not as a promise of confidence; plenty of people with a Leo Sun doubt themselves, and the rest of the chart usually shows why.

Because the Sun rules Leo, your Sun is also its own ruler, its own [dispositor](/astrology/dispositors-and-the-final-dispositor/) in the old language: it doesn't report to Mars, Venus or Saturn the way other Suns do. That gives its house and its aspects double weight. The house shows where you look for a life that feels like yours; the aspects show which parts of the chart help and which ones argue.

The Sun is in Leo from about 23 July to 22 August, about 31 days and sometimes 32, which gives it to about 1 person in 12. If your birthday sits at either end, your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) settles the sign.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Loving someone, a Leo Sun gives warmly and wants to be their particular person, not one admirer among many. You tend to show who you are by celebrating the people you love: the birthday planned for weeks, the praise said in front of others. What hurts is being seen inaccurately, praised for a version of you that isn't real or overlooked for the one that is. A partner who notices what you actually made, and says so, steadies this Sun more than any amount of general admiration. The lesson is to receive attention as well as give it, without having to earn it each time.

## At work {#at-work}

Professionally, a Leo Sun wants its own judgement to be part of the product: a design, a lesson, a menu, a decision with your name attached. Where the [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) works to shelter a team and the [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) to refine a method, the Leo Sun works to make something whole and stand behind it. Purpose fades in roles where your taste is overruled as a matter of routine. Recognition, here, isn't fame. It means the right people knowing that a piece of work came from you, and given that, a Leo Sun tends to work long, loyally, and with generous credit for others.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Because the Sun answers to itself here, no other planet shares the weight. When the light isn't reflected back, a missed thank-you, a project nobody noticed, the self can start to doubt its own reality, and pride may stop you asking for the help you'd give anyone else without a second thought. Some people with a Leo Sun respond by performing harder; others go quiet and stop showing what they make. Neither is vanity. Both are a creative centre waiting for a sign that it's still seen.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make something for no audience at all, and do it regularly, so your vitality doesn't hang on applause. Learn the difference between recognition and approval: the first says *I see what you did*, the second *I like it*, and you need far more of the first. Let other people's light into the room, since generosity is this Sun's native warmth rather than a loss of it. And when you doubt yourself, look at what you've kept making for years without being asked.

A Leo Sun sets a trap for someone like me: the chart's centre and the ruler of its sign are the same planet, so a reading could lean on it twice. [My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) is my check. The Sun sign held about a third of the time there, while Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90%, so before I let one planet speak twice, I look harder at everything else.
