Venus square Uranus is what you hold dear meeting what you need to change, at right angles. It asks a question that can take years to put into words: how close can I be, and still be myself?

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) attaches and evaluates: what attracts you, what you'd keep, what makes a bond feel fair. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) liberates and reforms: it wants room, difference, a new way of doing things. Joined by a [square](/astrology/square/), they meet in constructive friction: each keeps unsettling the other, and the adjustments are where you grow.

In plain words, the pull towards someone and the pull towards freedom can arrive together. You may be drawn to what surprises you and then need space from it, love steadiness and feel restless inside it, or change your taste just as it had settled. None of this is instability. It's a square asking you to design relationships, and a life, with room in them: closeness that doesn't cage, independence that doesn't walk away. Harmony here isn't the absence of change; it's change agreed on.

When this square is loud in a chart, I don't tell anyone to stay or to go. I ask what kind of room would let the bond breathe ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Squares usually link signs that share a modality, and the modality colours the rub. With Venus in Taurus and Uranus in Leo or Aquarius, all fixed, a love of the familiar meets a stubborn need to be different, and neither likes to move first. With Venus in Cancer and Uranus in Aries or Libra, all cardinal, care and independence each want to start things their own way. In the houses, Venus in the 3rd square Uranus in the 6th might set the pleasure of easy conversation against daily routines that keep changing shape.

About 1 person in 15 is born with Venus and Uranus within 6° of a square. Close to exact, the question of closeness and room tends to come up in every important bond; with a wider orb, mostly at turning points.

The rest of the pair's aspects:

- **[Venus conjunct Uranus](/astrology/venus-conjunct-uranus/):** the two together in one sign; a taste that's unmistakably yours, and a need for space in love.
- **[Venus sextile Uranus](/astrology/venus-sextile-uranus/):** small openings for change in what you love, through people and ideas, at your own pace.
- **[Venus trine Uranus](/astrology/venus-trine-uranus/):** the two in one element; originality that sits easily with affection.
- **[Venus opposite Uranus](/astrology/venus-opposite-uranus/):** closeness and freedom facing each other; a balance that has to be said out loud.

Between two charts, [Venus square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-uranus-synastry/) brings one person's affection up against the other's need for freedom: exciting, unsettling, and rarely dull. What feels like a breath of air to one can feel like a draught to the other, so I read it from both charts.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), [Transiting Uranus square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-venus/) comes about 21 years before Uranus reaches your Venus or after it leaves, and lingers for about a year: a season when what you love, or how you love, asks for more room. If the square is yours, it arrives with your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), around 42, when Uranus faces its own birth place and squares your Venus at the same time.

Weather, not a forecast: a wind that rearranges things, never a decision made for you.
