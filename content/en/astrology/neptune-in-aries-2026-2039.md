Neptune moves into a new sign about every fourteen years. In January 2026 it settled into Aries, the first sign of the zodiac, after a first crossing in March 2025 and a step back that October. It stays until March 2039.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) takes about 165 years to go round the Sun, so it crosses each sign in about fourteen. Its arrival in [Aries](/astrology/aries/) came in two steps, its yearly retrograde straddling the border, and its departure will too. In the dates this site keeps:

- **30 March 2025:** first crossing into Aries.
- **22 October 2025:** back into Pisces.
- **26 January 2026:** into Aries for good.
- **21 May 2038:** into Taurus, then back into Aries on 21 October 2038.
- **23 March 2039:** gone for good.

Its previous stay in Aries ran from April 1861 to April 1875, so the sign last had Neptune in it more than a lifetime ago. Children born since 2025 mostly carry it in their charts; that natal placement has its own page ([Neptune in Aries](/astrology/neptune-in-aries/)), and I don't read children's charts. For everyone else, it is a transit.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

A transit of Neptune in Aries reaches a chart in three ways.

- **Through a house.** Aries falls somewhere in every chart, and Neptune now crosses that house, slowly; [Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/) shows what such a stay can look like. Aries has one ruler, [Mars](/astrology/mars/), so I also look at where Mars sits in your birth chart: it shows where that house's story plays out ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Both need your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
- **Through natal planets in Aries.** Neptune meets them one degree at a time, the first degrees of the sign from 2025, the last ones by 2038 and 2039. With the orbs I use for timing, each meeting usually lasts about two and a half years.
- **Through aspects.** From Aries, Neptune squares planets in Cancer and Capricorn and opposes planets in Libra. People born between January 1984 and November 1998, with Neptune in Capricorn, meet their [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) in these years, at about forty. Those born between October 1942 and August 1957, with Neptune in Libra, see Neptune opposite their own Neptune, in their early eighties.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In the system I built for my readings, Neptune enables imagination and compassion and loosens boundaries that are no longer needed; Aries is a manner of beginning, direct and independent, the first movement. In the house Neptune crosses, I read a long stretch in which an ideal asks to be started and tried rather than kept as a picture, and where firm edges between what you hope for and what you do grow softer. The same system names one of Neptune's blind spots: taking imagination for evidence. So I read this transit with a question beside it: what is real here, and what is only hoped?

Neptune began the stay in company. Saturn met it at 0°45′ Aries on 20 February 2026 ([the Saturn–Neptune conjunction of 2026](/astrology/saturn-neptune-conjunction-of-2026/)), and the three outer planets all changed sign within eighteen months: Pluto settled in Aquarius in November 2024 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), Neptune in Aries in January 2026, Uranus in Gemini in April 2026 ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)). I read each through your houses and natal points, then together. None of them describes a generation's character, or forecasts an event ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Neptune in Aries will change everyone's life.”** It crosses one house of each chart, slowly, and meets what is there. That is the whole of its reach.
- **“It began in 2025.”** That was a first crossing. Neptune went back into Pisces and settled in Aries on 26 January 2026.
- **“Only people with Aries planets feel it.”** Aries sits somewhere in every chart, so everyone has a house it crosses.
- **“Babies born now will be impulsive dreamers.”** A sign shared by thirteen years of births isn't a personality, and I don't read children's charts.
