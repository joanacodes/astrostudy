Libra on the 4th house cusp places the sign of the scales at the IC, the deepest point of your chart. What steadies you underneath is a sense that things at home are in proportion: given and taken, decided together.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) opens the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) in Placidus, the house system I use, and I read it as foundation: what supports you, what you come back to, what you're rooted in ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Libra](/astrology/libra/) there, the foundation is an arrangement more than a place. Home works for you when the give and take is even: chores shared, rules made together, each person's corner respected, the decisions that matter talked over rather than handed down. You may rearrange a room until its proportions feel right, and settle more easily after an evening in company than in an empty flat. What you carry from the family you grew up in is often a feeling about fairness, about how disagreements were settled or left. That's your side of the story; the others have charts of their own.

Opposite, Aries is always on your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), so the vertical line of your chart joins [Venus](/astrology/venus/), below, to Mars, above ([Aries on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/)). I read that as a life that can push hard in public because home is the one place where nothing has to be won. The house Venus occupies shows what your private life leans on or flows into: the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Taurus, Venus's other sign, opens the 11th in about 2 London charts in 3 with this cusp, so the life you share with the people you've chosen and the home you return to answer to one planet. Otherwise, in about a third of these charts, Scorpio fits entirely within your 4th after Libra, and Taurus is left without a cusp ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Pluto and Mars then share your roots with Venus, and Mars, already ruling the Midheaven, has a hand at both ends of the meridian.

About 1 birth in 13 has Libra on the IC, and the figure is the same at London and New York: the sign at the bottom of a chart, like the one at the top, depends on the time of day, not on how far north you were born. Libra takes a little under two hours to pass the meridian, a few minutes less than the average sign, because the signs on either side of the equinoxes cross it at a slant. Latitude shapes the rest of the chart. At London, Leo rises in about 5 of 6 charts with this cusp, making the Sun your chart ruler, and the 4th stretches to nearly 40°, wider than any sign; at New York, Leo and Cancer rise about equally often, and the house is about 36° to 37° wide. Libra opens no house at all in about 1 London birth in 6 and 1 New York birth in 8, but when it holds the IC it never opens the 3rd or the 5th as well.

The IC turns with the clock like every angle. An hour or two earlier, Virgo could have been there and Libra lower, on the 5th ([Libra on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-5th-house-cusp/)); an hour or two later, Scorpio, with Libra lifted to the 3rd ([Libra on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-3rd-house-cusp/)). With an angle at stake I need the time itself, not a guess at it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
