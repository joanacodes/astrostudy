Jupiter and Neptune share a sign: Pisces belonged to Jupiter in the tradition and goes to Neptune in modern tropical astrology, and I read both rulers ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). When Neptune reaches your natal Jupiter, once in a life at most, the two sit on one degree for about two and a half years, and whatever you believe in tends to grow wider and softer at once.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) builds meaning: it widens what you understand, gathers experience into beliefs and principles, and looks for room to grow. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves edges and reaches for what lies beyond them. When two planets are in [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) they work as one process, and here both of them expand, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) there is little to hold the widening back.

It often feels like a season of faith in the broad sense: a spiritual practice, a vision of how life could be, a cause or an art that seems to explain more than it should. Generosity comes easily, and so does trust. The shadow is the same widening seen from the other side. Neptune's blind spot is mistaking imagination for evidence, Jupiter's is mistaking a belief for a certainty, and together they can make a teaching, a plan or a promise look larger than it is. Expansion isn't excess, though, and the conjunction asks for meaning that grows without letting go of the facts.

I don't read it for luck, money or success, none of which belong to Jupiter as I read it ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

With Jupiter in your 9th, the house of belief, study and long journeys, Neptune crosses it during its years there ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)), and the search for meaning takes centre stage. Your birth time decides which house holds your Jupiter ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Jupiter conjunct Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-neptune/) in a birth chart belongs to everyone born in the few months, about every thirteen years, when the two planets meet in the sky; the transit is that meeting arriving later, in your own chart.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

While Jupiter in the sky crosses two or three signs, Neptune stays within my orbs of your Jupiter, 1½° before exact to 1° after: about two years and seven months, rarely less than twenty-two. Neptune touches your Jupiter exactly three times in most passes, and about one pass in four brings five.

Only once, if at all: a little over half of the people born between 1930 and 2009 reach 80 without it. Jupiter and Neptune meet in the sky about every thirteen years, most recently in 1984, 1997, 2009 and 2022. Born soon after one of those meetings, with Jupiter just ahead of Neptune, you meet the transit young: at about 8 for someone born at the start of 1985. Born in the years before one, with Jupiter behind, you don't meet it before 80, as for births at the start of 1945, 1955, 1995 or 2005. In between, the ages spread out: about 29 for the start of 1935, about 42 for 1975, nearly 80 for 1965.

For that 1985 birth the rest of the sequence follows: [transiting Neptune sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-jupiter/) at about 35, [transiting Neptune square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-jupiter/) at about 49 and [transiting Neptune trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-jupiter/) at about 62. [Transiting Neptune opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-jupiter/) would come some eighty years after the conjunction, out of reach.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let your sense of meaning grow, and keep a foot on the ground. Study, travel, pray, make art, and keep one practical commitment exactly as it was.

Check a promise before you trust it. Under this conjunction a teaching, a venture or a person can look like the answer; give it time to show its workings.

Give generously, within a limit you set in advance. Jupiter and Neptune together find it hard to say enough.
