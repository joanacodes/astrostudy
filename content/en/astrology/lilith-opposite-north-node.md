Lilith opposite North Node puts the untamed point of the Moon's orbit on the South Node, the familiar end of the nodal axis. Refusing, standing apart and keeping your own counsel are things you learned early and do well; the growth across the chart asks for something they can't give.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's mean apogee, is read in many traditions as instinctive autonomy, boundaries and what was pushed aside, though readings differ and I keep it symbolic. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) holds consolidated, readily available capacities; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), exactly opposite, a complementary one you develop by practice. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, and with Lilith on the South Node, your refusals belong to the familiar end.

In plain words, saying no is old, practised ground, and a strength. The North Node asks you to practise something your refusal can't do, perhaps letting someone in, perhaps staying when you'd usually leave; the sign of the North Node says which. The pull, under pressure, is to reach for the familiar no even when the moment asks for the other answer.

Nothing here asks you to give up your autonomy. The opposition asks for balance: keep the no you trust, and build, beside it, a yes on your own terms.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Lilith usually shares a sign with the South Node, and the North Node's sign, opposite, names the less familiar capacity. In the sky, this is the Moon's farthest point from Earth lying where its path crosses the Sun's heading south. Lilith reaches the South Node once in each six-year cycle of the pair, for a couple of months of births, which makes this the least common of the pair's five aspects.

With a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), the houses name the two ends. Lilith on the South Node in the 11th, for example, could make not belonging to the crowd familiar ground, with the North Node in the 5th asking for joy and creations of your own.

About 1 person in 31 is born with Lilith within 6° of an opposition to the North Node, counted with the mean Lilith and the true node. Both points move slowly by the hour, so the birth time hardly changes the aspect itself; the choice of Lilith does ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

The rest of the pair's family, for contrast:

- **[Lilith conjunct North Node](/astrology/lilith-conjunct-north-node/):** the reverse, with the untamed part as the new direction rather than the old ground.
- **[Lilith sextile North Node](/astrology/lilith-sextile-north-node/):** the instinct easy with the familiar and offering openings to growth.
- **[Lilith square North Node](/astrology/lilith-square-north-node/):** the instinct across the path, at home at neither end.
- **[Lilith trine North Node](/astrology/lilith-trine-north-node/):** the instinct flowing straight into growth.

In [Lilith opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-opposite-north-node-synastry/), one person's untamed point meets the other's South Node, the ground they know best: a strangely familiar wildness that can feel like home, or like an old pattern returning. I go slowly with it, and only with both people's agreement ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), born with the opposition, Lilith comes back to your South Node about every nine years, and some four and a half years after each return it crosses your North Node instead, for a few weeks: a short season when the untamed part visits the growth point.

From the node's side, each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) brings the transiting South Node back over your Lilith, and each [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/) the North Node, with that season's eclipses close by.

Weather, not a forecast: these seasons ask how your no and your yes can share a life. The balance is yours to find.
