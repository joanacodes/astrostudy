A plan that has lived only in your head gets offered the structure that would let it exist when Saturn sextiles your Jupiter, two times in every 29½-year round. It's a practical window, and an easy one to miss.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) synthesises meaning and widens what seems possible: learning, principles, the projects you believe in. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) organises: method, discipline, the long term. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through opportunity and cooperation, and opportunity requires engagement: nothing grows under this transit without your step.

It tends to bring structure within reach of growth. A training that leads somewhere, a framework a mentor offers, a schedule that makes a long study possible, a belief turned into a practice. Potential isn't guaranteed realisation, and Jupiter isn't luck: what the sextile offers is the chance to make a hope workable, usually through ordinary means such as forms, timetables and a first meeting. Of Saturn's contacts with your Jupiter, it's the least dramatic and perhaps the most useful, because using it costs so little.

With your Jupiter in the 7th, Saturn makes the sextile from your 5th or your 9th ([Saturn transiting the 9th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-9th-house/)), so a partnership can grow through a shared course, a long-range plan or a principle the two of you put in writing. Your Descendant, where the 7th begins, moves with the minute of birth; without your birth time, the sextile goes unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Jupiter sextile Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-saturn/), you're probably already the person who turns big ideas into workable plans; this transit asks which idea deserves the next plan.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The window stays open for about nine months as a rule, the length of an academic year, which suits the courses and trainings this sextile favours; that's with my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after. The exact sextile comes three times over about nine and a half months whenever Saturn's yearly loop falls on the point, as in a little over half of its [transits](/astrology/transits/); with no loop nearby, three weeks can be the whole of it.

When it first comes depends on where Jupiter stood in relation to Saturn at your birth, and so on your birth year. The first sextile came in the first two years of life for anyone born on 1 January 1945, 1965, 1985 or 2005, a few years after a Jupiter–Saturn conjunction, and between about 14 and 16 for 1 January 1935, 1955, 1975 or 1995. The full spread, for births from 1930 to 2009, runs from the first weeks of life to about 20, with about 7 in the middle. Two a round then make five or six chances by 80 to give a plan its framework.

On the way out from [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-jupiter/), the sextile comes about five years after the conjunction and two or three before [transiting Saturn square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-jupiter/); [transiting Saturn trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-jupiter/) follow, and the second sextile comes on the way home.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Enrol, apply, write it down. The sextile rewards the form filled in and the first class attended.

Find someone who has done it. A mentor's framework is often the very structure this transit offers.

Make the plan smaller than the dream and bigger than a wish: something you could start this month.
