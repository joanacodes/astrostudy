Some people seem to give you permission just by standing next to you: to laugh louder, take up room, stop apologising for the way you walk in. Their Lilith in your 1st house sets the part of them that refused to be tamed right where you meet the world.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is a point rather than a body, the far end of the Moon's orbit, and I read it as the part of a person that was pushed aside early and keeps claiming its place. [The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is your manner, your body, the way you start things and the impression you make. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their unruly part meets your presence.

With them, you may stop toning yourself down. The volume you lowered years ago, the opinion you used to soften, the haircut or the coat you never dared can come back without fuss, because they don't seem to need you smaller. Some people find their first reactions grow more honest in this company, and quicker.

For them, your presence can be a provocation or a release. Your ease in your own skin may stir what they had to hide in theirs; your caution may make them want to shake you loose. As an angular house, the 1st puts whatever enters it in plain sight, and [Aries](/astrology/aries/), its natural sign, starts things without asking. If their Lilith sits right on your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), the meeting is louder: their refusal then touches the very degree you rise from, and I treat it as a conjunction too.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Permission can turn into pressure. If they want you bolder than you choose to be, "just be yourself" starts to sound like an order, and you may feel pushed to perform a wildness that isn't yours. Their frankness about your looks or manner can also sting, however well meant.

From their side, standing next to you can expose what they'd rather keep covered. If you hold back, they may read it as disapproval of them; if you don't, they may feel outshone, or tempted to compete.

Nothing here is a verdict on either of you. Lilith describes what was set aside and wants back in; it simply surfaces between you in your 1st house.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let each person's "too much" be optional: no dares about how to look or speak, only room. Say when their encouragement helped, and when it went one step too far. In a couple, enjoy the version of yourselves that turns up together without asking either of you to keep it on all the time; between friends, swap one small act of boldness each; between colleagues, a frank first impression can be useful if it stays about the work; between adult relatives, let old labels like "the loud one" retire.

Turned round, this is your Lilith in their 1st house, and your refusal to be tamed is what meets their presence. Nearby, [their Lilith in your 12th house](/astrology/lilith-in-12th-house-synastry/) works on what you hide even from yourself, and [their Lilith in your 2nd house](/astrology/lilith-in-2nd-house-synastry/) on what you allow yourself to want.

I read mean Lilith, in both charts; a true Lilith can sit tens of degrees away and land in another house of yours altogether, so check what each chart shows ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). Mean Lilith spends about nine months in a sign, so their birth date places it; your birth time places your Ascendant, and in Placidus, the house system I use, a few minutes decide whether their Lilith opens your 1st or closes your 12th. I begin only with both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and only once both people have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
