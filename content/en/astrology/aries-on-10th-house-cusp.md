With Aries on the 10th house cusp, the top of your chart is in the first sign. The 10th begins at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the point where the zodiac crossed the meridian when you were born, and it's where your public life, your work and your reputation take their direction.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the Midheaven is the style of the role you grow into in the world ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Aries](/astrology/aries/) makes it a role you'd rather define than inherit: work where a first attempt is allowed and owned, responsibility taken early, a reputation for opening things that others then carry on. You may be the first in your family to do the kind of work you do, or the person a team sends in when something has to begin. Titles matter less to you than what you've actually done, and a post where every step needs permission tends to wear you out faster than hard work does. Like every sign on the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), it describes how you want to stand in your work, whatever the work turns out to be.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules your 10th. Tradition gave Mars its exaltation in Capricorn, the 10th's natural sign, so the planet of effort had an old bond with this house even before Aries put it in charge of yours. Below, on the IC, sits Libra: a private life you want peaceable and shared, which is often what makes the public push possible. Scorpio usually opens your 5th in these charts, so Mars also shares that house with Pluto, and what you do for pleasure can turn into what you're known for; I read both of Scorpio's rulers. In about a third of London charts with this Midheaven, Scorpio lies wholly inside the 4th instead, and Mars then rules no other house by its cusp. To see what your public direction grows from, follow Mars to its house: the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

An Aries Midheaven turns up in about 1 birth in 13, at London as at New York. In Placidus, the house system I use, the Midheaven is the 10th cusp, and its sign is set by the hour rather than by how far north you were born; Aries spends a little under two hours a day on the meridian, slightly less than the average sign. The Midheaven also divides the cusps where Aries is common from those where it's rare: west of it, Aries opens the 9th for about 1 London birth in 11 ([Aries on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-9th-house-cusp/)); east of it, the 11th for 1 in 16 ([Aries on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-11th-house-cusp/)). With this cusp Aries never opens a second house, though in other charts it opens none at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), about 1 London birth in 6. Leo or Cancer rises with it: at London Leo far more often, at New York about evenly.

The Midheaven moves about a degree every four minutes, so I need the birth time before I can say your 10th opens in Aries at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Once it's known, it shows a direction to test against your life, never a promise of success or a job title.
