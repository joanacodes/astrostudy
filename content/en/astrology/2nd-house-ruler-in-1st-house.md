Your first resource is yourself. With the ruler of the 2nd in the 1st house, what you have and what you're worth keep coming back to you: to your effort, your presence and the skills you carry.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds what you can count on: money and belongings, the skills you could live from, and your sense of your own worth. Its ruler, the planet that rules the sign on its cusp, shows where those resources come from and where they go ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and here it has stepped back into the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), the house of the self, the body and the way you begin things.

I'd put it simply: you earn as yourself. Your income, your security and your sense of worth tend to depend on your own effort and presence more than on an employer, an inheritance or a partner. Working for yourself often suits it, and so does any work where who you are is part of what people pay for: the maker whose name is on the piece, the adviser hired for their judgement, the performer. Worth and identity sit close together, which is a strength when you know what you're worth and a strain when a bad month feels like a verdict on you. Appearance isn't superficiality here, either: what you spend on looking after yourself is part of how you hold your value.

With Gemini or Virgo on the 2nd cusp, Mercury rules your money from the 1st, the house tradition gave it as its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): you trade on your wits and your words, and you think about value out loud. And if one sign stretches across both cusps, which Placidus allows, the same planet rules the 1st and the 2nd ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); placed in the 1st, it ties self and resources into a single knot.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 1st is the stretch of sky just below the eastern horizon, so the ruler of your 2nd was about to rise when you were born. A little later, it would already have risen into the 12th, and your money would lean towards quiet work and what's done out of sight ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 12th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)); further from the Ascendant, it would stand in the 2nd itself, keeping its own house ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)). The Ascendant and the 2nd cusp both come from the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and a ruler this close to the horizon is the first thing I check.

With Aquarius on the 2nd cusp, Saturn and Uranus both rule your money, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 1st makes you careful and self-reliant about it, while Uranus, wherever it stands, keeps a streak of independence in the way you earn. With Scorpio there, Mars in the 1st goes out and gets what it needs, and Pluto adds a wish to own your resources outright.

None of this says how much you'll have. The 2nd house describes how you earn and what you value, and no chart can promise an income or tell you what to invest in ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). With its ruler in the 1st, what it does say is that your worth is something you carry, not something you're handed.
