Messages, lessons, errands, the people next door: the 3rd house is the mind at work on an ordinary day. With Virgo on the 3rd house cusp, that mind checks the facts before it speaks.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) gives the [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) an editor's eye ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). I read it as precise everyday thinking: you learn step by step, take notes that other people borrow, and notice the error in a message before anyone else does. Explanations come out clear because you've sorted them first, and you'd rather say less and be right than say more and be vague. With brothers and sisters, cousins and neighbours, care often shows as practical help: the form filled in, the letter explained, the lift to the station on time. Short journeys get planned, the timetable checked twice. The difficulty is a critical voice that can run ahead of tact, so that a correction lands as a reproach, or an inner editor that stops you sending anything until it's perfect. Opposite, Pisces opens the 9th, and on the big questions the pattern often reverses: careful with small facts, you tend to trust a feeling about the large ones.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), the 3rd house's natural ruler, rules it here in fact, through the sign where it sorts rather than gathers ([Mercury in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-in-virgo/)). Its house shows what your everyday thinking is for: as ruler of the 3rd, in the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Gemini, Mercury's other sign, falls three ways in these charts at London, about equally often: on the 11th cusp, on the 12th, or inside a house with no cusp of its own. So Mercury may rule your 3rd alone, or tie it to the house of your circles, or to the house of retreat. With [Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/) in about 3 London charts in 4, the Sun rules the chart, and the voice you lead with is warmer than the careful one you think in.

In Placidus, which I use, a day's births at London put Virgo on the 3rd cusp about 1 time in 11, and at New York about 1 time in 12. Virgo rises slowly in the north and passes the cusps below the eastern horizon slowly too, a little over two hours a day on this one at London; the further a cusp lies from the Ascendant, the less that slowness counts. The 3rd is about a sign wide in these charts, 27° to 32° at London, and Virgo opens a neighbouring cusp as well only now and then: the 2nd in about 1 London chart in 4, the 4th in about 1 in 11 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). In roughly 1 London birth in 6, Virgo has no cusp at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

The recorded minute matters more than the hour here. Some 130 minutes a day is a short stay, and the charts on either side, [Virgo on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-2nd-house-cusp/) for a later birth and [Virgo on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/) for an earlier one, read differently, so the time on record, not a guess, decides which of the three I read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
