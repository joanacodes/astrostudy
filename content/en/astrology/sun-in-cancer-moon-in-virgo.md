Looking after people is your Sun's purpose, and doing it properly is your Moon's comfort. With the Sun in Cancer and the Moon in Virgo, care comes with a checklist: the soup made, the form filled in, the detail nobody else noticed. You were born under a young crescent, a few evenings into the lunar month.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and mutable earth, two signs apart with the Moon ahead: a sextile by sign, and earth and water suit each other. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) makes its purpose out of protecting and providing for its people; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) calms down when things are in order and being useful. The two agree on what matters, which is taking care of others, and differ only on style: the Sun responds with feeling, the Moon with a plan.

Since the Moon rules Cancer, how your Moon fares in Virgo colours your sense of purpose as well, and Virgo in turn belongs to [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/). Mercury never strays more than about 28° from the Sun, so with a Cancer Sun it sits in Gemini, Cancer or Leo and never reaches Virgo: the planet that sorts this Moon's feelings always works from another part of the chart, and its sign and house tell me how. Neither light holds a dignity here.

The Moon had moved 31° to 89° ahead of the Sun, two and a half to about seven days into the lunar month, a crescent setting a little later each evening. Nearly all of these births are [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) births in the eight-phase count, the earliest few still [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) ones, and with the Moon around 60° ahead the sextile of signs becomes a waxing [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). I read the crescent as needs beginning to go their own way, and a Virgo Moon does that by finding a job to do. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the phase.

Roughly 1 person in 138 shares the pair. Two and a half days is the Moon's usual stay in Virgo, between Leo and Libra; if it changed sign on the day you were born, your page could be the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-leo/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-libra/), and only the birth time can say ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It gives the rising sign and the houses too, which is why I never read a chart without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love by looking after people in concrete ways. The Cancer Sun wants to keep a partner safe and close; the Virgo Moon expresses that by sorting, fixing, remembering and planning. Partners may feel extraordinarily well cared for, and occasionally managed. What's harder is receiving care in return, or leaving a small thing imperfect. Let someone look after you their way now and then, and tell them it helped.

## At work {#at-work}

Care work with a practical edge suits this pair: nursing and support roles, social work, running a household or an office so that everyone else can get on, organising the logistics that keep a team or a family going. The Sun wants the work to protect people; the Moon wants it to be done well. Chaotic, careless workplaces upset both. You may be the person who quietly holds the system together, and who deserves to be told so.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Care and worry are close cousins here. The Cancer Sun feels responsible for everyone; the Virgo Moon sees everything that could go wrong; together they can turn love into constant vigilance and a long list of things still to do. Criticism, of others or yourself, may be worry in disguise. What helps is naming the feeling under the fussing, *I'm frightened for you*, and putting limits on the list: decide what is truly yours to fix, and let the rest be good enough.
