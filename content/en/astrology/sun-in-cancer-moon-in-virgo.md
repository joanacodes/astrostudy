Looking after people is your Sun's purpose, and doing it properly is your Moon's comfort, so in this pair care comes with a checklist: the soup made, the form filled in, the detail nobody else noticed. A thin crescent hung in the evening sky around your birth.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and mutable earth, with Virgo two signs on from Cancer: a sextile by sign, and earth and water suit each other. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) makes its purpose out of protecting and providing for its people; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) calms down when things are in order and being useful. The two agree on what matters, which is taking care of others, and differ only on style: the Sun responds with feeling, the Moon with a plan.

Since the Moon rules Cancer, how your Moon fares in Virgo colours your sense of purpose as well, and Virgo in turn belongs to [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/). Mercury, on a short leash of about 28° from the Sun, has three signs open to it with this Sun, Gemini, Cancer and Leo, and Virgo is not one of them: the planet that sorts this Moon's feelings works from another corner of the chart, and its sign and house say how. Tradition grants neither light a dignity in these signs.

This pair needs the Moon to have travelled 31° to 89° past the Sun, which takes from two and a half days up to about a week. A few early births still count as [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) ones, and the eight-phase count puts all the rest in the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/); around 60° the sextile of signs becomes a waxing [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). The crescent is where I see needs beginning to find their own way, and this Moon's way is usually a task. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the phase.

Roughly 1 person in 138 was born with this Sun and this Moon. About two and a half days pass while the Moon crosses Virgo, between its time in Leo and its time in Libra; if it changed sign on your birthday, the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-leo/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-libra/) may be yours instead, which only the birth time settles ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It gives the rising sign and the houses too, which is why I never read a chart without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love by looking after people in concrete ways. The Cancer Sun wants to keep a partner safe and close; the Virgo Moon expresses that by sorting, fixing, remembering and planning. Partners may feel extraordinarily well cared for, and occasionally managed. What's harder is receiving care in return, or leaving a small thing imperfect. Now and then, accept being looked after in someone else's style, and tell them it helped.

## At work {#at-work}

Care work with a practical edge suits this pair: nursing and support roles, social work, running a household or an office so that everyone else can get on, organising the logistics that keep a team or a family going. The purpose is protection, and the comfort is a job done well. Chaotic, careless workplaces upset both. You're often the one quietly holding the system together, and you deserve to be told so.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Care and worry are close cousins here. The Cancer Sun carries everyone's welfare; the Virgo Moon sees everything that could go wrong; together they can turn love into constant vigilance and a long list of things still to do. Criticism, of others or yourself, may be worry in disguise. What helps is naming the feeling under the fussing, *I'm frightened for you*, and putting limits on the list: choose which things are yours to fix; the rest can be good enough.
