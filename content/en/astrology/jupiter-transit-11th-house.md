Tradition gave Jupiter its joy in the 11th house, the Good Spirit, and its year there is the one when friendship, belonging and shared hopes get room to grow. The 11th is other people in the plural: the circle, the group, the cause.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens, connects and gives things a future; the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is community, friendship, networks and the goals you pursue with others. The old books said Jupiter rejoices here ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), and I can see why: the planet that looks for meaning and the house of shared hopes want the same thing. Across Jupiter's year there, your circle can widen, a group can start to feel like yours, and the future you'd like to build gets clearer, often because you're talking about it with the right people.

Belonging isn't popularity, though, and the 11th isn't a contest for numbers. The growth here is in finding the people who share what you care about, and in letting them help you as you help them.

A natal Uranus or Saturn in the 11th, the modern and the traditional rulers of Aquarius, the house's sign in the natural order (I read both), turns Jupiter's pass over it into a question about belonging on your own terms.

Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), this one brings out what your chart already holds about friendship and hope. It doesn't decide who comes into your life or who leaves it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn for a London birth, Jupiter's stays in the 11th cluster around a little over a year, most between about 11 and 22 months; for New York they're a little tighter, with the long end at about 20 months, just as in the 10th next door.

The cusp of your 11th, like every cusp, comes from your birth time and birthplace, and it's also why there's no reading without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows Jupiter's public year in your 10th ([Jupiter transiting the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-10th-house/)) and leads into the quiet of your 12th ([Jupiter transiting the 12th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-12th-house/)).

Think of the next twelve years in three blocks of four signs. The first runs from June 2026 to October 2030: Leo, then Virgo from July 2027, Libra from August 2028 and Scorpio from September 2029. The second runs from October 2030 to April 2034: Sagittarius, Capricorn from November 2031, Aquarius from April 2032, and Pisces from April 2033. Aquarius is the 11th's natural sign, and Jupiter takes its time with it, slipping back into Capricorn for a while in 2032 and keeping Aquarius until December 2033. The last block runs from April 2034 to June 2038: Aries, Taurus from April 2035, Gemini from May 2036 and Cancer from May 2037. Your cusp's sign tells you the block, its degree the month.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Join something that shares your hopes rather than something that only fills your diary. Jupiter in the 11th widens circles, and the ones worth widening are the ones you'd still choose in a quiet year.

Help without being asked, and let help come to you the same way. Tradition called the people of this house benefactors, and most of them are friends who noticed.

Write down the future you're working towards, and the names of the people you'd like to build it with. Then tell at least one of them.
