Kindness that arrives before anyone asks for it is the mark of Sun trine Neptune between two charts. The Neptune of one person sits about 120° from the other's Sun, usually in the same element, and imagination and identity simply get along.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises who a person is and what they're aiming at. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings imagination, compassion, inspiration and a feel for meaning beyond the practical. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates the two through facilitation: each makes the other easier. It's one of the softest [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) Neptune can make.

For the Sun person, there's the feeling of being gently understood. Your best self is assumed, your ambitions are met with imagination rather than doubt, and you can be soft in front of this person without losing face.

For the Neptune person, there's someone to believe in who doesn't ask you to dissolve. Your compassion is welcome and comes back to you; your imagination finds a direction it can follow.

Between them, meaning gets attached to ordinary things: a shared walk, a meal, a piece of work done for someone else. Colleagues feel that the project matters, relatives tell the old family stories more kindly to each other, and friends find themselves talking about what they believe.

The element sets the tone: in fire, shared enthusiasm for a cause; in earth, kindness that turns up with soup; in air, long conversations about what matters; in water, an empathy that needs few words.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Ease of this kind rarely goes wrong loudly. Its risk is quieter: the picture each holds of the other never gets checked, and the hard conversations, about money, time or what each actually needs, keep being postponed because the mood is so good. The Neptune person's generosity may go unreturned without either noticing. Idealising isn't a delusion, but an ideal nobody ever compares with the person can drift a long way from them.

The pair's other angles, for contrast:

- **[Sun conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** the imagination lands on the identity itself, and the two can merge.
- **[Sun sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** the same inspiration has to be invited in.
- **[Sun square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-neptune-synastry/):** the ideal and the person rub until both speak plainly.
- **[Sun opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** each finds in the other the end they lack.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use the inspiration for something: a creative project, a cause, a tradition with meaning. And have the practical conversations anyway, on purpose, perhaps on a set day. A couple might take on something creative or voluntary together; old friends might say aloud what the friendship has given them; a relative who has always believed in you can be told what you actually need; colleagues on a project with a mission can add practical checkpoints.

About 1 pair of people in 7 has this trine, one way round or the other, within 6.8°. Neptune's sign marks an age group of about fourteen years; the trine is made by the Sun, and so by a birthday, which is why it belongs to this pair. Both birth times come before any reading: the trine seldom depends on the hour, at a degree or so of Sun a day, but the houses show where the kindness is spent, at home, at work or in the wider world ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one chart, [Sun trine Neptune](/astrology/sun-trine-neptune/) lets a person's identity and imagination cooperate without strain. Between two charts, the cooperation runs across two lives, and it takes both to keep it honest.
