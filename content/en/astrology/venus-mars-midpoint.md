[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs what you value and are drawn to; [Mars](/astrology/mars/) gets up and goes after something. Halfway between the two planets in your chart lies a degree that astrologers who use midpoints read as the place where wanting turns into doing.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

In the system I read with, Venus is the function of valuing: attraction, preference, the give and take of what each person brings. Mars is the function of acting: initiative, effort, the will to begin. The Venus/Mars midpoint stands for the two working as one, desire with a direction. That covers love and attraction, and it equally covers the project you can't stop working on, the art you make or the friendship you keep choosing.

The pair behaves differently from the Sun and Moon, whose midpoint follows the lunar phase ([Sun/Moon midpoint](/astrology/sun-moon-midpoint/)). Venus always stays within about 47° of the Sun, but Mars can be anywhere, so Venus and Mars can stand at any distance from each other. When they are together, the midpoint sits on both; with Venus opposite Mars, the midpoints sit at right angles to the pair, one on each side, square to both planets. Like every midpoint, it comes as a pair of opposite degrees, read as one axis ([midpoints](/astrology/midpoints/)).

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Venus moves at most about a degree and a quarter a day, Mars well under a degree, so the point between them crawls: about a degree a day at the most, and less, sometimes hardly at all, when one of them is retrograde. Births a few days apart have their Venus/Mars midpoints a few degrees apart, sometimes less.

What makes it personal is what touches it. Midpoint astrologers look for a planet or angle sitting on that point, or squaring or opposing it, and read it as a third function joining valuing and acting: the Moon brings feeling and need into it, Saturn structure and patience, Jupiter scope, Mercury the words. The Moon, the Ascendant and the Midheaven move fast, so whether they touch it depends on the birth time, and so does the midpoint's house.

Programs show these contacts as [midpoint trees](/astrology/midpoint-trees/): for each planet or point, a list of the midpoints within orb of it. If your Moon sits on your Venus/Mars midpoint, Venus/Mars appears in the Moon's tree.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

This degree never appears on its own in my readings, since I don't work with midpoints ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). I read Venus and Mars themselves: their signs, their houses, and any aspect between them, such as [Venus conjunct Mars](/astrology/venus-conjunct-mars/). Between two people, I read one person's Venus with the other's Mars as a contact in its own right ([Venus conjunct Mars in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-mars-synastry/)), not through a midpoint.

My notebook adds a note of caution. Venus was among the placements that held about a third of the time, like the Sun sign and the rising sign; Mars was checked like every placement and wasn't one of the three that held far more often ([my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)). A degree built from the two inherits their uncertainty, so I'd hold anything read from it lightly.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The Venus/Mars midpoint shows who you'll be attracted to.”** It describes how attraction and initiative combine in you. It names no one, and no sign.
- **“It's only about sex.”** Valuing and acting run through everything you care about, from work to friendship.
- **“A planet on it means passion is coming.”** A natal combination describes how functions link in you; it doesn't forecast an event.
- **“It's the same for everyone born that week.”** The degree may be, but what touches it and the house it falls in depend on the birth time.
