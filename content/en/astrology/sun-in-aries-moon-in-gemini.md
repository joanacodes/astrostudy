An Aries Sun acts; a Gemini Moon needs to put things into words. With this pair the two often happen at once, and you narrate what you're doing while you're doing it. You were born under a young crescent, a few evenings after a new moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fire and air are the elements that feed each other, and two signs apart they form a sextile by sign: cooperation that works when you use it. Aries is cardinal and opens things; Gemini is mutable and links them up. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) supplies a direction, and the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) works out how it feels about that direction by talking it through. That tends to make you good at explaining, on the spot, why you're doing what you're doing.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules the Sun's sign and [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) the Moon's. Mercury never strays more than about 28° from the Sun, so with an Aries Sun it sits in Pisces, Aries or Taurus, and never in Gemini itself: the planet that gives this Moon its words always travels in your Sun's company. Perhaps that's why naming a feeling and deciding what to do about it so often turn out, for you, to be one step. Only the Sun has a dignity here, its exaltation; Gemini gives the Moon none.

The Moon had moved 31° to 89° ahead of the Sun, two and a half to about seven days after a new moon: the thin evening crescent of the sky. Seven of these births in eight fall in the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) of the eight-phase count and the eighth in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase; near 60°, the two lights make a [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows which side of the 45° line a birth falls on.

Roughly 1 person in 143 shares the pair. The Moon spends only some 2½ days in Gemini; for a birth on the day it came in from Taurus or moved on to Cancer, the time decides whether this page is the right one, or the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-taurus/), or the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-cancer/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same time gives the rising sign, and only with it do the two lights become the outline of a chart rather than a pair of facts.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You want a partner you can talk to at full speed. Conversation is closeness for a Gemini Moon, and an Aries Sun likes it direct, so the exchange that feels most loving is quick, frank and a little argumentative, with nobody sulking afterwards. Your care often arrives as a message, a plan, the joke that breaks the tension. What can go missing is the slower feeling under the words: you can describe what happened, decide what to do next and still not have said that it hurt. A partner who asks one more question, and leaves a silence after it, helps you find it.

## At work {#at-work}

Few pairs pitch an idea faster. You think on your feet, explain as you go and like several things moving at once, so a day full of exchanges can leave you energised rather than drained. The risk is breadth without follow-through, since neither sign loves long sameness. Fast cycles tend to suit you: short projects, live problems, people to brief. For anything longer, a written plan you update as you go keeps the thread when your attention has already moved on.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Speed on both sides. The Sun acts before it reflects; the Moon talks before it has felt. Together they can keep you a step ahead of your own emotions, busy, articulate and slightly out of breath, until the feelings catch up with you on a quiet Sunday. What helps is a slower channel, kept on purpose: writing by hand, a walk without headphones, ten minutes of doing nothing before the next message goes out. The feelings this Moon can't quite say out loud tend to surface there first.
