The passports are always in date, and the argument about politics is never quite finished. Composite Mars in Sagittarius gives a relationship a drive that runs on conviction and distance: it acts for what it believes, goes after the horizon together, and argues about principles.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Each planet in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is the midpoint of two natal positions, and the composite [Mars](/astrology/mars/) is how the bond goes after what it wants. [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) is mutable fire ruled by Jupiter, a sign that gives Mars no dignity and plenty of room: the drive turns exploratory, generous and hungry for meaning. About 1 pair in 13 shares this composite Mars.

Such a pair acts by setting out. A long walk becomes a pilgrim route, a night class becomes a degree, a holiday becomes a plan to spend a year abroad, and a cause you both believe in gets both your weekends. Competition is about who's right or who goes further: the faster time up the hill, the better-read argument. Desire is spontaneous and good-humoured, and it needs air; a bond like this stays lively while each of you has room to roam. [Mars in Sagittarius](/astrology/mars-in-sagittarius/) in a birth chart sends one person after a horizon; in the composite the horizon is shared, and so is the restlessness.

Arguments tend to be about beliefs. A disagreement over the washing-up can become, within minutes, a debate about fairness in general, and two people defending principles rarely notice they're talking past the actual request. Rows are frank and can be loud, and the laughter usually comes back quickly. The risk is the crusade, where being right matters more than being close, or the pair promises more than it can carry. Vision isn't dogmatism and enthusiasm isn't recklessness, but both need checking against the calendar.

A loud debate is friction. If raised voices ever turn into fear, the chart is the wrong place to look: in the UK, call 999 in an emergency; in the US and Canada, 988 is free to call or text at any hour. And because a composite looks at two lives at once, it should be drawn only when both people agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Composites are not part of my practice; this page describes the method. Within it, astrologers would read this Mars with the composite [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), Sagittarius's ruler, for how far the pair expands, and with the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), where [Mars conjunct Jupiter in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-jupiter-synastry/) shows one person's enthusiasm feeding the other's drive. None of it forecasts a move abroad, a wedding or a parting.

Between [composite Mars in Scorpio](/astrology/composite-mars-in-scorpio/), which digs in one place, and [composite Mars in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-mars-in-capricorn/), which builds step by step, Sagittarius keeps the horizon in view. The best thing it can do for itself is bring the horizon home now and then: a principle is easier to live with once it has been turned into a plain request.
