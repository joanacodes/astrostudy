Jupiter square Neptune sets what you believe at right angles to what you dream. The two keep asking things of each other, which can be tiring and is also how an idealist becomes a wise one. The sky draws this square every six or seven years, and each time a short run of births inherits it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) gives experience a frame: beliefs, principles, a sense of where things are heading and why. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) loosens frames: it imagines, empathises, idealises and senses what lies beyond the obvious. A [square](/astrology/square/) puts them in constructive friction: neither can settle while the other keeps pressing, and each has to grow.

In plain words, your vision tends to outrun the map. You may give more than is wise, believe in a person or a plan before the evidence is in, or feel let down when the real thing is smaller than the picture. The other face of it is just as real: a refusal to settle for a narrow, merely practical life, and a compassion that keeps widening your sense of who counts.

An ideal isn't a delusion, and a rub isn't a defeat. Over time this square tends to teach discernment: kindness that also protects you, belief that can survive a hard question, and dreams that get tested rather than dropped.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Within 6°, it turns up in about one birth in 16 from 1930 to 2009, in spells about six or seven years apart: January to March 1930; December 1935 to February 1936 and June to November 1936; May to June 1942; December 1948 to February 1949; August 1954 to June 1955; April to July 1961 and December 1961 to February 1962; August to October 1967 and February to June 1968; March to May 1974 and September 1974 to January 1975; August to October 1980; March to April 1987; December 1992 to February 1993 and August to October 1993; June 1999 to April 2000; and December 2005 to May 2006 with August to October 2006.

Each spell's births share it, which is why I look to the houses for what is yours ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). Jupiter in the 1st square Neptune in the 4th, for instance, might set a confident, outward faith against a family story that is hard to pin down. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Weighed against the square:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-neptune/):** the meeting itself, for the births of 1945, 1970–71 or 2009, among others.
- **[Jupiter sextile Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-neptune/):** an opening instead of a rub, for those born in 1947–48 or 1973, for example.
- **[Jupiter trine Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-trine-neptune/):** belief and imagination in easy agreement.
- **[Jupiter opposite Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-neptune/):** the two facing each other, often through other people.

[Jupiter square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-neptune-synastry/) sets one person's principles across the other's dreams: inspiring and confusing by turns, and steadier when both say plainly what they hope for.

## In transits {#in-transits}

For this cohort, [transiting Neptune square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-jupiter/) is simply the aspect you were born with, fading over your first years, since Neptune barely moves; it comes back only after 80, when Neptune opposes its own place. The [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/), around 41, matters more: transiting Neptune then reaches either your Jupiter itself or the point opposite it, depending on which side of Neptune your Jupiter stood. [Transiting Jupiter square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-neptune/) is the brief version, a matter of days or weeks.

Weather, not a forecast: seasons for sorting a dream worth keeping from one worth letting go, at your own pace.
