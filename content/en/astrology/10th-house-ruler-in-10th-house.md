Here the 10th house rules itself. Its ruler sits inside it, near the top of the wheel, and the direction you take in the world is one you tend to set and steer on your own.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) names the ruler of the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), which in Placidus, the house system I use, begins at that point ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Usually that planet carries the 10th's business somewhere else in the chart. Here it stays put: vocation, standing, reputation and your part in public life answer to a ruler living among them. The old horary lists filled the 10th with kings, judges and governments, so the house’s ancient business, authority, is close at hand: you may hold it, or spend a working life negotiating with it.

Direction is the gift here. People with this placement tend to know early what they want to be known for, or to find out by doing it, and they prefer to climb their own hill rather than someone else's ladder. A ruler here is close to the Midheaven, among the most visible places in a chart, so what it describes is often what people see first ([angular planets](/astrology/angular-planets/)). Changes of direction tend to come from inside rather than from circumstance: when you change course, it’s usually because you decided to. What it can crowd out is the rest of a life. When the public self has its own ruler at home, the private self can go short of attention, and a career that answers to nothing else can forget to ask what it's for.

Lying in the degrees just after the Midheaven’s, the ruler is often in the Midheaven’s own sign as well, and so in a sign it rules. Mercury, ruling a Virgo Midheaven from the 10th, may be in Virgo, the only sign where a planet is both at home and exalted ([domicile](/astrology/domicile/)): a public life built on skill, precision and usefulness. But Placidus houses don't follow the signs exactly, and Mars, ruling an Aries Midheaven, can sit later in the 10th in Taurus, its detriment: ambition pursued slowly and stubbornly, in a manner Mars has to learn.

The Midheaven turns through the whole zodiac in a day, so a ruler near it can slip across after a small correction to the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Over the top, it would sit in the 9th, where the work leans on ideas, study and other countries ([Ruler of the 10th in the 9th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)). Further from the top, earlier in its climb, it would be in the 11th, among the networks and causes your work is built with ([Ruler of the 10th in the 11th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)).

With Pisces on the Midheaven, Jupiter and Neptune are the two rulers to look for ([Pisces on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Jupiter in the 10th, perhaps in Pisces, one of its own signs, tends to widen whatever field you enter, the teacher or leader people grow around ([Jupiter in the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-10th-house/)). Neptune in the 10th makes the public image harder to pin down: a vocation that feels like a calling, and asks for care with how others see you.
