Pleasure can start to feel like something you have to earn when Saturn squares your Venus. The contact comes twice a round, about seven years after Saturn crosses your Venus and seven before it returns, and for a while what you enjoy and what you're responsible for claim the same hours.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) values: it decides what you're drawn to, what feels beautiful and whether an exchange is fair. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) asks what lasts and what it costs. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates them through constructive friction: neither function wins, and each forces the other to adapt.

It often shows as pressure on whatever makes life pleasant. Time with people you love gets squeezed by obligations; a relationship has to talk about who does what; a pleasure starts to look like an indulgence you can't afford, in hours if not in money. Saturn's familiar trap is mistaking responsibility for self-worth, and on Venus it can whisper that you're only lovable when useful. The square is there to make you hear that whisper, not to obey it.

The first square of a round tests what began at the conjunction: the bond, the taste, the way of spending your time that started then. The second, on the way back, sorts what you'll carry into the next round. In neither case is friction failure. No transit ends a relationship, and I don't read this one for break-ups; if the pressure becomes a low you can't lift, the right door is a therapist's, not a chart's ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

With your Venus in the 7th, the friction sits in partnership. Saturn is then usually crossing your 4th or your 10th, so it's the demands of home ([Saturn transiting the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-4th-house/)) or of work that press on the bond. Placing Venus in the 7th, and Saturn in the 4th or 10th, takes your birth time; without it I don't read the square ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you were born with [Venus square Saturn](/astrology/venus-square-saturn/), the old question of whether you may enjoy what you love comes back under this transit with a date attached.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Saturn's yearly loop backwards covers about seven degrees ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)), so when it turns near the square it can cross the exact point three times. That happens in a little over half of these [transits](/astrology/transits/) and spreads them over about nine and a half months. The usual length, with my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after, is about nine months, and a single quick crossing can be over in three weeks.

Twice a round adds up to five or six squares by 80, and nobody gets there without meeting some. The waxing square comes between [transiting Saturn sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-venus/) and [transiting Saturn trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-venus/). The waning one follows [transiting Saturn opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-venus/) and the second trine, a few years before Saturn returns to your Venus at [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-venus/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Protect one pleasure on purpose. Put it in the diary like any other commitment, and keep it.

Count what you give and what you get, once and honestly, then talk about it. Fairness is Venus's business, and Saturn wants it said out loud.

Notice when you're trying to earn affection by being useful. Under this square, rest and enjoyment don't need to be deserved first.
