Your thinking needs a second voice. Ideas take shape between you and the person across the table, the planet of your everyday words having its seat in the house of partners.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) covers the words you exchange every day, in talk, letters and messages, and the people close at hand who hear most of them. Its ruler, the planet of the sign on that cusp ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), sits here in [the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), so the whole of that everyday mind is spent with someone: a spouse or long-term partner, a business associate, a close collaborator, even an open rival.

So I read it as thinking done in dialogue. You may not know what you think until you've said it to someone who answers back; a good sparring partner sharpens you more than an afternoon alone with a book. In love, being able to talk isn't a bonus but the ground a bond stands on. At work, it often shows as a gift for negotiation, mediation or any job where two sides have to understand each other. The risk is borrowing other people's opinions, or waiting for a partner's reply before trusting your own.

The old lists of the houses give the 3rd letters, paperwork and contracts as well as conversation, and the 7th marriage and partnerships of every kind ([horary astrology](/astrology/horary-astrology/)). With this ruler the two meet in the terms of your partnerships: the promises spoken and written, the agreements renegotiated over the years, the small print you're the one to read.

The 7th is angular and begins at the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the point that was setting in the west when you were born, so a planet here was above the western horizon, about to set. That angular place gives the ruler of a cadent house a loud voice in your relationships: your way of talking is one of the first things a partner meets, and often part of what draws them.

Siblings and neighbours can be part of the story too. A brother or sister may have been your first sparring partner, the one who taught you to argue fairly; a partner may slip easily into the sibling circle, or a sibling may become a business partner. That's how those ties feel and work for you; nobody's choices are written in your chart.

Venus, ruling a Taurus or Libra 3rd from the 7th, sits in the house of its own natural sign: words used to keep the peace, and a taste for partners with good manners. Mercury, ruling Gemini or Virgo on the cusp, likes to argue both sides. Jupiter shares a Pisces 3rd cusp with Neptune, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): in the 7th, Jupiter can make a partner your best teacher, while Neptune keeps a poetic thread of its own somewhere else in the chart.

Had the ruler already set at your birth, it would sit in the 6th, and your words would serve the working day ([Ruler of the 3rd in the 6th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)); a little higher in the western sky, in the 8th, they'd turn to research and the conversations people rarely have ([Ruler of the 3rd in the 8th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). That can be a matter of minutes, which is why the birth time comes before any reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
