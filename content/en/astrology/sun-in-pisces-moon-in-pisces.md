The Sun and Moon were together in Pisces when you were born, within a couple of days of the new moon, the dark night that starts each lunar month. Pisces is the last sign of the zodiac, so your birth joined an ending and a beginning in one place. What you aim for and what you need speak the same language here: compassion, imagination, devotion.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Both lights share mutable water, a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) by sign, so there's no clash of element or mode to manage. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) builds a self around devotion and imagination; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) picks up the moods around it, answers with ready compassion and needs regular quiet to find its own feelings again. When purpose and need come from the same place they rarely argue, and the work lies elsewhere: telling which of the feelings in you are yours.

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rule Pisces, Neptune in modern tropical astrology and Jupiter in the tradition, and here both rule both your lights, so I read both with particular care. Neptune shows where you're most open to images, music and other people's states; Jupiter, where that openness grows into a faith, a purpose or a generosity with some shape to it. Neither light has a dignity in Pisces, but [Venus](/astrology/venus/), never more than about 47° from the Sun, may share the sign, and Pisces is where tradition exalts Venus: love and pleasure then speak with the same tenderness as the two lights.

At your birth the Moon was anywhere from 28° behind the Sun to 29° ahead of it, within about two and a half days of a [new moon in Pisces](/astrology/new-moon-in-pisces/). Half these births fall just before it, in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) phase, the one that closes a cycle; half just after, in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase, the one that opens the next. Close to the exact new moon the chart holds a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/), purpose and need setting out together. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains what changes either side of that moment, and your birth hour decides which side is yours.

Both lights fall in Pisces for about 1 person in 149. Because the Moon spends about two and a half days in a sign, a birthday on which it entered from Aquarius or left for Aries could also belong to the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-aquarius/) or the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-aries/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Two double-Pisces charts differ most in their rising signs and houses, and those come from the hour, which is why I start a reading only once it is known.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with your whole imagination: you sense what a partner feels, forgive easily and give without counting. The risk is the same gift turned round: absorbing a partner's moods until you can't tell where theirs end and yours begin, or staying loyal to the person you imagined rather than the one in front of you. A partner who is glad to give you time alone, and who says plainly what they feel, keeps you safer than any grand gesture.

## At work {#at-work}

Music, film, poetry, painting, therapy, nursing, chaplaincy, work with animals or by the sea: anywhere empathy and imagination are the main tools. You do well where you can follow a feeling through to its end, and less well where everything must be decided fast and coolly. A colleague with a gift for deadlines is worth more to you than you might think.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With no second sign to offer a contrast, everything comes in through the same door. Other people's moods can settle in you, tiredness can come from carrying feelings that were never yours, and plans can drift. Your boundaries work best when you set them deliberately: a door you close, an hour that belongs to you, a no said kindly and kept. Somewhere else in your chart there is usually a planet that disagrees with all this water, and I'd go looking for it with you; it often holds the structure you need.
