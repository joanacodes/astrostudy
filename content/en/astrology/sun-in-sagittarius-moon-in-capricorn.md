Your Moon had already reached Capricorn, the sign the Sun enters at the December solstice, while your Sun was still in Sagittarius. You were born in the first days of a lunar month. I see the pair as a vision that needs scaffolding before it feels safe, with a long view on both sides.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

The signs are neighbours and share nothing: mutable fire for the Sun, cardinal earth for the Moon. The [Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/) is drawn to the horizon and the reason for going; the [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) finds safety in having the route planned and the bags packed by the door: the responsibility accepted, the timetable checked, the promise to be back on Tuesday kept. One part of you wants to say yes to the expedition; the other wants to know who will be in charge of it.

The dignities weigh on the Moon's side. Tradition puts the Moon in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Capricorn, the sign facing its own: needs tend to wait behind obligations here, which says something about the order of business and nothing about the size of the heart. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), your Sun's ruler, has its fall in Capricorn, so the planet of growth is least at ease in the sign of your needs, and expansion has to justify itself to a Moon that wants proof. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules Capricorn and has no dignity in Sagittarius. It also has a record in my own testing: of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Saturn is the one ruling your Moon here, so its sign and house are where I begin.

You were born at the start of a lunar month. The Moon had moved up to 60° ahead of the Sun, up to about five days after the new moon, and if it showed at all it was a thin crescent low in the west at dusk. In the eight-phase count the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase takes seven of these births in eight, the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) the rest; at the very end of the range the lights make a waxing [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). I read a new moon birth as purpose and need still close together, and this one as a need for structure setting out alongside the vision. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the phases.

About 1 person in 148 has this pair. If the Moon entered Capricorn, or left it for Aquarius, on your birthday, the time decides between this page, the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-sagittarius/) and the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-aquarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Born around 21 December, the same time may also decide whether your Sun is still in Sagittarius. Saturn's house and the rising sign need it as well.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with plans as well as feelings. The Capricorn Moon shows care by being reliable, the lift home, the problem solved before it's mentioned; the Sagittarius Sun brings humour and a sense that the relationship is going somewhere. Being looked after is harder: help offered can feel like a debt, and you may wait until a project is over before saying it was too much. A partner who shares your appetite for the long road, and takes some of the luggage without being asked, reaches both lights at once.

## At work {#at-work}

This pair can turn a vision into an institution. The Sun sees what a school, a publisher, an expedition or a firm could be for; the Moon builds the timetable, the division of roles and the chain of responsibility that will keep it standing. You tend to be trusted early with large projects. The risk is that the Moon's caution trims the Sun's aims until they no longer inspire anyone, you included. Keep one ambitious aim in the plan that is there purely because it matters.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The Sun wants to grow and the Moon wants to be sure, and with Jupiter in its fall in the Moon's sign, growth can feel irresponsible before it has begun. You may postpone the journey until everything is in order, and everything is never quite in order. Feelings can wait behind duties for a long time. What helps is a structure built for the adventure rather than against it: a date set, the first step booked, and someone to report back to who is glad when you go.
