About five years after Saturn crosses your Sun, it reaches 60° beyond it; about five years before it crosses again, it stands 60° short. Both times, Saturn's slow work becomes something available rather than something imposed.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds structure, carries responsibility and makes things last; the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises who you are and where you're going. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperation, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) offers more than it imposes: a framework your purpose can use, if you take it up. Saturn's help tends to come in practical forms: a teacher with high standards, an institution, a schedule, an older colleague who explains how things really work.

The two sextiles of a round do different jobs. The first, five years after a crossing, gives a shape to what you committed to then: the plan gets its timetable, the skill its practice. The second, five years before the next crossing, is quieter and more practical: what you've built is ready to be handed on, simplified or made to last without you watching over it.

The house Saturn is crossing sets the scene. From your 11th, for instance, the support comes through a group, a network or a friend with experience ([Saturn transiting the 11th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-11th-house/)). Born with [Sun sextile Saturn](/astrology/sun-sextile-saturn/), you know this cooperation from the inside, and each sextile in transit rehearses it in another part of your life.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile usually holds for about nine months. A little over half the time it's a loop, with three exact passes over about nine and a half months as Saturn turns retrograde and direct again; at full speed, it can pass in three weeks.

Your Sun's degree comes from your birth date, so you can roughly time this one yourself. While Saturn is in Aries, until April 2028, the sextile falls on birthdays in Gemini and in Aquarius; from then until mid-2030, with Saturn in Taurus, on birthdays in Cancer and in Pisces. The houses it plays out in still need your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have had five or six of these sextiles. In each round, the first follows [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-sun/) and comes a couple of years before [transiting Saturn square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-sun/); [transiting Saturn trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-sun/) and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-sun/) follow, and the second sextile is the last step before the next crossing.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask for the structure. Sign up for the course, book time with the mentor, put the long project into dated steps: the sextile rewards whoever goes looking for Saturn's help.

Make one promise you intend to keep for years, small enough to start this week. Saturn trusts what is repeated more than what is announced.

Offer your own experience to someone starting out. A Saturn sextile works both ways, and teaching what you know is one of the surest ways to find out how well you know it.
