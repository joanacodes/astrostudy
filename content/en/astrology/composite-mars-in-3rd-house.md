The car is where this couple sorts everything out, and where most of its rows happen too. Composite Mars in the 3rd house keeps a relationship's energy circulating close to home, in talk, errands and learning.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) covers the everyday mind: conversation, messages, learning, short journeys, siblings and neighbours. Its old name was the Goddess, and the Moon was said to rejoice there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) it describes the relationship's daily circuit, and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) there puts the bond's energy into that circuit. The house is cadent, a place of movement and adjustment rather than of foundations, and its natural sign is Gemini.

Such a pair is busy close to home. It knows the shortcuts, runs the errands at speed, takes evening classes together, joins the local campaign about the bus route, and argues happily about the best way to get anywhere. Brothers, sisters and neighbours can be drawn into its causes: the bond may defend a brother or a sister, or stand up to a difficult neighbour, as one. Competition is quick and verbal, word games, a race to finish the crossword. Desire lives in conversation: a message in the middle of the day, a long talk on a walk. Short trips suit it, a day out by train or a weekend two hours away, more than the long haul. [Mars in the 3rd house](/astrology/mars-in-3rd-house/) makes one person a quick debater; [their Mars in your 3rd house](/astrology/mars-in-3rd-house-synastry/) may sharpen your thinking or your temper; in the composite, the quickness is a habit of the relationship.

Disagreements happen on the move: in the car, in the supermarket queue, by message between meetings. They're quick, sometimes sharp, and they can multiply into a running commentary on small things. Words are this placement's tools, and words are also its weapons. What helps is a pause before replying, a rule that nothing important is decided in the car, and the habit of arguing while walking, shoulder to shoulder, instead of across a table.

A sharp exchange is friction. If words between you ever become threats, or one of you dreads coming home, that is not a question astrology can answer: 999 if it's an emergency in the UK, Samaritans on 116 123 otherwise.

I don't work with composites; astrologers who do read this house alongside each of your birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). They would also point out how much rests on the clock: a composite's 3rd-house cusp, whether averaged from your two charts or rebuilt around the composite Midheaven for the latitude you share, moves with both birth times, and a Mars near either edge can end up in a neighbouring house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The placement predicts no move, no break-up and no family quarrel.

[Composite Mars in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-2nd-house/) puts the effort into what the pair owns; [composite Mars in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-4th-house/) into the home itself. The 3rd keeps it moving between the two, and since a composite looks into two people's everyday lives, it's drawn only when both agree ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
