Mercury sextile Venus is the widest major aspect these two planets can make. They're never more than about 76° apart, and for them to be 60° apart, the Sun has to sit between them: one in the morning sky, one in the evening.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how a chart thinks: it takes information in, sorts it, and finds the words for it. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs value: what draws you, what you prefer, what feels fair, what seems beautiful. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) connects them by way of opportunity: each keeps its own work, and each can call on the other.

In plain words, you think and you value as two different acts, and the two are on good terms. When you want to explain why something matters to you, the words are there; when you meet a new idea, you can tell what's worth keeping in it. In company, it often shows as an instinct for the fair version of a sentence. But a sextile is an opening rather than a habit: it works when you reach for it.

Room is what the sextile gives this particular pair. Fused in a conjunction, thinking and liking can be hard to tell apart; here, with the Sun between them, you can notice which one is speaking. That helps on the days your taste wants to agree and your reasoning doesn't, or the other way round: you get to choose.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A sextile usually links signs two apart, so Mercury and Venus are likely to sit in compatible elements, fire and air or earth and water, often with your Sun sign between them: Mercury in Pisces and Venus in Taurus around an Aries Sun, for instance, might pair a thinking that works by intuition with a steady, sensory taste. Their houses tell you what the opening connects: work and friendship, say, or study and the love of something beautiful. The birth date is usually enough to know whether you have the aspect; the houses need the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

About 1 person in 9 is born with Mercury and Venus within 6° of a sextile, slightly more than are born with them conjunct. Which of the two leads the Sun and which follows it varies from chart to chart. Mercury, usually the quicker, does most of the moving; around its retrogrades, three or four times a year, the angle between them can stall or reverse for a while.

The pair's only other major aspect is [Mercury conjunct Venus](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-venus/): the two side by side near the Sun, thought and taste in one gesture, where this sextile keeps them apart and talking.

Between two charts, [Mercury sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-venus-synastry/) links one person's way of thinking with the other's way of valuing through an easy opening: talk that's pleasant to have, as long as both keep having it. I read it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Mercury in the sky sextiles your natal Venus about twice a year, once from each side, for a day or so each time, and more often when one of its retrograde loops is close. Venus sextiles your natal Mercury about twice a year too, staying two or three days, and in the months around a Venus retrograde, about every 18 months, the same sextile can come back two or three times. Weather, not a forecast: a good window for a conversation that needs clear thinking and goodwill at once.
