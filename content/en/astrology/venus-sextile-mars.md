Venus sextile Mars is the commonest of the five ways these two planets meet, and in my reading the most practical. It describes a working arrangement between what you like and how you act: not a fusion, not an easy flow, but a door between the two that you open when you choose.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) judges what's worth having and keeps an exchange fair; [Mars](/astrology/mars/) supplies the initiative to go and get it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity: each can set the other going, but only when something, or someone, offers the chance and you take it.

In plain words, liking can turn into a move without turning into a campaign. You can suggest the walk, offer the help, start the conversation, put a proposal on the table, and the way you do it tends to leave the other person room to say yes. Effort feels lighter when it serves something you enjoy, and enjoyment gets livelier when there's something to do. It reaches far beyond romance: friendship, teams, any place where a first step needs to be both clear and kind.

The condition is written into the aspect. A sextile is potential that needs engagement, and left alone this one can stay a pleasant intention you never act on. Its gift arrives the moment you take the step.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, the sextile usually joins two signs of complementary elements, fire beside air or earth beside water, so your Venus and Mars speak different but compatible languages. With Venus in Gemini and Mars in Leo, you like with words and act with warmth; with Venus in Scorpio and Mars in Capricorn, affection shows up as loyal, practical effort. The houses are usually two apart, and one feeds the other: Venus in the 9th with Mars in the 11th might turn a love of learning into a project among friends, or a friendship into a date.

About 1 person in 11 is born with Venus and Mars within 6° of a sextile. Within a degree or two, the knack is easy to see; near 6°, it's there when you look for it. Venus is usually the faster, so it's usually Venus moving towards the exact angle or away from it.

A sextile is the plainest example of what I mean by a tendency rather than a fate: it describes what's available, and you decide what to do with it ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

The pair's other aspects, each a different arrangement between liking and doing:

- **[Venus conjunct Mars](/astrology/venus-conjunct-mars/):** liking and going after it in a single gesture, usually in one sign, which decides which of the two leads.
- **[Venus square Mars](/astrology/venus-square-mars/):** the two keep crossing each other's path; friction that teaches timing in what you want.
- **[Venus trine Mars](/astrology/venus-trine-mars/):** the two keep pace within one element; an ease so smooth it's easy to overlook.
- **[Venus opposite Mars](/astrology/venus-opposite-mars/):** the rarest meeting, with the two facing each other across the chart; a balance often learned through other people.

Between two charts, [Venus sextile Mars in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-mars-synastry/) pairs one person's taste with the other's initiative from the start: warmth that makes plans easy, rather than a spark. The one who proposes and the one delighted to accept notice different things, so I look at each chart.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), twice a year or so, for a couple of days, Venus in the sky sextiles your natal Mars: a good moment to suggest the plan you've been turning over. Mars sextiles your natal Venus about once a year and lingers rather longer, at Mars's slower pace: energy that drifts easily towards something you enjoy.

Weather, not a forecast: an opening in the week, which you can take or leave, and no more than that.
