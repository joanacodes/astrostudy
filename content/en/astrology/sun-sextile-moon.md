Sun sextile Moon belongs to the thin crescents of the month: your birth fell a few days after a new moon, when the crescent hangs in the west at dusk, or a few days before one, when it rises ahead of the Sun at dawn. Purpose and need are far enough apart, at 60°, to lend each other a hand.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is where your purpose and your vitality come from. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) tends the system that keeps you steady: feelings, attachments, habits, the needs that have to be met before anything else goes well. In a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), the two cooperate on one condition: the help is offered, not imposed.

In plain words, your direction and your comfort are on speaking terms. The routine that settles you, a walk, a meal with someone close, can also feed the thing you're working on; and having somewhere to go gives your feelings a shape, so they don't simply circle. Neither function bends to the other. They trade.

Because a sextile doesn't push, its help is easy to leave unused: you can spend years treating rest and ambition as separate accounts when they could feed each other. That's why, in a reading, I point it out ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). Help you haven't noticed is help you don't use.

Which crescent you were born under gives the cooperation a direction. Under a [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/), the Moon about two signs ahead of the Sun, the month is filling, and I read the alliance as outward: your needs back what you're starting. Under a waning crescent, the Moon two signs behind and the next new moon a few days off, the month is emptying, and I read it as cooperation in finishing and letting go.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Two signs apart, the Sun and Moon usually sit in fire and air, or in earth and water: different temperaments that suit each other. A Sun in Leo with the Moon in Libra, a waxing sextile, puts a wish to shine beside a need for harmony; a Sun in Taurus with the Moon in Pisces, a waning one, puts steadiness beside a need to dream. The houses show where the trade happens: the Sun's house is where you make an effort, the Moon's where you restore yourself, and the sextile builds a footbridge between them.

Counting within 8°, about 1 person in 11 has this sextile. There are two each month, one either side of the new moon, so this is twice as common as being born at the new moon itself. A tight sextile is hard to miss; a wide one is easy to overlook. The Moon, much the faster, decides whether the sextile was still forming at your birth or already past ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)).

The month carries the same two lights through every other aspect:

- **[Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/):** the new moon your crescent is moving away from, or towards; there, purpose and need haven't separated enough to offer each other anything.
- **[Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/):** two or three days further from the new moon, the half moon; the offer turns into a demand, and the two want the same hours.
- **[Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/):** the gibbous moon; the same kind of help, given without being asked for.
- **[Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/):** the full moon, two weeks from the new; the lights stop helping from the side and face each other.

Between two people, [Sun sextile Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon-synastry/) is the friendly form of this contact: one person's direction and the other's needs, ready to help whenever either one asks.

## In transits {#in-transits}

The Moon in the sky reaches a sextile to your natal Sun twice a month and holds it for a few hours: an afternoon when the task that matters also feels good to do. The Sun makes the same angle to your natal Moon twice a year, over two or three days, and born with this aspect, one of those days is your birthday, when the Sun is back where it was, 60° from your Moon. The other falls about four months away.

These visits are short and mild ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). Weather, not a forecast: a light tailwind on a day or two, there if you choose to use it.
