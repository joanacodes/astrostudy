Cancer on the 10th house cusp raises the sign of home to the top of the chart. The Midheaven is where you are seen, and here what people see is someone who looks after things: a team, a place, a cause, the people who come to you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) as the face your work shows the world, and its sign as the manner of that face ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) there, a public role tends to be a protective one, and leadership, when it comes, looks like stewardship: you protect the team before you push it. You may be known as the person who remembers everyone, keeps the institution's history, makes newcomers welcome or fights for the people in your care. Reputation is personal here: people remember how you treated them more than what your title was. Work that feeds, houses, nurses, teaches, keeps or preserves fits the pattern, though the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) describes a direction, not a job, and no Midheaven chooses a profession. Visibility can feel exposing to a sign that would rather shelter than shine, and a public role is easiest when it also feels like belonging.

The meridian here is the natural one turned upside down. Cancer, the 4th house's own sign, sits at the top, and Capricorn, the 10th's own sign, sits at the IC, every time ([Capricorn on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/)). At home you are often the one who keeps things running, organised and responsible, while in the world you are the one who cares. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules your 10th and Saturn your 4th, and your work tends to gather around the house the Moon occupies: the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

The meridian needs about two hours and eight minutes to cross Cancer, as long as it takes for Gemini, its mirror image across the June solstice, and that gives a Cancer Midheaven to about 1 birth in 11. Latitude plays no part in the figure, which is the same at London and New York: the Midheaven, the 10th cusp in Placidus, moves with the time of day alone. [Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/) is the only Ascendant a Cancer Midheaven allows at London and New York, so Venus rules your chart: the courtesy people meet first, the care they come to rely on. With Venus ruling the Ascendant and the Moon the Midheaven, the two most visible points of your chart belong to the planets of attraction and of care. Leo, too, can fit inside your 10th: it does in about 1 London chart in 6, the house being about 34° to 36° wide there, and the Sun then joins the Moon in your public life ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Cancer opens no other house here, and the 11th begins in Leo, or in Virgo when Leo is intercepted, so the pages on [Cancer on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/) and [Cancer on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/) are written for other charts.

An hour's difference in the birth time can move the Midheaven into Gemini or Leo, so the time has to be exact before your Midheaven can be read at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
