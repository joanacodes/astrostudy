Cancer on the 10th house cusp puts the sign of home at the top of the chart. The Midheaven is where you are seen, and here what people see is someone who looks after things: a team, a place, a cause, the people who come to you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is the frame for how your capacities become visible, through roles, responsibilities and what you contribute, and its sign is the style of that ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) there, a public role tends to be a protective one, and leadership, when it comes, looks like stewardship: you protect the team before you push it. You may be known as the person who remembers everyone, keeps the institution's history, makes newcomers welcome or fights for the people in your care. Reputation is personal here: people remember how you treated them more than what your title was. Work that feeds, houses, nurses, teaches, keeps or preserves fits the pattern, though the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) describes a direction, not a job, and no Midheaven chooses a profession. Visibility can feel exposing to a sign that would rather shelter than shine, and a public role is easiest when it also feels like belonging.

The meridian here is the natural one turned upside down. Cancer, the 4th house's own sign, sits at the top, and Capricorn, the 10th's own sign, sits at the IC, every time ([Capricorn on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/)). At home you are often the one who keeps things running, organised and responsible, while in the world you are the one who cares. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules your 10th and Saturn your 4th, and the Moon's house shows where your working life tends to centre: the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

About 1 birth in 11 has Cancer on the Midheaven, at London as at New York. In Placidus, the house system I use, the Midheaven is the 10th cusp, and the sign it holds depends on the time of day rather than the latitude; Cancer, just after the June solstice, takes a little over two hours to cross the meridian. [Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/) comes with a Cancer Midheaven in every chart at London and New York, so Venus rules your chart: the courtesy people meet first, the care they come to rely on. With Venus ruling the Ascendant and the Moon the Midheaven, the two most visible points of your chart belong to the planets of attraction and of care. The 10th is wide in these charts, about 34° to 36° at London: Cancer never opens the 9th or the 11th as well, and in about 1 London chart in 6, the whole of Leo lies inside the 10th too ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), adding the Sun to your public life. [Cancer on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/) and [Cancer on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/) describe other charts.

An hour's difference in the birth time can move the Midheaven into Gemini or Leo, so the time has to be exact before your Midheaven can be read at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
