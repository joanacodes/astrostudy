Chiron opposite North Node in synastry puts one person's Chiron about 180° from the other's North Node, within 3°, which means right on their South Node: an opposition to one node is a conjunction to the other. One person's ways of coping land on the other's oldest, most practised ground. Their growth, meanwhile, waits on the far side of the zodiac.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the part of a chart that learned to function around a limitation that stayed: other routes, other tools, a pace that works. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) holds what a person already does with ease, and the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), always opposite, the less familiar capacity they're building. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) strings two functions along one line, each defining the other, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one parks the Chiron at the familiar end of the other person's axis.

If the Chiron is yours, the other person's old ground feels like somewhere you've been. You recognise their habitual ways of getting by, and they recognise your coping in turn. There's kinship in that: neither of you has to explain how you manage.

Carrying the North Node, you find the other person at home with what you already know best. Time together is restful, and your established strengths are understood without effort. What they're less likely to do is pull you towards the unfamiliar end, because their Chiron lives on your familiar one.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Seesaw rather than enmity, this opposition can still come to rest on its comfortable side. The pair may keep circling the same patch, trading old ways of coping and admiring them, while the node person's growth waits at the far end. The Chiron person may also find it hard to cheer on a direction that leaves their own way of managing behind.

- **[Chiron conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the Chiron on the new direction rather than the old ground.
- **[Chiron sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** a small opening towards the new, with an easy trine to the familiar.
- **[Chiron square North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-square-north-node-synastry/):** the difficulty crossing the axis, claimed by neither end.
- **[Chiron trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-trine-north-node-synastry/):** experience streaming forward, touching the old ground only lightly.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the familiar ground be where you start from rather than where you stay. Whoever carries the North Node can bring one unfamiliar attempt into the relationship each season and let the other see it; whoever carries the Chiron can praise the attempt as warmly as the old skills they admire. Old friends, siblings, a couple who feel like home to each other, two colleagues who cover each other's habits: all can use the comfort as a launch pad.

The dates come from the true node, with the mean one checked. A Chiron in Pisces belongs to anyone born in mid-1965, and people born from November 1978 to January 1979 have the North Node in Virgo, opposite it, and their South Node in Pisces right on that Chiron. Moving backwards through a full circle every 18.6 years or so, the node brought the same arrangement to births from January to June 1960 and from June to October 1997. The other way round, people born through most of 2000 have Chiron in Sagittarius on the 1965 South Node, opposite its North Node in Gemini.

My notebook doesn't name the nodes, so there is no figure for this contact. Nor does it make my synastry count, where every contact needs the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven on one side. In the houses at either end, it reads as one era's coping making another era's old ground feel familiar. Those houses need both birth times, though the hour barely shifts either point ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Within one chart, [Chiron opposite North Node](/astrology/chiron-opposite-north-node/) makes coping a person's own oldest skill. Shared between two, it's a kinship that deserves gentleness, and I read it only with both people's yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
