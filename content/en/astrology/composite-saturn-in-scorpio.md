Trust, for this relationship, is a long apprenticeship. Composite Saturn in Scorpio builds a bond slowly and at depth, and the bond tends to find out what it's made of when something hard happens.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The composite's [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) carries a relationship's long-term weight, the commitments it can't easily put down. In [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), fixed water, that weight settles on trust. Commitment means confidences kept, a shared life consolidated piece by piece, and a loyalty that holds under strain. The bond's rules often concern what stays between the two of you: the things you tell no one else, the decisions you make without outside advice. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) reads all of this as the relationship's character, not as either person's temperament.

Scorpio has two rulers, Mars in the tradition and Pluto in modern tropical astrology ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and I read both in any chart. Applied to a composite, that habit means looking at composite Mars, for how the bond acts and defends itself, and at composite Pluto, for how it changes over time. Tradition gives Saturn no dignity in Scorpio, neither home nor weakness, so its reading rests on the sign's own manner: depth first, surface later.

Most of the pairs who have it, about 1 in 13 of two million drawn from the births of 1930 to 2009, are of an age, with both people born between October 1953 and October 1956, or between November 1982 and November 1985. Pairs with an age gap reach it another way, through two Saturns that bracket Scorpio, and their bond may then be more patient with trust than either of them is alone. That's the composite at work: the relationship has a Saturn of its own, which neither of you carries into a room alone.

On its own, in a birth chart, [Saturn in Scorpio](/astrology/saturn-in-scorpio/) asks a person whom to let in, and how far. The composite puts that question to the relationship, and the two of you may set different paces: one ready to say everything, the other still checking that it's safe. Unlike [composite Saturn in Libra](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-libra/), which can settle a doubt with a clear agreement, or [composite Saturn in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-sagittarius/), which can appeal to a shared principle, this bond settles its doubts only with time.

The difficulty is guardedness. Trust given this slowly can leave one person feeling permanently on probation. Saturn's urge to control can tighten around what is shared, the money, the friends, the information, until privacy feels like surveillance. And a bond that discovers its strength in hard times may come to feel most real only in a crisis. What helps is naming what is private and what isn't, extending small trusts before large ones, and giving the relationship ordinary, light days that ask nothing of its depth.

This is the technique described, not a reading of mine, since I don't work with composites. Its sign comes from two birth dates, but its houses, which would place this Saturn in an area of the pair's life, need both birth times, and without them I wouldn't read any chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Read with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), it describes the depth a bond works at, never whether it will last.
