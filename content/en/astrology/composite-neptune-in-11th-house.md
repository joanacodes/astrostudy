Some couples are never really alone: there's always a friend at the table, a meeting to go to, a cause pinned to the fridge door. A composite Neptune in the 11th house makes that wider circle part of the relationship's dream.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) can give a friendship, as much as a love, a chart of its own: every planet halfway between two birth charts, read as a third thing with a social life of its own. Composite [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where its hopes lose their edges. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds friendships, groups, networks and causes, and the hopes a bond has for the future. With Neptune here, the relationship dreams with others. Friends become something like family, the two of you may host the gatherings or carry a group's spirit, and a cause, a choir, a community garden or an online circle can feel like an extension of the bond itself.

Jupiter, the tradition says, took its joy in the 11th, which it called the Good Spirit ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With Neptune here, the bond's hopes reach past the people it knows, to people it hasn't met yet.

The difficulty is seeing the circle clearly. A bond that idealises community can see a group as it hopes it is rather than as it is, follow a charismatic friend further than it meant to, or give its best energy to everyone's plans but its own. Shared hopes can stay hopes. Friendship isn't the same as belonging to a crowd, and a cause is better served by two people who still have time for each other.

Alone in a chart, [Neptune in the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-in-11th-house/) brings one person compassionate friendships and causes; in synastry, [their Neptune in your 11th house](/astrology/neptune-in-11th-house-synastry/) is one partner finding a longed-for community among the other's people. The composite 11th is the circle the relationship itself gathers. Aquarius is this house's natural sign, and the youngest adult couples have [composite Neptune in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-aquarius/) by sign; the house is the same longing reached through the birth times, open to any generation.

Composites aren't only for couples: they can be drawn for any close bond, and the 11th, the house of friends, is where a friendship's composite might hold its Neptune. That raises a plain problem. Friends rarely know each other's birth times, and a composite house needs both, exactly; the two ways of drawing the houses, averaging the two sets of cusps or building them from the composite Midheaven, don't place the cusps alike ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). It raises a plainer one too: two friends should both agree before anyone looks at them together ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Between [composite Neptune in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-10th-house/), which makes the dream a public vocation, and [composite Neptune in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-12th-house/), which keeps it private and inward, the 11th shares it with a circle. I leave the drawing of composites to others; the astrologers who use them read this house next to both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), as the shape of a bond's hopes, never a promise that they'll come true.
