Mars opposite Chiron sets the part of you that pushes ahead directly across from the part that still smarts. The two keep catching sight of each other, often in someone else's face, and the work is letting both be true at once.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is your capacity to act: to begin, assert, make an effort and defend a limit. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where something stays tender, and where, by adapting around it, you build a skill other people can use. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity: face to face, each shows up the other more clearly, and the task is balance rather than victory.

In everyday terms, when you push, you feel where you're sore; when you feel sore, the urge to push gets louder. You may swing between charging at a difficulty and steering well clear of it. Other people often make the axis visible: a rival, a coach, a partner who moves faster than you, whose push lands right on the tender spot, and whose drive you also recognise as your own. That's a mirror, not an enemy.

Polarity isn't contradiction. Over time this opposition tends to teach a particular strength: acting with your limits in view rather than in spite of them, and standing up for someone else without first having to prove that nothing in you is tender.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The two bodies then sit in signs facing each other, alike in modality and complementary in element. Mars in Capricorn, disciplined and aimed at the long game, across from Chiron in Cancer, where the tender place is belonging, might make work the way you avoid the question of home, or the way you finally answer it. The houses show the two ends in your life: across the 1st and 7th, between asserting yourself and what partnership stirs up; across the 2nd and 8th, between what you earn by your own effort and what you share with others. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

About 1 person in 34 has Mars within 6° of an exact opposition to Chiron. Tight, the two ends seem to trigger each other often; near 6°, the balancing happens mostly when life asks for it.

When I went through charts placement by placement, Chiron stood out with Saturn and Lilith: the three held 75 to 90% of the time, while about a third of placements held overall, and Mars was not among the three ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). When a reading of this opposition leans on Mars's end, I say so.

Mars meets Chiron at every angle, and each shapes how effort and tenderness get along:

- **[Mars conjunct Chiron](/astrology/mars-conjunct-chiron/):** the two in one place; every push starts from the tender spot, and that's where the skill comes from.
- **[Mars sextile Chiron](/astrology/mars-sextile-chiron/):** an opening between them, used when you choose.
- **[Mars square Chiron](/astrology/mars-square-chiron/):** friction at close quarters; the way you act has to keep changing.
- **[Mars trine Chiron](/astrology/mars-trine-chiron/):** an easy current; you adapt as you act, almost without noticing.

Between two charts, [Mars opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-chiron-synastry/) sets one person's drive across from the other's tender place: bracing, sometimes too close for comfort, and different seen from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-mars/) is the moment Chiron, moving on, stands opposite your Mars. Born with the opposition, you meet it at a particular moment: that point is where your Chiron stood at birth, so the transit arrives with the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50. Its passage over your Mars itself comes years before, at no fixed age: Chiron speeds up and slows down so much on its stretched orbit that each generation meets it at a different time.

Mars itself, going round the zodiac in about two years, opposes your natal Chiron once per lap for a few days, or for weeks when it turns retrograde nearby: a short spell in which effort and tenderness pull opposite ways.

I take both as weather, not a forecast. They set the mood of a season and leave what happens in it to you.
