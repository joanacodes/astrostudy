Encouragement is easy to get wrong: too much and it shoves, too little and it isn't there. Mars sextile Chiron in synastry tends to find the amount. The Mars of one and the Chiron of the other are about 60° apart, and what passes between them, once someone asks, is a nudge of the right size and a method that lasts.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is how a person starts, keeps going and stands up for an aim. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is how they've learned to take part despite a persistent limit: by compensating, adjusting, practising, and often by turning what they learned into advice worth having. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers the two an opening for cooperation, and as one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it lets each person borrow what the other has practised more.

From the Mars side, your drive comes with a feel for how much push someone can use. You can be the friend who says "one more go" at the right moment, and stops there. From the Chiron side, you have something the other person rarely gets from anyone else: how to keep going when effort meets a limit. A pacing trick, a better tool, a way of asking for exactly the help you need and no more, and the other's energy goes further for it.

The two signs usually suit each other by element. Mars in Gemini with Chiron in Leo might trade lively encouragement for the courage to be seen; Mars in Taurus with Chiron in Pisces, steady effort for a gentle, workable way through things that can't be controlled.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The ease can hide a missing first step. The Mars person tends to wait to be asked; the Chiron person, used to managing alone, doesn't like asking. Both can mean well for years without the exchange ever starting. There's another quiet trap: the Chiron person may be generous with their know-how and slow to receive anything back, as if helping others and being helped weren't both allowed.

Seen from the Mars side, your offers can look like politeness rather than real readiness. Seen from the Chiron side, accepting help can feel like admitting a weakness. It isn't: support is not dependence.

The sextile's neighbours in this pair:

- **[Mars conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-chiron-synastry/):** the push lands on the tender place itself, closer and harder to pace.
- **[Mars square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-chiron-synastry/):** a sign further round, the nudge catches the sore spot from the side.
- **[Mars trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-chiron-synastry/):** the same support, wider and steadier, without waiting to be invited.
- **[Mars opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-chiron-synastry/):** drive and tender place face each other, and each sees itself in the other.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make the first request plainly. The Chiron person can say what kind of help works, and the Mars person can offer once, clearly, then let it rest. Trade methods in both directions: coworkers swapping the techniques that keep them going, friends learning a skill together, an adult child and a parent working out a new routine that suits both.

Despite the narrowest orb of the five, 3.6°, about 1 pair of people in 13 has this sextile, one way round or the other. On the Chiron side, the position depends mostly on the year of birth, and how many years share it depends on the sign, Chiron's uneven orbit giving some signs under two years and others about eight; when in the year the Mars person was born does the rest. With so narrow an orb, a birth right at its edge is where the hour can matter; the houses where the exchange happens need both times in any case ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

For one person, [Mars sextile Chiron](/astrology/mars-sextile-chiron/) is a private workshop for acting within a limit. Between two people, the workshop has two benches, and both get used.
