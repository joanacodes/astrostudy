Holidays at neither parent's house, a kitchen run on rules no one taught them, a home that friends describe as "very you two": some pairs build a private world that belongs to no family tree. Composite Lilith in the 4th house puts a relationship's untamed side at its root, behind its own front door.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) sits at the bottom of the wheel: home, family, roots, and the private self underneath the public one. It is angular, Cancer is its natural sign, and its cusp is the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the point opposite the Midheaven. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the 4th describes the relationship's home life and the ground it stands on. With [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) there, the point I read as untamed and hidden, that ground is defended. The bond keeps its domestic world to itself and refuses to inherit everything handed to it: the roles each family expects, the traditions that come with a marriage, the way a home is supposed to be run.

Each of you may have left home easily; the bond is the one that wants a burrow, and wants it on its own terms. Two people from close families can make a relationship that holds its kin at arm's length, and two who grew up rootless can make one fiercely private about its nest. The home it builds may surprise both families, and it may say little about it.

The cusp of this house is the same in both ways of drawing composite houses, opposite the midpoint of your two Midheavens, and both birth times decide where it falls. The house's far edge is another matter. One method takes the midpoints of your two charts' cusps and needs no place; the other builds the houses from the composite Midheaven for the place where the pair lives, which raises a question no birth chart asks: for two people who live apart, or between two cities, which home? Until that is settled, a Lilith near the 5th cusp has no settled house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In a single chart, [Lilith in the 4th house](/astrology/lilith-in-4th-house/) is a private self kept closer than most. In synastry, [their Lilith in your 4th house](/astrology/lilith-in-4th-house-synastry/) is someone else's untamed side settling into your home life. The composite's 4th is the home the two of you make. Before it, [composite Lilith in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-3rd-house/) keeps its frankness for the car journey; after it, [composite Lilith in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-5th-house/) lets its untamed side out to play.

The hard part is the fortress. A home kept this private can shut out the people who would have helped, and a bond that refuses every inherited habit can mistake refusal for freedom. It helps to choose your traditions deliberately, keeping some and dropping others, and to tell the families, gently and once, what you are doing and why.

I don't draw composites in my readings; this is how the astrologers who use them read this Lilith, beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It forecasts no move, no wedding and no estrangement. A chart that looks behind a couple's front door should be drawn only when both have agreed to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
