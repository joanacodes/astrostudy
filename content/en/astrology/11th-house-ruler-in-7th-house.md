Ask who your closest friend is, and you might name your partner; ask how you met your partner, and you might name a friend. That's the 11th's ruler at work in the 7th house: the line between friendship and partnership runs thin.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) begins at the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the point of the zodiac sinking below the western horizon at your birth, and gathers the people you face as equals: a lover or spouse, a business partner, a close collaborator, the opponent across the table. The [11th](/astrology/11th-house/) is your chosen company, the alliances you make and the futures you plan together. Here the 11th's ruler has gone to meet one person at a time ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Partners tend to be friends first, and friends are people you could imagine sharing a life or a business with. You look for an equal who wants the same future, someone to plan with as much as to love, and a partnership with a circle of its own around it. Former partners often stay in your life as friends, which is a gift or a complication depending on who you ask. Some meet a partner through a network or a cause; others find that a friendship turns, over years, into a commitment. The care it asks is keeping the two distinct enough that each can be what it is, and not asking one person to be the whole crowd.

In the geometry of signs, partners and friends get on: the 7th stands a trine from the 11th, and bringing a partner into your circle, or a friend into a joint venture, is rarely a struggle. The 7th is also the house of open rivals, the opponent you can see, as against the hidden ones tradition gave the 12th. With the 11th's ruler here, rivalry tends to be friendly, and the people you compete with sometimes end up as allies.

A Leo 11th makes the Sun the planet of your friendships, and in the 7th it was sinking towards the western horizon as you were born: a late-afternoon light that falls on the person beside you. Aquarius on the cusp makes Saturn and Uranus co-rulers, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 7th builds partnership on long friendship and loyalty, while Uranus, wherever it stands, asks that the bond leave each of you free.

None of this says whom you'll marry, or when, or whether a partnership will last. It describes what you look for in a partner and how friendship runs into commitment for you. What a partner brings is in their own chart, read only with their agreement ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

The Descendant is where the turning sky sets each planet, so a ruler just inside the 7th was close to setting when you were born. Had you arrived a little later, it would have gone under into the 6th, where friends are made at work ([Ruler of the 11th in the 6th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)); placed near the 8th cusp instead, it needed only a slightly earlier birth to stand in the 8th, where friendship deepens into shared trust and resources ([Ruler of the 11th in the 8th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). Both turn on a birth time read in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the system I use ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The reverse, the ruler of the 7th in the 11th, brings a partner into the heart of your circle ([Ruler of the 7th in the 11th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)).
