Venus in the 11th house gathers people. The planet of affinity and reciprocity works here among friends, circles and causes: the company you keep, and the future you're building with it. I read it as a gift for warm, easy friendship, and a love that often begins as one.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is the company you keep and what you hope for with it: your friendships, your circles and clubs, the causes you lend your name to, the plans you make with others. It is succedent, the house after the 10th, and its natural sign is Aquarius. The old name for it was the Good Spirit, and Jupiter had its joy there. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) brings its talent for relating into this house of allies. You tend to make friends easily, to enjoy being part of a group, and to value the people who share your hopes as much as those who share your home.

The 11th is where the reciprocity of Venus works without a contract: friendship given and returned freely, with no vows to hold it in place. So this placement often describes someone generous with introductions, favours and time, and quick to notice when a friendship has stopped being mutual.

At your birth Venus was well up in the eastern sky, some hours past rising and still climbing; with the Sun beside it, that usually points to a morning birth. A later birth can lift Venus into [Venus in the 10th house](/astrology/venus-in-10th-house/), an earlier one keep it lower, in [Venus in the 12th house](/astrology/venus-in-12th-house/). In [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the system I use, the lines between the three depend on the exact minute ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, an 11th-house Venus often starts as a friend. You may meet partners through your circle, a club, a cause or a friend of a friend, and a relationship built on shared ideals and easy companionship tends to suit you. You value a partner who gets on with your friends and has a life of their own. The house across is the 5th, romance and play, and the question that runs between the two is when a friendship should stay a friendship, and when it might become more. The risk is keeping love at the friendly distance where nothing is risked.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Venus comes alive in teams, networks and communities. You're often the one who knows everyone, who introduces people, who makes a group feel welcoming. Charities, associations, community work, events, collaborative projects, anything built on people working together towards a shared aim, can suit it. You may enjoy work most when it serves a hope larger than yourself, and when colleagues become friends.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Spreading yourself thin. With many friends and many circles, you may give each a little and none enough, or find it hard to say no to the group. Keeping the peace in a group can mean swallowing your own view. And hopes for the future can keep you from enjoying what you have now, or from asking for closeness with one person in particular.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give your closest friends time alone with you, away from the group. Say what you hope for out loud, to the people who could share it. Let one friendship grow as deep as it wants to. And choose groups that reflect your values rather than the ones you joined out of habit, so that belonging feeds you instead of merely filling your diary.

Venus held roughly one time in three in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). This house is about the people around you, so your friends may know better than any chart whether this Venus sounds like you, and I'm happy to let them have the last word.
