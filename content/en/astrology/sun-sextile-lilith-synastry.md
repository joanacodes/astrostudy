Sun sextile Lilith in synastry links one person's Sun with the other's Black Moon Lilith across a sixth of the zodiac. It's the gentlest way this pair can meet: one person's direction holds a door open for the other's untamed side, and both can walk through it, or not.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) carries a person's purpose and the self they put into it: what they're for, and how they shine at it. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is a point, not a body, and astrologers read it in many ways; I read it as the instinct for one's own terms, along with whatever a person was taught to keep quiet about wanting or refusing. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the two through opportunity: it offers, and it works once someone accepts. Among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) it's one of the mildest, which is exactly why it can be so useful.

When it's your Lilith, the other person's direction tends to make your own terms easier to say out loud. Their direction isn't a spotlight on what you hide; it's more like a window opened in a stuffy room. Around them, the no you usually swallow, or the want you usually explain away, comes out with less apology, and nobody makes a scene of it.

When it's your Sun, their independence tends to sharpen you. Their refusal to play along gives your purpose an edge: permission to stop seeking approval, to trust the instinct you'd been outvoting. You don't have to become them. You borrow a little of their nerve.

Between co-workers, this can look like one person with a clear aim and another who won't pretend a bad rule is a good one, cutting through together. Between friends, it's the friendship that gives you courage without ever daring you.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A sextile hides what it doesn't use. The opening can stay at the level of teasing for years: jokes about the other's "wild side", admiration from a safe distance, an untamed streak welcome as long as it stays entertaining. The Sun person may enjoy the other's edge as seasoning and be less pleased when it turns, one day, towards them. The Lilith person may show just enough to be liked and keep the rest folded away, so the invitation never quite becomes real.

None of this is a fault. It's simply what an opportunity looks like when neither person picks it up.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take the offer on purpose. If the Lilith is yours, try saying one true thing you'd normally keep back, and see what happens; if the Sun is yours, make it plain that a no is welcome even when it's aimed at you. Among adult relatives, that can mean letting a sibling or parent speak to you more frankly than the rest of the family allows. A couple can use it to make room for wants that were never said.

About 1 pair of people in 10 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 4.8°, the narrowest orb I give this pair. Both charts need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and without them I don't read a synastry. With the Sun moving about a degree a day and Lilith slower still, the time only tips the contact in or out at the very edge of that orb, but it always decides the houses: which corner of each life the opening joins.

Inside one chart, the same angle is [Sun sextile Lilith](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith/). Between two, it's the quietest of the pair's five contacts. [Sun conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) puts the hidden part in full view; [Sun trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith-synastry/), its soft neighbour, flows without being asked; [Sun square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-lilith-synastry/) and [Sun opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith-synastry/) turn the same meeting into friction and into a mirror.
