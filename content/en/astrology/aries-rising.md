The first sign of the zodiac on the eastern horizon at the minute you were born: that's Aries rising. It describes how you start, what you do in the first minute of anything new, before you've had time to think. North of the equator it's also among the rarest rising signs, because [Aries](/astrology/aries/) crosses the horizon so quickly.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is the degree that was rising in the east when you were born, and here it sits in a cardinal fire sign: one that starts things and runs on enthusiasm. I read Aries rising as a starter's reflex. Faced with something new, you move towards it first and find out how you feel about it on the way. In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant is the cusp of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), so the first sign opens the first house, and your chart starts exactly where the zodiac does.

Aries also rises fast. It needs about 52 minutes to clear the horizon at London, 58 at Paris and 71 at New York, against two hours for the average sign. That makes it the rising sign of only about 1 London birth in 28, the fewest of any sign, level with [Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/) just before it. A fast sign is an easy one to miss. A time rounded to the hour can be half an hour out, and at London half an hour carries the Ascendant more than halfway across Aries: behind the same round hour there can be Pisces rising, Aries rising or [Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/). With a sign this quick, I don't read the rising sign from a guessed time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). These are northern figures, too: signs that rise fast in the northern hemisphere rise slowly in the southern, and Aries is one of them.

## First impressions {#first-impressions}

People tend to meet your readiness before anything else. You're often the one who breaks a silence, asks the direct question or offers to go first, and newcomers read that as energy and frankness. The misreading, when there is one, is speed taken for pushing. Assertion isn't aggression, but for the first five minutes the two can look alike: you say what you want plainly, because hinting feels like wasted time. Given longer, people find that the frankness runs both ways: a straight answer doesn't put you off.

How far would I lean on this? Not far on its own: in [my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the rising sign, among the placements everyone reads first, held about a third of the time. So I take Aries rising as a sketch of your first minute, not a portrait, and I check it against the rest of your chart.

## Its ruler {#its-ruler}

Mars rules Aries, so [Mars](/astrology/mars/) is your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/), the planet that carries your Ascendant's story into the rest of the chart. Its sign shows how your drive works; its house, the part of life your effort keeps coming back to. Mars stays about six to seven weeks in a sign, so your birthday decides its sign and your birth time its house.

Where it lands changes the reading. In Aries, Mars is at home, and the starter's reflex runs straight through the chart. In Capricorn, its exaltation, the drive turns patient and well aimed: the sprint becomes a long climb. In Cancer, its fall, effort goes first into protecting people. In Libra, the sign on your Descendant, Mars is in detriment: your chart ruler sits in the sign of other people, and much of what you start, you start with someone or for them. None of these is a verdict: in a difficult sign, Mars works by another method, not a lesser one.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Close up, Aries rising tends to say it first: the feeling, the plan, the problem put on the table. You'd usually rather clear the air than sit in a silence, and you can be over a row before the person you had it with. That speed is the thing to watch. A partner may need a day where you needed a minute, and closeness grows when you wait for their yes as well as your own.

Your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), opposite, is in Libra: you're drawn to people who weigh both sides and want a partnership of equals ([Libra on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/)).

What you bring is the start, and a bond stays lively for you while there's still something to begin together: a plan, a trip, a room to repaint.
