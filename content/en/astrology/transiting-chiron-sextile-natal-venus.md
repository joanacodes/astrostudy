Chiron sextile your Venus is a small opening in the way you relate and enjoy. For about ten months, usually, a bond can find a fairer footing because someone, often you, says what would help, and an old sensitivity about being liked or chosen gets in the way a little less.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) values and chooses: whom you draw close, what pleases you, whether what you give comes back in kind. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks an old tenderness about being liked or chosen, and the practical wisdom you gained around it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, links your likings and that wisdom through an opening that someone has to act on. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the openings tend to arrive as small, specific invitations. A friendship could be more even, if you said once what you need from it. A group built around something you love turns out to be a place where your taste is shared rather than judged. Someone with a hurt like yours would value what you've learned about getting close to people again.

Discernment is part of Venus too, and the sextile helps you use it: to notice which bonds give and which only take, and to choose a little more deliberately where your affection goes. None of it happens unless you move first, and the season ends whether or not you did.

The opening sits in whichever house your birth time gives it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With Venus in the 9th, one of these sextiles comes while Chiron crosses your 11th ([Chiron transiting the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-11th-house/)): friends, groups and shared causes become the place where you can relate more freely. Born with [Venus sextile Chiron](/astrology/venus-sextile-chiron/), you already know how to make room for others around a difficulty; the transit asks you to make some for yourself.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Ten months is the usual length within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, and nearly two passes in three cross the exact sextile three times. Expect a three-week single crossing in about a third of cases and a five-crossing pass of some twenty-one months in about one in twenty. As Chiron speeds up through Gemini in the 2030s, gaining six or seven degrees a year, some degrees start to get the quick, single crossing.

Most lives hold three of these sextiles before 80, a few two or four. Beside this one, Chiron's conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-venus/)) brings the same sore spot right onto your Venus, the square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-venus/)) rubs it, the trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-venus/)) soothes it, and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-venus/)) shows it in a partner or a friend; the square and trine come as often as this sextile, the conjunction and opposition once or twice.

Chiron's years in Taurus, 2027 to 2033, bring this sextile to Venus in Cancer and in Pisces, the sign tradition gives Venus as its exaltation. From 2033 to 2039, in Gemini, it reaches Venus in Aries and in Leo.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask once, plainly, for the change that would make a friendship or a partnership fairer.

Join something built around a pleasure of yours, where taste is shared rather than judged.

Offer what you've learned about trusting people again to someone who is only starting to.
