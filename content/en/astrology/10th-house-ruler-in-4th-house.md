The top of your chart answers to a planet at the bottom of it: the 4th house holds the ruler of your 10th, and your public direction keeps drawing on home, family and the ground you stand on.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Two angles face each other across your chart: the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), which in Placidus, the house system I use, opens the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) of vocation, standing and reputation, and the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), which opens the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) of home, family, roots and the private self. Together they make the [meridian axis](/astrology/meridian-axis/), and with the ruler of one living in the other, the axis tells a single story ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

The work stands on private ground. It may grow out of the family, a trade handed down, a farm, a shop with your surname over the door, or happen at home, at the desk in the spare room. For others the link is less literal: a career whose real purpose is a secure base, or a public role that only makes sense once the home front is settled. Your parents' own careers are another matter, since the 4th shows home as you experienced it; what it describes is what you made of what you were given. Horary astrology’s lists give the 4th houses, land and parents, and the 10th authority, and some people with this placement end up working with property, buildings or land, or carry a parent’s trade into public life. Of the four angles, the 4th is the most hidden, so a vocation ruled from here can look quieter than it is: much of the work happens where nobody is watching. The pull runs both ways, and it needs a border: work that never leaves the house can end up taking it over.

The meridian has a catch. The Midheaven and the IC sit in opposite signs, so the 4th usually opens in the sign facing your Midheaven's, and a ruler early in the house is in the sign that faces its own across the zodiac: its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/). Saturn, ruling a Capricorn Midheaven, lands in Cancer; Mars, ruling an Aries one, in Libra. Here that tends to describe a vocation pursued in an unexpected manner, through care rather than command, or through diplomacy rather than force, and it often works better than the old books suggest.

Like the Midheaven, the IC turns with the clock, so half an hour moves it by several degrees ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and a ruler close to it may switch houses if the time is corrected even slightly. Later, it would sit in the 3rd, among the errands and conversations of daily work ([Ruler of the 10th in the 3rd house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)); earlier, it would be back in the 5th, where the work has to bring joy ([Ruler of the 10th in the 5th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)).

With Aquarius on the Midheaven, the 10th answers to Saturn and to Uranus ([Aquarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Saturn in the 4th, often in Leo across from its own sign, builds a career on a home that had to be earned ([Saturn in the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-in-4th-house/)). Uranus in the 4th keeps rearranging the base: the move, the home office, the family that does things its own way.
