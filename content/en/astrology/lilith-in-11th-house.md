Some people find their place in a group straight away. With Lilith in the 11th house, belonging is something you negotiate. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point of the Moon's orbit farthest from Earth, was in [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) when you were born, the house of friends, groups and the hopes you share with other people.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The 11th closes the run of succedent houses. Aquarius is its natural sign, and the house tradition named the Good Spirit was where Jupiter had its joy. It covers friendship, groups, networks, communities and causes, and what you hope for alongside other people. With Lilith here, the hidden part shows up among friends: a self that didn't fit the group you grew up in, an opinion kept back to stay included, a cause you cared about more than anyone around you did.

What this Lilith refuses is membership at the price of silence. It will join, contribute and organise, but not as one more voice agreeing with the loudest. Its boundary is belonging on your own terms: a group where you're there as yourself, or no group at all for a while.

Friends born the same week as you share your Lilith's sign, but rarely its house: one may have [Lilith in the 10th house](/astrology/lilith-in-10th-house/), another [Lilith in the 12th house](/astrology/lilith-in-12th-house/), depending on the hour each was born. That's why this placement needs [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/). I read houses in Placidus ([which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)).

My notebook moved from my own chart to friends' charts, with their permission, and Lilith came out as one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time ([how I tested](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)).

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Friendship may be where love starts for this Lilith, and where it's tested. It wants a partner who is a friend as well, with a circle of their own, and it's wary of relationships that swallow your social world or expect you to give up the people who knew you first.

The hidden part often keeps one or two friends who know the whole of you. A partner who welcomes them earns a great deal of trust. Love that began in a group — a club, a cause, a circle where you first met as equals — tends to suit this Lilith, because it started with you as yourself.

## At work {#at-work}

At work this placement shows in teams, networks and collective projects. You may be the one who asks what the group is actually for, or who notices who's been left out of the meeting. The hidden part refuses peer pressure dressed up as team spirit, and does its best work where people are allowed to disagree on the way to a shared goal. Associations, unions, collectives and online communities belong here too, and you may be the one drafting the rules for a group that has none yet.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

You may stay on the edge of every group, welcome everywhere and at home nowhere, because the edge felt safer than being turned away from the middle. Or you blend in so well that nobody, you included, can find the part that didn't fit. Old friendships can also hold you to a version of yourself you've outgrown. All of these cost the same thing: being among people without being known by them.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look for one group where the hidden part is useful: a cause, a craft, a circle of people who see something that matters the way you do. Let one or two friends see the whole of you, and notice who stays.

And give your hopes a place among other people's. The 11th is the house of what you wish for together, and this Lilith needs to wish out loud sometimes, not only alone.
