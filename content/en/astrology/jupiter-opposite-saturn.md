Jupiter opposite Saturn marks the halfway point of the pair's twenty-year cycle, and the people born around it carry Jupiter and Saturn at opposite ends of the zodiac. As a theme it belongs to a birth year; your houses show which two areas of your own life it stretches between.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the function of growth and meaning: learning, belief, a generous sense of what's possible. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the function of form: responsibility, boundaries, discipline, things made to last. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity: each end shows up the other, and the task is balance and integration rather than a winner.

In plain words, the wish to widen your life and the wish to secure it face each other. You may swing between saying yes to everything and pulling back hard, or meet the two ends in other people: the mentor who tells you to aim higher and the one who tells you to slow down, an institution's rules set against your own sense of what matters. That isn't a contradiction: both voices are yours, and the opposition asks you to hear them together.

Over time this tends to build a particular judgement: knowing when expansion is wisdom and when it is overreach, when a limit protects and when it only confines.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Counted within 6°, it belongs to about 1 person in 34 born from 1930 to 2009, which makes it, with the conjunction, the rarest of the pair's aspects. The births fall in four stretches: three spells between July 1930 and July 1931; two between March 1951 and March 1952; three between November 1969 and November 1971; and three between August 1989 and June 1991. Anyone born in those months shares it with their whole year group.

So I read it through the houses, which name the two areas it stretches between. With Jupiter in the 4th and Saturn in the 10th, home may be where you expand and work where you hold the line; with the two across the 5th and 11th, your own creative risks and the commitments of a group may keep asking to be weighed against each other. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and contacts with your personal planets make the axis louder ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)).

The pair's other aspects, around the same cycle:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-saturn/):** the meeting point of the cycle, about ten years before and after this one.
- **[Jupiter sextile Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-saturn/):** an opening between the two, for those born in 1943–44 or 1984–85, among others.
- **[Jupiter square Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-square-saturn/):** friction at the quarter points, about every ten years.
- **[Jupiter trine Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-trine-saturn/):** growth and structure keeping step, for the births of 1953–55 or 1992–94, for example.

[Jupiter opposite Saturn in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-saturn-synastry/) sets one person's sense of possibility opposite the other's sense of limits: a pairing of encourager and realist, at its best when the roles are allowed to swap.

## In transits {#in-transits}

With the opposition at birth, its transits keep to a schedule. Each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/), about every 12 years, brings Jupiter back to its birth place and so opposite your Saturn again: that is [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-saturn/), a matter of days or weeks. Saturn's version, [transiting Saturn opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-jupiter/), comes with the Saturn return at about 29½; and around 14 or 15, when Saturn opposes its own birth place, it crosses your Jupiter instead ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)).

Weather, not a forecast: the balance between growing and holding is simply asked for again, with no outcome written in.
