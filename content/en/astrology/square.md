The square is the aspect people fear most when they first read their chart, and the one I'd most like them to stop fearing. Two planets a quarter of the circle apart want different things at the same time: the friction is real, and so is what it builds.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

A square is an angle of 90°: three signs apart. Count three signs on from Aries and you reach Cancer; three back, Capricorn. A square always joins two signs of the same [modality](/astrology/three-modalities/), cardinal, fixed or mutable, but of elements that don't mix easily: it pairs a fire or air sign with an earth or water sign. The two share a way of moving (both start things, both hold on, or both adapt) and disagree about almost everything else. That's why a square feels like two needs on a collision course: each pulls its own way, at the same speed.

I read a square as effort that builds something, not as a verdict. It shows where you can't have both at once, so you learn to alternate, negotiate and choose, and that practice is where a lot of strength comes from. A square doesn't get solved once and for all; it keeps asking, and you get better at answering.

Astrologers allow a few degrees either side of exact, more with the Sun and Moon (see [orbs](/astrology/orbs/)), and many feel an [applying square](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/), still tightening, more keenly than one that's separating. Near the end of a sign, a square can fall between signs two or four apart, and then the modalities no longer match: an [out-of-sign aspect](/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/).

Among the five major aspects, the square is one of Ptolemy's four, with the [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), the [trine](/astrology/trine/) and the [opposition](/astrology/opposition/); the [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) is a meeting rather than one of his four. With the opposition, it's one of the two [hard aspects](/astrology/hard-and-soft-aspects/). The difference, as I read it: an opposition faces you from across the chart, often through other people; a square comes at you from the side, from inside your own life. When two planets in opposition both square a third, astrologers call it a [T-square](/astrology/t-square/); four planets linked by two oppositions and four squares make a [grand cross](/astrology/grand-cross/).

In the sky, the Sun and Moon are square at every [first quarter](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) and [last quarter](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) moon: born at a half moon, you were born under a Sun–Moon square.

## Between which planets {#between-which-planets}

The Sun never squares Mercury or Venus: Mercury never strays more than 28° from it, Venus never more than 47°, and the two never get far enough from each other to make a square between them. These are the squares people look up most:

- **[Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/):** born near a quarter moon; what you want and what you need keep asking you to choose.
- **[Sun square Saturn](/astrology/sun-square-saturn/):** confidence earned slowly, often against doubt; once built, hard to shake.
- **[Moon square Saturn](/astrology/moon-square-saturn/):** feelings that learned early to wait; a self-reliance that slowly learns to ask for comfort.
- **[Venus square Mars](/astrology/venus-square-mars/):** what you love and what you want don't always arrive together; chemistry with friction in it.
- **[Moon square Pluto](/astrology/moon-square-pluto/):** strong feelings and a need to keep hold of them; learning that closeness doesn't need control.
- **[Venus square Saturn](/astrology/venus-square-saturn/):** love taken seriously, sometimes held back; affection that grows with time.
- **[Mars square Saturn](/astrology/mars-square-saturn/):** the accelerator and the brake pressed together; endurance, once you learn which to use when.
- **[Mercury square Saturn](/astrology/mercury-square-saturn/):** a mind that doubts itself before it trusts itself; precise, in the end.
- **[Sun square Jupiter](/astrology/sun-square-jupiter/):** big appetites and big promises; a generosity that needs a sense of measure.
- **[Moon square Chiron](/astrology/moon-square-chiron/):** a tender place close to your need for comfort; often, deep empathy for others in the same spot.

Saturn turns up in five of those lines, and they're the squares I read most slowly. [My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) counted placements, each planet in its sign and house, so it has no figure for the squares themselves. But Saturn's placement was one of the three that held 75 to 90% of the time, and where Saturn squares something, I've learned to take my time.

## The square in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), a square means one person's planet sits 90° from the other's: what comes naturally to one rubs the other up the wrong way. Squares tend to be where two people argue, and also where they grow, because each keeps nudging the other out of habit. [One person's Sun square the other's Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon-synastry/) can feel like two rhythms that never quite sync; [Venus square Mars](/astrology/venus-square-mars-synastry/) is attraction with friction in it, the kind that can keep a bond alive or wear it thin. Between two charts, even the Sun and Mercury can square, which no single chart allows: that's [Sun square Mercury in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-mercury-synastry/). A square between you isn't a reason to leave. It's a place where the relationship asks for effort, from both sides, and I read it in both directions.

## The square in transits {#in-transits}

A square [transit](/astrology/transits/) is pressure: a planet in today's sky reaches 90° from one of yours and asks for an adjustment. The fast planets pass in hours or days; the slow ones stay for months, and can cross the same degree up to three times as they turn retrograde. [Transiting Saturn square your natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-sun/) often reads as a season of effort and limits; [Saturn square your natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-moon/), as a time when feelings need more structure; [Pluto square your natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-moon/), as a slow change in what you need to feel safe.

Some squares keep a timetable. Saturn squares its own place around 7, 21 to 22, 36 to 37 and 51 ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)); the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) comes at about 41; the [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/) comes at an age that depends on the year you were born. Weather, not a forecast: a square transit tells you where the pressure is, not what it will make of you.
