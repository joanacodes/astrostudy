Nine years after the North Node crosses your Venus, the South Node takes its place there. For about a month the tastes, loyalties and ways of pleasing you learned long ago step into the light, and a less familiar way of relating faces them from across the chart.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) measures worth and draws you towards what you like. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), now on it, is the pole of what is already consolidated: preferences and habits of pleasing practised so long that they run by themselves. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) stands opposite, and an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) makes each end visible through the other. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), your familiar ways of relating are what you get to see clearly.

Typical of this month: noticing that you always say yes first, always choose the same kind of person, always pay for harmony with your own preferences, or always keep a little distance to stay safe. None of these is a flaw; each was learned for good reasons. The month asks which still serve you. The less familiar way faces you across the chart, often in someone who relates quite differently.

With your [Venus in the 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house/), the South Node crosses your 7th and the North Node your 1st ([North Node transiting the 1st house](/astrology/north-node-transit-1st-house/)): a gift for partnership, real as it is, meets an invitation to put your own wishes on the table. No transit decides whether a relationship lasts, and I never read one for a parting. Which houses the axis crosses comes from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Between 1½° before exact and 1° after, the South Node holds your Venus for about a month as a rule. Some passes are brisk, about three and a half weeks, others slow, three and a half months or more, a tenth of each; roughly one pass in twenty-five brings the South Node over your Venus three times. The slow ones come in eclipse seasons. A Venus near 12° or 13° Cancer, for instance, has the South Node on it through the winter of 2028 to 2029, and the total lunar eclipse of 31 December 2028 sets the eclipsed Moon at 10°34′ Cancer, within the three degrees I count ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

This is the far point of the node's round over your Venus, about nine years from the conjunction ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-venus/)) on either side, with [Transiting North Node trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-venus/), [Transiting North Node square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-venus/) and [Transiting North Node sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-venus/) in between. Four or five times by 80, and nobody misses it. For anyone born with [Venus opposite North Node](/astrology/venus-opposite-north-node/), this is the picture of each nodal return, and the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/) brings the North Node onto Venus instead.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice the move you make first in any relationship, and try the opposite once: wait if you usually rush, speak if you usually wait.

Keep one loyalty on purpose rather than by habit. Choosing it again makes it yours.

Ask plainly for something you want, in a relationship where you are usually the one who gives.
