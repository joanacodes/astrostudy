Each round of Neptune through the zodiac starts in Aries, and a new one began in 2025. A composite Neptune in the first sign would give a relationship an ideal that wants to act. No adult couple has it yet: the people who will one day share it are babies, or not yet born.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) builds one chart for a relationship out of the midpoints of two birth charts, and reads it as a third thing that belongs to neither person. Its [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the bond's own ideal: what the pair imagines together, where its compassion goes, the picture of the relationship that neither person would have painted alone. [Aries](/astrology/aries/), cardinal fire ruled by [Mars](/astrology/mars/), would give that ideal a first move. Such a pair would dream by starting: a cause taken up the day it was heard of, a rescue attempted together, a life begun again somewhere new. Its tenderness would look like courage on the other's behalf.

That is a sky described, not a couple, because the dates don't yet allow one. Neptune spends about fourteen years in a sign, so two people of the same age share their generation's Neptune, and a gap in age moves the composite to a point between two generations. Neptune reached Aries in March 2025 and, after some months of crossing back to Pisces, stays until March 2039. In two million pairs drawn at random from the births of 1930 to 2009, not one had a composite Neptune in Aries. The pairs who will share it are two people born in those fourteen years, close in age, and the eldest of them turn eighteen in 2043.

Adults meet something else in these years: Neptune in Aries in the sky, crossing their own charts by transit ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)), a passing season rather than a placement two people share. The children born with [Neptune in Aries](/astrology/neptune-in-aries/) appear on this site only as a generation, because I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/). When the first of them are grown and choose to look at a relationship this way, Mars will deserve as much attention as Neptune: in a composite, the sign and house of the ruler of Aries show how a pair turns what it dreams into what it does.

The neighbouring signs hold no adults either. [Composite Neptune in Pisces](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-pisces/) belongs to two people born from 2011 to 2026, children today as well, and [composite Neptune in Taurus](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-taurus/) to pairs born from 2038. The last couples with a composite Neptune in Aries were born between April 1861 and April 1875, during Neptune's previous stay in the sign.

On paper, the shadow of this ideal is haste: a bond so taken with beginnings that the thrill of starting stands in for the thing started, or that feels most alive while rescuing something. Whoever has it one day will also have two birth charts and a [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) to read it with, and the composite will wait, as it always should, for two people who have both said yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)). I describe the technique on this site without drawing composites myself; this page describes it for a generation that will be adult in the 2040s.
