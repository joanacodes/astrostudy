The two rulers of Aquarius, the traditional and the modern, meet in your chart when Saturn crosses the degree your Uranus held at birth: first at an age your birth year decides, then every 29½ years or so. They meet on the spot where you most need room to be different, and for some months freedom is asked to take a form that can stand up.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and reforms: it's where you need autonomy, where you experiment, where you refuse to repeat what was handed down. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds what lasts. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into one process, so for a while change and structure have to work together instead of taking turns.

Each has a blind spot the other corrects. Uranus can change a system before understanding what still works in it; Saturn can mistake flexibility for irresponsibility. Under this conjunction an idea you've carried for years may get its framework: a project formalised, independence given rules you chose, an unusual path made sustainable. It can also feel like freedom meeting a rule, a duty or a timetable you can't wish away. Autonomy isn't isolation, and the conjunction tends to reward the reformer who works with a structure over the rebel who only pushes against one.

Aquarius has two rulers, Saturn in tradition and Uranus in modern tropical astrology; I read both, and this transit puts the two on the same degree of your chart.

Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, so your natal Uranus is shared with everyone born within a few years of you, and its house is what makes the transit yours. With Uranus in the 11th, the conjunction comes as Saturn crosses that house ([Saturn transiting the 11th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-11th-house/)), and friendships, groups and causes that stand for change get a structure, or ask you to commit. No birth time, no reading: your 11th house can't be drawn without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Saturn sets the pace here: with my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, it usually takes about nine months to cross your Uranus. In a little over half of its [transits](/astrology/transits/) it passes the exact degree three times, over about nine and a half months, which gives a reform time to be tried, doubted and confirmed; a single crossing at speed can take three weeks.

Natal Saturn and natal Uranus are both shared with people born close to you, so the first conjunction comes at nearly the same age for everyone born in the same months. It came at 27.4 for someone born on 1 January 1945, 20.6 for 1955, 13.9 for 1965, 7.9 for 1975 and 2 for 1985, then 25.1 for 1995 and 18.2 for 2005: the later the birth after a Saturn–Uranus meeting, the sooner it came, until the next meeting started the count again. The whole range, for births from 1930 to 2009, runs from the first weeks of life to 29, about 16 in the middle; then the conjunction returns once a round, two or three times by 80. Born in one of the meeting years, with [Saturn conjunct Uranus](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-uranus/) in your chart, you have this transit built into your Saturn return.

After it, Saturn's round with your Uranus moves on to [transiting Saturn sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Saturn square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-uranus/), then [transiting Saturn trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-uranus/) and, at the halfway mark, [transiting Saturn opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-uranus/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the change a structure. Write down what freedom would look like in practice, then build the first piece of it.

Keep what still works. Before you reform a system, a routine or a relationship, find the parts worth saving.

Choose your own rules. Under this conjunction, discipline you designed yourself sits far better than discipline handed to you.
