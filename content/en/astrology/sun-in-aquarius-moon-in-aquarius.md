The Sun and Moon shared Aquarius when you were born, two or three days at most from their monthly meeting at the new moon. Your aims and your comforts come from the same place: a principle, a circle of friends, the sense that tomorrow could be arranged better than today.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

With both lights in fixed air there is no clash of element or mode to negotiate, and the [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) and the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) reinforce each other. The Sun builds an identity out of convictions and the people who share them; the Moon steadies itself through perspective, and a feeling makes sense to it once it can be seen in context, as part of a larger pattern. You may be the most consistent person in your circle, loyal to friends and principles for decades, and still find what you need hard to name until someone asks.

Aquarius has a modern ruler, [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), and a traditional one, [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), and here both speak for both lights. I read both, as a pair with different jobs: Uranus marks the places where you experiment and break with habit, Saturn the places where you commit and keep building. With no other ruler in play, their houses and aspects carry much of the chart's character. The Sun is in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Aquarius, which I read as a self more at ease in a network than at its centre; the Moon has no dignity here.

The Moon was anywhere from 28° behind the Sun to 30° ahead of it, so your birthday lay within about two and a half days of a [new moon in Aquarius](/astrology/new-moon-in-aquarius/), on one side or the other. These births split evenly: those just before belong to the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), the last phase of a cycle, and those just after to the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase, the first. The hour decides which, and close to the exact new moon the lights form a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows what separates the end of one month from the start of the next.

Double Aquarius births come to about 1 in 151. Aquarius keeps the Moon for only about two and a half days, so a birthday on which it entered from Capricorn or left for Pisces could just as well be the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-capricorn/) or the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-pisces/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With the lights this close, they usually share a house or sit in two neighbouring ones, and only the time shows which part of your life holds this concentration.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Friendship comes first for you, and it remains the ground of love. You need a partner who respects your independence, your opinions and your time with other people, and who doesn't read your calm as indifference. What's harder is the particular: saying *I want you here tonight* rather than *we should spend more time together*. Practise the first kind of sentence; it lets people find you.

## At work {#at-work}

Collaborative, idea-led work suits you: research, technology, design for many users, community or campaign work, any team that is trying something new together. You keep your principles under pressure, and you think about the people a decision will affect years from now. Workplaces that demand you fit a mould, or run on unspoken favours, drain you fast.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With no second sign in the pair to pull against, the strengths and the habits come doubled. You can understand a feeling thoroughly and still not let yourself have it, and keep the group's needs ahead of your own until you've lost track of them. The rest of your chart supplies the counterweight, so I look there first: a planet in water or earth, the rising sign, the Moon's house. Day to day, something physical and unshared, a swim, a garden, a meal made for yourself alone, brings the feeling back into the body.
