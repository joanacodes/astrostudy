The first decan of Cancer begins at the June solstice and runs from 0° to 10° of the sign. The Moon rules it twice, by sign and by element, and Venus holds its face; and in the lists that fitted the Egyptian decans to the zodiac, this is where their count began.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

After the Sun's face at the end of Gemini, the Chaldean order goes on to [Venus](/astrology/venus/), so Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) open [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) with her. The modern decans give the first decan of a sign to its own ruler, and the ruler of Cancer is the [Moon](/astrology/moon/).

A planet in these degrees therefore takes its tone from the Moon first: Cancer's protectiveness and care, the need to feel held and to hold others. The face adds Venus's sense of what is worth keeping close. I look at your Moon's sign and house before anything else, then at your Venus.

The water triplicity complicates the picture, because the old authors disagree about it ([triplicity rulers](/astrology/triplicity-rulers/)). In Dorotheus's scheme Venus rules the water signs by day, so a daytime [Venus in Cancer](/astrology/venus-in-cancer/) between 0° and 10° holds triplicity and face together; Ptolemy and Lilly gave water to Mars by day and by night, and in their scheme only the face is left.

## Its image {#its-image}

Here the Egyptian count began. In the table of decans as later writers set it out, the first place goes to Sepṭet, as Budge transliterates it: Sopdet, the goddess of Sirius, whom the Greeks called Sothis. The star's rising before dawn marked the Egyptian new year and the flooding of the Nile. Firmicus Maternus kept the name, Sothis or Socius, and so did Joseph Scaliger; the Greek Hermetic list writes Sotheir, and the *Testament of Solomon* Metathiax ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

Later lists added figures of their own. Cosmas of Maiuma set Nike, victory, over these degrees, and Athanasius Kircher Apollun. The star that gave the decan its name has moved on, meanwhile: in the tropical zodiac Sirius now stands at 14° Cancer, in the next decan ([Cancer, second decan](/astrology/cancer-second-decan/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun is here for births from about 21 June to 1 July ([Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/)). In 2026 it reached 0° Cancer, the June solstice, on 21 June at 08:25 UT, and 10° on 1 July at 20:04 UT ([solstices and equinoxes](/astrology/solstices-and-equinoxes/)). A birth on 20 or 21 June may have the Sun at the end of Gemini instead ([Gemini, third decan](/astrology/gemini-third-decan/)).

From here on, seen from the northern hemisphere, the signs rise slowly. At the latitude of London these ten degrees take about three quarters of an hour to cross the horizon, more than twice as long as the first decan of Aries ([Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/)).

The fixed star [Menkalinan](/astrology/menkalinan/), β Aurigae, crossed into Cancer in 2006 and stands at 0°16′ in 2026, at the decan's very start. Uranus held these degrees for births from June 1949 to July 1951 and returns from 2032 to 2035; Saturn passed in 1944–45, 1973–74 and 2003–04. A slow planet here, like any placement, describes how something tends to work in you, never what will happen to you ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).
