Aquarius on the 6th house cusp gives the sign of independence the running of your ordinary days: the work you do, the routines that hold you up, the colleagues beside you. You work best when you can set your own method, and a day planned entirely by someone else tends to wear you out.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) covers the tasks rather than the title: routines, habits, the people you work alongside, the care you give your body. With [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) on its cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), you tend to want a say in how all of that is organised. Flexible hours, a desk you can move, a team where nobody pulls rank: these matter more to you than a fixed timetable. You're often the one who spots the clumsy process, proposes a better rota or builds the shared document everyone ends up using. Routines suit you once you've understood why they work, and you may redesign your week every few months to keep it working. Colleagues tend to become friends, or at least allies, and an office run on favouritism grates on you far more than hard work does.

Your daily work answers to Uranus and Saturn, and I read both. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) shows where routines get broken and rebuilt, sometimes overnight, and where technology changes how you work; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) shows where you build habits that last and carry responsibility for how things run. It's Saturn I'd look at hardest, since it was one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). Capricorn opens the 5th in nearly 9 charts in 10 with this cusp, so Saturn rules your pastimes as well as your workdays, and a craft you enjoy can turn into your job, or a job into a craft. Where each ruler stands is where your effort goes, as ruler of the 6th in the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Only the Descendant holds Aquarius more often: about 1 London birth in 9 and 1 New York birth in 10 have it on the 6th, with the Placidus houses I use. The western side of the chart works the other way round from the eastern: a sign sets in the time its opposite takes to rise, and Leo needs nearly three hours to rise at London, so Aquarius lingers on the cusps around the Descendant, and longer the further north. [Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/) comes with this cusp in more than 9 charts in 10, so the 6th's own natural sign is on your Ascendant and Mercury rules your chart, often your Midheaven too, through Gemini. Pisces then usually holds the Descendant, and in about 1 London chart in 3 the 8th cusp as well. In the few charts with Leo rising, Aquarius opens the 7th as well as the 6th ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), and the people you work with and the person you share your life with answer to the same two planets. And in about 1 chart in 7 drawn for London, Aquarius misses every cusp ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Bad Fortune was the 6th's old name, and the old books looked there for illness. I don't read illness in any chart, and a placement here is no symptom ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); what I read is how you organise your days and look after yourself. The cusp moves on with the clock: had you arrived later, the 5th cusp would hold Aquarius ([Aquarius on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-5th-house-cusp/)); earlier, the Descendant would ([Aquarius on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/)), and only the recorded time can say which ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
