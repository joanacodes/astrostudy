Neptune reaching the bottom of your chart turns the long years ahead towards home: the one you live in, the one you came from, and the one you keep imagining. Everything starts from your IC, and the stay stretches, in the middle, to nearly sixteen years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) longs and forgives: it reaches past what is towards what could be, and it softens hard edges. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) covers where you come from and where you retreat to: home, family, roots, belonging, the part of life the world doesn't see. With Neptune crossing it, home can become as much a longing as a place. You may want a refuge, a quieter house, or a sense of belonging no address quite gives; you may see your family's story with more compassion, or find that memories soften and shift as you look at them; boundaries at home, who carries what and for whom, can blur and need redrawing.

The first thing Neptune crosses is your Imum Coeli, the 4th's cusp in Placidus, my house system, and so the point opposite your Midheaven ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-midheaven/)): for a while the public side of life can matter less than finding where you belong. With Saturn in your 4th from birth, the pass can soften an old sense of duty towards family into something more forgiving.

Tradition read a parent in the 4th, and land, and houses; I read none of them as events. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) can't tell you whether you'll move or what will happen to anyone in your family. It brings out what your chart already holds about home and gives it years of imagination and compassion.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Few houses hold Neptune as long: nearly sixteen years in the middle for a London birth, in Placidus, close to the 3rd's figure and about two years above the all-house middle there. The 4th varies little in size, so the long end stops at nearly nineteen years, and the short end sits at about nine. A New York birth gives the same middle and a long end of nearly eighteen.

The stay begins on the first day Neptune touches your IC, even if a retrograde then takes it back into your 3rd for a season, and ends on its last day in the 4th. Your IC turns with the sky every few minutes, so these dates need your exact birth time, the reason I don't read a chart without one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Neptune comes to the IC from your 3rd, after years of imagination in words and learning ([Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/)); after the 4th it moves on to play, art and love in your 5th ([Neptune transiting the 5th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-5th-house/)).

If your Midheaven is in Libra, your IC is in Aries, and this is its time: Neptune is in Aries from January 2026 to March 2039, after first touching it in March 2025. A Virgo Midheaven goes with a Pisces IC, crossed between April 2011 and January 2026; a Scorpio Midheaven with a Taurus IC, reached from May 2038. Cancer, the 4th's own sign, lies well beyond these years.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make one place that restores you, however small: a corner, a garden, a ritual at the end of the day. Neptune in the 4th asks for a refuge, and a refuge doesn't need square metres.

Look at your family's story with compassion and with dates. Memory softens under this transit; old letters, photographs and conversations with relatives keep the kindness honest.

Redraw the boundaries at home before they blur on their own: who does what, whose room is whose, what you're willing to carry. Care lasts longer when it has edges.
