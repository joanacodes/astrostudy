With Aries on the 3rd house cusp, the first sign of the zodiac opens the house of the next street and the next sentence. The everyday mind, the conversations of an ordinary week, the brothers, sisters and neighbours you grew up among: all of it starts in a sign that likes to go first.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In the [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/), the sign on the cusp shows how your mind moves through an ordinary day ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Aries](/astrology/aries/) moves it head first. You tend to ask the direct question others circle round, to learn a skill by having a go before reading the instructions, and to say what you think while it's still warm. Messages get answered quickly; errands get done on the way to something else. The 3rd's natural sign is Gemini, which collects and compares. Aries prefers to test an idea by acting on it, so your learning is often hands-on and your opinions are ones you've tried out. If you grew up with brothers, sisters or cousins, the 3rd describes how those years felt to you, not who they are: with Aries here, often lively, sometimes a contest for the floor. Opposite, Libra opens your 9th, so the pattern often runs both ways: quick words about everyday things, slow and weighed ones about what you believe. What helps is the pause before you reply; the second question you ask is often the one that teaches you most.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules Aries, and so it rules your 3rd. One more link comes with this cusp wherever you were born: Scorpio sits on the Midheaven, so Mars, its traditional ruler, also shares the rule of your 10th house with Pluto, and I read both. Your words and your public work answer to the same planet, a link I look at closely when someone's work runs on talking, teaching or writing. What your words work for shows in the house Mars occupies: the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Counted over a whole day of births, Aries opens the 3rd for about 1 in 16 at London's latitude and 1 in 15 at New York's, in Placidus, the system I use. The two cities are closer here than on the cusps beside the Ascendant: the further a cusp lies from the horizon, the less latitude changes it. Capricorn usually rises with this cusp, so the measured manner people meet first tends to give way to plain speaking as soon as you start to talk. In these charts Aries opens the 3rd alone, never the 2nd or the 4th as well. Having no cusp at all is in fact commoner for Aries than opening the 3rd: about 1 London birth in 6 has it wholly inside a single house ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Your birth time put Aries here rather than one cusp away. [Aries on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-2nd-house-cusp/) turns the same push towards earning, [Aries on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/) towards home. With a time out by an hour or two, either could be yours instead, which is why I don't read a chart until the time is known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
