Some people make every belief feel like a question again, including the ones you didn't know you held. With their Lilith in your 9th house, the other person's refusal of handed-down truths enters the part of your life where you keep your convictions, your studies and your far horizons.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), as I read it, is what a person refused to give up when they were told to fit, and in the 9th that refusal tends to be about ideas: the creed, the party line, the "that's just how it is". [The 9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) holds what you believe and where those beliefs came from, long study, travel and other cultures, teaching and the search for meaning. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), it sets their unwillingness to be told what to think against your convictions.

With them, a belief you inherited may start to look like a choice you never made. They tend to ask the question nobody in your family asked, recommend the book you were warned off, take you somewhere you'd never have gone alone, and your quiet doubts finally have company. Natal [Lilith in the 9th house](/astrology/lilith-in-9th-house/) is a philosophy entirely one's own; here, theirs unsettles yours.

What your beliefs do to them depends on how you hold them. A faith or a philosophy you hold firmly may stir their old fight with whatever they were raised to believe; views you hold lightly may tell them, for once, that their doubts are welcome. Cadent, the 9th is where experience turns into understanding; [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), its natural sign, looks for meaning beyond the next hill, and the Sun, according to tradition, rejoiced in this house, which was called the God. Late in the house their Lilith nears your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), and what they stir in your beliefs starts to show in your public direction.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Questioning can turn into contempt. If they mock what you hold sacred, or treat your certainty as naivety, your whole view of life can feel under attack, and arguments about religion, politics or how to live may run late into the night. Between a teacher and a student, the student may refuse the syllabus, or the teacher refuse to be challenged.

From their side, your convictions can sound like the dogma they once escaped, and they may push back harder than the moment deserves. Change your mind too quickly to please them, and they may lose respect for the very openness they asked for.

No chart decides what is true, and this overlay crowns no winner: it shows where two people's beliefs rub.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree on what you can argue about and what you'll simply respect, and read each other's books before discussing them. A couple can travel together and still keep a day apart to explore alone; friends can keep a running disagreement as a pleasure rather than a battle; in teaching or research, the awkward question can be treated as data; adult relatives raised in one faith can let one of them leave it without leaving the family.

Turned the other way, your Lilith in their 9th house, it is your refusal of received ideas that meets their beliefs. The houses on either side tell other stories: [their Lilith in your 8th house](/astrology/lilith-in-8th-house-synastry/) is about trust and intimacy, [their Lilith in your 10th house](/astrology/lilith-in-10th-house-synastry/) about your public life.

If they were born on the other side of the world, that changes their houses, not where their Lilith lands in yours: mean Lilith, the one I read in both charts, is practically the same for everyone born on a given day, wherever they were born ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). In Placidus, the house system I use, your 9th closes at your Midheaven, which only your birth time can place, so I wait for both times before reading the pair ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Beliefs are personal ground, and this reading starts once both of you have asked for it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
