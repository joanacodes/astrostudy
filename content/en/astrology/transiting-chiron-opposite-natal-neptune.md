Neptune finds it easy to see the best in someone, and Chiron knows exactly where it hurts. Facing each other across the chart, the two often meet through another person: the one you would like to save, or the one you hope will save you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines, idealises and feels with others; its blind spot is mistaking empathy for accurate knowledge of another person. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) knows where a limit sits and how to work beside it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) you tend to become aware of one end by meeting it outside yourself.

You may be drawn to someone whose difficulty mirrors an old one of yours, and pour into them a compassion you have never turned on yourself. You may be the one cast as the healer, the wise friend, the person who understands, and feel the weight of being idealised. Or a group, a faith or a cause you believed in shows its limits just where you are tender. What the opposition asks for is differentiation: their hurt and yours, their hope and yours, held apart long enough to see each clearly.

Nothing here predicts that a relationship begins or ends, or that anyone gets rescued ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

Your Neptune's house and the house across from it, where Chiron is, show the two ends. With [Neptune in the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-in-11th-house/) and Chiron crossing your 5th, a cause or a circle you idealise can face an old ache about joy and self-expression ([Chiron transiting the 5th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-5th-house/)). Those houses come from your birth time, and so does my reading of the rest ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A planet conjunct your Neptune is opposed by Chiron as well, a planet opposite it has Chiron right on it, and one square to it sits at the point of a T. Born with [Neptune opposite Chiron](/astrology/neptune-opposite-chiron/), between 1988 and 1991, you started life with this transit in place.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the usual figure is about ten months and three exact hits; a third of all passes are single crossings of about three weeks, and one in twenty, five hits long, takes about twenty-one months.

One opposition comes in each of Chiron's rounds of about 50 years, so most people meet two by 80 and everyone meets at least one. Its first arrives at any age up to about 50: about 28 for people born in 1935, about 24 for 1945, about 20 for 1955, about 15½ for 1965, about 10 for 1975, about 3½ for 1985, about 46 for 1995 and about 38 for 2005. The 1985 and 1995 groups, born ten years apart, meet it some forty years apart in age: Chiron crossed Cancer around 1990, opposite the early-Capricorn Neptunes, before the later ones were born.

The Scorpio-Neptune generation, born between about 1956 and 1970, meets its second opposition while Chiron crosses Taurus, between 2026 and 2034, mostly in their sixties and early seventies. Their first came the last time Chiron crossed Taurus, in the late 1970s and early 1980s.

Seen from [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-neptune/), where the round begins, this is its far side. On the way to it come [transiting Chiron sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-neptune/), [transiting Chiron square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Chiron trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-neptune/), and the same three return in reverse on the way home.

## What helps {#what-helps}

When you feel the pull to rescue someone, ask what they have asked for. Compassion that waits to be invited lasts longer.

If you are the one being idealised, say what you don't know. It frees both of you.

Notice whose hurt you are tending, theirs or an old one of yours, and give yours some of the same care.
