Across a table, one of you is reaching for the shared plan and the other is keeping a hand on their own coat. Venus opposite Lilith in synastry sets one person's Venus about 180° from the other's Black Moon Lilith, and the pair tends to hold one question from its two ends: how close, and how free.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the function of liking and relating: attraction, preference, what someone finds beautiful, and the wish for an exchange that feels even. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), a point at the Moon's greatest distance from Earth, carries different meanings in different traditions; I read it as untamed instinct, autonomy, and what a person was taught to keep out of sight. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions at the two ends of a line, a polarity in which each makes the other easier to see. As in every one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), each end has its own person.

Set a Venus in Leo across from a Lilith in Aquarius, two fixed signs face to face, and a warm, generous, show-loving taste faces a cool refusal to be anyone's audience. The Venus person brings the party; the Lilith person stands by the window, and is often the one the Venus person most wants to talk to.

Between them, each gets an education in the other's end. The Venus person sees what autonomy looks like when nobody has apologised for it; the Lilith person sees what warmth looks like when it isn't a demand. Over time, each tends to borrow a little of what the other carries.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

From the Venus end, you may feel the other's independence as a verdict on your affection: you offer closeness and they keep a margin. It's tempting to try to win that margin over, with charm, gifts or patience, and to feel hurt when it stays.

From the Lilith end, the other's affection can feel like a slope towards being tamed. You enjoy it, and you watch it. Sometimes you refuse a kindness only because accepting it would mean giving ground, and later wonder why.

None of this is enmity. The seesaw sticks when the roles harden: one person always the warm one, the other always the one who won't. Then each forgets they carry a bit of the other's end too.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take turns at each end. The Venus person can practise wanting something on their own, with no one to please; the Lilith person can practise accepting something without counting what it costs. Between friends, that can be as small as one choosing the holiday and the other choosing to go along; in a family, the relative who keeps their own counsel being invited without being pressed; at work, the colleague who skips the social things having their reasons respected. Asking each other openly is part of it, which is why I only read this for two people who both want it ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Within 6°, the opposition turns up in about 1 pair of people in 15, one way round or the other, with mean Lilith: level with the conjunction, and less common than the square or the trine. The houses it falls in, which need both birth times, show what the seesaw is about: one person's Venus in the other's 7th house turns it towards partnership, in their 10th towards public life ([their Venus in your 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house-synastry/), [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The same opposition inside one chart is [Venus opposite Lilith](/astrology/venus-opposite-lilith/), where one person balances both ends alone. At the pair's other angles between two charts, [Venus conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) brings the two ends into one place, [Venus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-lilith-synastry/) makes them friendly neighbours, [Venus square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-lilith-synastry/) turns the seesaw into a corner that catches, and [Venus trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-lilith-synastry/) lets them share an element.
