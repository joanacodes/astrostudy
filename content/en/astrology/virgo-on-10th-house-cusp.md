What you're known for, the work you put your name to, the standing you build over years: the 10th house holds all of it, and its cusp is the Midheaven. Virgo on the 10th house cusp puts the sign of skill and service at that summit.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where your work meets the public eye ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and with [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) there, what the public sees is competence. I read the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) here as a reputation for doing things properly: the person trusted with the detail, asked to check, called when something has to work the first time. Usefulness is the vocation, whatever the job, and you tend to rise through expertise rather than display. Service is no small thing in this chart; done well, it becomes a form of authority. The risks are standards so high that nothing ever feels ready to show, a habit of staying the indispensable second rather than taking the lead, and criticism of your work cutting deeper than it should. Whatever field you work in, the thread is care and precision.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your 10th house from the sign where it is both at home and exalted, so by sign the ruler of your public life is at its most precise. Its house shows where your working life tends to centre: Mercury as ruler of the 10th can occupy the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). With [Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/) in about 5 London charts in 6, Mars and Pluto rule your chart, and I read both: a private intensity behind a public reputation for calm competence. Gemini, Mercury's other sign, opens the 8th in nearly 3 London charts in 4, so your work and what you manage or share with others answer to one planet.

A Virgo Midheaven turns up in about 1 chart in 13, at London and New York alike: the Midheaven's sign follows the time of day, whatever the latitude, and Placidus, which I use, takes it as the 10th cusp. That's a shade below the average of 1 in 12, because Virgo lies close to the September equinox point, where the zodiac passes the meridian quickly, in about 111 minutes a day for this sign. Pisces always holds the IC opposite, so Neptune and Jupiter rule your home ground ([Pisces on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/)). Virgo never opens the 9th or the 11th as well from here; in about a third of London charts the whole of Leo lies inside the 9th, giving the Sun a say in what you study and believe ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Across all births, Virgo has no cusp of its own in about 1 London chart in 6.

Near either end of the sign, a quarter of an hour can carry the Midheaven out of Virgo, and then the next page along applies: [Virgo on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-11th-house-cusp/) for a birth a little earlier, [Virgo on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-9th-house-cusp/) for one a little later.
