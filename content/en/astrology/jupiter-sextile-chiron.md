Of the five aspects Jupiter makes to Chiron, the sextile is the one most people have: about 1 in 12 in the count. It's also the most modest, a standing invitation to bring what you understand to the place that stays tender, and to let that place teach you something in return.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) stands for learning and perspective, the urge to find a framework that makes experience mean something. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where something stays sensitive, and where you develop your own ways of working with it: a changed method, a gentler pace, a tool or an adjustment, a skill that grew because it had to. The [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperation. It suggests; you decide.

In plain words, the right book, teacher or conversation tends to turn up when you go looking for help with the tender thing, and what you learn there often proves useful to someone else later. It isn't automatic. Opportunity asks for engagement, and you have to name the sore spot, at least to yourself, before the sextile can do its work.

Because it's light, this sextile suits people who don't want their tender spot to become their whole story. It lets the limitation and the skill sit side by side, without either one defining you.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In the count, 1 person in 12 or so among births from 1930 to 2009 has the sextile within 6°, more than for any other angle between the two. It was in orb for spells of a few months to most of a year, in 1930–31, 1941–42, 1949–50, 1959–60, 1964–65, 1973–74, 1977–78, 1987–88, 1993–94 and 2006–07, at uneven gaps, since Chiron speeds up and slows down along its orbit. Shared by everyone born in those windows, it reads as a year-group's theme until the houses come in.

Sextiles join elements that get along, air with fire and water with earth. The houses decide where the opening appears: Jupiter in the 11th sextile Chiron in the 1st might bring friends or groups who make your sensitivity easier to carry; Jupiter in the 9th sextile Chiron in the 7th, study or travel that changes how you meet partners. All of that needs a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects ask more of you, or less:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-chiron/):** meaning and tenderness in the same place, so teaching from experience becomes close to unavoidable.
- **[Jupiter square Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-square-chiron/):** where the sextile invites, the square presses, and hope has to adjust its pace.
- **[Jupiter trine Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-trine-chiron/):** the same help without having to ask, which makes it easier to take for granted.
- **[Jupiter opposite Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-chiron/):** the tender spot and the wide view face each other, usually through other people.

Between two charts, [Jupiter sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-chiron-synastry/) lets one person's encouragement meet the other's sensitive place gently, as an offer rather than a push. I read it from each side: offering help and accepting it are different arts.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-jupiter/) comes when Chiron, on its long round, reaches 60° from your Jupiter: a few times in a life, at ages that differ widely from one generation to the next because of its uneven speed. It lingers for months, sometimes in several passes. Jupiter returns the favour more often, sextiling your natal Chiron twice in each Jupiter cycle, a few weeks at a time.

Weather, not a forecast: both describe an opening around the tender spot, and leave you free to take it or not.
