Mars likes something to do, and a sextile from Jupiter supplies something worth doing. Twice in each round of twelve years, a project, a team or a challenge turns up that your energy could serve, and it waits to see whether you'll pick it up.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is agency: starting, pushing, competing, protecting what's yours. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the larger aim that makes effort mean something. At 60°, the two cooperate by [sextile](/astrology/sextile/): nothing forces the effort, but a good place to put it turns up.

The forms are practical. A colleague invites you onto a project with a real purpose; a training becomes possible again; a cause you believe in needs exactly your kind of push; a contest looks worth entering. It suits shared effort particularly: a team, a crew, a campaign, anything where your push joins other people's. Competition isn't hostility, and under this sextile a challenge can feel like an invitation. Mars is also the part of you that sets limits, and the angle makes a clean no easier as well, the kind that protects the effort you do choose.

From your 6th house, where tradition gave Mars its joy ([Jupiter transiting the 6th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-6th-house/)), the opening tends to show in daily work and practice: a skill you can train, a routine that gives your energy somewhere to go. Your Mars's own house says what the effort is for. Both houses depend on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and I don't read this [transit](/astrology/transits/) without them. Born with [Mars sextile Jupiter](/astrology/mars-sextile-jupiter/), you'll find each Jupiter return lays the same angle across your chart.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Most sextiles to Mars are over in about eleven days, measured from 1½° before exact to 1° after. Three times in ten or so the sextile coincides with Jupiter turning back, and then it holds for about eight months, exact three times: long enough to train for something, start it and see it through.

By 80, anyone born between 1930 and 2009 has had thirteen or fourteen chances like this to put their drive behind something. One arrives while the push of [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-mars/) is still warm and wants a practical channel; the other as the round winds down, when you know what your effort is worth and can offer it to others. [Transiting Jupiter square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-mars/) and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-mars/) ask more of your energy; [transiting Jupiter trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-mars/), like this sextile, makes it easier to spend well.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to the project with a purpose, and no to two that are only busy.

Find a partner in effort. The sextile works through cooperation, and Mars goes further with someone to keep pace with.

Begin within the eleven days. A sextile doesn't wait, and a first session, a first call or a first draft is enough to catch it.

Write down why you're doing it. Jupiter hands out reasons freely while the sextile lasts, and the reason is what keeps the effort going once it has passed.
