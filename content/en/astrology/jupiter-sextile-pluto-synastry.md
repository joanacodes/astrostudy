This contact doesn't announce itself; it waits for a conversation to go one level deeper. Jupiter sextile Pluto in synastry is an angle of about 60°, within 3°, from one person's Jupiter to the other's Pluto: an opening between widening a life and deepening it.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) sees potential: the constructive development a situation could allow, and the meaning that would make it worthwhile. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) sees what lies underneath: hidden motives, attachments and power patterns, and what has to be reorganised for something to stay viable. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them by invitation, a cooperation that needs someone to respond, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) someone else to offer it.

Here, Jupiter's growth offers Pluto's depth a way forward after a change, and Pluto offers Jupiter's plans an honest look at what they rest on.

Seen from Jupiter's side, the other person is someone whose seriousness gives your ideas weight. You may notice you think more truthfully around them, and that suggestions offered lightly get taken further than you expected.

Seen from Pluto's side, the other person offers room to grow after something has ended or changed in you. Their sense of possibility doesn't push; it waits until you're ready, which is exactly why it helps.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The sextile's gift is easy to miss. Nothing forces depth and growth to meet, and a pair can keep things pleasant and shallow for years, the Jupiter person skating over what matters, the Pluto person keeping it private. Pluto can confuse secrecy with depth, and Jupiter's opportunity is not guaranteed success, however good it sounds. Potential is not realisation; this one waits for a choice.

Other angles make the meeting stronger or harder. [Jupiter conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-pluto-synastry/) fuses the two into one intense process; [Jupiter square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-pluto-synastry/) sets widening against deepening; [Jupiter trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-trine-pluto-synastry/) lets them run together in one element; [Jupiter opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-pluto-synastry/) puts them face to face.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Choose to go one level deeper, on purpose and with permission. Ask the question underneath the polite one, and let the other decide whether to answer. Use the link for things that need both breadth and honesty: a career change talked through, a family story finally sorted out, a shared project that needs restructuring. It works between friends, partners, siblings and colleagues alike, as long as nobody is pushed.

Between June 1948 and September 1950, every birth has Pluto sextile the Jupiter of a mid-1965 birthday; in the other direction the windows are short, January to April 1994 among them. I keep contacts like this off my list of synastry aspects, a list that begins and ends with the personal bodies: the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant and the Midheaven. A sextile between two slow planets I read in the houses, as the meeting of two eras' ways of growing and renewing.

Pluto, found in 1930 and classed a dwarf planet in 2006, barely moves in a day, so the hour of birth leaves the sextile alone; it decides the houses, and I need both times before I read them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Whoever is born with [Jupiter sextile Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-pluto/) decides alone when growth and depth will cooperate. Shared between two, that decision needs both of them.
