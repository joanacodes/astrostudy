Neptune opposite your Mars puts your will at one end of the chart and someone else's dream, need or cause at the other. Slightly more than half of all people meet it, once, for about two and a half years; the rest never do.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is assertion and directed effort: what you want, how you go after it, where you draw the line. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is compassion, longing and the pull of something larger than yourself. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, each showing up the other, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your drive tends to meet its other side in people: a partner's vision, a friend's need, a cause that asks for your strength.

You may find yourself putting your energy behind someone else's dream, or facing a rival or a partner who is hard to pin down, so that you can't quite tell what you're pushing against. You may admire in others a courage you don't claim, or notice in them a confusion that is partly yours. Neptune has a blind spot that matters here: sensing what someone wants isn't the same as knowing it. The opposition asks you to look across and then ask, before you give your strength away or turn it against anyone.

It foretells no quarrel, no betrayal and no parting. It describes the meeting of your will with other people's ideals, for as long as Neptune stands there.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the point opposite your Mars always falls in the opposite house: with Mars in your 3rd, Neptune is crossing your 9th ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)), and a teacher's vision, a faith or a faraway place pulls on the effort you put into everyday life. Those two houses, like all twelve, hang on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Mars opposite Neptune](/astrology/mars-opposite-neptune/) in a birth chart makes this tension a lifelong companion; the transit lends it, once, to charts that were born without it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune stays within my orbs of the opposition, 1½° before exact and 1° after, for about two years and seven months, seldom fewer than twenty-two. Mars comes back to its birth degree about every two years, so your Mars return falls inside the pass at least once, and for those few days Mars in the sky faces Neptune across your chart: the whole transit in miniature. Neptune itself crosses the exact point three times in most passes and five in about one in four.

Before 80 it reaches a little more than half of the people born between 1930 and 2009, and none of them twice.

If you meet the opposition young, [transiting Neptune trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-mars/) follows about twenty-seven years later and [transiting Neptune square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-mars/) about forty-one; met late, those came first. [Transiting Neptune sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-mars/) lies about fifty-five years from it on either side, and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-mars/) at the far end of the circle.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask whose fight it is. Before you take up a cause or a quarrel, say plainly what you want from it yourself.

Lend your strength without handing it over. You can work for someone else's vision and still keep hold of your own aims, your time and your no.

Check what you sense against what is said. Under this opposition, intuition about other people runs ahead of the facts; a direct question brings them level.
