Some hurts are about meaning: the belief you were handed and couldn't keep, the question a teacher laughed at, the year abroad when nobody understood you. Chiron's years in your 9th house bring them back.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) points to the sore spot you keep returning to, and to what living with it teaches you. The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is the far horizon: what you believe and where those beliefs came from, long study, travel and other cultures, teaching and the search for meaning. Its old name was the God, and the Sun was said to rejoice there. During this stay, an old hurt in that territory can come back: a faith that was imposed on you, or one that was taken away, a course you left, a teacher who decided you weren't the studying kind, the loneliness of being foreign somewhere. This stay asks you to find answers of your own instead of borrowed ones, without requiring the old doubt to vanish first. The 9th is also the house of teaching, and Chiron is named for the centaur who taught; people often come out of this stay able to pass on what they had to work out alone, which is how [Chiron in the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-in-9th-house/) reads at birth.

A natal [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) in the 9th, ruler of [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), the house's natural sign, is reached on the way and asked which of its beliefs it actually chose.

Chiron here predicts no exam result, no move abroad and no loss of faith; a [transit](/astrology/transits/) works on how you hold your beliefs, not on events.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Across all twelve houses, Chiron's middle stay in Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude is about 4 years, with most between about 2 and 9. The 9th sits right on that middle, about 4 years, with a slightly longer tail, up to about 10, when the house falls in the signs Chiron crosses slowly. It is the only house where New York's latitude gives exactly the same three figures as London's.

The 9th's second cusp is your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), and like the first it comes from your birth time, so the dates of this stay depend on it, and so does any reading I give ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Ahead of it lies your public life, entered as Chiron crosses the Midheaven ([Chiron transiting the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-10th-house/)); behind it, the house of trust and shared things ([Chiron transiting the 8th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-8th-house/)).

Chiron is speeding up in the coming years, leaving the signs it crosses most slowly. It spends nine years in Aries, from April 2018 to April 2027; about eight in Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034; about six in Gemini, from July 2033 to April 2039; and it enters Cancer in July 2038, a sign it crossed in about three years last time round. A 9th cusp in one of those signs has its stay within those dates, and the degree tells you when.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down one belief you were given and one you found for yourself, and notice how differently you hold them.

Go back, as a beginner, to a subject you were told wasn't for you, with no one marking your work.

If you travel, go somewhere slowly enough to feel far from home, and let that be fine.

Teach something, even informally. Explaining what you had to learn the hard way is often where this hurt finds its use.
