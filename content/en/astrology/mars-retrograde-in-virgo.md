Mars’s longest retrogrades in the dates I use both reach Virgo: 81 days in 2012, and 81 again in 2027. They come between January and May, and they turn the planet of drive back over the place where effort is at its most careful.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) is mutable earth, ruled by Mercury: Mars there fights by fixing things, and spends its energy on the detail that makes work hold. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when the red planet seems from Earth to stall and drift back for close to twelve weeks, that care turns on itself. What slows is the method: the routine you built to stay on top of things, the standard you hold yourself to, the task you keep redoing because it never feels finished. Frustration with things done badly is one shape anger takes here, and these weeks can show whether the complaint is about the task or about something else. Desire, in Virgo, wants to be of use; the review asks to whom.

The 2012 loop stayed inside Virgo, from 23°05′ on 24 January to 3°40′ on 14 April, with Mars opposite the Sun, then in Pisces, in early March. The next two follow each other closely. From 10 January to 1 April 2027, Mars stations at 10°25′ Virgo and backs into Leo ([Mars retrograde in Leo](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-leo/)), turning direct at 20°55′. From 14 February to 5 May 2029, it stations at 13°55′ Libra ([Mars retrograde in Libra](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-libra/)) and comes back to 24°55′ Virgo. So the first degrees of Virgo meet the 2027 loop, and the last ones the 2029 loop.

Being born with Mars retrograde in Virgo calls for a different reading ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)): the placement lasts a lifetime, the season a few months.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Virgo occupies comes from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); without it I can’t place these months in your life, and I don’t read the chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2027 loop runs from the Virgo house back into the Leo one, and the 2029 loop from the Libra house into the Virgo one.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/virgo-rising/):** Virgo rising: a careful, capable first impression; the urge to fix whatever you walk into, eased.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/virgo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** the skills you rely on; effort poured into being useful, measured against the self-respect it gives you.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/virgo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** precise words and daily errands; a correction you made to a sibling or neighbour, reconsidered.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home kept in order; the household system you run alone, shared out or simplified.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/virgo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** crafts and creative work done with care; the piece you never finish because it isn’t perfect, finished as it is.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/virgo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** Virgo’s natural house and Mars’s joy, which tradition also tied to illness, never a health forecast in my reading ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); the working habits you defend, adjusted.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Pisces rising puts Virgo on the Descendant: a partner who notices what needs mending; who fixes what for whom, talked through.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/virgo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** trust and what is shared, handled with care; a need for control in closeness, loosened.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/virgo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** study and method; a belief you defended point by point, reread as a whole.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Virgo on the Midheaven: a reputation for competence; the extra hours behind it, counted honestly.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/virgo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** groups you organise; the work you take on for friends, handed round.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/virgo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private routines and things mended in secret; the self-reproach you never voice, looked at kindly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Virgo improves things a little at a time, and these weeks suit that pace: one drawer, one process, one honest conversation about how the work is shared. Mercury rules Virgo, so the house of your natal Mercury shows where the review gets thought through, and your natal Mars where the drive begins. What I hope for in these months is fewer tasks, not more polished ones.

None of this predicts a mistake or a success at work. It describes the longest of Mars’s backward seasons, spent in the sign that likes to check its own work.
