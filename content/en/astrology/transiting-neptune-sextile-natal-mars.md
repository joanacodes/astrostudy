Most sextiles wait for you to act. This one is unusual, because the planet it touches is the one that acts: for about two and a half years, Neptune's imagination stands ready to give your drive a direction, and Mars is built to take the first step.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) starts things: it finds the will to begin, the courage to keep going and the energy to defend what matters. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings inspiration, compassion and a sense of something larger than the task. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates two functions through a cooperation that depends on taking part, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) taking part is precisely what Mars is for.

It often shows as an inspiration that turns practical. A story you've carried becomes a first draft; a vague wish to help becomes a few hours a week somewhere they count; a practice that joins body and imagination, such as dance, swimming, music or a martial art, starts to mean more than exercise. Effort comes more willingly when it serves something you care about, and less when it only serves a target. Neptune adds the reason and Mars the motion, and each keeps its own part.

I read none of it as a promise of success, or as a sign of what will become of a project: the sextile opens a way in, and what lies behind it is ordinary work.

With Mars in your 9th, the sextile often comes while Neptune crosses your 11th or your 7th: a group, a cause or a friend's idealism gives your convictions something to do ([Neptune transiting the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-11th-house/)). The 9th, the 11th and every other house are drawn from your birth time; without it, the chart stays closed ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

With [Mars sextile Neptune](/astrology/mars-sextile-neptune/) in a birth chart, this possibility lasts a lifetime; as a transit it lasts a season, and seasons end.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune holds the sextile within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, for about two years and seven months and rarely less than twenty-two: plenty of time for an idea acted on in the first months to have become a habit by the last. I watch the exact contacts, three in most passes and five in about one in four, as the moments when the idea asks for its next step.

The age it comes at says nothing about your generation. Mars crosses a sign in six or seven weeks when it moves direct, so two people born a season apart can have Mars two signs apart and meet this sextile some twenty-seven years apart. Before 80 it comes once to most people born between 1930 and 2009, twice to some, and never to about 1 in 6.

Whichever of your two sextiles it is, [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-mars/) and [transiting Neptune trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-mars/) lie about twenty-seven years from it, [transiting Neptune square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-mars/) about fourteen, and [transiting Neptune opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-mars/) about fifty-five.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take the first step within a month. A sextile from Neptune rewards an early, small commitment more than a perfect plan made later.

Tie the effort to a person or a purpose. Under this transit you'll keep going for what you care about long after you'd have stopped for a target.

Let the practice be physical as well as inspired. Rehearsals, training, drafts and repairs give the vision somewhere to land.
