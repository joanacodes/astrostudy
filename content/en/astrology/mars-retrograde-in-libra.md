Mars turns back in Libra in spring, and only twice in the dates I use: from March to May 2014, and from February to May 2029. Halfway through each, the Sun stands in Aries, Mars’s own sign, while Mars backs through the sign opposite: the planet of self-assertion sees the world for a while through the sign of the other person.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Libra](/astrology/libra/) is cardinal air, ruled by Venus, and Mars is in detriment there: the urge to act alone meets a sign that weighs both sides before it moves. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when Mars seems from Earth to halt and back up for eleven weeks or so, that negotiation turns inward. What slows is the bargain: the compromise you accepted to avoid a scene, the fairness you fought for on someone else’s behalf, the anger you smoothed over so well that nobody saw it. Desire here is bound up with another person, and these weeks ask what you want when nobody else’s wishes are in the room.

The 2014 loop stayed in Libra, from 27°32′ on 1 March to 9°01′ on 20 May: 79 days over about 18½°. The 2029 loop begins at 13°55′ Libra on 14 February and backs out into Virgo ([Mars retrograde in Virgo](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-virgo/)), turning direct at 24°55′ Virgo on 5 May. Libra’s last degrees, past 27°32′, meet no Mars retrograde in these years: the nearest, in 2031, keeps to Scorpio ([Mars retrograde in Scorpio](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-scorpio/)), between 21°38′ and 4°26′.

Mars already retrograde in Libra at birth is a placement with its own reading ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)), not this spring weather.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Where Libra falls among your houses depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and without it I don’t read a chart at all. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2029 loop crosses from the house Libra occupies into the Virgo one.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/libra-rising/):** Libra rising: a gracious, agreeable first impression; the effort it takes to keep everyone comfortable, noticed at last.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/libra-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you value, often shared with someone; effort spent keeping things even, weighed against what you need.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/libra-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** tactful words; a disagreement with a sibling or neighbour you let pass, raised gently.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/libra-on-4th-house-cusp/):** peace at home; a household compromise only one person keeps, renegotiated.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/libra-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romance and art; a rivalry in love or in creative work, judged more fairly.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/libra-on-6th-house-cusp/):** working relationships; the colleague you always accommodate, met halfway instead.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Libra’s natural house: a partnership where you keep the scales level; who asserts and who yields, rebalanced.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/libra-on-8th-house-cusp/):** intimacy and what is shared; a desire left unspoken to keep things smooth, voiced.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/libra-on-9th-house-cusp/):** ideals of justice; a principle you argued for, examined from the other side.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Libra on the Midheaven: a public role as the one who mediates; the stand you avoided taking at work, considered.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/libra-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friendships and alliances; the peace you maintain in a group, and what it costs you.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/libra-on-12th-house-cusp/):** anger kept so polite it disappears; the resentment under the courtesy, looked at alone.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Libra weighs, so I find these weeks go better with one plain statement of what you want, made before you start adjusting it for everyone else. Venus rules Libra, and the house of your natal Venus shows where harmony pulls the review; your natal Mars shows where the drive starts.

Disagreements that come back aren’t the sky’s doing, and no Mars retrograde decides who is right, in a couple or in court ([Can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)). It describes a spring in which fairness includes you.
