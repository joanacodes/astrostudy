Your Moon sits in the last sign of the zodiac and your Sun in the first: one sign apart, with the whole round of the signs between them the long way. The month was nearly over when you arrived, the Moon a thin crescent rising just before the Sun or already lost in its glare, and I read the pair as a fresh start that carries what came before.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aries is cardinal fire, Pisces mutable water: side by side in the zodiac, and with nothing in common but the border between them. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) draws a clear line and sets off; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) takes in the moods around it and needs quiet to find its own again. Here the Moon is a step behind, still in the sign where the zodiac ends, while purpose has already begun the next round.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules Aries. For Pisces I read both rulers: the traditional one, [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), shows the beliefs and wider horizons that give your feelings a frame, and the modern one, [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the place where your edges are thinnest. The Sun keeps its exaltation; the Moon has neither dignity nor debility in Pisces, which is also the sign where tradition exalts Venus, and that suits a Moon this tender.

More often than not, this is a balsamic birth. The Moon trailed the Sun by 1° to 59°, which means within five days before a new moon: the last crescent at dawn, or the dark of the moon itself. Seven times in eight the phase is the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/); the pair's earliest births still belong to the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/). With the Sun in the first degrees of Aries and the Moon in the last of Pisces the two can even be conjunct across the border, an [out-of-sign aspect](/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) covers the eighth phase and the one before it.

The pair turns up in about 1 person in 146. Because the Moon switches sign roughly every sixty hours, a birthday on which it went from Aquarius into Pisces, or from Pisces into Aries, has two possible pages, and only the time picks between this one and the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-aquarius/) or the double Aries of the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-aries/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign depends on it too, and with the two lights it makes the chart's first outline; without the time there's no reading at all, only a guess about which page is yours.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with initiative and with great tenderness. The Aries Sun makes the move; the Pisces Moon senses what a partner needs, often before they've said it. The risk is soaking up a partner's moods and then acting on them, fast, as if they were your own, which can surprise you both. Before you act on a strong feeling, check where it came from. A beat between sensing and doing protects the bond, and you.

## At work {#at-work}

You can start things out of compassion: the initiative that helps someone, the project nobody else had the heart to begin. The Sun brings the push and the Moon the imagination and empathy, so work with a human or creative purpose tends to suit. In a noisy, cut-throat workplace the Aries Sun may thrive for a while; the Pisces Moon pays for it later, in a weariness that takes days to lift. Schedule the quiet part of the job as firmly as the busy part; it's where your best ideas surface.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Hard edges meet soft ones. The Aries Sun goes straight at the world; the Pisces Moon has few walls and takes in whatever it meets. You can charge into situations that then flood you, or swing from fierce independence to exhaustion once everyone else's needs have soaked in. What helps is a rhythm of going out and coming back: action, then a deliberate retreat, so that the Sun sets off again carrying its own feelings rather than everyone else's.
