In Hesiod's genealogy, Uranus, the sky, is the father of Cronus, and Cronus in turn the father of Chiron. The two bodies carry names from the same family tree, and when Chiron in the sky reaches your natal Uranus, an old sore spot meets the part of you that insists on being itself.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates: it frees what has grown too tight, tries other arrangements and lets you be the one who does it differently. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is a limitation that persists and the adapting that keeps you taking part. Uranus reconfigures systems, Chiron adapts within realities, and a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes the two act as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your difference and your difficulty share a degree.

Often the sore spot is difference itself: being the odd one out at school, the person whose body, mind or history didn't match what was expected, the one who learned to hide the unusual part. The conjunction tends to bring a freer way of living with that. Some people find a tool, a technique or a community that turns the difficulty into something they handle in their own fashion; others stop apologising for the work-around they've always used. Uranus's risk is changing everything before seeing what still works, and Chiron's is building an identity on the limit. Together they can make something original that keeps the parts that help.

A whole generation shares Uranus's sign, since it stays about seven years in each, and its degree is close for people born a year or two apart, so this conjunction comes to them together. That makes it a generation's season. In one chart, the house it lands in, the personal planets and angles Uranus touches and the rest of the year's transits are what turn it into yours.

With [Uranus in the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-in-3rd-house/), the conjunction comes while Chiron crosses that house ([Chiron transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-3rd-house/)): the way you think, talk and learn, and an old hurt about not being understood, find a new voice. Only your birth time can place that house, and I don't read the conjunction without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). No birth between 1930 and 2009 has [Uranus conjunct Chiron](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-chiron/) in the chart itself; the meeting only happens by transit.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Change made your own way tends to come in steps, and so does this conjunction. Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, a typical pass takes about ten months, and nearly two in three see Chiron cross your Uranus three times. A third or so of passes are single crossings, done in some three weeks, and about one in twenty loops five times through some twenty-one months.

The age falls steadily with the birth year. In the site's count, someone born at the start of 1935 or 1945 met it at about 40, 1955 at 36, 1965 at 30, 1975 at 22, 1985 at 15, 1995 at 9 and 2005 at 6. Chiron was racing then: it crossed the six signs from Leo to Capricorn between 1991 and 2005, so people born over fifty years, from 1955 to 2005, met this conjunction within about twenty, from 1991 to 2011. By 80 most people meet it twice, the second about 50 years after the first, and everyone once. The round goes on through [transiting Chiron sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-uranus/), [transiting Chiron square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-uranus/), [transiting Chiron trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-uranus/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Try the unconventional fix you've been too embarrassed to try.

Stop explaining the work-around. If it works for you, it works.

Find the people who share your difference, online or in a room, and compare methods.

Before changing everything, list what already helps, and keep it.
