Pisces is Neptune's own sign, where edges soften and everything seems connected. A composite Neptune there would make a relationship's ideal one of oneness; no adult couple can have it yet, because the pairs who will share it were born between 2011 and 2026.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

A [composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is two birth charts merged into one, each planet at its midpoint, read as the chart of the relationship rather than of either person. In it, [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where the bond dreams: its shared ideal, its compassion, the place where the two people's outlines grow faint. In [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), mutable water, Neptune would be at home, and the composite's own idea, two lives made into one picture, would be at its fullest. A pair with this Neptune would dream of a love without walls: music or art made together, a shared faith or practice, forgiveness given easily, a tenderness that includes strangers, animals and anyone who is hurting. Its imagination would trust music, symbols and dreams more than arguments, and its faith would be a feeling before it was a creed.

Pisces has two rulers: Neptune itself in modern tropical astrology, and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) in the tradition, which knew Pisces long before Neptune was found in 1846. I read both. In a composite, this Neptune would be both the planet and the ruler of its sign, so nothing would slow the ideal down; composite Jupiter, the older ruler, would show where the bond's generosity takes a shape you can see.

Here the dates speak plainly. Neptune was in Pisces from April 2011 to January 2026, so the people born with it are children today, and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/). Two of them, close in age, will one day share this composite; the eldest turn eighteen in 2029. Every adult alive was born with Neptune somewhere before Pisces, so no midpoint of two adults' Neptunes reaches the sign: in two million pairs drawn at random from the births of 1930 to 2009, there was none. The last couples to share it were born during Neptune's previous stay in Pisces, from April 1847 to February 1862.

In one birth chart, [Neptune in Pisces](/astrology/neptune-in-pisces/) is read on this site as a generation's placement, never a child's. On either side, [composite Neptune in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-aquarius/) belongs to today's youngest adult couples, and [composite Neptune in Aries](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-aries/) to pairs born from 2025.

In principle, the shadow of this ideal is a bond with no edges at all: two people so merged that neither can say where one ends and the other begins, or a compassion so wide that it forgets the two people it started from. Neptune at its best stays open without losing the ability to tell self from other, and fact from hope. Composites are a technique I set out here without using it, and in Pisces it will have adult couples to describe from 2029, each to be read only if both partners agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
