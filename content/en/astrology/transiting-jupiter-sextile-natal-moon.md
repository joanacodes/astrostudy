The sextile is the lightest touch Jupiter makes to your Moon, and it comes twice a round, two years after each crossing and two years before the next. Nothing about it insists: it offers your feelings a little more room, and waits.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens and looks for meaning; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) tends needs and attachments, and gives you the feeling of being safe. At 60°, a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) links them through opportunity. On the Moon's side that means responding, and responding isn't the same as waiting: the openings of this [transit](/astrology/transits/) are real, and small enough to miss.

They tend to arrive close to home. Someone offers help you'd usually refuse; a circle of people makes room for you; a routine or a ritual becomes more yours; a conversation settles a worry you'd been carrying alone. Saying yes costs little, and it's often the start of a steadier way of looking after yourself. The Moon also keeps memory, and a sextile from Jupiter can bring something good from earlier years back into use: a recipe, a song, a friendship you'd let drift.

The house Jupiter is crossing says where the opening comes from. From your 3rd, where the Moon has its joy in the old scheme of houses, it tends to come through words, siblings, neighbours and the short trips of ordinary weeks ([Jupiter transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-3rd-house/)). Born with [Moon sextile Jupiter](/astrology/moon-sextile-jupiter/), you'll know this rhythm already: one of the two sextiles comes back with each Jupiter return.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, which open 1½° before exact and close 1° after, the sextile usually lasts about eleven days. In roughly three passes out of ten, Jupiter turns back near the point and touches it three times, and the sextile then lasts about eight months.

Births on the same day, even in the same town, can meet this sextile months apart. The Moon moves about 13° in a day, and Jupiter takes around five months, on average, to cover that much sky, so the hour of birth decides the season of the transit, not only its house. That alone would stop me reading a chart without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Thirteen or fourteen of them fall before 80 for anyone born from 1930 to 2009. One arrives two years after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-moon/), as the feelings that crossing stirred start to settle into habits; the other two years before the next crossing, as if to make room for it. Of Jupiter's five contacts with your Moon, this one asks least: [transiting Jupiter square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-moon/) crowds your space, [transiting Jupiter trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-moon/) eases it, and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-moon/) stretches it between home and elsewhere.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Accept the help that's offered this time. If you usually answer “I'm fine”, try saying what would make the week easier.

Do one thing that makes home more yours: move the furniture, invite the people you miss, take up a ritual you'd let lapse.

Keep it modest. A sextile offers a little more room, not a new life, and the little is easy to lose to a busy week.
