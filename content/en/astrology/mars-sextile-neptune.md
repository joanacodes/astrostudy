Mars sextile Neptune is a door between doing and imagining. The two planets sit two signs apart, on good terms, and when you open the door, your effort can follow an intuition, a picture in your mind, or a feeling for someone else.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the function of initiative: you assert, compete, make the effort and keep a boundary. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and connects: inspiration, compassion, symbolic perception, a feeling for what lies beyond the obvious. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity: each stays itself, and the angle offers a way through from one to the other.

In plain words, you can let imagination steer your effort without losing your footing. A hunch can become a plan; sympathy for someone can become practical help; a picture in your head can become something made. The sextile asks you to choose that link, though. Left alone, it stays a possibility, and people with it often discover it only when a cause or a creative project gives them a reason to act.

It also softens assertion in a useful way. You can stand up for something without needing to win at someone else's expense.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The element shapes how the link works: in water and earth, imagination tends to become care and craft; in fire and air, enthusiasm and ideas acted on. Mars in Capricorn, its exaltation, sextile Neptune in Pisces, Neptune's modern sign, gives a dream a practical engine. The houses show the field: Mars in the 9th sextile Neptune in the 11th, say, might put real effort into a shared ideal, or a group that dreams big.

About 1 person in 17 has it within 6°, a little fewer than have the pair's square or trine. Close to exact, inspired effort is a recognisable part of you; at the edge of the orb, it's a resource you tend to find when you need it.

Mars makes the sextile with Neptune twice in each cycle of about two years, for a few weeks of births each time, while Neptune spends some fourteen years in a sign. When I read Neptune, I describe what it opens rather than what it might cloud, and a sextile gives me plenty to describe ([How I read](/how-i-read/)).

Mars and Neptune in their other aspects:

- **[Mars conjunct Neptune](/astrology/mars-conjunct-neptune/):** effort and imagination fused, more intense than the sextile and harder to tell apart.
- **[Mars square Neptune](/astrology/mars-square-neptune/):** the same two functions in friction, where acting and imagining test each other.
- **[Mars trine Neptune](/astrology/mars-trine-neptune/):** a fuller flow in one element, where inspired action comes almost without asking.
- **[Mars opposite Neptune](/astrology/mars-opposite-neptune/):** will and ideal across the chart, often met first in other people.

Across two charts, [Mars sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-neptune-synastry/) tends to be a gentle encouragement: one person's drive and the other's imagination get on. I read it for each of you.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Neptune sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-mars/) comes at most twice in a life, since Neptune needs about 165 years to go round. It lingers, a year or two near exact in several passes: a slow season in which effort can follow inspiration, such as a project begun from a dream, or work that starts to serve something larger.

Mars in the sky sextiles your natal Neptune twice every two years, a few days each time: small windows for acting on a hunch.

Both are weather, not a forecast, and I treat them that way: invitations to follow an inspiration, with nothing decided in advance.
