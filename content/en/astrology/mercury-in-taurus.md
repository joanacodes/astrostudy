Mercury in Taurus doesn't take a new idea on trust. It turns it over, weighs it in the hand and keeps it only if it bears weight, which is why, once this mind is made up, it tends to stay made.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) notices, sorts and passes on what you learn; in [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), fixed earth ruled by Venus, it does all three at the pace of something growing: steady, sensory, hard to rush. Here Mercury thinks with its hands and its senses. You learn by doing a thing and remember it because you did it; an explanation lands when it comes with an example you could touch. Your words tend to be few and solid, and people often find them easy to lean on.

Taurus is where tradition exalts the Moon; Mercury gets no dignity here, for good or ill. What the sign changes is the tempo of the mind, not its quality: a mind that takes its time is not a mind that's behind.

The calendar gives this patient placement a short window: Mercury's stay in Taurus is usually about 16 days, stretching to as many as 69 in a year when it turns retrograde in the sign, which gives a sign that likes to chew things over several extra weeks to do it. That works out at about 1 person in 13 among those born between 1930 and 2009.

Since Mercury keeps within about 28° of the Sun, the Sun itself is in Aries, Taurus or Gemini. Born with the Sun in Aries, you may want to charge while this mind holds the reins; born with it in Gemini, your curiosity runs ahead and your Mercury asks for proof. Where [Mercury in Aries](/astrology/mercury-in-aries/) learns by trying and [Mercury in Gemini](/astrology/mercury-in-gemini/) by asking, Mercury in Taurus learns by repeating until a thing is part of you. Venus rules Taurus, so your [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is worth a look next: its sign and house show what this mind finds worth thinking about, and what it values enough to defend.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You listen well and reply slowly. In a disagreement you may go quiet, not to punish anyone but to think, and a partner who takes silence for a wall may push harder just when you need room. Pressure makes this Mercury dig in; time makes it move. You tend to show love in plain, repeated words, the same question every evening about the same small things, which can sound like routine and is really steadiness. What you say, you mean, and you notice when the promises made to you aren't kept.

## At work {#at-work}

Mercury in Taurus does its best work where getting it right matters more than getting it first. Craft, building, design, cooking, gardening, bookkeeping, teaching a practical skill step by step: anything where knowledge is built by hand and checked against results. You may be slower than colleagues to learn a new system, and you're far slower to forget it. Plans that change direction every week wear you down. Given a clear brief and a reasonable deadline, you tend to deliver something that lasts.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Being hurried. A question that demands an answer now can make your mind close like a fist, and what comes out is a no that a day's thought might have turned into a yes. Views can also outlive their reasons: you weighed the evidence once, carefully, and it's hard to reopen the file when the evidence changes. The comfortable phrase *I've always done it this way* is the one to listen for in your own voice.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask for time, out loud: *let me think about it until tomorrow* is a complete answer. Put a date on the decisions you'd like to revisit, so that changing your mind becomes a plan rather than a defeat. Learn new things with your hands first and the theory second. A pleasant, quiet place to think helps more than it should, in a sign ruled by Venus. And since this is a mind that wants proof, here is mine: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Mercury held about a third of the time, alongside the Sun sign, the rising sign and Venus. So I read a Taurus Mercury closely, and I tell you when part of a reading leans on it.
