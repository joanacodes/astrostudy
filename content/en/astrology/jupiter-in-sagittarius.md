When a planet sits in its own sign, the function and the manner want the same thing. Jupiter in Sagittarius is the search for meaning with nothing to translate: you grow by going further, in miles, in ideas, in what you're willing to understand.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands understanding and organises experience into beliefs and principles. [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire, aims past the horizon: it explores, questions, teaches and moves. Here the two pull the same way. You learn through range, other places, other languages, other disciplines, and you tend to build a philosophy of your own rather than inherit one. Your principles are held in the open and stated frankly, and your sense of meaning likes room.

Sagittarius is Jupiter's [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), the sign it rules. That has a practical consequence I like. When I want to know how a planet is faring in a sign, I look at that sign's ruler, and here the ruler is Jupiter in person. This Jupiter answers to no other planet in your chart, so its house and its aspects carry the rest of the story.

Jupiter was in Sagittarius from December 1982 to January 1984, December 1994 to January 1996, November 2006 to December 2007 and November 2018 to December 2019, among other years. Line up everyone born from 1930 to 2009 and about 1 in 11 has Jupiter here. Two spans were ragged at the edges: from February 1959 to October 1960, and from January 1971 to September 1972, Jupiter's backward months reached into [Jupiter in Scorpio](/astrology/jupiter-in-scorpio/) and [Jupiter in Capricorn](/astrology/jupiter-in-capricorn/), and for those births only the chart can say. The sign is shared by a whole year of births; the house shows the field you go exploring in.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

The partner who suits this Jupiter is going somewhere too: a fellow traveller, a partner in the big conversation, a person who gives you room and wants it too. Honesty matters to you, sometimes more than tact. You're generous with enthusiasm and with the wider view, and you often help a partner see past a problem to what it might teach. Restlessness is the snag, or treating closeness as a limit on freedom. A bond grows when exploring includes exploring each other, which can perfectly well happen at home.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Jupiter thrives on teaching, study, publishing, travel, translation, international or cross-cultural work, coaching, anything that widens what people know. It's often you who sees the larger purpose of a project and can explain it. Your openings tend to come from stepping beyond your patch: a course, a trip, a neighbouring field you dared to enter. Watch the gap between the vision and the detail; you may promise the destination before the route is mapped.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Conviction can tip into preaching. With Jupiter at home, your philosophy can feel so complete that other people's doubts sound like ignorance, and wisdom hardens into certainty, which is something else. The next horizon can always seem more interesting than the one you're standing on, so things are left half-explored. And frankness, useful as it is, sometimes lands on people who weren't asking for your view.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask questions you don't know the answers to, and stay with them. Spend time with someone who disagrees with you thoughtfully, and learn their best argument. Finish one journey properly, a course, a book, a project, before setting out on the next. Teach, but leave room for your students to correct you. Since this placement answers to no other ruler, its house and its aspects are where the brakes and the balance come from, and I always read them closely.

A planet in its own sign is easy to over-read, and I've learned to resist it. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the three that held far more often were Saturn, Chiron and Lilith; Jupiter was checked on the same terms as everything else and wasn't among them, while about a third of everything held. Being at home in a sign exempts no planet from that test.
