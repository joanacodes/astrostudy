Neptune at the top of your chart brings the planet of ideals into the most public part of your life: your work, your role, what people know you for. The stay usually runs to nearly sixteen years once the Midheaven is crossed.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) inspires and idealises: it looks for the calling inside the job and the meaning inside the role. The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is vocation, responsibility, reputation and what you'd like to leave behind. With Neptune crossing it, ambition can turn into vocation: work that serves something larger, a creative or caring path, a role chosen for meaning rather than status. Your public image can soften too; people may project onto you and see you as more, or other, than you are, and a direction you were sure of can blur for a while before something truer comes into focus.

Around the time Neptune stands on your Midheaven ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)), the 10th's cusp in Placidus, the house system I use, the question of what your work is for tends to be loudest. The 10th belongs to Capricorn in the natural order, the sign of long effort; under Neptune, effort wants a reason as well as a result. With your Moon in the 10th from birth, what you need and what you're known for can run together; it's worth knowing which is which.

No [transit](/astrology/transits/) decides a career, a dismissal or a success, and I don't read one for that. What Neptune brings out is the vocation your chart already describes, with more imagination and less certainty, for a long while.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

For a London birth, in Placidus, the 10th ranks with the 3rd and the 4th among Neptune's longest stays, nearly sixteen years in the middle, and its stays bunch fairly close: a little under ten years at the short end, nearly nineteen at the long. A New York birth keeps the same middle inside the narrowest band of any house there, from nearly eleven years to about seventeen.

The stay is dated from the day Neptune first stands on your Midheaven, retrograde slips back into the 9th included, to its last day in the 10th. Because the Midheaven depends on the exact minute of birth, those dates can't be given without your birth time, and neither can any reading of mine ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Behind it lie Neptune's years in your 9th, a long search for meaning ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)); ahead, its years in your 11th, among friends and shared ideals ([Neptune transiting the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-11th-house/)).

Check the sign at the top of your chart. An Aries Midheaven is Neptune's to cross between March 2025 and March 2039, the sign it settled in during January 2026. Those with the Midheaven in Pisces have had their crossing already, between April 2011 and January 2026; those with it in Taurus wait until May 2038 or later.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask what your work is for, and let the answer change your choices a little at a time. Neptune in the 10th rewards a vocation found step by step more than a dramatic leap.

Keep a clear account of what you actually do, alongside what people imagine you do. A public image others project onto is easier to live with when your own record is plain.

Make room for work that serves something larger, even in a small way: a cause, a craft, care for someone. Under Neptune the 10th finds its meaning in contribution more than in status.
