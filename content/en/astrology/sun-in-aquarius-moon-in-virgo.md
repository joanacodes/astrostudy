You arrived in the first days after a full moon, with the Moon in Virgo and the Sun in Aquarius, five signs round from each other. Your sense of self is drawn to how a whole system could run better; your feelings calm down over the exact task in front of you. Between the blueprint and the screwdriver, this pair needs both.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fixed air and mutable earth share neither element nor mode, and five signs separate them: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/), a relation that asks for continual adjustment. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) takes its bearings from principles and the future of people in general; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) steadies itself through precision and maintenance, through making one thing work as it should. You may draft a plan for the whole neighbourhood and still feel calmest once the first small part of it actually runs.

In Virgo, [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is both at home and exalted, which gives it real weight for your Moon. It is never seen more than about 28° from the Sun, which means that in your chart it can't be in Virgo at all: your Moon's ruler always works near your Aquarius Sun, and the tools you use to sort a feeling come from the same part of the sky as your sense of purpose. Two planets rule Aquarius, and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), for modern astrologers, shows where your Sun tests new methods, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), for the older ones, where it insists on a sound structure. The Sun is in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/); Virgo gives the Moon no dignity.

When you were born the Moon had passed the opposition, 182° to 240° from the Sun, some 15 to 20 days into the lunar month, rising after dark and a little later each night. The full moon a few days earlier was usually a [full moon in Leo](/astrology/full-moon-in-leo/). The [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase takes the large majority of these births, seven in eight, and the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) the rest; at 210°, inside the range, the lights make an exact waning quincunx. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains why the days after a full moon still belong to its phase.

Born between 1930 and 2009, about 1 person in 151 has this combination. Virgo is the Moon's for roughly two and a half days, from the Leo border to the Libra one; if it crossed into the sign or out of it on your birthday, the hour alone tells this page apart from the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-leo/) and the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-libra/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same hour sets the houses, which show whether the sorting happens at work, at home or somewhere else entirely, and it is one more reason I don't take on a chart without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love, for you, means being useful and respecting a partner's independence, both at once. The Virgo Moon notices what someone needs and quietly sees to it; the Aquarius Sun wants a relationship of equals, with each of you free to stay particular. A partner may hear a practical suggestion as a correction when you meant it as care, so say which it is. And accept help now and then: being helped isn't a debt.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that turns a good idea into something that runs suits you: process design, public services, data, research methods, technical support, quality work on projects meant for many users. The Sun sees the improvement; the Moon checks that it actually works on Monday morning. Grand plans with no attention to detail frustrate you, and so does polishing details nobody will ever use.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The quincunx keeps you moving between the big principle and the small fix, and neither ever feels finished. You may judge yourself by both standards at once, fair enough for the world and exact enough for the Moon, and come up short on each. What helps is to choose one system and improve it in small, real steps, and at the end of each week to write down what worked before you write down what didn't.
