The shed is full of tools bought together, and each one has a story: what it cost, and why it was worth it. That's composite Mars in the 2nd house: a relationship whose drive goes into what it owns, earns and values.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds resources, possessions and the values that decide what's worth having. For [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it is what the relationship builds and keeps, and with [Mars](/astrology/mars/) there, the bond's drive goes into that base: making, earning, mending, protecting. The house is succedent, steadying what an angle starts, and its natural sign is Taurus, where tradition puts Mars in detriment; a detriment is a matter of sign, not of house, but this placement shares Taurus's taste for effort that lasts.

Such a pair works for its security together: saving for a roof, building a workshop, turning a hobby into income, learning a trade side by side. It defends what it values, its time, its standards, its home, with real energy, and it can be generous with what it has made. Competition shows in who contributes what, and desire is grounded, expressed through comfort, touch and the good things you've chosen together. A single person with [Mars in the 2nd house](/astrology/mars-in-2nd-house/) works hard for what they have; [their Mars in your 2nd house](/astrology/mars-in-2nd-house-synastry/) stirs up your sense of worth; the composite turns effort and worth into a shared project.

Arguments tend to be about value: what to spend on, whose priorities come first, whether the new car or the holiday matters more. They're easy to mistake for arguments about money when they're really about worth, who feels valued for what they bring. Values aren't prices, and what helps is settling what matters before settling a budget, keeping the accounts clear, and letting each of you keep something that's entirely yours.

Nothing in this placement says how much you'll earn, own or lose; the 2nd describes how you hold resources, not their amount. And if money is ever used to control or frighten one of you, that isn't a chart question: dial 999 in a UK emergency; Shout takes texts at 85258 at any hour.

Like every composite house, the 2nd needs both exact birth times. Its cusp comes either from averaging the 2nd-house cusps of your two charts or from the composite Midheaven worked out for your home, and the two methods can disagree near a boundary, so a Mars close to the 1st or the 3rd may change house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). For birth charts my system is [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/); with a composite, the house system is only the first of two choices.

Composite charts are outside my work, so this is a description, not a reading: astrologers who use them set this Mars beside your two birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). [Composite Mars in the 1st house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-1st-house/) shows its energy off, and [composite Mars in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-3rd-house/) scatters it in talk and errands; the 2nd invests it. Because a composite lays two private lives open, nobody should draw it until the two of you have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
