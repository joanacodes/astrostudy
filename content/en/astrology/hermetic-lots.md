Seven planets, seven lots. The Hermetic lots give each traditional planet a calculated point of its own, and they are the set that the Part of Fortune and the Part of Spirit belong to.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Hellenistic astrologers attributed this set to Hermes Trismegistus, the legendary "thrice-greatest" Hermes, in whom the Greek god Hermes and the Egyptian Thoth were rolled into one. The formulas survive in the *Introductory Matters* of Paulus Alexandrinus, written in Alexandria in 378, and in the lectures his commentator Olympiodorus gave on the book some two centuries later.

Here are the seven, with their Greek names:

- **Fortune**, *klēros tychēs*, the lot of the Moon ([Part of Fortune](/astrology/part-of-fortune/)).
- **Spirit**, *klēros daimonos*, the lot of the Sun ([Part of Spirit](/astrology/part-of-spirit/)).
- **Eros**, *klēros erōtos*, the lot of Venus.
- **Victory**, *klēros nikēs*, the lot of Jupiter.
- **Necessity**, *klēros anankēs*, the lot of Mercury.
- **Courage**, *klēros tolmēs*, the lot of Mars.
- **Nemesis**, *klēros nemeseōs*, the lot of Saturn.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Every lot in the set is counted from the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), and every one has a day formula and a night formula, the night one reversed ([sect](/astrology/sect-day-and-night-charts/)). The lights come first, because the other five are built on them:

- **Fortune:** by day, Ascendant + Moon − Sun; by night, Ascendant + Sun − Moon.
- **Spirit:** by day, Ascendant + Sun − Moon; by night, Ascendant + Moon − Sun.
- **Eros:** by day, Ascendant + Venus − Spirit; by night, Ascendant + Spirit − Venus.
- **Victory:** by day, Ascendant + Jupiter − Spirit; by night, Ascendant + Spirit − Jupiter.
- **Necessity:** by day, Ascendant + Fortune − Mercury; by night, Ascendant + Mercury − Fortune.
- **Courage:** by day, Ascendant + Fortune − Mars; by night, Ascendant + Mars − Fortune.
- **Nemesis:** by day, Ascendant + Fortune − Saturn; by night, Ascendant + Saturn − Fortune.

In plain words, the two benefics' lots measure from Spirit to the planet: Eros by day lies as far from the Ascendant as Venus is ahead of Spirit. The lots of Mercury and of the two malefics run the other way, from the planet to Fortune: Nemesis by day lies as far from the Ascendant as Fortune is ahead of Saturn. Venus and Jupiter lean on the Sun's lot; Mercury, Mars and Saturn on the Moon's.

Because Fortune and Spirit hang on the Ascendant and on whether the birth came by day or by night, the other five hang on them twice over. [Without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), none of the seven can be placed.

The set travelled. Abu Ma'shar and Guido Bonatti both give the formulas, but their lists combine them with another tradition of lots that goes back to Vettius Valens, so it's worth checking whose formula a later book is giving before comparing two charts ([doctrine of the lots](/astrology/doctrine-of-the-lots/)).

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

Two of the seven are part of my work ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). The Part of Fortune and the Part of Spirit are the only lots the system I built draws, and the chapters of [zodiacal releasing](/astrology/zodiacal-releasing/), one of the techniques in my forecasts, are counted from the Lot of Spirit and the lots related to it. The other five I describe; I don't read them.

Their names say what tradition looked for in each, and a name is not a forecast. No lot of Victory promises success, and no lot of Nemesis threatens anyone. Lots made for a subject of life, such as the [Part of Marriage](/astrology/part-of-marriage/), aren't among the seven at all: they belong to the longer lists that grew up beside this set.

None of the seven was in my notebook, which went placement by placement through the planets, so I have no count of my own for any of them ([my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The Hermetic lots are Arabic parts invented in the Middle Ages.”** Paulus set them out in the 4th century; the name Arabic parts came centuries later.
- **“Each lot sits where its planet is.”** A lot is built from the planet, a light's lot and the Ascendant, and it can fall a long way from the planet.
- **“The night formulas are the same as the day ones.”** Every one of the seven is reversed by night.
- **“The Lot of Nemesis predicts misfortune.”** Its name comes from tradition; no lot can foretell what will happen to anyone.
