With their Mercury in your 6th house, the to-do list becomes a shared language. Another person's mind turns to how your days actually run: the method, the routine, the small fix that saves you ten minutes every morning.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the shape of an ordinary day: tasks, habits, the colleagues beside you, the way you look after yourself. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) analyses, sorts and explains. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), it brings their practical intelligence to your daily round.

For you, they're often the person who makes things work. They notice the step you keep skipping, suggest a better order for the jobs, show you the shortcut in the software, and talk a muddle through until it becomes a plan. Working beside them, you may find your own method sharpening; learning a skill from them, you get clear instructions and fair feedback.

For them, your way of working gives their mind a task it enjoys. They like solving your practical problems, learn from your habits, and feel useful in a way that matters to them. The 6th is cadent, a house of upkeep and adjustment, where tradition gave Mars its joy. Its natural sign, [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), is ruled by Mercury and is also where tradition exalts it, so this is ground their Mercury knows well.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Help can turn into supervision. Advice you didn't ask for about how you load the dishwasher, plan your week or answer your email can leave you feeling managed, and a running list of corrections wears thin, however accurate it is.

From their side, the risk is being wanted only as a fixer. If your conversations shrink to logistics, they may wonder whether you'd talk to them about anything else; if you resist every suggestion, they can feel their help is unwelcome.

Bad Fortune, the old name of the 6th, came with illness attached. This overlay can describe two people who talk about sleep, food or exercise, but it doesn't diagnose or predict anything about anyone's health, and remarks on how you eat or rest are opinions, not findings ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). A question about your health is one for a doctor.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask before advising, and say thank you when advice helps. Split tasks by skill rather than by who notices first. In a couple, keep one conversation a week that isn't about the household; between friends, a routine you set up together is the one that sticks; between colleagues, agree who checks whose work before anyone starts; between adult relatives caring for someone together, shared schedules and clear messages keep the care from turning into friction.

The 6th ends where your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) opens the house of partners. Close to that line, their Mercury also touches the angle, and conversations about who does what tend to become conversations between equals. With your Mercury in their 6th house, the method and the suggestions would be yours. Around it, [their Mercury in your 5th house](/astrology/mercury-in-5th-house-synastry/) is all play, and [their Mercury in your 7th house](/astrology/mercury-in-7th-house-synastry/) is talk across the table.

Four minutes of birth time move your Descendant by about a degree, so in Placidus, the house system I use, your time decides whether a Mercury near it is in your 6th or your 7th. Their own time hardly matters for a planet this slow over a day, unless it lies within a degree or so of a cusp; I still ask both people for theirs ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Colleagues included: a reading of two people needs both of them to agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
