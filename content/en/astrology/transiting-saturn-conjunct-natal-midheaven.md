Saturn spends years climbing the western side of your chart, through your 7th, 8th and 9th houses, before it reaches the top. When it crosses your Midheaven, about every 29½ years, the work you've been doing out of sight, or at the edge of view, becomes the subject.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) structures responsibility and earns authority slowly; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is the frame through which your capacities become visible: role, contribution, standing. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) responsibility and visibility arrive together. What you've built tends to be seen and asked to carry weight, and the gap between status and contribution becomes hard to ignore: a title without substance feels heavier, and real work without a title asks to be recognised, by you first. The Midheaven isn't only a job, either: raising a family, a role in a community or a long creative project can be the public life Saturn weighs here. And every crossing of your Midheaven is an opposition to your IC, so what supports the role at home is part of the reckoning.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Midheaven is where the 10th house begins, and this crossing starts [Saturn transiting the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-10th-house/). It's the high point of Saturn's arc through your angles: about fourteen or fifteen years after Saturn crossed your IC ([transiting Saturn opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-midheaven/)), what was rebuilt in private meets public life.

A transit doesn't hire or fire anyone. Whether a job begins or ends in these months depends on people and circumstances no chart can see; what Saturn describes is the weight of a role, and your relationship to it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The top of the chart holds Saturn for about nine months, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use. A little over half the time Saturn arrives, backs away and arrives again, three exact crossings in about nine and a half months; a single fast crossing can be done in three weeks. Everyone has it two or three times by 80.

The Midheaven moves through the zodiac at about a degree every four minutes of the day, so the hour and minute on your birth record decide the month: two recorded times five minutes apart can move this crossing by more than a month. Hence the minute matters to me as much as the day ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Before and after the crossing, symmetrically, come the sextiles, about five years either side ([transiting Saturn sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-midheaven/)); the squares, about seven, close to Saturn's crossings of your Descendant and Ascendant ([transiting Saturn square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-midheaven/)); and the trines, about ten ([transiting Saturn trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-midheaven/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you'd like your work to be known for in ten years, then check this year's commitments against it.

Make your contribution visible in plain terms: what you did, for whom, and how it holds up. Saturn at the top of the chart respects evidence.

Ask for the feedback you'd rather avoid. A frank review now weighs less than a surprise later.
