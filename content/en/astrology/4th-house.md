The 4th house sits at the bottom of a birth chart, under your feet: home, roots, family, the place you come from and the one you make. It's the most private of the four angular houses.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 4th house as the ground you stand on. It covers home — the one you grew up in and the one you build — family and the feeling of belonging, the past you carry, and the private self that only the people closest to you see. Tradition also gave it land and houses, the things that stay put.

I read it gently. The 4th describes your experience of home and family, what it felt like from inside; it says nothing certain about anyone's parents. Older books give it a parent, and astrologers have never agreed which one. The people who raised you have charts of their own.

- **An angular house.** It's one of the four houses that begin at the angles, and what sits here is strong, even when it works out of sight. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its angle.** Its cusp is the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), or IC: the point of the zodiac opposite the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), at the bottom of the chart. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, the IC is where the 4th house begins ([which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)).
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) and the [Moon](/astrology/moon/): shelter, need, memory.
- **Its opposite.** The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), career and public life: the 10th is what the world sees of you, the 4th what goes on behind your own front door.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/), just before it, is the street and the conversations; the 4th is the door you close behind you. The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/), just after it, is what you do once you feel at home: play, create, love.

The IC goes round the whole zodiac in a day, like the Ascendant, so the 4th house needs your birth time to be placed ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 4th house {#planets}

A planet in the 4th house lives at home: it shows in your private life, your family patterns and what you need in order to belong. It's often quieter in public than its strength would suggest.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), against about a third for most placements. If Saturn, [Chiron in the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-in-4th-house/) or [Lilith in the 4th house](/astrology/lilith-in-4th-house/) sits here, I take that part of a reading slowly: belonging is tender ground.

- **[Sun in the 4th house](/astrology/sun-in-4th-house/):** home and family are where you find your purpose; your best self is often the private one.
- **[Moon in the 4th house](/astrology/moon-in-4th-house/):** in the house of its own sign: a deep need for a nest, and a long memory for where you come from.
- **[Mercury in the 4th house](/astrology/mercury-in-4th-house/):** a home full of talk and books; your thoughts often go back to the past.
- **[Venus in the 4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house/):** peace at home matters more than most things; you make places lovely to come back to.
- **[Mars in the 4th house](/astrology/mars-in-4th-house/):** energy that goes into the home — building, fixing, defending — and sometimes friction there.
- **[Jupiter in the 4th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-4th-house/):** an open house; you need room, and you like it full of people.
- **[Saturn in the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-in-4th-house/):** responsibility early at home; the home you build later is the one you trust.
- **[Uranus in the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-in-4th-house/):** an unusual home life, or more than one home; you need freedom where you live.
- **[Neptune in the 4th house](/astrology/neptune-in-4th-house/):** a longing for the perfect home, and a home that's hard to pin down.
- **[Pluto in the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-in-4th-house/):** strong family undercurrents, and the long work of changing what you were handed.

No planet here? An [empty 4th house](/astrology/empty-houses/) doesn't mean no home and no family story. It's read through the sign on the IC, the place of the 4th's ruler, and the Moon.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the IC, the cusp of the 4th house, gives the tone of your private life: what home needs to feel like, and the atmosphere you grew up in, as you felt it.

- **[Aries on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a lively, independent home; you learned early to stand on your own feet.
- **[Taurus on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as comfort and stability: good food, familiar things, no rush.
- **[Gemini on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a busy household full of talk, and perhaps more than one home.
- **[Cancer on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a refuge, and family ties that run deep.
- **[Leo on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a warm, proud home, and a wish to be at its heart.
- **[Virgo on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an orderly home; care shown in practical help.
- **[Libra on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a need for harmony and beauty at home; quarrels cost you.
- **[Scorpio on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a private home life, deep loyalties, and things not said aloud.
- **[Sagittarius on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a base to set out from; an open door.
- **[Capricorn on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/):** structure and responsibility at home; a home built over time.
- **[Aquarius on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an unconventional home; friends who become family.
- **[Pisces on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a soft, porous home; a refuge, with boundaries to learn.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 4th house is the planet that rules the sign on the IC. Where it sits shows where your sense of home goes looking: the part of life that roots you, or the one family matters spill into. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the IC, there's a modern and a traditional ruler to read ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** you carry home with you; your roots show in who you are.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** home and money are linked: property, family resources, security.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** home life tied to siblings, neighbours and the local area.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** deep roots; home matters twice over.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** home as a place of play, creativity and pleasure.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** home and daily work overlap; looking after others at home.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** home made with a partner; the partner as family.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** a family history with depth: shared belongings, things inherited, things kept quiet.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** home far from where you began, or roots in a belief.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** family and career intertwined; a public life with private roots.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** friends as family; belonging found in a community.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** a home life kept private; a need to retreat.

## Transits through the 4th house {#transits}

The Moon passes over your IC every month and the Sun once a year, Mercury, Venus and Mars for a few weeks: short spells when home and family are on your mind. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 4th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-4th-house/):** about a year of wanting more room at home, more family around, more time inside your own life.
- **[Saturn transiting the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-4th-house/):** about two and a half years of responsibilities at home and repairs to the foundations, in every sense.
- **[Uranus transiting the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-4th-house/):** around seven years of change in how and where you live, and a need for freedom there.
- **[Neptune transiting the 4th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-4th-house/):** around fourteen years when home becomes a longing as much as a place.
- **[Pluto transiting the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-4th-house/):** a long, deep turning-over of roots and family patterns.
- **[Chiron transiting the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-4th-house/):** an old family hurt comes up to be tended, gently.
- **[North Node transiting the 4th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-4th-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled back towards home, roots and your inner life.

I read these as weather, not a forecast: none of them says whether you'll move or what will happen in your family, only what home may feel like for a while.
