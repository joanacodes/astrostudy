With Aquarius on the 10th house cusp, your Midheaven sits in the sign of fixed air, and so do the work you're known for and the standing you build. You tend to be seen as the one who asks how things could run better, and to want a role that changes something beyond yourself.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

At the top of the chart sits the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), where the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) begins in Placidus, the house system I use ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) there, ambition tends to attach to a cause, a group or an idea of the future more than to a title. People come to know you as the colleague who questions the procedure and then writes a better one, the manager who would rather run a team of equals than a ladder, or the freelancer whose work fits no single box. Authority sits oddly on you: you can carry it well, but you'd rather share it, and you push back when it's used without reason. Careers here often change shape, sideways more than upwards, and the reputation you build is for independence of mind.

Uranus and Saturn rule this Midheaven, and I read both, though Saturn has a special claim. Capricorn is the 10th sign, so [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the natural ruler of every 10th house; with Aquarius here, it rules yours in fact as well, by tradition. That's the ruler I'd look at first: Saturn was one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), while Uranus was checked like every placement and not one of the three. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern ruler, shows where your career turns suddenly or breaks with what was expected. Capricorn always opens your 8th or 9th house in these charts, often both, so Saturn also ties your public life to what you share and what you believe. Follow the two rulers to their houses and you find where your ambitions get their fuel and their tests, as ruler of the 10th in the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

The Midheaven is in Aquarius for about 1 birth in 12 at both London and New York: the meridian's signs come with the hour, not the latitude. [Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/) comes with it in about 4 London charts in 5 and 3 in 4 at New York, so Mercury rules the chart and the public role is often reached through talk, writing or putting people in touch; Cancer rises in the remaining London charts, Taurus in the remaining New York ones. Pisces opens the 11th in about 4 charts in 5, putting your allies under Neptune and Jupiter. Aquarius sometimes stretches over two cusps, the 9th and 10th in about 3 London charts in 10, the 10th and 11th in about 1 in 6 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); in about 1 London chart in 7 it opens no house at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Leo, on the IC opposite, gives the Sun the rule of your home ([Leo on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/)).

None of this names a job or promises a rise: it describes the direction your ambitions take. Two hours is about all the Midheaven spends in a sign, so earlier in the day Aquarius would have held the 11th cusp ([Aquarius on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-11th-house-cusp/)), and later the 9th ([Aquarius on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/)); only the time tells me which ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
