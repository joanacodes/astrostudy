Your reputation is made on ordinary weekdays, because the ruler of your 10th house works from the 6th, among tasks, routines and colleagues: you're known for what you do well, day after day, more than for the title on the door.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Vocation, standing and reputation belong to the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), whose cusp in Placidus, the house system I use, is the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/); whichever planet rules the Midheaven's sign carries them with it ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). In the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/), they meet the working week as it's actually lived: the tasks, the habits, the people beside you, the upkeep of your body.

The old horary lists frame it neatly. They give the 10th persons of authority and the 6th employees and the workplace; with the ruler of the first in the second, authority lives among the workforce. Practice builds this vocation: the manager who still does the job, the head chef on the line, the expert whose standing comes from twenty years of doing one thing well. Careers here are often built from the bottom up: the apprentice who becomes the master, the junior who ends up running the department, because no part of the work was skipped. Many people with this placement work in trades, care, administration or craft. The risk is a career that never looks up from the bench, with standards so high that nothing is ever finished, or a reputation for reliability that keeps you doing what you've outgrown.

With Aries on the Midheaven, Mars rules the 10th, and the 6th is the house tradition picked for Mars's joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): your drive goes into the job itself, with a taste for hard, practical problems ([Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/)). The Moon, ruling a Cancer Midheaven from here, makes care the daily substance of the work. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn Midheaven, builds a career the way a craftsman builds a wall, one course at a time.

Because the 6th was also tradition's house of illness, I'll be plain: nothing here forecasts your health or anyone else's, and diagnosis is a doctor's work, not a chart's ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). What I read is how your work and your daily care fit together.

The 6th lies just under the western horizon, and the Descendant above it moves with the clock, so the exact time decides where a ruler near that edge belongs ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Earlier, it would still be above the horizon in the 7th, where work runs through partners and clients ([Ruler of the 10th in the 7th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)). Later, it would have sunk into the 5th, where a job has to be a pleasure too ([Ruler of the 10th in the 5th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)).

A Scorpio Midheaven puts Mars to work here too, alongside Pluto ([Scorpio on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Mars keeps its joy in the 6th whichever sign it rules from, while Pluto, from its own house, pushes the work towards whatever needs fixing at the root.
