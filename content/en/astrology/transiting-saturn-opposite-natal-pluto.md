Saturn's round with your Pluto reaches its far point about fifteen years after the crossing: Saturn now stands across the chart from the degree. What you've been reworking deep down meets the world's structures, or somebody else's, head on, and the question of who decides comes into the open.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) holds structures and responsibilities in place; [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms, clears out what's obsolete and hands power back to whoever does the work. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets them at either end of one axis, and this [transit](/astrology/transits/) often makes the axis visible through other people: an institution that won't change while you need to; a partner or colleague who wants control of something you care about; or the reverse, you holding the line while someone else pushes for a deeper change.

Polarity isn't contradiction. I read this season as a call to stewardship: using whatever power you hold with care, and letting go of control that only protected you from uncertainty. Negotiation tends to outlast winning. Nor does the transit decide a dispute, at work or in court ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)): it describes the terms of the question, not its outcome.

The axis runs between two houses. With Pluto in your 5th, Saturn crosses your 11th, and what you create on your own terms meets the expectations of a group. Both houses rest on the minute of your birth, so without it I can say nothing about where this falls ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Saturn needs about nine months to pass through the opposition, by my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after. Slightly more often than not it comes as three exact hits spread over about nine and a half months, and when there's only one hit, three weeks can be the whole of it.

It returns once per round, two or three times by 80, and Pluto's slowness means a whole year group meets it together: born in 1975, at about 22 and 51; born in 1985, at about 14 and 43; born in 1965, inside the [Saturn opposite Pluto](/astrology/saturn-opposite-pluto/) of 1965–67, in the first months of life, then at about 30 and 59. For most people born between 1971 and 1984, Pluto sits in Libra, facing Aries, where Saturn is until April 2028: their opposition is now. From spring 2028, with Saturn in Taurus, it moves on to Pluto in Scorpio, born between 1983 and 1995.

The trines either side of it ([transiting Saturn trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-pluto/)) tend to be calmer company, the squares ([transiting Saturn square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-pluto/)) more insistent, and the sextiles ([transiting Saturn sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-pluto/)) the most easily missed; the round opens and closes with [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-pluto/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice where you grab control and where you give it away. The opposition shows both, often within the same relationship.

Negotiate terms rather than outcomes: who decides what, by when, and how it can be changed. Structures that share power tend to come through this transit well.

Let someone else's resistance show you what your change still needs. The far end of the axis often knows something useful.
