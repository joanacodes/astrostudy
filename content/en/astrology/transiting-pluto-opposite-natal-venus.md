Pluto opposite your Venus brings what you value into view through someone else. For two to three years the planet of depth faces your Venus across the chart, and what you desire, and what you expect from closeness, is shown back to you by another person's intensity.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) brings attachments and power patterns into the open. Your [Venus](/astrology/venus/) values, attracts and weighs what is given against what comes back. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates them through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) you tend to learn about your own values from the other end of a bond: an attraction stronger than you expected, a partner who wants more depth or more say, a friendship whose terms suddenly matter.

Some imbalance is ordinary. One person gives more this season, the other the next, and a long bond evens out. Pluto can make every imbalance look like a bid for power, and most aren't; the opposition is a good time to tell the two apart. Attraction isn't romance, either: what draws you under this transit may be a person, a cause or a way of living, and each asks the same question about what you will give for it.

A bond that frightens you, or asks you to give up your friends, your money or your own judgement, is not what this opposition is for. No transit makes that acceptable, and help from someone you trust, or from a professional, comes first ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

With Venus in the 2nd and Pluto crossing the 8th ([Pluto transiting the 8th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-8th-house/)), what is yours faces what is shared: values, belongings, the terms on which you let someone close. It predicts nothing about money. The 2nd and the 8th face each other across your chart only once the birth time has placed them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If [Venus opposite Pluto](/astrology/venus-opposite-pluto/) is in your birth chart, the season that echoes this one is the [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/): when Pluto squares its own birth place, it squares your Venus as well.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, 1½° before exact and 1° after, Pluto opposes your Venus for about two years and eight months, nearly four years in the longest passes. About three passes in five bring three exact hits; the others bring five.

This opposition and the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-venus/)) are the two Pluto contacts with Venus that more than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet by 80. The square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-venus/)) comes to about seven in eight, the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-venus/)) and the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-venus/)) to about three in four.

Across from Aquarius lies Leo, so until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)) the opposition falls on Venus in Leo.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say what is yours. Naming your own values aloud keeps them from being decided by the other end of the axis.

Judge the balance of a bond over months rather than days. Reciprocity is a long average, not a daily score.

Don't make a bond's future hang on a single exact hit. Pluto comes back over the point, and the picture moves each time.
