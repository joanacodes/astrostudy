Six years or so, sometimes many more: that's how long Uranus tends to spend in the house of the self once it has crossed your Ascendant. What it stirs is the way you meet the world, the image you've been carrying, and whether it still fits.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part of a chart that differentiates and frees: it questions what you inherited and tries out other arrangements. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is identity and presence, the way you begin things and the self people meet before they know the rest. With Uranus moving through it, a self-image you settled on years ago can start to feel like a costume. You may want to present yourself differently, start over in some visible way, or stop apologising for the parts of you that never fitted the first box.

Because Placidus, the house system I use, starts the 1st at the Ascendant, the first thing Uranus does here is cross your rising degree ([transiting Uranus conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)), and that crossing is often when the urge to be different is loudest. Planets you were born with in the 1st come after, each in the order of its degree: with your Moon there, the change tends to start with what you need in order to feel like yourself.

None of this turns you into someone else. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) works with what the chart already holds, and here it gives the person your chart describes more room to differ. A wish to change is the weather; what you change is up to you.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Uranus usually spends about six years in the 1st, in Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, well under its middle for a house there, about 7 years and 8 months. No house spreads wider: most stays last anywhere from about four years to nearly fourteen. Charts cast for New York, further south, put the middle just under seven years and end most stays by nearly twelve.

The count runs from Uranus's first crossing of your Ascendant to the day it leaves the 1st for good, so the back-and-forth of its retrograde years over the cusp sits inside a single stay. None of these dates can be placed without the minute you were born, since the minute moves the Ascendant, and the place of birth changes how wide the house comes out: that's why a chart without a birth time is one I don't read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Before the 1st come Uranus's years in your 12th, out of sight ([Uranus transiting the 12th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-12th-house/)); after it, the 2nd, where the question turns to what you live on ([Uranus transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-2nd-house/)).

Your rising sign gives the rough date. Uranus is in Gemini from April 2026 to May 2033, after a first visit from July 2025, so a Gemini Ascendant is crossed somewhere in that span: early in the sign sooner, late in the sign later. Cancer rising waits for the years between August 2032 and August 2039, and a Leo Ascendant starts to be reached from August 2039. Taurus rising had its crossing between May 2018 and April 2026, and Uranus may still be moving through that 1st house. For any other rising sign, Uranus reached your Ascendant before 2018, or won't until after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Try the new version of yourself in small, visible steps before announcing it: a different way of dressing, of introducing yourself, of starting things. Uranus works by experiment, and the 1st is where experiments show.

Keep what still works. Uranus can make the whole of the old you look stale at once; most of it isn't, and the people who know you may need time to catch up, which is not the same as holding you back.

Ask what you're freeing yourself for, not only what from. A freer image with nothing behind it wears out as fast as the one it replaced.
