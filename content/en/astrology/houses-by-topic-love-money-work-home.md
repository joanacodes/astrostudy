Most people come to the houses with a question: where's love in my chart, or money, or work? Here's the map, house by house, with the planets that join in and the limits I keep to.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Each of the twelve houses covers a part of life, and most of the topics people care about span more than one:

- **Love.** The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is romance, flirting and pleasure, the love you choose for joy; the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is partnership, the person across the table; many astrologers add the 8th for intimacy and trust. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) joins in wherever it sits.
- **Money.** The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is what you earn, keep and value; the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is what you share with others or owe them.
- **Work.** The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the work of an ordinary day, its routines and colleagues; the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is your public role and direction, the reputation you build.
- **Home.** The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, roots and family, the place you come from and the one you make; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) adds what you need to feel at home.

The rest of the circle: the self you lead with in the 1st, siblings, neighbours and everyday talk in the 3rd, study, travel and belief in the 9th, friends and groups in the 11th, solitude and rest in the 12th.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

A topic is read in layers. First the houses that cover it, with any planets inside. Then the signs on their cusps, which set the style, and the rulers of those signs, which show where each story plays out ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Then the planets that carry the topic in any chart, such as Venus for love. A house with no planet still answers through its cusp and ruler ([empty houses](/astrology/empty-houses/)), and in Placidus a sign can sit wholly inside a house without touching its cusp, adding its own voice there ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

What the houses describe is context: where in life a part of you goes to work, never the outcome. The 7th says how you meet partners and what you look for in them; it doesn't say when you'll meet one, or whether a marriage will last.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

Some houses carry old meanings I don't read as forecasts. Tradition gave the 6th illness, the 8th death, the 5th children and the 12th confinement, and money and disputes are easy to read as predictions off the 2nd and the 7th. I read all of them in an adult's life, and in the present tense:

- **Health.** The 6th is how you look after yourself day to day, not a diagnosis ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and a planet in the 12th is not a symptom ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).
- **Death.** The 8th is intimacy, trust and what's shared, read among the living ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
- **Children.** The 5th is play, creativity and romance. It can't say whether or when anyone will have a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/).
- **Money and the law.** The 2nd shows how you handle money, not how much there will be ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)), and no house can call the outcome of a dispute ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)).

That's [what I won't read](/how-i-read/), and why. And every house on this map needs a birth time before it can be placed at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The 5th house shows how many children you'll have.”** Tradition looked there for children; no chart can count anyone's future children, and I don't try.
- **“The 2nd house says whether you'll be rich.”** It shows how you handle money and what security means to you, not your bank balance.
- **“The 7th house tells you when you'll marry.”** It describes what you look for in a partner and how you tend to meet one, not a date.
- **“The 8th house shows how you'll die.”** I don't read death in a chart, for anyone. The 8th is busy with the living: intimacy, trust, what's shared and owed.
