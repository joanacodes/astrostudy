Aquarius is the eleventh sign of the zodiac, and despite the water-bearer that gives it its name, it's an air sign, not a water one. I read it as the sign of the group and of the future: thinking for others, and thinking differently.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Aquarius somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it asks *how could this work better, and for more people?* It's an [air sign](/astrology/air-signs/), so it works through ideas and conversation, and a fixed one, so once it holds an idea it keeps it (the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/) explain the difference). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), just before it, builds the structure; Aquarius asks who it's for and how it could be fairer. [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), next, lets the structure dissolve altogether.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/)** — born between about 20 January and 18 February. You shine by thinking differently, for others. You often feel most yourself in a group or a cause, and you need the freedom to be the exception.
- **[Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/)** — you feel safe with space, and with friends who don't crowd you. You understand feelings by thinking them through, which can look like distance from the outside.
- **[Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/)** — you meet the world in a friendly, curious, slightly detached way, and people remember you as a little different. With two rulers, there are two planets to look at as your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/): Saturn and Uranus. A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. Aquarius's traditional ruler was one of those three: Saturn held 75 to 90% of the time, so in a chart with a lot of Aquarius, I never skip it.

## Its rulers: Uranus and Saturn {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, and where that planet sits in your chart shows where the sign's energy goes. Aquarius has two. For most of astrology's history it was ruled by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), which also rules Capricorn: in Capricorn, Saturn builds for the long term; in Aquarius, it builds systems and rules for the group. After William Herschel discovered [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in 1781, modern astrologers came to give Aquarius to it, and read it as the planet of sudden change, independence and invention. Traditional astrologers still use Saturn alone; many modern ones read Uranus first and keep Saturn as co-ruler ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/) explains the split). Together they describe Aquarius well: the rebel and the rule-maker. Uranus's sign is shared by everyone born over about seven years, so its house is what makes it yours.

Tradition exalts no planet in Aquarius, and none is in fall here. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite its home in Leo: the planet of the individual in the sign of the collective. That isn't a dim Sun. It's a self that finds itself among others, and has to learn to stay itself there.

## Planets in Aquarius {#planets-in-the-sign}

Every planet passes through Aquarius, from the Moon in about two and a half days to Uranus, which comes home about every eighty-four years and stays about seven. In Aquarius, each one looks at the bigger system:

- **[Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/):** the Sun in detriment: a self that grows through ideas, friendships and causes, and keeps its independence.
- **[Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/):** comfort in friendship and freedom; feelings understood from a little distance.
- **[Mercury in Aquarius](/astrology/mercury-in-aquarius/):** original, inventive thinking; you see the pattern before others do, and hold on to your ideas.
- **[Venus in Aquarius](/astrology/venus-in-aquarius/):** love that starts as friendship; you need equality, space and someone who lets you be unusual.
- **[Mars in Aquarius](/astrology/mars-in-aquarius/):** drive for causes and for the group; you act against rules that make no sense.
- **[Jupiter in Aquarius](/astrology/jupiter-in-aquarius/):** growth through networks, friendships and new ideas; a generosity aimed at the many rather than the few.
- **[Saturn in Aquarius](/astrology/saturn-in-aquarius/):** Saturn at home in the tradition, shared by everyone born over about two and a half years: rules that serve the group, and responsibility in communities.
- **[Uranus in Aquarius](/astrology/uranus-in-aquarius/):** Uranus at home in modern astrology, shared by everyone born over about seven years: independence and invention. Its house shows where you break new ground.
- **[Neptune in Aquarius](/astrology/neptune-in-aquarius/):** shared by everyone born over about fourteen years: ideals of a connected, fairer world. Its house makes it personal.
- **[Pluto in Aquarius](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius/):** Pluto has only recently entered Aquarius, so everyone born with it is still a child. For adults it's a transit: the house it crosses in your chart is where its slow pressure is felt — weather, not a forecast.

## Aquarius on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

In most charts Aquarius opens one of the twelve houses. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can also open two, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Aquarius opens a house, that's where you do things your own way and think of the wider group, and Uranus and Saturn, wherever they sit, carry the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time and place.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aquarius-rising/):** Aquarius rising — a friendly, independent presence, a little apart from the crowd even when you're in it.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** an unusual relationship with money; you may earn through technology, networks or ideas, and value freedom over possessions.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** inventive speech and a questioning mind; you learn by challenging what you're told.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an unconventional home or family; you need independence even under your own roof.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** love that begins as friendship; creativity that experiments, and fun with a crowd.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work that needs freedom and new methods; you improve the system rather than follow it.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius on the Descendant, which means Leo rising: you want a partner who is also a friend, and an equal partnership with room for both.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** intimacy that needs space; shared resources handled in your own way; a cool head in a crisis.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** progressive beliefs; a pull towards science, the future and ideas that haven't caught on yet.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career in technology, science, social causes or any field you can reform, and a public image of an original.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius in its natural house: friendships, groups and shared causes are where you shine.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a private independence; you can feel apart even among friends, and may work for a cause behind the scenes.

## Aquarius in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Aquarius placements bring friendship, honesty and respect for each person's freedom, along with a detachment that can feel cool to a partner who wants closeness. Its opposite sign, [Leo](/astrology/leo/), is the pull: Leo shines as one, Aquarius thinks for the many, and each reminds the other of what it forgets. Comparing Sun signs can't tell you how two people will meet — [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind there is — so I read both whole charts and how they speak to each other.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Aquarius is a water sign.”** It's an air sign. The water-bearer carries the water; the sign itself belongs to air, the element of ideas and exchange.
- **“Aquarians are cold.”** Aquarius often cares about people in the plural first. Closeness comes through friendship, and the Moon and Venus say more about warmth than a Sun sign does.
- **“Aquarius is always the rebel.”** Its traditional ruler is Saturn, the planet of structure. Aquarius can be the one who builds the new system as much as the one who breaks the old.
- **“Born on 18 or 19 February, you're half Pisces.”** At the minute you were born the Sun was in one sign; near a change, your birth time settles which ([born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/)).
