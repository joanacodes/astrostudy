With Capricorn on the 11th house cusp, friendship is counted in years rather than in names. Your circle grows slowly, one person at a time, and most of it is still there a decade later.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) you pick your allies: friends who aren't family, the clubs, unions and professional bodies you join, and the long plans that need more than one pair of hands ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) on its cusp gives all of that structure and time. Its signature is the friend from your first job you still see every month, the association where you end up keeping the minutes or the accounts, the five-year plan you actually follow. Trust is earned by turning up: you notice who stays when there's work to do, and you tend to be that person yourself. Close friends may be older than you, or met through work. Hopes run long and concrete, with dates attached. The risk is that belonging starts to feel like a duty: contacts kept for their use, friendships fitted around the diary, or a hope dropped early because it didn't look realistic. One or two friendships kept purely for pleasure do this cusp a lot of good.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules this 11th house, and the house was partly its own already: until Uranus was found in 1781, Aquarius, the 11th's natural sign, belonged to Saturn alone, and astrologers who keep the old rulers still pair the two ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Saturn's sign shows how you keep your allies, and its house where your circle forms: the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). My notebook gives me a reason to read Saturn slowly here: with Chiron and Lilith, it held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), so where it sits says more about your friendships than the cusp alone.

In every chart with this cusp, at both latitudes, Saturn rules your 12th house too: Aquarius, the sign it shares with Uranus, opens it in about 7 London charts in 10 and more at New York, and Capricorn runs on to it in the rest. The planet of your circle is then the planet of your solitude as well, and time alone is often how you recover from groups, or where a cause gets its quiet work ([Capricorn on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-12th-house-cusp/)). Pisces is the commonest Ascendant here, in about 2 London charts in 5 and more than half at New York ([Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/)), so Jupiter and Neptune rule the chart: an open, gentle manner in front of a circle chosen with great care. Aries takes most of the remaining London charts, and Capricorn then reaches the Midheaven as well ([Capricorn on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/); [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)): colleagues turn into friends. Cancer faces this cusp from the 5th, so the people you love up close get your tenderness and the wider circle your reliability.

Counted in Placidus, the house system I use, the cusp falls in Capricorn for about one birth in 12 in each city. Capricorn crosses the Midheaven in a little over two hours and the Ascendant here in under two; the 11th cusp, between them, takes about two, an exact twelfth of the day.

Whether Capricorn describes your circle or your career can come down to less than an hour: later in the day the sign reaches the Midheaven, and earlier it stood on the 12th. So I ask for the minute before I say a word about your friends ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
