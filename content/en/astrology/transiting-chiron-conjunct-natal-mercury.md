Chiron on your Mercury reaches the part of you that thinks, speaks and learns. Once or twice in a life, an old sensitivity about being heard or understood sits right on that degree, and the ways you found around it come into view: the words you rehearse, the questions you don't ask, the subject you steer away from.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) processes and exchanges: it's how you take things in, make sense of them and pass them on. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks where a difficulty with being heard or learning stayed, and the skill you built around it. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), Chiron and Mercury share a degree, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the old sensitivity and your way of thinking aloud become one subject for a while. It might be a memory of being talked over, corrected in front of others, told you were slow, or never asked what you thought. It might be the habit that grew from it: explaining too much, saying too little, writing what you can't say.

Neither the memory nor the habit says anything about how clever you are. Mercury describes how you process and exchange, not a measure of intelligence. What the conjunction tends to offer is a different method: a way of learning that suits you, a medium that lets you say the hard thing, the patience to explain to someone else what took you years to work out.

With Mercury in the 3rd, Chiron crosses it during its stay in that house ([Chiron transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-3rd-house/)), and conversations, siblings, neighbours and everyday learning carry the subject. Mercury barely moves in an hour, so it's the house rather than the date that waits on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Mercury conjunct Chiron](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-chiron/) in your chart, the tenderness in your speech is old company; Chiron returns to it at your Chiron return, around 50.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Chiron usually crosses your Mercury three times, in nearly two passes out of three, which keeps it within my orbs (1½° before exact to 1° after) for about ten and a half months. A single, quicker crossing of about three weeks happens in roughly a third of passes, and a five-crossing one of some twenty-one months in about one in twenty.

Mercury is never more than about 28° from the Sun, so this transit comes in the same stretch of years as [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-sun/), before it or after it. With Chiron gaining four or five degrees a year through Taurus, the two can be up to six years apart; where Chiron moves fast, they can fall in the same year.

Once or twice by 80, twice for most people: Chiron needs about fifty years to come back to the same degree. The sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-mercury/)) and the trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-mercury/)) open easier ways to speak and learn, the square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-mercury/)) puts friction into them, and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-mercury/)) hands the subject to other people's words; the first three come about three times in a life, the opposition as rarely as this conjunction.

From 2027 to 2033 Chiron crosses Taurus, and with it any Mercury in Taurus: one that belongs to someone born with the Sun in Aries, Taurus or Gemini, between about late March and mid-June.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say the thing you've rehearsed for years to one person, plainly, once.

Learn something the way that suits you rather than the way you were taught to: by ear, by hand, by writing it out.

Explain something you understand well to someone who's struggling with it. Your old difficulty is what makes you good at it.
