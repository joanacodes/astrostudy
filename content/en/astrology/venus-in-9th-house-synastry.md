Their Venus in your 9th house falls for your horizons. Your ideas, your beliefs, the places you've been and the ones you dream of: this is what they find lovely in you, and travelling or studying together can feel like a gift.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is where life opens out: long journeys, foreign places, study, belief, the questions you keep asking about what things mean. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) brings appreciation and the pleasure of affinity. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), it turns their affection towards your outlook.

You may feel admired for what you think and believe, not only for how you look or what you do. They enjoy your stories from abroad, the books you press on them, the convictions you hold, and may introduce you to their own culture, music or faith with real pleasure. Travelling together often goes well, and so does learning: a class, a language, a long conversation in a café far from home.

Your worldview draws them. With you they may feel their own horizons widen, and they find something beautiful in the way you search for meaning. Tradition called this cadent house the God, the place of the Sun's joy, and [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), its natural sign, belongs to Jupiter: generosity, faith, the long road.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The far-off can be easier to love than the everyday. A bond that glows on holiday or in big conversations may struggle with the washing-up on a Tuesday, and an idealised picture of who you are, the traveller, the thinker, can leave little room for the ordinary person. Differences of belief can also be smoothed over for the sake of harmony instead of talked through.

They may feel loved as an adventure rather than as a partner for daily life, or admired as a teacher without being known. If you idealise their culture or their faith, they may feel seen as a symbol rather than a person.

Late in your 9th, close to your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), their Venus also brushes the top of your chart, and their appreciation of your ideas spills over into appreciation of your work and public life.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Bring the horizon home: share the ordinary days as well as the trips. Ask about the beliefs that matter to each of you, and be curious rather than polite. With a partner, plan journeys and also a normal week. With a friend, this is the travel companion or the study partner. With a colleague, teaching, training or publishing together. Within a family, an in-law from another culture can become a bridge rather than a guest.

Turned the other way, as your Venus in their 9th house, it's their outlook that you find beautiful. Of its neighbours, [their Venus in your 8th house](/astrology/venus-in-8th-house-synastry/) is about intimacy and trust, and [their Venus in your 10th house](/astrology/venus-in-10th-house-synastry/) about admiration for your work.

Their Venus is never far from their Sun and moves slowly, so their birth date places it. The open question is where your 9th ends, and that is your Midheaven, which in Placidus, the house system I use, depends on the minute you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A romance that began on a journey is often too new for a reading: give it a few months, and come back together ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#we-just-met)).
