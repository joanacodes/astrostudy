Of the five Neptune contacts with Uranus, this is the only one that everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets, and the first pass always comes by about 67. Over some two and a half years, your appetite for change and your capacity to imagine pull together.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) wants what's new: invention, independence, the reform of whatever has stopped working. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings intuition, compassion and the sense of something larger than yourself. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, usually links two signs of the same element, and under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your inventiveness listens to your empathy.

You may see it in hunches that prove sound, in an unconventional choice the people around you take in their stride, or in a habit or a role that no longer fits and that you leave without slamming the door. Interests that once seemed too far out, in psychology, the spiritual or new forms of art, can open quietly. Ideas about how a system could work better can come more easily, and so can the patience to explain them. The trine works on what you set in motion and leaves the rest where it was.

Your houses make it specific. If Uranus sits in your 5th, Neptune most often trines it from your 9th or your 1st: a philosophy, a journey or a course of study ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)), or a softer way of presenting yourself, can give your creative independence a direction. Those houses come from your birth time, which the date alone can't give ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

If your chart carries [Uranus trine Neptune](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune/), as many from the late 1930s to the mid-1940s do, the transit came in your first years and again in your mid-fifties, or only once, in your early fifties, if Neptune had already passed the exact trine at your birth.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after, a pass runs for about two years and seven months, the shortest about twenty-two months, and touches the exact trine three times in most passes, five in about a quarter.

The birth year sets the age of the first pass: about 47 for someone born at the start of 1935, around the first birthday for 1945, then about 13 for 1955, 25 for 1965, 36 for 1975, 46 for 1985, 54 for 1995 and 61½ for 2005. No one in my count waits past about 67.

Your Uranus has two trine points, 120° on either side, and Neptune needs about 55 years to travel from one to the other. About 1 person in 3, mostly born between the early 1940s and the mid-1960s, meets both before 80: at about 1 and 57 for the start of 1945, at about 13 and 68 for 1955.

Between 2024 and 2037, from the last degree of Pisces to late Aries, Neptune trines Uranus in Sagittarius ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)): people born with Uranus there, from late 1981 to 1988, meet this transit in those years, at about 42 to 49.

A birth at the start of 1965 shows the order: [transiting Neptune square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-uranus/) at about 11, this trine at about 25, [transiting Neptune opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-uranus/) at about 52 and the trine again at about 79, while [transiting Neptune sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-uranus/) stay out of reach before 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the idea you keep coming back to a working form, small enough to finish while the trine lasts: a prototype, a pilot, a first season.

Note the hunches that arrive when you're half awake, and test the ones that return.

Write the vision down in plain words. A trine makes ideas flow; a clear page keeps them from flowing away.
