This is the one Neptune contact with Uranus that every person born between 1930 and 2009 meets, and always by about 67. For about two and a half years, the part of you that wants to change things and the part that imagines how they could be run in the same direction.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) looks for another way: innovation, independence, the reform of whatever has stopped working. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings imagination, compassion and the sense of something shared. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets two functions reinforce each other while each keeps its character, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) invention gains a heart and imagination gains a method.

It often shows as a change you've wanted to make finally finding its form: work that uses technology for something humane, a way of living with more freedom and more meaning, a community or a creative project that runs on both ideas and goodwill. Intuitions about how a system could work better come more easily, and so does the patience to explain them. Ease isn't the same as impact, though: the trine helps whatever you set in motion, and leaves alone what you don't.

With Uranus in your 1st, the trine often comes while Neptune crosses your 9th or your 5th: a philosophy, a journey or a course of study ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)), or a creative pleasure, gives your independence a direction. Where your Uranus falls, and which houses Neptune crosses, depends entirely on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The generation born under [Uranus trine Neptune](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune/), from 1936 to 1946, carries this harmony from birth; for someone born at the start of 1945 the transit itself came within the first year.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured with my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, each pass lasts about two years and seven months and the briefest about twenty-two; most reach the exact trine three times, about a quarter five times.

Everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets it at least once, and for some it comes twice. Each later birth year pushes it later in life: about 13 for someone born at the start of 1955, 25 for 1965, 36 for 1975, 46 for 1985, 54 for 1995 and 61½ for 2005, with about 47 for the start of 1935.

From 2026 to 2039 Neptune in Aries trines Uranus in Sagittarius, so people born with Uranus there, from late 1981 to 1988, meet it in those years, in their forties.

For someone born at the start of 1965, [transiting Neptune square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-uranus/) came at about 11 and this trine at about 25, with [transiting Neptune opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-uranus/) later in life; [transiting Neptune sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-uranus/) don't come for them before 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick the change you've imagined longest and give it a working form: a prototype, a pilot, a first season.

Pair your independence with someone's idealism, or the other way round. Under this trine, the two make a good team.

Write the vision down in plain words. A trine makes ideas flow; a clear page keeps them from flowing away.
