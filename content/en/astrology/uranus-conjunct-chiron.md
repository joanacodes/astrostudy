Uranus and Chiron never met in the eighty years of the count: no birth between 1930 and 2009 has the two within 6° of each other. The meeting still has a meaning worth setting out, and its transit reaches many people born in those years.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, differentiation and liberation: it insists on a way of its own and reforms systems that leave no room for one. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a tender place, a limit you keep meeting, and the skill you build by adapting around it: a new method, a different tool, a setting arranged so that you can take part. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into a single process without erasing either.

Fused, the two would make your difference and your tender place hard to tell apart. The way you stand out might be the very thing that feels exposed, and the skill would be inventive: working out your own way around a limit, or changing the arrangement so that the limit stops shutting you, and others, out. Neither would swallow the other: the limit wouldn't vanish because Uranus wants freedom, and the freedom wouldn't shrink to fit the limit. Differentiation isn't alienation, and autonomy isn't isolation; this meeting would ask for both lessons at once.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Not a single birth in the count has it. Across the eighty years, the two bodies made every other major aspect: a sextile, a square and a trine in quick succession in the late 1930s and the 1940s, a long opposition from 1951 to 1990, then a trine, a square and a sextile again in the 1990s. The meeting falls outside, on both sides. Chiron was found in 1977, but its positions before then are calculated from its orbit, and the count used them like any other.

The pair's other aspects, with the births that have them:

- **[Uranus sextile Chiron](/astrology/uranus-sextile-chiron/):** November 1938 to August 1941, and November 1999 to November 2001.
- **[Uranus square Chiron](/astrology/uranus-square-chiron/):** October 1942 to August 1944, and November 1996 to September 1998.
- **[Uranus trine Chiron](/astrology/uranus-trine-chiron/):** January 1945, October 1945 to October 1946, and September 1994 to July 1996.
- **[Uranus opposite Chiron](/astrology/uranus-opposite-chiron/):** on and off from February 1951 to May 1990, about 1 birth in 4.

Between two charts, [Uranus conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-chiron-synastry/) does occur: one person's Uranus on the other's Chiron, as between someone with Uranus in Capricorn, born from 1988 to 1995, and someone with Chiron in Capricorn, born from 2001 to 2005. One person's insistence on difference lands on the other's tender place, which can free it or press on it. I read it gently, in both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-uranus/) comes at ages that vary a great deal between generations, because Chiron's pace varies so much: under two years in some signs, about eight in others. It can last months or stretch over a couple of years.

The reverse, transiting Uranus conjunct natal Chiron, comes once in Uranus's 84-year round. For the many people born with the opposition, from 1951 to 1990, it falls at the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), around 42: the point opposite natal Uranus is where their Chiron sits. Where it lands depends on your houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Weather, not a forecast: a season when an old tender place meets the urge to live differently, and what comes of it is yours to shape.
