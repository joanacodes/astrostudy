The 7th house starts at the Descendant, the western point of the horizon, opposite your Ascendant, and it holds the people you meet as equals. When Pluto crosses that point, the depth it brings is shared, and so is the work.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) looks for what is hidden and asks who holds the power. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is the other person: a partner, a close collaborator, anyone you face as an equal, and in the old books the open adversary too. With Pluto passing through, relationships tend to lose their surface politeness. Bargains you never spoke aloud, about who gives way, who decides and who needs whom more, come up and ask to be renegotiated. You may find yourself drawn to people with depth and impatient with anything shallow, or recognise in a partner an intensity of your own that you had left for them to carry. Contracts and agreements, personal or professional, tend to get read more carefully.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 7th begins at the Descendant, so the stay opens with Pluto on that point; a natal Sun in the 7th, met later, is asked how much of yourself you have been lending to others.

Books sometimes promise a fated meeting here, or warn of power struggles and endings. I don't forecast either. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) works with what your own chart holds, and Pluto in the 7th predicts no wedding and no break-up: it describes the depth and honesty your closest bonds are asked for, over many years. Because the 7th involves another person, I read it from your side only; their chart is theirs to share ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 7th and the 8th are where Pluto's stays run longest in the middle. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the 7th keeps Pluto about 24 years, against about 20 for all twelve houses together, and most stays fall between about 10 and 37, the same broad spread as the average. At New York's latitude the middle is also about 24 years, with most stays between about 12 and 33.

Your Descendant opens the stay and the cusp of your 8th closes it. Both come from your birth time; without it I can place neither, so I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Before the 7th, Pluto works through your routines and daily work ([Pluto transiting the 6th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-6th-house/)); after it, through what you share and merge with others ([Pluto transiting the 8th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-8th-house/)).

Through the whole stretch I counted, from 2026 to the end of 2039, Pluto is in Aquarius, which it first entered in March 2023 and has not left since November 2024. If your Descendant is in Aquarius, which means Leo rising, the crossing is now or coming, and your degree sets the year. With the Descendant in Capricorn, it ran between January 2008 and November 2024; in Pisces, it lies after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say the unspoken agreements out loud, gently, one at a time. Pluto in the 7th makes hidden contracts heavy; spoken ones can be changed.

Take back what you've asked a partner to carry for you: your ambition, your anger, your depth. A relationship breathes better when each of you holds your own.

Choose your allies with care, at work as in love. This is a long season for partnerships with real substance, and it favours the ones built on candour.
