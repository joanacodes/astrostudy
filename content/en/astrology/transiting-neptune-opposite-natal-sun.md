Opposite your Sun is where Neptune stands furthest from it, and an opposition tends to work through other people. For about two and a half years, a partner's need, a friend's ideal or a cause bigger than you stands across from your sense of direction, and the question is where yours ends and theirs begins.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) opens boundaries for compassion and imagination; your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) keeps your purpose and identity coherent. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates them through polarity, each defined by what faces it. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), what Neptune brings tends to arrive from across the room: someone you want to help, someone who idealises you, a collective hope you want to serve. Compassion isn't self-erasure, and the opposition is where that distinction gets tested. It can run the other way too: you may find yourself carrying someone else's picture of who you should be, and mistaking it for your own direction.

A Sun in the 1st puts Neptune in your 7th ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)), where a partner's or a close collaborator's dreams press closest; a Sun in the 4th puts it in your 10th, and home and public life trade ideals. These houses come from your birth time: no birth time, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

I don't read a separation, a betrayal or any other event from it ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). The opposition describes how close other people's wishes come to yours, not what anyone will do. What I look at is the balance: how much of your week, your attention and your plans is still steered by you.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The opposition stays within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, for about two and a half years, or not quite two when Neptune's loops are tight: long enough for a relationship or a commitment to show which way the balance leans. Most passes cross the exact point three times; about one in four crosses it five times.

By 80, just under half of people born between 1930 and 2009 have never met it, and nobody meets it twice, since Neptune's round lasts about 165 years. It's the furthest Neptune gets from your Sun. The conjunction ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-sun/)) lies half a circle away, and the sextile ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-sun/)), the square ([transiting Neptune square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-sun/)) and the trine ([transiting Neptune trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-sun/)) fall on either side between the two. [Sun opposite Neptune](/astrology/sun-opposite-neptune/) in a birth chart is this polarity for life; as a transit it stays a couple of years and moves on.

Libra Suns, birthdays from about 23 September to 22 October, face Neptune in Aries until March 2039, the earliest first.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Before you give, ask what is yours to give. Time and attention run out, and Neptune's generosity forgets it.

Keep part of the week for your own direction, however small: a project nobody else depends on, and nobody else gets to redesign.

Check idealisation both ways: the person you see as wonderful, and the one who sees you that way. Both pictures can be partly true, and neither is the whole person.
