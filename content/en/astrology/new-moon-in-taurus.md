The new moon in Taurus comes once a year, when the Sun and Moon meet in fixed earth, in the middle of the northern spring. Tradition exalts the Moon in Taurus, and I read this new moon as a quiet marker for building, securing and enjoying, wherever Taurus sits in your chart.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) joins the Sun and Moon in one sign: the Sun's sense of purpose and direction, and the Moon's needs and feeling of safety, starting from the same place. In [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), that place is earth and fixed. Taurus builds rather than launches, holds rather than chases, and wants to feel things with its hands. Venus rules it, the planet of what we value and enjoy, and tradition puts the Moon's exaltation here: its needs are at ease in a sign that takes comfort seriously. Taurus is also a fixed sign, the middle of the northern spring, so this new moon tends to steady what the Aries one set going.

The dates, in Universal Time:

- **2026:** 16 May, 20:01 UT, at 25°58′ Taurus
- **2027:** 6 May, 10:59 UT, at 15°42′ Taurus
- **2028:** 24 April, 19:47 UT, at 5°09′ Taurus
- **2029:** 13 May, 13:42 UT, at 23°05′ Taurus
- **2030:** 2 May, 14:12 UT, at 12°14′ Taurus

The 2028 one comes earliest, about five days after the Sun enters Taurus. There are no eclipses among them: the last solar eclipse in Taurus fell on 30 April 2022, and the next is the annular eclipse of 9 May 2032 ([eclipse in Taurus](/astrology/eclipse-in-taurus/)).

A new moon is brief, and I keep it in proportion. It counts in a birth chart only within a degree of a planet or angle, and even then as a moment of focus inside a period that something slower, a transit or a progression, has already opened. On its own it forecasts nothing. The 2028 new moon at 5°09′ Taurus, for instance, says something only to a chart with a planet or angle within a degree of that point.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

From 2026 to 2030 the new moon in Taurus falls anywhere between 5° and 26° of the sign, so where Taurus spans two of your houses, it can visit both in different years. Which houses those are takes [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); I use Placidus houses.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/taurus-rising/):** Taurus rising: a steadier way of carrying yourself; settling into your own pace and your own body.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/taurus-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** earnings, belongings, self-worth; a patient start on what you want to keep and what you're worth to yourself.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/taurus-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** daily exchanges, learning, siblings; a practical skill learned slowly, or a talk that settles something.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home and roots; comfort under your roof, a garden, a room made to last.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/taurus-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasure, romance, making things; a craft done by hand, a courtship that takes its time.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/):** the rhythm of your working days; a routine you can actually keep.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/):** partners, with Scorpio rising; steadiness you look for in someone else, or a commitment that grows step by step.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/taurus-on-8th-house-cusp/):** joint money and trust; shared arrangements put on solid ground.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/taurus-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs, study, travel; learning with a practical end, a journey taken for its pleasures.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/):** career and standing; something durable built at work, a reputation earned slowly.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/taurus-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends and groups; loyal friendships, a group with a concrete aim.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/taurus-on-12th-house-cusp/):** solitude and rest; quiet comforts alone, rest you don't have to justify.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look up the degree in your chart first. Note the house it falls in, then check for any planet or angle within a degree of it. With no contact, treat it as the month's mood in that house: a good moment to secure something small and real, with nothing you need to fear or chase.

With contact, ask what a slower movement is already doing to that point; the new moon will only throw light on it. Taurus' manner helps here: make sure of what you need first, then build on it at your own pace. Venus, by its house in your chart, shows where comfort and value come from for you.

None of this is a rule or a promise. A new moon doesn't decide a purchase, a contract or a love life, and it never predicts what money will do. A fortnight later the Moon is full in Scorpio, and in autumn a full moon returns to Taurus. Between the [new moon in Aries](/astrology/new-moon-in-aries/) and the [new moon in Gemini](/astrology/new-moon-in-gemini/), this is the one that asks you to slow down.
