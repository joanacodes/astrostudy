A Sagittarius Sun believes in the large picture; a Virgo Moon feels safe once the small parts work. Your birth came around the last quarter, the half moon of the small hours, and to me this pair is a philosophy with careful hands, open to revision.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Virgo sits three signs before Sagittarius: a square by sign between two mutable signs, earth against fire. The [Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/) grows by stepping back far enough to see what a life is for; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) finds its footing in the particular: the right tool, the checked fact, the small repair that means a thing will work again tomorrow. Both signs adapt, in opposite directions, one by widening and one by refining, so the same day can hold a grand plan and a careful list of what it will need.

Each ruler is uneasy where the other light stands. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) has both its rulership and its exaltation in Virgo, so your Moon's ruler is strongest in your Moon's own sign, and it has its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Sagittarius, your Sun's. As for [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), your Sun's ruler, its detriment falls in Virgo by the older scheme, which gave it Pisces as well as Sagittarius. Your lights take no dignity from these signs. I read the two rulers as two speeds of thought, Mercury's close attention and Jupiter's wide synthesis; their placements, and any aspect between them, show whether they take turns or interrupt each other.

You arrived after the full moon, around the last quarter. The Moon was 241° to 299° ahead of the Sun, which is 61° to 119° behind it, twenty to twenty-four and a half days after the new moon: a Moon of the early hours, still up in the morning sky after sunrise. Half these births fall in the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) and half in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/), and at 270° the square is exact, a waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). A last quarter birth is to me a time for review, keeping what has proved itself and clearing the rest, and a Virgo Moon does that by instinct; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the turn at 270°.

About 1 person in 146 is born with this pair. A birthday when the Moon left Leo or crossed into Libra needs the hour to settle it between this page, the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-leo/) and the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-libra/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)), and near the quarter it decides your phase as well. The rising sign waits on the same hour, and so do the houses, which say where this square does its daily work.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You show love by being useful and by being honest, sometimes in the same sentence. The Virgo Moon mends the bicycle, books the appointment, notices the dead lightbulb before anyone else; the Sagittarius Sun tells the truth and wants it told back. A partner may need help hearing the care inside your suggestions, and you may need help hearing praise without correcting it. What steadies the bond is a shared project with room for both: a trip you plan together, the Moon handling the details while the Sun chooses where.

## At work {#at-work}

Research, editing, methodical teaching, translation, the design of courses or of journeys: work where a big aim is served by precise means suits this pair. The Moon checks and improves; the Sun keeps asking what the whole thing is for, and together they produce work that holds up under a close look. Vague missions irritate the Moon, and work with no purpose beyond its procedures drains the Sun. Look for the role that gives you a large question and the time to answer it properly.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Under strain, each light finds fault with the other's way. The Sun's promises start to look careless to the Moon, the Moon's lists start to look small to the Sun, and you swing between saying yes to everything and second-guessing every yes. The inner reviewer can turn on your own beliefs until nothing seems worth starting. What helps is taking turns on purpose: choose the direction first, with the Sun, then hand the plan to the Moon, with a deadline after which it is good enough.
