Moon opposite Mars in synastry is a face-off across the chart between one person's Moon and the other's Mars. The need to rest and the urge to act look at each other from either end: often a strong pull, sometimes a tug of war, and always a question of pace.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is where a person goes to recover: comfort, routine, the feelings that need tending. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) is where they go to act: effort, courage, the line they won't let anyone cross. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions in polarity, each making the other easier to see, and none of this pair's other [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) sets push and pause so far apart.

Between them, each has what the other lacks. With the Moon on your side, the other person brings drive and nerve to what you'd rather put off: they'll make the call, have the difficult conversation, carry the heavy thing. Around them you may feel braver, and protected.

From the Mars side, the other person brings a reason to slow down: a home to come back to, a feeling worth stopping for. Around them you may discover that rest isn't defeat.

The signs give the seesaw its subject. With a Moon in Taurus and Mars in Scorpio, a need for calm and comfort faces an intensity that won't leave things alone; with a Moon in Sagittarius and Mars in Gemini, a need for room faces a restless, talkative push.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The seesaw can harden into roles: the doer and the worrier, the one who charges ahead and the one who gets left to pick up the feelings. The Moon person may feel pressed, rushed or managed; the Mars person may feel held back, criticised, or blamed for having energy at all. On a bad day, one wants to sort it out at once and the other wants to be left alone, or the other way round.

Opposite isn't hostile: a seesaw with only one person on it doesn't move.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let each end have its turn. If Mars is yours, ask before you push, and once in a while be the one who stops; if the Moon is yours, request the energy you want, and sometimes be the one who starts. At work, it's often the person who drives a project paired with the one who notices the team is tired, valuable if both are heard. Among friends and adult relatives, and in couples, it helps to agree in a calm moment how a pause will be asked for, and how it will end.

Around 1 pair of people in 12 has this opposition within 8°, with the Moon on one side or the other. In the hours either side of the right time, the Moon can slip into this opposition or out of it: it moves about 13° a day, while Mars, six or seven weeks to a sign, hardly moves at all. Both birth times are essential, for the houses as much as for the contact ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Inside one chart, the polarity is [Moon opposite Mars](/astrology/moon-opposite-mars/). [Moon conjunct Mars in synastry](/astrology/moon-conjunct-mars-synastry/) puts the push right on the need; [Moon square Mars in synastry](/astrology/moon-square-mars-synastry/) sets them at cross-purposes rather than face to face; [Moon sextile Mars in synastry](/astrology/moon-sextile-mars-synastry/) and [Moon trine Mars in synastry](/astrology/moon-trine-mars-synastry/) let them work as a team.
