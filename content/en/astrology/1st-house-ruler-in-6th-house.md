Your chart ruler, the planet of your rising sign, has settled in the 6th house, among the work, habits and routines of an ordinary day. You become who you are by doing things, and by doing them well.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Whatever planet rules your rising sign rules the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) too, and as your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/) it takes the self described by the Ascendant to the house where it sits ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the working day: the tasks rather than the title, the habits that hold you up, the colleagues beside you, the way you organise yourself, and the care you give your body, from sleep and food to movement and rest.

I read it as a self shaped by practice. You tend to know yourself through what you can do and how carefully you do it, and people often know you first by your work: the reliable one, the one who fixes it, the one who notices what's missing. Craft suits you, something you get better at year after year. Routines aren't a cage here but a way of being yourself, and a good week, for you, is often a well-run one. The difficulty is the mirror image. When usefulness becomes the measure of your worth, it gets hard to stop, and rest can start to feel like a fault. Service isn't servitude, and part of this placement's work is finding where one ends and the other begins.

The 1st describes your body and the 6th its daily upkeep, so this ruler ties the two together: how you feel in yourself and how you look after yourself tend to move as one. The old books looked in the 6th for illness, too. I don't read illness in a chart, and nothing here forecasts your health, which belongs with a doctor ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). What I read is the habit of care.

Old astrology was hard on a ruler here in other ways. In horary, a planet in the 6th, 8th or 12th counted as accidentally debilitated, held back by its place rather than its nature; and counted by sign, the 6th sign from your Ascendant makes no aspect at all to the rising sign. I read that as a ruler that works without much of a stage. With Aries or Scorpio rising, though, your chart ruler is Mars, and the 6th is its joy, the house where tradition said the planet of effort was glad to be ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). Toil suits it.

In Placidus, the house system I use, a ruler in the 6th had set below the western horizon a little before your birth. One house back, the same planet would point your life towards play and pleasure ([Ruler of the 1st in the 5th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)); one house on, across the Descendant, towards partners and the people you meet one to one ([Ruler of the 1st in the 7th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)). The Descendant moves with the clock like every angle, so a ruler close to it needs an exact birth time before I say which side it's on ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

With Aquarius rising, the chart answers to Saturn and Uranus, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Saturn in the 6th makes a patient, exacting worker of you, while Uranus, from its own house, keeps a door open to doing things differently. With Pisces rising the pair is Jupiter and Neptune, with Scorpio rising Mars and Pluto, and when only one of the two sits in the 6th, the other shows where the rest of your life pulls.
