Mercury sextile Mars in synastry sets about 60° between one person's thinking and the other's drive. Neither pushes; each hands the other a way in: a plan for the energy, and energy for the plan.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the thinking, talking part of a person: noticing, connecting, explaining, asking. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the part that gets up and goes: wanting, starting, pushing through, defending a limit. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the two through opportunity, which here means a standing offer between two people to turn talk into steps.

If Mercury is your side of it, your suggestions tend to become someone's first move. You may find that more of what you talk about actually happens around this person. With your Mercury in Gemini and their Mars in Leo, your quick, many-sided ideas give their warm, wholehearted push something to aim at, and they pick the one worth doing.

If Mars is your side of it, the other's words give you a place to start. Their plans are concrete enough to act on, and they often find the right phrase for what you're about to do, which makes it easier to do it well. Your energy, in turn, is what gets their ideas off the page, and your readiness to try lends their thinking the deadline many good ideas need.

What comes easily is practical cooperation: training partners who keep each other turning up, two colleagues who split a job into "you plan, I start", siblings organising a family move without a single row.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The opening can go unused. Both people may enjoy talking about what they'll do so much that the talking becomes the activity. The Mars person holds back until asked; the Mercury person holds back until sure. At 3.6°, the window is small, so the contact tends to hum rather than ring, and it's easy to underrate next to louder ones.

There's a smaller risk too: when one person always plans and the other always acts, the roles can set, and each loses the half they're not practising.

The other angles of the pair turn the exchange up or down:

- **[Mercury conjunct Mars in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-mars-synastry/):** no gap between saying and doing; faster, and harder to tell apart.
- **[Mercury square Mars in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-mars-synastry/):** the cooperation turns into debate, which tests every plan.
- **[Mercury trine Mars in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-mars-synastry/):** double the angle, and the relay runs without anyone asking.
- **[Mercury opposite Mars in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-mars-synastry/):** talk and action at opposite ends, pulling the pair two ways.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give it projects, with short deadlines. End each conversation by naming the first step and who takes it. Every so often, trade jobs: the Mars person draws up the plan, the Mercury person makes the first call. Couples can use it for the jobs nobody enjoys; friends, for the trip that keeps getting postponed; a parent and a grown child, for the paperwork that has waited all year.

About 1 pair of people in 13 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 3.6°. It's one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) that shows itself in shared tasks more than in feelings, which is why I look at what a pair actually does together when I read it. Over a few hours Mercury and Mars barely shift, so hardly any birth time can make or break the sextile; the houses, though, hang on both times, and both are on my list before any reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Mercury sextile Mars](/astrology/mercury-sextile-mars/) is the same opening inside a single chart, between a person's own ideas and their own drive.
