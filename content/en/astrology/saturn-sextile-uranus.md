Saturn sextile Uranus is a generation's aspect of workable reform: structure and change two signs apart, in elements that get on, ready to cooperate whenever someone does the work of bringing them together.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) gives shape and duration: responsibility, discipline, the boundaries that let something stand. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) gives difference and release: invention, autonomy, the reform of systems that no longer fit. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperative activation, a working link that needs someone to use it.

In plain words, the new idea and the sensible plan can be introduced to each other. You may find you're good at the step between invention and implementation: turning a different way of doing things into a procedure other people can follow, or bringing a fresh approach into an institution without tearing it down. The sextile doesn't do this on its own. It asks you to get involved, because potential doesn't realise itself.

This is the quietest of the pair's aspects, and that's its strength: change at a pace that structures can absorb.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The sextile shows up for about 1 person in 14 in the births of 1930 to 2009, within 6°, in four spells of a year or two each: 1934–36, 1947–49, 1978–81 and 1995–97, each with gaps as the retrogrades moved the pair in and out of orb. They fall several years either side of the two conjunctions, of 1942 and 1988. Shared by everyone born then, the sextile reads as a generation's resource more than a personal trait, until the houses place it.

A sextile usually pairs compatible elements, so structure and change speak languages that understand each other. In the houses, Saturn in the 1st sextile Uranus in the 11th might make you a steady presence in groups that want to change things; Saturn in the 5th sextile Uranus in the 7th, committed creativity in partnerships that stay alive. For the houses, I need the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Saturn, the faster of the two, is the one that makes and unmakes the angle.

The same two planets at other angles:

- **[Saturn conjunct Uranus](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-uranus/):** the generations of 1941–43 and 1987–89; structure and reform fused, not merely introduced.
- **[Saturn square Uranus](/astrology/saturn-square-uranus/):** born in the early 1930s, the early 1950s, the mid-1970s or around the turn of the century; the link becomes friction, and reform is earned against resistance.
- **[Saturn trine Uranus](/astrology/saturn-trine-uranus/):** born in the later 1950s, the early 1970s or the early 2000s; the cooperation runs by itself, needing less of the effort this sextile asks.
- **[Saturn opposite Uranus](/astrology/saturn-opposite-uranus/):** born in the mid-1960s or in 2008–09; stability and freedom face each other, and other people often carry one side.

Between two charts, [Saturn sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/saturn-sextile-uranus-synastry/) can make one person's reliability and the other's originality a useful team, if both take the opening; the link doesn't taste the same from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Saturn sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-uranus/) arrives twice per Saturn lap of about 29½ years, for some weeks or, with retrogrades, a few months: a good season, many astrologers say, to give an idea a structure. [Transiting Uranus sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-saturn/) lasts longer, a year or so close to exact, since Uranus spends about 7 years in a sign. Born with the sextile, your [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/) repeats it from Saturn's side.

Weather, then, and not a forecast: openings for reform that still need someone to walk through them.
