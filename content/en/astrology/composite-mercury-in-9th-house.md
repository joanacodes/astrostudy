Some pairs can turn a weekend away into a seminar, and a seminar into a weekend away. Composite Mercury in the 9th house describes a relationship whose mind is happiest at the edge of what it knows.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the relationship's [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the midpoint of your two natal Mercurys, and its house says where that thinking travels. [The 9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is the far horizon: higher learning, travel, philosophy, faith, law, teaching, the ideas a life is organised around. It is cadent, Sagittarius is its natural sign, and its old name was the God, the house where the Sun rejoices.

A relationship with this Mercury learns together. It reads the same books and argues about them, takes courses, plans journeys long before it can afford them and comes home with a new word or a new question. It may cross a language border, or a cultural one, and enjoy the translating. Big subjects come up naturally (what's true, what's fair, what life is for), and disagreements about them can be among the best conversations it has. Its arguments tend to end in a reading list rather than a sulk. A natal [Mercury in the 9th house](/astrology/mercury-in-9th-house/) feeds one person on study and travel; [their Mercury in your 9th house](/astrology/mercury-in-9th-house-synastry/) is someone who widens another's horizon. In the composite, the horizon belongs to both, and each of you may travel further in thought with the other than alone.

Composite Mercury normally keeps within about 28° of the composite Sun, so the Sun is usually in the 9th or close to it. The exception, about 1 pair in 26, puts the composite Sun across the chart around the 3rd: the near and the far facing each other, through midpoints rather than meaning.

The 9th climbs towards the composite [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), which is the midpoint of your two natal Midheavens whichever way the houses are drawn. The 9th's own starting cusp is where the methods part: one takes the midpoint of your two natal 9th cusps, the other rebuilds it from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live. Either way, both birth times are needed ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What can go wrong is the lecture. A bond that loves big ideas can talk about how to live instead of living, plan journeys it never takes, or let one of you become the permanent teacher and the other the pupil. Learning isn't superiority, and a belief isn't a fact because it was well argued. Taking turns to lead the conversation helps, and so does bringing each big idea down to one thing you'll actually do.

Between [composite Mercury in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-8th-house/), which talks about what's shared and owed, and [composite Mercury in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-10th-house/), which speaks in public, the 9th asks what it all means. I teach composites here as a technique and don't use them in readings; astrologers who do set them beside both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), with both people's agreement first ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
