Twice in each round of twelve years, a third of the zodiac separates Jupiter from your Sun: four years into the round, and four years before its end. Nothing pushes under this aspect: your sense of direction gets a following wind, which is exactly why it is so easy to waste.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) supports growth and looks for meaning; your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your centre, the vitality and purpose you build a life around. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates them through flow while each keeps its own nature, so growth and purpose walk at the same pace for a while and effort meets less resistance than usual.

The pairing has an old logic. Leo, the Sun's sign, and Sagittarius, Jupiter's, are both fire signs, four signs apart: in the natural zodiac, the trine is the angle between the Sun's home and Jupiter's, and astrologers have long read the two as easy company.

Day to day, it tends to feel like confidence that doesn't need to prove itself, plans that grow without being forced, people who seem readier to say yes. The trap is the trine's usual one: an easy season does nothing by itself, and flow can turn into drift. A season like this pays whoever uses it on purpose, for learning, teaching, making something, or being generous with what you know.

Which house Jupiter is crossing gives the ease its subject. From your 5th, the house tradition called Good Fortune ([Jupiter transiting the 5th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-5th-house/)), it often runs into creative work and play; your Sun's house says what that ease is for. For both, I need your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): it is what turns this [transit](/astrology/transits/) from a date into a reading. Born with [Sun trine Jupiter](/astrology/sun-trine-jupiter/), you'll meet one of these trines at each Jupiter return, a reminder of the quiet confidence you were born with.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine lasts about eleven days when Jupiter passes straight through, and about eight months in the three cases out of ten where Jupiter's loop lands on it and makes three exact hits.

Jupiter turns round when it stands about 115° from the Sun, close to a trine. So if one of Jupiter's [stations](/astrology/stations/) lands on the trine to your Sun, it lands within about a week of your birthday: a trine that barely moves, in the days the Sun comes back to its birth place.

By 80, anyone born between 1930 and 2009 has felt this following wind on their Sun thirteen or fourteen times. The first trine of a round, four years after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-sun/), tends to carry what began at the crossing into a form that works. The second, eight years in and two years after [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-sun/), lets you use what other people showed you then. Where [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-sun/) asks you to reach and [transiting Jupiter square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-sun/) makes you adjust, the trine smooths the road you're already on.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose what to lean on it with before it starts: the project, the course, the conversation. A trine helps whatever is already moving, and waiting to see what turns up usually means nothing does.

Use the ease for something that normally costs you courage: ask for the bigger part, show the work, say what you think in a room that matters to you.

Note what came easily during these weeks. Trines are often seen only afterwards, and the list tells you where your strengths and the world agreed.
