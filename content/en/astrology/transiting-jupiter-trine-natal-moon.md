Twice in every twelve years, Jupiter stands in a sign of your Moon's element, a third of the zodiac away. These are the weeks when feeling better takes less effort than usual, and they're over before many people notice them.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens and gives experience a meaning; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) processes feeling and keeps you feeling safe. At 120°, a [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets the two work together without either giving way: for a while, what you need and what life offers line up with little friction, and moods tend to recover faster than usual.

Usually it feels like ease at home, warmth that goes both ways, a lighter hold on old worries, some perspective on a feeling that once took all your room. You still have to use it. The trine helps what you do with it: a conversation you'd been putting off goes better, a gathering comes together, rest actually rests you. Responding comes more easily too: you notice what other people need without losing track of what you need yourself.

There's an old symmetry behind it. Cancer belongs to the Moon, and tradition placed Jupiter's exaltation there; Pisces, Jupiter's traditional home (Neptune is its modern ruler, and I read both), stands a trine away in the same element, water. The trine between the two planets echoes the trine between the signs where tradition placed them at their best.

The ease takes its subject from the house Jupiter is crossing. From your 12th ([Jupiter transiting the 12th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-12th-house/)), it tends to come through quiet: time alone, sleep, a private faith, care given without anyone needing to know. If [Moon trine Jupiter](/astrology/moon-trine-jupiter/) is in your own chart, each Jupiter return repeats one of these trines, and the ease with feeling you were born with has its season in the sky again.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted with my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine lasts about eleven days, or about eight months when a retrograde loop brings Jupiter over it three times, as about three passes in ten do.

Even the Moon's sign can hang on the birth time. The Moon spends only about two and a half days in a sign, so on some birthdays the hour decides which element your Moon is in, and so which signs can trine it at all. Its degree, and with it the dates of this [transit](/astrology/transits/), always depends on the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Thirteen or fourteen times by 80, for everyone born between 1930 and 2009, Jupiter lends your Moon this ease. The first trine comes about four years after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-moon/) and tends to make the new comforts of that crossing feel natural; the second comes two years after [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-moon/), when home and the wider world can sit together more easily. Where the conjunction fills your inner space and the opposition stretches it, the trine asks nothing at all; [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-moon/) at least wants a yes, and [transiting Jupiter square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-moon/) a change of habit.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Use the ease for something that's usually hard: the honest conversation at home, the request for help, the apology.

Rest properly while rest comes easily, and notice what restores you. It's worth knowing when the harder angles come round.

Pass the warmth on. This trine makes care flow both ways, and inviting people in, cooking, listening keep it moving instead of letting it settle into mere comfort.
