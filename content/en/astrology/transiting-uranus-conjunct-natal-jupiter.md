Once in its 84-year round, Uranus crosses your Jupiter, and the age at which it happens depends more on the year you were born than on anything else. For about twenty months, growth stops following the plan.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands understanding: meaning, belief, learning, the sense of what's possible. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and breaks through. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two for a while, so growth itself takes on Uranus's character: it arrives sideways, from a subject you weren't studying or a door you didn't know was there.

It can look like a sudden interest that becomes a vocation, a journey that changes how you see home, a teacher or a book that rearranges your beliefs, or the freedom to work at something larger than your current role. Principles get updated. What you used to think was true may turn out to be one view among several, and that tends to feel like relief rather than loss.

The blind spot here is mistaking difference for accuracy: a new belief isn't truer for being new. A wider horizon isn't a payout either, and I wouldn't move a penny on the strength of this transit or any other ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

A Jupiter in the 9th meets Uranus during the years the planet of change spends in the house of belief and long journeys ([Uranus transiting the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-9th-house/)), and study, travel and conviction are where the widening shows first. The 9th is only yours to read with a birth time, and so is this [transit](/astrology/transits/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the conjunction holds for about twenty months, and the briskest tenth of passes take eleven or fewer. Uranus's yearly retrograde carries it back over your Jupiter's exact degree, which makes three crossings the rule, in more than nine passes out of ten.

Jupiter goes round in about twelve years and laps Uranus about every fourteen, so the gap between Jupiter and Uranus at your birth, and therefore your age for this transit, depends mostly on your birth year. The site's count has someone born at the start of 1955 meeting it at about five months; 1985, at about 8; 1945, at about 23; 1975, at about 31; 1935, at about 43; 2005, at about 51; 1965, at about 57; and 1995, at about 71. Across every birth counted, the middle age is about 40, and for about 1 person in 50 it comes after 80.

The extremes belong to people born with [Jupiter conjunct Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus/): Jupiter just ahead means the transit comes in infancy, as for the 1955 birth above, and Jupiter just behind puts it out of reach until about 84. Uranus's other contacts with your Jupiter, [transiting Uranus sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Uranus square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Uranus trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-jupiter/), sit fourteen, twenty, 28 and 42 years from this one, so their ages are set by your birth year too.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Study something that changes your mind. The conjunction rewards learning that unsettles you a little.

Travel differently: somewhere you'd never have chosen, or somewhere familiar by a route you've never taken.

Test a new belief before you preach it. Live by it for a season and see what it does to your days before you ask anyone else to share it.
