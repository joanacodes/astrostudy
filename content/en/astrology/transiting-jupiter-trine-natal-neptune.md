In each Jupiter round there are two stretches when belief and imagination stop competing and start to carry each other: Jupiter forms a trine to your birth Neptune. Creative ideas come readily, kindness costs little, and the only real risk is drifting.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) holds your capacity to imagine and to feel for others, and a sense of something beyond the everyday. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) believes, widens and looks for meaning. The [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets the two flow into each other without either losing its character: imagination finds a reason, and belief finds an image. People often describe these weeks as easy to be generous in, easy to make things in, and easy to feel part of something larger. Help offered under this trine tends to land well, because it comes without the strain that usually makes giving tiring.

The element tells you where the ease comes from. People born between late 1957 and 1969 have Neptune in Scorpio, and for them Jupiter makes these trines from Cancer, its exaltation, and from Pisces, one of its two traditional homes. Jupiter's next years in those signs run from April 2033 to April 2034, for Pisces, and from May 2037 to June 2038, for Cancer. Other generations meet their trines from other signs, as a rule in the same element as their Neptune.

Of all Jupiter's [transits](/astrology/transits/) to your Neptune, the trine has one particular trap: vagueness. Ease can feel like depth, and a lovely mood can stand in for the work it inspired. Which part of life gets the ease depends on your Neptune's house and on the house Jupiter is crossing; both come from the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). People born with [Jupiter trine Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-trine-neptune/) meet one of these near every [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/), a familiar season by now.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A trine to your Neptune that Jupiter crosses in one go lasts about eleven days, the time it takes to cover my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after. Three times in ten Jupiter turns near the point, and the trine is touched three times over some eight months. Fourteen at most, thirteen at least, by the age of 80 for births from 1930 to 2009.

Two examples from the count: January 1965, Jupiter in Taurus, first trine at about 1.6; January 1955, Jupiter in Cancer, a sign past one trine point, about 7.2.

Where [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-neptune/) floods your Neptune and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-neptune/) hands it to someone else for a while, the trine lets inspiration run in its own channel. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-neptune/) is a smaller opening of the same kind, and [transiting Jupiter square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-neptune/) is the one that checks the results.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Build a habit rather than waiting for a peak. Ten minutes a day of the art, the practice or the kindness you care about will keep the trine's ease long after it has passed.

Write down what comes to you: images, phrases, ideas for helping someone. Neptune's gifts arrive quickly and leave just as fast.

And keep one practical anchor, a date, a budget, a person to report to, so that the flow has a riverbank.
