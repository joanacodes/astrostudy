The birthday party is at their place, the toast at the wedding is theirs, and a friend's first exhibition gets two loud fans in the front row. Composite Jupiter in Leo describes a relationship that grows by celebrating, and is generous where it can be seen.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) puts every planet halfway between its two natal places, and its [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the relationship's way of widening: what it believes in, what it encourages, what it gives. [Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire ruled by the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), gives that growth warmth, visibility and a creative streak. Leo holds none of Jupiter's dignities, though it shares the element of Sagittarius, Jupiter's own sign. Its ruler matters: the composite Sun, the centre the relationship organises itself around, shows what all this generosity serves.

About 1 pair in 11 has it, in two million random pairs born from 1930 to 2009. Two people born while Jupiter crossed Leo share it, everyone born from July 1943 to July 1944, from mid-August 1990 to early September 1991 or from August 2002 to August 2003, for example, and to that extent it belongs to a generation as much as to a couple.

Either way, the warmth is the relationship's own. Two private people can find that their bond is the one that throws the party, puts on the show, stands up to give the speech. [Jupiter in Leo](/astrology/jupiter-in-leo/) describes a person who grows in the open; a composite in Leo makes the open space shared. The pair's generosity is visible and wholehearted: backing each other's work, making things together, a band, a supper club, a garden opened to the street, and cheering other people's successes as loudly as its own. Joy, here, comes close to a principle. Leo is also the natural sign of the 5th house, of play and romance, and [composite Jupiter in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-5th-house/) says something close by house rather than by sign; only your two birth times show whether you have both.

The difficulty is that growth can start to be measured by applause. A relationship that shines in company may find the unseen work harder to value, the dull week, the private repair; one of you can drift into being the audience; and pride can make it hard for the pair to admit that a plan didn't work. Being seen isn't the same as seeking attention, and the bond needn't perform to be real. What helps is keeping some of its best things for the two of you alone, and celebrating the quiet achievements, a hard month got through, an argument mended, with the same warmth as the public ones.

One sign back, [composite Jupiter in Cancer](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-cancer/) is generous at home; one sign on, [composite Jupiter in Virgo](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-virgo/) through useful work. I write about composites as a technique rather than as part of my readings, and those who draw them read this Jupiter with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/): Leo's warmth in a midpoint says nothing about whether a relationship will last. Your two charts go into it, so it's drawn only once you've both agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
