Money is rarely just money, and with their Neptune in your 2nd house it becomes something closer to a story. What you own, what you earn and what you think you're worth take on a softer, more idealistic light whenever they're involved.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves hard edges: it imagines, idealises and feels for others. [The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds what you rely on: what you earn and keep, the talents that pay your way, and the quieter question of what you think you deserve. Through this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their ideals reach your resources.

Around them, possessions may matter less and meaning more. You might give away what you no longer need, price your work by what it means to you rather than by the going rate, or discover that a talent you never took seriously has value. Self-worth can feel kinder too: they rarely measure you by what you have.

To them, your relationship with what you own can look like an ideal: a steadiness they envy, or a lightness about things they admire. They may be generous with you without counting, or see in your skills a gift the world should have. Succedent, the 2nd is where resources settle and build up. Its natural sign, [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), is the most tangible of the twelve, and Neptune is the least tangible of the planets: that meeting is the whole flavour of this overlay.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The trouble is the haze around numbers. Who paid for what, which money was a gift and which a loan, whether the shared holiday came out of your savings or theirs: between you, these details drift. You may undersell your work as a labour of love, or feel that asking them for money would spoil something.

From their side, your values may prove more practical than the picture they held, and they can feel let down when you insist on a price, a receipt or a budget. Or they idealise your stability and lean on it more than either of you noticed.

An overlay in the 2nd describes how two people handle worth and resources and never forecasts a gain or a loss, which no chart can do ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep the dream and write down the figures. A short note of what was lent, a shared spreadsheet, an agreed price for work done between you: practical habits protect generosity rather than spoil it. In a couple, say what money means to each of you before merging any of it; between friends, decide plainly whether a favour is paid; between colleagues, settle fees before the project starts; between adult relatives, family belongings go better with a list.

Read from their chart, it's your Neptune in their 2nd house, with your ideals meeting their resources. [Their Neptune in your 1st house](/astrology/neptune-in-1st-house-synastry/) touches the face you show; [their Neptune in your 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-in-3rd-house-synastry/), the way you talk and learn.

Close in age, you share a generation's ideas about money and ownership. With fifteen years between you, their Neptune sits about a sign away from your own, and what each era dreamed of owning can differ more than the two of you do. Neptune advances only about two degrees a year, so their birth date fixes it firmly; their own houses still need their birth time, and I don't begin without it. Your birth time places the cusps of your 2nd, which in Placidus, the house system I use, can come out narrow or very wide depending on your latitude ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)).
