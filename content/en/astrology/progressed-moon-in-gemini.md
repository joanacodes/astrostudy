The progressed Moon reaches Gemini after the steady years of Taurus, and the pace picks up. For two years or so, what helps you feel at home is understanding: a conversation, a book, a new person to talk to, a word for what you feel.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Secondary progressions](/astrology/secondary-progressions/) turn the days after your birth into the years of your life, and [the progressed Moon](/astrology/progressed-moon/) is their clock for feeling. In [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), mutable air ruled by Mercury, the Moon’s needs work through exchange. You may want more company of the talking kind, more to read, more to learn; a feeling settles once it has been named, and naming it to someone else helps most of all. Variety comforts rather than distracts: two or three interests at once can be how you look after yourself in these years. The first months can feel like a window opening after Taurus; by the last, the curiosity tends to turn towards the people it wants to keep, which is where Cancer begins.

Gemini is a mutable sign, and as the progressed Moon crosses it, it talks, so to speak, with the rest of your chart: it passes over any planet you have in Gemini, squares planets in Virgo and Pisces and faces those in Sagittarius. Each of these passages is short, and each asks you to put into words what that planet stands for in your life.

The Moon has no dignity in Gemini, and the chapter isn’t shallow for being light: thinking things through is how feelings get processed here. Which area of life fills with these conversations depends on the house the progressed Moon crosses, and the houses come from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); in Placidus, the system I use, I can’t place them without an exact time, so I don’t read the chart. A natal [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) is your own lifelong way of feeling through words; this chapter lends that way to every chart for a while.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Here the day-for-a-year rule is easy to see. The real Moon takes about two and a half days to cross a sign; the progressed Moon takes as many years as the real one took days after your birth, so in the births I counted, from 1930 to 2009, its Gemini chapter lasted between two and two and a half years, usually about two years and four months. A quick Moon at birth makes it shorter, a slow one longer. To know when yours begins and ends, the progressed Moon has to be calculated from your exact date, time and place of birth; a birth time that is out by an hour moves both dates by weeks.

It follows the [progressed Moon in Taurus](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-taurus/) and leads into the [progressed Moon in Cancer](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-cancer/): from keeping, to talking, to belonging. The whole round of twelve chapters takes about 27 years, so the next Gemini chapter is a long way off.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Talk it through, or write it down: in a Gemini chapter, a feeling you can put into words is a feeling you can look after. Mercury rules Gemini, and the house your natal Mercury occupies shows where in your chart that need for words tends to lead.

Let curiosity have room without asking it to settle on one answer too soon, and if the chapter feels crowded, choose one thread to follow to its end without giving up the others. Keep in touch with the people near you; Gemini is a sign of short distances, the neighbour and the sister as much as the stranger. And when the chapter moves into Cancer, the conversations of these years will help you know which people you want close.
