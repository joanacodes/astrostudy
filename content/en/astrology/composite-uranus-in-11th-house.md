The 11th house is a relationship's company: its friends, its groups, the causes it joins and the future it hopes for. It's also the house that matches Aquarius, the sign Uranus rules in modern tropical astrology, so composite Uranus here works on familiar ground.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The cusps of [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) are intermediate ones, and [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) can set them in two ways. Taken from the midpoints of your natal cusps, they depend on the two places and times you were born; rebuilt from the composite Midheaven, they depend on where you live now, since the same Midheaven gives different intermediate cusps at different latitudes. Either way both birth times are needed ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and a Uranus near either cusp may shift house with the choice.

The 11th is succedent. The tradition called it the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy there; its natural sign, Aquarius, is ruled by Uranus in modern tropical astrology and by Saturn in the tradition, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). With [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in this house, the relationship lives inside a network. It may have begun among friends or in a group; it keeps an open door, hosts the gatherings, joins causes together, or counts among its closest ties people who came into its life as a pair. Friends come and go more than usual, and the circle keeps changing shape. Its hopes for the future are unusual and shared: a project for a neighbourhood, a community built from scratch, a life arranged around an idea. The bond often works as a connector, too: introducing people to each other, linking two circles of friends that would never otherwise meet, or bringing a new idea to a group that needed one. Two private people can find that, as a pair, they are the ones who bring everybody together.

For one person, [Uranus in the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-in-11th-house/) means friends and causes who share their idea of the future; between two people, [their Uranus in your 11th house](/astrology/uranus-in-11th-house-synastry/) brings new people and new hopes into a life. In the composite, the circle and the hopes belong to the pair. [Composite Uranus in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-aquarius/), the sign that matches this house, belongs to the youngest adult couples; the house is open to a pair of any age.

The trap is the group taking the time the couple needs, or an ideal of the future crowding out the person beside you. A bond this sociable can be generous to everyone except itself. What helps is a part of the future that is only for two: plans, places or habits the circle doesn't share.

Where [composite Uranus in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-10th-house/) carries change into public life and [composite Uranus in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-12th-house/) into private retreat, the 11th keeps it among friends. As with every composite I describe, this is the technique explained rather than one of my readings: it is set beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and drawn only with the consent of the two people whose data it uses ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
