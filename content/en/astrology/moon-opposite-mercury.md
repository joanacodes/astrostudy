Moon opposite Mercury is a full-moon birth with a conversation running across it. Mercury keeps near the Sun, so when the Moon stands opposite one, it stands opposite the other. It describes feelings and words on two sides of a table, and a life of learning how they talk to each other.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is how you take in and look after feeling: comfort, attachment, the way you respond. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how you take in and pass on information: questions, explanations, the words you pick. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions on one axis, each holding what the other leans away from, and its work is integration rather than victory.

In plain words, your feelings and your thoughts sit facing each other across the room. You can step outside a feeling and describe it with a clarity that surprises people, and lose touch with the feeling while you do. Often one end shows up in someone else first: a friend who says out loud what you feel, a partner whose mood answers your reasoning. Conversation is where the two meet, which is why you may think best in company and feel steadier after a long talk.

Opposite signs share a modality and complementary elements, so the two ends belong together. With the Moon in Aries and Mercury in Libra, feelings that want action now meet words that weigh both sides; with the Moon in Cancer and Mercury in Capricorn, a need for closeness meets words that stay practical and reserved.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

It often shows as someone who is told both “you explain feelings so well” and “but what do you feel?” The houses show where the mirror stands. With the Moon in the 1st and Mercury in the 7th, you often understand your own moods through what a partner says about them.

About 1 person in 23 has the two within 8° of opposition, and all of them came into the world in the days around a full moon: with Mercury close to the Sun, the Moon has to be nearly opposite the Sun to face Mercury. Some, born very close to the full moon itself, have [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/) too. At a tight orb, the back-and-forth between feeling and explaining is a theme of the whole chart; near 8°, it's more of a habit of checking your mood with someone.

Seen from this full-moon angle, the pair's other aspects are other moments of the month:

- **[Moon conjunct Mercury](/astrology/moon-conjunct-mercury/):** the far end of the month, near a new moon, with feeling and thought fused where here they face each other.
- **[Moon sextile Mercury](/astrology/moon-sextile-mercury/):** a friendly angle two signs apart, where the two help each other without a go-between.
- **[Moon square Mercury](/astrology/moon-square-mercury/):** the tension kept inside rather than met in other people, heart and head negotiating at right angles.
- **[Moon trine Mercury](/astrology/moon-trine-mercury/):** words and feelings flowing together in one element, with no mirror needed.

Between two charts, [Moon opposite Mercury in synastry](/astrology/moon-opposite-mercury-synastry/) can make one person the other's mirror: one feels, the other names it, and both learn something about themselves. Like every synastry I read, it needs both people's yes ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

The Moon's monthly round brings it opposite the Mercury in your chart for a few hours, and other people's moods walk into the conversation. Once a year or so, Mercury opposes your natal Moon, around the time the Sun does, and up to three times when a retrograde falls there, for a day or two each pass: what's said around you presses on what you feel.

Weather, not a forecast: this transit sets the tone of a conversation, never its outcome.
