Almost everyone meets a Neptune square to the Moon by 80, and of the two bodies' contacts it is the one that most often asks for patience. For about two and a half years, Neptune presses on the part of you that needs, and the usual ways of meeting those needs start to slip.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) loosens and idealises; your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) tends your feelings and your sense of safety. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, sets them at odds in a way that pushes adaptation. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), a routine that comforted you can lose its effect, the role of the one who always copes can feel heavy, and a longing you can't name can sit beside ordinary days. Nurturing isn't self-sacrifice, and compassion isn't endless availability: the square is often where those lines need redrawing, especially if you're the one others lean on. Felt security isn't the same as certainty, and under this square you may have to feel safe with less of the second.

Neptune's blind spot under this square is confusing empathy with knowing what someone needs, yourself included. Saying a need aloud, plainly, does more than guessing at it. If what you feel settles into a low mood that lasts, a doctor or a therapist can help, and nothing in a chart should stop you asking ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)). I read the transit itself as weather, not a forecast.

With your Moon in the 4th and Neptune crossing your 7th ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)), what you need at home and a partner's ideals pull different ways; with the Moon in the 10th and Neptune in the 1st, the feelings you carry at work rub against a softer sense of yourself. The houses come from the birth time, and so does the Moon's exact degree; I don't read either without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the square takes about two years and seven months to pass, sometimes about twenty-two months; most passes bring three exact hits, and about one in four brings five.

Your Moon can sit anywhere relative to Neptune, and still a square to it comes by 80 for almost everyone born between 1930 and 2009, twice for some. The squares fall between the conjunction ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-moon/)) and the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-moon/)), a quarter circle from each; the sextile ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-moon/)) lies between the conjunction and a square, the trine ([transiting Neptune trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-moon/)) between a square and the opposition. Born with [Moon square Neptune](/astrology/moon-square-neptune/), you know this tension from every day; the transit gives it to everyone else for one season.

Cancer and Capricorn Moons are next in line for this square, the early degrees first, through to 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name your needs in plain words, to yourself first, then to someone who can meet them.

Keep two or three fixed points in the week that don't depend on how you feel: a walk, a call, a meal with someone.

Let others carry their share. Under this square, the urge to absorb everyone's feelings is strongest just when you have least room for them.
