Some conversations feel like picking up in the middle, even with someone you met last week. With Mercury opposite North Node in synastry, one person's Mercury sits about 180° from the other's North Node, which puts it right on their South Node: the first one's way of thinking lands on the second one's most familiar ground.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the voice of the mind: how a person frames things, what they bring up, the vocabulary they reach for. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows the direction someone is growing in, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), always exactly opposite, what came easily or early: practised strengths, habits they fall back on. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions as two ends of one line, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one sets one person's words on the familiar end of the other's line, facing away from the new one.

On the Mercury side, you feel understood almost at once. Your references land, your jokes need no explaining, and this person seems to know the language you think in. You may notice they come to you for reassurance, and like to hear things put the way they have always been put.

On the node side, talking with them is restful. You slip into your best-practised ways of thinking, old stories, old skills, without effort. That can be a real refuge after a stretch of hard learning; it can also leave you more convinced than ever of what you already believed.

Take one person's Mercury in Cancer on the other's South Node in Cancer, with their North Node in Capricorn: a homely, family-minded way of talking sits right on the node person's familiar ground of care and belonging, while their growth points towards responsibility and a place in the wider world. Conversations drift, pleasantly, back to home and the past.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Familiarity is a resource, and the South Node is not a fault: established skill is what someone stands on to reach. The risk is overreliance. When every conversation returns to old ground, the node person's growth, which lies across the chart from the Mercury person, gets talked about less and less, and the Mercury person's ideas may even point gently away from it. Neither intends this; comfort simply has its own gravity.

The pair's other contacts move the Mercury person's words along the nodal line:

- **[Mercury conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the mirror image, words on the growing end, stretching rather than settling.
- **[Mercury sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** a bridge, trine to the familiar and sextile to the new.
- **[Mercury square North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-north-node-synastry/):** halfway between the ends, questioning both.
- **[Mercury trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-north-node-synastry/):** encouragement towards growth, keeping a friendly sextile with the old ground.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use the familiar as a launch pad. Talk about old strengths in order to apply them to something new; let the Mercury person ask, now and then, about the less familiar direction, and listen to the clumsy answer without steering it home. Old friends, siblings and colleagues of many years know this contact well; in a couple, it can be the conversation you have when you're tired, worth balancing with one you have when you're brave.

About 1 pair of people in 15 has this opposition, one way round or the other, within 6°, as many as have the conjunction, its mirror image. I check it against the mean node and the true node, which never differ by more than a degree or two ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). Mercury shifts at most a couple of degrees with the hour, but the hour decides which house of the node person's chart the familiar talk lands in, and that needs both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In the chart of someone born with [Mercury opposite North Node](/astrology/mercury-opposite-north-node/), a fluent mind sits on its own South Node. Between two charts the fluency comes from someone else, and who leans on whom is worth reading from each side ([the nodes in synastry](/astrology/nodes-in-synastry/)).
