Some kitchens are never empty. With the planet that rules your 11th house in the 4th, the people you've chosen find their way into your home, and the future you hope for usually has a front door in it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) begins at the lowest point of the chart, the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/): home, roots, family, the private self beneath the public one. The [11th](/astrology/11th-house/) is the house of the friends you pick rather than inherit, of the groups you join and of the plans you make for later. Follow its ruler and you find where those matters are lived ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)): here, by the hearth.

Friends tend to become household. The meeting happens at your table, the spare room has hosted half your circle, and the people you'd ring in a crisis are as likely to be friends as relatives. Some build a family of choice alongside the one they were born into, or in its place; others bring friends into the family they have until the line blurs. Your hopes take a domestic shape too: where you'll live and with whom, what kind of home you'd like to make, sometimes a house shared with friends or a group of households living close together.

There's a gap to cross, though. By sign, the 4th lies five signs from the 11th, one of the distances tradition counted as no aspect at all ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)): the house of friends doesn't see the house of home. I read it as two worlds that don't meet by themselves. Old friends and relatives may not mix easily, and a group can feel out of place at a family table until you make the introductions. The bridge is built on purpose, which is often why it holds.

The 4th is roots as well, and with the 11th's ruler there, belonging can run back towards where you come from: the community your family was part of, an association of people from the same village or country, a cause handed down along with the furniture. The friendships that last longest are often the ones that understand your origins. That is your experience of those ties; your parents' own lives belong to them.

The planet sets the atmosphere. With Leo on the 11th cusp, the Sun rules your friendships, and a Sun in the 4th means you were born as the Sun neared the lowest point of its night, around local midnight: the warmth of your circle shows best indoors. Under a Pisces cusp, Jupiter is the older ruler and Neptune the newer, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 4th keeps an open, generous house, while Neptune, wherever it is, adds the dream of a community where everyone has a place.

The IC moves with every minute of the birth time in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the system I use ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Sitting just past the IC, the ruler is a few minutes from the 3rd: born a little later, you'd find your friendships in conversation and among neighbours ([Ruler of the 11th in the 3rd house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)). Sitting near the far edge of the house, it's a few minutes from the 5th, where an earlier birth would have put your circle among pleasures and creations ([Ruler of the 11th in the 5th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)). The reverse placement tells the story from the other end, home going out to find its people ([Ruler of the 4th in the 11th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)).
