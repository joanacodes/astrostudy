Two of the widest functions in a chart meet when Jupiter reaches your birth Neptune, about once in twelve years: the one that looks for meaning and the one that softens edges. For a while, faith and imagination swell together.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is imagination, compassion and the sense of something larger than you, the part of a chart that dissolves boundaries that needn't be there. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) builds understanding and belief. Joined in a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), they work as one: ideals grow, generosity comes easily, creative ideas arrive whole, and the line between what is and what could be gets thin. Some people find a vocation under it, a cause or a kind of work that feels like more than a job; others simply notice that their sense of what matters grows wider and kinder.

There's an old link between the two. Tradition gave [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) to Jupiter, and modern tropical astrology gives it to Neptune; I read both, and this transit puts the two rulers of Pisces on the same degree of your chart.

Neptune's blind spots grow along with its gifts. A feeling that resonates can pass for the truth, compassion for being available to everyone, a beautiful picture for evidence. The useful habit in these weeks is simple: before committing to whatever inspires you, check one concrete fact about it. The skill underneath is staying open without losing your outline, feeling with other people while knowing which feelings are yours.

Where the inspiration gathers depends on your Neptune's house, which needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Everyone born with [Jupiter conjunct Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-neptune/) gets this [transit](/astrology/transits/) as a companion to each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The orbs I use for timing open 1½° before exact and close 1° after; going straight through, Jupiter crosses that span over your Neptune in about eleven days. About three conjunctions in ten loop instead, with three exact hits spread over some eight months. A life of 80 years holds six or seven of them for anyone born from 1930 to 2009.

Born in January 1985, with Jupiter some twenty degrees past that year's Neptune in Capricorn, people waited about eleven years for the first; born in January 1955, with Jupiter a quarter of the zodiac short of Neptune, about three.

In the twelve years that follow, Jupiter puts your Neptune to work in four other ways: through [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-neptune/), a chance to make something; [transiting Jupiter square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-neptune/), a reality check; [transiting Jupiter trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-neptune/), inspiration with little effort; and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-neptune/), a dream held by someone else for a while.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make something while the inspiration is strong. Write it, paint it, play it, plan it: Neptune's ideas fade if they're only admired.

Give generously, with the limit set in advance. Compassion is one of the best things this transit brings, and it lasts longer when it isn't asked to be endless.

Keep one foot on the ground. A friend who asks practical questions, a budget of time, a night's sleep before saying yes: small things that let you enjoy a large feeling without being carried off by it.
