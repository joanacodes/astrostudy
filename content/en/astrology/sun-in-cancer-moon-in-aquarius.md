Your Sun is devoted to the people it counts as family; your Moon needs to belong on its own terms, among equals, with a door it can open. The pull between kinship and freedom runs through this pair, and you were born as the Moon, just past full, began to wane.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and fixed air, Aquarius five signs back from Cancer: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, with no shared element, no shared mode and a lifetime of small adjustments. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) holds on to family, memory and the people it has promised to look after; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) settles with some distance, a view of its own and friends who treat it as an equal. One light wants to keep everyone close, the other to keep everyone free, and you may find yourself doing both at once: hosting the gathering and slipping out for air.

Through the Moon, which rules Cancer, your Moon's sign reaches into your Sun's purpose: here, the wish to belong is shaped by the wish to stay free. I read both of Aquarius's rulers. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional one, shows the principles and loyalties this Moon keeps; [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), found by William Herschel in 1781 and given to Aquarius by modern tropical astrology, shows where it needs to step outside and change the rules. Neither Cancer nor Aquarius is a sign of dignity for its light.

Your Moon had just passed full: it rose in the evening, 180° to 239° ahead of the Sun, anywhere from the full moon itself to almost five days after. The eight-phase count calls most of these [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) births and the latest [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) births. With the Sun in Cancer, the full moon just gone usually fell in Capricorn, a [full moon in Capricorn](/astrology/full-moon-in-capricorn/), and your Moon had since moved on a sign; near 210° the lights form an exact quincunx, the waning one. A full moon birth, as I read it, puts purpose and need in plain view of each other, learned through other people: here, through the family you come from and the friends you choose. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says more.

Out of every 140 people, about one has this pair. The Moon spends two and a half days or thereabouts in Aquarius, on its way from Capricorn to Pisces; if it moved on to a new sign the day you were born, your birth time settles it: this page, or else the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-capricorn/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-pisces/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Your rising sign hangs on that moment as well; for a pair this torn between near and far, it often tells me which side people meet first.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love, for you, works best with a friend in it. The Cancer Sun wants closeness, continuity and a home to share; the Aquarius Moon needs space, honesty and to be met as an equal rather than held as a possession. You can be deeply loyal and still need an evening, or a cause, of your own. Trouble comes when the Moon's need for distance is read as coolness, or the Sun's need for reassurance as clinging. Saying plainly *I'm not leaving, I just need air* helps both of you.

## At work {#at-work}

Community work, social care, cooperatives, public health, technology that serves families, history projects that gather many people's stories: anything where looking after people meets improving the system suits this pair. Care is your reason to work; fairness and fresh ideas are how you go about it. You may be the one who brings people together and then lets them run things themselves. Rigid hierarchies frustrate the Moon, and impersonal ones leave the Sun without a home.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Closeness and freedom can take turns at the wheel. When the Cancer Sun is hurt, the Aquarius Moon may step back and explain the feeling rather than feel it; when the Moon pulls away, the Sun can feel like a deserter. The people close to you can get mixed signals. What helps is naming which need is speaking, *I want you near* or *I want room*, and trusting that a home can keep its windows open.
