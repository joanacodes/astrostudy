What you feel shows on your face in a second; what you are takes years to show at all. The Scorpio Sun works slowly and privately towards the root of things, the Aries Moon reacts at once and out loud, and one planet, Mars, has a hand in both.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Scorpio is fixed water, Aries cardinal fire, five signs on: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, two parts that never quite share a rhythm and keep recalibrating. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) builds its identity in depth, holding on until it understands; the [Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/) deals with a feeling by acting on it straight away, then lets it go. People who meet your quick reactions may not guess at the patience underneath, and people who know your patience can be startled by the speed of your feelings.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules Aries outright and Scorpio in the old books, so it speaks for your Moon and, in part, for your Sun: where your effort and courage go is written in its sign and house, and I read them early. Scorpio's other ruler, for modern astrologers, is [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), and I read both; Pluto's house is where the Sun's slower work of ending and renewal happens. The Moon has no dignity in Aries, but Aries is the sign of the Sun's exaltation, so your feelings live where tradition says the Sun is strongest.

Ten to nearly fifteen days into the lunar month, the Moon had travelled 121° to 180° from the Sun, so no more than five days separated your birth from the full moon. The [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) holds about seven in eight of these births and the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) the earliest, and around 150° the quincunx is exact by degree as well. I take the gibbous days as a time to polish what is almost done, which suits a pair that acts fast and then reconsiders at length. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) walks through the eight phases.

Mars's two signs, if you count the old rule, hold the lights of about 1 person in 147. On the days the Moon passes from Pisces into Aries, or from Aries into Taurus, the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-pisces/) and the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-taurus/) stay possible too, and only the time can choose between them ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign depends on that time as well, and with this pair it often decides which shows first, the patience or the speed.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you are loyal and intense, and quick to say when something is wrong. The Aries Moon wants the matter out in the open now; the Scorpio Sun goes on thinking about it long afterwards. A partner may feel the storm has passed when, for you, the question has only gone underground. Telling them calmly, a day later, what you are still turning over spares you both a great deal of guessing.

## At work {#at-work}

You can be formidable in work that needs both nerve and staying power: investigation, crisis management, competitive fields, the repair of something badly broken, any job where you have to act fast and then follow through for months. The Aries Moon gives the first push; the Scorpio Sun supplies the persistence. Bureaucratic delay frustrates the Moon, shallow work bores the Sun, and a role that offers neither speed nor depth won't hold you for long.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Fast feelings and a long memory make an awkward team: a flash of irritation is over for the Aries Moon in minutes, while the Scorpio Sun may file it as evidence. You can also act on a feeling before the deeper self has decided what it means, then live with the consequences for a long time. Give the first reaction an outlet that commits you to nothing, a run, a hard walk, a page torn up afterwards, and let the decisions wait for the Sun.
