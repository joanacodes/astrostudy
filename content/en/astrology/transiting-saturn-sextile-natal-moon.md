Of Saturn's angles to your Moon, the sextile asks least and offers most quietly. It comes twice in each round of about 29½ years, and it suits the kind of care that takes a little organising.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) organises, sets limits and thinks in years; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) looks after feelings, needs and the habits that make you feel safe. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) links them through cooperation and asks you to take part, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) tends to bring structure within reach rather than pressing it on you: a routine you choose, a support you set up, an arrangement at home that finally makes sense.

Saturn's better side shows here as stewardship rather than control. Instead of holding feelings down, you organise around them: fixed times for rest, a plan for the household, an arrangement for the person who depends on you. Needs that were met in a rush start to be met on purpose. The Moon also keeps memory, and this sextile is a good season for giving it some order: photographs labelled, recipes written down, a family story asked for from the person who knows it best.

The first sextile, about five years after Saturn crosses your Moon, tends to give what began then a working routine; the second, about five years before the next crossing, is a good time to simplify. From your 6th, for instance, Saturn offers the structure through daily work and habits ([Saturn transiting the 6th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-6th-house/)). If [Moon sextile Saturn](/astrology/moon-sextile-saturn/) is in your chart, this transit refreshes a way of caring you already know.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, you usually have about nine months of this support, long enough for a new routine to become yours. A little over half the time Saturn loops, retrograde and then direct again ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)), and makes the sextile exact three times in about nine and a half months; at full speed, three weeks.

Your Moon's sign may itself depend on the minute you were born. The Moon changes sign about every two and a half days, and on the day it does, only the birth time tells which side of the line yours fell on, and so which signs Saturn sextiles it from. Without that minute, the sextile has no dates I'd stand behind ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

People born between 1930 and 2009 have had five or six of these quiet seasons by 80. Each one sits between a crossing, [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-moon/), and a square, [transiting Saturn square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-moon/), about two years from the square; the round's trines, [transiting Saturn trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-moon/), and its opposition, [transiting Saturn opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-moon/), fall between the two sextiles.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give one need a fixed place in your week. A sextile from Saturn works best with small structures: the class you go to on Tuesdays, the hour that's always for reading, the walk that happens whatever the weather.

Organise the help you'd otherwise improvise: who to ring, what to keep in the cupboard, which evening is yours. Saturn's sextile rewards care arranged in advance.

Take the first step yourself. A sextile offers rather than insists, and the support on offer this season tends to wait until you ask.
