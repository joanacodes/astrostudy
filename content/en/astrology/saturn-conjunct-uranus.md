Saturn conjunct Uranus brings the two planets of [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) into one place: Saturn, its ruler in tradition, and Uranus, its ruler in modern tropical astrology. They met only twice in the eighty years counted, about 46 years apart, in the early 1940s and the late 1980s, and each generation born during a meeting shares one question: how to change things so that the change holds.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the function that builds structure: responsibility, boundaries, discipline, the patience that lets something last. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the function that differentiates and frees: it reforms, experiments and changes systems grown too tight. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses them into one process without either losing its identity, so keeping and changing arrive together.

In plain words, the urge to secure and the urge to free share one address. That can feel like holding the brake and the accelerator at once, and it's also a rare alliance: reform with an engineer's patience, tradition with an inventor's curiosity. People born with it often care about rules, and about which rules deserve to stay. Reform isn't destruction, and structure isn't a cage; this conjunction is at its best when each keeps the other honest.

I read both of Aquarius's rulers, and this conjunction shows why they belong together.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 27 of the 1930–2009 births has the conjunction within 6°, and they come from just two windows of births: June 1941 to March 1943, with a pause in the second half of 1942, and February 1987 to September 1989, on and off. Born in either window, you share the aspect with your generation, so its sign says little about you; the house it falls in, and its links to your personal planets, say much more.

In the 2nd house, the conjunction may show as security built by unconventional means; in the 3rd, as a way of thinking that respects method and still questions it; in the 10th, as responsibility put to work modernising the structure you belong to. Without a birth time the houses stay unknown ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and for a generation's aspect I then have little personal to say. Saturn, at about 2½ years a sign against Uranus's 7, is the one that catches up.

Saturn's other aspects to Uranus each belong to a few birth years:

- **[Saturn sextile Uranus](/astrology/saturn-sextile-uranus/):** born in the spells of 1934–36 and 1947–49, then 1978–81 and 1995–97; structure and change cooperate when someone takes the opening.
- **[Saturn square Uranus](/astrology/saturn-square-uranus/):** born in 1930–32 and 1950–53, then again in 1975–77 and 1999–2001; keeping and changing pull across each other, a friction that teaches reform without demolition.
- **[Saturn trine Uranus](/astrology/saturn-trine-uranus/):** born in 1955–58, 1971–74 or 2002–04; the old and the new flow together, so change tends to be built rather than fought for.
- **[Saturn opposite Uranus](/astrology/saturn-opposite-uranus/):** born in 1964–67 or 2008–09; facing each other, with the balance between stability and freedom often worked out with other people.

Between two charts, [Saturn conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) sets the structure in one chart on the urge to break free in the other, at the same degree. It can steady a restless partner or chafe a free one, and I read it from each person's side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Saturn conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-uranus/) comes about every 29½ years, as Saturn completes a lap; for people born with the conjunction it arrives with the [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/), around 29 or 30, when Saturn regains its birth place beside your Uranus. [Transiting Uranus conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-saturn/) is slower and rarer: at about 7 years a sign, Uranus can linger near your Saturn for a year or more, in several passes. For this generation, everything starts at the meeting point. Uranus leaves it in the first years of life and needs about 84 years to return; halfway round, at about 42, transiting Uranus faces natal Saturn and natal Uranus together ([Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/)).

Take them as weather, not a forecast: seasons when what you've built and what wants to change enter the same conversation.
