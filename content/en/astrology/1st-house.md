The 1st house is where a birth chart starts: the stretch of the zodiac that was coming up over the eastern horizon at the minute you were born. It's the house of the self — less who you are deep down than who walks into the room first.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 1st house as the front door of a chart. It describes your body and your manner, your first reaction, the way you start things and the impression you make before anyone knows you. Whatever the rest of the chart holds comes out through this door, which is why I look here before anything else.

- **An angular house.** It's one of the four houses that begin at the angles of the chart, and what sits in an angular house shows. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its angle.** Its cusp is the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), the degree that was rising on the eastern horizon when you were born. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, the Ascendant is where the 1st house begins; in whole sign houses, the 1st house is the whole of the rising sign ([which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)).
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Aries](/astrology/aries/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/): beginnings, drive, the first move.
- **Mercury's joy.** Tradition said [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rejoices here ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): the planet of thought and speech, at home in the house of the self.
- **Its opposite.** The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), the house of partners: the 1st is *me*, the 7th is *you*.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/), just before it, holds what is private and unseen; the 1st is where you step out into the open. The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/), just after it, holds what you gather and keep once you've arrived.

Like every house, the 1st needs a birth time. The Ascendant changes sign about every two hours on average, faster or slower with the sign and the latitude, so without the time you were born the 1st house can't be placed: [what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/).

## Planets in the 1st house {#planets}

A planet in the 1st house is part of how you show up: people meet it when they meet you, often before they know your name. The closer it sits to the Ascendant, the more it shows.

Three bodies make me slow down wherever they are: Saturn, [Chiron in the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-in-1st-house/) and [Lilith in the 1st house](/astrology/lilith-in-1st-house/). In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), those three held 75 to 90% of the time, against about a third for most placements, so if one of them sits in your 1st house, that's where I'd spend the most time in a reading.

- **[Sun in the 1st house](/astrology/sun-in-1st-house/):** you lead with yourself; who you are and how you come across are hard to tell apart.
- **[Moon in the 1st house](/astrology/moon-in-1st-house/):** your moods show on your face; people can tell how you are before you say a word.
- **[Mercury in the 1st house](/astrology/mercury-in-1st-house/):** in its joy: you meet the world with questions, talk and a quick, curious manner.
- **[Venus in the 1st house](/astrology/venus-in-1st-house/):** an easy charm; people warm to you, and you care how things look.
- **[Mars in the 1st house](/astrology/mars-in-1st-house/):** you arrive at full speed — direct, energetic, quick to act and sometimes to flare up.
- **[Jupiter in the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-in-1st-house/):** an open, generous manner; you take up room, and people expect good things from you.
- **[Saturn in the 1st house](/astrology/saturn-in-1st-house/):** serious early, slow to feel at ease in yourself — and steadier for it later.
- **[Uranus in the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-in-1st-house/):** you don't fit the first box people put you in, and you'd rather not.
- **[Neptune in the 1st house](/astrology/neptune-in-1st-house/):** a soft, changeable presence; people see in you what they hope to see.
- **[Pluto in the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-in-1st-house/):** you give little away at first, and you've remade yourself more than once.

No planet here? An [empty 1st house](/astrology/empty-houses/) isn't an empty self: with ten planets and twelve houses, every chart has at least two houses with nothing in them. Your rising sign and your chart ruler still describe how you meet the world.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the cusp of the 1st house is your rising sign. It sets the style of the whole house — the pace, the manner, the first reaction — and since the Ascendant moves through all twelve signs in a day, two people born on the same day can have different ones. In my notebook, the rising sign held about a third of the time, like the Sun sign, so I read it as a first sketch, never the whole person. If you don't know yours, start with [how to find your rising sign](/astrology/how-to-find-your-rising-sign/).

- **[Aries rising](/astrology/aries-rising/):** you meet things head-on and get going before others have finished thinking.
- **[Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/):** a calm, unhurried first impression; you take your time and won't be rushed.
- **[Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/):** curious and quick to talk; you meet people with questions.
- **[Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** you protect first and open later; the warmth is there once you trust.
- **[Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/):** warm and visible; you light up a room, or people expect you to.
- **[Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/):** observant and careful; you see what needs doing before anyone asks.
- **[Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/):** pleasant and tactful; you smooth the first awkward minutes for everyone.
- **[Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/):** reserved and watchful; you read people before you let them read you.
- **[Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/):** frank, friendly and restless; you meet life as somewhere to go.
- **[Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/):** composed and serious at first, often taken for older than you are.
- **[Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/):** friendly but a little apart; you do things your own way.
- **[Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/):** soft and hard to pin down; you take in the mood of a room as you walk in.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The planet that rules your rising sign rules your 1st house, and astrologers call it the [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/): Mars with Aries rising, Venus with Taurus rising, and so on round the zodiac. Where it sits is where your life tends to gather — the house that keeps drawing you in, whatever your rising sign promised. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces rising there are two to look at, the modern ruler and the traditional one ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Every house has a ruler that works this way ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)); the ruler of the 1st is simply the one that speaks for you.

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** you're your own project; what people see is very much what there is.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** you find yourself through what you earn, make and keep.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** talk, learning and your close circle carry your story.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** home and roots are where your life keeps coming back to.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** you come alive through what you create, play at and love.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** daily work and being useful shape who you become.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** you find yourself through partners and the people across the table.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** intimacy, trust and what you share run through your story.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** travel, study and belief: a life spent looking further.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** your work and public role carry much of who you are.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** friends, groups and shared causes are where you find yourself.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** much of your life happens in private, out of sight.

## Transits through the 1st house {#transits}

Every planet passes through your 1st house sooner or later. The Moon crosses it in a couple of days each month, the Sun in about a month each year, Mercury, Venus and Mars in a few weeks — short spells when you're more visible, or more aware of yourself. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house in your chart:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-1st-house/):** about a year of feeling bigger, readier to start something or to show up differently.
- **[Saturn transiting the 1st house](/astrology/saturn-transit-1st-house/):** about two and a half years of taking yourself seriously; heavier, steadier, more defined.
- **[Uranus transiting the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-transit-1st-house/):** around seven years of shaking off an old image and trying a freer one.
- **[Neptune transiting the 1st house](/astrology/neptune-transit-1st-house/):** around fourteen years when your outline softens: less sure who you are, more open to who you could be.
- **[Pluto transiting the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-transit-1st-house/):** a long, slow remaking of how you stand in the world, over a decade or more.
- **[Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/):** an old doubt about being right as you are comes up to be looked at; its pace is uneven, from under two years to about eight in a sign.
- **[North Node transiting the 1st house](/astrology/north-node-transit-1st-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards your own path rather than other people's needs; the nodes move backwards through the houses.

I read all of these as weather, not a forecast: they describe the season you're in, not what will happen to you.
