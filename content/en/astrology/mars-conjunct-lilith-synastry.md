Picture someone saying "go on, then" to the plan everyone else talked you out of. Mars conjunct Lilith in synastry puts one person's Mars within 6° of the other's Black Moon Lilith, and one person's drive lands on the very part of the other that never agreed to be managed.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the will to act on a want: initiative, assertion, effort, the line someone holds. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's averaged far point, gets read in different ways by different traditions; as I read it, it's instinctive autonomy, the material someone learned to keep hidden, and how they hold their own power. Put them on one degree, as a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) does, and they act together; of all the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), few hand one person's energy so directly to another's hidden side.

With the Mars, you may find your directness met by someone who doesn't flinch from it, and who can answer with a no just as direct. Their refusal to be steered can energise you rather than put you off. With Lilith, you may feel the other's drive arrive right where you keep your "too much". Some days that feels like permission, someone acting as if your untamed side were simply welcome; other days, like being called into the open before you'd chosen to come.

Houses show the setting. If their Mars falls in your 10th house with your Lilith, the meeting happens in public: your work, your authority, how much room you're allowed to take up.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

With both on one degree, whose autonomy is it? The Mars person can start borrowing the other's refusal as licence for their own push, or keep daring them to be bolder than they wish. The Lilith person can feel their independence turned into someone else's project. Questions of power come up early, who sets the terms, who decides what's shown, and they're questions to answer together, never a verdict on either of you.

Where [Venus conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) touches what someone desires and values, this conjunction touches how they act and push: the same untamed point, a different door. The pair's other angles:

- **[Mars sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** an opening onto the unconventional option, taken when both choose.
- **[Mars square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-lilith-synastry/):** a push that meets a refusal from the side, and boundaries learned there.
- **[Mars trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-lilith-synastry/):** independence and drive at ease together, with no friction to speak of.
- **[Mars opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** drive and refusal facing each other, each the wild one in the other's eyes.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the Lilith person decide what comes into the open, and when. The Mars person can offer instead of dare: "do you want company in this?" leaves room for a no. Between colleagues, it might be backing an unconventional proposal without taking it over; between friends, a trip one of you never dared plan alone.

Counted with mean Lilith, about 1 pair of people in 15 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 6°. Mean Lilith moves on to a new sign roughly every nine months, so anyone born a month or two either side of the Lilith person has a Lilith within a few degrees of theirs; the Mars narrows that down to the two of you. The true point swings so widely around the mean that the choice of Lilith matters more here than any hour of birth ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)); the houses need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Lilith is in good company in my notebook: with Saturn and Chiron, it held 75 to 90% of the time. That was each person's own chart, though, and I've no count for contacts between two. A contact that reaches what someone keeps hidden is read with both people's say-so, or not at all ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Carried by one person, [Mars conjunct Lilith](/astrology/mars-conjunct-lilith/) makes drive and refusal a single gesture. Between two, the drive and the refusal belong to different people, and both get a vote.
