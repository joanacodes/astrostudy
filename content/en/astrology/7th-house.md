The 7th house is where your chart turns to face someone else. It begins at the Descendant, directly across from your Ascendant, and describes the people you meet one to one: partners in love and in work, and the rivals who stand in front of you rather than behind.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 7th as the house of the person across the table: a spouse or long-term partner, a business associate, a close collaborator, anyone you meet as an equal. Tradition gave it marriage and contracts, and also *open enemies* — the adversary you can see coming, unlike the hidden ones of the [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/).

It doesn't tell you who you'll end up with, or when. It describes what you look for in people and, very often, the qualities you find easier to see in someone else than in yourself. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), across the chart, is *me*; the 7th is *you*, and I read the two together.

It's one of the four **angular** houses, which begin at an angle of the chart and tend to show ([angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/)). Its angle is the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the degree setting in the west when you were born; in Placidus and the other quadrant systems, it's the 7th house cusp. Its natural sign is [Libra](/astrology/libra/) and its natural ruler [Venus](/astrology/venus/): balance, fairness, the pleasure of company.

A house is read with its neighbours: the 7th follows the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/), the daily work, and comes before the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/), where a bond deepens into trust, intimacy and money in common. And it needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): without it, there's no Descendant to read.

It's also the house a synastry looks at first. When someone's planets fall in your 7th — a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) — they tend to feel like a partner, or a mirror: [their Venus in your 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house-synastry/) can feel like exactly the warmth you hoped for. A [synastry](/synastry/) reads two people, so both must have said yes: [why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## Planets in the 7th house {#planets}

A planet in the 7th describes what you look for in others, or keep meeting in them — often a part of you that comes out first in company. [Chiron in the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-in-7th-house/), [Lilith in the 7th house](/astrology/lilith-in-7th-house/) and the [North Node in the 7th house](/astrology/north-node-in-7th-house/) have their own pages; I never skip the first two, which held best in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) with Saturn.

- **[Sun in the 7th house](/astrology/sun-in-7th-house/):** you come into focus through other people; partnership shows you who you are, as long as you keep your own light on.
- **[Moon in the 7th house](/astrology/moon-in-7th-house/):** you need someone close to feel at home, and look for care — or someone to care for.
- **[Mercury in the 7th house](/astrology/mercury-in-7th-house/):** you think best with someone to answer, and you're good at seeing both sides.
- **[Venus in the 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house/):** in the house of its own sign; you love being half of a pair, and may keep the peace a little too willingly.
- **[Mars in the 7th house](/astrology/mars-in-7th-house/):** drawn to strong, direct people; relationships are where you meet desire and friction, and learn to fight fair.
- **[Jupiter in the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-7th-house/):** generous and hopeful with partners; the lesson is not to expect one person to give your whole life its meaning.
- **[Saturn in the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-in-7th-house/):** commitment taken very seriously, often later than others; partners who test or teach you, and bonds that strengthen with time.
- **[Uranus in the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-in-7th-house/):** you need freedom inside a relationship; unusual arrangements, sudden meetings, partners who wake you up rather than settle you down.
- **[Neptune in the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-in-7th-house/):** you idealise the people you love; great tenderness, and the task of seeing a partner as they really are.
- **[Pluto in the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-in-7th-house/):** bonds that change you; partnership is where you meet power and trust, and learn to tell closeness from control.

No planet here? An empty 7th house isn't an empty love life: with twelve houses and ten planets, every chart has empty ones, and the sign on the cusp and the ruler still tell the story ([empty houses](/astrology/empty-houses/)).

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on your 7th house cusp is your Descendant's sign, always the one opposite your rising sign. It colours what you look for in a partner — often the qualities your rising sign finds hardest to show.

- **[Aries on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Libra rising; drawn to direct, independent people, you learn through them to stand your ground.
- **[Taurus on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Scorpio rising; you look for steadiness and physical ease, a bond that feels safe rather than stormy.
- **[Gemini on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Sagittarius rising; you need a partner you can talk to, and a bond that stays curious.
- **[Cancer on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Capricorn rising; you look for warmth and care, the softness you don't always show yourself.
- **[Leo on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Aquarius rising; drawn to warm, expressive people, you want admiration that runs both ways.
- **[Virgo on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Pisces rising; a practical, attentive partner, and love that shows in small, useful acts.
- **[Libra on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Aries rising; the house's natural sign, and partners who bring tact and balance to your directness.
- **[Scorpio on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Taurus rising; depth or nothing — intense, loyal bonds where trust is everything.
- **[Sagittarius on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Gemini rising; free spirits and people from elsewhere, partners who widen your world.
- **[Capricorn on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Cancer rising; commitment taken seriously, and a bond built to last, even if it starts slowly.
- **[Aquarius on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Leo rising; you need friendship and room to breathe inside a couple.
- **[Pisces on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Virgo rising; you look for tenderness and a kindred soul, and learn to see a partner clearly.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

Your 7th house ruler is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp: Mars for Aries, Venus for Taurus or Libra, and for Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces both the modern ruler and the traditional one. Where it sits is where the story of your partnerships tends to play out ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** partners shape who you become; you may recognise yourself in the people you choose.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** partnership tied to money and values; a bond has to feel secure, and worth it.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** partners met close to home, through studies, siblings or everyday talk; a bond that's a long conversation.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** partnership and home overlap; a bond that feels like family, or a home built together.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** partnership through romance, play and creation; love that keeps its sense of fun.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** partners met through work or daily life; a bond built on shared routines and practical help.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** the ruler at home; relationships are central, and partners tend to be what they seem.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** deep, intense partnerships; shared money and trust, and bonds that change you.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** partners from elsewhere, or met through travel and study; a bond that widens what you believe.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** partnership tied to your work and standing; working with a partner, or one to one with clients.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** partners met through friends or groups; love and friendship hard to tell apart.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** partnerships with a private side; quiet devotion, and bonds that need time apart to stay well.

## Transits through the 7th house {#transits}

The Moon crosses your 7th house in a few days, the Sun in a month, Mercury, Venus and Mars every year or two. The slow planets stay for years, and I read them as the weather in your relationships, not a forecast: no transit decides whether a bond begins or ends. The times are rough, as houses vary in size:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-7th-house/):** about a year; relationships tend to feel roomier and more hopeful, other people more generous.
- **[Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/):** about two and a half years; a sober look at commitment — what's unequal asks to be renegotiated, what's solid shows it.
- **[Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/):** about seven years; a need for more freedom in close bonds, and people who shake up your idea of partnership.
- **[Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/):** about fourteen years; ideals and blurred lines in partnership, and the effort of seeing clearly.
- **[Pluto transiting the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-7th-house/):** many years; power and trust in your closest bonds come up for review, slowly and deeply.
- **[Chiron transiting the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-7th-house/):** from under two years to about eight; old relationship wounds surface, with the chance to tend them.
- **[North Node transiting the 7th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-7th-house/):** about eighteen months, with the South Node in your 1st; a pull towards cooperation, away from going it alone.
