Mars is the next planet out from Earth, and it carries the name of the Roman god of war. Astrologers read it as the force in you that moves, wants and pushes back: your drive, your desire, and the way you handle anger.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

I read Mars as drive rather than aggression. It answers *how do you go after what you want?* — and what you do when someone stands in the way. Anger is one of its uses; courage, effort and desire are others.

- **Its sign** shows the style of your drive: quick, patient, strategic, fierce.
- **Its house** shows where you put your energy, and where you're most ready to stand your ground.
- **Its aspects** show whether that energy flows freely, gets blocked, or comes out sideways.

Mars works with [Venus](/astrology/venus/): Venus describes what attracts you, Mars how you pursue it. With the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), it shows how much force your purpose has behind it; with [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), where you learn to pace that force; with [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), where it grows bigger and bolder. It rules [Aries](/astrology/aries/) and, traditionally, [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), which modern astrologers give to [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) — the split explained in [traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/). It is said to be exalted in [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), where effort is steady and aimed. It is weakened in Libra and Taurus, opposite its own signs, and in Cancer, its fall: places where it fights less directly, not less well. Tradition called Mars a [malefic](/astrology/benefics-and-malefics/), a planet of heat and conflict; I read it as the part of you that can act, refuse and protect.

Its aspects show how that force is wired. A [Sun square Mars](/astrology/sun-square-mars/) often describes a strong will that has to learn its own strength. [Venus conjunct Mars](/astrology/venus-conjunct-mars/) puts attraction and pursuit in the same place. A [Moon conjunct Mars](/astrology/moon-conjunct-mars/) tends to react quickly, feeling and acting in one movement. And a [Mars square Saturn](/astrology/mars-square-saturn/) is the foot on the accelerator and the brake at once: effort that has to wait, and gains stamina because of it.

## Mars through the signs {#through-the-signs}

Mars usually spends six to seven weeks in each sign, and much longer when it turns retrograde, so people born a month or two apart often share it; the house and the aspects make it personal. In plain words:

- **[Mars in Aries](/astrology/mars-in-aries/):** direct, quick and brave; anger flares and passes. Its own sign.
- **[Mars in Taurus](/astrology/mars-in-taurus/):** slow to start and hard to stop; you push with patience.
- **[Mars in Gemini](/astrology/mars-in-gemini/):** you fight with words and wit, and do several things at once.
- **[Mars in Cancer](/astrology/mars-in-cancer/):** you act to protect; anger can come out sideways, or on someone else's behalf.
- **[Mars in Leo](/astrology/mars-in-leo/):** proud, warm and bold; you act with heart, and like it to show.
- **[Mars in Virgo](/astrology/mars-in-virgo/):** effort goes into precision; you fight by fixing things.
- **[Mars in Libra](/astrology/mars-in-libra/):** you act through others and fight for fairness; open conflict costs you.
- **[Mars in Scorpio](/astrology/mars-in-scorpio/):** intense, strategic and persistent; you commit fully or not at all.
- **[Mars in Sagittarius](/astrology/mars-in-sagittarius/):** you act on conviction, and need a cause or a horizon.
- **[Mars in Capricorn](/astrology/mars-in-capricorn/):** disciplined and ambitious; effort aimed at the long game. Its exaltation.
- **[Mars in Aquarius](/astrology/mars-in-aquarius/):** you act for ideas and causes, in your own unusual way.
- **[Mars in Pisces](/astrology/mars-in-pisces/):** you act on instinct and feeling, and fight hardest for others.

## Mars in the houses {#in-the-houses}

Its house shows where you spend your energy and where you're most ready to stand your ground. Tradition gave Mars its joy in the 6th house: effort, work and the tasks of every day.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/mars-in-1st-house/):** people notice your energy and directness first; you meet life head-on.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/mars-in-2nd-house/):** you work hard for what you have, and defend your worth.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/mars-in-3rd-house/):** a quick, sharp tongue; you enjoy a lively debate.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/mars-in-4th-house/):** energy goes into home and family, where tempers can run high.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/mars-in-5th-house/):** play that likes to win, bold romance, creative drive.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/):** Mars's joy: great energy for work, at your best with a task in hand.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/mars-in-7th-house/):** partnerships bring spark and friction; strong characters draw you.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/mars-in-8th-house/):** intense desire, and a fierce need for trust in closeness.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/mars-in-9th-house/):** you fight for your beliefs, and go after adventure, travel and learning.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/mars-in-10th-house/):** ambition shows in public; you want to lead, or build things your own way.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/mars-in-11th-house/):** you put your energy into friends, groups and causes.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/mars-in-12th-house/):** drive and anger work behind the scenes; you act best quietly.

## Mars in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Mars shows where two people energise each other — and where they clash. When one person's Mars meets the other's Venus — [Venus conjunct Mars in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-mars-synastry/) — the attraction is often physical and immediate. When their Mars falls in your 1st house — [Their Mars in your 1st house](/astrology/mars-in-1st-house-synastry/) — they can feel energising or pushy, depending on the day. Mars contacts with Saturn ask both people to slow down; with the Moon, they stir feelings quickly. I read Mars contacts in both directions: who pushes and who pushes back isn't fixed.

## Transits and Mars retrograde {#mars-retrograde}

Mars moves more slowly than Mercury and Venus, so its transits last longer: a few days of extra energy, impatience or courage when it crosses one of your planets. About every two years it turns retrograde for about two and a half months — [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/) — and can stay in one area of your chart for months, which astrologers read as a time to rethink how you use your energy rather than push harder. It's weather, not a forecast: a Mars transit describes a mood, never an event.

Being born during a Mars retrograde isn't a lack of drive; it often describes energy that works inward first, and I wrote about that rhythm in [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/). When a slow planet meets your Mars — [Transiting Saturn conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-mars/), for instance — the season is longer, and effort asks for patience.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

Mars was on my list like every placement — its sign and its house, a tick or a cross — in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), for my own chart and then for family, friends and strangers who agreed to it. It wasn't one of the three that held far more often: those were [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), which held 75 to 90% of the time. Overall, about a third of what I checked held: 30 to 40%. So I read Mars in every chart, but I don't rest a reading on it alone, and when part of what I tell you depends on it, I say so. It's a notebook, not a study, and it shapes [how I read](/how-i-read/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Mars makes you aggressive.”** Mars is drive. Anger is one way it shows; effort, courage and desire are others. Its placement says how you act, not whether you'll hurt anyone.
- **“Mars is the men's planet, Venus the women's.”** Everyone has both, whatever their gender: Mars describes how anyone acts and desires, Venus how anyone loves and enjoys.
- **“A Mars in Libra, Taurus or Cancer can't stand up for itself.”** Tradition calls these its harder signs. Mars there fights through fairness, persistence or protection — less directly, and often more patiently.
- **“Being born with Mars retrograde means having no energy.”** It often means your drive works inward first; as a transit, Mars retrograde is a time to rethink rather than push. Neither is a lack of strength.
