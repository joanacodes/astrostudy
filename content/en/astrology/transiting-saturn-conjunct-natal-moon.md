Saturn comes back to your Moon's birth degree roughly every 29½ years, and stays for most of a year when it does. The part of you that looks after feelings, comfort and the habits that keep you steady gets Saturn's full attention.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) structures, limits and makes things last; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feelings, needs, attachment and the sense of being safe. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) merges them, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your emotional life is asked to take a sturdier form: routines that support you rather than ones you keep from habit, people you can actually lean on, a home that works for the life you have now.

It can feel heavy. Comfort is harder to come by, old ways of soothing yourself stop working, and you may feel more alone with your responsibilities than usual. That heaviness is information about what needs rebuilding, not a measure of your worth. If low mood or poor sleep lasts for weeks, though, that's a matter for a doctor or a therapist, not for a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

The Moon isn't your mother in my reading, and this transit is no forecast about her or anyone at home. Your Moon's house says where the rebuilding happens: with the Moon in the 4th, it falls inside [Saturn transiting the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-4th-house/), and home itself is what gets reworked. If [Moon conjunct Saturn](/astrology/moon-conjunct-saturn/) is in your chart, your Saturn return doubles as this crossing.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Most crossings of the Moon keep Saturn close for about nine months, with orbs, as I use them, of 1½° before exact and 1° after. A little over half the time Saturn loops over it three times, retrograde in the middle, for about nine and a half months ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)); at full speed, it can take three weeks.

Among the bodies in your chart, the Moon is the one whose degree leans hardest on the birth time. It moves about half a degree an hour, so a birth time missing altogether leaves its degree uncertain by up to 13°, more than a year of Saturn's travel. I'd rather give no dates than wrong ones, so the time comes first ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)).

Everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets it two or three times by 80, and nobody misses it. The other Saturn transits to your Moon spread out between two crossings: [transiting Saturn opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-moon/) marks the halfway point, about fifteen years on; [transiting Saturn square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-moon/) falls about seven years either side of a crossing; [transiting Saturn trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-moon/) about five years either side of the opposition; and [transiting Saturn sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-moon/) about five years from a crossing.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Build one routine that looks after you rather than everyone else: a regular bedtime, a meal cooked properly, a weekly call with someone who steadies you.

Ask for practical help early. Saturn on the Moon often reveals an old habit of coping alone, and the structure this season wants includes other people.

Let go of comforts that no longer comfort. If something you reach for leaves you more tired, this is the season to replace it with something slower and surer.
