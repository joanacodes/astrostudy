Once every 29½ years or so, Saturn crosses the degree your Moon held when you were born. The part of you that looks after feelings, comfort and the habits that keep you steady gets Saturn's full attention for most of a year.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) structures, limits and makes things last; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feelings, needs, attachment and the sense of being safe. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) merges them, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your emotional life is asked to take a sturdier form: routines that support you rather than ones you keep from habit, people you can actually lean on, a home that works for the life you have now.

It can feel heavy. Comfort is harder to come by, old ways of soothing yourself stop working, and you may feel more alone with your responsibilities than usual. That heaviness is information about what needs rebuilding, not a judgement on how much care you deserve. If low mood or poor sleep lasts for weeks, though, that's a matter for a doctor or a therapist, not for a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

The Moon isn't your mother in my reading, and this transit is no forecast about her or anyone at home. Your Moon's house says where the rebuilding happens: with the Moon in the 4th, it falls inside [Saturn transiting the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-4th-house/), and home itself is what gets reworked. Born with [Moon conjunct Saturn](/astrology/moon-conjunct-saturn/), you meet this crossing at your Saturn return.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the conjunction usually lasts about nine months. A little over half the time Saturn loops over your Moon three times, retrograde in the middle, for about nine and a half months ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)); at full speed, it can take three weeks.

Among the bodies in your chart, the Moon is the one whose degree leans hardest on the birth time. It moves about half a degree an hour, so a birth time missing altogether leaves its degree uncertain by up to 13°, more than a year of Saturn's travel. That's why I won't date this transit without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)).

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have met it two or three times. The other Saturn transits to your Moon spread out between two crossings: [transiting Saturn opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-moon/) marks the halfway point, about fifteen years on; [transiting Saturn square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-moon/) falls about seven years either side of a crossing; [transiting Saturn trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-moon/) about five years either side of the opposition; and [transiting Saturn sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-moon/) about five years from a crossing.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Build one routine that looks after you rather than everyone else: a regular bedtime, a meal cooked properly, a weekly call with someone who steadies you.

Ask for practical help early. Saturn on the Moon often reveals an old habit of coping alone, and the structure this season wants includes other people.

Let go of comforts that no longer comfort. If something you reach for leaves you more tired, this is the season to replace it with something slower and surer.
