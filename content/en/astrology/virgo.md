Virgo is the sixth sign of the zodiac, the one the Sun crosses at harvest time in the northern hemisphere, as summer turns to autumn. It's the sign of craft and care. Wherever it falls in a chart, I read it as the place where you want to make things work better, for yourself and for others.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Virgo somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it brings attention, skill and a quiet wish to be useful: noticing what's off, fixing it, learning to do it properly. Virgo asks *how could this work better?* It's an [earth sign](/astrology/earth-signs/), like Taurus and Capricorn, so it trusts what it can check; and it's mutable, one of the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/), so it adjusts and refines rather than starts or holds. In the sequence of signs, each one answering the last, Virgo answers [Leo](/astrology/leo/): after the performance, the practice that makes it good. [Libra](/astrology/libra/), next, turns from the work to the other person.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/):** born between about 23 August and 22 September. You grow by making things work better and mastering a craft, and being useful is how you feel you matter.
- **[Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/):** you feel safe when things are in order and you have something useful to do. You care for people in practical ways, and when you're unsettled, you tidy, plan or fix.
- **[Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/):** you meet the world attentively, and people see you as capable and modest, a little reserved at first. Mercury becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/). A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So I read Virgo through the whole chart, beginning with where Mercury sits.

## Its ruler: Mercury {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, the one that carries the sign's business into the rest of the chart. Virgo belongs to [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), which also rules [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/): in Gemini it gathers, in Virgo it sorts. Mercury is [exalted](/astrology/exaltation/) in Virgo too — the only sign where a planet is both at home and exalted — so here it is at its most precise. Wherever your Mercury sits, by sign and house, shows where your Virgo side puts its skill. With Virgo on the cusp of the 6th house and Mercury in the 10th, say, everyday craft becomes a public reputation.

Three planets are weakened in Virgo:

- [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is in its [fall](/astrology/fall/): love shows itself through care and practical help rather than grand gestures, and can be shy about asking for what it wants. Different, not lesser.
- [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite Pisces, which tradition gives it: big faith meets close detail, and growth comes through doing small things well.
- [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern ruler of Pisces, is in detriment here too for modern astrologers: the dream meets the checklist.

## Planets in Virgo {#planets-in-the-sign}

A planet in Virgo sharpens its focus and wants to be useful; the house it sits in shows where that plays out.

- **[Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/):** a careful, useful will; you grow by improving things and mastering a craft.
- **[Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/):** feelings settled by order and usefulness; you look after people in practical ways.
- **[Mercury in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-in-virgo/):** Mercury at home and exalted — precise, observant and analytical; a mind that sorts and fixes.
- **[Venus in Virgo](/astrology/venus-in-virgo/):** Venus in its fall — love shown through care and attention to detail; you love by helping.
- **[Mars in Virgo](/astrology/mars-in-virgo/):** methodical, efficient energy; you act through skill and preparation.
- **[Jupiter in Virgo](/astrology/jupiter-in-virgo/):** Jupiter in detriment — growth through skill, study and service; faith in doing things well.
- **[Saturn in Virgo](/astrology/saturn-in-virgo/):** competence learned the long way; high standards that need room for mistakes.
- **[Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/uranus-in-virgo/):** a generation that changes how work and everyday life are organised, shared by everyone born over about seven years; its house makes it personal.
- **[Neptune in Virgo](/astrology/neptune-in-virgo/):** Neptune in detriment — an ideal of service, and the dream of a world put right; shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so I read it through its house.
- **[Pluto in Virgo](/astrology/pluto-in-virgo/):** a generation's deep transformation of work, method and daily life; shared over twelve years or more, made personal by its house.

## Virgo on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Virgo on the cusp of one of its twelve houses. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Virgo opens a house, that's where you notice the details and want things done properly, and Mercury, wherever it sits, carries the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/virgo-rising/):** Virgo rising — an attentive, careful presence; you notice everything, and people see you as capable and modest.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/virgo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** careful with money and possessions; you value skill and usefulness, and like to know exactly where things stand.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/virgo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** precise, careful words; you learn by analysing, and like to get the facts right.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an orderly, practical home; you show care by keeping things running.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/virgo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** creativity through craft; you enjoy making things well, and romance lives in small attentions.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/virgo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** Virgo in its natural house — work and daily routines matter; you like a method, and being useful.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Virgo on the Descendant, which means Pisces rising — drawn to capable, thoughtful partners; you want a relationship that works day to day, where each helps the other.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/virgo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** careful with trust and shared resources; you need to understand before you let go.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/virgo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs examined in detail; you study carefully, and travel well prepared.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career built on skill and reliability; a reputation for doing things properly.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/virgo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends you help in practical ways; in groups, you're the one who organises.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/virgo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** worries kept private; quiet help given behind the scenes, and a need to switch the critical mind off.

## Virgo in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Virgo planets bring care, attention and help: the person who has them shows love in practical ways — fixing, noticing, remembering. To the other person that can feel like being looked after or like being corrected, depending on where those planets land in their chart and what they touch. Across the zodiac sits [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), the opposite sign: Virgo sorts and refines, Pisces accepts and lets the edges blur. Between two charts, that axis can be a lovely balance of order and compassion — and a friction between the one who sees what's wrong and the one who would rather let it be. As for comparing Sun signs, [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind: what two people share shows in their whole charts, not in two Sun signs.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Virgos are neat freaks.”** Virgo cares about things working. That can mean a tidy desk, or a messy desk and a very precise mind: order is about function, not appearance.
- **“Virgos are cold and critical.”** Virgo notices what could be better, and it often turns that eye on itself first. Its criticism is meant as help.
- **“Virgo is the virgin, so it's prudish.”** The sign's name comes from an old image of a maiden; it describes a way of working, not anyone's love life.
- **“Born on the cusp, you're half Leo.”** At the minute you were born, the Sun was in one sign or the other; within a day of a change, your birth time settles which. More on being [born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/).
