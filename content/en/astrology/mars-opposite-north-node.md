Mars opposite North Node gives your drive a home on the South Node, the practised side of the nodal axis. Effort is something you already know how to make; the growth your chart points towards lies across from it, and asks for a different kind of strength.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the capacity to act: initiative, assertion, the will to compete and to finish. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) names a less familiar capacity that grows by practice; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), exactly opposite, names consolidated ground, what you do readily and fall back on. Here Mars is conjunct the South Node, so an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) runs from your drive to your direction of growth: a polarity, in which each end shows up the other.

In plain words, pushing is the move you know. When something needs doing, you reach for effort, speed or a fight; under pressure, you reach for it faster. Across from it, the North Node calls for the complementary move, which might be patience, partnership, listening or letting a situation unfold, depending on the signs. That can feel like being asked to put down your best tool.

It isn't. Development doesn't erase existing capacity, and growing towards the North Node doesn't mean abandoning Mars. The work is to stop leaning on effort alone, and to let your drive serve the less familiar aim instead of standing in for it.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The signs show what the practised drive is, and what it's asked to learn. With the North Node in Libra and Mars on the South Node in Aries, going it alone is the familiar skill and acting with someone the growth; with the North Node in Taurus and Mars in Scorpio, a practised intensity is asked to make room for calm and steady pleasures. The houses say where: if Mars and the South Node share the 10th house, ambition may come easily while home, across the chart in the 4th, is the less practised art. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

About 1 person in 29 is born with Mars within 6° of the South Node, which is the same as opposing the North Node, using the true node. I also check the mean node, since it can move the result by a degree or two. Tight, the familiar drive is the first thing people meet in you; wide, it's one strength among several.

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Mars conjunct North Node](/astrology/mars-conjunct-north-node/):** Mars on the North Node instead; action is the new skill, not the old one.
- **[Mars sextile North Node](/astrology/mars-sextile-north-node/):** an opening towards growth, with Mars trine the familiar end.
- **[Mars square North Node](/astrology/mars-square-north-node/):** Mars between the two ends, choosing case by case.
- **[Mars trine North Node](/astrology/mars-trine-north-node/):** effort flowing towards growth, with old skills a sextile away.

[Mars opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-north-node-synastry/) brings one person's drive to the other's South Node: familiar and energising, and sometimes a pull back towards old habits that both people do well to notice.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-mars/) is when the node in the sky stands across from your Mars. For anyone born with this opposition, that moment coincides with each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/), around 18½, 37 and 56, when the node comes back to where it began. Halfway between, at each [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), around 9, 28 and 46, the North Node itself crosses your Mars, and for a few weeks the familiar drive sits right on the path of growth.

Weather, not a forecast: seasons for noticing when you rely on effort out of habit, and when it's carrying you somewhere new.
