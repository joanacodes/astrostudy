From 2026 to 2028, Aquarius has three annular solar eclipses in a row, one each winter: 17 February 2026, 6 February 2027 and 26 January 2028. Aquarius thinks for the group and for the future, and its eclipses fall on the axis where it meets Leo.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) is fixed air, with two rulers I read side by side: Saturn, from the tradition, and Uranus, from modern tropical astrology. It is the sign of friendship, ideas and the wider circle, and its eclipses lie on the Aquarius–Leo axis, the group and the individual, alternating with Leo’s as the Moon’s nodes move. Aquarius had a [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) on 17 February 2026 and has another on 6 February 2027, at 17°37′; its next total [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/), on 7 August 2036 at 15°13′, puts the full Moon here and the Sun in Leo.

Whether any of this reaches you depends on the AGS rule I read by: an eclipse counts within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of its axis, through that point and its house, and a penumbral lunar one weighs less. Nodes I read as mean and as true. What it brings is emphasis; it is never an omen.

Aquarius’s fifteen, in order:

- **2000:** 5 February 2000, partial solar, 16°01′
- **2008–2009:** 7 February 2008, annular solar, 17°44′ · 16 August 2008, partial lunar, 24°17′ · 26 January 2009, annular solar, 6°29′ · 6 August 2009, penumbral lunar, 13°35′
- **2017–2018:** 7 August 2017, partial lunar, 15°30′ · 15 February 2018, partial solar, 27°07′ · 27 July 2018, total lunar, 4°45′
- **2026–2028:** 17 February 2026, annular solar, 28°50′ · 6 February 2027, annular solar, 17°37′ · 17 August 2027, penumbral lunar, 24°04′ · 26 January 2028, annular solar, 6°10′
- **2035–2037:** 19 August 2035, partial lunar, 26°00′ · 7 August 2036, total lunar, 15°13′ · 27 July 2037, partial lunar, 4°26′

Nineteen years separate pairs such as 26 January 2009 and 26 January 2028, or 27 July 2018 and 27 July 2037, each within half a degree. Capricorn and Pisces, which frame it, are treated on their own pages ([Eclipse in Capricorn](/astrology/eclipse-in-capricorn/), [Eclipse in Pisces](/astrology/eclipse-in-pisces/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Where Aquarius sits among your houses depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), since the time sets the cusps; that is why I don’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, my house system, a line applies only when an Aquarius eclipse meets a natal point in that house; the Leo degrees opposite reach the house across.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aquarius-rising/):** Aquarius rising: your independent, friendly manner; the way you stand slightly apart.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** unusual values; what you spend on ideas and causes.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** ideas exchanged freely; a conversation that changes your thinking.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an unconventional home; the room each person is given.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** friendship within romance; creative work done with a group.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work among like-minded colleagues; new methods in a daily routine. Illness belonged to this house in the old books; what I look at is your working week, and health questions go to medical people, never to a horoscope ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius on the Descendant, with Leo rising: a partner who is also a friend; space within closeness.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what a group holds in common; trust given without control.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs about the future; a cause that widens your outlook.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** work for a wider purpose; a reputation for doing things differently.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius in its natural house: friends, groups, shared hopes; the people you are building a future with.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private ideals; a hope you keep to yourself.
