With Venus in Gemini, the first thing that has to attract you is the conversation. Venus judges what you like and how you relate; Gemini, mutable air, does its judging by asking, comparing and talking things through. I read it as a taste for minds, and an affection that stays alive while there is still something to say.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs attraction and preference: what draws you, what you choose, what you'd like back. [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), ruled by [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), gives it a quick, curious, many-sided manner. You notice the turn of phrase before the haircut, the question someone asks before what they're wearing, and you can like several things at once for quite different reasons.

Venus has no dignity in Gemini: it is neither at home, exalted, in detriment nor in fall. So I read this Venus through its manner and its ruler. Mercury rules the sign, and your Mercury, the way you think, write and talk, has a large say in how you flirt, make up and apologise.

Venus usually crosses Gemini in about 25 days, but when it turns retrograde there it can stay up to 125, more than four months. Those long stays make it a fairly common Venus sign: about 1 person in 11 born from 1930 to 2009. If yours is a [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/), the talking often happens inside first, and the conversation you finally have is one you've already rehearsed. Your Sun sits in Gemini or within two signs of it, from Aries to Leo.

Look at the neighbours and the manner stands out. [Venus in Taurus](/astrology/venus-in-taurus/) keeps what it loves, and [Venus in Cancer](/astrology/venus-in-cancer/) keeps the people it loves close; between them, Venus in Gemini keeps things moving, which is its gift and, at times, its puzzle.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Talk is how this Venus gets close. Long messages, running jokes, the conversation that starts over dinner and is still going at two in the morning: for a Gemini Venus that is intimacy, as much as touch is. You tend to be drawn to people who are curious, quick and a little surprising, and to drift when someone stops asking questions. Reciprocity means replies: an answer to your message, interest in your day, a mind that meets yours halfway. Lightness is a strength here, and often a kindness. The thing to learn is that some feelings are carried rather than discussed, and that a silence between two people can be full.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Venus values variety, conversation and colleagues who think out loud. You're often good at the parts of a job that connect: introducing people, writing the email that settles a quarrel, explaining a product so that others warm to it. Teaching, sales, editing, journalism, anything with words and contacts can suit it. A role that repeats one task for months, with nobody to talk to, dulls it fast. Its pleasure in work is often the exchange itself, a meeting where ideas bounce.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Restlessness can look like a lack of commitment, even when it isn't one. You may keep several options open, a few friendships at the same pitch, two or three possible plans, because choosing one seems to close the rest. Feelings can be talked about so well that they never quite get felt. And when a conversation goes quiet, the fear that something has ended can arrive long before the evidence.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose, and keep talking: commitment needn't end curiosity, and asking new questions of the same person is a pleasure of its own. Practise saying one heavy thing plainly, without a joke to soften it. Write things down, since a letter can hold what a conversation keeps slipping past. And learn the other person's language of affection, which may be less verbal than yours.

When I tested my own chart and other people's, Venus held about a third of the time, close to what a flattering horoscope scores ([my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)). A Gemini Venus will enjoy arguing with that figure, and should: it's a notebook, not a study, and your own experience is the better test.
