Gemini on the 10th house cusp places the sign of exchange at the Midheaven, the highest point of the chart and the start of the house of public life. What people know you for, and the role you take in the world, tends to be built from words, links and versatility.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where your capacities become visible, through roles, responsibilities and what you contribute, and its sign is the style of that visibility ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) there, you tend to be known as the person who explains, connects, translates or keeps information moving, and to be trusted with the message as much as with the task. A public life under this sign often runs on more than one strand at a time, or changes direction as you learn, and both are ways of following curiosity rather than failures to settle. The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) describes a direction, not a job title, and a Gemini Midheaven fits a teacher, a driver, a seller, a writer, a coordinator or an engineer equally well: what matters is that the work keeps you learning and talking.

At the latitudes of London and New York, every chart with this Midheaven has [Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/). [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) therefore rules both the Ascendant and the Midheaven: it is your chart ruler as well as the ruler of your public life, and how you meet people and what you're known for come from one planet, Gemini's quick version at the top and Virgo's precise one at the front. Few cusps tie a chart so closely to a single planet, so Mercury's sign, house and aspects carry more weight than usual. Where Mercury sits shows where your working life tends to centre: the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In about 1 London chart in 4, Virgo opens the 12th as well, and the house of retreat joins the list of what Mercury rules.

About 1 birth in 11 has Gemini on the Midheaven, at London as at New York. In Placidus, the house system I use, the Midheaven is the 10th cusp, and its sign comes from the time of day alone, which is why the two cities agree. Gemini lies next to the June solstice, where the Sun's path runs nearly level with the equator, so the meridian takes a little longer than average to cross it: about two hours and eight minutes a day. Sagittarius is always on the IC opposite, and Jupiter rules your home life ([Sagittarius on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/)). The 10th is wide in these charts, about 36° to 38° at London: Gemini never opens the 9th or the 11th as well, and in about 1 London chart in 5 the whole of Cancer lies inside the 10th, giving the Moon a voice in your public life ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). [Gemini on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/) and [Gemini on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/) describe other charts.

The Midheaven moves through a sign in about two hours, so a birth time rounded to the hour can put it in the wrong one. I check the time before I say anything about your working life ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
