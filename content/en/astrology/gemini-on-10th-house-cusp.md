Gemini on the 10th house cusp puts a messenger at the top of the chart. What people know you for, and the role you take in the world, tends to be built from words, links and versatility.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where what you can do becomes visible, through roles, responsibilities and contributions, and its sign gives that visibility a style ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) there, you tend to be known as the person who explains, connects, translates or keeps information moving, and to be trusted with the message as much as with the task. A public life under this sign often runs on more than one strand at a time, or changes direction as you learn, and both are ways of following curiosity rather than failures to settle. The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) describes a direction, not a job title, and a Gemini Midheaven fits a teacher, a driver, a seller, a writer, a coordinator or an engineer equally well: what matters is that the work keeps you learning and talking.

At the latitudes of London and New York, every chart with this Midheaven has [Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/). [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) therefore rules both the Ascendant and the Midheaven: it is your chart ruler as well as the ruler of your public life, and how you meet people and what you're known for come from one planet, Gemini's quick version at the top and Virgo's precise one at the front. Few cusps tie a chart so closely to a single planet, so Mercury's sign, house and aspects carry more weight than usual. Mercury's house is the part of life your work keeps coming back to: the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In about 1 London chart in 4, Virgo opens the 12th as well, and the house of retreat joins the list of what Mercury rules.

Gemini lies next to the June solstice, where the Sun's path runs nearly level with the equator, so the meridian needs a little longer than average to cross it: about two hours and eight minutes a day, which comes to about 1 birth in 11. London and New York agree on that figure, because in Placidus the Midheaven is the 10th cusp, and it takes its sign from the hour, not the latitude. Sagittarius is always on the IC opposite, and Jupiter rules your home life ([Sagittarius on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/)). In about 1 London chart in 5, the whole of Cancer sits inside your 10th, which spans about 36° to 38° there, and the Moon gets a voice in your public life ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). With Virgo rising, Gemini opens only this house: [Gemini on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/) goes with Libra rising, [Gemini on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/) mostly with Leo.

The Midheaven moves through a sign in about two hours, so a birth time rounded to the hour can put it in the wrong one. I check the time before I say anything about your working life ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
