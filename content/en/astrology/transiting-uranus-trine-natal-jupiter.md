Jupiter and Uranus both like to widen things: one your understanding, the other your room to differ. When transiting Uranus stands 120° from your natal Jupiter, for about twenty months, an outlook can grow without anything having to break first.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) innovates and differentiates; it wants room to test other arrangements. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) synthesises: it turns experience into principles and gives your life its sense of direction. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates two functions through flow while each keeps its identity, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) lets the new and the meaningful travel together.

Ideas that would normally meet resistance, yours or other people's, go through. The field you've been reading about turns out to have room for you; a different way of working fits your values without a fight; a belief you held loosely finds the form that makes it yours. It often feels less like change than like recognition, as if you'd meant this all along.

The trine's risk is passivity. Nothing pushes, so it's easy to enjoy the lift and do nothing with it, or to say yes to every opening at once. A trine to Jupiter is also the transit most often sold as luck. I don't read it for windfalls or wins, and I never forecast money ([how I read](/how-i-read/)): what it gives is ease in changing your mind, which lasts longer.

With Jupiter in your 10th and Uranus crossing your 6th ([Uranus transiting the 6th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-6th-house/)), a new tool or routine in your daily work can widen what you think your vocation is. Your birth time draws those houses; without it, I don't give the reading at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use for timing, the trine lasts about twenty months, eleven at the briefest, sometimes with a single exact hit: most passes cross the exact degree three times, but in about one in fourteen Uranus touches it only once, turning just short of it, and lingers about as long.

Trines usually join signs of one element, so Uranus trines your Jupiter from the other two signs of its element. Uranus is in Gemini until May 2033 ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)): with Jupiter in Libra or Aquarius, one of your trines falls between 2025 and 2033, its month set by Jupiter's degree.

In the site's count the ages scatter widely: someone born at the start of 1965 met a trine before their first birthday, someone born at the start of 1945 not until about 49, with the second waiting until about 79; for the start of 1965, the second came at about 27. Nobody reaches 80 without at least one. Born with [Jupiter trine Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-trine-uranus/), you've carried this ease from the start, and its transits bring it back into season. The rest of the round runs through [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Uranus sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Uranus square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-jupiter/), while [transiting Uranus opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-jupiter/) falls between the two trines.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick the opening that fits your principles. A trine to Jupiter offers several at once, and growth comes from following one through, not from sampling them all.

Do the slow part while it's easy. Study and practice go well under this trine, and twenty months of steady work leave something the next square won't shake.

Share what you've found. Jupiter's meaning grows when it's taught, and the trine makes a new idea easier to explain now than it may be later.
