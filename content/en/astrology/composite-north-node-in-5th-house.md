They met in a choir, a running club or a campaign office, and some of those couples have never quite been on a date. Under composite North Node in the 5th house, the growth lies in leaving the meeting early to go and have fun.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

A composite is a chart I don't draw, so here is the technique as its users describe it. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) gives a relationship its own chart, made of midpoints. Its [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the direction the bond grows in, its [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, the ground it already stands on. With the South Node in [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), that ground is collective: friends, causes, a circle you both belong to, the shared ideal that brought you together. Across from it, [the 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) holds the North Node. Greek astrologers called this succedent house, Leo's by nature, Good Fortune, and made it Venus's joy; its matters are play, romance, pleasure, and things created for the love of creating them.

Two people who are perfectly capable of fun on their own can find that, together, everything they do is for something or with someone: the fundraiser, the friends' weekend, the team. The 5th-house direction asks for time that is only yours and only for joy, a game, a dance class, a ridiculous project, an evening planned purely because it will be lovely.

[North Node in the 5th house](/astrology/north-node-in-5th-house/) asks one person for joy and creations of their own; [their North Node in your 5th house](/astrology/north-node-in-5th-house-synastry/) makes your pleasures part of how the other person grows. In the composite, the pleasure has to be shared, and so does the courage to be a little silly together.

Tradition also gave the 5th to children. No chart predicts a pregnancy or a child, in a composite or anywhere else ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and this site never reads children's charts ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). Here the house speaks of what two adults enjoy and create.

Its cusp needs both birth times, drawn either as the midpoint of your two natal 5th cusps or from the composite Midheaven for the place you live ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The times matter more than people expect: an hour's error in one of them moves that person's Midheaven by roughly fifteen degrees, and the composite one by half that, often enough to carry the node across a cusp into [composite North Node in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-4th-house/) or [composite North Node in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-6th-house/). On top of that comes the degree or two between the mean and the true node; I look at both.

Pleasure can turn into performance: a bond that only enjoys itself in front of friends, or turns romance into a show for the group it came from. The 11th-house end stays useful, since friends who know you both keep the fun honest, and a shared ideal gives play something to come home to.

Composite readers set this node beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it makes no promise about what the relationship becomes. Pleasure is shared freely here, and the chart should be too: drawn only when both of you have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
