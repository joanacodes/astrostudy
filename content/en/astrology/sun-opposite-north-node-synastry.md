Sun opposite North Node in synastry is a Sun sitting on someone else's South Node, the end of their nodal axis opposite the North. That's the familiar end: who the Sun person is matches what the other already knows how to be, and the direction the other is growing in lies across the chart from both of them.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the identity a person lives from and is recognised by. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a direction of growth, built by practice, and never comes alone: the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), exactly opposite, holds what is already consolidated, the capacities someone reaches for without thinking. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two ends as a polarity. Here, among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the Sun sits on the familiar end and the growth on the far one.

On the North Node side, the other person tends to feel known almost at once. Their way of being matches the strengths you already rely on, so time together is restful: nothing has to be explained or learned. With your South Node in Aries, say, an Aries Sun is the decisive, go-it-alone self you've long known how to be, while your North Node in Libra, learning give-and-take, faces you both from across the chart.

If the Sun is yours, you're likely to feel recognised and welcomed quickly. The other person gets you without effort.

What flows, then, is familiarity, and familiarity is a resource: established strengths are the ground growth pushes off from.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

This seesaw tips towards comfort. Around the Sun person, the North Node person may lean on what they already do well and put off what they're learning, and the pair can settle into that. Growth may start to feel like moving away from the Sun person, since it points across the chart from them. The Sun person, without meaning to, may reward the old pattern, simply because they liked the other that way.

None of this is inevitable. Comfort only hardens into habit when nobody looks up.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Treat the familiarity as a base, not a destination. If the North Node is yours, bring what you're learning home to this person instead of practising it where they can't see; if the Sun is yours, welcome the other's new direction even when it looks nothing like you. Co-workers with this contact fall into an easy rhythm that can stretch to include a new task; friends, relatives and couples can let the old ease carry new things.

This is the contact people most often describe as having known each other before. Some astrologers read it as a tie from another life; I can't check that, so I read what I can see, familiarity, and leave the rest to you. A feeling that strong isn't consent either: I read two charts only when both people want the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Within 8°, it turns up between about 1 pair of people in 11, whichever way round. Mean or true, I check both nodes; close to the edge of the orb, they may not agree. Both birth times are needed, because the houses show where in each life the familiar feeling sits ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Within a single chart, this polarity is called [Sun opposite North Node](/astrology/sun-opposite-north-node/). [Sun conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) is its reverse, the Sun on the growing end; [Sun square North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-north-node-synastry/) sits between the two ends, while [Sun sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-north-node-synastry/) and [Sun trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-north-node-synastry/) touch both more lightly.
