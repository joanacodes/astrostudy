Who empties the dishwasher, who walks the dog, who answers the post, and at what hour: the 6th house is the small machinery of a shared life. Composite Uranus there keeps redesigning the machinery.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The tradition called the 6th Bad Fortune, gave Mars its joy there and gave the house illness. A composite predicts no illness and no health problem, for either person or for the pair ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); in a relationship chart, [the 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the routine, the shared chores and the work done side by side. It ends at the composite Descendant, which the two methods place differently, so a Uranus late in the 6th can move into the 7th when the houses are rebuilt for the place where you live. Either way, the house needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Like Virgo, its natural sign, this cadent house is about how things get done. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) there makes the pair's daily rhythm its own invention: night shifts and late breakfasts, chores that rotate by the week, work from home or from two cities, a small shared business run on methods nobody else uses. The bond is happiest when the routine is chosen rather than imposed, and it will change a system the moment a better one appears. Care has its inventive streak too: the two of you look after each other in practical, unexpected ways, a meal left ready after a long day, a task quietly taken over. Two creatures of habit can find their relationship surprisingly experimental about how the days go; two improvisers, that together they build routines that actually stick. Where the two of you also work together, the house describes that arrangement too: who leads which task, how a disagreement about method gets settled, and how much of each person's way of working is left alone.

A single person with [Uranus in the 6th house](/astrology/uranus-in-6th-house/) needs freedom in daily work; a partner whose Uranus falls in yours, [their Uranus in your 6th house](/astrology/uranus-in-6th-house-synastry/), unsettles your routines. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the unsettled routine is the shared one. [Composite Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-virgo/), the sign of this house, belongs to couples born in the 1960s, or whose births fall either side of those years; the house goes to whichever pair's two birth times put it there.

The trap is a routine that never sets, or a revolt against routine as such, with one of you quietly left doing the steady part. What helps is to agree which tasks are fixed and which are free to change, and to swap the dull ones openly rather than leaving them to whoever minds least.

[Composite Uranus in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-5th-house/) puts the invention into play; [composite Uranus in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-7th-house/), into the terms of the partnership. I don't work with composites, so take this as the method's view: it belongs next to both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and to two people who each said yes to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
