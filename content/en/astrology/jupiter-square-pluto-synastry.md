More room, says one; what's underneath, asks the other. Jupiter square Pluto in synastry crosses the two at right angles, give or take 4.25°: Jupiter in one chart, Pluto in the other.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) pushes outward: more scope, more possibility, a bigger frame for experience. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) goes down: to the motives, attachments and power patterns beneath a situation, and to what has to go so that the rest can live. A [square](/astrology/square/) makes two functions work against each other, which is how each one learns; in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), each person supplies one of them.

At this angle, Jupiter's growth keeps adding while Pluto's depth keeps stripping back, and each tests the other: is the expansion real, and is the clearing necessary?

If you're the Jupiter person here, you want to widen, include, believe the best; the other keeps asking what's underneath and what has to go. It can feel as if your hopes are being interrogated, or as if nothing good is allowed to stay simple.

If you're the Pluto person, you want to get to the root before anything is added, and the other keeps adding. It can feel as though they won't look at what matters, or as though their enthusiasm is a way of not looking.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Friction, never failure, and no power struggle by necessity. Pluto can confuse control with safety, and can assume that every unequal exchange is a deliberate bid for power; Jupiter can push for growth before the ground is cleared. Transformation is not destruction, and expansion is not excess, but in the heat of this square each person can see the other's function at its worst.

Its four relatives:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-pluto-synastry/):** growth and depth fused, one intense movement rather than a push and pull.
- **[Jupiter sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-pluto-synastry/):** a sign closer, an opening between the two that waits for a choice.
- **[Jupiter trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-trine-pluto-synastry/):** a sign further, renewal and growth carrying each other.
- **[Jupiter opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** the full width of the zodiac, the wide view facing the root.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Separate what you're adding from what you're clearing, and do them in order. When the Pluto person needs something examined, let it be examined before the Jupiter person proposes the next step; when the Jupiter person sees an opening, let the Pluto person say what it would cost. Couples renovating a home or a habit, friends rethinking a shared plan, colleagues restructuring a team, adult siblings sorting out a family business: each needs the same sequence.

The ages can be close: the Pluto of everyone born between October 1988 and January 1991 squares the Jupiter of a mid-1985 birthday, three to five years earlier, and people born between June and December 1961 have the reverse. It fails the one test I set for a synastry aspect, a personal body at one end (the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Ascendant or Midheaven), because everyone born in those years shares it. In the houses, though, it reads as two eras' habits of growth and change rubbing, and that I do read.

The hour of birth is far too short a time for either planet to shift the square; it matters for everything I'd say about it, the houses, so I won't read it without both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Jupiter square Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-square-pluto/) within one chart sets a person's own convictions against their own depths. Between two people the meeting needs care and consent, so I read it only with both ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
