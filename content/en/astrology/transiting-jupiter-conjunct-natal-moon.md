Once every twelve years or so, Jupiter passes over the place your Moon held when you were born. The planet of scope touches the part of you that tends your feelings and needs, and for a while there is more room inside, and often at home too.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens what it touches and looks for meaning; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feeling, attachment and the sense of being safe. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) runs the two together, so for the length of this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your needs receive the generosity Jupiter usually spends on plans and ideas.

People often describe a softening: more patience with their own moods, more warmth for others, an urge to gather people round a table or to make home roomier, in space or in spirit. Old needs can be named and taken seriously. The risk sits on the same side: comfort that grows past what comforts, care handed to everyone except yourself, a promise to be there for more people than one person can be there for. Weather, not a forecast: it doesn't predict a move or a pregnancy, two things it's often sold as ([Can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)).

Tradition gave Jupiter its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Cancer, the Moon's own sign: of all the places Jupiter can stand, the old authors thought it at its best where the Moon rules. I take that as a picture rather than a rule: growth that starts from feeling looked after.

The crossing falls inside Jupiter's year in your Moon's house. With your Moon in the 4th, that's [Jupiter transiting the 4th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-4th-house/), and home and family are the setting. Born with [Moon conjunct Jupiter](/astrology/moon-conjunct-jupiter/), you have this crossing at each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/), and the warmth you were born with gets a little more room again.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, my orbs give this crossing about eleven days when Jupiter goes straight over your Moon. In about three cases out of ten, Jupiter's retrograde loop takes it over your Moon three times, and the season stretches to about eight months.

Here the birth time matters twice. It places your Moon's house, and it places the Moon itself: your Moon advances about half a degree every hour, and Jupiter needs about six days on average to cover half a degree, so each hour of doubt about your birth time moves these dates by about a week. Without the time, there is nothing I can responsibly read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By the age of 80, people born from 1930 to 2009 have had Jupiter cross their Moon six or seven times, so most know several versions of this softening. What it opens gets revisited from other angles in the years between: as help worth accepting at [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-moon/), as a squeeze on habits and space at [transiting Jupiter square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-moon/), as unforced warmth at [transiting Jupiter trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-moon/), and as home weighed against the wider world at [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-moon/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Accept care as well as giving it. Jupiter on the Moon makes generosity easy, and easiest of all to give away.

Make room on purpose: an evening a week that belongs to no one else, a corner of the house that's yours, an old habit dropped to make space for one you'd rather keep.

Name a need out loud to someone who can meet it. This crossing tends to make needs feel less embarrassing, and that is worth using while it lasts.
