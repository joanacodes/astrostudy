Some pairs forgive each other before the apology is finished. Under composite Venus in Pisces, the part of a relationship that matters most can't be itemised: compassion, imagination, an hour spent listening to music without a word.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is the halfway point between two natal Venuses, and it shows what the bond holds dear and how it gives. [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), mutable water, is where tradition places Venus's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), the sign where it is said to work at its strongest; Virgo, opposite, is its fall. The sign answers to [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) in modern tropical astrology and to [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) in the tradition, and both count when I read a birth chart. Neptune takes about fourteen years over a sign, so two people of similar age simply share their generation's Neptune; Jupiter moves on about once a year, which makes composite Jupiter the more personal of the two for a pair.

Practical people can find that between them tenderness is the default. The bond values what can't be measured and gives without a ledger, to each other and often to whoever turns up needing help. Its pleasures run to music, film, the sea, art, a shared spiritual practice or simply quiet: anything the two of you can disappear into together. A forgiving heart is what [Venus in Pisces](/astrology/venus-in-pisces/) gives one person at birth; the composite gives it to the bond, and friends may feel it before either of you notices. As composites go, about 1 pair in 13 has it; among births, about 1 person in 14 has Venus there.

For nine pairs in ten the composite Sun stays near, under 47° away, and with a Pisces Venus it then sits in one of the two signs before Pisces, in Pisces itself, or in one of the two after. Pairs born far apart in the year are the exception, because their two midpoints can split, one on each side of the circle: about 1 pair in 10 ends up with composite Venus more than 47° away, and Virgo, the sign of Venus's fall, receives the composite Sun in about 1 pair in 22, right across from an exalted Venus. That symmetry is a coincidence of the arithmetic, and no comment on the two of you.

Acceptance without limits is its risk. A bond that forgives easily can let go of something that needed saying, and a shared dream of the relationship can drift away from how the days actually go. Compassion doesn't require giving up every boundary, and imagination isn't pretending. It helps to keep a few plain agreements in writing, and every so often to compare, gently, the picture you share with the life you share.

On one side, [composite Venus in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-venus-in-aquarius/) values the freedom inside the bond; on the other, [composite Venus in Aries](/astrology/composite-venus-in-aries/) starts the circle again with a first move. This page describes the technique as composite astrologers use it, with the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and both people's natal charts beside it; I leave composites out of my own readings, and none of this is a forecast. Kindness includes asking first, so the chart waits until both people know about it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
