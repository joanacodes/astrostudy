Cancer on the 2nd house cusp gives the house of what you own to the sign that looks after things. Money, possessions and self-worth all come back here to one question: is there enough to keep my people safe?

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the cusp of the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is your way with money and things ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) handles them as a household. You tend to spend most easily on home, food and comfort for others, and least easily on yourself. Belongings are kept for what they hold: the chipped bowl from a grandparent's kitchen, the letters in a shoebox, the coat you can't throw away. A reserve put by, however small, calms you more than a large sum spent. Self-worth is tied to care: you feel valuable when you can provide, and you may undervalue the care itself, because it came naturally. The 2nd's natural sign is Taurus, where tradition exalts the Moon, so the house of resources has always been good ground for the planet that rules it here.

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules Cancer and so your 2nd house. Its house shows where your sense of security comes from and where it's spent: the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). The Moon changes sign every two and a half days and runs through its phases in a month, and I read the feeling of having enough as tidal in the same way: it moves with how safe you feel in general, more than with the figure in the account. In nearly half the London charts with this cusp, Cancer is also rising ([Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/)), and the Moon then rules both you and your resources ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); in the others Gemini rises, and Mercury rules the chart ([Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/)).

About 1 birth in 10 has Cancer on the 2nd house cusp, at London and New York alike, in Placidus, the house system I use. Cancer is the first of the signs that rise slowly in the north, and the cusps just below the eastern horizon cross it slowly too: well over two hours a day at London. In about 2 London charts in 5, Cancer runs on into the next cusp, so [Cancer on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/) describes your chart as well and the Moon rules your everyday talk along with your money. Opposite, Capricorn opens the 8th: Saturn rules what you share and owe, the Moon what is yours. Elsewhere, about 1 London birth in 8, and 1 in 13 at New York, has Cancer wholly inside a single house ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Cancer holds this cusp for about two and a quarter hours a day, so an hour's error is often enough to lose it, and I read nothing until the birth time is settled ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The 2nd never gives a figure, either: it describes how you hold and spend, never how much there will be, and where to put savings is a question for an adviser, not for a chart ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).
