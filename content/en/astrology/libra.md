Libra is the seventh sign of the zodiac, and the only one drawn as an object rather than a living creature: a pair of scales. The Sun enters it at the September equinox, when day and night are close to equal, and that's how I read it — as the sign that keeps weighing one side against the other until both can rest.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Libra somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it asks one question: *is this fair?* It's an [air sign](/astrology/air-signs/), so it works through ideas, conversation and comparison, and a cardinal one, so it starts things — usually by turning towards someone else (the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/) explain what that means). [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), just before it, perfects what's yours. Libra is where the zodiac turns to face another person, and [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), next, goes deep into that bond.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/):** born between about 23 September and 22 October. You grow through relationships and a strong sense of fairness. You often see the other person's side before your own, and part of your path is learning that your own weight counts in the balance too.
- **[Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/):** you feel safe when things are calm between you and the people you love. Tension unsettles you more than it seems to unsettle others, so you may smooth things over before you've said what you need.
- **[Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/):** you meet the world with tact, warmth and an eye for how things look and feel, and people find you easy to approach. Venus becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/), so its sign and house matter a great deal. A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So I read Libra as one voice in a chart, never the whole of it.

## Its ruler: Venus {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, and where that planet sits in your chart shows where the sign's energy goes. Libra is ruled by [Venus](/astrology/venus/), which also rules Taurus: in Taurus it's the Venus of the body and of what you keep; in Libra, the Venus of agreements, courtesy and beauty as proportion. If Libra is on your 10th house cusp and Venus sits in your 4th, say, your public life draws on what you build at home.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is [exalted](/astrology/exaltation/) in Libra: tradition saw the planet of rules and time at its best where rules are fair and agreed by both sides. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) here, opposite its home in Aries — a fighter in the sign of negotiation — and the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is in [fall](/astrology/fall/), opposite its exaltation: the self has to learn to share the stage. A planet in detriment or fall isn't broken. It's speaking a second language, and often speaks it with more care than a native would.

## Planets in Libra {#planets-in-the-sign}

Every planet passes through Libra sooner or later: the Moon for about two and a half days a month, the Sun for a month each year, Saturn for about two and a half years in every twenty-nine and a half. In Libra, each one goes looking for its counterpart:

- **[Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/):** the Sun in fall: a self that grows through others, and has to remember to stay in the picture.
- **[Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/):** comfort comes from harmony and good company; you soothe other people more easily than you soothe yourself.
- **[Mercury in Libra](/astrology/mercury-in-libra/):** a diplomat's mind: you think by weighing views and can say hard things gracefully, though deciding may take a while.
- **[Venus in Libra](/astrology/venus-in-libra/):** Venus at home: love as partnership and courtesy, a fine sense of beauty, and a need for the exchange to be fair before love feels real.
- **[Mars in Libra](/astrology/mars-in-libra/):** Mars in detriment: you fight best for someone else or for what's right, and can take a long time to say what you want for yourself.
- **[Jupiter in Libra](/astrology/jupiter-in-libra/):** growth through partners, agreements and fair dealing; a generosity that wants everyone to get their share.
- **[Saturn in Libra](/astrology/saturn-in-libra/):** Saturn exalted: commitments taken seriously, a strong sense of justice, and relationships that mature slowly and hold.
- **[Uranus in Libra](/astrology/uranus-in-libra/):** shared by everyone born over about seven years: new ideas about partnership and equality. Its house shows where you rewrite the rules of relating.
- **[Neptune in Libra](/astrology/neptune-in-libra/):** shared by everyone born over about fourteen years: an ideal of perfect harmony and perfect love. Its house shows where you go looking for it.
- **[Pluto in Libra](/astrology/pluto-in-libra/):** shared by everyone born over many years: relationships as the place where deep change happens. Its house makes it personal.

## Libra on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Libra somewhere in its twelve houses, usually on the cusp of one of them. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Libra opens a house, that part of life takes on its question — *what's fair here, and who am I doing it with?* — and Venus, wherever it sits, carries the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/libra-rising/):** Libra rising — you meet the world with grace and diplomacy, and people often turn to you to keep the peace.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/libra-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money and possessions are a matter of fairness and taste; you may earn through people, beauty or negotiation.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/libra-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** tactful speech, learning by discussion, and often the go-between with brothers, sisters and neighbours.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/libra-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home that needs harmony and beauty; tension at home weighs on you more than tension anywhere else.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/libra-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romance with courtship and courtesy; creativity drawn to balance, design and duets.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/libra-on-6th-house-cusp/):** daily work goes best with a partner, in pleasant surroundings, with fair shares for everyone.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Libra on the Descendant, which means Aries rising: you look for an equal, someone gracious enough to balance your directness.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/libra-on-8th-house-cusp/):** shared money and intimacy are negotiated rather than seized; trust grows when the exchange feels even.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/libra-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs formed by hearing both sides; a pull towards law, philosophy and justice.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career in which you mediate, advise, design or represent others, and a public image of fairness.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/libra-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friendships that feel like partnerships, and groups where everyone gets a fair say.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/libra-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a private need for peace; you may avoid conflict without noticing, and time alone puts you back in balance.

## Libra in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Libra placements bring courtesy, attention to the other person and a wish to keep things even. The risk is keeping the peace at the cost of saying what you need. Its opposite sign, [Aries](/astrology/aries/), is the pull: Aries goes first, Libra waits for the other, and between them they cover the whole distance from *me* to *us*. That's why I read where your Libra planets fall in the other person's houses, and theirs in yours, rather than comparing Sun signs: [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind there is.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Libras are indecisive.”** Libra is slow to decide because it's honestly weighing more than one side. That's a method, not a flaw — and a Sun sign is only one placement among many.
- **“Libra is all about romance.”** Libra is about relationships of every kind — partners, colleagues, rivals, contracts — and about fairness in all of them.
- **“Born on 22 or 23 October, you're half Scorpio.”** At the minute you were born the Sun was in one sign. Near a change, your birth time settles which: that's the whole question of being [born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/).
- **“The stars have moved, so Libra is really Virgo now.”** The signs follow the seasons, not the constellations. In the [tropical zodiac](/astrology/tropical-zodiac/), Libra begins at the September equinox, whichever stars have drifted behind it.
