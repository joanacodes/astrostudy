Pluto conjunct Chiron sends the deepest part of a chart straight to its most tender place. Two groups were born with it in the eighty years counted, almost sixty years apart, and each shares it; the houses make it personal.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) reaches for the root: it releases what has outlived its use, regenerates, and hands back a deeper strength. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the place that stays tender or uneven, and the know-how that grows around it: a changed method, a different pace, help you learn to ask for and to give. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), the two act as one while each keeps its nature, so depth and the tender spot work as a single process.

In plain words, you don't skim the surface of what hurts; you go down into it, and that is where your strength comes from. You may have little patience for remedies that only change the surface, and a gift for staying beside someone else through a deep change, because you know the ground. Pluto is good here at clearing out old ways of coping that no longer serve. The care it asks is twofold: not every limit needs overcoming, since some only need working around, and the sore place is part of you, never the whole of you.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 36 born between 1930 and 2009 has Pluto within 6° of Chiron, in two groups: everyone born from August 1940 to September 1941 and from February to May 1942, and everyone born from October 1999 to November 2000.

Shared by everyone born in those months, it's a generation's aspect, made personal by its house and by any contact with your Sun, Moon or angles. In the 7th, the depth and the tender place may live in close relationships; in the 3rd, in the way you think, learn and speak about hard things. The house is the part a birth time unlocks ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Here Chiron does the travelling: even at its slowest it outpaces Pluto, which needs 12 to 31 years for a sign. Whether you were born as it closed in or as it drew away is the difference between an applying and a separating meeting ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)).

Pluto and Chiron at their other angles, and when:

- **[Pluto sextile Chiron](/astrology/pluto-sextile-chiron/):** born 1930–34, 1945–46 or 1994–95; depth offers the tender place an opening.
- **[Pluto square Chiron](/astrology/pluto-square-chiron/):** born 1947–48 or 1992–94; the urge to clear everything away rubs against what can only be accommodated.
- **[Pluto trine Chiron](/astrology/pluto-trine-chiron/):** born 1949–50 or 1988–91; renewal and adaptation support each other.
- **[Pluto opposite Chiron](/astrology/pluto-opposite-chiron/):** depth and the tender spot facing each other across the chart.

Between two charts, [Pluto conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-conjunct-chiron-synastry/) lays one person's Pluto on the other's Chiron: an intensity right at the sore place, which can help it change or press on it too hard. It is a contact I read slowly, and only with both people's consent ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-pluto/) is Chiron, moving on, reaching your Pluto. If you have the conjunction, it comes back at your [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, with Chiron home at its birth place and so at Pluto's side: for the group of 1940–42 that return has come and gone, and for those born in 1999–2000 it lies ahead. Pluto, already beside Chiron at birth, moves off and never comes back, since it needs about 248 years to go round.

Weather, not a forecast: that return brings your depth and an old sore place into the same season again. It doesn't say what will be let go.
