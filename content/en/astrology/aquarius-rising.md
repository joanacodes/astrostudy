Born with [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) on the eastern horizon, you tend to arrive friendly and a little apart. You're interested in people, often openly so, and you meet them as equals while staying unmistakably your own person.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

With fixed air on the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), ideas are held steadily and independence is kept. My shorthand for it is a friendly distance: you're curious about whoever is in front of you, and you keep enough room around yourself to see clearly. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) begins at the Ascendant in Placidus, the house system I use, and Aquarius, whose natural house is the 11th, friends, groups and hopes, the Good Spirit of the old names, sets friendship at the threshold: you tend to meet people as possible friends, and to ask what they're part of.

In the north, Aquarius begins the fastest stretch of the zodiac. At London it rises in about 69 minutes, barely two-thirds of the time [Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/) takes before it, and Aquarius, Pisces and Aries together need under three hours, about as long as Libra alone. It takes 74 minutes at Paris and 86 at New York, and in London it's the rising sign of about one birth in 21. At London the Ascendant crosses about thirteen degrees of Aquarius in half an hour, so a time rounded to the hour pins the sign down only when the Ascendant falls near its very middle; elsewhere it may be Capricorn rising or [Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/), and I won't guess ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Signs that rise fast in the northern hemisphere rise slowly in the southern, so an Aquarius Ascendant there takes its time.

## First impressions {#first-impressions}

Friendly and hard to place: that's how a first meeting with you often goes. You're open in conversation, interested in other people's ideas, and plainly your own person. You treat everyone much the same, whatever their title, which many find refreshing and a few find puzzling.

Independence is sometimes read as coldness, and difference as a pose. Autonomy isn't isolation: an Aquarius Ascendant keeps a little distance in order to see clearly, not to keep people out, and it's often the one asking whether the group's way of doing things is fair to everyone in it.

As for how far a first impression goes: in [my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the rising sign is among the placements everyone reads first, and it held about a third of the time. I treat it as one voice among many, which Aquarius would approve of.

## Its rulers {#its-ruler}

Your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/) comes as a pair with this rising sign, one traditional and one modern. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional one, shows where you build structures you can rely on: habits, commitments, the rules you set yourself. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern one, shows where you change them once they stop fitting, and where you insist on doing things your own way. I read both, and I read them as the builder and the reformer of the same independence ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Saturn, the older ruler, is also one of the three placements that held best in my notes.

Look at how the two meet in your chart. When they make an aspect, as in [Saturn square Uranus](/astrology/saturn-square-uranus/), your two chart rulers speak to each other directly, and the pull between keeping and changing becomes a running theme of your life.

Leo, on your Descendant, is the sign where both are in detriment, Saturn by tradition and Uranus in modern tropical astrology: a chart ruler there has to learn to be seen as one particular person, not one of many.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In a partner, Aquarius rising looks for a friend: someone who respects your independence and doesn't need it explained. You can be loyal for decades and still need an evening alone or a cause of your own, because closeness, for you, includes room.

Leo, the opposite sign, is on your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), so big-hearted people who love openly and like to celebrate tend to catch your eye ([Leo on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/)).

Love, in the end, can be a long friendship with someone who also likes a party.
