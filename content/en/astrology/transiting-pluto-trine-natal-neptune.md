Pluto trine your natal Neptune is one of the rarest Pluto transits a life can hold. About 5 people in 6 never meet it by 80, and those who do meet it near the end of a long life, when imagination and renewal can at last move together without strain.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines, idealises and feels with others; it holds your faith and your sense of a larger meaning. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) renews by letting what is finished end. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates them through flow, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the ideals of a long life can be looked at without pressure: kept, released or handed on. It tends to be a quiet transit, and a generous one, a time for memories to find their meaning and for compassion to reach people once held at a distance.

The trine's caution is the usual one: ease isn't passivity. What this flow offers still has to be taken up, in a conversation, a piece of writing, a gift made while it can be made. Neptune's blind spot of confusing symbolic meaning with literal prediction matters too, because the trine speaks about meaning, never about how long anyone lives ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).

Many people read about this transit for a parent. Their chart is theirs: share what you find with them, not about them ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

It comes last in a long sequence. Pluto crossed your Neptune in your twenties ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-neptune/)) and squared it in your sixties or seventies, if at all ([Transiting Pluto square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-neptune/)), before it reaches the trine. Nobody born from 1930 to 2009 has [Neptune trine Pluto](/astrology/neptune-trine-pluto/) at birth. With Neptune in your 5th, the trine comes from around your 9th ([Pluto transiting the 9th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-9th-house/)) or your 1st: what you have loved and made can be seen in the light of what you came to believe. Your birth time is what sets those houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use, the trine lasts about two years and eight months, twenty-two months in the quickest passes. Three passes in five or so bring three exact hits; the rest bring five.

About 5 people in 6 born between 1930 and 2009 don't meet it by 80, and nobody meets it twice. The reason is distance. In every chart of those years Pluto began some 45° to 65° behind Neptune, so it must travel that gap and then 120° more, which at Pluto's pace takes close to 80 years or longer. Births from 1930 to about 1943, with Neptune in Virgo, met it between about 78 and 81 while Pluto crossed Capricorn: at 79.1 for 1 January 1935. Births from 1943 to 1957, with Neptune in Libra, meet it between about 79 and 86 as Pluto crosses Aquarius, until early 2044. For later births it comes after 85, if at all.

The sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-neptune/)) reaches almost everyone, often twice, and the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-neptune/)) no one at all.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tell the story of what you have believed, from the beginning, to someone who wants to hear it.

Give something away while you can choose where it goes: a collection, a skill, a family story, a cause.

Let the easy days be easy. A trine at this age asks for nothing to be fixed.
