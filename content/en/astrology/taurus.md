Taurus is the second sign of the zodiac, the one the Sun crosses in the middle of spring in the northern hemisphere. Where Aries starts things, Taurus makes them last. Wherever it falls in a chart, I read it as the place where you take your time, and where you'd rather keep what's good than chase what's new.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Taurus somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it brings steadiness, patience and pleasure in what's real: food, touch, a well-made thing, a place that's yours. Taurus asks *is this worth keeping?* It's an [earth sign](/astrology/earth-signs/), like Virgo and Capricorn, so it trusts what it can touch; and it's fixed, one of the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/), so once it has chosen a course, it holds it. In the sequence of signs, each one answering the last, Taurus answers [Aries](/astrology/aries/): after the spark, the slow work of making something grow. [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), next, will want to talk about it.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/):** born between about 20 April and 20 May. You grow by building what lasts — a skill, a home, a bond — and your sense of purpose rarely changes course on a whim.
- **[Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/):** the Moon is exalted here, at its steadiest. You feel safe with routine, comfort and people you can count on, and your moods take their time to change, in both directions.
- **[Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/):** you meet the world calmly and at your own pace, and people often find you reassuring before they know you. Venus becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/). A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

Each of these is one part of the picture. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So I read Taurus through the whole chart, beginning with where Venus sits.

## Its ruler: Venus {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, the one that carries the sign's business into the rest of the chart. Taurus belongs to [Venus](/astrology/venus/): the Venus of comfort, beauty and value, while Libra, its other sign, gets the Venus of fairness and partnership. Wherever your Venus sits, by sign and house, shows where your Taurus side looks for security and pleasure. With Taurus on the cusp of the 2nd house and Venus in the 7th, say, your sense of security is bound up with a partner.

Three more planets have a particular bond with Taurus:

- The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is [exalted](/astrology/exaltation/) here: feelings find solid ground, and comfort steadies the heart.
- [Mars](/astrology/mars/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite Scorpio, the second sign tradition gives it: drive works slowly and stubbornly in Taurus, more siege than charge. It gets there, at its own pace.
- [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the modern ruler of Scorpio, is in detriment here too for modern astrologers: an urge to tear down and rebuild meets a sign that would rather keep what works.

## Planets in Taurus {#planets-in-the-sign}

A planet in Taurus slows down and settles; the house it sits in shows where that plays out.

- **[Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/):** a patient will; you grow by building something that lasts, and by staying with it.
- **[Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/):** the Moon exalted — steady feelings and a need for comfort, routine and touch; slow to upset, slow to let go.
- **[Mercury in Taurus](/astrology/mercury-in-taurus/):** a practical, deliberate mind; you think things through, and once you've decided, you rarely change your view.
- **[Venus in Taurus](/astrology/venus-in-taurus/):** Venus at home — loyal, sensual love, shown through care, food, time and presence.
- **[Mars in Taurus](/astrology/mars-in-taurus/):** Mars in detriment — energy that builds slowly and lasts; hard to push, and hard to stop.
- **[Jupiter in Taurus](/astrology/jupiter-in-taurus/):** growth through patience; generosity with comfort, and faith in what's built step by step.
- **[Saturn in Taurus](/astrology/saturn-in-taurus/):** security and self-worth learned the long way; you build what's yours brick by brick.
- **[Uranus in Taurus](/astrology/uranus-in-taurus/):** a generation's shake-up of what it means to own, earn and keep, shared by everyone born over about seven years; its house makes it personal.
- **[Neptune in Taurus](/astrology/neptune-in-taurus/):** ideals that look for beauty in the material world; shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so I read it through its house.
- **[Pluto in Taurus](/astrology/pluto-in-taurus/):** Pluto in detriment — a generation's deep reckoning with value, land and resources; shared over twelve years or more, made personal by its house.

## Taurus on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Taurus somewhere in its twelve houses, usually on the cusp of one of them. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Taurus opens a house, that's where you go slowly and want what lasts, and Venus, wherever it sits, carries the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/taurus-rising/):** Taurus rising — a calm, steady presence; you take life at your own pace, and people find you reassuring.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/taurus-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** Taurus in its natural house — a patient relationship with money and possessions; you value quality, and keep what's good.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/taurus-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** a deliberate way of speaking and learning; you need time to think, and you say what you mean.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a sanctuary — comfort, good food, a place that stays the same; you put down roots and keep them.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/taurus-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasure through the senses — music, food, making things with your hands; romance that takes its time.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/):** steady, reliable work at a pace you can keep; a good routine, once found, lasts you for years.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Taurus on the Descendant, which means Scorpio rising — drawn to steady, loyal partners; you want a relationship you can rely on, and you offer the same.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/taurus-on-8th-house-cusp/):** trust built slowly; in intimacy and shared money, you want to feel secure before you share.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/taurus-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs grounded in experience; you learn by seeing and touching for yourself, and travel at your own pace.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career you'd rather build steadily than reinvent; a public image of reliability.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/taurus-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a few loyal friends, kept for years; groups that build something concrete together.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/taurus-on-12th-house-cusp/):** comfort sought in private; a quiet inner calm, and a stubborn streak you may not see in yourself.

## Taurus in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Taurus planets bring steadiness and loyalty: the person who has them shows love by staying, turning up and taking care of small things. To a restless partner that can feel like safety or like standing still, depending on where those planets land in their chart and what they touch. Across the zodiac sits [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), the opposite sign: Taurus wants to keep things as they are, Scorpio to go beneath the surface and change them. Between two charts, that axis can make a powerful attraction — and a tug of war over trust and control. As for comparing Sun signs, [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind: I read both whole charts before I say anything about a pair.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Taureans are lazy.”** Taurus moves at its own pace, and once it starts, it rarely stops. Slow to begin isn't the same as idle.
- **“Taurus only cares about money and things.”** Taurus values what's real and lasting, and that includes people, time, and the work of looking after them.
- **“Taurus never changes.”** Fixed signs hold a course; they change when the reasons are solid. Steadfast and stubborn are the same quality, seen from two sides.
- **“Born on the cusp, you're half Aries.”** At the minute you were born, the Sun was in one sign or the other; within a day of a change, your birth time settles which. More on being [born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/).
