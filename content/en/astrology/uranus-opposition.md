Uranus takes about 84 years to go round the zodiac. Halfway, it stands opposite the place it held when you were born. That is the Uranus opposition, and depending on your birth year it comes anywhere between about 39 and 44½.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) needs about 84 years to circle the Sun, so the opposition, transiting Uranus 180° from natal Uranus, is the halfway point of a cycle most lives see once ([transiting Uranus opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-uranus/)). In the site's count for births from 1930 to 2009, the first exact opposition fell between 38.9 and 44.5 in all 160 birth dates computed. A quarter of the way round, between 18.4 and 22.9, came the first square ([transiting Uranus square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-uranus/)); three quarters round comes a second square, in the sixties; and the full circle, at about 84, is the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/).

The age moves because Uranus's orbit is slightly stretched, so the planet crosses some stretches of the zodiac faster than others. Births on 1 January of every fifth year show the drift:

- **1935 to 1955:** about 39. Born in 1940, you met it at 38.9, 39.4 and 39.7.
- **1960 to 1970:** later each time: 40.3 for 1960, 42.2 for 1965, 43.3 for 1970.
- **1980 and 1985:** the latest, about 44½: 44.4 for 1980, 44.5 for 1985.
- **1990 to 2005:** earlier again: 43.7 for 1990, 42.7 for 2000, 41.7 for 2005.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Like any Uranus transit, the opposition stays in orb about twenty months with the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and the yearly retrograde loop usually brings three exact passes ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). It works through polarity, as every opposition does: the far side of the circle, where the part of you that wants to differ and rearrange faces the life it has actually built.

Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, so your natal Uranus sign is shared by everyone born over the same seven years, and so is the sky that brings your opposition. People born between February 1981 and December 1988, with Uranus in Sagittarius, meet it while Uranus crosses Gemini, from 2025 to 2033 ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)); those born in the late 1970s met theirs with Uranus in Taurus. What makes it yours is the house your natal Uranus sits in, which needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and the aspects it makes to your personal planets.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

Uranus, in the system I built for my readings, is the function of innovation and difference: what makes you distinct, and the freedom to rearrange what no longer fits. The same system names its blind spot plainly: changing a system before understanding what still works. That is the question I bring to the opposition. What genuinely needs more room, and what is only restlessness? It isn't chaos, and it isn't rebellion by decree.

It rarely comes alone. In the same count, the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) fell between 39.4 and 42.1, and for births from the mid-1940s to the mid-1980s the [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/) came between about 37 and 42. In the mid-forties, Saturn's second opposition can join them ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)). I read each transit through its own planet first, then together, and I tell you which of them rest on placements that held less often: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Uranus was checked like every placement and wasn't one of the three that held far more often.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Everyone has the Uranus opposition at 42.”** It came between 38.9 and 44.5, depending on the birth year.
- **“It means walking away from everything.”** It asks for room where life has grown too tight. What changes, if anything, is yours to decide.
- **“It's one moment.”** It lasts about twenty months, usually with three exact passes.
- **“People your age go through the same thing.”** The timing is shared; the house and the aspects of your Uranus are not.
