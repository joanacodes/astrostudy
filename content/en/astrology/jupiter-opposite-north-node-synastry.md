Across from the other's North Node, right on top of their South Node: that's where Jupiter opposite North Node in synastry puts one person's Jupiter. The Jupiter person's warmth and faith land on what the node person already knows how to do; the direction the node person is growing in sits on the far side of the chart.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) enlarges whatever it touches: it appreciates, explains, finds the meaning in things. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a capacity someone is learning, and its partner, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), to the consolidated skills they can rely on. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one shines one person's light on the other's familiar end.

If the North Node is yours, this person admires what you're already good at. The skills you take for granted, the way you've always coped, the knowledge you grew up with: they find it impressive, and say so. It's restful and it's affirming, after years of being told to grow.

If the Jupiter is yours, the other person's established strengths fit your idea of a full life. You enjoy what they do well, you widen it, you may even build on it together. What they're reaching for across the way may interest you less.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opposition is a seesaw, never enmity, and this one can come to rest on one side. Praise for the familiar is pleasant to give and to receive, so the relationship can become the place where the node person's old strengths are celebrated and the new thing is never practised. The Jupiter person may not even notice they're encouraging the past.

Across the axis, the other angles shift the weight. [Jupiter conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) flips the picture, putting the same warmth on the growth itself; [Jupiter sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-north-node-synastry/) lights both ends a little; [Jupiter square North Node in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-north-node-synastry/) stands at the junction between them; [Jupiter trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-trine-north-node-synastry/) leans the support towards the new.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use the comfort as a base. If the Jupiter is yours, ask now and then about the unfamiliar thing, and treat it as seriously as the strengths you admire. If the North Node is yours, enjoy being appreciated, and bring one new attempt into the relationship each season, where it can be seen and not only the old skills. Old friends with this contact can be each other's safest audience; a couple, each other's home; a parent and an adult child, the place where a first try at something different is welcome.

In the count, a birth in mid-1975 pairs this way with births from February to August 1977, whose South Node falls on the 1975 Jupiter, and with births from July to November 1976, whose Jupiter sits on the 1975 South Node. Whole spells of births carry each side, so this opposition stays outside my count of a synastry, which only includes contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Read through the houses, it describes how one era honours what another has already learned, more than anything between two people.

I weigh both nodes, the mean and the true, which never part by more than a degree or two; the windows above were worked out with the true node ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). Reading it through the houses takes both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

When one chart holds [Jupiter opposite North Node](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-north-node/), a person carries their own gifts across. Here the gifts are admired by someone else, and a reading of it needs both people's yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
