Trying harder is the reflex this square tests. When Chiron makes a right angle to your Mars, the old way of getting through a difficulty, more effort, more speed, more stubbornness, meets something that doesn't give way to force, and the bill for all that pushing arrives.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is effort with a direction: wanting, starting, competing, holding a line. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) describes a difficulty that persists and the craft of working around it. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates two functions through constructive friction, which under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) means your drive and your sore spot keep catching on each other until one of them changes shape.

Usually it is the method that has to change. You may hit the same wall in the same place and feel the old frustration rise. You may realise that the extra hours, the refusal to ask, the habit of proving yourself have been compensating for the difficulty at a price nobody counted. Or a boundary gets crossed and you notice an old flinch: the delay before you object, or the sudden heat once you do. Assertion is not aggression, and courage is not recklessness; the square asks for a way of holding your ground that is neither collapse nor explosion.

With [Mars in the 10th house](/astrology/mars-in-10th-house/), the friction tends to sit in ambition and work, while Chiron crosses your 1st ([Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/)) and touches an old unease about how you come across. That pairing depends on the minutes of your birth; without them I can't place it, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Mars square Chiron](/astrology/mars-square-chiron/), you know this wall already, and the transit is a chance to stop climbing it the same way.

None of it predicts an injury, an accident or a falling-out. If frustration settles into something heavier, a low mood that stays or anger that frightens you, that is for a professional rather than a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the square usually lasts about ten months. In nearly two passes out of three Chiron crosses the exact square three times as its retrograde loop doubles back; about one in three is a single crossing of some three weeks; about one in twenty is a slow five-crossing stretch of some twenty-one months, long for a planet that likes quick results.

Two squares fall in each round of about 50 years, so most people meet three by 80, a few two, a few four, and nobody none. The first follows [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-mars/) and [transiting Chiron sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-mars/) and tests the way of acting that began then. The second comes after [transiting Chiron opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-mars/) and the second [transiting Chiron trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-mars/), and revises it before Chiron returns. Chiron's speed changes so much along its orbit that the two squares are rarely spaced evenly.

Ahead: Chiron in Aries, until April 2027, squares Mars in Cancer and Capricorn; in Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, Mars in Leo and Aquarius; in Gemini, from July 2033 to April 2039, Mars in Virgo and Pisces.

## What helps {#what-helps}

When something isn't working, change the method before you add force.

Treat rest as part of the effort, scheduled like the work itself.

Say one boundary calmly, early, before the old heat builds.

Write down what coping has cost you this year in time and energy. Seeing the bill is the first step to paying less of it.
