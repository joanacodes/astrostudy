Their Venus in your 6th house shows affection in practical form. Coffee made the way you like it, the job done before you asked, a pleasant word on a hard Tuesday: with this overlay, care lives in the ordinary routines of the week.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) runs on chores, schedules and the job in hand, along with the people you work beside and the small disciplines of looking after yourself. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) brings appreciation, affinity and the wish for things to run in harmony. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their fondness goes into your routines.

Daily life can feel lighter with them around. They make dull tasks pleasant company, appreciate the way you do things, and may help without fuss: the shelf put up, the rota sorted, the lunch packed. At work, they're often the colleague who makes the office better simply by being in it.

They enjoy your competence and your habits, and helping you gives them a quiet satisfaction. Being part of your daily round is, for them, a form of closeness. The 6th is cadent, and tradition placed the joy of Mars here. Its natural sign, [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), is the sign of Venus's fall: affection that shows in details and service more than in grand gestures.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Love can turn into service. If their care mostly takes the form of doing things for you, it can be hard to tell affection from duty, and easy to take it for granted. Kindness wrapped around criticism is another trap here: a gentle suggestion about how you could load the car better is still a suggestion.

They can feel appreciated only for being useful, or notice that the chores they do quietly go unseen. If the work between you is uneven, the reciprocity Venus looks for wears thin.

The 6th was read for illness in the old books, under the name Bad Fortune. Their Venus here says nothing about health, yours or theirs ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); what it describes is the pleasure of looking after daily life together.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Say thank you for the small things, and share the dull ones. Keep some time together that has nothing to do with tasks. In a couple, a fair split of the household and an evening a week with no list. With a friend, a walking or cooking habit you both enjoy. With a colleague, warmth at work is a gift, best kept clear of favours that could be misread. Among adult relatives caring for someone together, this can be the one who makes the rota kinder.

Right before your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), their Venus also sits close to that angle, and the person who makes your days easier can start to look like a partner. Your Venus in their 6th house is the same overlay seen from the other side: the warmth you bring would go into their routines. Next to it, [their Venus in your 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house-synastry/) is play, and [their Venus in your 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house-synastry/) partnership.

Venus can stay in one sign far longer than its usual three to four weeks when it turns retrograde, about every eighteen months, for some six weeks; but even at full speed it is slow over a single day, and their birthday settles where it falls. Your birth time places the 6th: in Placidus, the house system I use, the line between your 6th and 7th is your Descendant, which a small change in the time can move past their Venus ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). And the people we work beside have the same right as anyone to know their chart is being read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
