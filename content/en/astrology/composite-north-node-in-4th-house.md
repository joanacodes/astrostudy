Known at every conference and work dinner, almost never seen at home: more couples fit that description than you might think. Composite North Node in the 4th house turns a relationship like that towards its own front door.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) reads a relationship through a chart made of midpoints, its own and nobody else's. There the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows where the bond is growing, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), always opposite, what it does by reflex. With the South Node in [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), the reflex is public: work, reputation, the role the two of you play in other people's eyes. At the very base of the chart is the North Node, in [the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), an angular house with Cancer as its natural sign: home, roots, family as the two of you define it, and the private life that nobody else needs to see.

The pull outward is a habit of the couple. Each of you may love a quiet evening, and the relationship may still live on calendars and invitations, as if it only existed when observed. The 4th-house direction gives it a hearth: rituals that are only yours, a household that runs on your own rules, a place where neither of you has to perform.

Compare [North Node in the 4th house](/astrology/north-node-in-4th-house/), where one person learns that home counts as much as achievement, and [their North Node in your 4th house](/astrology/north-node-in-4th-house-synastry/), where the other person develops through contact with your home and roots. In the composite, the home being built is shared, and so is the learning.

This house begins at the composite [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), opposite the composite Midheaven, and it's one of only two composite cusps that both house methods agree on: the Midheaven is the midpoint of your two natal Midheavens either way, and the IC lies exactly opposite it. The far edge of the house is less settled. When the cusps are rebuilt from that Midheaven for the place where you live, a move far north or south can shift the 5th cusp, and with it the end of this house, while the IC stays put. Every version needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A node near the IC can fall in [composite North Node in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-3rd-house/) by one reckoning; one near the 5th cusp, in [composite North Node in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-5th-house/). The mean and the true node can make that difference too, so I look at both.

Retreat is what to watch. A bond learning to value its home can shut the door on the world, or let old family patterns take over the household it meant to make its own. The 10th-house end keeps a window open: the competence and the sense of purpose it brings are part of what makes a home secure.

I've left composites out of my practice; those who keep them in read this node with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It forecasts no move, no purchase, no wedding and no children; it describes where the relationship grows. And like a home, the chart is entered only by invitation: both people agree before it is drawn ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
