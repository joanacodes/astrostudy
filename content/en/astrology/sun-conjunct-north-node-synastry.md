Sun conjunct North Node in synastry puts one person's Sun on the other's North Node. Who the first person is, plainly and without effort, turns out to be the direction the second is learning to grow in; across from that Sun, on the other end of the axis, lies the second person's South Node, what they already know how to do.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is a person's centre: identity, purpose, the vitality they bring to being themselves. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a point, not a planet, where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's heading north, and I read it as a less familiar capacity that grows through practice. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) merges two functions into a single process. Among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one puts one person's nature and the other's next step on the same degree.

If the North Node is yours, the other person shows you what you're reaching for without meaning to. With your North Node in Taurus, say, where the growth is towards calm and steady pleasures, a Taurus Sun beside it is that calm, lived in front of you. Being around them can be encouraging, and a little clumsy-making too: anything new is slow to practise at first, and you're practising next to someone for whom it comes naturally.

If the Sun is yours, you may find yourself admired for what is simply how you are. The other person may ask how you do something you've never had to think about. You get to be an example without having volunteered.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

What gets mixed up here is growth and imitation. The North Node person can start trying to become the Sun person instead of building their own version of the capacity; the Sun person can slip into the role of teacher, and then feel responsible for the other's progress. Development isn't copying, and nobody can practise for someone else.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) adds its own catch. With the other's familiar ground opposite your Sun, the moves they usually rely on, the strengths that came easily, don't always land with you, and they may feel that around you what they're good at doesn't count. It does: a growing capacity is added to the established ones, not swapped for them.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

If the North Node is yours, borrow the example, then practise your own way and at your own pace, and keep the strengths you came with in use. If the Sun is yours, offer the example rather than instructions; you don't owe anyone their growth. Between co-workers, this is the colleague whose ease makes a skill look learnable; between friends, relatives or partners, the one who makes the next step look possible by already standing on it.

Take 12 pairs of people at random and about one of them has this conjunction, either way round, within 8°. The Sun covers about a degree a day, so whoever brings the Sun has a birthday within about eight days of the date when the Sun passes the other's North Node, whatever the year. I look at both nodes, mean and true; the two never differ by more than a degree or two, so they disagree about this contact only near the edge of its orb ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). Both birth times are needed, as for any synastry I read: less to find the conjunction than to place it in the houses of each chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Node contacts are often called fated; I read this one as a direction made visible, never a destiny. Nor is it [Sun conjunct Jupiter in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-jupiter-synastry/), where one presence widens the other's horizons in general: the node points to a particular capacity, and asks for practice.

In one chart, the same meeting is [Sun conjunct North Node](/astrology/sun-conjunct-north-node/). Between two, the Sun can also sit 60°, 90° or 120° from the node, as [Sun sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-north-node-synastry/), [Sun square North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-north-node-synastry/) and [Sun trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-north-node-synastry/), or right across from it, on the familiar end, as [Sun opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-north-node-synastry/).
