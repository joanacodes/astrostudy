One of you did exactly what was asked; the other had pictured something else entirely. With Mars square Neptune in synastry, a right angle opens between one person's Mars and the other's Neptune, and the gap between what gets done and what was imagined becomes something the two of you keep running into.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) aims: it initiates, asserts and puts effort behind a goal. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) widens: imagination, compassion, ideals, a sense of what lies past the obvious. A [square](/astrology/square/) puts them at a working angle, and when it runs between two people, as it does among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), each one's way of wanting keeps catching on the other's.

What the friction makes possible is precision. The Neptune person, pressed to say what they actually mean, learns to put an ideal into words someone can act on. The Mars person, met by a picture larger than the task, learns to aim at what the effort is for, not only at the next step. Pairs with this square often end up better at briefs, promises and plans than pairs who never had to check.

The signs usually share a modality. Mars in Aries square Neptune in Capricorn, both cardinal, puts a quick starter beside an ideal of slow, solid structures; Mars in Leo square Neptune in Scorpio, both fixed, sets a wholehearted push beside a dream of depth and privacy.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

If yours is the Mars, your effort keeps landing slightly off: you did the thing, and it wasn't what was meant. It's easy to feel that the target moves, or that the other person won't say what they want, and to press harder for clarity than they can give in the moment.

If yours is the Neptune, your hopes keep being hurried into action, or turned into a plan before you'd finished imagining them. Their directness can feel blunt to your sensitivity, so you say yes to avoid a scene and feel let down later. Neither of you is misleading the other. A square is friction, never failure: two honest ways of wanting, crossing.

Seen from this corner, the pair's other contacts:

- **[Mars conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** zero degrees between them; the push lands on the dream, and blur replaces friction.
- **[Mars sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** a sign closer, the same functions help each other on request.
- **[Mars trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-neptune-synastry/):** a sign further on, in one element, the expectations match without being spelled out.
- **[Mars opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** the angle opens into a seesaw, and the crossing becomes a division of roles.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Before starting, check whether you're looking at a plan or a hope; "is this a wish or a request?" saves a great deal. Write things down where you can: a list for a shared house, a brief for a project, dates for a trip. The Mars person can ask what the effort is for; the Neptune person can name one concrete thing they'd like, and one they wouldn't. In a family, that might settle who does what at a gathering; between friends, it turns "we should, one day" into a plan with a date.

My count finds the square in about 1 pair of people in 9, one way round or the other, within 5.1°. Its Neptune half is generational: a sign holds Neptune for about fourteen years, so that person's side is shared with nearly everyone born around the same time, and it is where the other's Mars stood, a matter of weeks, that turns a common backdrop into a contact. The hour of birth hardly moves either body; it does settle the houses, the areas of life where the crossing shows, and I don't read without them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

When one chart holds both ends, [Mars square Neptune](/astrology/mars-square-neptune/) is a person whose own effort and ideals keep negotiating. Between two people the negotiation can happen out loud, which is this square's great advantage.
