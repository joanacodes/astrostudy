Hope and imagination start to outrun the facts a little whenever Jupiter squares your birth Neptune, as it does twice in every round. The work of those days or months is to find out which parts of the dream can stand up.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the part of you that imagines, idealises and feels with others. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) believes and enlarges. Ninety degrees apart, they rub: a [square](/astrology/square/) is friction that builds, but friction all the same, and here each pushes the other further than the facts go. A plan becomes mostly hope, a person is seen at their best and nowhere else, a cause asks for more than you can give, a belief starts to explain everything.

Neptune's blind spots show most clearly here: imagination taken for evidence, a resonant feeling taken for truth, a symbol taken for a prediction. That last one is worth remembering for astrology itself. I read charts as symbols that describe, never as forecasts, and this square is a good season to hold every grand story, including the astrological kind, to that standard.

Much of modern tropical astrology gives Neptune a reputation for confusion and escape. I don't read either into a [transit](/astrology/transits/), and if you're struggling, a doctor or a therapist will help more than a chart can ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)). Which area of life the square touches depends on your Neptune's house and on the house Jupiter is crossing, both set by your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Jupiter square Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-square-neptune/) at birth, this is a conversation your chart already knows, and each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) restarts it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured from 1½° short of exact to 1° past it, a square to your Neptune usually holds Jupiter for about eleven days. About three times in ten it isn't: a station close by brings three exact hits and some eight months of the square. Thirteen or fourteen squares fall before 80 in births from 1930 to 2009.

A birth in January 2005 put Jupiter in Libra, less than a sign from a square to Neptune in Aquarius: the first came at about one. From Virgo, in January 1945, it took about 3.9 years.

The square is the reality check in Jupiter's round with your Neptune. [Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-neptune/), [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-neptune/), [transiting Jupiter trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-neptune/) feed the dream or share it; this one asks it to stand on its own feet, which is why what survives a square tends to last.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask what would have to be true for the plan, the person or the belief to work as you hope, and check one of those things this week.

Find a second opinion from someone who doesn't share the dream. Not to talk you out of it, but to name the part you can't see from inside.

Sleep on large promises, your own and other people's. Most of what feels urgent under this square looks different, and clearer, a few days later.

If money is part of the dream, take advice from someone qualified to give it. Investments are outside what I read in any transit, and this one least of all ([Can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).
