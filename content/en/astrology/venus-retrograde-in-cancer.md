Cancer is one of the three signs where Venus didn’t turn back once between 2000 and 2040, in the dates I use. The two series nearest to it, Gemini’s in late spring and Leo’s in high summer, stop short on either side, and only one of them is moving its way.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) is cardinal water, ruled by the Moon: the sign that shelters, looks after and makes a place where people belong. Venus there values what feels safe and familiar, and shows affection by taking care. A [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) here would have the planet seem, from Earth, to stop and back through the sign for about six weeks, and the care would turn round to look at itself: whom you look after and who looks after you, the home or circle that feels like yours, the kindness you give out of love and the kindness you give out of duty.

My list never comes to that. Venus’s retrogrades from 2000 to 2040 keep to five places, and Cancer lies between two of them. The May and June series stays in Gemini, stationing at 26°08′ Gemini in 2004 and lower each time after, so it moves away from Cancer ([Venus retrograde in Gemini](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-gemini/)). The summer series turns direct in Leo, at 16°35′ in 2007 and then about 2° lower every eight years, down to 7°50′ Leo in 2039: that one drifts towards Cancer, though at the end of my list it is still nearly eight degrees away ([Venus retrograde in Leo](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-leo/)).

A natal Venus retrograde in Cancer therefore belongs to a birth before 2000 or after 2040; like every natal retrograde, I read it as affection on its own timetable, not affection held back ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Cancer sits on a different house in every chart, and only [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) says which; without the time there are no houses, which is why I don’t read a chart without it. These lines use Placidus, the house system I work with.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** Cancer rising: a protective, welcoming first manner; the way you look after people from the moment you meet them.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/cancer-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** things kept for their memories; belongings valued for whom they remind you of.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** caring words and family news; the message of support you meant to send.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** Cancer in its natural house: home and family; who belongs under your roof, and what makes it feel like yours.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/):** tender romance and gentle play; affection that grows when you feel safe, and what lets it grow.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/):** looking after others at work; the care you put into a team, valued at its true weight.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Cancer on the Descendant, with Capricorn rising: a partner who feels like family; who shelters whom, talked over gently.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/):** trust of the heart; what you share only with those you feel safe with.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/):** homesickness and far places; a sense of belonging found abroad, or in a set of ideas.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** care as a calling; work that looks after people, and what it gives you in return.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends who are family; the circle you’ve gathered, and how you look after one another.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/cancer-on-12th-house-cusp/):** tenderness kept private; old attachments and memories, tended in quiet.

## What helps {#what-helps}

The Moon rules Cancer, so the sign and house of your natal Moon show what you need to feel safe; read them beside Venus, which shows what you value. With no retrograde due here, Venus still passes through Cancer, usually for three or four weeks, and those are weeks worth using to notice whom you care for and how.

Cancer’s Venus loves by sheltering. Its review, when it comes, asks only that the shelter has room for you too.
