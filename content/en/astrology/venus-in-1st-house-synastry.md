Their Venus in your 1st house likes what it sees. The other person finds something pleasing in the way you look, move and arrive, and the appreciation is usually plain from the first meeting, whatever kind of bond follows.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) describes what a person values and is drawn to: what they find beautiful, what they prefer, how they give and return affection. [The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is your presence, your body and manner, the impression you make before a word is said. This [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) points their taste straight at you.

You may feel more at ease in your own skin around them. Their pleasure in your company shows in small ways, a compliment on your coat, a smile when you walk in, an easy warmth, and being liked for how you are, before you've done anything at all, can loosen something. Some people dress a little more boldly with this overlay; others simply stop worrying how they look.

In their eyes, your manner has a kind of rightness: it fits what they find lovely, in a friend as much as in a partner. They enjoy being near you, and your presence can make an ordinary room feel nicer to them. The 1st is angular, and its natural sign is [Aries](/astrology/aries/), where tradition puts Venus in detriment: liking that is immediate and direct rather than slowly courted.

Sitting right on your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), their Venus also makes a conjunction with your rising degree, and I give that contact its own weight: affection that meets you at the door.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Being admired for your presence can turn into a role. You may catch yourself keeping up the look or the mood they like best, or wonder whether they'd still warm to you on a tired, unglamorous day. Their taste can creep into your appearance too, the shirt they bought, the haircut they suggested, until you're not sure whose style it is.

They, for their part, can idealise how you come across and be thrown when you're short-tempered or flat. And appreciation wants to be returned: if they give you warmth and you take it as your due, the reciprocity Venus looks for goes missing.

Beauty here isn't vanity, and liking isn't a promise of romance. The same overlay turns up between friends, colleagues and relatives, and what grows from it is decided by both whole charts, not by one house.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let them see the ordinary days as well, and ask what they enjoy in you beyond the obvious. Give appreciation back in your own way. With a partner, enjoy the attraction and keep it about the whole person. With a friend, this can be the one who makes you feel good in your skin. At work, a colleague who warms to you on sight is best kept warm and professional; in a family, it may be the adult relative whose face lights up when you arrive.

Read in reverse, as your Venus in their 1st house, the charm runs the other way: their presence is what pleases you. One house before, [their Venus in your 12th house](/astrology/venus-in-12th-house-synastry/) is fondness for the self you keep private; one house after, [their Venus in your 2nd house](/astrology/venus-in-2nd-house-synastry/) is shared pleasure in what you own and value.

Venus needs three or four weeks to cross a sign, about a degree a day, which lets their birth date settle where it is. Your rising degree is the moving part: born a quarter of an hour later, you'd have an Ascendant several degrees further on, enough in Placidus, the house system I use, to move their Venus out of your 1st and into your 12th. I read the overlay only with both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)) and with the agreement of both of you ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
