A sextile asks for very little, a step in its direction. While Chiron stands 60° from your Neptune, the part of you that imagines, prays, makes music or looks after others is close at hand for an old sore spot, and taking the step is up to you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and feels its way past the usual frame: art, faith, compassion, the longing for something larger than the day. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts, finding a way to take part in life around a limit that hasn't gone away. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the two through opportunity, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) imagination can become a working tool for the sore spot rather than a place to hide from it.

From outside it often looks small. A song that says what you never managed to say about an old hurt. A drawing class taken up because your hands needed something to do while you thought. A volunteer shift beside people whose difficulty you know from the inside, where your experience is suddenly of use. Neptune's blind spot is mistaking compassion for unlimited availability, so the opening works best in a contained form: an hour a week, not a new identity.

Your Neptune's house says where imagination already lives in your chart, and the house Chiron is crossing, about two houses away, says where the opening comes from. With Neptune in your 12th, for instance, a sextile from your 2nd can give a private practice some practical use. Both houses need the time you were born, as the rest of the reading does ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The sextile passes the contact on, too. A planet of yours that sits 60° from your Neptune on the same side may have Chiron right on top of it, so a natal Venus sextile Neptune turns this into a crossing of your Venus as well. And if your chart holds [Neptune sextile Chiron](/astrology/neptune-sextile-chiron/), the transit repeats an opening you have always had.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The opening can stand for three weeks or for most of two years. Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, it usually lasts about ten months: in nearly two passes out of three, Chiron touches the exact sextile, retreats over it in its retrograde months and touches it once more. The single quick crossings, about a third of all passes, take three weeks or so; the rare long ones, one in twenty, take five exact hits and roughly twenty-one months.

Neptune moves so slowly that people born within a few years of each other have it at almost the same degree, so this sextile goes round by year group. The first comes between a few weeks old and 42: about 4 for people born in 1945, about 39 for 1955, about 30 for 1965, about 21 for 1975, about 12 for 1985, about 4 again for 1995 and about 16 for 2005. Where the wait is long, Chiron had just passed one of the two sextile points when you were born, and the other lay beyond the signs it crosses most slowly. Most lives to 80 hold three of them. In these years it is the turn of the Aquarius-Neptune generation, born between 1998 and 2012: Chiron in Aries, until April 2027, sits two signs from their Neptune.

Each round starts at [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-neptune/). The first sextile follows it, the second comes just before the next one, and in between lie [transiting Chiron square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-neptune/), [transiting Chiron trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-neptune/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one creative or contemplative practice and give it a regular slot for the length of the transit. Ten months is long enough to find out whether it helps.

Offer what you know to one person who is where you once were, and let listening be enough. The sextile doesn't need you to fix anything.

Keep the practice yours. If it starts turning into a duty to everyone, bring it back to the hour that does you good.
