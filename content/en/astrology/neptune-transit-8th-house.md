Neptune in your 8th house reaches the part of the chart where you let other people in: intimacy, trust, what you hold in common and what you owe. For close to fourteen years, the walls between you and others tend to thin.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves boundaries and opens the heart: it wants to merge, to understand, to reach beyond the separate self. The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is intimacy, shared resources, trust and the obligations closeness brings. With Neptune crossing it, intimacy can feel more like merging, and trust can be given whole. You may feel others' burdens as if they were yours, grow more compassionate about what people carry, or find shared arrangements, money held in common, debts, promises, harder to keep clear. The beauty and the risk are the same thing: fewer walls. Counted from the 7th, the 8th is also your partner's 2nd, their money, which is why joint accounts and shared costs belong here; under Neptune they ask for kindness and clear terms in equal parts.

The 8th's natural sign is Scorpio, which I read through both its rulers, Mars from the tradition and Pluto from modern astrology. With Saturn in your 8th from birth, an old guardedness about sharing may ease a little, enough to let someone help.

The 8th was the house of death and inheritance in the old books. I read neither in a transit ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and Neptune here forecasts no legacy, no debt and no loss. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this shows how your chart already shares and trusts, and here it asks for compassion and clear agreements at once.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Nearly fourteen years is the middle of Neptune's stays in the 8th for a London birth, in Placidus, exactly its figure for all houses there, with most stays lasting from nearly nine years to nearly twenty-two. A New York birth adds a little in the middle, a little under fifteen years, and trims the long end to about nineteen.

Neptune's retrograde months can take it back over the cusp into your 7th more than once before the 8th keeps it; all of that belongs to one stay, which ends on its last day in the house. Where the cusp falls, and so when the stay starts, is a matter of your birth time, the first thing I ask for and the one thing I can't read without ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The years before were spent in your 7th, idealising partnership ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)); the years after, in your 9th, turn the longing towards meaning and faith ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)).

Three windows matter in these years. Pisces cusps on the 8th were crossed from April 2011 to January 2026; Aries cusps meet Neptune between March 2025 and March 2039, its span in that sign; Taurus cusps come into range from May 2038.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep a simple record of what's shared: accounts, loans, promises, who pays for what. Neptune in the 8th softens the edges of joint arrangements, and a plain note keeps everyone's trust intact.

Notice when you're carrying someone else's feelings as your own. Compassion is a gift; taking on another person's whole weight isn't asked of you.

Let closeness be deep and still have two people in it. The most trusting bonds under this transit are the ones where each person keeps a little of themselves.
