Halfway between two crossings of your Midheaven, Saturn reaches the lowest point of your chart, the IC. Nothing about this transit is on display, and that's its nature: for a while, the work Saturn asks for happens under the floor, in the foundations everything else stands on.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds what can carry weight and checks what can't; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is your public direction, and an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) to it puts Saturn on the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the foundational point: continuity, belonging, the private supports that let you function in public. In this [transit](/astrology/transits/), those supports come up for repair. A home that has been running on goodwill needs a structure; rest that keeps being postponed needs a place in the week; the sense of where you belong, once taken for granted, wants to be chosen.

The IC isn't only a house, a family or a childhood, though it can be any of those: it's whatever regenerates you. Public life may feel quieter, not because anything has gone wrong but because the effort has moved out of view. Where Saturn's crossing of your Midheaven asks what you're known for, this one asks what keeps you going when nobody is looking, and the two answers are meant to fit together. Nor does a transit to the IC say anything about the health of the people you come from ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); the foundations I read here are yours.

The IC also begins the 4th house in Placidus, the system I use, so this is where [Saturn transiting the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-4th-house/) starts. Saturn reaches it at the low point of its arc through your angles, roughly midway between two crossings of your Midheaven ([transiting Saturn conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Saturn takes about nine months over your IC, counted with my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after. A little over half the time the crossing happens three times as Saturn doubles back, within about nine and a half months; at its fastest it's over in three weeks. Two or three of these crossings come by 80, for everyone.

The IC is exactly opposite the Midheaven, so it inherits the same precision: a birth time out by eight minutes moves this crossing by about two months. Without the time there is no IC to cross, and no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

From the IC, Saturn starts climbing again: [transiting Saturn trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-midheaven/) comes about five years on, [transiting Saturn square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-midheaven/) about seven, near your Descendant, and [transiting Saturn sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-midheaven/) about ten, before Saturn reaches your Midheaven.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Repair one thing at home that you've been working around: the door, the arrangement, the conversation. Saturn on the IC rewards foundations fixed properly.

Put rest on the calendar as firmly as work. The structure that sustains you deserves the same respect as the one that's seen.

Ask what belonging means to you now, rather than what it meant. Roots can be rebuilt, and this is the season for it.
