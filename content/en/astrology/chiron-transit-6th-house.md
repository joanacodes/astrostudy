Some hurts don't announce themselves; they turn up as habits. Staying late because leaving on time feels like failing, saying yes to one more task, apologising for a day off: in the 6th house, Chiron finds the old ache behind the way you work.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a lasting sore spot and the skill you build around it; as I read it, its work is less about making a hurt vanish than about learning to live and work well beside it. The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the shape of an ordinary day: the tasks you do, the routines that hold you up, the colleagues beside you and the care you give your body. Tradition called it Bad Fortune and gave [Mars](/astrology/mars/) its joy there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): effort, at home in the house of toil. With Chiron passing through, an old belief about your worth can surface through your working life: that you're only acceptable when useful, that rest has to be earned, that asking for help means you've failed. These years tend to show where that belief came from, and to ask for a working week built for the person you are, limits included. People who have lived this often become the colleague who notices when someone else is running on empty, as [Chiron in the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-in-6th-house/) does from birth.

If Mercury sits in your natal 6th, you may notice, as Chiron passes it, how often you say "I'll do it" before anyone has asked.

The 6th is also where tradition put illness, which makes this transit easy to misread. No chart can diagnose or predict a health problem ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and anything about your body belongs with a doctor. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) stirs what is already in your chart, and here that means how you work and how you care for yourself.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Of Chiron's twelve houses, the 6th keeps it most steadily. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude its stays centre on about 4 years, and most fall between about 3 and 7, against 2 to 9 across all twelve houses. The reason is geometry: at that latitude a 6th house is widest when it falls around Virgo and Libra, signs Chiron crosses in about two years each, and narrowest around Taurus and Gemini, where it takes five to eight, so the size of the house and Chiron's speed even each other out. At New York's latitude the middle is the same and the range a little wider, about 3 to 8.

Your 6th ends where your Descendant begins, and both its cusps come from your birth time, which is also why I never read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay sits between your house of play and creation ([Chiron transiting the 5th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-5th-house/)) and the house of partners ([Chiron transiting the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-7th-house/)).

Your 6th cusp always faces your 12th, so Scorpio on the cusp of your 12th puts Taurus on your 6th, whose stay falls between June 2026, when Chiron first crosses into Taurus, and May 2034. An Aries 6th cusp has it before April 2027, the end of Chiron's nine years in Aries; a Gemini one, between July 2033 and April 2039; a Cancer one, from July 2038, with Chiron still there when my count stops in December 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down your real working week, then the one you'd design for someone you love. The gap between the two is what this stay asks you to look at.

Build rest into the routine before you need it: a fixed finishing time, a lunch break taken away from the desk.

Let someone help with a task you always do alone. Being helped is a skill too, and the one Chiron in the 6th most often asks you to practise.

Find the routine that actually steadies you, a walk, a meal eaten away from a screen, and protect it as you would a work deadline.
