When the node squares your Midheaven, its axis crosses the vertical line of your chart at a right angle instead of lying along it, usually not far from your horizon. The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) and the Ascendant both come from your birth time, and this square involves both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## What changes {#what-changes}

The Midheaven organises your public direction: roles, responsibilities, what you contribute where others can see. Its opposite, the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), holds the foundations that public life stands on. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the capacity you are still building, and a [square](/astrology/square/) sets the two at odds in a way that asks for adjustment. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the newer skill fits neither the role you hold nor the routine at home without something giving a little.

Both nodes square both ends of the meridian at once. For those weeks the two axes cross at right angles, the shape a [grand cross](/astrology/grand-cross/) makes with four planets, drawn here by four points. Neither end of the nodal line touches your Midheaven or your IC: the pull runs sideways, between you and the people you meet. What tends to show is a role that asks you to behave as you always have, just as a less practised way of dealing with others is trying to come in.

The square usually falls near your horizon. In Placidus, the house system I use, one comes with the North Node in your 1st or 12th house and the other with it in your 7th or 6th. Your Ascendant is seldom exactly 90° from your Midheaven, though: at London's latitude the gap from a right angle is about 21° in the middle case and can reach about 36°, so this square and the node's crossing of your Ascendant or Descendant come about a year apart in the middle case, sometimes nearly two ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-ascendant/), [Transiting North Node opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-ascendant/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, the square takes about a month and seldom less than 25 days. One pass in ten runs beyond 100 days, which means an eclipse season arrived while the node was near the square, and three exact hits come about once in twenty-five passes.

Eclipses in that season fall near the nodes, so roughly a quarter of the circle from your Midheaven, and that can put one close to your Ascendant or Descendant. Within about three degrees of either, I count it there, as an eclipse on that angle rather than on the Midheaven ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Neither node lands on your Midheaven or your IC, which is why my forecast doesn't count this square. Eight or nine of these squares come by 80, two in each round: the first about four and a half years after the node crossed your Midheaven ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)), the second about four and a half before it crosses again. Each has the sextile and the trine about a year and a half either side ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-midheaven/), [Transiting North Node trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-midheaven/)), and halfway round the circle the North Node reaches your IC ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-midheaven/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the part of your role that leaves no room for the newer skill, and change one thing about it, even a small one.

Practise the new way with people outside work and home first: a class, a club, a stranger's question.

When work and home both claim the same evening this month, ask which choice gives the newer skill some practice.

Expect a series of small corrections rather than one decision. That is how a square gets worked.
