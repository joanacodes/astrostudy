With this pair, a sense of what's fair between two people keeps reaching for a sense of what's right in general. The Libra Sun weighs the case in front of it; the Sagittarius Moon needs to know what it all means, and to have some room ahead.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Sagittarius is two signs on from Libra, a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) by sign: air and fire, which suit each other, cardinal and mutable, which keep different paces. The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) sets things going for the sake of fairness and good relations; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) relaxes once there is space, honesty and something ahead. The two meet easily in conversation, in travel, in any argument about how people ought to live.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) governs your Sun's sign and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) your Moon's: taste and fairness on one side, meaning and growth on the other. Tradition puts the Sun in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Libra and gives the Moon neither dignity nor debility in Sagittarius; I read the fall as a self whose strength is consultation, and this Moon as needs that rest on perspective rather than routine. Jupiter's sign and house show where your Moon goes looking for meaning, and Venus's where your Sun finds something worth making fair.

Two and a half to seven and a half days after the new moon, the Moon had pulled 31° to 90° ahead of the Sun: a crescent in the west after sunset. These are [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) births, apart from the earliest, about one in eight, which still belong to the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase, and near 60° the lights form a [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). I take the crescent to be the stage at which needs start to go their own way, which suits a Moon that likes the road. For where the crescent begins and ends, see [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/).

About one birth in 143 has a Libra Sun and a Sagittarius Moon. Should the Moon have entered Sagittarius, or left it, on your birthday, only the time can separate this page from the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-scorpio/) and the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-capricorn/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Sun, Moon and rising sign together make a first sketch of a chart, never the finished drawing, and the rising sign needs the time as much as the houses do.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

The partner who suits you is a fellow traveller as much as a lover: fair, good company, and willing to go somewhere new with you, in thought or on foot. The Libra Sun gives a great deal to the relationship's balance; the Sagittarius Moon needs room, honesty and a shared horizon. Love made only of routine can feel quietly airless to you, and too much freedom lonely. Shared plans, going somewhere, learning something, building something long, keep both lights content.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that ties fairness to a larger purpose suits you: law, teaching, publishing, diplomacy, cultural or international work, anything that helps people agree on what's right. The Libra Sun brings tact; the Sagittarius Moon brings conviction and humour. Narrow, repetitive work leaves the Moon short of air, and constant conflict wears on the Sun. You're often the one who reminds a team what the work is for.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The Libra Sun wants to keep everyone on side; the Sagittarius Moon wants to say what it thinks. You may swing between tactful silence and a blunt remark you then rush to soften. You may also promise more than a season can hold, because every plan looks fair and every horizon looks near. Keep a short list of what you've promised, and say the frank thing kindly, once, rather than not at all or twice.
