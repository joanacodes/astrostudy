Your daily work and your income run on the same track when the 6th's ruler sits in the 2nd. The job pays the rent, the skills are the savings, and a well-run week feels like solid ground.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Count what you earn in hours and you have this placement in a sentence. The planet ruling your [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) cusp ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), the planet of tasks, habits and the care you give yourself, sits in the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/), where earnings, belongings and values are kept. The 6th's business pays its way: daily work is how money comes in, and competence becomes a kind of capital.

I read it as skills that pay. You may know exactly what an hour of your time is worth, take pride in a wage honestly earned, or build security one reliable habit at a time: the budget kept, the tools bought once and looked after for years, the side job that became the main one. Your sense of worth tends to rest on being useful. The risk is the hourly mindset spreading to everything, until rest feels like lost earnings and a day off needs a reason.

The two houses get along easily. Astrologers group the 2nd, 6th and 10th as the earth houses, the houses of material life, a trine apart, so work and resources speak the same practical language. The old books saw the link too: William Lilly gave the 6th small domestic animals "and the profit and loss got thereby", a reminder that the 6th has always had an eye on what labour brings in.

The planet decides what you're paid for. With Taurus or Libra on the 6th cusp, Venus does the work, and from the 2nd it earns through the pleasant and the beautiful: design, food, flowers, a well-kept shop. When Mercury holds the 6th (a Gemini or Virgo cusp), money comes from detail and talk; the Moon, ruling a Cancer 6th, ties income to looking after people.

Hard graft is how Mars earns from the 2nd when it rules a Scorpio 6th ([Scorpio on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/)): physical work, long shifts, money made by effort. Pluto rules that cusp as well ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and I read it beside Mars; from its own house, it can add a need to be fully in charge of your own resources.

Two cusps bracket this ruler, and the birth time places both. Nearer the 2nd cusp, a slightly later birth would have it in the 1st, where how you work shows in your manner ([Ruler of the 6th in the 1st house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/)). Nearer the 3rd cusp, a slightly earlier one would put it in the 3rd, where work runs on messages and short trips ([Ruler of the 6th in the 3rd house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)). Only the birth record can settle it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

This placement forecasts neither pay rises nor lean years, and investments are beyond any chart's judgement ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). The 6th's old link with illness doesn't become a forecast either: a chart knows nothing of your health ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and if worry about money or work is weighing on your mind, a doctor or a therapist will help more than any reading ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).
