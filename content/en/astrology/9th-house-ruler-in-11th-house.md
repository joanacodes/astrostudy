Your search for meaning is rarely solitary. Its ruler, the planet of your 9th house, sits in the 11th among friends, groups and hopes for the future, so the search gathers people, and people gather around it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Through the sign on its cusp, the [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), house of study, travel, philosophy and faith, has a ruler, and its matters follow that ruler into the house where it stands ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), which tradition called the Good Spirit, holds the friends you choose, the groups and networks you belong to, the people who help you without having to, and the future you'd like to build with them.

Your beliefs tend to come with company. You may have friends all over the world, or one circle that began in a classroom, a pilgrimage or a political campaign and has lasted for decades. Groups gathered around ideas suit you: the reading group, the language exchange, the congregation, the society for a subject nobody else in your family cares about. Your hopes tend to be large and shared, a cause more than a career. Where it can go wrong is in the crowd. A circle that agrees on everything stops teaching anyone anything, and loyalty to a group can quietly take the place of the search that brought you to it.

Horary astrology gave the 11th wishes and hopes as well as friends, and here the hopes take their shape from what you believe the world could be: a fairer arrangement, a language kept alive, a place protected. In practice you may teach groups rather than individuals, the workshop, the online course, the study circle that meets for years.

Tradition put Jupiter’s joy in the 11th ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), so with Sagittarius or Pisces on the 9th cusp, the planet of growth rules your search from the house where tradition said it was happiest. That tends to mean generosity between friends, mentors met through groups, and a network that keeps widening your world ([Jupiter in the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house/)). Venus, ruling a Taurus or Libra 9th from here, makes friends of people who share your taste in art and ideas; Mars, ruling an Aries 9th, gathers allies for a cause.

Pisces on the cusp ([Pisces on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/)) adds Neptune to Jupiter, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). In the 11th, Neptune draws people together around a shared ideal, a spiritual community or an artistic one, and asks you to keep your judgement while you belong. When Jupiter is the one here, the friendship comes first; when it's Neptune, the dream does.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 11th takes up the middle third of the sky's climb from the eastern horizon to the Midheaven, and the cusps on either side depend on the minute and place of birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With a later time, the ruler would have climbed into the 10th, and what you believe would go public ([Ruler of the 9th in the 10th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)). With an earlier one, it would still be in the 12th, where belief is lived in solitude ([Ruler of the 9th in the 12th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)).
