There's a kind of attention that goes straight past the polite version of you to what you kept back. Pluto conjunct Lilith in synastry is one shape of it: the Pluto of one chart rests on the Lilith of another, and depth comes looking precisely for what was kept back.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) goes to the bottom of things: it reorganises what has become unsustainable, regenerates, and wants access to what lies under the surface, motives, attachments, power. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), a calculated point where the Moon's orbit reaches farthest from us on average, is read in many traditions, each with its own emphasis, as instinctive autonomy, a person's sense of their own power, and what was pushed out of view; I keep it symbolic. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) puts the two to work together, neither losing itself, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the work is split between two people.

When the Lilith is yours, this person sees the part you keep back, the anger, the appetite, the flat refusal, and isn't frightened by it. They may even think it's your strength. Being recognised there can be a great relief; it can also feel like being dug into, with no say over what gets brought up.

When the Pluto is yours, the other's untamed side holds your attention. You sense its power, you respect it, and you may want to help it into the open. Your gift is that nothing in them shocks you; the care it asks is to let them choose what comes out, and when.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Two kinds of power meet in one place, and it can blur whose is whose. The Pluto person may start to treat the other's refusal as something to work on, or to hold, mistaking control for safety. The Lilith person may show their hidden side only to this person and nobody else, until the secret becomes the bond. Neither is sinister: Pluto is not domination and Lilith is not wickedness. Intensity, though, isn't the same as truth, and a shared secret isn't the same as depth.

The other angles of this pair:

- **[Pluto sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/pluto-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** a quieter invitation to be less guarded, accepted or not.
- **[Pluto square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/pluto-square-lilith-synastry/):** fascination with an edge, where each can feel pushed.
- **[Pluto trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/pluto-trine-lilith-synastry/):** room made for the untamed side, without the push.
- **[Pluto opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/pluto-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** power and refusal facing each other across the chart.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make uncovering a choice, never a project. If the Lilith is yours, decide what you're willing to show, and say when something is off limits. Carrying the Pluto, ask before going deeper, and leave what you find alone unless you're invited to touch it. Close friends can keep this conjunction honest by agreeing what stays private; a couple, by keeping a room each; a mentor and a protégée, by keeping the focus on the work.

The dates reach a long way, as a birth in mid-1945 shows. There, Pluto in Leo meets the Lilith of people born from July to September 1943, and, mean Lilith being back roughly every nine years, that of people born from March to May 1961, from October to December 1987, and so on. The 1945 Lilith, in Libra, lies under the Pluto of everyone born between October 1980 and October 1983, often a child's generation. All of this follows the calendar rather than two characters, so it never enters my synastry count, which keeps to the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant and the Midheaven; the houses tell me where in each life the hidden and the deep meet.

The hour can't create or cancel the conjunction, both points being slow; both birth times are needed for the houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The windows assume mean Lilith ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

[Pluto conjunct Lilith](/astrology/pluto-conjunct-lilith/), in one chart, is a person who changes by taking something back. Between two, the taking back happens in someone else's presence, and that needs the consent of both before I'll read it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
