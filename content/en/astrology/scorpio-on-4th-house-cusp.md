Scorpio on the 4th house cusp puts the sign of regeneration at your IC, the root of the chart. Home, for you, is less a fixed place than the ground you find again after something has changed.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In Placidus, the house system I use, the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) is where the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) begins, at the very bottom of the chart, where I look for what holds you up and what you come home to ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) there, that foundation is the kind that has been rebuilt. Home is often where you go to recover, and you tend to come out of it changed. The things you keep there are few and full of meaning, and you may need one room, or one corner, that is yours alone. Your sense of belonging may have been made, unmade and made again, and it holds best among people you've been through something with. What you carry from the family you grew up in is something you'd rather understand fully than keep polite. That is your experience of home; the people in it have their own story.

Opposite, Taurus is always on your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), so Venus rules your public life while Pluto and Mars rule your roots ([Taurus on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/)): a steady, even easy face in the world, with the intensity kept for the life behind it. I read both of Scorpio's rulers, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), and their houses show what your private life feeds and draws on: the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In about 13 London charts in 14, Aries opens your 9th, so Mars also carries your roots out into the wider world: study, travel, the convictions you'd defend. In about 3 in 10, the 4th holds the whole of Sagittarius after Scorpio ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and Jupiter joins them at the bottom of the chart.

About 1 birth in 12 has Scorpio on the IC, at London as at New York, since the signs on the meridian depend on the hour and not on latitude. Scorpio takes about two hours to cross it, close to the average for a sign. Latitude still shapes the rest of the chart. At London, Leo rises in about 2 charts in 3 with this cusp, making the Sun your chart ruler, Virgo in the others, and the 4th is wide, about 38° to 40°; at New York, Leo rises in about 4 in 5 and the house measures about 35° to 36°. Scorpio never opens the 3rd or the 5th as well here, and it opens no house at all in about 1 London birth in 7 and 1 New York birth in 11.

Your IC changes sign about every two hours. Before this window it was in Libra, with Scorpio not yet there and often still on the 5th cusp ([Scorpio on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/)); after it, the IC moves into Sagittarius and Scorpio climbs towards the 3rd ([Scorpio on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-3rd-house-cusp/)). Telling those charts apart takes the minute on the record, not the nearest hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
