Half a Saturn round after crossing your Neptune, about fifteen years on, Saturn looks at that degree from the opposite side of your chart. What you know and what you hope end up facing each other, and the people around you often take one side each.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) measures, limits and keeps account of time; [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines, forgives and longs for what can't be measured. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets them across from each other: each keeps its nature, and each makes the other visible. In this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the realist and the dreamer tend to turn up as separate people. A colleague asks for evidence while you're describing a vision; a friend's idealism makes your caution look thin; someone who needs more help than you can give asks, without saying so, where your limit is.

Polarity isn't contradiction, and I don't read a winner into it. The season asks for practical idealism: a dream you can describe in hours and steps, a structure whose purpose you can name. Whatever was set in motion when Saturn crossed your Neptune, around fifteen years before ([transiting Saturn conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-neptune/)), is now seen from the far side, and some of it needs re-weighing.

Neptune's house shows what you idealise; the opposite house, where Saturn is, shows where the call for realism comes from. With Neptune in your 1st, Saturn crosses your 7th, and partners may be the ones asking for a clearer outline. Your birth time settles which two houses those are ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From the day Saturn comes within 1½° of opposing your Neptune to the day it is 1° past, my orbs, about nine months usually go by. In a little over half the cases Saturn turns retrograde on the far side and comes back, so the opposition is exact three times; in the rest it's exact once, and sometimes lasts only three weeks.

Once per round, about every 29½ years, means two or three oppositions by 80, and everyone has at least two. Neptune moves so slowly that each year group keeps its own timetable: born in 1955, at about 14, 43 and 72; born in 1975, at about 26 and 56; born in 1935 or 2005, within the first two years, close to the [Saturn opposite Neptune](/astrology/saturn-opposite-neptune/) those births carry. The opposition is under way for most people born between 1942 and 1957, whose Neptune is in Libra, while Saturn crosses Aries until April 2028. The generation with Neptune in Scorpio, born between 1956 and 1970, follows while Saturn crosses Taurus, from 2028 to 2030.

Each of the other Saturn–Neptune transits falls twice between two crossings, once on the way out to this opposition and once on the way back: [transiting Saturn sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-neptune/), [transiting Saturn square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-neptune/), [transiting Saturn trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-neptune/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let the realist speak, then decide. A careful question about a dream is information, not an attack on it.

Give one ideal a structure you can name: the hours, the place, the limit. Practical idealism is mostly a matter of edges.

If someone is asking more of your compassion than you can give, say where your limit is. Kindness lasts longer when it has a shape.
