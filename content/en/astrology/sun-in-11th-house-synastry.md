Their Sun in your 11th house tends to make a friend of someone, whatever else they are to you. Their sense of purpose arrives among the people you choose and the future you hope for, and the plans you'd stopped mentioning start to sound possible again.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) opens a chart onto other people in the plural: friends you chose rather than inherited, teams, networks, communities, the people who help without having to, and the hopes you carry with them. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises a person around what they mean to become. Seen as a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), what their Sun lights here is who your people are and what you're all working towards.

With them, the future can feel roomier. They take your long-term plans seriously, introduce you to people who share them, or join a cause you care about and bring it energy. There's often an easy companionship to this overlay, the kind that survives months apart and picks up mid-sentence.

For them, your circle can be where their purpose finds company. They may feel they belong among your friends sooner than they expected, and your hopes leave room for theirs. The 11th is succedent, the house where what you achieve in public is shared and kept alive with others, and [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) is the sign that goes with it. Tradition called it the Good Spirit and placed Jupiter's joy there.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Friendship is a generous frame, and sometimes a limiting one. If one of you wants more closeness than the 11th easily gives, the bond can stay pleasantly sociable while something goes unsaid; or you may find you mostly see them with others around, sharing them with the group when you'd like them to yourself. Their goals can also quietly become your group's goals, until your own hopes are the ones nobody mentions.

From their side, being part of your world can mean being one of many. They may feel welcomed by your friends and still unsure where they stand with you, or carry the energy of the group while missing time that's only the two of you.

An overlay doesn't decide what a bond becomes. The 11th brings friendship into it; what else it holds is up to the people in it.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make time for the pair as well as the circle. In a couple, protect evenings that are just yours, alongside the friends you share; between friends, say what the friendship means, which people rarely do; between colleagues, this overlay suits a team or a network, as long as credit and direction are shared; between adult siblings or cousins, it can turn family into friendship.

Turned round, the Sun is yours and the circle theirs: in their 11th house, you're the friend whose purpose finds a place among their people. Next door, [their Sun in your 10th house](/astrology/sun-in-10th-house-synastry/) is about public life, and [their Sun in your 12th house](/astrology/sun-in-12th-house-synastry/) about what happens out of sight.

In Placidus, the house system I use, your 11th is one of the houses between your Midheaven and your Ascendant, and its width depends on when and where you were born. Their birthday places their Sun to within a degree, and only if that degree sits on one of the 11th's two cusps does their hour of birth count as well; their own houses, read the other way round, depend on it, one more reason I start only with both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Friends deserve the same courtesy as partners: a synastry is something both of you know about ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
