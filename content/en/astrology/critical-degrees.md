Some astrology books treat certain degrees of the zodiac as hotter than the rest, and give them a name: critical degrees. The best-known list looks arbitrary at first sight. Laid over the Moon's old calendar, it falls into place.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

The list usually given has seven degrees, spread across the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/): 0°, 13° and 26° of the cardinal signs (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn); 9° and 21° of the fixed signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius); and 4° and 17° of the mutable signs (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces). A planet, an angle or a house cusp on one of them is said, by astrologers who use the list, to act more strongly or more urgently.

Those numbers come from the [28 lunar mansions](/astrology/28-lunar-mansions/), a division of the Moon's path into 28 stations, roughly one for each day of its month. Divide the zodiac into 28 equal parts from 0° Aries and each mansion is about 12°51′ long. The first three begin at 0°, 12°51′ and 25°43′ Aries, the next two at 8°34′ and 21°26′ Taurus, then two more at 4°17′ and 17°09′ Gemini. Rounded to the nearest degree, that is the list. And seven mansions make exactly 90°, so the eighth begins at 0° Cancer and the pattern starts again, which is why it repeats every three signs, cardinal, fixed and mutable.

The mansions themselves are old. Indian, Chinese and Arabic astronomy each had their own, and they appear in Hellenistic astrology from the 2nd century on, then in Arabic and medieval European books. Western astrology today generally doesn't use them, and the critical degrees are one of the few traces they have left in it.

Two neighbouring ideas are worth keeping apart. 0° of a cardinal sign, already on the list, is also the [Aries Point](/astrology/aries-point/) and its three partners, the equinoxes and solstices. The last degree of any sign, 29°, is watched by many astrologers for reasons of its own ([the anaretic degree (29°)](/astrology/anaretic-degree-29/)).

## How it works {#how-it-works}

The list is rounded, and that leaves room for disagreement. Does 13° mean the mansion's exact start at 12°51′, the thirteenth degree of the sign, or anything from 13°00′ to 13°59′? Books differ, and since degrees are counted from 0, the same figure can mean different stretches of the sign ([degrees, minutes and seconds](/astrology/degrees-minutes-and-seconds/)). Ask what a source means before deciding whether a planet qualifies.

A slow planet can hold one degree for weeks or months, so a critical degree held by Saturn or Pluto belongs to a whole generation of births. The fast points are another matter. An [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) passes through a degree in roughly four minutes of clock time, the Midheaven at a similar pace, the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) in about two hours, so whether an angle, a [house cusp](/astrology/house-cusps/) or the Moon sits on a critical degree depends on an exact birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I don't read critical degrees as a category. A planet on 13° Cancer is the planet in Cancer, in its house, with its aspects; the degree adds a question, not an answer. I never tested degrees in my notebook, only signs and houses: nothing of my own tells me whether these mean more, and I'd rather admit it than guess ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

The word itself needs a gentle correction. Critical, in these books, means sensitive or decisive, the way a critical moment is one where things turn. It doesn't mean dangerous, and nothing about a degree can warn anyone that a crisis is coming. If someone has told you a planet on a critical degree is a bad sign, the degree is innocent of it.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Critical means a crisis is coming.”** It means sensitive. No degree predicts an event, good or bad.
- **“Every book gives the same critical degrees.”** The mansion list is the commonest, but books round it differently and add degrees of their own.
- **“The critical degrees come from the stars.”** They come from dividing the zodiac into 28 equal mansions from 0° Aries; the stars that named the mansions have since drifted away from those degrees.
- **“A planet on a critical degree outweighs the rest of the chart.”** A degree is one detail of one placement. The sign, the house and the aspects still carry the reading.
