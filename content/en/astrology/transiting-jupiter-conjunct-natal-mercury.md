About once in twelve years, Jupiter reaches the degree where Mercury stood when you were born. The planet of meaning sits on the planet of thought, and for a while your mind is more interested in the big picture than in the small print.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) processes information: it notices, names, connects and passes things on. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) synthesises: it looks for the principle behind the facts and the meaning behind the principle. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two move as one, so while this [transit](/astrology/transits/) lasts, learning something and making sense of it tend to happen together.

It often shows as an appetite for a subject that suddenly opens out, a course you finally sign up for, a book you want to write or to read slowly, a conversation that changes how you see your work. Explaining comes more easily, and so does teaching. The risk is on the generous side: big statements on small evidence, more projects than you can keep track of, the feeling of being right arriving before the facts. Reasoning isn't certainty, and a crossing of Mercury is a good time to remember it.

This conjunction always comes during Jupiter's year in your Mercury's house. With Mercury in the 3rd, the house that belongs to Gemini, Mercury's own sign, in the natural order, it comes during [Jupiter transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-3rd-house/), and words, lessons, neighbours and short journeys are the setting. If [Mercury conjunct Jupiter](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-jupiter/) is in your birth chart, this crossing and your [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) arrive together.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Jupiter usually spends about eleven days crossing your Mercury within my orbs, which run from 1½° before exact to 1° after. About seven crossings in ten take only that long; in the other three, Jupiter retrogrades over your Mercury and passes it three times in about eight months.

Mercury is never further than about 28° from the Sun, while Jupiter covers about 30° a year, so this crossing comes within about a year of [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-sun/): after it if your Mercury runs ahead of your Sun in the zodiac, before it if Mercury trails behind. Mercury moves a couple of degrees a day at most, so your birth date fixes it closely and the dates barely depend on the hour. The house does, and the house tells me what the crossing is about; without your birth time I don't read it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Six or seven such crossings come by 80 for everyone born between 1930 and 2009, which makes them one of the regular seasons of a thinking life. From here, Jupiter's round of your Mercury passes four more stations, each with its own kind of thinking: the offer of [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-mercury/), the overload of [transiting Jupiter square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-mercury/), the fluency of [transiting Jupiter trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-mercury/) and the debate of [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-mercury/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick the subject you've been circling and give it a structure: a course, a reading list, an hour a week that belongs to it.

Write things down. Jupiter brings ideas faster than Mercury can file them, and the notes of these weeks are often worth more later than they seem at the time.

Check one fact for every big claim. The conjunction makes conclusions feel solid; a quick check keeps them honest, and keeps the generosity of this season from turning into overstatement.
