Every eight years, around the start of October, Venus stops in Scorpio and starts back, in a sign tradition counts as its detriment. It’s the retrograde that asks what lies under an attachment: why you want what you want, and what you would keep if no one were looking.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) is fixed water with two rulers, Pluto in modern tropical astrology and Mars in the tradition, and I read both. Opposite Venus’s own Taurus, it is one of the two signs where Venus is said to be in detriment: valuing there is intense, loyal, wary of the surface. When [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) begins in Scorpio, the planet seems from Earth to stop and burrow back through the sign, and the review goes deep: what you share and with whom, which attachments are chosen and which are held for fear of losing them, what a desire is really asking for.

Every autumn retrograde in my list, from 2000 to 2040, starts in Scorpio, about 2⅓° lower every eight years: 15°36′ on 10 October 2002, 13°13′ in 2010, 10°50′ in 2018, 8°29′ on 3 October 2026, 6°07′ on 30 September 2034. By its stations the 2002 one stayed wholly in Scorpio, turning direct at 0°03′. The four after it cross back into Libra and turn direct there ([Venus retrograde in Libra](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-libra/)): the most recent, from 3 October to 14 November 2026, at 22°51′ Libra; the next, from 30 September to 11 November 2034, at 20°27′ Libra. None reaches forward into Sagittarius, which has no Venus retrograde in my list ([Venus retrograde in Sagittarius](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-sagittarius/)).

As a birth placement, Venus retrograde in Scorpio is deep feeling that takes its time to trust, and I read it on its own ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house holds Scorpio in your chart comes from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); I can’t place a single cusp without it, and so I don’t read the chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2026 retrograde, like those of 2010, 2018 and 2034, began in the Scorpio house and ended in the one where Libra sits.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/scorpio-rising/):** Scorpio rising: an intense, private presence; how much of your feeling you let people see, reconsidered.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you hold onto, and why; things kept for what they mean, weighed for whether they still mean it.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** words that go deep; a conversation about feelings, had more honestly.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/):** loyalty at home; family bonds and old attachments, looked at in their depth.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/):** passionate romance and absorbing creative work; desire, and what it’s really after.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work you commit to completely; a working bond that asks more than it gives, weighed again.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio on the Descendant, with Taurus rising: an intense partnership; trust and closeness, explored rather than tested.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio on its natural house: intimacy and what two people hold in common, looked at closely. Tradition tied this house to death, and I never read a death from a chart ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
9. **[9th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs about love and fidelity; a conviction tested against experience.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a public life with private depths; ambitions you hold fiercely, and what they serve.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a few deep friendships; whom in the group you would trust with anything.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-12th-house-cusp/):** feelings kept under the surface; an old longing, let go of slowly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look at both rulers: the house of your natal Mars shows where desire acts, the house of your natal Pluto where it changes you, and together they show where these weeks tend to dig. Ask what lies under a strong feeling before deciding what to do with it.

Venus retrograde in Scorpio settles nothing about a relationship’s future. It lets you see the relationship, and yourself in it, a little more truly.
