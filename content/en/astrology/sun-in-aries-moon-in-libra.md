Born close to the full moon of the Aries month, you have the two lights facing each other across the sky: the Sun in Aries, the Moon in Libra. It's the axis astrologers read as self and other, and your purpose and your needs sit at its two ends.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Opposite signs are closer relations than they look. Aries and Libra are both cardinal, both quick to set things in motion, and their elements, fire and air, feed each other. What differs is the direction. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) starts things for its own sake; the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) starts things for the sake of the bond: the call that clears the air, the dinner that settles a disagreement. You're an initiator at both ends of the axis.

Tradition makes the pair a study in mirror images. Libra is where the Sun has its fall, the mirror of its exaltation in Aries: your Moon sits on the ground tradition calls the Sun's hardest. The rulers repeat the pattern: [Mars](/astrology/mars/), which rules Aries, is in detriment in Libra, and [Venus](/astrology/venus/), which rules Libra, is in detriment in Aries. Each light's sign is the other's hardest ground, which is why I read this pair as a polarity to hold, never a contest to win.

The Moon was 151° to 208° ahead of the Sun, twelve and a half to seventeen days into the lunar month. These births split evenly between the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/), the days before the full, and the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase, the days after it; close to 180°, the chart has a [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/). A full moon that comes while the Sun is in Aries is always a [full moon in Libra](/astrology/full-moon-in-libra/), so you may well have been born under one, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) tells you on which side of it.

About 1 person in 146 has this pair. Some sixty hours after entering a sign, the Moon leaves it, so a birthday on which it crossed into Libra, or out of Libra into Scorpio, needs the time before I can say whether this is your page or the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-virgo/) or the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-scorpio/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The time also gives the rising sign, the third part of the chart's first sketch. Near a full moon the two lights usually land in opposite houses, in Placidus, the house system I use, and which two depends entirely on that time.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Partnership is where this pair shows most. The Aries Sun knows what it wants and says so; the Libra Moon feels steady only when things are fair and calm between you. In love that can look like someone who leads and then checks that everyone is all right, a lovely combination when it works. When it doesn't, you swing: frank to the point of a row, then conciliatory to the point of giving way. A partner who can hear the Aries request and the Libra need in the same conversation helps you stop swinging.

## At work {#at-work}

You tend to do well where initiative and diplomacy are both needed: negotiating, representing others, building a team, chairing the meeting that would otherwise turn into a fight. The Sun wants to push the project; the Moon wants the room on side. That makes you persuasive, as long as winning agreement doesn't take so long that the Aries Sun loses interest. Decide before the meeting what you'll compromise on, and what you won't.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The pull between your own aims and the peace between you never quite settles. Some days the Aries Sun resents every compromise; on others the Libra Moon gives away more than it meant to, and the Sun is angry about it later. Neither side is the true you. What helps is naming which one is talking before you answer someone, and keeping one corner of life, a project, a sport, an early walk, entirely your own, so that sharing everything else costs less.
