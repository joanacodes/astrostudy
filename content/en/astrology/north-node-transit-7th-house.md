While the North Node backs through your 7th house, the South Node backs through your 1st, and the axis of *you and me* sits under the light for around sixteen months. The direction points across the table.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the less familiar way, the one that grows with use. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is partnership: love, marriage, close collaborators, even open rivals, the people you meet as equals. Here the growth is cooperation: asking before deciding, hearing a view that isn't yours, letting a plan become joint.

Across the chart the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) moves through your 1st, the house of self-reliance. Its familiar ground is managing alone and deciding alone. That isn't a fault, and it stays available. The stretch is to find out what a decision feels like with two people in it.

Tradition gave Libra, the 7th's natural sign, to Venus, and placed Saturn's exaltation there: fairness built to last. A natal Venus in your 7th, met by the node for a month or so, can make closeness easier to ask for.

No [transit](/astrology/transits/) starts or ends a relationship, and none settles a dispute: I don't read the outcome of a marriage, a separation or a case ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)). What this one brings out is what your chart already holds about partnership.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 7th gives the node its shortest middle and one of its longest tails. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the true node usually stays about 16 months, against a year and a half for the average house, yet most stays run from about 11 months to about 2 years and 10 months, because a 7th can be very narrow or very wide that far north. At New York's latitude the middle is about 17 months and the long end about 2 years and 5 months.

A dip of the true node back over the cusp within 120 days counts as the same stay; the mean node runs a degree or two from it at most, and I look at both ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

In Placidus, the house system I use, the stay ends at your Descendant, as the North Node crosses it and the South Node crosses your Ascendant ([transiting North Node opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-ascendant/)). Both points need your birth time, which is why I read nothing without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node enters the 7th from your 8th ([North Node transiting the 8th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-8th-house/)) and leaves for your 6th ([North Node transiting the 6th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-6th-house/)).

That last crossing happens while the South Node is in your rising sign. It is in Leo from July 2026 to March 2028, in Cancer from then to September 2029, in Gemini to March 2031, in Taurus to December 2032, and in Aries to June 2034, while the North Node crosses Libra. Pisces follows to November 2035, Aquarius to May 2037 and Capricorn to February 2039. With Virgo rising, the stay ended between January 2025 and July 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask one person's opinion before a decision you'd normally make alone, and let it change something.

Practise the joint plan in small things: a weekend organised together, a project with two names on it.

Keep your own centre. The South Node in your 1st knows how to stand alone, and cooperation goes better when both people bring a self to it.
