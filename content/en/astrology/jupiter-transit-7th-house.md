Halfway round the chart from your Ascendant, Jupiter's year in your 7th house turns its growth towards other people: the partner, the collaborator, the person across the table. What widens now is what you learn through someone else.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) broadens understanding and helps experience make sense; the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is partnership, cooperation, agreements and the people you meet as equals, in love and in work. Jupiter's passage gives that part of life more room. People who widen your world can come closer, existing bonds can take on more generosity, and you may find you understand yourself better by watching what you look for in others.

On the day Jupiter reaches your Descendant, it also stands opposite your rising degree ([transiting Jupiter opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-ascendant/)): the cusp of the 7th, in Placidus, the system I use for houses, is always the point facing the Ascendant. The 1st house was *me*; this one is *you*, and Jupiter's year is about the space between.

Then come any planets your chart holds in the 7th. Born with Venus there, the house's natural ruler, you may feel that pass as warmth given and received; with Saturn there, as a long commitment given some air.

What the [transit](/astrology/transits/) won't do is decide anything for two people. It doesn't say whether you'll meet someone, marry or part, and tradition's contracts and open rivals carry no verdict either: I never read a transit for the outcome of a dispute or a case. It brings out what your chart already holds about partnership, for a year.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Your 7th mirrors your 1st across the chart, and at London's latitude both can be very wide or very narrow, so Jupiter's stay there varies more than in most houses: about a year in the middle, but anything from about 10 months to just over 2 years in Placidus charts drawn there. At New York's latitude, where the houses are more even, the spread narrows to about 11 months to nearly 2 years.

The Descendant's degree comes straight from your birth time, which is why the time comes first, before any reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Jupiter reaches it after a year in your 6th, the house of daily work ([Jupiter transiting the 6th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-6th-house/)), and leaves the 7th for the shared depths of your 8th ([Jupiter transiting the 8th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-8th-house/)).

Your Descendant is always the sign opposite your rising sign. With Aquarius rising it's in Leo, where Jupiter is from June 2026 to July 2027. Pisces rising puts it in Virgo (July 2027 to August 2028), Aries rising in Libra (August 2028 to September 2029), Taurus rising in Scorpio (September 2029 to October 2030), Gemini rising in Sagittarius (October 2030 to November 2031) and Cancer rising in Capricorn (November 2031 to November 2032). Leo rising waits for Aquarius (April 2032 to December 2033), Virgo rising for Pisces (April 2033 to April 2034), Libra rising for Aries (April 2034 to April 2035), Scorpio rising for Taurus (April 2035 to May 2036), Sagittarius rising for Gemini (May 2036 to May 2037) and Capricorn rising for Cancer (May 2037 to June 2038).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask what you'd like to learn from someone else this year, and let them teach it. The 7th grows through the people who stand opposite you, including the ones who disagree.

Read agreements as carefully as you sign them. Jupiter's generosity can make terms look kinder than they are, and fairness is the part of partnership this year asks you to look at.

Give as much room as you hope to be given. And keep a life of your own inside the bond: one person can widen your world, but no partnership should have to carry all of its meaning.
