When Pluto reaches your Mars, your drive takes on the weight of the planet of depth. For two to three years effort and transformation work as one, and whatever you decide to do, you tend to do with more force and for longer than you are used to.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) empowers: it builds authentic strength by clearing what has become obsolete. Your [Mars](/astrology/mars/) initiates, asserts and carries effort through. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your energy concentrates. Half-measures lose their appeal. A project, a cause, a training plan or a long-postponed decision can take hold of you, and you find a staying power you didn't know you had.

With so much force behind your effort, the temptation is to clear everything out at once: the job, the routine, the people who seem to slow you down. Cutting away isn't the same as renewing. What this conjunction rewards is effort aimed at rebuilding one thing properly. Courage isn't recklessness, either; Pluto's stamina can outrun your judgement, and the most useful strength in these years is knowing when to stop.

Mars is your drive, not your anger, and this transit forecasts no accident, injury, illness or violent event ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). Agency isn't domination: the force is yours to use on your own work, not on other people.

The other Pluto contacts work on your effort from a distance: the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-mars/)) and the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-mars/)) lend it staying power, the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-mars/)) gives it resistance to push against, and the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-mars/)) sets an equal force across from it. The conjunction gives it Pluto's own weight. With [Mars conjunct Pluto](/astrology/mars-conjunct-pluto/) in your birth chart, the transit came while you were small or not at all, depending on which of the two stood ahead; Mars covers about half a degree a day, so a birth a few days earlier or later could tip the balance.

With Mars in the 6th, the conjunction comes during Pluto's long stay in the house of work and daily routine ([Pluto transiting the 6th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-6th-house/)), and the drive goes into how you work and how you organise your days. Whether your Mars is in the 6th is a question for the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counting from 1½° before exact to 1° after, as I do for timing, Pluto stays on your Mars for about two years and eight months, nearly four years in the longest passes. Three passes in five cross the exact degree three times; the others five times, as Pluto backs over it twice.

More than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet this conjunction by 80, and nobody meets it twice: Pluto would have to go all the way round.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it reaches Mars in Aquarius, a sign Mars passes through for about six or seven weeks every two years or so.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one aim and give it the force. Spread over ten, this much energy wears you out; aimed at one, it builds something that lasts.

Schedule rest the way you schedule work. Under this conjunction tiredness is easy to ignore, and it is better met early.

Find a physical outlet that rewards endurance: long walks, swimming, building or digging something. Pluto's force is easier to carry when the body has a share of it.
