At the minute you were born, [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) was coming up over the eastern horizon, and you tend to meet the world the way the archer faces the distance: looking past what's in front of you to where it might lead. Sagittarius rising is a frank, open way in, and a restless one.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) sits in a mutable fire sign: enthusiasm that adapts and moves on. I read Sagittarius rising as an open horizon. New places look like possibilities, and new people like company for the road. In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant is the cusp of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/). Sagittarius is the natural sign of the 9th, the house of beliefs, study and long journeys, the God in the old names, so with Sagittarius rising the search for meaning walks in first: you tend to ask people what they think, not only what they do.

Sagittarius closes the slow half of the zodiac, the six signs from Cancer to Sagittarius that take more than two hours to rise in the north. It needs about 151 minutes at London, some twenty fewer than Scorpio before it, 148 at Paris and 142 at New York, and about 1 London birth in 10 has it. A time rounded to the hour leaves an hour of doubt, and at London an hour spans about two-fifths of Sagittarius: enough to keep the sign safe in its middle and to lose it at either end, to [Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/) or [Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/). Before I read it, I want the minute ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). South of the equator, it's the other way about: signs that rise fast in the northern hemisphere rise slowly in the southern, and the slow ones, Sagittarius among them, speed up.

## First impressions {#first-impressions}

People often find you easy company: frank, curious about their views, quick to laugh, and they may tell you more than they meant to. You tend to arrive with a question or a plan, and you treat a new acquaintance as the start of a conversation that could go anywhere.

The misreading is frankness taken for tactlessness, or restlessness for a lack of commitment. Beliefs aren't dogma: what sounds like a strong opinion is usually you thinking out loud, and you change your mind when someone shows you a bigger picture.

A frank note to match: in [my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the rising sign is among the placements everyone reads first, and it held about a third of the time. So I hold this first sketch loosely, as Sagittarius would, and look at where it leads in your chart.

## Its ruler {#its-ruler}

Jupiter rules Sagittarius, so [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/): the planet of growth, meaning and learning speaks for the whole chart. It isn't luck. I read it as the part of you that grows by understanding, and its house shows where your life keeps asking you to learn and widen.

Jupiter takes about twelve years to go round the zodiac, a year or so in each sign, so your chart ruler's sign is shared with people born around the same time, and the house carries more of the personal story. It also comes back to its birth position at about 12, 24, 36 and so on: with Sagittarius rising, each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) is a return of your chart ruler.

In Sagittarius, Jupiter is at home, and the ruler doubles the Ascendant's reach. In Cancer, its exaltation, the search for meaning turns towards care and belonging; in Capricorn, its fall, faith wants proof and builds slowly. In Gemini, on your Descendant, Jupiter is in detriment, and your chart ruler learns through questions and conversations rather than through one grand answer.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Close up, Sagittarius rising wants a companion for the road: someone to share plans, arguments and a sense that life is going somewhere. Honesty matters more to you than polish, and so does room to move. A bond that travels with you, in ideas or on the map, tends to last.

Your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is in Gemini, opposite: you're drawn to quick, curious people who ask good questions and keep the talk going ([Gemini on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/)).

They keep your big ideas honest by asking how, exactly; you give their questions somewhere to go.
