At the minute you were born, [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) was coming up over the eastern horizon, and you tend to meet the world the way the archer faces the distance: looking past what's in front of you to where it might lead. Sagittarius rising is a frank, open way in, and a restless one.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

Here the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) falls in mutable fire, enthusiasm that adapts and moves on. Think of it as an open horizon: new places look like possibilities, and new people like company for the road. Because the Ascendant is the cusp of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) in Placidus, the house system I use, Sagittarius rising lets the search of its natural house, the 9th, beliefs, study and long journeys, the God of the old names, walk in first: you tend to ask people what they think, not only what they do.

Sagittarius closes the slow half of the zodiac, the six signs from Cancer to Sagittarius that take more than two hours to rise in the north. It needs about 151 minutes at London, some twenty fewer than Scorpio before it, 148 at Paris and 142 at New York: enough for about 1 London birth in 10. A time rounded to the hour leaves an hour of doubt, and at London an hour spans about two-fifths of Sagittarius: enough to keep the sign safe in its middle and to lose it at either end, to [Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/) or [Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/). That's why the minute matters to me more than the hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). South of the equator, it's the other way about: signs that rise fast in the northern hemisphere rise slowly in the southern, and the slow ones, Sagittarius among them, speed up.

## First impressions {#first-impressions}

To a stranger you're usually easy company: frank, curious about their views, quick to laugh, and they may tell you more than they meant to. You tend to arrive with a question or a plan, and you treat a new acquaintance as the start of a conversation that could go anywhere.

Your frankness may be heard as tactlessness at first, and your restlessness as a lack of commitment. Beliefs aren't dogma: what sounds like a strong opinion is usually you thinking out loud, and you change your mind when someone shows you a bigger picture.

A frank note to match: in [my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the rising sign is among the placements everyone reads first, and it held about a third of the time. So I hold this first sketch loosely and follow where it leads in your chart.

## Its ruler {#its-ruler}

Sagittarius is Jupiter's sign, so [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the planet that speaks for your chart, your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/): growth, meaning and learning carry the whole of it. It isn't luck. I read it as the part of you that grows by understanding, and its house shows where your life keeps asking you to learn and widen.

Jupiter takes about twelve years to go round the zodiac, a year or so in each sign, so your chart ruler's sign is shared with people born around the same time, and the house carries more of the personal story. It also comes back to its birth position at about 12, 24, 36 and so on: with Sagittarius rising, each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) is a return of your chart ruler.

Jupiter in Sagittarius, at home, widens the Ascendant's reach still further. In Cancer, its exaltation, the search for meaning turns towards care and belonging; in Capricorn, its fall, faith wants proof and builds slowly. In Gemini, on your Descendant, Jupiter is in detriment, and your chart ruler learns through questions and conversations rather than through one grand answer.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

What Sagittarius rising wants in a partner is a companion for the road: someone to share plans, arguments and a sense that life is going somewhere. Honesty matters more to you than polish, and so does room to move. A bond that travels with you, in ideas or on the map, tends to last.

The sign on your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is Gemini, and it draws you to quick, curious people who ask good questions and keep the talk going ([Gemini on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/)).

A good argument with someone like that can be the best part of your week.
