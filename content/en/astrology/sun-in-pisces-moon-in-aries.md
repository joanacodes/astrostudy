Your Moon had already crossed into Aries, the sign that opens the zodiac, while your Sun was still in Pisces, the sign that closes it. Within a few weeks the Sun itself would cross the same line at the March equinox; your feelings got there first. I read the pair as a gentle, absorbing self with feelings that like to move first.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and cardinal fire share neither element nor mode, and as neighbours they make a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) forms its sense of self through compassion and imagination, taking in what is around it; the [Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/) regains its footing by acting, and the first thing it wants after a shock is something to do. You may soak up the mood of a room and then, a minute later, be the one who stands up and does something about it.

Your Moon takes its lead from [Mars](/astrology/mars/), ruler of Aries: where Mars sits by sign and house is where your feelings turn into action. For Pisces I keep two rulers in view and read both, the modern [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) for where your sense of self opens into something larger, the traditional [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) for where it finds meaning and room to grow. Aries is also the sign of the Sun's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), the place the Sun reaches each year at the equinox; your Moon is already standing there, while your Sun, in Pisces, has no dignity of its own.

You were born up to about five days after a new moon, usually the [new moon in Pisces](/astrology/new-moon-in-pisces/), the last before the March equinox, with the Moon 1° to 59° ahead of the Sun. Roughly seven in eight of these births are [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) births and the rest [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) ones. I read the new moon phase as a beginning that hasn't yet separated what you want from what you need; with this pair the needs are already off the starting line, at 0° Aries, where the [tropical zodiac](/astrology/tropical-zodiac/) begins. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains where the new moon phase ends.

The count puts this end-and-beginning pair at about 1 person in 147. Aries holds the Moon for about two and a half days, so if it arrived from Pisces or moved on to Taurus on your birthday, the time is the only way to choose between this page, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-pisces/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-taurus/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same hour gives the house of Mars, which is where much of this Moon's feeling goes.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love tenderly and react honestly. The Pisces Sun gives, forgives and senses a partner's mood; the Aries Moon speaks its feelings the moment they arrive, and wants the air cleared quickly. A partner may be surprised that someone so gentle can flare, and that the flare is over in minutes. It helps to name the flash for what it is, a feeling looking for an outlet, and then come back to the gentleness, which is just as real.

## At work {#at-work}

Work where compassion has to act fast suits you: crisis and emergency roles, advocacy, campaigning, care that can't wait, art that needs nerve as well as imagination. The Sun brings a feeling for what people need; the Moon brings the courage to start. Long waits and passive roles frustrate the Moon, and workplaces without kindness wear down the Sun.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Absorbing and reacting can follow each other too closely. You may take in someone's distress and answer it at once, before you know whose feeling it was, or carry a mood all day and then release it in one sharp word. A pause between the two is what helps: something physical for the Aries Moon, a walk or a run, and something quiet for the Pisces Sun, music or water, before you decide what to do.
