A small, kind opening comes twice in every Jupiter round, when the planet of growth stands 60° from your birth Neptune. Of all the cycle's moments, it's the easiest for letting a dream earn its keep.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your natal [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where you imagine, empathise and long for something larger than daily life. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens, believes and gives reasons. Linked by a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), they cooperate only if someone takes part: the angle makes imagination easier to reach, but only you can do something with it. Jupiter brings the belief that the dream is worth something, which is often exactly what Neptune lacks.

These weeks suit a dream tried at small scale. A first chapter, a class in the art you've always meant to study, an hour a week given to a cause, a quiet practice begun in the morning: Neptune's ideas stay fragile until they have a form, and this is the point of the round where giving them one costs least. Neptune also carries a sense of the collective, so the opening sometimes comes through a group: a choir, a volunteer rota, a circle that meditates or makes things together.

The sextile is also the gentlest test Neptune gets. What you imagine meets a little of the real world, enough to show what works, without the friction a square would bring. Where it happens depends on your Neptune's house and on the house Jupiter is crossing, and only the birth time can place either ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If [Jupiter sextile Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-neptune/) is in your chart, each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) brings one of these sextiles with it, an echo of an ease you were born with.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Jupiter needs about eleven days to travel from 1½° before a sextile to your Neptune to 1° beyond it, the orbs I time [transits](/astrology/transits/) with. Three in ten loop, and the sextile then comes back three times over some eight months. Births from 1930 to 2009 collect thirteen or fourteen of them before 80.

In January 1965 Jupiter stood in Taurus, about four signs short of a sextile to that year's Neptune, and got there at about 3.7; in January 1975 it had already passed one sextile point and needed about six years for the other.

Of the five contacts Jupiter makes with your Neptune, this is the lightest touch: quieter than [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-neptune/), which floods the same place with inspiration; gentler than [transiting Jupiter square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-neptune/), which asks a dream for proof; smaller than the steady ease of [transiting Jupiter trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-neptune/); and more private than [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-neptune/), where the dream is held by someone else.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the dream a slot in your week, at a time you'd keep for anything else that mattered. A sextile rewards regularity more than intensity.

Learn the craft that would carry your imagination: a technique, a tool, a teacher. Neptune sees the picture whole; the craft is what gets it onto the page.

Show someone what you've made. One honest reader or listener does more for a Neptune idea than months of keeping it private, and the sextile makes that first showing easier than usual.
