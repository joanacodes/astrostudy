Leo is the fifth sign of the zodiac, the one the Sun crosses at the height of summer in the northern hemisphere, and the only sign the Sun rules. It's the sign of the heart. Wherever it falls in a chart, I read it as the place where you want to make something of your own — and have it seen.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Leo somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it brings warmth, pride, play and a wish to be recognised for what you make. Leo asks *do you see me?* It's a [fire sign](/astrology/fire-signs/), like Aries and Sagittarius, so it runs on enthusiasm; and it's fixed, one of the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/), so its warmth is steady rather than a flare — loyalty is one of its quieter strengths. In the sequence of signs, each one answering the last, Leo answers [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/): after the safety of home, the courage to step out and be seen. [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), next, will ask whether it works.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/):** born between about 23 July and 22 August. The Sun at home: you grow by creating and being generous with it, and recognition matters because it tells you that what you made was real.
- **[Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/):** you need warmth, play and to feel special to the people who matter. Hurt pride runs deep, and your generosity comes back the moment you feel seen.
- **[Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/):** you meet the world with warmth and presence, and people notice you, sometimes before you've said a word. The Sun becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/). A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So I read Leo through the whole chart, beginning with where the Sun sits.

## Its ruler: the Sun {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, the one that carries the sign's business into the rest of the chart. Leo belongs to the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), the only sign it rules: tradition gave the two lights the two signs of high summer, Leo to the Sun and Cancer to the Moon. Wherever your Sun sits, by sign and house, shows where your Leo side wants to shine. With Leo on the cusp of the 10th house and the Sun in the 4th, say, your public life draws its strength from home.

In the classical scheme, no planet is exalted in Leo or has its fall there. Two are weakened:

- [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite Aquarius, which tradition gives it: discipline meets a sign that wants to play, and the lesson is to take your own joy seriously.
- [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern ruler of Aquarius, is in detriment here too for modern astrologers: the urge to break the mould meets a sign that wants to stand at the centre of it.

## Planets in Leo {#planets-in-the-sign}

A planet in Leo warms up and wants an audience; the house it sits in shows where that plays out.

- **[Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/):** the Sun at home — a warm, creative will; you shine by making things and sharing them.
- **[Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/):** a need for warmth, play and appreciation; generous with love, and hurt when it goes unnoticed.
- **[Mercury in Leo](/astrology/mercury-in-leo/):** a vivid, expressive mind; you speak with conviction and tell a good story.
- **[Venus in Leo](/astrology/venus-in-leo/):** generous, demonstrative love; you love wholeheartedly, and like to be adored a little in return.
- **[Mars in Leo](/astrology/mars-in-leo/):** energy with heart; you act with pride and courage, and fight for what you love.
- **[Jupiter in Leo](/astrology/jupiter-in-leo/):** growth through generosity and creative risk; a big heart and a sense of occasion.
- **[Saturn in Leo](/astrology/saturn-in-leo/):** Saturn in detriment — confidence and self-expression learned slowly; joy you have to give yourself permission for.
- **[Uranus in Leo](/astrology/uranus-in-leo/):** Uranus in detriment — a generation's push to express itself on its own terms, shared by everyone born over about seven years; its house makes it personal.
- **[Neptune in Leo](/astrology/neptune-in-leo/):** ideals of romance, creativity and glamour; shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so I read it through its house.
- **[Pluto in Leo](/astrology/pluto-in-leo/):** a generation's deep transformation of self-expression and personal power; shared over twelve years or more, made personal by its house.

## Leo on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Leo somewhere in its twelve houses, usually on the cusp of one of them. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Leo opens a house, that's where you want to put your heart into things and be seen doing it, and the Sun, wherever it sits, carries the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/leo-rising/):** Leo rising — a warm, noticeable presence; you meet the world generously, and people remember you.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/leo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** generous with what you have; you value quality and things that express who you are, and your pride is tied to earning your own way.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** warm, expressive speech; a natural storyteller who likes to be heard.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home to gather people in and be proud of; family loyalty runs deep.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** Leo in its natural house — play, romance and creativity are where you come alive; you love making things and sharing them.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** you need to take pride in your daily work; you do your best when it's appreciated and you can put your heart in.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Leo on the Descendant, which means Aquarius rising — drawn to warm, confident, generous partners; you want a relationship that celebrates you both.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/leo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** loyal, wholehearted intimacy; pride can make it hard to show what you need.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs held with conviction and shared with enthusiasm; you learn best from what inspires you.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a public life where you want to lead and be recognised; a reputation for warmth and presence.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/leo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** fiercely loyal to your friends; in groups, you often end up at the centre, or at the head.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/leo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** warmth and creativity kept to yourself; generosity given quietly, without needing applause.

## Leo in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Leo planets bring warmth and generosity: the person who has them lights the other up — praising, celebrating, making them feel chosen. To the other person that can feel like sunshine or like a spotlight, depending on where those planets land in their chart and what they touch. Across the zodiac sits [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), the opposite sign: Leo wants to be loved as one particular heart, Aquarius to stay free, one among many. Between two charts, that axis brings a strong pull — and a question of how much attention a bond needs. As for comparing Sun signs, [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind: two people are far more than two Sun signs, Leo or otherwise.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Leos are arrogant.”** Leo is proud, and pride can be quiet. Much of what looks like ego is a wish to be seen for what you've made, and a generosity that wants to share it.
- **“Leos need constant attention.”** Leo needs warmth and appreciation, like everyone, and says so more openly. It gives attention as readily as it asks for it.
- **“The Sun in Leo makes you a born leader.”** The Sun at home gives a strong sense of self. Leading is one way to show it; creating, teaching and caring are others.
- **“Born on the cusp, you're half Cancer.”** At the minute you were born, the Sun was in one sign or the other; within a day of a change, your birth time settles which. More on being [born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/).
