Their Moon in your 8th house is closeness at depth. Another person's feelings reach the part of your life you share with very few: intimacy, trust, what you hold in common and what you owe, and the moments that change people.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is where a bond turns serious: intimacy of every kind, trust and its cost, money and decisions shared, the debts people carry between them. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds a person's emotional memory and their need to feel safe. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their need for safety looks for it in depth.

For you, emotional closeness with them may come quickly and go further than usual. They seem to sense what you haven't said, ask the question underneath the question, and stay with you through feelings you'd normally keep to yourself. Being known that well can be a relief; it can also feel like a door opened before you'd reached for the handle.

For them, your capacity for intimacy can be the safest place they know. They may confide feelings they keep from everyone else, and the bond becomes somewhere they can be undefended. The 8th is succedent, and its natural sign, [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), keeps its feelings close and deep.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The deeper the bond, the more trust it needs, and their need for it can outrun yours. If they want emotional closeness faster than you can give it, you may feel pressed to share before you're ready, or exposed by how much they sense. Shared matters, money, plans, confidences, can carry heavy feelings with this overlay, and it helps to say who holds what before it's assumed.

From their side, your privacy can feel like a closed door. When you withdraw to process something alone, they may take it personally, as if the safest place they know had been locked.

Death and inheritance were the 8th's in the old tradition. A Moon here says nothing of either, in synastry or in a single chart, and I never read for them ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); it describes how feeling and trust move between two people.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let trust grow at the pace of the slower person, and say out loud what each of you needs to feel safe. In a couple, intimacy of every kind goes best when both people can say "not yet" and "more" with equal ease; between friends, agree which confidences stay between you; between colleagues, keep sensitive matters professional and clearly bounded; between adult relatives, conversations about what's held in common go better when feelings are named alongside the figures.

Read from their side, the overlay places your Moon in their 8th house, so your need for depth meets their private life, with the same pace to respect. [Their Moon in your 7th house](/astrology/moon-in-7th-house-synastry/) is partnership face to face; [their Moon in your 9th house](/astrology/moon-in-9th-house-synastry/) finds comfort in shared meaning and far horizons.

Their Moon spends only two days or so in each of your houses, so their birth date alone can't place it in your 8th; their time can. Your time draws the 8th itself, and in Placidus, the house system I use, its width varies a great deal from chart to chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Feelings this private are read only with both people's knowledge ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
