Your public life is rarely a solo act. From the 7th, the house of the person across the table, the ruler of your 10th house builds your vocation with someone, or for someone, one person at a time.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your reputation and the work you're known for are [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) matters, and in Placidus, the house system I use, that house begins at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), whose ruler you'll find here ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), which opens at the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), is the house of the people you meet as equals: lovers and business partners, collaborators, clients, and the rivals you can see coming. Counted by sign, the two houses are a square apart, so partnership and ambition don't always agree, and this ruler is where they negotiate.

Your career is made in company. Some people with this placement build a business with a partner, or a practice with a spouse; others work one to one, as consultants, therapists, agents, lawyers or coaches, anyone whose work is the person in front of them. Reputation comes from relationships: you’re recommended rather than advertised. Contracts are part of the trade, and you may be the one who drafts, reads or negotiates them for others. Rivals matter too, and a well-matched competitor can do more for your career than a friend. The difficulty is a public life that hangs on one other person. When a partnership ends, the career shouldn't have to end with it, so it helps to know which part of the work is yours alone.

Turn the chart and the link runs the other way. Counted from the 7th, the 10th is the 4th, and horary astrologers read it for a partner's or an opponent's property ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)). I use that sparingly. It can describe how a partner's ground and your own vocation overlap, the firm you join through marriage or the family house that becomes an office, never the details of their affairs, which belong to their own chart.

Both the Midheaven and the Descendant move with the clock, and a ruler near the Descendant can sit on either side of it within a few minutes ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If it had already set, it would be in the 6th, where the job is learned and done day by day ([Ruler of the 10th in the 6th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)). With an earlier time, higher in the sky, it would be in the 8th, where the work handles what others entrust to you ([Ruler of the 10th in the 8th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). Below the horizon or above it, the work still runs through people; what changes is the kind of people.

Pisces on the Midheaven puts the 10th under Jupiter and Neptune together ([Pisces on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Jupiter in the 7th tends to attract generous partners and clients who open doors ([Jupiter in the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-7th-house/)). Neptune in the 7th draws you to partnerships built on a shared ideal, and asks you to read the contract as carefully as the dream.
