Their Mars in your 4th house brings another person's drive through your front door and into the rooms where you rest. Home, family and the private life you come back to grow busier when they're around: things get built, moved, repaired and, now and then, argued over.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) initiates: it sets things in motion and stands up for what matters. [The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is your foundation: the home you live in and the one you came from, the family you belong to, the side of you that only your nearest see. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their Mars here puts energy into the base of your life.

Around them, home may stop being a place where things wait. The shelves go up, the move finally happens, the family conversation you'd put off for years gets started. They may also stand guard over your private life, the way someone stands at a door: defending your time at home, or you, when family matters grow heavy.

For them, your home can feel like ground worth working on. They're at ease putting effort into your space, cooking, fixing, organising, and they may feel protective of what you've built there. The 4th is the most inward of the angular houses; [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), its natural sign, is where tradition puts Mars's fall, and I read that as drive that does best in a home when it protects rather than pushes. Right on your [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), their Mars presses on the root of your chart, and that conjunction joins the reading.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Energy in the place you go to rest can feel like having nowhere left to rest. You may feel pushed in your own home: plans for your rooms, opinions on how your household runs, a pace that doesn't stop at the doorstep. Friction under one roof is felt at the base of you, far more than an argument at work.

From their side, you can seem to close a door just as they want to help. Their effort may land as an intrusion, and they end up feeling like a visitor in a home they hoped to share.

This house holds how home and family feel from the inside, not the story of anyone's parents, and their Mars doesn't rewrite your past. What it shows is energy in your private life today, between two adults.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree on the house rules, literally. Whose space is whose, how much change the place can take, when the door stays shut: settled calmly, these leave room for their energy to do some good. In a couple living together, give each person a corner that is theirs alone; between friends, a project at one of your homes can be the best of this overlay; between adult relatives, this may be the person who gets the whole family moving, provided someone says when to stop.

Swap the two charts and your Mars enters their 4th house, so the push into a private home comes from you. [Their Mars in your 3rd house](/astrology/mars-in-3rd-house-synastry/) is the talk on the way in; [their Mars in your 5th house](/astrology/mars-in-5th-house-synastry/) turns the same energy towards play and desire.

Your Imum Coeli turns with the sky, through every sign in the course of a day, so the start of your 4th comes from your birth time; in Placidus, the house system I use, as in every quadrant system, the IC is that cusp. In the hours around their birth, their Mars moved by only a fraction of a degree, so their time counts only if it sits right on the cusp, and I wait for both times regardless ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you live together, the reading is about both people under that roof, and it starts once each of you has said yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
