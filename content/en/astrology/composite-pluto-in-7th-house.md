For most pairs, the relationship is the setting; for this one, it is also the subject. A composite Pluto in the 7th house puts the bond's deepest changes in the partnership itself: its terms, its commitments, and the way the two of you face other people as a pair.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) begins at the composite [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) and covers partnership in every sense: the agreement between the two of you, the contracts you sign together, the other couples you befriend, the business partners, allies and occasional opponents you meet as a unit. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) puts the partnership itself under renovation. Astrologers read a bond that keeps rewriting its own agreement and comes out of each crisis more committed rather than less: an arrangement renegotiated after a move abroad, a joint venture that changes both of you, a hard year that forces the question of what the pair is for, and an answer that holds.

[Pluto in the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-in-7th-house/) in one birth chart describes a person whom partnerships transform; [their Pluto in your 7th house](/astrology/pluto-in-7th-house-synastry/), one partner who makes the other's idea of partnership feel bigger. In the composite, the 7th belongs to neither person, so the power it describes isn't one person's over the other: it's the weight the relationship carries in both lives. Two independent people can find that, together, the partnership becomes the most demanding and most rewarding thing they do.

The trap is reading every imbalance as domination. A pair this aware of power can start treating each unequal moment, who earns more, who chooses the holiday, who apologises first, as a deliberate bid for control, when most are simply two people with different strengths. Balance in a relationship is kept over years, not audited daily. It helps to take the long view and ask who has given way over the past year, rather than the past week.

Real fear is something else. If one of you is frightened of the other, threatened, or kept from leaving, that's not a 7th-house dynamic to interpret: in a UK emergency ring 999, 988 answers calls and texts in the US and Canada, and the full list of lines is on my Disclaimers page ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)). None of this foretells a wedding, a divorce or a separation, either; a composite never says how long two people will stay together, only how their partnership works on itself.

Like every composite house, the 7th needs both birth times, and it has a quirk of its own ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). When the houses come from the midpoints of your two charts' cusps and your two Ascendants lie almost opposite each other, a small difference in either birth time can flip the composite Ascendant to the other side of the circle, so a Pluto near the composite Descendant suddenly sits near the Ascendant instead. The other method, from the composite Midheaven for where you live, can give yet another house. Two yeses are an agreement the 7th would recognise: the composite of a partnership is drawn when both partners want it, not when one is curious about the other ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

[Composite Pluto in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-6th-house/) transforms the daily round; [composite Pluto in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-8th-house/), what the pair shares at depth. In the 7th, the change is in the bond's own terms.
