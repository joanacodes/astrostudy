You start doing things you'd never have tried, and it feels less like being changed than like being let out. Put one person's Uranus on the other's Sun and that's often the story, as the Sun person tells it. The Uranus person tells it differently.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity: who a person is becoming, and where they are headed. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) brings innovation, differentiation and liberation, the part of a chart that wants to try another way. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) co-activates the two into one process, each keeping its own nature; between two people, one supplies the self and the other the urge to renew it. Of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) Uranus can make, it's one of the most noticeable.

For the Sun person, the other's presence loosens the shape you've given yourself. Habits get questioned, a long-postponed plan suddenly looks possible, and you may be more original in their company than anywhere else. The self doesn't disappear; it gets an update.

For the Uranus person, the other's identity is where your inventiveness finds a target and a use. You see what they could be with a constraint or two removed, and being the one who opens a door is a pleasure. Your own need to differ feels welcome rather than tolerated.

What flows is renewal: a couple who reinvent their routines together; friends who travel or retrain because one of them suggested it; a sibling who left the family pattern and smooths the way for the other; colleagues who rebuild a process from scratch.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

When two functions share one spot, it gets hard to tell whose changes are whose. The Sun person may adopt the other's reforms wholesale and wonder, later, which of the new habits they actually chose. The Uranus person may come to treat the Sun person's life as a project. Their interest is often sharpest while something is changing, and a settled stretch can feel to them like a pause to fill, and to the Sun person like a test.

Differentiation isn't alienation, and autonomy isn't distance. Still, the Uranus person's need for room can arrive just when the Sun person has opened up most, and the timing stings.

I don't read the sign of Uranus in a synastry at all. Uranus stays some seven years in each sign, so it's common to everyone born in the same stretch, classmates and colleagues alike. What singles out this pair is the Sun, whose place is set by the birthday.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Choose the changes. The Sun person can keep one thing constant on purpose, a routine, a friendship, a way of working, as an anchor while the rest moves. The Uranus person can offer ideas as options rather than verdicts, and ask before reforming something that belongs to the other. Couples do well with novelty they share; friends, with adventures either can decline; family members, with room to differ without a fuss; coworkers, with a clear owner for each project, however radical the redesign.

About 1 pair of people in 12 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 8°. A birth hour seldom creates or cancels it, the Sun covering a degree or so in a day, yet I don't read it without both times: the houses decide where the renewal lands in each life, and the [house overlays](/astrology/house-overlays/) show where one person's Uranus falls among the other's houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The other angles hold the change further off: [Sun sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus-synastry/) offers it, [Sun square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-uranus-synastry/) makes it a question of direction, [Sun trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus-synastry/) lets it in easily, and [Sun opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus-synastry/) sets the freedom on the far side. In one chart, [Sun conjunct Uranus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus/) is a single identity built around being different; here the difference arrives from someone else. Because it reaches the core of one person's sense of self, I read it with both people's agreement, never as a report on someone absent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
