Their Mercury in your 5th house turns talk into play. Running jokes, stories told for the pleasure of telling them, flirting that happens mostly in sentences: with this overlay, conversation itself becomes one of the things you enjoy together.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) holds the things you do for no reason but delight: play, pleasure, creativity, romance, the risks you take for fun and the way you show yourself when nobody is grading you. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is curiosity and exchange, the mind that likes to connect one thing to another. This [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) lets their mind wander into your playground.

For you, they make things fun to think about. They pick up your jokes and hand them back improved, suggest the game, the puzzle, the film worth arguing over afterwards, and take an interest in what you make: a drawing, a song, a half-finished story. You may grow more inventive with them, and readier to show work you'd usually keep in a drawer. When there's attraction, it often starts in words, a message read twice, a joke only the two of you get.

For them, your pleasures are an education. They learn from what delights you, and their own ideas loosen up in your company. The 5th is succedent, where things grow once you feel at home, and [Leo](/astrology/leo/), its natural sign, belongs to the Sun: warmth, self-expression, an audience. Tradition named the house Good Fortune.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Banter can tip into teasing. A joke at your expense, repeated because it got a laugh once, or a clever review of something you made purely for the pleasure of making it, can take the fun out of both. And in a friendship or at work, playful words can be read as meaning more than they did.

From their side, they may feel you don't take their ideas seriously: everything turns into a game, and the conversation they wanted about something that matters becomes another round. They can also end up feeling like your audience rather than a partner in the play.

Old books looked in the 5th for children, and some astrologers still do. This overlay says nothing about whether or when you'll have a child, and no synastry does ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)); if the two of you talk about it, that conversation is yours, not the chart's.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree on a signal for "this one isn't funny", and use it without making a scene. Make things together: a playlist, a recipe, a story written in alternating paragraphs. In a couple, keep flirting in words long after the first months; between friends, this is the one you write nonsense with; between colleagues, brainstorms go well as long as someone writes the good ideas down; between adult relatives, the one who still plays the old family games, and wins.

Swap places and it's your Mercury in their 5th house: your humour and curiosity become part of what they enjoy. On one side, [their Mercury in your 4th house](/astrology/mercury-in-4th-house-synastry/) keeps the conversation at home; on the other, [their Mercury in your 6th house](/astrology/mercury-in-6th-house-synastry/) puts it to work on the daily round.

Placidus, the house system I use, gives the 5th no angle of its own: both its cusps come from your birth time and birthplace, and a quarter of an hour either way can move them by several degrees. Their Mercury, placed by their birth date to within a degree or two, needs their time only when it sits on one of those lines, and I read the pair once I have both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If the flirting is new, give it a few months before asking a chart about it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#we-just-met)).
