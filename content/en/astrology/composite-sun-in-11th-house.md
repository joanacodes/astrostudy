Friends, groups and the future you're working towards belong to the 11th house. Put the composite Sun there and the relationship finds its centre in its circle: the bond knows itself among the people it gathers and the hopes it shares.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) begins at the first cusp after the composite Midheaven, and that's where the two ways of drawing composite houses part company. By one, the 11th cusp is the midpoint of your two natal 11th cusps; by the other, it's recalculated from the composite Midheaven for your home's latitude. Both rest on your two birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), so the 11th's borders, and a [Sun](/astrology/sun/) near them, can shift with the method as well as with the times.

Friendship and the future are the 11th house's concerns. A relationship like this is often a friendship at heart, whatever else it is, and it tends to sit at the centre of a circle: the house where people drop in, the group chat that started with the two of you, the projects friends join because you asked. For a couple, it often means being each other's closest friend first; for two colleagues, the kind of alliance that outlasts the job. Birthdays tend to turn into gatherings. It's revived by shared hopes, and its purpose often points ahead, towards something you're building for later, or for more people than yourselves.

Jupiter had its joy in the 11th, which tradition called the Good Spirit ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)); the house is succedent, with Aquarius as its natural sign. A birth chart's [Sun in the 11th house](/astrology/sun-in-11th-house/) shows one person among friends and causes; the overlay [their Sun in your 11th house](/astrology/sun-in-11th-house-synastry/) shows someone brightening another's hopes and circle. The composite's circle forms around the pair itself, and people who each had few friends can find a small crowd gathering once they're together.

A bond centred on the circle can rarely be alone. The calendar fills with other people, the relationship becomes a hub, and the two of you can end up catching up with each other last. Friendship isn't popularity, and the bond doesn't have to host everyone to stay itself. A hope that belongs only to the two of you, said out loud now and then, keeps the centre where it is, and so does knowing which friendships are the bond's and which are each person's own, and keeping both. None of this forecasts how long the relationship will last or what it will become.

Before it, the [composite Sun in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-10th-house/) centres a bond on its public role; after it, the [composite Sun in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-12th-house/), on its inner life. Composites belong to other astrologers' toolkits rather than mine, and in their hands a composite never goes without both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). Friends ask before they borrow: a composite borrows two people's birth data, so it asks both ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
