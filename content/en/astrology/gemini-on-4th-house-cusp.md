Gemini on the 4th house cusp lays the foundations of the chart in the sign of talk and curiosity, at the IC, where home, family and roots are read. Where other charts look for quiet at the base, this one looks for company and conversation.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) is the ground under a chart, and its sign is the kind of ground that lets you rest ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) there, belonging comes through news and words: home is where things are heard first, where people drop in, where rooms change use as the day goes on. I often find family stories kept in language more than in objects: sayings, nicknames, a second tongue, the way things were explained at the kitchen table. You rest by switching what you're thinking about, a book, a call, a different room, more than by sitting still. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) describes home as you lived it and the private self underneath, not who your parents were; here that private self thinks aloud, and a home where nobody talks can feel empty however full it is.

The Midheaven is always the opposite degree, so with an IC in Gemini, Sagittarius is at the top of your chart every time, and Jupiter rules your public life while Mercury rules your private one ([Sagittarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)). In public you tend to be asked for the overview and the opinion; at home you trade the details. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), as ruler of the 4th, carries home life into whichever house it occupies: the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Its other sign, Virgo, is tied to your 7th house in more than 9 charts in 10 with this IC, opening it in about 2 London charts in 5 and lying wholly inside it in most of the others ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Mercury then speaks for your home and for the person across the table alike.

In Placidus, the system I work in, the 4th cusp is the IC itself, which follows the turning of the sky rather than your distance from the equator, so London and New York give the same count: about 1 birth in 11 has Gemini at the bottom of the chart. Gemini holds it for a little over two hours each day, longer than the average sign: near the solstice the zodiac runs almost parallel to the equator, and Gemini crosses the meridian slowly. At London, Aquarius rises with it in just over half the charts and Pisces in about 2 in 5; at New York it's the other way round. Saturn and Uranus rule the chart, or Jupiter and Neptune, and I read both rulers either way; with Pisces rising, Jupiter has a hand in your Ascendant and your Midheaven at once. More than 1 London chart in 3 also has Gemini on the 3rd, and about 2 in 5 on the 5th ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), which brings [Gemini on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/) or [Gemini on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/) into your chart as well.

The IC passes through all twelve signs every day, and only the minute you were born can fix it in Gemini; I don't read a chart until that minute is known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
