Some people know exactly which of your old talents to get excited about. Uranus opposite North Node in synastry puts one person's Uranus about 180° from the other's North Node, within 4°, which means right on their South Node: change arrives on the ground the second person knows best, while their growth points the other way.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) experiments with what exists: it reforms and reconfigures, and refuses to leave a pattern alone just because it works. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) is consolidated capacity, the skills a person reaches for without thinking; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) across from it is the less practised direction they are building. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates functions as a polarity, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one puts Uranus at the familiar end of the other's axis and leaves the far end open.

On the node side, old abilities can come back to life: the other person finds a new use for something you've always done, or shakes up a routine you'd stopped seeing. A stirred South Node can free you to move, because the familiar no longer holds you by default.

On the Uranus side, the other's most practised self is the part that fascinates you, and you may keep encouraging it, sometimes more than their new steps.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The seesaw can tilt back. Excited by the refreshed familiar, the node person may stay there; unsettled, they may cling to it harder. The Uranus person can cheer on old rebellions instead of the quieter growth across the chart. The South Node is no mistake to leave behind; the work is to use what Uranus stirs as a springboard, not a home.

- **[Uranus conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the restlessness sits on the path of growth itself.
- **[Uranus sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** a side door to growth, with the old end flattered by a trine.
- **[Uranus square North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-north-node-synastry/):** Uranus off to the side of both nodes, crossing the path.
- **[Uranus trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-north-node-synastry/):** growth carried by change, old skills tagging along as tools.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask what the stir is for. The node person can notice when excitement pulls them back to familiar ground, and give one step towards the North Node the same energy; the Uranus person can celebrate the new, not only the revived. Colleagues can apply a veteran's skill to a new kind of task; a couple can turn an old shared pastime into something neither has tried; friends can revisit an old plan only to change it.

In mid-1975 Uranus stood in Libra, on the degrees where the South Node had stood for births from July 1967 to January 1968, and would stand again for births from February to July 1986. Anyone born, on and off, from May 1941 to May 1943 has Uranus on the 1975 South Node. The year of birth settles all of this, which is why I leave it uncounted: the contacts I count between two charts each include the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Through the houses, it shows one era stirring up what another took for granted.

I take the true node for these windows and compare it with the mean node, never more than a degree or two apart, before saying the contact is there ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The hour can't make or break the opposition, but it places the axis: Uranus on a South Node in the 4th house, among home habits, puts the node person's growth in their 10th, in public life, and only both times can show that ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Uranus opposite North Node](/astrology/uranus-opposite-north-node/) in one chart makes change and the fresh start something a person already knows by heart. Across two charts, the restlessness belongs to one person and the habits to the other, and I read it only when both of you want it read ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
