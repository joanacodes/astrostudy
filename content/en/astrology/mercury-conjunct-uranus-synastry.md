You start a sentence with one opinion and finish it with another. Mercury conjunct Uranus in synastry lays one person's Mercury over the other's Uranus, and talk between them tends to jump the rails, in the best ways and the most tiring ones. Both sides of it are worth reading: the mind that gets jolted awake, and the urge to change that finally gets words.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how a person thinks, learns and puts things into words. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is how they break from the expected: invention, independence, the urge to reform what has grown too tight. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), two functions work as a single process, neither losing its nature, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) like this one the fusion happens between two people.

In conversation the pair moves fast. The Mercury person describes how something works, the Uranus person asks why it has to work that way, and within minutes you're redesigning it. Decisions can be made on a flash of insight, and some of the best ideas either of you has may come out of these exchanges. Explaining yourselves is quick, sometimes too quick, because each catches the other's drift before the sentence ends.

With the Mercury, you may find this person wakes your mind up: you think thoughts around them that you wouldn't think alone. With the Uranus, you may find someone who can put your odd ideas into words other people understand, which is a rare and freeing thing.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A conjunction blurs, and here it blurs an insight with a whim. When every idea arrives with the same electricity, it's hard to tell which one is worth acting on. Plans change mid-week; a decision reached on Monday is reopened on Wednesday for the pleasure of it. The Mercury person can lose their own train of thought in the other's leaps; the Uranus person can find their need for independence turning into a topic instead of a freedom. Innovation isn't chaos, but it needs somewhere to land.

Uranus takes about seven years to cross a sign, so the Uranus person's Uranus is, give or take, the Uranus of everyone born in the same few years. What makes this conjunction particular is the Mercury that lands on it, and the houses where it lands.

The same two functions keep a different distance at each angle:

- **[Mercury sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-uranus-synastry/):** an opening; the new idea comes when one of you goes looking for it.
- **[Mercury square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-uranus-synastry/):** a right angle, where explanation and disruption cut across each other.
- **[Mercury trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-uranus-synastry/):** a smooth current in one element; surprise without the jolt.
- **[Mercury opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-uranus-synastry/):** the usual and the unusual on two sides of the table, each held by one person.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give the pace a fixed point. Write down what you've decided, so the next brilliant idea has to argue with it rather than replace it. Let the Uranus person's suggestions be tried before they're adopted, and let the Mercury person finish an explanation before it's redesigned. Between colleagues this can be an inventive partnership; between friends, the person you ring when you're stuck; in a couple or a family, the one who keeps everyone's thinking from going stale.

About 1 pair of people in 15 has this conjunction between a Mercury and a Uranus, one way round or the other, within 6°. Mercury moves slowly enough that the hour of birth rarely decides the contact; the hours decide its setting instead. Falling in the Mercury person's 11th house, the house of friends and shared causes, it takes on the reading of [their Uranus in your 11th house](/astrology/uranus-in-11th-house-synastry/), and both birth times are needed to see it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Inside one person, [Mercury conjunct Uranus](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-uranus/) is a mind that reaches its conclusions early. Split between two, it's a conversation that reaches them together, and then has to agree to keep them.
