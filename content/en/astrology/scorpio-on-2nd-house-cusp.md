Scorpio on the 2nd house cusp gives the sign of depth charge of what you own and what you think you're worth. Scorpio consolidates here: fewer things, held longer, each one known to the bottom.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) answers the question of what you can count on, and with [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) on its cusp the answer has to be tested before you believe it ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). You'd rather build one reserve you trust than spread yourself across ten small comforts, and you want to know what a thing is really worth, beneath the price on the label. What you own tends to be a few things kept for years, often with a story attached. Your sense of worth may have been forged in a hard season, and it tends to come back stronger after a loss than it was before. The natural zodiac puts Taurus on the 2nd and Scorpio on the 8th. This cusp turns that axis round, with Taurus on your 8th: you handle what's yours with an 8th-house intensity, and what you share with a 2nd-house calm.

This house answers to two rulers, and I read both. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the modern one, moves so slowly that its sign belongs to your generation and its house to you alone; [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the traditional one, speaks faster and more personally. Their houses show where your resources are made and spent, and when they sit apart, the 2nd's story has two settings: the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Mars carries a second house here: Aries opens your 7th in about 9 London charts in 10 with this cusp, so what you own and whom you partner with answer to the same planet, and in about 2 in 5, your 6th.

Scorpio takes nearly three hours to rise at London, and the cusps on either side of the Ascendant share that long passage: after the Ascendant itself, the 2nd and the 12th are its commonest cusps there, about 1 birth in 9 each, and 1 in 10 at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. In about 9 London charts in 10 with this cusp, Libra rises, and Venus rules both your chart and, through Taurus, your 8th: your manner and what you share answer to Venus, what you own to Pluto and Mars. The remaining charts have Scorpio rising too, on two cusps in a row ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), and Pluto and Mars then rule your chart as well as your resources. The 2nd runs a little wider than a sign, about 31° to 35° at London, and in about 1 London chart in 8 it holds the whole of Sagittarius after Scorpio ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Scorpio itself has no cusp at all in about 1 London birth in 7 and 1 New York birth in 11.

Your birth time puts Scorpio here rather than on the Ascendant ([Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/)), where it could have been a little later, or on the 3rd ([Scorpio on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-3rd-house-cusp/)), where it might have sat a little earlier. The minute decides which of those charts is yours, and I need it before I read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Nothing here says how much you'll earn, keep or lose. I read the 2nd for how you handle and value what you have, never for amounts, and no chart can pick an investment ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). With Scorpio on this cusp, it shows a way of holding on to what matters.
