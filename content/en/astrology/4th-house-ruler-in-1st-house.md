Home travels with you. Where you come from shows in the way you walk into a room: the planet of your roots stands in the house of the self.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is the ground under a chart, home, family, belonging and the private self, and it starts at the IC. The planet ruling the sign on that cusp ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)) sits just below the eastern horizon, in [the 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/): your body, your manner, the way you start things.

I read it as roots you carry in plain sight. Your accent, your habits, the way you lay a table or greet a stranger may carry your background more plainly than most people's, and others often sense where you come from before you say. Home is less an address than something you take with you: you can settle quickly in a new place because you bring the essentials. The other side is that family habits can pass for your own personality, and part of adult life is telling the two apart.

The 1st is also the body, so home can be something you feel physically: the food you were raised on, a way of resting, the weather you grew up in. Many people with this ruler notice homesickness in the body before they name it, and feel their shoulders drop when they're back among familiar things.

Both houses are angular, the strongest kind in the old scheme ([angularity](/astrology/angularity/)), and they lie a quarter of the wheel apart, so the ruler of the bottom of the chart looks back at home from the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) side, across a square. The private ground and the front door answer to one planet, and that planet's manner shows in both.

With Cancer on the IC, the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), the 4th's natural ruler, rules it from the 1st: your moods and needs are in plain view, and people tend to feel at ease with you quickly. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn IC from the 1st, gives a graver bearing, someone others lean on, who may have carried responsibility at home for a long time.

I read the family in this placement from your side. It describes how home marked you and how you carry it, not what your parents were or are, and it passes no judgement on them. If you'd like to compare charts with a parent, that's a reading for two adults who both agree to it ([Charts for friends, colleagues and family](/astrology/charts-for-friends-colleagues-and-family/)).

Pisces on the IC gives home two rulers, Jupiter and Neptune, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 1st can make you the open, welcoming one in any room, while Neptune keeps a softer, more elusive sense of home elsewhere in the chart.

Which house the ruler stands in comes down to minutes, since the IC and the Ascendant both turn with the sky ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Had it already risen, it would be in the 12th, and home would be a retreat ([Ruler of the 4th in the 12th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)); a little further below the horizon, in the 2nd, home and money would tie together ([Ruler of the 4th in the 2nd house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)).
