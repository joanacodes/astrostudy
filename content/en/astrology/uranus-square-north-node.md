Uranus square North Node places the planet of change at right angles to the nodal axis, square to the North Node and the South Node alike. It stands across the path between where you started and where you're growing, and both ends of that path have to make room for it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables differentiation, liberation and reform: it needs room to do things its own way. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows a capacity less familiar than what came easily, built by repeated practice; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite holds the familiar strengths. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates Uranus to them through constructive friction, the kind that forces adaptation.

In daily life, the urge to differ interrupts any tidy route from old habits to new ones. Just as a direction settles, an insight or a restlessness reshapes it; just as you fall back on familiar strengths, the need for something new pulls you off them. That can feel like a string of detours. I read it differently: the square asks for a growth that includes your independence, rather than one you would have to give it up for. Development isn't destiny, and it isn't a straight line either.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 16 born between 1930 and 2009 has Uranus within 6° of a square to the North Node. My figure uses the true node; mean and true sit within a degree or two of each other, so they rarely disagree about a square, and when they do I say so ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The node, moving backwards through a sign in about 18 months, does nearly all the moving, so the square forms and dissolves within months.

A planet square the nodes usually sits in a sign of the same modality as both of them. With the North Node in Sagittarius, the South Node in Gemini and Uranus in Virgo, all mutable, an urge to improve methods sits across a path from gathering facts to finding meaning. In the houses, Uranus in the 10th square the North Node in the 7th and the South Node in the 1st could set a working life that insists on its own way across a path from self-reliance to partnership. Without a birth time there are no houses to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct North Node](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-north-node/):** independence at the growing end itself.
- **[Uranus sextile North Node](/astrology/uranus-sextile-north-node/):** experiment opening the way, with a trine to the South Node.
- **[Uranus trine North Node](/astrology/uranus-trine-north-node/):** change and growth moving together.
- **[Uranus opposite North Node](/astrology/uranus-opposite-north-node/):** independence at the familiar end, beside the South Node.

[Uranus square North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-north-node-synastry/) can feel like one person's need for freedom crossing the other's path of growth: stimulating, sometimes in the way. I read it from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-uranus/) comes twice in each 18.6-year round of the nodes and stays near the exact square for a month or two. Transiting Uranus squares your natal nodes far more rarely, over several months each time.

Born with the square, you meet it again about every nine years: at each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) and at each [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), since your Uranus lies 90° from both of your nodes. Weather, not a forecast: recurring moments to ask whether your path still leaves room for you.
