Scorpio on the 6th house cusp brings the sign of depth into the ordinary working day. Effort, for you, tends to come in cycles: full commitment, then a recovery that really restores, then full commitment again.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the shape of your ordinary days: the tasks, the habits, the colleagues and the upkeep of body and tools ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) on its cusp, a routine lasts when you know why it matters, and a habit kept for its own sake tends not to survive the month. A task you take on becomes yours entirely, and handing it over half-done can be harder than finishing it alone. You may work in long, absorbed stretches followed by rest that truly restores, rather than at an even pace. Trust among colleagues is earned slowly, and you notice who carries their share when things get hard. Looking after your body works the same way: maintenance with a purpose, and recovery taken seriously.

Tradition put the joy of Mars in this house and made Scorpio one of Mars's homes ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With this cusp, [Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules the house where tradition says it rejoices, from a sign of its own, and I read effort here as something that comes naturally to you. I read [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), Scorpio's modern ruler, alongside it, and the houses of both show where your working day leads: the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Aries, Mars's other sign, has no cusp in more than half the London charts with this one, so the 6th is often the only house Mars rules by its cusp; in the rest, Aries opens your 11th and Mars rules your allies as well as your work. Taurus, opposite, gives Venus your 12th.

Your 6th is broad at London, about 41° to 51°, broad enough that in about 3 charts in 5 Sagittarius fits inside it whole, behind Scorpio ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and Jupiter joins Mars and Pluto among the rulers of your working day. Not one London chart with this cusp lacks an intercepted pair. Scorpio's share of this cusp is about 1 London birth in 17 and 1 New York birth in 15, with Placidus cusps, the house system I use. The 6th lies just below the western horizon, and Scorpio takes only as long to set as Taurus takes to rise, about 69 minutes at London. Cancer or Gemini rises with this cusp, so the Moon or Mercury rules your chart. And of all the houses, the 6th is the likeliest to hold Scorpio whole when the sign misses every cusp, as it does in about 1 London birth in 7 and 1 New York birth in 11.

Scorpio's reputation and the 6th's old link with illness can make this cusp sound grim. It isn't a health forecast: no chart can foresee or diagnose an illness, and anything about your body belongs with a doctor ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). I read here the way you work and keep yourself going.

The Descendant is close by: an hour or so earlier, Scorpio could have been setting there ([Scorpio on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/)), and later it moves down to the 5th ([Scorpio on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/)). I don't read a chart until the recorded minute has told me which of these it is ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
