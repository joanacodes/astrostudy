Gemini on the 7th house cusp sets the sign of conversation at your Descendant, the point where the chart turns from you to the person in front of you. Partnership, here, is something two people keep talking into being.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is the frame through which you meet an equal: a partner in love or in work, a close collaborator, even the opponent you can see coming. With [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) there, that frame is a dialogue ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). You're drawn to people who notice the detail you skipped, who ask the second question, who can change their mind when the facts change and say so. Agreements tend to be talked through and then written down, because you want both sides to have understood the same thing. Even conflict takes the form of debate: an opponent who argues well earns your respect sooner than one who goes quiet. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) describes what you look for in another person and what you find easier to see in them than in yourself; with this cusp, that is often lightness and quick exchange. It says nothing of who you'll meet, or when.

Opposite, the Ascendant is always in Sagittarius ([Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/)), so Jupiter rules your chart while [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your partnerships, and each is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in the other's sign: Mercury in Sagittarius, Jupiter in Gemini. I read that as two ways of knowing that need each other, the broad view you lead with and the close attention you look for in someone else, never as a fault in either. Mercury's house is where your relationships tend to take shape: the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Its other sign, Virgo, opens the 9th in about 3 London charts in 5, so partners and far horizons often share a ruler; at New York it is on the Midheaven in more than 1 chart in 3, tying partnership to your public role.

About 1 birth in 10 has Gemini on the Descendant, at London as at New York, counting Placidus cusps, the system I use. Quick to rise in the northern hemisphere, Gemini is slow to set: it stays on the Descendant for as long as Sagittarius takes to rise on the other side, about two and a half hours at London. The 7th is wide in these charts, between about 33° and 47° at London, and in about 1 London chart in 3 the whole of Cancer lies inside it too, so the Moon has a say in your partnerships as well ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). In nearly half the London charts Gemini also opens the 6th, and work and partnership answer to the same planet ([Gemini on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-6th-house-cusp/), [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); it never opens the 8th as well, so [Gemini on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-8th-house-cusp/) describes other charts.

Your rising sign and your Descendant come as a pair, and both turn with the clock: a different hour gives a different pair, which is why I don't read a chart without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
