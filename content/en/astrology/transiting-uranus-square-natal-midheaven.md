A square to your Midheaven is a square to your IC as well, the top and the bottom of the chart pressed at once. Uranus makes it twice in its round, and both times it stands close to your horizon: near your Ascendant, or near your Descendant.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reworks structures; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where your abilities become visible as a role, and its opposite, the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), is where your roots and support lie. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates functions through friction that pushes adaptation. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the way you take part in the world and the ground you stand on both ask for an update: a role that has grown too narrow, an arrangement at home that no longer supports what you do, or the two negotiating with each other. Neither end is as fixed as it looks from inside: a reputation moves more than people think, and so does what a family expects of you.

The squares come from the horizon. In Placidus, the house system I use, one falls in your 12th or 1st house, close to your Ascendant, and the other in your 6th or 7th, close to your Descendant. Your Midheaven is rarely exactly 90° from your Ascendant, though: at London's latitude the gap runs from about 54° to 126°, so this square and Uranus's crossing of your Ascendant or Descendant can be several years apart, and only your birth time and place say how many ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Coming from near your Ascendant, the friction tends to start with your manner and how you come across ([Uranus transiting the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-transit-1st-house/)); from near your Descendant, with a partner, a client or a colleague who changes the terms ([Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/)). Which of the two squares you're under usually shows in what moves first.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the square takes about twenty months to pass, eleven in the quickest cases, nearly always in three exact passes. Where your Midheaven is close to 90° from your Ascendant, this square and Uranus's crossing of your Ascendant or Descendant overlap, and I read the two as one long season.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have been through both squares, and everyone through at least one. They come about twenty-one years before and after [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-midheaven/), halfway between it and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-midheaven/); [transiting Uranus sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-midheaven/) lies about seven years closer to the crossing, [transiting Uranus trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-midheaven/) about seven years further from it.

Until May 2033, Uranus in Gemini squares Pisces Midheavens from near the Ascendant and Virgo ones from near the Descendant; from 2032 to 2039, in Cancer, Aries and Libra Midheavens take their turn.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name which end is pressing. Some of this friction starts at work and some at home; treating them as one problem makes each harder to solve.

Adjust the role before you abandon it. A Uranus square usually asks for different terms more than for a different life, and a role renegotiated keeps what you've already built.

Use the quieter months between the exact passes to think the change through. The three hits come in waves, and the lulls are part of the timetable.
