Venus is at home in Taurus, and a retrograde there would be Venus going back over its own ground. In the dates I use, from 2000 to 2040, it never happens: Venus turns back on either side, in Aries and in Gemini, and leaves Taurus alone.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) is fixed earth, ruled by Venus, the sign of keeping: what lasts, what’s maintained, what grows by steady additions. If [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) came back here, the planet would seem from Earth to stop and back through its own sign for about six weeks, and the review would be about holding on: preferences settled long ago, the things and habits you look after, the bonds you’ve kept steady, and whether each is still kept for love or only from habit.

Between 2000 and 2040 it doesn’t. Venus’s retrogrades keep to five places round the zodiac, each revisited after eight years and each a couple of degrees lower at every return. The spring series turns in Aries and slides into Pisces; its highest station in my list is 17°43′ Aries, in 2001, and it moves further from Taurus each time ([Venus retrograde in Aries](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-aries/)). The May series stays in Gemini, and that one is coming the other way: it turned direct at 9°37′ Gemini in June 2004 and will turn direct at 1°01′ Gemini in June 2036, about a degree short of Taurus ([Venus retrograde in Gemini](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-gemini/)).

If your birth chart shows Venus retrograde in Taurus, you were born outside the forty years my list covers, and I read the placement as a rhythm of your own ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Taurus opens in your chart depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): the houses are drawn from it, and a chart without the time is one I don’t read. In Placidus, the house system I use, this is where a Venus retrograde in Taurus would slow things.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/taurus-rising/):** Taurus rising: your steady, unhurried presence; a style you’ve kept for years, checked to see whether it still suits you.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/taurus-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** Taurus on its natural house: what you own and look after, valued again for what it means to you rather than what it’s worth.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/taurus-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** a calm, considered way of talking; the kind word you kept back, said at last.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home built to last; the rooms, objects and routines that make it yours, sorted with care.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/taurus-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasures that deepen with time; a hobby or a romance you took slowly, picked up again at the same pace.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/):** steady working habits; a routine you enjoy keeping, asked whether it still gives you something back.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Taurus on the Descendant, with Scorpio rising: a partner who stays; loyalty, and what each of you puts in, talked over without ruling on the bond.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/taurus-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what you hold jointly; an old understanding about who keeps what, gone over gently.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/taurus-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs held steadily; a place you love going back to, revisited in thought.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a reputation built slowly; work you’ve stood by for years, valued again.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/taurus-on-11th-house-cusp/):** faithful old friends; the friendships you’ve kept up, and what each still asks of you.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/taurus-on-12th-house-cusp/):** attachments kept out of sight; something you hold onto in private, looked at calmly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Venus rules Taurus, so the house of your natal Venus shows where your sense of value already lives; read it beside the Taurus house above. And when Venus does turn back elsewhere, in the March and May series, look at which of your houses those weeks cross instead.

Until a retrograde returns to this sign, its lesson stays a quiet one: what you keep deserves a second look now and then, not because it was wrong to keep it, but because you’ve changed.
