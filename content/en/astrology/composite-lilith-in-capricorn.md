They don't talk about their plans at parties, and their families have long since stopped asking. Composite Lilith in Capricorn is the ambition behind that silence: long plans the relationship keeps to itself, and a patient refusal to live by anyone else's timetable.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

I read [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) as what a chart hides or won't let anyone tame; [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), built from the midpoints of two natal charts, describes the relationship as a whole, so its Lilith is the bond's own. In [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), cardinal earth ruled by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), that part is serious and long-sighted. A relationship with it tends to be building something, a business, a home, a body of work, a way of life, and to say little about it until it stands. It has its own authority, and it doesn't hand it over: well-meant questions about when you'll marry, buy, settle or progress meet a polite and immovable silence.

The ambition doesn't have to be either person's. Free spirits can find themselves in a bond that turns out methodical, even a little stern with itself, each surprised to catch the other keeping lists. How much it wants, and how hard it works, stay out of sight. Its pace stays its own: a plan that runs on its own calendar, not the one families and friends keep for couples.

Its numbers are plain. Mean Lilith moves forward at a steady pace and never turns back, so the composite version spreads almost evenly round the zodiac: in two million pairs of birth dates from 1930 to 2009, Capricorn came up for about 1 pair in 12, like every other sign. Composite Chiron behaves quite differently, crowding into the signs where Chiron moves slowest ([composite Chiron in Aries](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-aries/) is about 1 pair in 7). Any two people born within one Capricorn stay of mean Lilith have it: August 1973 to May 1974, June 1982 to March 1983, April 1991 to January 1992, March to November 2000.

Where one person with [Lilith in Capricorn](/astrology/lilith-in-capricorn/) learned to make an authority of their own look modest, a pair learns it together, and each may mistake the bond's reserve for the other's coldness, or its drive for the other's. The neighbours keep something else back: a restless search for meaning in [composite Lilith in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-sagittarius/), a need for room inside the bond in [composite Lilith in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-aquarius/).

The hard part is a relationship run like a firm. Duty can crowd out pleasure, competence can hide tiredness or doubt, and progress can become the only proof that the bond is well. What helps is to say the plan aloud to each other, often, so that neither carries it alone, and to keep some time that has no purpose at all.

Composites aren't something I draw: this is the technique as its practitioners read it, always with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it foretells no success, purchase or wedding date. Its quiet ambitions belong to two people, which is why a composite should only be looked at when both of them agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
