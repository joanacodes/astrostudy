The sign of the summit sits at the summit when Capricorn is on your 10th house cusp. Your Midheaven is in the 10th house's own sign, and the working life it describes tends to be climbed the way mountains are: in stages, with the gear checked.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In Placidus, the house system I use, the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) opens your [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/): what you're known for, the position you grow into, the standing you build ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) there tends to build a career storey by storey: the apprenticeship, the promotions taken in order, the title that comes late and stays. People hand you responsibility because you carry it quietly and well, and in many fields you end up as the one others check things with. Recognition matters less as applause than as proof that the work stands, and setbacks tend to be taken as information rather than verdicts: you adjust the route and keep climbing. When you're young, the public face can look older than its years; with time it eases, as if the role finally fitted. The cost is the order of priorities, work first and life after, unless you deliberately put the rest of you on the timetable.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is twice at home in this house: Capricorn is its sign, and the 10th is the house Capricorn holds in the natural order of the signs. Its house and sign therefore carry extra weight for your working life, and show where your direction gets decided: the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). I trust what Saturn shows more than most placements: it was one of the three in my notebook that held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)).

Aries is on the Ascendant in about 2 of these London charts in 5, and in more than half at New York ([Aries rising](/astrology/aries-rising/)). Mars, your chart ruler then, has its exaltation in Capricorn, so personal drive and public ambition pull the same way. Otherwise Taurus usually rises, and Venus rules a steadier, more patient chart, one that climbs at its own pace and enjoys the view on the way. With Cancer always at the IC beneath ([Cancer on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/)), both ends of the chart's vertical axis sit in their home signs: the Moon keeps the private base, Saturn the public summit. The Midheaven reaches Capricorn once a day, for a little over two hours, wherever you are, which is why London and New York agree on about 1 birth in 11. With only 18° to 24° between the Midheaven and the 11th cusp at London, Capricorn often carries on to a neighbouring cusp, tying what you know or whom you work with to what you do ([Capricorn on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-9th-house-cusp/); [Capricorn on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-11th-house-cusp/)).

Born a little earlier, you'd have Capricorn on the 11th; a little later, on the 9th, with the Midheaven in Sagittarius or Aquarius instead. An hour's rounding is enough to confuse these charts, so I begin with the minute ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
