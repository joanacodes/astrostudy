Uranus sextile Chiron came in two short runs in the count, from late 1938 to the summer of 1941 and from late 1999 to late 2001. Two small groups of births share it: an opening between the urge to do things differently and the skill of adapting around a tender place.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, experiment and autonomy: it tries what hasn't been tried and frees what has grown too tight. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a place that stays tender, a limit you keep meeting, and the skill that grows from adapting to it, which you can often pass on. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the two through opportunity and cooperation, a link that switches on when it's engaged.

In plain words, experiment is available to the tender place. A different method, an unusual tool, a change of setting can make an old limit easier to live with, and what you work out along the way can become an original contribution, something you show other people who meet the same limit. The sextile doesn't make this happen. It makes it possible, and the first step is usually a small trial rather than a grand plan.

Each group has its own signs. In the late 1930s Uranus was in Taurus and Chiron mostly in Cancer, earth and water: adaptations that tend to be practical and protective. Around 2000 Uranus was in Aquarius and Chiron in Sagittarius, air and fire: adaptations that tend to come through ideas, groups and wider horizons.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 23 born between 1930 and 2009 has this sextile within 6°: in November 1938, from August 1939 to October 1940, from January to August 1941, from November 1999 to May 2000, and from September 2000 to November 2001. No other month of the count gives it.

In those years Chiron was the faster of the two, crossing a sign in three or four years against Uranus's seven, which is why each run was short. The houses make it yours: Uranus in the 9th sextile Chiron in the 11th, for example, could let an unusual path of study or belief open a way into groups where you once felt on the outside. Contacts with your Sun, Moon or angles count too, and the houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Chiron](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-chiron/):** no birth of 1930 to 2009 has it.
- **[Uranus square Chiron](/astrology/uranus-square-chiron/):** born 1942 to 1944 or 1996 to 1998; change pressing on the tender place.
- **[Uranus trine Chiron](/astrology/uranus-trine-chiron/):** born in 1945 and 1946, or 1994 to 1996; inventive adaptation that comes easily.
- **[Uranus opposite Chiron](/astrology/uranus-opposite-chiron/):** born on and off from 1951 to 1990; freedom and the tender place face to face.

[Uranus sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-chiron-synastry/) lets one person's taste for experiment offer the other a new approach to an old sore spot, if it's welcome. Tender places deserve care, so I read it slowly and in both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-uranus/) depends on Chiron's uneven pace and can last from a few months to a couple of years. The reverse, transiting Uranus sextile natal Chiron, comes twice in Uranus's 84-year round, for months at a time.

For people born with the sextile, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), around 42, also brings transiting Uranus into a trine with natal Chiron: the midlife wish to live differently arrives with an easy line to the tender place. Weather, not a forecast: an invitation to experiment, not a promise of what the experiment will show.
