One of you is learning, slowly and with effort, to do something new; the other keeps arriving with a beautiful reason to do something else. That is the shape of Neptune square North Node in synastry: one person's Neptune stands across the other's nodal axis, at right angles to both ends.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) longs, imagines and sympathises; it is drawn to causes and ideals bigger than the task in hand. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a capacity built by repetition and feedback, awkward before it becomes natural; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), its other end, holds the established skills. A [square](/astrology/square/) works by constructive friction; and since the nodes face each other, whatever squares one squares both, so Neptune here crosses the whole road. As in all [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the crossing has a person at each side.

The friction can produce a practice with imagination in it. A node person who learns to keep their direction under the pull of something lovely ends up with a sturdier path; a Neptune person who learns to aim their vision at someone's actual steps ends up with dreams that help.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Carrying the North Node, you'll find this person's dreams keep offering side roads: a cause to join, a fantasy of a different life, a softer option just when the practice gets dull. Some days the detours feed you; others they blur which way you meant to go, and the old habits at the South Node, also squared, start to look like the easier escape.

From the Neptune side, the other's careful practice can look dry, even joyless, and your visions seem to bounce off it. You offer inspiration and get "not now". It's easy to feel that your imagination is unwelcome, when it's only arriving at a right angle.

A square is friction, never a verdict on the pair. Both people are right about something: the dream matters, and so does the discipline.

The pair's remaining aspects:

- **[Neptune conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the dream lands on the road ahead instead of crossing it.
- **[Neptune sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** a smaller, friendlier offer running alongside the road.
- **[Neptune trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-north-node-synastry/):** imagination and growth pulling the same way.
- **[Neptune opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** the dream rests on the old ground and faces the new.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give the dream and the discipline their own hours. If the Neptune is yours, ask "does this help where you're going?" before you share the next vision, and offer imagination for the practice rather than instead of it. If the North Node is yours, take one idea from the other's dreaming and build it into your routine; it will feel less like a detour. A visionary colleague and a methodical one can work this way; so can a couple, or adult siblings with different callings.

Someone born in mid-1945 has it both ways with people born from September to November 1945: each one's Neptune, early in Libra, squares the other's nodes, then across Cancer and Capricorn. Their Neptune also squares the nodes of people born from March to August 1964, nineteen years younger. Because whole seasons of births share it, a synastry reading of mine never lists it among the contacts, which must involve the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven; in the houses, I read it as the ideals of one time crossing the growth of another.

The hour doesn't decide the square, both points being slow; the houses do need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node here is the true one; I compare it with the mean node too, and the gap between them never exceeds a degree or two ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

When one chart holds [Neptune square North Node](/astrology/neptune-square-north-node/), the person's own imagination cuts across their path. Between two, the cut comes from someone else, and it's worth reading only if both people want to ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
