About four and a half years after the North Node crosses your Uranus, and again four and a half years after the South Node does, the axis of the nodes lies across your Uranus at a right angle. For about a month, the part of you that needs room to be different stands in the way of both the old routine and the new plan.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) describes where you need autonomy: to question what you were handed, to reform it, to try another arrangement. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the capacity you are building; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, the one you built long ago. A [square](/astrology/square/) works through friction that forces an adjustment, and since the two nodes are exactly opposite, this [transit](/astrology/transits/) squares your Uranus from both ends at once.

Lived, it can feel like restlessness with nowhere obvious to go. The habit that used to suit you starts to feel like a cage, and the direction you have been practising turns out to leave you too little say. Uranus's blind spots sharpen under a square: taking distance for objectivity, or reading other people's slower pace as resistance. Nothing here foretells a sudden break, in a job, a home or a relationship. What the friction tends to produce is a renegotiation: more room inside the plan, on terms you set.

The generations meet it together. From July 2026 to March 2028, with the North Node in Aquarius and the South Node in Leo, both ends square the Uranus of everyone born with [Uranus in Scorpio](/astrology/uranus-in-scorpio/), from November 1974 to November 1981, each within weeks of the others born near them. The person meets it in the houses: those of your Uranus and of the axis across it, which need your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The true node usually takes about a month over the square, counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after. Should an eclipse season fall in those weeks, the node lingers while the Sun passes it and the square runs to three months or more; about one pass in 25 makes three exact hits. Neither node touches your Uranus here, so my forecast, which counts the node only head on, leaves the square out, just as my eclipse rule leaves out the eclipses that fall a quarter of the zodiac away.

The squares come twice a round, nine years and a few months apart, eight or nine of them by 80. Some meet the first square almost at once: at three and a half months for a birth on 1 January 1965, against 6.5 for 1 January 1975. One falls between [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-uranus/) and [Transiting North Node opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-uranus/), the other on the way back, each between [Transiting North Node sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-uranus/) and [Transiting North Node trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-uranus/). [Uranus square North Node](/astrology/uranus-square-north-node/) in a birth chart holds the same right angle for life.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the constraint. Write down exactly where you feel hemmed in, and what a little more room there would look like.

Change the terms before the whole arrangement: hours, roles, the order of things. Reform tends to hold better than a clean break.

Allow other people their pace. What you are ready to change this month may take them a season.
