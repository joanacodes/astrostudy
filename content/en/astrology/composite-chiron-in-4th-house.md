Under every relationship there is a floor: a home, two families, a history of where each person comes from, the private life nobody else sees. Composite Chiron in the 4th house puts the bond's tender spot at that floor, and describes the foundations two people lay for themselves.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is the root of a chart: home, family, origins, the private self. It is angular, with Cancer as its natural sign, and it begins at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) it describes the relationship's home life and what the bond rests on, and with [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) there, the foundations are where it keeps meeting a limit. Making a home together may feel harder than it should, the two families' histories may weigh on the pair, or the relationship may never feel quite settled in any of the places it has lived.

That unsettledness belongs to the relationship. Two people who each feel at home in themselves can find that, together, they keep rearranging the furniture; two who left difficult homes behind can find that the home they make together is the first that feels like one. The adapting is slow and deliberate: rituals that are the pair's own rather than either family's, a private life protected from outside opinion, a house or a room made comfortable one decision at a time. Pairs like this are often the ones whose door is open on holidays to people with nowhere else to go.

This house shares one advantage with the 10th alone: its cusp is the same whichever method draws it. The composite IC lies opposite the composite Midheaven, the midpoint of your two Midheavens, in both. What differs is the rest. With houses built from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, a move to another latitude shifts the 5th cusp, the far edge of the 4th, so the house of home depends partly on where home is; with the midpoints of your two charts' cusps, it doesn't. Either way the IC comes from both birth times, and without them there is no 4th house to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A natal [Chiron in the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-in-4th-house/) belongs to one person's search for ground of their own; in synastry, [their Chiron in your 4th house](/astrology/chiron-in-4th-house-synastry/) brings the other's old hurt into your private life. In the composite, the ground is shared, and each of you may feel its tenderness as the other's restlessness, or the other's homesickness.

The hard part is the past setting the terms. A bond tender about its roots can repeat a family pattern it meant to leave, retreat from the world entirely, or treat the home as the measure of whether the relationship is working. It helps to decide, together and out loud, which traditions you keep and which you drop.

The houses around it frame it: [composite Chiron in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-3rd-house/) is tender in daily exchanges, [composite Chiron in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-5th-house/) in play and pleasure. I don't draw composites; the astrologers who do read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it never predicts a move, a house, a family or an ending. Like any chart of two people, it is for two adults who both know it is being read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
