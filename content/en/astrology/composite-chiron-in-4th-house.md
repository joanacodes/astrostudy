Under every relationship there is a floor: a home, two families, a history of where each person comes from, the private life nobody else sees. With composite Chiron in the 4th house, that floor creaks, and the two people living on it learn to lay foundations of their own.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is the root of a chart: home, family, origins, the private self. Angular, and naturally Cancer's, it opens at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart. For a relationship it is the home life and what the bond rests on, so when [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) puts [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) here, it is the foundations that keep catching. Making a home together may feel harder than it should, the two families' histories may weigh on the pair, or the relationship may never feel quite settled in any of the places it has lived.

Moving in together tends to show it: two people who each knew how to make a room their own can find that a shared one never quite settles, while two who left difficult homes behind may find the one they make together is the first that feels like home. Foundations get laid on purpose: rituals that are the pair's own rather than either family's, a private life protected from outside opinion, a house or a room made comfortable one decision at a time. Friends going through a move or a family rift often end up on their sofa, and leave steadier.

This house shares one advantage with the 10th alone: its cusp is the same whichever method draws it. The composite IC lies opposite the composite Midheaven, the midpoint of your two Midheavens, in both. What differs is the rest. With houses built from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, a move to another latitude shifts the 5th cusp, the far edge of the 4th, so the house of home depends partly on where home is; with the midpoints of your two charts' cusps, it doesn't. Either way the IC comes from both birth times, and without them there is no 4th house to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Where a natal [Chiron in the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-in-4th-house/) belongs to one person's search for ground of their own, and [their Chiron in your 4th house](/astrology/chiron-in-4th-house-synastry/) brings the other's old hurt into your private life, the composite's 4th is ground the two of you stand on together, restlessness and homesickness included.

Where it goes wrong, the past sets the terms: a family pattern repeated that the pair meant to leave, a retreat from the world, the home treated as the measure of whether the relationship is working. Decide together, out loud, which traditions you keep and which you drop.

The houses around it frame it: [composite Chiron in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-3rd-house/) is tender in daily exchanges, [composite Chiron in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-5th-house/) in play and pleasure. I leave composite charts to other astrologers; those who draw them read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it never predicts a move, a house, a family or an ending. And a chart of two people's private life belongs to both of them: it is read only between adults, and only when both know ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
