Of all the houses Jupiter crosses, the 9th is the one where it's most at home: the far horizon of your chart, where study, travel and belief live. For a little over a year the questions get bigger, less about what you know than about what it all means.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens understanding, gathers experience into meaning and looks for principles to live by. The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is the search for meaning itself: long journeys, other languages and cultures, higher study, teaching, publishing, philosophy and faith. In the natural order it's Jupiter's own house, since Sagittarius, the sign Jupiter rules, belongs to it, so the planet's function and the house's area point the same way. I read the stay as a year when your world can get larger, in miles or in thought: a course of study, a long trip, a book you write or one that rewrites you.

Being at home has its own risk. Jupiter likes answers, and the 9th is where answers harden into certainties if nobody asks the next question. A belief isn't a dogma, and the growth here comes from keeping your convictions open to the world you're exploring.

Jupiter's first meetings in the 9th are with whatever you were born with there. A natal Sun, in the house where tradition gave it its joy and which it called the God, can make the pass feel like a sense of purpose finding its words.

Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), this one adds nothing your chart didn't hold: it brings your own appetite for meaning forward for a while.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Expect a little more than a year: that's the usual stay in the 9th for a birth at London's latitude, in Placidus, with most between about 11 and 22 months, and New York's figures are identical. It closes at an angle. The day Jupiter reaches your Midheaven, the stay is over and the 10th begins, and since Jupiter takes about twelve years to go round the zodiac, the last time it crossed your 9th was about twelve years earlier.

Your Midheaven's degree, like every cusp, comes from your birth time, so I don't start a reading until I have it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows a year in your 8th, the house of what you share ([Jupiter transiting the 8th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-8th-house/)), and hands on to a year of public life in your 10th ([Jupiter transiting the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-10th-house/)).

Jupiter's own sign, Sagittarius, the 9th's in the natural order, has it from October 2030 to November 2031; a Sagittarius cusp on your 9th puts Jupiter in its own sign and its own house at once. Before that come Leo (June 2026 to July 2027), Virgo (to August 2028), Libra (to September 2029) and Scorpio (to October 2030). After it come Capricorn (to November 2032), Aquarius (April 2032 to December 2033, with a return to Capricorn in between), Pisces (April 2033 to April 2034), Aries (to April 2035), Taurus (to May 2036), Gemini (to May 2037) and Cancer (to June 2038). For the exact month, look at the degree.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Study something properly, past the introduction. Jupiter's year in the 9th is long enough for a real course of it, and understanding is the growth this house knows best.

Go somewhere unfamiliar, on a map or on a page. Other places and other minds are how the 9th widens you, and you don't need a passport for most of them.

Teach what you know, or write it down: this house grows by passing understanding on. And choose one belief you hold firmly to examine, kindly. A conviction that survives a good question is worth more at the end of the year than at the start.
