When Pluto reaches your Venus, what you value and what draws you are changed from the inside. For two to three years the planet of depth sits on the degree of your tastes and affections, in a transit that more than half of people never live at all.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms by going to the root. Your [Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs value: what attracts you, what you prefer, what you consider a fair exchange. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two work as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the act of valuing itself deepens. A taste you have had for years can fall away and a new one take hold with surprising force; you may want fewer things, and want them more. I read it more widely than a love story: what you find beautiful, whom and what you are drawn to, what you would give your time for.

An attraction's intensity isn't proof that it is right for you. Pluto turns the volume up, and a strong pull is worth listening to and worth questioning. Nor is intensity a reason to accept control: jealousy, possessiveness and pressure are not the price of a deep bond, under this transit or any other. No transit forecasts falling in love, a break-up or money, and I don't read this one for any of them ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

If [Venus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/venus-conjunct-pluto/) is in your birth chart, what this transit describes is already part of how you love; the transit itself came early, or will not come.

With Venus in the 5th, the conjunction falls during Pluto's long stay in the house of pleasure, play and creative work ([Pluto transiting the 5th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-5th-house/)), and what you make, or do for the love of it, changes most. The house comes from your birth time, like everything that places Venus in your life ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, the window I use, Pluto stays on your Venus for about two years and eight months: about twenty-two months in the quickest passes, nearly four years in the slowest. Most passes cross the exact degree three times; about two in five cross it five times.

On its way to your Venus, Pluto passes a trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-venus/)), a square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-venus/)) and a sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-venus/)) first, while the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-venus/)) lies half a circle away. A lifetime covers too little of that circle for most people to arrive: more than half of those born between 1930 and 2009 never meet the conjunction by 80.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it reaches Venuses in Aquarius. Venus is never more than about 47° from the Sun, so these belong to birthdays between about early December and early April.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give new attractions time before you act on them. Pluto crosses this degree more than once, and what pulls hardest at the first pass may look different by the last.

When something draws you strongly, ask what it gives you: depth, truth, escape, a feeling of being alive. The answer says more about this transit than the object does.

Keep the friendships that predate it. They are the people who knew your taste before, and they can tell you which changes look like you.
