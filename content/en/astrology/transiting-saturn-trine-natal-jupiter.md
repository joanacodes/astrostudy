For a while the planet that widens and the planet that holds work in step, and plans that once seemed too large start to look buildable. It happens twice in each 29½-year round, when Saturn trines the degree your Jupiter held at birth.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is where you grow: learning, meaning, principles, the sense of a wider life. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is where you build: time, method, responsibility. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates them through facilitative flow, each keeping its identity: Jupiter still wants more, Saturn still wants it sound, and for once they agree on how.

It often shows as knowledge settling into competence. What you studied becomes something you can teach; a belief becomes a practice others can rely on; a long-range plan acquires a realistic timetable. Tradition gives the two planets neighbouring homes, Sagittarius beside Capricorn and Pisces beside Aquarius, and under this trine the neighbours get on.

Wisdom isn't certainty, though, and the trine's comfort can make a view feel settled before it has been tested. Keep asking the question that started it.

With your Jupiter in the 11th, Saturn trines it from your 3rd ([Saturn transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-3rd-house/)) or your 7th, so a group's shared plan or a friend's project gets the patient study, the letters and the steady communication it needs. Jupiter in the 11th and Saturn in the 3rd are statements only a birth time can make, and without one I read no transit ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In a birth chart, [Jupiter trine Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-trine-saturn/) describes someone who grows by building; as a transit, the same cooperation comes to any chart for some months.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

This trine tends to last about nine months with my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, time enough to put the first stage of a long plan in place. In a little over half of Saturn's [transits](/astrology/transits/), its yearly loop backwards ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)) makes three exact passes of it, spread over about nine and a half months; a fast single crossing can be done in three weeks.

Its first visit follows your birth year, through the angle between Jupiter and Saturn when you were born. For someone born on 1 January 1935, 1955, 1975 or 1995, a few years before a Jupiter–Saturn conjunction, it came within the first year or so; for 1 January 1945, 1965, 1985 or 2005, a few years after one, at about 15 to 17. Taken over every birth from 1930 to 2009, the first trine could come in the first weeks of life or as late as about 20½, and half the births had met it by about 7; by 80, everyone has met five or six.

The first trine of a round follows [transiting Saturn square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-jupiter/), about ten years after [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) and five before [transiting Saturn opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-jupiter/). The second comes about ten years before the next conjunction, with [transiting Saturn sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-jupiter/) nearer to it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Turn what you know into something others can use: a course, a guide, a method written down.

Give the long plan its timetable. Under this trine, a five-year plan feels less like a fantasy and more like a schedule.

Keep one question open. A trine can settle a belief too comfortably, and curiosity is what keeps Jupiter honest.
