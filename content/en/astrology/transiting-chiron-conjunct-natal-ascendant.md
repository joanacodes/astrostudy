The Ascendant is the degree that was rising at the minute you were born, so this transit exists only in a chart with a recorded birth time. When Chiron reaches that degree, an old sore spot about being yourself in front of others arrives at the threshold, the place where every meeting starts.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is orientation: how you position yourself at the start of contact, before you have had time to think. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts around a sore spot that stays. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes two functions one process, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the way you arrive and the way you protect an old hurt become hard to tell apart, and for that reason easier to see.

Most people have a reflex at the door: the joke made before anyone else can make one, the apology for taking up room, the armour of competence, the quick scan of a room for whoever might disapprove. Under this crossing such reflexes tend to show themselves. The Ascendant's blind spot explains why they deserve a second look: it mistakes a mismatch with a setting for something wrong with you, and many of the sore spots gathered at the threshold come from rooms that didn't fit rather than from any flaw.

The Ascendant is sometimes read as the body. I read it as orientation, and nothing here predicts anything about your health ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant opens the 1st house, so this crossing ends Chiron's years in your 12th ([Chiron transiting the 12th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-12th-house/)) and begins its stay in your 1st ([Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/)). With a loop, Chiron crosses the horizon of your chart three times, from the hidden side to the visible one, back, and over again, so what had been private tends to come into view in stages. The Descendant sits exactly opposite, which is why the people you meet one to one often see the change first, and sometimes name it before you do.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the crossing usually takes about ten months, and in nearly two passes out of three it is the triple crossing just described. A third are single, done in about three weeks, mostly in Chiron's fast years through Virgo and Libra; one in twenty, in its slowest stretches, loops twice, with five hits over about twenty-one months. With Aries or Taurus rising, the signs Chiron crosses from 2018 to 2034, expect the long kind.

Chiron's round of about 50 years brings this crossing twice by 80 for most people and once for those whose first comes late. The age depends on where Chiron sat at your birth: high in the 12th, just above the horizon, it arrives early; just inside the 1st, it needs most of a round.

A crossing of the Ascendant is only as precise as the clock time on your birth record. The rising degree moves about a degree every four minutes, and Chiron in its slow signs can take months over a degree, so a time out by ten minutes can shift this transit by months. Without a recorded time there is no rising degree, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)); [how to find your birth time](/astrology/how-to-find-your-birth-time/) lists the records worth asking for.

After the crossing, Chiron works through the lower half of your chart, making [transiting Chiron sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-ascendant/), [transiting Chiron square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Chiron trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-ascendant/) on its way to your Descendant, where [transiting Chiron opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-ascendant/) falls; the same three come again, in reverse order, across the upper half.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Watch the first thirty seconds of a meeting for a week: what you do before you have decided to do anything.

Try one new way of arriving, the greeting you would give if you weren't bracing for something.

Let someone close tell you what they notice changing. The other side of the chart often sees it first.
