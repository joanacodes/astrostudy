Neptune makes two trines to every Ascendant, one from around the 9th house and one from around the 5th, and about 1 person in 6 meets neither by 80. When one comes, the way you meet the world finds an easy gentleness for some two and a half years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is the posture you meet life in, before you have decided anything. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) lends it imagination, intuition and a feel for what people need. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets the two run in step, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) a softer, more intuitive way of arriving comes without effort: you read a situation by its mood, you put people at ease, and a creative or spiritual side shows in your manner without being pushed.

Easy isn't automatic. A trine can pass as a pleasant spell and leave nothing behind, and the Ascendant's blind spot, assuming people see you as you mean to be seen, is gentler here but still there. Spend the ease on something that needs it: a new start you have been putting off, a way of presenting your work that is closer to how the work feels.

From above the horizon, Neptune usually moves through your 9th house, and the ease runs through study, belief or travel ([Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/)); from below, through your 5th, by way of what you make and enjoy ([Neptune transiting the 5th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-5th-house/)). Both are rough positions that only the birth time settles: no birth time, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine holds for about two and a half years; even the briefest passes take some twenty-two months, most bring three exact hits and roughly a quarter bring five. Because the rising degree moves so quickly through the day, two people born a few hours apart on the same date can meet this trine years apart, or one of them not at all.

The two trines lie 120° apart. In the count for births from 1930 to 2009, most people meet one by 80, some both and about 1 in 6 neither. Born with Neptune near the bottom of the chart, around your 4th house, you meet both, with [transiting Neptune opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-ascendant/) between them. Born with it near the top, around your 10th, you are among the 1 in 6: Neptune has just left one trine and won't reach the other in time.

The trine from the 5th-house side comes between [transiting Neptune square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-ascendant/) and the opposition. The one from the 9th-house side follows the opposition and leads, by way of the square at the top of the chart, to [transiting Neptune sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-ascendant/). Until March 2039, Neptune in Aries trines [Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/) from the 9th-house side and [Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/) from the 5th, both fire signs like Aries.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take the gentler manner somewhere new: a course, a trip, a group you would usually watch from the edge.

Make something you would show. This trine supports imagination you can share, not only the kind you keep to yourself.

Notice what comes easily now. It is worth knowing what your manner looks like when it isn't working against anything.
