Mercury square Venus in synastry is a contact nobody is born with. Inside one chart, Mercury and Venus never stand more than about 75° apart, so a right angle between them takes two people: one person's way of reasoning, the other's way of valuing, meeting crosswise.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how someone works things out and says them: questions, arguments, explanations, the facts they find interesting. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how a person decides what's worth having: preference, pleasure, fairness, a sense of the beautiful. A [square](/astrology/square/) sets them at right angles, a friction that can build rather than wear, and here it runs between "is it true?" and "is it good?"

With Mercury on your side, you may find the other judges your words by their tone and their taste rather than their logic. You make a point; they hear how it was put. With your Mercury in Leo and their Venus in Scorpio, your warm, theatrical way of telling a story can strike them as too much, while their reserve about what they love can look to you like a closed door.

With Venus on your side, the other can seem to analyse what you simply like. You say you love a place; they ask why, or mention its flaws, and it can feel as if your taste were being marked like homework.

The friction buys a rare kind of feedback. The Venus person teaches the Mercury person that how something is said is part of what is said; the Mercury person teaches the Venus person to give reasons for a preference. Many good editing partnerships, and some very honest friendships, run on exactly this.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The classic exchange goes: "It's not what you said, it's how you said it." "I was only stating a fact." Praise from the Mercury person can sound like a review; displeasure from the Venus person can sound like a verdict on the argument. Neither is quite what was meant. It's friction, not failure, and most of it comes from two people answering different questions.

Turn the angle and the conversation changes:

- **[Mercury conjunct Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-venus-synastry/):** a month or so between birthdays, and words and taste fused.
- **[Mercury sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-venus-synastry/):** an easy opening, where curiosity and preference trade at a comfortable distance.
- **[Mercury trine Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-venus-synastry/):** the same two functions flowing together instead of crossing.
- **[Mercury opposite Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-venus-synastry/):** face to face, each holding the half of the conversation the other leaves.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Say which you're offering, and ask which is wanted: "Do you want my opinion, or shall I just enjoy it with you?" Separate content from delivery when you disagree. Colleagues can agree that drafts get two rounds, one for substance and one for style; a couple choosing a holiday can list reasons and wishes in separate columns; friends can simply laugh at the classic exchange when it starts.

About 1 pair of people in 9 has this square, one way round or the other, within 5.1°, and the two birthdays usually lie about three months apart, whatever the years: rarely closer than three weeks, rarely further than five months or so. It belongs to the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) that exist only between two people, like [Sun square Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-venus-synastry/), because no single chart can stretch Mercury and Venus that far apart. Neither planet moves enough in a few hours for the birth time to decide this square, but the houses depend on both times, and they're why I always ask for both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
