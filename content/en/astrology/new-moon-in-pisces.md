Each year the last new moon before the March equinox falls in Pisces: the Sun and Moon meet in the zodiac's final sign, a few weeks before Aries starts the round again. The month it opens tends to begin softly, with rest, an image or a feeling, or something let go.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

The Sun and Moon share a degree at a [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/), and the Moon's phases start from nothing. In [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), mutable water, that start is less a launch than a soaking in: a feeling allowed to settle, an idea that arrives in a daydream, compassion for someone, yourself included, that changes what you do next. Pisces has two rulers, [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) in modern tropical astrology and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) in the tradition. Neptune's house shows where you dream and where old edges dissolve; Jupiter's, where the dream finds room to grow. I look at both.

In a reading this is a soft signal, and I keep it soft. Unless a planet or angle of yours sits within a degree of it, it's simply the month's weather in the house Pisces occupies. When it does land on one, I take it as a cue: a few days in which something already stirred by slower movements, transits and progressions, solar arcs or eclipses, comes to the surface. By itself it predicts nothing.

The most recent came on 19 March 2026, at 28°27′ Pisces, less than two days before the equinox. The next is on 8 March 2027 at 09:30 UT, at 17°35′; then 25 February 2028 at 6°21′, 15 March 2029 at 24°52′ and 4 March 2030 at 13°44′. None is an eclipse, though the [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/) of 20 February 2027, with the Sun already in Pisces, is a penumbral lunar eclipse, two weeks before the next one.

The [new moon in Aquarius](/astrology/new-moon-in-aquarius/) precedes it by a month; a month after it, the [new moon in Aries](/astrology/new-moon-in-aries/) opens the next round of the zodiac.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house? That's the one thing a date can't tell you: it takes the time of birth, and [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) says why. In the Placidus houses I use, read the house with Pisces on its cusp. If Pisces sits on two cusps in a row, the new moon's degree decides between them; if it sits on none, look for the house that holds it.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Pisces rising: a gentler start in how you meet the world, your intuition allowed to show rather than tidied away.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** money and what you'd give it away for: generosity looked at again, clearer edges round what's yours.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** words for what's hard to say: a journal begun, a poem, a talk with a sibling where you listen between the lines.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** home as a refuge: a quieter corner, family memories softened, a place to withdraw to that's truly yours.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** creativity drawn from the imagination: music, painting, film, a romance with something of a dream in it.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** work with more kindness in it: helping others, a routine that makes room for quiet, firmer limits on what you take on.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** a partnership seen with fresh tenderness: forgiving, or seeing the other person as they are rather than as you hoped.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** a deeper bond: the trust that lets you be vulnerable, and blurred lines about shared money made clearer.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a spiritual or artistic search: a retreat abroad, a philosophy that comforts rather than argues.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** a public role guided by a vision: art, care, a vocation, work that serves something beyond your name.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** friends who share your ideals: a charity, a circle of mutual support, a collective dream given practical shape.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** Pisces's own house: solitude, rest, the inner life, and a weight set down before the zodiac's new year.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Compare each position above with your chart, allowing a degree either way. If nothing of yours is there, let the month be gentle in that one house.

Then find Neptune and Jupiter, and read them together: one dissolves, the other enlarges, and a Pisces beginning needs a little of each. Rest counts as a beginning here, and so does saying what you will take on and what you won't, since clear edges help a sign that has few. And if what surfaces is heavy, a chart isn't the right tool for it: [astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/) is where I say when the right door is someone else's.
