What you believe gets tested where life goes deepest. The ruler of your 9th house sits in the 8th, among intimacy, trust and what you share with others or owe them, and your convictions come back from there changed.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is the search for meaning, carried out through study, travel, philosophy and faith, and its ruler takes that search to the house where it sits ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/), the house just before the 9th, holds closeness and trust, joint money and debts, and the passages that change a person: everything that binds you to someone else.

Your convictions are forged in depth, and rarely borrowed. They tend to grow out of what you've been through with other people, a loss, a crisis, a bond that asked everything of you, and they last because they were tested. Many people with this placement are drawn to psychology, history, research, anything that goes under the surface, and to the questions every philosophy has to face about change and endings. Study and travel can also be bound up with what's shared: the course paid for with a loan or a partner's support, the trip that's really about someone else's family. One warning: intensity isn't truth, and a belief that feels deep isn't right for that reason alone.

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance. Death isn't something I read in a chart, and neither is the length of a life or what anyone leaves behind ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). What it can describe is how you think about endings, and how what you believe helps you through them.

Counted from the 7th, the 8th is your partner’s 2nd house, their money and their values ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)). With the 9th’s ruler here, a partner’s means or convictions can open the road to study or travel, and who pays for what, and who believes what, is worth saying out loud.

With [Scorpio on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/), the two rulers I read, Mars and Pluto ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), are also Scorpio's own, and the 8th is the house the natural zodiac gives to Scorpio. Neither is a stranger here. Mars brings the nerve to look hard at what you believe ([Mars in the 8th house](/astrology/mars-in-8th-house/)); Pluto, the slow willingness to let a conviction go and grow back different. Jupiter, ruling a Sagittarius 9th from the 8th, tends to find meaning in what others have survived.

Placidus, the house system I use, draws its cusps by time: the 8th covers the middle third of each planet's way down from the Midheaven to the western horizon. So the clock decides this placement ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With a slightly later birth, the ruler would have sunk to the 7th, where the search is shared with a partner ([Ruler of the 9th in the 7th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)). With a slightly earlier one, it would still stand in the 9th, its own house, where the search answers to nobody else ([Ruler of the 9th in the 9th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)).
