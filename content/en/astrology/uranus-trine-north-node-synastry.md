With some people, trying the unfamiliar feels less like a leap than a change of air. Uranus trine North Node in synastry places one person's Uranus about 120° from the other's North Node, within 3.4°, and 60° from their South Node, so the first person's taste for change runs with the second person's direction of growth.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) brings originality, reform and the nerve to depart from what was handed down. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the complementary capacity a person develops by repeating it, with feedback, until it settles; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) keeps the capacities that settled long ago. Through a [trine](/astrology/trine/), two functions move in step without losing their identities, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one puts one person's freedom behind another's development.

For the node person, the other makes the new direction look natural. They've done unusual things and come through, and their ease is catching. The sextile to the South Node means old skills don't have to be thrown away; they come along as tools.

For the Uranus person, the other's growth is a pleasure to watch, partly because it resembles the way you live: by changing what no longer fits.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Ease can stand in for practice. A pair this comfortable with change may mistake novelty for development, enjoying fresh starts without staying long enough for any of them to become a capacity. The node person can let the Uranus person do the experimenting and simply admire it; the Uranus person can move on before the other's new skill has settled. The trine carries the growth only if someone keeps doing the slow part.

- **[Uranus conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the push lands on the path itself, faster and harder to steer.
- **[Uranus sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** the easy line runs to the familiar end, and the step forward needs choosing.
- **[Uranus square North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-north-node-synastry/):** change crosses the path, and growth has to make room for it.
- **[Uranus opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** Uranus sits on what the other already knows and rattles it.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Add repetition to the excitement. The node person can turn one inspiring change into a weekly habit; the Uranus person can stay around for the dull middle, or at least ask about it. Friends can keep at a new activity past the first thrill; colleagues can turn a fresh idea into a procedure; a couple can let one experiment become a tradition.

Someone born in mid-1955, with Uranus in Cancer, has it with anyone born from March to July 1969 or from January to June 1994, whose North Node lies 120° away. Anyone born, on and off, from September 1960 to August 1962 has Uranus in Leo trining that 1955 North Node in Sagittarius. These are cohorts, not choices. A synastry of mine is built on contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven, and this trine has none of them; through the houses, it reads as a generation at ease with change carrying the next one forward.

I take the dates from the true node and keep an eye on the mean node, never more than a degree or two away ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The hour leaves the trine alone but decides the houses, the Uranus falling in the node person's 9th house, say, among study and travel; both times come first ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The natal version, [Uranus trine North Node](/astrology/uranus-trine-north-node/), is one person whose originality already eases their own growth. Between two charts that ease is someone else's to lend, which is why I ask both people before reading it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
