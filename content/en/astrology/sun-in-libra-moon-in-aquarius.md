Fairness between two people and fairness for everyone: your Sun in Libra cares about the first, your Moon in Aquarius needs the second. Both lights are in air, a trine apart by sign, and the Moon at your birth was half lit or a little more.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Libra is cardinal air and Aquarius fixed air, four signs on: a [trine](/astrology/trine/) by sign, the easiest relation two signs can have, with a shared element besides. The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) seeks the fair arrangement between particular people; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) feels safe among friends and principles, with a little distance and room to be itself. You think readily about how people ought to treat each other, at the dinner table and in the world.

Tradition gives the Sun its fall in Libra and its detriment in Aquarius, so your two lights occupy the Sun's two hardest signs. I read that as a self and a set of needs that work through other people rather than by standing out alone, which suits air. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra. Aquarius has two rulers and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), given to it by modern astrologers, shows where your Moon has to do things its own way; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), its ruler in the older books, shows what it is willing to commit to. Saturn is also exalted in Libra, so it touches both lights. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Saturn, Chiron and Lilith were the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time, and overall about a third of what I checked held; with Saturn on both sides of this pair, I give it a careful look.

Between 7½ and 12½ days after the new moon, the Moon stood 90° to 150° from the Sun on its waxing side. The [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) holds the greater part of these births, the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) the latest few, about one in eight, and near 120° a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/) keeps purpose and need moving in step. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) separates the two phases.

Roughly one birth in 144 brings this pair. The Moon's crossings into and out of Aquarius happen every month, and on those days your page might be the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-capricorn/) or the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-pisces/) instead, and only the birth time can choose ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With it come your rising sign and houses, and so the places in your life where Uranus and Saturn, the Moon's two rulers, actually do their work.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love best where there is friendship underneath. The Libra Sun wants a real partnership, fair and attentive; the Aquarius Moon needs that partner to be a friend as well, someone who respects your independence and your opinions. You may be warm and sociable with many people and slower to show what you feel to one. Saying the feeling plainly, before you explain it, helps the person who is waiting to hear it.

## At work {#at-work}

You do well in work that makes things fairer for many people: policy, design for public use, community or professional bodies, research, any collaboration with a principle behind it. The Libra Sun negotiates; the Aquarius Moon keeps an eye on the whole system. Colleagues may find you calm under pressure and even-handed in a dispute. Workplaces that hide their rules, or dictate every step, wear on you.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two air signs can turn feelings into ideas. You may understand an emotion perfectly and never quite feel it, or discuss fairness in general while one particular person waits to hear that you care. Your Moon's need for distance and your Sun's need for partnership can also pull against each other. A feeling said in a single sentence, before any explanation, and an evening kept for one person rather than for the group, go a long way.
