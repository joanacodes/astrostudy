Partnership goes public with this ruler. The planet that governs your 7th house stands near the top of the chart, among work, standing and reputation, and the people you deal with one to one become part of how the world sees you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is the public side of a life: work, standing, reputation, what you want to build. Put the ruler of the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) there, and partners, associates, clients across the desk and the rivals you meet openly become part of that public picture ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Here a career is made in relationship. Many people with this placement do their best work one to one: advising, representing, negotiating, teaching, consulting, any job where the other person is the point. Others build a business with a partner, or find that a partner's work and their own are hard to separate. A couple is often seen as a couple, at work or in a community, and what you do together can shape how you're known. Open rivals tend to be competitors in your field, and they can sharpen you as much as any ally. The questions this raises are about credit and boundaries: whose ambition leads, and what's left of the relationship when work comes home with you.

The two houses are both angular and a square apart round the wheel ([angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/)): ambition and partnership pull at your time from different sides as often as they help each other. In Placidus, the house system I use, the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) opens the 10th, and a ruler inside it hadn't yet culminated when you were born: the planet of your setting degree was climbing to its highest point. I read that as partnership on display, with the visibility that brings.

The planet sets the tone. When Aries rises, Venus rules the 7th, and in the 10th it can make diplomacy a profession; Saturn, when Capricorn sets, gives business partnerships that are slow to form and built to last; Mercury, ruling a Virgo 7th for Pisces rising, suits contracts, editing and any work done line by line with someone. With [Scorpio on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/), Mars and Pluto rule your partnerships together, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 10th makes you a formidable colleague or competitor, while Pluto's house shows how much power you invest in your closest bonds.

Shift the birth time forward and the ruler crosses the top of the chart into the 9th, where partners come from further off ([Ruler of the 7th in the 9th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)); shift it back and it sits in the 11th, where they arrive through friends ([Ruler of the 7th in the 11th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)). The Midheaven turns with every minute, and a time rounded to the hour can carry this ruler across a cusp ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Whether you'll marry a colleague, or how a business partnership will fare, isn't in the chart. What it shows is where partnership lives in your life, and here it lives in plain sight.
