Venus in Capricorn measures affection in years. The planet of what you value sits here in cardinal earth, and what it values is what has been built and proved: trust earned slowly, work done well, a bond that has weathered something. I read it as love that commits, often quietly, and keeps its word.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) judges value and the way you relate. [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), cardinal earth ruled by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), gives it a patient, structured, serious manner. You tend to value what lasts: the coat that ages well, the friendship that has survived a move and a decade, the craft learned over years. You're often the one who turns *we should* into a plan with dates.

Capricorn gives Venus no dignity, good or bad, so much depends on the ruler, and the ruler here is Saturn, the planet of time and structure. Saturn's sign and house show where this Venus learned to be careful with what it values, and where its loyalty gets built.

Expect about 24 days for an ordinary pass through Capricorn and up to 62 when Venus is retrograde there; about 1 person in 13 born from 1930 to 2009 has it. Saturn rules Capricorn and, in the tradition, Aquarius as well, so [Venus in Aquarius](/astrology/venus-in-aquarius/), one sign on, shares this Venus's ruler in the old scheme. [Venus in Sagittarius](/astrology/venus-in-sagittarius/), one sign back, answers to Jupiter instead, and holds things more loosely.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, a Capricorn Venus shows affection through reliability: turning up, keeping promises, making plans that include you a year from now. You may be slow to open and slower to say it, but once you commit, you stay loyal for a long time. Capable, grounded people who take their own lives seriously attract you, and flakiness puts you off. Reciprocity means respect: being taken seriously, having your efforts noticed, a partner who builds alongside you. The risk is that love starts to feel like one more responsibility, when it needs room for play too.

## At work {#at-work}

This Venus respects competence, structure and a reputation earned honestly. You often have an eye for quality that lasts: classic design, sound materials, a well-run project. Management, architecture, craft, public institutions, anything with a long horizon can suit it. You may find pleasure in work more readily than in leisure, and a colleague's respect can mean more to you than praise. What wears this Venus down is chaos, and commitments that others break.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Affection postponed until it feels earned. You may wait until you've proved yourself before you let yourself be loved, or work instead of resting with someone. Feelings can be kept behind a sense of duty, and a partner may read your reserve as coolness when it is really caution. And because you take commitment so seriously, you may stay in something that has become nothing but a duty.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put pleasure in the diary, literally, and keep the appointment. Let someone love you before you've finished becoming who you think you ought to be. Say one warm thing out loud each day, even when it feels unnecessary. And look at what you have already built with the people you love: the years themselves are evidence.

Here the notebook has something particular to add. Venus came out at about a third ([my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)), but this Venus answers to Saturn, and Saturn belongs to [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) 75 to 90% of the time. That is why, with a Capricorn Venus, I read Saturn closely alongside it, by sign, house and aspect, before I say much about how you love.
