Across from your Moon, Neptune works through the people you're close to. Over some two and a half years, someone you love, someone who needs you, or a mood in the air around you can feel as near as your own, and the task is to keep both in view.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) opens: compassion, empathy, a willingness to meet others more than halfway. Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) keeps you emotionally steady, and attachment isn't dependency. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, pairs two functions across the circle, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) tends to work from across the room: a partner's longing, a friend's need or a family member's mood arrives in your own feelings, and it can be hard to tell what you feel from what you've absorbed. None of this is a weakness: an opposition shows each side by contrast, and you may see your own needs more clearly for having met someone else's.

Neptune's blind spot here is confusing empathy with knowing: feeling sure of what someone needs without asking. Asking keeps the opposition honest, and so does saying what you need in return. If carrying other people's feelings starts to weigh on you for weeks, a doctor or a counsellor is the right person to tell ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

Take a Moon in the 2nd, with Neptune crossing your 8th ([Neptune transiting the 8th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-8th-house/)): what makes you feel secure meets what you share with someone close. With the Moon in the 11th and Neptune in your 5th, the hopes you share with friends and the pleasures you'd choose alone pull two ways. The houses need your birth time, and I won't read the chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition is in force for about two and a half years, sometimes less than two when the loops are tight; Neptune crosses the exact point three times in an ordinary pass and five times in roughly a quarter of them.

Nearly every person born between 1930 and 2009 meets, by 80, either Neptune's conjunction with their Moon ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-moon/)) or this opposition, half one and half the other, and nobody meets both: the two points are 180° apart, and Neptune covers a little less than that in 80 years. Between the two lie the trines ([transiting Neptune trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-moon/)), a sixth of the circle from this opposition, the squares ([transiting Neptune square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-moon/)), a quarter, and the sextiles ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-moon/)), a third. People born with [Moon opposite Neptune](/astrology/moon-opposite-neptune/) know this pull from the inside; the transit brings it, for a while, to anyone it reaches.

Across from Aries lies Libra, so until 2039 the opposition falls on Moons in Libra.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask before you assume. Under this opposition, a question about what someone needs is kinder than a guess, however loving.

Say what you need in return, in a sentence. A balance needs both ends to speak.

Keep a little time each week that belongs to your own feelings, with nobody else's in the room: a walk alone, a notebook, an hour that is only yours.
