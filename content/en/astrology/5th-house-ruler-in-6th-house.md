Tradition named the 5th house Good Fortune and the 6th Bad Fortune, and here the first works for the second. In plainer words, what you love doing turns into something you do every day.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The planet ruling the sign on your [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) cusp speaks for creativity, play and romance ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Placed in the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/), it lives among tasks, habits, tools and the people you work beside, so pleasure gets turned into practice. The daily sketch, the scales before breakfast, the sourdough fed every morning, the allotment, the dog you walk whatever the weather: joy tends to arrive through repetition, and you may like the method as much as the result.

Love can come through work too, someone met over a shared project or across the office, and affection often shows as service: the tea made without asking, the bike mended, the form filled in for them. The risk runs the other way. A pleasure can feel allowed only once it's useful, and a hobby can harden into one more obligation on the list. Rest, for you, may need to go in the diary like everything else, and that's fine as long as it stays there.

With Aries or Scorpio on the 5th cusp, Mars rules the house, and [Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/) is in its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): the planet of exertion takes pleasure in the house of labour. Your pleasures lean towards training and competition, the run you time, the craft that needs strength. With Venus as the ruler, on a Taurus or Libra cusp, the workplace itself turns pleasant and ordinary tasks gain some beauty.

Joy works out of its own sight here. The 6th follows straight on from the 5th, and next-door houses have no aspect between them ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)), so you may not even call what you do fun, though it's plainly what keeps you going. Placidus adds a twist. Its houses vary in size, and the 5th and 6th cusps sometimes fall in the same sign ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); in that case a single planet governs the pair, and from the 6th it rules the second from inside.

Odd hours, odd tools and a method of your own: that's Uranus in the 6th, ruling an Aquarius 5th alongside Saturn ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). I read the pair, and Saturn, from its own place, asks for patience with the long apprenticeship.

A ruler in the 6th had dipped below the western horizon shortly before you were born. Had you arrived a little earlier, one near the 6th's far end would still have been above that horizon, in the 7th, where pleasure looks for a partner ([Ruler of the 5th in the 7th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)); a little later, and one near its start would have sunk back into the 5th, its own house ([Ruler of the 5th in the 5th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)). I don't choose between them without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Tradition put children in the 5th, and illness, servants and small animals in the 6th. If this placement shows up in your life as a dog walked twice a day, that's a fair reading; a forecast about children or about your health isn't. No chart can tell anyone whether they'll become a parent ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/), and questions about your body are for a doctor to answer ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
