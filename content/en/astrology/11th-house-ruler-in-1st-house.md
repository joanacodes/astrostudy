Some people walk into a room with their friends already in it. With the planet that rules your 11th house standing in the 1st, the people you've chosen and the future you're working towards show in your bearing, in the badge on your bag, in how quickly you say "we".

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds the company you keep by choice: allies, the teams and movements you sign up to, and the plans you make with them for later. Whichever planet rules the sign on its cusp carries that business along with it ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Here it's done in the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), the house of the body, of temperament and of first contact, where you begin things and where other people meet you before they know anything else.

So belonging becomes something people can see. You tend to pick up the look, the vocabulary and the posture of your circle, and often to stand for it: the one a group sends to speak, to make introductions, to welcome the newcomer. Your hopes show early too. Within minutes you're talking about what you're working towards, and people often join a plan because they met it through you. Friends can change you more than you'd guess, and the best of them notice when you start dressing for the group instead of for yourself. This placement asks you to keep a shape of your own inside the one you share.

Counted by whole signs, the 11th falls two signs before the rising sign, the distance of a sextile, which makes the house of friends one of the places tradition said could see the Ascendant. I read that as an easy line between the people you choose and the person you are: help tends to come through someone you know, and you know whom to ask. The 1st is also an angle, and William Lilly put it plainly: "the angles are most powerful". With the ruler of the 11th there, your social life is among the first things about you.

The planet decides the style. Gemini or Virgo on the 11th cusp hands the house to Mercury, and the 1st is where Mercury has its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): you're the one who keeps everybody in touch, good with names and introductions. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn 11th, gives you the air of the steady member of any group, the one who knows the rules and turns up. An Aquarius cusp gives the house to Saturn and Uranus together, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 1st carries the group's responsibilities in your bearing, while Uranus, from wherever it stands, adds a taste for circles that don't look like anyone else's.

The Ascendant moves quickly, so this placement hangs on a precise birth time. A ruler just under the eastern horizon would have risen into the 12th with a slightly later birth, and your friendships would keep to quieter rooms ([Ruler of the 11th in the 12th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)). Near the lower edge of the 1st, a little earlier would have left it in the 2nd, where networks turn towards income and what you value ([Ruler of the 11th in the 2nd house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)). [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, builds each cusp out of the hour and the place of birth, and without them I don't read the chart at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

It's worth telling apart from its mirror image. With your chart ruler in the 11th, you go out to live among your people ([Ruler of the 1st in the 11th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)); with the ruler of the 11th in the 1st, your people come to live in you.
