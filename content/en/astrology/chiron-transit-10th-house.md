Being seen is a gift and a risk, and most people learned one before the other. As Chiron climbs to the top of your chart and crosses your Midheaven, an old lesson about being in view comes back to be looked at.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Where [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) sits, an old sore spot meets the skill you gain from working around it. The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is your public life: your work, your role in the world, your reputation, the authority you earn or push against. Chiron's arrival here can bring back an old hurt about recognition: a failure everyone saw, a talent praised so early it became a weight, a parent's ambition you carried as your own, the fear of being found out as less capable than people think. What being seen has cost you grows clearer in these years, and so does the need for a public role that fits the person you are, sore spot included, rather than the one you built to hide it. People who live this often end up doing work that helps others in the same exposed place; [Chiron in the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-in-10th-house/) describes that calling from birth.

The stay opens with Chiron on your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), which in Placidus, the house system I use, is the cusp of the 10th ([transiting Chiron conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)); a natal [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in the 10th, reached later, is asked whose standards it has been working to.

No promotion, dismissal or change of career is foretold here. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) describes how you hold your public life for a while; what you do with your work stays your decision.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, Chiron spends a little under 4 years in the 10th in the middle, close to its figure for all twelve houses, but the long end stretches to about 11. A Midheaven in Aries or Taurus tends to open one of the wider 10th houses at that latitude, and those are two of the signs Chiron crosses most slowly: that is where the long stays come from. With the Midheaven in Sagittarius or Capricorn, the 10th tends to be narrow. New York's latitude gives the same middle and a slightly shorter tail, up to about 10.

The Midheaven goes round the whole zodiac in a day, so only your birth time can place it, and with it the start of this stay; it is the first thing I ask for ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron climbs here out of your 9th ([Chiron transiting the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-9th-house/)) and, once past the 10th, moves on to the house of friends and shared hopes ([Chiron transiting the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-11th-house/)).

If your Midheaven is in Aries, the crossing comes before April 2027, or has come already: Chiron has been in Aries since April 2018. For a Midheaven in Taurus the crossing falls between June 2026 and May 2034; in Gemini, between July 2033 and April 2039; in Cancer, from July 2038 onwards, and Chiron has not left Cancer by December 2039, where my figures stop.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Separate your worth from your title: list what you'd still be proud of if nobody knew about it.

Before showing something in public, show it to one person you trust, and let the private verdict come first.

Notice whose ambitions you have been carrying, and put down the ones that were never yours.

If you lead others, let them see you learning. It gives them permission to learn too.
