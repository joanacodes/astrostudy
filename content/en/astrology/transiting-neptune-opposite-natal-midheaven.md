The lowest point of your chart, the IC, faces your Midheaven, so a transit opposite the one sits on the other: here, Neptune on your roots. Half of people reach it once by 80, and for some two and a half years home and belonging take on a softer, more longing light.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) is the ground your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) stands on: belonging, continuity, the private support that public life draws on. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings longing, compassion and the loosening of fixed lines. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) works through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your sense of where you come from softens: family stories, a place, a language, an old house can take on a glow or lose their hold.

The IC's blind spot that Neptune touches most is equating belonging with a place. Under this crossing, belonging can loosen from an address and settle on people, a practice, a landscape or a faith. Neptune's own risk is idealising the past, so a family story can grow kinder, or sadder, than it was. I read it as a time to soften your roots without pulling them up, and it predicts no move and no family event.

Neptune reaches the IC from your 3rd, the house of everyday thought and talk ([Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/)); in Placidus the IC opens the 4th, where a long stay then begins ([Neptune transiting the 4th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-4th-house/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In my orbs, from 1½° before the IC to 1° past it, the crossing lasts some two and a half years, twenty-two months at the quickest; Neptune touches the exact degree three times in most passes and five times in about one in four.

With Neptune on your Ascendant at birth, a quarter of the zodiac before the IC, it gets there at about 41; born with it in your 3rd, within a few years; born with it just past your Midheaven, close to 80. Born with Neptune anywhere in the western half of the chart, you meet [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-midheaven/) instead, which is why half of people born from 1930 to 2009 have this crossing by 80 and half don't.

Whether a birth certificate records the time depends on the country, and sometimes on the year or the copy; without a time there is no IC to cross ([How to find your birth time](/astrology/how-to-find-your-birth-time/), [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Before the IC, Neptune's road runs through the square close to your Ascendant ([transiting Neptune square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-midheaven/)) and then the trine from around your 2nd ([transiting Neptune trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-midheaven/)), whichever still lie ahead; the sextiles ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-midheaven/)) belong to the upper half of the chart. With Neptune in Aries until March 2039, Libra Midheavens have it on their IC now ([Libra on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make home a place of rest rather than a project: one room, one ritual that asks nothing of you.

Tell the family story twice, once as you would like it and once as it was. Neptune on the IC makes the first easy and the second worth the effort.

Let belonging travel. A friendship, a practice or a place you keep returning to can root you as much as an address.
