The planet that rules your Midheaven has just gone over the top. It sits in the 9th, the house of study, travel and belief, so your public life keeps looking past its own field towards ideas, other countries and what it all means.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The planet ruling your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/)'s sign rules the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) too, since in Placidus, the house system I use, the Midheaven is where that house begins, and wherever that planet stands, your vocation, standing and reputation go with it ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Here that's the [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), the God of the old astrologers: universities and long journeys, philosophy and faith, teaching and books.

Your career comes with a philosophy. The work you're known for tends to rest on something you believe or have studied, and it often crosses borders: the lecturer, the editor, the translator, the lawyer, the cleric, the guide, the researcher with colleagues on three continents. The old horary lists put the learned professions in the 9th, with universities, long journeys and religion, so a profession that took years of study is often the obvious route, though never the only one. Even in an ordinary job you may be the one who asks what it’s all for. The risk is living on the next horizon, with a vocation always about to begin after one more qualification, one more trip, one more book. If the work takes you abroad, some astrologers recast the chart for the new country; I don’t draw relocation charts, and I read the chart of the place where you were born, wherever the work takes you ([relocation charts](/astrology/relocation-charts/)).

The 9th and the 10th are neighbours, and their rulers can trade places. If the ruler of your 9th sits in the 10th while this ruler sits in the 9th, each is in the other's house, and if each also stands in the other's sign, tradition calls it [mutual reception](/astrology/mutual-reception/). With Leo on the 9th cusp and Virgo on the Midheaven, for instance, Mercury in Leo in the 9th and the Sun in Virgo in the 10th have swapped both signs and houses ([Ruler of the 9th in the 10th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)). I read that as belief and vocation lending each other their manner.

A ruler just past the Midheaven is in the first third of its way down to the western horizon, by Placidus's measure, and the Midheaven moves about a degree every four minutes ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A slightly earlier birth keeps it in the 10th, still climbing to the top, where a public life steers itself ([Ruler of the 10th in the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)). A later one lets it sink into the 8th, where your work deals in trust and shared resources ([Ruler of the 10th in the 8th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)).

Scorpio on the Midheaven means two rulers to follow, Mars and Pluto ([Scorpio on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Mars in the 9th fights for a cause in public, the advocate or the campaigner ([Mars in the 9th house](/astrology/mars-in-9th-house/)). Pluto in the 9th turns the work into a search for what's really true, however long it takes, and has little patience with received answers.
