Two people who'd both rather do it their own way can make an excellent team, or two parallel ones. In Mars trine Lilith in synastry, a trine joins one person's Mars to the other's Black Moon Lilith, most often across one element, and the unconventional choice tends to be the one they make together without discussing it.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is a person's will in action: getting started, putting in effort, competing, standing their ground. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is the Moon's apogee, averaged into a smooth path; readings differ, and mine is the instinct for autonomy, what someone learned to keep hidden, and power that answers to no one else. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) keeps two functions in step without blurring them, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one pairs action with independence at no visible cost.

With the Mars on your side, you may feel freer to be unconventional around this person: the plan that would raise eyebrows elsewhere gets an enthusiastic yes. With Lilith on your side, your independence is met with action rather than alarm. Nobody asks you to explain why you won't do it the usual way; they help you do it yours.

Element sets the style. In earth, Mars in Taurus with Lilith in Capricorn, it's a quiet refusal to be rushed and a joint habit of building things to their own standard; in air, Mars in Libra with Lilith in Aquarius, freedom in ideas and in changing one's mind; in fire, out in the open; in water, private and guarded.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Easy agreement has a blind spot: the moment when "our own way" quietly becomes one person's. The Lilith person's style can turn into the pair's default; the Mars person's plans can carry both along. Two independent people can also forget to leave each other room, precisely because neither feels restricted.

There's an outward risk too: a pair this comfortable bending rules can stop noticing who else the rules were protecting, or forget to explain itself to the people around it. Ease doesn't make a choice wise, and the current still needs steering.

The pair's other contacts, beside the trine:

- **[Mars conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** the drive lands on the untamed point itself, with far more charge.
- **[Mars sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** a narrower opening, where someone has to take the first step.
- **[Mars square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-lilith-synastry/):** a sign nearer, and the same two meet as push and refusal.
- **[Mars opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** two signs further, each becomes the other's wild side.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep two separate noes. Each of you holds at least one thing that stays your own call, and checks now and then that the pair's way is still each person's way. It also helps to explain your choices to the people around you, so your shared independence doesn't read as dismissal. This suits co-founders, travel companions, adult siblings who share an unconventional family role.

Counting with mean Lilith, I find about 1 pair of people in 9 with this trine, one way round or the other, within 5.1°. Lilith's side moves on within months, about nine to a sign, so the Lilith person shares it closely with only a season's worth of births; Mars, crossing a sign in six or seven weeks, picks out this pair. Both bodies are slow by the hour, so the time of birth hardly changes the trine; the houses, where the shared freedom is used, need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). My notebook singles out three placements, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith, as holding far more often than the rest; it tested single charts, though, so this trine gets my attention and no number ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)).

One person with [Mars trine Lilith](/astrology/mars-trine-lilith/) in their own chart carries both halves of this alliance. Across two charts, the alliance has two members, and each keeps a vote.
