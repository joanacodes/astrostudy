Some relationships are best seen at home: the table always laid for one more, the photographs up the stairs, the old armchair nobody will let go. Composite Venus in the 4th house keeps the bond's pleasures in its private world.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) opens at the composite [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the IC, and here the two house methods agree more than usual. Taking midpoints of the natal cusps gives the midpoint of your two ICs; rebuilding the houses from the composite Midheaven gives the point opposite that Midheaven, which is the same spot. Where the 4th ends is another matter: the next cusp differs between methods, and under the second it is drawn for the latitude where you live, so a move far north or south can shift it. Both birth times are needed either way ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Angular, with Cancer as its natural sign, the 4th is home, family, roots and private life, and [Venus](/astrology/venus/) there makes them the bond's favourite ground. Its taste shows in the rooms you make together, the meals that turn into traditions, the relatives who feel welcome; its affection is felt most where nobody else is watching. Even a pair who live in different cities can have it: home, for them, may be a ritual or one place they always come back to. A natal [Venus in the 4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house/) describes how one person finds joy at home; [their Venus in your 4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house-synastry/), someone who makes your home feel sweeter; the composite's home belongs to the two of you.

Most of the time the composite Sun keeps company with this Venus, no more than 47° away, as at any birth; that holds for about 9 pairs in 10. Pairs whose birthdays are far apart are the ones that break it, and in about 1 pair in 22 the composite Sun sits near the opposite point, up by the Midheaven, where the 10th house starts ([composite Sun in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-10th-house/)). Each midpoint is taken on its own, and that alone explains it.

Home has a pull that can turn into a wall. A bond that loves its private world may keep the peace there at any price, letting a tension sit unspoken because raising it would spoil the evening, or let family obligations shape its pleasures more than its own wishes do. Home isn't confinement and family needn't mean duty, but it helps to say so out loud: let the house change as the two of you change, and keep a few pleasures that happen out of the door.

[Composite Venus in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-3rd-house/) enjoys the street outside; [composite Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-5th-house/) goes out to play. The composite is a technique I describe here, not one I use, and it is read in the company of the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and both birth charts. A private chart for a private bond: it should only be drawn with both people knowing ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
