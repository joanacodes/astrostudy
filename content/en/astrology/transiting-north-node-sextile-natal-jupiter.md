The node's sextile to Jupiter pairs two growth points at a friendly distance. For about a month the node stands 60° from your Jupiter, the South Node trine it, and an opening arrives to put something you believe in into practice, usually with other people's help.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is your sense of meaning and possibility: what you believe, what you're curious about, what you think a life can become. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is growth by practice, and a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates two functions through opportunity that has to be engaged. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), a principle and a practice can meet halfway, if you take the step.

Opportunity isn't guaranteed success, and a sextile shows the difference clearly: a course that starts this month, an invitation to teach what you know, a collaboration with someone whose outlook is wider than yours. Each is there to be taken, and none takes itself. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), trine your Jupiter, lets the convictions you already hold support the new step rather than resist it.

With [Jupiter in the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-7th-house/), the opening tends to come through partners and agreements: a joint project, a mentor, someone whose generosity widens your plans. Your 7th depends on your birth time, and so does the part of your life this sextile concerns ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Allow 1½° before exact and 1° after, and the node usually needs about a month for the sextile. Its pace is set by the Sun: brisk between eclipse seasons, with a tenth of passes done in three and a half weeks, and almost still inside them, when a tenth last three and a half months or more and about one in twenty-five touches the exact sextile three times.

Where Jupiter stood at your birth, relative to the node, decides when the first sextile came. For births from 1930 to 2009, that was anywhere from the first weeks of life to about 12½, at 4½ or so in the middle: under a year for someone born at the start of 1935, about 5 for the start of 1965, about 12 for the start of 1945. Every first sextile falls in childhood, which I don't read ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)); from then on two come in each round, eight or nine by 80.

Jupiter spends about a year in each sign, so people born within a year or so of each other share its sign, and the node sextiles their Jupiters within the same year and a half or so. The house, from the birth time, is what makes the month yours.

The round also holds [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-jupiter/), [Transiting North Node square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-jupiter/), [Transiting North Node trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-jupiter/) and [Transiting North Node opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-jupiter/). Born with [Jupiter sextile North Node](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-north-node/), you'll recognise one of these sextiles as your nodal return.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to teaching, mentoring or sharing what you know. This sextile works through exchange.

Pick one belief and find one practical way to act on it before the month is out.

Look for the person whose view is wider than yours, and ask to learn from them.
