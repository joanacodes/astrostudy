The node's sextile to Jupiter pairs two growth points at a friendly distance. For a month or so, with the node two signs from your Jupiter and the South Node trine it, an opening arrives to put something you believe in into practice, usually with other people's help.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is your sense of meaning and possibility: what you believe, what you're curious about, what you think a life can become. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is growth by practice, and a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers two functions an opening that only works if you use it. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), a principle and a practice can meet halfway, if you take the step.

Opportunity isn't guaranteed success, and a sextile shows the difference clearly: a course that starts this month, an invitation to teach what you know, a collaboration with someone whose outlook is wider than yours. Each is there to be taken, and none takes itself. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), trine your Jupiter, lets the convictions you already hold support the new step rather than resist it.

With [Jupiter in the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-7th-house/), the opening tends to come through partners and agreements: a joint project, a mentor, someone whose generosity widens your plans. Your 7th depends on your birth time, and so does the part of your life this sextile concerns ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Allow 1½° before exact and 1° after, and the node usually needs about a month for the sextile. Its pace is set by the Sun: brisk between eclipse seasons, with a tenth of passes done in three and a half weeks, and almost still inside them, when a tenth hold on for three and a half months or more, with about one in twenty-five touching the exact sextile three times.

Where Jupiter stood at your birth, relative to the node, decides when the first sextile came. Over the birth years 1930 to 2009 it came as early as a few weeks old and as late as about 12½, with 4½ or so in the middle: under a year for a start-of-1935 birth, about 5 for one at the start of 1965, about 12 for the start of 1945. No one meets the first sextile as an adult, and I don't read a child's chart ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)); from then on two come in each round, eight or nine by 80.

Jupiter spends about a year in each sign, so people born within a year or so of each other share its sign, and the node sextiles their Jupiters within the same year and a half or so. The house, from the birth time, is what makes the month yours, though you won't find that month in my forecast, which has room for the node only in conjunction or opposition.

The round also holds [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-jupiter/), [Transiting North Node square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-jupiter/), [Transiting North Node trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-jupiter/) and [Transiting North Node opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-jupiter/). Born with [Jupiter sextile North Node](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-north-node/), you'll recognise one of these sextiles as your nodal return.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to teaching, mentoring or sharing what you know. This sextile works through exchange.

Pick one belief and find one practical way to act on it before the month is out.

Look for the person whose view is wider than yours, and ask to learn from them.
