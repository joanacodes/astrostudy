Of Chiron's contacts with your Sun, the sextile is the quietest. Nothing presses, and for a few weeks or most of a year, what you learned around an old difficulty becomes easy to put to use for your own purpose.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your purpose and the self you'd like to be recognised for. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a limitation that stayed, and the skill you built by working with it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through cooperation, and cooperation needs someone to make the first move. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the openings tend to be concrete and modest. Someone asks you to explain the very thing you once struggled with. A project turns up where your experience counts more than your polish. An ambition you set aside because of the old limitation becomes possible again, by the route you've since learned to take.

The sextile also runs the other way. People who are good at helping are often slow to be helped, and this is a mild season to let someone support your own direction: a colleague who reads your draft, a friend who backs your plan, a teacher who's been where you are.

Where the opening comes from depends on your houses, so on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Sun in the 9th, Chiron sextiles it from your 11th ([Chiron transiting the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-11th-house/)): a group, a network or a circle of friends becomes the place your experience is wanted. People born with [Sun sextile Chiron](/astrology/sun-sextile-chiron/) carry this opening in their charts already; the transit is a season for using it in their own work, not only on behalf of others.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The pace is set by Chiron's lopsided orbit. Chiron was closest to the Sun in 1996 and farthest in May 2021; near the far end it crawls, near the close end it runs. Counted with my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, about one sextile in three is a single crossing over in some three weeks, the shape of Chiron's faster years. Nearly two in three take about ten and a half months, as Chiron's retrograde loop brings it over the exact degree three times; about one in twenty crosses five times, over some twenty-one months. Through the late 2020s, with Chiron slow in Taurus, the long shapes are the ones to expect.

The sextile comes round more often than its quietness suggests: most people meet three by 80, because your Sun has two sextile points, a third of the zodiac apart, and Chiron passes each once in a round of about fifty years. Your Sun meets the same sore spot more forcefully under the square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-sun/)) and more easily under the trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-sun/)), each as frequent as this sextile; the conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-sun/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-sun/)), single points, come once or twice.

At the end of 2026, Chiron sextiles the Suns of mid-February and mid-June birthdays. From Taurus, between 2027 and 2033, it reaches Pisces and Cancer Suns, a few more days of each every year: birthdays around the start of March and the start of July meet it about 2029.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to the next request for what you know, and set the terms yourself: the time, the scale, the audience.

Pick up one plan you ruled out because of the old limitation, and try it by the route you've since learned.

Let someone help with your own project. The sextile works in both directions.
