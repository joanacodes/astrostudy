The second decan of Taurus runs from 10° to 20° of the sign, the middle of fixed earth, and the Sun crosses it in the first ten days of May. Its face belongs to the Moon, the planet exalted in Taurus.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) give these degrees to the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), the planet after Mercury in the Chaldean order. The Moon is already exalted anywhere in [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), so here it holds two of the five dignities at once. In a night chart it holds a third, because the Moon rules the earth triplicity by night: a [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) between 10° and 20°, born after sunset, scores exaltation, triplicity and face, four, three and one, though the sign itself is Venus's.

By element, the modern decans give the middle of an earth sign to the ruler of the next earth sign, Virgo, and that is [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/).

So a planet in these degrees answers to [Venus](/astrology/venus/) for the sign, then to the Moon or to Mercury for the decan, depending on the list: need and comfort in one, sorting and reasoning in the other. In a reading I don't settle which list is right. I look at how the Moon and Mercury are placed in your chart, and let the better-placed one say a little more.

## Its image {#its-image}

The first two decans of Taurus seem to have traded names between the lists. Budge's Egyptian sequence runs Ārt, then Khau; the Greco-Egyptian lists run Chau, then Arat. Firmicus Maternus calls this one Aryo, or Ason, and the *Testament of Solomon* gives it its longest name, Kairoxanondalon, or Iudal.

Later lists dressed it in figures. Cosmas of Maiuma gave Taurus three in a row, Charis, the Horae and the Litai, and this decan has the middle one: the Horae, goddesses of the seasons. Athanasius Kircher, in 1652, put Helitomenos here. A season for the decan where spring turns towards summer is an apt picture, and I leave it at that ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun is here for births from about 30 April to 10 May ([Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/)). In 2026 it reached 10° Taurus on 30 April at 08:04 UT and 20° on 10 May at 15:50 UT. On either of those days the year and the hour decide between this decan and its neighbours ([Taurus, first decan](/astrology/taurus-first-decan/), [Taurus, third decan](/astrology/taurus-third-decan/)).

The Moon's own place here is a matter of hours. The Moon covers ten degrees in well under a day, so whether a Moon in Taurus sits in its own face can turn on the time of day you were born, and that is a detail I only give with the time in hand ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Slower planets stay for years. Uranus held these degrees for births from February 1938 to June 1939, with shorter stays in 1937 and from November 1939 to April 1940, and again from 2021 to 2023. Saturn passed in 1940–41, 1970–71 and 1999–2000, and comes back from July 2028. The fixed star [Menkar](/astrology/menkar/), α Ceti, the nose of the sea monster Cetus, stands at 14°41′ Taurus in 2026, near the middle of the decan.
