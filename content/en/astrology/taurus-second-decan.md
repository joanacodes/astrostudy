Halfway through Taurus the Moon, already exalted in this sign, finds its own face as well. The second decan covers 10° to 20° of the sign, and early May is its season for the Sun.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/)'s face falls where the Moon is already exalted: Lilly's table of [faces](/astrology/faces/) gives it the middle of [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), after Mercury's face, so here it holds two of the five dignities at once. In a night chart it holds a third, because the Moon rules the earth triplicity by night: a [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) between 10° and 20°, born after sunset, scores exaltation, triplicity and face, four, three and one, though the sign itself is Venus's.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), which hands the face over to the Moon here, gets the decan back in the modern list, as ruler of Virgo, the earth sign that follows Taurus.

Under [Venus](/astrology/venus/), ruler of the sign, the two lists offer different second voices: need and comfort through the Moon, sorting and reasoning through Mercury. In a reading I don't settle which list is right; the better placed of the two planets in your chart gets to say a little more.

## Its image {#its-image}

The first two decans of Taurus seem to have traded names between the lists. Budge's Egyptian sequence runs Ārt, then Khau; the Greco-Egyptian lists run Chau, then Arat. Firmicus Maternus calls this one Aryo, or Ason, and the *Testament of Solomon* gives it its longest name, Kairoxanondalon, or Iudal.

Later lists dressed it in figures. Cosmas of Maiuma gave Taurus three in a row, Charis, the Horae and the Litai, and this decan has the middle one: the Horae, goddesses of the seasons. Athanasius Kircher, in 1652, put Helitomenos here. A season for the decan where spring turns towards summer is an apt picture, and I leave it at that ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Born between about 30 April and 10 May, you have the Sun in the middle of Taurus, in the face of the Moon ([Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/)); in 2026 its stay ran from 08:04 UT on 30 April to 15:50 UT on 10 May. On either of those days the year and the hour decide between this decan and its neighbours ([Taurus, first decan](/astrology/taurus-first-decan/), [Taurus, third decan](/astrology/taurus-third-decan/)).

The Moon's own place here is a matter of hours. The Moon covers ten degrees in well under a day, so whether a Moon in Taurus sits in its own face can turn on the time of day you were born, and that is a detail I only give with the time in hand ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Slower planets stay for years. These were Uranus's degrees for births from February 1938 to June 1939, with shorter stays in 1937 and from November 1939 to April 1940, and again from 2021 to 2023; Saturn was here in 1940–41, 1970–71 and 1999–2000, and comes back from July 2028. The fixed star [Menkar](/astrology/menkar/), α Ceti, the nose of the sea monster Cetus, stands at 14°41′ Taurus in 2026, near the middle of the decan.
