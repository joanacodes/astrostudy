Some changes of manner happen without a fight, and this is the transit for them. About fourteen years before Uranus reaches your Descendant, and again about fourteen years after, it trines your Ascendant, and a freer way of meeting the world can settle in almost before you notice.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) frees and reworks; the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) sets the orientation you start from. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets two functions run together without either losing its nature, and flow isn't the same as drift. For about twenty months, the urge to differ and your way of entering the world pull in the same direction: a change of look or of tone, a different opening line, a readiness to begin things differently, all arriving with less friction than usual. The same Uranus sextiles your Descendant, which gives your one-to-one relationships an opening to change their terms too.

The two trines come from different corners of the chart. One rises from low down, usually your 4th or 5th house in Placidus, the house system I use: the freer self tends to show first in what you do for pleasure, in creative work or at home ([Uranus transiting the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-5th-house/)). The other comes, as a rule, from your 8th or 9th, where study, travel or a belief you've revised spill over into how you present yourself ([Uranus transiting the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-9th-house/)).

With [Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/), the trine from Gemini in these years comes from your chart ruler, in its modern reading. Saturn is Aquarius's traditional ruler and I read both ([chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/)), but while Uranus is there, the planet your chart leans on and the degree it starts from are in easy contact.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the [transit](/astrology/transits/) lasts about twenty months, or eleven when it's quick. About thirteen passes in fourteen bring three exact hits. The rest bring one, and still last about twenty months, because Uranus turns round inside the orb without crossing the exact degree again.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 meet both trines, and nobody meets fewer than one. They frame [transiting Uranus opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-ascendant/), fourteen years or so from it on each side; [transiting Uranus square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-ascendant/) lies about seven years from each trine on the side of the crossing, with [transiting Uranus sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) further on.

Libra and Aquarius rising are trined while Uranus is in Gemini, to May 2033; Scorpio and Pisces rising once it moves into Cancer, between 2032 and 2039. Your rising sign gives the years, your rising degree the months ([how to find your rising sign](/astrology/how-to-find-your-rising-sign/)). The Ascendant changes sign about every two hours on average, so if you were born close to a change, only an accurate time says which pair of years is yours ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Use the ease on purpose. Write down the one thing about how you come across that you've wanted to change for years, and start it while starting is easy.

Let pleasure or study do the testing. A trine tends to work through what you enjoy, and a manner tried out in a class or a new place travels home well.

Don't wait to be pushed. A trine never insists, so its twenty months can pass with nothing to show unless you lean on them.
