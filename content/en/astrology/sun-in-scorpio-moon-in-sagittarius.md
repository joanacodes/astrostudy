After the dive, the open air. Your Sun in Scorpio goes down into what is difficult and hidden; your Moon in Sagittarius needs to come back up, see the horizon and find out what it all means. You were born a few days into a new lunar month, just after a new moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Sagittarius follows Scorpio round the zodiac, a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign, so the lights differ in element and in pace: fixed water, mutable fire. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) holds on, goes deep and says little about it; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) settles when it has room to roam, frank talk and somewhere new in view. One light dives, the other surfaces, and much of your life is the rhythm between them.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules your Moon's sign, so where Jupiter sits by sign and house tells you where you go looking for meaning, and where you go to breathe. On the Sun's side there are two rulers and I read both: [Mars](/astrology/mars/), from the older books, for the force you bring to hard things, and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), from modern astrology, for what you will let end so that it can be rebuilt. Jupiter's generosity and Pluto's intensity meet in this pair, and where they agree, a crisis tends to become a lesson rather than a wound.

Your birth came at most five days or so after a new moon, with the Moon 1° to 59° past the Sun, and the conjunction behind it usually fell in Scorpio, a [new moon in Scorpio](/astrology/new-moon-in-scorpio/). Counted in eight phases, nearly all of these are [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) births, with a few, about an eighth, in the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/); and at the very start of the range, a Moon only just into Sagittarius can still be conjunct a Sun in the last degrees of Scorpio. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) describes this opening stretch of the month.

One person in 146 or so is born with the Moon just past the Sun and into Sagittarius. A few hours on the day the Moon changed sign can decide between this page, the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-scorpio/) and the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-capricorn/); settling that is the birth time's job ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Your houses hang on that time too, and they show where in your life you dive and where you come up for air.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love deeply and need room to breathe inside the love. The Scorpio Sun wants intimacy that is total and honest; the Sagittarius Moon wants freedom, humour and a shared sense of adventure. A partner who can go to the depths with you and then laugh on the way back up suits both lights. One who asks for closeness without air, or air without closeness, will feel half of you missing.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that combines depth with meaning suits you: research with a purpose, psychology, teaching difficult subjects, journalism, law, travel or international work, anything where you dig into something serious and then explain what it means. The Scorpio Sun finds the truth; the Sagittarius Moon wants to share its significance. Work that is deep but narrow, or broad but shallow, can lose you quickly.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The two lights handle pain differently: the Scorpio Sun goes into it, the Sagittarius Moon looks for the way out, and you may swing between brooding and escaping into plans. Neither on its own heals much. Let the Moon's optimism wait until the Sun has finished looking, then let it help you make sense of what you found: depth first, meaning second, and a trip or a long walk to mark the change.
