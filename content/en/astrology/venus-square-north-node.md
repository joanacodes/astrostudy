Venus square North Node puts what you value at 90° to the North Node, and so at 90° to the South Node as well: it squares the whole axis. Neither the familiar nor the new quite fits your taste on its own terms, and the friction keeps asking your growth to include what you love.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a chart that values and relates: what you like, whom you're drawn to, and whether an exchange feels fair. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a complementary capacity you develop by practice; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), to the established strengths you began with. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates Venus to both through constructive friction: preference and growth keep asking each other for adjustments.

In plain words, what you enjoy and the way you're growing don't line up neatly. The relationships or pleasures that suit you best may seem to pull you away from your new direction, while the old familiar ones no longer suit you either. That's friction, not failure. The square asks for a third way: growth that keeps room for what you value, and a way of relating that changes as you do. Practice may feel inefficient at first, and it still counts.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

With the nodes across Gemini and Sagittarius, Venus squaring both sits in Virgo or Pisces, mutable like them: the friction is about fitting your taste to learning and travel, or the other way round. With the nodes across Taurus and Scorpio, Venus in Leo or Aquarius squares them from fixed signs, and the question becomes how much of what you value can change without being lost. The houses show where the square bites, and they need a birth time.

About 1 person in 16 is born with Venus within 6° of a square to the nodes, with the true node. My notebook has no figure for the nodes: the angle it reports is the rising sign, which held about a third of the time, and the three that stood out were Saturn, Chiron and Lilith. So I treat this square as something to explore with you, not a fact about you ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)).

The pair's other angles:

- **[Venus conjunct North Node](/astrology/venus-conjunct-north-node/):** what you value sitting right where the growth is headed.
- **[Venus sextile North Node](/astrology/venus-sextile-north-node/):** a light, helpful angle to the growing end, and a trine to the familiar one.
- **[Venus trine North Node](/astrology/venus-trine-north-node/):** an easy current towards growth, with the familiar end a sextile away.
- **[Venus opposite North Node](/astrology/venus-opposite-north-node/):** Venus at the starting point; the familiar way of loving faces the new direction.

Between two charts, [Venus square North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-north-node-synastry/) can feel like a delightful detour: one person's charm crosses the other's path of growth. Whether it distracts or enriches depends on what both do with it, so I read it from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-venus/) comes twice in each 18.6-year round, for a month or two each time, and that year's eclipses fall roughly a quarter of the zodiac from your Venus. It's read as a time when relationships and pleasures ask how they fit with where you're going.

On its own yearly round, Venus squares your natal nodes twice as well, a day or so each time.

Weather, not a forecast: a question about fit, asked kindly, with no answer imposed.
