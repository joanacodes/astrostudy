Uranus gets to the rising degree of your birth chart once in a life at most, and for some people not before 80. For about twenty months the planet of change sits on the threshold of your chart, where you meet the world and begin things, and the way you do both is open to revision.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is your orientation: the angle you approach life from, the first move you make before you've had time to think. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) co-activates two functions as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the way you begin and present yourself takes on Uranus's need to be distinct.

It often shows in visible, everyday changes: a different way of introducing yourself, of dressing, of starting the day or a project; less patience with a role you learnt to play on arrival; more interest in being met as you are. It can feel freeing, or exposed, or both. Either way, the first impression you give starts to match a newer version of you.

The Ascendant's blind spot is mistaking a first response for a permanent identity, and Uranus's is mistaking difference for accuracy: a new manner can feel like the real you simply because it's new. I read the crossing as a change of orientation, not of character; how far you take it is yours to decide.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant is the cusp of the 1st house, so this crossing joins two long stays: Uranus's years in your 12th, out of sight ([Uranus transiting the 12th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-12th-house/)), and its years in your 1st ([Uranus transiting the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-transit-1st-house/)). With [Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/), it's the crossing of these years, since Uranus stays in Gemini until 2033.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus takes about twenty months to cross your rising degree, eleven when it's quick, touching it three times in more than nine passes out of ten, so for a while the crossing goes back and forth between your 12th and your 1st. It comes once at most.

Where your Uranus sits in relation to your Ascendant sets the age. With Uranus in the 12th, just above the eastern horizon, it arrives in your first years; with Uranus just inside the 1st, a few degrees past the rising degree, it needs almost its whole 84-year round: that group is the 1 person in 33, among births from 1930 to 2009, who reach 80 without it. Born with Uranus on the Ascendant ([Uranus in the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-in-1st-house/)), you had it at birth.

A transit to the Ascendant stands or falls with the birth time. A recorded time rounded to the quarter hour, as many are, already leaves a degree or two of doubt either way about the Ascendant, and with it months of doubt about this crossing. I don't read a chart without a time, nor from a time worked backwards out of events ([rectification](/astrology/rectification/)); if yours is missing, it may be written down somewhere ([how to find your birth time](/astrology/how-to-find-your-birth-time/), [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The other steps of the same round, [transiting Uranus sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-ascendant/), [transiting Uranus square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-ascendant/), [transiting Uranus trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-ascendant/), hang on the minute of birth in exactly the same way.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Watch your first reactions for a month. Under this crossing they change before your opinions do, and they show you what is actually shifting.

Let the new manner earn its place. Keep what still introduces you well, and drop only what no longer sounds like you.

Keep one familiar ritual for beginnings, a walk before work, the way you open the week. With Uranus on the Ascendant every start can feel new, and one steady habit lets you notice the difference.
