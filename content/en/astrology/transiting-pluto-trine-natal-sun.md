A trine from Pluto gives your Sun the deep end of change without the usual struggle: 120° apart, for two to three years. What you do with the ease is the whole question.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) stands for renewal after release, the part of a life that grows back stronger where something was let go. Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your sense of who you are and what you are for. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets the two run together, and under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) a change of direction can happen without a crisis to force it: a vocation deepens, a responsibility you were ready for becomes easy to take on, a self-image you had outgrown falls away quietly.

Pluto's blind spot here is confusing exposure with humiliation. Real change shows, and a trine makes it easier to let people see you as you are becoming, unfinished, without feeling shamed by it. The Sun's caution is that recognition is not fame: being seen more truly by the people around you is the gain, whatever the size of the audience.

Flow is not passivity, though. The trine carries what you set moving, and carries nothing otherwise.

The current runs between the house of your Sun and the one Pluto is crossing, about four houses away. With the Sun in your 6th, the trine often comes from around your 2nd, so your resources, skills and values support a change in your daily work ([Pluto transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-2nd-house/)). A recorded birth time settles which houses they are, and I ask for one before anything else ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Sun trine Pluto](/astrology/sun-trine-pluto/), you carry the angle itself, an ease with depth that a transit can echo for a few years but never replaces.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Between 1½° before exact and 1° after, the trine comes and goes over about two years and eight months; at Pluto's quickest it needs only twenty-two months or so. The yearly loops make it exact at least three times, and five times in roughly two passes in five, which then stretch to around three years and seven months.

The two trines sit 120° apart, on either side of the conjunction, and an 80-year stretch of Pluto's road holds one, both or neither. Born in 1990, with Pluto in Scorpio, birthdays from late November to early March miss both trines, and a few in July meet both, the first at birth and the second at about 62. In the count for 1930 to 2009, about 1 birth in 9 meets both, mostly in the older generations, and a little over 1 in 5 neither, a third of those born in the 2000s.

Before Pluto reaches the conjunction ([transiting Pluto conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-sun/)), it passes the first trine, the square ([transiting Pluto square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-sun/)), then the sextile ([transiting Pluto sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-sun/)); the second trine comes after the conjunction's own sextile and square, and the opposition ([transiting Pluto opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-sun/)) lies 60° beyond it.

The Suns Pluto trines from Aquarius, a sign it leaves in early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), are Gemini ones, from 120° behind, and Libra ones, from 120° ahead: in 2026, birthdays around 23 to 26 May and 25 to 28 September; by the early 2040s, around 20 June and 22 October.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick the change you have been circling and make it now. A trine is the easiest weather you will get for it.

Let people see the work in progress. Under a trine, being seen mid-change costs less than usual.

Put the extra strength somewhere lasting: a skill, a structure, a commitment that will still stand when Pluto has moved on.
