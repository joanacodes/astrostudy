Almost everyone meets Neptune square their Ascendant before 80, and a very few meet it twice. It comes from near the top of the chart or near the bottom, and it asks the way you meet the world to make room for something that won't be pinned down.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is your opening stance, and the Descendant sits exactly opposite it, so any square to the one squares the other: how you arrive and how you meet another person both come under pressure. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings longing, imagination and softer edges. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates them through friction, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the manner that has served you meets something it can't organise: a vocation that blurs, or a home that asks for more gentleness than your usual way allows.

The Ascendant's blind spot here is using one way of meeting people in every setting; Neptune's is underestimating the value of boundaries. The square presses on both. The confident manner that works at work may not work at home, and an open, porous one may need its edge back. I read it as friction that teaches you to meet different rooms differently.

In Placidus, my house system, the squares fall close to the meridian: one in your 9th or 10th house, near the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) ([Neptune transiting the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-10th-house/)), the other in your 3rd or 4th, near the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) ([Neptune transiting the 4th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-4th-house/)). Close, not on: the Midheaven and the Ascendant are seldom a neat quarter of the zodiac apart, since the gap follows the hour and the latitude of birth, and with it the years between this square and Neptune's arrival at your Midheaven or IC ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Two and a half years is the rule within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and about twenty-two months the shortest; three exact hits come in about three passes out of four, five in the others. In the count for births from 1930 to 2009, almost no one reaches 80 without one of the two squares, and a very few, born with Neptune just short of one, also reach the other.

On Neptune's road the squares come before the horizon is crossed. From the top of the chart, the square is followed by [transiting Neptune sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-ascendant/) and then [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-ascendant/); from the bottom, by [transiting Neptune trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-ascendant/) and then [transiting Neptune opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-ascendant/). From now until March 2039 the squares fall on [Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/), from near the top of the chart, and [Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/), from near the bottom; at the end of 2026 Neptune stands at about 2° of Aries, so the first degrees of both signs feel it first.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep two manners on purpose. Let the way you meet colleagues and the way you come home differ; the square eases when one stance no longer has to do the other's job.

Draw one edge clearly: an hour when messages wait, a door you close, a request you decline without explaining it away.

Give the blurred part a form you can test. If it's a vocation, a course or a trial project; if it's home, a change tried for a season before anything permanent.
