With the Sun in the 4th house, the light is underneath. It was heading for the lowest point of its daily round when you were born, beneath your feet, and the self it describes grows from the roots up: private first, and public only on its own terms.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, roots and family: where you come from, what you build in order to belong, and the private self underneath. Angular, opening at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the point opposite the Midheaven, it has Cancer for its natural sign. With the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) here, the function that organises identity, vitality and purpose works from the foundations. You may know who you are best at home, or in the place you're trying to make into one, and much of your sense of direction comes from understanding where you started.

By the Sun's clock rather than yours, and in Placidus, the house system I use, this is a late-evening birth: the Sun was travelling towards the lowest point of its daily circle, before the middle of the night. Near the cusps a few minutes count, so your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) has to be exact for this reading to stand.

Because the 4th house starts at the Imum Coeli, a Sun inside it usually shares the IC's sign or sits in the next one. Its opposite, [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), is the public one; with the Sun on the private side of that axis, your public life tends to draw on what you've built at home rather than the other way round.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Compared with the [Sun in the 3rd house](/astrology/sun-in-3rd-house/), which bonds by talking, and the [Sun in the 5th house](/astrology/sun-in-5th-house/), which bonds by playing, a 4th-house Sun bonds by making a home. The space you create says a lot about you: the table, the rituals, the welcome at the door. The risk is that your identity merges with the household role you play, until there's little of you left outside it. The lesson is to keep one part of yourself that isn't about home at all, and to let a partner meet it.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Sun likes foundations: the job that keeps a place running, the family firm, the craft practised from home, work with land, buildings, history or care. You may do your best in the back rooms of an organisation, where its base is laid. Recognition tends to come from those closest to the work rather than from the public, and it means a great deal when it does. Purpose fades in places that feel rootless, however exciting they look from outside.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

When identity grows from the roots, it can stay tangled in them. You may carry a family's expectations long after you've left the house, or find it hard to build a home of your own because the first one still sets the standard. Some people with this Sun hide their light so well in private that the outside world barely knows it exists. None of this is weakness. It's a strong Sun pointed inwards, waiting for a reason to turn round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make a home that is yours, even if it's one room, and arrange it on purpose. Tell your story to yourself, where you come from and what you've chosen to keep, so that the past becomes material rather than instructions. Bring one private thing into the open each year: a project shown, a skill shared, a door opened. And read a 4th-house Sun with the sign on your IC and the 10th house opposite, the private and the public halves of the same axis.
