A Neptune sextile to Mercury rarely announces itself. For about two and a half years, imagination comes closer to language than usual, and the distance shrinks further each time you use it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) supplies images, empathy and a feel for meaning beyond the literal; [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) turns experience into language and links one idea to the next. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, is an aspect of opportunity, and under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) imaginative thinking is easier to reach and easier to share. A story you've wanted to write, a language learned by ear, a way of explaining things through pictures: the sextile offers, and curiosity has to take it up. Analysis isn't overthinking, and the sextile can loosen a habit of going round the same thought: an image gives a problem another way in. Communication isn't constant talking, either: some of the sextile's best work happens in silence, on the page or in your head.

Sextiles join fire and air. Neptune in Aries, a fire sign, sextiles Mercury in Gemini and Aquarius, two air signs, from now until 2039, and Gemini is one of Mercury's own signs: with Mercury in [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), the opening arrives on home ground. The opening often comes through someone else: a teacher, a reader, a friend who asks what you meant.

With your Mercury in the 9th and Neptune crossing your 11th ([Neptune transiting the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-11th-house/)), a group or a shared ideal can give your studies a new direction; with Mercury in the 1st and Neptune in the 3rd, the way you speak and the way you learn soften together. Both houses depend on your birth time, and so does any reading I give ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Neptune spends about two and a half years in orb of the sextile, under two at the shortest; three exact passes are usual, and five happen in about one pass in four. Mercury turns retrograde three or four times a year, so [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) will fall inside this transit more than once; I don't count those weeks against it. They're good times to revise what the sextile helped you start.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have had one sextile to their Mercury, some two, and about 1 in 6 none. The sextiles are the nearest easy contacts to the conjunction ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-mercury/)); a square ([transiting Neptune square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-mercury/)) lies about fourteen years from each, a trine ([transiting Neptune trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-mercury/)) about twenty-seven, and the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-mercury/)) across the circle. The natal [Mercury sextile Neptune](/astrology/mercury-sextile-neptune/) gives some people this ease for life; the transit offers it to anyone it reaches, for a while.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sign up for the thing you'd learn for pleasure: a language, an instrument, a writing class. This sextile favours learning that's a little impractical.

Write a page a day, or a paragraph, and keep the pages. Openings for the mind stay open with use.

Talk about what you imagine with someone who listens well. Exchange is half of Mercury's work.
