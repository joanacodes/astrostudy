The trine is the aspect everyone hopes to find in their chart: two planets a third of the circle apart, in signs of the same element, working together as if they'd always known each other. It's real ease. The only catch is that ease rarely asks to be used.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

A trine is an angle of 120°: four signs apart. Count four signs on from Aries and you reach Leo; four more, Sagittarius; four more and you're back at Aries. The three make a triangle of one element, here fire, and every trine joins two signs of the same element: fire, earth, air or water (see [the four elements](/astrology/four-elements/)). The two planets don't need to translate for each other. They share a temperament, if not a method, since the three signs of an element each belong to a different modality.

I read a trine as a talent. Whatever those two planets do together comes easily, often so easily that you don't count it as a quality: it's just how you are. That's its gift and its risk. Ease asks nothing of you, so a trine can go unused for a long time, or settle into a habit too comfortable to question. The [square](/astrology/square/) pushes you to build; the trine hands you the materials and leaves the building to you.

As with every aspect, astrologers allow a few degrees either side of exact, more with the Sun and Moon (see [orbs](/astrology/orbs/)), and many give more weight to an [applying trine](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/), still forming, than to one that's separating. Near the end of a sign, a trine can join two different elements, fire and water, say, which takes some of the smoothness out of it: an [out-of-sign aspect](/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/).

Among the five major aspects, the trine is one of Ptolemy's four, with the [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), the square and the [opposition](/astrology/opposition/); the [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) is a meeting rather than one of his four. With the sextile, it's one of the two [soft aspects](/astrology/hard-and-soft-aspects/), and the stronger of the two. Three planets each trine the other two make a [grand trine](/astrology/grand-trine/): a closed triangle of ease in a single element.

## Between which planets {#between-which-planets}

The Sun never makes a trine with Mercury or Venus, which never stray more than 28° and 47° from it, and the two never get far enough apart to make one with each other. These are the trines people look up most:

- **[Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/):** born a few days from a full moon; what you want and what you need get along.
- **[Venus trine Mars](/astrology/venus-trine-mars/):** affection and desire in step; an easy warmth people respond to.
- **[Moon trine Saturn](/astrology/moon-trine-saturn/):** emotional steadiness; a calm others lean on.
- **[Sun trine Jupiter](/astrology/sun-trine-jupiter/):** confidence and generosity; a natural optimism that opens doors, when you knock.
- **[Mercury trine Saturn](/astrology/mercury-trine-saturn/):** a patient, organised mind; what you learn, you keep.
- **[Moon trine Venus](/astrology/moon-trine-venus/):** affection given and received without fuss; tenderness in everyday things.
- **[Sun trine Pluto](/astrology/sun-trine-pluto/):** a quiet intensity; strength that shows when things get hard.
- **[Venus trine Saturn](/astrology/venus-trine-saturn/):** loyalty and constancy in love; affection that lasts.
- **[Mars trine Saturn](/astrology/mars-trine-saturn/):** effort that doesn't give up; stamina with a good sense of timing.
- **[Moon trine Chiron](/astrology/moon-trine-chiron/):** an old hurt that has turned into empathy; a calm presence when others struggle.

One honest note. [My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) counted placements, each planet in its sign and house, so it says nothing about trines as such. It does say that Saturn and Chiron, both in that list, were among the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time. A trine from either one is something I like to point out, because people rarely notice the help they've always had.

## The trine in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), a trine between two charts feels like ease: you understand each other without explaining, and time together costs little. [One person's Sun trine the other's Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon-synastry/) is one of the most comfortable contacts two people can share; [Venus trine Mars](/astrology/venus-trine-mars-synastry/) brings attraction without the sharp edges; [the Moon trine Venus](/astrology/moon-trine-venus-synastry/), an easy, everyday tenderness. Ease isn't everything, though. A bond made only of trines can lack the friction it needs to grow, because nothing asks either of you to stretch; most pairs mix easy contacts with harder ones, and that mix is what I read. In both directions, as always.

## The trine in transits {#in-transits}

A trine [transit](/astrology/transits/) is a tailwind: a planet in today's sky reaches 120° from one of yours and, for a while, things in that part of life move more easily. Trine transits are easy to miss, because nothing pushes; the help is there if you lean on it. [Transiting Jupiter trine your natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-venus/) reads as a season for pleasure, warmth and generosity; [Saturn trine your natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-sun/), as a good time to consolidate what you've built; [Saturn trine your natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-moon/), as steadier ground under your feelings. The fast planets pass in hours or days; the slow ones can stay in orb for months, and cross the same degree up to three times as they turn retrograde. Weather, not a forecast: a trine tells you the wind is behind you, not where you'll land.
