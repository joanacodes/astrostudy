One of you tends to become the helper and the other the one who is helped, and it isn't always clear who chose which. Sun opposite Chiron between two charts sets one person's Chiron six signs from the other's Sun, and identity and sore spot face each other.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity, vitality and the confidence to take up space. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts around a limitation that doesn't go away, building resilience, compensation and the kind of wisdom only someone who has lived it can pass on. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity and complementarity. In [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) of this kind, each person holds the end the other is missing.

Holding the Sun, you may find yourself cast as the strong one. You see the other's resilience close up and learn from it, and their experience can make you more honest about limits of your own. Your confidence may also make their sore spot more visible, without your meaning anything by it.

Holding Chiron, you see in the other what you had to adapt around. You may lean on their steadiness, or find yourself teaching them, since your practical wisdom is often exactly what they lack when life asks for adaptation rather than confidence.

What flows is complementarity: one brings ease, the other know-how, and together they cover more ground than either could alone.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A seesaw of strength and tenderness. The more the Sun person helps, the more the Chiron person is seen as needing help; the more the Chiron person leans, the less the Sun person shows any weakness. Roles harden, and both can come to resent them. Polarity isn't contradiction: the Sun person has sore spots too, and the Chiron person has strengths far beyond this one area.

Chiron was only found in 1977, and its uneven orbit gives each sign a cohort of very different size. The opposition itself comes from a birthday six signs away, and that is what ties it to this pair.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep the roles moving. The Sun person can ask for help now and then, in an area where the other is the expert; the Chiron person can take the lead wherever their experience counts. Couples can share the caring rather than assign it; friends can take turns at leaning; families can retire the old story of the strong one and the sensitive one; colleagues can mentor each other on different subjects.

About 1 pair of people in 11 has this opposition, one way round or the other, within 8°. Over a day, the Sun covers a degree or so and Chiron far less, so the hour rarely decides the opposition; the houses decide its axis, and they need both birth times, which I ask for first ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). I take this contact on only when both people have agreed and the Chiron person knows what will be named ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

The pair's other angles put the sore spot elsewhere: [Sun conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-chiron-synastry/) right under the Sun person's ease, [Sun square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-chiron-synastry/) at an angle where it rubs, [Sun trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-chiron-synastry/) and [Sun sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-chiron-synastry/) within comfortable, helpful reach. In one chart, [Sun opposite Chiron](/astrology/sun-opposite-chiron/) sets a person's own sense of self across from their own tender place; between two, each holds one end, and either can hand the other a turn.
