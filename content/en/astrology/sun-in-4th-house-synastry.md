With their Sun in your 4th house, another person's sense of purpose comes to live under your roof, figuratively and sometimes literally. It's the overlay of the person whose presence makes a place feel lived in, and of the one who feels, with you, that they've found somewhere to stand.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) sits at the bottom of your chart: home, family, roots, the private self only the people closest to you see. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) carries a person's identity and direction, the life they're trying to grow into. Through this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their direction comes to rest in your foundations.

For you, that can feel like warmth arriving in rooms that were quiet. Evenings at home take on more life when they're there, plans for the place you live start to include them, and you may find yourself telling them about the family you grew up in, or the one you've chosen, sooner than you usually would. The 4th is angular, so even this, the most private of the angular overlays, tends to show, and [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), the Moon's own sign, is the one that goes with it by nature: shelter, memory, the need to belong.

For them, your private world can be where their purpose finally takes root. They may feel more themselves at your kitchen table than anywhere public, and want a place in the base you've built rather than a seat at its edge. When their Sun sits just past your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the point at the very bottom of the chart, it also meets the foundation the rest of your chart stands on, and I read that meeting with particular care.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A strong light at home can crowd it. The place you go to rest may slowly become the place their plans unfold, their work spread across your table and their schedule setting the evening. Your experience of family can also come up under that light: old habits, old roles, the way things were done in the house you grew up in. The overlay says nothing about your parents; it says that this person brings those memories into view.

From their side, your home can feel like a house with rules they didn't write. Their warmth wants to spread, and your private self may close a door they didn't know was there. They can feel kept on the threshold when they hoped to be inside.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Talk about home as a shared thing with private corners. Which rooms, routines or evenings are yours alone? Which would you like to share? Couples who live together often do well to name this early; friends with this overlay tend to drift into each other's houses, and an honest word about when a visit suits keeps that easy. Between adult relatives, it can make family gatherings feel brighter, or heavier, depending on who is carrying them.

Swap the charts and you have your Sun in their 4th house: you become the warmth in their home, facing the same questions about space and privacy. [Their Sun in your 3rd house](/astrology/sun-in-3rd-house-synastry/) stays in the conversation; [their Sun in your 5th house](/astrology/sun-in-5th-house-synastry/) turns it towards play and pleasure.

Your IC goes round the whole zodiac in a day, like your Ascendant, so in Placidus, the house system I use, your 4th house can only be found with your birth time. Their Sun shifts by only about a degree in a day, so their own hour of birth counts just at the 4th's two borders ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Home is private ground, and I look at it only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
