A full moon in Leo was a couple of days from your birth at most, the Moon shining in the Sun's own sign and the Sun across the sky in Aquarius. Your sense of purpose is built for the many; your feelings want warmth that comes back from someone in particular. It reads to me as a public conscience with a private need to be cherished.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aquarius and Leo sit opposite each other, both fixed, air facing fire: an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) by sign, two signs that complete each other and hold their ground. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) finds itself in what it shares, a principle, a circle of friends, a plan for the future; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) needs to give warmth and watch it land, to make something from the heart and have it recognised. At a gathering you're likely to make sure everyone is included, then go home hoping someone noticed what you brought.

Leo has one ruler, the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) itself, so your Moon takes its cue from your Sun: when your sense of purpose is alive, your feelings warm up with it, and when it stalls, they cool. That Sun is in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), facing its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/) across the axis, which I read as a self more at ease lighting a room than standing in the middle of it, and a Moon that keeps reminding it the middle has its uses. Behind the Sun stand the two rulers of Aquarius, and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in the modern scheme, for the way your Sun breaks with custom, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in the traditional one, for the commitments that keep it steady.

The lights were 151° to 209° apart, 12½ to 17 days into the lunar month. With the Sun in Aquarius a full moon can only be a [full moon in Leo](/astrology/full-moon-in-leo/), and yours came at most two and a half days before or after your birth. The [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) days before it and the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase after it share these births equally, so the hour can put you on either side, and near 180° there is a [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/) in the chart: what you aim for and what you need, face to face and looking for balance, often through other people. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) describes the phases on either side of the full.

In the count this is the least common of the twelve Aquarius pairs, if only just: about 1 person in 152. If the Moon moved into Leo from Cancer, or on into Virgo, on the day you were born, three pages are still possible until the hour is known: this one, the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-cancer/) and the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-virgo/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)); the Moon takes about two and a half days over each sign. The time also gives the houses: the one holding your Sun is where you give yourself to the group, the one holding your Moon where you need to be cherished, and I can't place either without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You are loyal and generous, and you need to be special to someone. The Aquarius Sun treats people as equals and gives a partner plenty of freedom; the Leo Moon hopes to be chosen, admired a little, celebrated on the right day. A partner may not guess that someone so fair-minded minds being treated like everyone else. Tell them: you'd like to be treated fairly in public and adored at home.

## At work {#at-work}

You can lead a project for the common good with real warmth: community arts, campaigns, teaching, any work where an idea needs a face and a voice. The Sun cares about the cause; the Moon cares that your part in it is seen. Credit given to the group rather than to anyone in particular satisfies the Sun and quietly starves the Moon, so make sure your name appears somewhere.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two fixed signs in opposition can swing between principle and pride. You may turn down recognition on principle and then feel hurt that none came, or dig in on a point of fairness when what's really wounded is your heart. What helps is giving both lights their due: say what you want for everyone, then what you want for yourself, and notice that the second isn't selfish.
