You were born within a couple of days of a full moon in Leo, the Moon shining in the Sun's own sign and the Sun across the sky in Aquarius. Your sense of purpose is built for the many; your feelings want warmth that comes back from someone in particular. I read the pair as a public conscience with a private need to be cherished.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aquarius and Leo face each other across the zodiac, both fixed, air across from fire: an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) by sign, two signs that complete each other and hold their ground. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) finds itself in what it shares, a principle, a circle of friends, a plan for the future; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) needs to give warmth and watch it land, to make something from the heart and have it recognised. At a gathering you may be the one who makes sure everyone is included, then go home hoping someone noticed what you brought.

Leo has one ruler, the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) itself, so your Moon takes its cue from your Sun: when your sense of purpose is alive, your feelings warm up with it, and when it stalls, they cool. That Sun is in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), facing its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/) across the axis, which I read as a self more at ease lighting a room than standing in the middle of it, and a Moon that keeps reminding it the middle has its uses. Aquarius has two rulers, and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern one, for the way your Sun breaks with custom, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional one, for the commitments that keep it steady.

The Moon stood 151° to 209° from the Sun, 12½ to 17 days into the lunar month. With the Sun in Aquarius a full moon can only fall in Leo, and yours, a [full moon in Leo](/astrology/full-moon-in-leo/), was within about two and a half days. Half of these births come in the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) days before it and half in the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase after, so the hour can put you on either side; close to 180° the chart holds a [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/), purpose and need facing each other and looking for balance, often through other people. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) describes the phases on either side of the full.

In the count this is the least common of the twelve Aquarius pairs, if only just: about 1 person in 152. The Moon stays in Leo for some two and a half days, between Cancer and Virgo, and on a birthday when it crossed one of those borders the hour decides between this page, the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-cancer/) and the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-virgo/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The time also gives the houses: the one holding your Sun is where you give yourself to the group, the one holding your Moon where you need to be cherished, and I can't place either without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You are loyal and generous, and you need to be special to someone. The Aquarius Sun treats people as equals and gives a partner plenty of freedom; the Leo Moon wants to be chosen, admired a little, celebrated on the right day. A partner may not guess that someone so fair-minded minds being treated like everyone else. Tell them: you'd like to be treated fairly in public and adored at home.

## At work {#at-work}

You can lead a project for the common good with real warmth: community arts, campaigns, teaching, any work where an idea needs a face and a voice. The Sun cares about the cause; the Moon cares that your part in it is seen. Credit given to the group rather than to anyone in particular satisfies the Sun and quietly starves the Moon, so make sure your name appears somewhere.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two fixed signs in opposition can swing between principle and pride. You may turn down recognition on principle and then feel hurt that none came, or dig in on a point of fairness when what's really wounded is your heart. What helps is giving both lights their due: say what you want for everyone, then what you want for yourself, and notice that the second isn't selfish.
