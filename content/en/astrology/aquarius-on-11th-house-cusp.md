Aquarius on the 11th house cusp sets the sign in the house that has always been its own. The people you choose, the circles you join and the plans you make with others run the Aquarian way: between equals, with room for everyone's differences, and with one eye on the future.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Aquarius is the 11th sign, so on this cusp sign and house agree ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) gets the manner it has in the natural zodiac. Tradition called it the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy here. I read it as your allies: the people who help without having to, the networks you belong to, the causes that outlast any one meeting. With [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) on the cusp, friendship tends to cross ages, trades and backgrounds, and it survives distance: a friend you haven't seen in a year is still a friend the moment you speak. You may belong to several circles that never meet each other. In a group you want everyone to have a voice, and you're often the one who leaves when a circle turns into a clique, or who rewrites its rules so it doesn't.

Uranus, the 11th's modern ruler by nature as well as here, and Saturn, its traditional one, share this house, and I read both. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) shows where new people burst into your life and where you outgrow a circle; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) shows the friendships kept for decades and the duties you take on for a group, the treasurer's job nobody else wanted. Capricorn holds the Midheaven in about 4 London charts in 5 and nearly 9 at New York, so Saturn rules your career as well as your allies, and the people who open doors for you at work are often friends first. Their houses show the setting your alliances grow in, as ruler of the 11th in the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

With the Placidus cusps I use, Aquarius opens the 11th about once in 14 births at London and once in 13 at New York. These charts give the 11th plenty of room, about 29° to 42° at London, and in about 1 London chart in 4 Pisces fits inside it whole ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)): Neptune and Jupiter then join the circle, and friendship takes on something devoted and less defined. Otherwise Pisces opens the 12th. [Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/) comes with this cusp in about 2 charts in 3, giving the chart to Venus; Gemini rises in most of the other London charts, Aries in the other New York ones. In about 1 London birth in 7, Aquarius is intercepted instead, and no house of the chart opens in it.

Your birth time is what sets the sign here rather than on the Midheaven above ([Aquarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)) or on the 12th below ([Aquarius on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-12th-house-cusp/)): a later birth moves it up to the 10th, an earlier one down to the 12th. Without the time I can't place it, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
