When Saturn squares your Midheaven, it squares your IC at the same moment: the line running from the top of your chart to the bottom feels the pressure at both ends. It happens twice in each round, and it tends to ask how your public direction and your private foundations are sharing the load.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) brings structure, duty and the long view; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where your capacities become visible in a role, and its opposite point, the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), is the foundation that sustains it. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates Saturn to both through friction. In this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the arrangement between work and the rest of life gets tested: a role that has grown takes more than it gives back, a commitment at home asks for time the job had claimed, or a direction you've drifted along needs choosing properly. Which end gives way first varies: some people reshape the job to suit the home, others the home to suit the job.

Friction isn't failure, and nothing here predicts a lost job or a stalled career. A square to the Midheaven usually ends with a better-built direction: responsibilities pruned to the ones you'd choose, a schedule that lets home and work both stand, a goal stated plainly enough to plan for.

The first square comes about seven years after Saturn crosses your IC ([transiting Saturn opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-midheaven/)), near your Descendant; the second about seven years after it crosses your Midheaven ([transiting Saturn conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)), near your Ascendant. Near, not on: the Midheaven and Ascendant are rarely exactly 90° apart, so these squares and Saturn's crossings of your horizon are neighbours rather than twins ([transiting Saturn square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-ascendant/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Count about nine months for this square, between 1½° before exact and 1° after, my usual orbs. About half of them, slightly more, are loops with three exact hits across some nine and a half months; the rest are single hits, which can be over in three weeks. Five by 80 is usual, six for some.

Your Midheaven's degree sets the dates, and it comes from the birth time: an error of forty minutes would move this square by about ten months, longer than the transit itself. A chart without a time isn't one I read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A Cancer or Capricorn Midheaven is having its square now, from Saturn's years in Aries, which run until April 2028. The sextiles ([transiting Saturn sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-midheaven/)) and trines ([transiting Saturn trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-midheaven/)) to your Midheaven fall two or three years either side of each square.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Draw your week as it is, then as you'd like it: work in one colour, home and rest in another. A square to the Midheaven is often solved on paper first.

Drop one responsibility that belongs to an old version of your role. Saturn's pressure eases when the structure fits the direction.

Say out loud where you're going, to someone who'll ask about it later. A stated aim is easier to build around than a vague one.
