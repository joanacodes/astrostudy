Once in a life, for everyone born between 1930 and 2009, Uranus stands opposite the degree Neptune held at birth. For about twenty months your ideals and the urge to change face each other across your chart, and the age you meet it at depends on the decade you were born in.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where you imagine, idealise and feel for others. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and wants what is true brought into view. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates two functions through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the dream and the wish to see clearly stand at either end of one line.

The other end tends to appear outside you: a movement you believed in seen from the outside, a partner or a friend whose dream pulls against yours, an institution that wants to modernise what you held sacred. Or you're the one who sees through something while the people around you still believe. A dream you'd lived inside becomes visible as a dream, which isn't the same as false.

Both planets bring a blind spot. Uranus can confuse distance with objectivity, and Neptune empathy with knowing another person; under the opposition you can feel sure, too soon, that you've seen the truth of someone or of something. The polarity asks you to keep the compassion and the clear sight both.

With Neptune in your 7th and Uranus crossing your 1st ([Uranus transiting the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-transit-1st-house/)), ideals you placed in partners meet your own wish to be yourself, plainly. Your birth time places that axis; without it, I don't give the reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition lasts about twenty months, about eleven at the shortest, time enough to see a dream from outside and still choose what to keep; in more than nine passes out of ten it is exact three times. Each birth from 1930 to 2009 meets it once before 80, since Uranus needs its whole 84-year round to come back.

Uranus in Gemini, until 2033, stands opposite every natal Neptune in Sagittarius ([Neptune in Sagittarius](/astrology/neptune-in-sagittarius/)), the Neptune of births from January 1970 to November 1984: they are meeting this opposition now, at about 48 to 55.

In the site's count it came at about 71 for someone born at the start of 1935, about 62 for 1955, about 53 for 1975 and about 37 for 2005: roughly five years earlier with each decade of birth, because Uranus, the faster planet, keeps gaining on Neptune. No one born in those eighty years has [Uranus opposite Neptune](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune/) in the birth chart, so for all of them this polarity comes only by transit.

[Transiting Uranus trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-neptune/) falls about fourteen years either side of it and [transiting Uranus square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-neptune/) about twenty-one; [transiting Uranus sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-neptune/) lie on the far side of the round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Before you decide you've seen through something, ask what it gave you. An ideal can be outgrown and still have been worth having.

Let other people's dreams stay theirs. The opposition shows you a different vision; it doesn't make you its judge.

Choose any new commitment for its practice, not its promise. Under this transit, a cause or a creed is easiest to weigh by what it asks of you on an ordinary day.
