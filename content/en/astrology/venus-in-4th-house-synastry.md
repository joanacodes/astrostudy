Their Venus in your 4th house loves your home. They tend to feel at ease where you live and among the people you come from, and the private life behind your front door can seem more beautiful, and more welcoming, for having them in it.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

Home, family, roots, belonging and the private self are the matter of [the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), the foundation under the rest of your chart. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) adds taste, affection and the wish for harmony. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), it brings what they find lovely to your most private ground.

With them, home can feel more like home. They notice the lamp you chose, enjoy the meals you make, settle into your sofa as if it were theirs, and may have a gift for making a room pleasant: a plant, a rug, a better place for the table. If they meet your family, they often find something to like there, and say so.

For them, your private world is a pleasure in itself. They may prefer an evening at yours to a night out, and feel that your way of living is a kind of shelter. Angular houses carry weight even when they work in private, and the natural sign of this one, [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), belongs to the Moon, which makes the overlay about comfort as much as taste.

Conjunct your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), at the very start of the house, their Venus touches the point your chart stands on, and the warmth goes deeper still.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Taste can clash at home more than anywhere. Who chooses the curtains, how tidy a kitchen should be, whether a house is for guests or for hiding: with Venus in this house, these questions carry feeling. Keeping the peace at home can also become a habit, with differences smoothed over rather than talked through.

They can feel like a much-loved guest who is never allowed to move the furniture, welcome but not quite at home. And if they idealise your family, they may be hurt when the reality turns out more complicated than the Sunday lunches.

The 4th describes your experience of home and roots, not what will happen: this overlay doesn't say you'll live together, marry or start a family. It says where the warmth between you tends to gather.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make decisions about the home together, and let each of you keep a corner that's entirely your own. Say what bothers you about the shared space before it builds. With a partner, a move or a redecoration goes better when taste is discussed early; with a friend, this may be the one who's always welcome at your kitchen table; with a colleague who calls in at your home office, keep the welcome and the work apart; among adult relatives, the in-law or cousin who brings warmth to family gatherings.

In reverse, your Venus in their 4th house would make you the one who loves their home and the people they come from. Around it, [their Venus in your 3rd house](/astrology/venus-in-3rd-house-synastry/) is kindness in daily talk, and [their Venus in your 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house-synastry/) pleasure, play and romance.

Because the 4th opens at your IC, the minute you were born decides whether their Venus sits just inside the house or still in your 3rd; in Placidus, the house system I use, that line moves by about a degree for every four minutes. Their own time matters only when their Venus is within a degree of it, and I ask for it all the same ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A family's home is private ground, read only with both people's knowledge ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
