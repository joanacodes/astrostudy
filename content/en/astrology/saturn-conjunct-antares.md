Saturn conjunct Antares belongs to only two stretches of births between 1930 and 2009: December 1956 to October 1957, and February to November 1986. About 1 person in 163 born in those eighty years has Saturn within a degree of the Scorpion's red heart.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) as the part of a chart that builds slowly and keeps: structure, responsibility, the boundaries you hold and the work that lasts. [Antares](/astrology/antares/) brings it a borrowed colour. Its name makes it the rival of Mars, and the medieval Behenian list gives it the natures of Mars and Jupiter; nothing in its lore belongs to Saturn. Laid over Saturn, the old picture is of drive set inside a slow, patient frame: heat that has to wait its turn. Where Saturn sits, I read the part of life you learn to look after for the long run, and that holds on this star as on any other.

The 1986 stretch is unusual. Saturn turns retrograde once a year, for about four and a half months, when the Earth overtakes it on the inside track and it seems to stop and go back; in 1986 that turn fell almost on the star. Saturn crossed the star's degree on 6 March, came to a standstill on 19 March about a sixth of a degree past it, and turned back over it on 2 April; from February to the end of April it never left the one-degree window. Births in those weeks have Saturn almost motionless on the star. Saturn then went back as far as 3° Sagittarius before returning to cross the degree a last time on 12 November ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)).

The earlier stretch ran differently. Saturn crossed the star on the last day of 1956, turned retrograde some five degrees further on, came back over it at the end of June 1957 and crossed it a third time in late September. Both stretches put Saturn at around 9° Sagittarius ([Saturn in Sagittarius](/astrology/saturn-in-sagittarius/)), and the next came about 29½ years after 1986, beyond the births counted here.

In the sky the two never meet. Antares lies 4.6° south of the ecliptic, and Saturn's path runs north of it at this point of the zodiac, so on every pass Saturn stood about six degrees away: a shared degree of the zodiac and nothing more.

A contact shared by everyone born in the same few months can't say much about any one of them. What makes Saturn yours is its house, which only the birth time can give, and its aspects to the rest of your chart; those are what I read, since the system behind my readings places no stars ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Next to Saturn's two stretches, the star's other contacts come round far more often: the [Sun conjunct Antares](/astrology/sun-conjunct-antares/) every 1–2 December, the [Moon conjunct Antares](/astrology/moon-conjunct-antares/) every 27⅓ days, [Mars conjunct Antares](/astrology/mars-conjunct-antares/) about every two years, and [Venus conjunct Antares](/astrology/venus-conjunct-antares/) in most years between October and January. [Conjunctions to fixed stars](/astrology/conjunctions-to-fixed-stars/) explains the rule they share, and [fixed stars in astrology](/astrology/fixed-stars-in-astrology/) the tradition behind it.
