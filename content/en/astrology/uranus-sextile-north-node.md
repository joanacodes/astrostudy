Uranus sextile North Node gives the planet of change a hand in the direction you're growing in. It's the most common aspect this pair made in the count, about 1 birth in 11, and it works like an invitation: experiment offers growth a way in, and you decide whether to accept.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, experiment and liberation from habits that no longer fit. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity less familiar than what came easily, built by trying, getting feedback and adjusting. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the two through opportunity and cooperation: each can activate the other, and neither does the other's work.

The geometry matters here. A point 60° from the North Node is 120° from the South Node, so Uranus makes an easy link to the familiar as well as an opening to the new. In practice, the way forward often starts by renewing something you already know: an old skill used in an unusual way, a familiar setting rearranged, a habit updated rather than abandoned. That fits how growth works: development doesn't erase what you can already do; it adds range. Expect the first experiments to make you slower before they make you better. Opportunity requires engagement, and practice under feedback is what turns the opening into a path.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 11 born between 1930 and 2009 has Uranus within 6° of a sextile to the North Node. Counted with the true node; I look at the mean node too, and the two agree except within a degree or two of the orb's edge ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The node moves backwards through a sign in about 18 months while Uranus moves forwards through one in seven years, so the sextile forms and dissolves within months.

A sextile usually links elements that get on. Uranus in Aquarius sextile the North Node in Aries, air and fire, might let unconventional ideas carry you towards more initiative and courage. The houses show the route: Uranus in the 1st sextile the North Node in the 3rd, for example, could let an independent way of meeting the world open your growth into learning and conversation. You'll need a birth time for that ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct North Node](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-north-node/):** growth itself asks you to differ, with Uranus on the node.
- **[Uranus square North Node](/astrology/uranus-square-north-node/):** change cuts across the path at right angles to both nodes.
- **[Uranus trine North Node](/astrology/uranus-trine-north-node/):** a wider, easier flow towards the new, with a sextile back to the familiar.
- **[Uranus opposite North Node](/astrology/uranus-opposite-north-node/):** Uranus beside the South Node, on familiar ground.

Between two charts, [Uranus sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-north-node-synastry/) lets one person's originality offer the other a way into their direction of growth, if they want it. I read it from both sides, as I do every node contact between charts ([the nodes in synastry](/astrology/nodes-in-synastry/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-uranus/) comes twice in each 18.6-year round of the nodes and stays near the exact angle for a month or two. Transiting Uranus sextiles your natal North Node more rarely, twice in 84 years, over several months.

Born with the sextile, you see it formed again at each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/); and at each [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), when the transiting North Node reaches your natal South Node, it trines your Uranus instead. Weather, not a forecast: brief openings to try something new, nothing that has to happen.
