With some people you never have to explain why you do things your own way. Uranus trine Lilith in synastry places one person's Uranus about 120° from the other's Black Moon Lilith, within 3.4°, usually in signs of one element, and two kinds of independence fall into step.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) innovates and differentiates: it wants a life that isn't a copy of anyone's, and the systems around it loosened enough to allow that. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point where the Moon swings farthest from Earth, is read symbolically and with varying emphasis; I take it as raw autonomy, a boundary, and whatever a person once learned to tuck out of sight. In a [trine](/astrology/trine/), two functions lend each other strength and stay themselves; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one lets one person's originality and another's untamed side vouch for each other.

Holding the Uranus, you find the other person's refusals refreshing rather than difficult: their no to convention sounds like your own yes to something new.

Holding the Lilith, you can stop rehearsing. The part of you that usually waits for a safe moment gets one, often without having to ask, and you return the favour by taking the other's oddities seriously.

When the Uranus falls in the Lilith person's 3rd house, for instance, the ease is in speech: the two say things to each other they'd say to no one else.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Ease can skip the hard questions. Two people who make each other feel free may stop asking whether a provocation is honest or just entertaining, or turn into a small club of two against everyone else's conventions. Because their limits rarely collide, they may also never practise negotiating them, and the first real clash can arrive with no habits to meet it. Flow still needs steering.

- **[Uranus conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** the two land on one degree, and recognition can turn into exposure.
- **[Uranus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** a door to try, not a current to ride.
- **[Uranus square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-lilith-synastry/):** restlessness and refusal grinding until they agree terms.
- **[Uranus opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** each sees in the other the freedom or the refusal they hold back.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep a window open to the rest of the world. Check, now and then, whether the freedom you give each other also reaches the people around you; and talk about limits while it's easy, before you need to. Partners can bring friends into the private jokes; friends can let a third voice into their plans; colleagues who work well against the grain can still explain their method to the team.

Someone born in mid-1985, with Lilith in Taurus, has it with anyone born, on and off, from February 1988 to November 1990, whose Uranus trines that Lilith. Their own Uranus in Sagittarius trines the Lilith of anyone born from October to December 1993, or in the same months of 1996. Each of those spans covers months or years of births, and nobody in them chose the trine. In a synastry I only weigh contacts that reach the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven; read through the houses, this one shows two eras agreeing about how much freedom a person may take.

These spans come from mean Lilith, the one I use; true Lilith wobbles widely, at times by tens of degrees, and a chart drawn with it may disagree ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). The hour won't move either body far enough to matter to the trine, only the houses, so I begin with both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A person born with [Uranus trine Lilith](/astrology/uranus-trine-lilith/) finds it easy to be themselves without edits. Between two charts that ease is lent and borrowed, and what it touches deserves care, so I look only when both people ask ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
