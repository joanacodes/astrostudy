Across from the Ascendant sits the 7th house, the chart's place for the person facing you. With Chiron there, the tender spot tends to show up between people rather than inside you: in what partners bring out, in the bonds that teach you most, and in how you learn to be close without losing yourself.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Partners of every kind belong to [the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) — love, marriage, business, the open rival across the table — an angular house whose natural sign is Libra. With [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) here, the adapting happens inside relationship itself. The sensitivity often concerns being met as an equal, choosing people who echo an old hurt, or meeting in someone else a part of yourself you'd rather not see. It is done with another person: learning how much to give, how to ask, how to stay yourself in company — and it often turns into an unusual talent for one-to-one work.

The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the degree that was setting in the west as you were born, is the cusp of the 7th house in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, so a Chiron right beside it is angular and loud. Without your birth time there is no Descendant to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). It follows [Chiron in the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-in-6th-house/), where the adapting is about the working day, and comes before [Chiron in the 8th house](/astrology/chiron-in-8th-house/), where closeness goes deeper still.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

This is where the placement lives. You may be drawn to people who need looking after, or who repeat a pattern you know too well, and recognise the choice only later. You may also be the person others open up to on a second date. The adapting that works is mutual: support that goes both ways, needs said aloud, repair after a quarrel rather than retreat. A partner who can hear *that hurt* without getting defensive gives this Chiron its best chance. And because a chart is personal, I only read a couple's charts when both people have agreed ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## At work {#at-work}

One-to-one work is the natural ground: counselling, coaching, mediation, consulting, any partnership where you sit opposite one person and help them. You often hear what isn't said, and adapt your approach to whoever is in front of you. Business partnerships can be rich and tricky; clear agreements protect both sides, you included. In a team, you often become the person colleagues come to one at a time, a strength worth pricing properly if you work for yourself.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The risk is losing yourself in the other person, or keeping everyone at arm's length to avoid it. On a hard day this Chiron may rescue a partner so as not to need rescuing, accept less than it deserves, or leave at the first sign of an old hurt. Rivals and critics can loom larger than they are. Endings can weigh more than they should, too, as if one goodbye proved an old fear right. And because the 7th mirrors the 1st, what you can't yet see in yourself often turns up first in someone else.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice the pattern before you choose, not after: write down what drew you to the people who mattered, and what you would want next time. Ask for what you need early in a relationship, while it's still easy. Let someone close help you without rushing to even the score. Couples counselling, or simply a regular, calm check-in with a partner, suits a placement that learns best in conversation.

My notebook's three steady placements — Saturn, Chiron and Lilith, each holding 75 to 90% of the time — get the most care in my readings ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), and a 7th-house Chiron gets it twice over, because it's about you and someone else.
