The 11th house is where a chart opens onto other people in the plural. Not the one person across the table, as in the 7th, but the friends, groups and causes you belong to, and the hopes you carry with them.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 11th house as the house of belonging and of the future. Friends, especially the ones you choose rather than inherit; the groups, teams, networks and communities you're part of; the people who help you without having to; and the hopes and plans you'd like to see through. It's where you ask *who are my people?* and *what are we working towards?*

- **A succedent house.** It follows an angle, and succedent houses are where things settle and build up: what you achieve in the 10th, you share and keep alive with others in the 11th. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) and [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), with [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) as Aquarius's traditional ruler: community, independence, the long view.
- **The Good Spirit.** Tradition gave it that name and counted it among the fortunate houses, the place of friends, hopes and benefactors. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the planet of growth and generosity, rejoices here ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)).
- **Its opposite.** The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/): the 5th is what you enjoy for yourself and with the people you love, the 11th what you share with many.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), just before it, is your public role; the 11th is the people who stand with you in it. The [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/), just after it, is where the crowd thins out into solitude and rest.

Like every house, the 11th is placed by your birth time: without it, there's no telling which of your planets sit here ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 11th house {#planets}

A planet in the 11th house shows itself among friends and in groups: it describes who you gather with, and what you hope for together.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith are the three I read most slowly: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) they held 75 to 90% of the time, against about a third for most placements. With Saturn, [Chiron in the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-in-11th-house/) or [Lilith in the 11th house](/astrology/lilith-in-11th-house/) here, a reading of mine would slow down around belonging: where you were let in, and where you weren't.

- **[Sun in the 11th house](/astrology/sun-in-11th-house/):** you shine in groups, friendships and shared causes; your purpose grows bigger than you.
- **[Moon in the 11th house](/astrology/moon-in-11th-house/):** friends are your family; you feel safe in a circle, and look after the people in it.
- **[Mercury in the 11th house](/astrology/mercury-in-11th-house/):** a natural networker; ideas travel fast through your friendships.
- **[Venus in the 11th house](/astrology/venus-in-11th-house/):** warm, easy friendships; you love being part of a group, and friends sometimes become more.
- **[Mars in the 11th house](/astrology/mars-in-11th-house/):** you fight for causes and for your friends; energy for group projects, and some friction in teams.
- **[Jupiter in the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house/):** in its joy: generous friendships, big hopes, and people who help you as much as you help them.
- **[Saturn in the 11th house](/astrology/saturn-in-11th-house/):** few, chosen friends; responsibility in groups, and hopes you build slowly.
- **[Uranus in the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-in-11th-house/):** in the house of its own sign: unusual friends and causes, and a need to belong without having to fit in.
- **[Neptune in the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-in-11th-house/):** ideals shared with others; compassionate friendships, and the need to see friends as they are.
- **[Pluto in the 11th house](/astrology/pluto-in-11th-house/):** intense loyalties and strong group dynamics; you change the groups you join, or they change you.

No planet here? An [empty 11th house](/astrology/empty-houses/) doesn't mean a life without friends. It's read through the sign on its cusp and the place of its ruler, the next two sections.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the cusp of the 11th house describes how you make friends and join in, and the style of your hopes.

- **[Aries on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-11th-house-cusp/):** you make friends quickly and often lead the group; hopes you chase rather than wait for.
- **[Taurus on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-11th-house-cusp/):** few, lasting friendships; hopes grounded in comfort and security.
- **[Gemini on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/):** many friends and many conversations; groups that trade ideas.
- **[Cancer on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends who become family; groups where you look after people.
- **[Leo on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** loyal, generous friendships; you bring warmth to a group, and big-hearted hopes.
- **[Virgo on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** you show friendship by helping; groups with a practical purpose.
- **[Libra on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-11th-house-cusp/):** you make friends easily and keep the peace in a group.
- **[Scorpio on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a few deep, loyal friends; trust in groups is earned slowly.
- **[Sagittarius on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends from everywhere; groups that gather around ideas, travel or belief.
- **[Capricorn on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friendships built over years; long-range goals, and responsibility in groups.
- **[Aquarius on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** the house's natural sign: community, causes and freedom, and friends who share your ideals.
- **[Pisces on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-11th-house-cusp/):** compassionate friendships; groups bound by art, faith or care, and boundaries to keep in a crowd.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 11th house is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp, and where it sits shows where your friendships and hopes play out: where you find your people, and what you build with them. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the cusp, there's a modern and a traditional ruler, and I read both ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** friends shape who you are; you often embody what your group stands for.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** friendships and networks connect to what you earn and value.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** friends among neighbours, siblings or classmates; groups for talking and learning.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** friends who feel like family; groups that gather at home.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** friends who share your pleasures and creations; friendship and play overlap.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** friends from work; groups built around service or a daily practice.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** a partner who's also a friend; friendships that turn into partnerships.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** deep, intense friendships; resources or secrets shared with a group.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** friends from abroad; groups gathered around study, travel or belief.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** your network and your work feed each other; friends in your professional world.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** the ruler at home: a strong social life, and hopes you pursue with others.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** few or private friendships; groups that work quietly, behind the scenes.

## Transits through the 11th house {#transits}

The Moon passes through your 11th house every month, the Sun once a year, Mercury, Venus and Mars for a few weeks at a time: an invitation, a reunion, a group chat that won't stop. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-11th-house/):** about a year in the house of its joy, when friendships and hopes tend to grow and new circles open.
- **[Saturn transiting the 11th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-11th-house/):** about two and a half years of finding out which friendships hold, and of taking on responsibility in a group.
- **[Uranus transiting the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-11th-house/):** around seven years of new, unusual circles, and of needing to belong on your own terms.
- **[Neptune transiting the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-11th-house/):** around fourteen years of shared ideals and causes — and of learning to see groups and friends clearly.
- **[Pluto transiting the 11th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-11th-house/):** a long, deep change in your friendships and in the groups you give your energy to.
- **[Chiron transiting the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-11th-house/):** an old wound around belonging or being left out comes up to be tended; its pace is uneven, from under two years to about eight in a sign.
- **[North Node transiting the 11th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-11th-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards shared hopes, while the South Node crosses your 5th.

I read these as weather, not a forecast: they describe how your social life and your hopes may feel for a while, not who will come or go.
