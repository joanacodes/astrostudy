The progressed Moon reaches Cancer, its own sign, after the talkative years of Gemini. For two years or so the Moon is at home, and your needs tend to come home with it: to the people closest to you, the place you live, the past you come from.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The progressed Moon](/astrology/progressed-moon/) moves through your [secondary progressions](/astrology/secondary-progressions/) a sign every two years or so, and in [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) it is in its domicile, the sign it rules. Cardinal water gives it a protective manner: the need to belong, to look after and be looked after, to have a place to come back to, moves to the centre. Feelings run closer to the surface and to memory, and tone matters more than words. It might show as a call to a relative you’d lost touch with, a wish to cook for people, a pull towards the place you grew up. Being in its own sign, the Moon speaks plainly here, and I read the chapter as needs that know what they are.

Cancer’s care isn’t dependence, and its shelter isn’t a wall: the chapter can be as much about building a home as retreating into one. Because the Moon rules Cancer, the house your natal Moon occupies shows where these needs tend to lead, while the house the progressed Moon crosses shows where they gather now. Both take [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), which sets every cusp in Placidus, the house system I use; I don’t read a chart without it.

Tradition linked the Moon with mothers and Cancer with the family, and these years are sometimes read as a forecast of pregnancy. I don’t read them that way, and no progression predicts one ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)); what the chapter describes is your own need for closeness, at any age and in any household.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Its own sign doesn’t keep it longer. In the births I counted, from 1930 to 2009, the homecoming lasted between two and two and a half years, usually about two years and four months, the number of years matching the days the real Moon took to cross Cancer after each birth. If you want your own dates, a progressed chart shows the month the Moon crosses into Cancer and the month it leaves; they are only as accurate as the birth time behind them.

It comes after the [progressed Moon in Gemini](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-gemini/) and before the [progressed Moon in Leo](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-leo/). With a natal [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/), this is a homecoming twice over: the progressed Moon returns to its birth sign about every 27 years, the time of one round of the zodiac, and its first stay there, from birth, was only part of a crossing, since it began wherever your Moon stood. The last months of a Cancer chapter can feel like stepping out of a shell, which is where Leo takes over.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make your space feel like yours. In a Cancer chapter, a room that holds you, a meal shared with people you trust, an evening at home without guilt are not luxuries but the chapter’s own way of restoring you.

Let yourself be looked after as well as looking after others: care that flows only one way tires even the most caring sign. If memories come up, of family, of places, of who you used to be, write them down; they are material for the life you are living now. And keep a door open, because Leo comes next and will want you to step out and be seen, which is easier from a home that feels secure.
