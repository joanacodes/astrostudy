The scales and the twins: both of your lights sit in signs whose symbols come in twos, and both in air. Your sense of self forms by weighing one thing against another, and your feelings settle by comparing, naming, turning a question over with someone else.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Libra is cardinal air and Gemini mutable air. Counted back from the Sun, Gemini is four signs away, a [trine](/astrology/trine/) by sign, and the two lights share an element and a way of taking in the world: through words, ideas and other people. The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) wants the fair answer, the one that does justice to every side; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) needs the conversation that gets there, with its detours and second thoughts. Most days the two keep each other good company.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra and [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Gemini: judgement and taste for the Sun, language and curiosity for the Moon. The Moon has no dignity in Gemini, and the Sun's [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Libra I read as a self that does its best thinking in consultation. Both rulers are on the list my [notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) calls the placements everyone reads first: in my count they held about a third of the time, as the Sun sign and the rising sign did, a long way short of Saturn, Chiron and Lilith at 75 to 90%. So I read the two rulers with interest, and weigh them against the rest of the chart.

With the Moon 211° to 269° ahead of the Sun, you were born in a lunar month 17 to 22 days old, a few days after a full moon. Most of these births, about seven in eight, fall in the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/), a phase that turns what you've learnt into something you hand on; the earliest are still in the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase. A Moon close to 240° gives a waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/), and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows where the two phases meet.

This air pair turns up in about 1 birth in 145. A Moon that changes sign every two and a half days or so may have moved from Taurus into Gemini, or from Gemini into Cancer, on your birthday, and only the hour can say whether you belong here or with the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-taurus/) or the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-cancer/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It decides the rising sign too, and with both lights in air, it is often the rising sign and the houses that bring earth or water into the picture.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love runs on conversation for you, and on fairness when you disagree. The Libra Sun cares about the balance between you; the Gemini Moon about the liveliness: news shared, questions asked, an argument enjoyed rather than feared. Silence is your hardest weather, and a partner who withdraws can leave you talking to fill the space. Because you explain your feelings so well, people may assume they've been felt. Now and then, say the short version, *that hurt*, and stop there.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that carries understanding from one person to another suits this pair: teaching, editing, translating, interpreting, mediating, any role where you explain one side to the other. The disseminating Moon likes to pass on what it has worked out, and the Libra Sun wants it passed on fairly. You may be the colleague who writes the summary everyone actually reads. Make sure your own view gets into it, not only everyone else's.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two air signs can hold a question open for a very long time. The Sun weighs, the Moon gathers one more opinion, and a decision that needed a day takes a season. Feelings, too, can be discussed so thoroughly that they never quite get felt. Give the conversation an ending: a date by which you'll choose, a walk taken alone and in silence, and a moment afterwards to notice what you feel when there is nobody to tell.
