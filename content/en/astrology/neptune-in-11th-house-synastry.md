Their Neptune in your 11th house is the overlay of shared dreams. Another person's ideals reach your friends, your circles and what you hope for, and together you may imagine a better world, or at least a better weekend.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dreams in the plural: its sign is a generation's dream, and its compassion reaches beyond the people it knows. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is where you belong among many: the friends you chose, teams and networks, and the hopes you work towards with others. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their ideals enter your circle.

Around them, friendship can feel like kinship and causes like callings. You may join a choir, a campaign, a collective; your hopes for the future grow larger and kinder; and your friends may take to them as if they had always been one of you.

Your circle can look to them like the community they always wanted. They may idealise your friends, throw themselves into your causes, or feel that among your people their compassion finally has a place to go. The 11th is a succedent house, where what has been achieved is shared and kept alive. Its natural sign, [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), has two rulers, Saturn for the old astrologers and Uranus for modern tropical astrology; I read both, and the old name, Good Spirit, belonged to the house where Jupiter rejoiced.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Shared dreams can float. Projects imagined together may never find a treasurer, friendships drift because nobody checks in, and a group you idealise can disappoint when it turns out to be made of ordinary people. You might also lend them your circle's trust before you know them well.

From their side, being one of many can feel lonely: they may lose themselves in your crowd, or give to your causes until little is left for themselves.

No one is being misled. Neptune here describes hope and belonging, and hope is sturdiest when someone keeps the minutes.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give the dream a plan and the friendship a rhythm. Decide who does what in a shared project, set a date to meet, and let a cause stay one part of life rather than all of it. In a couple, shared friends and causes are lovely when each of you keeps a few of your own; between friends, the dream of a community grows from small, regular meetings; between colleagues in a network, idealism needs a budget; between adult relatives, family hopes go further when every voice is heard.

Seen from the other chart, it is your Neptune in their 11th house, your ideals moving through their friendships. [Their Neptune in your 10th house](/astrology/neptune-in-10th-house-synastry/) lends a glow to your public life; [their Neptune in your 12th house](/astrology/neptune-in-12th-house-synastry/) reaches your inner world.

This is the house where generations meet most plainly. Neptune's sign is shared by everyone in an age band several years wide, so if you're close in age you may each be living a version of the same collective dream, with their Neptune near your own ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)); with a wide gap, two generations' hopes for the future meet in your circle, and each has something to teach the other. Their birth date fixes Neptune's degree and their birth time their houses, and I start only when I have both. Your birth time places your 11th, which in Placidus, the house system I use, comes from dividing the time between your Midheaven and your Ascendant into three ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A friend's chart is no less private than a partner's, and it waits for their agreement too ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#instead)).
