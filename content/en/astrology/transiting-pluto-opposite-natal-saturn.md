Power measured against duty: that is Pluto opposite your natal Saturn. What you've built tends to meet someone else's pressure first, and the boundary between their power and your duty has to be found again.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) holds structure and responsibility: the commitments you keep, the authority you carry, the lines you draw. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) brings power and hidden motives to the surface so that what is unsustainable can be reorganised. Facing each other across an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, the two meet through other people more often than within you, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) usually works from the outside in: an employer's restructuring, an institution's rules, a partner's needs, a parent who now depends on you.

Saturn's fear is chaos and wasted effort; Pluto's blind spot is assuming every unequal exchange is deliberate domination. Together they can turn a hard negotiation into a siege. Empowerment isn't domination, on either side of the axis, and what tends to come out of this opposition is a cleaner boundary: duties you choose, authority you can hold without strain, and a clear no where one was needed.

For many people born from 1939 to 1941 or from 1973 to 1975, with Saturn about 90° behind Pluto ([Saturn square Pluto](/astrology/saturn-square-pluto/)), this opposition falls within a few years of their [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/), and I read the two together.

The axis runs through two houses. With Saturn in your 10th, Pluto opposes it from your 4th ([Pluto transiting the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-4th-house/)): what home and family need presses on the responsibilities of your working life. With Saturn in the 7th and Pluto in the 1st, your own changes test a commitment you made to someone. To know which houses the axis runs between, you need a reliable birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). And no transit forecasts a dismissal, a dispute or how one ends ([disclaimers](/disclaimers/#refusals)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Expect a long stay: with my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, the opposition lasts about two years and eight months, anywhere from twenty-two months to nearly four years, and Pluto's yearly retreat makes it exact three times in about three passes out of five, five times in the rest.

It misses just over half of the people born between 1930 and 2009 by 80; the rest meet it once. On the first day of the year, a birth in 2005 meets it at 15.1; in 1935 it came at 18.7, in 1975 at 40.1 and in 1945 at 66, while births on 1 January 1955, 1965, 1985 and 1995 never do by 80.

It is Saturn in Leo that faces Pluto in Aquarius until early 2044: the opposition comes between about 18 and 36 for a birth from 2005 to 2007, and between about 47 and 65 for one from 1975 to 1978 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)).

With the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-saturn/)), which misses 3 people in 5, it is one of the two rarer Pluto contacts with Saturn. The square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-saturn/)) comes to about 6 in 7, the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-saturn/)) to nearly 4 in 5 and the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-saturn/)) to about 3 in 4.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you are responsible for, and what you are not. An opposition eases once the line is visible to both sides.

Negotiate with the structure as well as the person. Institutions, rules and contracts have their own logic, and naming it takes the heat out of the exchange.

Keep one authority that is plainly yours: a skill, a standard, a promise you hold yourself to whatever anyone else decides.
