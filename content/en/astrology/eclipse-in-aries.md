Aries is the sign of starting out, and an eclipse there lands on whatever in your chart sits in the same few degrees, or in the matching degrees of Libra across the zodiac. Between 2000 and 2040 there are fourteen, in four bursts about nine years apart.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Aries](/astrology/aries/) is cardinal fire, ruled by Mars: initiative, directness, the urge to begin something under your own name. A [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) in Aries is a new moon with the Sun and Moon together in the sign; a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) in Aries is a full moon with the Moon in Aries and the Sun opposite, in Libra. Either way the line drawn is the Aries–Libra axis, self and other, and as the Moon’s nodes travel along it the eclipses alternate between this sign and its opposite.

In the rule I follow, from the AGS library, an eclipse counts only within about three degrees of a natal planet or angle, at either end of its axis, and I read it through that point and the house it sits in; a penumbral lunar eclipse weighs less. So the total lunar eclipse of 18 October 2032, at 25°59′ Aries, would underline a point near 26° Aries or 26° Libra and pass the rest of the chart by. Emphasis, then, never an omen. If your natal nodes lie on this axis, I look at both the mean and the true node, because they can differ by a degree or two and the rule is tight.

The fourteen, from 2000 to 2040:

- **2004–2006:** 19 April 2004, partial solar, 29°49′ · 8 April 2005, hybrid solar, 19°06′ · 17 October 2005, partial lunar, 24°07′ · 29 March 2006, total solar, 8°34′
- **2013–2015:** 18 October 2013, penumbral lunar, 25°51′ · 8 October 2014, total lunar, 15°07′ · 28 September 2015, total lunar, 4°38′
- **2023–2025:** 20 April 2023, hybrid solar, 29°50′ · 8 April 2024, total solar, 19°23′ · 29 March 2025, partial solar, 8°59′
- **2032–2034:** 18 October 2032, total lunar, 25°59′ · 30 March 2033, total solar, 10°20′ · 8 October 2033, total lunar, 15°27′ · 28 September 2034, partial lunar, 4°58′

The most recent was the partial solar eclipse of 29 March 2025; the next is the total lunar eclipse of 18 October 2032. Notice the pairs nineteen years apart, 2004 and 2023, 2006 and 2025, 2013 and 2032: the same date within a day, the same degree within a fraction. On either side, the eclipses fall in Pisces and in Taurus ([Eclipse in Pisces](/astrology/eclipse-in-pisces/), [Eclipse in Taurus](/astrology/eclipse-in-taurus/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house an Aries eclipse falls in depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), since the time sets every cusp; without it I don’t read a chart at all. In Placidus, the house system I use, each line applies only when an eclipse here meets a natal point in that house, and each reaches the opposite house too, through the same degrees of Libra.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aries-rising/):** Aries rising: how you step forward and present yourself; a fresh start made on your own terms.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aries-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you value and earn by your own effort; your sense of worth, standing on its own feet.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aries-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** speaking first; a conversation you open, or a sibling who goes their own way.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home and roots; a wish to set up on your own, or to change your part in the family.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aries-on-5th-house-cusp/):** play, romance and creative risk; something begun for the joy of it. Tradition also gave this house children; I read an adult’s pleasures here and never forecast a pregnancy ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)).
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aries-on-6th-house-cusp/):** working days; taking charge of how they run.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aries-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Aries on the Descendant, with Libra rising: partners who spur you on; who leads, and how often.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aries-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what’s shared and trusted; standing up, honestly, for your part of it.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aries-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs and journeys; a leap into study or travel that you decide alone.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/):** your public role; an initiative at work with your name on it.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aries-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends and groups; leading a shared project, or stepping out of one.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aries-on-12th-house-cusp/):** inner life; a quiet resolve forming before anyone else can see it.
