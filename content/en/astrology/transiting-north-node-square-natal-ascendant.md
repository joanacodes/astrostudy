Twice a round, halfway between the North Node's crossing of your Ascendant and its crossing of your Descendant, the axis of the nodes stands at right angles to your horizon. Placing either end takes an exact birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and for about a month your way into things and the way you are growing pull across each other.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is the orientation you start from, and your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), opposite, the frame in which you meet someone else. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the capacity you are practising and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) the one you consolidated long ago. A [square](/astrology/square/) works through friction that pushes adaptation, and with two axes crossing at a right angle, this [transit](/astrology/transits/) squares both ends of your horizon from both ends of the nodes.

Lived, it tends to show as a manner that no longer fits. The first move you have always made, the joke, the caution, the quick yes, gets in the way of what you are learning, and the newer approach doesn't feel like you yet. The way you meet others gets the same friction, which can make a conversation harder for a few weeks without saying anything about where a relationship is going. The square asks the manner to adjust: same person, a different way in.

In Placidus, the house system I use, one square usually comes while the node crosses your 4th or 3rd house, before it reaches your Ascendant ([North Node transiting the 4th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-4th-house/)), the other while it crosses your 10th or 9th, after ([North Node transiting the 10th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-10th-house/)). Your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is rarely exactly 90° from your Ascendant, so the squares come near the top and the bottom of your chart without sitting on them. From July 2026 to March 2028, with the node in Aquarius, Scorpio rising meets the first kind of square and Taurus rising the second.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The true node usually needs about a month over the square, counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after. An eclipse season in the same weeks slows it almost to a halt while the Sun is near the nodes, and the square lasts three months or more; about one pass in 25 brings three exact hits, and the season's eclipses fall at right angles to your horizon. With your rising degree midway along the axis, neither node touches it, so this square isn't among my forecast's seasons; I read it as background to the transits that do touch your angles.

A few minutes of birth time make a real difference. Twins born ten minutes apart have Ascendants about two and a half degrees apart on average, and the node squares one of them about seven weeks after the other.

It comes twice a round, eight or nine times by 80, about four and a half years either side of [Transiting North Node opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-ascendant/). Between each square and [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) comes [Transiting North Node sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-ascendant/); on the side of the opposition, [Transiting North Node trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-ascendant/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Watch your first move for a week: what you do in the first minute of a meeting, a call, a new task. Change one part of it.

Let a new approach feel awkward. A manner that is being adjusted looks unfinished for a while, and that is all it is.

When a conversation catches, say so: “I’m trying a different way of doing this.” Most people make room for it.
