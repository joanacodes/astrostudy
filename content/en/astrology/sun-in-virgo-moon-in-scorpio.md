A Virgo Sun notices the detail everyone else missed; a Scorpio Moon can't rest until it knows what is going on beneath the surface. The Moon was a young crescent in the evening sky when you were born, and I read the pair as close attention with depth behind it: you see the small thing, and you feel what it means.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable earth and fixed water, two signs apart with the Moon ahead: a sextile by sign, two signs that cooperate as long as both are given work to do. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) builds itself on discernment, on telling the right thing from the nearly right; the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) settles once trust has been tested and what was hidden has been understood. Between them very little gets past you, and most of it gets kept.

Scorpio is the Moon's [fall](/astrology/fall/) in the old scheme, which describes effort, not a smaller heart: your feelings need more time and more truth before they rest. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your Sun's sign and is exalted there. Of Scorpio's two rulers I read both, [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the older one, for the lines this Moon draws and the battles it chooses, and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the modern one, for the depths it goes to when something matters. With a Virgo Sun, the useful question is often which of the two the analysing mind is working for.

Look west after sunset in the first week of a lunar month and you see the Moon you were born under: a crescent, its lit side growing, 31° to 89° out from the Sun and two and a half to seven days old. Rudhyar's [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) phase takes the great bulk of these births, about seven in eight, and his [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase the earliest, and where the gap reached about 60° the lights also make a [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). A crescent birth, as [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains, is about needs that have just begun to separate from purpose and want their own say.

This pair belongs to roughly 1 person in 143. Should the Moon have been passing from Libra into Scorpio, or on into Sagittarius, on your birthday, the hour separates this page from the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-libra/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-sagittarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). A pair this keen on what lies beneath the surface deserves the whole chart beneath it, and the rising sign and houses wait on the same hour.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love discreetly and deeply. The Scorpio Moon gives its trust after a long look and then holds nothing back; the Virgo Sun shows love through attention, the remembered worry, the practical help that arrives before it's asked for. What you need is honesty, all of it, and privacy respected. A small lie unsettles you more than a hard truth. Hurts can be filed in great detail, so say the difficult thing while it's still one sentence long.

## At work {#at-work}

Research, investigation, editing, auditing, restoration: you're well suited to work that rewards close attention and the patience to look underneath. The Sun sees the error, the Moon senses what the error means, and together they make you good at problems other people have given up on. Gossip and hidden agendas wear on the Moon, careless work on the Sun. You tend to be trusted with confidential things, and rightly.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Attention and depth can turn inward and circle. The Virgo Sun analyses a hurt and the Scorpio Moon keeps it, so between them a single wound can be filed in such detail that it stays open for years. A mind this observant can also find a reason for suspicion in almost anything. What helps is closing files on purpose: say what happened, to the person or to a page, decide what you'll keep, and hand the rest to something physical, a long walk, a swim, a slow evening.
