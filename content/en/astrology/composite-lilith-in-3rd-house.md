Some of a couple's frankest conversations happen in the car, on the way back from a family lunch, and go no further. With composite Lilith in the 3rd house, that running commentary is where a relationship's untamed side lives, along with everything it refuses to repeat.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Everything within a short walk or a quick message belongs to [the 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/): the chat over breakfast, the errands, the lessons picked up on the way, brothers, sisters and the people next door. A cadent house with Gemini as its natural sign, it was called the Goddess by tradition, which gave the Moon its joy there. Lilith is a point of the Moon's own orbit, but that is a coincidence of names, not a rule: the joys were shared out among the seven traditional planets, and [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) has none. Read in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the 3rd is the relationship's ordinary traffic: how it talks, and how it moves through its days. With Lilith here, that talk has a hidden channel: frank, funny, sometimes subversive in private, guarded on the doorstep, at the school gate, with the siblings on either side.

That channel needn't reflect how either of you talks with anyone else. Two discreet people can share a conversation that would startle their friends, and two chatty ones a bond that clams up the moment a relative walks in. What it refuses is repetition: the family line, the neighbourhood's view, the opinion everyone in the group chat holds. The bond checks things for itself, or says nothing.

Which house Lilith sits in rests on both birth times, and the composite can only be as sure as the less certain of the two. With the midpoints of your charts' cusps, an error in either time moves the composite cusps by half as much as it moves that person's own; with houses built from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, it moves that Midheaven and everything hung on it. A Lilith near either edge of the 3rd needs both times checked before it is read at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

If one of you has [Lilith in the 3rd house](/astrology/lilith-in-3rd-house/) in your own chart, you know a voice that learned when not to speak; if the other's Lilith falls in your 3rd ([their Lilith in your 3rd house](/astrology/lilith-in-3rd-house-synastry/)), you know their frankness turning up in your daily exchanges. Neither is this placement, which belongs to the conversation itself. [Composite Lilith in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-2nd-house/) keeps its values quiet instead, and [composite Lilith in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-4th-house/) withdraws indoors, into home.

The difficulty is the backlog. What the pair can't say to the people nearby still has to go somewhere, and it can turn into sharpness about them, or into a habit of talking about everyone else instead of about the two of you. It helps to let the private channel carry your own business as well as other people's, and now and then to say one true thing outside it, kindly, to someone close.

Composites aren't part of my readings; I describe the method as its practitioners use it, alongside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it foretells no quarrel and no rift with family. As with any chart that reads two people's private talk, both should know it is being looked at ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
