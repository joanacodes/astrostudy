After the depths of Scorpio, the progressed Moon comes up for air in Sagittarius. For two years or so, what comforts you is room: a wider view, a question worth following, a plan with a horizon in it, and the feeling that life still has somewhere to go.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The progressed Moon](/astrology/progressed-moon/), quickest of the points in [secondary progressions](/astrology/secondary-progressions/), sets the mood of each chapter, and in [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire ruled by Jupiter, that mood opens out. Needs turn towards meaning and perspective: to learn something that changes how you see, to talk about what matters rather than what is urgent, to move, whether across a map or across an idea. Feelings in this sign are frank and buoyant, and a hard week is easier to carry when you can see where it fits in a longer story. It can show as a course you finally take, a belief you re-examine, a friendship with someone whose world is wider than yours.

The Moon has no dignity in Sagittarius, and the sign’s freedom isn’t restlessness for its own sake: it is the need to keep growing, which can live just as well inside a settled life as on the road. Honesty matters too; in a Sagittarius chapter, comfort that depends on pretending tends to wear thin quickly, and a true word, even an awkward one, can feel like relief. Generosity grows too, in Jupiter’s sign: giving what you can, and enjoying the giving, is part of how this chapter looks after you.

None of this foretells a journey or a move; a progression describes a need, and where you meet it is your choice. Which area of life the chapter’s search for meaning gathers in depends on the house the progressed Moon crosses, and that depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): in Placidus, the system I use, an exact time places each cusp, and I don’t read a chart without one.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

For births from 1930 to 2009, the progressed Moon crossed Sagittarius in two to two and a half years, usually about two years and four months, a year of the chapter for each day the real Moon spent in the sign after the birth. Mutable signs are no quicker than fixed ones in this respect: the pace is the Moon’s own, set in the first days of your life.

It takes over from the [progressed Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-scorpio/) and hands on to the [progressed Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/progressed-moon-in-capricorn/): from the depths, to the horizon, to the long climb. The progressed Moon goes round the zodiac in about 27 years, so in a long life this chapter can come round two or three times, and each visit tends to ask a slightly different question about where you are heading.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the chapter a horizon. It needn’t be a trip: a subject to study, a reading list, a plan for the next stage of your life or a conversation with someone whose views differ from yours can all serve. Jupiter rules Sagittarius, and the house of your natal Jupiter shows where the wish to grow tends to look for space.

Say yes to the invitation that widens your week, and no to the one that only fills it. Keep your word, and keep your sense of humour; both are Sagittarius’s own ways of looking after itself. If freedom and commitment pull against each other in these years, look for the version of each that can live with the other. Capricorn comes next and will want something built on what you discovered, so it helps to write down what you learned while it is fresh.
