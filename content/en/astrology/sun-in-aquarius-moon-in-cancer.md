Your Moon is at home in Cancer, the sign it rules, while your Sun stands in Aquarius, the sign tradition calls its detriment, and no other pair has that combination. You were born under a swelling Moon, a few days short of full. I read the pair as a self that works for people in general, with feelings that need one home and the people in it.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Five signs separate Aquarius from Cancer, which makes a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign: fixed air and cardinal water, with no element, mode or polarity in common, so each light keeps adjusting to the other. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) builds its sense of self around fairness for everyone and the future of the group; the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/) protects, and settles once the people it counts as its own are safe and fed. One light thinks in populations, the other in names. You may campaign for strangers all week and still need your evenings to be about the same five people.

The Moon rules Cancer, its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), so your Moon answers to no other planet: its own house and aspects carry its story. Aquarius, facing Leo, is the Sun's [detriment](/astrology/detriment/). It has two rulers, and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern one, for where your Sun insists on doing things differently, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional one, for the obligations it takes on. Saturn has its own detriment in Cancer, so the planet that gives your Sun its structure is least comfortable in your Moon's sign. I read that as principles that need translating before they feel like home, and a home that does better with a few rules of its own.

The Moon was 121° to 179° ahead of the Sun, ten to almost fifteen days after a new moon, high in the evening and nearly round. The [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) phase holds seven of these births in eight, the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) phase the earliest, and at 150°, in the middle of the range, the lights make an exact quincunx as well. The full moon that followed, within about five days, was usually a [full moon in Leo](/astrology/full-moon-in-leo/). I read the gibbous phase as needs adjusting themselves before they show, which is the quincunx's work too; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains how it differs from the phase before.

Of every 149 people born in the years counted, about one has this pair, and the date alone can't always settle it. The Moon passes from Gemini into Cancer, and from Cancer into Leo, within about two and a half days, so a birthday on a crossing could be the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-gemini/) or the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-leo/) until the hour is known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With the Moon carrying so much here, its house matters more than usual, and like the rising sign it can't be found without the time.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You protect the people you love and give them room to be themselves, which is a rarer pair of gifts than it sounds. The Cancer Moon remembers, feeds and shelters; the Aquarius Sun insists that everyone, a partner included, stays free and equal. What can get lost is your own need. You may find it easier to defend someone's right to independence than to tell them you missed them, and a partner who asks, kindly and often, how you are reaches the Moon.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that builds care into a system suits you: housing, community services, mutual aid, public organisations, design for homes and neighbourhoods. The Sun sees how care could be made fair and available to everyone; the Moon makes sure it actually reaches someone. A cold, purely procedural workplace costs the Moon a great deal, even when the Sun agrees with every rule in it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The quincunx asks for constant small adjustments, and here they run between the general and the personal. You may feel guilty for putting your own people first, or restless when home closes in. Neither feeling is a fault. What helps is giving each light its own territory and time, the cause in its hours and the household in its own, and keeping a few friends who belong to both.
