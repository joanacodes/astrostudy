Gemini on the 11th house cusp opens the house of the people you build a future with in the sign of the go-between. Friendship, here, lives on news, ideas and introductions, and your circle tends to mix people who would never otherwise have met.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), the house of the people you choose to stand with, the sign on the cusp is the way you join in ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) there, you join in by connecting: the friend who knows someone who knows someone, the group chat that keeps a dozen people in touch, the club you came to for one subject and stayed in for the talk. You often belong to several circles at once and act as a bridge between them. Collective plans take shape in conversation, half-formed ideas tried out loud until one of them becomes a project. Friendship isn't measured by numbers here: a few people you can think with matter more than a crowd that agrees. Belonging doesn't mean agreeing, either: you can stay in a group whose views you argue with, as long as the arguing is welcome. Tradition called the 11th the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) here, and with Gemini on the cusp, the house of good company runs on exchange.

[Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/) comes with this cusp in nearly every chart at London and New York, so the Sun rules your chart while [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your friendships: warmth is what people meet first, and among friends a quicker, more curious side comes out. Mercury's house is where your networks form and your shared plans head: the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Its other sign, Virgo, opens your 2nd house in nearly 9 charts in 10, sometimes the 3rd as well, so the people you know and what you earn and value answer to one planet. I read your friendships and your resources side by side, never as a promise that one will feed the other.

About 1 birth in 12 has Gemini on the 11th house cusp, at London as at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use: two hours or so of each day, the even share. The 11th is wide in these charts, about 34° to 39° at London, so Gemini never opens the 10th or the 12th as well, and in about 1 London chart in 6 the whole of Cancer lies inside the 11th too ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), adding the Moon's care to your friendships. The Midheaven is usually Taurus, in about 7 London charts in 10, and Aries otherwise. Opposite, Sagittarius opens your 5th, so Jupiter rules your pleasures and loves while Mercury rules your friendships: a romance tends to begin in a wide, generous mood, a friendship in a long conversation. [Gemini on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/) and [Gemini on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-12th-house-cusp/) are other people's charts, with Virgo and Cancer rising.

A difference of an hour moves every one of these cusps, the Ascendant included, so the time on your birth record decides whether this page is yours ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
