Some 42 years after Uranus crosses your Venus, it reaches the far side of the zodiac from it. For about twenty months love and freedom face each other, and freedom usually arrives wearing someone else's face.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is attraction, reciprocity and value: what you want, and what feels fair between you and the people you love. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees. In an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, each function becomes visible against the other, so here your attachments meet a demand for room that seems to come from outside you.

Often it's a partner, a friend or a relative who changes first: new interests, new people, a wish for time of their own. Sometimes the attraction is the change: someone quite unlike your usual taste turns your head, and the question is what that says about what you've been missing. Either way, the opposition shows what you need from closeness by showing you what you can give.

No transit ends a relationship, and I don't read one for it. If you're tempted to look up a partner's chart to see what they'll do, talk to them instead; I don't read anyone's chart without their consent ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

With Venus in your 5th and Uranus crossing your 11th ([Uranus transiting the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-11th-house/)), friends, groups and causes pull on the time you'd give to romance and play, and a balance between the two has to be found. Your birth time decides whether this is the axis you have, and without it I don't read the opposition at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Venus opposite Uranus](/astrology/venus-opposite-uranus/), you'll know the pattern well: freedom tends to show up first in someone else.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The opposition keeps within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, for about twenty months; a few passes manage it in about eleven. Uranus crosses the exact point three times in more than nine passes out of ten, so a conversation started at the first is rarely the last word.

It falls about 42 years after [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-venus/): if Uranus crossed your Venus at 10, the opposition comes at about 52, and if the crossing comes at 50, the opposition came at about 8. [Transiting Uranus sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-venus/), [transiting Uranus square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-venus/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-venus/) each come twice in the same round, which is why most people meet them more often than this. By 80, all but about one person in 50 have had it.

The Sun is never far from Venus, at most about 47° away, so the opposition to your Sun ([transiting Uranus opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-sun/)) falls within about eleven years of this one, whichever comes first. Like every [transit](/astrology/transits/), each describes a climate, not an outcome.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask what the other person needs before deciding what it means. A request for room is information about them as much as about you.

Let a bond change shape before you decide what it is. Under this opposition arrangements loosen and settle again, and judging at the first loosening is premature.

Notice what attracts you in someone very different. It usually points to a part of your own life that wants more air.
