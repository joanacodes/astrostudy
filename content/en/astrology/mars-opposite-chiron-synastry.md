One of you goes straight at a thing; the other has learned that some things only open from the side. Mars opposite Chiron in synastry puts one person's Mars and the other's Chiron at opposite ends of the zodiac, and each keeps seeing, in the other, the approach they use least.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) goes straight at things: initiative, effort, assertion, competing openly, holding a limit. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the way round: adapting to a limit that persists, compensating, building skill through repeated practice until something becomes possible after all. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets the two as poles of one axis, and among the pair's [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this is the mirror.

Across from your Mars, the other person can teach you that a closed door sometimes has a window: their patience with a limit, their inventive workarounds, their sense of what effort really costs. Across from your Chiron, the other person can show you what it looks like to act without first proving you're entitled to: to try the direct route now and then, and find it holds.

Mars in Pisces opposite Chiron in Virgo is a case in point, on the mutable axis: a fluid, intuitive drive across from a sensitivity around getting things exactly right.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The mirror can be uncomfortable. If the Mars is yours, the other person's tender place may stir your own, and you may push harder, or rush to rescue, rather than sit with it. If the Chiron is yours, their directness can land right where you're tender, and you may defer to it, or bristle against it, instead of offering your own method.

When the roles freeze, the pair can settle into a story of the strong one and the one with the limit. It's the wrong story. Adapting is a strength, and leaning on someone now and then is not the same as depending on them; an opposition is a balance, not a ranking.

Turned to the pair's other angles:

- **[Mars conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-chiron-synastry/):** no mirror, just one meeting point, where pace becomes everything.
- **[Mars sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-chiron-synastry/):** four signs nearer, an exchange of nudges and methods, on request.
- **[Mars square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-chiron-synastry/):** a quarter-turn away, where the push catches the sore spot sideways.
- **[Mars trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-chiron-synastry/):** support that flows within one element, with nothing to reflect.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take turns teaching. Let the Chiron person lead where their methods work better, and the Mars person where a direct approach saves time. Say out loud what each of you finds hard and what each finds easy; the lists are usually mirror images. Between colleagues, that might mean pairing on a task instead of dividing it; between friends, swapping who plans and who adapts; in a couple, noticing who always rescues and who always copes, and trading.

About 1 pair of people in 15, one way round or the other, has this opposition within 6°, a little more often than the conjunction. The Chiron end is a matter of birth years, how many depending on the sign; Mars, a few weeks in each sign, is what turns that shared position into a mirror between two particular people. The houses decide which areas of life the mirror shows, and they need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron stands out in my notebook, one of three placements, with Saturn and Lilith, to hold 75 to 90% of the time; though every result came from someone's own chart, no pair was ever counted, and so this opposition gets no figure, only care ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). Because it touches what someone guards, a reading of it needs a yes from each of you ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Inside one chart, [Mars opposite Chiron](/astrology/mars-opposite-chiron/) belongs to someone who keeps catching sight of their tender place in other people's pushes. Between two charts, those pushes are real, and so is the chance to answer them.
