Sun sextile North Node in synastry places one person's Sun 60° from the other's North Node, and therefore 120° from their South Node. It works as two links at once: an opening towards what the second person is learning, and an easy flow with what they already do well.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is purpose and vitality, the self a person expresses. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a direction of growth, a capacity built by repeated practice, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite holds what came early and easily. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the Sun to the first through opportunity and cooperation; the trine it makes to the second lets the familiar come along. Few [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) offer a stretch with this much safety under it.

If the North Node is yours, this person tends to enjoy you as you already are, and that's exactly what makes it easier to try something new around them. You don't have to give up what you're good at to experiment. Their presence says, without saying it, *go on, and you can always come back.*

Carrying the Sun, you'll likely find the other person easy company, and you may catch yourself suggesting things for them: the course, the role, the conversation they keep postponing. Nothing about it asks you to change. You simply happen to stand where their next step and their old ground can both see you. With the North Node in Gemini, say, where the growth is towards asking rather than already knowing, a Sun in Leo shares the fire of the Sagittarius South Node and still has an ear for a good question.

A colleague with this contact is the one who values your solid skills and still hands you the unfamiliar task; a friend, the one who comes along to the first class. In a family or a couple, it can be the person who makes trying feel safe.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Of the two links, the trine to the South Node is the easier one, and ease pulls. A pair can settle into the North Node person's established strengths, admired and comfortable, and never take up the sextile's opening. The Sun person may, without meaning to, praise the familiar more than the attempts. An opening needs someone to step through it; potential isn't a promise.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Say the new thing out loud, then do a small piece of it together. If the Sun is yours, notice the attempts, not only the polished skills; if the North Node is yours, treat the welcome as a base to push off from rather than a place to stay.

The orb is 4.8°, the narrowest I use for this pair, and the count about 1 pair of people in 10, with the Sun on either side. With an orb that tight, the degree or two between the mean and the true node can matter: near the edge, one may show the sextile and the other not. I look at both ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). Each chart also needs its birth time, as every synastry I read does ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), because the houses say where in each life the opening sits.

Inside a single birth chart this is [Sun sextile North Node](/astrology/sun-sextile-north-node/). Among the pair's other contacts, [Sun trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-north-node-synastry/) reverses the emphasis, flowing towards the growth and only opening onto the familiar; [Sun conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) and [Sun opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-north-node-synastry/) sit on each end of the axis, and [Sun square North Node in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-north-node-synastry/) across its middle.
