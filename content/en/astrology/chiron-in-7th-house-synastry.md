When a bond is one to one, partners, business associates, the friend you mostly see alone, there's nowhere for an old hurt to hide. Their Chiron in your 7th house brings the other person's sore spot, and the skill it gave them, into the partnership itself.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) points to a hurt that taught: its work is to adapt, not to erase, and the adapting often becomes something others can lean on. [The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is the person across the table, a spouse, a business associate, anyone you meet as an equal, and what you look for in them. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their Chiron here sets that hard-won skill between the two of you.

For you, they can be an uncommonly good partner to talk to. They tend to listen one to one with real attention, notice the pattern you keep repeating with people, and stay with it without trying to fix you. With them, it may be easier to say what you need in a relationship, because they seem to know what it costs to ask.

For them, being your equal can be the tender part. Being chosen, signing an agreement, sitting opposite you at a negotiating table may recall the place where they once had to adapt, and they may brace for an old story to repeat itself, even while they enjoy that it doesn't. Angular, the 7th puts its contents on display in whoever you choose and whoever you argue with; [Libra](/astrology/libra/), its natural sign, keeps weighing both sides. A Chiron of theirs right on your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the degree that was setting in the west when you were born, makes the contact louder still: I read it as a conjunction to that angle as well as an overlay.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The roles can harden. One of you becomes the helper, the other the one helped, and support that started as care turns into a quiet ranking. You may feel responsible for a past you had no part in, or find yourself proving, again and again, that you're not the person who once let them down.

From their side, the risk is testing. They may watch for signs of the old pattern, pull back a little at the first misunderstanding, or keep a careful tally of who gave what. In business, a hard negotiation can land on them more heavily than it looks.

The hurt predates you. Partnership is simply where the two of you meet it, and it asks for an equal footing more than for a cure.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask before helping, and say plainly what you need in return. Keep the footing level: a partnership in which one person is always the healer doesn't stay a partnership for long. In a couple, a calm, regular check-in suits this overlay better than grand reassurances; business partners do well with written agreements that protect both people; friends who mostly meet alone can name what each brings; colleagues who work as a pair can take turns leading.

Read in reverse, as your Chiron in their 7th house, the page asks the same of you: equal footing, and no rescue missions. Their date of birth fixes their Chiron to within a fraction of a degree; it's your time that decides whether it lands before or after your Descendant, which in Placidus, the house system I use, opens the 7th, and I wait for both times before I start ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
