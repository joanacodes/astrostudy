In Moon conjunct Lilith in synastry, one person's Moon lies on the other's Black Moon Lilith: the far end of the Moon's orbit as it stood when that other person was born. Where one person keeps their comforts, the other keeps the part of themselves that refuses to be tamed, and the two share a degree.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds what soothes a person, and the bonds and habits they rely on. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is a symbolic point with several readings; mine is instinctive autonomy, the material someone learned to push out of sight, and their relationship with their own power and edges. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes them act as one; as [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) go, this one brings a hidden side right into someone else's home.

On the Lilith side, the part of you that was too much tends to be let into ordinary life. With your Lilith and their Moon both in Taurus, say, appetites you were once told to apologise for meet someone who loves simple, physical comforts, and at their table the apology can stop.

Holding the Moon, you find the other's refusals landing right where you keep your comforts. You may find your idea of what's safe widening: someone who won't be managed turns out to be restful company. Or you may feel your nest stirred, because their directness touches needs you hadn't said aloud.

What flows is a kind of honesty in feeling. In a couple, this may be the first home where nobody is asked to be smaller; between friends, the one place both can say what they really want.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The risk, at such close quarters, is that the untamed part stops belonging to its owner. The Moon person may take on the other's refusals as their own and lose track of what they themselves need. Or the Lilith person's wild side gets folded into the shared routine, made cosy, and quietly tamed by the very comfort that welcomed it.

Intensity is the other edge. Being seen this closely can be thrilling and exposing in the same evening, for both of you.

Further apart, the two meet on different terms:

- **[Moon sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/moon-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** an opening, where frankness and comfort help each other when invited.
- **[Moon square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/moon-square-lilith-synastry/):** care and autonomy cross, and each can feel the other pressing.
- **[Moon trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/moon-trine-lilith-synastry/):** one element, and acceptance that hardly needs saying.
- **[Moon opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/moon-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** the nest and the one who won't be kept, facing each other.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep the untamed part with its owner. The Lilith person can name what is theirs, "this is how I am, not how we have to be", and the Moon person can ask themselves, every so often, which of their new habits they actually chose. Ask before you fold something into the routine. And say out loud what is welcome, so neither of you has to guess where the edges are.

About 1 pair of people in 11 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 8°. I use mean Lilith. The true point swings widely around it, sometimes by tens of degrees, so software set to true Lilith can give a different answer for this contact ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). One thing my notebook does say: Saturn, Lilith and Chiron held 75 to 90% of the time, each checked by sign and house in a single person's chart. Between two charts I have no count, and I give none. Whether the contact exists is the Moon's call: in a day it covers about 13°, while mean Lilith needs some nine months to cross a sign, so on any given day the hour of birth moves the Moon a long way and Lilith hardly at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

On its own in one chart, [Moon conjunct Lilith](/astrology/moon-conjunct-lilith/) describes someone whose comfort already includes their untamed side. Between two, one person is shown what the other hid, which is why both have to want the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
