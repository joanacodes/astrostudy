Their Moon in your 4th house describes someone whose need for comfort finds its way under your roof. With them, home can feel more like home: the kettle on, the evening unhurried, the people around the table relaxed enough to be ordinary.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is the ground you stand on: the place you live, the people you call family, where you come from, and the part of you the world doesn't see. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds a person's need for safety and belonging, the habits and memories that make a place feel like theirs. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their need to belong settles in your foundations.

For you, it often means being able to drop your guard. You can be tired, unglamorous, half-dressed on a Sunday, and they seem to like you more for it. They may care for your home in small practical ways, and the rooms you live in can feel warmer for having them in them.

For them, your private world can feel like a place they belong. Your routines, your kitchen, the way your home sounds in the evening soothe them, and they may want to make some of it theirs. The 4th is angular, and what lands here shows even though it lives in private; it corresponds to [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), the Moon's own sign, so their Moon is visiting the house most closely tied to it. Just past your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart, it also touches the root of your chart, the place your sense of continuity grows from.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Comfort at home raises the question of whose home it is. Their habits can settle into your space faster than you noticed, their cup, their chair, their way of doing the washing up, and your refuge can start to feel shared before you agreed to share it. Their need for comfort may also stir your own family memories: the way feelings were handled where you grew up, for better and worse. The overlay doesn't describe your parents; it describes what this closeness brings back.

From their side, your home can be a comfort they're never quite sure of. If you need it to yourself, or change it, or close the door for an evening, they may feel it more than they expected, as if a safe place had moved.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Talk about home early and concretely: which rituals are shared, which rooms or evenings stay yours, how each of you likes to be looked after when tired. In a couple who live together, this overlay rewards a few agreed habits more than any grand plan; between friends, it's the friend whose sofa you sleep on after a bad week, and who deserves thanking for it; between adult relatives, it can make visits feel like coming home, or bring old roles back into the kitchen.

Flip the charts and your Moon lands in their 4th house instead, your need for comfort settling in their home, with the same care owed to their private space. [Their Moon in your 3rd house](/astrology/moon-in-3rd-house-synastry/) lives in conversation; [their Moon in your 5th house](/astrology/moon-in-5th-house-synastry/) in play and pleasure.

Without their birth time, their Moon could be anywhere in a stretch of about 13°, enough to straddle your IC. Your IC itself goes round the zodiac in a day, so in Placidus, the house system I use, your 4th can only be drawn with your own time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Because this overlay reaches into someone's home life, I read it only for two adults who have both said yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
