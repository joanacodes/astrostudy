Halfway between two of Saturn's crossings of your Uranus, about fifteen years after the last, Saturn reaches the point directly across from it. The commitments you live inside and the part of you that needs room end up at either end of one line, and for a while you can see both.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) holds things together: rules, duties, the structures that let a life run. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the urge to differ and to free what has grown tight. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets them face to face, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the face is often someone else's. A manager who wants procedures followed, a partner who wants things settled, an institution that won't bend; or you're the one holding the rules while someone close asks for air. Whichever end you stand at, the other is in plain view now.

I read it as a renegotiation rather than a fight. Polarity isn't contradiction: the work is to see what each end is protecting, then redraw the arrangement so that both keep a place. The wish to walk out can be strong, and so can the wish to clamp down. The transit requires neither, and it says nothing about whether a job or a relationship lasts; that stays yours to decide, with more of the picture in view.

Your Uranus's house says where you need room; the house opposite, which Saturn is crossing, says where the call for structure comes from. With Uranus in your 10th, Saturn reaches your 4th: the needs of home weighed against independence at work. Both houses are fixed by the time of birth, and a chart without it stays closed to me ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured with my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, Saturn stays opposite your Uranus for about nine months as a rule. Saturn spends about four and a half months of every year retrograde ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)), and in a little over half of these oppositions the turn falls close enough to bring three exact hits, over about nine and a half months; with a single hit, it can be over in three weeks.

It comes once in each round of Saturn, about every 29½ years, so most people meet it three times by 80 and some twice. Uranus moves so little that a whole year group shares the timing: born in 1975, you had the first at about 23, and the second comes at about 53, in 2028; born in 1955, they fall at about 6, 35 and 65. Born in 1964–67 or 2008–10, with [Saturn opposite Uranus](/astrology/saturn-opposite-uranus/) in your own chart, you meet this transit at each [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/). And Saturn faces Libra from Aries until April 2028: with Uranus in Libra, as for most people born between 1968 and 1975, the opposition is yours now.

In the round, it sits about five years from each trine ([transiting Saturn trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-uranus/)) and about seven from each square ([transiting Saturn square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-uranus/)). [Transiting Saturn sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-uranus/) comes about five years either side of [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-uranus/), on the opposite stretch of the round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the end you've been holding. If you usually keep order, listen to what the restless person near you is saying about your life; if you're usually the free spirit, ask what the careful one is protecting.

Renegotiate before you leave. A schedule, a role, a shared arrangement can often be redrawn to give you room, and the redrawing tends to hold better than a dramatic exit or a tighter grip.

Keep one small promise to your independence and one to your commitments. The opposition settles when each end trusts that it won't be dropped.
