Of the ways Pluto can meet your Moon, the sextile makes the least noise. For two to three years the planet of renewal stands 60° from the part of you that needs and attaches, and offers a chance to change how you look after yourself, which nothing forces you to take.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) renews by letting go of what has stopped working. Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds your needs, your attachments and your habits of comfort. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through opportunity and cooperation, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) it becomes easier than usual to change an emotional habit, mend a family bond or rebuild part of your home life on firmer ground. A sextile doesn't act for you, though: an opening needs someone to walk through it.

The risk is to dismiss it because it's quiet. Pluto's work here comes in small steps: going to bed before the worrying starts, saying a need once instead of hinting at it, a weekly phone call that turns into a real conversation. Each looks minor. Two years of them can change how safe you feel, and change made gradually tends to stay made.

With your Moon in the 6th and Pluto crossing the 8th ([Pluto transiting the 8th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-8th-house/)), a closer bond, one where trust has to be shared, can show you new ways of caring for yourself day to day. The houses come from your birth time, and so does the Moon's degree: two people born on the same day, twelve hours apart, have Moons six or seven degrees apart, which at Pluto's present pace puts their sextiles about four years apart. I don't read a chart without the time for reasons like this one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Moon sextile Pluto](/astrology/moon-sextile-pluto/), you know this kind of opening from the inside. The transit lends it to everyone else for a season.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile lasts about two years and eight months as a rule, and about twenty-two months when Pluto's loops are tight. Pluto spends about five months of each year retrograde ([Pluto retrograde](/astrology/pluto-retrograde/)), so the exact angle is crossed at least three times, and five times in about two passes out of five.

Pluto makes this sextile from two sides. One comes 60° before it would reach your Moon, on the way to the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-moon/)); the other comes 60° after it has passed, on the way to the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-moon/)), the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-moon/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-moon/)). A lifetime covers less than half of Pluto's circle, so nearly one person in four born between 1930 and 2009 meets neither sextile by 80, and only a few meet both.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044, it sextiles Moons in Aries and Sagittarius, the early degrees of both signs first.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick one habit you use to settle yourself that no longer settles you, and replace it on purpose rather than simply dropping it.

Say yes to help that is offered. Under a sextile, the opening often comes from outside: a friend's suggestion, a group, a course, a therapist with a free hour.

Keep a note of small shifts as they happen. They are easy to forget, and looking back over two years of them is how you see what changed.
