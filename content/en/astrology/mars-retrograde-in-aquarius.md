Aquarius has one of the slimmest shares of Mars’s retrogrades in the dates I use: a single loop between 2000 and 2040, in the summer of 2018, and only across its first nine degrees. Over the rest of the sign, Mars moves forward the whole time in these years.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) is fixed air, ruled by Uranus in modern tropical astrology and by Saturn in the tradition; I read both. Mars there acts for ideas and causes, often in its own unusual way, and puts its energy into the group as much as the self. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when Mars seems from Earth to stop and drift back, that drive turns over its principles: the campaign you threw yourself into, the stand you took on behalf of everyone, the independence you defended so firmly that it kept people at a distance. Anger here often argues from principle, and these weeks may show the feeling under the argument. Desire wants freedom and friendship at once, and the review asks how the two fit.

In 2018, Mars stationed at 9°13′ Aquarius on 26 June and backed into Capricorn ([Mars retrograde in Capricorn](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-capricorn/)), turning direct at 28°36′ Capricorn on 27 August: 62 days over about 10½°, in a year when Mars came close to Earth. Around the end of July it stood opposite the Sun, then in Leo. The list I work from also files the 2003 loop under Aquarius, but both its stations fell in Pisces ([Mars retrograde in Pisces](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-pisces/)), at 10°08′ and 0°07′, so between them Mars never backed into Aquarius. No Mars retrograde from 2019 to 2040 reaches the sign.

A natal Mars retrograde in Aquarius is a separate matter, a placement read on its own terms ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Aquarius occupies comes from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and without it I don’t read a chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2018 loop could run from the Aquarius house back into the Capricorn one.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aquarius-rising/):** Aquarius rising: an independent, unconventional first impression; the need to stand apart, examined kindly.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** values built on principle; effort spent on what you believe in rather than what you keep, weighed.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aquarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** debates with siblings and neighbours; a point of principle you pushed, revisited.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home run like a shared workshop; house rules you set for everyone, renegotiated.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** unusual loves and inventive play; an experiment in romance or art, looked at again.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work improved by new systems; the reform you led at work, reviewed with the people it affects.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius on the Descendant, Leo on the Ascendant: a partner who needs room; how much freedom each of you has, talked through.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** closeness on your own terms; the distance you keep in intimacy, understood.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** ideals and causes; a cause you fought for in public, re-examined.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius on the Midheaven: a career as an innovator; the change you are pushing at work, timed again.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** Aquarius’s natural house: friends, groups and networks; a collective project you drove, handed back to the group.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aquarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** ideals held in private; a rebellion carried out only in your head, thought through.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Aquarius thinks in systems, so these weeks suit asking whether a cause still serves the people in it, you included. The house of your natal Saturn shows where commitments shape the review, the house of your natal Uranus where the urge to change things presses hardest, and your natal Mars where the drive starts.

None of this foretells a split in a group or a new alliance. It describes a single summer in which independence was asked what it is for.
