Six years or so after crossing your Sun, Jupiter stands across the zodiac from it, halfway round its circuit. This is the turning point of the round: what you set out to become at the crossing now meets the world that faces it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens and gathers meaning; your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) holds your identity and the direction you've chosen. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity: they look at each other across the chart, and each shows the other something it couldn't see alone. Here the growth Jupiter describes tends to arrive from outside you, in someone else's vision, a larger arena, an offer or a disagreement that makes your own aims clearer.

It can take the form of a mentor who sees more in you than you do, a partner whose plans outsize yours, an audience, a rival, a debate you didn't choose. None of that is conflict by nature: the opposition is about awareness, and its usual work is balance, borrowing the larger view without handing over your direction.

In Placidus, the house system I use, opposite houses mirror each other exactly, so Jupiter is crossing the house that faces your Sun's. With your Sun in the 1st, the opposition comes during [Jupiter transiting the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-7th-house/), and partners carry the theme; with your Sun in the 10th, during [Jupiter transiting the 4th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-4th-house/), and home answers back to work. Your birth time places both houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)); without it I don't read the [transit](/astrology/transits/). Born with [Sun opposite Jupiter](/astrology/sun-opposite-jupiter/), which means born in the weeks Jupiter shone brightest, you meet this opposition again at every Jupiter return.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My orbs run from 1½° before exact to 1° after. Within them, about seven oppositions in ten come and go in some eleven days; the other three are retrograde loops, with three exact hits over about eight months.

Your birthday works the other way round from the crossing. Jupiter is retrograde in the months it faces the Sun, and on your birthday the Sun is back on your natal Sun, so a three-pass opposition has its middle pass close to your birthday, within about six weeks of it nine times in ten. Around then Jupiter is at its brightest of the year, rising as the Sun sets. A quick single pass comes around your half-birthday instead.

This winter shows it. Jupiter is retrograde in Leo from 13 December 2026 to 13 April 2027; for a Sun at 22° Aquarius, a birthday around 11 February, the three oppositions fall in mid-October 2026, mid-February 2027 and mid-June 2027, the middle one in the birthday week.

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 meet it six or seven times, once a round, halfway between two passes of [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-sun/). On the way out Jupiter has already made [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-sun/), [transiting Jupiter square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-sun/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-sun/); on the way home it makes them again, in reverse.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Hear the larger view out before you answer it. The opposition often arrives as someone else's idea of what you could be: take what fits, and say plainly what doesn't.

Keep your own direction in sight. It's easy, under this transit, to grow into somebody else's plan; the season is for growing into yours, with their help.

If the long version falls near your birthday, look east after sunset. Jupiter rises as the Sun goes down, brighter than any star, standing where this transit puts it: across the sky from your Sun.
