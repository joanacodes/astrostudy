The 11th house holds the company you keep and the future you're working towards: allies, circles, causes, wishes. With Leo on the 11th house cusp, that company is chosen with the heart and kept with loyalty.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Leo](/astrology/leo/) on this cusp makes the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) warm and loyal, since a house takes its manner from the sign it opens in ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). I read it as friendship held almost as a vow: you remember the birthdays, host the reunions, and take a slight against a friend as a slight against you. In any circle, a club, a team, a cause, you tend to end up near the middle, sometimes at its head, because you bring energy and make people feel included. Tradition named the 11th the Good Spirit, and with Leo on its cusp I read that spirit as generosity: you're the one who opens doors for others, and gladly. Your aims are big-hearted, and you're proud of them; you'd rather aim at something worth celebrating than settle for something safe. The harder side is the need to matter to the group. When your effort goes unnoticed, a friendship can cool faster than you'd like, or you find yourself performing for people you came to be with.

Friendships and aims lead wherever the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) sits, since it rules the house: as ruler of the 11th, in the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Opposite, [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) opens the 5th, and the two signs have traded places: Leo is the 5th's natural sign and Aquarius the 11th's. I read the trade in the life as well, as fun taken in a crowd or for a cause, and friends given the devotion other people keep for romance. With [Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/) in about 4 London charts in 5 and nearly 9 in 10 at New York, Venus rules the chart, and the people around you are a large part of how you meet the world.

Leo on the 11th is about average in frequency: roughly 1 birth in 11, at London as at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. The 11th cusp sits above the eastern horizon, two houses up from the Ascendant, where Leo's slow rise in the north still shows: a little over two hours a day on this cusp at London. Yet it's far enough from the horizon for latitude to make little difference to the count. The house runs close to a sign's width in these charts, about 28° to 31° at London, so from here Leo never spills onto the 10th or the 12th cusp. As for Leo going without any cusp, that happens in about 1 London birth in 7, and 1 New York birth in 11.

For a birth a little earlier, Leo would have opened the 12th ([Leo on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-12th-house-cusp/)); for one a little later, it would have climbed to the Midheaven ([Leo on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/)). Which of the three is yours is a matter of minutes, and the minutes are why I don't read a chart without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
