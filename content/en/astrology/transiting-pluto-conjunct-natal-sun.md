When Pluto arrives on the degree of your Sun, the slowest body in the chart sits at its centre for close to three years. Fewer than half of people ever live through it, and nobody twice: a long renovation of who you are, from the inside.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the part of a chart that empowers by stripping back: obsolete structures go, and the strength underneath them can act. Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the centre the chart organises around: identity, vitality, the purpose that makes you someone in particular. Fused by a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), the two act together, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) does its stripping back at the centre itself. Some notice it first as a loss of appetite for what used to define them, a title or a way of being admired; others as a purpose that has waited for years and will not wait any longer.

Of Pluto's blind spots, the one this transit tests is mistaking a survival adaptation for a permanent identity. The self you built to get through something, the one who copes with everything or never asks, can feel like the whole of you until this transit loosens it. Clearing out is not yet renewal: what counts is what you let grow in the space.

Pluto on the Sun has an old reputation for darkness. It predicts no death and no illness ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and vitality is not a constant level of energy: a slower stretch belongs to a long change, not to a verdict. If what comes up feels too heavy to carry, a therapist or a doctor can help where a chart can't ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

The conjunction falls in your Sun's own house: in the 10th, for example, work and public standing are where the change shows ([Pluto transiting the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-10th-house/)). The house needs a recorded birth time, which is also my condition for reading any chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If your birth chart has [Sun conjunct Pluto](/astrology/sun-conjunct-pluto/), this transit either came in early childhood, with Pluto still a degree or two short of your Sun, or will never come, because Pluto had already gone past.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the conjunction lasts about two years and eight months. The quickest passes take about twenty-two months; about two in five bring five exact hits instead of three and run to some three years and seven months.

Pluto has to travel from where it stood to the degree of your Sun, and its 80-year road is uneven, because its orbit is stretched: about 170° for births of the 1940s and 1950s, against 110° for births of 2009. About half of people born from 1930 to 1969 meet the conjunction by 80, and only a third of those born in the 2000s. Born in 1965, with Pluto in Virgo, a birthday between late February and early September put your Sun out of reach.

On Pluto's road the trine ([transiting Pluto trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-sun/)), the square ([transiting Pluto square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-sun/)) and the sextile ([transiting Pluto sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-sun/)) come before this conjunction and again after it, in reverse, with the opposition ([transiting Pluto opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-sun/)) last; the conjunction is a single point on that road, and most lifetimes miss it.

Since November 2024 Pluto has been settled in Aquarius, where it remains until January 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), so the Suns it meets now are Aquarius Suns, whatever the birth year: in 2026, birthdays around 22 to 26 January, at 76 as at 26; the last, around 18 February, in the early 2040s.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Close one chapter on purpose. Pick a role, a habit or an image of yourself that no longer fits and end it cleanly, rather than waiting for it to give way.

Write down who you are at the first exact hit. By the last, the comparison is often the clearest account of what this transit did.

Give the new direction a small, regular shape before a public one: a weekly practice, a piece of work you answer for, a promise you keep.
