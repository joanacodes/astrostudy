Twice in every round of Saturn, about ten years apart, the planet of structure reaches a trine to the degree Uranus held when you were born. Of all the Saturn–Uranus transits it pushes least, which makes it the easiest to let slip by: a few months when steadiness and change can stand on the same side.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds what lasts: commitments, boundaries, the patience that turns effort into something solid. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part of your chart that wants room to do things differently, to test an alternative and reform what has grown too tight. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets the two help each other. For the length of this [transit](/astrology/transits/), a change you've been circling can be given a frame: the side project gets fixed hours, the unconventional arrangement gets written down.

What I notice in this season is a kind of permission. Rules you've lived by stop feeling like the enemy of your independence, and your independence stops feeling like a risk to what you've built. The trine won't do the work, though: ease isn't inevitability, and plenty of these transits pass with nothing updated at all.

Where it lands depends on the house of your Uranus, the part of life where you insist on your own way. With Uranus in your 6th, it's daily work and its methods; in your 4th, the way a household runs. Houses come from your birth time, and without it I don't read the chart at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you were born with [Saturn trine Uranus](/astrology/saturn-trine-uranus/), in 1955–58, 1971–74 or 2002–04, you know this cooperation from the inside, and the transit wakes it up again.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine usually holds for about nine months. A little over half the time, Saturn's retrograde carries it across the exact degree three times, in about nine and a half months; the rest touch it once, most still lingering about eight months as Saturn turns nearby, and the quickest pass in three weeks.

Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, so people born in the same year have it within a few degrees of each other and meet Saturn's trine within a year or so of the same age. That age shifts a great deal from one birth year to another: about 19 for someone born in 1975, about 12 for 1985, within the first year for 1955, when the two planets were already in trine. After that the trines come in pairs, about ten years apart, with Saturn's opposition to your Uranus between them ([transiting Saturn opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-uranus/)), and nearly twenty years pass before the next pair. Most people meet it five times by 80, a few six.

Saturn's crossing of Aries, from 2025 to April 2028, trines every degree of Leo and Sagittarius. If your Uranus is in Leo, as for most people born between 1955 and 1962, or in Sagittarius, as for those born between 1981 and 1988, this is your trine. [Transiting Saturn square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-uranus/) usually falls about two and a half years before or after it; [transiting Saturn sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-uranus/) lie further off in the round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick one experiment that has proved itself and give it a structure: a schedule, a written method, a set number of hours. The trine supports making a change durable far more than starting something from nothing.

Update a rule rather than break it. If an arrangement you live by no longer fits, rewrite the clause that pinches and keep the rest; this transit is generous to reforms that respect what still works.

Teach it. A way of working you invented tends, under Saturn, to settle into something other people can use, and explaining it is often how you find out which parts are essential.
