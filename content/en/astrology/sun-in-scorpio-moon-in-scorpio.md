Some pairs carry a counterweight inside them. This one doesn't: both lights are in Scorpio, under the same two rulers, born around the new moon in Scorpio, and both your purpose and your needs go to the depths.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fixed water at both ends: the [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) arranges its identity around what is essential and true, and the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) can rest only where it trusts completely. Purpose and need agree about what matters: depth, honesty and loyalty, worth waiting a long time for. What the pair lacks, from inside, is lightness, and that has to come from elsewhere.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) rule both lights, the first in the tradition, the second in modern astrology, and since together they speak for your whole centre I read both with more weight than usual; the two kinds of ruler have a page of their own, [traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/). Tradition places the Moon's [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Scorpio, which I read as comfort that takes effort to reach, never as a lesser heart. The Sun keeps it company there, and the rising sign and the planets in other elements are what open a window in this pair.

Your Moon and Sun were within 30° of each other, the Moon either closing in or just past: a birth around the [new moon in Scorpio](/astrology/new-moon-in-scorpio/), when the Moon is often lost in the Sun's light. Slightly more of these births come in the dark days before the conjunction, the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), than in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase that follows, though the split is close to even, and births close to the meeting carry a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains why the hour matters so much here.

Double Scorpios are among the rarer pairs, about 1 person in 150. Each month the Moon slips from Libra into Scorpio and, two and a half days or so later, out into Sagittarius; a birth on either of those days may belong to the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-libra/) or the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-sagittarius/), so the time comes first ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same time sets the rising sign, and in a chart this concentrated, the rising sign is often the first place where a different element comes in.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love completely or not at all, and you need to know it is safe before you begin. Trust is built in stages and tested along the way; once it is given, loyalty runs very deep. What you need from a partner is honesty, including about hard things, and respect for your privacy, which is not the same as secrecy. Someone light where you are deep, and steady where you are intense, can be a great gift.

## At work {#at-work}

You can stay with what others avoid: crisis, research, investigation, therapy, rescue, restoration, anything that asks for depth, discretion and staying power. Both lights want the work to matter, and you'll give it everything when it does. Shallow environments, office politics and work done for appearances drain you fast. You may be more effective than visible, and that suits you, as long as the people you respect know what you did.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in Scorpio there is little inside the pair to lighten the intensity. Hurts go deep and stay long, endings can be fought for years, and guarding your core can become a way of being alone. Look for lightness on purpose: people who make you laugh, work that uses the body, and a rhythm of small endings, a drawer cleared, a letter sent, so that the large ones don't have to carry everything.
