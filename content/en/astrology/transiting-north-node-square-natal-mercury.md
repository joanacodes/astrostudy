With the node square your Mercury, the line between where you come from and where you're heading crosses the way you think at right angles. Through the month, the old end of the axis and the new one both claim your attention, and Mercury, standing between them, has to work out what to make of both.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) reasons, interprets and exchanges: it is how information becomes meaning and meaning becomes words. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a capacity built by practice and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) one already built, while a [square](/astrology/square/) relates functions through friction that calls for adjustment and problem-solving. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the way you reason gets in the way of a step, or the step gets in the way of how you reason, and something has to give a little.

In practice it often feels like an argument with yourself. The old explanation no longer quite convinces, the new one hasn't earned your trust yet, and you keep rehearsing both. A plan may need rethinking, a skill relearning, a story you tell about yourself retelling. That friction is useful: it's how a square turns information into a decision, and it rarely has to be settled in one sitting.

With your [Mercury in the 10th house](/astrology/mercury-in-10th-house/), the friction tends to show at work: in what you say in public, how you present an idea, the reputation your words have built. None of it forecasts how a contract, an exam or a dispute will turn out, which no chart can tell ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)). The 10th, like every house, is drawn from your birth time; without it, I wouldn't know where in your life this square falls ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Every transit I follow gets the same orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, and the node clears them, in the middle case, in about a month. One tenth of passes need no more than three and a half weeks; a further tenth need three and a half months or more: that's the true node lingering because the Sun has reached the nodes and an eclipse season has begun. About one pass in twenty-five touches the exact square three times.

Squares are the evenly spaced pair: nine years and four months apart every time, one from each side of your Mercury, and my forecast has neither, since the node enters it only at the conjunction and the opposition. The node passes [Transiting North Node sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-mercury/) before the first and [Transiting North Node trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-mercury/) after it, reaches [Transiting North Node opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-mercury/) halfway, and makes the same angles again in reverse on its way back to [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-mercury/). By 80, most people have met nine squares, eight at the fewest. Born with [Mercury square North Node](/astrology/mercury-square-north-node/), you meet this one at every [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) and every [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), since your Mercury already sits at right angles to the axis you were born with.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write out both arguments, the old one and the new one, at full strength. On paper they stop going round in circles.

Change one habit of attention: the news you read first, the order in which you deal with messages, the voice you consult before deciding.

If a decision can wait a month, let it. If it can't, make it with the facts you have, and note what you'd want to know next time.
