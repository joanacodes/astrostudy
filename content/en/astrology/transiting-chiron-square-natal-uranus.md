For people born at the start of 1945, 1955, 1965 or 1975, the first Chiron square to Uranus came between about 17 and 21, in the years when many people leave school or home and try to become their own person. At any age, the square tends to feel like that: an urge to break free meeting a limitation that comes along.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) liberates: it wants room, independence and a way of living that is yours rather than inherited. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the limitation that stays and the adapting that keeps you in the game. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates the two through friction, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the wish to be free and the old difficulty keep jostling for the same space.

It can show as impatience with the limit itself: a body, a history, a sensitivity that won't be outgrown however much you want to leave it behind. It can show as a plan to change everything at once, the job, the city, the circle of friends, in the hope that the sore spot stays behind with the old life. Uranus's blind spots are assuming that disruption brings progress by itself and confusing freedom with having no ties; Chiron's is mistaking adaptation for a cure. The square asks for something less dramatic and more lasting: freedom within the difficulty, changes made one at a time, so that what works can stay.

Everyone born within a few years of you has Uranus in almost the same place, so your generation meets this square together, and in a reading it tells me about the climate you grew up in as much as about you. Your own version comes from the houses: where your Uranus sits, where Chiron is passing, and which personal planets Uranus touches.

With [Uranus in the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-in-5th-house/), where you create and play in your own way, Chiron is usually crossing your 2nd or your 8th ([Chiron transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-2nd-house/)): your freest self-expression meets an old sore spot about worth. Which of those two houses Chiron is in depends on your birth time, and I don't read the square without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Uranus square Chiron](/astrology/uranus-square-chiron/) at birth, the friction is part of your chart already, and the transit sets it moving again.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Restlessness can make any season feel endless; this one usually lasts about ten months, counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, with three crossings of the exact square in nearly two passes out of three. About one pass in three is over in some three weeks, after a single crossing, and about one in twenty runs to five crossings and roughly twenty-one months.

In the site's count the first square came at about 17 in the middle, by about 31 at the latest: before 2 for someone born at the start of 1995, about 6 for 1935, 10 for 1985, 17 for 1945 and 1975, 20 or 21 for 1955 and 1965, and 29 for 2005. Most people have been through three by 80; a few stop at two and a few reach four. Chiron reaches the first square on its way out, after [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Chiron sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-uranus/), and the second on its way home, after [transiting Chiron opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-uranus/) and the second [transiting Chiron trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-uranus/).

Next in line are most people born between April 1995 and December 2003, with Uranus in Aquarius: a square falls for them while Chiron crosses Taurus, between June 2026 and May 2034, in their twenties or thirties.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change one arrangement at a time, and give it a month before the next.

Adapt a rule rather than breaking it. Often a rule needs a new shape, not a bonfire.

Ask the people around you how fast they can change, and meet them partway.

Write down what you want to be free of, and what you want to be free for.
