Everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets this transit, and meets it young: Pluto reaches their natal Neptune once, between about 22 and 30. It is the season when the ideals you grew up with meet the depth of adult life.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination and compassion: it is what you idealise, what you long for, the larger meaning you sense behind ordinary life. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms, taking things to the root and clearing out what no longer holds. Joined in a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), depth and imagination work as a single process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your ideals are not only questioned; they are reworked from the bottom. A vocation you dreamt of meets the real work. A faith, a cause or a picture of love you inherited is tested until you know whether it is yours.

Neptune and Pluto share a blind spot, and it concerns truth. Neptune confuses resonance with truth, and Pluto confuses intensity with truth, so in these years a powerful feeling can pass for a revelation. Idealisation isn't delusion, and what tends to dissolve in these years is the borrowed part of your ideals.

Neptune's fog is an image, not a diagnosis, and no transit causes or explains a crisis. If what you feel at this age stays heavy or confusing for weeks, talk to a doctor or a therapist ([disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help)); [Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/) explains why a chart can't stand in for that help.

Your Neptune's house says which ideal is being tested. With Neptune in your 7th, it is the ideal of partnership, during Pluto's long stay in that house ([Pluto transiting the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-7th-house/)); with Neptune in the 10th, the dream of a calling. Only your birth time puts Neptune in a house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Two years and eight months is the usual stay, counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, with anything from about twenty-two months to nearly four years possible. In about three passes out of five Pluto crosses your Neptune three times; in the others, five.

The age is so regular because of how these births were placed. In every chart from 1930 to 2009, Neptune stood some 45° to 65° ahead of Pluto, close to the long sextile between them ([Neptune sextile Pluto](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto/)), and Pluto covers that distance in 22 to 30 years. Born on 1 January 1935 or 1945, you met it at 28.8; in 1965, at 25.9; in 1985 and 1995, at about 23; in 2005, you will at 26.1. It came soonest for births of the 1980s and 1990s, while Pluto crossed Scorpio and Sagittarius near its fastest; for births of the 1930s and 1940s, within a year or so of the first [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/).

Births from 1998 on have Neptune in Aquarius, the sign Pluto crosses until early 2044, so those born up to 2009 meet this conjunction between about 23 and 30.

No other Pluto contact with Neptune is so universal. The sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-neptune/)) reaches almost everyone too; the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-neptune/)) about 2 in 3, in their sixties or seventies; the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-neptune/)) about 1 in 6, near 80; the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-neptune/)) nobody.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you believed at eighteen, and read it again at the end of this transit. The difference is the work.

Give the ideal a form small enough to live: a practice, a craft, a commitment you can keep this year.

Choose who you confide in. Ideals tested this deeply deserve a listener who doesn't need you to agree.
