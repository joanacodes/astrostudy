The bottom of the chart answers to the top. What you come from and what you do in the world tend to be told as one story, the ruler of home standing in the house of career and public life.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) and [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) are the two ends of the chart's vertical line, the [meridian axis](/astrology/meridian-axis/): the IC below, home and roots, and the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) above, career and standing. With the 4th's ruler in the 10th ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), home's planet has climbed to the opposite end.

Home, here, has a public face. Home and work keep borrowing from each other: a family trade, a surname that opens or closes doors, work done from the kitchen, a career in homes, land, food, care or history. Even without any of that, where you come from tends to show in what you're building: you may work to honour a background, to prove it wrong, or to give the next generation firmer ground. The difficulty is the boundary, since family expectations can follow you to work and work can fill the house.

Work can also be how you make a home possible: the job taken to keep a roof over the people you love, the career chosen because it lets you stay near them. When home and work line up, each strengthens the other; when they compete, the 10th tends to win the hours and the 4th the loyalty, and that tug is worth naming.

From the top of the chart the ruler faces home directly, across the full width of the axis, and tradition counted that opposition as an aspect: home is never out of its sight. At your birth, the ruler of the lowest point of the chart was close to the highest, still climbing towards the meridian.

The old books split the parents between the 4th and the 10th, without ever settling who goes where. With the ruler of one in the other, some astrologers read a parent's career shaping yours. I keep to what's yours: the way family and ambition meet in your own life. A parent's working life belongs to their own chart.

Saturn, ruling a Capricorn IC, is the 10th's own natural ruler, and from there it builds a career slowly, perhaps carrying a family responsibility into public life. In my notebook, Saturn was one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), which is why I take a Saturn ruler slowly.

An Aquarius IC brings Uranus in beside Saturn, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Uranus in the 10th can turn an unconventional home life into an unconventional career, while Saturn keeps the foundations.

In the 9th, the ruler of home would set it far away or in a belief ([Ruler of the 4th in the 9th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)); in the 11th, among friends ([Ruler of the 4th in the 11th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)). The 10th puts it in public view, and since the Midheaven shifts by the minute, the birth time has to be exact ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
