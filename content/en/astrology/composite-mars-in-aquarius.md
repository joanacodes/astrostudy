Composite Mars in Aquarius is the rarest sign for a composite Mars, and the drive it describes is an independent one: a relationship that acts for ideas and for groups, wants freedom inside closeness, and argues in principles.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The composite sets [Mars](/astrology/mars/) at the halfway point of your two natal Marses ([the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/)), and reads it as the relationship's drive. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), a fixed air sign, has two rulers, [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) for the tradition and [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) for modern astrologers, and I keep both in view. Neither gives Mars a dignity here, so the sign supplies a manner: independent, inventive, turned towards the group and the future. About 1 pair in 16 has this composite Mars, the lowest share of any sign. Mars crosses Aquarius in about 39 days, one of its two quickest stays, and averaging pushes composites slightly further from it still than birth charts, where about 1 person in 15 has Mars here.

Such a pair acts for something larger than itself: a cooperative, a campaign, a club it ends up running, a home that doubles as everyone's meeting place. It likes doing things its own way, and the arrangements it invents, separate hobbies, unusual hours, friends who have become a kind of family, can puzzle people outside. Competition is intellectual: the better idea, the more original solution. Desire starts in friendship and needs freedom to stay alive; being expected to merge cools it. [Mars in Aquarius](/astrology/mars-in-aquarius/) in a single chart marks an independent streak; in the composite, independence becomes a value the bond holds for both of you.

Arguments are conducted in principles. A complaint about being left out turns into a discussion of fairness, rules and what any reasonable person would do, and the feeling underneath can go unspoken. One of you may step back mid-argument, not from coldness but because distance is how this Mars thinks. Objectivity isn't detachment, though, and a relationship can't be run like a committee meeting. What helps is saying how it feels before saying what's fair, and keeping regular time for the two of you rather than for the group.

Cool debate is friction. If one of you is ever frightened, or cut off from friends and support, that isn't a matter of style or sign: in the UK, ring 999 if anyone is in danger, or text SHOUT to 85258 to reach someone at any hour. A composite reads two private lives, so both people should have said yes to it ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

I don't draw composites; this page explains one so that you can weigh what you read elsewhere. Those who use them would add the composite Saturn and Uranus, and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), where [Mars conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) shows the spark, and the jolt, when the push of one runs into the other's appetite for freedom. Uranus, the modern ruler, is a generational planet, about seven years in each sign, so for a couple born a few years apart or less, a composite Uranus speaks of the era you share more than of your bond. No composite foretells a wedding or a parting.

[Composite Mars in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-mars-in-capricorn/) builds something to last; [composite Mars in Pisces](/astrology/composite-mars-in-pisces/) acts from compassion and intuition. Aquarius acts from an idea.
