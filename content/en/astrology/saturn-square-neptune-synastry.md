In Saturn square Neptune in synastry, a quarter of the zodiac separates one person's Saturn from the other's Neptune. One of them keeps asking how, with what and by when; the other keeps asking why not. Neither question is wrong, and the two arrive at right angles.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the function of limits: what can be held, afforded, finished. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the function of ideals: imagination, compassion, the urge to give beyond what's measured. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through constructive friction, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one gives each to a different person, often of a different generation.

Squares usually share a modality. One person's Saturn in Pisces with the other's Neptune in Sagittarius, both mutable: a conscientiousness that worries about everyone, crossing a large, roaming faith in what could be. Neither is rigid, which keeps the argument moving rather than stuck.

What the friction makes possible is an idealism that has been tested, and a realism that hasn't forgotten what it's for. Pairs who keep talking end up with hopes that survive a budget and rules that remember people.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

If the Saturn is yours, the other's hopes can seem to skip the hard parts: who pays, who does it, what happens in winter. You may find yourself cast as the voice of disappointment, a role you never asked for, and you may tighten your limits further each time they're ignored.

If the Neptune is yours, the other's caution can seem to drain the colour from what you care about. Every hope comes back with a "but", and you may start keeping your ideals to yourself, or giving without limits just to prove a point. None of that makes the square a fault line: it rubs because both are trying to protect something.

- **[Saturn conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** the two on one degree, where duty and dream merge.
- **[Saturn sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/saturn-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** an exchange of steps and reasons, offered politely.
- **[Saturn trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/saturn-trine-neptune-synastry/):** caution and faith trust each other.
- **[Saturn opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/saturn-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** facing each other across the zodiac, each holding one end.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree on what you're protecting before you argue about how. The Saturn person can learn to say "yes, if"; the Neptune person, "then a smaller version". Partners deciding how much to give to family, colleagues in a school or a hospital, a parent and an adult child arguing about a life in the arts: each can turn the crossing into a plan that keeps its soul.

My two examples involve the same two signs, with the roles swapped. Someone born in mid-1965 has it with anyone born between November 1976 and January 1980, whose Neptune in Sagittarius squares that Saturn in Pisces; someone born in mid-1975 has it with anyone born from March 1994 to February 1995, whose Saturn in Pisces squares the 1975 Neptune. Since everyone born across those spans shares it, I leave it out of the contacts a synastry counts, which all touch the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. I read it through the houses, as two eras disagreeing about the possible, more than two people.

The houses need both birth times; the square itself ignores the hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In my notebook, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time, each in a person's own chart; a square between two charts was never counted, and has no figure ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)).

[Saturn square Neptune](/astrology/saturn-square-neptune/), in one chart, is a person testing their own faith against their own limits. Across two charts, the test has two parties, and both should want the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
