Gemini is the third sign of the zodiac, the one the Sun crosses as spring turns into summer in the northern hemisphere. It's the sign of questions and connections. Wherever it falls in a chart, I read it as the place where you need to keep learning and talking to feel awake.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Gemini somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it brings curiosity, speed and a light touch: the wish to know, to name, to compare, and then to pass it on. Gemini asks *what else is there?* It's an [air sign](/astrology/air-signs/), like Libra and Aquarius, so it lives through ideas and exchange; and it's mutable, one of the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/), so it adapts as it goes. In the sequence of signs, each one answering the last, Gemini answers [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/): after building and keeping, the need to move, ask and connect. [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), next, will bring it all home and ask how it feels.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/):** born between about 21 May and 20 June. You grow by learning and by connecting ideas, people and places, and your sense of purpose often has more than one strand.
- **[Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/):** you understand your feelings by putting them into words. Talking things through is how you settle, and boredom unsettles you more than most things.
- **[Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/):** you meet the world with questions and quick conversation, and people find you lively and easy to talk to. Mercury becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/). A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So I read Gemini through the whole chart, beginning with where Mercury sits.

## Its ruler: Mercury {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, the one that carries the sign's business into the rest of the chart. Gemini belongs to [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), the planet of thought, speech and exchange, which also rules [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/): in Gemini it gathers and connects, in Virgo it sorts and refines. Wherever your Mercury sits, by sign and house, shows where your Gemini side turns its curiosity. With Gemini on the cusp of the 10th house and Mercury in the 3rd, say, your working life runs on words, messages and the people close by. Mercury never strays more than about 28° from the Sun, so it is always in your Sun sign or a sign next to it.

In the classical scheme, no planet is exalted in Gemini or has its fall there. One is weakened:

- [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite Sagittarius, the sign it rules: the one big picture breaks into many small ones. Jupiter grows here by collecting rather than believing — a different kind of wisdom, not a lesser one.

## Planets in Gemini {#planets-in-the-sign}

A planet in Gemini speeds up and starts asking questions; the house it sits in shows where that plays out.

- **[Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/):** a curious will; you grow by learning, linking and passing on what you find.
- **[Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/):** feelings understood by naming them; talking settles you, and variety comforts you.
- **[Mercury in Gemini](/astrology/mercury-in-gemini/):** Mercury at home — quick, versatile and witty; a mind that follows several threads at once.
- **[Venus in Gemini](/astrology/venus-in-gemini/):** love that starts in conversation; you're drawn to wit, and need a partner you can talk to.
- **[Mars in Gemini](/astrology/mars-in-gemini/):** energy that argues, writes and juggles; you act through words, and get restless when you're stuck.
- **[Jupiter in Gemini](/astrology/jupiter-in-gemini/):** Jupiter in detriment — growth through learning many things; faith in good questions more than in final answers.
- **[Saturn in Gemini](/astrology/saturn-in-gemini/):** confidence in your own mind, learned slowly; careful words, and the courage to think for yourself.
- **[Uranus in Gemini](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini/):** a generation that changes how people talk, learn and connect, shared by everyone born over about seven years; its house makes it personal.
- **[Neptune in Gemini](/astrology/neptune-in-gemini/):** ideals carried by words and stories; shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so I read it through its house.
- **[Pluto in Gemini](/astrology/pluto-in-gemini/):** a generation's deep change in how ideas and information travel; shared over twelve years or more, made personal by its house.

## Gemini on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Gemini somewhere in its twelve houses, usually on the cusp of one of them. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Gemini opens a house, that's where you stay curious and keep things moving, and Mercury, wherever it sits, carries the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/gemini-rising/):** Gemini rising — a quick, curious presence; you lead with questions, and people find you easy to talk to.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/gemini-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** you value ideas, skills and contacts; you like money to circulate rather than sit still, and may enjoy having more than one string to your bow.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** Gemini in its natural house — talk, reading, short trips; a busy everyday mind and a wide circle close by.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a lively home full of conversation, books and comings and goings; a home that changes as you do.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/):** play through words and games; you flirt with wit, and create through many small projects.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/gemini-on-6th-house-cusp/):** varied daily work; you need several tasks on the go, and a routine with room to change.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Gemini on the Descendant, which means Sagittarius rising — drawn to partners who make you think; a relationship that is also a long conversation.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/gemini-on-8th-house-cusp/):** you approach intimacy and shared resources by talking them through; understanding is how you come to trust.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/):** a mind that collects beliefs and compares them; you study widely, and travel to meet people.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a working life built on communication, often with more than one strand; a public image of someone articulate.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends from many different circles; groups where ideas flow.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/gemini-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a busy private mind; thoughts you keep to yourself, and writing as a way to quieten them.

## Gemini in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Gemini planets bring conversation and lightness: the person who has them connects through talk, jokes and shared curiosity, and keeps a bond alive by keeping it interesting. To the other person that can feel like play or like restlessness, depending on where those planets land in their chart and what they touch. Across the zodiac sits [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), the opposite sign: Gemini gathers the facts, Sagittarius looks for the meaning that ties them together. Between two charts, that axis makes for long talks and real disagreements, each person stretching the other's mind. As for comparing Sun signs, [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind: how two people talk to each other shows far more in their Mercury and Moon contacts than in their Sun signs.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Geminis are two-faced.”** The twins of the symbol describe a mind that can see two sides at once, not a person who lies. Holding two views is a way of thinking, not a flaw of character.
- **“Geminis are superficial.”** Curiosity that ranges widely isn't curiosity that stays on the surface. Gemini can go deep, in more than one place.
- **“Geminis can't commit.”** What Gemini can't do is stay bored. A bond that keeps it curious is a bond it keeps.
- **“Born on the cusp, you're part Taurus.”** At the minute you were born, the Sun was in one sign or the other; within a day of a change, your birth time settles which. More on being [born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/).
