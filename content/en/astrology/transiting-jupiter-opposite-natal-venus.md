For a few days every twelve years, or a few months when Jupiter loops, what you like, love and value has company across the room: another person's tastes, another way of living, a larger offer with its own terms. Jupiter has reached the point opposite your Venus.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs what matters to you and keeps relationships fair; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) adds scope and asks what things mean. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) holds them in polarity, each defining the other. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), growth in what you value tends to come from outside: a partner's world, a friend's enthusiasm, an invitation into a circle with different habits.

It's often a season of comparison. Someone else's way of enjoying life shows you yours; a generous offer comes with conditions worth reading; a relationship asks to be rebalanced, more given here and less there. Polarity isn't conflict, and the opposition rarely asks you to choose between people. It asks for reciprocity, the oldest thing Venus stands for. Venus is attraction too, and you may find yourself drawn to people unlike you, bigger, louder, freer, more certain. Attraction isn't romance, and what draws you says a good deal about what you'd like more of in yourself.

Jupiter will be crossing the house across from your Venus's, since opposite cusps match in Placidus, the house system I use, and here tradition adds a neat detail. The 5th house is where Venus has its joy and the 11th, facing it, is where Jupiter has its own ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): with your Venus in the 5th, this opposition comes during [Jupiter transiting the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-11th-house/), with play and love on one side and friendship and shared hopes on the other. All of this rests on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): no time, no houses, and no reading. With [Venus opposite Jupiter](/astrology/venus-opposite-jupiter/) in your own chart, each Jupiter return reopens the same give and take.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Opposite your Venus, Jupiter usually stays inside my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, for only about eleven days. In about three in ten, a retrograde loop crosses the point three times and keeps it there for about eight months.

By 80, everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has met it six or seven times, each a chance to see their own tastes through someone else's eyes. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-venus/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-venus/) bring pleasures to you, and [transiting Jupiter square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-venus/) makes you count them; here, at the far point six years after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-venus/), they are held up beside someone else's.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask what the other person values before you offer what you'd want. Under this opposition, generosity lands best when it's translated.

Read the terms of anything generous. A Jupiter opposition often brings offers that carry someone else's expectations, and knowing them early keeps the warmth intact.

Keep something that's only yours, a taste, a pleasure, a corner of the week, while you make room for theirs. Balance is easier to hold when you know which part of it is not for trading.
