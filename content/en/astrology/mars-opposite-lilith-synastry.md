Each of you can look like the wild one to the other. In Mars opposite Lilith in synastry, one person's Mars stands across the zodiac from the other's Black Moon Lilith, and two kinds of strength, open push and untamed refusal, keep catching each other's eye across the gap.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) acts in the open: initiative, effort, competition, a line held for all to see. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's mean far point, is read in more than one way; for me it is autonomy, the part someone learned to keep out of view, and their dealings with their own power. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions at the poles of one line, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one shows each person a force they rarely allow themselves.

Facing your Mars, the other person's Lilith shows you a freedom you may not use: the plain refusal, the route nobody approved, the untroubled no. Facing your Lilith, their Mars shows you something just as foreign: drive that asks nobody's permission because it never thought it needed any, effort made in public without apology.

Opposite signs share a modality and want complementary things. Mars in Cancer opposite Lilith in Capricorn, both cardinal, sets a protective, home-focused drive across from a refusal to bow to anyone's authority; Mars in Sagittarius opposite Lilith in Gemini, both mutable, a push towards the big picture across from an untamed, restless mind.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The pull can become an attempt to tame. The Mars person may try to recruit the other's independence into their own plans, or to make it more orderly; the Lilith person may try to slow the other's push, or to provoke it. Each can end up acting out the part the other has cast them in: always the pusher, always the refuser.

An opposition is a polarity, never enmity, and nothing about Lilith marks anyone as dark. The fascination of the first months is real; what keeps it kind later is respect for two ways of acting that don't need to merge.

The other angles of the pair, seen from across the gap:

- **[Mars conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** no gap at all; drive and refusal meet on one degree.
- **[Mars sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** four signs nearer, an invitation to take the unusual route together.
- **[Mars square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-lilith-synastry/):** a quarter-turn away, where the push and the refusal meet at a corner.
- **[Mars trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-lilith-synastry/):** independence and drive in one element, with no seesaw to balance.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree that neither of you is a project. Trade roles on purpose: let the Lilith person lead with a plain, direct ask now and then, and let the Mars person practise a calm refusal. Name the areas where each of you does things your own way, and leave them alone. Between colleagues, that can mean one sets the pace and the other questions it, by agreement; in a couple or a friendship, keeping a part of life each person runs alone.

About 1 pair of people in 15 has this opposition, one way round or the other, within 6°, with mean Lilith. Lilith's end of the seesaw changes hands quickly by the standards of slow points: about nine months to a sign, so it's shared with the Lilith person's season of births, while the Mars end narrows it to this pair. Hours make little difference to either body, but the houses decide where the pull is felt ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). My notebook found Saturn, Lilith and Chiron holding 75 to 90% of the time, in single charts; it gives no figure for a contact between two, and I don't invent one. Because it touches what each person keeps guarded, I read it only for two people who both want it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Born with [Mars opposite Lilith](/astrology/mars-opposite-lilith/), a person often meets their untamed side in others first. Here the other really is another person, and the meeting goes both ways.
