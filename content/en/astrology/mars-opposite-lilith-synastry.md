To each of you, the other can seem the untamed one. In Mars opposite Lilith in synastry, one person's Mars faces the other's Black Moon Lilith from the far side of the zodiac, and two kinds of strength, open push and untamed refusal, keep catching each other's eye across the gap.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) acts in the open: it starts things, competes, and holds its line where everyone can see. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's mean far point, invites more than one reading; mine is autonomy, the part someone learned to tuck away, and the power they rarely show. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions at the two poles of a single line; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it's the one that shows each person a force they seldom allow themselves.

If the Mars is yours, the other's Lilith shows you a freedom you may not use: the plain refusal, the route nobody approved, the untroubled no. If Lilith is yours, their Mars shows you something just as foreign: drive that asks nobody's permission because it never thought it needed any.

The two signs face each other across one modality. Between Mars in Cancer and Lilith in Capricorn the axis is cardinal: a protective, home-focused drive across from a refusal to bow to anyone's authority. Between Mars in Sagittarius and Lilith in Gemini it turns mutable: a push towards the big picture across from an untamed, restless mind.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The pull can become an attempt to tame. The Mars person may try to recruit the other's independence into their own plans; the Lilith person may try to slow the other's push, or to provoke it. Each can end up acting out the part the other has cast them in: always the pusher, always the refuser.

Polarity isn't enmity, and Lilith isn't a mark against anyone. The fascination of the first months is real; what keeps it kind later is respect for two ways of acting that don't need to merge.

The other angles of the pair, seen from across the gap:

- **[Mars conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** no gap at all; drive and refusal meet on one degree.
- **[Mars sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** four signs nearer, an invitation to take the unusual route together.
- **[Mars square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-lilith-synastry/):** a quarter-turn away, where the push and the refusal meet at a corner.
- **[Mars trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-lilith-synastry/):** independence and drive in one element, with no seesaw to balance.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree that neither of you is a project. Trade roles on purpose: let the Lilith person lead with a plain, direct ask now and then, and let the Mars person practise a calm refusal. Between colleagues, that can mean one sets the pace and the other questions it, by agreement; in a couple or a friendship, keeping a part of life each person runs alone.

With mean Lilith, the opposition comes to about 1 pair of people in 15, one way round or the other, within 6°. Lilith's end of the seesaw changes hands quickly by the standards of slow points: about nine months to a sign, so it's shared with the Lilith person's season of births, while the Mars end narrows it to this pair. Hours make little difference to either body, but the houses decide where the pull is felt ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). When I tested single charts, Saturn, Lilith and Chiron held 75 to 90% of the time; pairs of charts were never in that count, and I won't put a number on this one. It reaches what each person keeps guarded, so both people need to have asked before I read it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

In a single person's chart, [Mars opposite Lilith](/astrology/mars-opposite-lilith/) is a balance between pushing and refusing, struck inside one life. Here it's struck between two, and both can move the point of balance.
