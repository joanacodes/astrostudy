Mars in the 5th house turns play into a contest and pleasure into a pursuit. Whatever you do for joy, a sport, a painting, a flirtation, a game of cards at the kitchen table, you tend to do it at full strength.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is drive and desire: what you go after, and how. [The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is what you do for joy: creativity, pleasure, play, romance, the way you show yourself when nobody is grading you. With Mars here, your leisure has energy in it. You like games with a score, sport, performance, creative projects with a deadline, and romance with a chase in it. Courage shows in self-expression: you'll get up and sing, show your work, ask someone out. Sport and physical play are often where this Mars feels most itself, because the body gets to compete as well as the will.

It's a succedent house, and tradition called it Good Fortune and gave Venus its joy there, so Mars works here in Venus's own house of pleasure, where desire and enjoyment can be hard to tell apart. Its natural sign is Leo. Across the chart is the 11th, the house of friends and groups, so your pleasures can now and then pull against your loyalties.

Tradition also gave the 5th children. I don't read that in a chart: none can say whether or when anyone will have a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/) at all. What I read here is your own play and creativity, as an adult.

Like every house, the 5th rests on the birth time. I calculate houses in Placidus, and with a time that's only roughly known, a Mars near either cusp could belong to the house next door ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). [Mars in the 4th house](/astrology/mars-in-4th-house/) guards the home front; [Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/), in its joy, gets the work done.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, this Mars is bold. You pursue openly, enjoy the early excitement of attraction, and bring energy and play into a relationship: adventures, jokes, physical affection. The chase can be so enjoyable that the quieter stage after it feels flat; the skill is finding the play inside commitment, not only before it. Competitiveness can creep into romance too, a need to win the argument or the game, and a partner who laughs and plays back helps keep it light.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, Mars in the 5th wants to create, perform or compete. Sport, the arts, entertainment, design, coaching, launching your own projects: anything that lets you put yourself into the work tends to motivate you. Hobbies can turn into second careers here, since effort and enjoyment run on the same fuel. You're often at your best when a job feels like play, with stakes and a little risk. Routine that leaves no room for self-expression drains this Mars faster than hard work does.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Turning every pleasure into a competition, until a friendly game stops being friendly. Taking risks for the thrill of it: courage isn't recklessness, and the 5th is where the two are most easily confused. Restlessness when life is calm, or when a romance moves past its first spark. And pride in your creative work that makes criticism hard to hear.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep some play that has no score at all. Choose risks that cost little if they fail, a stage, a race, a new art form, rather than your safety or someone's trust. Give the creative drive a deadline and an audience. In love, plan adventures together, so that excitement has a place inside the relationship. And when you lose a game, practise losing well: it's one of the best things this Mars can learn.
