Friendships kept in good repair, groups you help to last: that's the territory of Taurus on the 11th house cusp. The house of belonging, and of the future you build with other people, opens here in the sign of continuity, and what it gathers, it tends to keep.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Belonging, for this cusp, is a matter of staying: [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) on the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) cusp sets the manner of your friendships and groups ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and the manner is constancy. Friendships are tended for decades: the call returned, the birthday remembered, the yearly walk nobody has missed since university. In a group you're often the one who keeps it going, the person who knows where the minutes are kept and why the rules were made, and a club or a cause can count on you long after its founders have moved on. Networks grow through reciprocity, favours done and returned, introductions made with care. Shared plans move one practical step at a time, and a community garden or a cooperative suits you better than a manifesto. What helps, when a circle closes too tightly, is letting one new person in each year: the group stays alive that way. Aquarius, the sign that belongs to the 11th by nature, reaches for the new; with Taurus on your cusp, you're the one who makes sure the new thing lasts.

Tradition called the 11th the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy there; with Taurus on the cusp, the good that comes through your people tends to arrive as steady support rather than lucky breaks. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your 11th, and Libra, its other sign, always falls in your 4th with this cusp, on the IC itself or wholly inside the house: the circle you choose and the home you keep answer to one planet, and the two tend to overlap, the same faces at your table year after year. Aries usually holds the Midheaven above, so your public direction may be quick and self-starting while your friendships stay patient, and each steadies the other. Friends often become the people you trust with practical things: a spare key, a signature, the loan of your tools. Friendships take their colour from Venus's house, wherever it falls: the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Counting Placidus houses, which I always use, Taurus opens the 11th for roughly one London birth in fourteen and one New York birth in thirteen. Next door, the count drops to about 1 London birth in 17 on the 12th ([Taurus on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-12th-house-cusp/)) and rises to 1 in 12 on the Midheaven ([Taurus on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/)); in these charts Taurus never holds either as well. Taurus also lies wholly inside the 11th, with no cusp of its own, in about 1 London birth in 27 ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Scorpio holds the opposite cusp, the 5th, so the loves and pleasures you keep closest run deep and private, while the wider circle stays easy and steady.

A birth time out by an hour or so could move Taurus off this cusp altogether; it's the time that tells me which house opens in Taurus, and I don't read without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
