Here the 8th's business comes to the negotiating table. The ruler of your 8th house has stepped back into the 7th, the house of partners and open rivals, and trust, joint money and intimacy are worked out with the person in front of you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Partnership between equals, in love, in business or across a dispute, is the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/)'s subject; the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/), just after it, gathers what a bond accumulates: intimacy, trust, joint money and debts, the change closeness brings. Here the 8th's ruler steps back into the 7th ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Partners, with this ruler, bring depth or ask for it. The people you pair with may come with resources, debts, histories or secrets that become part of your life, or meet you in a period of change and change with you. Joint finances are often a central question in a partnership, personal or professional, and you do best with arrangements spelled out in advance. Intimacy and commitment are hard to separate for you: you want a partner to be both. Power and fairness are the open questions: who controls what's held in common, and how two people stay equal when one of them holds more. Open rivals, the 7th's other people, can carry the 8th's themes as well: a competitor for the same resources, a negotiation over what's owed.

Counted from the 7th, the 8th is the 2nd, a partner's resources in the language of [derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/). With the ruler of that house in the 7th itself, I read a partner as someone who brings their resources into the bond, or who takes charge of what you hold together. I keep to your side of it: a partner's own finances are theirs to read.

Because the 7th is angular, what the 8th usually keeps private tends to show here: people may know you as a couple who share everything, or as partners with money at stake. How much of your joint affairs you make public is worth agreeing between you.

Its planet and sign add the detail. Venus, ruling a Libra 8th, may stand in Virgo in the 7th, its fall: generous in sharing, and anxious to get the details right. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn 8th, makes trust in partners slow and lasting, and contracts formal. [Pisces on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/) brings Jupiter and Neptune, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 7th brings generosity to what you share with a partner, while Neptune's house shows where the line between yours and theirs blurs.

When trust and money are both on the table, arguments can follow; I read how you negotiate them, never how a divorce or a lawsuit would end ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)).

Minutes decide the side. Just below the Descendant lies the 6th, where the 8th's business becomes a job ([Ruler of the 8th in the 6th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)); just above the 7th lies the 8th, the ruler's own house ([Ruler of the 8th in the 8th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). In Placidus, the house system I use, both lines are drawn by the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The gift here is a partner who isn't afraid of the serious parts, and a partnership strong enough to carry them.
