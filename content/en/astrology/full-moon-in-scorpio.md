The full moon in Scorpio comes in the middle of the northern spring, with the Sun in Taurus and the Moon opposite, in the sign where tradition says it is least at ease. In 2027 it happens twice, a month apart, on the very first and very last degrees of the sign.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

At full, the Moon faces the Sun from the other side of the sky. Here the Moon is in [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), a fixed water sign, and the Sun at the matching degree of [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/). Scorpio is where tradition places the Moon's [fall](/astrology/fall/), which I read as feeling that runs deep and private rather than as damage: what surfaces at this [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) is often what has been held under for a while, a question of trust, a jealousy, a need you haven't named. The Sun in Taurus keeps the month on steady ground: comfort, the body, what you own and enjoy.

This is the [full moon in Taurus](/astrology/full-moon-in-taurus/) read the other way round: here the Moon carries *ours*, intimacy and what is owed, and the Sun *mine*. Scorpio's two rulers are [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), both of which I read, and [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Taurus.

Depth isn't drama, though. A full moon is a brief marker: it counts in a reading when the Moon or the Sun at that moment falls within a degree or so of something in your chart, a planet or an angle, and even then only as the peak of something slower, a transit or progression, that has already stirred. It foretells nothing.

The most recent fell on 1 May 2026, at 11°21′ Scorpio. In 2027 there are two: on 20 April at 22:27 UT, at 0°37′, with the Sun just into Taurus, and on 20 May at 29°13′, with the Sun about to leave it. The Sun takes a little longer than a lunar month to cross Taurus, so now and then two full moons fit in. Then come 8 May 2028 at 18°44′, 28 April 2029 at 8°26′ and 17 May 2030 at 26°37′. None is an eclipse.

Between the two of 2027 falls the [new moon in Taurus](/astrology/new-moon-in-taurus/), on 6 May. Before it comes the [full moon in Libra](/astrology/full-moon-in-libra/), after it the [full moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/full-moon-in-sagittarius/), a month either way.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Only a birth time shows which two houses it lights; without one, I'd be guessing ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The Moon sits in the house holding its Scorpio degree, in the Placidus houses I draw, and the Sun across from it. The two full moons of 2027 sit at opposite edges of Scorpio, so with any cusp inside the sign they fall in two different houses, a month apart.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Scorpio rising: feelings you usually guard show in your face, while a partner offers calm and steadiness.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** what you own and what you're afraid to lose, while shared resources ask for a simple, practical arrangement.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** a conversation that goes straight to the truth, while a belief or a journey asks for patience.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** family matters that run deep come to the surface, while work asks for a steady, unhurried hand.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** a passion felt in full, in love or creative work, set against friends who want things kept easy.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** intensity at work comes to a head, while something private asks for rest and comfort.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Scorpio on the Descendant: a partner's depth, or their need for honesty, while you want things calm and simple.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** Scorpio's own house: trust, intimacy and joint money at a peak, while your own values ask what's worth holding on to.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a belief examined to the root, while daily life asks for plain, ordinary talk.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Scorpio on the Midheaven: power and responsibility in your public role, while home asks for comfort.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** loyalty among friends put to the test, while a pleasure of your own asks to be enjoyed slowly.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** what you've kept to yourself rises in quiet moments, while the working day needs a steady routine.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Check both ends, and in 2027 twice.** A degree around each position above, in Scorpio or at the same degree of Taurus.
- **Say one true thing, gently.** Scorpio wants the truth; Taurus wants it told in a way people can live with.
- **Read Pluto, Mars and Venus by house.** They show where you transform, where you act and where you hold on.
