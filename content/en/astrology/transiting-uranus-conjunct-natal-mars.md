Once in its 84-year round, Uranus reaches the degree of your Mars, and for about twenty months the planet of change sits on the planet of drive. What you do, and how you go about it, wants doing your own way.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) initiates: effort, assertion, courage, the move from wanting to doing. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates, reforms and insists on autonomy. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes the two act as one, so here initiative arrives as a break from the usual: the job done a new way, the project started without waiting for a green light, the boundary drawn where it should have been years ago.

The pairing has a reputation for recklessness, even in a birth chart ([Mars conjunct Uranus](/astrology/mars-conjunct-uranus/)), and I think it's undeserved: what it describes is initiative that won't wait for permission. Drive tends to come in spurts. Impatience with slow systems grows, and so does the appetite for doing things alone. Competition changes too: you may want to win on your own terms, or leave a contest you no longer believe in. And Mars guards boundaries, so a no you've postponed for years can become easy to say.

People sometimes come to me afraid this transit means an accident. I don't read transits for injuries or illness ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and a restless drive with something to do is a resource, not a hazard.

With Mars in your 1st, the conjunction falls in Uranus's years there ([Uranus transiting the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-transit-1st-house/)): the way you act in plain view loosens, quicker, freer and less apologetic. The 1st house begins at your Ascendant, the degree rising at your birth, so it needs the time to the minute, and without it I don't read this [transit](/astrology/transits/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, Uranus holds your Mars for about twenty months, and only about one pass in ten takes eleven months or less. Uranus's retrograde months fall in the middle, so most passes, more than nine in ten, hit the exact degree three times.

Mars moves quickly, about half a degree a day on average, so two people born a month apart can have their Mars 15° apart and meet this conjunction three or four years apart. If Uranus reaches your Mars at 30, say, the sextiles ([transiting Uranus sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-mars/)) fall at about 16 and 44, the squares ([transiting Uranus square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-mars/)) at about 9 and 51, the trines ([transiting Uranus trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-mars/)) at about 2 and 58, and the opposition ([transiting Uranus opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-mars/)) at about 72. The conjunction itself, though, stays out of reach before 80 for about 1 person in 33.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the energy a target. A project with a start date absorbs restlessness that would otherwise go into arguments.

Start something of your own on a small scale: a side project, a new way of training, a business tried out at weekends. The conjunction favours trials you run yourself.

Leave a pause between impulse and action. A night's delay costs this transit nothing, and it keeps the boldness while losing the haste.
