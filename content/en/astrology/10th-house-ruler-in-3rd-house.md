The work happens in conversation. With the ruler of your 10th house in the 3rd, your vocation and your standing are built from messages, errands, short trips and the people within reach.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) holds vocation, standing and reputation, and in Placidus, the house system I use, it opens at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/); the planet ruling the Midheaven's sign takes all of that to the house where it sits ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) covers the comings and goings of every day: talk, writing, reading on the way somewhere, the brothers, sisters, cousins and neighbours you grew up with, and the first lessons before anyone asked what you wanted to study.

Your public role is made of many small exchanges. Reputations here are built locally and by word of mouth: the teacher a whole town remembers, the shopkeeper who knows every customer's name, the writer of a newsletter people forward to each other. Words are often the tool, whether you sell, teach, translate, report or drive the van that gets things where they need to go. A brother, sister or neighbour may figure in the story of your work, as a first colleague or a first rival, and the 3rd describes your side of those ties, not their lives. Horary astrology’s lists also give the 3rd letters, paperwork and contracts, and the schooling of childhood, so the paperwork can become the profession, or a teacher from your first school years can set the direction. The risk is scatter: a career that is all messages and no direction, busy every day and hard to describe at the end of a year.

It was in the 3rd, named the Goddess by the ancients, that the Moon was said to rejoice ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With Cancer on your Midheaven, the Moon rules the 10th, and from the 3rd it brings the warmth of the familiar to your working life: regular routes, regular faces, a public role that feels like looking after a neighbourhood ([Moon in the 3rd house](/astrology/moon-in-3rd-house/)). Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo Midheaven, is on home ground in the 3rd, Gemini’s house in the natural zodiac, and makes words the whole trade.

There's a symmetry in where it sits. The planet that rules the top of your chart was, at your birth, just past the bottom of it, starting the climb back from the IC, and that position is only as reliable as the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born a little earlier, you'd have it on the far side of the IC, in the 4th, where work and home run together ([Ruler of the 10th in the 4th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)). Born a little later, you'd find it higher, in the 2nd, where the work has to pay its way in what you value ([Ruler of the 10th in the 2nd house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)).

Scorpio on the Midheaven calls on Mars and Pluto at once ([Scorpio on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Mars in the 3rd makes you quick and direct with words, the negotiator or the debater. With Pluto there instead, the work turns towards investigation: the reporter or researcher who keeps asking until the story comes out.
