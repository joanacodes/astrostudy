Twice in each round of about 29½ years, Saturn stands at right angles to your Moon. These are the stretches when what life asks of you and what you need to stay steady are most likely to pull in different directions.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) brings structure, obligations and the long view; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feelings, needs and the routines that keep you feeling safe. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction that asks for adjustment, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your needs and your responsibilities compete for the same hours. Sleep, meals, time alone and time with the people you love get squeezed first, and the old ways of coping may not fit the new timetable.

It can be one of the heavier Saturn seasons, and it's worth being gentle with yourself: heaviness here points to a routine that needs rebuilding, not to something wrong with you. It's also a season when professional support earns its place. If your mood stays low or you're exhausted for weeks, a doctor or a therapist can help in ways no chart can ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

The first square, about seven years after Saturn crosses your Moon, tests whether the way you've arranged your care can carry real weight; the second, about seven years before the next crossing, asks you to drop the obligations that only habit keeps. When Saturn is crossing your 10th, for instance, work and public duties press on your private needs ([Saturn transiting the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-10th-house/)). With [Moon square Saturn](/astrology/moon-square-saturn/) in your chart, you'll recognise the tension; the transit gives it a new setting and, often, better tools.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, this squeeze usually lasts about nine months, which is why pacing matters more than pushing. A little over half the time it's a loop of three exact passes over about nine and a half months ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)); a quick single pass takes about three weeks. Like everything that hangs on your Moon's degree, the dates need your birth time, and without it I won't name them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have lived through five or six of them. [Transiting Saturn conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-moon/) lies about seven years before the first and seven after the second. [Transiting Saturn sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-moon/) and [transiting Saturn trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-moon/) sit a couple of years either side of each square, gentler stretches you can plan around, and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-moon/) stands exactly halfway between the two squares.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Lower the bar at home for a while. A Saturn square isn't the season for perfect routines; it's the season for ones that survive a hard week.

Say what you need in plain words, to the people who can help. The friction eases faster when needs are named before they turn into resentment or exhaustion. If you look after someone else, ask who looks after you, and put that person in the diary too.

Book rest the way you'd book an appointment. With responsibilities pressing, rest that isn't scheduled tends not to happen, and your Moon needs it more than ever.
