Uranus in your 8th house reaches the part of life that is hardest to change in a hurry: what you share with other people, what you owe them, and how close you let them come. For nearly eight years as a rule, the planet of freedom asks how much room there is inside intimacy.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) liberates: it wants enough freedom to question what was handed down and try other arrangements. The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is shared resources, intimacy, trust and the obligations that come with closeness, the things that are hard to take back once they're shared. Under Uranus, the terms of that sharing tend to change. Joint arrangements can be redrawn, a dependence you'd stopped noticing can come into view, and intimacy may want more air or more honesty, sometimes both. What you owe others, and what they owe you, can look different once you see it fresh.

Scorpio, the 8th's sign in the natural order, has two rulers, Pluto for modern astrologers and Mars for the tradition, and I read both. With Venus in your 8th from birth, Uranus's pass can change what closeness means to you, and what you want from it.

The old books gave the 8th death and inheritance, and some still dramatise this transit. I read neither death nor legacy in a transit ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and no chart can say what money you'll gain or lose. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this one brings out how your chart already shares and trusts, and gives it room to change.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

For a London birth, a Placidus 8th keeps Uranus nearly eight years in the middle, and most stays end somewhere between just under five years and nearly eleven. The middle barely moves for a New York birth, about eight years, but the band narrows, from nearly six to about nine, one of the tightest of any house at that latitude.

The count runs from Uranus's first crossing of the 8th cusp to its last day in the house, back-and-forth over the cusp included. Where that cusp falls, and how far the house stretches before your 9th begins, both depend on your birth time, so the time is where any reading of mine starts ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Before the 8th, Uranus was busy with the shape of your partnerships ([Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/)); after it, the change moves on to what you believe ([Uranus transiting the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-9th-house/)).

Your 8th cusp sits exactly opposite your 2nd's, degree for degree, so either will do to find the window. A 2nd cusp in Sagittarius means an 8th in Gemini, which Uranus crosses between July 2025 and May 2033. A 2nd in Capricorn puts the 8th in Cancer, reached between August 2032 and August 2039; a 2nd in Aquarius puts it in Leo, from August 2039. An 8th cusp in Taurus, across from a Scorpio 2nd, was crossed between May 2018 and April 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the arrangements you've outgrown before resentment names them for you. Uranus in the 8th makes old terms chafe, and saying so early keeps the change chosen rather than forced.

Keep some independence inside closeness: money of your own, friends of your own, an hour of your own. Freedom within intimacy is not distance from it.

When a sudden insight about a shared pattern arrives, write it down and sleep on it before acting. Uranus brings breakthroughs; the 8th asks you to use them with care for the other person.
