Their Mars in your 5th house brings another person's drive into what you do for the joy of it. Games turn competitive, creative ideas turn into attempts, and attraction, when it's there, tends to announce itself rather than wait.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is desire and courage as much as effort: it goes after what it wants. [The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) holds play, pleasure, creativity and romance, everything you do for the delight of it. Their Mars in this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) slips a dare into your fun.

With them, you may risk more for the pleasure of it: enter the contest, show the song or the painting, take up the sport you'd only ever watched, say out loud that you're interested. Their energy turns enjoyment into something you do rather than wait for. Attraction may be part of it, and when it is, it tends to be direct rather than coy.

For them, your sense of fun is a challenge they enjoy. They want to win your attention, beat you at the game, keep up with your ideas, and making things alongside you can bring out their boldest side. Succedent, the 5th is where pleasure builds into a habit of joy; its natural sign is [Leo](/astrology/leo/), warm and wanting to be seen, and tradition called it Good Fortune and said Venus rejoices there. Their Mars arrives in the house where Venus is happiest, and desire meets delight.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Play that has to be won can stop being play. Every game, joke or creative project may turn into a contest, or they may push you towards risks you only half want: the faster ride, the bigger stake, the bolder move. And if desire is in the picture, its pace may not match yours.

From their side, your lightness can feel like a game they can't win. They may push for more intensity and feel brushed off, or find that your easy touch doesn't take their feelings seriously.

Tradition handed the 5th the matter of children. I leave that out entirely: a pregnancy is not something a chart can foresee ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/) are no part of my work. Nor does an overlay say what two adults will do with an attraction; that is theirs to decide, together.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep the fun, and keep it mutual. Desire goes at the pace of whichever of you is less sure, and a clear yes from both is part of what makes it good; the same goes for any dare. In a couple, a playful rivalry, a sport or a hobby with a little edge can keep things alive; between friends, this is the friend who gets you onto the stage or the pitch; between colleagues, creative competitions work when nobody's pride is the prize; between adult relatives, a board game can carry old rivalries more kindly than words.

With the charts the other way round, it's your Mars in their 5th: you bring the dare into their pleasures, so check that they're still enjoying it. [Their Mars in your 4th house](/astrology/mars-in-4th-house-synastry/) works on home ground; [their Mars in your 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house-synastry/) takes the same push into your working day.

Your 5th house is placed by your birth time, cusp and all, and in Placidus, the house system I use, its width varies from one chart to the next. Mars is slow by comparison: morning or night, their Mars sat in nearly the same degree on the day they were born, so your time decides the house unless their Mars lies right on a cusp. A synastry still needs both times, since their houses carry their side of the story ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). And attraction is exactly the kind of thing that shouldn't be looked up in secret ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
