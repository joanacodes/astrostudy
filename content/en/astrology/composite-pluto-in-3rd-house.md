Talk is how this relationship is made, and remade. With a composite Pluto in the 3rd house, its deepest changes come through what the two of you say to each other every day, what you learn together, and the small world around you.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) covers the relationship's everyday exchanges: talk over breakfast, messages through the day, the books passed between you, the shops and streets of your neighbourhood, and the siblings and neighbours who make up its nearest circle. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) in this house describes a pair transformed by talking. A conversation can change the course of the relationship, in a kitchen at midnight or on a long drive; a class taken together can leave both of you thinking differently for good; a move across town can remake the bond's whole daily round. Nothing important stays unsaid for long, and what is said tends to go to the root.

This is the relationship's mind at work, which is neither the single digging mind of [Pluto in the 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-in-3rd-house/) nor one person's words reaching into another's thinking, as with [their Pluto in your 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-in-3rd-house-synastry/). In the composite, the depth lives in the exchange itself: each of you may be a light, easy talker with everyone else, and still the conversation between you keeps finding its way to the subjects most people avoid. Tradition called the 3rd the Goddess and gave the Moon its joy there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), a reminder that this house deals in the daily rhythm of things rather than in grand statements.

Heat can pass for honesty here, and that's the snag. A pair this intense in conversation can start trusting only what's said at full volume: the row seems truer than the calm talk, and the last word starts to feel like the true one. Volume isn't depth. Some conversations are worth keeping small on purpose, and a hard subject can rest overnight before you come back to it.

If talk between you has turned into checking, messages read, passwords demanded, every call accounted for, it has stopped being communication, and no chart can make sense of it: ring 999 if anyone is in danger, and Shout takes texts at any hour on 85258; the other lines are on my Disclaimers page ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)).

A birth time remembered as mid-morning leaves either chart's cusps loose by the better part of an hour, and the horizon moves about a degree every four minutes, so a composite Pluto near the edge of the 3rd can slip into the 2nd ([composite Pluto in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-2nd-house/)) or the 4th ([composite Pluto in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-4th-house/)); the second way of drawing composite houses, from the composite Midheaven for where you live, can move it again ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Ask each other for the exact times, and for each other's agreement before drawing anything: that conversation is the first one this placement asks for ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Of all the houses, this is the one where a relationship can be heard changing.
