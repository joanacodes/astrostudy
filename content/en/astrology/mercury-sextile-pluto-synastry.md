There's usually one person you call when something is hard to say. Mercury sextile Pluto in synastry often marks that person: one Mercury about 60° from the other's Pluto, and between everyday talk and real depth, a door that opens when either of you knocks. Each side of it is worth a look, since the one who confides and the one who receives carry different things.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how someone works things out and passes them on: observing, analysing, explaining. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is how someone transforms and empowers: clearing out what's obsolete, bringing up what was buried, finding a strength that is their own. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers two functions an opening to cooperate, which someone has to take, and in the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) each function has its own person.

What comes easily is honest talk about difficult things. The Mercury person can raise a painful subject and find it met calmly; the Pluto person can bring up something long kept down and find the Mercury person has words for it. Problems that need the real story, a family question, a stalled project, a decision with a lot at stake, get looked at properly. Decisions made together tend to deal with the cause rather than the symptom.

On the Mercury side of the pair, hard things become sayable in front of this person. On the Pluto side, this is someone who helps you say them out loud, and find they weren't as heavy as you feared.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opening to depth can stay closed, or open only one way. The pair may keep to pleasant small talk and never use what they have; or one of you may become the permanent confidant, receiving the other's depths without ever offering your own. The sextile also asks for timing: depth on request means depth when it's wanted, not whenever one person feels like digging.

Astronomers have classed Pluto a dwarf planet since 2006; astrologers still read it, and at 12 to 31 years a sign, its half of this sextile belongs to everyone born in the same span. The Mercury half is what makes it a bond between two people.

Inside one chart, [Mercury sextile Pluto](/astrology/mercury-sextile-pluto/) lets one person reach depth when they choose; here the choice involves two people, and both have to agree to go there. At the pair's other angles:

- **[Mercury conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-pluto-synastry/):** the two fused; every conversation reaches depth, invited or not.
- **[Mercury square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-pluto-synastry/):** a right angle, where light talk and depth catch on each other.
- **[Mercury trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-pluto-synastry/):** a smoother flow in one element, where insight comes without being asked.
- **[Mercury opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** words and depth face to face, each person holding one end.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use the door, and knock first. Ask "can I talk to you about something hard?" rather than launching in, and let the other say not now. Over time, make confiding mutual. In a couple, the sextile can carry the conversations that keep a relationship honest; between friends, the late talk that changes how one of you sees a situation; between colleagues, the pair who can name the real problem in a meeting without blame; between relatives, a way of talking about what happened years ago. Because it opens onto private life, I prepare this reading only for two people who both want it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

About 1 pair of people in 13 shares a Mercury–Pluto sextile, one way round or the other, within 3.6°. Where it lands is a matter of houses: if the Pluto falls in the Mercury person's 10th house, [their Pluto in your 10th house](/astrology/pluto-in-10th-house-synastry/), the honest talk reaches work and public life ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
