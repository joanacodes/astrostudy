Sun opposite Lilith in synastry puts one person's Sun at the far end of the chart from the other's Black Moon Lilith. The two people face each other across the circle, and each sees, very clearly, what the other leaves out.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) stands for the self a person lives from: identity, direction, the way they're recognised. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), a point astrologers read in many ways, is for me what a person refused to tame in themselves, or was refused: instinct, autonomy, the boundaries they defend, the power they weren't meant to use. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions as a polarity, each end lighting up the other, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) that polarity is split between two people.

What flows is awareness. With the Lilith on your side, the other person's presence can show you, by contrast, exactly what you've kept out of view, and sometimes why: standing across from someone so plainly themselves, your refusals become visible to you. From the Sun's end, the other person carries what your own identity left out: the appetite, the no, the unapologetic streak. They may fascinate you for it, or exasperate you, often both. Either way, you learn something about yourself from across the room.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opposition is a seesaw, and seesaws like fixed seats. Here the seats come ready-made: the Sun person becomes the reasonable one, the Lilith person the difficult one; one shines, the other refuses. Each can start to define themselves against the other. The Sun person may hear the other's refusals as a verdict on who they are; the Lilith person may take the Sun person's ease as a standard they're expected to meet, and refuse it on principle. Between adult siblings, it can echo an old family casting of the easy child and the difficult one.

None of this is enmity. Polarity isn't contradiction, and how much tension there is depends on what the two of you do with it.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Trade seats. If the Sun is yours, try on a refusal of your own and see what it's like to be the one saying no; if the Lilith is yours, let yourself be seen when you're doing well, not only when you're objecting. Notice when a role is speaking for you. In a couple or a friendship that alone can loosen things; at work, it may mean letting the person who usually objects run something, and the person who usually runs things object.

Within 8°, this opposition joins about 1 pair of people in 11, one way round or the other. That count follows mean Lilith, the one I use. True Lilith wobbles and can sit tens of degrees from the mean, so if your software shows it, the opposition may or may not be there ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

Both birth times are needed: no times, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The opposition itself rarely hangs on the time; the houses do. In the Lilith person's chart, the other's Sun usually lands in the house opposite their Lilith: if their Lilith sits in their 7th, your Sun falls in their 1st, and the seesaw runs between how they meet the world and how they meet the people closest to them.

In one chart, the same polarity is [Sun opposite Lilith](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith/). Between two, it shares its intensity with [Sun conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) and its friction with [Sun square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-lilith-synastry/), while [Sun sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith-synastry/) and [Sun trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith-synastry/) meet the same refusal with less at stake.
