The full moon in Pisces puts the Moon in the last sign of the zodiac, opposite a Sun in Virgo, in the weeks before the September equinox. It sets the dream against the to-do list, and in 2029 it comes twice, at the very start of Pisces and on its final degree.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

At a [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/), Moon and Sun stand at opposite ends of the sky. This time the Moon is in [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), mutable water, and the Sun on the facing degree of [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/). The Moon brings up what Pisces needs: rest, imagination, a softer edge, the feelings that don't fit on a list. The Sun in Virgo keeps the month practical: work, routines, the details that make things run. This is the axis of the [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/) read from the dreaming side, the Moon carrying the whole and the Sun the parts.

It often shows where you've been holding everything together and need to let something go, or drifting where a little order would help. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) share the rule of Pisces, and I read both; [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Virgo.

Soft as it sounds, it's still just a marker. I give it weight only when a natal planet or angle sits under one of its two degrees, a degree at most away, and then as the crest of a wave that slower movements, transits or progressions, have already raised. Nothing in it is a forecast.

The most recent, on 28 August 2026 at 4°54′ Pisces, was a partial [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/), two weeks after the total solar eclipse in Leo of 12 August ([eclipse in Pisces](/astrology/eclipse-in-pisces/)); for an eclipse I allow about 3°. The next falls on 15 September 2027 at 23:04 UT, at 22°53′, then 3 September 2028 at 11°59′. In 2029 the Sun's month in Virgo holds two: 24 August at 1°12′ and 22 September at 29°57′, the zodiac's last few arc-minutes, with the Sun just short of the equinox. The last in this list, on 11 September 2030, falls at 19°11′. Only the 2026 one is an eclipse.

Every year the [new moon in Virgo](/astrology/new-moon-in-virgo/) falls in the same solar month as this one; in 2029 it sits between the two, on 8 September. A month before comes the Aquarius full moon ([full moon in Aquarius](/astrology/full-moon-in-aquarius/)); a month after, the Aries one ([full moon in Aries](/astrology/full-moon-in-aries/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Your birth time places both houses, the Moon's and the Sun's opposite it; [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) explains my asking. Placidus gives me the cusps. Don't skip the Sun's side: it lights the house Virgo opens, where the practical answer to this full moon usually lies.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Pisces rising: your sensitivity shows, open and porous, while a partner wants practical help and clear plans.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** what you give and what you keep begin to blur, while shared accounts ask for exact figures.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** intuitions and daydreams fill everyday talk, while a study or a journey asks for careful preparation.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** a longing for a quiet, gentle home, while work demands precision and service.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** art, music, a romance with a dreamy edge, set against a group that needs your organising skills.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** compassion at work, helping where it's needed, while a private muddle in the 12th wants sorting.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Pisces on the Descendant: a partner who needs understanding, or understands you without words, while you want things clear.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** the edges of shared money or intimacy grow vague, while your own resources ask for a careful count.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a spiritual or artistic longing, a journey to somewhere that moves you, while daily errands want attention.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Pisces on the Midheaven: a vocation, a calling to help or create, while home asks for order.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** a shared ideal, friends in need, set against a craft or pleasure you want to do well.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** Pisces's natural house: time alone and the life within at their fullest, while the working day asks for method.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Both ends.** Allow a degree around each position above, in Pisces or at the same degree of Virgo; in 2029, check twice.
- **Let one thing go, put one thing in order.** Pisces asks you to release, Virgo to sort. Neptune, Jupiter and Mercury, by house, show where each belongs.
- **Rest counts.** Pisces's side of the month is not a lapse in discipline; it's the other half of it.
