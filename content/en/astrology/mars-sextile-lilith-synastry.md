Some people make the unusual option feel like an ordinary one. In Mars sextile Lilith in synastry, one person's Mars and the other's Black Moon Lilith are about 60° apart, and the less expected route stops looking like a risk and starts looking like a plan.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) starts things: it asserts, competes, makes the effort and protects a boundary. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is a calculated point, the Moon's averaged far point, and traditions disagree about it; in my reading it stands for autonomy, for what was pushed aside, and for a person's own sense of power. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) opens a door between the two that someone has to walk through, and within the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this door leads to independence that has somewhere practical to go.

Carrying Mars, you may find your initiative gets bolder around this person. Their refusal to follow a script shows you directions you hadn't considered, and you enjoy acting on them. Carrying Lilith, you may find your independence finally has a partner rather than a critic: someone who helps you do the thing your own way instead of asking why you can't do it like everyone else.

The signs usually combine elements that get on: fire with air, earth with water. Mars in Capricorn with Lilith in Pisces might give a dream nobody else took seriously a business plan; Mars in Aries with Lilith in Aquarius, a quick start for an idea that breaks with the group.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The ease can keep it all talk. Two people can enjoy agreeing that rules are there to be bent and go on following every one of them, because a sextile never forces the issue. The Lilith person may still keep the truly unconventional part of themselves out of sight, sharing only the acceptable version; the Mars person may wait for a clear signal that never comes.

From the Mars side, there's a temptation to cheer on the other's independence while risking nothing of your own. From the Lilith side, a temptation to let the other's energy carry your refusals into the world, so that you never practise carrying them yourself.

Seen from the sextile, the other angles:

- **[Mars conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** two signs closer, the drive lands on the untamed point itself.
- **[Mars square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-lilith-synastry/):** a sign further, the push and the refusal meet at a sharper angle.
- **[Mars trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-lilith-synastry/):** two signs further, independence flows without needing an invitation.
- **[Mars opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** the two face each other, and each sees the other as the free one.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Do one unconventional thing for real. Plan the trip off the usual route, run the project your own way, hold a family celebration the way you both actually want it. Let the Lilith person choose which parts of their independence come out, and let the Mars person bring a risk of their own to the table.

About 1 pair of people in 13 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 3.6°, using mean Lilith. On the Lilith side the position is close to a cohort's: mean Lilith crosses a sign in about nine months, so anyone born within a few weeks of the Lilith person has it at nearly the same degree. With an orb this narrow, the version of Lilith decides more than the birth time: true Lilith can sit tens of degrees away, and a pair can have the sextile with one and not the other. I use mean Lilith, and the houses still need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

On its own in one chart, [Mars sextile Lilith](/astrology/mars-sextile-lilith/) is a side door a person can choose to open. Shared between two, the door has two handles, and the less expected route gets taken more often.
