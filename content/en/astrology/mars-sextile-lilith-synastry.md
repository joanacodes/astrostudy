Some people make the unusual option feel like an ordinary one. In Mars sextile Lilith in synastry, one person's Mars and the other's Black Moon Lilith are about 60° apart, and the off-script option stops looking like a risk and starts looking like a plan.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) starts things: it asserts, competes, makes the effort and protects a boundary. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) isn't a body but a calculated position, the Moon's greatest distance from Earth averaged out; traditions disagree about it, and in my reading it stands for autonomy, for what was pushed aside, and for a person's own sense of power. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) opens a door between the two that someone has to walk through, and within the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this door leads to independence that has somewhere practical to go.

On the Mars side, initiative gets bolder around this person. Their refusal to follow a script shows you directions you hadn't considered, and you enjoy acting on them. On the Lilith side, independence finally finds a partner rather than a critic: someone who helps you do the thing your own way instead of asking why you can't do it like everyone else.

The signs usually pair fire and air, or earth and water. Mars in Capricorn with Lilith in Pisces might give a dream nobody else took seriously a business plan; Mars in Aries with Lilith in Aquarius, a quick start for an idea that breaks with the group.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The ease can keep it all talk. Two people can enjoy agreeing that rules are there to be bent and go on following every one of them, because a sextile never forces the issue. The Lilith person may still keep the truly unconventional part of themselves out of sight, sharing only the acceptable version; the Mars person may wait for a clear signal that never comes.

From the Mars side, there's a temptation to cheer on the other's independence while risking nothing of your own. From the Lilith side, a temptation to let the other's energy carry your refusals into the world, so that you never practise carrying them yourself.

Seen from the sextile, the other angles:

- **[Mars conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** two signs closer, the drive lands on the untamed point itself.
- **[Mars square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-lilith-synastry/):** a sign further, push and refusal meet at a sharper angle.
- **[Mars trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-lilith-synastry/):** two signs further, independence flows without needing an invitation.
- **[Mars opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** the two face each other, and each sees the other as the free one.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Do one unconventional thing for real. Plan the trip off the usual route, run the project your own way, hold a family celebration the way you both actually want it. Let the Lilith person choose which parts of their independence come out, and let the Mars person bring a risk of their own to the table.

Using mean Lilith, about 1 pair of people in 13 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 3.6°. The Lilith side is nearly a cohort's: the mean point takes about nine months over a sign, so anyone born within a few weeks of the Lilith person has it at almost the same degree. With an orb this narrow, the version of Lilith decides more than the birth time: the true point wanders far enough from the mean that a pair can have the sextile with one and not the other. I use mean Lilith, and the houses still need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

For the person who has it in their own chart, [Mars sextile Lilith](/astrology/mars-sextile-lilith/) is an option kept for themselves, to take or leave. Shared between two, each can hold it open for the other.
