The planet that rules your 9th house sits in your 4th, at the bottom of the chart. The search for meaning starts at home, and sometimes the far place becomes home.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Through the sign on its cusp, the [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) has a ruler, and the house where that ruler sits is where travel, study and belief get lived out ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Here it's the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), which begins at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/): home, family, roots, the past you carry, and the private self only the people closest to you see. Horary astrology's lists give the 9th foreigners and foreign lands, and the 4th parents, houses and land, which is close to the story this placement often tells.

I read it as belief with roots. Some people with this placement grow up inside a faith, a language or a culture that came from somewhere else, and spend years working out how much of it is theirs. Others make the move the other way and build a home in a country they once only visited, until the foreign becomes the familiar. Study can live at home too: a long project at the kitchen table, a parent who taught you to ask questions, a household where ideas were argued over dinner. The 4th describes home as you lived it from the inside, so this says nothing certain about what your parents believed; it describes what you made of it.

Loyalty is where it pinches. When convictions come with the family, questioning them can feel like leaving home, and keeping them unexamined can feel like staying a child for good. Most people with this placement end up doing a little of both: keeping the table, changing the conversation.

The Sun gives the clearest picture. With Leo on the 9th cusp, the Sun rules the house, and a Sun in the 4th means a birth late in the evening, in the hours before local midnight, when the Sun was below the horizon and still sinking towards its lowest point ([Sun in the 4th house](/astrology/sun-in-4th-house/)). In Placidus, the house system I use, the 4th covers the last third of that way down, so the ruler of your far horizons was close to the very bottom of the sky, and the search begins from the foundations.

The IC turns with the clock like every angle, so a few minutes decide which side of it a ruler near the cusp stands on ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A little later, it would have crossed the IC into the 3rd, and your beliefs would speak through everyday conversation ([Ruler of the 9th in the 3rd house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)). A little earlier, near the other end of the house, it would still be in the 5th, where meaning comes through play, love and the things you make for pleasure ([Ruler of the 9th in the 5th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)).

If your 9th opens in Pisces, it answers to Jupiter and to Neptune at once ([Pisces on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Jupiter in the 4th tends to make an open house where guests and ideas from far away are welcome. Neptune in the 4th makes home feel like a sanctuary, or leaves a longing for one. When the two sit in different houses, both stories run side by side.
