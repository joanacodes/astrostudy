Jupiter opposite Lilith puts your principles on one side of the chart and the part of you that won't be tamed on the other. It's the rarest of Jupiter's contacts with Lilith, at about 1 person in 44.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) stands for what you hold to be true and worth growing towards: principles, learning, a larger view of life. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's apogee and a point rather than a body, symbolises the instinct and autonomy you learned to keep back, along with your relationship to your own power and limits. Astrologers disagree about Lilith more than about most things, and I read it as a symbol, without moral weight. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates the two through polarity, so each end grows clearer by contrast with the other.

In plain words, a set of beliefs and an untamed instinct look at each other across the room. Often one end arrives through other people: a community, a teacher or a tradition whose rules seem to require that you hide something, or a person whose freedom makes your principles look narrow. The work is awareness. Neither the belief nor the refusal is the villain; the opposition asks what each one protects, and how both can be yours.

Polarity isn't contradiction. Lived well, this aspect makes people who can hold firm principles and a free instinct at once, and who are hard to recruit into anything that asks them to be less than whole.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Only about 1 person in 44 is born with it, counting within 6° and with mean Lilith: the smallest figure among Jupiter's aspects to Lilith. With true Lilith, which can stray far from the mean, the opposition may be wider, tighter or absent ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

An opposition links two signs that share a modality but sit in complementary elements. In the houses, the axis shows the stage: Jupiter in the 9th opposite Lilith in the 3rd might set a large, studied philosophy against a blunt everyday voice; Jupiter in the 11th opposite Lilith in the 5th, a group's ideals against your own unruly creativity or play. Placing the axis takes your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

From this opposition, the pair's other aspects look like this:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Lilith](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-lilith/):** the two ends in one place, so belief and instinct no longer need someone else to carry either.
- **[Jupiter sextile Lilith](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-lilith/):** a short, friendly distance in which the hidden part slips out through whatever widens you.
- **[Jupiter square Lilith](/astrology/jupiter-square-lilith/):** the square sets them crosswise instead of face to face, and the argument happens inside you.
- **[Jupiter trine Lilith](/astrology/jupiter-trine-lilith/):** belief and instinct agree, and the welcome comes with nobody standing in between.

Between two charts, [Jupiter opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-lilith-synastry/) can feel like meeting your own refusal in someone else's principles, or your own principles in someone else's freedom. I read it from both sides, because each person holds the end the other is learning.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Jupiter opposes your natal Lilith once a lap, for a few weeks; mean Lilith opposes your natal Jupiter once in each round of about nine years. Born with the opposition, you meet the first at your Jupiter return and the second at your Lilith return, since each body then regains its birth degree, opposite the other's natal place.

Weather, not a forecast: each of these brings the two ends back into view for a while, and decides nothing between them.
