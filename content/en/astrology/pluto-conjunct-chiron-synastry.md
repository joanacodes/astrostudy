Some people walk straight up to the thing you've spent years learning to step around, and they don't flinch. In Pluto conjunct Chiron, one person's Pluto sits on the other's Chiron: depth arrives at the tender place, and what happens next depends almost entirely on pace.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the urge to reach the root: to clear out what has stopped working, regenerate, and take back strength that was given away. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a limit that doesn't go away, and the adaptations built around it: a changed method, a different rhythm, knowledge earned the hard way and passed on. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes the two act together while each stays itself, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the acting is shared: one person brings the depth, the other the tender ground.

On the Chiron side, you may feel understood further down than usual. This person isn't embarrassed by your difficulty or by the workarounds you've built; they find them interesting, even admirable. That can be a great relief. It can also feel like a push to tear down a coping strategy that, for you, still works.

On the Pluto side, the other's tender place draws your whole attention. You see which of their habits around it are alive and which only guard an old fear, and you want to help clear the dead ones out. Your gift is unflinching honesty; your blind spot is Pluto's own, mistaking removal for renewal.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

With a conjunction, it gets hard to tell whose project the change is. The Pluto person can adopt the other's difficulty as a cause, with a plan for how it should be transformed; the Chiron person can hand the sore place over to someone else's intensity and lose their own say. Some limits are meant to be lived alongside rather than overcome, and some adaptations are skills, not symptoms. Neither person is doing harm; depth has simply arrived before an invitation.

The same two at other distances:

- **[Pluto sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-sextile-chiron-synastry/):** an offer of renewal that waits for the tender place to say yes.
- **[Pluto square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-square-chiron-synastry/):** intensity and adaptation at cross purposes, learning a slower pace.
- **[Pluto trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-trine-chiron-synastry/):** depth meeting the sore spot with steadiness instead of pressure.
- **[Pluto opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-opposite-chiron-synastry/):** renewal and accommodation facing each other, each with its own question.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

The Chiron person sets the pace and the agenda; that is the whole rule. If the Pluto is yours, ask before you dig, and treat their workarounds as knowledge rather than resistance. If the Chiron is yours, say which old way of coping you'd be glad to let go of, and which you're keeping. A mentor and an apprentice can use this conjunction well, and so can old friends who have seen each other through changes, or a couple willing to go slowly.

A birth in mid-1945 shows how the dates work. Pluto there, in Leo, receives the Chiron of people born from July to October 1941 and from January to June 1942, and, about fifty years on, of people born between September 1991 and August 1992, with a gap in early spring. The 1945 Chiron, in turn, sits under the Pluto of anyone born from August 1969 to October 1972. That spread keeps it off the list of contacts a synastry of mine counts, each of which involves the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven; in the houses, it shows how two generations met the same kind of tender ground.

The hour plays no part in whether the conjunction is there; finding its houses needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron is one of the three placements my notebook found holding 75 to 90% of the time, with Saturn and Lilith ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)); that was a person's own chart, and it gives this contact no number.

[Pluto conjunct Chiron](/astrology/pluto-conjunct-chiron/), in a single chart, gathers depth and tenderness in one person. Spread over two, it touches someone's most private ground, and that is ground I enter only when both people invite me ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
