Halfway between two Saturn returns, Saturn stands opposite the place it held when you were born. Everyone meets it three times by 80: in the mid-teens, around 44 and in the early seventies. It's the hinge of the cycle, the point where adding gives way to keeping.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your natal [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the long project of your chart: where you build slowly, carry weight and earn authority. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, works through polarity: each end shows what the other lacks. When Saturn faces itself, the structure you have put up since the last return stands in full view, and other people's expectations often hold the mirror: a role others count on, rules you enforce, a reputation you now have to live up to.

The question it asks is what all this structure is for. Before the opposition, you mostly add to the round's building; after it, you mostly keep, refine and let go ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)). This is where you can tell which commitments are load-bearing and which were scaffolding that could now come down.

The second opposition, around 44, can fall close to the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), which comes between about 39 and 44½, and the two together often feel like wanting stability and change at once. I read each on its own terms first. The first opposition, in the teens, belongs to an age this site doesn't read for ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).

With natal Saturn in the 10th, transiting Saturn faces it from your 4th ([Saturn transiting the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-4th-house/)), and the authority you built at work meets the needs of home, family or your inner life. Only your birth time can place natal Saturn in the 10th and draw its line to the 4th; without it I don't read this opposition ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each opposition lasts about nine months with my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, so the one in your mid-forties is a season rather than a birthday. A little over half of Saturn's [transits](/astrology/transits/) come as three exact passes over about nine and a half months; a straight crossing can take three weeks.

Among births every fifth day from 1930 to 2009, nobody met the first opposition before 12.8 or after 16.2, and half had met it by 14.3: someone born on 1 January 1965 at 12.9, on 1 January 1975 at 15, on 1 January 1985 at 15.4. The second follows one cycle later, around 44, and the third in the early seventies: exactly three by 80, for everyone.

On the way out from the [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/) come [transiting Saturn sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-saturn/), [transiting Saturn square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-saturn/) and [transiting Saturn trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-saturn/); after the opposition, the same three come again in reverse, until the next return.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask what each structure is for. Keep those that still serve the life you want, and take down the scaffolding.

Listen to the people who rely on you, then decide. Their expectations are information, not instructions.

Plan the second half of the round: what would you like to have finished, or put down, by your next return?
