Lilith sextile North Node in synastry sets one person's Black Moon Lilith a sextile, held to 1.8°, from someone else's North Node, and so a trine from that person's South Node. The untamed streak of one stands at a friendly angle to the growth of the other. It nudges; it doesn't shove.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is the mean apogee of the Moon, linked by readers to self-rule, to the instinct that guards a person's edges and to what they once pushed aside; given how traditions vary, I hold all that as symbol rather than fact. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity grown through practice and feedback, set against the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), which holds the strengths a person reaches for without thinking. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) makes cooperation possible without making it happen, and as one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) it comes with a twin: the Lilith that sextiles one node trines the other.

If the Lilith is yours, your independence nudges the other towards something they're learning, and from an easy angle. Your Lilith also trines their South Node, so you like the person they already are, and your frankness lands as encouragement rather than attack.

Carrying the North Node, you get a small permission. Watching the other turn down what doesn't suit them, you see that trying the unfamiliar needn't mean becoming someone else, and their ease with your old strengths makes the attempt feel safe.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The comfortable trine can swallow the sextile. Two people with this contact may enjoy each other on familiar ground and keep the nudge as banter, a dare that's never taken up. The Lilith person may also soften the frankest version of what they think, precisely because things are pleasant, and the opening goes unused.

- **[Lilith conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the untamed side planted on the other's path, not offered from the side.
- **[Lilith square North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-square-north-node-synastry/):** instinct cutting across the axis instead of opening a door.
- **[Lilith trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-trine-north-node-synastry/):** the balance reversed, a strong pull to growth and a small opening back.
- **[Lilith opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** Lilith settled right on the other's South Node.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take the nudge, on purpose. If the Lilith is yours, say the suggestion out loud instead of leaving it implied; if the North Node is yours, try one thing it points to and report back. A couple might use it to choose a holiday neither would plan alone; friends, to dare each other kindly; colleagues or adult siblings, to break a habit both secretly find too safe.

A Lilith in Leo, for a birth in mid-2005, has the Libra North Node of everyone born from August to December 1996 at 60°, with the South Node in Aries trining that Lilith. The other way round, people born in June or July 1995 have Lilith in Gemini, sextile the 2005 North Node in Aries. Mean Lilith completes its circuit in less than nine years and reaches 60° on either side of a fixed point, so windows on its side come every three to six years: May and June 2001, then April and May 2004. The dates use the true node, with the mean node compared.

Like every contact between two points that aren't personal, it falls outside what I count in a synastry: only contacts that include the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven go in. Through the houses, it shows what one era's independence can offer another era's growth. An hour's difference moves Lilith and the node by far less than the orb, so the sextile depends on the birth dates, but the houses it links need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

One chart holding [Lilith sextile North Node](/astrology/lilith-sextile-north-node/) gives a person's own instinct a say in their growth. Between two people, that say belongs to someone else, so I wait for both to agree before reading it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
