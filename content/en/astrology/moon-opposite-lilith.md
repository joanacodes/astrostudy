Moon opposite Lilith is an aspect between the Moon and a point of its own orbit. Black Moon Lilith marks the far end of the Moon's orbit; a Moon opposite it is on the near side. I read it as your need for comfort and your untamed part facing each other across the chart.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) tends your feelings and needs: attachment, habit, comfort, the sense of being safe. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is read more variously from one astrologer to the next; I read it as the instinctive, autonomous part of you, what you learned to hide, the boundary nobody gets to move. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity: each stays itself, and each becomes clearer by facing the other.

In plain words, the side of you that wants to be held and the part that won't be handled sit at the two ends of a see-saw. Well looked after, you may feel the wild part stir and want out; out on your own terms, you may catch yourself missing the nest. The opposition asks for both to stay visible: care that leaves room for your refusals, and an independence that still lets someone look after you.

What sets this pair apart is that Lilith is a point of the Moon's own orbit, so both ends of the opposition are lunar: the need to be safe, and the need to be safe from being tamed. People who have it are often quick to notice where care turns into control, in their own habits and in other people's kindness.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The opposition usually joins two opposite signs, which share a pace and complete each other's element: a Moon in Cancer opposite Lilith in Capricorn needs to belong and refuses to be ruled. The houses tell you which two areas of life face each other; a Moon in the 4th with Lilith in the 10th sets home's comfort against a public life that doesn't ask permission.

About 1 person in 25 is born with the Moon and mean Lilith within 8° of an opposition, a little rarer than their conjunction: the Moon moves fastest when it's nearest Earth, so it crosses this stretch of its orbit more quickly. Within a degree or two the polarity is loud; near 8°, it's a background tug. Since the Moon covers about 13° a day, a birth a few hours earlier or later may have the aspect or not ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

I read mean Lilith. True Lilith can drift tens of degrees from it, so the two can disagree about whether you have this aspect ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). Lilith came out as one of [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) far better than everything else in my notebook, so I never skip its contacts with the Moon.

Each month the Moon makes every aspect to Lilith in turn:

- **[Moon conjunct Lilith](/astrology/moon-conjunct-lilith/):** the Moon at the far end of its orbit, on Lilith itself; need and refusal arrive as one feeling.
- **[Moon sextile Lilith](/astrology/moon-sextile-lilith/):** the two cooperate when you let them; comfort that makes room for your no.
- **[Moon square Lilith](/astrology/moon-square-lilith/):** being looked after and refusing to be handled rub together, and you learn by trial where the line goes.
- **[Moon trine Lilith](/astrology/moon-trine-lilith/):** the untamed part and the need for comfort keep step, so naturally you may never name it.

Between two charts, [Moon opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/moon-opposite-lilith-synastry/) sets one person's need for comfort across from the other's untamed part: magnetic, a little unsettling, and felt differently from each side; I read it from both.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), the Moon in today's sky opposes your natal Lilith once a month, briefly: a mood that wants company while something in you wants the door left open. Mean Lilith, about nine months in each sign, stays within a degree of opposing your natal Moon for two or three weeks: a stretch for noticing which kinds of care you've outgrown. Weather, not a forecast: they colour an evening or a few weeks, and decide nothing.
