Uranus's own cycle opens and closes with a sextile. About thirteen years after your birth, Uranus has moved 60° on from its natal place; about seventy years after, it comes within 60° of that place again, on its way home.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Here [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) meets itself. The planet in the sky and the planet in your chart are the same function, innovation and difference, at two moments: the one you were born with and the one moving now. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through opportunity, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) offers a chance to try your own way of being different, with little pushing either way.

The first sextile falls at about 13, before the age at which I read anyone's chart ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). An adult can look back on it and ask what began then: a friendship, an interest, a first taste of doing things differently.

The second comes at about 70, and it's the one I read as it happens. Uranus is nearly home, and the sextile tends to open small, practical freedoms: a new skill, a technology you'd avoided, a way of living that suits you better than the one you kept while other obligations came first. It asks for engagement, like every sextile; the room is there if you use it.

With your natal Uranus in the 11th, the second sextile reaches it from your 9th ([Uranus transiting the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-9th-house/)): study, travel or a new philosophy feeds the friendships and causes you've built. Your Uranus's house is set by your birth time, and so is every reading I give; without the time, there isn't one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each of the two sextiles, the one at 13 and the one near 70, stays within my orbs for about twenty months, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and for about eleven when the pass is short; in more than nine passes out of ten it is exact three times.

Because the transiting and the natal planet are the same, the age is shared by everyone born within a few years of you. In the site's count, the first sextile came between about 11½ and 15 for every birth from 1930 to 2009, at about 13 in the middle: 12.7 for someone born at the start of 1935, 14.3 for the start of 1995. The second, in the planets' positions, falls between about 67 and 71, so everyone who reaches 80 has met both.

People born between August 1955 and August 1962, with Uranus in Leo, are meeting the second one now: Uranus in Gemini, until 2033, stands 60° short of their natal Uranus, nearly round ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)).

Between the two sextiles lies the whole cycle: [transiting Uranus square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-uranus/) around 20 and again in the sixties, [transiting Uranus trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-uranus/) in the late twenties and the mid-fifties, and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-uranus/), the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 42. After the second sextile, only the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/) is left, at about 84.

## What helps {#what-helps}

At about 70, try one new tool on purpose. The sextile rewards a small experiment, and a class, a device or a new route through the week all count.

Look back to about 13 for the thread. What you wanted then, before anyone told you what was practical, often says what the second sextile is for.

Spend time with people of other ages. Uranus marks a generation, and company from another one shows you which of your assumptions belong only to your cohort.
