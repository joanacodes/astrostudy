Halfway round the node's circuit of your chart, the South Node comes to your Mars. The ways you have pushed, competed and defended for longest are lit from the other side for about a month, while the North Node, opposite, shows the effort you have rarely made.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is how you assert yourself and spend effort. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), now on it, marks consolidated capacity: drive that has run the same course for years and knows the way. Across from it waits the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), and an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) works by contrast. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), you get to see your habits of effort from outside.

Perhaps you always take the lead, always do it yourself, always win the argument, always keep going past tiredness. Or the reverse: always wait to be asked, always give way. Whatever the habit, it was learned for a reason and still has its uses. What the month shows is its cost, and an alternative that someone across the table seems to practise without trying.

With your [Mars in the 2nd house](/astrology/mars-in-2nd-house/), the South Node crosses your 2nd and the North Node your 8th ([North Node transiting the 8th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-8th-house/)): effort you have always spent securing your own resources, doing it all yourself, meets an invitation to share the work and the means. Tradition gave the 8th to death and inheritance; I read it, in an adult's life, as what is shared and owed between people, and no transit foretells either ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). These houses come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The South Node's stay on your Mars, measured from 1½° before exact to 1° after, is about a month in the common case, and as short as three and a half weeks in a tenth of passes. Eclipse seasons make it long: a tenth run past three and a half months, about one in twenty-five touching three times. A Mars near 27° Gemini, for instance, carries the South Node from October 2029 into the new year, and the total lunar eclipse of 20 December puts the eclipsed Moon at 29°18′ Gemini, close enough to count by my three-degree rule ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)). Even that I read as emphasis on how you spend effort, never as an accident foretold.

Add about nine years to your age when the North Node crossed your Mars ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-mars/)) and you have the first of these; then add 18.6 years at a time. If the conjunction came at 10, the oppositions fall at about 19, 38, 56 and 75. [Transiting North Node trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-mars/), [Transiting North Node square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-mars/) and [Transiting North Node sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-mars/) fill the intervening years. If [Mars opposite North Node](/astrology/mars-opposite-north-node/) is in your chart, mark your nodal returns: this transit falls on them.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the move you always make first when something needs doing, and try another one once.

Let someone else lead a task you would normally take over, and watch what you learn from how they do it.

Share one job you have always carried alone, and keep the old drive for the places it still serves.
