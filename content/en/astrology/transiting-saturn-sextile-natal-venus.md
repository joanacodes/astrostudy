About five years after Saturn crosses your Venus, and again about five years before it comes back, the two make a sextile. It's the gentlest of Saturn's visits to Venus and the easiest to sleep through: a door held open for something you value to take a steadier shape.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of you that chooses what you like, whom you're drawn to and what feels fair between people. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is how you build to last. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, links two functions through opportunity and cooperation, and opportunity asks to be taken up: nothing under a sextile happens unless you take the step.

What's on offer is structure for a pleasure or a bond. Lessons for the instrument you've meant to learn for years. A standing arrangement with a friend you otherwise see by chance. A routine shared with a partner that keeps an evening for the two of you. Potential isn't a promise, and the chance tends to arrive as a practical, slightly unglamorous offer, which is exactly why it's easy to let pass.

The house of your Venus says what the step concerns. With Venus in your 3rd, the house of everyday exchange and learning, Saturn often makes the sextile from your 1st ([Saturn transiting the 1st house](/astrology/saturn-transit-1st-house/)), and the move tends to be yours to make: a class, a letter you've owed, a habit of seeing a sibling or a neighbour. Your houses come from your birth time; without it, I don't read the transit ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Venus sextile Saturn](/astrology/venus-sextile-saturn/), you'll find this transit keeps company with your own Saturn cycle: it comes with each Saturn return, and with one of the two trines Saturn makes to its birth place on the way round.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

I count a Saturn [transit](/astrology/transits/) from 1½° before exact to 1° after ([orbs](/astrology/orbs/)), and with those limits this one usually lasts about nine months. A little over half the time Saturn's retrograde loop carries it across the exact sextile three times, over about nine and a half months; a single pass at full speed can take as little as three weeks. That's time enough to sign up, show up, and find out whether a new routine holds.

The sextile comes twice in each round, so by 80 everyone has met it five or six times. The first of each pair follows [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-venus/) by about five years and comes a couple of years before [transiting Saturn square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-venus/); [transiting Saturn trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-venus/) and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-venus/) lie further on, and the second sextile comes on the way back, about five years before the next conjunction.

From February 2026 to April 2028 Saturn crosses Aries, so it's Venus in Gemini or Aquarius that gets this sextile now, a few degrees at a time.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take the practical offer. If someone suggests a regular day, a class or a plan, say yes before you talk yourself out of it.

Build one ritual. A weekly walk, a set evening, a monthly letter: Saturn sextile Venus is good at turning affection into a habit.

Keep it small enough to keep. This sextile helps what you can sustain, not what you can promise.
