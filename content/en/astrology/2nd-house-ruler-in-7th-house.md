Your money tends to arrive by way of another person. With the ruler of the 2nd in the 7th house, what you earn and what you value pass through partners, clients and the agreements you make with them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Everything the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) calls yours, money, belongings, the skills you could live from, your sense of worth, answers here to a ruler standing in the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), the house of the person across the table: a partner, a client, a collaborator, anyone you deal with as an equal, the open rival included.

Worth, for you, grows in negotiation. Your income may come through clients, contracts, commissions or a business partnership, and your sense of what's fair tends to be sharp. A partner's way with money can shape yours, for better or for harder, and you may find out what you value by seeing it in someone else. The risk is leaving your security in another person's hands: an agreement you didn't read, a joint plan that is mostly theirs. The skill is the one the 7th always asks for, balance, applied to money: a fair price, a clear contract, a share you can stand behind.

Horary astrology used the 7th for the other party in any matter, partner, client or opponent, and the 2nd for your own goods, which puts the ruler of what's yours in the house of whoever you're dealing with. Counted by sign, the two houses also sit 150° apart, a distance that makes no aspect, which tradition called aversion ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)). I take that as money that has to pass through someone else before it comes home to you.

With Taurus or Libra on the 2nd cusp, Venus rules your money from the house matched with Libra in the natural zodiac: fairness and charm are part of how you earn, and you may spend generously on the people you love. Mars here bargains hard; Saturn draws up the contract and reads every clause.

When you were born, the planet of your money hung low in the west, close to setting: that strip of sky above the Descendant is the 7th in Placidus, the house system I use. The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) moves with the minutes ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): just below it, in the 6th, your income would come from daily work and your own skills ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 6th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)); further up, in the 8th, it would move into what's held in common, the joint account, the loan and the trust they need ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 8th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)).

With Aquarius on the 2nd cusp, Saturn and Uranus share the house, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 7th wants terms agreed and kept, while Uranus, from its own house, keeps some part of your money independent of anyone else.

None of this predicts a marriage, a lawsuit or a payment. It describes how your resources travel: through other people, and through the agreements you make with them.
