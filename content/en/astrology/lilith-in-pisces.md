Two fish, tied together, swimming in opposite directions: that's the old picture of Pisces, and it suits Lilith here. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point of the Moon's orbit farthest from Earth, was in [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) when you were born, and it marks the fish that went quiet — an imaginative, feeling inner life — while the practical one swam on for everyone to see.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Pisces is mutable water: it imagines, feels its way into other lives, and senses how things connect. With Lilith in it, what was taken off the table is often that porous, dreaming side — the sensitivity, the inner or spiritual life that didn't fit a practical household. You may have been told to toughen up, to stop daydreaming, to be sensible, and so you learned to be all three in public.

The refusal here rarely argues. It goes around: it keeps the inner life alive in private, where nobody can tell it to grow up. What it refuses is hardness — cruelty, cynicism, the claim that only the measurable counts. Its boundaries are real, but drawn in feeling rather than in fences, so other people don't always see them until they've crossed one.

Pisces is the end of the round. Mean Lilith arrives from [Lilith in Aquarius](/astrology/lilith-in-aquarius/) and, about nine months later, starts its lap again at [Lilith in Aries](/astrology/lilith-in-aries/). It was in Pisces from November 1930 to August 1931, September 1939 to June 1940, July 1948 to April 1949, May 1957 to February 1958, April to December 1966, February to November 1975, December 1983 to September 1984, October 1992 to July 1993 and August 2001 to May 2002. Those are mean Lilith's dates ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). Everyone born in one of those stretches shares the fish; the house, which [needs your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-it-doesnt), says which waters are yours.

Pisces is a sign of faith, but I'd rather you didn't take this on faith: when I checked charts line by line, Lilith came out among [the three placements that held most often](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), at 75 to 90%.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Lilith in Pisces feels a great deal in love, and has often learned to keep most of it out of sight. It's drawn to tenderness and to people with an inner life, and it recoils from mockery, or from a partner who treats its sensitivity as a problem to be fixed. The hidden part needs room to be dreamy, spiritual or simply quiet without being called vague.

What it sometimes has to refuse is the role of rescuer. Compassion is its gift; carrying someone else's whole weight is a different thing.

## At work {#at-work}

At work this Lilith brings imagination, empathy and a feel for what isn't being said, in the arts, in teaching, in any work where people are more than their job titles. It wilts in places that count only what can be measured. The hidden part refuses to harden itself to get on, and needs some corner of the working week where intuition counts as knowledge.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Feeling everything has a cost. You may take in other people's moods so readily that your own wishes get lost underneath them, or hide the sensitive part so well that you seem untouchable while feeling everything. If the inner life was mocked, it can go private for good, and the world only ever meets the practical fish.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the imagination a form: a notebook, a practice, music, a place you go to be quiet on purpose. Pisces has two rulers to say where, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern one, shows by its house where the dreaming needs room; its sign changes only about every fourteen years. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the traditional one, shows by sign and house where that inner life can grow into something you share.

Practise the small, clear no. It's an act of compassion too, for you as much as for anyone, and it keeps the two fish tied together instead of pulling apart.
