About ten years after Saturn crosses your Venus, and ten before it comes back, the two stand a trine apart. For some months the things you love and the structures that hold them seem to agree, and staying power comes without a struggle.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how you value and relate: what you find beautiful, whom you choose, what you'd keep. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) gives things time and form. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets two functions reinforce each other while each keeps its identity, so Venus stays warm and choosy and simply finds Saturn's patience on its side.

This is a good season to formalise something you already value: a creative practice put on a schedule, a home made more beautiful one room at a time, a friendship or a partnership given the practical arrangements that let it last. Taste matures without turning severe; affection steadies without growing heavy. Tradition gave Saturn its exaltation in Libra, one of Venus's own signs, and of all their contacts the trine is the one that most often lives up to that pairing.

Ease isn't inevitability, though. Trine transits ask for nothing, so they can pass with nothing built. Flow isn't passivity either: the season helps what you choose to work at and leaves the rest as it found it.

With your Venus in the 10th, the trine tends to show in work you love doing, or in how you're seen doing it. Saturn is then often crossing your 2nd ([Saturn transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-2nd-house/)) or your 6th, and the support comes from the way you manage your own resources or your days. Only a birth time can say whether Venus falls in your 10th or just short of it, so without one the trine stays unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Venus trine Saturn](/astrology/venus-trine-saturn/), you already know loyalty that doesn't weigh you down; this transit gives it a season to show.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

I give Saturn 1½° while it applies to the exact trine and 1° once it separates ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)). Within those limits the transit usually lasts about nine months; in a little over half of Saturn's [transits](/astrology/transits/) its retrograde loop brings three exact passes over about nine and a half months, and a straight crossing can be done in three weeks.

The trine comes twice a round, five or six times by 80 for everyone. The first follows [transiting Saturn square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-venus/) by two or three years, as if the friction had been worth something, and leads on to [transiting Saturn opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-venus/) five years later. The second comes on the way back, ten years before [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-venus/) begins a new round; [transiting Saturn sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-venus/) offers a smaller version of the same help nearer each conjunction.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Formalise what already works. If a bond, a practice or a home routine is good, give it the structure that lets it last: a regular time, a written plan, a commitment said aloud.

Spend the ease on craft. Lessons, practice, a long project in something you find beautiful: Saturn trine Venus rewards the patient version of pleasure.

Don't coast. Choose one thing to build while the wind is behind you.
