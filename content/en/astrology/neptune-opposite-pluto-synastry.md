Some of the most surprising friendships span more than half a century, and this contact belongs to them. Neptune opposite Pluto in synastry puts one person's Neptune about 180° from the other's Pluto, within 4°, and in every pair my data gives, the Pluto belongs to someone born at least fifty years earlier.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the dreamer's function: compassion, imagination, a feeling for meaning, and the softening of boundaries that have hardened for no good reason. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the function of deep change: it ends what has run its course, recovers what was buried and rebuilds from the root. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions as a polarity, asking that both be integrated; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it gives one pole to each person.

The signs face each other in one modality. Pluto in Leo for births of the mid-1940s, Neptune in Aquarius for births of the early 2000s: a power found through self-expression, across from a dream of community and a fairer, shared future.

Holding the Pluto, you know what endings cost and what rebuilding takes. The younger person's ideals may look untested, or may remind you of hopes you set down long ago.

Holding the Neptune, you meet someone who has already been through the kind of change you can only imagine. Their depth can steady your dream, or look like a weight you'd rather not carry yet.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Across that many years, projection comes easily. The elder can read the younger's ideals as naïve; the younger can read the elder's realism as control or cynicism. Each may also idealise the other, as the wise one or the hopeful one, and stop seeing a person. The seesaw only moves when each side lends weight to the other: the elder some hope, the younger some respect for what change costs.

- **[Neptune conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-pluto-synastry/):** the two on one degree, the Neptune an elder's, some 22 to 31 years older.
- **[Neptune sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto-synastry/):** an opening that people of nearly the same age often share.
- **[Neptune square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-pluto-synastry/):** an ideal and a clearing-out at right angles, mostly ten to twenty-four years apart.
- **[Neptune trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-pluto-synastry/):** depth steadying ideals across roughly a generation.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask each other the questions only the other can answer. The younger person can ask what the elder had to let go of, and how; the elder can ask what the younger hopes for, and listen without correcting. A grandparent and an adult grandchild, a retired professional and a newcomer, neighbours of very different ages, two volunteers in the same cause: each can trade realism for hope and back.

Neptune with Pluto is the slowest contact on the site, and its opposition asks for more than half a century between births. Someone born in mid-1945, with Pluto in Leo, has it with anyone born, on and off, from February 2000 to November 2004, whose Neptune is in Aquarius; someone born in mid-1995, with Neptune in Capricorn, with anyone born, on and off, from September 1930 to June 1938. The gap runs from about 52 to 65 years. Whole cohorts on each side share it, so I keep it out of my synastry count, which only admits contacts touching the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Through the houses, it is two eras facing each other, the one that rebuilt and the one that still dreams.

Neither planet moves enough in a day to change the opposition; the houses it crosses, such as the Neptune person's 3rd and 9th, everyday words and beliefs, come from both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Neptune opposite Pluto](/astrology/neptune-opposite-pluto/) never occurs inside a chart born in the years I counted. Between two, it bridges generations, and I read it only when both of them ask ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
