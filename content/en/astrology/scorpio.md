Scorpio is the eighth sign of the zodiac, a fixed water sign, and the one with the darkest reputation. I read it more simply: it's the sign that stays — with a feeling, a person, a question — long after everyone else has moved on.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Scorpio somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it wants what's under the surface and won't settle for less. It's a [water sign](/astrology/water-signs/), so it works through feeling, and a fixed one, so it holds on — to people, to loyalties, to whatever it has decided matters (the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/) explain the difference). [Libra](/astrology/libra/), just before it, makes the agreement; Scorpio wants to know what the agreement costs and what each person is really bringing. [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), next, comes up for air and looks for meaning.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/):** born between about 23 October and 21 November. You grow by going deep, and staying. You'd rather know one thing completely than many things a little, and you can be at your steadiest in a crisis, when others step back.
- **[Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/):** your feelings run deep and private. You feel safe with very few people, and once you trust someone the bond is whole. The Moon is in its fall here, which in practice often means strong feelings kept out of sight.
- **[Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/):** people sense your intensity before you say a word, and you watch before you speak. With two rulers, there are two planets to look at as your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/): Mars and Pluto. A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So a Scorpio Sun tells me where to start, not what to conclude.

## Its rulers: Pluto and Mars {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, and where that planet sits in your chart shows where the sign's energy goes. Scorpio has two. For most of astrology's history it was ruled by [Mars](/astrology/mars/), which also rules Aries: in Aries, Mars charges in the open; in Scorpio, it's the strategist who waits. After [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) was discovered in 1930, modern astrologers gave it Scorpio and read it as the planet of depth, power and transformation. Traditional astrologers still use Mars alone; many modern ones read Pluto first and keep Mars as co-ruler ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/) explains the split). Pluto's sign is shared by a whole generation, so its house is what makes it yours; Mars moves faster and speaks more personally. I read both, and tell you what each one says.

Tradition exalts no planet in Scorpio. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) here, opposite its home in Taurus: easy pleasure meets a sign that wants all or nothing. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is in [fall](/astrology/fall/), opposite its exaltation in Taurus: comfort is harder to come by, and feelings go underground. Neither is a flaw. They're the places where love and comfort have to be earned, and often feel all the more real for it.

## Planets in Scorpio {#planets-in-the-sign}

Every planet passes through Scorpio, from the Moon in about two and a half days to Pluto, which can spend anywhere from 12 to 31 years in a sign. In Scorpio, each one goes looking for what's underneath:

- **[Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/):** a self built on depth and loyalty; you'd rather be trusted than liked.
- **[Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/):** the Moon in fall: strong, private feelings, and a need for one or two people who can hold them.
- **[Mercury in Scorpio](/astrology/mercury-in-scorpio/):** a probing mind that reads between the lines, keeps secrets well and asks the question no one else will.
- **[Venus in Scorpio](/astrology/venus-in-scorpio/):** Venus in detriment: love as all or nothing — loyal, intense, and asking for the same depth in return.
- **[Mars in Scorpio](/astrology/mars-in-scorpio/):** Mars at home in the traditional scheme: quiet, sustained drive; you rarely show your hand and almost never give up.
- **[Jupiter in Scorpio](/astrology/jupiter-in-scorpio/):** growth through depth — research, psychology, shared resources — and a generosity that shows up in a crisis.
- **[Saturn in Scorpio](/astrology/saturn-in-scorpio/):** shared by everyone born over about two and a half years: lessons in trust and control, and boundaries in intimacy built slowly.
- **[Uranus in Scorpio](/astrology/uranus-in-scorpio/):** shared by everyone born over about seven years: taboos questioned, and sudden changes in what's kept private. Its house shows where.
- **[Neptune in Scorpio](/astrology/neptune-in-scorpio/):** shared by everyone born over about fourteen years: a fascination with mystery and with what lies hidden. Its house makes it yours.
- **[Pluto in Scorpio](/astrology/pluto-in-scorpio/):** Pluto at home in modern astrology, shared by everyone born during its passage through Scorpio, the quickest it makes through any sign: intensity and change as a generation's backdrop. Its house says where it lands.

## Scorpio on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Scorpio somewhere in its twelve houses, usually on the cusp of one of them. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Scorpio opens a house, that's where you go deep and hold on, and Mars and Pluto, wherever they sit, carry the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/scorpio-rising/):** Scorpio rising — an intense, watchful presence; people feel you before they know you.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money kept private and handled strategically; you may earn through research, other people's resources or what others overlook.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** words with weight; you notice what goes unsaid and prefer one deep conversation to ten light ones.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a private refuge, closely guarded; family stories with depth, and sometimes secrets.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/):** love affairs taken seriously, never casually; creativity that goes to the dark, honest places.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work you can throw yourself into completely; a talent for investigating, fixing and getting to the root of a problem.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio on the Descendant, which means Taurus rising: you're drawn to intense, private partners, and commitment is all or nothing.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio in its natural house: intimacy, trust and shared resources are where you're most at home, and least willing to compromise.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs tested to the bottom before you keep them; a pull towards psychology and hidden knowledge.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career in research, investigation, crisis or care work, or wherever power and secrets are handled; a reserved public image.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-11th-house-cusp/):** few friends, fiercely loyal; in groups you hold quiet influence rather than the microphone.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-12th-house-cusp/):** an intensity few people see; strength found in private, and feelings worked through alone before they're shared.

## Scorpio in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Scorpio placements bring depth, loyalty and a need for complete honesty; they can also bring a fear of betrayal that looks like control. Its opposite sign, [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), is the pull: Taurus holds on to what's simple and present, Scorpio to what's hidden and deep, and each has what the other lacks. Scorpio's love of privacy is also a good reminder of why I [won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/). And I don't judge a pair by their Sun signs: [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind there is.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Scorpios are vengeful.”** Scorpio remembers, because it takes trust seriously. What looks like a grudge is often a hurt that was never acknowledged, and the rest of the chart says far more about how someone handles it.
- **“Scorpio is the sex sign.”** Scorpio is about intimacy in every sense: trust, shared money, what people keep hidden. Desire is part of it, not the whole.
- **“Ophiuchus is the real sign between Scorpio and Sagittarius.”** The Sun does cross the constellation Ophiuchus, between Scorpius and Sagittarius, but the signs are twelve equal parts of the [tropical zodiac](/astrology/tropical-zodiac/), counted from the March equinox. [Ophiuchus](/astrology/ophiuchus-and-the-13th-sign/) is a constellation, not a missing sign.
- **“Born on 21 or 22 November, you're half Sagittarius.”** At the minute you were born the Sun was in one sign; near a change, your birth time settles which ([born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/)).
