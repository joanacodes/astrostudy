For a year and a half or so, the North Node backs through your 2nd house, where you keep what you have and what you value, while the South Node backs through your 8th, where what's shared and owed is kept. The axis runs between *mine* and *ours*.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to growth that feels unfamiliar at first and steadier with practice. In the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/), which holds your belongings, your earning and the sense of your own worth underneath them, that growth is self-reliance of a concrete kind: learning what you value rather than what you were told to want, building a skill that could support you, looking after what's yours.

Across the chart, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) moves through your 8th, and with it a familiar pattern: sharing so much that nothing feels quite your own, relying on a partner's means or a family's, or carrying feelings and debts that began as someone else's. Depth and trust are real gifts of that house, and nothing asks you to drop them. The stretch is to stand on ground you've built.

With Venus in your 2nd, ruler of Taurus, the house's natural sign, the month or so the node spends on it tends to ask what you actually enjoy owning and spending on. Other natal planets there are met from the top of the house down.

I don't read money outcomes in a [transit](/astrology/transits/): not a rise, a loss, an inheritance or a good investment ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). The nodes describe a direction and a habit, never fortune, and never a debt carried over from another life.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Here the node keeps to its average. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the true node's stays in the 2nd last about a year and a half in the middle, with most between about 11 months and 2 years and 5 months, a month longer at the top than the figure for all houses together. At New York's latitude the middle holds at a year and a half and the range tightens, to between about a year and 2 years.

Those figures follow the true node, the one I use for timing, and count a wobble back over the cusp within 120 days as part of the same stay. I look at the mean node as well ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)): the two sit a degree or two apart at most, enough to move a date near a cusp.

Where your 2nd begins and ends depends on your birth time, which is why I don't read without one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node comes into the 2nd after its stay in your 3rd ([North Node transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-3rd-house/)) and leaves it for your 1st ([North Node transiting the 1st house](/astrology/north-node-transit-1st-house/)).

To find your dates, look at the signs your 2nd spans and at the months the node reaches each one, travelling backwards: Aquarius in July 2026, Capricorn in March 2028, Sagittarius in September 2029, Scorpio in March 2031, Libra in December 2032, Virgo in June 2034, Leo in November 2035, Cancer in May 2037 and Gemini in February 2039. Before Aquarius it was in Pisces, from January 2025. Taurus, the 2nd's natural sign, comes after Gemini, beyond 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you'd keep if you had to choose. Values become usable when they're specific.

Build one skill you could be paid for, or one habit of looking after what you own. The North Node in the 2nd grows through small, practical proof.

Untangle gently. Notice which obligations you chose and which you took on by default, and keep the loyalty the South Node knows so well for people who return it.
