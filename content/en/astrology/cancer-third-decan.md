Cancer closes with ten degrees the Moon holds twice, as ruler of the sign and by face, while Pisces lends the decan its two rulers. The third decan of Cancer, 20° to 30°, is also where the brightest stars of the constellation Gemini now stand, and the Sun crosses it in the second half of July.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

The Chaldean order reaches the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) at the end of [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), so Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) give the Moon a face inside its own sign. Of the Moon's five faces, this is the only one in a sign it rules: a [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/) between 20° and 30° is at home twice over.

Pisces, the third water sign, supplies the ruler by element, and Pisces has two: [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) as modern tropical astrology assigns it, [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) as the tradition does. I read both. Jupiter brings a twist of its own, being exalted in Cancer: on the traditional side, the decan goes to a planet the sign already honours.

So a planet here hears the Moon twice, by sign and by face, before anything else: care, protection, the need to feel safe. Then comes Pisces's pair. Through Neptune it reaches for imagination and compassion; through Jupiter, for growth and meaning. I look at your Moon first and longest, then at your Neptune and Jupiter, and only then let the decan add its shade.

## Its image {#its-image}

Kenmut alone is Budge's name for the middle decan of the three that carry it, between Ṭepā-Kenmut before it ([Cancer, second decan](/astrology/cancer-second-decan/)) and Kher-khept-Kenmut after it, at the start of Leo; the list had grouped neighbours around Saḥ in the same way, from late Taurus to the end of Gemini. The Greco-Egyptian names pair off as well, Knum here and Char-Knum next, and the Greek Hermetic version is Chnouphos.

Firmicus Maternus and Joseph Scaliger spell it almost alike, Thiumis, or Thumus, and Thuimis, while the *Testament of Solomon* goes its own way with Saphthorael. Hekate is the figure Cosmas of Maiuma gives these degrees, and Mercophta the one in Athanasius Kircher's list ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

From about 12 to 22 July the Sun finishes its time in Cancer in this decan ([Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/)), and its border with Leo moves from year to year: 19:13 UT on 22 July in 2026, 01:04 UT on 23 July in 2027. The 22nd and 23rd therefore belong to one sign or the other depending on the year and the hour ([Leo, first decan](/astrology/leo-first-decan/)).

The two brightest stars of the constellation Gemini stand in the sign of Cancer, in this decan: [Castor](/astrology/castor/) at 20°36′ and [Pollux](/astrology/pollux/) at 23°34′ in 2026, followed by [Procyon](/astrology/procyon/), the Little Dog's star, at 26°08′.

Pluto spent most of the 1930s here, steadily from April 1932 to October 1937, with visits from 1930 and until 1939, so it is the Pluto of many people born in that decade ([generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/)). Uranus took over for births from August 1953 to August 1955, and Saturn for those of 1945–46, 1975–76 and 2004–05. On 22 July 2028 a total eclipse of the Sun falls at 29°50′ Cancer, on the very edge of Leo, six months after a partial eclipse of the Moon at 21°33′ on 12 January.

In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) the Moon, checked like every placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often, and overall about a third held. Its double rule here makes the Moon the planet to read closely, not a certainty to lean on.
