A Leo Sun is sure of its own centre; a Libra Moon feels steady only once things are even between you and someone else. Here the Moon sits in the sign of the Sun's fall, a few days into the lunar month, and I read the pair as a strong *I* that takes its comfort from a fair *we*.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Two signs ahead of the Sun lies the Moon, fire to air: a sextile by sign, an opening rather than a given, which pays off when you take it up. Leo is fixed and keeps its loyalties; Libra is cardinal and goes out to make peace rather than wait for it. The [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) wants to create from the heart and be recognised for it, while the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) settles through balance, good company and a room that looks right. With warmth and grace together, the people near you tend to feel welcome and admired in the same moment.

Tradition adds a twist. Libra, where your Moon is, is the sign of the Sun's [fall](/astrology/fall/), while Leo, where your Sun is, is its home. I read that as needs that ask the self to share the stage: comfort, for you, comes from fairness and partnership, which a Leo Sun left to itself might not think to look for. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra, and since it stays within about 47° of the Sun, in a Leo month it can be anywhere from Gemini to Libra; its sign shows which kind of harmony restores you. Libra gives the Moon no dignity, so Venus speaks for it more than usual.

Between day 2½ and day 7 of the lunar month, your Moon had pulled 31° to 89° ahead of the Sun, a crescent setting in the west a few hours after sunset. Nearly nine births in ten belong to the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/), the remaining few to the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase, and near 60° ahead the chart holds a waxing [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). In a crescent birth I see needs that have just left the Sun's side and are learning to ask for themselves; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) follows that thread through the month.

Around 1 person in 140 is born under this pair. The Moon moves through Libra in some two and a half days, so a birthday on which it came over from Virgo or went on into Scorpio needs the hour to tell this page from the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-virgo/) or the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-scorpio/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). That hour also sets the rising sign, the third of the [big three](/astrology/big-three/), and only with all three in hand does a first impression become a place to start.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Romance comes naturally to you, and so does courtesy. The Leo Sun loves openly and wants to be someone's particular person; the Libra Moon needs the exchange to be fair, the evening pleasant, the quarrel settled before bed. You're often the partner who plans the beautiful dinner and smooths the difficult conversation. What can slip is your own wish, when a partner's mood is easier to read than yours. Say what you want first, then weigh it against theirs.

## At work {#at-work}

Presence and diplomacy both count in the work that suits you: hosting, design, the arts, negotiation, any role where you represent something to the public. The Sun gives you the confidence to stand in front of people, the Moon the tact to read the room and bring it round. Hostile or graceless workplaces cost you more than they cost most people, and a role where you only polish other people's work, never present your own, starves the Leo Sun.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Pride and peace can pull against each other. The Leo Sun doesn't like to back down; the Libra Moon can't rest while a quarrel hangs in the air. You may give way to keep things pleasant and then feel, privately, that your own light has left the room, or hold your ground and lose sleep over the chill. What helps is telling the two apart: some things are worth a disagreement and some aren't, and naming which is which, early, lets you be generous to others and fair to yourself.
