Pluto's renewal lends depth to your wish for change when it stands 60° from your natal Uranus. A reform you've been turning over can find a workable route in the two years or more this sextile lasts, if you take it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation and reform: the constructive revision of a system, the experiment, the room to be different. The part [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) plays is renewal: what can no longer last is cleared away. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, gives reform an ally rather than a push: under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), nothing changes on its own, but a reform you choose tends to meet less resistance and more support than usual. A team can adopt the method you've been arguing for, a household can settle into a new arrangement.

The sextile works through other people as well as through you, which makes one of Uranus's blind spots worth watching: treating other people's speed of adaptation as resistance. Allies move at their own pace, and a reform that brings them along usually outlasts one imposed at yours.

Those born with [Uranus sextile Pluto](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto/), in the 1940s or the 1990s, came into the world close to this transit. For many of those born from 1993 to 1999, it returns from Uranus's other side between about 65 and 79.

The houses tell you which system is ready. With Uranus in your 4th, the sextile comes from around your 6th ([Pluto transiting the 6th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-6th-house/)) or your 2nd: a new way of working, or a different relation to what you own, makes room to change how you live at home. Only the birth time can tell whether this is yours ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Taken from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the span I allow, this sextile lasts about two years and eight months, and the briefest passes about twenty-two months. Three exact hits are the rule, in about three passes out of five; the other passes bring five and stretch to about three years and seven months.

Before turning 80, about five in six of the people born between 1930 and 2009 have met it, and about one in sixteen twice. Each decade of births adds roughly ten years to the wait, because Uranus keeps pulling away from Pluto and the distance Pluto must cover keeps growing. Read down the decades for births on 1 January: 13.9 for 1955, 23.9 for 1965, 33.1 for 1975, 47.2 for 1985 and 66.4 for 1995. On 1 January 1945, inside the Uranus–Pluto sextile of the 1940s, it was there at 0.1; on 1 January 2005, it came at 4.1, from Uranus's other side; on 1 January 1935, not by 80.

In the sky now, the sextile is with Uranus in Sagittarius: people born from 1981 to 1989 meet it between about 40 and 54, before Pluto leaves Aquarius in early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)).

No other Pluto contact with Uranus reaches so many people. The square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-uranus/)) misses about 2 in 7, the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-uranus/)) more than 1 in 3, the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-uranus/)) everyone born before the mid-1960s, and the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-uranus/)) about 3 in 5.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose the one reform you'd regret not making, and give it a plan: a timetable, the people it needs, a first step.

Find allies before you announce it. A sextile works through cooperation, and the people who share a change are the ones who make it stick.

Keep a record of what you try. Uranus learns by experiment, and the experiments a sextile allows are the cheapest you'll get.
