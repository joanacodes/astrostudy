Two lights in the sign of the twins, and still they speak with one voice. Your curiosity and your comfort usually point at the same thing: a question worth chasing, and someone to talk it over with. You were born within a couple of days of a new moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Same element, same mode, same ruler: mutable air on both sides. The [Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/) builds a self through exchange, through the next conversation, idea or connection; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) settles once a feeling has been put into words and shared. Nothing needs translating between purpose and need. A good talk that sorts out a plan also sorts out a mood, and the reverse holds too: an evening with nobody to talk to can leave both lights flat at once.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules both, and since Mercury is never more than about 28° from the Sun, the one planet behind your two lights always travels with them, in Taurus, Gemini or Cancer. When it stands in Gemini as well, three of the chart's main voices share one sign. Gemini gives neither light a dignity, so Mercury's condition, house and aspects carry the weight, and I read them very early.

The gap between the lights was under 30°, the Moon either still catching up with the Sun, by up to 29°, or just past it, by up to 28°: a birth no more than two and a half days or so from a new moon. Half these births close the old cycle in the [balsamic](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) phase; the other half open the next in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase. Close to the conjunction, the chart has a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). A new moon that falls while the Sun is in Gemini is a [new moon in Gemini](/astrology/new-moon-in-gemini/), and most of these births sit right beside one. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sets out what each side of that new moon means.

Roughly one person in 142 has both lights here. Gemini keeps the Moon for barely two and a half days, so a birth on the day it arrived from Taurus or left for Cancer could belong to the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-taurus/) or the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-cancer/) instead, and the time is the only way to know ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). From that time come the rising sign and the houses: whether the two lights fall where friends and neighbours live in a chart, or in its quieter rooms, changes how a double Gemini shows.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

A partner has to keep your mind company as well as your heart. Love, for a double Gemini, lives in the running conversation: messages through the day, a joke carried over from last year, a book argued about at dinner. You often understand your own feelings by telling someone about them, so a partner who listens well gives you more than they know. What can go wrong is a gap between talking about closeness and allowing it: you can describe what you feel with great precision and keep it at the level of description. A partner who asks *and how is that for you?*, and waits, helps.

## At work {#at-work}

Variety isn't a luxury for this pair; it's fuel. Jobs with many contacts, quick turnarounds and things to learn suit you: media, teaching, sales, research, translation, anything that runs on information. Both lights tire the same way, from sameness, so a job that narrows to one task can drain purpose and mood together. Keep one steady base and let the variety happen inside it, so the pair has somewhere to come back to.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in mutable air, the pair has no slow gear of its own. You can scatter across too many interests, start more than you finish, and fill every quiet moment with input until your head feels crowded rather than rested. Feelings that need time rather than words, grief, slow love, a disappointment, can be talked around instead of felt. What helps comes from outside the pair: daily movement, a walk or a swim, an earth or water planet elsewhere in your chart, a few fixed routines, and some silence kept on purpose, without a screen.
