On 3 March 2026 the Moon passed through the Earth’s shadow at 12°51′ Virgo, nineteen years to the day after a total lunar eclipse at 13°01′ Virgo. Virgo sorts, checks and improves, and its eclipses underline that craft wherever they meet your chart.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) is mutable earth, ruled by Mercury: discernment, method, the care that goes into doing something properly. Its eclipses fall on the Virgo–Pisces axis, the detail and the whole, what can be mended and what has to be accepted, and they alternate with those of Pisces as the nodes move. A [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) in Virgo joins the Sun and Moon in the sign, as on 2 September 2035 at 9°27′; a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) sets the full Moon in Virgo against a Pisces Sun, as on 3 March 2026.

I read these eclipses by the AGS rule: only within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of the axis, through that point and its house. Penumbral lunar eclipses weigh less, and the next one here, on 20 February 2027 at 1°58′, is of that kind. For natal nodes, I take the mean and the true node together. Emphasis, never an omen.

Fourteen from 2000 to 2040:

- **2006–2008:** 14 March 2006, penumbral lunar, 24°20′ · 22 September 2006, annular solar, 29°19′ · 3 March 2007, total lunar, 13°01′ · 11 September 2007, partial solar, 18°24′ · 21 February 2008, total lunar, 1°50′
- **2015–2016:** 13 September 2015, partial solar, 20°10′ · 1 September 2016, annular solar, 9°21′
- **2025–2027:** 14 March 2025, total lunar, 23°58′ · 21 September 2025, partial solar, 29°04′ · 3 March 2026, total lunar, 12°51′ · 20 February 2027, penumbral lunar, 1°58′
- **2034–2035:** 12 September 2034, annular solar, 19°59′ · 22 February 2035, penumbral lunar, 3°39′ · 2 September 2035, total solar, 9°27′

Several answer each other across nineteen years: 21 February 2008 and 20 February 2027, 1 September 2016 at 9°21′ and 2 September 2035 at 9°27′. Leo’s and Libra’s eclipses have their own pages ([Eclipse in Leo](/astrology/eclipse-in-leo/), [Eclipse in Libra](/astrology/eclipse-in-libra/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house that receives a Virgo eclipse in your chart is set by [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); the cusps shift every few minutes, so without the time I don’t read the chart. In Placidus, my house system, a line counts only when a Virgo eclipse meets a natal point in that house; the Pisces end of the axis falls in the house opposite.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/virgo-rising/):** Virgo rising: your careful, capable manner; the way you present your work, rethought.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/virgo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** practical values; what you do with your skills, and what they are worth to you.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/virgo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** precise words and daily learning; a fact checked, a message corrected.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an ordered home; the routines that hold a household together.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/virgo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** craft in pleasure; a hobby or a creative skill you refine.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/virgo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** Virgo in its natural house: daily work, service, habits. This is where tradition placed illness; my reading stays with routines and work, and anything to do with health belongs with a doctor ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
7. **[7th house](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Virgo on the Descendant, with Pisces rising: a partner who helps in practical ways; how everyday tasks are shared.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/virgo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** the details of what’s shared; joint accounts and agreements put in order.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/virgo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** knowledge put to use; a course or a method that changes how you work.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a reputation for competence; a professional standard you set or meet.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/virgo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** useful friendships; the part you play in keeping a group running.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/virgo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** quiet service; work no one sees, and the rest you owe yourself.
