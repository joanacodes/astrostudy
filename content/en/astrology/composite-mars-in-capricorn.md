Not every relationship has a five-year plan. This one usually does, and it's usually on schedule. Composite Mars in Capricorn gives the bond a drive that works in long, disciplined strokes: it builds, it commits, and its quarrels are about who carries what.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Composite charts average each planet's two birth positions ([the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/)), and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) there is the pair's effort and ambition. In [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), the cardinal earth sign that Saturn rules, tradition says Mars is exalted ([exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/)): its effort at its most aimed and economical, opposite its fall in Cancer. An exaltation describes an effective style, not a better relationship. It's on the rare side, about 1 pair in 15, because Mars stays only some forty days in Capricorn.

Such a pair acts like a small firm. Capricorn is cardinal, so the pair starts things too, but it starts them with a plan, choosing a first step because it leads to the tenth. It sets goals and meets them: a house renovated room by room, a business run together, two careers planned so that each gets its turn. Competition is turned into achievement, and you may push each other to do better without much fuss. Desire is steady, private and proven by reliability more than by declarations; this bond shows love by turning up. Someone with [Mars in Capricorn](/astrology/mars-in-capricorn/) in their own chart climbs patiently towards a summit; in the composite, the two of you are roped together on the same climb.

[Composite Mars in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-mars-in-sagittarius/) aims at a distant horizon; [composite Mars in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-mars-in-aquarius/) works for an idea or a group. Capricorn wants something it can build and finish.

Arguments are controlled and practical, about time, duty and the fairness of the load: who earned the evening off, why the plan changed, whose career comes first this year. Score is often kept in hours: who worked late, who cooked, who drove. Rows run cold more often than hot, and the danger is that the relationship becomes a project, with tenderness postponed until the work is done. Discipline isn't rigidity, and ambition isn't coldness, but this Mars needs time that serves no purpose at all: an evening with no goal, praise given before the next task.

Coldness is friction, not danger. If one of you ever feels controlled or afraid, that calls for real help, not a chart: in the UK, 999 in an emergency, Samaritans on 116 123 the rest of the time. Nothing in this composite predicts a marriage, a business's fortunes or a separation, and nobody should draw it without both people's agreement ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Capricorn's ruler is [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), and in my notebook of tested birth charts, Saturn was one of three placements, with Chiron and Lilith, that held 75 to 90% of the time ([The three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). None of that carries over to composites, which I don't draw. Astrologers who use them would weigh this Mars against the composite Saturn and with the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), where [Mars square Saturn in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-saturn-synastry/) shows one person's push meeting the other's brakes.
