Uranus at the bottom of your chart is change working on the foundations: where you live, how you live, whom you call family, and what makes a place feel like yours. It begins at your IC and, as a rule, lasts about eight years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reconfigures: it questions what you inherited and tests other arrangements. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, family, roots and the private self, everything underneath the life other people see. With Uranus here, an arrangement at home that once suited you can start to pinch. Some people move; many more change how they live where they are, who shares the space, how the household runs, or what they think a family has to look like. Roots get looked at again too, and a family pattern you can finally name is one you can choose not to repeat.

The stay opens at your Imum Coeli, which Placidus, my house system, uses as the 4th's cusp. On the same day Uranus faces your Midheaven ([transiting Uranus opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-midheaven/)), so a shift at home and a question about your public direction often arrive together. With Mars in your 4th from birth, the push to change things at home can come out as impatience first; it gets further as a plan.

Older books gave the 4th land, houses and a parent. I read none of them as events in a transit: nothing here predicts a move, a family occasion or anything that happens to the people you live with. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) works on your own experience of home, as your chart already describes it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

About eight years is the middle for Uranus in the 4th, in Placidus charts drawn for London, and few houses are as regular: most stays fall between nearly five years and just under ten, because the 4th changes size less than most from one birth time to another. Charts drawn for New York tighten it further, with the same middle and a long end of just under nine years.

The count starts the first time Uranus touches your IC, even if a retrograde then pulls it back across, and ends on its last day in the house. Your IC travels round the whole zodiac in a day, so its degree, and with it the start of these years, needs the minute you were born; without that minute, I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Before the IC comes the stay in your 3rd, about ideas and talk ([Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/)); after the 4th, Uranus moves into the house of play and love ([Uranus transiting the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-5th-house/)).

Look for the sign of your IC. A Gemini IC, the one that goes with a Sagittarius Midheaven, is reached somewhere between July 2025 and May 2033. A Cancer IC, the 4th's own sign, has its turn between August 2032 and August 2039, and a Leo IC from August 2039. If your IC is in Taurus, Uranus crossed it between May 2018 and April 2026, and your 4th may still be hosting it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change one arrangement at a time. Uranus in the 4th can make the whole house feel wrong at once; trying a new routine, room or rhythm first shows what really needs to go.

Remember that belonging isn't only a place. If moving isn't possible or wanted, freedom at home can come from a door that closes, a shared rule rewritten, an hour that's yours.

Talk to the people you live with before a change rather than after it. Others adapt at their own speed, and slowness is not the same as resistance.
