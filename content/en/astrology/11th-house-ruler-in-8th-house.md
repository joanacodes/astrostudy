There are friends you see every week, and friends who know the thing you've told nobody else. With the ruler of your 11th house in the 8th, the second kind matter most, and the circle you choose is held together by trust more than by numbers.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is where closeness changes you: intimacy and trust, the money and obligations you share, what you owe and what's owed to you. The [11th](/astrology/11th-house/) is the house of the friends and allies you choose and the future you build with them. When its ruler stands in the 8th ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), friendship is carried into deeper water.

Your friendships tend to go a long way down. You may keep few close friends and keep them for decades: the people who stood with you through a loss or a crisis, who know your debts and your fears. Groups suit you when they ask for commitment, a co-operative, a mutual-aid fund, a circle where confidences are kept. Money can move through these bonds as well: a loan between friends, a joint venture, resources pooled for a plan you share. Handled well, that's trust made practical. The risks are the 8th's own: secrets that bind a group too tightly, and debts, of money or of gratitude, that weigh on a friendship.

In the [natural zodiac](/astrology/natural-zodiac/), the 8th goes with Scorpio and the 11th with Aquarius, two fixed signs a square apart, and counted by sign the houses make the same square. Both are succedent houses, where things settle and last, so these loyalties are rarely casual. I read the square as a pull between the open circle and the private pact: the group wants everyone in on things, the 8th wants a few people sworn to it, and you live with both.

[Scorpio on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-11th-house-cusp/) gives the house to Mars by tradition and to Pluto in modern tropical astrology, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Pluto in the 8th, the house the natural zodiac pairs with Scorpio, turns a friendship into something close to a pact, while Mars, from its own house, adds the nerve to defend the people you've chosen. The Moon, ruling a Cancer 11th from here, makes friends into confidants and keeps their secrets as if they were its own.

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance, and I read neither here: no chart says when anyone will die, or what they'll leave behind ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). What a chart can describe is how you meet endings and grief, and with this ruler you rarely meet them without your people.

Four minutes of birth time move a cusp by about a degree, so a ruler near either edge of the 8th leans on the clock ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Near the 8th cusp, a later birth would have carried it down into the 7th, where friends become partners ([Ruler of the 11th in the 7th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)); near the 9th, an earlier one would have left it among friends from abroad and shared beliefs ([Ruler of the 11th in the 9th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)).
