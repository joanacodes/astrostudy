Cancer is the fourth sign of the zodiac. The Sun enters it at the June solstice, the start of summer in the northern hemisphere. It's the sign of belonging, and wherever it falls in a chart, I read it as the place where you look after people — and need looking after too.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Cancer somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it brings care, memory and a strong instinct to protect what's yours: people, places, the past. Cancer asks *who is mine, and are they safe?* It's a [water sign](/astrology/water-signs/), like Scorpio and Pisces, so it reads the room through feeling; and it's cardinal, one of the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/), so it doesn't wait to be asked — it takes the first step to look after someone. In the sequence of signs, each one answering the last, Cancer answers [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/): after gathering ideas and people, the need to choose which ones to keep close. [Leo](/astrology/leo/), next, steps out of the shell to shine.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/):** born between about 21 June and 22 July. You grow by caring for what's yours, and your sense of purpose is tied to the people and places you belong to.
- **[Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/):** the Moon at home — deep, tidal feelings, a long memory, and a need for a safe place to come back to. You often sense other people's moods before they say a word.
- **[Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** you meet the world carefully, a little guarded at first and warm once you feel safe. The Moon becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/), so its sign and house weigh more than usual. A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So I read Cancer through the whole chart, beginning with the Moon.

## Its ruler: the Moon {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, the one that carries the sign's business into the rest of the chart. Cancer belongs to the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), the only sign it rules: tradition gave the two lights the two signs of high summer, Cancer to the Moon and Leo to the Sun. Wherever your Moon sits, by sign and house, shows where your Cancer side looks for safety and where it gives care. With Cancer on the cusp of the 10th house and the Moon in the 4th, say, your public life and your home life are woven together. The Moon changes sign about every two and a half days, so even people born close together can have their Cancer working through very different Moons.

Three more planets have a particular bond with Cancer:

- [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is [exalted](/astrology/exaltation/) here: generosity finds its warmest form in care, protection and a full table.
- [Mars](/astrology/mars/) is in its [fall](/astrology/fall/): anger goes round the side rather than straight at the problem, and it defends better than it attacks. I read it as protective, not weak.
- [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite Capricorn, the sign it rules: structure meets feeling, and the lesson is emotional boundaries — not hardening, but knowing where you end and others begin.

## Planets in Cancer {#planets-in-the-sign}

A planet in Cancer turns protective and remembers everything; the house it sits in shows where that plays out.

- **[Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/):** a protective will; you grow by looking after people and keeping what matters.
- **[Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/):** the Moon at home — deep feelings, a long memory, a strong need for home and belonging.
- **[Mercury in Cancer](/astrology/mercury-in-cancer/):** a mind that feels its way; you remember how things were said as well as what was said.
- **[Venus in Cancer](/astrology/venus-in-cancer/):** tender, loyal love; you show it by caring for someone, and need to feel safe before you open up.
- **[Mars in Cancer](/astrology/mars-in-cancer/):** Mars in its fall — energy that protects rather than attacks; anger that comes out sideways when it isn't named.
- **[Jupiter in Cancer](/astrology/jupiter-in-cancer/):** Jupiter exalted — generous and nurturing, at its best when it looks after others.
- **[Saturn in Cancer](/astrology/saturn-in-cancer/):** Saturn in detriment — emotional safety learned slowly; boundaries around feeling and family that take time to build.
- **[Uranus in Cancer](/astrology/uranus-in-cancer/):** a generation that changes what home and family can look like, shared by everyone born over about seven years; its house makes it personal.
- **[Neptune in Cancer](/astrology/neptune-in-cancer/):** ideals of home, homeland and belonging; shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so I read it through its house.
- **[Pluto in Cancer](/astrology/pluto-in-cancer/):** a generation's deep transformation of family and security; shared over twelve years or more, made personal by its house.

## Cancer on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Cancer somewhere in its twelve houses, usually on the cusp of one of them. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Cancer opens a house, that's where you protect and nurture, and the Moon, wherever it sits, carries the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** Cancer rising — a careful, caring presence; guarded at first, warm once you feel safe.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/cancer-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money and possessions as a source of safety; you keep things for what they mean, and like to have something put by.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** words chosen for how they'll land; you remember conversations by how they felt, and the people close by matter.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** Cancer in its natural house — home, family and memory are the foundation, and a place to come back to is essential.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/):** creativity drawn from feeling and memory; romance that's tender, protective and a little shy at first.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/):** you care through your daily work; a workplace that feels like a family, and routines that comfort.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Cancer on the Descendant, which means Capricorn rising — drawn to caring partners, or to caring for your partner; you want a relationship that feels like home.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/):** deep emotional bonds; trust and shared resources tied to a feeling of family.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs rooted in where you come from; travel that ends with feeling at home somewhere new.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a public life built on care; work that looks after people, and a reputation for being dependable.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends who become family; in groups, you're the one who looks after people.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/cancer-on-12th-house-cusp/):** feelings kept private; a hidden tenderness, and a need to withdraw to recharge.

## Cancer in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Cancer planets bring care and protection: the person who has them shows love by feeding, remembering and looking after. To the other person that can feel like home or like being smothered, depending on where those planets land in their chart and what they touch. Across the zodiac sits [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), the opposite sign: Cancer guards the private world, Capricorn builds in the public one. Between two charts, that axis can make a strong partnership — warmth and structure, home and work — and a quarrel about which comes first. As for comparing Sun signs, [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind: for how two people take care of each other, I look at their Moons long before their Sun signs.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Cancers are clingy.”** Cancer holds on to what it loves, and from outside that can look like clinging. It's a need to belong; given enough safety, Cancer lets people go, and grow.
- **“Cancer is a soft, passive sign.”** It's cardinal: it starts things. Its way of starting is to protect, gather people and make a home — action through care.
- **“Cancers are moody because the Moon rules them.”** Everyone has a Moon, and everyone has moods. Cancer simply notices them sooner, its own and other people's.
- **“Born on the cusp, you're a bit of Gemini.”** At the minute you were born, the Sun was in one sign or the other; within a day of a change, your birth time settles which. More on being [born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/).
