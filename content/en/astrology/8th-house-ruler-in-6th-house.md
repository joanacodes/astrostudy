Debts, claims, confidences, emergencies: what the 8th holds tends to land on your desk. That's the 8th's ruler at work in the 6th, the house of daily routine and care: you're often the one who handles what others would rather not.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) holds the working day: tasks, habits, colleagues and the care that keeps a life running. The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is about what's shared and owed, trust and intimacy, and the times that change you. Ruled from the 6th, those deep matters become daily business ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Depth, here, is put to work. You may do the jobs that deal with what others can't face or can't manage alone: debts and accounts, claims, research, crisis lines, the confidential side of an office. Colleagues may trust you with what they don't tell anyone else. Tradition gave the 6th employees and the 8th other people's money, and you may be the one handed the payroll, the budget or the keys: responsibility for what belongs to others, carried as an everyday task. A turning point can reorganise your routines from top to bottom, and you're often better than most at building a new routine afterwards. The care needed is about load: being the person who can handle anything at work doesn't mean you should handle everything, every day.

Tradition gave both houses hard names: the 6th was Bad Fortune, the 8th the house of death, and horary astrologers still speak of the 6th, the 8th and the 12th as traditionally bad houses ([horary astrology](/astrology/horary-astrology/)). Those are a tradition's words. I read the 6th as work and care and the 8th as what's shared and changed, and I don't read illness or death into either ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/), [can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).

Counted round the wheel, the 6th lies two houses before the 8th, a sextile apart, an easy working link. Mars has its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) in the 6th, so with Aries on the 8th cusp, Mars rules your 8th from a house where it likes to work: tasks that need nerve, done without fuss. Scorpio there instead adds Pluto to Mars ([Scorpio on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Pluto's house shows where you go deepest, and Mars in the 6th turns that depth into practice. The Moon, ruling a Cancer 8th, may stand in Taurus here, exalted: steady, calming hands in a crisis.

The 6th sits just under the western horizon, so the planet ruling your 8th had already set when you were born. A little later, it would be lower still, in the 5th, where depth turns to romance and play ([Ruler of the 8th in the 5th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)); a little earlier, still above the horizon, in the 7th, where partnership tests trust ([Ruler of the 8th in the 7th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)). In Placidus, the house system I use, that horizon line is the Descendant, and it comes from the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What you offer, day after day, is steadiness with difficult things. It's a skill, not a burden you were handed.
