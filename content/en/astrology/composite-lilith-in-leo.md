At a party they seem the quietest pair in the room; at home there's a whole theatre. Behind that door, composite Lilith in Leo keeps a hidden pride: a wish to be seen whole and enjoyed, and a stubborn refusal to make itself smaller so that others feel more at ease.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The composite places each body halfway between its positions in two birth charts, and [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) reads the result as the relationship's own. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is the part of a chart I read as what was hidden or wouldn't be tamed, so here it shows what the bond keeps to itself and won't let others domesticate. [Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire ruled by [the Sun](/astrology/sun/), puts that in its dignity and delight. Such a bond often has a grandeur it doesn't show everyone: rituals of celebration, a creative project only the two of you take seriously, a private certainty that what you have is rather splendid.

Neither of you has to be a show-off for this to be true; modesty in each of you is no obstacle to a relationship that wants an audience, or that loves being admired while acting shy about it. The wanting itself is what the bond tends to hide, especially around people who once called it vain. Its shine is another matter: asked to tone itself down, for a family occasion or a colleague's comfort, it can dig in with surprising pride.

The sign can be the pair's even when it is neither person's. A birth in March 1987 puts Lilith at about 12° Cancer, a birth in October 1988 at about 17° Virgo, and the midpoint of the two, the composite Lilith of such a pair, at about 15° Leo. Two people born in the same Leo stay of mean Lilith, August 1987 to May 1988 or June 1996 to March 1997, for instance, have it too. Across all pairs of birth dates from 1930 to 2009, about 1 pair in 12 does.

[Composite Lilith in Cancer](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-cancer/) hides a need for shelter and [composite Lilith in Virgo](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-virgo/) an exacting judgement; this one hides its pride, which is harder, because pride wants to be seen. Anyone with [Lilith in Leo](/astrology/lilith-in-leo/) in their own chart knows that bind from the inside: a wish to be seen that someone once called showing off. Shared by a pair, it can turn into each person's suspicion that the other is the vain one.

The hard part is pride on both sides of the door. A bond like this can refuse help to save face, sulk when it isn't noticed, or compete with itself, each person wanting the spotlight the pair has earned. What helps is to celebrate the relationship on purpose, in small ways, and to let either of you take centre stage without the other feeling robbed.

Composites aren't part of my own readings: I describe the technique here as the astrologers who use it read it, with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) beside it. It promises no recognition and foretells no ending, and it belongs to two people who both know it is being drawn ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)). In my notebook, natal Lilith was one of three placements that held far more often than the rest ([Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)); I never tested a composite, so that count says nothing about this midpoint.
