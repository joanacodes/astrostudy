Mars conjunct Neptune puts the part of you that acts right beside the part that imagines. Effort and inspiration arrive together, so you tend to move most readily for a vision, a cause, or someone who needs you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is effort with a direction: starting out, asserting yourself, measuring up to others, protecting what matters. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination, compassion and symbolic perception, and dissolves boundaries that aren't needed. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes them work in a single gesture without either erasing the other: your drive takes on imagination, and your imagination gets a drive.

In plain words, you act best when something inspires or touches you. You may throw yourself into a cause, a creative project or someone else's need with real force, and find it hard to push for something you merely ought to want. Your way of asserting yourself may be gentle, indirect or generous, quicker to defend others than yourself. Compassion is not self-erasure, though, and part of this conjunction's work is learning to want things for yourself as clearly as you want them for others.

The other part is giving the vision steps. Imagination without effort stays a dream, and effort without imagination goes flat; the meeting asks for both, in that order.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Neptune spends about fourteen years in a sign, so the sign of this conjunction is shared by a long run of births, and the conjunction itself by the few weeks in which Mars passes Neptune, about every two years. That makes it personal, and the house tells you where: in the 5th, creative work and play; in the 6th, the daily work of care; in the 12th, effort made quietly, behind the scenes.

Only about 1 person in 32 is born with it, counting within 6°. Within a degree or two, the bond between your drive and your imagination sits at the centre of the chart; towards 6°, it shows mostly when something moves you.

Neptune was found in 1846, so this conjunction is read entirely through modern tropical astrology, and Neptune isn't one of the placements that stood out in my notebook: those were Saturn, Chiron and Lilith. So I hold it a little more lightly than a Saturn contact, and tell you so ([The invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)).

How the pair's other aspects compare:

- **[Mars sextile Neptune](/astrology/mars-sextile-neptune/):** imagination on hand for your effort, there when you choose to use it.
- **[Mars square Neptune](/astrology/mars-square-neptune/):** drive and ideals rub at a right angle, and you learn to act before the picture is perfect.
- **[Mars trine Neptune](/astrology/mars-trine-neptune/):** inspired action flows in one element, with more ease and less intensity than the conjunction.
- **[Mars opposite Neptune](/astrology/mars-opposite-neptune/):** your will and other people's ideals face each other, and the balance is learned through relationships.

Between two charts, [Mars conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-neptune-synastry/) can feel like inspiration in motion, as one person's drive takes on the other's dreams. It asks both of you to say plainly what you want, and I read it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Neptune crosses a sign in about fourteen years and needs about 165 to go round, so [Transiting Neptune conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-mars/) comes once in a life at most, lingering for a year or two in several passes. Born with the conjunction, you either met it in your first years, if Mars was a little ahead of Neptune, or won't meet it at all. Around 41, the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) squares your Neptune and your Mars together.

Mars in the sky meets your natal Neptune about every two years, crossing your own Mars within the same few days: a short stretch when inspiration and energy turn up together, or when it's harder than usual to push for anything that doesn't move you.

These are weather, not a forecast: they colour how you act for a while, and decide nothing.
