Venus conjunct Fomalhaut puts Venus in the first degrees of Pisces, the sign the tradition calls its exaltation, on the degree of the bright star in the mouth of the Southern Fish. Only births between early January and early April can have it, and about 1 person in 216 does.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read [Venus](/astrology/venus/) as judgement of value: what attracts you, what you choose when you could have either, what you count as a fair exchange between two people. Of the stories told about [Fomalhaut](/astrology/fomalhaut/), one comes unusually close to Venus's own name. Ctesias, a Greek historian of the fifth century BC, wrote of a fish in a lake near Bambyce, in Syria, that saved Derceto, a daughter of Aphrodite, and was set among the stars for it; for that reason, the account goes on, many Syrians held fish sacred and would not eat them. Aphrodite is the goddess the Romans called Venus, so in one of its oldest tellings the Southern Fish rescues a member of her family. An Egyptian version gives the fish the same part with Isis.

Those stories are the tradition's colour for this contact, and they are kindly ones: a star that saves rather than threatens. They remain stories. Venus keeps its own work here, and what describes it best is the sign. The star's degree, 2°52′ Pisces in 1930 and 4°13′ in 2026, always makes this a [Venus in Pisces](/astrology/venus-in-pisces/), and Pisces is where the old books held Venus in [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), its most honoured place. Read without any star, that is a Venus whose sense of value tends to run through tenderness, imagination and acceptance.

At about 1 person in 216 born between 1930 and 2009, the contact is rarer than the 1 in 180 that a one-degree window gives on average. Venus explains it. In those eighty years it crossed Fomalhaut's degree exactly once a year, always moving forward, each crossing lasting a day or two, and it never once turned retrograde on this stretch of the zodiac, so no year handed it the doubled-back passes that make some star contacts more frequent.

Venus also never strays more than about 47° from the Sun, which fixes the season. From 1930 to 2009 the earliest birthday with Venus on Fomalhaut was 6 January and the latest 10 April; in between, the year decides, since Venus reaches the star on a different date each time. In the sky they stay far apart, Venus passing between about 19½° and 21½° north of the star.

My notebook counts Venus among the placements that held about a third of the time, which is reason enough to read it with care, and a star with more care still ([The invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)). I don't place Fomalhaut, or any other star, in a reading.

The same degree is reached by the [Sun conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/sun-conjunct-fomalhaut/) on 21–22 February, the [Moon conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/moon-conjunct-fomalhaut/) for a few hours each month, [Mars conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/mars-conjunct-fomalhaut/), which crossed it three times in 2003, and [Saturn conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-fomalhaut/), which held it for months at a time. How a star contact is measured, and why only the conjunction counts, is explained under [conjunctions to fixed stars](/astrology/conjunctions-to-fixed-stars/).
