Neptune square Pluto is an angle that no birth chart drew between 1930 and 2009. It still earns a page, because the friction it describes arrives as a transit in many lives, and sometimes between two people's charts.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) holds the ideal: the picture of how things could be, the compassion that wants to include everyone, the softening of hard lines. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) holds the clearing-out: it lets go of what has stopped living so that something can regenerate, and hands back a deeper strength. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction: the ideal keeps bumping into what has to end, and the ending keeps bumping into the ideal.

In plain words, the gentlest part of you and the most uncompromising one would be asked to work side by side. Compassion would hesitate to let anything go; renewal would look ruthless to the dreamer. Neither is wrong, and the square doesn't ask you to choose. It asks the two to adjust to each other, and the adjustment is the gift: ideals that are honest about what must end, and endings that keep their compassion. I would never read it as a crisis, only as two deep needs that refuse to be simplified.

Were it in a chart, it would belong to a whole generation, as anything between these two planets does, and its two houses, usually three apart, would show where the ideal and the clearing-out met: home and partnership, say, or work and the way you meet the world.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The square is missing from every birth between 1930 and 2009, counting within 6°. In those decades the two never came within reach of 90°: for most of the time they held a sextile, about 60° apart ([Neptune sextile Pluto](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto/)), with Neptune the one ahead throughout. If you were born in those years, the square isn't in your chart, whatever else Neptune and Pluto are doing there.

Where you can meet it is outside your own chart. Between two people born years apart, one person's Neptune can square the other's Pluto: [Neptune square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-pluto-synastry/) can feel like one person's ideals pressing on the other's need for deep change, and the other's depth unsettling those ideals in return. I read it in both directions, and gently, as I read every hard angle ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

The pair's other aspects, set against this one:

- **[Neptune conjunct Pluto](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-pluto/):** no friction at all, the vision and the renewal fused; absent from these years as well.
- **[Neptune sextile Pluto](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto/):** the angle most people born 1943–1993 really have, cooperation offered instead of friction.
- **[Neptune trine Pluto](/astrology/neptune-trine-pluto/):** the easy version, which these charts don't show either.
- **[Neptune opposite Pluto](/astrology/neptune-opposite-pluto/):** the full polarity, for no one born in these eighty years.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Neptune square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-pluto/) is the form of this square that reaches people born in these years. Neptune is already ahead of natal Pluto at birth and keeps pulling away, so in time it gets to the square. It is slow: Neptune lingers near the point for a year or more, crossing it several times as it turns retrograde.

[Transiting Pluto square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-neptune/) is the other way round. Pluto trails Neptune in these charts and would have to pass your natal Neptune first, so if it comes at all, it comes much later, depending on your birth year.

Neither is the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/), at about 41, when transiting Neptune squares its own birth place rather than Pluto's.

Weather, not a forecast: transiting Neptune square natal Pluto describes a season when an ideal and an ending have to come to terms. It doesn't say which ideal, or what ends.
