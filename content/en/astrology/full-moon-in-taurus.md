Late in the year, while the Sun is in Scorpio, the Moon comes full in Taurus, the sign where tradition says it is most at ease. It's a full moon about what you keep: comfort, money, the body's own pace, set against what the Sun in Scorpio asks you to share or let go.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

At a [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/), the Moon stands opposite the Sun and shows its whole face all night. Named for the Moon's sign, a full moon in [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) has the Sun at the same degree of [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/). Tradition gives the Moon its exaltation in Taurus: feeling at its steadiest, needs plain and physical, such as rest, food, touch, a routine you can rely on. So this full moon brings up what you need in order to feel secure, and whether you have it.

The axis between the two signs runs from *mine* to *ours*. Taurus holds on to what it values; Scorpio, where the Sun is, asks what you share, what you owe, and what you're ready to release. The full moon tends to show where those pull against each other: comfort kept at the cost of closeness, or intensity that has left no room for rest. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Taurus. Scorpio has two rulers, Pluto and Mars, and I read both.

Treat it as a marker, not a message. A full moon enters a reading only when its Moon or its Sun falls within about a degree of a planet or angle in your chart, and even then it brings to a head what a transit or a progression has already set going. It forecasts nothing on its own.

The most recent, on 26 October 2026, fell at 2°46′ Taurus. The next is on 14 November 2027 at 03:26 UT, at 21°31′, then 2 November 2028 at 10°29′, 21 November 2029 at 29°07′, Taurus's last degree, and 10 November 2030 at 17°46′. None is an eclipse.

The [new moon in Scorpio](/astrology/new-moon-in-scorpio/) belongs to the same month: on 9 November 2026 it follows the full moon by two weeks, and in 2027 it comes first, on 29 October. A month before falls the [full moon in Aries](/astrology/full-moon-in-aries/); a month after, the [full moon in Gemini](/astrology/full-moon-in-gemini/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

This full moon lights a pair of opposite houses: the Moon in the one holding its degree of Taurus, the Sun across from it, in the one holding Scorpio. Which pair is yours, only a birth time can say; [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) is why I won't guess. With Placidus houses, start from the house with Taurus on its cusp:

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Taurus rising: your need for calm and your own pace comes up, while a partner's intensity fills your 7th.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** Taurus's own house: what you earn and keep, set against what you share or owe; both columns in plain view.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** a wish for simple, steady days, against a pull in the 9th towards deeper study or a belief examined.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** home as a place of comfort comes into focus, while a demanding phase at work asks you to dig in.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** pleasures of the senses, food, music, a garden, set against friends or a group asking more of you.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** the body's need for rest and an even pace surfaces, while something private, in the 12th, asks to be faced.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Taurus on the Descendant: a partner who offers steadiness, or wants it, while your own mood runs deep and searching.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** shared money or intimacy wants to be made solid and simple, while your own resources ask what you really value.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a belief or a journey you want on solid ground, while everyday conversations dig under the surface.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Taurus on the Midheaven: steady work and a solid reputation come into view, while home asks for depth.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** loyal friends and groups you can rely on, against a passion of your own that wants all your attention.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a quiet need for comfort and retreat surfaces, while the working day demands focus and change.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look for a planet or angle of yours within a degree of 21°31′ Taurus or Scorpio for 2027, then 10°29′, 29°07′ and 17°46′ for the three years after. If there's one, ask what transit or progression is already working on it; if not, take the full moon as a month's weather in two houses.

Then make two short lists. In the Taurus house, what you want to keep; in the Scorpio house, what you're ready to share or release. The houses of Venus, Pluto and Mars will tell you where those decisions really sit. Taurus likes to decide slowly, and a full moon doesn't need an answer by morning.
