A language course booked the evening it was first mentioned, a move to another city settled in one weekend, two bikes bought the day before a first long ride. With composite Jupiter in Aries, a relationship widens through first times, and trusts what the two of you have tried more than anything you were told.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the part of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) that describes how a relationship grows: what you learn together, what you come to believe in, what you make room for. [Aries](/astrology/aries/), cardinal fire, gives that growth the manner of a beginning: quick, direct, drawn to the untried. Aries is none of Jupiter's signs, and neither its exaltation, detriment nor fall, so the sign's ruler has a say: where the composite [Mars](/astrology/mars/) falls shows what supplies the push behind this pair's fresh starts.

It is the rarest composite Jupiter, with Pisces: about 1 pair in 15, in two million pairs of birth dates drawn at random from 1930 to 2009. Jupiter spends about a year in a sign, so age plays its part. Anyone born while Jupiter crossed Aries, from April 1963 to April 1964, from March 1975 to March 1976 or from March 1987 to March 1988, shares it with everyone born in the same months, and many couples of the same age carry it without it saying much about them in particular. A gap in age moves the composite to the midpoint, and that is where it becomes the relationship's own. Someone born on 1 May 1985, with Jupiter at about 15° Aquarius, and someone born on 1 May 1989, with Jupiter at about 10° Gemini, meet at about 12° Aries, a sign neither of them has.

That is the composite's point. The bond's way of growing may belong to neither of you: two careful people can find that, together, they are the first to say yes, and that their relationship trusts a plan once it has been tested rather than once it has been argued for. One person with [Jupiter in Aries](/astrology/jupiter-in-aries/) widens their own life by going first. Here the pioneering is shared, and the generosity takes the form of a push: a word of courage before an interview, a hand on the back at a start line, a weekend cleared for the other's new idea.

The trap is that novelty feels like progress. A pair that widens through first times can keep moving on, to the next project, place or cause, without asking what the last one taught, and the slower of you may be carried by the bond's pace rather than choosing it. What helps is a second step made on purpose: one shared venture finished before the next begins, and a moment, after each fresh start, to say what it changed in what you both believe.

The signs on either side read otherwise. With [composite Jupiter in Pisces](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-pisces/), a bond grows through compassion and imagination; with [composite Jupiter in Taurus](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-taurus/), by keeping what works. I don't draw composites in my own readings: this is the technique as the astrologers who use it read it, beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and never as a promise of luck or a measure of how long you'll last. A relationship chart is made of two people's birth data, so it is drawn only when both have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
