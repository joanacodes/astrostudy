The middle of Capricorn pairs Mars, the planet the sign honours with its exaltation, with Venus, its old opposite. In 1993 two of the slowest planets, Uranus and Neptune, met here as well.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

Mars takes the middle face of [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), next in the [Chaldean order](/astrology/chaldean-order/) after Jupiter ([Capricorn, first decan](/astrology/capricorn-first-decan/)), and the Sun the face after it ([Capricorn, third decan](/astrology/capricorn-third-decan/)). This is one of five places in the zodiac where a planet holds a face in the sign of its exaltation: Mars is exalted in Capricorn, so [Mars in Capricorn](/astrology/mars-in-capricorn/) between 10° and 20° holds exaltation and face together ([faces](/astrology/faces/)). By element, the middle third belongs to Taurus, the next earth sign, and Venus rules it on Taurus's behalf.

Venus has always been Mars's opposite number. Its two homes, Taurus and Libra, face the two signs Mars rules, Scorpio and Aries, so wherever either planet is at home, the other is in detriment. Here they share ten degrees of the sign of structure: the will to act, and the sense of what is worth acting for. I read the pair as effort with a value attached, work done because it's worth something and not only because it's due.

## Its image {#its-image}

Wallis Budge's name for the Egyptian decan here, Ṭepā-semṭ, is built on its neighbour's, the Semṭet of the first decan; the Greek-Egyptian lists have Srat. [Firmicus Maternus](/astrology/firmicus-maternus/) wrote Epiemu or Epimen, which became Epima in Joseph Scaliger's list, and the Hermetic lists give Epitek in Greek and Manethois in Latin. Athanasius Kircher, in 1652, called it Riruphta.

In the sky, these degrees hold [Vega](/astrology/vega/), the bright star of the Lyre, at 15°40′ Capricorn, though it stands almost 62° north of the Sun's path: a planet here shares its number, never its patch of sky. Medieval magic counted it among the fifteen Behenian stars.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun crosses these degrees from about 1 to 10 January, so a [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) of the second decan has its birthday in the first days of the year. With Mars exalted and in its own face, and Venus by element, I read the shade as Capricorn's patience with a strong engine: steady work, aimed at something that matters. A Venus placed here rules its own decan in the modern scheme, and I read it as affection that shows itself through effort: the present that took months to make.

[Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/) in its middle decan takes only about 35 minutes to rise at the latitude of London or Paris, so a birth time out by a quarter of an hour can already change the decan.

Many people born from 1988 to 1993 carry slow planets here. [Neptune in Capricorn](/astrology/neptune-in-capricorn/) crossed these degrees from early 1988 to the end of 1993, and [Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/uranus-in-capricorn/) from early 1991 to 1993; in 1993 the two met in them three times, at 18° and 19° Capricorn, a conjunction that comes round about every 171 years ([Uranus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune/)). Their back-and-forth that year was the work of retrograde motion ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)). As with any shared placement, the house is what makes it yours.
