Pluto trine your Venus deepens what you love without asking for a fight. For two to three years the planet of depth stands 120° from your Venus, and attachments, tastes and the things you care for grow steadier, the way a garden fills in over several summers.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) regenerates: what it renews comes back stronger. Your [Venus](/astrology/venus/) appreciates and relates: what you find beautiful, whom you choose, what you would keep. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets the two flow together while each keeps its own nature, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) attachment deepens with time. A friendship settles into something you can lean on. A craft or an art you love matures. Home grows more beautiful by becoming more yours.

Showing what you love can feel like exposure: a poem read aloud, a feeling said first, a taste that isn't fashionable. Under the trine that costs less, and being seen in what you love turns out not to be the humiliation it can feel like under harder skies. Pleasure isn't indulgence, either; this is a good season to take it seriously, as nourishment rather than reward. Nothing in this trine forecasts love, money or any event; it describes a climate in which what you value can settle.

Of Pluto's five contacts with Venus, this trine and the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-venus/)) are the two that ask nothing by force; the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-venus/)), the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-venus/)) and the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-venus/)) bring change whether or not you go looking. Read in a birth chart, [Venus trine Pluto](/astrology/venus-trine-pluto/) is attachment that deepens with time; this transit is when that becomes your season.

With Venus in the 11th and Pluto crossing the 3rd ([Pluto transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-3rd-house/)), conversations, letters and everyday exchanges deepen your friendships. The houses rest on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The trine holds for about two years and eight months within my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, and for about twenty-two months in the quickest passes. Pluto's yearly loops cross the exact point three times, or five times in about two passes out of five. When Pluto turns direct or retrograde within a degree of the trine ([Stations](/astrology/stations/)), it barely moves for weeks, and I read those weeks as the transit at its most concentrated.

More than one person in five born between 1930 and 2009 never meets a Pluto trine to Venus by 80, and some meet both, one from each side of the circle.

From Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), Pluto’s trines reach Venus in Gemini and in Libra. Venus never strays far from the Sun, so a Venus in Gemini goes with a birthday between about early April and early August, and a Venus in Libra with one between about early August and early December.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Commit to something long and beautiful: an instrument, a garden, a piece of work you will be glad of in ten years.

Tend a friendship on purpose. The trine rewards the call you make, not the one you wait for.

Repair something you love instead of replacing it. It is a small version of what this transit does.
