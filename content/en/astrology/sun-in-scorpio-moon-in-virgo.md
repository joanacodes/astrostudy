What your Sun digs up, your Moon wants to sort. The Scorpio Sun goes beneath the surface for the truth of things; the Virgo Moon settles once that truth has been examined, labelled and put to some use. You were born late in the lunar month, around the last quarter.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Virgo is two signs back from Scorpio, a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) by sign, and water and earth suit each other: one supplies depth, the other form. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) wants to understand what is really going on; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) needs to do something careful and useful with what it understands. Fixed and mutable keep different paces, but here they tend to cooperate: the Sun stays with a problem, the Moon refines the answer.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) both rules Virgo and is exalted there, so your Moon's way of coping, by analysing, listing and mending, is strongly marked, and following Mercury by sign and house shows where it works best. For the Sun I read both of Scorpio's rulers: [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the older, for the persistence you bring to a hard question, and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the newer, for the depth you are prepared to go to. When Mercury and those two agree, you can be remarkably good at getting to the cause of a problem.

Counted backwards, your Moon trailed the Sun by 30° to 89°, late in a lunar month 22 to 27 days old. The [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) accounts for most of these births and the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) for the rest, about one in eight; around 60° behind the Sun the lights make a waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). A waning sextile suits careful revision, and this pair revises well: the Scorpio Sun knows what has to go, the Virgo Moon knows how to tidy what remains. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) gives the reasoning behind the eight phases.

Roughly 1 person in 147 is born with these two signs for the lights. The pages on either side, the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-leo/) and the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-libra/), share this one's birthdays whenever the Moon changed sign that day, and the hour sorts them ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). From the hour come the rising sign and the houses too; for a Virgo Moon, the house says which corner of life it keeps tidy.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You show care through attention to detail and through a loyalty that doesn't waver. The Virgo Moon notices what a partner needs and quietly provides it; the Scorpio Sun takes its time to trust, and then trusts without reserve. You may express concern as a correction, or keep your deepest feelings behind practical help. Saying the feeling itself, now and then, without the help attached, lets a partner meet the depth behind the care.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that combines investigation with precision suits you: research, analysis, auditing, editing, archives and expert reports, quality and safety roles, any job where finding the real cause matters as much as fixing it. The Scorpio Sun gets to the bottom of things; the Virgo Moon makes sure the details are right. Sloppy work troubles the Moon and superficial work bores the Sun, so you may end up redoing other people's.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

An eye that sees beneath the surface and an eye for flaws can turn inward together. You may examine your own motives with the Scorpio Sun's thoroughness and judge what you find by the Virgo Moon's standards, which few people could meet. Allow some things to stay unexamined and imperfect. A practical kindness to yourself, done before the analysis starts, is often the best answer this pair has.
