Aries is where the zodiac starts again, and Uranus is the part of a chart that wants things to start differently. Put the two together and change stops being a theory: it becomes a first move, made in person, before anyone has agreed to it. This is the restless edge of a whole generation, and it's sharpest where your own house puts it.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the function of change in a chart: innovation, independence, release from a constraint that no longer fits. [Aries](/astrology/aries/), cardinal fire ruled by [Mars](/astrology/mars/), gives it a quick, direct, physical manner. Here, change doesn't wait for a plan or a consensus. It begins with one person doing the new thing and finding out, by doing it, whether it works.

Uranus has no traditional dignity, since the astrologers of antiquity never saw it, and Aries is neither Aquarius, its modern sign, nor the sign opposite. So the placement takes its colour from Mars: your Mars, by sign and house, shows how fast this Uranus moves and what it pushes against.

Uranus was in Aries from April 1927 to March 1935, and again from May 2010 to March 2019. Of everyone born from 1930 to 2009, about 1 person in 16 has it. In the first months of each stay it slipped back into Pisces, and in the last ones forward into Taurus, so for births in those months only a chart can say which sign it was: [Uranus in Pisces](/astrology/uranus-in-pisces/) and [Uranus in Taurus](/astrology/uranus-in-taurus/) describe the neighbours. The sign belongs to an age group; the house, which your birth time sets, is the part of your own life where you're the one who goes first. The younger group is still growing up, and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/): what follows is written for adults.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, this Uranus asks for room to make the first move. You may be the one who suggests the unusual plan, who wants a relationship to keep surprising you, who needs to act on an impulse without asking leave. Freedom, here, means the freedom to initiate, not the freedom to vanish. A partner who can hear *I want to try this* as an invitation rather than a threat suits it best. Libra, across the zodiac, is the reminder this placement needs: a fresh start can also be made by two people at once.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, Uranus in Aries is at its best on the first day of something: the prototype, the trial run, the venture nobody has attempted. It changes a system by acting rather than arguing: build the new way, show that it works, let the others follow. Waiting for approval drains it. It needs roles where trying and failing fast is part of the job, and a team in which someone else is glad to finish what it starts.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard part is the beginning that never turns into a middle. Uranus asks for change and Aries for speed, so a life can fill up with first chapters: projects dropped once the novelty fades, quarrels with a rule simply because it was there first. That isn't what Uranus is, since innovation is not chaos, but it's how this placement looks when the push goes against something instead of towards something. Impatience can also make every limit sound like an insult to your independence.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give each experiment an ending: decide in advance how you'll know it worked. Let Mars do the physical work of starting, through sport, movement or a craft, so that feeling new doesn't require turning your life over. For the group born from 1927 to 1935, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 42, came while Uranus crossed Libra, from September 1968 to September 1975: a midlife question, put to people who start alone, about starting with others. Uranus then came back to Aries from May 2010 to March 2019, a full turn of about 84 years. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Uranus was checked like every placement and wasn't one of the three that held far more often; overall, about a third held.
