The full moon in Libra is the first full moon of the northern spring: the Sun has just entered Aries, and the Moon shines opposite, in Libra. It's a full moon about other people, set against a Sun that wants to get going on its own.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

The Moon is full when it stands opposite the Sun, its lit side turned wholly to the Earth. With the Moon in [Libra](/astrology/libra/) and the Sun on the same degree of [Aries](/astrology/aries/), this [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) brings your need for harmony, fairness and company to a peak, just as the Sun, exalted in Aries by tradition ([exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/)), pushes the month towards initiative and your own direction. It's the axis of the [full moon in Aries](/astrology/full-moon-in-aries/) seen from the other side: here the Moon carries *we* and the Sun *I*.

So this full moon tends to raise a question between you and someone else: an agreement that needs renegotiating, a partner whose needs you've been working around, or your own wish for peace after a burst of activity. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) Aries, and their houses in your chart show how those two sides play out.

None of this happens because the Moon is full. A full moon earns a place in a reading when its Moon or its Sun sits on a natal planet or angle, give or take a degree; then it marks the peak of something a transit or a progression has already brought into play. As a forecast, it says nothing.

The most recent was on 2 April 2026, at 12°21′ Libra. The next falls on 22 March 2027 at 10:44 UT, at 1°35′, less than two days after the equinox; then 9 April 2028 at 20°06′, 30 March 2029 at 9°40′ and 18 April 2030 at 28°09′. None is an eclipse.

In the same month, with the Sun in Aries, comes the [new moon in Aries](/astrology/new-moon-in-aries/): on 6 April 2027, a fortnight after this one. A month earlier falls the [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/); a month later, the [full moon in Scorpio](/astrology/full-moon-in-scorpio/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Without a birth time, these two houses can't be placed at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Using Placidus, as I do, start from the house with Libra on its cusp; the Sun lights the one across from it. If Libra rises in your chart, this full moon lies along your own horizon, the Moon on the Ascendant's side and the Sun on the Descendant's.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Libra rising: your wish for harmony shows in how you meet people, while a partner pushes ahead with plans of their own.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** what's fair to spend and keep, set against a shared venture that wants bold moves.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** a conversation that restores the peace with someone close, while a new belief or journey fires you up.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** harmony at home comes to the fore, while your career asks you to take the lead.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** romance and shared pleasures, set against a group that needs you to act.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** fairness in daily work, colleagues to balance, while a private urge to start again stirs in the 12th.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Libra's own house: a partnership at its fullest, asking for fairness, while you want to go your own way.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** a joint arrangement weighed for fairness, money and intimacy alike, while you want to act on your own resources.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a philosophy of balance, a journey taken with someone, while everyday talk is quick and pointed.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Libra on the Midheaven: a public role as mediator or partner, while home asks you to assert yourself.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** friendships and alliances at a peak, while a passion of yours insists on coming first.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a private longing for peace, while the working day demands energy and speed.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Check the degree at both ends.** A degree around each position above, in Libra or at the same degree of Aries.
- **Weigh, then act.** Libra's side asks what's fair, Aries's side what you want. This full moon tends to go best when both questions get an answer.
- **Read Venus and Mars by house.** The first shows how you make peace, the second how you make a start.
