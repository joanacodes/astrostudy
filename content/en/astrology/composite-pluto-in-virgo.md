Composite Pluto in Virgo is the relationship Pluto of a great many pairs now aged about 54 to 70, born while Pluto crossed Virgo from October 1956 to July 1972. It sets the bond's deepest changes in work, method and repair.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), mutable earth ruled by Mercury, refines, sorts and keeps things in working order. Where [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) sets [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) in Virgo, deep change arrives through the practical: a household reorganised from top to bottom after a hard year, a shared trade learnt and mastered, a way of living together rebuilt one habit at a time until it actually works. Astrologers read a bond that renews itself by repair rather than replacement. When something breaks between the two of you, the instinct is to take it apart, understand it and put it back better.

A composite belongs to neither person, and this one shows it nicely: two people who are each fairly chaotic on their own can find that, together, they keep lists, mend things and run a tight ship. Its trap is the endless review. A pair that analyses itself this thoroughly can turn every conversation into an audit and each other into a project, until improving the relationship leaves no room to enjoy it. Analysis isn't criticism, and a bond is allowed to be good enough. Virgo is one of [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/)'s two signs, so composite Mercury deserves a look as well: how the pair thinks problems through and talks them over is where this Pluto does much of its work.

Two people born between October 1956 and July 1972 share [Pluto in Virgo](/astrology/pluto-in-virgo/), and so does their composite, give or take a few births at either end, when Pluto was crossing back and forth. Wider age gaps reach it too, since the composite takes the halfway point: one person born with Pluto in late Leo and another with it in Libra can meet in the middle, in Virgo. For pairs born from 1930 to 2009 it's the second most common composite Pluto, about 1 pair in 4, after Libra.

So the sign mostly marks the generation the two of you belong to. The house, which needs both birth times and a stated method, is where the reading turns personal: with [composite Pluto in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-6th-house/), the theme of daily work would come twice, once by sign and once by house. [Composite Pluto in Leo](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-leo/) is its older neighbour, common among pairs born in the 1940s and 1950s; [composite Pluto in Libra](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-libra/), the younger one, is the commonest of all.

Help with daily life can slide into control of it. If one of you has taken over the other's money, phone or comings and goings, and saying no feels dangerous, put the chart away: the emergency services come first, then the helplines on my Disclaimers page, such as Samaritans on 116 123 ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)). Precision includes asking, too: a composite needs each person's date, time and place of birth, and each of you should hand over your own ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
