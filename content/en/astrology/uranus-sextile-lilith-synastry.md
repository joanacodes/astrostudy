Some friendships hand you a room where your stranger side is the normal one. Uranus sextile Lilith in synastry sets one person's Uranus about 60° from the other's Black Moon Lilith, within 2.4°, and what it offers is exactly that: an outlet, open whenever the other decides to use it.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates: it finds the unconventional route, the unusual group, the method nobody else uses, and it insists on autonomy. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) marks, symbolically, an instinct for self-rule and the material a person learned to keep out of sight; its emphasis varies between traditions, so I hold it lightly. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) joins two functions through opportunity, a cooperation that starts only when someone engages it; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one offers one person's guarded side a way out through the other's originality.

With the Uranus on your side, you tend to bring the other person into settings where difference is ordinary: a club, a scene, a way of working. You don't push; you leave the door open.

With the Lilith on your side, you find that what you usually hide has somewhere to go. You can try it out in company that won't flinch, and keep it to yourself when you'd rather.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opening can stay unopened. The Lilith person may prefer to keep the guarded side private, and the Uranus person may read that as timidity rather than a choice; or the Uranus person may treat the outlet as a project and keep offering it until the offer turns into pressure. Opportunity needs engagement, and engagement here includes the right to say not yet.

- **[Uranus conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** originality and refusal fused, so permission can tip into exposure.
- **[Uranus square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-lilith-synastry/):** the urge to break out pushes against the line the other keeps.
- **[Uranus trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-lilith-synastry/):** no door to open: two people who do things their own way, in step.
- **[Uranus opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** each acts out the end of the axis the other holds back.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Offer, then step back. The Uranus person can describe the room without dragging anyone into it; the Lilith person can visit on their own terms. A couple might share a pastime where neither has to be conventional; friends might travel somewhere neither is known; colleagues might start a project that rewards the odd idea.

Lilith's speed shapes who has it. Someone born in mid-1955, with Uranus in Cancer, has it with anyone born from April to June 1968 or in April and May 1971, and after that with two short windows in every nine-year round of mean Lilith. Anyone born, on and off, from October 1970 to July 1972 has Uranus 60° from that 1955 Lilith, so a person born in April or May 1971 has it both ways round. Every birth in those weeks shares it, and contacts like that never make my synastry tally, which I reserve for the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant and the Midheaven. I read this one through the houses, as one era offering another a door, more than one person offering another.

Mean Lilith dates all of this; true Lilith can wander tens of degrees away, so a chart set to it may show the sextile for a different pair, or not at all ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). The hour can't make or break the sextile, but it decides whether the Uranus lands in the Lilith person's 11th house, among friends, or their 1st, in how they meet the world, and that needs both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Within one chart, [Uranus sextile Lilith](/astrology/uranus-sextile-lilith/) is a person whose own originality gives their untamed side an outlet. Between two charts the outlet belongs to someone else, and like any invitation it works only if it can be refused. I treat the reading the same way: both people ask, or I don't look ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
