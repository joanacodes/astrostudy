This overlay is about what's yours. The other person's North Node falls in your 2nd house, among the things you can count on, from your spending habits to the skills you could live from, while their South Node falls opposite, in your 8th, where things are pooled and shared.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) covers what you have and what you value: money, belongings, the talents you could earn from, and the sense of worth underneath them. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is where a person practises a capacity they don't yet have by heart. Through this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), what they're practising resembles the way you look after your own.

For you, they may take an unusual interest in your ordinary arrangements: how you budget, why you've kept that old coat, what you'd never sell. Questions that would sound nosy from someone else come from them as genuine study. Answering them, you may find your sense of your own worth is steadier than you thought.

For them, you show what having something of their own looks like. Building values and resources that don't depend on anyone else is new ground, and your steadiness makes it look possible. The 2nd is succedent, a house where things settle and accumulate, and [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), its natural sign, belongs to Venus: the pleasure of having enough.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Their [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) sits in [the 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/), so sharing is where they feel at home with you: joint plans, confidences, a common pot, intensity. Out of habit rather than design, they may reach for merged arrangements faster than you'd choose, even while saying they want to learn independence.

From their side, your separateness can sting. You keep your accounts, your things and your sense of worth distinct, and to someone used to pooling everything, that can look like distrust. They may also lean on your resources while they build their own.

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance. Their South Node there says nothing about either, or about what anyone will gain or lose ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)): it describes their habits around sharing, and how those meet yours.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Talk about money and belongings plainly, early and without judgement. In a couple, keep a pot that is each person's own beside any you share; between friends, agree what's lent and what's given; between colleagues, make sure each person's skill is credited to them; between adult relatives, write down who owns what long before it matters.

Read the other way, this is your North Node in their 2nd house, and their way of valuing things becomes your lesson. One house before, [their North Node in your 1st house](/astrology/north-node-in-1st-house-synastry/) learns from your manner; one house after, [their North Node in your 3rd house](/astrology/north-node-in-3rd-house-synastry/) from your everyday talk.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the cusp of your 2nd is worked out from the minute and the place of your birth, and the house is narrow in some charts and wide in others; without your birth time, there's no telling whether their node sits among your resources at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Their node takes about a year and a half to cross a sign, so their birth date places it. Near the cusp between your 1st and 2nd, the mean node and the true node can disagree about whether they learn from your manner or your values, and their South Node then hesitates between your 7th and 8th; I look at both. Habits with money and worth are personal ground, so this waits for two adults who have both said yes and both found their birth times ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
