Aquarius on the 4th house cusp puts your IC, the base of the chart, in the sign of friendship and independence. Home is where you can be the version of yourself nobody asked for, and the door tends to stay open to the people you've picked.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) as the ground under everything else, and with [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) there ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), that ground is shared: a flat where friends drop in without calling, a household of people who aren't related, a family that kept its own rules whatever the street thought. You belong where you're allowed to differ. In private you're often the observer, the one who needs a room, a walk or an hour alone before feeling settled, and a home that crowds you can feel like no home at all. Belonging is what I read in the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), never a verdict on the people who raised you. Being fixed, Aquarius keeps the house rules it has worked out; being Aquarius, it may write them differently from anyone you grew up with.

The IC hands the 4th to the rulers of its sign, here Uranus and Saturn, and I read both. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) marks where home gets reinvented: sudden moves, households that change shape, a taste for living differently. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) marks where you build a home slowly and keep it in repair, the long project rather than the experiment. Capricorn holds the cusp just before, the 3rd, in 7 London charts in 10 and more than 8 in 10 at New York, which gives Saturn your street as well as your door: neighbours, errands and local life count for a lot in whether a place feels like home. Where each planet stands shows where your sense of home goes looking, as ruler of the 4th in the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

About 1 chart in 12 has its IC in Aquarius, at London and New York alike. In Placidus, the house system I use, the IC and the Midheaven are the two ends of the meridian, and the meridian, unlike the horizon, takes no account of latitude: Aquarius crosses it in just under two hours wherever you were born. Leo always holds the Midheaven opposite ([Leo on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/)), so the Sun rules your public life, and the Sun is in detriment in Aquarius: the planet that wants to be seen at work is weakened in the sign of your home. I read that as two lives asking different things of you, warmth in public and independence in private, not as a flaw. Scorpio rises with this IC in about 2 London charts in 3 and 4 New York charts in 5, Libra in the rest, and Pisces nearly always opens the 5th next door. In about 1 London chart in 7, and 1 New York chart in 11, Aquarius gets no cusp to open at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and none of this applies.

The IC moves through a whole sign in about two hours, so it's the birth time that sets Aquarius at the base of your chart rather than on the cusp before or after it ([Aquarius on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/); [Aquarius on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-5th-house-cusp/)). Without the time there is no IC to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
