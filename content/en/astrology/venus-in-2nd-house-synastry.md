What you enjoy, their Venus in your 2nd house tends to enjoy too. Taste in food, in objects, in the small comforts of a day often runs close between you, and their pleasure in what you value can make you feel richer in a way that has nothing to do with money.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is where you keep what is yours: money and belongings, the skills you're paid for, and the quieter question of what you're worth. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how a person judges what's worth having, and how they show appreciation. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), it sets their appreciation on your resources and your values.

You may find that they notice the things you've chosen with care, the good knife, the second-hand chair, the work you're quietly proud of, and say so. Their appreciation can lift your sense of worth. They may be generous in practical ways too: a meal paid for, a present that shows they were listening.

They take real pleasure in your way of living: your comforts, your possessions, the unhurried pace at which you enjoy things. With you they may feel well looked after, simply because the good things in your life are shared. The 2nd is a succedent house, and [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), its natural sign, belongs to Venus, so their taste arrives somewhere it feels at home.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Affection can get tangled up with things. Gifts may start to stand in for attention, shared spending can drift past what either of you meant, and comfort can settle into a routine neither of you questions. If their approval of what you own matters too much, your own sense of worth may begin to lean on it.

They can feel valued for what they bring rather than for who they are, or notice that their generosity isn't returned in kind. And when your tastes clash, over a purchase, a home, a way of spending a weekend, a quarrel about objects can carry a quarrel about values.

Nothing here forecasts money: not whether they'll help you financially, not whether a joint purchase will pay off ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). It describes what the two of you value, and how you enjoy it together.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Talk about what money and possessions mean to each of you, not only what they cost. Give gifts of time as well as things. With a partner, agree a budget for pleasure as well as for bills. Between friends, splitting costs fairly keeps generosity a pleasure. At work, a colleague who values your skills and says so is a gift; let their praise inform how you value yourself rather than replace it. In a family, a shared taste for the same old things can make an adult relative feel close.

Swapped round, your Venus in their 2nd house, it's you who delights in their comforts and their way of keeping things. The pages on either side change the setting: [their Venus in your 1st house](/astrology/venus-in-1st-house-synastry/) is pleasure in your presence, [their Venus in your 3rd house](/astrology/venus-in-3rd-house-synastry/) warmth in your everyday words.

Their Venus is never more than about 47° from their Sun, and it covers only about a degree in a day, which is why their birth date is enough to place it. Your birth time draws the 2nd: in Placidus, the house system I use, two charts cast for the same minute in different towns can have their 2nd cusps several degrees apart. I wait for both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and since what someone owns and earns is private, I read it only with their agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
