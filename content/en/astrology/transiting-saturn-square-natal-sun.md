Seven years or so after Saturn crosses your Sun, it reaches a right angle to it, and seven years or so before it crosses again, it makes the other one. These are the two turns in Saturn's round where your sense of direction meets the most resistance, and learns the most from it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) stands for structure, limits and what lasts; the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) for identity, vitality and purpose. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction that pushes adaptation, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your purpose runs into its practical limits: timetables, obligations, the number of hours in a day. Progress slows, effort costs more, and the plans that were too loose show it first.

The first square tests what you started at the last crossing: whether the commitment has foundations, or only intentions. The second, seven years before the next crossing, tests what you've built since, and often asks you to let go of the parts that have done their job. Saturn is a timetable, not a wall: what takes longer in these months isn't refused, it's asked to be done properly.

The house Saturn is crossing shows where the pressure comes from. With your Sun in the 1st, for instance, it often comes while Saturn crosses your 4th, from home and family needs that ask you to reorganise ([Saturn transiting the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-4th-house/)). Born with Saturn on your Sun, you meet this square at the ages Saturn squares its own place ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)); with [Sun square Saturn](/astrology/sun-square-saturn/) in your chart, you know this friction already, and each transit gives it a new setting.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the square usually lasts about nine months. A little over half the time it comes as a loop, three exact passes over about nine and a half months while Saturn turns retrograde and direct again ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)); when Saturn moves at full speed, it can pass in three weeks.

Your birthday is enough to place it roughly. While Saturn is in Aries, until April 2028, every Sun in a cardinal sign meets it at some angle: Aries by conjunction, Libra by opposition, and Cancer and Capricorn by this square. From April 2028 to mid-2030 it's the fixed signs' turn, and the squares fall on birthdays in Leo and Aquarius. The house it lands in needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have had five or six of these squares, two in every round. The first comes about two years after [transiting Saturn sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-sun/) and about seven after [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-sun/); [transiting Saturn trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-sun/) follows two or three years later, then [transiting Saturn opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-sun/) at the halfway mark.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Decide what you're no longer going to carry. A Saturn square is the season to cut the commitment that was never yours, so that the ones that are can have your energy.

Do one thing properly rather than three things fast. Structure that holds now will hold for years, and Saturn rewards the slow version.

Count rest as part of the plan. Tiredness in these months isn't weakness; it's information about where your effort goes, and a planned pause usually saves more time than it costs.
