The planet that rules your partnerships has gone to the bottom of the chart, into the house of home and family. Whoever you pair with tends to end up at your kitchen table: partnership and belonging are worked out in the same rooms.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is where you meet another person as an equal, in love, in work or across a dispute. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) covers where you come from and the base you build: home, family, the life behind your front door. A ruler carries its house's business to the house it stands in ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and here the business of partnership goes home.

I read it as partnership that wants an address. Commitment, for you, often means a shared roof, a household, a family made or joined, and the people you commit to tend to become kin in the full sense: people you look after and are looked after by. A business may run from home, or with relatives; you may meet someone through family, or find that a partner's place in your life feels like a homecoming. The other side is that the patterns you grew up with come into your relationships, for better and for worse. The way the people at home dealt with each other is often the first model of partnership you knew, and part of the work here is choosing which parts of it to keep.

Both houses are angular ([angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/)). In Placidus, the house system I use, the 7th begins at the Descendant and the 4th at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart, so the ruler of your setting degree was below the earth when you were born, heading for that low point. I read the image plainly: much of what holds a partnership together for you happens in private, and people outside may never see how much it carries.

The planet colours the home you make with someone. The Moon, ruling a Cancer Descendant with Capricorn rising, puts care at the centre of the bond, and the 4th is the Moon's own house in the natural zodiac; Saturn, ruling a Capricorn Descendant with Cancer rising, builds slowly and keeps its promises about the roof over your heads. [Scorpio on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/), with Taurus rising, hands the 7th to two planets, Mars and Pluto, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 4th brings energy, and some heat, into a shared home, and Pluto's house, wherever it falls, shows how deeply a bond reaches into your sense of belonging.

Ten minutes can change the story. Earlier, the ruler might still be in the 5th, where love starts as play ([Ruler of the 7th in the 5th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)); later, it might have moved back into the 3rd, where they grow out of everyday talk ([Ruler of the 7th in the 3rd house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)). The Imum Coeli itself moves with the clock, so I read this placement only from a recorded time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Living together, marrying or starting a family aren't written here, and I don't read them as due. What is written is a need: a partnership, for you, has to feel like somewhere to come home to.
