Mars reaches Gemini on three of its retrogrades in the dates I use, each about fifteen years after the last: 2007–08, 2022–23 and 2037. All three fall between October and January, and each turns the planet of drive back over the arguments and plans of the sign of talk.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) is mutable air, ruled by Mercury: Mars there fights with words, chases several aims at once and changes tack quickly. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), that scattered drive is called back over ground it has already covered: the debate you keep winning in your head, the projects started side by side, the wanting that jumps from one thing to the next. Anger in Gemini tends to come out in sentences, and these weeks can replay a sharp exchange until you hear it another way. Mars here rarely lacks options; what the retrograde asks for is focus.

The most recent loop stayed in the sign, from 25°36′ Gemini on 30 October 2022 to 8°07′ on 12 January 2023: 74 days over about 17½°, with Mars opposite the Sun, then in Sagittarius, in early December. The 2007–08 loop came in from Cancer ([Mars retrograde in Cancer](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-cancer/)): it stationed at 12°27′ Cancer on 15 November 2007 and turned direct at 24°04′ Gemini on 30 January 2008. The next, from 12 October to 23 December 2037, starts at 6°15′ Gemini and backs out into Taurus ([Mars retrograde in Taurus](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-taurus/)).

Born with Mars retrograde in Gemini, you may rehearse an argument inwardly long before you voice it; that is a natal placement, with its own reading ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house Gemini fills in your chart depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); without it I don’t draw the houses, and I don’t read the chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, the loops of 2007–08 and 2037 each cross a sign line, so they can span two houses.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/gemini-rising/):** Gemini rising: a quick, talkative first impression; the way you argue your corner, slowed to listening speed.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/gemini-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** the skills you trade on; effort spread over too many sidelines, pared back.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** Gemini’s natural house: siblings, neighbours, short trips; a running dispute by message, answered at last with care.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a lively, busy home; the household debate that never settles, approached from a new angle.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/):** wordplay, games and flirtation; a rivalry kept up for fun, seen for what it is.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/gemini-on-6th-house-cusp/):** workdays full of messages and errands; the habit of saying yes to every task, rethought.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Gemini on the Descendant, with Sagittarius rising: a partner you spar with in words; an old argument between you, reopened to be understood rather than won.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/gemini-on-8th-house-cusp/):** talk about trust, desire and what is shared; a hard question you kept postponing, asked more simply.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/):** debates about belief, study and travel; a cause you argued for loudly, re-examined.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Gemini on the Midheaven: a working life of many roles; the one you push hardest, chosen again or let go.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/):** group chats and wide networks; a disagreement among friends, worked through slowly.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/gemini-on-12th-house-cusp/):** what goes unsaid, in a house tradition tied to confinement, which I never read as a forecast ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)); arguments rehearsed silently, finally set down.

## What helps {#what-helps}

With Mars backing through Mercury’s sign, I find writing helps more than talking at first: put the argument on paper, then decide whether it needs saying at all. Pick one project of the several on the go and give it the effort. Your natal Mercury, ruler of Gemini, shows by its house where your mind takes the review, and your natal Mars where the drive starts from.

None of this predicts a falling-out or a reconciliation. It describes ten weeks or so in which a restless energy finds out what it wants to finish.
