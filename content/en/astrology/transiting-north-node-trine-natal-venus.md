A trine from the node to Venus is the rare transit in which growing and enjoying ask for the same thing. The node is a third of the zodiac from your Venus, its partner a sixth on the far side, and for about a month the less practised direction turns up looking like a pleasure.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how you value and enjoy, and how you relate when things are going well. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity you are still building, and a [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets the two help each other without either losing its character. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), what you enjoy and what you're learning point the same way, so growth can feel like pleasure for once.

It's the kind of month in which a hobby becomes a craft, a friendship deepens without effort, or a new way of spending time simply sticks because it's enjoyable. The risk is only the trine's usual one: that ease passes for luck and goes unused. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), sextile your Venus in the same weeks, adds an old talent to the new pleasure.

With your [Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house/), where tradition gave Venus its joy, the trine is likely to show in play, romance and the things you make for the pleasure of making them. Tradition also gave the 5th to children; I read it in an adult's life as joy and creativity, and no transit forecasts a pregnancy ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)). The 5th, like every house, can only be drawn from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My window opens 1½° short of the exact trine and closes 1° past it, and a month or so is the node's usual stay inside it. Its pace follows the Sun's angle to the nodes: the quick passes, about three and a half weeks, come between eclipse seasons, the slow ones of three and a half months or more inside them, each about a tenth of the total, and about one in twenty-five touches the exact trine three times.

An earth Venus is trined from the other earth signs, an air Venus from the other air signs, and so on round the elements. Take Venus in Taurus, one of its two home signs. The node trines it from Capricorn, March 2028 to September 2029, stands opposite from Scorpio by December 2032 ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-venus/)), and trines it again from Virgo before November 2035; the conjunction ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-venus/)), the sextiles ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-venus/)) and the squares ([Transiting North Node square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-venus/)) fill the rest of the round. Of those three dates, my forecast would show only the Scorpio one: it keeps the node's conjunctions and oppositions and leaves its trines out. By 80 that makes eight or nine trines. Born with [Venus trine North Node](/astrology/venus-trine-north-node/), you'll find your nodal returns among them.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Turn a pleasure into a practice: the instrument you play for fun, the garden, the recipes. Give it a regular hour and see where it leads.

Spend time with the people who make growing feel easy, and tell them so.

Notice what you'd do for nothing. That is often where the node is pointing.

Accept the invitation that sounds like fun rather than self-improvement; this month the two may turn out to be the same.
