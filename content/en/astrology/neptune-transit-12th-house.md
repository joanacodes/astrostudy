Before Neptune reaches your Ascendant it spends a long season in your 12th, the house of the inner life, and of all the houses this is the one where Neptune is most at home. Nearly thirteen years is the usual stay.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) transcends: it softens the walls of the separate self and opens it to imagination, compassion and the sense of something larger. The [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/) is solitude, retreat, rest, the inner life, and the care given and received out of sight. With Neptune crossing it, the pull inward can grow strong. You may want more time alone, a spiritual practice, art made in private, or simply more sleep and quiet; dreams and intuitions can feel vivid; compassion for others, and for yourself, can deepen. The same openness asks for good boundaries, because a house without walls can let in too much.

This is the house the natural zodiac gives Pisces, so Neptune, the modern ruler of Pisces, crosses home ground here; the tradition gave Pisces to Jupiter, and I read both. Saturn, the old planet of solitude, was said to rejoice in this house. With Venus in your 12th from birth, love can turn quietly devotional, given without needing to be seen.

The old books put confinement and hidden enemies in the 12th, and a careless reading can make this transit sound like a slide into fog. Neither belongs in my reading of this transit ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)). A [transit](/astrology/transits/) brings out what the chart already holds about the inner life, and if low feelings take hold, a doctor or a therapist is the right help, not a chart.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn for London, Neptune's middle stay in the 12th is nearly thirteen years, a little under its middle for all houses there, but a wide 12th can hold it nearly twenty-six: only the 6th, its opposite, reaches a little further, and the 1st draws level. The New York count gives nearly fourteen years for the middle and about twenty-one for the long end.

When Neptune touches your 12th cusp the stay begins, and it lasts, through every retrograde return, until your Ascendant is reached. Your rising degree marks that end, and only your birth time can place it; without the time I give no reading at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Neptune enters the 12th after its years among friends and causes in your 11th ([Neptune transiting the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-11th-house/)), and leaves it by crossing your Ascendant into the 1st, where the softening reaches the self you show ([Neptune transiting the 1st house](/astrology/neptune-transit-1st-house/)).

Neptune spent April 2011 to January 2026 in Pisces, the 12th's natural sign, so a Pisces 12th cusp has had its crossing. Aries is Neptune's sign from January 2026 to March 2039, after a first visit in 2025, and an Aries 12th cusp is reached within those years; Taurus follows, from May 2038.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Protect a little solitude every day, and give it a shape: a walk, a page, a quiet half hour. Neptune in the 12th restores you through the inner life, and it restores best when the time is yours.

Keep good boundaries around your compassion. This house opens you to other people's moods and needs; noticing what is yours and what isn't is a kindness to everyone.

If what surfaces in the quiet feels heavy, talk to a doctor or a qualified therapist early; there's no merit in carrying it alone.
