Money with a horizon: with the ruler of the 2nd in the 9th house, what you earn and what you value are tied to study, travel, belief and the search for meaning.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The planet ruling the sign on the cusp of your [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) has carried money, belongings and the sense of your own worth out to the far horizon ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)): the [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) of travel abroad and other cultures, university and long study, philosophy and faith, teaching and publishing.

Your resources tend to serve your sense of meaning. You may earn through knowledge, by teaching, writing, translating, guiding or trading with other countries, and you spend readily on what widens your view: courses, books, a trip you'll talk about for years. Values and beliefs run close together: you want your money earned in a way you can defend, and spent on things you believe in. Keep a foot on the ground, though: a big idea of what money is for can sit beside a vague idea of what's in the account.

With Leo on the 2nd cusp, the Sun rules your money from the 9th, where tradition placed its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), and pride in your work and pride in your principles tend to go together. With Sagittarius on the cusp, Jupiter handles your money from Sagittarius's own house, which suits learning that pays and generosity that travels. Horary astrology gave the 9th long journeys, foreign lands, universities and books, a fair list of where this ruler's money comes from and where it goes.

Opposite the 9th lies the 3rd, the everyday round of errands and local trade. With the ruler of your 2nd at the far end of that axis, small daily sums can feel less real to you than the big plan, the course or the trip they're being saved for, and a budget that names the trip makes saving much easier.

The planet that governs your money had just gone over the top of the sky when you were born; in Placidus, the house system I use, the first stretch past the Midheaven is the 9th. The minutes decide which side of the 9th's cusps it stands on ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): further down, in the 8th, your money would mingle with other people's, in joint accounts and shared plans ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 8th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)); with an earlier birth, still short of the Midheaven in the 10th, it would come from your career and the standing it brings ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 10th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)).

When Scorpio sits on the 2nd cusp, I read both of its rulers ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 9th spends on the causes it believes in and works hardest for money it can justify, while Pluto, from its own house, asks what your money is really for.

A job abroad, a scholarship or a book deal can't be read from a chart, and no chart can tell you where your money would do best ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). This one describes where your resources want to go: further than home, and towards what you believe in.
