Every page of this site ends with a line of small print most people skip: readings are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. This page is the long version of it, so you know which questions to bring to an astrologer, and which to take somewhere else.

## The short answer {#the-short-answer}

See a professional instead whenever your question is about your body, your mind, the law, your money or someone's safety. Those are questions where somebody can examine, advise, treat, represent or protect you, and a chart can do none of that. [Not everything is in the stars](/how-i-read/): when what you're carrying is medical or psychological, I'm not the one to help, and I'll tell you so. Going through that door doesn't mean astrology has let you down. It means you've picked the tool that fits.

A reading can still have a place, before or after: how you meet hard news, what you need when you're frightened, how you tend to make decisions. But it comes alongside the professional's answer, never instead of it.

## Why it matters {#why-it-matters}

These are the questions that belong elsewhere, and where they go:

- **Your body.** A symptom, a test result, a pregnancy, a treatment: a doctor, a midwife, a pharmacist. A chart can't diagnose or predict anything about your health ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
- **Your mind.** Low mood that won't lift, anxiety, panic, grief that has stopped moving, thoughts of harming yourself: a doctor or a properly trained therapist, and in an emergency the emergency services. [Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/) says what a chart can and can't do there.
- **The law.** A court case, a separation, a dispute with a landlord or an employer: a lawyer or a legal advice service. A chart can't tell you the outcome ([Can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)).
- **Your money.** Debts, investments, a pension, a large purchase: a qualified, independent financial adviser, or a debt advice service. A chart can describe your habits with money, never what to do with it ([Can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).
- **Someone's safety.** If you or anyone else is being hurt, threatened or controlled: the emergency services first, then a specialist support service. No chart should stand between anyone and safety.
- **A death.** Yours or a loved one's: I don't read it, and no chart can tell it ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). Grief has its own people: those who love you, and those trained to help the bereaved.

What these share is that time counts, and that the wrong kind of answer can cost something real: a symptom left too long, a deadline missed, savings lost, someone left in danger. A forecast, however kindly meant, can delay the call that would have helped, and that delay is the harm I'm most careful about.

## What to do instead {#what-to-do-instead}

A few signs that a question isn't astrology's to answer, whatever it looks like at first:

1. **It has a deadline.** A hearing, a payment, a symptom that's getting worse. Act first; reflect later.
2. **It starts with ‘should I’ and ends in your body, the law or your bank account.** That ‘should’ needs someone who can see the facts.
3. **Someone's safety is involved.** Yours or anyone else's: get help before anything else.
4. **You've asked it again and again.** If you keep going back to the sky with the same question, the answer you need may be one only a person can help you reach.
5. **You're hoping the chart will decide for you,** or take the blame if it goes wrong. That's a decision asking for support, not for a forecast.

When you do go, make it easier on yourself: write down what you want to ask, take someone with you if that helps, and ask about cost and next steps before you leave. Then come back to astrology afterwards if you want to, with the facts in hand. A reading makes a far better companion to a decision than a replacement for one.
