Moon conjunct Lilith is a meeting between the Moon and the far end of its own orbit. Born with the two together, your need for comfort and the part of you that refuses to be tamed share one place, so what makes you feel safe may be exactly what you were once taught to keep quiet.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds your needs: attachment, felt security, nurturing, the comforts you return to. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is a symbolic point, read differently by different astrologers; I read it, as most do, as instinctive autonomy, the material that was pushed out of sight, and your relationship with your own power and boundaries. Together in a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), they act as one and stay themselves: needing and refusing to be tamed arrive together.

What this often means is that comfort, for you, has conditions: you need to be allowed your whole self, including the parts that are too much, too strange or too direct for polite company. Being looked after in a way that asks you to shrink doesn't soothe you; being accepted does. You may care for others the same way, without fuss about how it's supposed to be done. None of this is immoral or dark. Lilith describes what didn't fit, not what's wrong, and here it asks that your feelings be allowed their full size.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Lilith marks the average far point of the Moon's orbit, so this conjunction means you were born with the Moon near the far end of its path, measured from that average. The Moon moves more slowly out there, which is why about 1 person in 20 has the conjunction within 8°, a little more often than the 1 in 22 of the Moon's conjunctions with the slow planets.

Mean Lilith spends about nine months in each sign, so its sign is shared with people born within the same months; the house of the conjunction, which needs your birth time, shows where your needs insist on being met on your own terms. One caution: mean and true Lilith can sit tens of degrees apart, so a chart using the true point may not show this conjunction at all ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

The Moon's other angles to its own far point:

- **[Moon sextile Lilith](/astrology/moon-sextile-lilith/):** your untamed side is available to your comfort, when you let it in.
- **[Moon square Lilith](/astrology/moon-square-lilith/):** the need to belong and the refusal to shrink rub, and you learn to keep both.
- **[Moon trine Lilith](/astrology/moon-trine-lilith/):** instinct and comfort agree, and being yourself costs you little.
- **[Moon opposite Lilith](/astrology/moon-opposite-lilith/):** what you hide is met in others first, across the chart.

When one person's Lilith meets another's Moon, [Moon conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/moon-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) can be fascinating, sometimes unsettling, and best when both can be honest about what it stirs.

How I read Lilith, gently and without fixing it, is set out in [The three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#lilith).

## In transits {#in-transits}

Mean Lilith moves through the zodiac at a steady pace, about nine months to a sign, so when transiting Lilith crosses your natal Moon it stays close for a couple of weeks. Born with the conjunction, you meet that crossing on a schedule: Lilith comes back to its birth position about every nine years, around 9, 18, 27, 35, 44 and 53, and each return brings it back to your Moon. The Moon itself reaches your natal Lilith every month, for a few hours.

These are weather, not a forecast: brief seasons when a need you've kept quiet asks to be spoken, with nothing decided for you.
