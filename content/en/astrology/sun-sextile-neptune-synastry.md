A song recommended at the right moment, a book left on the table, a question about what a project is really for: that's often how Sun sextile Neptune works between two charts. One person's Neptune is some 60° from the other's Sun, and inspiration moves towards the Sun person's aims whenever they leave the door ajar.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is purpose and identity, the direction a person puts their energy into. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is imagination, compassion and symbolic sense: the part of a chart that perceives meaning and longs for something larger. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) links functions through opportunity and cooperation, and it's the most understated of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) between these two bodies: it asks the least and gives in the quietest way.

Holding the Sun, you find your plans acquiring a sense of what they're for. The other person helps you see the human side of your work, or the beauty in it, and you feel understood without having to explain much.

Holding Neptune, you see your imagination given a channel. The Sun person's direction gives your dreams something to attach to, and your sensitivity is put to use rather than merely admired.

The elements colour it. Between fire and air, the inspiration tends to come as ideas and causes; between earth and water, as compassion made practical: care, craft, a home that feels kind.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

What an easy angle can hide here is vagueness. The inspiration stays in the air, lovely and unused: plans described with feeling and never given a date. The Neptune person's suggestions can be so gentle that the Sun person doesn't register them as suggestions at all, and the Sun person's directness can roll over a hint that mattered. Neither says what they actually want, because the mood seems to make it unnecessary.

Neptune's sign is the same for nearly everyone born in a fourteen-year stretch, so on its own it describes an age group. The sextile with this Sun, two signs on, is what brings it home to one pair.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give one inspiration a concrete form: a date in the diary, a first draft, a visit booked. The Neptune person can say plainly what they hope for; the Sun person can ask "what did you mean by that?" instead of guessing. A couple might keep a small ritual that means something to both; friends, a series of concerts or a cause they volunteer for; a family, a tradition whose meaning gets said aloud; colleagues, one sentence on what the project is for, written where everyone can see it.

About 1 pair of people in 10 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 4.8°. With so narrow an orb, a contact near its edge can hang on the birth time, since a day moves the Sun only a degree or so; the houses, which place the inspiration in a particular part of each life, always do, and I wait for both times before reading them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Turn the angle and the contact changes:

- **[Sun conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** the imagination settles on the Sun person's identity itself.
- **[Sun square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-neptune-synastry/):** the ideal and the person rub, and plain speaking becomes urgent.
- **[Sun trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-neptune-synastry/):** kindness flows without being invited.
- **[Sun opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** each holds the end the other is missing.

In one chart, [Sun sextile Neptune](/astrology/sun-sextile-neptune/) is an invitation a person can accept from their own imagination. Between two people it comes from someone else, which makes it easier to notice, and just as easy to decline.
