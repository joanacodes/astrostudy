Your Sun's first loyalty is to your own people; your Moon cannot rest until things are fair for everyone. Both signs act first, Cancer to protect and Libra to reconcile, and much of your life is spent deciding which of the two is called for. The Moon was close to its first quarter when you were born, half lit or nearly so in the evening sky.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and cardinal air, the Moon three signs on: a square by sign, and both signs like to make the first move. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) gives its loyalty first to family, old friends and the people it shelters; the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) settles when relationships are balanced and the room is calm, and it moves quickly to restore that balance. The friction comes when the two ask for different things: standing by your own, or being even-handed with everyone, the outsider included.

Whatever your Moon needs, your Sun needs as well, since the Moon rules Cancer: here that means harmony with other people becomes part of your purpose, not an optional extra. Your Moon in turn answers to [Venus](/astrology/venus/), which with a Cancer Sun sits somewhere between Taurus and Virgo, its sign showing what kind of beauty and peace restores you. Neither sign is a place of dignity for its light.

Somewhere between a thick crescent and a half moon, your Moon stood 61° to 119° in front of the Sun; at least five days had gone by since the new moon, and fewer than ten. The eight-phase count has two names for these births, [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) for the first half and [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) for the second, and close to 90° the square of signs is mirrored by a waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). The first quarter, as I read it, turns friction outward into action, and for this pair the action is often a conversation that clears the air. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) gives the details.

Around 1 person in 137 shares it. Libra is a two-and-a-half-day stop for the Moon, coming from Virgo and going on to Scorpio; for a birth on a day when it changed sign, the time alone separates this page from its neighbours, the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-virgo/) and the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-scorpio/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). That moment also decides your rising sign and your houses, and they show whether this tug between home and fairness plays out mostly in private or in public.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You want a home that is both safe and gracious: nurturing, courteous, beautiful to come back to. The Cancer Sun shelters a partner; the Libra Moon needs things to be fair between you and conflict to be handled kindly. You may go to great lengths to avoid a scene, and then feel hurt that your own needs went unsaid. Raising small things early, gently and in words, protects the peace you care about more than silence does.

## At work {#at-work}

Mediation, family law, human resources, hospitality, interior design, counselling couples or teams: work where care and fairness have to meet suits this pair. The Cancer side looks after people, the Libra side after the process, which must be just, and the atmosphere, which must stay civil. You're often the colleague who notices someone being left out and quietly fixes it. Hostile, partisan workplaces cost you a great deal, so choose teams that argue honestly and make up properly.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Loyalty and fairness can pull you in two. When your own people are in the wrong, the Libra Moon knows it and the Cancer Sun wants to defend them anyway; when you're fair to an outsider, the Sun may feel it has betrayed its own. Peacekeeping can also become a job you never resign from. What helps is asking two questions separately, *who do I love?* and *what is fair?*, and answering both honestly, even when the answers differ.
