Mars’s retrogrades meet Sagittarius three times in the dates I use, each a little less than the one before: the whole loop of 2001, part of the loop of 2016, and in 2033 barely a quarter of a degree. All three fall between April and August.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) is mutable fire, ruled by Jupiter: Mars there acts on conviction and needs a cause or a horizon to aim at. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when Mars seems from Earth to pause and run back for two months or more, the arrow returns to the bow. What slows is the crusade: the argument you made with total certainty, the plan to leave, study or start again somewhere bigger, the freedom you insisted on without asking what it was for. Anger here can sound like righteousness, and these weeks can show where a principle was covering a hurt. Desire looks for meaning as much as for adventure.

In 2001 the loop stayed in Sagittarius, from 29°03′ on 11 May to 15°06′ on 19 July, with Mars opposite the Sun, then in Gemini, in mid-June. In 2016 it began at 8°54′ Sagittarius on 17 April and backed into Scorpio ([Mars retrograde in Scorpio](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-scorpio/)), turning direct at 23°03′ Scorpio on 29 June. The next starts at 12°30′ Capricorn ([Mars retrograde in Capricorn](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-capricorn/)) on 26 May 2033 and turns direct at 29°47′ Sagittarius on 1 August, so in this sign it reaches only the very last degree.

Born with Mars retrograde in Sagittarius, you carry a drive that tests its beliefs inwardly before it acts, and I read that placement on its own terms ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Sagittarius occupies depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and I don’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2016 loop can start in the Sagittarius house and end in the Scorpio one.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/):** Sagittarius rising: an eager, outspoken first impression; the speed with which you promise, slowed.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you think worth pursuing; effort spent on a big plan, matched against what it means to you.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** frank talk and short journeys; a lecture you gave a sibling or neighbour, reconsidered.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home you keep leaving or enlarging; the restlessness under your roof, looked at.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** adventure in love and play; a dare taken for the thrill, seen more clearly.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work with a mission; the cause you serve at work, asked whether it still serves you.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius on the Descendant, Gemini on the Ascendant: a partner who brings the horizon; whose freedom comes first, talked over.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** beliefs about trust and closeness; a philosophy that kept intimacy at arm’s length, rethought.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius’s natural house: study, travel and belief; a journey or a course begun with zeal, taken up again more slowly.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius on the Midheaven: ambition as a vocation; the mission you announced at work, rewritten.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends who share your causes; a campaign run with them, reconsidered together.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** the house tradition called the Bad Spirit and tied to confinement, never a forecast in my reading ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)); a private faith, questioned gently.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sagittarius aims far, so I find these weeks good for checking the map rather than changing the destination: is the goal still yours, and is the route kind to the people travelling with you? Jupiter rules Sagittarius, and the house of your natal Jupiter shows where the wider view steers the review; your natal Mars shows where the drive begins.

None of this decides a journey, an exam or a debate. It describes a summer season in which conviction is asked what it is for.
