Uranus trine Lilith puts the planet of difference and the point of what you learned to hide a third of the circle apart, usually in signs of one element. The two get on: your originality and your untamed side tend to move in the same direction without much negotiation.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables differentiation, autonomy and new ways of living: it wants a shape of life that is genuinely yours. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), in my reading, is instinctive autonomy: the part of you that was once called too much, and the boundaries that protect your own power. Readings of Lilith differ between traditions, and none of the serious ones make it a moral failing. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates the two through flow, each strengthening the other.

Lived, it often looks like ease with your own strangeness. The wish to do things differently and the instinct to stay untamed agree, so the tug between fitting in and being yourself may barely register. That ease is real, and it can also hide things. You may not see how unusual your freedom looks to others, or why they're startled by it; and a trine left to itself can drift, a quiet independence that never asks anything of you. Flow isn't passivity. It does most when you put it to use, including for people who are still keeping their own untamed side out of sight.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 15 born between 1930 and 2009 has Uranus within 6° of a trine to mean Lilith. Lilith moves forward through a sign in about nine months, so the trine comes about twice a decade and lasts a few months, while Uranus, seven years to a sign, barely moves meanwhile. True Lilith can be tens of degrees away, so check which point your chart shows ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

A trine usually joins signs of one element. Uranus in Pisces trine Lilith in Scorpio, both water, might give an independence that moves by intuition and a privacy nobody can talk you out of. The houses say where the flow runs: Uranus in the 4th trine Lilith in the 12th, say, could give a home arranged your own way that offers the most private part of you a safe place. Houses need your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The tighter the orb, the less you notice the trine working; at the edge of 6°, you may have to go looking for it.

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Lilith](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-lilith/):** the two fused, so that your difference is your untamed side.
- **[Uranus sextile Lilith](/astrology/uranus-sextile-lilith/):** a narrower opening, taken by choice.
- **[Uranus square Lilith](/astrology/uranus-square-lilith/):** the pair rubbing until new terms are found.
- **[Uranus opposite Lilith](/astrology/uranus-opposite-lilith/):** the untamed part seen across the chart, often in another person first.

[Uranus trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-lilith-synastry/) lets two people's appetites for doing things their own way encourage each other. It's at its best when neither tries to steer the other's freedom, and I read it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Transiting Lilith trines natal Uranus twice in each round of about 8 years and 10 months, for a couple of weeks. Transiting Uranus trines natal Lilith twice in its 84-year round, for months, often passing three times.

Born with the trine, your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) around 42 also brings transiting Uranus into a sextile with natal Lilith, so the midlife urge to change course comes with a door open to your untamed side. Weather, not a forecast: easy conditions, which you're free to use or let pass.
