Money that answers to its own house: with the ruler of the 2nd in the 2nd, the planet in charge of what you have and what you value never leaves home. Your resources and your sense of worth are built up slowly and kept close.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Each house takes its ruler from the sign on its cusp ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and the ruler of the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) usually sends money and worth off to another part of life: to work, to a partner, to a cause. Here it stays in the 2nd. The house of money and belongings, of the skills you could live from and the sense of your own worth, is governed from inside, by a planet working on its own ground.

What you have tends to build on what you had. You keep things, mend them, add to them; your values are your own and change slowly; your sense of security comes from what you've put together yourself rather than from what anyone else provides. That self-sufficiency is the strength of it. Its shadow is a closed circle: when nothing from the rest of your life is allowed to feed your security, holding on can start to feel like the only safety there is. Stewardship isn't greed, and security isn't fear, but the line between them needs watching.

Its sign shows how comfortable it is. If the ruler stands in the sign on the 2nd cusp, it's also in its own sign, its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/): Venus in Taurus, with Taurus on the cusp, is the planet the natural zodiac pairs with the 2nd, in the 2nd's own sign, in the 2nd. Saturn in Capricorn there builds savings like a wall, one course at a time. If the house runs on into the next sign, the ruler may sit somewhere less easy: Mars, ruling an Aries 2nd from Taurus, the sign of its detriment, has to earn at a slower pace than it likes.

[Horary astrology](/astrology/horary-astrology/), as William Lilly practised it, gave the 2nd the questioner's money and moveable possessions, the things you can carry, sell or spend, and read their state through the 2nd's ruler. With that ruler at home, I read your resources as being in your own keeping: you tend to manage them yourself, and to want them within reach.

The planet in charge of your money was still under the eastern horizon at your birth, a few hours from rising, which is where Placidus, the house system I use, puts the 2nd. The 2nd cusp itself comes from the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and near either edge a few minutes can carry the ruler across: back into the 1st, where your worth would ride on your own effort and presence ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 1st house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/)), or on into the 3rd, where money moves through words, lessons and trade ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 3rd house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)).

With Pisces on the 2nd cusp, Jupiter and Neptune rule it together, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 2nd gives freely and keeps a hopeful view of what's enough, while Neptune, in whatever house holds it, keeps asking what money is for.

Whatever the planet, a ruler of the 2nd in the 2nd describes habits and values, not a balance. What to buy, and what your savings will come to, are questions it can't answer ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). It tells you that, for you, security is something you build and keep yourself.
