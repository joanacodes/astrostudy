Every autumn, a few weeks into Libra's month, the Moon shines full in Aries, and the two ends of the self-and-other axis face each other: what you want for yourself, and what the Sun in Libra asks of you with other people.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) is the Moon directly opposite the Sun, fully lit, rising as the Sun sets. It's named for the Moon's sign, so a full moon in [Aries](/astrology/aries/) has the Moon in Aries and the Sun at the same degree of [Libra](/astrology/libra/). Where a new moon starts something, a full moon shows how it's going: what has grown, what can no longer be ignored.

The Aries–Libra axis runs between *me* and *we*. The Moon in Aries brings up what you need for yourself, plainly and quickly: room to act, your own say, a little courage. The Sun in Libra is pointing the month towards fairness and other people, and tradition puts it in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) there, a self that works through agreement. So this full moon tends to show where the balance has slipped: where you've given way for too long, or pushed without asking. Neither end is the right one, and the opposition asks for both. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules Aries and [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra, so their houses in your chart show where each side of the question lives.

Like every lunation it passes in a few days, and it enters a chart only when one end of its axis lands on a natal planet or angle, about a degree either way. Then it works as a trigger: something already in motion, a transit, say, or a progression, comes to a head. On its own it forecasts nothing.

The most recent was on 26 September 2026, at 3°37′ Aries. The next is on 15 October 2027 at 13:47 UT, at 21°59′; then 3 October 2028 at 10°59′, 22 October 2029 at 29°16′, on Aries's last degree, and 11 October 2030 at 18°10′. It's always the first full moon after the September equinox, and none of these five is an eclipse.

The [new moon in Libra](/astrology/new-moon-in-libra/) shares its month, a fortnight away on either side; in 2027 it comes first, on 30 September. A month earlier comes the [full moon in Pisces](/astrology/full-moon-in-pisces/), a month later the [full moon in Taurus](/astrology/full-moon-in-taurus/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

A full moon lights two houses at once: the one holding the Moon's degree, and opposite it the one holding the Sun's. Placing either needs the hour of your birth, as [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) explains. In Placidus, my house system, opposite cusps always fall at the same degree of opposite signs, so with Aries on one cusp, Libra is on the cusp across from it.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Aries rising: your own wishes surface, sharp and clear, just as a partner's needs fill your 7th; the work is to hold both.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** a need for money of your own comes up, while what you share in the 8th asks for fairness.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** you say what you think, fast, and the Sun in your 9th asks how it fits what you believe.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** a need for your own space at home peaks while your public role, in the 10th, calls for diplomacy.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** a pleasure you want for yourself, now, a passion or a risk taken for fun, set against what your friends expect.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** impatience with the daily routine comes to a head, while something out of sight, in the 12th, asks for rest and give.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Aries on the Descendant: a partner's directness, or a disagreement in the open, while you try to stay fair; who leads?
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** a strong feeling about shared money or intimacy surfaces, set against your sense of what's fair to keep.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a conviction flares, a journey you want to take now or a cause to defend, while everyday talk asks for tact.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Aries on the Midheaven: an ambition you've held back comes into view, while at home the Sun asks how to keep the peace.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** you take the lead in a group or speak up among friends, while the Sun asks what you enjoy, and with whom.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a drive you usually keep to yourself surfaces in private, while your working days in the 6th call for cooperation.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Check both ends.** Is anything of yours within a degree of one of the positions above, in Aries, or at the same degree of Libra? If so, ask what slower movement is already working there.
- **Name the two needs.** In the house the Moon lights, write down what you want for yourself; in the house opposite, what the other person or the situation asks. This full moon rarely asks you to drop either.
- **Say it once, plainly.** Aries prefers a direct word to a long resentment, and Libra prefers it said kindly. Then listen.
