Money owed, desire, grief, what's really going on: the subjects people go quiet about are the ones you want to discuss. Your 8th house is ruled from the 3rd, the house of talk, learning and the people near you, and depth turns into questions.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Thinking aloud, writing, learning, brothers and sisters, the neighbours and their news: that's the [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/). The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is what's shared and owed, and the intimacy and change that come with letting someone close. Its ruler in the 3rd works those matters through in words ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

This is a mind that digs. You may be drawn to research, investigation, psychology, history, the kind of question that needs patience and nerve: what happened, who holds what, why people do what they do. Conversations with you rarely stay on the surface, and people find themselves saying more than they meant to. The paperwork of sharing may land on your desk as well, the forms, contracts and letters that go with joint accounts and debts, the 3rd being, in horary, the house of letters and contracts ([horary astrology](/astrology/horary-astrology/)). With siblings or neighbours, the bond can be unusually close, or the place where trust and fairness were first tested. Discretion is what this asks of you: knowing which secrets are yours to tell, and when curiosity becomes intrusion.

Tradition placed the Moon's [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) in this house, so with Cancer on your 8th cusp, the ruler of your depths is at home in conversation: feelings get talked through until they settle, in a diary, a long phone call, a sibling who knows. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 8th, gives a real taste for research, at ease with archives and awkward questions; Saturn, ruling a Capricorn 8th, studies slowly and says only what it can stand behind; Venus, ruling a Taurus or Libra 8th, brings tact to hard subjects, so you can talk about money or desire without bruising anyone.

When [Scorpio on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/) gives the house two rulers, Mars and Pluto, I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 3rd argues with force, and Pluto's house shows where your curiosity about power goes deepest.

Brothers and sisters belong to the 3rd and death, in the old books, to the 8th, a pairing that can sound like bad news about relatives. It isn't, and I never read a chart that way ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); your own way of talking about what matters is what I read.

The cusps around this ruler are set by the clock. In Placidus, the house system I use, a slightly later birth could leave it in the 2nd, where shared money meets your own ([Ruler of the 8th in the 2nd house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)); a slightly earlier one could put it in the 4th, where trust and debt are lived at home ([Ruler of the 8th in the 4th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)), and I don't guess between them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Ask the questions; it's what this ruler is for. Just choose the people you ask, and let them decide how much to answer.
