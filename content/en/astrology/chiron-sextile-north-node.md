Chiron sextile North Node turns what you learned by living around a difficulty into equipment for growth. The skills sit comfortably with your familiar strengths, and they open a door towards something new, if you carry them through it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a chart adapts: it builds compensating skills around a limitation that stays, so that you keep participating. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows the less familiar capacity you develop by practice and feedback; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, the consolidated capacities you start with. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) to the North Node is a trine to the South, so Chiron here gets on with both: easy with what you know, and offering an opportunity towards what you're learning.

In plain words, your work-arounds widen your range. A method you built because the usual one failed you, a way of pacing yourself, an accommodation you learned to ask for: each can become a step towards the growth your chart points to. It's growth by addition, not replacement, with new responses joining the old ones rather than pushing them out.

The sextile still asks you to act. Because Chiron sits so easily with the South Node, it's tempting to keep your adaptations where they were born, useful and private. The opportunity is to let them travel.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A sextile places Chiron two signs from the North Node and four from the South Node, usually in compatible elements. With Chiron in Pisces, the North Node in Taurus and the South Node in Scorpio, adaptations made through imagination and compassion sit easily with an old habit of intensity, and can open the way to the calm, steady pleasures the North Node in Taurus asks for. Chiron moves forward and the node backwards, so the sextile comes in short windows of births, a few months each.

The houses show where the opening is, given a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron in the 4th with the North Node in the 6th, for instance, might let a home life adapted around a difficulty feed a new daily routine, with the quiet habits of the 12th as familiar ground.

About 1 person in 17 born between 1930 and 2009 has this sextile within 6°, counted with the true node. Charts drawn with the mean node can differ by a degree or two, which only matters at the edge of the orb ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

Chiron and the North Node at their four other angles:

- **[Chiron conjunct North Node](/astrology/chiron-conjunct-north-node/):** adaptation and growth at one point, the work-around becoming the way forward.
- **[Chiron square North Node](/astrology/chiron-square-north-node/):** the difficulty crossing both ends of the path, asking for room.
- **[Chiron trine North Node](/astrology/chiron-trine-north-node/):** the reverse balance, a flow towards growth and an opening back to the familiar.
- **[Chiron opposite North Node](/astrology/chiron-opposite-north-node/):** adapting as old, practised ground, with growth across the chart.

Between two charts, [Chiron sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-sextile-north-node-synastry/) is a friendly contact: one person's hard-won adaptations can give the other practical ideas for growing. It helps most when offered rather than prescribed, and I read it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), the North Node sextiles your natal Chiron twice in each 18.6-year round, for a month or two at a time. Born with the sextile, each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) recreates it exactly, while the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/) halfway between sets the transiting North Node trine your Chiron instead.

Around 50, the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/) brings Chiron back into sextile with your North Node, a season I'd use to ask which of your hard-won skills is ready to go somewhere new.

Weather, not a forecast: these seasons don't hand you the growth; they show you which tools you already have for it.
