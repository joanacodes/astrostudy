An opposition is two planets facing each other across the chart, as far apart as they can be. It's the aspect of the seesaw and the mirror: each end shows you what the other is missing.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

An opposition is an angle of 180°: six signs apart, in opposite signs — Aries and Libra, Taurus and Scorpio, Gemini and Sagittarius, Cancer and Capricorn, Leo and Aquarius, Virgo and Pisces. Opposite signs share a modality and a [polarity](/astrology/polarity/), and they join complementary elements, fire with air, earth with water. So an opposition isn't between strangers: it's two ends of one axis, each holding what the other leans away from. Planets in opposition often sit in opposite houses too: self and partner (1st and 7th), what's mine and what's shared (2nd and 8th), home and career (4th and 10th).

I read an opposition as a conversation across a table rather than a fight. Lean too far one way and the other end pulls you back. Very often you live one end yourself and meet the other in someone else, a partner, a boss, a friend who seems to have exactly what you lack, before you recognise both as yours. The work isn't choosing a side; it's holding both.

Astrologers allow a few degrees either side of exact, more with the Sun and Moon (see [orbs](/astrology/orbs/)), and many read an [applying opposition](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/), still building, as stronger than a separating one. Near the end of a sign, an opposition can fall between signs five apart, which share neither element nor modality: an [out-of-sign aspect](/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/).

Among the five major aspects, the opposition is one of Ptolemy's four, with the [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), the [square](/astrology/square/) and the [trine](/astrology/trine/); the [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), where the cycle between two planets begins, is a meeting rather than one of his four. With the square, it's one of the two [hard aspects](/astrology/hard-and-soft-aspects/). When both ends of an opposition square a third planet, astrologers call it a [T-square](/astrology/t-square/). Some oppositions are built into every chart: the North Node always faces the South Node, and when the [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) is known, the Ascendant faces the Descendant and the Midheaven faces the IC. And the sky makes one every month: at each [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/), the Sun and Moon stand opposite.

## Between which planets {#between-which-planets}

The Sun never opposes Mercury or Venus, which never stray more than 28° and 47° from it, and the two never get far enough from each other to oppose. These are the oppositions people look up most:

- **[Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/):** born at a full moon; what you want and what you need face each other and ask to be balanced.
- **[Venus opposite Mars](/astrology/venus-opposite-mars/):** drawn to people who are your opposite; love and desire learning to share the room.
- **[Moon opposite Saturn](/astrology/moon-opposite-saturn/):** a need for comfort that meets duty, or a demanding other; learning to give yourself what you look for.
- **[Sun opposite Saturn](/astrology/sun-opposite-saturn/):** self-belief tested against authority; confidence earned by standing your ground, sometimes against yourself.
- **[Moon opposite Pluto](/astrology/moon-opposite-pluto/):** feelings that run strong in close bonds; learning closeness without control.
- **[Sun opposite Jupiter](/astrology/sun-opposite-jupiter/):** big hopes that need a sense of proportion; a generosity that learns its limits.
- **[Venus opposite Pluto](/astrology/venus-opposite-pluto/):** all-or-nothing love; a pull towards intensity that asks for trust and boundaries.
- **[Mercury opposite Saturn](/astrology/mercury-opposite-saturn/):** a mind in conversation with doubt, often a critical inner voice; sound judgement in the end.
- **[Moon opposite Chiron](/astrology/moon-opposite-chiron/):** an old hurt often met through other people's needs; a gift for comfort learned the hard way.

My [notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) counted placements, each planet in its sign and house, so I can't give you a figure for oppositions. What I can say is that Saturn and Chiron were among the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time, and when either sits at one end of an opposition, I read both ends slowly.

## The opposition in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), an opposition between two charts is the pull of opposites: one person's planet sits across from the other's, so each holds what the other leans away from. It's often magnetic at first, and later a question of balance. [One person's Sun opposite the other's Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon-synastry/) often reads as attraction: two ends of one axis, each lighting up what the other needs. [Venus opposite Mars](/astrology/venus-opposite-mars-synastry/) is chemistry with a push and pull in it. [Saturn opposite the Moon](/astrology/moon-opposite-saturn-synastry/) can feel like structure to one person and distance to the other. I read every opposition in both directions, because each of you lives a different end; and, as with every synastry, only when both of you have agreed: [why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## The opposition in transits {#in-transits}

An opposition [transit](/astrology/transits/) is a planet in today's sky reaching the point across from one of yours: what you've been doing meets its other side, often through someone else. It tends to bring things into view rather than out of nowhere. [Transiting Saturn opposite your natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-sun/) reads as a season of review, when effort and responsibility face what you want; [Saturn opposite your natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-moon/), as a time when feelings meet duty; [Uranus opposite your natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-sun/), as a pull to change what no longer fits. The Moon opposes each of your planets once a month and the Sun once a year — about six months after your birthday, it faces your natal Sun — while the slow planets can stay for months, crossing the same degree up to three times.

Some oppositions keep a timetable. Saturn opposes its own place around 14 to 15 and again around 44 ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)), and the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) comes at about 42. Weather, not a forecast: an opposition shows you both ends of something, not which one wins.
