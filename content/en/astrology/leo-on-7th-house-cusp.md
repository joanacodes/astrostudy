The Descendant is the degree setting in the west when you were born, and the door of the 7th house: partners, close collaborators, the people you meet as equals. Leo on the 7th house cusp puts the sign of the heart at that door, so the person across from you tends to be someone who shines.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) describes what you look for in the person facing you ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and with [Leo](/astrology/leo/) there it's warmth that doesn't apologise for itself. You're drawn to people who are fully themselves: generous, demonstrative, at ease being looked at, able to turn an ordinary Tuesday into an occasion. I read the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) as a mirror as much as a door, and with this cusp it often shows the warmth and self-display you keep cooler in yourself; a partner who lives them openly gives you permission to do the same. In return you want to be admired as much as you admire, and a bond where only one of you gets the applause doesn't wear well. The 7th also holds open rivals and partners in work: you respect an opponent with flair who plays in the open, and in business you tend to pair up with someone who has presence while you keep the overview. None of this says who you'll meet, or when, or how long anything will last.

Opposite, [Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/) comes with every Leo Descendant, so Uranus and Saturn rule your chart, and I read both, while the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules your partnerships. Tradition puts the Sun in detriment in Aquarius and Saturn in detriment in Leo, so the planets that rule you and the one that rules your partners are each weakened in the other's sign. I don't read that as a mismatch: it's an axis between independence and devotion, and the 7th is where you learn the devotion. Where the Sun sits shows where partnerships take shape: as ruler of the 7th, in the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Born with the Sun in Leo too, you arrived around sunset, the Sun close to the Descendant itself.

This is Leo's rarest cusp. Placidus, which I use, gives a Leo Descendant to about 1 London birth in 21 and 1 New York birth in 17. The Descendant is simply the point opposite the Ascendant, so Leo sets while Aquarius rises, and Aquarius rises fast in the north: Leo, nearly three hours rising at London, takes only about 69 minutes to set. At London the 7th house of these charts is huge, between about 58° and 60°, and the whole of Virgo lies inside it in almost every one ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), with Pisces inside the 1st opposite. Mercury therefore gets a second say in your partnerships: the warmth you look for comes with an eye for detail, and a bond tends to work best when it's practical as well. From this cusp Leo never opens the 6th or the 8th too. At the other extreme, about 1 London chart in 7, and 1 New York chart in 11, give Leo no cusp at all.

With barely an hour and ten minutes a day at London, a Leo Descendant is easily missed by a rounded time. The pages on either side, [Leo on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-8th-house-cusp/) and [Leo on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/), describe what a birth a little earlier or a little later would have given. The Ascendant moves with it, so a different minute changes the frame of the whole chart, and I don't read one without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
