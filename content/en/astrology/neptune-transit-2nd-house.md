With Neptune in your 2nd house, the planet of ideals settles for years into the part of the chart that counts things: money, possessions, what you'd call security, and what you think you're worth. The usual stay is close to fifteen years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) idealises and inspires: it looks past the literal towards what something means. The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is resources and values, the income you rely on, the things you keep, the skills you could live from and the worth you feel underneath them. With Neptune crossing it, the material side of life can lose some of its hold. Values may turn towards meaning, beauty or giving; a way of earning that serves something larger may start to matter more than the figure attached to it; possessions can feel less like security and more like weight. At the same time money itself can get harder to keep track of, the practical face of the same softening.

With Jupiter in your 2nd from birth, the older ruler of Neptune's own sign, generosity can run ahead of the budget for a while; with Saturn there, the pass can soften a long fear of not having enough.

None of this foretells a loss, a windfall or a swindle. I don't read money in a [transit](/astrology/transits/), and no chart can tell you where to put your savings ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). What Neptune brings out is how your chart already values things, and it asks for imagination and clear accounts in equal measure.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Close to fifteen years is the middle for Neptune in the 2nd when the chart is drawn for London in Placidus, about a year above the figure for all houses there; the shorter stays last nearly nine years and the longer ones nearly twenty-two. New York charts keep much the same middle, just under fifteen, inside a tighter band of nearly eleven to about nineteen.

Those years count from Neptune's first crossing of your 2nd cusp, slow retrograde returns included, to its last day in the house, and how many of them you get depends on how much of the zodiac your 2nd spans. That span, and the cusp's degree, are set by your birth time, and a chart without one is a chart I don't open ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Neptune reaches the 2nd from your 1st, after years of softening how you present yourself ([Neptune transiting the 1st house](/astrology/neptune-transit-1st-house/)), and leaves it for your 3rd, where words and thinking take on the blur ([Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/)).

Earlier, now, next: Pisces had Neptune from April 2011 to January 2026, so a 2nd cusp in that sign has already had its turn. One in Aries is reached somewhere between March 2025 and March 2039, the span Neptune gives that sign after its back-and-forth over the Pisces border. One in Taurus, the 2nd's natural sign, waits until May 2038 or later.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep accounts you can read at a glance. Neptune in the 2nd isn't careless, but it does make numbers slippery, and a simple, regular look at what comes in and goes out keeps your ideals affordable.

Let your values change, and spend accordingly. If meaning matters more than possessions now, let your money follow it: a cause, a craft, time bought back.

Before you lend, give or invest, ask one practical question of the person or the plan, and wait for a plain answer. Generosity stays generous when it's chosen with open eyes.
