Work that feels like play: that's the promise of the 6th's ruler in the 5th. The tasks of an ordinary day go where you have fun, make things and fall for people.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your daily work answers to a planet that lives, in this chart, among pleasures. The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is where the week's chores and habits live, and the planet ruling its cusp sits in the [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/), where you create, play and fall in love ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The 6th's business takes on the 5th's mood: you work best at what you'd happily do for nothing.

That can look like a craft turned into a living, a job in the arts, in games, sport or entertainment, or simply a way of making any job more fun than it was designed to be. Teaching an art, coaching a team or running workshops can suit it, since the work there is helping others enjoy something. Colleagues often become playmates, and love can grow out of shared work, a project, a rehearsal, a kitchen at closing time. The hazard is boredom. When the fun goes out of a task, motivation can go with it, and a dull routine feels heavier than hard work. Passion projects can also swallow evenings that nobody pays for.

From the 5th, the 6th can't be seen: next-door houses share no aspect ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)). I read that as work that doesn't feel like work: you may not notice how much effort you're putting in, which is a gift until you're tired and can't say why. Venus is a special case here: it rules the 6th when Taurus or Libra is on the cusp, and [Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house/) rejoices ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), in the house once named Good Fortune. Beauty enters the work itself, and the house of toil hands its business to the house of joy.

Uranus in the 5th wants work that stays interesting, hours that suit you and room to experiment. Uranus and Saturn split the 6th between them when Aquarius is on the cusp ([Aquarius on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/); [traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and I read both: Saturn keeps the deadlines that Uranus would rather forget.

The 5th's borders move with the clock. Born a little later, a ruler close to the 5th cusp would fall back into the 4th, where the working day happens at home ([Ruler of the 6th in the 4th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)); born a little earlier, one close to the 6th cusp would sit in the 6th itself, where routine is the point ([Ruler of the 6th in the 6th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)). The recorded time settles it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Neither the 6th's old illness nor the 5th's old children belong in this reading. If work you love starts costing you sleep or calm, it's worth talking to someone qualified to help ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)); no chart can foretell your health ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and the placement holds no promise about children either ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)).
