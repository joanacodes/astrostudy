Where most Suns ask *who am I?*, a Libra Sun tends to ask *who am I, in relation to this?* Libra is the sign tradition calls the Sun's fall, and the self it describes comes into focus through proportion: through what it finds fair, fitting and well made.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A chart's [Sun](/astrology/sun/) holds its identity, vitality and direction together. [Libra](/astrology/libra/), cardinal air ruled by [Venus](/astrology/venus/), lends it a manner built on judgement and proportion. Your sense of self may rest less on what you want than on what you think is right, fitting or beautiful, and many people with a Libra Sun find that their taste, in a room, a sentence or a decision, is the most reliable portrait of who they are.

Tradition calls Libra the Sun's [fall](/astrology/fall/), the sign opposite its exaltation in Aries. Ptolemy's reason was the season again: at the September equinox the Sun crosses into the southern half of the sky, and in the northern hemisphere the days begin to shorten. I don't read a fallen Sun as a dimmer person. I read a self whose agency works through weighing and consulting rather than deciding alone, and which knows, perhaps better than any other Sun, that acting from your centre doesn't mean pushing anyone else out of theirs.

The Sun is in Libra for 30 or 31 days, from about 23 September to 22 October, and about 1 person in 12 is born under it. Within a day of the equinox, or of the change into Scorpio, you'll need your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/).

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

With a partner, the [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) tends to offer help and the [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) depth; the Libra Sun offers fairness, and hopes for it back. You may become the one who holds the shape of a relationship: its rituals, its shared home, the way the two of you are together in company. The thing to watch is agreeing in the moment and disagreeing for weeks afterwards, in private. Harmony with room for an argument lasts better than peace kept by silence, and the right partner wants to hear your *no* as much as your *yes*.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that needs a fair judgement, and needs to be seen as fair, suits this Sun: mediating, designing, negotiating, curating, building a team where every contribution counts. You often see how a thing could be balanced before anyone else does. Purpose sharpens around a principle, fairness to a client, proportion in a design, and blurs when you're asked to take sides for no good reason. Recognition, for a Libra Sun, often means being trusted by people who disagree with each other.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The difficulty is knowing what you think before you've heard what everyone else thinks. A Libra Sun can become a very good mirror: attentive, tactful, quick to find the middle, and slow to notice that its own preference went missing somewhere along the way. Deferred wishes don't disappear. They turn into tiredness, or into a resentment that surprises you when it finally speaks. And a decision put off is often a way of not disappointing anyone, you included.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Decide one small thing a day entirely alone, and don't consult anyone about it afterwards. In a conversation, say your preference before you ask for theirs. Keep a practice that belongs to your own sense of proportion, a garden, a wardrobe, a piece of music, so your taste has somewhere to live that isn't negotiated. And read your Venus, by sign and house, for what this Sun finds worth balancing for.

Libra likes to hear the other side, so here it is. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign held about a third of the time, in the same company as the rising sign, Venus and Mercury, while Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90%. A Libra Sun is worth reading, and worth weighing against the rest of the chart.
