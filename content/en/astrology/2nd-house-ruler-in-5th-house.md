Pleasure and money keep meeting here: a ruler of the 2nd in the 5th ties what you earn and what you value to play, creativity, romance and the things you make for the joy of it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Each house has a ruler, the planet of the sign on its cusp ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and the one ruling your [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/), money, belongings and the sense of your own worth, sits in the [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/), the house of what you do for joy: creating, playing, performing, flirting, falling in love, and the risks you take for fun.

Your resources tend to follow your pleasures, in both directions. Money can come from what you'd do anyway: the craft, the stage, the kitchen, the hobby that grew into a trade. And it goes, readily, on what delights you: a concert, a good meal, an instrument, a present that makes someone's face light up. What you value is joy itself, and a life with money but no pleasure tends to feel poor to you. Counted by sign, the 5th stands square to the 2nd, and you may know the friction: what you'd like to keep and what you'd like to enjoy pull at each other, and a budget can feel like a cage. The skill is spending on pleasure without spending your security.

With Taurus or Libra on the 2nd cusp, Venus rules your money from its joy, the 5th ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), the house tradition called Good Fortune: taste is your capital, and beauty is worth paying for. Horary astrology also filed gambling and speculation under the 5th, alongside games and places of entertainment. A ruler of the 2nd here doesn't tell you to bet, or promise a win; no chart can pick a stake or an investment ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

The 5th was also the old house of children. Whether children come into your life is not something a chart can say ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I read this placement in an adult's life, for your own pleasures and creations.

Sunk a few hours past its setting, the ruler of your money was below the western horizon and still going down when you were born; Placidus, the house system I use, files that part of the sky under the 5th. Its cusps depend on the minute of birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): further down, in the 4th, your money would turn towards home and family ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 4th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)); a little higher, towards the Descendant, in the 6th, it would come from the daily round of work ([Ruler of the 2nd in the 6th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)).

Pisces on the cusp splits the rule between Jupiter and Neptune, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 5th spends generously on joy and on other people, while Neptune, from its own spot in the chart, can make art, music or a cause the thing you value most.

What this placement describes is a way of valuing: money, for you, is worth having for what it lets you enjoy and make.
