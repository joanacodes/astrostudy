Uranus's two squares to your Venus bracket its crossing, about twenty years on either side. Each time, for some twenty months, what you love and the room you need stop fitting together without effort.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs attraction, reciprocity and value: who and what you want, what feels fair, what you find beautiful. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) wants enough freedom to try something else. In a [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, two functions keep catching on each other until something adapts, and this is the rub that [Venus square Uranus](/astrology/venus-square-uranus/) describes in a birth chart: closeness and air, wanted at once.

In practice the familiar can lose its shine. A routine of affection feels rehearsed, a friendship feels narrower than it was, and someone or something quite unlike your usual taste becomes interesting. Inside a couple, one or both people may want more independence: separate interests, different hours, a looser arrangement, or simply more honesty about what each needs.

I don't read this square as love running out, and no transit decides whether a bond lasts ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). Values can be part of it too, because Venus measures what's worth having. Spending on impulse is a common way of easing the itch, and no transit tells me what your money will do ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

With Venus in your 7th and Uranus crossing your 10th ([Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/)), changes in your working life press on your partnership: new hours, a new direction, and a relationship asked to make room for them. The 7th and the 10th both come from your birth time, and without it this square isn't something I read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, the square holds for about twenty months, and the shortest passes take about eleven. Retrograde motion brings Uranus back over the exact square in more than nine passes out of ten, so most people meet it three times: enough to try a new arrangement, look at it again, and keep what works.

Counting from [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-venus/), the first square comes at about twenty years, after [transiting Uranus sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-venus/) and before [transiting Uranus trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-venus/). The second comes about twenty years before Uranus is back at the conjunction point, with [transiting Uranus opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-venus/) between the two. Of the two squares, a life of 80 years usually holds both; none of the births counted from 1930 to 2009 holds neither, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) is one most people live through twice.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tell restlessness from incompatibility. A bond that feels too small may need a new shape rather than an ending, and the square is better at asking that question than answering it.

Renegotiate time together and apart. Give each other one evening a week with no explanation owed, and see what it changes.

Wait a month before a big purchase. Under this square the urge for something new can fasten on things; if it still delights you four weeks later, it's yours.
