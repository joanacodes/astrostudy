Tradition's two benefics meet in your chart once every twelve years or so, when Jupiter in the sky reaches the degree of your natal Venus. No transit has been sold more often as luck in love and money, and few are better read as something quieter: a season when what you value gets room to grow.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) evaluates: what attracts you, what you prefer, what feels fair in the give and take of a bond. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens things and gives them meaning. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act together, so while it lasts, liking something and wanting to understand or share it arrive as one impulse.

It often shows as appreciation. You notice beauty more, give praise more freely, enjoy company, want to make, learn or keep something that pleases you. Relationships may feel more generous in both directions. The watchpoint belongs to two planets that both like a yes: pleasure isn't indulgence, and Jupiter on Venus can make too much of a good thing feel like exactly the right amount.

Astrologers called these two the [benefics](/astrology/benefics-and-malefics/), Venus the lesser and Jupiter the greater, and that is where the reputation comes from. I take the old word as a description of what they do, warming and widening, not of what they deliver. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) can't tell you whether you'll fall in love or make money, and I don't read it for either ([Can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

Because a conjunction puts Jupiter in your Venus's own house, that house sets the scene. With Venus in the 7th, the meeting comes during [Jupiter transiting the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-7th-house/), and partnership is where the generosity shows; with Venus in the 2nd, it's more about what you value and choose to keep. Born with [Venus conjunct Jupiter](/astrology/venus-conjunct-jupiter/), you'll have it at each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, most visits, about seven in ten, are a single pass of about eleven days. In the rest, Jupiter's retrograde brings it back over your Venus, and the meeting runs to about eight months with three exact hits.

Venus never moves more than about 47° from the Sun, so this conjunction comes within about a year and a half of [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-sun/): what you value gets its widening close to the season when your sense of self gets its own. Venus moves little more than a degree a day, so the dates come from your birth date; the house comes from your birth time, and without the house I don't read the transit ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Counted for births from 1930 to 2009, the meeting comes six or seven times by the age of 80, so by middle age most people have felt their Venus widened like this three or four times. In the years after each meeting, Jupiter keeps coming back to your Venus at other angles: [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-venus/) for pleasures you choose, [transiting Jupiter square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-venus/) for appetite tested against value, [transiting Jupiter trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-venus/) for warmth that flows without asking, and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-venus/) for other people's tastes and terms.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Spend the generosity on people and things you'd still value without the glow: friends, making something, learning an art you've admired from a distance.

Say thank you, specifically. Venus is reciprocity as much as pleasure, and this conjunction tends to make appreciation flow both ways once someone starts it.

Sleep on large purchases and large promises. Two benefics together make yes feel natural; the question is whether you'd say it in an ordinary week.
