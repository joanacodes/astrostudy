Neither of you has to be stubborn for the relationship to dig its heels in. Composite Lilith in Taurus describes a bond whose untamed part is its own pace and its own pleasures: what it won't let anyone hurry, price or improve.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) puts each body at the midpoint of its two natal places, and the composite [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) marks what the relationship keeps to itself and won't have tamed. [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), fixed earth ruled by [Venus](/astrology/venus/), gives that refusal weight. A bond with it tends to guard its comforts: the long breakfast nobody else is invited to, a home arranged exactly to its taste, a way of spending its weekends that it never explains. Pressure from outside, a parent's plan for the holidays, a friend's verdict on the flat, meets a calm and immovable no.

That weight belongs to the pair, not to either person. Two quick, adaptable people can make a relationship that refuses to be rushed, and find themselves defending its rhythm with a firmness neither shows alone. What the bond hides is often how much it enjoys itself: the pleasure it takes in its routines, the senses it shares, the worth it quietly sets on being together. It rarely says so, because saying so would invite someone to put a price on it.

About 1 pair in 12 has composite Lilith in Taurus, in a count of two million pairs of birth dates from 1930 to 2009, neither more nor less than any other sign. Two people born within one of mean Lilith's Taurus stays, June 1985 to February 1986 or April 1994 to January 1995, for instance, share it. The midpoint can also land here when neither of you has it anywhere near. Born in June 1975, one person has Lilith at about 14° Pisces; born in March 1978, the other has it at about 6° Cancer; the short way between them crosses the start of the zodiac, and their composite Lilith sits at about 10° Taurus.

In a single birth chart, [Lilith in Taurus](/astrology/lilith-in-taurus/) is a person's quiet claim to their own ground. The composite turns that claim into the pair's, and each of you may feel it as the other's obstinacy. On one side, [composite Lilith in Aries](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-aries/) refuses by moving first; on the other, [composite Lilith in Gemini](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-gemini/) refuses by talking its way round.

Its difficulty is inertia. A bond that won't be hurried can also refuse a change it needs, simply because the change was someone else's idea, and it can hold its comforts so close that friends feel kept at the gate. It helps to name together what you are protecting, and now and then to choose a change that comes from inside the pair rather than from outside it.
