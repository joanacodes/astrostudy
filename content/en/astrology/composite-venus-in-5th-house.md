Board games with house rules nobody else understands, a band that plays two gigs a year, a dress-up dinner on an ordinary Thursday: composite Venus in the 5th house sets a relationship's pleasures where tradition says Venus is happiest.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The 5th was where Venus had its joy, the house in which tradition said it rejoiced ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), and the old books called it Good Fortune. [The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is succedent and takes Leo as its natural sign; it covers play, romance, creativity, art and recreation. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [Venus](/astrology/venus/) can be placed in it only when both birth times are in hand, and then by one of two methods, cusp midpoints or houses drawn from the composite Midheaven for where you live, which can disagree about a Venus near the edge ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Venus in its joy makes pleasure the bond's natural language. It enjoys making things for their own sake, a garden designed for colour rather than yield, a song written for no audience, and it treats romance, where there is romance, as something to keep inventing rather than a stage that passed. Friends and creative partners can have this placement as fully as lovers, and creative work suits it well: two people who write, cook, sing or build together often find the making becomes part of the affection. In one birth chart, [Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house/) makes romance and play come easily; [their Venus in your 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house-synastry/) brings out your own sense of fun; the composite's pleasures are the ones only the two of you make.

The composite Sun normally lies within 47° of Venus here, for roughly 9 pairs in 10, as it would in any sky. Two people born many months apart can see the midpoints separate instead, and in about 1 pair in 22 the composite Sun stands near the point across from this Venus, over in the 11th house of friends ([composite Sun in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-11th-house/)). The arithmetic makes that split; the two of you didn't.

A placement this comfortable can coast. The pleasures stay the same because they still work, the jokes are the old jokes, and the two of you stop surprising each other without noticing. It helps to take turns choosing something neither of you has tried; novelty isn't the point, attention is. Tradition also gave this house children, and whether two people will have any is a question no chart answers ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)); I never read a child's chart either ([Children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). Here the 5th describes the bond's delight, nothing more.

[Composite Venus in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-4th-house/) takes its pleasures at home, [composite Venus in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-6th-house/) in work done side by side. The composite isn't part of my practice; its users set the 5th beside the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and the natal charts of both people. Pleasure taken together includes agreeing together: nobody's birth data goes into a composite without their yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
