At the bottom of every chart sits the IC, where the zodiac crosses the meridian below the horizon. When Pluto reaches it, a long season begins in the most private part of your life, the part most people show to only a few.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) digs, and what it finds it won't put back unexamined. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home and roots: the family you came from, the place you live, the private self that only a few people see, and the foundations everything else in the chart stands on. With Pluto here for years, the family story tends to come up for review. A rule nobody ever said aloud, a loyalty you took on without choosing it, a way of keeping the peace at home: these become visible, and once they're visible you can decide whether they still belong to you. Home itself may start to feel different, less a place you were given and more a place you make.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 4th begins at the IC, so the stay opens with Pluto on that point; a natal Moon in the 4th is met later, and asked what nourishes you beyond the habits of the house you grew up in.

Some books forecast losses or upheavals at home during this transit. I don't. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) works on what your own chart describes, and Pluto in the 4th predicts nothing about your parents' health or lives; it describes your relationship with your origins, reworked slowly from the inside.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 4th is one of the steadiest houses for Pluto. Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude give it about 19 years in the middle, and most stays fall between about 17 and 28, a far tighter range than the 10 to 37 years Pluto spends in a house across all twelve, whose middle is about 20. At New York's latitude the middle is about 20 years, with most stays between about 16 and 29.

The IC that opens this stay is set by your birth time, like the cusp that closes it, so without the time I can place neither, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Pluto comes here from the house of words and daily exchanges ([Pluto transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-3rd-house/)) and goes on to the house of pleasure, creation and play ([Pluto transiting the 5th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-5th-house/)).

Since March 2023 Pluto has been in Aquarius, its last step back into Capricorn over by November 2024, and it will still be there when my figures stop, at the end of 2039. An IC in Aquarius therefore puts you in this stay now or soon, and the exact degree gives the year. An IC in Capricorn had it between January 2008 and November 2024; an IC in Pisces, not before 2040.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look at what you inherited the way you'd look at furniture in a house you've just moved into: some of it you'll keep, some you'll give away, some you'll repaint. Pluto in the 4th gives you time to decide piece by piece.

Make one corner of your life truly private, a place or a habit that is only yours. This transit does its work in the quiet, and it needs somewhere quiet to do it.

If the family story you're uncovering is painful, you don't have to carry it alone or all at once. Talking with someone you trust, or someone trained to help, can make the years lighter.
