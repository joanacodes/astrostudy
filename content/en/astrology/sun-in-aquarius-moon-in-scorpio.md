Your Sun thinks in the open, about what would be better for everyone; your Moon works below the surface, where it needs to know what lies underneath. Your birth came around the last quarter, under a half moon that rises near midnight, and the pair carries a distinction no other has: detriment for the Sun and fall for the Moon, both at once.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aquarius and Scorpio are both fixed, air and water, with Scorpio three signs before Aquarius: a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign, each sign holding its position. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) bases its identity on reason, fairness and the long view, on what could be improved for everyone; the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) finds its calm at the bottom of a feeling, staying there until it understands it, and it trusts slowly. You can discuss almost anything in general and still take a long time to tell one person how you feel.

Ptolemy places the Moon's [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Scorpio, and the Sun's [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) is Aquarius, across from Leo. I read both as effort, never as a lesser self or a smaller heart: ease takes work here, and the work makes you unusually good at hard conversations, in principle and in private. Each sign has two rulers, and for each I read both, four planets in all. Aquarius has [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/); Scorpio has [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/). The modern pair moves slowly, Uranus about seven years to a sign and Pluto twelve to thirty-one, so their signs belong to a generation and their houses to you; Saturn and Mars change sign far more often and speak about you in particular.

The Moon was 240° to 298° on from the Sun, the lunar month 19½ to 24½ days old, and these births split evenly between the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) and the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/): the hour can put you in either. Near 270° the lights make a waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/), friction I read as worked on inwardly, by reviewing and letting go rather than by pushing, and two fixed signs need that kind of square more than most. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) goes into the difference between the two phases.

One person in roughly 144 has this pair, the Sun's detriment and the Moon's fall together. When the Moon changed sign on the day of your birth, coming in from Libra or leaving for Sagittarius, only the hour can say whether this page applies, or the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-libra/), or the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-sagittarius/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)); the Moon needs about two and a half days to cross a sign. The hour also places all four rulers in their houses, and that is most of the reading here.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love loyally and intensely, and you need room to breathe inside that love. The Scorpio Moon wants depth, honesty and proof that trust is safe; the Aquarius Sun wants friendship, equality and space to think. A partner may meet the cool, reasonable side long before the depth of what you feel. Show a little of the depth earlier than feels comfortable, and say when you need distance, so the space isn't taken for a verdict.

## At work {#at-work}

Research, investigation, crisis work, data, psychology, the reform of institutions from the inside: you see how a whole system works and where it hides its problems. The Aquarius Sun designs the fairer version; the Scorpio Moon has the stamina to stay with what's difficult until it changes. You do less well anywhere you're asked to look away from what you've noticed.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two fixed signs at a square can both refuse to move: a conviction on one side, a hurt on the other, each able to last for years. You may explain your position calmly while something underneath stays unresolved, or keep a grievance alive for the sake of a principle. What helps is the last quarter's work, as I read it: review, sort, decide what can go. Let one old thing go each season, on purpose, and notice how much room it leaves.
