Sun trine Moon belongs to the gibbous moon, the one that is more than half lit and a few days from full, on its way there or on its way back. Born under it, you have the two lights 120° apart, usually in signs of one element, and a chart in which purpose and comfort keep each other company.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your centre: identity, purpose, the vitality you bring to what you do. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) responds and regulates: what you feel, what you need, the habits that keep you safe. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates them through flow, so that each reinforces the other and neither has to give way.

In plain words, the life you're building and the life you need tend to be the same life. When you set out to do something, your feelings usually come along; when you stop, your purpose waits without sulking. You don't often have to choose between being yourself and feeling safe.

I read it as coherence, a word that turns up in the definitions of both the Sun and the trine: what you show and what you feel tend to match. That ease is real, and it isn't automatic. A trine describes a flow between two functions, not a guarantee that nothing will disturb them, and flow isn't passivity either: the ally is there, but it works best when you put it to work.

The gibbous phase gives it a direction. Waxing, with the Moon four signs ahead of the Sun and the full moon a few days off ([waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/)), I read the ease as still gathering strength. Waning, with the Moon four signs behind and the full moon just past ([waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/)), I read it as ease you hand on: what you've found, shared.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Because a trine usually joins signs of one element, your Sun and Moon share a temperament. A Sun in Gemini with the Moon in Libra, a waxing trine, both run on air: ideas, exchange, people. A Sun in Capricorn with the Moon in Virgo, a waning one, both run on earth: what's real and what's useful. The houses show where the agreement is most at home, the Sun's house where you aim and the Moon's where you rest, with an easy road between them.

About 1 person in 11 has the Sun and Moon within 8° of a trine, which the sky makes twice a month, before and after the full moon. The tighter it is, the more of the chart it steadies. The Moon is the one moving, about 13° a day, so a birth a few hours earlier or later tightens or loosens it. My [notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) counted placements one by one, not aspects, so it holds no figure for this trine.

The other aspects of the pair are other days of the same month:

- **[Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/):** about ten days from your phase, the new moon; agreement there comes from standing in one place, not from two places that rhyme.
- **[Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/):** the crescent; the two get along there too, but their help has to be asked for.
- **[Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/):** the half moon, a couple of days from yours; the same lights, in signs that clash instead of rhyming.
- **[Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/):** the full moon your gibbous moon is growing into, or leaving behind; agreement turns into a face-to-face.

Between two charts, [Sun trine Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-moon-synastry/) gives two people a version of this agreement: one brings the direction, the other the needs, and each tends to find the other easy company. I read it from both sides, since comforting and being comforted don't feel the same.

## In transits {#in-transits}

The trines the sky makes to your Sun and Moon are among the gentlest transits a chart gets. Twice a month the Moon trines your natal Sun, for a few hours; twice a year the Sun does the same to your natal Moon, for a couple of days ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). None of them asks anything of you, which is exactly why they're worth noticing: they show you, in small, what your trine does all the time. Weather, not a forecast: a mild, cooperative mood, nothing more.
