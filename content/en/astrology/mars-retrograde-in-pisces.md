Mars’s two shortest retrogrades in the dates I use both fall in Pisces: 60 days in 2003 and 61 in 2035, thirty-two years apart, each beginning in late summer. Between them they cover roughly the first and last thirds of the sign and leave its middle, from about 10° to 17½°, untouched.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) is mutable water, ruled by Neptune in modern tropical astrology and by Jupiter in the tradition; I read both. Mars there acts on instinct and feeling, and often fights hardest for someone else. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when Mars seems from Earth to stop and slip back for about two months, that drive turns to its own currents: the sacrifice made without being asked, the cause taken up because someone was hurting, the want you set aside for what others needed. When anger comes in Pisces it can arrive as sadness, and these weeks may show whose feeling it was. Desire longs to merge; the review asks where you end and others begin.

In 2003, Mars stationed at 10°08′ Pisces on 29 July and came to rest at 0°07′ Pisces on 27 September, seven minutes of arc from Aquarius ([Mars retrograde in Aquarius](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-aquarius/)). The list I work from files it under Aquarius as well, but with both stations in Pisces, Mars never crossed back between them. It faced the Sun, then in Virgo, at the end of August, in a year when Mars came very close to Earth. The next runs from 15 August to 15 October 2035, wholly in Pisces, from 28°26′ to 17°45′; its first station falls within about a degree and a half of Aries ([Mars retrograde in Aries](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-aries/)).

If you were born with Mars retrograde in Pisces, that is a lifelong placement rather than this late-summer weather, and I read it on its own ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Pisces occupies depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); without it I don’t read a chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, a loop of about 10° usually stays within one house, unless a cusp falls inside it.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/pisces-rising/):** Pisces rising: a gentle, receptive first impression; the way you take in a room before acting, noticed.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/pisces-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you part with easily; effort spent on other people’s needs, weighed against your own.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/pisces-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** words chosen to soothe; a truth softened for a sibling or neighbour, said more clearly.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home that takes everyone in; the edges of your household, redrawn kindly.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/pisces-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romance and art made from feeling; an idealised attraction, seen as it is.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work as service; tasks done for others until nothing is left for you, handed back.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Pisces on the Descendant, Virgo on the Ascendant: a partner you want to rescue, or be rescued by; who carries whom, talked through.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/):** merging and trust; a closeness in which you lost track of yourself, looked at tenderly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/):** faith and far horizons; a belief followed on instinct, examined.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Pisces on the Midheaven: a vocation of care or imagination; the effort it costs, measured.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/pisces-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends and causes that move you; a group you gave yourself to, met with clearer limits.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/pisces-on-12th-house-cusp/):** Pisces’s natural house, tied by tradition to confinement and never a forecast in my reading ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)); feelings that belong to no one in particular, sorted in quiet.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pisces moves like water, so I find these weeks suit gentle effort: swimming, music, a long sleep, time by the sea or a river, anything that lets drive flow without a fight. Then, once, name something you want for yourself. Jupiter and Neptune rule Pisces, and their houses in your chart, with that of your natal Mars, show where the review is likely to take you.

None of this foretells a loss or a rescue. It describes a short season in which a drive that usually serves others asks to be heard.
