For about a year and a half, the North Node backs through your 5th house, where you keep what you do for joy, and the South Node backs through your 11th, where friends and shared plans live. The stretch runs between the group's project and your own pleasure, and these months favour the pleasure.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to what grows with practice, and the [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is creativity, play, romance and the way you show yourself when nobody is grading you. Practising delight can sound like a contradiction, yet that's the work: taking up the instrument again, writing the thing you'd write if no one read it, flirting, dancing badly, saying yes to an afternoon with no use.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in your 11th knows the other way of living: belonging to a group, serving a cause, being a good friend to many, often at the cost of your own wants. Those bonds stay. The question is whether there's a version of you outside them.

The 5th's old name was Good Fortune, and Venus had its joy there. A natal Venus in the 5th tends to make pleasure easier to accept when the node reaches it; a natal Saturn there can make play feel like a skill you're finally allowed to learn.

The old books also put children in this house. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) doesn't predict a pregnancy ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and this site never reads a child's chart ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)): what I read is the adult's own joy.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the true node usually spends about a year and a half in the 5th, the same middle as the average house, with most stays between about 11 months and 2 years and 5 months. At New York's latitude the middle doesn't move, but the short end lengthens to about 13 months and the long end shortens to about 2 years, a narrower range than the 2nd, 8th or 11th give there.

Those stays follow the true node and its wobble: a return over the cusp within 120 days doesn't end them. The mean node, a degree or two away, tells nearly the same story; I look at both ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

How wide your 5th is depends on your birth time, and so do its cusps, which is why the time comes first for me ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node comes into the 5th from your 6th ([North Node transiting the 6th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-6th-house/)) and continues into your 4th ([North Node transiting the 4th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-4th-house/)).

From July 2026 to March 2028 the node is in Aquarius, with the South Node in Leo, so a 5th house holding Aquarius has its stay under way or close. Each following sign comes about eighteen months later, going backwards: Capricorn from March 2028, Sagittarius from September 2029, Scorpio from March 2031, Libra from December 2032 and Virgo from June 2034. Leo, the 5th's natural sign, has the North Node from November 2035 to May 2037, then Cancer to February 2039, and Gemini after that.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Book time for something you do purely because you like it, and protect it the way you'd protect a meeting.

Make one thing with your name on it: a song, a meal, a garden bed, a story. Finished is better than impressive.

Stay a good friend without being only a good friend. The South Node in your 11th will still answer the group chat; let it wait an hour.
