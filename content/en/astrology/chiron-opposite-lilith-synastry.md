Half the zodiac, give or take 3°, separates the Chiron in one chart from the Black Moon Lilith in another when two people have Chiron opposite Lilith in synastry. One of you makes room for what won't change; the other turns down whatever tries to tame them. From opposite ends of the zodiac, each sees the stance the other doesn't take.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) shows where a person built a workable way of taking part despite a limitation that stays: patience, compensation, a skill nobody else needed. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the mean apogee of the Moon, is read by many astrologers as the autonomy and instinct a person guards, and the parts of them kept out of view; readings vary, so I keep it symbolic. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions up as a polarity, where each throws the other into relief; as one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the two poles live in two different people.

Holding the Chiron, you meet someone who refuses what you've learned to accept. Their stance can look like a challenge to the way you've coped, and, on a good day, like a glimpse of a refusal you never allowed yourself.

Holding the Lilith, you meet someone who accommodates what you'd never tolerate. Their patience can read as a quiet reproach, or as an option you'd stopped believing in: getting through by bending, without giving yourself away.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Seesaws tip, though this one never turns two people into enemies. Around each other, the Chiron person can become the reasonable one, endlessly accommodating, and the Lilith person the difficult one, refusing on principle, each exaggerating a role because the other has taken its opposite. Polarity isn't contradiction: the pair is at its best when each lets the other's stance be a resource rather than an accusation.

- **[Chiron conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/chiron-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** the two ends folded onto one degree, with no seesaw, only a blur.
- **[Chiron sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/chiron-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** a light exchange where nobody has to take a side.
- **[Chiron square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/chiron-square-lilith-synastry/):** the same pair meeting at a corner instead of face to face.
- **[Chiron trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/chiron-trine-lilith-synastry/):** coping and refusing already in step, with nothing to balance.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Trade a little of each stance, on purpose. The Chiron person tries one refusal; the Lilith person tries one accommodation; each watches the other's attempt kindly. In a couple, that might be a weekend run the Lilith way; between friends or colleagues, a task done the other's way once; across generations, an adult grandchild and a grandparent comparing what each refused and what each made do with.

The examples show how far apart in age the two people can be. Someone born in mid-2005 has Lilith in Leo, and anyone born between January 2006 and January 2007 has Chiron in Aquarius, opposite it. Chiron takes about fifty years to come round, and it crossed the same stretch of Aquarius in spells of 1955 and 1956, so people born then meet that 2005 Lilith in the same opposition. A contact someone shares with a younger friend and with their grandparents' generation is a contact of eras, which is why my count of a synastry leaves it out: that count only admits contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. What I read instead are the houses at either end.

In my notebook, read one chart at a time by sign and house, Chiron and Lilith both held 75 to 90% of the time, alongside Saturn ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)); it never counted a contact between two people, so this opposition gets no figure from it.

Over a whole day, neither end travels far enough to matter, so the birth time doesn't decide the opposition; it decides the two houses the seesaw sits in, and I wait for both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Chiron opposite Lilith](/astrology/chiron-opposite-lilith/), in one chart, is someone who adapts in one area of life and won't bend in another. Split between two charts, the axis has two owners, and neither end gets read without its owner's yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
