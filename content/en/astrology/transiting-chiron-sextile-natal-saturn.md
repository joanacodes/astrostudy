At its closest to the Sun, Chiron slips just inside the orbit of Saturn. It was last there in 1996, the stretch of its round when it moves fastest through the zodiac, crossing Libra in two years. Its sextile to your natal Saturn is a gentler meeting of the two: an offer of help in building something that can carry an old difficulty.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) organises: it sets the routine, keeps the commitment and builds what will still stand in ten years. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the part of you that adapts around a limitation that hasn't gone away. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through cooperation that needs a first step, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) structure and adaptation are ready to work together if you set them going.

The opening is usually practical and unglamorous. A routine that fits your energy rather than someone else's. A way of organising the week around the difficulty instead of pretending it isn't there. A framework you'd resisted, a training plan, a timetable, a professional's method, that turns out to make the difficulty lighter to carry. Recovering a usable way of functioning, step by step, is one of Chiron's roles, and Saturn is the planet that knows how steps add up.

Saturn's sign is shared by everyone born within about two and a half years, so your classmates meet this sextile within months of you. For an individual chart, the date says little; what I read is the house your Saturn sits in, the personal planets it touches, and what else is moving in the chart that year ([generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/)).

With [Saturn in the 6th house](/astrology/saturn-in-6th-house/), where the daily round of work sits, Chiron is usually crossing your 4th ([Chiron transiting the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-4th-house/)): a routine that suits you can start at home. Your 4th begins at the IC, a point only the time of birth can place; I don't read the sextile without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you were born with [Saturn sextile Chiron](/astrology/saturn-sextile-chiron/), this cooperation is already in your chart; the transit gives it a season of use.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A routine takes repetition, and the sextile usually offers about ten months of it within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after: three exact crossings in nearly two passes out of three. A single crossing of some three weeks, about a third of passes, is barely time to start; the rare five-crossing passes, about one in twenty, give some twenty-one months to make a routine stick.

In the site's count the first sextile came at about 16 in the middle and as late as about 40: around 5 for someone born at the start of 1975, 7 for 1995, 10 for 1985, 11 for 1965, 16 for 1935, 27 for 2005, 32 for 1945 and 40 for 1955. Three by 80 is the usual tally; some people have two, others four. One comes after [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-saturn/) and before [transiting Chiron square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-saturn/); the other after [transiting Chiron opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-saturn/), [transiting Chiron trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-saturn/) and the second square.

Over the coming years, while Chiron crosses Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, it sextiles Saturn in Cancer, as for most births from August 1973 to June 1976, and Saturn in Pisces, as for most births from May 1993 to April 1996.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Design one routine around your real energy, not the energy you think you ought to have.

Accept the framework you've been avoiding, for a trial month, and judge it by results.

Break a long goal into steps small enough to keep on a bad day.

Ask a professional for a plan rather than a pep talk.
