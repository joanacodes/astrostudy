A map on the kitchen wall with pins in places neither of you has been yet, an evening course taken together, a running argument about what life is for that neither of you wants to win: composite Venus in Sagittarius finds a relationship's pleasure in widening its world.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) works by midpoints, and its [Venus](/astrology/venus/), halfway between the two natal Venuses, describes the relationship's tastes and what it treasures. [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire ruled by [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), gives Venus neither dignity nor debility; it gives it range. In tradition's division of [benefics and malefics](/astrology/benefics-and-malefics/), Jupiter and Venus stand together on the generous side, Jupiter ranked first, and here Venus borrows its manner from Jupiter's sign. The bond values discovery, meaning and perspective, and its affection often comes as encouragement: a ticket bought for the other's dream, a book that changed one of you handed to the other.

If one of you hates flying and the other has never left their home county, the relationship can still be the one with the atlas open, and what it travels into may as well be a language, a faith or a field of study as a country. [Venus in Sagittarius](/astrology/venus-in-sagittarius/) belongs to one person's appetite for the unfamiliar; here the appetite is shared, and it's often the two of you together who talk your friends into the trip. This is also the sign composite Venus reaches least often, by a little: about 1 pair in 13, while at birth Venus is in Sagittarius for about 1 person in 14.

Its tender spot is the near and the ordinary. A bond that comes alive on the road can find the weeks at home flat and treat them as a wait between departures; a shared worldview can also harden into a cause that leaves no room to disagree. Expansion isn't excess and a vision isn't dogma, but it helps to bring the far into the near: one small discovery a week within walking distance, and a standing permission to believe different things.

Sagittarius is a sign of distances, and oddly enough composite Venus can travel far from the composite Sun. Most of the time it doesn't: about 9 pairs in 10 have the two within 47° of each other, as a real sky always does, and the composite Sun is then never further round than Libra or Aquarius. In the remaining pairs the midpoints part, and about 1 pair in 22 finds the composite Sun near Gemini, across the chart from this Venus. The distance is in the arithmetic, not in the bond.

[Composite Venus in Scorpio](/astrology/composite-venus-in-scorpio/) goes deep where this one goes wide, and [composite Venus in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-venus-in-capricorn/) values what is built to last.
