The house most people keep locked opens, with Sagittarius on the 8th house cusp, onto the frankest sign of all. What others keep behind closed doors, money, desire, loss, you would rather talk about in plain words.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is where your life gets tangled with other people's: intimacy, trust, joint money, debts ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). Its natural sign is Scorpio, which guards; [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) on its cusp opens the door instead. I read it as trust offered first, on principle, and intimacy that feels like exploring together, closeness that teaches you something about yourself. Curiosity and laughter count for as much as intensity here. Shared money tends to run on goodwill and a handshake, which works beautifully until someone needs the paperwork, so the lesson is to make an agreement as honest on paper as it is in spirit. When a crisis comes, you go looking for its meaning, in a book, a faith, a conversation, or a long walk somewhere far away.

Death and inheritance were what the old books put in the 8th. I don't read a chart for either ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and Jupiter on this cusp promises no legacy. What it describes is how you share, trust and owe while you're living it.

What you share is in [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/)'s keeping: its house shows where joint resources and close trust get tested, and its sign how you handle them, in the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Your own money sits under Gemini, on the 2nd cusp across the chart, so the two ends of the money axis belong to Mercury and Jupiter: small change on one side, big gestures on the other. Pisces, the sign Jupiter rules alongside Neptune, falls in your 11th or 12th house in all of these charts, most often on the 12th cusp.

Sagittarius opens the 8th in roughly one London birth in 13, and a little more often at New York, 1 in 12, in Placidus, the house system I use. The 8th cusp lies just above the western horizon, and Sagittarius goes down quickly in the north, so it doesn't stay there long. Taurus rises with it in 3 London charts in 5, Gemini or Aries in the rest, so Venus is usually your chart ruler: careful with what's yours, generous with what's ours. Spanning only about 17° to 21° at London, the 8th lets Sagittarius open a neighbouring house too in 3 London charts in 5 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)): the 9th, which ties what you go through with others to what you make of it, or the 7th, which ties it to the partner you go through it with. No London chart with this cusp is free of interception: Aries usually lies whole inside the 12th, and Libra inside the 6th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Earlier in the day, this would have been [Sagittarius on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-9th-house-cusp/); later, [Sagittarius on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/). To know which describes you, I need the minute you were born; a rounded hour won't do ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
