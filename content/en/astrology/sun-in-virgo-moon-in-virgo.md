Both lights were in Virgo, side by side close to a new moon, and Mercury, which rules the sign, answers for them both. I read the pair as one exacting voice speaking for purpose and comfort alike: you feel most yourself and most at ease in the same moment, when something has been done well.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Purpose and need share a manner here, mutable earth, the sign of method, on both sides. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) knows itself by the distinctions it can make; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) steadies itself with a task in hand and a place for everything. With no second sign to translate between them, the moment that gives your life direction, a job finished properly, is very often the moment that settles your nerves.

Virgo is unusual in being both the home and the exaltation of one planet, [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), and here that planet rules both lights. Mercury keeps to within 28° or so of the Sun, so it may share the sign with both your lights at full strength, or sit just outside, in Leo or Libra. Its sign, house and aspects are the first thing I read after the lights themselves. The Sun and the Moon have no dignity of their own in Virgo, and tradition puts Venus's fall here too, so pleasure, for a double Virgo, often needs permission.

Twenty-nine degrees at most separated your lights, on one side of the Sun or the other: in time, a new moon just gone or about to come, never more than about 2½ days off. The [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) and the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phases take these births in equal parts, the exact new moon nearest your birth was usually a [new moon in Virgo](/astrology/new-moon-in-virgo/), and with the lights this close the chart holds a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). Which side of the dark of the moon is yours depends on the hour, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says what each side means.

About 1 person in 143 is a double Virgo. If the Moon came in from Leo or moved on into Libra on your birthday, the hour decides whether you are one, or have the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-leo/) or the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-libra/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). A double Virgo will appreciate the precision: that same hour settles the rising sign and the houses, and until it's found I keep the chart closed.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love attentively: the spare charger in your bag, the form filled in, the small change in someone's mood noticed before they mention it. What you need back is the same precision, appreciation for what you actually did, and a partner who stays on the days you have nothing useful to offer. With both lights in a sign of service, being cared for can feel like a debt. It isn't one. Practise receiving without balancing the books.

## At work {#at-work}

Give a double Virgo a process that matters and a free hand to improve it, and both lights come into their own: auditing, editing, research, restoration, any work whose quality can be checked. Purpose and calm rise together when your method is trusted. When you're asked to cut corners you can see, both sink together, which is why the wrong workplace costs you twice.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With one planet speaking for both lights and the same sign on both sides, nothing inside the pair says *that will do*. The standard you hold for your work can become the standard you hold for your feelings, so a low mood gets treated as a mistake to correct, and rest needs a reason. Help tends to arrive from elsewhere in the chart: the rising sign and the slower planets, a body that gets used every day, and pleasures kept on purpose, unimproved, since Venus is least at ease in Virgo.
