Chiron sextile North Node in synastry puts about two signs, held to 1.8°, between one person's Chiron and the other's North Node, and four between that Chiron and their South Node. One person's hard-won know-how sits within reach of the other's next step. Reaching for it is the part nobody can do for them.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks where a person found another way to take part when the first way was closed: a slower rhythm, a tool, an accommodation they had to learn to ask for. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) describes growth that comes by doing, a capacity less automatic than its partner, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), which holds a person's practised, ready strengths. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) invites cooperation without forcing it, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one has a second face, a trine to the South Node across the way.

If the Chiron is yours, your experience fits the other person's growth like a spare key. A routine you built, a way of pacing a hard week, the words you found to ask for adjustments: any of these may be exactly what they need to try. And since your Chiron trines their South Node, you also like the person they already are, so nothing you offer arrives as criticism.

Holding the North Node, you get a foothold. The other person's tricks make the unfamiliar direction look less steep, and because they're at ease with your familiar strengths, you can borrow from them without feeling that your old self has been marked down.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The ease has a pull towards the past. Time together can settle on the comfortable trine, swapping stories about what both already know, while the sextile's opening gets mentioned and never used. The Chiron person may also wait to be asked, and the node person may wait to be told, so the offer sits on the table between them like a gift nobody unwraps.

- **[Chiron conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the Chiron squarely on the path instead of two signs to one side.
- **[Chiron square North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-square-north-node-synastry/):** a rub against both ends of the axis rather than an open door.
- **[Chiron trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-trine-north-node-synastry/):** the balance reversed, a strong current to the growth and a small opening back.
- **[Chiron opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** the Chiron resting right on the other's familiar South Node.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make the offer concrete and short: "try this for a month and tell me what happened". Between colleagues, that might be a technique handed over with no strings; between friends, a habit worth testing; in a couple or between adult siblings, a routine one of you lives by and the other has never tried.

The dates rely on the true node, with the mean node checked beside it. For a birth in mid-1995, Chiron is in Virgo, and anyone born from November 1981 to March 1982 has the North Node in Cancer, 60° away, and the South Node in Capricorn, an earth trine with that Chiron. Turn it round and the Capricorn Chiron of people born from July to October 2002 is sextile the 1995 North Node in Scorpio. The node, moving backwards, comes to 60° on either side of a Chiron, so the windows on its side alternate at gaps of about six and twelve years, among them September 1975 to January 1976 and June to August 1994.

None of this enters my count of a synastry, which lists only contacts made with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Through its houses, the sextile shows what one generation's coping can offer another's growth, and those houses wait on both birth times, since neither point moves enough in a day to decide anything else ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Within one chart, [Chiron sextile North Node](/astrology/chiron-sextile-north-node/) lets a person's own adaptations feed their growth. Shared between two, the tools change hands, and I read the exchange only when both have agreed to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
