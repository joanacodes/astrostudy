The full moon in Gemini is the last full moon before the December solstice: opposite a Sun at its lowest, the Moon climbs high in northern skies and stays up through the long nights. It sets Gemini's questions against Sagittarius's search for meaning, and in 2029 and 2030 it's an eclipse.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) comes when the Moon is opposite the Sun, lit from edge to edge. This one puts the Moon in [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) and the Sun on the matching degree of [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/). The Moon brings Gemini's side to a peak: the messages and errands, the dozen open questions of a mind that wants to know. Across the axis, the Sun asks what it all means. The Gemini–Sagittarius axis runs from the near to the far, and this full moon often reveals where the two have drifted apart: plenty of information and no direction, or a firm belief with too few facts under it. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Gemini and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) Sagittarius, and their houses tell you where each half of that conversation happens.

Like any full moon, it lasts a few days and matters in a chart only when the Moon's or the Sun's degree comes within about 1° of one of your planets or angles. Even then it works as a trigger in a period that slower movements have already opened, a transit perhaps or a progression; I never read it as a forecast.

The next falls on 24 November 2026 at 14:54 UT, at 2°20′ Gemini. Then:

- **13 December 2027**, at 21°25′;
- **2 December 2028**, at 10°24′;
- **20 December 2029**, at 29°21′: a total [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/), on Gemini's last degree, the Sun less than a day from the solstice;
- **9 December 2030**, at 17°54′: a penumbral lunar eclipse.

Both are part of the eclipse series these years bring to Gemini and Sagittarius ([eclipse in Gemini](/astrology/eclipse-in-gemini/)). An eclipse weighs more than a plain full moon, so I widen the orb to about 3°, at both ends of the axis. A penumbral one, where the Moon passes only through the Earth's outer shadow, weighs less. Each comes two weeks after a solar eclipse in Sagittarius, on 5 December 2029 and 25 November 2030: the [new moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/new-moon-in-sagittarius/), which always shares this full moon's month. The [full moon in Taurus](/astrology/full-moon-in-taurus/) comes a month before, the [full moon in Cancer](/astrology/full-moon-in-cancer/) a month after.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Your Gemini house holds the Moon, your Sagittarius house the Sun, and finding them takes the time of birth, for the reasons in [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); the houses I draw are Placidus. In 2029 the eclipse sits on Gemini's last degree: if your next cusp also falls late in Gemini, it belongs to that next house.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Gemini rising: your curiosity and your many sides on show, while a partner brings a bigger picture, or a firm opinion.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** the details of what you earn and spend come to a head, while shared plans in the 8th ask what it's all for.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** Gemini's own house: messages, errands and short trips peak, while a longer journey or a belief calls from the 9th.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** a busy, talkative home, family in and out, while your public life asks for a vision.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** wordplay, games, flirting by message, against a group or a cause that wants the larger you.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** too many small tasks come to a head, while a quieter search for meaning, in the 12th, asks for room.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Gemini on the Descendant: conversations with a partner multiply, while you feel the pull of your own direction.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** questions about shared money or trust want clear answers, while your own values set the terms.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a study or a journey needs its details sorted, while everyday talk fills up with big ideas.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Gemini on the Midheaven: a public role built on words and contacts comes forward, while home asks what it all means.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** friends and networks buzz with news, while a passion of your own wants a wider horizon.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** thoughts you don't voice crowd in at night, while the working day asks for a sense of purpose.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Check both degrees each year.** Allow a degree either side for 2026 to 2028; for the two eclipses that follow, widen it to about three, at the Gemini end and the Sagittarius end.
- **Write it down.** Gemini thinks by putting things into words: list the questions this full moon raises in its house, then, across the chart, the one aim or belief they serve.
- **Look at Mercury and Jupiter.** Their houses show where facts and meaning meet in your chart, or miss each other.
