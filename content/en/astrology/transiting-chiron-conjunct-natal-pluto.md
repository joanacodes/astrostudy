Between 1991 and 1999, Chiron crossed the natal Pluto of almost everyone born from the mid-1940s to the mid-1990s, at ages from about 4 to about 47. It was moving at its fastest, close to the point of its orbit nearest the Sun, which it reached in 1996, and the degrees Pluto had held through those decades lay right in its path.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) regenerates: it clears out what has stopped working and reorganises what is left, at depth. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts, building a way of living around a limit that stays. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes the two act as one, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the question of what to clear out and the question of what to live alongside arrive together, about the same thing.

What is usually in question is a way of coping. Most people built one early, around an old sore spot: staying in control, keeping things secret, never needing anyone, always being the one who handles the crisis. Under the conjunction its costs tend to show, and Pluto's blind spot, mistaking a survival strategy for who you are, gets a hearing. Some of it is ready to go. Some of it is still the best tool you have for a limit that hasn't changed, and Chiron's kind of wisdom is knowing which is which.

No death, loss or crisis is foretold here: Pluto isn't destruction, and Chiron isn't illness. If what comes up feels heavier than you can carry alone, a therapist or a doctor is the right door ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

Your whole year group had this conjunction within months of each other, so what makes it yours is where Pluto sits. With [Pluto in the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-in-4th-house/), the coping in question tends to be the one your family taught you; in the 10th, the control you keep over how you are seen. Finding that house takes the minute you were born, not just the day ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Planets conjunct your Pluto are crossed in the same season, and planets square or opposite it receive Chiron's square or opposition too. Born with [Pluto conjunct Chiron](/astrology/pluto-conjunct-chiron/), in 1940–42 or 1999–2000, you started life with the two already joined.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the crossing usually takes about ten months, with three exact hits as Chiron turns back over the point and forward again. The 1990s wave came while Chiron moved fast, the pace that gives the single crossings of about three weeks, which make up about a third of all passes. At the slow extreme, one pass in twenty loops twice and lasts roughly twenty-one months.

Ages at the first, by birth year: about 5½ for people born in 1935, about 47 for 1945, about 39 for 1955, about 30 for 1965, about 21 for 1975, about 13 for 1985, about 4 for 1995 and about 46 for 2005. The second comes about fifty years after the first, so by 80 most people have had two and the rest one. For the 1990s wave, the second falls in the 2040s. If yours came in childhood, it belongs to an adult's look back: this site doesn't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/).

The round opens here: [transiting Chiron sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-pluto/), [transiting Chiron square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-pluto/) and [transiting Chiron trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-pluto/) then lead to [transiting Chiron opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-pluto/), halfway round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List the ways you learned to cope, and mark each one: still useful, tired, or ready to go. Release one, not all of them.

Tell one trusted person about the strategy you are retiring. Habits Pluto guards tend to loosen once they stop being secret.

Leave room to grieve what a coping method protected you from. It did its job for a long time.
