Behind every relationship there is a base: the place it sleeps, the family it makes, the roots it puts down. Composite Uranus in the 4th house builds that base differently from anything either of you grew up with.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Of the two ways of drawing composite houses, one uses the midpoints of your natal cusps and the other rebuilds the houses from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, but both share that Midheaven, and so both share the composite [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) opposite it, where [the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) begins. The start of this house is therefore steadier across methods than most; its far edge, the 5th cusp, is not. The IC itself still rests on both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The 4th is angular and the natural house of Cancer: home, family, roots, private life, what a life stands on. With [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) at the bottom of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the relationship invents its own idea of home. It might keep two flats a street apart, share a house with friends, move every few years, work from the kitchen table, take in a parent, or gather a family made more by choice than by birth. What looks unsettled from outside can be exactly the freedom the bond needs to feel at home. Two people who both crave a fixed address can find their relationship restless at the root; two wanderers can find it wants a hearth, only not the one either of them was raised by. The 4th also holds what each of you inherited, and with Uranus there the pair tends to keep some of its families' ways and drop others on purpose, sorting heirlooms, recipes and rules into what stays and what goes.

Set beside [Uranus in the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-in-4th-house/), one person's home life off the usual mould, and [their Uranus in your 4th house](/astrology/uranus-in-4th-house-synastry/), someone who unsettles your sense of home, the composite placement is different: the unsettling and the reinventing belong to the household you share. Couples now in their seventies hold the matching sign, [composite Uranus in Cancer](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-cancer/); this house, by contrast, can come with any birth years.

The trap is a base that never sets. A pair like this can answer every unease by rearranging the home, and whichever of you needs roots more may feel permanently in transit. What helps is to decide together what home must always contain, wherever it is and whatever shape it takes: a person, an object, a habit that travels with you.

The houses on either side point elsewhere: the 3rd changes the near world around the home ([composite Uranus in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-3rd-house/)), the 5th the bond's play ([composite Uranus in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-5th-house/)). Nothing here predicts moving in, moving out or moving away. I leave composites out of my readings and describe them for what they are, a technique, read beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and drawn only for two people who have each agreed to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
