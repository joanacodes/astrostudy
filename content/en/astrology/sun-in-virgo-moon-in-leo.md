Your Moon is in Leo, one sign back from your Sun and in the very sign the Sun rules, which puts your feelings under the direct authority of your sense of purpose. The lunar month was nearly over at your arrival; I read the pair as a discreet worker with a warm heart that needs, now and then, to be seen.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Side by side in the zodiac, Virgo and Leo still have nothing in common: mutable earth for your Sun, fixed fire for your Moon. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) builds a self from discernment and good method, and is often modest about its gifts; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) needs warmth returned, to know that its care has landed and that it matters to someone in particular. In public you may play down what you do. In private you need someone to notice it.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo, so your Moon reports to your Sun: what you feel depends a good deal on how your purpose is going, and a good day's work can lift a low mood faster than any treat. Your Sun in turn answers to [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler and exaltation of Virgo, which is never far from the Sun and sits in Leo, Virgo or Libra. The chain runs Moon, Sun, Mercury, and when Mercury is in Leo with your Moon, the planet that orders your thinking takes on its warmth as well. No dignity applies to either light in these signs.

With the Moon no more than 58° behind the Sun, the birth fell in the last four or five days of the lunar month: the last sliver of crescent in the east before sunrise, or no Moon to see at all. The [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) claims all but about one in eight of these births, the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) the earliest, and the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) that came within five days was usually a [new moon in Virgo](/astrology/new-moon-in-virgo/). A balsamic birth, as I read it, knows how to close a chapter well, which suits a Sun that likes to finish things properly; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows how this phase ends the cycle.

Around 1 person in 143 has this pair, and the time matters twice for it. On a day the Moon left Cancer or entered Virgo, the hour decides between this page, the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-cancer/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-virgo/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)); and in the first day or so of Virgo's month, around 23 August, the Sun's own sign hangs on the hour as well. The rising sign, which completes the first sketch, needs it too.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love warmly and show it practically: the celebration planned to the last detail, the favour done before it was asked for. Underneath, the Leo Moon wants to be cherished, and to hear it. You may not ask, because asking feels immodest to a Virgo Sun. A partner who praises you specifically, for what you actually did, reaches both lights; vague compliments reach neither.

## At work {#at-work}

You do careful work and want it to carry your signature. The Virgo Sun brings method and a maker's standards; the Leo Moon brings pride in the result and a need for the right people to know whose work it was. Roles where quality is visible suit you: design, cooking, teaching, any craft that ends in something shown. Being taken for granted is the quiet cost, and the Moon dims long before the Sun complains.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The Moon wants applause and the Sun distrusts it. You may undervalue what you've made, then feel unseen when nobody else values it either, and pride can stop you saying that it hurt. High standards and a need for recognition make an uncomfortable pair, because every slip feels public. What helps is a record of what you've finished, kept for yourself, and one or two people allowed to see both the work and the wish to be praised before it's perfect.
