Sometimes it's asked as a dare, sometimes in a whisper, after a loss or before a diagnosis. Sometimes it arrives as fear: someone, somewhere, has told a person that their chart shows an early death. Whatever the reason behind the question, it deserves a clear answer and a kind one.

## The short answer {#the-short-answer}

No. No birth chart, transit, eclipse or degree can tell when or how anyone will die. Not yours, and not the life of someone you love. Death, mine or a loved one's, is on my list of [what I won't read](/how-i-read/), and that holds for every chart, every reading and every question.

If someone has predicted your death, what they told you was not knowledge. And if the question is weighing on you because you're thinking about ending your life, please skip to the end of this page and talk to someone now.

## Why it matters {#why-it-matters}

Astrology did once try. Ptolemy wrote a chapter on the length of life and another on the quality of death (*Tetrabiblos*, books III and IV), and later astrologers built elaborate methods for counting a life's years ([lots of death and illness, in history](/astrology/lots-of-death-and-illness-in-history/)). The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) was given death. I describe that history because it's real, and because people still meet it in old books and on websites. I don't practise any of it.

The reasons go beyond the fact that it can't be done.

- **A prediction of death changes a life.** Believed even a little, it can bend decisions for years: what you dare, what you postpone, how you treat your body and the people around you.
- **Fear sells.** Some readers who claim to "see death" around a person then offer, for a fee, to remove it. That is a scam, every time ([curse removal scams](/astrology/curse-removal-scams/)).
- **The symbols don't mean that.** Pluto describes transformation, not destruction. An eclipse on a natal point is emphasis, never an omen. The 29th degree of a sign is a late degree, not a fatal one ([the anaretic degree (29°)](/astrology/anaretic-degree-29/)). The 8th house, read in an adult's life, is about intimacy and trust, what you share, owe and let go of.

What a chart can describe is how you meet endings: change, loss, letting go, grief. Saturn's lessons about limits, the Moon's way of looking after feeling, Pluto's deep reworking. Those are real conversations, and they belong to the living. Questions about illness have the same answer, for the same reasons ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and so do questions about birth ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)).

## What to do instead {#what-to-do-instead}

- **If you're thinking about ending your life, talk to someone now.** In the UK and Ireland, Samaritans answer on 116 123, free from any phone, at any time. In France, the national suicide prevention line is 3114, free, every day, day and night. Anywhere else, call your local emergency number. You don't have to be sure what you feel to call.
- **If a prediction or a fear of dying keeps coming back:** talk to someone you trust, and to a doctor or a therapist. Fear like that deserves real support, not more readings.
- **If someone you love has been given a frightening prediction:** the kindest thing is often to say plainly that no chart can know that, and to stay with them while the fear settles.
- **If you're grieving:** your doctor, a bereavement service or a counsellor can help you carry it, and so can the people who loved the same person.
- **If what you want is to be ready:** a doctor, for your health; a solicitor or a notary, for a will; the people you love, for everything else.

And if you'd like to understand how you meet change and loss, that's a question a chart can hold, gently, in a reading of your own life.
