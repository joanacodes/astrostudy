Sun trine Lilith links your Sun with Black Moon Lilith across a third of the zodiac, usually within one element. It describes a part of you that might once have been called too much, and that now moves with who you are rather than against it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) gives you vitality and a way of expressing yourself: how you show up, and the purpose that carries you. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the mean far point of the Moon's orbit, is read in many ways; in mine, it's the part that would not obey: instinct, desire, refusal, and your relationship to your own power. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets them flow together without either giving way.

In plain words, you can say no without a long apology, and want what you want without feeling you have to explain it. People may find you unusually at ease with the parts of yourself others hide, and some find that freeing to be around. Because none of this feels like effort, you may not realise how rare it is. A trine's one risk applies here too: flow isn't passivity, and ease can turn into a habit of never looking underneath. The gift is real, and it does most when you use it on purpose, for yourself and for people who are still learning to say no.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as self-possession and a relaxed relationship with desire and refusal. One element usually holds both. With Lilith in Pisces, a Sun in Cancer or Scorpio can live close to hidden feelings without being swamped by them; with Lilith in Sagittarius, a Sun in Aries or Leo holds a philosophy entirely its own, and holds it warmly. Which two areas of life support each other is a matter for the houses, and so for your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A Sun–Lilith trine within 8° comes to about 1 person in 11. The Sun gains on Lilith only slowly, so each trine falls about four and a half months from their meeting, twice in every 13½-month round. Mean Lilith, the one my count uses, never runs backwards, so each trine forms and passes in one go. If your software shows a different Lilith, the angle in your chart may not be this one, so it's worth checking first ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

The pair's other aspects ask more of the same point, or less:

- **[Sun conjunct Lilith](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith/):** at the centre of who you are, intense rather than easy.
- **[Sun sextile Lilith](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith/):** a lighter link that waits for an invitation.
- **[Sun square Lilith](/astrology/sun-square-lilith/):** the refused part as friction, where calm has to be earned.
- **[Sun opposite Lilith](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith/):** met in other people before it's owned.

Between two charts, [Sun trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith-synastry/) lets one person's way of being themselves fit the other's instinct easily: a closeness in which neither has to be less.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Mean Lilith takes about nine months to cross a sign, so its trine to your Sun is a matter of weeks, never months. The Sun in the sky makes the same trine to your natal Lilith twice a year, for about a day. Weather, not a forecast: a few easy weeks in which saying what you want comes more simply, and nothing is decided for you.
