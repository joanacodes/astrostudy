Libra is where tradition finds the Sun weakest, and Scorpio is where it says the same of the Moon; this is the only pair that has both. I don't read that as a double handicap. I read a self that finds its strength through other people and needs that find theirs by going to the bottom of things, born as a new lunar month was getting under way.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Scorpio comes straight after Libra, so the signs are next-door, a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign: cardinal air beside fixed water, sharing only a border. The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) aims for harmony and fair dealing between people; the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) settles only when it knows what is really going on underneath, and gives its trust slowly, and completely, to very few. Graciousness comes naturally to you in company; what the Moon wants, in private, is an honesty most company never reaches.

The Sun's [fall](/astrology/fall/) is in Libra, the Moon's in Scorpio: Ptolemy's hardest signs for the two lights, here side by side. A fall describes how much effort ease takes, never how much a person can love or achieve. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your Sun's sign. Scorpio has two rulers and I read both: in the older books [Mars](/astrology/mars/), which shows how this Moon defends what it cares about, and for modern astrologers [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), which shows where it lets things end and then rebuilds. Where Venus and those two disagree, fairness and intensity have to negotiate.

The Moon had only just left the Sun, 0° to 58° ahead of it, in a lunar month no more than about five days old. The [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase claims most of these births, the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) about one in eight, and the conjunction that opened the month usually took place in Libra. At the very start of the range, a Sun in Libra's last degrees and a Moon in Scorpio's first can be conjunct across the sign line. Where the new moon phase ends is set out in [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/).

About 1 person in 144 is born with both lights in their fall. On some birthdays the time decides it: a little earlier, the Moon may still have been in Libra, a little later already in Sagittarius, which would make yours the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-libra/) or the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-sagittarius/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). From that time come the rising sign and the houses too, and with the lights so close together, the house they share, or the two they straddle, says where this double fall is lived.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You want a relationship that is fair and pleasant, and an intimacy that is total and honest. A pleasant surface with nothing underneath leaves the Scorpio Moon hungry; intensity without fair dealing offends the Libra Sun. Your trust is slow to arrive and total once it does, so small breaches can hurt more than you show. Telling a partner early what you need in order to trust, rather than quietly testing them, protects both lights.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that combines diplomacy with depth suits you: negotiation that gets to what each side really wants, counselling, research, investigation, mediation where feelings run high. You can stay courteous under pressure and still notice what nobody is saying. Workplaces that run on appearances wear you down quickly. You're at your best when the polite surface is there to make hard truths easier to hear, not to hide them.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Courtesy and depth can pull against each other. You may smile through a hurt that the Scorpio Moon then keeps for years, or hold on to a grievance while the Libra Sun insists everything is fine. Neither keeps the peace for long. Say the true thing early, in the Libra Sun's gentlest words: the Moon gets its honesty, the Sun its fairness, and nothing has to go underground.
