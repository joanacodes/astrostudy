Home is where the fun is. Its ruler stands in the house of play, pleasure and creativity, and belonging tends to come through joy rather than duty.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, family and the private ground you stand on. Here the planet that rules its cusp ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)) has moved one house on, into [the 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/): creativity, pleasure, play and romance, the things you do for no reason but delight.

I read it as a home that's meant to be enjoyed. You may need room to make things where you live: a table covered in a project, an instrument out of its case, a kitchen used for more than meals. Gatherings matter, the people you love, the friends you cook for, the evenings that run late. The family memories that last tend to be the happy ones, games, holidays, a shared joke, and as an adult you may build home around keeping them going. The flip side: when home stops being fun you feel it more than most, and a household run on duty alone drains you fast.

With Taurus or Libra on the IC, Venus rules the 4th, and from the 5th, the house of its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), it makes a home beautiful and welcoming, comfort and affection under one roof. With Leo on the IC, the Sun rules home from the house of its own natural sign, and home is where you shine most easily, among people who know you.

The 5th is also romance, so love and home tend to mix. You may be drawn to people who make a place feel like yours, or need a relationship to have some play in it before it can feel like home.

Home can also be your first stage. The song tried out in the kitchen, the new recipe tested on friends, the play rehearsed in the living room: the people you live with often see your work before anyone else does, and their pleasure in it matters to you.

The 5th once stood for children, and a 4th-house ruler there has been read as a growing family. That's not how I read it. Pregnancy is not something I read in a chart ([Can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/); the family in this placement is the one you share as an adult, and the 5th is your own pleasure in it.

With Scorpio on the IC, Mars and Pluto share the 4th, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 5th brings energy and competition to home life, the board game played to win, the project finished at midnight, while Pluto, from its own house, keeps a deeper current running underneath.

Compare the houses either side: in the 4th itself, home is its own subject ([Ruler of the 4th in the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)); in the 6th, it becomes a workplace or a place of daily care ([Ruler of the 4th in the 6th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)). The 5th puts it to play, and the birth time decides which of the three is yours ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
