Their Jupiter in your 7th house brings another person's generosity to the place where you meet someone as an equal. A partnership with them, in love or in work, tends to make your life larger: new people, new places, new ideas of what two can do.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the reach of a chart: growth, generosity, the search for what things mean. [The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) gathers the people you meet as equals: the one you build a life with, the associate you sign with, the ally you count on, the opponent you face openly. This [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) makes partnership itself a place to grow.

With them, partnership can feel generous. They trust you from the start, share opportunities, bring you into their world and take an interest in yours. Agreements, from a shared lease to a joint project, may come more easily, because good faith is where you both begin.

For them, facing you is a chance to grow. Across from you, their generosity and their beliefs are answered by someone on an equal footing, and that can be rare. Angular, the 7th shows what it holds in the partners you choose and how you treat them; [Libra](/astrology/libra/), sign of balance and fair exchange, is its natural sign. If their Jupiter sits on your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the western point of your chart, it makes a conjunction with the angle through which you meet any counterpart, and that contact counts in the reading.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Generous beginnings can hide uneven terms. You may let them take more than their share because it all feels so open-handed, or sign up to plans built on enthusiasm rather than detail. You might also expect the relationship to supply the meaning you're looking for, which is a lot to ask of one person.

From their side, generosity can feel unreturned if you keep count more carefully than they do, or they may feel held to promises they made in a hopeful mood.

An overlay in the 7th never says whether two people will marry, stay together or part. It describes how generosity and growth move between you as equals.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep the good faith, and write down the terms. Generosity between equals lasts longer when both know what was agreed. In a couple, check that giving flows both ways; between business partners, a contract read slowly is a gift to the friendship; between friends, a shared plan works best when someone owns the details; between rivals, generosity can turn a contest into a respectful one.

From where they stand, it's your Jupiter in their 7th house, and your generosity that faces them. [Their Jupiter in your 6th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-6th-house-synastry/) helps your working days; [their Jupiter in your 8th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-8th-house-synastry/) widens trust and what you share.

The setting point of your chart, your Descendant, is as tied to your birth time as your Ascendant, since the two always face each other; in Placidus, the house system I use, it opens your 7th. Their Jupiter barely shifts in a day, so the day they were born places it and their hour matters only on that angle; I still want both times, because the other half of the reading lives in their houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Two adults, both aware, both willing: that is the condition for this reading, as for every synastry ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
