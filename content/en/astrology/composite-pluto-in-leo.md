Composite Pluto in Leo is the relationship Pluto of many pairs now in their late sixties, seventies and eighties. Pluto spent October 1937 to June 1958 in Leo, and pairs born in those years, or on either side of them, carry it in the chart of their bond.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire ruled by the Sun, expresses, creates and lets itself be seen. Give [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) a Leo [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and the bond's deep changes come through what it makes and how openly it loves. It reads as a pair transformed by creating together, a business, a garden, a family tradition, a body of work with both names on it, and by the nerve it takes to stand up as a pair in front of others. What such a bond regenerates, it regenerates warmly: after a hard season it tends to come back by making something new.

Its sore point is pride. A relationship that has put its heart on show can feel any setback others witness as a humiliation, and then hide, or fight to save face, rather than change. A stumble seen by friends is not a disgrace. Leo's ruler is the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), and the composite Sun, what the relationship organises itself around, shows where all this heat is aimed.

The sign follows the birth years. Pluto stays 12 to 31 years in a sign and spent October 1937 to June 1958 in this one, so two people born in those years usually have [Pluto in Leo](/astrology/pluto-in-leo/) in their own charts and in their composite. With an age gap the composite moves to the midpoint, which can give a relationship a sign neither person has: a parent born in June 1934, with Pluto at about 23° Cancer, and a child born in June 1966, with Pluto at about 16° Virgo, share a composite Pluto at about 19° Leo. Draw two birth dates at random between 1930 and 2009, and about 1 pair in 5 will have it.

Shared that widely, the sign speaks for the decades the two of you were born in more than for the pair. The reading becomes yours through the house, which needs both birth times and depends on the method used to draw it; a pair whose composite Pluto also lands in the house of play and creation would find the theme doubled ([composite Pluto in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-5th-house/)). Older pairs still belong to [composite Pluto in Cancer](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-cancer/); pairs born from the mid-1950s to the early 1970s, for the most part, to [composite Pluto in Virgo](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-virgo/).

Pride can also hide trouble. If one of you is frightened of the other, or made small in front of other people, that isn't a placement to interpret: in a UK emergency ring 999, and for someone to talk to, Samaritans answer on 116 123 ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)). And since this composite is about being seen, let both of you choose whether to be seen this way: it's drawn from two birth charts, and only with two people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
