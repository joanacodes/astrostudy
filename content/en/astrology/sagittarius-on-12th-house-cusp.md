The house you keep behind the curtain opens in Sagittarius when the archer sits on your 12th house cusp. Much of your optimism, and much of your searching, happens where nobody sees it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/) is the side of life that goes on out of sight: solitude, rest, recovery, the work nobody applauds, the help nobody sees ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) on its cusp, I read hope as something you carry in private. You may not talk about your beliefs, yet they hold you up on bad days; you may help strangers and never mention it; you may need to go somewhere alone, a long walk, a library, another city, to find out what you think. Rest tends to work when it has air in it rather than walls, and a retreat with a purpose, a course, a pilgrimage, a week of silence, often restores you more than a beach does. The hidden enemies of the old books become, with this cusp, optimism that skips over a problem until it has grown, or a restlessness that can't sit still long enough to rest.

The old books also put confinement and hospitals in the 12th. I don't look for either in a chart, and a 12th house cusp is never a diagnosis; when a question belongs with a doctor or a therapist, I say so ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)). What I can describe is how you look after your inner life, and with Sagittarius that's usually by widening it rather than shutting it away.

The 12th answers to [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) twice over: Pisces, its natural sign, was Jupiter's long before Neptune was discovered in 1846, and now Sagittarius opens it. Where Jupiter sits shows what you do out of sight, and where you go to recover: the [1st](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Pisces itself, Neptune's sign now as much as Jupiter's, sits in your 2nd or 3rd house in every London chart, tying your inner life to your resources or to your everyday thinking.

This is among Sagittarius's commonest cusps, held in about 1 birth in 10 in both cities, in Placidus, the house system I use: just above the eastern horizon, the sign lingers as it rises. In nearly half the London charts, and about a third at New York, Sagittarius goes on to rise as well ([Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/)): Jupiter is then your chart ruler too, and the person people meet and the one who retreats share a ruler ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). Otherwise Capricorn rises, and Saturn, the planet tradition said rejoices in the 12th, rules your chart from the other side of the line. The 12th is narrow here, 16° to 20° at London, and in 2 London charts out of 5 Sagittarius opens the 11th as well, linking your solitude with your friendships ([Sagittarius on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-11th-house-cusp/)).

Born a little earlier, you'd have had the 12th cusp still in Scorpio and Sagittarius on the Ascendant; a little later, the archer would have climbed to the 11th. Only the minute of birth settles it, so that's what I ask for first ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
