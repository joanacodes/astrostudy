The Midheaven sits at the top of a birth chart, marked MC for its Latin name, *Medium Coeli*: the middle of the sky. Where the Ascendant describes how you meet the world, the Midheaven describes where you're heading in it, and what you'd like to be known for.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Picture a line running across the sky from north to south through the point straight overhead: the meridian. At the minute and place of your birth, the zodiac crossed it in two points: above the horizon, the Midheaven; below it, exactly opposite, the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/). Together they make the [meridian axis](/astrology/meridian-axis/). The Midheaven isn't the point straight overhead, and it isn't always at a right angle to the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/): it's simply where the zodiac crossed that line. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems it is the cusp, the starting line, of the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), which tradition gave to action, honour and reputation.

I read the Midheaven as a direction rather than a job: what you're building in public, how you want to be seen for your work, the standing you're after. It's something you grow into, not something you're handed.

- **Its sign** shows the style of your ambition, and the reputation you'd like to have.
- **Its ruler**, the ruler of the 10th house, shows where your work draws its energy from.
- **Planets close to it** are [angular planets](/astrology/angular-planets/): among the most visible in a chart, and often what people know you for.

It works with the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), your purpose: born around the middle of the day, by the sun rather than the clock, you have the Sun close to your Midheaven. And it works with [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the planet of slow mastery, which rules Capricorn, the 10th sign. The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) and the IC complete the four angles.

Because the Midheaven and the IC are always exactly opposite, an aspect to one is an aspect to the other. A planet in [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) with the Midheaven is in [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) to the IC: what it describes tends to show in your public life first. A [square](/astrology/square/) to the Midheaven is a square to the IC too, the old pull between the world and home; a [trine](/astrology/trine/) to it is a sextile to the IC.

## The Midheaven through the signs {#through-the-signs}

The whole zodiac crosses the meridian once a day, so the Midheaven changes sign about every two hours. People born on the same day can have Midheavens in very different signs, and only your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) settles yours. What each one wants to be known for:

- **[Midheaven in Aries](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/):** initiative; work where you can lead or start things.
- **[Midheaven in Taurus](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/):** reliability; a career that builds something lasting and real.
- **[Midheaven in Gemini](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/):** ideas and words; work with variety, and people to connect.
- **[Midheaven in Cancer](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** care; work that protects, feeds or looks after others.
- **[Midheaven in Leo](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** presence and creativity; work where you're seen.
- **[Midheaven in Virgo](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** skill and precision; work that makes things better.
- **[Midheaven in Libra](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/):** fairness and taste; work with people, or with beauty.
- **[Midheaven in Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/):** depth; work that investigates, or deals with what others avoid.
- **[Midheaven in Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** vision; teaching, travel, ideas with a horizon.
- **[Midheaven in Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/):** responsibility; a long climb, and authority earned.
- **[Midheaven in Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** originality; work for groups, causes or the future.
- **[Midheaven in Pisces](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/):** imagination and compassion; work that creates or serves.

## Its ruler in the houses {#in-the-houses}

The planet that rules the sign on your Midheaven is the ruler of your 10th house ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)): Saturn for a Capricorn Midheaven, the Sun for Leo, Mercury for Gemini or Virgo, and so on; Scorpio, Aquarius and Pisces have a modern ruler and a traditional one, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Where it sits shows which part of life feeds your work and your standing:

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** your work is personal; you are, very visibly, what you do.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** work tied to earning, and to making things of value.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** work with words: teaching, writing, explaining, getting around.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** work that grows from home or family, or that you do from home.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** creative work, performance, anything that needs joy.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** a career built on skill, method and daily usefulness.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** work with or through others: partners, clients, the public.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** work with what others entrust to you: their resources, their secrets, their hardest moments.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** teaching, publishing, travel or belief carry your work.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** ambition stands on its own feet; your career is a world of its own.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** work in groups, networks and shared causes.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** work out of the spotlight: behind the scenes, in large institutions, or caring for others.

## The Midheaven in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), the Midheaven shows how another person touches your direction and your standing. [Their Sun in your 10th house](/astrology/sun-in-10th-house-synastry/) can feel like someone who believes in your ambitions, or who wants a say in them; [their Jupiter in your 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-10th-house-synastry/) tends to feel encouraging, like a door held open. These contacts matter between partners as much as between colleagues: who supports whose work is a real question in any couple. I read them in both directions, and because the Midheaven needs both birth times, only [with both people's agreement](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## Transits to the Midheaven {#transits}

The angles have no return: what moves is the sky. Jupiter reaches your Midheaven about every 12 years, Saturn about every 29½, after climbing through your 9th house. When [Saturn crosses your Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-midheaven/), people often describe a peak of responsibility: what you've built is seen, and asks to be carried. Saturn then moves on through your 10th house ([Saturn transiting the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-10th-house/)). [Jupiter on the Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-midheaven/) tends to feel like visibility and encouragement; [Pluto](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-midheaven/), which many people never meet there, like a slow change of direction. Uranus and Neptune, too, reach it once in a lifetime at most.

A transit is weather, not a forecast: it describes the climate around your work, never a promotion or a dismissal written in advance. And it is only as precise as your birth time.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

My notebook doesn't report the Midheaven on its own, so I won't give it a figure. The angle it reports is the rising sign, which held about a third of the time: 30 to 40%, like most of [what I checked](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). The three that stood out were [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), at 75 to 90%. So I read your Midheaven, and I tell you which parts of a reading rest on it, so you can weigh them for yourself. It's a notebook, not a study, and it shapes [how I read](/how-i-read/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“No planets in the 10th house, no career.”** An [empty house](/astrology/empty-houses/) still has a sign on its cusp and a ruler, and they still describe your work.
- **“Your Midheaven tells you which job to do.”** It describes a direction and a style; many different jobs can follow it.
- **“The Midheaven is always the cusp of the 10th house.”** Only in Placidus and the other quadrant systems. In Whole Sign and equal houses it can fall in the 9th or the 11th: it depends on the [house system](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/) you use.
