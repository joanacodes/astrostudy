Your sense of what's possible gets a counterweight once in each Saturn round, when Saturn stands across the chart from the degree your Jupiter held at birth. What you believe is possible faces what others, an institution or plain circumstance consider realistic, and the season asks you to keep both in view.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands: it wants meaning, growth, a wider frame. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) contains: rules, costs, time. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, so the tension often arrives through someone else: the cautious partner who asks for numbers, the institution with its criteria, the teacher who questions your thesis, the colleague who has seen plans like yours stall.

Beliefs aren't dogmatism, and this opposition is when you find out which of yours can hold a conversation. Some convictions come out sharper for being questioned; some turn out to have been borrowed. Saturn's caution isn't always wisdom either, so the task is to weigh it, not simply to obey it. Nothing here forecasts a refusal, a loss or a failure; it describes two needs, growth and limits, asking to be balanced.

The axis runs between two houses. With your Jupiter in the 3rd and Saturn crossing your 9th ([Saturn transiting the 9th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-9th-house/)), everyday ideas and learning meet the standards of higher study, publishing or a foreign system. I'd need your birth time to draw the line from your 3rd house to your 9th at all; without it, the opposition stays unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Expect about nine months, with my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after: long enough for a proposal to be made, questioned and revised. A little over half of Saturn's [transits](/astrology/transits/) come in three exact passes, the middle one while Saturn moves backwards, over about nine and a half months; a straight crossing can be over in three weeks.

Its first visit follows your birth year. Born on 1 January 1935, 1955, 1975 or 1995, a few years before a Jupiter–Saturn conjunction, you met it at about 4 to 6½; born on 1 January 1945, 1965, 1985 or 2005, a few years after one, at about 19 to 22. Over all births from 1930 to 2009 the first opposition fell anywhere between the first weeks of life and 29, with a median of 14; after that it comes once a round, two or three times by 80. Born about ten years after a great conjunction, with [Jupiter opposite Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-saturn/) in your chart, you meet this transit with your Saturn return, when Saturn comes back to face your Jupiter again.

It is the far point of the round that [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) opens; [transiting Saturn sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Saturn square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Saturn trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-jupiter/) lead up to it, and come again in reverse as the round heads home.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Listen to the cautious voice once, properly. Then decide what, if anything, it changes.

Put the vision in terms others can check: steps, figures, evidence. A plan that can be examined survives this opposition better than one that can only be believed.

Let someone else's rules teach you something before you decide whether to follow them.
