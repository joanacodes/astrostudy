The Sun was high in the afternoon sky when you were born, in a house that tradition gave to what is hidden and shared. I read the Sun in the 8th house as a self that comes into its own in depth: in intimacy, in what others entrust to it, and in the times when things change for good.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) holds intimacy and trust, and what you share with other people or owe them: joint money, debts, the closeness that changes you. It follows the 7th as a succedent house, with Scorpio as its natural sign. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) here meets its own questions in private, and in what you hold together with someone else. You may feel most yourself in conversations that go somewhere real, in work that handles what others can't, and in the stretches of life when something old has to give way.

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance. No chart can predict a death, and I don't read one for it ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); nor can a chart say what anyone will inherit or owe. In an adult's life I read this house among the living: trust, intimacy, shared resources and renewal.

In Placidus, the house system I use, an 8th-house Sun was well up in the western half of the sky, in the middle of the afternoon. A little earlier you'd have the [Sun in the 9th house](/astrology/sun-in-9th-house/); later, closer to sunset, the [Sun in the 7th house](/astrology/sun-in-7th-house/); as with every house, only the [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) can say which. The 2nd house faces it across the chart: what is yours, set against what is shared.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

The 8th-house Sun reveals itself in intimacy rather than in company. It's in closeness, emotional and physical, that you tend to feel most fully yourself, and you may find small talk harder than a conversation at two in the morning about what matters. The risk is merging so completely that you lose your outline, or guarding it so tightly that nobody gets in. Joint money can carry a lot of weight as well. The lesson is to let closeness be mutual and gradual, with your own self intact on both sides of it.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that deals with what others entrust to you suits this Sun: research, therapy and counselling, investigation, managing shared funds or other people's affairs, restoration, crisis work. Colleagues tend to bring you the problem they can't say out loud, and you can hold it without flinching. Recognition is often private, given by the few who know what you did. Purpose fades in work that only skims the surface, however well it pays. And the care you bring to other people's money or secrets is worth turning, now and then, on your own.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Depth can turn from a gift into a requirement. If you feel real only there, ordinary days can seem thin, and lightness can feel like a betrayal of who you are. Control is the other difficulty: wanting to know where everything shared is going, and finding it hard to depend on anyone. Much of this comes from a self that learned to be most alive in private, and still has to trust that it can be just as alive in daylight.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let some of your life be light on purpose: a hobby with no depth to it, friends who simply make you laugh. Make clear agreements about anything shared, money, time, space, and revisit them as life changes. Keep one person you can tell anything to. When something ends, name what it gave you before you count what it cost. And read this Sun with its sign: a Gemini Sun in the 8th digs with questions, a Taurus Sun with patience, and each finds a different treasure.
