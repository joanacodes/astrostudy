Not every pleasure wants an audience. A ruler of the 12th placed in the 5th makes the things you do for joy, as often as not, the things you do alone, and play one of the ways you rest.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is joy in its many forms: creativity, games, pleasure, romance. The [12th](/astrology/12th-house/) is what you live out of view: solitude, rest, imagination, the unspoken. Your pleasures are drawn into that quieter life, because the 12th's ruler lives among them ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

You may make things for yourself first: the sketchbook nobody sees, the piano played when the house is empty, the novel in a drawer. Creative work often draws on dreams, memory and a private imagination, and the making can matter more to you than whether it's shown. Love may begin quietly too, at a slow pace and away from friends' commentary, and you may wait a long time before introducing someone new to anyone. The care it asks is not hiding your joy so well that nobody gets to share it. There's a practical side too: when you're stretched thin, what helps is rarely another night out, and usually an evening alone with something you love doing. Knowing that, and protecting it, keeps the 12th's need for retreat from turning into simply disappearing.

Counting round the zodiac, the 5th sits five signs past the 12th, a distance tradition counted as no aspect at all ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)): the house of retreat doesn't see the house of pleasure. The old names set them apart as well, Good Fortune for the 5th and the Bad Spirit for the 12th. I read the gap as a detour: your inner life reaches joy by an indirect road, through solitude rather than company, and finds it there all the same.

Venus would be at its happiest here. The 5th is the house tradition gave it as its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), and with a Taurus or Libra 12th it's Venus that rules your retreats, so pleasure itself becomes how you recover: music, colour, a long bath, an afternoon alone in a gallery. When the 12th opens in Scorpio, I watch Mars and Pluto together, reading both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 5th plays hard and alone, the solo run, the climbing wall, while Pluto, wherever it stands, deepens the art you make from what you've lived through.

Children were the 5th's subject in tradition, and I leave them out, as I leave out children's charts altogether ([Children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). Here the 5th is your own creative and romantic life.

Between the 5th cusp and the 6th, a few minutes decide where this ruler stands ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If it sits just inside the 5th cusp, a later birth would have dropped it into the 4th, where retreat has an address at home ([Ruler of the 12th in the 4th house](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)); if it sits near the 6th cusp, an earlier birth would have carried it into the 6th, opposite its own house, where the quiet enters your working week ([Ruler of the 12th in the 6th house](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)).
