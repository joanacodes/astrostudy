With Venus in Aries, liking something and saying so tend to happen in the same breath. Venus weighs what is worth having and how you relate to it; Aries, the first sign, does that weighing at the speed of a first impression. I read it as taste that declares itself, and affection that would rather hear a no than be kept waiting.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is your sense of what's worth wanting: the pull of attraction, the shape of your preferences, the give-and-take you expect. [Aries](/astrology/aries/), the cardinal fire sign that [Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules, hands it a manner that starts things rather than waits for them. You choose the flat, the paint, sometimes the person, while others are still comparing.

Tradition calls this Venus's [detriment](/astrology/detriment/): Aries faces Libra, one of Venus's own signs, and the scales Libra brings to every choice are missing here. What's missing is the long weighing, not the warmth, so the work is to add a pause, never to turn anything down. Mars rules the sign, and your Mars, by sign, house and aspect, tells me how much push this Venus has behind it.

Venus usually crosses Aries in about 25 days. When it turns retrograde there it can stay up to 124, and those long stays make Aries one of the more common Venus signs: about 1 person in 10 born from 1930 to 2009 has it. Born during a [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/), you may know at once and say so later. Born on a day Venus changed sign, you may have [Venus in Pisces](/astrology/venus-in-pisces/) or [Venus in Taurus](/astrology/venus-in-taurus/) instead, and only a chart cast for your time can say. Your Sun, meanwhile, will be no further off than Aquarius on one side or Gemini on the other.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, this Venus tends to make the first move, and to mean it. You'd rather say *I like you* and see what happens than spend a month decoding signals. Reciprocity, for an Aries Venus, means an answer: yes, no, or at least an honest *not yet*. What hurts is ambiguity, being kept on a string by someone who won't say what they want. You often bring courage into a couple: you name the problem, book the trip, start the reconciliation. The skill to learn is the slow part of courtship, letting someone reveal themselves at their own pace; the person who took longer to warm up was sometimes worth the wait.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, an Aries Venus values independence and a quick, honest exchange. You may judge a project, a design or a new colleague within minutes, and often rightly, which is useful wherever someone has to choose. Pitching, launching, the first draft of anything: this Venus enjoys work most when something moves because you pushed it. Courtesy without candour wears it down. A team where disagreement is spoken plainly and settled fast suits it far better.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Speed can pass for certainty. The spark of a first attraction may feel like proof that something is valuable, and when the spark settles you can wonder whether it was ever real. Impatience with people who need time can feel, to them, like pressure. And when your frank interest isn't answered in kind, it's easy to hear the silence as a verdict on you rather than a difference of pace.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the second impression a vote. Before you decide whether a person, a job or a place is for you, let a week pass and look again. Ask one more question than you feel like asking. Shared effort, a walk that turns into a run, a project with a deadline, lets this Venus be close at its own tempo. And when someone needs longer than you, say plainly that you can wait, then wait.
