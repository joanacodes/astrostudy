Some pairs are forever being asked when they'll make it official, and quietly decline to answer the question as asked. Their refusal to answer is a fair picture of composite Lilith in the 7th house: a partnership on its own terms, facing the people across the table with rules of its own.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Opposite the 1st, [the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) faces whoever sits across the table: a spouse, a business partner, a client, an open rival. Angular, with Libra as its natural sign, it opens at the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the degree setting in the west. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), where two people have already been merged into one chart, this house turns to the agreement between them, and to the world the pair faces together: other couples, rival firms, the families' idea of what a pair should be. Once [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is there, the point I read as untamed, those terms are the pair's own. The bond may refuse labels, contracts or customs that others take for granted, keep its real agreements private, and meet anyone who tries to set its rules with a polite, immovable no.

None of that has to come from either partner's history. Two people who were conventional in earlier relationships can find this one won't fit the usual form, and each may first see the refusal in the other: the one who won't sign, the one who won't explain. It belongs to the partnership.

The Descendant is simply the composite Ascendant's opposite point, so it carries all of the Ascendant's uncertainty. In one method it is opposite the midpoint of your two Ascendants; in the other, opposite the degree rising with the composite Midheaven where you live; and every birth-time error moves it too. Astrologers often give a body near an angle more weight, so before reading a Lilith close to the composite Descendant as a contact with it, check both birth times and say which method drew it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The 7th is where natal Lilith is most often met in other people: [Lilith in the 7th house](/astrology/lilith-in-7th-house/) describes a hidden part a person tends to find first in a partner, and [their Lilith in your 7th house](/astrology/lilith-in-7th-house-synastry/) brings the other's untamed side into what partnership means to you. The composite version cuts out the go-between: the refusal belongs to the partnership itself. [Composite Lilith in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-6th-house/) keeps its rebellion in the daily round, [composite Lilith in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-8th-house/) takes it into trust and what is shared.

The hard part is defining the bond against others. A pair with this Lilith can come to need an opponent, a disapproving parent, a conventional couple, a rival, to know what it is, or can refuse every agreement on principle, including the ones it needs. It helps to write your own terms down together, revisit them when life changes, and let other partnerships be different without casting them as the enemy.

Composites are a technique I explain here rather than use; the astrologers who use it read this Lilith with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It predicts no wedding, no affair and no parting. And a chart of a partnership's private terms should be looked at only with both partners' knowledge ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
