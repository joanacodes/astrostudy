Some people keep a workplace running without anyone noticing how; with Taurus on the 6th house cusp, that's often you. The 6th is the house of daily work, habits and the body's upkeep, and Taurus brings it the art of making things last.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your manner with an ordinary working day comes from the sign on the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) works by tending. You tend to look after the tools, systems and routines that others take for granted, to refine a skill by doing it again and better, and to feel a day was well spent when something was kept in good order. You'd rather improve a method than tear it up, and colleagues come to count on the fact that what you look after keeps working. The body's daily care runs the same way: regular meals, enough sleep, a walk that happens whatever the weather. The habits you keep do more for you than the resolutions you make. What helps is a change of method now and then, chosen rather than imposed: you adapt best when you can see what the new way will keep. Scorpio opens the 12th opposite, so the steadiness people see at work often covers a private life of strong feeling.

Tradition gave the 6th to [Mars](/astrology/mars/) as its joy, the planet of effort at home in the house of toil, and Taurus is one of the two signs where Mars is in detriment. Effort in this house comes to you less in bursts than as persistence: the job done steadily for as long as it takes. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your 6th, and Libra, its other sign, usually opens your 11th or your 10th in these charts, so the people you work beside can become friends, or the daily work can feed your public standing. Look to Venus's house for what makes the work feel worth doing: the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

At London's latitude Taurus sits on the 6th cusp in about 1 birth in 9, a little more often than at New York's, 1 in 10; the houses are Placidus, which is what I use. It's also the cusp where Taurus most often holds a second one: about 4 London charts in 5, and more than half at New York ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). When Scorpio rises, Taurus runs on to the Descendant, so [Taurus on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/) is yours as well and Venus rules both your work and your partners; when Sagittarius rises, it has usually opened the 5th already ([Taurus on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-5th-house-cusp/)), and pleasure and work share a ruler. Only in other charts does it vanish from the cusps altogether ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

In this house I read work and the upkeep of the body. Illness, which the old books put here, isn't something a chart can diagnose or predict ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and Bad Fortune is an old name, not a verdict. And because the cusp comes from your birth time, I don't start without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
