Capricorn is the sign of structures and Uranus the planet that redesigns them, so a relationship with this composite tends to reform the frame it lives in rather than escape it. Commitments get rewritten instead of dropped, and every new arrangement is meant to last.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the bond's capacity to change its own form and keep its people free. [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), cardinal earth ruled by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), gives that capacity patience and a builder's eye. Uranus has no dignity in Capricorn, but the meeting is an old one: Saturn ruled Aquarius before Uranus was found in 1781, and still does for astrologers who work traditionally ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), so here Uranus sits in the home sign of the planet that held Aquarius before it. Composite Saturn shows the structure this pair keeps while it reforms the rest.

As a third thing, the relationship changes from inside the frame. It doesn't throw out commitments; it rewrites them: a contract with an unusual clause, a household run like a small cooperative, a career moved as a joint decision. Its freedom comes with responsibility attached, and its inventions are meant to work in ten years' time, not only this week. Two restless people can find that together they plan; two cautious ones, that together they reform. [Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/uranus-in-capricorn/) belongs to a generation that reshaped institutions from within; in the composite, the institution is the two of you.

Saturn's sign holds about 1 composite Uranus in 12 among the random pairs in the count, born 1930–2009. Same-age couples take it from their birth years, February 1988 to January 1996, so they are in their thirties now. An age gap can land here too: a pair born in mid-1985 and mid-2000, aged about 41 and 26, with Uranus at about 16° Sagittarius and 21° Aquarius respectively, has a composite Uranus at about 18° Capricorn.

The trap is reform postponed into the plan, or a new structure as rigid as the one it replaced. A bond that likes order can turn its freedom into another set of rules, and the change it promised itself stays scheduled for next year. What helps is to put a date on the change and keep it, and to ask, every so often, which of your rules still serve you and which only serve the habit of having rules.

The sign comes with your generation; the house, which needs both birth times, is the part that belongs to the pair: [composite Uranus in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-10th-house/), Capricorn's natural house, carries the reform into public life and shared aims. [Composite Uranus in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-sagittarius/) widens beliefs; [composite Uranus in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-aquarius/) builds networks. The composite is a technique I describe without using it. Set beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), it says nothing about success, money or how long you'll last, and it is drawn only when both people want it read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
