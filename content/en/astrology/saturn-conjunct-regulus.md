Saturn conjunct Regulus belongs to three short stretches of birth dates, each about 29½ years after the last: September 1948 to May 1949, October 1977 to July 1978, and August to September 2007. About 1 person in 151 born between 1930 and 2009 has Saturn within a degree of the Lion's heart.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) as the part of a chart that builds to last: structure, responsibility, the boundaries you keep and the long work of making something hold. It is where life asks for patience. [Regulus](/astrology/regulus/) is a king's star by its names, and Ptolemy gave it the nature of Mars and Jupiter. Laid over Saturn, those names make an old image of authority built slowly and kept by work. I'd put it more plainly: the part of life Saturn occupies may ask you to take responsibility early and to build carefully, and that is Saturn's work whatever star it meets. A timetable, not a wall.

All three stretches put Saturn in the last degrees of Leo ([Saturn in Leo](/astrology/saturn-in-leo/)). Leo faces Aquarius, one of Saturn's traditional homes, so the old books count Saturn there in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/): their word for a planet working in unfamiliar conditions, not for a lesser Saturn. The three stretches still ran very differently. In 1977–78 Saturn crossed the star's degree three times: forward in November, back in January while retrograde, forward again in July. In 1948 it crossed once, in September, came back towards the star the following spring and stood still on 1 May 1949, about a fifth of a degree from it, before moving on; births in April and May 1949 have Saturn almost motionless beside the star. In 2007 it went straight over, on 2 September, and was clear of it by mid-month ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)).

The stretches are each other's Saturn returns. Saturn takes about 29½ years to come round, so those born with it on Regulus in 1948–49 had their [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/) on the same star in 1977–78, and those born then had theirs in 2007. Its next visit, about thirty years after 2007, will find the star in Virgo, where precession has carried it since around 2012.

In the sky the two really meet. Regulus lies close to the Sun's path and Saturn's orbit tilts only a little, so on each pass Saturn went by about a degree north of the star; through the winter of 1977–78 the pair stood near enough to see together.

Saturn is one of the three placements that held most often in my notebook, 75 to 90% of the time, with Chiron and Lilith, and Regulus was never on the list ([The three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). So the part of this contact I'd stand behind is Saturn's sign, house and aspects; the star I leave to the tradition, since the charts I read are drawn without stars. Saturn's contact is shared by everyone born in the same few months, where the [Sun conjunct Regulus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-regulus/), [Moon conjunct Regulus](/astrology/moon-conjunct-regulus/), [Venus conjunct Regulus](/astrology/venus-conjunct-regulus/) and [Mars conjunct Regulus](/astrology/mars-conjunct-regulus/) change with the day or the hour. [Conjunctions to fixed stars](/astrology/conjunctions-to-fixed-stars/) sets out the rule all five follow, and [fixed stars in astrology](/astrology/fixed-stars-in-astrology/) where it comes from.
