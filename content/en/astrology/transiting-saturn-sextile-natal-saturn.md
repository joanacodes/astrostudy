The Saturn return and the squares get the attention, but Saturn's cycle also has quiet steps. About five years after each return, and again about five years before the next, Saturn makes a sextile to its own birth place: an opening on the long project your Saturn describes, offered rather than imposed.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your natal [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) shows where you build slowly: the responsibility you carry, the structure you're learning to make, the area of life where things take longer. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates two functions through opportunity and cooperation, and opportunity asks for engagement. Here the function meets itself two signs on, so the question becomes practical: what could make the work of your Saturn easier to sustain?

The two kinds of sextile do different jobs. The waxing ones, about five years after a return, at about 34 and 64, tend to bring the first workable routines for whatever the return made you choose. The waning ones, about five years before a return, at about 24 and 54, suit preparation: tidying what you carry, learning the skills the next review will ask for. Neither forces anything. Of all Saturn's checkpoints these are the easiest to sleep through, and the cheapest to use.

Everyone born in the same few months meets them at almost the same age, so what makes yours personal is your Saturn's sign, aspects and house. With natal Saturn in the 1st, the sextile comes as Saturn crosses your 3rd or your 11th ([Saturn transiting the 11th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-11th-house/)), and a course, a network or a group offers a better structure for the way you carry yourself. Your Saturn's house is the part your birth time decides, and the part I won't do without ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each sextile stays in orb for about nine months, counting 1½° before exact and 1° after, which is plenty of time to set up one new routine and see it through a season. In a little over half of Saturn's [transits](/astrology/transits/) the opening comes in three exact passes over about nine and a half months; once in a while Saturn crosses it in three weeks and moves on.

The first came between 3½ and 6 for everyone born from 1930 to 2009, at 4.6 in the middle: at 4.3 for someone born on 1 January 1965, at 4.6 for 1 January 1975, at 5.2 for 1 January 1985. That one belongs to childhood, and this site doesn't read children's charts ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)); I name it only because the cycle starts there. By 80, everyone has met exactly five sextiles.

In the cycle, each waxing sextile comes a couple of years before [transiting Saturn square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-saturn/), then [transiting Saturn trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-saturn/) and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-saturn/) follow ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)). The waning sextile is the last step before the [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/), about five years ahead of it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take one practical step on your Saturn's long project: a course, a routine, a commitment you can keep for years.

At the sextile before a return, put things in order. What you tidy now is what you won't have to sort under pressure later.

At the sextile after one, turn the return's decisions into a schedule. A choice without a structure tends to fade by the next checkpoint.
