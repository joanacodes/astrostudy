Moon sextile Chiron puts about 60° between your need for comfort and the place where you've had to adapt, close enough for the two to trade. What you've learned by finding your own way round a sensitive spot becomes something you can use, gently, on a bad day.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) describes what you need to feel steady: attachment, felt security, familiar habits. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the knack of living around a sensitivity or limit that persists: it compensates, accommodates, builds skill by repetition and passes on what experience teaches. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperation. Neither takes over; each offers the other something, if you take it up.

On an ordinary day, this can be as simple as knowing what you need when something tender is touched: a walk, a call, an early night, a few honest words. The adapting you've done gives your comfort practical tools rather than wishes. It can also make you good company for others in a similar place, because you share methods rather than advice. The condition, as with every sextile, is engagement: this know-how waits until you reach for it.

The risk is underrating it. Skills learned around a sensitivity often don't feel like skills, so they go unused, or get offered to everyone except yourself.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, this sextile tends to show as resourcefulness around care. Sextiles pair elements that help each other along: with Chiron in an earth sign and the Moon in a water sign, adaptations become solid routines that steady your feelings; with Chiron in an air sign and the Moon in a fire sign, they become words and ideas that lift your spirits. How long a run of birth years shares Chiron's sign depends on the sign, from under two years to about eight; the opening itself sits wherever your Moon falls.

This sextile is in the chart of about 1 person in 11, within 8°; the closer it is to exact, the more readily that know-how comes to hand.

Each other Moon–Chiron aspect in a line:

- **[Moon conjunct Chiron](/astrology/moon-conjunct-chiron/):** comfort and adaptation are fused, and your way of caring carries the tender spot inside it.
- **[Moon square Chiron](/astrology/moon-square-chiron/):** the sensitivity and the need for comfort rub, and asking for help is the lesson.
- **[Moon trine Chiron](/astrology/moon-trine-chiron/):** adapting is second nature, and care flows without effort.
- **[Moon opposite Chiron](/astrology/moon-opposite-chiron/):** the tender place is met through others, before you recognise it as your own.

When one chart's Chiron sextiles another's Moon, [Moon sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/moon-sextile-chiron-synastry/) can be a gentle, useful bond: one person's experience helps the other feel looked after, without pressure.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-moon/) forms whenever Chiron, in the sky, reaches about 60° from your natal Moon. Chiron spends about five months of every year retrograde, so its transits often come in passes ([Natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)), and how long this one lasts depends on how fast Chiron is moving through that part of its orbit. Astrologers read it as a favourable time to try a new way of looking after yourself, or to share what you know with someone who needs it.

Briefer still, the Moon passes 60° from your natal Chiron twice a month. Treat both as weather, not a forecast: an easier moment to be kind to a tender spot, if you choose to.
