The part of you that wants more and the part that wants it to last stop arguing for a while each time Jupiter trines your birth Saturn, usually from a sign of the same element. It happens twice a round, for days or months, and patient work gets a tailwind.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the part of you that takes the long view: it builds slowly, keeps its word and measures progress in years. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens, looks for meaning, wants to go further. Under a [trine](/astrology/trine/), Jupiter's growth arrives in a form Saturn can work with, and Saturn's caution reads as good sense rather than a brake.

In practice, long efforts meet support. A project you've been carrying alone finds a collaborator, a commitment you renewed out of duty starts to feel chosen, a skill you've practised for years becomes easy to show. It's a good season to consolidate what has grown: put it on firmer ground before the next push.

The element matters. Born with Saturn in Aries, its fall in tradition, you meet the trines with Jupiter in Leo and in Sagittarius, Jupiter's own sign, all three of them fire. Your Saturn's house, and the house Jupiter is crossing, say where the support turns up, and both depend on the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). I call Saturn a timetable, not a wall: the trine is one of the stretches when the timetable runs on time.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within the orbs I work with, 1½° before exact to 1° after, a trine Jupiter crosses once holds for about eleven days. Stretched by a retrograde loop, as happens in about three [transits](/astrology/transits/) out of ten, it holds for about eight months, with three exact hits. Two trines in every round add up to thirteen or fourteen by 80 for births between 1930 and 2009.

The first one came early for some and late for others: at about 1.8 for a birth on 1 January 1945, at about 6.6 for 1 January 1995. The trine is native to anyone born with [Jupiter trine Saturn](/astrology/jupiter-trine-saturn/), and for them each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) brings it round again.

Around the trine, the rest of the round keeps its rhythm. [Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-saturn/) comes about four years before the first trine and four after the second; the squares ([transiting Jupiter square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-saturn/)) sit a year or so further out, towards the conjunction; [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-saturn/) between the two; [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-saturn/) is the trine's lighter cousin. With [transiting Saturn trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-jupiter/) the roles turn round, and Saturn lends your beliefs a frame for weeks or months.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Consolidate. Give whatever has grown in recent years a sturdier form: a routine that will hold when you're busy, an agreement put in writing, a skill taken through to its qualification.

Accept the support that's offered. Saturn is used to doing things alone, and a trine is wasted on someone who turns every hand away.

And use the ease for maintenance as well as progress. Repairs, renewals and the dull upkeep of long commitments cost less in these weeks than at any other point of the round.
