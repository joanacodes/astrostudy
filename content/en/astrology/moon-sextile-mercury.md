Moon sextile Mercury is the most easily overlooked of the pair's contacts: nothing about it insists. It describes feelings and words that get on well and can lend each other a hand, a door between heart and head that opens as soon as you push it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) keeps track of what you feel and need, and of how you respond when something changes. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) makes sense of things and says them: it learns, interprets, asks and answers. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates two functions through cooperation, the kind that switches on when it's used.

In plain words, a thought can calm a feeling and a feeling can steer a thought, provided you let them meet. Writing a worry down shrinks it to its real size; naming a mood before a decision keeps the mood from making the decision alone. Other people notice it too, because you can help them find words for what they feel without taking the feeling away from them. The catch is the sextile's usual one: it waits. If you never stop to say what's going on inside, the link sits idle, and you may not know you have it.

Two signs apart, the sextile pairs elements that get on without being alike. With the Moon in Taurus and Mercury in Cancer, a steady need for comfort meets words that look after people; with the Moon in Leo and Mercury in Libra, warmth that wants to be seen meets words chosen to keep things fair.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Day to day, this sextile shows as conversations that comfort and learning that steadies, with a knack for fitting what you say to the mood in the room. The houses show where the door is: with the Moon in the 4th and Mercury in the 6th, talking through the day's work at home is what settles you.

About 1 person in 11 has this sextile within 8°, twice the share of the conjunction, because the Moon passes a sextile to Mercury on both sides each month: once pulling ahead, once closing in from behind. And since Mercury stays near the Sun, those two windows fall in the crescent days or close to a quarter: nobody with this sextile was born under a full moon. Close to exact, the link is easy to find; at 7° or 8° you may have to go looking for it. My notebook counted Mercury by sign and house, where it held about a third of the time, like most of what I checked ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)); this aspect has no figure of its own.

The sextile sits between the pair's other contacts like a friendly middle:

- **[Moon conjunct Mercury](/astrology/moon-conjunct-mercury/):** the two as a single process, from a birth close to the new moon; there's no door to open, because they share a room.
- **[Moon square Mercury](/astrology/moon-square-mercury/):** the same two at cross purposes; where the sextile offers, the square insists.
- **[Moon trine Mercury](/astrology/moon-trine-mercury/):** the wider, easier cousin, feeling and words in one element, flowing before anyone asks.
- **[Moon opposite Mercury](/astrology/moon-opposite-mercury/):** feeling and thought facing each other across the chart, with other people often holding one side.

Between two charts, [Moon sextile Mercury in synastry](/astrology/moon-sextile-mercury-synastry/) lets one person's needs and the other's words find each other easily, as long as somebody starts the conversation. I read it both ways round, since who opens up and who finds the words isn't always mutual.

## In transits {#in-transits}

When the Moon in the sky sextiles your natal Mercury, twice a month for some hours, it's an easy window for a talk that matters. Mercury sextiles your natal Moon about twice a year, once from each side, and again when a retrograde brings it back: a day or two when the right words for a feeling are within reach.

Weather, not a forecast: what this transit offers is an opening, and only if you take it.
