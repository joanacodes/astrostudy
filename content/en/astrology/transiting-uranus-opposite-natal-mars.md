The far end of Uranus's round with your Mars comes when the planet of change stands directly across the zodiac from it. For about twenty months, much of what tests your drive comes from other people's moves rather than your own.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is initiative and assertion: going after what you want, and defending your ground. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) liberates and differentiates. In an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, the two are set across from each other, so your drive is seen against someone else's need for freedom, or theirs against yours.

It often shows in shared plans. A partner changes direction; a colleague or a rival makes a sudden move; a team you rely on reorganises. Your effort has to adapt to circumstances you didn't choose, and the useful question becomes what you'd do if you stopped waiting for them. Sometimes you're the one breaking away, and the people around you have to adapt to you. Either way, the opposition goes better when both sides say early what they're about to change. Competition shifts character too: a rival's new approach can teach you more than beating them would.

Disputes can flare when two people want to act freely at once, and I don't read how any of them will end, in court or out of it, from this [transit](/astrology/transits/) or any other ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)).

With Mars in your 2nd and Uranus crossing your 8th ([Uranus transiting the 8th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-8th-house/)), something you share with others, a joint project, a household arrangement, the terms of a partnership, changes and presses on what you build by your own effort. Without your birth time there's no 2nd and no 8th to speak of, so there's no reading of this opposition either ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Mars opposite Uranus](/astrology/mars-opposite-uranus/), you'll find your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), around 40, lands on Mars rather than across from it: for you, the midlife transit is the conjunction.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Uranus spends about twenty months on this opposition, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use for timing; a tenth of passes are over in eleven months or less. In more than nine passes out of ten Uranus crosses the exact point three times, so a standoff has time to turn into an agreement.

Uranus needs about 42 years to get here from [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-mars/), and passes [transiting Uranus sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-mars/), [transiting Uranus square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-mars/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-mars/) on the way, each of which it meets again on the other side. The opposition is missed by about one person in 33 before 80, and met once by everyone else, since one round of Uranus holds only one.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Adapt the method, keep the goal. When someone else changes the plan, your aim can usually survive on a new route.

Negotiate space rather than fight for it. Two people who both want freedom can usually have it, once they agree where each one's room begins.

Notice when you're pushing back for the sake of it. The opposition can make resistance feel like independence, so check which one it is.
