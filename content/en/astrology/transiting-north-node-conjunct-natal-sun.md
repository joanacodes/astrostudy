In the year the North Node crosses your Sun, your birthday falls in eclipse season, the weeks when the Sun stands near the Moon's nodes. For about a month, the direction your chart grows towards sits on your sense of who you are.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity, vitality and purpose. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a capacity you have practised less. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses them, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) becoming more yourself and practising the unfamiliar turn out, for a while, to be the same work.

It often shows as a role you are ready to try before you feel qualified for it: speaking for yourself where you usually let others speak, leading where you have always assisted. The node's blind spot is taking difficulty as proof that something is right for you. The Sun's test is gentler: after a little practice, does it feel more like you? Across the chart the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) faces your Sun, holding the version of you that people already know.

Some astrologers of a modern school call this a meeting with destiny: their school's word, not a fact. I read a direction worth practising, and the choice stays yours.

Your Sun's house says where the practice is asked for: a Sun in the 10th meets the node during its months at the top of the chart ([North Node transiting the 10th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-10th-house/)), and the new step tends to be public. The birth date alone fixes the Sun's degree to within half a degree, since it moves about a degree a day, and with it this transit's dates; the house rests on the minute of birth, and a chart without that minute is one I leave unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

That year an eclipse can even land on your birthday: with the node in Aquarius until March 2028, the annular solar eclipse of 26 January 2028, at 6°10′ Aquarius, falls on the birthday of people born around 26 January, close enough to their Sun to count as a contact of its own ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)): emphasis, never an event.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Nineteen times in twenty the true node crosses your Sun's degree once and moves on, after about a month inside my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after. Its pace follows the Sun's angle to it, so here the calendar sets the length. If the exact crossing falls near your birthday, the Sun is lined up with the node, the node barely moves, and the pass can last three months or more, as about one in ten does; about one in twenty-five crosses your Sun three times. If it falls three months or so from your birthday, the Sun is square to the node, which is then at its quickest, and the pass can be over in three and a half weeks. The mean node, which I look at too, takes about seven weeks every time ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

One conjunction per round of the nodes, roughly every 18.6 years, means four or five by 80. To find your first, count the signs from your Sun forwards to your natal North Node: the node slides back through them at about eighteen months a sign. With the two together at birth ([Sun conjunct North Node](/astrology/sun-conjunct-north-node/)) there is nothing to count, and the transit keeps time with your [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/).

The rest of the round, counted in birthdays: [transiting North Node sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-sun/) about three later, [transiting North Node square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-sun/) between the fourth and the fifth, [transiting North Node trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-sun/) around the sixth, and [transiting North Node opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-sun/), with the South Node on your Sun, around the ninth.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one thing you would do if you trusted that it suited you, and practise it during the month the node is on your Sun.

Treat the birthday of that year as a starting line rather than a review.

Keep the familiar version of you within reach. The South Node across the chart is a resource, not a rival.
