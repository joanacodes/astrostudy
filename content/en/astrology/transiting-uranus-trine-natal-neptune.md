When transiting Uranus reaches 120° from your natal Neptune, imagination and invention run the same way for about twenty months. Most people meet this trine twice, and the first comes about five years earlier with each decade of birth: in the early fifties for people born in the mid-1940s, in the early twenties for those born in 2005.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and empathises; it senses meaning beyond the literal and longs for something larger. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) invents and frees. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates two functions through flow while each keeps its identity, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) lets what you imagine and what you can newly do support each other.

It tends to show as ease wherever you dream: making art comes more readily, a spiritual practice finds a form that suits you, a wish to help others finds a channel, and new ideas arrive with some poetry in them. Things you've pictured for years can start to look possible, without the friction a square would bring.

The trine's risk is drift. Neptune likes to float and a trine doesn't push, so twenty months can pass in pleasant inspiration with nothing made. A new enthusiasm can also look more finished than it is: the ease is real, but it's an ease for beginning.

With Neptune in your 1st and Uranus crossing your 5th ([Uranus transiting the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-5th-house/)), play, creative work and what you make for the joy of it take on the imaginative colour of your whole manner. Those houses hang on your birth time; a chart without it is one I don't read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The trine stays within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, for about twenty months, eleven at the shortest, long enough to finish something begun in its first months; in more than nine passes out of ten Uranus is exactly 120° from your Neptune three times.

Uranus is crossing Gemini until 2033 ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)), which puts it 120° from every natal Neptune in Aquarius, the Neptune of births from January 1998 to early 2012: those born up to about 2008 are meeting their first trine in their twenties. The Libra Neptune of births from late 1942 to 1957 gets its second trine in the same years, near 80.

In the site's count the first trine came at about 52 for someone born at the start of 1945, about 42 for 1965, about 33 for 1985 and about 23½ for 2005. Born in 1936–46 with [Uranus trine Neptune](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune/), one trine point sat right beside your natal Uranus, so that transit fell in your first year or two, or just before you were born; the other comes in your early or mid-fifties. For the Libra Neptune of the mid-1940s the second can fall after 80, at about 82 for someone born at the start of 1945, which leaves that generation one; every birth from 1930 to 2009 has at least one.

[Transiting Uranus square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-neptune/) comes about seven years before the first trine, and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-neptune/) about fourteen after it. [Transiting Uranus conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Uranus sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-neptune/) belong to the far side of the round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make one thing from the inspiration. A trine to Neptune is generous with ideas; what it rewards is the one you finish.

Learn the tool that matches the vision: an instrument, a piece of software, a technique. Uranus in trine makes the learning feel light.

Say the dream out loud while it's easy to talk about. Told to the right person now, it often finds the collaborator it lacked.
