Pluto in Scorpio is Pluto in its own sign in modern tropical astrology, and its shortest stay anywhere: November 1983 to November 1995. The planet of deep change works here in the manner that suits it best, and I read it as intensity, intimacy and a refusal to leave anything unexamined.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the function of transformation: release, regeneration, the slow integration of what lies deep. [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), fixed water, is the manner most in tune with it: intense, private, loyal, unwilling to stay on the surface. When planet and sign agree this closely, nothing dilutes the meaning. This generation goes to the bottom of things, feelings, secrets and taboos included, and comes back changed. Being fixed, it holds on while it does so: transformation here is thorough, and slow to let go.

Modern tropical astrology makes Scorpio Pluto's [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), its home sign. Tradition gave Scorpio to [Mars](/astrology/mars/) long before Pluto was found, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). With Pluto in its own sign, Mars matters even more than usual. It is the personal planet of the pair, and its sign and house say how a generation's intensity turns into one person's way of acting, desiring and defending what matters.

Pluto was in Scorpio from November 1983 to November 1995, the quickest it crosses any sign, because this is the part of its orbit nearest the Sun. It returns from December 2229 to October 2242. The first months, to August 1984, are shared with [Pluto in Libra](/astrology/pluto-in-libra/), and January to November 1995 with [Pluto in Sagittarius](/astrology/pluto-in-sagittarius/); for those births, the chart decides. About 1 person in 7 born from 1930 to 2009 has it. A short stay still makes a generation's placement: the house is what makes it yours, and the house needs a [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/).

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Intimacy is the heart of the matter for Pluto in Scorpio. Love is all or nothing, and trust, once given, is given completely, which is why it is given slowly. There is a capacity to stay present through someone's worst moments, and a wish to be known that deeply in return. The tender points are jealousy and secrecy: fear of betrayal can turn into testing a partner, or into keeping parts of oneself locked away. Relationships thrive here on honesty about feelings, the hard ones included, and on privacy respected rather than pried open.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Pluto is at its best where others won't go: crisis, research, investigation, psychology, the problem everyone has been avoiding. It sees what lies beneath the official story, and it has the stamina to stay with a hard situation until it changes. Other people's confidences and shared resources are safe in its hands, because it takes trust seriously. It needs work with depth; shallow tasks drain it faster than hard ones.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With Pluto in Scorpio, the hard part is control. When feelings run this deep, the urge to manage them, and the people who stir them, can be strong: knowing where everyone stands, keeping the upper hand, never being caught off guard. Suspicion can outlive its reasons. And letting go can be the hardest thing of all, whether of a grievance, a relationship or an old version of oneself. This is depth without enough safety, and safety can be built.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Trust built in steps, with someone who has earned the next one. Saying what you feel while it is still small. Letting an ending finish: marking it, writing it down, allowing it to be grieved. Watching Mars, the sign's other ruler, since where it sits shows how you fight for what matters, and so where a fight can be chosen rather than fallen into. And remembering that transformation doesn't have to be dramatic. With Pluto in Scorpio most of it happens quietly, from the inside, and that is enough.
