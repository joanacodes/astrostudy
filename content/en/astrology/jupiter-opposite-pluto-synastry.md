In this pair, someone is always opening windows and someone is always checking the foundations. Across the zodiac from each other, about 180° apart within 5°, one person's Jupiter and the other's Pluto make Jupiter opposite Pluto in synastry.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) takes the wide view: it relates one experience to many and looks for the principle that joins them. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) takes the deep view: it looks beneath appearances for the motive, the attachment, the pattern that keeps a situation as it is. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) makes each function visible by setting it against the other; in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the setting-against happens between two people, and so does the seeing.

Facing each other, Jupiter's growth shows Pluto's depth what it might become, and Pluto's depth shows Jupiter's growth what it might be avoiding.

Hold the Jupiter, and you speak for possibility, generosity, the bigger picture. Facing you is someone who wants to know what each hope rests on, and their questions can feel like suspicion of anything hopeful.

Hold the Pluto, and you speak for the truth under the surface, for clearing what has stopped working. Facing you is someone who keeps widening the frame, and their breadth can feel like a way of never landing on what hurts.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opposition is a seesaw, not enmity, and this one swings between breadth and depth. Each person can exaggerate their end: the Jupiter person growing more expansive the more the Pluto person digs, the Pluto person digging harder the more the other widens. Pluto can mistake secrecy for depth, Jupiter can mistake scope for wisdom, and the middle, where a deep thing gets room to grow, goes unvisited.

Closer angles, for comparison:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-pluto-synastry/):** growth and depth on the same degree, intensity instead of distance.
- **[Jupiter sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-pluto-synastry/):** four signs nearer, a quiet opening between the two.
- **[Jupiter square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-pluto-synastry/):** three signs nearer, widening and deepening at right angles.
- **[Jupiter trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-trine-pluto-synastry/):** two signs nearer, a current in which growth and renewal lean on each other, in one element.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Visit the middle on purpose. When a decision comes up, let the Pluto person name what must be faced and the Jupiter person what could open, then look for the step that does both. Swap ends now and then: the Jupiter person asks the deep question, the Pluto person names the hope. This opposition shows up between partners who meet change differently, friends who process things at different depths, a parent and an adult child, colleagues of whom one builds and the other audits.

The dates are oddly selective here. Someone born in mid-1975 has Jupiter opposite the Pluto of everyone born between July 1979 and December 1981; for a person born in mid-1935, 1945, 1955, 1995 or 2005, nobody born between 1930 and 2009 has Pluto opposite their Jupiter at all. A contact the calendar hands out this unevenly tells me about eras, not individuals, so it stays out of the aspects I count in synastry, which need the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. In the houses, it reads as two eras facing each other across breadth and depth.

Pluto barely shifts in a month, let alone between morning and evening, so the hour can't change this opposition; it sets the houses, which this reading rests on, and I ask for both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

For a person born with [Jupiter opposite Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-pluto/), breadth and depth pull at two ends of one chart. Between two charts, the pull happens across a room rather than inside one person, which makes it easier to see and easier to share.
