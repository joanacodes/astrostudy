Venus in the 10th house stands where a chart is most visible, near the top, in the house of public life. Your values and your way of relating are on show here: at work, in your reputation, in what you build in the world. I read it as a gift for being liked in public, and a working life that goes best when it stays true to your taste.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is the life other people see: your work and standing, the name you make, the thing you're trying to build out in the world. Think of it as a direction of travel rather than a title on a door. Angular, it begins at the Midheaven, which in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), my house system, is where the zodiac crossed your meridian at the moment of birth. Its natural sign is Capricorn. With [Venus](/astrology/venus/) here, grace, taste and a talent for relating become part of how others see you at work: people tend to find you easy to deal with, and you may be known for fairness, good judgement or a sense of style.

Venus was high in the sky, approaching its highest point, when you were born; since the Sun is never far away, that usually means a birth in the late morning or around midday. The Midheaven moves quickly with the time. A few minutes later, Venus would have crossed it into [Venus in the 9th house](/astrology/venus-in-9th-house/); earlier, it might still be in [Venus in the 11th house](/astrology/venus-in-11th-house/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

I don't read a chart for career outcomes. Venus here promises no success, promotion or fame. It describes what you value in public life, and how you tend to relate to the people you work with and for.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, a 10th-house Venus can blur the line between private and public. You may meet partners through work or within your field, and a relationship can become part of how others see you, which brings pride and some pressure. You tend to admire competence and purpose in a partner, and to want each of you to be proud of the other. The house across is the 4th, home, and the balance runs between them: the person who knows you at home deserves as much of your warmth as the people who see you at work.

## At work {#at-work}

This is where this Venus shines most. Work with people, beauty or diplomacy, the arts, design, fashion, public relations, hospitality, negotiation, any role where the way you deal with others is part of the job, can suit it. You may lead through charm and fairness rather than force, and colleagues often trust your judgement on how things should look or sound. A harsh, ugly or unfair workplace can sap you faster than most; one that values grace lets you do your best work.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Approval can become a compass. You may shape choices around how they'll look to others, or find it hard to risk disapproval even when it would be right to. Reputation can matter so much that a single criticism lingers for weeks. And because public life comes easily, private life can get less of your attention than it needs.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose by your own taste first, and let reputation follow. Practise disagreeing in public with the same grace you bring to agreeing. Keep a measure of success that has nothing to do with being liked: a piece of work you're proud of, a skill you've earned. And guard unseen time at home as fiercely as you guard your public commitments.

Venus held about one time in three in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). A Venus this close to the Midheaven is one of the more visible things in a chart, but visible isn't the same as certain, and I tell you as much whenever a reading leans on it.
