Young adult couples are the ones most likely to have composite Pluto in Sagittarius. Pluto crossed Sagittarius from January 1995 to November 2008, so pairs born in those years, now aged about 18 to 31, carry it, and it sets the bond's deep changes in belief, learning and the long road.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) of such a pair, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) stands in [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire ruled by Jupiter, the sign that explores, learns and looks for meaning. The bond's deep changes come through what it believes and how far it will go to find out: a move abroad decided over one weekend, a faith taken up or set down together, years of study that leave both people thinking differently, a shared sense of what life is for, rebuilt after it stopped making sense. In astrological terms, the relationship renews itself by widening its horizon.

A creed for two is what to watch for. A worldview the pair fought hard to reach can harden until doubting it feels like disloyalty, and the relationship stops asking the questions that made it. A conviction that can be questioned is still a conviction. Because the sign is Jupiter's, composite [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where this appetite for meaning grows, and where it has room to.

For a pair born between January 1995 and November 2008 the sign is close to a given: both birth charts almost always hold [Pluto in Sagittarius](/astrology/pluto-in-sagittarius/), and the halfway point between them can't leave it. Partners of different ages can land on it from either side: born in June 1992, with Pluto at about 21° Scorpio, and in June 2005, with Pluto at about 24° Sagittarius, a pair has its composite Pluto at about 7° Sagittarius. The count of random pairs born 1930–2009 finds it in only about 1 pair in 16, because Sagittarius sits at the young end of those years; for two people born within its years, it's the expected one.

Being a generation's placement, the sign describes the era more than the pair. The house carries the reading, and it needs both birth times: the house of distant travel and beliefs, for one, would make the search for meaning the relationship's main business ([composite Pluto in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-9th-house/)). [Composite Pluto in Scorpio](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-scorpio/) is the composite Pluto of pairs a decade or so older; [composite Pluto in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-capricorn/) is only now reaching couples old enough to have one.

Conviction can be used as pressure.
