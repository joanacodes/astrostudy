The 6th's ruler in the 4th brings the working day home. The desk in the spare room, the family business, the care of a household: your routines start and end at your own door.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Home is where the 6th's business gets done when its ruler sits in the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), at the root of the chart, among home, family and the past ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) covers the tasks of an ordinary day and the care you give yourself, and here they settle indoors.

I read it as work with a front door. You may work from home, run a business with relatives, keep a house as a real job, or look after someone you live with. The routines of the household you grew up in, the way meals were made or chores shared, often shaped the way you work now, for better or worse. The risk is a missing border: the laptop at the kitchen table at ten at night, or the chores that never count as work because nobody pays for them.

By sign, the 4th sits a sextile from the 6th, two signs back, and tradition counted the sextile among the helpful aspects, so work and home tend to cooperate. The 4th is also angular. It opens at the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the chart's foundation, and a ruler working from there works from underneath: steady, private, structural, holding up more of your life than anyone sees.

The planet shapes the household routine. The Moon, ruling a Cancer 6th, makes care itself the work: feeding people, keeping the house warm, noticing who needs what before they ask. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn 6th, runs a home on timetables and lists. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 6th, turns a corner of the house into an office and keeps the paperwork of family life in order.

Neptune in the 4th blurs the line between home and work, sometimes beautifully, as with the studio in the attic, sometimes less so, as with the job that seeps into every room. It rules your 6th along with Jupiter when Pisces is on the cusp ([Pisces on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/); [traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). I read both, and Jupiter, from its own house, tends to take on more than one household's share.

Which side of the IC the ruler falls is the question a birth time answers here. Close to that point, a few minutes later and it would sit in the 3rd, where work runs on messages and short trips ([Ruler of the 6th in the 3rd house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)). Towards the other edge of the 4th, a few minutes earlier would put it in the 5th, where work feels like play ([Ruler of the 6th in the 5th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)). I need the recorded minute to know ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

If your daily work includes looking after a relative, that care is real work, and it deserves to be counted as such. A chart can't tell you anything about their health or yours ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). And when home and work have no border, tiredness can build quietly; that's worth taking to a doctor or a therapist, not to a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).
