Gemini’s solar eclipses in my list are mostly annular: the Moon, too far from the Earth to cover the Sun, leaves a thin ring of light around it. Gemini links and compares, and an eclipse there lights up whatever connects you, when it touches your chart at all.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) is mutable air, ruled by Mercury: curiosity, comparison, the exchange of news and ideas between people. Its eclipses lie on the Gemini–Sagittarius axis, the near and the far, the question and the answer, and as the Moon’s nodes go round they pass from this sign to its opposite and back. A [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) in Gemini puts the Sun and Moon together in the sign, as on 10 June 2021 at 19°46′; a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) puts the Moon here and the Sun in Sagittarius, as on 20 December 2029, with the Moon at 29°18′ Gemini.

An eclipse in Gemini touches your chart only if it falls within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of its axis: that is the AGS rule I read by, and I read the point through its house. The penumbral lunar ones, four of the sixteen, weigh less. Nodes I take both ways, mean and true. None of it is an omen.

Sixteen between 2000 and 2040:

- **2002–2003:** 10 June 2002, annular solar, 19°54′ · 31 May 2003, annular solar, 9°19′
- **2010–2012:** 21 December 2010, total lunar, 29°22′ · 1 June 2011, partial solar, 11°02′ · 10 December 2011, total lunar, 18°08′ · 20 May 2012, annular solar, 0°20′ · 28 November 2012, penumbral lunar, 6°40′
- **2020–2021:** 30 November 2020, penumbral lunar, 8°44′ · 10 June 2021, annular solar, 19°46′
- **2029–2031:** 12 June 2029, partial solar, 21°29′ · 20 December 2029, total lunar, 29°18′ · 1 June 2030, annular solar, 10°50′ · 9 December 2030, penumbral lunar, 17°47′ · 21 May 2031, annular solar, 0°04′
- **2038–2039:** 11 December 2038, penumbral lunar, 19°52′ · 30 November 2039, partial lunar, 8°22′

The last was the annular eclipse of 10 June 2021; the next, the partial solar eclipse of 12 June 2029. Several come back on almost the same day after nineteen years, at almost the same degree: 1 June 2011 and 1 June 2030, 21 December 2010 and 20 December 2029. The signs on either side have their own pages ([Eclipse in Taurus](/astrology/eclipse-in-taurus/), [Eclipse in Cancer](/astrology/eclipse-in-cancer/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Only [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) places Gemini on a house cusp, so these lines wait on it; a chart without the time is one I leave unread. In Placidus, my house system, a line applies when a Gemini eclipse meets a natal point in that house, and the Sagittarius end of the axis can reach the house across from it.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/gemini-rising/):** Gemini rising: your quick, sociable way of meeting the world; how you introduce yourself, changing.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/gemini-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** the skills you trade on; the worth you put on what you know.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** Gemini in its natural house: siblings, neighbours, messages; a conversation that rearranges your everyday circle.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/):** talk at home; the way your household shares its news.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/):** play with words and ideas; a light romance, a game, a piece of writing.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/gemini-on-6th-house-cusp/):** varied daily work; how information moves through your working day.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Gemini on the Descendant, with Sagittarius rising: partners you talk with; what you tell each other, and what you leave out.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/gemini-on-8th-house-cusp/):** conversations about what’s shared; the paperwork of a joint life, read again.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/):** learning across distances; a language, a course or a journey that sets you comparing.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a public voice; work in words, contacts or teaching, in focus.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/):** networks and friends; whom you keep in touch with, and why.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/gemini-on-12th-house-cusp/):** the mind’s quiet hours; thoughts you don’t say aloud. The old books called this house Bad Spirit and tied it to confinement; in an adult’s chart I read private time here, and questions of mental health go to professionals, not to a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).
