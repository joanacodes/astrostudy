Their Moon in your 11th house brings someone's need to belong into your circle. Your friends, your groups and the future you're building with them become somewhere they can settle, and friendship between the two of you tends to have real tenderness in it.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) turns a chart towards many people at once: chosen friends, teams, communities, the networks that help without being asked, and the hopes you share with them. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds a person's need to belong and their way of looking after others. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), that need meets your people.

For you, they can make your circle feel more like family. They remember your friends' birthdays, notice who's been quiet, and bring a warmth to gatherings that others feel too. Your hopes for the future seem to matter to them personally, and talking plans with them can feel like being taken care of.

For them, your friends and communities can become a home. Your people can feel like theirs surprisingly quickly, and your shared causes give their feelings somewhere to go. A succedent house, the 11th keeps going what has been built in public, and [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), the sign of community and independence, corresponds to it. Its old name is the Good Spirit, and Jupiter rejoices there.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Group life and emotional needs don't always fit together. They may want one-to-one comfort when what you're offering is a crowded table, or feel left out when your friendships carry on without them. You might also find that your circle starts to feel like theirs, with their feelings setting the mood of evenings that used to be lighter.

From their side, belonging to your world can make them over-invest in it: looking after everyone, taking every disagreement in the group personally, or wondering where they stand with you once the guests have gone.

Whatever else the bond is, friendship is part of it here; what the two of you make of the rest is yours to decide.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep both the circle and the pair. Plan time that's only for the two of you as well as time with friends, and let each of you keep some friendships of your own. In a couple, shared friends are a joy as long as each person still has a few people of their own; between friends, this is often the one who quietly holds the group together, and who should be looked after too; between colleagues, it brings team spirit, provided feelings aren't left to run the team; between adult relatives of the same generation, family can start to feel like friendship.

From their chart, the picture reverses: your Moon finds its belonging in their 11th house, among their friends and hopes, and the same balance applies. Next door, [their Moon in your 10th house](/astrology/moon-in-10th-house-synastry/) cares about your public life, and [their Moon in your 12th house](/astrology/moon-in-12th-house-synastry/) about what you keep private.

Their birthday narrows their Moon to a stretch of about 13°, and within that stretch it can cross one of the 11th's cusps; only their birth time settles it. Your own time draws the house, and in Placidus, the house system I use, its size depends on the moment and the place of your birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). A reading about a friendship is still a reading of two charts, so both friends need to know about it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
