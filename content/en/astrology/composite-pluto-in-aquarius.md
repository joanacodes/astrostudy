Composite Pluto in Aquarius belongs to couples who don't exist yet: the people who could form them are, for now, in nurseries. Pluto entered Aquarius in March 2023 and settled there in November 2024, so a pair needs at least one person born since March 2023.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, thinks in systems and networks, values each person's individuality and keeps an eye on the future. Were [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) to put [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) there, the relationship would transform through what it belongs to: the circle of friends it gathers and remakes, the causes it signs up for, its experiments with how two people might live, share a home or divide their work. Astrologers would read a bond that renews itself by rethinking rules everyone else takes for granted. Aquarius is a fixed sign, though, so the experiments would be held to with real constancy: such a pair might change the world's habits more readily than its own.

Where it could go astray is in loving the idea of the relationship more than the two people in it: a partnership run on principle, where feelings get reviewed like a policy and anything personal looks like a lapse. Objectivity isn't coldness, and a shared ideal can make room for a bad day. Aquarius has two rulers, Uranus in modern tropical astrology and Saturn in the tradition, and I read both: composite [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) would show where the pair breaks with convention, composite [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) what it keeps.

For now this is a page about the future. Until one partner is born after March 2023, there is no Aquarian Pluto to average with, and the halfway point can't reach the sign. Pluto stays until January 2044, which means everyone with [Pluto in Aquarius](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius/) in their own chart is still a small child, and children's charts are never read here ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). A count of pairs born from 1930 to 2009 had no chance of finding it, and didn't. The first people who could share it as adults turn eighteen in 2041; the sign will then belong to a generation of couples, and its house, once both birth times are known, to each one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In the house of friends and shared hopes, it would make the circle around the pair the relationship's main subject ([composite Pluto in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-11th-house/)).

Pluto's previous stay in Aquarius ran from April 1777 to December 1798. Next door, [composite Pluto in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-capricorn/) is just reaching its first adult couples, and [composite Pluto in Pisces](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-pisces/) waits for people born from 2043. For the transit under way now, which touches every chart, see [Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/).
