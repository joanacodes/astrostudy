Jupiter opposite Uranus sets your sense of meaning at one end of an axis and your urge for change at the other. It comes round about every fourteen years, halfway between the pair's meetings, so its births come in short batches; your houses show where it pulls.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) stands for belief and growth: principles, learning, the frame that gives experience its sense. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part that frees and reforms: originality, independence, the readiness to break a frame that has become a cage. Set in [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), they become two poles of one line, each lighting up the other, with a tension that varies with circumstances instead of being fixed.

In plain words, two kinds of growth face each other: the steady widening of a worldview, and the leap that throws it open. You may hold to a philosophy and then feel stifled by it, or chase freedom and then miss having something to believe in. Often other people carry one end: a mentor with firm principles, a friend who refuses every rule. That isn't enmity; they are showing you half of your own range.

Seen from Jupiter's end, freedom can look careless; seen from Uranus's, a belief can look like a cage. The work is noticing which end you have been leaning on, and over time the axis tends to teach timing: when a framework deserves trust, and when it deserves to be broken.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

It is carried, within 6°, by about 1 person in 30 of those born from 1930 to 2009, in six stretches about fourteen years apart: 1933–34, 1948, 1962–63, 1975–76, 1989–90 and 2003–04, most of them in two spells. Being common to those months, it speaks for a cohort more than for a person ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)).

The houses bring it home. With Jupiter in the 6th and Uranus in the 12th, daily routines may carry your sense of meaning while your need for freedom works out of sight; with Jupiter in the 8th and Uranus in the 2nd, shared commitments you believe in may sit across from a very personal way of earning and owning. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Across the same fourteen-year cycle:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus/):** the meeting point, roughly seven years before and after this one.
- **[Jupiter sextile Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-uranus/):** an opening, for those born in 1971–72 or 1994, among others.
- **[Jupiter square Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-square-uranus/):** the friction of the quarter points, about every seven years.
- **[Jupiter trine Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-trine-uranus/):** the two flowing together, for the births of 1987–88 or 2000–01, for example.

[Jupiter opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-uranus-synastry/) sets one person's beliefs opposite the other's need for freedom: magnetic, at times maddening, and most fruitful when each lets the other widen their view.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Born with the opposition, you meet its transits on a pattern. [Transiting Jupiter opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-uranus/) comes back with each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/), every 12 years or so, for days or weeks. [Transiting Uranus opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-jupiter/) falls either in the first year or two of life or not until about 84, because the point it needs lies right beside your natal Uranus. The moment that matters more comes in between: at the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), around 42, transiting Uranus crosses your Jupiter itself.

Weather, not a forecast: a long season of reassessing what you believe and what you want to be free of, with nothing decided in advance.
