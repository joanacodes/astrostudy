Every chart that has a Part of Fortune has a Part of Spirit too, waiting on the other side of the Ascendant. They are built from the same three points and the same distance, counted the opposite way, and the old astrologers paired them on purpose: the Moon's lot and the Sun's.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

A lot is a point worked out rather than observed: a distance between two parts of a chart, measured again from the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/). The Part of Spirit is one of the seven [Hermetic lots](/astrology/hermetic-lots/), one for each of the traditional planets, and the one that belongs to the [Sun](/astrology/sun/). Its Greek name, *klēros daimonos*, is the lot of the daimon, the guiding spirit.

Its partner is the [Part of Fortune](/astrology/part-of-fortune/), the Moon's lot. Tradition split the work between them. Fortune describes the body and the circumstances that come to you; Spirit describes the mind and what you set out to do, the initiative you take, or the use you make of what you're given.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

By day, the Part of Spirit is the Ascendant plus the Sun minus the Moon. In plain words: see how far ahead of the Moon the Sun is, counting in the order of the signs, and count that same distance on from your Ascendant. By night it is reversed: the Ascendant plus the Moon minus the Sun. These are the formulas the Hermetic lots table gives, after Paulus Alexandrinus, and they are exactly the reverse of Fortune's.

Day or night is a matter of [sect](/astrology/sect-day-and-night-charts/): a day chart has the Sun above the horizon at birth, a night chart below it. Whichever applies, Spirit and Fortune always sit at the same distance from the Ascendant, one on each side of it. Born at a new moon, with the Sun and Moon together, you'd find both close to your Ascendant; born at a full moon, both close to the Descendant, at the same spot.

Three things make it delicate to calculate:

- **The birth time.** Spirit moves with the Ascendant, round the zodiac in about a day, so an unknown time could put it in any sign at all. [Without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) I don't read a chart, and this lot is one of the reasons.
- **Day or night.** If the Sun was right on the horizon at birth, a few minutes decide which formula applies.
- **The software.** Programs don't all reverse the formulas at night. For a night chart, one that never reverses Fortune is in fact showing the point that others call Spirit.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

Astrologers who work with the lots read Spirit as they read any lot: its sign and house, the planet ruling that sign and how well placed it is, and planets close to it. Where Fortune asks what keeps arriving for you, Spirit asks what you keep reaching for: the work, the choices and the direction you put your own will behind.

Spirit also has a place in timing. [Zodiacal releasing](/astrology/zodiacal-releasing/), one of the techniques in my forecasts, measures the chapters of a life from the lots, with the Lot of Spirit among them, and reads its periods for changes of emphasis in what you're working towards. It describes chapters, not events, and nothing in it promises success or warns of failure.

The [doctrine of the lots](/astrology/doctrine-of-the-lots/) explains how the other lots are built, several of them from Spirit itself.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Spirit means religion or the soul.”** The name translates *daimon*, a guiding spirit, and tradition read the lot for the mind and its intentions, not for faith.
- **“It's the same point as Fortune.”** Only when the Sun and Moon are exactly together or exactly opposite. Otherwise they sit on either side of the Ascendant.
- **“Every app uses the same formula.”** Some switch it at night and some don't; check before comparing.
- **“A strong Spirit guarantees success.”** It describes what you aim at, not whether you'll get there.
