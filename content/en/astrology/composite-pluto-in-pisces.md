No one alive was born with Pluto in Pisces, so no living couple has it in their composite. Pluto last crossed Pisces from 1797 to 1823 and returns in March 2043; this page describes what the sign would mean in a relationship, for the couples of the 2060s and after.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), mutable water, imagines, forgives, empathises and dissolves the lines between things. Placed there by [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) would transform a relationship through compassion: an old grievance finally let go rather than won, a shared spiritual life or practice that changes both people, music or pictures made together, care given to someone outside the pair that remakes the pair as well. Astrologers would read a bond that renews itself by understanding more, and that comes back from hard times softened rather than hardened. There's a fit here, too: a composite is itself made by blending two charts into one, and Pisces is the sign of blending.

Dissolving into each other would be the risk. Two people this attuned can lose track of whose feelings are whose, and forgiveness offered again and again can stand in for changing whatever keeps causing the hurt. Empathy needs edges to stay kind. Pisces belongs to Neptune in modern tropical astrology and to Jupiter in the tradition, and I read both: a composite like this would send you to composite [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) for where the pair blurs old lines, and to composite [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) for where its faith grows.

In practice, no couple has it yet: it would take one partner born before 1823, or after Pluto's return in March 2043. Pluto stays in Pisces until February 2068, and the earliest pairs who could share it as adults will appear in the 2060s, when the first people born with [Pluto in Pisces](/astrology/pluto-in-pisces/) since the 1820s turn eighteen. The count, which stops with births in 2009, never meets it. When such couples exist, a Pluto in the 12th house of their composite, the house of retreat and the inner life, would turn the theme further inward ([composite Pluto in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-12th-house/)).

Aquarius, before it, needs a partner born since 2023 ([composite Pluto in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-aquarius/)); Aries, after it, waits for births from the 2060s ([composite Pluto in Aries](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-aries/)). Imagination is this sign's gift, and the risk of a page like this one, so I've kept to what the sign means and what the dates allow ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).
