Uranus conjunct North Node puts the planet of change on the point that shows where you grow. The direction you're developing in asks you to differ, and the South Node, the ground you started from, lies opposite Uranus, as what you're learning to move away from.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, differentiation and liberation: it questions what's always been done and tries the alternative. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a capacity less familiar than what came easily, one you build through practice and feedback, while its partner the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) holds the familiar strengths you lean on. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) joins two functions in a single movement, neither losing its nature.

Here, growth and difference travel together. The new thing you are learning is, in part, the freedom to do things your own way: to experiment, to step out of roles that came ready-made, to update the methods you inherited. With Uranus opposite the South Node, the familiar can feel like the thing to break from, and the work is not to reject it outright. Development doesn't erase existing capacity; it adds range. Autonomy isn't isolation, either: the growth asks for independence, not distance from everyone.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 37 born between 1930 and 2009 has Uranus within 6° of the North Node. The count uses the true node; the mean node, a degree or two away at most, would change the verdict only for a birth at the very edge of the orb, and I check both ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The node is much the faster of the two, moving backwards through a sign in about 18 months against Uranus's seven years forwards, so the meeting lasts months, not years: it's personal, not a generation's.

The sign the two share describes the kind of difference growth asks for: in Capricorn, reforming a structure from within; in Gemini, learning to think and speak in your own voice. The house says where: with both in the 10th and the South Node in the 4th, the growth might lie in public, working life, done your own way, away from family patterns that once held you. For the houses, the birth time is essential ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus sextile North Node](/astrology/uranus-sextile-north-node/):** the pair's most common aspect in the count; experiment offers growth an opening.
- **[Uranus square North Node](/astrology/uranus-square-north-node/):** Uranus across the path, square to both nodes.
- **[Uranus trine North Node](/astrology/uranus-trine-north-node/):** change and growth flow together.
- **[Uranus opposite North Node](/astrology/uranus-opposite-north-node/):** Uranus on the South Node, renewing the familiar.

[Uranus conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) places one person's Uranus on the other's direction of growth: stimulating, sometimes unsettling, and different from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-uranus/) comes once in each 18.6-year round of the nodes and stays close for a month or two. Eclipses happen near the nodes, so any eclipse in that season lands in the neighbourhood of your Uranus.

Born with the conjunction, you'll find the transiting North Node back on your Uranus at every [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/), around 18½, 37 and 56. Between them comes the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), when the transiting South Node lies on your Uranus instead, and the question turns from where you're heading to which old habits still need an update. Weather, not a forecast: checkpoints on a direction, never events to wait for.
