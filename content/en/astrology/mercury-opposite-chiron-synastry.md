Across a table, you see the other person's face and not your own. Mercury opposite Chiron in synastry works a little like that: one person's Mercury sits about 180° from the other's Chiron, so each sees clearly what the other carries, and the tender subject between them becomes a shared one, looked at from two sides.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how someone makes sense of things and says so: reasoning, naming, asking why. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the place where a person has had to find another way, and the know-how that came from it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) makes two functions the two ends of one line, each easier to see from the other, and asks for balance rather than a winner. Among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the two ends are two people, facing each other.

On the Mercury side, your reasoning meets the other's tender subject from the outside. You are often the one who asks "but why?", or who sees a pattern the other is too close to see. You may find yourself explaining them to friends or family, and, through them, learning where thinking stops being enough: some things aren't solved, only managed well.

On the Chiron side, you see in the other person an ease with words, or with the very subject, that you had to build the long way. Being understood by someone standing that far from your experience can be a relief, or a strain. You are also the one who can teach them where reasoning ends, which is real knowledge, and rarer than clever words.

Opposite signs share a modality and face each other. One person's Mercury in Cancer with the other's Chiron in Capricorn: a protective, family-style way of talking faces someone whose tender spot is about authority, and caring advice can sound like being told what to do.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The seesaw tends to settle into roles: the clear one and the sensitive one, the explainer and the explained. The Mercury person can turn the other's difficulty into a topic, discussed even when the Chiron person would rather not; the Chiron person can keep the other at arm's length, sure in advance that they won't understand. Each can come to see the other as the problem. An opposition is a seesaw, never enmity, and the pair's other angles show different arrangements:

- **[Mercury conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-chiron-synastry/):** no table between them; one person's words fall right on the tender place.
- **[Mercury sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-chiron-synastry/):** know-how and questions trade easily once someone opens the subject.
- **[Mercury square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-chiron-synastry/):** remarks catch at an angle, and a better vocabulary has to be found.
- **[Mercury trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-chiron-synastry/):** understanding flows so well it can stay unsaid.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Swap the roles on purpose. Let the Chiron person do the explaining, and the Mercury person the asking: the subject belongs to the one who lives it. Agree on what may be shared with others, and what stays between you. Between a parent and an adult child, that may mean the child telling their own story at family gatherings; between colleagues, the person with the experience leading the briefing on it; in a couple or a friendship, simply asking before retelling.

About 1 pair of people in 15 has this opposition, one way round or the other, within 6°, as many as have the conjunction: this pair's two most direct contacts turn up equally often. With an orb that wide and Mercury moving at most about two degrees a day, the hour of birth rarely changes whether you have it. It changes everything about where it plays out, though, which houses of each chart the two ends fall in, and that needs both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one chart, [Mercury opposite Chiron](/astrology/mercury-opposite-chiron/) has a person meet their tender spot through other people's words. Between two charts, the other people are one person, with a name, which makes the seesaw easier to talk about, provided that person agreed to be read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
