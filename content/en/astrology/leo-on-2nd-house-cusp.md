The 2nd house is where a chart keeps its accounts: what you earn, what you own, and what you think you're worth. With Leo on the 2nd house cusp, those accounts are kept with pride, and with an eye on what can be given away.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on a cusp gives a house its manner ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Leo](/astrology/leo/) brings to the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) a fixed fire that wants what it owns to say something about its owner. I read it as a sense of worth that grows when you can put your name to something: the piece of work you'd sign, the skill people come to you for because it's yours. Money earned that way does more for you than money handed over. You tend to buy fewer things and better ones, to keep what you love for years, and to spend most freely on other people: the round you insist on paying for, the present chosen with care. Generosity is one of your values, not a leak in the budget. The 2nd's natural sign is Taurus, fixed like Leo, so the house keeps its steadiness; what changes is the motive, from comfort to pride. Pride has its cost here: it can make it hard to admit that money is tight, or to accept help when you'd rather be the one giving it.

Your 2nd house answers to the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), which rules Leo and no other sign. The Sun's house is where your resources are earned and spent, and its sign gives the style. As ruler of the 2nd, it can sit in the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/) house. In nearly half the London charts with this cusp, Leo is rising as well, and the Sun rules both the 1st and the 2nd: the impression you make and the living you make become one subject. In the rest, Cancer rises, and the two lights divide the chart between them, the Moon ruling the way you meet the world and the Sun what you live on.

Counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use, over a whole day of births, about 1 in 9 has Leo on the 2nd at London's latitude and 1 in 10 at New York's. Only the Ascendant holds Leo more often. North of the equator Leo is a sign of long ascension, nearly three hours rising at London, so it lingers on the cusps either side of the horizon, and longer the further north you go. The 2nd is a narrow house in these charts, about 18° to 23° wide at London, which is why Leo so often runs on to the 3rd cusp as well, in about 1 London chart in 3 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). The reverse can happen too: Leo opens no house at all, [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) in about 1 London birth in 7 and 1 New York birth in 11.

Leo holds this cusp for about two and a half hours a day at London, and a time rounded to the hour can still land on the wrong side of it: a little later, and Leo would be rising ([Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/)); a little earlier, and it would open the 3rd instead ([Leo on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/)). I need the minute, which is why I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What this cusp can't do is put a figure on anything. I read the 2nd for the way you earn, spend and decide what matters, never for amounts, and no chart can choose an investment for you ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).
