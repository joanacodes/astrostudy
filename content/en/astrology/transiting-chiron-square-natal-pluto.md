Some sore spots invite a fight. When Chiron squares your Pluto, the wish to defeat an old hurt for good, or to control everything around it, rubs against the fact that some limits are lived with rather than beaten.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the drive to get to the bottom of things and reorganise them, and with it the question of power: who has it, who lost it, who takes it back. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts to what persists. A [square](/astrology/square/) puts them at right angles, in friction that asks for problem-solving rather than victory, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the sore spot and your sense of power get in each other's way.

It can show as anger at an old unfairness you thought you had put away, or as a tightening grip on a routine, a person, a version of events, anything that seems to keep the hurt in check. The square tends to show two of Pluto's blind spots: mistaking control for safety, and exposure for humiliation. What usually comes of it is a better division of labour: the part of the problem you can change, worked on hard; the part you can't, given a way of living with it that doesn't cost you your whole strength.

This square foretells no violence, loss or illness. If anger or old pain gets too much, a therapist is a better ally than any chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

Ninety degrees is about three houses: the drive for control or renewal comes from the house of your natal Pluto, the sore spot from the one Chiron is crossing, three houses on. With [Pluto in the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-in-1st-house/) and Chiron in your 4th, the intensity you meet the world with can run into an old ache about home and belonging ([Chiron transiting the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-4th-house/)). The houses depend on a recorded birth time, which is what places this square in your chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Any planet conjunct or opposite your Pluto is squared as well, and a planet square your Pluto has Chiron on it or opposite it, so a natal Sun–Pluto square turns this into a Chiron contact with your Sun too. Born with [Pluto square Chiron](/astrology/pluto-square-chiron/), in 1947–48 or 1992–94, you have known this friction from the start.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In nearly two passes out of three, Chiron hits the exact square, backs over it and hits it again: three contacts in about ten and a half months, measured with my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after. About a third are single crossings of some three weeks, and one in twenty runs to five contacts over roughly twenty-one months.

Everyone has met the first square by about 32: about 12 for people born in 1935, about 2 for 1945, about 27 for 1955, about 21 for 1965, about 14½ for 1975, about 6½ for 1985, about 15 for 1995 and about 10 for 2005. Two more usually follow before 80. In these years a square comes to the Leo-Pluto generation, born between 1937 and 1958, from Chiron in Taurus between 2026 and 2034, the same sign as around 1980; from 2033 it is the turn of the Virgo-Pluto generation, born between 1956 and 1972, with Chiron in Gemini, as in the mid-1980s.

Of the round's two squares, the first comes between [transiting Chiron sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-pluto/) and [transiting Chiron trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-pluto/), on the way from [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-pluto/) to [transiting Chiron opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-pluto/); the second mirrors it on the way back.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Split the problem on paper: what you can change, and what you have to live with. Put your strength into the first list.

Notice where you grip hardest, and loosen one hold a little to see what happens.

Name the old unfairness to someone who can hear it. Anger that has been spoken usually needs less control.
