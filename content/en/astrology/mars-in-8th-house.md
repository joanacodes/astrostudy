Mars in the 8th house goes where most people hesitate: into intimacy, trust, shared stakes and the moments that change a life. It wants the real thing or nothing, and it has the nerve to stay when things get hard.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is desire, will and courage. [The 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is what you share with others or owe them: intimacy, trust, joint money, debts, and the closeness that changes you. With Mars here, your drive goes deep. Desire is strong, the will to protect what's shared is stronger, and you're often the one who steps forward when something difficult has to be faced. Power runs through it all: where yours ends, where another person's begins, and how the two can be shared.

The 8th's natural sign is Scorpio, which Mars rules in the tradition, so this is the house that answers to Mars in the old scheme, as the 1st does through Aries; modern tropical astrology gives Scorpio, and the 8th with it, to Pluto. It's a succedent house, and the 2nd, the house of what's yours alone, sits opposite.

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance. I don't read death in any chart ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and I don't forecast what anyone will inherit or owe. In an adult's life, the 8th is the house of what we share with the living, and Mars there describes how you handle it: with intensity, loyalty and a strong need to trust.

When you were born, Mars was in the western sky, past its highest point and on its way down. My houses are Placidus houses, drawn from the minute and the place of birth, so without the time the 8th can't be found at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Before it, [Mars in the 7th house](/astrology/mars-in-7th-house/) meets the other person face to face; after it, [Mars in the 9th house](/astrology/mars-in-9th-house/) takes the fight to ideas.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In intimacy, Mars in the 8th is passionate and committed, and it needs trust before it opens. You want to know where you stand, and half-hearted closeness can feel worse than none at all. When trust is shaky, jealousy or a wish for control can appear; steady, mutual honesty is what settles this Mars. Shared resources matter too: you may feel strongly about how joint money or a shared home is handled, and clear agreements protect the relationship as much as the money.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Mars is at home with high stakes: crisis management, research, investigation, negotiating over shared resources, finance, insurance, therapy, any role where you deal with what others hide or fear. You're not easily shaken, and you take responsibility for other people's resources seriously. In an emergency, others often find you calm when they aren't, because this Mars saves its full force for the moments that matter. Shallow work bores you; a problem with real depth gets your full commitment.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Power struggles, in love, over money, at work. All-or-nothing commitments that leave no room to breathe. Difficulty letting anyone in, followed by holding on too tightly once they're there. And testing people, often without meaning to, to see whether they'll stay.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Build trust in small steps, and say out loud what you need from it. Make agreements about shared money and responsibilities early, in writing if that helps. Find a physical practice that takes all your concentration, so that intensity has somewhere to go besides the people you love. When you feel the urge to control, ask what you're afraid of losing. And let depth be a gift you offer, rather than a test other people have to pass.
