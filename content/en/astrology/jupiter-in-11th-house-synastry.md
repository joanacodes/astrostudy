Their Jupiter in your 11th house is Jupiter in the house of its joy, the one tradition said it liked best: friends, groups, networks and the hopes you share with them. Around this person, your circle tends to widen and your plans for the future to grow more generous.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) grows what it touches and looks for the meaning that binds people together. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds your chosen people: friends and allies, communities and networks, the collaborators behind a shared vision, and the hopes you carry with them. Tradition gave Jupiter its joy in this house ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), and as a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) it brings their generosity straight to your circle.

With them, you may meet people you'd never have met otherwise: an introduction here, an invitation there, a group that turns out to be yours. They encourage the hope you'd barely said aloud and help you find the people to make it real. Your hopes start to sound less like daydreams and more like plans. Friendship with them, if that's what it is, tends to be generous and roomy.

For them, your circles are a place where their generosity feels at home. They may love bringing people together around you, joining your causes or simply belonging to your world of friends. Succedent, the 11th is where a public role finds companions. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), its natural sign, answers to Saturn in the old scheme and to Uranus since that planet was found; I read both, and the house's old name is the Good Spirit.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A wide circle can thin out the closeness. Their enthusiasm for new people and big plans may leave you longing for one friend instead of a crowd, or signed up to more groups than you can show up for. Shared hopes can also drift from yours towards theirs without anyone deciding it.

From their side, your circle may not open as quickly as their generosity does. They may feel kept at the edge of your friendships, or find that their plans for the group meet polite caution.

A big circle is not the same as a close one, and no overlay makes anyone popular: their Jupiter here describes generosity meeting the people and hopes you share.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Choose which circles you belong to. Say yes to the introductions that excite you and no to the rest, and keep some friendships small on purpose. In a couple, decide together which gatherings you'll host and which hopes you're building towards; between friends, a shared cause or a yearly reunion can carry this overlay well; between colleagues, networks and collaborative projects thrive on their goodwill; between adult relatives, the wider family may stay in touch thanks to them.

Exchange the charts and the overlay becomes your Jupiter in their 11th house, with your generosity widening their circle and their hopes. [Their Jupiter in your 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-10th-house-synastry/) supports your public role; [their Jupiter in your 12th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-12th-house-synastry/) gives quietly, out of sight.

From the date alone, their Jupiter can be placed to within a fraction of a degree; your house cusps, on the other hand, shift with every minute on the clock of your birth, in Placidus, the house system I use, as in any other. Their own time matters only at the edge of your 11th, and for the other half of the reading, which I never skip ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Old friends included, nobody's chart is read without their knowing ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#the-question)).
