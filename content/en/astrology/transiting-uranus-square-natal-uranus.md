About a quarter of the way round its 84-year orbit, Uranus squares the place it held when you were born, and about three quarters of the way, it squares it again. The first comes around 20, the second in the sixties, and each sets the part of you that wants to differ against the life that has formed around it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Natal [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part of your chart that wants room to live its own way; transiting Uranus is the same function, years later, asking whether you've taken that room. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates them through constructive friction, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) presses on the gap between the freedom you were born wanting and the freedom you've actually used.

At about 20, the friction tends to be with the first structures you've joined as an adult: a course of study, a first job, a shared home, the expectations that travelled with you from your family. It can be the season of finding out what you won't do, before you know what you will. I read it as the first time your own Uranus is tested in the open.

In the sixties the square comes back from the other side. The life you've built is longer and more settled, and the urge to rearrange it can be as strong as at 20, with far more to weigh. The risk is the one Uranus always runs, changing a system before understanding what still works; the gift is that by now you know a great deal about what does.

With your natal Uranus in the 3rd, the first square reaches it from your 6th ([Uranus transiting the 6th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-6th-house/)): a quick, restless mind rubs against the routines of daily work and study. Both houses depend on the time of your birth, and I don't read a chart that doesn't have one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each square lasts about twenty months within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, or about eleven at the shortest, and is usually exact three times: long enough, at 20, to try a different path for a year and more.

Everyone born within a year or two of you meets it at about the same age. In the site's count the first square came within orb between about 17½ and 23: at 18.8 for someone born at the start of 1945, at 21.3 for the start of 1985. Its first exact hit fell between 18.4 and 22.9, and the second square, three quarters round, comes between about 59 and 65. By 80, everyone has met both.

Uranus in Gemini, until 2033, squares two generations at once ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)). People born from March 2003 to March 2011, mostly with Uranus in Pisces, are meeting their first square in their early twenties; those born from November 1961 to June 1969, with Uranus in Virgo, their second in their early sixties.

[Transiting Uranus sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-uranus/) comes about seven years before the first square and [transiting Uranus trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-uranus/) about seven after it. Halfway between the two squares stands [transiting Uranus opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-uranus/), the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) at about 42, and the cycle closes with the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/) at about 84. Around 21, Saturn squares its own place as well ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)), so the first Uranus square often shares its years with a Saturn one.

## What helps {#what-helps}

At 20, try the different path at a small scale first: a term somewhere else, a side project, a year of work before more study. The square rewards an experiment you can still learn from.

In the sixties, list what you'd keep before what you'd change. The second square has more to rearrange, and more that has earned its place.

Talk to someone at the other square. People in their twenties and in their sixties are meeting the same transit, and each has what the other lacks: momentum on one side, judgement on the other.
