When Neptune reaches your Descendant, the planet of ideals turns towards the people who stand opposite you: partners in love and in work, and anyone you meet as an equal. For close to thirteen years, as a rule, bonds take on more tenderness and less outline.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) idealises and feels with others: it sees the best in people and blurs the line between self and other. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is partnership, commitment, cooperation and agreements, the person facing you and what you look for in them. With Neptune here, partnership can grow more devoted, more forgiving and more romantic, and also harder to see clearly. You may long for a soulmate, give a great deal, sense a partner's moods before they speak, or find that agreements you thought were clear can be read two ways. The task of these years is to love the real person, not only the picture.

Your Descendant, the 7th's cusp, faces your Ascendant across the chart, so Neptune's arrival there ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-ascendant/)) touches both ends of the axis at once: for a while, who you are and who you're with can be harder to tell apart. With Mars in your 7th from birth, quarrels may soften, and so may your sense of what you want from a partner; it's worth saying it aloud.

Neptune here doesn't mean you'll be deceived, and it never announces a parting or a meeting. I don't read the outcome of a relationship in a [transit](/astrology/transits/), or of any dispute or contract; this one brings out the way your chart already loves and cooperates, with more compassion and softer edges.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Of all the houses, the 7th gives Neptune its shortest middle stay in Placidus charts drawn for London: about twelve years and eight months. The range is wide all the same: most stays last from nearly nine years to about twenty-five, since a London 7th can be very narrow or very broad. With a New York birth the middle rises to a little over thirteen and a half years, and most stays fall between nearly ten and just under twenty-two.

However often Neptune slips back over your Descendant in its retrograde months, the stay is measured from its first crossing to its last day in the 7th. That Descendant comes from your birth time exactly as the Ascendant does, and I read no chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Neptune reaches the 7th from your 6th, after years of looking for meaning in daily work ([Neptune transiting the 6th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-6th-house/)), and later moves on to your 8th, where what is shared and owed softens in turn ([Neptune transiting the 8th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-8th-house/)).

Libra rising, the sign of the 7th in the natural order, puts your Descendant in Aries, and Neptune is in Aries from January 2026 to March 2039, after a first visit in 2025: your Descendant's turn falls within that span. For Virgo rising the Descendant is in Pisces, and its crossing came between April 2011 and January 2026; for Scorpio rising it's in Taurus, which Neptune starts to reach in May 2038.

## What helps {#what-helps}

See your partner in ordinary weather. Neptune in the 7th makes it easy to love a person at their best; love that lasts also knows them tired, wrong and late.

Say plainly what you need, and ask plainly what they need. Sensing each other is beautiful, and it isn't the same as being told.

Give shared plans clear words: who does what, what's expected, what happens if things change. Under Neptune, clarity is a form of tenderness.
