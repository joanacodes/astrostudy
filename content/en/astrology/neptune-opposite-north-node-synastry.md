Some people meet you exactly where you're most at ease, on the ground you could walk blindfold. Neptune opposite North Node in synastry is that kind of meeting: the Neptune of one chart faces the other's North Node, so it rests on their South Node, a dreamy, familiar home ground, with the other's growth on the far side of the chart.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) softens and imagines: compassion, a feel for atmosphere and meaning, the knack of making someone feel understood without a word. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) holds a person's established capacities, practised so long they feel like nature; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), exactly opposite, is the capacity they're building. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) works through polarity and awareness, and here, as one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it hands a person's familiar end to another's dreaming.

Holding the North Node, you'll feel at home with this person. Their imagination lands on the gifts you grew up with, the ones you hardly notice using, and they seem to love you most fluently there. Around them there's no pressure to be anyone new, which is restful, and sometimes the very thing that makes your new direction harder to practise.

When the Neptune is yours, you're drawn to the other's familiar, easy self, perhaps to their past. You may idealise the strengths they're trying to grow beyond, and feel a little left behind when they reach for something you can't picture yet.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The seesaw runs between comfort and growth. Together, the pair can keep circling old ground: the same stories, the same roles, the same version of the node person, lovely and slightly too small. When the node person does reach across the chart, the Neptune person may feel it as a loss. That's a polarity at work, not a conflict: each end makes the other visible, and the familiar is only a trap when it's the only place you meet.

The other four, by contrast:

- **[Neptune conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the dream sits on the road ahead instead of the road behind.
- **[Neptune sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** a modest bridge, with Neptune trine the old ground rather than on it.
- **[Neptune square North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-north-node-synastry/):** ideals crossing the road at right angles, neither behind nor ahead.
- **[Neptune trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-north-node-synastry/):** imagination flowing towards the new, sextile to the familiar.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Bring the new into the old company. If the North Node is yours, show this person the thing you're learning, badly done and all, so their imagination has somewhere new to go. If the Neptune is yours, ask about it, and try to picture it as warmly as you picture what you already know. Old friends can keep one shared ritual and add one new thing a year; a parent and an adult child, swap the familiar stories for a question about what's next.

The pairs are easy to find in the calendar. Someone born in mid-1985 has their Neptune, early in Capricorn, on the South Node of people born from October 1982 to April 1983 and from August to November 2001. As for the 1985 South Node, in Scorpio, it sits beneath the Neptune of all births from August 1963 to December 1965, whose Neptune therefore faces the 1985 North Node in Taurus. Since whole cohorts carry it, it has no place among the contacts I tally in a synastry, all of which involve the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven; its houses show one era's dreams resting on another's inheritance.

No hour of birth will move this opposition in or out, both points being slow; the houses need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Whether you use the mean node or the true one changes these windows very little, since the two stay within a degree or two; I look at both, and the months above follow the true node ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

[Neptune opposite North Node](/astrology/neptune-opposite-north-node/), in one chart, makes imagination a person's own home ground. Between two charts the home is someone else's gift, and it is read only at the request of both ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
