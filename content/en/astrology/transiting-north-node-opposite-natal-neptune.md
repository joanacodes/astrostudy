Halfway through each round, the South Node reaches the degree of your birth Neptune while the North Node stands opposite. For a month or so, the dreams and refuges you have carried longest are lit from the familiar end, and some of them turn out to be habits.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) holds what a chart longs for: ideals, refuge, compassion, the sense of a larger meaning. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) is the consolidated end of the axis, where things come easily and sometimes too easily; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), opposite, is the end of practice. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two poles through awareness, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your Neptune is seen from where you have always stood, with the less familiar direction facing it.

It often shows as a dream held so long it feels like a fact. The partner, the place or the cause you idealised years ago. Help offered by reflex, before anyone has asked. A way of withdrawing that once restored you and now mostly passes the time. None of this is a failure, and nothing asks you to stop dreaming; the season asks which ideals still nourish you. Some astrologers read the South Node as memory of past lives, especially in [evolutionary astrology](/astrology/evolutionary-astrology/); I read it in this life, as what is familiar.

With Neptune in your 7th, the South Node crosses your 7th while the North Node crosses your 1st ([North Node transiting the 1st house](/astrology/north-node-transit-1st-house/)): an idealised picture of partnership, or a habit of caring for the other first, meets a direction that asks what you want. That predicts nothing about a relationship. It needs your houses, and so your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The South Node's visit to your Neptune usually lasts about a month, counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after. Caught by an eclipse season, the node idles near the degree while the Sun is close, and the pass lasts three months or more; about one in 25 brings three exact hits. In the year or so around it, an eclipse at the South Node end may fall within about 3° of your Neptune and count as a contact of its own.

The generation born with [Neptune in Capricorn](/astrology/neptune-in-capricorn/), from January 1984 to November 1998, met this opposition while the North Node crossed Cancer, from November 2018 to May 2020, with the South Node on their Neptune, halfway between the node's crossings of 2009 to 2011 and 2028 to 2029. The next comes from May 2037 to February 2039. Of two people born ten years apart in that generation, on 1 January 1985 and 1 January 1995, the first met the opposition at 16.7, the second at 5.7; by 80, four or five. With [Neptune opposite North Node](/astrology/neptune-opposite-north-node/) in your chart, each nodal return repeats it.

Three years or so either side lie the trines ([Transiting North Node trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-neptune/)); further out, the squares ([Transiting North Node square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-neptune/)) and the sextiles ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-neptune/)); nine years away, [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-neptune/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name one ideal you have carried for years and ask what it gives you now. Keep it if the answer is real.

Offer help once this month only when it is asked for, and notice how that feels.

Look across the chart: the sign and house opposite your Neptune show another way of meeting the same need. Try it for a week, as a beginner.
