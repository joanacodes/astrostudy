Your Moon is in Libra, the sign where Saturn, the older ruler of your Aquarius Sun, is exalted. You were born as the Moon waned after the full, rising a little later each evening. What I see here is a sense of justice that wants to be shared, and needs that settle in a fair exchange between two people.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fixed air and cardinal air, with Libra four signs behind Aquarius: a [trine](/astrology/trine/) by sign, and this time the Moon trails the Sun instead of leading it. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) gives itself to principles that ought to hold for everyone; the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) settles when things are even between you and the person opposite, when each has given and received in proportion. You can argue for fairness on a large scale and need it on a small one: who cooked, who called last, who listened.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra and shows what your Moon finds fair and pleasing. Aquarius answers to [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in modern tropical astrology and to [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in the tradition, and I read both: Uranus for the rules your Sun wants rewritten, Saturn for the ones it keeps. Saturn's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Libra ties it to your Moon as well. It is one of [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) far more often than the rest in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), 75 to 90% of the time against about a third overall; here it rules your Sun's sign and is honoured in your Moon's, so its house and aspects get a long look from me. The Sun is in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Aquarius; Libra gives the Moon no dignity.

By then the full moon was two and a half to seven days gone, the Moon 211° to 269° from the Sun and the lunar month 17 to 22 days old. The [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) takes about seven of these births in eight, the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase the earliest, and near 240° the lights make a waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). The disseminating phase, as I read it, is the part of the month when what you've understood wants an audience, and an air pair finds one by talking: a good idea about how people should treat each other rarely stays private with you. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) covers the waning half of the month.

The count puts this pair at about 1 person in 144, which makes it, alongside the Scorpio Moon pair, the most frequent of the twelve with an Aquarius Sun, by a whisker. Libra holds the Moon for about two and a half days between Virgo and Scorpio; when the change fell on your birth date, only the time can tell this page from the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-virgo/) and the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-scorpio/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The time also gives Saturn its house, and with Saturn this central I wouldn't begin without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Partnership matters to you as an equal exchange between friends. The Libra Moon needs harmony with the person closest to it and notices quickly when the balance tips; the Aquarius Sun needs that person to respect its independence and its convictions. You may find it easier to discuss what would be fair than to say what you'd like. Try the second first: a want stated plainly is easier to balance than a principle.

## At work {#at-work}

You do well where fairness has to be designed into something: mediation, policy, standards, public design, cooperative structures, any team that has to agree its own rules. The Libra Moon reads the room and finds the agreement; the Aquarius Sun makes sure it holds for people who weren't in the room. Workplaces run on favouritism or hidden rules wear on you quickly.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two air signs can live inside the idea of fairness and forget the feeling underneath. You may smooth over something that bothered you and find it still there a month later, or reason a hurt away because the case against it is so well made. The waning phase helps here, as I read it: it is a time for passing on what you know, and a feeling is knowledge too. Tell it to the person it concerns, in one sentence, before you explain the principle behind it.
