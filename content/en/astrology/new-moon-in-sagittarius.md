Every year, the last new moon before the December solstice falls in Sagittarius: the Sun and Moon meet in the archer's sign while northern nights are still lengthening. It starts a month aimed at something further off, an idea, a journey, a question worth following.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) joins the Sun and Moon at one degree, the Moon unseen and the month's phases about to begin. [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) is mutable fire: it runs on enthusiasm and adapts as it goes, so this start looks outward and ahead. The month tends to open on a question of meaning rather than a task: what you believe, where you'd like to go, what you want to learn next and why. Its ruler is [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the planet that broadens and makes sense of things, and Jupiter's house in your chart is where a Sagittarius beginning heads.

I don't read it as a promise of travel or luck. It's a brief marker, and it matters in a chart only when it lands on one of your natal points, give or take a degree, and then as a spark in a period already opened by something slower: transits, progressions, solar arcs, eclipses. Without that, it's a month's weather.

The dates, in Universal Time:

- **9 December 2026**, 00:52 UT, at 16°57′ Sagittarius (in the Americas, still 8 December).
- **28 November 2027**, at 5°39′.
- **16 December 2028**, at 24°39′.
- **5 December 2029**, at 13°45′: a partial [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/).
- **25 November 2030**, at 3°02′: a total solar eclipse.

Those last two belong to a run of eclipses on the Gemini–Sagittarius axis ([eclipse in Sagittarius](/astrology/eclipse-in-sagittarius/)). An eclipse weighs more than an ordinary new moon: I let it count within about 3° of a natal point, at either end of the axis, read through that point and its house, and never as an omen.

Two weeks either side, with the Sun in Sagittarius, falls the [full moon in Gemini](/astrology/full-moon-in-gemini/). In December 2029 both are eclipses, a fortnight apart: the solar one here on the 5th, a total lunar eclipse in Gemini on the 20th. The [new moon in Scorpio](/astrology/new-moon-in-scorpio/) comes a month before this one, the [new moon in Capricorn](/astrology/new-moon-in-capricorn/) a month after.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house holds its degree is a question for your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In the Placidus houses I work with, read the line for the house with Sagittarius on its cusp, or the house before if the cusp sits at a later degree than the new moon. With the 2030 eclipse at 3°02′, that's common: any cusp past 3° Sagittarius puts it in the earlier house.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Sagittarius rising: you meet the world more openly and frankly, with room to try a new direction on for size.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** money with a horizon: saving for a journey or a course, earning from what you know.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** everyday learning: a subject followed for the joy of it, short trips, talk that turns philosophical by the second coffee.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** home opens up: family beliefs questioned, a house full of books and guests.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** pleasure as adventure: a trip taken for fun, a sport, a romance that feels like a journey, creative work with something to say.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** a working routine with more room in it: learning on the job, a daily habit tied to a bigger aim.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** a partner as fellow traveller: a relationship renewed around a shared plan, or a collaborator from another background.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** what you share put towards growth: a joint plan for study or travel, an intimacy that is frank and generous.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** Sagittarius's own house: a fresh start in study, travel abroad, teaching or writing, whatever widens your map.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** a public role with a mission: teaching, publishing, or work that has to mean something to be worth doing.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** friends from far away or from other worlds; a group gathered around ideas, a faith or a cause.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a private search for meaning: a retreat, a spiritual practice, or a belief you're quietly letting go of.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Hold the five positions above against your chart, a degree either side; for the two eclipses, about three either side, and at the Gemini end of the axis too. If nothing is there, take the new moon as a month's weather in one house.

Then look at Jupiter's sign and house: that's where a Sagittarius beginning goes looking for room. And ask the sign's own question in the house the new moon falls in: what would make this mean something? A course you can actually start, a journey you can actually take, a conversation with someone who sees it differently. Sagittarius begins better with a good question than with a list.
