Your Moon is at home in its own sign, and your Sun works in the sign of method and service, so care runs through both lights in two different accents. The Moon had passed its last quarter at your birth. Practical tenderness is how I read the pair: love that keeps lists, and lists kept out of love.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Your Moon is two signs behind your Sun, water behind earth, cardinal behind mutable: by sign a sextile, a link that helps as soon as you lean on it. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) knows itself through what it can do well, and for others; the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/) is happiest with a home to keep and people under its roof. The Sun brings method to the Moon's care, and the Moon gives the Sun's usefulness somewhere to belong.

Your Moon is in its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/): the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules Cancer, so no other planet stands between you and your feelings, and the Moon's own placement says nearly everything about them. Your Sun, without dignity in Virgo, takes its lead from [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), the sign's ruler, which is exalted there and never far from the Sun, sitting in Leo, Virgo or Libra. Tradition adds a crossing worth knowing: Jupiter is exalted in Cancer, your Moon's sign, and in its older detriment in Virgo, your Sun's. I read it as generosity that flows easily at home and grows more careful in the working world.

Your Moon trailed the Sun by 30° to 89°, a half moon or a thinning crescent up before dawn, 22 to 27 days into the lunar month. The [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) phase claims the great majority of these births, close to nine in ten, and the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) the rest, while near 60° behind the Sun a waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/) appears. The last quarter reviews and sorts what to keep, which suits a pair that already likes to put the house in order, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the eight phases.

Roughly one person in 140 is born with these lights. Sixty hours or so is all the Moon spends in Cancer, so a birthday that saw it arrive or leave needs the hour to choose between this page, the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-gemini/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-leo/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Since this Moon rules itself, its house carries the whole weight of your need for home, and that house, like the rising sign, is something only the time can give.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You look after the people you love in practical, remembered ways: the plasters in the bag, the favourite meal on a hard day, the anniversary nobody else recalled. The Cancer Moon wants belonging and a door that's always open; the Virgo Sun wants to be of real use. Being on the receiving end is harder. You may refuse help, or accept it and then quietly redo the job. Let someone else cook now and then, clumsily, and stay in your chair.

## At work {#at-work}

Caring work done with skill suits the pair: running a household or a small business, teaching, looking after a team, any job where people's wellbeing depends on details being right. The Moon brings loyalty and protectiveness, the Sun method and reliability, so you're often the one who keeps a workplace humane and functioning at once. A cold, sloppy environment offends both lights. Notice when you start carrying everyone's worries as well as your own job.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Worry has two homes here. The Virgo Sun anticipates what might go wrong and the Cancer Moon remembers what did, so between them the mind can tidy and retidy the same fear. Looking after everyone else can turn into a way of not looking at your own needs. What helps is putting yourself on the list by name, keeping one evening a week that no one else's needs can claim, and noticing when tidying has turned into fretting, then stopping for tea.
