Their Mercury in your 11th house talks about the future with you, usually in company. Group chats, introductions, plans hatched round a table of friends, causes argued late into the night: this overlay lives where conversation and community meet.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is the house of your people and your plans: the friends you choose, groups, teams, networks, and the hopes you'd like to see through. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) connects people and ideas. The [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) sets their mind loose in your circle.

For you, they may be a connector. They introduce you to the person you needed to meet, pass on the opportunity, bring a new idea to the group and keep the conversation going between gatherings. Talking with them about what you hope for can make it feel more possible, because they ask the practical question next: who do we know?

For them, your friends and causes are full of interest. They enjoy your circle, learn from the people around you, and find their thinking sharpened by your hopes. The 11th is succedent, and tradition called it the Good Spirit, the house where Jupiter has its joy. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), its natural sign, answers to Uranus and, in the older system, to Saturn as well; I read both, the wish for change and the patience to build it.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Talk about plans can stand in for carrying them out. The group chat buzzes, the ideas multiply, and nothing moves. Information also travels fast in a circle: something you told them in passing may reach your friends before you do.

From their side, they may feel like one voice among many. If you only ever meet in a crowd, they can wonder whether you'd talk to them alone, and if you rally the group around their idea without asking, they may feel more borrowed than heard.

None of this says what you'll achieve together, or whether a friendship will turn into something else. It shows how ideas move between the two of you and the people around you.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree what stays out of the group, and turn at least one plan into a date in the diary. Make time for conversation with just the two of you. In a couple, friends in common are a pleasure as long as you still have conversations of your own; between friends, this is the one who keeps the circle connected; between colleagues, committees, networks and volunteer projects suit you both; between adult relatives, the cousin who organises the reunion and keeps everyone in touch.

Seen the other way, with your Mercury in their 11th house, it's your ideas and introductions that circulate in their world. Before it, [their Mercury in your 10th house](/astrology/mercury-in-10th-house-synastry/) is all about your work; after it, [their Mercury in your 12th house](/astrology/mercury-in-12th-house-synastry/) goes quiet, into what you leave unspoken.

Your birth time sets both cusps of the 11th, as it does for every house in Placidus, the house system I use. Their Mercury travels close to their Sun, never more than about 28° from it, and their birthday places it to within a degree or two; their time matters only when it sits right on a cusp, and I ask for both anyway ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Being part of someone's circle isn't the same as their consent, so if they can't know about a reading, start with your own chart ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#instead)).
