Give some relationships an emergency and they shine; give them a quiet Sunday and they're lost. For a bond like that, composite North Node in Taurus marks the way to the quiet Sunday: calm, plain pleasures, and things that grow slowly.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), built from the midpoints of two birth charts, the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows where the relationship itself is heading, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), exactly opposite, what it already does by reflex. Here the reflex is [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/): intensity, the late-night talk that changes everything, a closeness that seems to need high stakes to prove itself. The direction is [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), fixed earth ruled by [Venus](/astrology/venus/): steadiness, the body's pleasures, a home that is comfortable rather than charged, value built up a little at a time.

Neither of you has to be dramatic for this to fit. Two calm people can still find that their bond comes alive mainly when something is at stake, a move, a family upheaval, a rival, and goes flat when nothing is. The way forward is shared as well: learning that a meal cooked together, a walk every Saturday or a garden planted in spring are not a lesser kind of closeness. Composite Venus, the ruler of the sign, shows where those pleasures come most easily.

In a single birth chart, [North Node in Taurus](/astrology/north-node-in-taurus/) describes a person growing towards peace after one crisis too many. In a composite, the peace has to be agreed by two, and one of you may feel a calm stretch as relief while the other reads it as distance. Saying out loud that quiet isn't cooling saves a good deal of worry.

Among two million pairs of birth dates drawn at random from 1930 to 2009, about 1 pair in 11 had it. Same-age pairs share it if both births fall in one of the node's own Taurus stretches: August 1947 to January 1949, February 1966 to August 1967, September 1984 to March 1986, or April 2003 to December 2004. With a wide gap it arrives by a detour. Born in October 1969 and June 1982, with natal North Nodes near 21° Pisces and 13° Cancer, two people have a composite North Node near 17° Taurus, close to where the South Node stood in February 1976, halfway between their births: with a gap like theirs, the shorter arc runs the other way round, and the composite lands on the far end of the axis.

At the sign's lower edge, the two ways of calculating the node can disagree for weeks. In spring 1986 the true node crossed from Taurus into Aries on 24 March, came back for about two days at the start of April and crossed again, while the mean node stayed in Taurus until 21 April. A birth in those weeks brings a Taurus node by one reckoning and an Aries node by the other. In a composite the difference carries through, and it settles the sign only when the midpoint lies within a degree or two of the border with [composite North Node in Aries](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-aries/); at the far edge, [composite North Node in Gemini](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-gemini/) takes over. I look at both.

Where it goes wrong, Taurus mistakes stillness for safety. A bond growing towards calm may hold on to routines long after they've stopped serving it, or dodge every conversation that might rock the boat. The Scorpio end's honesty about difficult things remains an asset: the aim is a relationship that can afford to be peaceful, not one that is afraid of depth.

I don't draw composites. The technique belongs to the astrologers who use it, and they read it beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). Nothing here forecasts money, property or how long two people will last; the node marks a direction, and the pace is theirs. Like any chart of two people, it waits until both have agreed to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
