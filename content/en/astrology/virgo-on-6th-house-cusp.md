Work, routine, the people you work beside and the way you look after your body: that's the 6th house, and Virgo, the sixth sign, is its own. With Virgo on the 6th house cusp, the house runs on its own terms.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Here sign and house agree ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)): [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) is the natural sign of the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/), so its methods need no translating. I read it as a working day built on method: lists, systems, a place for each tool, and a better way of doing the job found by the third time you do it. You improve how things are done, often quietly, and colleagues come to rely on you for the detail they'd rather not handle. Routines steady you, and a day with no shape can leave you restless. The body's care tends to be informed and attentive, read up on and kept to. The difficulty is that the standards rarely switch off: worry about whether the work is good enough, or a habit of taking on the task nobody else will do because you'd do it better.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), the 6th's natural ruler, rules it here too, from the sign of its exaltation. Its house shows where your daily work is directed: as ruler of the 6th, in the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). At London this 6th is enormous, 57° to 60° wide, almost two signs, and in 9 charts in 10 it holds the whole of Libra as well ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Every one of those charts has [Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/), so Venus rules both your Ascendant and the sign tucked inside your 6th: the planet that leads the chart has a voice in how you work, the method coming from Mercury, a sense of fairness and of pleasure in the work from Venus. Gemini, Mercury's other sign, opens the 2nd in every London chart with this cusp, and sometimes the 3rd as well, so the planet of your working day also rules what you earn.

Virgo on its own house's cusp is uncommon in the north: in Placidus, my house system, 1 London birth in 20 has it, and 1 New York birth in 17. Only the Descendant sees Virgo less often. Pisces, opposite, rises fast in the north and hurries across the 12th cusp, and Virgo keeps pace with it on the 6th: about 72 minutes a day at London. In about one London chart in six, Virgo falls inside a house without opening any.

About 72 minutes is a narrow margin. The pages either side, [Virgo on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-5th-house-cusp/) and [Virgo on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/), describe charts only minutes away from yours, and the recorded time tells me which applies ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Because the 6th was once searched for illness, it's worth saying plainly: I read here how you look after your body, not what might go wrong with it, and no chart can foretell an illness ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). With Virgo on the cusp, the looking-after is usually careful already; the kinder habit to build is rest.
