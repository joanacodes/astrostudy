Your chart ruler has travelled: it stands in the 9th house, the far horizon of long journeys, higher study, belief and the search for meaning, and the self you lead with keeps looking past the place it started from.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/) rules the sign on the cusp of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), and here it has taken the self of the Ascendant to the [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)): what you believe and how you came to believe it, travel abroad and other cultures, university and long study, philosophy and faith, teaching and publishing, everything that takes you, in miles or in thought, further than the place you were given.

Going further is how this self grows. What you're looking for tells you who you are: a question you keep coming back to, a subject you've studied for years, a country that changed how you see your own. People often meet you through your convictions, or through your curiosity about theirs. Teaching suits it, and so does being a student for life. The risk is certainty: a seeker can harden into a preacher, and the 9th is at its best while belief stays a question you're still asking. Not every trip abroad is an escape, either; some are the way home.

The Sun had its joy in the 9th, the house tradition called the God ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), so with Leo rising, where the Sun is your chart ruler, a Sun in the 9th rules your chart from the house where the old astrologers said it rejoiced. With Sagittarius rising, or with Pisces rising through its traditional ruler, Jupiter in the 9th works in the house matched with Sagittarius, its own sign: the planet of growth in the house it has always been paired with. A Capricorn Ascendant's Saturn, in the 9th, believes only what it has tested.

Your ruler had crossed the Midheaven shortly before you were born and was starting down the western side of the sky: in Placidus, the house system I use, that's the 9th. The old word for such a house is cadent, from the Latin for falling ([angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/)), because a planet there has just dropped away from an angle. The 9th also faces the 3rd across the chart; from this far end of the axis, your ruler makes the everyday something you come back to with fresh eyes.

A ruler close to either cusp of the 9th is worth checking against a reliable birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Back in the 8th, its story would turn on intimacy and what you share with other people ([Ruler of the 1st in the 8th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)); over the Midheaven, in the 10th, on your public role and the work you're known for ([Ruler of the 1st in the 10th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)).

Aquarius rising gives the chart two rulers, Saturn and Uranus, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 9th builds a philosophy from study rather than inspiration, while Uranus, from its own place in the chart, keeps questioning whatever framework Saturn builds. The two together make a mind that wants foundations and freedom at once.

Emigrating, taking a degree or finding a faith aren't foretold here. The placement describes the direction your life leans in: outward, and towards meaning.
