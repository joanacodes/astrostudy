Your chart ruler, the planet of your rising sign, sits in the 9th house, the house of the far horizon: long journeys, higher study, belief and the search for meaning. The self you lead with keeps looking past the place it started from.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/) rules the sign on the cusp of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), and its house tells me where the self of the Ascendant goes to live ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) holds what you believe and how you came to believe it, travel abroad and other cultures, university and long study, philosophy and faith, teaching and publishing: everything that takes you, in miles or in thought, further than the place you were given.

I read it as a self that grows by going further. You tend to know who you are by what you're looking for: a question you keep coming back to, a subject you've studied for years, a country that changed how you see your own. People often meet you through your convictions, or through your curiosity about theirs. Teaching often suits it, and so does being a student for life. The care it asks for concerns certainty. A seeker can harden into a preacher, and the 9th is at its best while belief stays a question you're still asking. Not every journey is an escape, either; some are the way home.

Tradition called the 9th the God and gave the Sun its joy there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With Leo rising, the Sun is your chart ruler, and a Sun in the 9th rules your chart from the house where the old astrologers said it rejoiced. With Sagittarius rising, or with Pisces rising through its traditional ruler, Jupiter in the 9th works in the house matched with Sagittarius, its own sign: the planet of growth in the house it has always been paired with. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn Ascendant from here, studies slowly and believes only what it has tested.

In Placidus, the house system I use, a ruler in the 9th had crossed the Midheaven shortly before you were born and was starting down the western side of the sky. The 9th is cadent, the quieter kind of house in the old ranking ([angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/)), and it faces the 3rd across the chart. A ruler here starts from the far end of that axis, and the everyday becomes something you come back to with fresh eyes.

Which side of a cusp your ruler stands on depends on the minute of birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Back in the 8th, its story would turn on intimacy and what you share with other people ([Ruler of the 1st in the 8th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)); over the Midheaven, in the 10th, on your public role and the work you're known for ([Ruler of the 1st in the 10th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)).

Aquarius rising gives the chart two rulers, Saturn and Uranus, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Saturn in the 9th builds a philosophy from study rather than inspiration, while Uranus, from its own house, keeps questioning whatever framework Saturn builds. The two together make a mind that wants foundations and freedom at once.

None of this predicts that you'll emigrate, take a degree or find a faith. It describes the direction your life leans in: outward, and towards meaning.
