On a seesaw, each end only moves because the other does. Saturn opposite Chiron in synastry sets one person's Saturn across the zodiac from the other's Chiron, within 5°, and the two start to balance each other: competence at one end, a lasting limit and the skill grown around it at the other.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the part of a chart that holds things up: steadiness, responsibility, the structure that copes. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the part that has had to adapt: an uneven place that stays uneven, worked around with patience and practical skill. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, so each grows clearer against the other, and of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this is the one that puts coping and adapting at the two ends of a single axis.

If the Saturn is yours, you see the other person's limit plainly, and the ingenuity of their way round it. You may admire it more than you expected, and learn through them that a sore point doesn't have to hide behind competence.

If the Chiron is yours, you meet someone who seems to cope with everything, and you may discover that the structure they show has its own cost. Your know-how is real expertise; around this person, it can be offered as such rather than kept quiet.

The signs usually face each other. One person's Saturn in Pisces with the other's Chiron in Virgo: a structure built on acceptance and compassion, across from a way of coping made of precision and small, steady improvements.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Roles freeze. The Saturn person becomes the one who always carries, and starts measuring their worth by it; the Chiron person becomes the one who always needs allowances, and the limit starts to stand for the whole person. Then the seesaw stops: one end stuck high, the other low. An opposition is a seesaw, never enmity, and it only works while both ends move.

- **[Saturn conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-chiron-synastry/):** both at one point instead of either end, so the roles blur rather than split.
- **[Saturn sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/saturn-sextile-chiron-synastry/):** a friendly distance, and help that has to be asked for.
- **[Saturn square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/saturn-square-chiron-synastry/):** half the distance, where the standard grinds against the limit.
- **[Saturn trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/saturn-trine-chiron-synastry/):** two signs closer than the opposition, and the fit is easy.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Swap ends on purpose. Let the Chiron person teach something they know because of their limit; let the Saturn person name a limit of their own and accept help with it. A parent and an adult child, two siblings, a couple, two colleagues years apart: each pair keeps the balance by letting strength and difficulty show on both sides.

A parent born in mid-1965, with Saturn in Pisces, and an adult child born between March and July 1995, Chiron in Virgo, would have it; so would someone born in mid-1975, Chiron in Aries, and anyone born between March and September 1983, whose Saturn stood opposite. These are cohorts, not individuals: every birth in those months repeats the same opposition, so my synastry count passes over it and keeps to contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Read through the houses, it shows how two eras balance strength and limitation, more than how two people do.

Neither body shifts in a day by enough to decide the opposition; the axis it falls on, house by house, depends on the hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If the Saturn falls in the Chiron person's 7th house, the seesaw lives in the partnership itself: who carries, who is carried ([Their Saturn in your 7th house](/astrology/saturn-in-7th-house-synastry/)).

Within one chart, [Saturn opposite Chiron](/astrology/saturn-opposite-chiron/) sets a person's own strength across from their own sore point. Between two charts each person holds one end, and the reading needs both of them present and willing ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
