Capricorn begins on the shortest day of the northern year, and its first ten degrees put Saturn, the sign's ruler, beside Jupiter, the planet the sign treats most sternly.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

Jupiter holds the first face of [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/): in the [Chaldean order](/astrology/chaldean-order/) it follows Saturn, which closed Sagittarius ([Sagittarius, third decan](/astrology/sagittarius-third-decan/)), and the sequence runs on unbroken ([faces](/astrology/faces/)). Capricorn then repeats the faces of Gemini: Jupiter, Mars ([Capricorn, second decan](/astrology/capricorn-second-decan/)), the Sun. The gift goes to a planet the sign makes uneasy. Jupiter has its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Capricorn, opposite its exaltation in Cancer, so [Jupiter in Capricorn](/astrology/jupiter-in-capricorn/) between 0° and 10° holds the one dignity it has anywhere in the sign.

In the modern decans, the first ten degrees belong to Capricorn's own ruler, Saturn, which rules here twice over, through the sign and through the decan. Of the two rulers, Saturn is the one I watch. In my notebook it held 75 to 90% of the time, along with Chiron and Lilith ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). Jupiter's face is the softer note: room to grow inside the structure rather than outside it. That the planet of growth should hold the first face of the sign of limits is one of the old tables' small ironies, and I like it.

## Its image {#its-image}

Wallis Budge's list puts the Egyptian decan Semṭet here, which the Greek-Egyptian lists wrote Smat. [Firmicus Maternus](/astrology/firmicus-maternus/) called the decan Themeso, and Joseph Scaliger, twelve centuries after him, kept the name exactly as it was. The Hermetic lists know the same decan as Tair in Greek and Renpsois in Latin.

The sky adds a picture with a pun in it. [Facies](/astrology/facies/), the globular cluster Messier 22, takes its name from the Latin for face, and its centre lies at about 8°40′ Capricorn, inside the first face of the sign. It sits less than a degree from the Sun's path, close enough for the Moon to pass in front of it in some years.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun enters these degrees at the December solstice and stays until about 31 December, so a [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) of the first decan is born in the days right after the shortest day in the north. With Saturn twice and Jupiter's face, I read the decan's shade as ambition that wants to grow on solid ground: a plan first, then the reach. Because the solstice moves by a few hours from year to year, births on 21 and 22 December are the ones I can't place in this decan without the clock.

[Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/) in its first decan takes about 41 minutes to cross the horizon at the latitude of Paris or London. Capricorn is the first of the quick-rising signs in the northern hemisphere, and each of its decans rises faster than the one before it, which makes the birth time matter all the more.

Two slow planets crossed this decan in the 1980s: [Neptune in Capricorn](/astrology/neptune-in-capricorn/) from early 1984 to 1989, and [Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/uranus-in-capricorn/) from early 1988 to 1991, and for a few months of 1988 and 1989 the two shared it. Each is a generation's placement, personal only through its house.
