A Pisces Sun can be hard to describe, even to itself, and that isn't a fault in the description. In the last sign of the zodiac, mutable water, the Sun's self is held together less by a fixed outline than by what it is devoted to.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is where a chart keeps its sense of self, its vitality and its purpose. [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), mutable water, makes all three permeable and imaginative. Your sense of self may feel more like a current than a shape, and coherence, for this Sun, often comes from devotion rather than definition: a vocation, an art, a person, a faith, something you'd give yourself to. Plenty of Pisces Suns say who they are more clearly in an image, a melody or an act of care than in a sentence about themselves.

No dignity, no debility: the tradition neither honours nor weakens the Sun in Pisces. What shapes it is a pair of rulers, and I read both. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the traditional ruler, shows by sign and house where this Sun finds meaning and grows. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), its ruler in modern tropical astrology, moves so slowly that its sign belongs to a generation; its house is where this Sun's imagination and compassion want to work.

The Sun spends 29 or 30 days in Pisces, from about 19 February to the March equinox, about 20 March, and about 1 person in 12 is born under it. In the first day or so you may still be the [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/), in the last day or so already the [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/): your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) settles it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

A Pisces Sun gives itself generously in love, and often senses what a partner needs before a word is said. The risk is taking on the other person's outline, their moods, their plans, their version of you, until your own direction blurs. Compassion isn't self-erasure, and a relationship gets better, not worse, when you keep a part of yourself that doesn't depend on it. A partner who asks what *you* imagine for your life, and waits for the answer, is worth more than one who simply adores you.

## At work {#at-work}

This Sun tends to work best where imagination or care is the point: art, music, writing, teaching, care work, spiritual or community work, anything that serves something larger than a quarterly target. You may find your purpose by being moved: a cause, a need, an image that won't let you go. Recognition is complicated for a Sun that blends so easily, and you may prefer your work to be known by its effect rather than by your name. That's lovely, and it's still worth signing what you make.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Identity can dissolve into whoever and whatever is closest. You may lose track of what you want because you can feel so clearly what everyone else wants, and say yes to things that drain you because saying no feels unkind. Vitality goes with it: this Sun recharges through imagination and quiet, and runs low in harsh, noisy places that leave room for neither. And when the ideal and the real don't match, the disappointment can be total, because the ideal was so vivid.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give your imagination a form with edges: a daily practice, a craft, a discipline with a beginning and an end. Ask what you want before you ask what someone else needs. Keep places and hours where you come back to yourself, and protect them without apology. Let a vision be tested by small, real steps, so that it can survive meeting the world.

A Pisces Sun can take a description to heart and start living inside it, so here is a word of caution from [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/): the Sun sign held about a third of the time there. Jupiter and Neptune were checked like every other placement, and neither was among the three that held far more often, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith. Keep what fits you, and leave the rest.
