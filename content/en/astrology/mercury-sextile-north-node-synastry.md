Good advice often sounds like something you half knew already. In Mercury sextile North Node in synastry, one person's Mercury sits about 60° from the other's North Node, and so about 120° from their South Node: the Mercury person's ideas tend to build a bridge from what the other already does well to what they are learning.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is a person's thinking in circulation: what they notice, suggest, explain and pass on. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the direction someone grows in, a capacity that's less practised and gets stronger with repetition; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, holds their established strengths. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) joins two functions by way of opportunity, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the opportunity passes from one person's words to the other's path.

When the Mercury is yours, your suggestions actually get used. You understand where this person comes from, so your ideas land as help rather than pressure, and you may enjoy being their sounding board while they try something new.

When the node is yours, their way of putting things makes the next step feel like an extension of what you already know. You can try the less familiar thing without throwing away the old skills, because they keep translating one into the other.

Take one person's Mercury in Aquarius with the other's North Node in Aries and South Node in Libra. Independent, systems-minded thinking speaks the airy language of the node person's familiar end, weighing, fairness, partnership, while nudging towards acting on their own say-so: "you've weighed it enough; now decide." At work, this is the colleague who helps you turn an old skill into a new role; between friends or siblings, the one whose practical encouragement gets you started.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opening has to be used. Without a question from the node person, or an offer from the Mercury person, the sextile stays a pleasant compatibility of outlook. Its warm trine to the South Node has a pull of its own: they understand where you come from so well that it is tempting to stay there, talking about the old ground instead of practising the new.

The Mercury person's helpfulness can also turn into a running commentary on the other's growth, which no one enjoys for long.

Seen from the sextile, the pair's other contacts do different work:

- **[Mercury conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the words move onto the path itself, stretching more than bridging.
- **[Mercury square North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-north-node-synastry/):** a sign further, square to both nodes, unsettling before it helps.
- **[Mercury trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-north-node-synastry/):** the mirror image, trine to growth and sextile to the familiar.
- **[Mercury opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** all the way to the other end, where talk is cosy and slow to change.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make it concrete. The node person can name one thing they are practising; the Mercury person can ask "what are you working on?" instead of guessing, and offer one idea at a time. A monthly coffee between colleagues, a shared course between friends, or in a couple a habit of asking each other about progress can keep the bridge in use.

About 1 pair of people in 13 has this sextile, one way round or the other, within 3.6°. With so narrow an orb, the degree or two between the mean and the true node can decide it more often than for the wider aspects, and I look at both ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). Mercury can also travel up to about two degrees in a day, a real share of 3.6°, so the hour counts near the edge; the houses always need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

When a single chart holds [Mercury sextile North Node](/astrology/mercury-sextile-north-node/), a person bridges their own old and new; between two charts, someone else's words build the bridge. Who is stretching whom is worth both people seeing, so I read it only at both people's request ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
