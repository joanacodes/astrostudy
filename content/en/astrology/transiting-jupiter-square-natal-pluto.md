What you want to grow and what needs to change underneath stop agreeing for a while, twice in every Jupiter lap, when Jupiter squares your birth Pluto. The friction is about depth and scale, and it tends to bring questions of control to the surface.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is where you hold on hardest and change most deeply: it reorganises what can no longer go on as it is, and gives back a quieter, sturdier strength. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) wants more scope, more meaning, a larger plan. At a [square](/astrology/square/) the two pull across each other, and the pull is useful: neither can win outright, so both have to adjust.

The friction usually takes one of two forms. Either the ambition outruns the deeper change it depends on, a bigger role taken on while something underneath still needs mending, or the depth resists the growth: holding on, controlling, refusing a wider view because the old one felt safe. Pluto's blind spots are close at hand here, mistaking control for safety and intensity for truth, and assuming that every unequal exchange is a deliberate attempt to dominate. Jupiter can enlarge each of them, and the square makes them easier to see.

Squares between these two have a reputation for power struggles. I don't read any [transit](/astrology/transits/) for the outcome of a dispute or a negotiation ([Can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)). Where the friction lands depends on your Pluto's house and on the house Jupiter is crossing, both drawn from the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In a chart that has [Jupiter square Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-square-pluto/), one of these squares shadows each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The square holds Jupiter for about eleven days on a straight pass (my orbs: 1½° before exact, 1° after). When a station falls near the point, as it does in three of every ten, the square comes three times over about eight months. Before 80 there are thirteen or fourteen of them for anyone born from 1930 to 2009.

Jupiter's distance from the square points at birth set the first age: a single sign on 1 January 1965, so about six months; almost half the zodiac on 1 January 2005, so about 5.3 years.

The square is the hinge of each half of the round: about three years after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-pluto/) and three before [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-pluto/), then again on the way back. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-pluto/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-pluto/) bracket it, one a year before, the other a year after.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look at the motive behind the ambition. If you want more, ask what the more is for, and whether something underneath needs attention first.

Renegotiate control, in both directions: what you are holding too tightly, and what you have let someone else hold over you for longer than it fits.

Let the goal find its true size. Some plans need shrinking under this square, others need to grow into what they were always meant to be; the friction tells you which.
