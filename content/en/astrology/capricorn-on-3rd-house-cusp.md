With Capricorn on the 3rd house cusp, the most careful of signs runs the house of daily talk. Your mind tends to work like a well-kept notebook: dated, sourced, and slow to throw anything away.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) holds the ordinary traffic of the mind: messages, errands, what you read, how you learn, your neighbours, and the brothers, sisters or cousins you grew up with ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) on its cusp points to a mind that builds. You learn by method and repetition, and what you learn stays; you write things down; your messages are short, exact and free of exclamation marks; your humour is dry enough that some people miss it. Writing, when you do it, comes with headings before paragraphs, and short journeys tend to follow the same timed route, the commute turned into study time. The risk is the opinion never voiced because it wasn't finished, and a manner on paper that reads colder than you are.

In your ties with siblings and neighbours, this cusp often describes a sense of responsibility in those ties, the one who checked the homework or kept the peace. It describes your side of them, never who the others are. Neighbours tend to know they can count on you, even when they hardly know you.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules this 3rd house: its sign shows how you think and speak, its house where your thinking does its real work, the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Saturn is one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), which is why its house carries real weight in this reading. In most of these charts Aquarius, Saturn's other sign, opens the 4th, and with Uranus sharing it, the way you think and the home you keep come under one ruler.

In Placidus, the house system I use, one birth in 12 or so has Capricorn here, at either latitude, an even share: further below the horizon than the 2nd, this cusp feels the sign's quick rising less. [Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/) comes with it in nearly every chart, Libra in a few, so your chart has two rulers, Pluto and Mars, and I read them both. Mars has its exaltation in Capricorn, which puts your traditional chart ruler at its most disciplined in the sign of your everyday mind. The 3rd is about 39° wide at London, so Capricorn opens no second house, and in nearly a third of the London charts Aquarius sits wholly inside the 3rd ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), adding an independent, inventive streak to a careful mind. The Moon, ruling Cancer on the 9th cusp opposite, gives your beliefs the warmth your daily words keep in reserve.

Shift the birth later and Capricorn moves up to the 2nd cusp ([Capricorn on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-2nd-house-cusp/)); earlier, and it sits at the IC ([Capricorn on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/)). This cusp needs the birth time to the minute, and without it I don't begin ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
