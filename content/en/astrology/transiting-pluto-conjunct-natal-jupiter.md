When Pluto reaches your Jupiter, what you believe and how you want to grow are reworked from the root. For two to three years the planet of depth sits on the degree of your convictions, and growth tends to go down rather than out.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms by clearing away what has become unsustainable. Your [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands understanding: it makes meaning, holds your principles and reaches for what could grow. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) belief itself is examined to the bottom. A faith you were raised in, a philosophy chosen at twenty, the plan your life was meant to follow: each can be taken apart to see what it rests on, and what survives tends to be held more deeply and more simply.

Some beliefs got you through a hard time. It doesn't make them the ones to keep for life. The conjunction is a season for telling the convictions that carried you from the ones that are true for you now. Wisdom isn't certainty, either: a conviction that has been through Pluto can harden, and the wiser kind keeps a little doubt in reserve.

Jupiter isn't luck or wealth, and this conjunction forecasts neither money nor success nor their loss; I keep transits to what they describe ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

Each of Pluto's contacts with Jupiter works on belief in its own way: the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-jupiter/)) invites study, the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-jupiter/)) lets understanding deepen unprompted, the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-jupiter/)) tests a conviction under pressure, the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-jupiter/)) sets it against someone else's. The conjunction asks what the conviction is made of.

With Jupiter in the 9th, the conjunction comes during Pluto's long stay in the house of belief, study and travel ([Pluto transiting the 9th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-9th-house/)). The cusp of the 9th, like every cusp, moves with the minute of birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within the window my timing uses, 1½° before exact to 1° after, Pluto stays on your Jupiter for about two years and eight months, nearly four years in the longest passes, crossing the exact degree three times in about three passes out of five and five times in the rest.

Jupiter moves about a sign a year, so when the conjunction comes depends on your birth year and month. Fewer than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 meet it by 80; for those who do, the first pass came within orb at anything from a few weeks old to 80, about 32 in the middle. A birth on 1 January 1945 met it at about 25, one on 1 January 1985 at about 33, one on 1 January 1995 at 2, one on 1 January 1935 at about 55; births on 1 January 1955, 1965 and 2005 never meet it by 80. The youngest ages in the count, a few weeks after birth, belong to people born with [Jupiter conjunct Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-pluto/), whose transit was already under way.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it conjoins Jupiters in Aquarius: people born between February 1985 and February 1986, January 1997 and February 1998, or January 2009 and January 2010, and older generations born in 1961–62 and 1973–74.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Study the belief you hold most firmly. Read its best critics and its best defenders, and see which of them you still agree with at the end.

Write down the principles you would keep if you had to start again from nothing. The list will be shorter than you expect, and sturdier.

Keep a little doubt in reserve. Convictions that come through this conjunction are stronger for it, and kinder.
