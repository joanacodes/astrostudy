Truth or peace: with this pair the two keep asking you to choose. Your Sun in Scorpio needs to know the truth under the surface; your Moon in Libra needs things to be fair and calm between you and those you love, and sometimes the truth disturbs the calm.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Libra sits just behind Scorpio, the two signs touching at a border: a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign, air and water, cardinal and fixed. The Sun itself passed through Libra in the weeks before your birth, and your Moon has stayed on there; I read that as needs that keep the manners of an earlier season. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) goes beneath the surface; the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) settles through harmony, an even exchange, a room where nobody is at war. Between them you have depth and diplomacy, and the task of letting each speak at the right time.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your Moon's sign, so what restores you, beauty, fairness, good company, follows Venus's sign and house. Scorpio, your Sun's sign, answers to [Mars](/astrology/mars/) by the old rule and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) by the modern one, and I read both: Mars says how you stand up for the truth, Pluto what you are willing to let end for its sake. When Venus and those two pull apart, the chart shows where courtesy and candour have to strike a deal.

Only 1° to 59° separated the Moon from the Sun it was about to overtake, 24½ to 29½ days into the lunar month. The [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), the month's last stretch, is the phase of almost all these births; the earliest few, about one in eight, still fall in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/). The new moon that came a few days later was usually a [new moon in Scorpio](/astrology/new-moon-in-scorpio/), and in the last hours of the range a Moon at the end of Libra can already be conjunct a Sun at the start of Scorpio. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) places the balsamic phase among the other seven.

One person in 147 or thereabouts has the Moon still in Libra after the Sun has moved on to Scorpio. If your birthday saw the Moon cross into Libra or out of it, the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-virgo/) or the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-scorpio/) could be yours as easily as this page, and I'd need the time to say ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). From the same time comes the rising sign, and with this pair the rising sign often says which goes out to meet people, the diplomat or the investigator.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You want a relationship that is both peaceful and true, and you may not always get both at once. The Libra Moon smooths, adjusts and keeps the evening pleasant; the Scorpio Sun knows when something underneath has gone wrong and can't pretend otherwise for long. A partner who can hear a hard truth said kindly, and say one back, gives this pair what it needs most: a peace that can survive the truth.

## At work {#at-work}

Tact and insight together make you effective in mediation, counselling, negotiation, human resources, law, any role where you have to understand what people aren't saying and then find a fair way forward. The Libra Moon reads the room; the Scorpio Sun reads beneath it. Workplaces full of polite pretence are the hardest for you: the Moon goes along with them, and the Sun pays for it later.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Peace kept at the expense of truth costs this pair a great deal. You may agree to keep things calm and then carry the unspoken part for months, until it bursts out harder than you intended. The balsamic Moon knows about endings, so let small things end early: let small truths out while they are small, kindly, and the calm you keep will be the real thing.
