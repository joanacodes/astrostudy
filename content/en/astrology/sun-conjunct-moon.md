Born with Sun conjunct Moon, you arrived at a new moon: the Moon had drawn level with the Sun and turned its unlit side towards us, at the very start of a lunar month. In your chart, purpose and need set out from the same point.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) gives a chart its centre: who you are, what keeps you engaged, the direction you're growing in. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) looks after that growing self: it registers feeling, forms attachments, remembers what reassured you and adjusts when things change. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two into one movement, and neither gives up its own task.

In plain words, what you're after and what you need tend to arrive together. When something matters to you, you feel it as much as you think it; when a need is met, your direction steadies too. Where other people describe a tug between doing and resting, you're more likely to describe one drive with the feeling already inside it.

That unity has a cost, and I name it gently. Because the two come as one, a setback in either can feel like a setback in both: a plan that falls through can leave you unsettled, and an unsettled week can make you doubt the plan. The work isn't to split them for good. It's to learn which of the two is speaking, so that each gets what it actually asks for.

The new moon opens the month's cycle, and I read a birth there as a beginning more than a balance: an impulse that starts things and discovers what they are by living them. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) places it in the whole cycle.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The sign the two lights share sets the temperament, twice over: in Capricorn you want structure and need it; in Gemini you want variety and run on it. Near a sign's border, the two can sit in neighbouring signs. The house matters as much: that area of life, work or home or friendship, receives your effort and your need for comfort at once.

About 1 person in 24 has the Sun and Moon within 8° of each other, half as many as for the square or the trine, because a month holds one [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) but two half moons and two gibbous moons. Since the Moon pulls ahead of the Sun by about 12° a day, the birth time decides which side of the exact meeting you fell on. Before it, the Moon was still catching up, in the last hours of the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/); after it, the Moon had moved ahead and a new month had begun ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)). One more reason I ask for the birth time before reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A new moon near the Moon's nodes is a [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/), and a few people with this conjunction were born on one.

As the month moves on, the same two lights make the pair's other aspects:

- **[Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/):** about five days after the new moon, or five days before it, a crescent; purpose and need now have room to help each other.
- **[Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/):** a week into the month, or a week before its end, the half moon; the two have parted far enough to get in each other's way.
- **[Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/):** the gibbous moon either side of full; two distinct voices that happen to agree.
- **[Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/):** the full moon, the far end of the month from yours; the two lights face each other instead of standing together.

Born with it, you carry both halves of a pairing that synastry looks for between two people: in [Sun conjunct Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon-synastry/), the Sun belongs to one and the Moon to the other. The natal version needs nobody else.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Every month the Moon in the sky passes over your natal Sun and Moon, a few hours on each: a small private new moon, easy to miss. Once a year, within about a week of your birthday, the Sun crosses your natal Moon and stays close for a couple of days: a good moment to check whether what you're doing still feeds you ([transits](/astrology/transits/)).

Weather, not a forecast: a day with a quieter, more inward tone, never an event.
