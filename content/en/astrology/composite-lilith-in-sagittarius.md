Some pairs come back from every holiday already planning the next one, or with a new conviction to argue about over dinner. Composite Lilith in Sagittarius describes a relationship whose untamed part is its hunger for a wider world, and its refusal to believe what it was handed.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), in my reading, marks what a chart keeps hidden or won't have tamed. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) gives a relationship its own chart by taking the midpoint of each pair of positions, so composite Lilith describes the bond's untamed part. In [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire ruled by [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), it reaches outward. A bond with this Lilith often holds views that neither family shares, plans that sound far-fetched to friends, a frankness that can startle a dinner table. Quietly, it may find the world around it rather small.

Those views needn't be either person's on their own. Two homebodies can make a relationship that keeps looking at the horizon; two people raised in firm beliefs can make one that questions all of them. What it hides is its restlessness, and sometimes its impatience with the people who stayed put. What it won't tame is its own search: a meaning arrived at together, never borrowed from anyone else.

The sign of composite Lilith depends on the two birth dates, not the two birthplaces. Mean Lilith is the same for everyone on Earth at a given moment, so a birthplace only matters through the hour it gives in Universal Time, a few hundredths of a degree at most for the composite. Two people born in Lisbon and in Sydney have the same composite Lilith sign as two people born on the same days in one town. About 1 pair in 12 has it in Sagittarius, as for every sign; pairs born in the same Sagittarius stay of mean Lilith share it, September 1981 to June 1982 or July 1990 to April 1991, among others. The houses, by contrast, need both places and both times.

In one chart, [Lilith in Sagittarius](/astrology/lilith-in-sagittarius/) is a person's hunger for more world than they were offered. In the composite, the hunger is shared, and each of you may meet it first as the other's restlessness. Beside it, [composite Lilith in Scorpio](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-scorpio/) keeps its truths below the surface, while [composite Lilith in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-capricorn/) hides a patient ambition behind a modest face.

The hard part is mistaking movement for meaning. A relationship like this can always be on the way somewhere, and miss the life it has; it can also preach, or let its frankness turn careless. It helps to name the question the two of you are actually chasing, and to bring the horizon home now and then: a course, a walk you've never taken, a conversation with someone who sees the world differently.

I don't draw composites; this is how the astrologers who use them read the technique, together with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It predicts no journey, no move and no parting, and it is for two adults who are both curious enough to look ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
