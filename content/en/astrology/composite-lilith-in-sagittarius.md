Some pairs come back from every holiday already planning the next one, or with a new conviction to argue about over dinner. Composite Lilith in Sagittarius points to that hunger for a wider world, half hidden, and to a refusal to believe whatever the pair was handed.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), in my reading, marks what a chart keeps hidden or won't have tamed. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) gives a relationship its own chart by taking the midpoint of each pair of positions, so composite Lilith describes the bond's untamed part. In [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire ruled by [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), it reaches outward. Pairs with it often hold views that neither family shares, plans that sound far-fetched to friends, a frankness that can startle a dinner table. Quietly, they may find the world around them rather small.

Those views needn't be either person's on their own: someone who loves staying in and someone raised in firm beliefs can, between them, make a relationship that keeps looking at the horizon and questioning everything. Restlessness is what it hides, along with some impatience towards people who stayed put; its search is what it keeps untamed, a meaning arrived at together and never borrowed.

The sign of composite Lilith depends on the two birth dates, not the two birthplaces. Mean Lilith is the same for everyone on Earth at a given moment, so a birthplace only matters through the hour it gives in Universal Time, a few hundredths of a degree at most for the composite. A pair born in Lisbon and in Sydney gets the same composite Lilith sign as a pair born on the same two days in one town. About 1 pair in 12 has it in Sagittarius, as for every sign, and it comes to any two people born within the same Sagittarius months of mean Lilith: September 1981 to June 1982, or July 1990 to April 1991, among others. The houses, by contrast, need both places and both times.

[Lilith in Sagittarius](/astrology/lilith-in-sagittarius/), in one person's chart, is a hunger for more world than they were offered; the composite version is the same hunger with two people feeding it. Turn back a sign and [composite Lilith in Scorpio](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-scorpio/) keeps its truths below the surface; go on a sign and [composite Lilith in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-capricorn/) hides a patient ambition behind a modest face.

The hard part is mistaking movement for meaning. A relationship like this can always be on the way somewhere, and miss the life it has; it can also preach, or let its frankness turn careless. It helps to name the question the two of you are actually chasing, and to bring the horizon home now and then: a course, a walk you've never taken, a conversation with someone who sees the world differently.

I don't draw composites; this is how the astrologers who use them read the technique, together with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It predicts no journey, no move and no parting, and it is for two adults who are both curious enough to look ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
