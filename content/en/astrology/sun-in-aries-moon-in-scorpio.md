Aries and Scorpio share nothing, no element, modality or polarity, except the planet the old books gave them both: Mars. With the Sun in the first and the Moon in the second, you were born in the days just after a full moon, and I read the pair as courage that shows and courage that stays out of sight.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aries is cardinal fire, quick and open; Scorpio is fixed water, slow to trust and deep once it does. Counted from the Sun, the Moon sits seven signs on, five back the short way round: a quincunx by sign, the relation of signs that keep having to adjust to each other. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) puts its purpose out in front of everyone; the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) keeps its feelings where only a few are allowed.

The rulers are where this pair is unusual. In the traditional scheme [Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules both Aries and Scorpio, so one planet carries the self's drive and also guards the heart's borders. Modern astrologers give Scorpio to [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) instead, and I read both: Mars for where you act and defend, Pluto for where you go deep and rebuild after a loss. Tradition also puts the lights at the two ends of its scale, the Sun exalted in Aries and the Moon in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Scorpio: purpose at ease, need asked to work harder for its comfort. The old label describes effort, and nothing about your capacity for feeling.

The Moon had passed the full by up to 59°: it stood 181° to 239° on from the Sun, fifteen to nineteen and a half days after a new moon, its light already beginning to shrink. Seven births in eight with this pair fall in the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase of the eight-phase count, the days just after the full, and the rest in the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains why the astrologers' full moon lasts several days when the sky's lasts a moment.

About 1 person in 147 has this pair. If the Moon left Libra for Scorpio, or Scorpio for Sagittarius, on the day you were born (it moves to a new sign roughly every 2½ days), only the time tells this page from the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-libra/) and the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-sagittarius/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign hangs on the same time. The three together make an opening sketch, and a Scorpio Moon, of all Moons, keeps a good deal off the first page.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You may be frank about everything except what you feel most. The Aries Sun says what it wants straight away; the Scorpio Moon tests the ground before it shows anything that matters. Partners can be surprised by the gap: someone so direct, and so private. Trust, once given, is complete, and a betrayal is not quickly forgotten, which the Aries side may not admit even to itself. Letting a partner see a little more of what moves you, and a little earlier, gives the bond the depth this Moon is looking for.

## At work {#at-work}

Nerve and staying power come in the same person here. You can take on a hard problem quickly and then stay with it long after others have drifted off. Turnarounds, negotiations with real stakes, work that needs both boldness and a closed mouth tend to suit the pair. Office politics is harder going: the Aries Sun can say too much, and the Scorpio Moon can remember too long. Choose places where candour is welcome and confidences are kept.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Fire meets fixed water, and the steam builds. Hurt makes the Aries Sun want to do something about it at once; the Scorpio Moon wants to hold it, turn it over and decide later. Under strain you may flare and then seal up, which leaves people unsure whether the matter is closed. What helps is giving the anger a clean, physical outlet, and soon, and the deeper feeling a slower one, a notebook, one trusted listener, so neither has to wait for the other to finish.
