Sun square Chiron sets the two a quarter of the zodiac apart, so a tender place meets your sense of self from the side rather than at its centre. It describes the effort of shining around something that once didn't work, and the practical skill that effort builds.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is agency and self-expression: acting from your own centre, and being recognised for it. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is a place where something didn't come easily, and where you learned to compensate and adapt. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction that drives development: the tender place keeps getting in the way of how you want to act, and so keeps asking for a new approach.

In plain words, there's a subject on which your wish to shine and your tender place collide. You go to speak, and the old worry about not being heard comes back; you want to lead, and an old doubt about being trusted with it returns. The square doesn't ask you to push through by force, or to give up. It asks you to adapt: change the method, the pace, the tool or the setting, so that you can take part fully. That is a skill, and a rare one. Friction isn't failure, and needing another way isn't a flaw. I read Chiron squares the way I read the three placements that held most often in my tests ([how I read them gently](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#gently)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as an area of life where ease had to be built by hand, and as a talent for workarounds that other people find surprisingly useful. The two signs share a modality but not an element: with Chiron in Aries, a Sun in Cancer or Capricorn meets the subject of confidence through home or through ambition. With the Sun in the 10th and Chiron in the 1st, for instance, a public role keeps meeting an old feeling of not being quite right as you are, and often learns to put others at ease because of it.

Counting within 8°, about 1 person in 11 is born with this square. The closer to exact, the more often the tender place makes itself felt, and the more skill you build around it.

The pair's other aspects set the tender place at other angles:

- **[Sun conjunct Chiron](/astrology/sun-conjunct-chiron/):** the tender place at the centre, so identity and sore spot are one subject rather than two that collide.
- **[Sun sextile Chiron](/astrology/sun-sextile-chiron/):** the same experience at a friendly angle, offered to your purpose when you want it.
- **[Sun trine Chiron](/astrology/sun-trine-chiron/):** adapting comes easily, and the tender place rarely blocks the way.
- **[Sun opposite Chiron](/astrology/sun-opposite-chiron/):** the friction moves across the chart and arrives through other people.

Between two charts, [Sun square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-chiron-synastry/) is one person's way of shining pressing on the other's tender place, often without meaning to. Gentleness and plain words help, and I read it in both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-sun/) tends to bring the old friction back to the surface: a season to notice which adaptations still serve you and which you've outgrown. When it comes depends on your generation, since Chiron's speed changes so much along its orbit. A shorter echo comes twice a year, when the Sun in the sky squares your natal Chiron for a day or two. Weather, not a forecast: a Chiron transit says nothing about your health and nothing about events; it describes where attention is asked for.
