Gemini on the 6th house cusp opens the house of ordinary days in the sign that can least bear a dull one. Work, routines and looking after yourself all go better here with a change of task, a conversation and something to find out.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Read through its cusp, the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) shows how you like a working day to run ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) there, it runs on several tracks: a list with more than one column, tasks you switch between, messages answered in the gaps. Work that involves coordinating, explaining, writing, selling or moving about tends to suit the pace, though no cusp chooses a trade. Colleagues matter as people to think aloud with, and a workplace where nobody talks is usually the tiring kind. Routines stick when they keep some variety: the same walk by a different street, the same task in a new order. Looking after yourself follows the same rule, and what helps you unwind is often a change of activity rather than rest in the strict sense.

The 6th already belongs to [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) by nature, through Virgo, its natural sign. Gemini on the cusp hands it to Mercury in your chart as well, though to the quick, many-handed Mercury of Gemini rather than the careful, exacting one of Virgo. Mercury's house shows what your daily work serves: the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Virgo itself usually lands further on: on the 9th cusp in about 2 London charts in 5 and more than half at New York, otherwise on the 8th cusp or wholly inside the 8th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). When it opens the 9th, your working days and your studies, travels or teaching answer to one planet.

This is one of Gemini's commonest cusps, held for nearly two and a half hours of each day: about 1 birth in 10, at London and at New York, with Placidus houses. Gemini rises fast but sets slowly, and the 6th cusp lies just below the western horizon, where the sign moves at its slow setting pace. The Ascendant is Capricorn in about half the London charts and 2 in 3 at New York, and Sagittarius in the rest. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn Ascendant, likes a plan; Mercury, ruling the 6th, keeps changing it, and the days that work best make room for both. In nearly half the London charts, the ones with Sagittarius rising, Gemini also opens the 7th, so your colleagues and your partners deal with the same Mercury ([Gemini on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/)); in about 2 in 5, it opens the 5th as well, and work and play share a ruler ([Gemini on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/), [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)).

Bad Fortune was the 6th's old name, and illness its old subject; I read it for the working day and the care you take of yourself, and a chart has no way to diagnose an illness or foresee one ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). None of this can be placed without the birth time, either: it alone settles which cusp Gemini is on ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
