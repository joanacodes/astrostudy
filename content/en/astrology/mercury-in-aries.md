Some minds wait until a thought is finished before they let it out. With Mercury in Aries the thought finishes on its way out: you speak to discover what you think, and the first version is often the honest one.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), the part of a chart that takes in information and passes it on, runs here on the fuel of [Aries](/astrology/aries/): cardinal fire, ruled by Mars, quick, direct and self-starting. Together they make a mind that reacts first and reflects second: you see the problem, name it and start on it while the room is still describing it. This Mercury likes questions with an answer you can act on today.

Aries exalts the Sun, not Mercury, and Mercury has neither a home nor a weakness here, so there's no dignity to weigh: I read the sign for its manner and nothing more.

Its visits are as brisk as the sign: about 17 days as a rule, though in the years Mercury turns retrograde in Aries it can linger for up to 69, doubling back over the same degrees. If you were born during those backward weeks themselves, I read this quick mind as one that also goes over its first answers in private, later ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)). Of everyone born from 1930 to 2009, about 1 person in 13 has Mercury in Aries.

Mercury is never more than about 28° from the Sun, so your Sun is in Aries too, or in the sign on either side. With a Pisces Sun, this Mercury gives plain, quick words to a self that feels its way; with a Taurus Sun, it's the fast tongue of a patient person; with an Aries Sun, mind and purpose run at the same speed. The neighbours read differently: [Mercury in Pisces](/astrology/mercury-in-pisces/) works by impression, [Mercury in Taurus](/astrology/mercury-in-taurus/) takes its time. Then look at [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the ruler of Aries: its sign and house say where this mind spends its fire.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, Mercury in Aries says the thing. If something is wrong, you'd rather name it today than carry it for a week, and you tend to trust people who do the same. Arguments can flare fast and be over fast: once it's said, you're ready to move on, sometimes before the other person has started. That's the gap to watch. A partner who needs an evening to find their words isn't avoiding you; they're on a different clock. The sentence that helps this Mercury most in a relationship is a short one: *take your time, I'll wait*.

## At work {#at-work}

This Mercury is good at the beginning of things: the brainstorm, the first draft, the meeting where somebody has to say what everyone is thinking. It copes well with urgency, because it can decide with half the information and correct on the way. Troubleshooting, pitching, live problem-solving and jobs with short feedback loops tend to suit it. Long reviews and slow approvals test its patience. The answer isn't to slow your mind down. It's to hand the second pass to a process, or to a colleague who enjoys it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The first answer can harden into the final one. Speed feels like clarity, and a quick conclusion is easy to mistake for a sure one; reasoning isn't certainty, however fast it arrives. You may finish other people's sentences, and finish them wrong, or say something true in a way that lands harder than you meant. Slow explanations can feel like time lost, which is hard on the people giving them, and on you when you're the one who needs to learn slowly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep the first draft and add a second look. Write the reply, then send it after lunch. Ask one question before you give your answer, and listen to the end of it. Moving helps too: a walk, a run, anything that gives the body something to do while the mind works, which suits a Mercury in the sign of Mars. None of this is about thinking less quickly. It's about letting a fast mind earn the trust that people give to a careful one.
