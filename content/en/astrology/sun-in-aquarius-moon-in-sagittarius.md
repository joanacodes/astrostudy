Two signs behind your Sun, in Sagittarius, sits your Moon, and both signs look ahead: Aquarius to a future people could share, Sagittarius to the horizon and what it might mean. You were born just after the last quarter, when the waning half moon is still up in the morning sky. It's a pairing I think of as one long view held from two sides, by ideas and by a sense of meaning.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Air and fire, fixed and mutable, with Sagittarius two signs before Aquarius: a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) by sign, each element keeping the other alight. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) finds its purpose in what could be better for people in general, and holds to it steadily; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) gets its balance back by widening the frame: a long walk, a big question, a plan for somewhere it hasn't been. Both like room. When something goes wrong, you may reach first for the larger picture, the cause and the lesson, before the small hurt in the middle of it.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules Sagittarius and speaks for your Moon: its sign and house show where your feelings look for growth and meaning. Aquarius comes with a pair of rulers, and I read both: here they pull in useful directions, [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) towards what hasn't been tried yet and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) towards what can be built and kept. Side by side, your Moon's ruler widens and your Sun's older ruler gives shape, and life tends to go best when they take turns. Sagittarius gives the Moon no dignity, and Aquarius is the Sun's [detriment](/astrology/detriment/).

The Moon was 271° to 329° on from the Sun, which puts your birth 22 to 27 days into the lunar month. The [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) phase holds most of these births, roughly seven in eight; the latest are [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) births, a few days from the next new moon. Around 300° the lights make a waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). I read the last quarter as the month's time for sorting and dropping what no longer fits, and a Sagittarius Moon sorts by meaning: what still matters goes on, the rest is let go with surprising ease. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the last quarter and the balsamic phase after it.

Some 1 person in 147 is born with these two long views together. The Moon takes about two and a half days to cross Sagittarius, from Scorpio to Capricorn, so on a day it changed sign the time is what separates this page from the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-scorpio/) or the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-capricorn/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Jupiter's house, which says where this Moon goes looking for room, also waits on that time, and so does the rising sign.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Freedom and honesty are the two things you need most from someone close. The Sagittarius Moon wants a partner who laughs easily, tells the truth and lets your evenings be your own; the Aquarius Sun wants a friend and an equal. Both lights can make closeness look optional, even when it isn't. Say out loud that you're staying, especially on the days you need room, so a partner doesn't have to guess.

## At work {#at-work}

Teaching, research, publishing, travel, international or humanitarian work: anything that serves a future you believe in and lets you keep learning. The Aquarius Sun brings the plan for people in general; the Sagittarius Moon brings the enthusiasm that keeps everyone going. Routine with no horizon wears you down faster than hard work, and so does a manager who asks you to stop asking why.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two forward-looking signs can live a little ahead of the present. A plan for next year can stand in for a feeling about today, and a principle can answer a question nobody asked. Because the last quarter lets go easily, you may also drop something too soon, a friendship or a project, when it only needed a pause. Keep one small, ordinary commitment going through every season, a weekly call or a shared meal, to anchor the long view.
