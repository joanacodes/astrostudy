Mars conjunct Neptune in synastry puts one person's drive on the very degree where the other dreams. What one of you can picture, the other wants to get up and do. It makes a fine engine for shared projects, as long as both of you notice whose wish is being carried out.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is how a person goes after what they want: initiative, effort, assertion, the push to finish. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is how they imagine what might be worth wanting: inspiration, compassion, a feel for meaning, and an easing of edges that needn't be there. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) runs two functions as one process without erasing either, and as one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one splits the process between two people: one brings the push, the other the picture.

Holding the Mars, you may find your energy suddenly has somewhere to go. The other person's ideals make effort feel worthwhile, and you'll often work harder for a cause you share with them than for one you'd chosen alone. Holding the Neptune, you may feel that someone at last takes your imagination seriously enough to act on it: the idea you'd only half said aloud becomes a plan, a repainted room, a fundraiser with a date.

Take Mars in Capricorn on Neptune in Capricorn: a patient, ambitious drive meets an imagination that dreams in practical shapes, and the journey two friends have discussed for years finally gets booked.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

At this distance, whose wish is whose gets hard to say. The Mars person can start acting on what they believe the other wants, and the Neptune person, glad to see the dream move, may not mention that it was never quite this one.

Carrying Mars, the risk is effort spent on a picture rather than a request: you deliver, and the result doesn't land the way the image did. Carrying Neptune, it runs the other way: you take in their drive so readily that their goals start to feel like your ideals, until you can't find your own want any more. Compassion isn't self-erasure, on either side of this meeting.

Pulled apart to other angles, push and picture keep different company:

- **[Mars sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** two signs apart, they help each other when asked and never merge.
- **[Mars square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-neptune-synastry/):** at right angles, effort and ideal test each other instead of fusing.
- **[Mars trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-neptune-synastry/):** one element carries both, so inspiration flows without anyone standing on the other's degree.
- **[Mars opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** across the zodiac, the two turn into roles to trade.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Say the wish in words before anyone acts on it. "Is this yours, mine or ours?" sounds stiff the first time and becomes a private joke by the fifth. The Mars person can check the brief before starting; the Neptune person can describe the picture in concrete terms, including what they'd rather leave undone. In a couple that might be the holiday nobody quite planned; in a family, an adult child carrying out a parent's dream; at work, a founder's vision and the colleague who builds it.

Within 6°, one way round or the other, about 1 pair of people in 15 shares this conjunction, and only half of it is personal. Neptune needs about fourteen years to cross one sign, which means the Neptune person shares their position, to within a few degrees, with everyone born a couple of years either side of them; the Mars, which changes sign every six or seven weeks, makes the meeting theirs. Neither body covers much ground in a day, and the hour rarely makes or breaks the meeting, but the houses, where in each life the dream gets its legs, need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Where one birth chart holds both, [Mars conjunct Neptune](/astrology/mars-conjunct-neptune/) describes someone moved to act by their own imagination. Between two charts, one of you imagines and the other acts, which is why it pays to ask before starting.
