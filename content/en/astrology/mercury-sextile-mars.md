Mercury sextile Mars joins thought and action at an easy distance, two signs or so apart. Of the five aspects these two planets make, it's the most common, and the one that asks most plainly to be used.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) gathers and connects: it notices, reasons, asks and explains. [Mars](/astrology/mars/) turns intention into motion: it initiates, asserts, and keeps going when a goal costs effort. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers them cooperation: neither takes over, and each can switch the other on.

In plain words, your thinking has access to your drive, and your drive to your thinking, whenever you call on them. A plan doesn't stay a plan for long; a frustration can become a clear sentence instead of a slammed door. You're likely to learn well by doing something small with an idea straight away, and to speak up at the moment it counts rather than an hour later. The sextile won't do it for you: it opens the door, and you still have to walk through.

The sextile's gift to this pair is a working distance. Fused, thought and action can blur; at right angles, they argue. Two signs apart, they can hear each other, so you can be decisive without being hasty and frank without being harsh: there's just enough room to choose your moment.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A sextile usually links signs of compatible elements, so your Mercury and Mars tend to speak the same language: quick and outspoken in fire and air, practical and quietly determined in earth and water. The houses show where the opening sits: Mercury in the 6th sextile Mars in the 4th might bring the same practical energy to daily work and to the home.

About 1 person in 12 is born with Mercury and Mars within 6° of a sextile, more than for any of the pair's other aspects: seen from Earth, Mars spends much of its time within a couple of signs of the Sun, and Mercury never leaves it. Mercury is usually the faster; born while it was retrograde, you may find the same opening works through rehearsal first, thinking a move through before making it. In a reading, I treat a sextile as an invitation, never as a gift already received ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

Where this sextile sits among the pair's aspects:

- **[Mercury conjunct Mars](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-mars/):** no distance at all; idea and act come out together, faster and less chosen.
- **[Mercury square Mars](/astrology/mercury-square-mars/):** a quarter of the circle apart, where the same two functions push against each other and learn from the push.
- **[Mercury trine Mars](/astrology/mercury-trine-mars/):** a wider, rarer angle; the flow is smoother, and asks less of you.
- **[Mercury opposite Mars](/astrology/mercury-opposite-mars/):** across the chart, only while Mars is retrograde; the words and the drive can seem to belong to two different people.

Between two charts, [Mercury sextile Mars in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-mars-synastry/) lets one person's ideas set the other's energy going, and that energy give the ideas legs. I read it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Transiting Mars makes this sextile to your Mercury twice per lap of the zodiac, a lap of about two years, holding it a few days at a time. Mercury reaches the sextile to your natal Mars twice a year or so and lingers about a day, with extra passes when its retrograde loop falls nearby. Weather, not a forecast: small windows for the email you've been putting off, or the first step of something you've only talked about.
