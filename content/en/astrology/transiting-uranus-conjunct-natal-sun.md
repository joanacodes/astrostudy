Uranus takes about 84 years to go round the zodiac, so it reaches the degree of your Sun once at most, and about 1 person in 25 hasn't met it by 80. When it comes, it stays well over a year, and its question is simple to state: is the self you've built still the one you want to be?

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity, vitality and purpose: the centre you act from and the direction you're heading. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees: it experiments, reforms what has grown too tight and insists on autonomy. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses them, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) being yourself and doing things differently become the same project. A role that once fitted can feel like a costume; a direction followed out of habit can lose its pull; a part of you that was waiting grows restless to be tried.

I read it as renewal rather than rupture. Innovation isn't chaos and autonomy isn't isolation: the people who come through this transit best tend to change on purpose and stay connected while they do. Some change visibly, a new field, a new way of living; others quietly, in how they decide and what they no longer agree to. Neither is required, and nothing here foretells a move, a break-up or a lost job ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

Your Sun's house shows where the room is needed. With the Sun in your 10th, the conjunction falls during [Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/), and work is where you'll want to reinvent; in your 4th, it's home. That house comes from your birth time, and without it I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus stays on your Sun for about twenty months. More than nine times in ten it crosses the degree three times as it turns retrograde, and even the shortest passes last about eleven months.

The age it comes at depends on how far behind your Sun Uranus stood when you were born: about seven years for every sign between them. If you were born just after Uranus had passed your Sun, as many people with [Sun conjunct Uranus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus/) were, you're among the 1 in 25 who don't meet it by 80. Uranus is in Gemini until 2033, after a first visit in 2025: with the Sun in Gemini, born roughly between 21 May and 20 June, yours is under way or close, earlier for the earlier birthdays. Taurus Suns had theirs between 2018 and 2026; Cancer Suns come next, from 2032.

Counting from this crossing, Uranus needs about fourteen years to reach [transiting Uranus sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-sun/), twenty-one for [transiting Uranus square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-sun/), twenty-eight for [transiting Uranus trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-sun/) and forty-two for [transiting Uranus opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-sun/). In one lifetime, most people see only part of that sequence.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change one thing on purpose before change is made for you: a habit, a schedule, a way of working. Uranus on the Sun favours experiments you choose.

Give the new self a trial period rather than a verdict. Try the role, the city, the craft for a season, and decide afterwards what stays.

Keep your people close while you change. The freedom this transit asks for works best with connection, not at its expense.
