Some people refuse to lower their voice for anyone with a title, and they may be standing beside you just as you're trying to be taken seriously. Their Lilith in your 10th house puts that refusal at the top of your chart, where your work, your standing and your reputation are.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), in my reading, is what a person kept of themselves after being told it was too much, and it rarely bows to rank. [The 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is the part of your life lived in view: your work, the role you play in the world, your reputation and the authority you earn or push against. This [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) puts their unbowed side in the middle of your public life.

With them, you may stop waiting for permission to be visible. They tend to say out loud that you deserve the credit, that the job is too small for you, that a change of direction is allowed, and a career built to please a parent or a boss can start to feel like someone else's. Natal [Lilith in the 10th house](/astrology/lilith-in-10th-house/) is authority that doesn't ask permission; with this overlay, you borrow a little of theirs.

For them, your ambitions or your standing can stir an old quarrel with authority, or show them that a public life needn't cost them their edges. The 10th is angular and the most public house of the chart; [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), its natural sign, builds slowly and means it to last. Close to the cusp, their Lilith meets your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) itself, and I read that contact as one of the louder notes in the whole synastry.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Visibility cuts both ways. Their frankness may surface in your working world at the wrong moment, a remark at a colleague's leaving drinks, a public quarrel with someone you need, and you may feel your reputation is being gambled with. If one of you employs or manages the other, who holds the authority can become the whole question.

From their side, your ambition may look like the very hierarchy they refused to join, and your caution in public like shame about them. They may push you to rebel more than your work can bear.

Nothing here forecasts a promotion, a dismissal or a fall from grace; I don't read careers as events. It shows how two people's refusals and ambitions meet in public.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Decide together what part of your bond belongs in public, and keep the rest for yourselves. When their courage helps you ask for more, use it; when it would cost you something you need, say so. A couple can agree how they talk about each other at work; friends can be each other's honest critics before a big step; colleagues and business partners can give the outspoken one the job of saying what everyone thinks; adult children can stop working for a parent's approval without loving them any less.

The reverse, your Lilith in their 10th house, makes you the unbowed one in their public life. Its neighbours work elsewhere: [their Lilith in your 9th house](/astrology/lilith-in-9th-house-synastry/) on your beliefs, [their Lilith in your 11th house](/astrology/lilith-in-11th-house-synastry/) on your circles and hopes.

In Placidus, the house system I use, your 10th begins at your Midheaven; in whole sign houses the Midheaven can fall in another house, so two astrologers can honestly put their Lilith in different houses of yours ([which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)). Your birth time fixes the Midheaven, their date fixes mean Lilith, the one I read in both charts ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)), and I don't read the pair until both times are known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Careers are personal territory as well, and I read this pair only when both people agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
