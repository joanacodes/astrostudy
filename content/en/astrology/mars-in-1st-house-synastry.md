Their Mars in your 1st house puts another person's drive right at your front door. The way you arrive, move and begin things meets someone whose first instinct is to act, and both of you tend to feel the charge before much has been said.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the planet of drive: it starts things, pushes for what it wants and holds a line when someone crosses it. [The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is your manner and your body, the speed and style with which you meet whatever comes next. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their Mars here aims its energy at the way you show up.

With them, you may find yourself moving faster. You stand straighter, decide sooner, say the blunt thing you'd normally soften. It can feel like being woken up: their push lands on your person, and your own courage answers it. If you've been hovering at the edge of a new start, they're often the one who says "go on, then".

From their side, you're hard to ignore. Your way of entering a room seems to call their energy out, so they react to you fast: they tease, challenge, offer to help with whatever you're beginning. [Aries](/astrology/aries/), the natural sign of this angular house, is the sign Mars rules, so in a sense their Mars arrives somewhere that already speaks its language, and what lands in an angular house is seen at once. Close to your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), their Mars is also conjunct the degree that was rising when you were born, and I give that contact its own place in the reading.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A push aimed at your person can feel personal. You may feel hurried, crowded or quietly tested, as if every meeting were a small contest of pace, or catch yourself bristling at their directness before they've finished the sentence. When irritation rises between you, it shows on your face and in your shoulders first, and they will see it.

For them, the friction is that you answer in kind. Their energy meets a person rather than a plan, so your reactions come straight back at them, and they may feel their drive judged by your expression. Some find that bracing; others feel they must turn themselves down around you.

None of this describes harm. Mars is drive, courage and the wish to act; in the 1st, that drive simply travels the shortest distance there is, from their will to your bearing.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the pace be something you can talk about. If they move faster than you, say so in plain words; if their challenge does you good, say that too, since people with a lot of push rarely hear it. In a couple, agree how you'll disagree before the first heated evening; between friends, this can be the one who gets you to the start line, of a race or a hard conversation; between colleagues, give each of you something to lead, so that two starters aren't fighting over one beginning; between adult relatives, an old rivalry may simply need naming.

Turn the charts round and this is your Mars in their 1st house: you become the one whose pace they feel on their skin. On either side of it, [their Mars in your 12th house](/astrology/mars-in-12th-house-synastry/) stirs what you keep out of sight, and [their Mars in your 2nd house](/astrology/mars-in-2nd-house-synastry/) pushes at what you own and value.

Your 1st house starts at your Ascendant, and the horizon moves a degree in about four minutes, so your birth time decides where the house begins; in Placidus, the house system I use, it also sets how wide the house is. Mars takes about two years to go round the zodiac, about half a degree a day, so their birth date usually places it and their time matters only right on your Ascendant. Both times are on my list before I open a synastry all the same, because their houses are the other half of it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). And a first meeting is the wrong moment to read a near stranger's chart ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#we-just-met)).
