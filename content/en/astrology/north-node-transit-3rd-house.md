The North Node reaches your 3rd house by crossing the lowest point of your chart, the IC, and spends around twenty months there while the South Node crosses your 9th. The axis runs from the next street to the far horizon, and these months lean towards the next street.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows growth that asks for practice. The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is the everyday mind: how you talk, write and learn, the people near you, the short trips of an ordinary week. With the node here, the practice is curiosity: asking instead of assuming, learning a skill in small steps, listening to the neighbour, the sibling, the colleague two desks away.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) meanwhile crosses your 9th, the house of belief and distant places. Its familiar move is the big answer: the theory that explains everything, the certainty, the plan to be somewhere else. Your convictions stay yours; the stretch is to test them against the details in front of you.

The Moon, whose path makes the nodes where it crosses the Sun's, had its joy in the 3rd, the house tradition called the Goddess. If your Moon sits in the 3rd, the node's passage over it tends to turn feelings into words.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the stay begins at your IC, and as the North Node crosses it, the South Node crosses your Midheaven ([transiting North Node opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-midheaven/)). A [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this one brings out what your chart already holds about learning; it doesn't decide what you'll hear or say.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 3rd is one of the houses where the node lingers longest in the middle. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, its stays there last about 20 months as a rule, against a year and a half for the average house, though the range is narrow, from about 11 months to about 2 years. New York's latitude gives about 19 months, from about 13 months to just under 2 years.

The count follows the true node, wobbles and all: if it slips back over the cusp and returns within 120 days, the stay hasn't ended. I look at both nodes, mean and true ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), and when they disagree about a cusp, I say so.

Your IC and the width of your 3rd both come from your birth time, the thing I ask for before anything else ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node arrives from your 4th ([North Node transiting the 4th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-4th-house/)) and moves on to your 2nd ([North Node transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-2nd-house/)).

The sign of your IC, opposite your Midheaven's, gives you the start. The node is in Aquarius from July 2026 to March 2028, so an Aquarius IC is crossed in that window. A Capricorn IC follows (March 2028 to September 2029), then Sagittarius (to March 2031), Scorpio (to December 2032), Libra (to June 2034), Virgo (to November 2035), Leo (to May 2037) and Cancer (to February 2039). For a Pisces IC, the crossing fell between January 2025 and July 2026. Gemini, the 3rd's natural sign, has the node from February 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask one more question than usual in each conversation, and let the answer move your mind a little.

Learn something small and practical: the first hundred words of a language, a tool, a route across town. The North Node in the 3rd likes progress you can hear yourself making.

Keep the big picture, but put it in short sentences. The South Node's conviction is a resource when it has to explain itself to the person next door.
