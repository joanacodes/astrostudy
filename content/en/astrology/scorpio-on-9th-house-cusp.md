Scorpio on the 9th house cusp gives the far horizon, belief, study and long journeys, to the sign that goes to the bottom of things. Your convictions tend to be ones you've paid for.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is where a chart looks for meaning, and it shares something with [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/): both are about the search for truth ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With Scorpio on its cusp, that search tends to be personal and thorough. A worldview is something you rebuild after a crisis rather than inherit, and you'd rather hold a truth that unsettles you than a comfort that doesn't hold. Study, for you, is investigation: one subject followed for years, down to the original sources. Travel may mean going back to the same place until you know it from the inside. And when you teach, you tend to teach what you've lived through. You may distrust a teacher who can't say what they don't know, and trust one who has changed their mind for good reasons; a faith, if you hold one, is likely to be one you've argued with.

Your search for meaning answers to [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), Scorpio's two rulers, and I read both; the houses they occupy show where it leads: the [1st](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Aries, Mars's other sign, opens your 2nd in about 7 London charts in 10 and 4 in 5 at New York, so what you believe and what you value and own are run by the same planet: what you'd spend yourself on tends to follow your convictions. In the other charts Aries has no cusp. Venus, through Taurus on the opposite cusp, has your everyday words and errands.

High in the western sky, the 9th sees less of Scorpio than the cusps around the Ascendant do: the sign opens it in about 1 London birth in 14, and 1 New York birth in 13, counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use. The house is narrow here, about 20° to 25° at London, so Scorpio often runs on to the Midheaven. In about 1 London chart in 5, and 1 in 8 at New York, it holds both the 9th and the 10th ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), and Pluto and Mars rule your public role as well as your beliefs ([Scorpio on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/)). Aquarius rises in about 2 London charts in 3, giving the chart to Saturn and Uranus, both of which I read; Capricorn rises in most of the rest. When Scorpio has no cusp at all, as in about 1 London birth in 7 (1 in 11 at New York), it is the houses below the horizon that hold it, and once in a while the 7th, never the 9th.

Within a couple of hours either side of your birth, Scorpio moves through three positions: up on the Midheaven, on the 9th, then down on the 8th ([Scorpio on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/)). Where in that passage you were born is a matter of minutes, and minutes are what a rounded time loses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
