Jupiter conjunct Lilith in synastry lays one person's Jupiter over the other's Black Moon Lilith. Whatever the first person believes in, and the room those beliefs make for other people, falls on exactly what the second learned to keep out of view: the part once called too much.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens: it learns, finds meaning, forms principles, makes room for growth. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point where the Moon's orbit takes it farthest from Earth, is read very differently from one school to the next; I read it as instinctive autonomy, the material someone set aside to be acceptable, and their own sense of power and limits. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes two functions act as one, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one gives the widening to one person and the untamed part to the other.

If the Lilith is yours, you may find in this person a philosophy with room for what you hid. They don't flinch at your appetite or your refusals; often they seem to think those are worth something. That can feel like air after years in a small room, and on other days like being turned into an example, your wildness admired and fitted into their view of the world.

If the Jupiter is yours, the other person's untamed side tends to make sense to you, and to stretch your principles. What they refuse, you find yourself defending; what they keep hidden, you sense deserves room. Your generosity of mind has found something it wants to make larger.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A conjunction blurs whose story it is. The Jupiter person can adopt the other's refusal as a cause, complete with a theory of what their freedom should look like; the Lilith person can start showing the hidden part only in this company, shaped to fit the other's view of it. Expansion isn't excess and autonomy isn't immorality: the risk is quieter, a private refusal turned into a public principle before its owner chose that.

- **[Jupiter sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** room offered at a slight distance, as an invitation that can be declined.
- **[Jupiter square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-lilith-synastry/):** principles catch on a refusal, and both get examined.
- **[Jupiter trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-trine-lilith-synastry/):** the same welcome without the blur, four signs apart.
- **[Jupiter opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** belief and refusal face each other, each person holding one end.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the person whose Lilith it is decide what gets said, and to whom. If the Jupiter is yours, admire without recruiting: you can value someone's refusal without turning it into your argument. If the Lilith is yours, you can enjoy the room and still keep some of it private. Friends with this conjunction often become each other's permission; in a couple it can make a home where nobody has to shrink; at work it's often enough to know the other person won't be shocked.

Who has it depends on two birth dates more than on two people. Someone born in mid-1975 has it with anyone born between March and June 1976, whose Lilith falls on that 1975 Jupiter, and again with people born from February to May 1985, once mean Lilith has come round. Everyone born in those months shares it, so it never enters my count of a synastry, which takes only contacts involving the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. I read it through the houses, as a difference between two eras, here two spells of a few months, more than between two people.

Those houses need both birth times; the hour itself never decides whether the conjunction exists, since Jupiter and Lilith barely move in a day ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The months above use mean Lilith, the one I read ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). My notebook found that Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), but for Lilith in a person's own chart; this conjunction has no figure.

In one chart, [Jupiter conjunct Lilith](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-lilith/) belongs to a person whose beliefs grew out of what they wouldn't give up. Between two, it touches something private in one of them, so I look at it only when both have asked ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
