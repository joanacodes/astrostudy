Some of the node's openings are practical; this one is made of imagination. For a month or so, twice a round, the direction your chart grows in stands two signs from your Neptune, and a gentler skill is invited in without ceremony.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where a chart dreams, empathises and senses something larger than itself. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the capacity you build by practising it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through an opportunity that has to be taken up, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) imagination and growth can cooperate for a while, if you give them a time and a place.

The invitation tends to be small and easy to pass by: a drawing class, a choir, a hand to lend at a shelter, ten minutes of quiet in the morning. Taken up, it lets the direction you are growing in borrow some of Neptune's softness. The one blind spot worth watching is Neptune's habit of confusing the idea of a thing with the thing: imagining the class is not taking it. At the other end of the axis the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) trines your Neptune, so your familiar ways of switching off and dreaming stay easy too; restful, as long as they don't take the place of the new step.

For a generation the dates line up. From July 2026 to March 2028 the node crosses Aquarius and sextiles the Neptune of everyone born with [Neptune in Sagittarius](/astrology/neptune-in-sagittarius/), from January 1970 to November 1984, while the South Node in Leo trines it. For many born from 1981 to 1984, Uranus was in Sagittarius too, so the same months bring them [Transiting North Node sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-uranus/). Where in your own life the invitation lands depends on your houses, and so on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

About a month is usual for this sextile, counted the way I count every transit, from 1½° before exact to 1° after. If an eclipse season overlaps, the node creeps while the Sun is near it and the sextile lasts three months or more; about one pass in 25 brings three exact hits. My forecast doesn't list this sextile, since it counts the node only by conjunction or opposition, and I treat it as background colour.

Two a round means eight or nine sextiles to your Neptune in eighty years. The wait for the first sextile ran from 2.3 years, for a birth on 1 January 1970, to 9.9, for one on 1 January 1980. One sextile comes about three years before [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-neptune/), the other about three years after it, on the way to [Transiting North Node square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-neptune/), [Transiting North Node trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-neptune/) and [Transiting North Node opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-neptune/). [Neptune sextile North Node](/astrology/neptune-sextile-north-node/) is this angle fixed at birth.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to one small creative or caring commitment for the month: a class, a rota, a page a day.

Pick a time for it. Neptune's invitations dissolve when they have no hour attached.

Notice the difference between rest that restores you and drifting that only fills time, and choose the first.
