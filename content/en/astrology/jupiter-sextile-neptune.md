Jupiter sextile Neptune opens a door between what you believe and what you imagine. Neither takes over: the sextile simply lets an inspiration find a purpose, or a principle find a picture, whenever you reach for it. It is shared within short spells of births, so your houses decide where the door is.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens understanding: it learns, believes and ties experience into a meaning. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves unnecessary boundaries: it imagines, empathises, inspires and senses the symbolic side of things. Linked by a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), they offer each other chances: either can set the other going, as long as someone takes the opening.

In plain words, your imagination tends to serve your sense of purpose, and the other way round. A story, an image or a feeling for someone else's situation can become the start of a project, a course or a commitment; a belief can find its language in music, writing or care. Nothing forces it, so the sextile can stay a pleasant daydream for years, and it shows most once inspiration is given a job to do.

Potential isn't a guarantee. Put to work, this aspect becomes a quiet skill: turning compassion into help that actually helps, and hope into plans with some poetry in them.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Thirteen short spells between 1930 and 2009 carry it within 6°, about 1 birth in 16 in all: June to August 1930; November 1934 to January 1935 and May to September 1935; October to November 1942 and May to July 1943; November 1947 to January 1948; September 1955 to July 1956; March to June 1960 and November 1960 to January 1961; September 1968 to July 1969; March to December 1973; September to November 1981 and May to July 1982; February to April 1986; September to November 1994; June to September 1998 and January to March 1999; and January to May 2007 with September to November 2007.

The sextile belongs to everyone born in those spells; your houses make it particular ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). With Jupiter in the 3rd and Neptune in the 5th, for instance, everyday words might become a way into creative work; with Jupiter in the 10th and Neptune in the 12th, a public role may draw quietly on an inner life. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-neptune/):** the meeting itself, about every thirteen years, as in 1957–58 or 1983–84.
- **[Jupiter square Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-square-neptune/):** ideals and principles in friction, for the births of 1961–62 or 1980, among others.
- **[Jupiter trine Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-trine-neptune/):** a wider, easier current between belief and imagination.
- **[Jupiter opposite Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-neptune/):** belief and imagination facing each other, asking to be balanced.

[Jupiter sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-neptune-synastry/) links one person's sense of meaning to the other's imagination: an easy, inspiring contact, good for shared creative or caring work.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Neptune sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-jupiter/) is among the slowest transits there are: at roughly fourteen years per sign, Neptune lingers by the sextile over several passes, a gentle season for imagination to feed what you believe. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-neptune/) is short and frequent by comparison, a few days or weeks at a time.

Weather, not a forecast: an invitation to give a dream something useful to do, which you are free to accept or decline.
