Cancer on the 4th house cusp is the IC in its own sign: the house of home, family and roots opening in the sign that belongs to it by nature. The ground under the whole chart is made of care, memory and the need to belong.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) as what lets you rest and start again, and with [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) there, it is a place where people are fed and remembered ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). Home tends to be somewhere you tend as well as live: the pot on the stove for whoever turns up, the drawer of photographs, the recipe nobody ever wrote down. You recharge in familiar surroundings, and a move can weigh more than a change of address. The private self underneath is tender and protective, slow to show itself and fiercely loyal once it has. Belonging isn't only a family matter here: a friend's kitchen, a town or a community can become the ground you stand on. Roots of this kind can travel, too: a few objects, a recipe and a voice on the phone can make a new place home within weeks. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) describes home as you lived it and what you need from it now, not who your relatives were.

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules the 4th with this cusp, as it does in the natural zodiac, so sign, house and ruler agree. Its house shows where your sense of home leads: the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). The Moon is a quick, changeable ruler, round the zodiac in about 27⅓ days, and I read the roots it describes as roots that need tending rather than roots that are simply there. Across the chart, Capricorn is always on the Midheaven, and Saturn rules your public life ([Capricorn on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/)): the meridian axis sits in its natural signs, care at the base and responsibility at the top, and much of a life with this IC is the balance between the two.

About 1 birth in 11 has Cancer on the IC, at London as at New York. In Placidus, the house system I use, the IC is the 4th cusp and takes its sign from the hour, whatever the latitude; Cancer, the first sign after the June solstice, crosses the meridian slowly, in a little over two hours. [Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/) comes with it in just over half the London charts and [Aries rising](/astrology/aries-rising/) in about 2 in 5; at New York Aries is the commoner of the two. With Taurus rising, Venus rules the chart and comfort is what people meet first; with Aries rising, Mars does, and the manner is quicker than the private self it protects. Cancer also opens the 3rd in about 2 London charts in 5 and the 5th in about 1 in 3 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), so [Cancer on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/) or [Cancer on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/) may be yours as well.

Ask me to read an IC without a birth time and I'd have nothing to read: the time is what puts Cancer at the base of your chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
