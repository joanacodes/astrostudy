Your Leo Sun wants to make one thing whole and stand behind it; your Gemini Moon settles by talking, comparing and moving between several. The Moon had already passed its last quarter when you arrived, and I read the pair as a heart with a running commentary.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fire and air sit well together, and only two signs separate your Moon from the Sun it follows: a sextile by sign, where the two lend each other a hand as soon as there's an opening. Their modes differ, fixed Leo and mutable Gemini. The [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) gives itself to one great loyalty, a person, a project, a craft, and wants it recognised; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) needs words, news and variety to digest what happens. Air feeds a fire, and in this chart the Moon's conversation is often how the Sun's warmth reaches other people.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo and so answers only to itself. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Gemini, and with this pair it can never sit beside your Moon: Mercury stays within about 28° of the Sun, which keeps it in Cancer, Leo or Virgo while the Sun is in Leo. Its sign tells me how this Moon finds its words, tenderly from Cancer, with flair from Leo, exactly from Virgo. Since neither light holds a dignity in these signs, the rulers carry much of the description.

In the sky, your Moon trailed the Sun by 31° to 88° (272° to 329° ahead, as astronomers count), the lunar month 22 to 27 days old. Almost nine births in ten fall in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) phase, the rest in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), and near 60° behind the Sun the chart holds a waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). I read a last quarter birth as someone who settles a pull between want and need by revising the plan, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) tells the eight phases apart.

This pair belongs to about 1 person in 139. If the Moon left Taurus or reached Cancer on your birthday, your page could be this one, the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-taurus/) or the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-cancer/), and the hour decides ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Your rising sign comes from that same hour. With the two lights it sketches how you come across, and a sketch is where I start a reading, never where I stop.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you're warm, funny and easy to talk to, and you need both halves returned: loyalty for the Sun, conversation for the Moon. A partner who admires you in silence leaves the Gemini Moon hungry; one who chats about everything except what you mean to them leaves the Leo Sun cold. You may joke your way past a hurt and then wonder why nobody saw it. Say the one plain sentence first, *that stung*, and let the wit back in afterwards.

## At work {#at-work}

Speaking up for a cause, teaching, writing: the pair comes alive when you speak for something you believe in. The Sun brings conviction and a sense of occasion, the Moon quickness, range and an ear for what people are really saying, so you can hold a room and read it at the same time. Long silent stretches drain the Moon, and work that never carries your name drains the Sun. A role with plenty of variety inside one clear mission keeps both lights awake.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The Sun commits, the Moon keeps its options open, and the pull between them can scatter you. You may promise wholeheartedly on Monday and feel hemmed in by Thursday, or describe a feeling so well that you never quite let yourself feel it. Fixed fire takes a hurt to heart while mutable air moves on, so wounded pride can sit unspoken under a stream of bright talk. What helps is one committed thing at the centre, room for variety around its edges, and a friend who asks the second question.
