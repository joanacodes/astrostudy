Every eight years, between mid-May and late June, Venus turns back in Gemini and stays there until it turns again. It’s the retrograde of the second conversation: the flirtation carried on in messages, the friendship that grew through talk, the small likes collected because they were interesting.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) is mutable air, ruled by Mercury, a sign that compares, connects and likes to keep its options open. Venus there values what it finds interesting, and is often won over by words. When [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) comes to Gemini, the planet seems from Earth to stop and back through the sign for about 43 days, and the curiosity turns to sorting: which of the people you enjoy talking to really matter, which tastes picked up in passing you’ve kept, which of two possibilities you prefer now that you’ve looked at both.

Every one of the five retrogrades in my list, from 2000 to 2040, stays wholly inside Gemini. They come in the same weeks every eight years, each about 2° lower: 26°08′ to 9°37′ in 2004, 23°59′ to 7°29′ in 2012, 21°50′ to 5°20′ in 2020, 19°41′ to 3°10′ in 2028, 17°31′ to 1°01′ in 2036. The most recent ran from 13 May to 25 June 2020; the next runs from 10 May to 22 June 2028. Their arc of about 16½° is the widest of Venus’s five retrograde places. The series drifts towards Taurus ([Venus retrograde in Taurus](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-taurus/)) and away from Cancer ([Venus retrograde in Cancer](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-cancer/)), neither of which has a Venus retrograde in my list.

Born with Venus retrograde in Gemini, you have a way of liking things that compares before it commits, and I read that as part of you for life ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) decides which house Gemini occupies, and so where these weeks fall; it’s also what lets me draw the chart at all, so without it I don’t read one. In Placidus, the house system I use, a retrograde that stays inside one sign often stays inside one house, though an arc of 16½° can still meet a cusp.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/gemini-rising/):** Gemini rising: your lively first manner; the charm you switch on in conversation, noticed and adjusted.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/gemini-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** things collected because they interest you; what you spend on novelty, sorted from what you go on enjoying.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** Gemini in its natural house: chats, messages, short trips and neighbours; an exchange you enjoyed, picked up again.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home full of talk and comings and goings; what makes it pleasant to be there, discussed at the table.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/):** flirting through wit and wordplay; a light romance or a pastime you dropped, remembered for the fun it gave.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/gemini-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a varied working day; the colleagues you like working beside, and why.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Gemini on the Descendant, with Sagittarius rising: a partnership kept alive by conversation; what you still enjoy telling each other.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/gemini-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what’s shared, and what’s said about it; a talk about trust, had more openly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/):** a love of learning; a course, a language or a city that once charmed you, considered again.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/):** work that runs on contacts and words; the network you enjoy, told apart from the one you keep out of duty.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a wide circle of friends; which friendships are still a pleasure and which run on habit.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/gemini-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private daydreams and unsent letters; what you like and haven’t told anyone, sorted quietly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Find your natal Mercury, Gemini’s ruler: its house is where the comparing usually happens. In these weeks, as I read them, talking helps more than deciding: say what you like out loud, to someone or on paper, and listen to how it sounds.

Nothing in the sky sets a deadline on a choice of the heart. Gemini’s gift is to see more than one side, and a retrograde there simply gives you time to look at each.
