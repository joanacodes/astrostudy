A mind full of connections and a heart loyal to its friends and its principles: in this pair both lights live in air, and ideas sit close to the centre of who you are and how you feel. You were born under a waning Moon, a few days past full.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable air meets fixed air four signs back from the Sun: a trine by sign, with the element shared and the mode different. The [Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/) gathers, links and passes on information, and comes alive in exchange; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) steadies itself by understanding, by seeing how a feeling fits a larger pattern, and by staying true to its friends and its principles. Where Gemini moves quickly between ideas, Aquarius picks a few and holds them, sometimes for life.

Your Sun answers to [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/). On the Moon's side I read both rulers together: [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), Aquarius's ruler in traditional astrology, and [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), which modern astrologers assigned to the sign after William Herschel found the planet in 1781. Uranus marks where this Moon needs to do things its own way; Saturn, where it keeps faith. Neither sign confers a dignity on its light, so these three planets carry the detail of the pair.

The Sun was 91° to 149° ahead of the Moon, seventeen to twenty-two days after a new moon: past full and shrinking, the Moon rose in the middle of the evening or later. The [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) is the phase of almost all of these births, the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) of the earliest; near 120° behind, the trine of signs becomes a waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). I read the disseminating phase as understanding that wants to be shared, and with two air signs that wish is strong: you may only know what you think once you've explained it to a room full of people. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sets the phases side by side.

Roughly one birth in 140 carries this pair. Between Capricorn and Pisces, the Moon gives Aquarius about two and a half days, and on either changeover day I'd want the time before telling you which of three pages is yours: this one, the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-capricorn/) or the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-pisces/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Rising sign and houses come from that time too, and they show where all this thinking and befriending takes place.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love grows best for you out of good talk and shared ideas. The Gemini Sun needs talk and change; the Aquarius Moon wants equality, honesty and room to stay an individual inside the bond. You're loyal, interesting and rarely possessive. The difficulty can be emotional heat: when a partner needs you to feel something with them, right now, you may reach for an explanation instead. Staying with the feeling a little longer before you analyse it is a real gift to the people you love.

## At work {#at-work}

Networks, technology, science, campaigning, teaching, writing about ideas: work that connects people around a purpose suits the pair. The Sun brings range and communication; the Moon brings principle and persistence. Often you're the one who knows everyone and can explain the project to each of them. Places where nobody may question anything frustrate both lights; you do your best where good questions are welcome.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in air, a feeling can be understood, discussed and filed before it has been felt. Friends may get a brilliant account of what's happening to you and very little of the thing itself, and others can read that as coolness. What helps is something that goes round the mind rather than through it: music, movement, a long walk outdoors, or one feeling said in three words to one person, without the analysis that usually follows.
