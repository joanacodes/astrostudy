When Chiron sextiles your Moon, the opening is domestic and small: a better routine, a kinder arrangement at home, a way of looking after yourself that works with an old sensitivity instead of around it. Each pass usually lasts long enough for a new habit to take.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates: it keeps you steady through needs met, habits repeated and care given and received. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) describes a sensitivity that stayed and the ways you found to function with it, including getting back something you'd lost. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, connects your habits of care with that know-how through an opening you have to use. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the openings are rarely dramatic. A routine you've always forced yourself through can be changed to fit the person you are. A comfort you gave up after an old hurt, cooking for friends, a walk at dusk, a craft you loved, can come back. A group of people who share the same sore spot turns out to be closer than you thought.

The sextile also makes your care for others easier to offer without costing your own. Nurturing isn't self-sacrifice, and this is a good season to find the kind of help you can give someone in a hard place while still keeping your evenings.

The houses say where the opening is, and they need your birth time. With the Moon in the 4th, one of these sextiles comes while Chiron crosses your 6th ([Chiron transiting the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-6th-house/)), the shape of your days and your work habits. Tradition linked the 6th to illness; I read it as routine in an adult life, and nothing here forecasts your health ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). Born with [Moon sextile Chiron](/astrology/moon-sextile-chiron/), you probably already know how to look after a tender place in practical ways; the transit is a reminder to use that know-how on yourself.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, 1½° before exact to 1° after, nearly two passes in three cross the exact sextile three times, over about ten and a half months. About one in three crosses once: some three weeks as a rule, barely two and a half when Chiron is moving at its quickest. Five crossings, spread over some twenty-one months, come in about one pass in twenty.

Your Moon's degree sets the dates, and it moves about half a degree for every hour of the day you were born; without the time I can't say which year this falls in, let alone which month ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Most people have three of these sextiles by 80, and a few have two or four, since the Moon has a sextile point on either side and Chiron passes each once a round. Where this sextile offers a practical way in, the trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-moon/)) makes the same care feel natural and the square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-moon/)) makes it urgent; all three come about three times in a life, while the conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-moon/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-moon/)) come once or twice.

Crossing Taurus from 2027 to 2033, Chiron sextiles Moons in Pisces and in Cancer, the Moon's own sign. Once it moves into Gemini, from 2033 to 2039, Moons in Aries and Leo take their turn.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change one small routine so that it suits the sensitivity you have, not the person you were once told to be.

Join, or start, something regular with people who share the sore spot: a class, a group, a weekly walk.

Offer what you know to someone who's struggling, in a form that leaves you your own rest.
