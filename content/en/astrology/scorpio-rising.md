Scorpio was rising in the east when you were born, and it gives you a quiet way in: you notice a great deal and show very little until you know where you are. With [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) on the Ascendant, trust is the door, and you hold the key.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) sits in a fixed water sign: feeling held deep and held long. I read Scorpio rising as a watchful threshold. You take in a place and its people, sense what isn't being said, and let yourself be known by stages. In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant is the cusp of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/). Scorpio is the natural sign of the 8th, the house of what is shared and what is kept out of sight, so with Scorpio rising the sign of private depths stands at the front door. People sense there's more to you than you're showing, and they're right.

Scorpio is the last of the four signs that take nearly three hours to rise at London: about 170 minutes there, 165 at Paris, 153 at New York, where it ties with Leo as the slowest of all, if only by two minutes. After it, the signs start to rise faster again. About 1 London birth in 8 has Scorpio rising. At London half an hour covers less than a fifth of the sign, so a rounded time rarely changes it; the risk sits at the edges. In its first few degrees a round hour may really be [Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/), in its last few [Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/). Scorpio would want to know which, and so do I ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In the southern hemisphere the long rise shortens: signs that rise fast in the northern hemisphere rise slowly in the southern, and a slow northern sign like Scorpio comes up faster there.

## First impressions {#first-impressions}

People often feel your attention before they hear your opinion. You listen closely, ask the question under the question, and give away little about yourself at first, and newcomers tend to describe that as intensity. Some find it magnetic, some unsettling; most find it sincere once they know you.

The misreading is privacy taken for secrecy, or reserve for coldness. With a Scorpio Ascendant you are deciding whom to trust, and you take the decision seriously, because once it's made it tends to hold.

In [my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the rising sign is among the placements everyone reads first, and it held about a third of the time. So I take what people sense in you as a starting point, and look underneath it, as Scorpio would.

## Its rulers {#its-ruler}

Scorpio has two rulers, so you have two [chart rulers](/astrology/chart-ruler/). [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the traditional one, shows where you act, protect and stand your ground. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the modern one, shows where change runs deepest, and where you come out of it with more of your own strength. Traditional astrologers read Mars alone; many modern ones lead with Pluto. I read both, and say which one a part of the reading rests on ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)).

Their houses are where the two meet your life. If they fall in different houses, your chart rulers speak in two places: Mars where you take action, Pluto where transformation comes. If they share a house, that part of life carries the weight of both. Both are in detriment in Taurus, the sign on your Descendant: a chart ruler there has to work at Taurus' pace, slowly and patiently, and often learns it from the people closest to you.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Close up, trust comes in stages with Scorpio rising, and each stage is real. You let someone in a room at a time, and you notice whether what you shared was kept. Once you've chosen a person, the loyalty is whole, and you want the same honesty back: half-truths sting more than hard ones.

Your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is in Taurus, opposite: you're drawn to steady, loyal people who keep things simple and stay ([Taurus on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/)).

Their calm doesn't ask you to be less intense. It gives your intensity somewhere safe to land.
