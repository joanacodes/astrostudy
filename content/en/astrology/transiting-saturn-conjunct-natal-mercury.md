Saturn reaches your Mercury about once every 29½ years, and because Mercury never strays far from the Sun, it gets there within a couple of years of crossing your Sun as well. For a while, the way you think, speak and learn is asked to slow down and stand up to scrutiny.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) brings structure, standards and the long view; [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) processes information, makes meaning and carries it in words. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) merges them, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) ideas are asked to prove themselves: drafts get rewritten, arguments checked, quick answers replaced by considered ones. Thinking can feel heavier and slower, and the result tends to be more exact.

It's a good season for work that needs method: a long piece of writing, a course with a syllabus, a skill that takes practice, papers that have to be right. It's also a season when criticism can feel sharper than usual, your own included. Saturn's standards aren't a verdict on your mind; a slower Mercury isn't a weaker one, and no transit touches intelligence.

Your Mercury's house shows what the thinking is about. With Mercury in the 3rd, it falls inside [Saturn transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-3rd-house/), among the everyday exchanges, letters and learning that house covers. If you were born with Mercury retrograde, the pace may feel familiar: a mind that works inward before it speaks ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)). With [Mercury conjunct Saturn](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-saturn/) in your chart, this crossing is part of your Saturn return.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, your thinking usually has Saturn alongside it for about nine months, or about nine and a half when Saturn loops back over Mercury three times, as it does a little over half the time ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)). At full speed, three weeks.

Mercury is never more than about 28° from the Sun, so this crossing falls within about two and a half years of [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-sun/), before it or after it depending on which side of the Sun your Mercury sat. For many people the two make one long Saturn season, first about who you are and then about how you put it into words, or the other way round. Mercury's degree comes mostly from your birth date; its house needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, it has come two or three times for people born between 1930 and 2009, usually in the same Saturn season as the crossing of their Sun. The round it opens runs through [transiting Saturn sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-mercury/) and [transiting Saturn square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-mercury/) in its first seven years or so, reaches [transiting Saturn opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-mercury/) around fifteen, and passes [transiting Saturn trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-mercury/) on either side of that.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take on one piece of serious work with words: the report, the long letter, the course you keep postponing. Saturn on Mercury rewards a single sustained effort more than many quick ones.

Read before you sign, and reread before you send. Not because something will go wrong, but because this season makes careful words count.

Give your thinking a timetable: an hour at the same time each day, a deadline you set yourself. Structure is what this transit asks of Mercury, and it's easier to give than to resist.
