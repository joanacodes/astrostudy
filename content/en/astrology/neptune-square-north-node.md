Neptune square the North Node stands across the nodal axis, a quarter of the zodiac from both nodes. Your imagination and your ideals don't sit at either end of the road you're travelling; they cross it, and the crossing is where the work is.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is a chart's capacity to dream, to feel with others and to let fixed lines go soft. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the direction of growth, a capacity built by repetition and feedback; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, is what you already do well. Linked by a [square](/astrology/square/), they rub, and here Neptune squares both ends at once: it belongs neither to the old habits nor to the new direction, and keeps cutting across the line between them.

In plain words, imagination can pull you sideways. A vision of something else, a cause, someone else's need, a longing you can't quite name, may keep interrupting the slow practice your North Node asks for, or blur which way you meant to go. That isn't failure, and it isn't a wrong turn. The square asks you to bring the dream into the practice instead of out of it, letting imagination feed the new skill rather than replace it. When that happens, the friction becomes fuel.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Counted within 6° of the true node, the square is in about 1 chart in 15 among births from 1930 to 2009, about as many as for the sextile or the trine, since a square too can form on either side each time the node goes past. The windows last some months, and the mean node, a degree or two away, changes the answer only for borderline charts.

A square usually joins signs of the same modality, so Neptune and the nodes share a way of moving and disagree about the rest: in mutable signs, the detours tend to be many and brief; in fixed signs, one dream can sit across the path for years.

The houses make the cross visible. Neptune usually falls three houses from each node: with the nodes across the 1st and 7th and Neptune in the 10th, say, ideals about work might keep tugging at the balance between your own path and your partnerships. Those houses come from the hour of birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Neptune and the node at their other angles:

- **[Neptune conjunct North Node](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-north-node/):** Neptune on the road itself, at the growing end.
- **[Neptune sextile North Node](/astrology/neptune-sextile-north-node/):** imagination beside the path, offering help instead of a detour.
- **[Neptune trine North Node](/astrology/neptune-trine-north-node/):** imagination and growth flowing the same way.
- **[Neptune opposite North Node](/astrology/neptune-opposite-north-node/):** Neptune at the familiar end, on the South Node.

Between two charts, [Neptune square North Node in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-north-node-synastry/) can make one person's dreams a lovely distraction from the other's direction. Seeing it clearly is most of the work.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-neptune/) comes twice per nodal round, about a month at a time, and is always a square from the South Node as well. Born with the square, you meet it at the [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) and again at the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/): the nodes swap ends at the reversal, and Neptune stays across them. Transiting Neptune squaring your natal node is a slower matter, a year or more, in several passes.

Weather, not a forecast: a month when a dream crosses your path again. It asks to be folded in, neither obeyed nor banished.
