Aries on the 2nd house cusp puts the first sign of the zodiac at the door of your resources. The 2nd is where you keep what you have and weigh what you're worth, and here it opens in a sign that would rather go and get something than wait for it to arrive.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on a cusp sets the manner of the house that follows ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Aries](/astrology/aries/) brings to the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) the manner of someone who trusts their own push. I read it as money that follows effort you started yourself: the first job you found without help, the sideline nobody asked you to begin, the fee you name without apologising for it. Your sense of worth tends to grow the same way, each time you act for yourself. The 2nd's natural sign is Taurus, the sign that accumulates; in its place, Aries makes earning easier to start than savings are to leave alone, and patience with money is something you practise rather than something you're born with.

Your 2nd house then belongs to [Mars](/astrology/mars/), Aries' ruler. The house Mars sits in is where the 2nd's business gets done, earning, spending, standing up for what's yours, and its sign shows how. This cusp brings a second link with it, at London as at New York: Scorpio always opens the 9th, and Mars, Scorpio's traditional ruler, shares that house with Pluto. What you earn and what you'd cross the world or go back to study for answer to the same planet, and I read both of Scorpio's rulers. Mars in each house, as ruler of the 2nd: the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Aries is an uncommon sign on this cusp. Counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use, over a whole day of births, about 1 in 20 has Aries on the 2nd at London's latitude and 1 in 17 at New York's. Aries rises quickly north of the equator, so it rarely lands on the cusps either side of the Ascendant, and the further north, the faster it rises. What rises with it is nearly settled in advance: at London almost always Aquarius, at New York Aquarius or Pisces. With Aquarius rising, Pisces sits wholly inside your 1st house, an [intercepted sign](/astrology/intercepted-signs/), and in every case the Midheaven is in Sagittarius. Aries itself can open no house at all: it's intercepted in about 1 London birth in 6, and 1 New York birth in 8.

The cusps turn with the sky, so the birth time decides all this. An hour or two later and you might have had [Aries rising](/astrology/aries-rising/); an hour or two earlier, [Aries on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-3rd-house-cusp/). Without the minute I can't tell which, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

None of this says how much you'll have. The 2nd describes how you earn, spend and value, and no chart can promise an income or choose an investment ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). With Aries on its cusp, what it describes is a way of earning that begins with you.
