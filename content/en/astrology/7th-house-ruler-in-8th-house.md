A partnership, with this ruler, isn't complete until something real is held in common: trust, money, a secret, a change you both came through. Your 7th house's ruler has crossed into the 8th, where a bond stops being an arrangement and becomes a shared life.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Where the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) brings two people face to face, the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) holds what their meeting comes to contain: intimacy, trust, joint money and debts, the closeness that leaves people changed. Once the 7th's ruler settles there, partnership is lived at that depth ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Relationships with weight are the ones that hold you. Casual arrangements rarely hold your interest; you want to know someone, and be known, at a level that takes time and nerve. Partners may bring change with them, or arrive when you're already changing, and the bonds that matter tend to leave both people different. In business, the placement often shows as shared capital, joint ventures, contracts that bind money as well as people. The questions it raises are about power and trust: who holds what, who decides, and whether closeness ever slides into control. Intimacy isn't ownership, and the healthiest version of this placement keeps both people's keys on their own rings.

Counted from the 7th, the 8th is the second house, the one tradition read as a partner's resources ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)). So the ruler of your partners sits in what, from their side, is their money and what they hold. I use that only to describe how sharing works in your life, what you build and agree to hold with someone; a partner's own finances belong to their chart.

The planet decides the flavour. Venus, when Taurus sets, wants shared comfort and steady loyalty, and money held together can feel like an act of love; the Moon, when Cancer sets, needs emotional safety before any money or secret changes hands. When Scorpio sets, Mars and Pluto share your 7th ([Scorpio on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Pluto in the 8th stands in the house of its own sign in the natural zodiac, and with Mars it describes bonds that are all or nothing.

Old books gave the 8th death and inheritance, and I don't follow them there. Nothing in this placement says what a partner might leave you, or how long anyone will live ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); it describes how you share a life with someone.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 8th can be narrow or wide, depending on your latitude and the time of day you were born. A little later, and your ruler could fall back into the 7th, where partners meet you face to face, without detours ([Ruler of the 7th in the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)); earlier, it could sit higher, in the 9th, where a partner widens what you believe ([Ruler of the 7th in the 9th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)). I need the recorded time to tell ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What this describes is a taste for depth in company, and the nerve to hold things in common with another person.
