Some people go out to have fun. With the ruler of the 5th in the 4th, the fun comes home: the kitchen, the garden, the spare room with the sewing machine are where your pleasures settle.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read this placement as joy with a front door. The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) covers what you do for delight, from making things to falling in love, and its ruler is the planet that speaks for all of it ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Standing in the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), that planet lives among home, family and the past, so the 5th's business goes indoors. You might cook for the pleasure of feeding people, paint at the kitchen table, play music with whoever's in, or spend Sundays repotting plants. Horary astrology even lists gardens and orchards under the 4th. A house tends to feel like yours once something is being made in it.

The 4th is roots as well, and what you create often grows from them: a craft learned from a grandmother, songs in the language you grew up with, recipes you refuse to change. Romance wants somewhere to go home to. You may feel easiest with someone once you've cooked for them or shown them where you live, and a night in can matter more than a night out. The catch is waiting for the house to be ready, the right flat, the right kitchen, before joy is allowed in.

The two houses are neighbours, and in the old count by sign neighbouring signs make no aspect, so the 4th can't see the 5th it serves ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)). I read that as pleasure that doesn't perform: it happens out of view and needs no witnesses. The 4th is angular all the same, opening at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the bottom of the chart, so this quietness isn't weakness. What you do for joy at home can be the strongest thread in your life.

The planet decides the mood. The Moon, ruling a Cancer 5th, stands in the house the [natural zodiac](/astrology/natural-zodiac/) gives to Cancer, its own sign, and makes comfort itself a pleasure: food, warmth, the people you love within reach. The Sun, ruling a Leo 5th, places your birth late in the evening, with the Sun nearing the bottom of its daily circle, and gives home life a warm, central place.

[Scorpio on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/) gives the house two rulers, Pluto and Mars ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and I read them together. Pluto in the 4th makes home the place where your most absorbing creative work happens, intense and private. Mars, wherever it stands, adds the appetite for sport and competition that no house can hold.

The 4th opens at the IC, which turns with the clock just as the Ascendant does, so this ruler's house can depend on minutes. A slightly later birth would carry a ruler near the IC back into the 3rd, and your fun would live in talk and short trips ([Ruler of the 5th in the 3rd house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)). A slightly earlier one would leave a ruler near the far edge of the 4th inside the 5th itself, the house it rules ([Ruler of the 5th in the 5th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)). I wait for the recorded time before reading it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Home, family and the 5th's traditional children make a tempting sum, and it's easy to read this placement as a houseful of them. I don't. No chart can say whether you'll have a child, or how many ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/) are outside what I read. This is about the pleasure you take in home life, whether you share it with a partner, friends, family or no one at all.
