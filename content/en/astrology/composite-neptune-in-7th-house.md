Some couples talk about the day they met as if it had been arranged in advance. A composite Neptune in the 7th house gives the relationship that feeling about itself: being a pair seems to mean something larger than the two of you.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Every planet of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is a midpoint between two birth charts, and the chart is read as the relationship's own, a third thing beside the two people. Its [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the bond's ideal, the place where it imagines and forgives. [The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), which opens at the composite [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), is partnership: the commitment itself, the agreements the pair lives by, and the way it faces other people as a pair, in business, in friendships between couples, across a negotiating table. With Neptune here, partnership is the dream. The relationship may feel fated, give the benefit of the doubt for years, forgive easily, and meet others with a gentle, generous front.

The difficulty is loving the picture as well as the person. A bond that idealises partnership can expect the other to live up to an ideal, and treat ordinary faults as a betrayal of the dream; it can also run on unwritten agreements that each of you remembers differently. Trust is this placement's strength, and spoken terms are what protect it: who does what, what each of you expects, what happens when one of you wants something else. Clear eyes aren't the opposite of romance here; they are what keeps it kind.

In one birth chart, [Neptune in the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-in-7th-house/) describes a person's lifelong longing for a kindred partner; in synastry, [their Neptune in your 7th house](/astrology/neptune-in-7th-house-synastry/) is one person meeting the other's idea of partnership. The composite 7th is different again: the partnership's ideal of itself, which neither of you could hold alone.

The house begins at the composite Descendant, always opposite the composite Ascendant, whichever method draws it; the methods disagree about the Ascendant itself, so they disagree here too. The birth times matter more. Each natal Ascendant moves about a degree every four minutes on average, so the composite Descendant, a midpoint, moves about a degree for every eight minutes of error in either time, and a Neptune close to the angle can cross it within a quarter of an hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

None of this says whether the two of you will marry, stay together or part; a composite describes the character of a partnership, never its outcome. And because this house is about the two of you as a pair, it should only be looked at when both of you have agreed to be looked at together ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

On either side, [composite Neptune in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-6th-house/) dreams in daily care, and [composite Neptune in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-neptune-in-8th-house/) in intimacy and trust. Composites are a technique I explain rather than practise; the astrologers who practise it set this house beside both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), where the aspects between the two of you tell the rest.
