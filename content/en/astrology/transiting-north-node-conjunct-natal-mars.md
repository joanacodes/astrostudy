Mars is whatever in you gets things started. When the North Node, travelling backwards, reaches it, the less familiar direction gets an engine for a month or so, and the South Node, opposite, sits on your oldest ways of pushing.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) initiates: it is agency, effort, courage and the boundaries you defend. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity you build only by practising it, and a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes the two one process. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), growth is something you do rather than something you think about: a first step, a training, an effort aimed somewhere new.

The direction of that effort is the point. Some people's drive has always gone into the same channel, winning the same kind of contest, defending the same ground; the node asks for some of it elsewhere. Others have rarely let themselves push at all, and the month asks them to try. Assertion isn't aggression, and courage isn't recklessness: what tends to help is effort that is steady and chosen. Opposite, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) holds the old way to win, still useful and still yours.

With [Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/), where tradition gave Mars its joy, the conjunction comes during the node's months in that house ([North Node transiting the 6th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-6th-house/)), and the new effort belongs to the daily round: a routine you finally build, a way of working you change. The old texts named the 6th Bad Fortune and assigned it illness; in an adult's chart I read it as work and habit, and no transit says anything about your health ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). Your houses need the birth time to exist at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted my way, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the node's month on your Mars is usually just that, a month, with one exact crossing nearly every time. Its speed is the Sun's doing. Between eclipse seasons the true node hurries and can be gone in three and a half weeks; during a season it hardly moves, so a tenth of these conjunctions last three and a half months or more, and three exact hits turn up about once in twenty-five passes. Those long passes are also the ones in which an eclipse can land on or opposite your Mars ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Each round brings one: four times by 80 for most people and five for some, with nobody left out. In between, the node makes each of the other four aspects to Mars: [Transiting North Node sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-mars/) first, then [Transiting North Node square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-mars/) and [Transiting North Node trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-mars/), and [Transiting North Node opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-mars/) at the halfway mark, before the sequence runs back the other way. With [Mars conjunct North Node](/astrology/mars-conjunct-north-node/) in your chart, it's your nodal returns that bring it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one new direction for your effort and give it a fixed slot: twice a week, the same hour, for the whole month.

Train rather than strain. A new kind of effort grows with repetition, not with one heroic push.

Keep the old way to win for where it still serves, and notice where it no longer does.
