Trust has a history. Somewhere in yours there may be a moment when you let someone hold what mattered and it slipped, and that is the moment Chiron finds in the 8th house.

## What changes {#what-changes}

In a chart, [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) shows a hurt that keeps its shape over the years, and the skill of living around it. The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) begins where a bond gets serious: intimacy, sexual and otherwise, trust and what it costs, money held in common, debts in both senses, what people owe each other. As Chiron crosses it, an old wariness about depending on anyone can come up: a confidence betrayed, a shared account that went wrong, a closeness that ended in being used. The stay tends to reveal what that wariness protected you from, and whether it still has to stand guard. As with [Chiron in the 8th house](/astrology/chiron-in-8th-house/) at birth, people who know this hurt are often the ones others want beside them in their hardest moments, because they don't flinch.

Chiron also passes any planet you were born with in the 8th; a natal Mars there may find it has been guarding a door that could now open a little.

The old texts filed death and inheritance under the 8th. I don't read death in any chart ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and no [transit](/astrology/transits/) forecasts money gained or lost. Counted from the 7th, the 8th is your partner's 2nd house ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)), which is why shared money lives here; what Chiron touches is how you handle sharing, not what you will receive.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, Chiron's stay in the 8th centres on just under 5 years, among the longest of the twelve houses, with most stays between about 2 and 9. At New York's latitude the middle drops to about 4 years while the spread stays the same. Only the 1st shows as large a gap between the two cities: houses vary less in size at New York's latitude, and the 8th's typical stay comes out almost a year shorter there.

In Placidus your 8th mirrors your 2nd across the chart, and the birth time that draws both is the one thing this stay can't be dated without; I don't read a chart that lacks it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Before it comes Chiron's time in the house of partners ([Chiron transiting the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-7th-house/)); after it, the house of beliefs and far horizons ([Chiron transiting the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-9th-house/)).

Between June 2026 and April 2027 Chiron moves back and forth across the line between Aries and Taurus, so an 8th cusp in the first degrees of Taurus may be crossed, crossed back and crossed again; in my count, a step back over a cusp within a year belongs to the same stay. After that, Taurus holds Chiron until May 2034, Gemini from July 2033 to April 2039, and Cancer from July 2038 until at least December 2039, where my count ends. An 8th house that opens in Aries may already be hosting Chiron, which entered that sign in April 2018.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice where you still keep score of what you're owed, or what you owe, and ask whether that account can be closed.

Let trust grow in small steps you can check; nothing has to be handed over all at once.

If shared money is part of the old hurt, make the next arrangement explicit, and put it on paper.

When someone near you is going through something hard, sit with them. The steadiness you learned the hard way is real.
