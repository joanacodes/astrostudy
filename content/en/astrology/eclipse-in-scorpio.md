Scorpio’s eclipses are in a quiet stretch: the last, on 5 May 2023, and the next, on 7 May 2031, are both penumbral, the faint kind. Scorpio goes deep, and when one of its eclipses does land on your chart, it asks what lies underneath.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) is fixed water, with Mars as its ruler in the tradition and Pluto in modern tropical astrology; I read both. It is the sign of depth, trust and transformation, and its eclipses fall on the Scorpio–Taurus axis, what is shared and what is one’s own, alternating with Taurus’s as the Moon’s nodes move. A [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) in Scorpio joins the Sun and Moon in the sign, as on 14 November 2031 at 22°17′; a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) puts the full Moon in Scorpio and the Sun in Taurus, as on 25 April 2032 at 6°00′.

Whatever the sign’s reputation, the AGS rule I read by is the same for every eclipse: within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of its axis, read through that point and its house, with a penumbral lunar eclipse weighing less. Nodes I check twice, once for the mean node and once for the true. It is symbolic emphasis, never an omen and never a forecast of loss.

Fifteen from 2000 to 2040:

- **2003–2005:** 16 May 2003, total lunar, 24°55′ · 4 May 2004, total lunar, 14°39′ · 24 April 2005, penumbral lunar, 4°13′
- **2012–2014:** 13 November 2012, total solar, 21°56′ · 25 April 2013, partial lunar, 5°52′ · 3 November 2013, hybrid solar, 11°15′ · 23 October 2014, partial solar, 0°24′
- **2022–2023:** 16 May 2022, total lunar, 25°16′ · 25 October 2022, partial solar, 2°00′ · 5 May 2023, penumbral lunar, 14°52′
- **2031–2032:** 7 May 2031, penumbral lunar, 16°31′ · 14 November 2031, hybrid solar, 22°17′ · 25 April 2032, total lunar, 6°00′ · 3 November 2032, partial solar, 11°21′
- **2040:** 4 November 2040, partial solar, 12°58′

Nineteen years bring some of them back to the day: 16 May 2003 at 24°55′ and 16 May 2022 at 25°16′, 3 November 2013 at 11°15′ and 3 November 2032 at 11°21′. For Libra and Sagittarius, the signs next door, see their pages ([Eclipse in Libra](/astrology/eclipse-in-libra/), [Eclipse in Sagittarius](/astrology/eclipse-in-sagittarius/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Scorpio’s house in your chart comes from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), like every house; that dependence is why I never read a chart without the time. Here, in Placidus, my house system, is what a Scorpio eclipse underlines when it meets a natal point in each house, the Taurus degrees opposite reaching the house across.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/scorpio-rising/):** Scorpio rising: your intense, private presence; how much of yourself you let others see.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you hold onto; the meaning, more than the worth, of what you keep.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** words that go deep; a conversation that gets to the truth.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/):** family depths; old loyalties and what lies beneath them.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/):** passion and absorbing creative work; desire, looked at honestly.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a working life you give everything to; a problem followed down to its cause.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio on the Descendant, with Taurus rising: an intense partnership; trust, examined closely.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio in its natural [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/): intimacy, trust and what’s held jointly. Tradition linked this house with death; an eclipse here, as I read it, speaks of closeness and change in an adult’s life, never of anyone dying ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
9. **[9th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/):** convictions tested to the bottom; a belief you can no longer take on trust.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/):** work with serious stakes; ambitions you hold fiercely.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a few deep friendships; loyalty within a group.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-12th-house-cusp/):** what you work through alone; feelings that surface in their own time.
