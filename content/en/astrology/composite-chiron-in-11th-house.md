Two sets of friends who never quite mix, a club where only one of you feels at home, a future each of you pictures differently: the 11th house is where a relationship meets its wider circle and its hopes. Composite Chiron there puts the bond's tender spot in that circle, and describes the people and plans the pair gathers for itself.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds friends, groups, networks and hopes for the future. Older astrologers called it the Good Spirit and counted it among the fortunate houses, with [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) in its joy there; for this Chiron, help tends to arrive the same way, through friends who vouch for the pair. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) it maps the pair's social world and hopes, and [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) in it makes that world uneasy ground. Old friends may take sides or drift away, the two friendship groups may never merge, a community one of you belongs to may not quite welcome the other, or the pair may hesitate to make plans that assume a shared future.

Count the invitations and it shows: each of you is asked everywhere on your own, and the two of you together, somehow, less often. It answers by gathering people: friends who know you both and need nothing explained, separate friendships kept up without guilt, a cause or a club the two of you join on equal terms, plans for the future made in pencil and revised together. Pairs like this tend to notice who is standing at the edge of a party, and to bring them in.

The house needs both birth times, and the method matters: the midpoints of your two charts' cusps and houses built from the composite Midheaven where you live can place the 11th's cusps differently. If one of you doesn't know a birth time, that is the conversation to have before anyone draws a composite, and a full birth record, a hospital record or a parent's memory may hold it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#finding-your-time)).

If one of you has a natal [Chiron in the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-in-11th-house/), the tenderness about groups is that person's own; if the other's Chiron falls in your 11th ([their Chiron in your 11th house](/astrology/chiron-in-11th-house-synastry/)), it is know-how brought into your circles. In the composite, the circle and its tenderness are shared.

Closing ranks is the temptation. A bond sore about belonging can drop old friends, lean on a single group for everything, or keep the future so vague that no plan can disappoint. It helps to keep a few friendships that are each person's own, and to say out loud one hope you share.

With the 10th before it tender in public ([composite Chiron in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-10th-house/)) and the 12th after it tender in private ([composite Chiron in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-12th-house/)), the 11th's tenderness is social, somewhere in between. I describe composites as a technique rather than use them: astrologers who draw them read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and no composite can tell you who will stay a friend.
