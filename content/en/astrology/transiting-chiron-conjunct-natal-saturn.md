In the myth, Chiron's father is Cronus, the Titan the Romans identified with Saturn. When Chiron in the sky reaches your natal Saturn, the old sore spot meets the part of your chart that keeps the rules, and you get a long look at which of those rules were built to protect the hurt.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds structure: duties, boundaries, discipline, the long effort that makes anything last. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) describes a difficulty that persists and the adapting you've done around it. Saturn structures constraints, Chiron adapts to them, and a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the limits you live by and the way you cope look like a single thing.

What tends to surface is a set of rules. Never ask for help. Always finish before resting. Be useful first, wanted later. Plan for everything, so nothing can hurt the way it once did. Many of them did their job, and some still do. The conjunction asks you to sort them: the ones that are care, kept, and the ones that are fear, loosened. Saturn's hardest lesson is that control doesn't guarantee safety, and Chiron's is that a limitation can be lived with rather than defeated; meeting on one degree, they make a structure that has room for the difficulty.

Saturn's sign is shared by everyone born within about two and a half years, so this conjunction reaches your whole school year at about the same age. The shared part is the date. The personal part is the house your Saturn sits in, the planets and angles it touches, and what else your chart is doing at the time, which is what I read.

With [Saturn in the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-in-10th-house/), the conjunction falls during Chiron's stay there ([Chiron transiting the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-10th-house/)), and the rules in question are those of your working and public life. Your 10th starts at your Midheaven, which goes round the whole zodiac in a day, so I won't read the conjunction without your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Saturn conjunct Chiron](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-chiron/), you have the limit and the work-around on one degree already; read that page alongside this one.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Sorting a lifetime of rules is slow work, and the conjunction usually allows for it: about ten months within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, with Chiron passing over your Saturn three times in nearly two cases out of three. In roughly a third of cases it crosses once, in some three weeks; in about one in twenty it makes five passes over some twenty-one months.

In the site's count the first conjunction came at about 28½ in the middle, anywhere from infancy to 49: at about 14 for someone born at the start of 1975 or 1985, 17 for 1995, 24 for 1935, 36 for 2005 and between 43 and 45 for 1945, 1955 and 1965. Most people have had two by 80, and nobody reaches 80 without one. After it, in order, come [transiting Chiron sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-saturn/), [transiting Chiron square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-saturn/), [transiting Chiron trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-saturn/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-saturn/).

The next ones fall while Chiron crosses Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, for most births from April 1969 to early 1972 and from June 1998 to early 2001, with Saturn in Taurus; then, in Gemini from July 2033 to April 2039, for most births from June 1971 to early 1974 and from August 2000 to mid-2003.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down the rules you live by, and mark which ones you'd teach to someone you love.

Keep the structures that hold you up, and loosen one that only holds you in.

Hand over one responsibility you've carried because nobody else could be trusted with it.

If the weight feels heavier than a season, talk to someone trained to help ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).
