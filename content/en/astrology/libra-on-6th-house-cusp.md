Libra on the 6th house cusp puts the sign of the scales in charge of your working day. Tasks, habits and the upkeep of body and tools tend to go best here when the load is shared and the hours keep some proportion.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the shape of an ordinary day, and with [Libra](/astrology/libra/) on its cusp you tend to judge a day by its balance ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). Effort and rest, solitary work and company, the urgent and the important: a routine holds when those are in proportion and wears thin when one side takes over. In a team, you notice quickly when the load is uneven, and you'd rather redistribute it than carry it in silence. Your craft is often adjustment, the fine-tuning that makes a system or a schedule work for everyone who has to use it. Looking after your body follows the same rule: habits that keep you even suit you better than strict ones.

Tradition put the joy of Mars in this house, the planet of effort in the house of toil, and Libra is the sign of Mars's [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). This cusp sets the house of hard work in a sign that would rather agree than push, and you tend to get further here by arrangement than by force. Mars keeps a say all the same, through Aries on your 12th, opposite, and through the sign that usually lies behind Libra in this house.

At London the 6th is often the widest house in these charts, about 51° to 60°, and in all but a handful of them it holds the whole of Scorpio behind Libra ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The working day opens by agreement and then goes deep, and Pluto and Mars join [Venus](/astrology/venus/) among its rulers; I read both of Scorpio's. At New York the house runs to about 43° to 48°, and holds Scorpio in more than half the charts. When Libra itself opens no house, in about 1 London birth in 6, the 6th is where it most often lies.

Venus's own house shows where your working day leads: the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). At London, Taurus, Venus's other sign, has no cusp in nearly every chart with this one, so the 6th is the only house Venus rules by its cusp. At New York, Taurus rises in about 4 charts in 9, and Venus then rules both your chart and your daily work.

About 1 birth in 20 has Libra on this cusp at London, 1 in 17 at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. A slow riser in the north, Libra spends little time on the cusps near the Descendant, and the 6th is the last one before it: Libra holds it for little more than an hour of each day at London. Almost every London chart with this cusp has Gemini rising, so Mercury, ruler of Virgo, the 6th's natural sign, becomes your chart ruler.

The old books called the 6th Bad Fortune and looked there for illness. I don't: no chart can diagnose anything, and questions about your health are for a doctor ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). What this cusp describes is how you work and how you keep yourself going.

An hour or two later, Libra could have moved down to your 5th cusp ([Libra on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-5th-house-cusp/)); earlier, it could have been setting, on your Descendant ([Libra on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/)). The minute also decides how wide your 6th is, and I read neither without the recorded time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
