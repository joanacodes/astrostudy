Uranus and Neptune last met in the sky in 1993, at about 19° Capricorn. When transiting Uranus reaches your natal Neptune, the urge to invent and the capacity to imagine share one degree of your chart for about twenty months, and when that happens depends almost entirely on the year you were born.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination, compassion and the sense of something larger than you. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) innovates and frees what has set too hard. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) co-activates two functions into one process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) a dream finds a new form, or an old ideal is suddenly seen from a fresh angle.

It shows where you imagine and believe: a different kind of art or music, a new way to practise a faith or to care for others, a cause that finally has the tools it lacked. Ideals you'd stopped examining are refreshed, and a few are set down because a newer one fits better.

Both functions have a blind spot that this transit turns up. Neptune can mistake a strong sense of rightness for truth, and Uranus novelty for innovation, so a vision that arrives fast and feels like revelation deserves a slow second look. Neptune isn't confusion or deception, and the transit brings neither; it asks you to test what inspires you.

With Neptune in your 3rd, Uranus crosses it there ([Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/)): new ways of writing, speaking or picturing what you think. Your birth time sets that house, and without it I wouldn't read this or anything else in your chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Uranus stays on your Neptune for about twenty months within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, or about eleven for the shortest passes, and in more than nine passes out of ten crosses the exact degree three times, so a vision that arrives at the first hit can be tried out before the last.

Uranus and Neptune are both slow, so the timing is shared by everyone born within a year or so of you, and it moves steadily with the birth year. Before 1993 Uranus trailed Neptune and was catching up: in the site's count the transit came at about 30 for someone born at the start of 1935, about 19 for 1955, about 8 for 1975 and about 3 for 1985, roughly five years earlier for each decade of birth. For most people born after the mid-1960s it fell in childhood, and I only ever read it looking back ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).

Born between 1990 and 1996 with [Uranus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune/), you had it at birth. Born just after the 1993 meeting, you started life with Uranus already past your Neptune, and it needs almost its whole 84-year round to come back: for most births of the mid and late 1990s the transit can't happen before 80, and in all about 1 person in 17 born between 1930 and 2009 never meets it. Someone born at the start of 2005 waits until about 77 ([generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/)).

From the conjunction, [transiting Uranus sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-neptune/) lies about fourteen years away on either side, [transiting Uranus square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-neptune/) about twenty-one, [transiting Uranus trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-neptune/) about twenty-eight and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-neptune/) about forty-two.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the new vision a form you can test: a draft, a practice, a small project. Imagination moves fast under Uranus, and a form slows it enough to show what's real.

Let an old ideal go with thanks. Some of what this transit replaces served you well, and it ends better honoured than mocked.

Keep one sceptical friend close. Neptune and Uranus together can make a hunch feel certain, and a fond critic saves time.
