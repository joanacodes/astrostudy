Two houses of love meet in this placement. The romance of the 5th goes looking for the partner of the 7th, and the fun you enjoy most tends to be for two.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is falling for someone; the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is choosing them, the person across the table you meet as an equal. When the planet ruling your 5th cusp sits in the 7th ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), the first keeps leading to the second. Flirting turns serious quickly, and you rarely stay casual for long. Your pleasures want company too, one person at a time: the dance partner, the co-writer, the friend you go to every gig with. Joy you share feels doubled, and joy alone can feel unfinished.

Tradition gave the 7th open enemies as well as spouses, the rival you can see coming. With your pleasures there, a worthy opponent can be part of the fun: tennis with someone who hits back, chess with a friend who never lets you win, an argument you'd both happily have again. The risk is handing your joy over. If the fun only happens when a partner is around, the 5th's own play withers, so keep one pleasure that's entirely yours.

Romance and partnership are on good terms by sign as well, the 7th a sextile on from the 5th. The 7th is also angular, opening at the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/): at your birth, a ruler there hung low in the western sky, close to setting. Pleasure, here, shows in how you choose people and is seen by them.

The planet gives the tone of the courtship. Venus, ruling a Taurus or Libra 5th, brings the same charm to courting and to committing, and wants fairness in both. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 5th from here, wants a partner to talk to above everything; the Moon, ruling a Cancer 5th, wants one who feels like home.

Neptune in the 7th can cast a film-like glow over partners, delightful as long as you also see the person in daylight. It gets there as co-ruler of a Pisces 5th, with Jupiter beside it ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)); I read them side by side, and Jupiter adds a generous, expansive kind of fun from its own position.

Being angular, the 7th has a sharp border at the Descendant, and a ruler near it can land on either side depending on the minute you were born. A little later, and it would have set into the 6th, where pleasure becomes daily practice ([Ruler of the 5th in the 6th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)); a ruler near the 7th's far end would, with a slightly earlier time, stand higher in the 8th, where romance wants depth and trust ([Ruler of the 5th in the 8th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). Without the time I can't say which ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

None of this says you'll marry someone you date, or how long a love will last. Nor does partnership plus the 5th's traditional children add up to a family plan: whether two people will have a child is theirs to find out, not a chart's ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/) aren't part of my work. How your 5th's ruler meets one particular person is a question for a [synastry](/synastry/), and that needs their agreement as well as yours ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
