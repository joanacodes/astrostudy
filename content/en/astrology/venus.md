Venus is the light people call the morning or evening star: it shines low in the sky after sunset or before dawn because, like Mercury, it never strays far from the Sun. Astrologers read it as what you love and how you love it — people, beauty, comfort, pleasure — and what matters enough for you to keep.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

I read Venus as taste and attachment rather than luck in love. It answers *what draws you, and how do you show it?* — what you find beautiful, how you like to be loved, what you'd rather not live without. Tradition called it a [benefic](/astrology/benefics-and-malefics/), a planet that brings good things; I read it as the place where life feels sweet to you, and what you do to keep it that way.

- **Its sign** shows your taste and your way of loving: bold, slow, playful, devoted.
- **Its house** shows where pleasure, beauty and connection come most easily.
- **Its aspects** show what supports your affection and what complicates it.

Venus works with [Mars](/astrology/mars/): one describes what attracts you, the other how you go after it, and astrologers read the two together for desire. With the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), it shows the difference between what you want and what you need; with [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), how you put affection into words. It rules [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) and [Libra](/astrology/libra/) — pleasure of the senses, grace between people — and is said to be exalted in [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/). It is least at ease in Aries and Scorpio, opposite its own signs, and in Virgo, its fall: love there is more direct, more intense or more careful, never smaller.

Its aspects are where it becomes yours. Venus is never more than about 47° from the Sun, so the only major aspect the two can make is the conjunction — [Sun conjunct Venus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-venus/): warmth at the centre of who you are. [Venus conjunct Mars](/astrology/venus-conjunct-mars/) puts attraction and pursuit in the same place. A [Venus square Saturn](/astrology/venus-square-saturn/) often describes love learned carefully, sometimes late, and then kept. And a [Moon trine Venus](/astrology/moon-trine-venus/) tends to make affection feel natural, given and received without fuss.

## Venus through the signs {#through-the-signs}

Venus spends about four to five weeks in each sign, and longer when it turns retrograde. Because it stays within about 47° of the Sun, it is in your Sun sign or no more than two signs away from it. In plain words:

- **[Venus in Aries](/astrology/venus-in-aries/):** you love boldly and quickly, and like to make the first move.
- **[Venus in Taurus](/astrology/venus-in-taurus/):** loyal and sensual, slow to commit and slower to let go; its own sign.
- **[Venus in Gemini](/astrology/venus-in-gemini/):** a mind is what wins you; conversation and lightness keep love alive.
- **[Venus in Cancer](/astrology/venus-in-cancer/):** you love by caring and protecting, and need to feel at home with someone.
- **[Venus in Leo](/astrology/venus-in-leo/):** generous, warm and loyal; you love to admire and to be admired.
- **[Venus in Virgo](/astrology/venus-in-virgo/):** you show love through small, useful attentions.
- **[Venus in Libra](/astrology/venus-in-libra/):** you love harmony, beauty and life as a pair; its own sign.
- **[Venus in Scorpio](/astrology/venus-in-scorpio/):** all or nothing: deep loyalty, intense attachment.
- **[Venus in Sagittarius](/astrology/venus-in-sagittarius/):** you need freedom, laughter and an adventure to share.
- **[Venus in Capricorn](/astrology/venus-in-capricorn/):** you love seriously, and show it through commitment over time.
- **[Venus in Aquarius](/astrology/venus-in-aquarius/):** friendship first; you need room to stay yourself in love.
- **[Venus in Pisces](/astrology/venus-in-pisces/):** tender, romantic and forgiving; its exaltation.

## Venus in the houses {#in-the-houses}

Its house shows where life feels sweetest to you. Tradition gave Venus its joy in the 5th house, once called the house of Good Fortune: pleasure, play and love affairs.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/venus-in-1st-house/):** a charm that shows first; people find you easy to like.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/venus-in-2nd-house/):** you value comfort and beautiful things, and the security they bring.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/venus-in-3rd-house/):** a kind way with words; warm ties with siblings and neighbours.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house/):** you love a beautiful, peaceful home, and find joy with family.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house/):** Venus's joy: romance, play and the pleasure of making things.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/venus-in-6th-house/):** you need harmony at work, and enjoy a job well done.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house/):** partnership matters deeply; you look for grace and fairness in the other person.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/venus-in-8th-house/):** love that runs deep, with intimacy and trust at its heart.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/venus-in-9th-house/):** you're drawn to the foreign and the far, and to love that widens your world.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/venus-in-10th-house/):** grace in public life; work with beauty, people or diplomacy.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/venus-in-11th-house/):** friendships are a great source of joy; love can begin as friendship.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/venus-in-12th-house/):** a private, tender heart; affection given quietly, without needing to be seen.

## Venus in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Venus shows where two people enjoy each other: shared taste, affection, the plain pleasure of being together. Its best-known contact is with the other person's Mars — [Venus conjunct Mars in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-mars-synastry/) — read as attraction, the meeting of what one finds lovely and what the other goes after. When their Venus falls in your 7th house — [Their Venus in your 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house-synastry/) — they can look a lot like what you hoped to find in a partner. I read Venus contacts in both directions, because who delights in whom isn't always mutual, and only when both people know: [why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## Transits and Venus retrograde {#venus-retrograde}

Venus crosses a sign in about four to five weeks, so its transits are short and gentle: a few days of warmth, pleasure or longing when it touches one of your planets. About every 18 months it turns retrograde for about six weeks — [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) — which astrologers read as a time for looking again at what, and whom, you value. It's weather, not a forecast: I don't give anyone dates for falling in love, marrying or leaving.

Being born during a Venus retrograde changes the rhythm of affection rather than its depth; I wrote about that rhythm in [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/). When a slow planet meets your Venus — [Transiting Saturn conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-venus/), for instance — the season is longer, and love and values are asked to become more solid.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

Venus is one of the placements everyone reads first, and in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) it held about a third of the time: 30 to 40%, with the Sun sign, the rising sign and Mercury. That's close to what a flattering, vague horoscope scores. It isn't nothing, and I read Venus in every chart. But [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) held 75 to 90% of the time, so that's where I spend the most time — and when part of a reading rests on Venus, I tell you, so you can weigh it for yourself. It's a notebook, not a study, and it shapes [how I read](/how-i-read/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Venus is the women's planet, Mars the men's.”** Everyone has both in their chart, whatever their gender. Venus describes how anyone loves and enjoys; Mars, how anyone acts and desires.
- **“Venus is only about romance.”** It's also friendship, taste, pleasure and what you value. Venus says as much about your friendships and your home as about your love life.
- **“Never start a relationship during Venus retrograde.”** A transit describes weather, not an outcome. I don't give dates for love: that choice is yours.
- **“Venus in Aries or Scorpio can't love well.”** Tradition calls these its hardest signs. They describe love that is more direct or more intense, not love that fails.
