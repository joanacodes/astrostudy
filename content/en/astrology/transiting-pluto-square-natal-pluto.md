This is the Pluto square seen as a transit: Pluto 90° on from where it stood when you were born. Everyone meets it once, at an age the birth year decides, and for two to four years the defences you built early are asked to change shape.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Natal [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is where you hold on hardest and where, in time, you can let go and begin again; it is also where you learned early how to protect what matters to you. Usually a [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, sets two functions in constructive friction; here it is between one function at two ages. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the ways of holding on from your first decades rub against what the rest of your life needs: a protective guardedness can start to keep you small, and a need for control can cost more than it saves.

Pluto's quieter blind spots surface at the square: confusing secrecy with depth, and exposure with humiliation. Holding power doesn't mean holding others down: the square often ends with authority held more lightly.

Its timing ties it to other transits. For people born in the mid-1960s, with Uranus beside Pluto, it came together with Pluto's square to their natal Uranus ([Transiting Pluto square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-uranus/)). The square also lands, in the same years, on any planet your Pluto touched at birth.

The house of your natal Pluto says where the reworking lands. With Pluto in your 4th, the square comes from around your 7th ([Pluto transiting the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-7th-house/)) or your 1st: a partnership, or the way you meet the world, presses on what home has meant to you. Without your birth time, that house can't be known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If this stretch weighs on you for weeks, a doctor or a therapist is the right help ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/); [disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The square holds for about two years and eight months between 1½° before exact and 1° after, my orbs, and up to about three and three-quarter years; Pluto's retrograde loop makes it exact three times in about three passes out of five, five times in the rest.

Everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets it once. Pluto comes within orb about a year before the first exact square the [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/) gives: for births on 1 January, at 45.9 in 1935, 36.1 in 1965 and 46.3 in 1995, and 54.3 for 2005. Entry ages run from 34.5 to 59.2, a swing that comes from Pluto's stretched orbit. After a birth in the 1960s, the quarter circle ran through Libra and Scorpio while Pluto was speeding towards its closest approach to the Sun, in 1989; after a birth in the 2000s, it runs through Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces, where Pluto is slowing down.

Pluto in Aquarius, until early 2044, squares [Pluto in Scorpio](/astrology/pluto-in-scorpio/): people born from 1983 to 1995 enter the orb between about 38 and 47.

Before it comes the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-pluto/)), between about 21 and 36; after it, the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-pluto/)), from about 48½. The opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-pluto/)) waits until the eighties, for the few who live to see it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name one thing you protect by controlling it, and try holding it more loosely for a season.

Let someone see the change while it is happening. The square goes better witnessed than hidden.

Write down what you are afraid of losing, and set it beside what you would actually miss. This square often shows that the two lists are not the same.
