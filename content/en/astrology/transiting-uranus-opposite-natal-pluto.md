Everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets this opposition once, and for people born from 1995 to 2008 it is happening now. For about twenty months, the urge to break free and the pull of deep attachments stand at opposite ends of one axis of the chart.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is where you hold on hardest and where, in time, you renew: power, trust, the patterns you can't leave alone. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates two functions through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) freedom and attachment each become visible against the other.

The other end usually shows up in someone or something outside you: a family system with its own rules, an institution that holds power over part of your life, a relationship in which one person has more say. Or you're the one holding the power, and someone close to you wants out of the arrangement. The opposition shows the pattern from both sides at once.

I read it as a call to rebalance, not to win. Pluto can confuse control with safety and secrecy with depth; Uranus can confuse distance with objectivity, so walking away may look like clarity. The polarity asks what each end needs: freedom that doesn't have to destroy, and depth that doesn't have to cling. It predicts no break-up, no loss and no ending ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

With Pluto in your 6th and Uranus crossing your 12th ([Uranus transiting the 12th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-12th-house/)), the control you keep over daily routines meets a loosening of what you hold in private. Both houses rest on the minute of your birth, which is why I won't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition lasts about twenty months, eleven when the pass is short, and its three exact hits, in more than nine passes out of ten, each show the pattern of power from a slightly different side. Every birth from 1930 to 2009 meets it once before 80: no generation escapes it, and none gets a second.

This opposition belongs now to the people born with [Pluto in Sagittarius](/astrology/pluto-in-sagittarius/), from January 1995 to November 2008: Uranus, in Gemini until 2033, reaches the point opposite their Pluto when they are about 24 to 30, younger than any generation born before them.

The age has fallen with every decade of birth: in the site's count, about 59 for someone born at the start of 1935, about 47 for 1955, about 37 for 1975 and about 26 for 2005. The two planets last stood opposite each other in the sky in 1901 and 1902, so no one born from 1930 to 2009 has [Uranus opposite Pluto](/astrology/uranus-opposite-pluto/) in the birth chart, and the pair's polarity reaches all of them by transit only.

For the generation meeting it now, born from 1995 to 2008, [transiting Uranus trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-pluto/) came about fourteen years earlier, in childhood or the teens, and comes again about fourteen years later; [transiting Uranus square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-pluto/) stands some twenty-one years off on either side, and [transiting Uranus sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-pluto/) and [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-pluto/) lie on the far side of the round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Map the power in the situation honestly: who decides what, and who pays which cost. Seeing it plainly is half of changing it.

Ask for the freedom you need in specific terms. Under this opposition a precise request is heard better than a general rebellion.

Loosen some of your own grip first. Pluto's end of the axis eases once it stops waiting for the other side to move.
