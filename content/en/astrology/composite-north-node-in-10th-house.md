Two people can spend ten years building a home together and still be strangers to each other's working lives. Composite North Node in the 10th house asks such a relationship to step outside, and to make something together that the world can see.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Composites are other astrologers' tool, not mine, so this is the technique as they read it. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) builds the relationship a chart from midpoints. Its [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the direction of growth, its [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, the habits the bond falls back on. With the South Node in [the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), those habits are private: home, family, the life behind the front door. The North Node sits in [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), angular, naturally Capricorn's, at the top of the chart: a public role, shared responsibility, a reputation, a contribution others can point to.

The privacy belongs to the relationship. Each of you may have a busy professional life, yet the bond itself has no public face, nothing the two of you do together beyond your own walls. The 10th-house direction gives it one: a project you run jointly, a cause you're known for, a role you take on for a community, even a family business, built with the same care you give your home.

Compare one person's [North Node in the 10th house](/astrology/north-node-in-10th-house/), learning to take up a public role, and [their North Node in your 10th house](/astrology/north-node-in-10th-house-synastry/), where someone's growth runs through your ambitions. The composite node is ambition held jointly, which also means it has to be agreed jointly.

The house opens at the composite [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the midpoint of your two natal Midheavens, and both ways of drawing composite houses start from that degree, whatever they do with the other cusps. It needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and it carries a quirk of its own: when the two natal Midheavens sit almost opposite each other, the nearer midpoint can jump to the other side, and the composite 10th and 4th change places. Short of that, a node close below the Midheaven may belong with [composite North Node in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-9th-house/), one near the far cusp with [composite North Node in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-11th-house/), and the mean and true node can settle a borderline case either way. I look at both.

The risk is the relationship turning into an institution: managed rather than lived, judged by what it achieves, its private warmth spent on its public image. The 4th-house end is the counterweight, since a home that stays tender gives a public role its roots. It helps to choose the shared role deliberately, small enough to begin this year, and to keep one evening a week on which neither of you mentions it.

None of this forecasts success, status, fame or failure; it shows a direction worth taking, and its practitioners read it beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). Whatever role the pair takes on in public, the chart itself stays private, drawn only when both people have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
