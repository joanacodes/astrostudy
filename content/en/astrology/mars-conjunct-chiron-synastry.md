Imagine the friend who signs you both up for the very thing you'd quietly decided wasn't for you. Mars conjunct Chiron in synastry can feel like that from one side, and like finally being taken seriously from the other, when one person's Mars lands within 6° of the other's Chiron.

## What flows {#what-flows}

With [Mars](/astrology/mars/), a person goes first, makes the effort and stands up for what they want. With [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/), they adapt around a limit that hasn't gone away: they change the speed, the equipment or the approach so they can still take part, and the know-how they build often becomes something they can teach. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) binds the two into a single movement, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), few bring someone's energy so directly to another person's tender place.

If the Mars is yours, you may become the one who gets the other to try again, or you may learn, watching how they work round their limit, a better way to pace your own effort. If the Chiron is yours, the other person's drive arrives exactly where you once had to find another route. It can feel like encouragement you didn't know you wanted, and some days like pressure on a sore spot.

Take Mars in Aries on Chiron in Aries: a quick, go-first drive meeting a sensitivity around confidence and going first, as with the colleague who insists the other present their own work.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

On one degree, whose limit is it? The Mars person can start treating the other's tender place as a problem to fix, and push harder than was ever asked. The Chiron person can hide the effort it costs to keep up, so the other never learns how much adapting is going on. Needing support isn't the same as being incapable, and a limit that comes back isn't a failure.

Where [Mars conjunct Saturn in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-saturn-synastry/) meets a limit being set, this one meets a limit already there, to be worked around rather than removed. The other angles:

- **[Mars sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-chiron-synastry/):** a nudge of the right size, offered across two signs.
- **[Mars square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-chiron-synastry/):** the push catches the tender place sideways, and methods have to change.
- **[Mars trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-chiron-synastry/):** support that adjusts itself without being told.
- **[Mars opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-chiron-synastry/):** drive and tenderness face each other like a mirror.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the Chiron person set the pace, and let them name the adjustment that works: shorter sessions, a different tool, company or none. The Mars person can check first, a push or a hand, and take either answer at face value. In a couple, that might be a fear one of you has carried for years; between siblings, a late driving test, with the other in the passenger seat.

Of the pair's five contacts, this is the least common: about 1 pair of people in 16 has it, one way round or the other, within 6°. How big the crowd sharing the Chiron person's position is depends on the sign: in Aries or Pisces, where Chiron lingers about eight years, a wide one; in Virgo or Libra, under two, a narrow one. Mars, there for a few weeks, is what makes the meeting these two people's. The times decide which house of each chart receives it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron is one of three placements, with Saturn and Lilith, that held 75 to 90% of the time when I tested charts; what I tested was each person's own chart, never a contact between two, so this one carries no figure ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). Naming someone's tender place is intimate, so I do it only when both have asked ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

In one person, [Mars conjunct Chiron](/astrology/mars-conjunct-chiron/) is courage shaped by having had to adapt. Shared between two, the courage is offered, and the adapting is respected.
