To be seen at your best can feel like a gift, and on some days like a costume. When one person's Neptune lies on the other's Sun, the Sun person's way of being becomes the place where the other's imagination and compassion come to rest.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises a person's identity and vitality, the self they're trying to express. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings imagination, compassion and inspiration: the capacity to see meaning beyond the literal, and to idealise. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into a single process, and among [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this fusion is unusually tender.

The Sun person is seen generously. Their potential is taken on trust, their rough edges forgiven before they're mentioned, and in that company many people grow gentler with themselves, more creative, readier to take an inner life seriously.

The Neptune person finds their imagination given a face. The other's presence moves them, gives a vague sense of meaning a direction, and calls up an empathy whose depth can surprise them. Giving to this person rarely feels like a cost.

Couples who make music or a garden together, friends who share a spiritual practice, a teacher who saw what a student could become, colleagues held together by a cause: this conjunction often looks like that.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

With the two this close, the person and the ideal can merge. The Sun person may start to feel they're living up to a picture, and that their tired, ordinary days are a letdown. The Neptune person can lose track of their own direction while tending the other's, and compassion slides towards self-erasure, which it was never meant to be. Both may avoid plain words for fear of spoiling the mood.

Idealising someone isn't a delusion; it's one of the things Neptune does. It just needs checking against an ordinary Tuesday now and then.

Neptune stays about fourteen years in a sign; its sign therefore belongs to the Neptune person's generation, shared with nearly everyone they went to school with. The Sun, which crosses a sign in about a month, is what makes this conjunction belong to two people rather than to an era.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Describe each other plainly now and then: habits, preferences, what irritates you. The Sun person can say, kindly, "that's not quite me" when the image drifts; the Neptune person can keep projects and limits of their own, so that giving stays a choice. In a couple, that means practical conversations about money and time alongside the shared dreams; between friends, honesty when something hurts; in a family, letting a relative be ordinary; at work, a clear practical lead for the inspired project.

About 1 pair of people in 12 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 8°. Both birth times are needed. The conjunction itself hardly ever depends on the hour, the Sun moving a degree or so a day, but the houses show which part of each life holds the idealising, love, work or home, and they can't be drawn without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other angles give the ideal more distance: [Sun sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-neptune-synastry/) as a gentle offer, [Sun square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-neptune-synastry/) as friction between the picture and the person, [Sun trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-neptune-synastry/) as easy kindness, [Sun opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-neptune-synastry/) as fascination from the far side. Inside one chart, [Sun conjunct Neptune](/astrology/sun-conjunct-neptune/) blends a person's identity with their own imagination; here the blending comes from someone else's way of seeing you. Because it touches how one person imagines another, I read it with both people taking part, never about one of them ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
