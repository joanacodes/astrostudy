Some couples are at their best at two in the morning, when the conversation finally reaches the thing that matters. Composite Jupiter in Scorpio takes a relationship deep: into trust, into hard truths, into what the two of you have come through.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Composite [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the midpoint of your two natal Jupiters in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), is the bond's capacity to grow: what it understands, what it holds true, what it opens itself to. [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), fixed water, lends that growth depth, intensity and staying power. Scorpio is not one of Jupiter's signs, though the other two water signs are its exaltation and, traditionally, its home. Scorpio itself has two rulers: [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) in modern tropical astrology and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) in the tradition. I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and in a composite they say different kinds of thing. Composite Mars belongs to the two of you alone. Composite Pluto barely moves between people born a few years apart, since Pluto spends 12 to 31 years in a sign, so it speaks more of your generation than of your bond.

About 1 pair in 10 has composite Jupiter here, close behind Libra and Virgo, among two million random pairs born from 1930 to 2009. Pairs of the same age share it when both were born while Jupiter crossed Scorpio: from October 1934 to November 1935, from late November 1981 to late December 1982, from November 1993 to early December 1994, or from late October 2005 to November 2006.

Here depth is a joint discovery. Two light-hearted people can discover that together they talk about what others avoid: money owed, old griefs, what each of them is afraid of. In a birth chart, [Jupiter in Scorpio](/astrology/jupiter-in-scorpio/) sends one person beneath the surface; the composite sends the pair, and what it finds belongs to both. Such a bond often grows most after a hard season, a job lost, a family quarrel, a long separation, met together and understood afterwards. Its generosity is loyal: confidences kept, help given without being asked, a long memory for kindness. Friends often bring this pair what they can't tell anyone else, because the bond has shown it can hold a secret without ever using it.

The risk is that intensity becomes the only measure. A pair that grows through depth can come to distrust lightness, read every silence as a secret and give its trust all at once or not at all. Depth isn't secrecy, and transformation needn't mean upheaval. What helps is building trust in stages, keeping some easy pleasures on purpose, and letting a hard conversation end once it has done its work.

Its neighbours differ: [composite Jupiter in Libra](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-libra/) grows through fairness, [composite Jupiter in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-sagittarius/) through a widening search for meaning. I don't draw composites in my readings; the technique described here reads a Scorpio Jupiter with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and nothing in its depth measures how long a bond will last or what it will survive. Scorpio may keep secrets, but a composite must never be one: it is drawn only with both people's knowledge ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
