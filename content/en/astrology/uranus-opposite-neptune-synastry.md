No one born in the eighty years the count covers has Uranus opposite Neptune in their own chart, yet between two charts it is ordinary. In Uranus opposite Neptune in synastry, one person's Uranus faces the other's Neptune across the zodiac, within 4°: change at one end, ideals at the other, and usually decades between the two births.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) wants what is next: new arrangements, freedom from habits that no longer serve, systems rebuilt from the ground. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) wants what is larger: compassion, imagination, a meaning beyond the practical. Across an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), each function shows most clearly against the other; in the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the two ends belong to two people, here usually of different generations.

If the Uranus is yours, the other person seems to hold a faith you traded for practicality, or never had. Their ideals can look like a refusal to deal with how things are, and also like the reason your changes were worth making.

If the Neptune is yours, the other person seems to carry the drive to change things that you left to others. Their reforms can feel like a threat to what you care about, and also like the only way it will ever be built.

A Cancer Uranus from the mid-1950s facing a Capricorn Neptune from the late 1990s: new ways of making a home and a family, opposite an ideal of lasting structures and duty.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Polarity hardens into labels. The Uranus person becomes the one who breaks things, the Neptune person the one with their head in the clouds, and each defends their end by pointing at the other's. The seesaw stops, and a conversation between eras turns into a standoff. An opposition is a seesaw, never enmity, and both ends carry something the other lacks.

- **[Uranus conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** change and ideal on one degree, fused rather than facing.
- **[Uranus sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** a friendly angle where they trade help.
- **[Uranus square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune-synastry/):** half the distance, where they rub.
- **[Uranus trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune-synastry/):** two signs closer, where they run together.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let each name what the other's era got right. The Uranus person can say which of the other's ideals they would keep; the Neptune person, which change they would welcome; then trade one each. Grandparents and grown grandchildren, parents and adult children, a long-serving and a newly arrived colleague: across that kind of gap, the opposition becomes a way of passing things on in both directions.

The age gap is usually wide: about forty years between a mid-1955 birth, Uranus in Cancer, and anyone born from January 1995 to February 1998, when Neptune was in the last degrees of Capricorn; some twenty-six years between a mid-1965 birth, Neptune in Scorpio, and anyone born from April 1938 to July 1939, Uranus in Taurus. Whole runs of births share each end, so my synastry count, which keeps to contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven, leaves it out. Through the houses, I read a difference between two eras' ideas of change and of meaning, more than between two people.

The planets don't move with the birth hour; the houses do ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Neptune in the Uranus person's 7th house, the polarity lives in the partnership: the other person seems to embody the ideal the Uranus person keeps at arm's length ([Their Neptune in your 7th house](/astrology/neptune-in-7th-house-synastry/)).

Within a single chart, [Uranus opposite Neptune](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune/) belongs to no one born in the count's eighty years. Between two charts it belongs to two generations at once, and I read it only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
