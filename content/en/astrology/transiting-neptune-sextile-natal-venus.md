Neptune's sextile to your Venus asks for nothing and insists on nothing. For about two and a half years a door stands open between what you love and what you can imagine, and what lies beyond it depends on whether you walk through.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) appreciates: it notices beauty, prefers one thing to another and keeps the give and take of a bond in balance. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) adds compassion, intuition and a feeling for what can't quite be seen. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) links two functions at 60° through a cooperation that waits for you, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your taste and your affections can borrow Neptune's imagination whenever you reach for it, and not before.

The openings are small and easy to walk past. Someone might ask you to join a choir; an old wish to draw might come back; you might notice that a disagreement would soften if you said what you'd hoped for rather than what went wrong. Kindness comes a little more easily, and so does the sense that a relationship could hold more tenderness than it has been given. Each of these is an offer, not an event. A Neptune sextile left unanswered simply passes, and nothing is lost but the chance.

Two houses take part: the one your Venus sits in, where the opening is, and the one Neptune is crossing, where the invitation comes from. With Venus in your 1st, the sextile often comes while Neptune crosses your 3rd or your 11th: through words, lessons and letters ([Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/)), or through friends. Your birth time places both houses, and without it there is no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In a birth chart, [Venus sextile Neptune](/astrology/venus-sextile-neptune/) is this same openness carried for life. The transit lends it for a season, to a chart that may not have it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune keeps the sextile within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, for about two years and seven months, seldom fewer than twenty-two: time enough for a class to become a practice, while Neptune, looping back each year, touches the exact point three times, or five in about one pass in four.

Your Venus has two sextile points, 60° on either side, and in Neptune's slow time they are about fifty-five years apart. Most people meet one before 80 and some meet both, with [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-venus/) falling halfway between the two. Neither comes before 80 for about 1 person in 6 born between 1930 and 2009.

Of the other contacts, [transiting Neptune square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-venus/) comes to almost everyone, [transiting Neptune trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-venus/) to about as many people as this sextile, and [transiting Neptune opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-venus/), like the conjunction, to about half.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to one invitation. A class, a rehearsal, a morning in a gallery, the letter you've been meaning to write: the sextile rewards a step small enough to take this week.

Tell someone what you've been hoping for between you. Hope offered gently is easier to hear as tenderness than as a complaint.

Bring the practical part yourself. Neptune supplies the inspiration; the diary, the materials and the hour are yours.
