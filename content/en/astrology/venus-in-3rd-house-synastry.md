Everyday life gets kinder with their Venus in your 3rd house. Messages arrive with a kind word in them, errands turn into outings, and the ordinary back-and-forth of a week acquires a little grace.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

The words you trade each day, what you read and write, the streets around you, brothers, sisters and cousins, the short trips of an ordinary week: that's [the 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/), the world within reach. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) brings appreciation, taste and the wish for harmony. With this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), what they value finds its way into your words and your daily round.

You may notice how kindly they speak to you, and how easily you speak back. They enjoy your turns of phrase, laugh at your stories, send you the song or the picture that made them think of you. A walk to the shops with them is somehow better than alone; learning something together, a recipe, a skill, a few words of a language, has a lightness to it.

Your everyday world charms them: your street, your local café, your way of telling the day's news, the people you grew up alongside. They feel at ease in your ordinary life, which is rarer and more valuable than being impressed by it. The 3rd is a cadent house, one tradition called the Goddess, and [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), its natural sign, gives it a light, sociable touch.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Pleasant talk can avoid the hard conversation. When everything between you is easy and kind, raising a difficulty can feel like breaking something, and harmony slides into keeping the peace. Their compliments may also seem light, sweet but not quite serious, just when you need them to engage with what's wrong.

On their side, they can feel that you take their warmth for granted, or that words come to you more easily than tenderness. If they bring kindness to every exchange and get efficiency back, they may wonder what they mean to you.

Near the end of your 3rd, close to your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), their Venus also warms the root of your chart, and fondness for your daily world becomes fondness for your home and family.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use the ease to say difficult things kindly; that's what it's for. Keep sending the small messages, and make sure some of them say something that matters. With a partner, love notes and long walks suit this overlay. With an adult sibling or cousin, it can describe the one you're simply fond of, without old history in the way. With a friend, a letter-writing kind of closeness, even when you live round the corner. With a colleague, the pleasant voice on the morning call, worth thanking now and then.

Reversed, as your Venus in their 3rd house, the warmth would flow from you into their daily messages and errands. Next door, [their Venus in your 2nd house](/astrology/venus-in-2nd-house-synastry/) enjoys what you own and value, and [their Venus in your 4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house-synastry/) feels at home under your roof.

When Venus turns retrograde, about every eighteen months, it slows almost to a halt for some days, and a birth date in those days pins it down even more precisely. The 3rd itself is drawn by your birth time, and in Placidus, the house system I use, the IC that closes it travels round the whole zodiac in a day ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A relative's chart belongs to them as much as yours does to you, so I read the two of you only when both agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
