Jupiter's year in your 10th house begins at the top of your chart, on the Midheaven, and spends its growth in public: your work, your role, the direction you'd like to be known for.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands, gathers meaning and looks for principles to act on; the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is public life: work, responsibility, reputation, the role you play and what you'd like to leave behind. Ambitions can feel within reach in this year, and the direction of your work clearer. You may take on more responsibility, be asked to lead, or simply understand better what you want your work to mean.

The stay opens at the Midheaven, the cusp of the 10th in Placidus, the house system I use ([transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)), and that crossing is often the most visible stretch of the year. Capricorn, the 10th's sign in the natural order, is where tradition placed Jupiter's fall, and the old picture suits the house: big plans asked to show their foundations.

After the angle, Jupiter reaches whatever your 10th holds from birth: a natal Saturn there, the house's natural ruler, can make the pass feel like a long, patient effort finally finding room.

What the [transit](/astrology/transits/) doesn't do is hand out titles. It doesn't promise a promotion, a reputation or success: it brings out what your chart already holds about work and direction, and the season you're in at work, not what will happen to your career.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

For a birth at London's latitude, Placidus puts Jupiter's stay in the 10th at a little over a year in the middle, with most between about 11 months and 22. At New York's latitude the long end is shorter, about 20 months, which makes the 10th and the 11th the steadiest houses there.

Without your birth time there's no Midheaven, and without the Midheaven I'd be guessing, so I don't read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). What you came to believe during Jupiter's year in your 9th ([Jupiter transiting the 9th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-9th-house/)) gets acted on here, and what you build here is shared with others when Jupiter moves into your 11th ([Jupiter transiting the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-11th-house/)).

Look up your Midheaven's sign and find it by modality. Cardinal, like Capricorn, the 10th's natural sign: Libra (August 2028 to September 2029), Capricorn (November 2031 to November 2032), Aries (April 2034 to April 2035) and Cancer (May 2037 to June 2038). Fixed: Leo (June 2026 to July 2027), Scorpio (September 2029 to October 2030), Aquarius (April 2032 to December 2033) and Taurus (April 2035 to May 2036). Mutable: Virgo (July 2027 to August 2028), Sagittarius (October 2030 to November 2031), Pisces (April 2033 to April 2034) and Gemini (May 2036 to May 2037). Jupiter reaches the Midheaven when it reaches its degree, and in 2032 it moves back and forth between Capricorn and Aquarius.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say what you're working towards, out loud, to the people it concerns. The 10th is the public house, and Jupiter widens whatever is already visible.

Take on the responsibility that fits the direction you want, and turn down what only adds weight. A year like this tends to offer more than one person can carry well.

Find someone to learn from, or someone to teach. And measure the year by direction rather than titles: if by the end of it you know better what your work is for, Jupiter's season in the 10th has done what it does.
