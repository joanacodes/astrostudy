Sagittarius sits just behind Capricorn, so your Moon trails your Sun by a sign: an explorer's needs inside a builder's life. You were born in the last days of a lunar month, under a thin morning crescent. I read the pair as a structured life that keeps a window open.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Neighbouring signs share nothing, and these two make the difference plain: cardinal earth for the Sun, mutable fire for the Moon. The [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) takes on responsibility and builds in stages; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) gets its breath back when the walls move out: the weekend away, the course in a subject with no practical use, the laugh shared at the end of a hard day. You may run a tight schedule all week and feel restored only by the one afternoon that isn't on it.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), your Moon's ruler, has its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Capricorn, the sign of your Sun: the planet of meaning and growth is least at ease on the Sun's ground, so the Moon's need for room has to argue its case to a self that wants proof first. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), your Sun's ruler, has no dignity in Sagittarius, and the lights have none here. I read the two rulers together, and their relation in the chart shows whether duty and hope negotiate or simply take turns.

This is a birth from the end of the month. Your Moon was 301° to 359° ahead of the Sun, which is up to 59° behind it, twenty-four and a half to twenty-nine and a half days after the last new moon: a fine crescent rising before dawn, or no Moon to see at all. Nearly nine births in ten fall in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), the rest at the end of the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) phase, and the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) that followed was only days away. A balsamic birth, to me, carries the feeling of a cycle closing, and this Moon closes it by looking beyond it. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says more.

About 1 person in 152 has this pair. If the Moon left Scorpio or reached Capricorn on your birthday, only the time of birth can say whether this page is yours, or the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-scorpio/), or the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-capricorn/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Born on 21 or 22 December, the same time may also decide your Sun sign. The rising sign and the houses of Saturn and Jupiter wait on it too.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You bring loyalty and humour in equal measure. The Capricorn Sun commits seriously and shows love by carrying responsibility; the Sagittarius Moon needs a partner who laughs, tells the truth and doesn't ask for an account of every evening. The risk is a relationship that is all duty and no adventure, or adventure only when you're away from it. A partner who plans the trip with you, and lets you be light without thinking you're not serious, keeps both lights fed.

## At work {#at-work}

Work with a structure and a horizon suits you: higher education, publishing, international organisations, law, travel and its planning, any institution built around an idea. The Sun brings discipline and the willingness to stay; the Moon brings perspective, humour in a crisis, and the question of what all this is for. A job that is only rules wears the Moon down; a job that is only vision unsettles the Sun. Look for long projects that keep teaching you something.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Duty can keep the window shut. The Sun adds responsibilities, the Moon quietly starves for room, and the result can be a conscientious life that feels oddly airless, or sudden escapes that the Sun then regrets. Jupiter in its fall here suggests a need for meaning that doesn't quite trust itself. What helps is building the horizon into the calendar: a trip booked a year ahead, a subject studied for pleasure, one conversation a week about something larger than the to-do list.
