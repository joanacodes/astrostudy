A career, for you, has to stand on something solid: placed in the 2nd, the ruler of your 10th house ties what you do in public to what you earn, keep and value.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is your public direction: the work and the standing you build, and the part you play in the world. In Placidus, the house system I use, it begins at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), and the ruler of the Midheaven's sign carries its business into whatever house it occupies ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Here that's the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/): your income and the way you earn it, what you own, the skills that could keep you, and beneath all that, how much you think you're worth.

Worth is the ground this vocation stands on. You may choose work for the security it gives, or build security through the work you're known for: the craft that becomes a business, the expertise people pay for. What you value tends to show in public, like the shop that sells only what its owner believes in, or the professional who won't take a client they can't respect. A first job taken for the money can turn, years later, into the thing you're known for. Money isn't a dirty word here, but it isn't the measure either. The trap is letting your standing rise and fall with your income, or the reverse: staying in a well-paid role that no longer says anything about you.

The natural zodiac gives the 2nd to Taurus and the 10th to Capricorn, and with Virgo's 6th they make the three earth houses ([the natural zodiac](/astrology/natural-zodiac/)), the practical side of life, where things are made, kept and maintained. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn Midheaven from the 2nd, builds slowly, a career that accumulates like savings ([Saturn in the 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-in-2nd-house/)). Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo Midheaven, earns through skill with words, numbers or trade. The Moon, ruling a Cancer Midheaven, wants a career that feels like a safe base.

What it can't say is how much you'll earn, or what to do with it: no chart can pick an investment or forecast an income ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). It shows the link between your work and your sense of what's worth having.

In Placidus, neither the 2nd cusp nor the Midheaven stays put for long, and a ruler near the edge of the 2nd can change house with a small correction to the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Later, it could cross into the 1st, where your work becomes part of your person ([Ruler of the 10th in the 1st house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/)). An earlier time leaves it in the 3rd, where the work moves and talks ([Ruler of the 10th in the 3rd house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)).

A Pisces Midheaven has two rulers, Jupiter and Neptune ([Pisces on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Jupiter in the 2nd tends to share what the work brings in, and to value growth over hoarding. Neptune in the 2nd can blur the line between a calling and a living, so it helps to know which one you're paying for.
