Mercury conjunct North Node sets the planet of the mind on the path a life is asked to grow along. It's one of the rarer Mercury contacts, and it points growth towards words: asking, learning, explaining, saying what you think.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the chart's function for information and exchange: noticing, learning, interpreting, speaking. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a point, not a planet: where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's going north, read as the direction of development, a less familiar way of working that grows stronger with practice. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes Mercury part of that direction, so thinking and growth move as one while each keeps its nature.

Lived, this tends to mean that your next step involves the mind. You may be asked, again and again, to learn something new, to speak up where you'd rather listen, to ask the question rather than know the answer, or to put into words what you've only ever felt. At first it can feel clumsy, the way any new skill does. With repetition and feedback it gets easier, and more rewarding.

The North Node never works alone. Exactly opposite sits the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), so Mercury conjunct one is opposite the other, and the familiar, practised resources across the [nodal axis](/astrology/nodal-axis/) stay yours. Development doesn't mean abandoning them; it means adding a new range to what you already do well.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The North Node spends about 18 months in each sign, moving backwards, so its sign is shared by everyone born over a year and a half or so; Mercury's position, and the house of the pair, make the conjunction personal. In the 3rd house, growth runs through everyday conversation and learning by asking; in the 9th, through study and bigger questions; in the 10th, through a public voice. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

About 1 person in 42 is born with Mercury within 6° of the North Node, one of the rarer Mercury contacts in the count. I look at both the mean and the true node ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)): they differ by a degree or two at most, so they usually agree, but near the edge of the orb one can show the conjunction and the other not, and then I say so. My notebook has no count for the nodes, and I won't invent one, so I offer this conjunction as a direction to test for yourself ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)).

Mercury's other aspects to the North Node place the mind differently along the path:

- **[Mercury sextile North Node](/astrology/mercury-sextile-north-node/):** an easy opening towards growth, with an easy link back to the familiar.
- **[Mercury square North Node](/astrology/mercury-square-north-node/):** the mind stands at right angles to the path, between familiar and new, asking to be brought along.
- **[Mercury trine North Node](/astrology/mercury-trine-north-node/):** thought supports the direction of growth while keeping an easy sextile to the South Node.
- **[Mercury opposite North Node](/astrology/mercury-opposite-north-node/):** Mercury sits on the South Node, so your thinking belongs to the familiar end and growth asks you to stretch beyond it.

When one person's Mercury sits on the other's North Node, [Mercury conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) can make conversation a push towards what the other finds hardest, sometimes more firmly than they'd like. The effect isn't always mutual, so I read it from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

The nodes move backwards and take about 18.6 years to go round, so [transiting North Node conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-mercury/) comes once in each cycle, for some weeks. Astrologers read it as a time when learning, writing or speaking up comes forward as the next step. Born with the conjunction, you meet it at each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/), at about 18½, 37 and 56, when the nodes come back to their birth places; halfway between, at the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), it's the South Node that crosses your Mercury.

Weather, not a forecast: these moments bring the question of direction back into view, and leave the answer with you.
