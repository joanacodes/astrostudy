Uranus opposite Lilith sets the planet of difference across the chart from the point that marks what you learned to hide. The two kinds of independence face each other here, and you often meet one end in another person before you recognise it as yours.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables liberation, differentiation and reform: the part of you that wants room to live another way. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is, as I read it, instinctive autonomy: the self once found too much or too strange, and the boundaries that guard it. Interpretations of Lilith vary from one tradition to the next, and I offer mine as a reading, not a law. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, each becoming clearer from the other's side.

Here the polarity can swing. At times your appetite for change leads and the untamed part retreats; at others, that part breaks cover and your wish to reform things goes quiet. Other people often carry one end for a while: someone whose unpredictability brings your own refused side to the surface, or a partner whose need for freedom touches the boundaries you guard most closely. What you meet in them is your own far end, not an adversary. Polarity isn't contradiction, and over time this opposition tends to teach a particular integration: an originality grounded in instinct, and an instinct free enough to change.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 30 born between 1930 and 2009 has Uranus within 6° of an opposition to mean Lilith, about half as many as for the square or trine, since there is one point opposite Uranus against two of theirs. Lilith, moving forward through a sign in about nine months, passes that point roughly once a decade, and the opposition holds for a few months. A chart drawn with true Lilith may not show it ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

An opposition joins opposite signs of one modality. Uranus in Libra opposite Lilith in Aries, say, could set an independence expressed through relationships against a raw, solitary instinct. The houses name the two areas facing each other: Uranus in the 5th opposite Lilith in the 11th, for example, could set a playful, creative originality against an untamed side that shows in friendships and groups. Houses depend on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Lilith](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-lilith/):** both ends in one place, acting as one.
- **[Uranus sextile Lilith](/astrology/uranus-sextile-lilith/):** a side door between them, opened by choice.
- **[Uranus square Lilith](/astrology/uranus-square-lilith/):** friction at right angles rather than a face-to-face polarity.
- **[Uranus trine Lilith](/astrology/uranus-trine-lilith/):** the two in easy agreement.

[Uranus opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-lilith-synastry/) places one person's urge to differ across from the other's untamed side. Each sees in the other what they keep at the far end of themselves, so I read it both ways.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Transiting Lilith opposes natal Uranus once in each round of about 8 years and 10 months, for a couple of weeks; transiting Uranus opposes natal Lilith at most once in a life, for months.

Born with the opposition, you see the two ends brought together twice over. Halfway between Lilith's returns, transiting Lilith crosses your natal Uranus; and at your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), around 42, transiting Uranus reaches your natal Lilith. Weather, not a forecast: moments when the far end comes close, and asks to be owned.
