Capricorn ends under the Sun. Its last ten degrees give the Sun its only standing in the sign, hold the exact degree of Mars's exaltation, and lead to the border with Aquarius in mid-January.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

The [Chaldean order](/astrology/chaldean-order/) moves from Mars ([Capricorn, second decan](/astrology/capricorn-second-decan/)) to the Sun, so the last face of [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) is the Sun's ([faces](/astrology/faces/)). As in the middle of Scorpio ([Scorpio, second decan](/astrology/scorpio-second-decan/)), that face is the Sun's only dignity in the sign, so a [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) between 20° and 30° is the one Capricorn Sun the old tables don't treat as a stranger.

The modern decans give the last third to Virgo, the remaining earth sign, and so to Mercury. The two rulers are never far apart in the sky: seen from Earth, Mercury never strays more than about 28° from the Sun. I read their pairing here as the Sun's sense of purpose with Mercury's eye for method: knowing where you're going, and how to get there, step by step. It also means that if your Sun is in this decan, your Mercury is in Sagittarius, Capricorn or Aquarius, and never further away.

Tradition puts the exact point of Mars's exaltation in the 28th degree of Capricorn ([exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/)), so the degree where [Mars in Capricorn](/astrology/mars-in-capricorn/) shows its exaltation most plainly lies inside this decan of the Sun.

## Its image {#its-image}

Wallis Budge's Egyptian list has Sert here, written Si-srat in the Greek-Egyptian lists, and Sert opens a run of three decans whose names share it: Sasa-sert and Kher-khept-sert follow in Aquarius ([Aquarius, first decan](/astrology/aquarius-first-decan/)). [Firmicus Maternus](/astrology/firmicus-maternus/) wrote Omot, Joseph Scaliger Homoth; the Hermetic lists give Epichnaus in Greek and Marcois in Latin. Cosmas of Maiuma calls it Tolma, and Athanasius Kircher, in 1652, Monuphta.

The sea-goat's own stars have moved on. [Deneb Algedi](/astrology/deneb-algedi/), the tail of the sea-goat and the brightest star of the constellation Capricornus, stands at 23°54′ Aquarius in 2026: precession has carried the goat's tail out of the goat's sign ([constellations versus signs](/astrology/constellations-versus-signs/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Births from about 11 to 19 January put the Sun in these degrees. A Capricorn Sun of the third decan holds the Sun's own face, and I read the shade as ambition that is also personal: the goal is yours, and so is the method. A Mercury placed here rules its own decan by element, and I read it as thinking that plans ahead: the list, the schedule, the next three steps written down. For a birthday on 19 or 20 January, only the time of birth puts the Sun on one side or the other of the border with Aquarius.

[Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/) in its last decan takes about half an hour to rise in Paris or London, quick enough that a birth time rounded to the hour tells me little about which decan was rising ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The 1990s left two slow planets here: [Neptune in Capricorn](/astrology/neptune-in-capricorn/) from early 1993 to 1998, and Uranus from early 1993 to the start of 1996. Pluto crossed the same degrees from 2018 to 2024.
