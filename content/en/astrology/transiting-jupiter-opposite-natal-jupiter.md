Halfway round each twelve-year cycle, Jupiter in the sky reaches the sign opposite the one it held when you were born. The growth that began at your last return is at its fullest there, and you get to look at it from the far side.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Where your natal [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) stands tells you what growth means to you and what you believe in. The [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates functions through polarity: two ends of one axis, each showing up what the other lacks. With Jupiter on both ends, it's your own convictions that meet their counterweight, and quite often the counterweight arrives as a person: a teacher, a partner, a colleague who believes the opposite with the same warmth.

The sign across matters. Born with Jupiter in Capricorn, the sign of its fall in tradition, you meet every one of these oppositions with Jupiter in Cancer, its exaltation; born with Jupiter in Cancer, the other way round. Jupiter's own signs, Sagittarius and traditionally Pisces, face Gemini and Virgo, its detriments. Either way, the sky shows your way of growing its mirror image.

In the cycle, this is the midpoint between two [Jupiter returns](/astrology/jupiter-return/). What you started about six years ago is now in full view: the course has a qualification attached, the move has a life around it, the belief has met other believers. It's a natural moment to ask what has grown as you meant it to and what has simply grown. Which part of life gets the review is a matter of your Jupiter's house, and a house can't be placed without the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

I count it from 1½° before exact to 1° after, which at Jupiter’s usual pace gives eleven days or so; nearly a third of these oppositions turn into a loop of three exact passes over about eight months.

Seven is the number by 80 for every birth from 1930 to 2009. The earliest first opposition in the count came at 4.9, the latest at 6.4; for a birth on 1 January 1985 it was about 5.5, for 1 January 1945 about 6.3. After that, one comes about every twelve years, and the fourth, at about 40 to 42, falls in the same stretch of life as the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), between about 39 and 44½. That one is the halfway point of an 84-year cycle and comes once; Jupiter's halfway point keeps coming back.

On the way to it and on the way back, Jupiter passes the other angles of the cycle: [transiting Jupiter trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-jupiter/) about two years either side, [transiting Jupiter square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-jupiter/) about three, and [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-jupiter/) about four. Of all these [transits](/astrology/transits/), the opposition is the only one that comes once per cycle rather than twice.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take stock on paper. Write what you wanted at your last return, then what you have now, and look at the gap without judging it: some of it is shortfall, some of it is growth you didn't plan.

Listen to the person who sees it the other way. An opposition between Jupiter and itself is a good season for reading the book you'd normally argue with, or asking someone you respect why they believe what they do.

Then choose what the second half of the round is for. The six years to the next return are shaped by what you decide to stop expanding as much as by what you decide to keep.
