Their Moon in your 3rd house turns ordinary conversation into a place of comfort. Another person's feelings come into the talking, texting and small exchanges of your week, and the two of you may find that a ten-minute chat settles more than a long evening.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) covers ordinary thinking and the people nearby: how you talk, what you read and write, your neighbours and relatives, the short trips of a normal week. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) describes what a person needs to feel comforted, and how they respond before they reason. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their Moon here lets their feelings move through your words.

For you, they can become the person you tell things to first, and the one whose news you want each day. Conversations with them have warmth, and you may notice your own way of talking soften or loosen in their company. The bond often takes on a sibling's familiarity: easy, a little teasing, as much a part of the day as the post.

For them, your words can be a place to settle. Telling you how the day went is how they find out how they feel about it, and your questions, your jokes, your ordinary company soothe them. Cadent houses are where things are adjusted and passed on, [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) is the 3rd's natural sign, and tradition named it the Goddess and gave the Moon its joy there, in the chart of the person born with it. A Moon visiting from another chart isn't the same thing, but the old name helps explain why talk and feeling sit so easily together here.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

When feelings ride on everyday talk, words can weigh more than they should. A quick reply may read as coolness, a joke as a slight, and a day without a message can unsettle them more than either of you expected. You may also find yourself becoming their daily outlet, hearing every mood while your own news waits.

From their side, your quick, many-sided way of talking can feel like being skimmed. They want to stay with a feeling; you've moved on to the next topic, the next errand, the next person who called.

If their Moon sits at the far end of your 3rd, it is also close to your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), and talk with them easily drifts towards home and memory.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Agree a rhythm of contact that suits both of you: a call at the end of the day, a message when you're thinking of each other, and permission for quiet days that mean nothing. Say feelings in words rather than leaving them to tone. In a couple, talk about the feeling, not around it; between adult siblings or cousins, this is often the one you ring first; between friends, a running conversation that never quite ends; between colleagues, watch that being the person they talk to doesn't become being their only support.

With your Moon in their 3rd house instead, you'd be the one who settles by talking to them, and who should leave room for their news. Beside it, [their Moon in your 2nd house](/astrology/moon-in-2nd-house-synastry/) settles around what you have, and [their Moon in your 4th house](/astrology/moon-in-4th-house-synastry/) in your home.

Their Moon moves about half a degree an hour, so a birth a quarter of a day earlier or later moves it by some three degrees, enough to carry it across your IC if it sits near there. Your own time draws the cusps, and in Placidus, the house system I use, I need both before I read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). A synastry between relatives is best done together, with both of you there ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#instead)).
