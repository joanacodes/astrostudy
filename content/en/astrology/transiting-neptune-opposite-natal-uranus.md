Few transits move with the birth year like this one. Neptune needs decades to reach the point opposite your Uranus, and because Uranus travels about twice as fast as Neptune, each year of birth adds roughly a year to the wait.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in your chart is where you need room to differ, to test another way and to reform what has stopped working. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) pulls the other way, towards imagination, compassion and what people hold in common. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets them on opposite sides of the chart, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the independence you have built meets its counterpart: a longing to belong to something larger, an ideal a group holds, a dream that asks for loyalty rather than distance.

Each side brings its blind spot. Uranus can take distance for objectivity; Neptune can take a shared feeling for the truth. The opposition shows both, often through other people: a friend who gives everything to a cause you'd never join, a circle that welcomes you as long as you stop arguing. What it asks for is integration, not surrender either way: which of your freedoms serve something you care about, and which ideals could use your habit of thinking for yourself.

The houses say where it happens. With your Uranus in the 11th, the opposition comes from your 5th ([Neptune transiting the 5th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-5th-house/)): the room you take among friends and groups faces what you create and love for its own sake. Houses rest on the birth time, and I don't read this transit, or any other, without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Neptune stays opposite your Uranus for some two and a half years, twenty-two months or so when the pass is short. Three exact hits are usual, five come in roughly one pass in four ([Neptune retrograde](/astrology/neptune-retrograde/)), and nobody meets it twice.

The age is what sets it apart. In the site's count it came within orb at 19 for someone born at the start of 1935, at 29 for 1945, 40 for 1955, 52 for 1965 and 63 for 1975; born at the start of 1985, at 72½. From the mid-1990s on the wait runs past 80, which is why about 1 person in 5 born between 1930 and 2009 never meets it. The angle between the two planets at birth explains the spread: born under their trine, from 1936 to 1946, you met it in your twenties; under the square of the 1950s, between about 35 and 44; under the sextile of the 1960s, in your fifties. Born under their conjunction, from 1990 to 1996, you'd meet it in your late seventies or after 80, a few years either side of [transiting Neptune opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-neptune/). The natal [Uranus opposite Neptune](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune/) is missing from all these birth years; this transit is how its polarity reaches them.

Neptune meets the other steps first, whichever still lie ahead of it at birth: [transiting Neptune sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-uranus/), [transiting Neptune square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-uranus/), [transiting Neptune trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-uranus/). [Transiting Neptune conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-uranus/) reaches only births of the 1990s and 2000s, in childhood.

Neptune is in Aries until March 2039 ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)), so these are the years of people born with Uranus in Libra, between late 1968 and 1975, who meet it between about 55 and 63.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name one freedom you'd keep even inside a cause you love. Knowing it in advance lets you join something without disappearing into it.

Lend your independence to an ideal instead of guarding it from every ideal: an experiment run for a community, a reform that serves a vision you share.

Compare a group's picture of the future with what it does week by week. Under this opposition the vision is easy to see, and the plain arrangements that carry it deserve the same attention.
