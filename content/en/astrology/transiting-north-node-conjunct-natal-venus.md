Once in each round of the nodes, the point of growth passes over the degree of your Venus: what you value, what you enjoy, the way you draw close to people and things. For a month or so, the South Node holds the degree opposite, where your familiar tastes live.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) evaluates. It decides what is worth having, what is beautiful, who and what you are drawn to, and how giving and receiving balance out. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the less practised direction, and a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) joins the two into a single process. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), developing and appreciating become the same act: you grow by learning to value something you've overlooked.

It tends to show as a change of taste or of measure. A pleasure you never allowed yourself, a kind of person you usually don't notice, a generosity that runs both ways for once, a standard of beauty other than the one you were taught. Venus isn't romance, and the transit isn't a meeting on the calendar; it is your sense of value being asked to widen. Opposite, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) rests on the preferences you could list without thinking, and they remain good company.

With [Venus in the 2nd house](/astrology/venus-in-2nd-house/) in your chart, the node meets it during its time in your house of resources ([North Node transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-2nd-house/)), and the question becomes what you are worth to yourself: the skills you price too low, the things you keep for the wrong reasons. It isn't a forecast of money gained or lost, and I don't read charts for finance ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). Without your birth time, there is no 2nd house to speak of ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My forecast counts a transit from 1½° before exact until 1° after; the true node is usually inside that span for about a month, roughly the time Venus itself takes to cross a sign of the sky. Passes vary with the Sun. A tenth are done in three and a half weeks; those that overlap an eclipse season, when the node barely moves, run to three and a half months or more, and roughly one pass in twenty-five has three exact hits. The summer of 2027 has one: with Venus near 11° or 12° Aquarius, the node sits on it from June to September, and the total solar eclipse of 2 August, at 9°55′ Leo, falls opposite ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Four or five such months come by 80, one in every round. From here the node works its way round your chart: the sextiles, squares and trines ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-venus/), [Transiting North Node square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-venus/), [Transiting North Node trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-venus/)) come in pairs on either side of [Transiting North Node opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-venus/), which falls about nine years on. If [Venus conjunct North Node](/astrology/venus-conjunct-north-node/) is part of your chart, every nodal return lays the node back on your Venus.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Spend an hour on something you've always called a waste of time: a concert, a class, flowers on a weekday. Notice whether it was.

Ask for what you'd like in an exchange, instead of accepting whatever is offered.

Name one thing you love because you were taught to, and one you love in spite of it. The second points the node's way.
