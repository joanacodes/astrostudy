Some people never need to be told that your bluntness is a kind of honesty, or that your odd appetite is just taste: they already think so. Venus trine Lilith in synastry puts one person's Venus about 120° from the other's Black Moon Lilith, usually in one element, and the welcome tends to come without a word.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a person that values and chooses: attraction, preference, the eye for what's good, and fairness in what's given and returned. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is a calculated point, the Moon's apogee, whose meaning differs between traditions; I read it as what someone learned to keep out of view, their instinct for autonomy and their own relation to power. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets two functions run together easily, neither losing its identity. In [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), each of you carries one of them, so the ease runs between you.

For the one whose Lilith it is, there's no case to make. With the Venus in Aries and the Lilith in Sagittarius, both fire signs, a taste for the direct and the brave meets a refusal to believe what one is told, and the Venus person likes that refusal on sight. You can disagree with everyone else in the room and look across to find them pleased.

For the one whose Venus it is, the other's autonomy reads as good taste rather than as trouble. Their refusals don't threaten your sense of what's lovely; often they sharpen it. You may like yourself a little better in their company, because your own preferences feel less in need of defending.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Ease isn't inevitability, and welcome can hide two things. The first is a real no. When the Lilith person's refusals are usually enjoyed, one that's meant seriously, aimed at the Venus person or at something they love, can be taken for style and pass unheard.

The second is a shelter. A pair like this can become the one place the hidden side lives, while it stays folded away at work, with family, everywhere else. The Lilith person never has to practise showing it where it isn't welcome; the Venus person can mistake being the exception for having changed anything.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Check the no. If the Lilith is yours, say plainly when a refusal isn't a joke; if the Venus is yours, ask when you're not sure, and believe the answer. Then take the ease outside: someone who already likes your untamed side is good company the first time you show it somewhere harder, at a family table or in a meeting. Couples, adult siblings and long-standing colleagues all know this trine as the relationship where nobody has to tidy themselves first.

The trine and the square turn up equally often: each joins about 1 pair of people in 9, either way round, at up to 5.1° from exact, with mean Lilith. Mean Lilith creeps forward about 40° a year, so the trine is fixed by the two birth dates and placed in each life by the two birth times, through the houses; I don't begin without both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Like every synastry, it's read with both people's consent ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

One chart can hold this angle too, as [Venus trine Lilith](/astrology/venus-trine-lilith/), and there a single person's taste and autonomy simply get on. Set at its four other angles across two charts, the same pair behaves like this:

- **[Venus conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** no distance at all between the liking and what was hidden, so it's closer and less relaxed.
- **[Venus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** a lighter version that comes as an invitation, between elements that get on.
- **[Venus square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-lilith-synastry/):** taste asks the untamed side to soften, and the welcome has to be negotiated.
- **[Venus opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** the two face each other, and liking the other's freedom comes with a pull to tame it.
