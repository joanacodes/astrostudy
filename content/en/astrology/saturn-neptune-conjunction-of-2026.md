On 20 February 2026, at 18:29 UT, Saturn and Neptune met at 0°45′ Aries, within a degree of the start of the zodiac. They meet about once every 36 years, and this time they did it on the first degree of the first sign, in a single pass.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) goes round the zodiac in about 29½ years and [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) in about 165, so Saturn catches Neptune up about every 36 years. Both had only just changed sign: Neptune entered Aries on 26 January 2026 and Saturn on 14 February, six days before they met. Some meetings come in threes, when a retrograde loop takes Saturn back over Neptune; this one was a single exact [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/).

In the dates this site keeps, here are their meetings from 1800 to 2100:

- **1 December 1809:** 6°40′ Sagittarius.
- **1846:** three times in Aquarius, between 27°18′ and 25°42′, the year Neptune was discovered.
- **12 May 1882:** 16°28′ Taurus.
- **1 August 1917:** 4°45′ Leo.
- **1952–53:** three times in Libra, between 22°46′ and 21°12′.
- **1989:** three times in Capricorn, 3 March, 24 June and 13 November, between 11°54′ and 10°21′.
- **20 February 2026:** 0°45′ Aries.
- **Next:** 7 June 2061 at 20°23′ Gemini, then 27 August 2096 at 8°09′ Virgo.

Each meeting falls about 80° on from the one before, and the signs follow a pattern: fire, air, earth, three times over from 1809 to 2096, with no water sign at all.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

In a birth chart, the meeting matters where its degree lands. A planet or angle within a few degrees of 0° Aries received transiting Saturn and Neptune by conjunction in the same stretch; near 0° Libra, by opposition; near 0° Cancer or 0° Capricorn, by square. Saturn's transits to one point usually last about nine months with the orbs I use for timing, Neptune's about two and a half years ([transits](/astrology/transits/)), so for those charts the two overlapped. The Sun sits on those degrees for about a day at each equinox and solstice, around 21 March, 21 June, 23 September and 22 December, so people born on those days are among the ones it reached.

For everyone else, it marks a house: wherever the first degrees of Aries fall in your chart, which needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Saturn stays in Aries until April 2028 and Neptune until 2039 ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)), so that house hosts both for a while.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In the system I built for my readings, Saturn establishes structure and responsibility, and Neptune enables imagination and compassion and loosens boundaries that are no longer needed. A conjunction fuses two functions into one process. Read in a chart it touches, this one asks for an ideal to be given a form you can live in, and for a form that has hardened to remember what it was for. Neither planet defeats the other.

The degree adds a question. Some astrologers read 0° Aries, the [Aries Point](/astrology/aries-point/), as a link between a personal chart and the wider world; I hold that as a question to try on, not a finding. The sky around it had already turned: Pluto settled into Aquarius in November 2024 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), and Uranus entered Gemini two months after this meeting, on 26 April 2026.

Saturn and Neptune meet far less often than Jupiter and Saturn, every 36 years rather than every 20 ([Jupiter–Saturn great conjunctions](/astrology/jupiter-saturn-great-conjunctions/)). Those born in 1952–53 or 1989 carry the conjunction in their own charts ([Saturn conjunct Neptune](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-neptune/)). A collective cycle like this is weather, not a forecast: it predicts no event, and it says nothing about the character of anyone born under it ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The 2026 conjunction will change the world.”** I read it in personal charts, as a season for the points it touches. No transit predicts an event.
- **“It touched everyone the same way.”** Only charts with points near the cardinal degrees met it head on. For the rest, it marks a house.
- **“It lasted all year.”** It was exact once, on 20 February 2026. Its transits to natal points last as long as Saturn's and Neptune's do: months for Saturn, years for Neptune.
- **“Neptune dissolves what Saturn builds.”** A conjunction joins two functions. Here, the dream and the structure have to work together.
