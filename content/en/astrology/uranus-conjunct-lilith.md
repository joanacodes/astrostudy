Uranus conjunct Lilith joins the planet of difference to the point that marks what you learned to hide, and where you refuse to be tamed. Mean Lilith overtakes Uranus roughly once a decade, so this is a personal aspect, shared only with people born within a few months of you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables differentiation, liberation and new ways of doing things. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's mean apogee, is read by many astrologers, and by me, as instinctive autonomy: the part of you that was called too much, too loud or too strange, and the boundaries you keep around it. Readings of Lilith vary a good deal between traditions; this one is mine. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) binds two functions into one process while each keeps its identity.

Here, being different and being untamed are hard to separate. What makes you original may be the very thing you once kept out of sight, and the urge to live your own way carries an instinctive refusal with it: of rules that don't fit, of roles you didn't choose. That isn't rebellion for its own sake, which Uranus isn't, nor anything to judge, which Lilith isn't. Lived well, it's a self that stops editing itself, and an originality rooted in instinct rather than in fashion.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 30 born between 1930 and 2009 has Uranus within 6° of mean Lilith. A single meeting point explains the figure: the sextile, square and trine each have two points in the circle, and are about twice as common. Lilith is much the faster, about nine months in a sign against Uranus's seven years; mean Lilith moves forward at a steady pace, overtakes Uranus roughly once a decade and stays within orb for a few months. True Lilith can sit tens of degrees from the mean, so a chart that shows another Lilith may tell a different story ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

The two usually share a sign, which colours both: in Aquarius, a difference that lives in ideas and in groups; in Taurus, one that shows in what you refuse to give up. The house shows where in life the untamed originality lives, and that needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A contact with your Sun, Moon or an angle makes it hard to miss.

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus sextile Lilith](/astrology/uranus-sextile-lilith/):** an opening for the untamed part, through new ideas or new company.
- **[Uranus square Lilith](/astrology/uranus-square-lilith/):** freedom and the hidden part in friction, asking for a new arrangement.
- **[Uranus trine Lilith](/astrology/uranus-trine-lilith/):** being yourself, unedited, comes easily.
- **[Uranus opposite Lilith](/astrology/uranus-opposite-lilith/):** the untamed part met across the chart, often in someone else.

With [Uranus conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-lilith-synastry/), one person's urge to differ lands on the other's untamed side, which can feel freeing or unsettling. I read it in both directions, because the one who unsettles and the one unsettled rarely feel it the same way.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Transiting Lilith reaches natal Uranus about every 8 years and 10 months, and stays within a degree or two for a couple of weeks. Transiting Uranus reaches natal Lilith at most once in a life, since it takes about 84 years to go round, and lingers for months, often in three passes.

Born with the conjunction, you see it formed again at each of Lilith's returns to its birth place, around 9, 18, 27, 35, 44 and 53. Weather, not a forecast: short seasons when an old refusal and an urge to live differently surface together, and ask what still needs hiding.
