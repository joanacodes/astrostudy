When the North Node backs into your 4th house, the South Node backs into your 10th, and the vertical line of your chart, from roots to reputation, gets its turn. For around twenty months the pull is downwards and inwards: home, family, the self nobody sees at work.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is where practice pays off, and the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home and roots: where you come from, what you build in order to belong, the private self underneath. The growth here can be very plain: cooking more often, calling a parent or a sibling, deciding what home means now, letting yourself rest in it. For someone used to measuring days by output, those can be the hard part.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) at the top of the chart, in your 10th, describes what's familiar: ambition, being useful in public, the role people know you by. You don't have to give any of it up. These months simply ask whether that role has been carrying all of your weight.

With your Moon in the 4th, ruler of Cancer, the house's natural sign, the month the node spends over it often brings old family feeling to the surface, to be looked at kindly. I read nothing about a relative's health or future in your chart: their lives are their own.

Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), this one stirs what your chart already says about home. It doesn't move you house or settle anything for you, and I don't read it as a destiny calling you back.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the true node's stays in the 4th last about 20 months in the middle, longer than the year and a half of the average house, and they rarely stray far: most fall between about a year and 2 years, so even the 4th's short stays run a month past most houses'. At New York's latitude the middle is about 19 months and the range about 13 months to just under 2 years.

I time it with the true node and keep a wobble back over the cusp within 120 days inside the same stay. I look at the mean node as well ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), a degree or two away, and tell you where it differs.

Your IC sits where your birth time puts it, and without that time there's no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The North Node enters the 4th from your 5th ([North Node transiting the 5th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-5th-house/)) and leaves across the IC for your 3rd ([North Node transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-3rd-house/)), while the South Node crosses your Midheaven ([transiting North Node opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-midheaven/)).

Your Midheaven's sign dates that last crossing from the other end, since the South Node reaches it while in that sign. The South Node is in Leo from July 2026 to March 2028, so with a Leo Midheaven the stay ends in that window. Then come Cancer to September 2029, Gemini to March 2031, Taurus to December 2032, Aries to June 2034, Pisces to November 2035, Aquarius to May 2037 and Capricorn to February 2039, while the North Node crosses Cancer, the 4th's natural sign. A Virgo Midheaven had its turn between January 2025 and July 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give home an hour a week that isn't upkeep: a meal, a call, a corner made comfortable.

Ask where you feel you belong, and let the answer be smaller than you expected.

Keep doing good work, but stop letting it speak for all of you. The South Node in the 10th has earned its competence; it doesn't need your whole evening as well.
