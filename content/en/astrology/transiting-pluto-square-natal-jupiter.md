Pluto square your Jupiter puts your convictions under pressure. For two to three years a 90° angle sets the planet of depth against what you believe and the way you want to grow, and plans or principles that had grown too big, or too easy, are tested until what is sound remains.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) eliminates what has become obsolete so that something sounder can be built. Your [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands, believes and plans for growth. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates them through friction that forces adaptation, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) growth meets resistance: an ambition that outgrew its foundations, a belief that can't explain what is happening, a principle that turns out to cost more than you thought.

When one belief cracks, it is tempting to throw them all out. Elimination isn't regeneration, though, and what this square tends to leave is fewer convictions, better tested, rather than none. Expansion isn't excess, and belief isn't dogma; the square finds the places where one has quietly turned into the other.

[Jupiter square Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-square-pluto/), born in certain months of certain years, makes this friction part of a whole year group's outlook; the transit is the personal version, timed to your own Jupiter.

With Jupiter in the 5th and Pluto crossing the 2nd ([Pluto transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-2nd-house/)), what you would like to grow in play and creative work rubs against a reckoning of what you truly value; it forecasts no money, gained or lost. Without the birth time there is no 5th or 2nd house to speak of: no birth time, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Between 1½° before exact and 1° after, the orbs of my timing, the square lasts about two years and eight months, and about twenty-two months in the shortest passes. About three passes in five bring three exact hits; the rest bring five, over about three years and seven months.

Because Jupiter moves on by about a sign a year, the ages spread widely. The first square came at about 11 for a birth on 1 January 2005, about 14 for 1935, 25 for 1975, 27 for 1955, 51 for 1995, 61 for 1945 and 67 for 1965; for 1 January 1985, not by 80. Across all births from 1930 to 2009 it came at about 36 in the middle, and about seven people in eight meet one of the two squares by 80.

Each birth date meets Pluto's contacts with Jupiter in its own order. Someone born on 1 January 1955 met the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-jupiter/)) at about 14, this square at about 27, the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-jupiter/)) at 38 and the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-jupiter/)) at 65, and never the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-jupiter/)); someone born on 1 January 1985 met the sextile at about 6 and the conjunction at 33, and never this square.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it squares Jupiters in Taurus and Scorpio: most births from March 1976 to April 1977, March 1988 to March 1989 or June 1999 to June 2000, and births from November 1981 to December 1982, November 1993 to December 1994 or October 2005 to November 2006, among others.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Prune one plan to the part you can actually build, and build that part well.

Seek out a teacher, a book or a friend who disagrees with you. A conviction that has met its best opponent is worth keeping.

Keep the principle and drop the slogan. Under this square, the words you repeat most are the first to be tested.
