An earth pair, but with the weight placed differently from most: your Moon is in the sign of its exaltation, while your Sun works without any dignity at all. The Moon was already waning when you arrived, a few days past full, and I read the pair as a careful self standing on unusually steady feelings, with practical knowledge it wants to hand on.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Both lights are in [earth signs](/astrology/earth-signs/), with Taurus coming four signs before Virgo, so the pair forms a trine by sign: what feels solid to one tends to feel solid to the other. Mutable Virgo and fixed Taurus differ in pace, though. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) is mutable and keeps refining, always after the better method; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) steadies itself through touch, habit and the comforts it already knows. The Moon gives the Sun a floor to stand on, and the Sun gives the Moon's routines a reason to improve.

Tradition is generous to your Moon and silent about your Sun. Taurus is the Moon's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), so your need for safety and the sign's steadiness point the same way, while Virgo gives the Sun no dignity. The rulers cross: [Venus](/astrology/venus/), ruler of your Moon's sign, has its fall in Virgo, your Sun's sign, where [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of Virgo, is exalted. I read that as a self that thinks clearly and has to learn to enjoy, and comforts that work best when they're simple.

In the third week of the lunar month or just after it, 17 to 22 days from the new moon, the Moon had fallen 91° to 149° behind the Sun and was rising later each night. About seven births in eight are [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) births and the earliest are [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) ones; near 120° behind, the chart holds a waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). In a disseminating birth I see knowledge that doesn't stay private for long, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains where that comes from.

About one person in 142 has these lights. On a day when the Moon was moving out of Aries or on into Gemini, only the hour tells this page from the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-aries/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-gemini/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign depends on the same hour, and a pair this practical deserves a chart that has been properly set up before anyone builds on it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love steadily and practically: the car checked before a long drive, the favourite dish remembered, the evening kept free. The Taurus Moon wants touch, constancy and a partner who turns up; the Virgo Sun shows care by noticing what needs doing. Partners usually feel safe with you. The thing to practise is enjoying an imperfect evening without fixing it, since Venus, ruler of your Moon, is least at ease in the Sun's sign: let the pleasure be the point.

## At work {#at-work}

Craft and teaching suit you, often in that order: learning to do something very well, then showing others how. The Sun brings precision and the Moon patience, so you can stay with a slow, detailed job long after others have lost interest. Rushed work and constant change sit badly with both lights. Places that value doing things properly, and give you time to pass on what you know, tend to keep you for years.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Doubled earth can grow a life that is safe and a little airless. The Taurus Moon holds on to routines that work; the Virgo Sun keeps improving them, so evenings fill with upkeep, and worry gets soothed by tidying or by the same comfort every night. Change, when it comes, may feel like a threat to both lights at once. What helps is deliberate novelty in small doses, a new walk, a new recipe, and pleasures taken without first earning them.
