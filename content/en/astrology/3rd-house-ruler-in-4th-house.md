Conversations find their way home. The planet of your everyday words lives in the house of roots, and the way you think carries an accent from where you come from.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) covers how you think, talk and learn from day to day, and the people close by: siblings, cousins, neighbours. The planet ruling that cusp takes all of it one house further down, into [the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) at the bottom of the chart, where home, family, belonging and the private self live ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

I read it first as a mind shaped at home. The words used round the kitchen table, the way arguments went, what was explained and what was never mentioned tend to become the first frame you think in, and you may catch them in your own sentences long after you've moved out. Many people with this ruler like to think, read, write or work from home, and their best conversations often happen behind their own front door. As an adult you get to sort the family vocabulary: what you keep, what you translate, what you put down.

Brothers and sisters belong to the 3rd, so this ruler can tie them into what home means to you. For some that's a shared past retold every time you meet; for others, a cousin or a neighbour who became family. It describes your side of those ties as they feel now, not where anyone will live or who anyone is; each of them has a chart of their own.

The 4th is an angular house, beginning at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart, so the ruler of a quiet cadent house gains weight here, though it works out of sight. Now and then the same sign sits on both cusps, because the 3rd is narrower than a sign; then one planet rules both houses, and in the 4th it's also the ruler of the 4th at home ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/), [Ruler of the 4th in the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)). Talk and roots answer to a single voice.

Each ruler gives home's conversation its own accent. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 3rd from the 4th, tends to fill the house with news, lists and opinions; the Moon, ruling a Cancer 3rd, sits in the house of its own natural sign, and memory does much of the talking. Scorpio on the 3rd cusp brings in two rulers, Mars and Pluto, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 4th can make home the place where you think things through out loud, while Pluto works from its own house.

Placing the line between the 3rd and the 4th takes the birth time: the IC moves on average about a degree every four minutes ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Just before it, the ruler stays among the conversations it rules ([Ruler of the 3rd in the 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)); a little further along the zodiac, past the 5th cusp, words turn to play and writing for pleasure ([Ruler of the 3rd in the 5th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)).
