With this person, nobody asks you to explain why you do things your own way. In a Sun trine Uranus contact, one person's Uranus and the other's Sun stand a third of the zodiac apart, often in one element, and originality passes between the two as easily as conversation.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the organising centre of a person, the self they're becoming. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and liberates: originality, autonomy, the experiment that finds a better way. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets the two run together, each reinforcing the other without losing its own character. In [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) like this one, the two functions sit in different people and still strengthen each other.

With the Sun, you feel free to be yourself, including the parts that don't fit a pattern. Your unusual choices meet curiosity rather than alarm, and you can change your mind without a scene. Your direction comes out of the friendship refreshed rather than threatened.

With Uranus, you're accepted without having to fight for room. Your independence isn't read as rejection, and your ideas seem to fit what the other is already doing. Being different and belonging stop being opposites.

The shared element gives the freedom its form: in fire, the freedom to act on impulse; in earth, practical inventions; in air, independence of mind; in water, permission to feel in a new way.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

So much room can leave commitments vague. Two people this good at giving each other space can forget to meet in it, and autonomy, which isn't isolation, can drift that way when nobody plans anything together. Novelty can turn into a habit too: a stream of new projects, none of them finished.

The ease can also hide how much each relies on the other's tolerance. Because no one objects, no one asks whether this is still a choice or just the way things have always been done.

Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, which makes its position a fact about the Uranus person's whole cohort. The trine with this particular Sun is a fact about one birthday, and so about this pair alone.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use the freedom for something shared. A couple can plan an adventure that needs both of them; old friends, a project with a finish date; a parent and a grown child, the child's new direction, backed without fuss; colleagues, an experiment with a deadline and a review.

Set beside the pair's other angles:

- **[Sun conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus-synastry/):** the urge to renew sits right on one identity, and the two can blur.
- **[Sun sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus-synastry/):** the same freshness arrives as a suggestion, and needs taking up.
- **[Sun square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-uranus-synastry/):** change cuts across a plan and has to be argued for.
- **[Sun opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus-synastry/):** the freedom stands across from the Sun person, and the pair has to find a balance.

About 1 pair of people in 7 has this trine, one way round or the other, within 6.8°. In a day the Sun moves about a degree, so the trine seldom hangs on the hour, but the houses it falls in, which say what area of each life the freedom serves, are set by the time; I read it with both birth times or not at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In one chart, [Sun trine Uranus](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus/) lets a person be both themselves and different without strain. Between two, the ease comes from the company you keep, and like every synastry I take on, I read it only with the knowledge and agreement of both people ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
