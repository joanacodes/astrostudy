The North Node reaches your 11th house after your 12th, travelling backwards as it always does, and spends about a year and a half there while the South Node crosses your 5th. Between your own spotlight and the group around you, these months turn towards the group.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows growth that comes with practice. The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is friends, groups and hopes: the people you build a future with, and the future you'd like to build. With the node here, the practice is belonging by choice: joining something, offering a skill to a shared aim, keeping up the friendships that point forward.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in your 5th knows the solo performance: your own creations, your own pleasure, being the one people watch. It's a real gift. The stretch is to let it serve something bigger than one stage.

Tradition called the 11th the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy there. Aquarius, its natural sign, has two rulers, Saturn, the traditional one, and Uranus, the modern one, and I read both. With either in your natal 11th, the node's month over it tends to show which groups are really yours.

A [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this one doesn't bring friends by itself or decide which circle will take you in; it brings out what your chart already holds about community.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

This is the node's most ordinary house. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, its stays in the 11th match the all-house figures exactly: about a year and a half in the middle, most between about 11 months and 2 years and 4 months. At New York's latitude they match again: a year and a half, from about a year to about 2 years.

Those stays are counted with the true node, and a wobble back over the cusp is forgiven if the node returns within 120 days. I look at the mean node beside it ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), and when the two differ near a cusp, I give both dates.

The cusps of your 11th come from your birth time, and so does every date here ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node enters from your 12th ([North Node transiting the 12th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-12th-house/)) and leaves for your 10th ([North Node transiting the 10th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-10th-house/)).

The sign on your 12th cusp dates the start, since the node crosses that cusp on the way in. Right now the North Node is in Aquarius, the 11th's natural sign, with the South Node in Leo, the 5th's, from July 2026 to March 2028: with Aquarius on your 12th cusp, the stay begins in that window. With Capricorn there, it begins between March 2028 and September 2029; with Sagittarius, by March 2031; and so on, one sign back about every eighteen months: Scorpio by December 2032, Libra by June 2034, Virgo by November 2035, Leo by May 2037 and Cancer by February 2039. A Pisces 12th cusp was crossed between January 2025 and July 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Join one group whose aim you'd work for even without credit.

Say yes to the friend who's building something, and bring what you're good at. If a group asks more than it gives, leave it kindly: the North Node points to belonging, not to obligation.

Keep a little of the 5th's spotlight for yourself. The South Node's joy makes you better company, not worse.
