Compassion for nearly everyone, trust for very few: that is a Pisces Sun with a Scorpio Moon in one line. Both lights are in water, the Sun in the sign that takes the whole sea in, the Moon in the one that keeps its depths to itself. You arrived a few days after a full moon, as its light began to shrink.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and fixed water: a trine by sign, the Moon four signs behind the Sun, and feeling moves easily between them. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) forms a self around devotion and imagination, and tends to let people in before it knows them; the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) feels safe with the few it has tested over time, and keeps what it feels at depth. You may be the person strangers confide in, and still take years to tell your closest friend what once hurt you.

Each sign has two rulers, and I read both of each. For Pisces, [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), in modern tropical astrology, shows where you open to images and to other people, and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), in the tradition, where that openness finds a purpose. For Scorpio, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) shows where your feelings go deepest and come back changed, and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) where they stand guard over what you love. Neptune and Pluto move so slowly that a whole generation shares their signs, so it is their houses, which need your birth time, that make them yours. Tradition calls Scorpio the Moon's [fall](/astrology/fall/), and I read the old word kindly: feelings that protect themselves and trust slowly, not a weaker heart. Your Sun gets no dignity from Pisces either; what the two lights share instead is their element.

Your Moon had passed full by 31° to 89°, two and a half to about seven days before your birth, and that full moon was usually a [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/). Roughly seven of these births in eight fall in the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) phase; the earliest still count as [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) births. Sixty degrees past full, inside that range, the lights make an exact waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). The disseminating phase, as I read it, is about passing on what you have understood, and a Scorpio Moon has usually understood something the hard way. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) tells the two kinds of birth apart.

This pair comes up in about 1 person in 143. The Moon spends only two and a half days or so in Scorpio, so if it arrived from Libra or left for Sagittarius on your birthday, only the hour tells this page from the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-libra/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-sagittarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same hour gives the rising sign, which sketches the chart's outline with the two lights, and the houses of all four rulers. A reading would have no ground without them, which is why I wait for the time.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with devotion and, once you've decided, with complete loyalty. The Pisces Sun sees the best in a partner and forgives quickly; the Scorpio Moon needs honesty, and a bond strong enough to hold the whole truth, hard parts included. The risk is the silent test: watching to see whether someone proves trustworthy instead of telling them what you need. Say it plainly. A partner can only pass a test they know about.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that goes below the surface for someone else's sake suits you: therapy and counselling, research, investigation, crisis support, archives, music or writing that doesn't look away. The Sun brings compassion and imagination; the Moon brings stamina, discretion and an ear for what isn't being said. Places that run on gossip or forced openness wear you down. You do your best where confidences are kept.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Water takes in, and water keeps. The Pisces Sun absorbs other people's pain; the Scorpio Moon can hold it, unspoken, long after everyone else has moved on, so that a hurt only shows once it has grown too big to hide. What helps is a regular way of letting things out before they settle: one trusted person who hears everything, a notebook nobody reads, a swim if water steadies you. Forgiving someone and trusting them again are two different jobs, and you're allowed to do them at different speeds.
