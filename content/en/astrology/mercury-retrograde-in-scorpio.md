No sign has more of Mercury’s retrogrades in the dates I use than Scorpio: nineteen between 2000 and 2040, mostly three years in a row every six or seven years. The latest ended on 13 November 2026, and the next begins almost on the degree where it stopped.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) is fixed water, with two rulers, Mars in the tradition and Pluto for modern astrologers; I read both. Mercury there goes looking under the surface and doesn’t settle for the first answer. When [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) reaches it, the planet appears from Earth to stop and burrow back through the sign, and the digging goes over ground already turned: the question you asked once and never had answered, the conversation you’ve been circling, the research you put down. Mercury holds no dignity in Scorpio, and the review it brings here is thorough, more interested in what’s true than in what’s comfortable.

The most recent ran from 24 October to 13 November 2026, wholly inside Scorpio, from 20°58′ back to 5°02′. The next begins on 7 October 2027 at 4°55′ Scorpio, barely a tenth of a degree from where the 2026 one ended, and backs into Libra ([Mercury retrograde in Libra](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-libra/)) to turn direct at 19°18′ on 28 October. The year before, from 9 to 29 November 2025, Mercury had come in from Sagittarius ([Mercury retrograde in Sagittarius](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-sagittarius/)), from 6°51′ Sagittarius to 20°42′ Scorpio. Each lasts about 20 or 21 days, among the shortest of the year, and covers about 16°, among the widest.

Born during one of these weeks, you carry Mercury retrograde in Scorpio in your own chart, a mind that turns its questions over at depth before asking them aloud, and I read it on its own terms ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house these weeks fall in depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): the houses turn with the hours, and without the time I don’t read a chart at all. In Placidus, the house system I use, 16° of retrograde can cross a cusp; and because the 2026 and 2027 retrogrades nearly meet around 5° Scorpio, a point of your chart there meets a station in both years.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/scorpio-rising/):** Scorpio rising: your watchful presence; what people sense in you and what you actually say, brought closer together.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you value most deeply; the reasons behind it, looked at without flinching.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/scorpio-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** words with weight; a conversation that stopped short, picked up at depth.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a well-guarded home; family stories with more to them, told again.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/):** love taken seriously and creative work that goes to honest places; a feeling you held back, put into words.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work you throw yourself into; a problem traced back to its root.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio on the Descendant, with Taurus rising: an intense partnership; the question you’ve both stepped around, asked.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/):** Scorpio in its natural house: trust, intimacy and what’s shared, read for what they are and never for anyone’s death ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
9. **[9th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs tested to the bottom; a conviction re-examined until you know why you hold it.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/):** work where serious matters are handled; a professional question looked into more deeply.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a few loyal friends; a friendship’s unspoken rules, talked about at last.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/scorpio-on-12th-house-cusp/):** feelings worked through alone; old thoughts brought to the surface before you share them.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask the question once more, plainly, and give the answer time to come: Scorpio’s way is depth, and one honest conversation can do more than several careful ones. Look at both rulers, the houses of your natal Mars and Pluto, to see where the review tends to lead.

And if what comes up is heavy, you don’t have to dig alone: these weeks describe a season of looking, and looking is easier with company.
