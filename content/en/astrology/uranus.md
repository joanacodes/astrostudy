Uranus was the first planet found with a telescope, by William Herschel in 1781, and astrology reads it as the planet of change and freedom. In a chart it shows where you need room: the part of you that wakes up when something new arrives, and grows restless when life gets too tight.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Uranus moves so slowly that its sign is shared by everyone born over about seven years, which makes it one of the [generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/). So I don't read your Uranus sign as yours alone. I read where it lands in your chart, and what it touches:

- **Its sign** describes what a whole generation wants to change, and how.
- **Its house** shows where, in your own life, you need room to do things your way — and where change tends to arrive without warning.
- **Its aspects** to your Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus and Mars are what make it personal: the closer the aspect, the louder Uranus speaks.

Uranus is read against [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional ruler of [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/): Saturn keeps the shape, Uranus breaks it open, and most lives need a little of both. With [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), it's one of the three planets beyond Saturn that the astrologers of antiquity never knew. Like them, it spends about five months of every year retrograde, so many people are born with [Uranus retrograde](/astrology/uranus-retrograde/). I read it as change that brews inside before it shows: [a rhythm, not a delay](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

Its aspects are where it gets personal. With the [Sun conjunct Uranus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus/), being different is part of who you are rather than a phase. A [Moon square Uranus](/astrology/moon-square-uranus/) often describes feelings that change fast, and a need for space even with the people you love. A [Venus opposite Uranus](/astrology/venus-opposite-uranus/) wants closeness and freedom at once, and learns to ask for both. And a [Mercury trine Uranus](/astrology/mercury-trine-uranus/) thinks in leaps — and usually lands.

## Uranus through the signs {#through-the-signs}

Uranus stays about seven years in each sign, so everyone born in the same stretch shares it: read the sign as the mark of a generation, and look to the house and the aspects for what's yours. In plain words:

- **[Uranus in Aries](/astrology/uranus-in-aries/):** change by going first; freedom means the right to start again.
- **[Uranus in Taurus](/astrology/uranus-in-taurus/):** change in what felt solid: possessions, food, land, what we value.
- **[Uranus in Gemini](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini/):** new ways of talking, learning and keeping in touch.
- **[Uranus in Cancer](/astrology/uranus-in-cancer/):** new ideas of home and family, and of who counts as family.
- **[Uranus in Leo](/astrology/uranus-in-leo/):** the freedom to create and to stand out, loudly.
- **[Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/uranus-in-virgo/):** change through work, method and the tools of everyday life.
- **[Uranus in Libra](/astrology/uranus-in-libra/):** new rules for partnership and fairness, and new ways of being together.
- **[Uranus in Scorpio](/astrology/uranus-in-scorpio/):** taboos broken around intimacy, power and what's hidden.
- **[Uranus in Sagittarius](/astrology/uranus-in-sagittarius/):** freedom of belief, of thought and of movement.
- **[Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/uranus-in-capricorn/):** structures and institutions reshaped, often from the inside.
- **[Uranus in Aquarius](/astrology/uranus-in-aquarius/):** Uranus in its own sign: networks, groups and ideas ahead of their time.
- **[Uranus in Pisces](/astrology/uranus-in-pisces/):** change through imagination, art and compassion; old borders blur.

## Uranus in the houses {#in-the-houses}

The house is where Uranus becomes yours, and it needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/):

1. **[1st house](/astrology/uranus-in-1st-house/):** you come across as unusual, and you're at your best when you stop apologising for it.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/uranus-in-2nd-house/):** your own idea of security and worth; values that don't follow the usual rules.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/uranus-in-3rd-house/):** a quick, original mind; you learn by jumping ahead.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/uranus-in-4th-house/):** a home life that doesn't fit the usual mould; you need a home with room to breathe.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/uranus-in-5th-house/):** creativity and romance that need surprise; you play by your own rules.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/uranus-in-6th-house/):** you need freedom in your daily work; routines work best when you invent them.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/uranus-in-7th-house/):** you need partners who give you room, and who don't mind you giving them the same.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/uranus-in-8th-house/):** closeness and trust on your own terms; deep bonds that still need air.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/uranus-in-9th-house/):** beliefs you work out for yourself; a mind that travels.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/uranus-in-10th-house/):** a working life off the usual path; you do best where you're free to change things.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/uranus-in-11th-house/):** at home in the house of Aquarius: friends and causes that share your idea of the future.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/uranus-in-12th-house/):** a quiet independence; insights that arrive when you're alone.

## Uranus in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), I don't read the sign of Uranus at all: people close in age share it. I read where one person's Uranus falls in the other's chart, and what it touches. [Their Uranus in your 7th house](/astrology/uranus-in-7th-house-synastry/) can make a relationship feel exciting and unpredictable at once. [Venus conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) is often described as a spark: instant attraction, and a need for both people to keep some freedom. I read these contacts both ways, because the person whose Uranus it is often feels freer, while the other feels more stirred up. They wake people up; they last when the surprise suits you both, and when you stay once the novelty wears off.

## Transits and the Uranus opposition {#uranus-opposition}

Uranus takes about 84 years to go round the zodiac, and about seven to cross each sign. A quarter of the way round, it makes a [square](/astrology/square/) to the place it held at your birth; halfway, at about 42, it stands opposite it. That's the **[Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/)** — in the language of transits, [transiting Uranus opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-uranus/). Everyone meets it at roughly the same age, and it's often tied to the restlessness so many people describe around midlife.

In readings, it looks less like a crisis than a wake-up: what you've outgrown starts to feel too tight, and something you set aside asks for room. As Uranus moves through a house it asks for change there, for years at a time: [Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/) asks for more room in partnership; [Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/), in work. That's weather, not a forecast: it describes the wind, not where you'll land.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

I checked Uranus like every other placement in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) — its sign and its house, in my own chart and then in other people's, with their consent. It wasn't one of the three that held far more often: those were [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), which held 75 to 90% of the time, while across everything I checked, about a third held. With a generational planet there's one more reason for care, since its sign says more about your generation than about you. So I lean on its house and its aspects, I hold it lightly, and I tell you when part of a reading rests on it. It's a notebook, not a study.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Uranus means chaos.”** It means change. Change can arrive suddenly, but it's usually something that was already pushing to get out.
- **“Your Uranus sign describes you.”** It's shared by everyone born over about seven years. The house, and the aspects to your personal planets, are what's yours.
- **“The Uranus opposition is a midlife crisis.”** It comes at about 42 for everyone, crisis or not. It describes the restlessness, not what you do with it.
- **“Uranus has always ruled Aquarius.”** It was only found in 1781. Before that, Aquarius belonged to Saturn, and many astrologers still read it that way: [traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/).
