Two people on a ladder painting a ceiling and laughing at the drips; colleagues who arrive early for the coffee before the meeting; a market stall run every Saturday for years: composite Venus in the 6th house finds a relationship's pleasure in the working day.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is cadent, with Virgo, Venus's fall, as its natural sign. Its old name, Bad Fortune, belongs to the tradition that coined it, and the joy there was Mars's rather than Venus's. It covers daily work, routine, service and craft. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) its cusps come from both birth times, by either of the two methods, so a [Venus](/astrology/venus/) close to an edge may sit in the 5th or the 7th instead ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Illness, the other thing the old books put here, is not something I read in any chart: Venus in this house describes how a bond enjoys its days, not anyone's health ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

Venus in the house of the working day makes routine itself pleasant: the job split by who enjoys which part, a list written together on a Sunday evening, a skill both of you keep refining. Even the dull jobs, the filing, the ironing, the end-of-month paperwork, can turn into small rituals the two of you look forward to. Affection is practical here, offered as a task done before anyone asked, which suits Virgo's manner. Pairs of colleagues often have this placement, and so do couples who met at work. Where a natal [Venus in the 6th house](/astrology/venus-in-6th-house/) shows one person at ease in the working day, and [their Venus in your 6th house](/astrology/venus-in-6th-house-synastry/) someone who lightens your routines, the composite's routines are a craft the two of you share.

This Venus rarely has the composite Sun more than a house or two away: nine pairs in ten have them less than 47° apart. Birthdays months apart are what change that, and in about 1 pair in 22 the opposite point, around the 12th house of retreat, is where the composite Sun turns up ([composite Sun in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-12th-house/)). Midpoints worked out one planet at a time can do that; it says nothing about your bond.

The difficulty is that care can shrink to chores. A bond that shows love through useful work may stop showing it any other way, and the tasks each of you does quietly can become invisible to the other. Service isn't servitude, and routine needn't be the only shape affection takes. It helps to thank each other for the unseen jobs by name, and now and then to swap them, just to see the other's day from the inside.

[Composite Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-5th-house/) plays; [composite Venus in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-7th-house/) partners and negotiates. Composites are a method I explain without practising, read by those who do practise it together with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). Between colleagues especially, a chart of two people needs both to agree, not merely to know ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
