Put one person's Jupiter on the other's Uranus and a conversation about what life means tends to turn into a conversation about what could change. Jupiter conjunct Uranus in synastry is that overlap, within 5°. It belongs to two birth years, so I read it as one era's beliefs meeting another era's experiments.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) synthesises: it combines experience into a coherent picture and keeps widening it. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates: it questions inherited assumptions, tests alternatives and reforms systems from the inside. Fused by a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) into one process, the two stay distinct functions; as with any of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), each belongs to a different person.

Jupiter's growth gives Uranus's change a reason to happen: a departure starts to mean something, and an experiment becomes part of a larger understanding. In return, Uranus keeps Jupiter's picture from setting into a creed.

Carrying the Jupiter, you meet someone whose urge to rethink things lands right where your sense of meaning lives. Talks with them open your beliefs up, and your principles can feel permanently under renovation, exhilarating some weeks and tiring in others.

Carrying the Uranus, you meet someone who sees what your experiments could mean. You feel understood rather than tolerated; you may also feel your need for freedom being turned into somebody's philosophy, which isn't quite the same as being free.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Fused this closely, novelty and growth are easy to confuse. Both people can get excited at once, and nobody asks whether the new arrangement is better or only different. Uranus tends to assume that disruption produces progress, Jupiter to find meaning in whatever is happening, and opportunity is not guaranteed success. A conjunction also blurs who started what: the Jupiter person can feel they supply the vision for someone else's restlessness, the Uranus person that their independence has been adopted as a cause.

Its four neighbours spread the same two functions out:

- **[Jupiter sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-uranus-synastry/):** an opening between belief and change, there for whoever takes it.
- **[Jupiter square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-uranus-synastry/):** the two at right angles, where principles and revisions rub.
- **[Jupiter trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-trine-uranus-synastry/):** change and meaning running comfortably in one element.
- **[Jupiter opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-uranus-synastry/):** a framework across from a departure, each person holding one.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give each experiment a question. Before you change something together, a job, a city, the way a household runs, a process at work, say what you hope to learn and when you'll look back at it. Finish one adventure before starting the next. The conjunction suits travel and study between friends, a couple's long project, siblings rethinking a family habit, colleagues piloting something new.

The two birth years do the choosing. With a birthday in mid-1985, Jupiter sits on the Uranus of everyone born from December 1998 to February 2001; in the reverse direction, the Uranus of that same birth is met by the Jupiter of people born between January and November 2007. A whole run of birthdays shares the conjunction, so it has no place among the aspects I count in synastry, which all involve the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. I look instead at the houses where each person's Jupiter and Uranus sit, which shows where in these two lives the meeting of two eras takes place.

Uranus spends about seven years in a sign and Jupiter about one, so the hour makes no difference to whether the conjunction is there, only to the houses it falls in, and I need both times to see them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Inside one chart, [Jupiter conjunct Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus/) makes understanding and change a single habit. Shared out between two people, it becomes a partnership in rethinking things, with two sets of reasons.
