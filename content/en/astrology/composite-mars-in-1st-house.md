People notice this couple's energy before they notice either person: the pair that arrives on bicycles, organises the game, says what it thinks. With composite Mars in the 1st house, the relationship's drive is on show.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [the 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) opens at the composite Ascendant and shows how the relationship presents itself and meets the world. With [Mars](/astrology/mars/) there, the bond's way of acting becomes its calling card: direct, energetic, quick to begin. The house is angular, the strongest kind in tradition, and its natural sign is Aries, Mars's own; a house borrows nothing from its natural sign's dignities, but the match shows.

Such a pair does things together where they can be seen: it runs, climbs, campaigns, renovates, starts a business with its name on the door. It's often the one that suggests the plan and the one that leaves first. Competition is part of how others see you, and friends may joke about how much you spar, sparring that is usually affectionate. Desire shows on the surface, in how you look at each other across a room. One person's [Mars in the 1st house](/astrology/mars-in-1st-house/) makes that person direct; [their Mars in your 1st house](/astrology/mars-in-1st-house-synastry/) describes someone who energises or pushes you; the composite puts the directness in the relationship itself.

The difficulty is that disagreements show too. Impatience with each other can surface at dinner with friends, and a teasing argument you both enjoy can sound sharper to whoever else is at the table. The bond may also define itself so much by action that stillness feels like failure. What helps is a shared outlet, a sport, a project, a long walk, so that the energy goes outwards, and an agreement about which disagreements wait until you're home.

Sparring in public is friction. If either of you is ever afraid in private, a chart can't weigh that: in the UK, 999 is for emergencies, and Samaritans answer on 116 123.

This house depends on both times and on the method. Its starting point, the composite Ascendant, is found in two ways: by averaging your two Ascendants, or by building it from the composite Midheaven and your home's latitude, and the results can sit degrees apart. A birth time four minutes out shifts a chart's horizon by about a degree, so both times have to be exact ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)). A Mars near the composite [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) may belong to the 12th house in one version and to the 1st in the other.

I don't cast composites; this is how the method reads one, next to each birth chart and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It forecasts no wedding and no parting. Next door, [composite Mars in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-12th-house/) keeps its effort out of sight and [composite Mars in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-2nd-house/) builds security; the 1st acts in plain view. A chart that shows a relationship this plainly deserves both people's yes before anyone draws it ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
