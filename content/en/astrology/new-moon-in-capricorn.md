Capricorn begins at the December solstice, which makes this the first new moon of every northern winter, and it never strays far from the calendar's new year: from 2026 to 2030, each one falls between 24 December and 18 January. Whatever begins under it tends to be meant to last.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

The Moon sits on the Sun's degree at a [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/), and the lunar month starts counting again. [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) is cardinal earth: it starts things, and it starts them on solid ground. Its month usually opens on a responsibility taken on, a plan with milestones, or a structure finally put under something that has been wobbling. Its ruler is [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), so where Saturn sits in your chart shows where a Capricorn beginning wants to build.

Tradition puts the Moon in its detriment in Capricorn, the sign opposite its home in Cancer. I read that as feeling expressed through doing: care shown by turning up, needs postponed until the work is done. At this new moon, the month can start with duty before comfort, and it helps to notice when that's a choice and when it's a habit.

Capricorn would want the small print: a new moon is a monthly marker of a few days. It enters a reading only when it sits on one of your natal planets or angles, give or take a degree, and only to sharpen something the slower methods have already set moving, whether a transit, a progression, an eclipse or a solar arc. It sets nothing going on its own, and it forecasts nothing.

Because it straddles New Year, the calendar treats it unevenly. The most recent fell on 18 January 2026 at 28°44′. Then come two in 2027, on 7 January at 20:25 UT (17°18′) and on 27 December (5°50′); none in 2028; one on 14 January 2029 (24°50′), which is also a partial [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/); and two again in 2030, on 4 January (13°47′) and 24 December (2°57′).

The eclipse of 14 January 2029 comes two weeks after a total lunar eclipse in Cancer, on 31 December 2028, at the other end of the same axis ([eclipse in Capricorn](/astrology/eclipse-in-capricorn/)). I give it a wider orb, about 3°, at either end of the Cancer–Capricorn axis, and read it through the natal point it touches and that point's house, never as an omen.

In the same weeks, with the Sun in Capricorn, falls the [full moon in Cancer](/astrology/full-moon-in-cancer/). A month separates it from the [new moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/new-moon-in-sagittarius/) before it and from the [new moon in Aquarius](/astrology/new-moon-in-aquarius/) after.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house can't be known without the hour of birth; [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) explains why. I draw houses in Placidus, where a new moon belongs to the house containing its degree: usually the one with Capricorn on its cusp. Late degrees can cross into the next house when that house's cusp also falls in Capricorn, before them; the 2029 eclipse, at 24°50′, is the one to check.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Capricorn rising: a more composed start in how you present yourself, taking your own ambitions seriously in public.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** money built to last: a budget you can keep, your skills priced at a professional rate.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** a disciplined start in learning or writing: a course with a qualification at the end, a daily hour of practice.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** family responsibilities: a repair that can't wait, a role at home accepted or redefined.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** play taken seriously: a craft practised towards mastery, a creative project with a deadline, pleasure you allow yourself.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** the working day restructured: a schedule you can keep for years, not weeks, and a task at work handled properly.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** commitment: a relationship or collaboration put on a solid footing, its roles and duties made clear.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** shared money and obligations set in order: a debt with a timetable, trust that is earned.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** study as a long climb: a degree or a professional training, beliefs tested by experience rather than taken on faith.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Capricorn's own house: a new start in your career or public role, with a goal measured in years and a first concrete step.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** groups with a purpose: a role taken on in an organisation, plans with milestones.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a quiet start on what you carry alone: duties set down for a while, rest scheduled as seriously as work.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Three things, in Capricorn's order:

1. **The degrees.** Check your chart within a degree of each position above, and within about three degrees of 24°50′ Capricorn or Cancer for the 2029 eclipse.
2. **Saturn.** Its house and sign show where this beginning wants a foundation.
3. **One aim, one step.** If you like New Year resolutions, this is the one sign where they're in character. Keep it to a single aim, and a first step you can take this month.
