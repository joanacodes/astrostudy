Pisces on the 11th house cusp gives the house of allies and shared plans to the most compassionate sign of the zodiac. Your circle tends to gather around feeling rather than status, and the future you hope for is often one you'd like everyone to share.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds the people who stand with you by choice: allies, circles, the causes you join and the help that turns up without being asked for ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). Tradition called it the Good Spirit, and with [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) on its cusp, your friendships tend to be made of sympathy rather than common interests. You may collect people others overlook, be the one your friends confide in at two in the morning, and feel most at home in a group held together by music, faith, care or a shared imagination: a choir, a theatre company, a night-shelter rota. Hopes are held loosely here; they change shape as you go, and a wish for the world can matter more than a plan for yourself. The care it asks for is to notice when a group's moods are becoming yours, and to say no to the cause that would drain you.

In the house of your allies the modern ruler is Neptune and the traditional one Jupiter, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows where friendship turns into fellow feeling, and where the ideal of a group can hide what it really is; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where friends widen your world and where generosity comes back through them. Jupiter, whose joy tradition placed in this very house ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), also rules your Descendant through Sagittarius in about 6 London charts in 7, so friends and partners answer to the same planet, and a friendship often grows into something more. Your kindred spirits tend to turn up in the parts of life where the two rulers sit, as ruler of the 11th in the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Only about 1 London birth in 16, and 1 New York birth in 15, has this cusp in Placidus, the house system I use, since the cusps above the eastern horizon also pass quickly through Pisces. Every one of these charts has Aquarius on the Midheaven ([Aquarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)), so Uranus and Saturn run your public life while Neptune and Jupiter run your friendships, and Gemini rises in nearly 9 in 10 of them, giving the chart to Mercury. The 11th is wide, about 42° to 48° at London, and in about half the London charts it holds the whole of Aries as well ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)): Mars then joins the circle, and you'll fight for a friend as readily as you'll comfort one. Pisces itself has no cusp in about 1 London chart in 6.

Born earlier that day, you would have had Pisces on the 12th cusp ([Pisces on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-12th-house-cusp/)), and born later, on the Midheaven ([Pisces on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/)): the hour and the minute decide, and I won't read the chart without them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
