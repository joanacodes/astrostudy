Money is the surface of the 2nd house; underneath lie the questions of what keeps you safe and what you're worth. When Pluto enters, it heads for that lower layer and stays there for a long time.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) brings up what has been buried and asks who holds the power. The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is what you have and what you value: earnings, belongings, the talents you could live from, and the sense of your own worth beneath them. Over the years of this stay, your idea of security tends to be tested from below. Things you thought you needed may lose their grip on you, while one or two you'd taken for granted turn out to matter enormously. Where money and dependence are tangled, with a partner, a family or an employer, the knot becomes harder to ignore. And the value you put on your own time and skill often rises, sometimes after a long stretch of underselling it.

Planets in your natal 2nd are met on the way, sometimes years apart: a Saturn there may find its caution re-examined, a Jupiter may be asked what "enough" would look like.

None of this forecasts money. [Transits](/astrology/transits/) wake up what a birth chart already contains, and this one rebuilds an attitude from the foundations; no sum arrives or leaves because of it. I don't read finances in a chart at all ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)), and this transit is no exception.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Pluto moves so slowly that its house stays are counted in decades as easily as in years. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the figure across all twelve houses is about 20 years, most often between about 10 and 37. The 2nd comes in a little shorter: about 16 years in the middle, with most stays between about 12 and 38. At New York's latitude the 2nd keeps Pluto about 18 years in the middle, and the range narrows slightly, to about 13 to 36.

Which end of that range is yours depends on how wide your 2nd house is, and that comes from the minute and the place of your birth. Without a birth time the cusp of the 2nd is unknown, so I can't say when Pluto arrives, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Pluto reaches this house after its long work on your identity ([Pluto transiting the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-transit-1st-house/)), and leaves it for the house of words, neighbours and everyday learning ([Pluto transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-3rd-house/)).

Aquarius has held Pluto since March 2023, without a break since November 2024, and still holds it at the close of 2039, where my count ends. So if your 2nd house opens in Aquarius, Pluto is crossing it now or will be soon, and the degree on the cusp gives the year. If it opens in Capricorn, the crossing ran between January 2008 and November 2024. A 2nd house beginning in Pisces waits until after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take an honest inventory, not of your bank account but of what you rely on to feel safe. Pluto in the 2nd rewards knowing which of those supports are real and which are habit.

Where someone else has a hand on your security, name it, at least to yourself. Dependence is easier to change once it is seen clearly, and this stay gives you years to change it gently.

Price your own time and skill as if a stranger were asking. The stay tends to correct an old habit of underselling yourself, and the correction starts with what you're willing to say out loud.
