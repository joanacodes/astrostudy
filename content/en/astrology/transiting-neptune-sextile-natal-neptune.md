A sixth of Neptune's orbit takes about twenty-seven years, so everyone meets this sextile in the mid-to-late twenties. It is the first major aspect the planet makes to its own birth place, and it comes just before the louder transits of the late twenties.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Natal [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the dream you were born into: what your generation idealises, given a place in your life by its house. In the sky the same planet has now moved two signs on, and a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates the two through an opening that needs your answer. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the ideals and imagination you have carried since childhood find it easier to take a working form: a craft, a practice, a kind of service, a way of caring you can actually keep up.

The opening arrives through the house transiting Neptune is crossing, roughly two houses on from your natal Neptune. With Neptune born in your 10th, the sextile usually comes from around your 12th, and the hopes you hold for your work can draw on solitude, rest or an inner life you have neglected ([Neptune transiting the 12th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-12th-house/)). Only the birth time places those houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Nothing in a sextile happens by itself. Neptune's blind spot, taking imagination for evidence, is easier to avoid here than at the square, because this transit asks for something small and real rather than a leap of faith. It improves the climate for the work; the work stays yours.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets it exactly once. In the site's count the orb, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, opened between 24.6 and 27.1, and since Neptune's orbit is close to a circle the age barely moves: about 27 for births from the 1930s to the 1970s, 26 for the 1980s and 1990s, 25½ for 2005. Born at the start of 1950, you met it exact at 28.0, 28.4 and 28.9; at the start of 1990, five times, from 26.4 to 28. A whole pass takes about two and a half years, and the quickest about twenty-two months ([Neptune retrograde](/astrology/neptune-retrograde/)).

It shares those years with other steps. [Transiting Uranus trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-uranus/) came within orb between 24.6 and 30.2 in the same count, and the [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/) follows, first exact between 28.4 and 29.9. Born between the mid-1950s and the mid-1980s, with Neptune about 60° ahead of Pluto, you also meet [transiting Neptune trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-pluto/) within a year or so of this sextile: the same stretch of Neptune's road, seen from your Pluto.

After it come [transiting Neptune square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-neptune/), the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) at about 41, then [transiting Neptune trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-neptune/) in the fifties; [transiting Neptune opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-neptune/) waits until about 80. Until March 2039 Neptune is in Aries, two signs on from Aquarius, so this is the sextile of everyone born with [Neptune in Aquarius](/astrology/neptune-in-aquarius/), from 1998 to 2012.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give one ideal a shape you can repeat: a weekly class, a practice, an hour a week for a cause. A sextile rewards what you do regularly more than what you decide once.

Sort the dream you were handed from the one you'd choose. Your Neptune sign is shared by everyone born within about fourteen years of you; the version with your name on it is the one worth the effort.

Keep it in view among the louder demands of these years. A quiet opening is easy to postpone, and this one lasts about two and a half years.
