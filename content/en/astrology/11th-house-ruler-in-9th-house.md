Some address books read like an atlas. Your 11th house ruler sits in the 9th, and the people you choose often come from far away, from another language or another way of seeing things, and your circle gathers around a question as much as a place.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is the house of distance: travel that takes you far, study past the basics, faith, and the questions about what life means. The [11th](/astrology/11th-house/) is the company you seek out and the future you hope to share with it. Read through its ruler ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), the 11th's business here is done at a distance, in other countries, other languages, other frames of mind.

Friendships form where the world widens. You meet your people on a year abroad, in a seminar, on a retreat, through a translation project or an exchange, and you keep them across time zones. The groups that suit you gather around ideas: a philosophy circle, a faith community, a campaign with a view of the world behind it, a class of adults learning the same language. Your hopes tend to be ideals, sometimes large ones, and friends are the people who share the horizon. The care it asks: a circle held together by a conviction can turn into an echo, and the best of your friends are the ones who still ask you awkward questions.

Horary astrology gives the 9th foreigners and foreign lands, universities and books, churches and long journeys, and any of those can be where your allies turn up. The two houses are a sextile apart by sign, a distance tradition counted as friendly, so the line between your circle and your search for meaning runs easily: friends open doors to ideas, and ideas lead you to new friends.

With Pisces on the 11th cusp, the house answers to Jupiter by the old rule and to Neptune by the new, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Jupiter in the 9th, the house the natural zodiac gives to its own Sagittarius, finds friends among teachers and fellow seekers, while Neptune, from wherever it stands, adds the dream of a community of kindred spirits. A Gemini or Virgo 11th puts Mercury in charge, and from the 9th it keeps a correspondence going in more than one language. With Leo on the cusp, the Sun rules the house, and the 9th is the one place tradition gave the Sun its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): friendships that throw light on big questions.

None of this says you'll move abroad or take up a faith. It shows where your circle tends to grow, and the choices stay yours.

The 9th ends at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), so a ruler at that edge had only just passed the top of the sky when you were born. A slightly earlier birth would have kept it in the 10th, where your allies gather around your work ([Ruler of the 11th in the 10th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)); lower in the house, near the 9th cusp, a slightly later one would have set it in the 8th, among deeper and more private loyalties ([Ruler of the 11th in the 8th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). In [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the system I use, the Midheaven opens the 10th and moves with the minute of birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
