The 1st house is the doorway of a chart, the part of you that arrives first. With Chiron standing there, the doorway itself was often the tender place: how you come across, how you take up space, whether you're met as you are. Many people with this placement end up unusually good at making a room feel safe for whoever walks in next.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Whatever [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts around, [the 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) puts on show: your manner, your presence, the way you start things. Angular, opening on one of the chart's four angles, and naturally Aries's, it shows what it holds early and in plain view. The sensitivity is often about being seen as you are — a sense, early on, that you came across wrong, or didn't fit the shape expected of you. The adapting builds a way of showing up: a manner chosen rather than imposed, a warmth or a wit that carries you into a room.

In [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, a Chiron in the 1st house lies just under the eastern horizon, below the degree that was rising, and within a few degrees of that point it tends to colour every first impression the chart makes. In whole sign houses the 1st is the entire rising sign, so a Chiron that rose shortly before your birth can count as 12th house in Placidus and 1st in whole sign: worth knowing if you've read both. None of this exists without the time of birth, since four minutes move the Ascendant by about a degree ([what the time decides](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)). The neighbouring placements read differently: [Chiron in the 12th house](/astrology/chiron-in-12th-house/) keeps its adapting private, while [Chiron in the 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-in-2nd-house/) turns it towards worth and resources.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Chiron in the 1st house tends to meet partners with an early question: will you like me once you see me properly? You may lead with a version of yourself that's easier to accept — funny, capable, unbothered — and only later let the rest show. A partner who is glad of the unpolished version, and says so, does this placement a great deal of good. In return you're often unusually accepting of other people's oddities, because you've been on the receiving end of a first-sight judgement.

## At work {#at-work}

First impressions are where this Chiron does its professional work. You may have built a presence deliberately — the steady voice, the open manner — and it makes you good at roles where people need to feel at ease quickly: teaching, reception, coaching, care, anything public-facing. Many people with Chiron here are drawn to helping others start over, or feel at home in their own skin. The method works best offered lightly: your way of walking in is one way, not a rule.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Self-consciousness gets in the way. On difficult days, this Chiron may over-manage its image, apologise for taking up space, or avoid being looked at altogether. It can also go the other way: a bold front that never lets anyone near the uncertainty behind it. Either way, a lot of energy goes into how you're seen, and less into what you actually want.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let people meet you before you've finished preparing. Notice which parts of your manner are chosen and which are armour, and keep the chosen ones. Movement that feels good from the inside, rather than looking right from the outside, helps a house so tied to the self you show. And I'll say it plainly: this placement describes how you meet the world, not how you look, and a chart has nothing to say about your body.

A 1st-house Chiron is often where a reading of mine begins: Chiron held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook, and here it stands at the door of the chart ([how I tested](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/#the-method)).
