Improving how things work is what a Virgo Sun wants; an Aquarius Moon is comforted by friends, ideas and a system that is fair to everyone. The Moon was gibbous and growing, a few days short of full, and I read the pair as an engineer of better arrangements, for people as much as for things.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Nothing is shared between the lights here, neither element nor mode, and they sit five signs apart: a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, the aspect of constant small adjustments. The [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) builds a self from discernment and careful work; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) makes sense of a feeling by standing back from it, and settles among friends, ideas and room to differ. One improves the part, the other cares about the whole, and you may be at your best fixing the part that makes the whole fairer.

Aquarius has a modern ruler and a traditional one, and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern, for where this Moon needs room to differ and to change the rules, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the older ruler, for the loyalties and routines that make closeness feel safe rather than confining. As for [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), Virgo is both its home and its exaltation, and it travels with the Sun, never further off than Leo or Libra. No traditional dignity applies to either light, so the three rulers carry much of the reading.

On the day, a gibbous Moon was already up in the afternoon sky, swelling towards full, 120° to 179° from the Sun. By Rudhyar's reckoning seven in eight of these are [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) births and the remaining eighth [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) ones; a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/) may hold at the very start of the range, and around 150° the quincunx becomes an exact aspect. I take the gibbous phase to be the part of the month spent refining something nearly ready, which suits a Virgo Sun almost too well; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sets the eight phases side by side.

These two lights come together in roughly one birth in 142. Where the Moon was entering Aquarius from Capricorn, or leaving it for Pisces, on your birthday, the hour decides between this page, the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-capricorn/) and the [Sun in Virgo, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-virgo-moon-in-pisces/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Both of this Moon's rulers move slowly, so their signs say a good deal about your generation; their houses, which need the hour, say what they mean for you.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Friendship is the ground your love grows from. The Aquarius Moon needs a partner who respects your independence and your odd ideas and doesn't need reassurance every day; the Virgo Sun shows love by being useful, reliable and attentive to detail. What can go missing is warmth said out loud. You may fix a partner's problem when they wanted to be held. Ask which they'd like, since both are gifts.

## At work {#at-work}

You do well improving systems that serve many people: research, data, engineering, public services, technology, any field where a careful method meets a fair aim. The Sun brings precision, the Moon an eye for how the whole fits together and the nerve to propose a better way. Favouritism offends the Moon and sloppiness bothers the Sun. You're often the colleague who quietly rewrites the process so that it works for everyone.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Both lights keep feelings at a little distance, the Virgo Sun by analysing them and the Aquarius Moon by understanding them in principle. Between the two, a feeling can be described perfectly and never felt, and people close to you may read that as coolness, which it isn't. What helps is a habit of naming the feeling before the theory, one person who hears the unpolished version, and a body that gets to feel things too: a run, a swim, music loud enough to move to.
