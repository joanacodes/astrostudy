The sextiles are the first step Uranus takes away from your Moon and the last one back towards it. Each time, for about twenty months, the way you live can change without anything having to break first.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) tests alternatives and frees what has gone stale; your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds your needs and the habits that keep you steady. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, puts two functions within easy reach of each other: it opens a door between your appetite for change and your need for comfort, and leaves you to decide whether to walk through.

Small, practical changes are the usual form, and they turn out to matter. A routine you try for a week and keep; a room rearranged so the house works differently; a friendship that brings another kind of ease into your evenings; a way of resting you'd never have chosen before. Feelings adjust faster than usual, which makes this a good stretch for changes the people you live with will have to get used to as well.

Left alone, though, a sextile changes nothing. People often look back on it as a time when something could have changed easily, and didn't, because nothing forced it.

With your Moon in the 4th and Uranus crossing your 2nd ([Uranus transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-2nd-house/)), a fresh idea of what security is made of opens up the way you live at home: less about owning the right things, more about what actually comforts you. The Moon changes sign about every two and a half days, so for some births even its sign waits on the hour, and its house always does; without that hour I don't read the transit ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counting from 1½° before exact to 1° after, as I do for every transit, the sextile holds for about twenty months: long for an opening, with time to try a change, drop it and try another. Three exact hits are the rule, in more than nine passes out of ten, and the quickest passes still take about eleven months.

From the first sextile it's about seven years on to [transiting Uranus square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-moon/), fourteen to [transiting Uranus trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-moon/) and 28 to [transiting Uranus opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-moon/); the second falls in the last fourteen years or so before the round closes at [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-moon/). Nobody born between 1930 and 2009 gets to 80 without one of them; most get both.

Born with [Moon sextile Uranus](/astrology/moon-sextile-uranus/), you began life inside one of these sextiles; the other comes at about 28 or about 56, when Uranus makes a trine to its own birth place. And with Uranus in Gemini until 2033, sextile the fire signs Aries and Leo, a Moon in either of them gets its [transit](/astrology/transits/) some time in these years.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change one routine on purpose. Pick the habit that comforts you least and replace it for a month; the sextile makes the experiment cheap.

Say yes to the invitation that feels slightly unlike you. A new circle, a class, a different way to spend a Sunday: under this transit, the unfamiliar can turn into a comfort quickly.

Rearrange a room. It sounds small, but with Uranus sextile the Moon, small changes to the place you live travel further than they look.
