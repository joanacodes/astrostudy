Aquarius on the 5th house cusp brings the sign of friendship into the house of pleasure, play and romance. What you do for joy tends to be done with other people or for an idea, and love often arrives by way of a good conversation.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is what you do for the pleasure of it, and its natural sign is Leo, the opposite of Aquarius; with [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) on the cusp instead ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), self-expression goes through the group. You may enjoy making things most when others make them with you, in a band, a choir, a writing circle or a project nobody is paid for, and a solo turn can feel less natural than a shared piece of work. Your taste in fun leans to the experimental: the unusual instrument, the new medium, the game whose rules you invented. In romance, minds tend to come first. Someone's ideas catch you before anything else does, you flirt through debate, and you need a love that leaves you room; possessiveness switches the light off quickly. Being fixed, the sign stays loyal to a pastime or a person for longer than its cool manner suggests.

Uranus lights your pleasures up and Saturn makes them last, and I read both. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) shows where enthusiasms flare up without warning, and where you'll try what others haven't; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) shows where pleasure becomes a practice, the hobby kept up for decades, the craft learned slowly. In 8 London charts in 10, and nearly 9 at New York, Libra rises with this cusp and [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules the chart: the planet that tradition said rejoices in the 5th leads the whole chart, while Uranus and Saturn run its pleasures. The same charts have Capricorn on the IC, so Saturn rules home as well as play, and what you enjoy often needs a settled base first. Look to each ruler's house for where your fun actually happens, as ruler of the 5th in the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

With Placidus, the house system I use, this cusp falls to Aquarius in about 1 birth in 11, at London and New York alike: midway between the horizon and the meridian, the cusp moves at close to an average pace, and the 5th comes out near 30° wide in these charts. Pisces opens the next house, and when Virgo rises instead of Libra, in about 1 London chart in 5, Pisces opens the 7th as well ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)): Neptune and Jupiter then rule both your daily work and your partners. Not every chart gives Aquarius a cusp, though: about 1 in 7 at London keeps it wholly inside a house ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Tradition put children in the 5th. I don't read it for them: no chart can say whether or when anyone will have a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and what this cusp describes is your own pleasure and creativity. The house also depends on your birth time, to the minute when a planet sits near the cusp. A couple of hours later and the sign would be on your IC ([Aquarius on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/)); a couple of hours earlier, on the 6th ([Aquarius on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/)), which is why I need the time before I say anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
