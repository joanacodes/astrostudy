Jupiter conjunct Uranus is growth by way of the new: the planet of meaning and the planet of change in one place. The two meet about every fourteen years, so a few months of births share it at a time; through your houses, it becomes the particular way you widen your world.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands understanding and builds meaning: it learns, believes, generalises and looks for the larger pattern. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees: it innovates, experiments, questions systems and reforms what has grown too narrow. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two work as one process without losing their natures: growth arrives through change, and change goes looking for meaning.

In plain words, your outlook widens by being shaken. A new idea, a new place or a new method tends to excite you more than refining an old one, and your beliefs keep updating rather than settling. That's a gift for breakthroughs in understanding, and for spotting possibilities before others do. Innovation isn't chaos, and expansion isn't excess: the conjunction works best when the new thing you reach for has a reason beyond novelty.

I read it as restlessness with a purpose: a mind that grows by changing its frame, and a freedom that wants to mean something.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Counting within 6°, the conjunction concerns about 1 person in 27 born between 1930 and 2009, gathered in five windows: April to June 1941; August 1954 to June 1955; October 1968 to September 1969; December 1982 to November 1983; and January to March 1997. Jupiter, about 12 years round, laps Uranus, about 84, every fourteen years or so, and the windows last from about three months to about a year.

On its own, then, it describes a cohort: Jupiter is a social planet and Uranus a generational one ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). Your own version lives in its house, and in any contact it makes with your Sun, Moon or angles. In the 3rd, it might show as a mind that learns by jumping between subjects; in the 6th, as daily work you keep reinventing; in the 7th, as partnerships that need room. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects mark other years:

- **[Jupiter sextile Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-uranus/):** an opening for growth through the new, for the births of 1952–53, 1966–67 or 1985–86, among others.
- **[Jupiter square Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-square-uranus/):** friction between belief and change, coming round about every seven years.
- **[Jupiter trine Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-trine-uranus/):** growth and change in easy agreement, for those born in 1950–51 or 1964–65, for example.
- **[Jupiter opposite Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-uranus/):** the two face to face, roughly halfway between meetings, as in 1948, 1962–63 or 1989–90.

[Jupiter conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) joins one person's appetite for meaning to the other's appetite for change: stimulating, sometimes unsettling, and best for adventures both people have chosen.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-uranus/) is tied to your [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/): every 12 years or so, Jupiter comes home and finds your Uranus next door, for a few days or weeks. [Transiting Uranus conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) is another matter: Uranus is so slow that it either reaches your Jupiter in your first year or two, if Jupiter stood a few degrees ahead of it at birth, or not until the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/), at about 84. On the way, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 42, opposes both planets at once.

Weather, not a forecast: seasons when growing and changing ask the same thing of the same corner of your life, with no event written in.
