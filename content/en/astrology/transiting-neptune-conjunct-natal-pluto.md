This is the one step between Neptune and your Pluto that no life now under way contains. The two planets last met in the sky in 1891 and 1892, at about 8° Gemini; they meet about every 490 years, and everyone born since has Neptune ahead.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) in your chart is where you hold on hardest and where, in time, you can let something end and begin again. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) softens what has set hard and widens it towards imagination and compassion. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes two functions act as one, so as a [transit](/astrology/transits/) this would be renewal carried by an ideal, or an ideal that changes you at the root.

For people born between 1930 and 2009 it has no date. At their births Neptune already stood 40° to 63° past Pluto in the zodiac, and it moves on at a little over 2° a year; to come round to the natal Pluto again it would need close to 300° more, well over a century. In the site's count no one in those eighty years meets it by 80, and the natal [Neptune conjunct Pluto](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-pluto/) is missing from the same charts for the same reason.

The reverse is common. Pluto trails Neptune in these charts and closes the gap on the quick stretch of its orbit, so [transiting Pluto conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-neptune/) reaches everyone born in these years, between about 22 and 30. There Pluto is the one moving, and I read it as Pluto's transit, through the house of your natal Neptune, which only the birth time gives ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

If it came, it would last like any Neptune transit in my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after: about two and a half years, twenty-two months at the shortest, with three exact hits in most passes and five in roughly one pass in four ([Neptune retrograde](/astrology/neptune-retrograde/)). Only people born before 1892, when Neptune still trailed Pluto, could meet it at all.

What does come is the rest of the same road, Neptune drawing away from your Pluto at the same ages as everyone born within a year or two of you:

- **[Transiting Neptune sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-pluto/):** in the first years of life, for about 5 people in 6.
- **[Transiting Neptune square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-pluto/):** between about 10 and 20.
- **[Transiting Neptune trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-pluto/):** between about 24 and 34.
- **[Transiting Neptune opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-pluto/):** between 51 and 62, for everyone.

The conjunction would come only after the opposition, another half-circle on, some eighty years later still.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Check which planet is moving. If a list of your transits shows Neptune and Pluto conjunct, for anyone born in these years it can only be Pluto, on your natal Neptune, and that reads as Pluto's season, not Neptune's.

Read the step your age has reached. Past fifty, it is the opposition; in the late twenties and early thirties, the trine; the sextile and square belong to childhood and adolescence, read in hindsight.

Look at the pair as your chart holds it. About 7 people in 10 born in these years have [Neptune sextile Pluto](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto/) at birth, and the houses of the two planets say more about you than a conjunction no one meets.
