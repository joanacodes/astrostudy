Some pairs are watched: colleagues who became a couple, partners who run a business, a relationship one family or one small town has opinions about. Under that gaze, composite Chiron in the 10th house finds the relationship's sore spot, and the role in the world the two people shape for themselves is its answer.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is the summit of a chart: public life, vocation, reputation, what a life is known for. Its cusp is the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the highest angle, and Capricorn is its natural sign. In a relationship's chart, [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it shows how the pair appears to the world and what it does there; with [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) here, being seen and judged is the sore point. The pair may feel scrutinised by a workplace, a community or a family's expectations, or may find that working together in public brings out a doubt that private life never does.

Being public as a pair is different from being public alone: someone who handles a stage easily can find that standing on it with a partner, or behind a shared shop counter, is another matter. Its answer is usually a vocation: a shared project the pair defines for itself, a way of being visible that protects what is private, often a job that helps others in the same position, mentoring, advocacy, a business built around a need the two of you know well.

Its cusp, with the IC opposite, is where the two ways of drawing composite houses agree: the composite Midheaven is the midpoint of your two Midheavens, whichever method builds the rest. That doesn't free it from the birth times. A Midheaven moves about a degree every four minutes, so an error of eight minutes in one birth time moves that person's by about two degrees and the composite's by about one, enough to carry a Chiron near the cusp across it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Three charts can carry this Chiron, and they say different things: a natal [Chiron in the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-in-10th-house/) is about one person's reputation and vocation, [their Chiron in your 10th house](/astrology/chiron-in-10th-house-synastry/) meets one person's ambitions with the other's experience, and the composite's 10th is the face the two of you show together, ambition and reluctance alike.

Reputation can end up running the relationship: choices made for how they look, a bond that performs success, or one that hides from view to avoid comment. A composite predicts no promotion, failure or scandal. It is worth deciding together what you want to be known for, and keeping some of your life out of the shop window.

Behind it, [composite Chiron in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-9th-house/) is tender about the wider world; ahead of it, [composite Chiron in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-11th-house/) about friends and groups. Drawing composites isn't part of my work; the astrologers who do it read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and for a pair in the public eye more than most, both should know before their charts are compared ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
