With Capricorn on the 6th house cusp, the daily round runs on structure. Your days tend to be planned, kept and finished, and the to-do list is a contract you've signed with yourself.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is ordinary working life: the tasks, the habits that hold a day together, the colleagues you see more often than your friends, the care you take of your body ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) on its cusp turns all of it into a structure. I read it as the alarm set for the same time on Saturdays, the colleague who finishes what others start, the person handed responsibility at work before asking for it. Colleagues tend to trust you with the long, unglamorous jobs, and you tend to do them well. Mars, the planet the old astrologers thought happiest in the 6th, has its exaltation in Capricorn: the planet of effort is at its best in this sign, so the energy you put into a working day is usually well aimed. The hard part is stopping. Tiredness can feel like a personal failing, and rest like something you haven't earned yet.

Illness was the 6th's other business in tradition. I leave it out: a chart can't diagnose or foresee a health problem, and that's a doctor's work, not mine ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). What the cusp shows is how you look after your body, and with Capricorn that's usually a long programme kept with care: the exercises actually done, the check-up booked on time.

The rhythm of your days belongs to [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/): its house shows what all that routine is for and what it adds up to, its sign how you carry it, in the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Saturn is among the placements I trust most in a reading, as one of the three that held 75 to 90% of the time when I tested charts line by line ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). At both latitudes, every chart with this cusp has [Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/), which puts Aquarius on the western horizon, on your Descendant, and Saturn, with Uranus, rules your partnerships as well as your working days. The two parts of life can mix, a business run with a partner, a relationship that is also a working team, and both go better with clear terms.

Only the Descendant has Capricorn more often: the 6th cusp falls in it in roughly one birth in 10 in both cities, in Placidus, the house system I use. Capricorn rises quickly in the north, but below the western horizon it moves slowly, mirroring Cancer's slow climb on the opposite cusps, so it lingers here. The house is close to 30° wide at London, so Capricorn opens only this cusp, and close to a third of the London charts have Gemini and Sagittarius intercepted ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), with Sagittarius inside your 5th, where it makes room to play.

A birth a little earlier gives [Capricorn on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/), a Capricorn Descendant; a little later, [Capricorn on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-5th-house-cusp/). The birth time decides, to the minute, and I don't guess it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
