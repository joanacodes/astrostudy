Some bonds start with a look across a room. Yours more often start across a desk, on a rota, or over a job that needed two pairs of hands: the ruler of your 7th house works in the 6th, the house of the working day.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Equals face each other in the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/): the partner, the associate, the open rival. The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) covers the working day: tasks, habits, colleagues, the upkeep of a body and a life. With the 7th's ruler in the 6th, the matters of partnership are lived in that daily round ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Partnership, here, is a practice. You may meet people at work, or through a routine you share, the same train, the same gym, the same night shift, and what draws you is often competence: someone who turns up and does the job well. Love shows in errands run, a meal ready, a problem quietly fixed. One-to-one work suits you too, the kind where your job is to help one person at a time. What needs watching is equality. Tradition gave the 6th servants and employees and the 7th equals, and this ruler sits on the line between them, so notice when a bond slides from partnership into service, or when one of you does all the upkeep.

Neighbouring houses make no aspect by sign, and tradition says a planet in one can't see the other, a relation it calls [aversion](/astrology/aversion/). Here that describes a partnership whose life is in the doing rather than in talk about the relationship: you may show what someone means to you long before you find the words.

The 6th is where Mars rejoices, in the old scheme of [planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/), and its name, Bad Fortune, is the tradition's, not a verdict. When Libra rises and Aries sets, Mars is your 7th's ruler and thrives here: shared projects, a partner to train with, quarrels settled by getting on with the job. Mercury, when Virgo sets, makes the bond a matter of attention: lists for two, details remembered, help offered before it's asked for. With [Pisces on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/), the daily care is shared between two rulers, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 6th makes generosity a habit, and Neptune's house shows how far you idealise the people you help.

Illness was the 6th's in the old books too, and I leave it there: no chart tells me anything about a partner's health, or yours ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 6th runs up to the Descendant itself, so a ruler near its end had only just set when you were born. Born a few minutes earlier, it would still be above the horizon, in the 7th, where relationships stand on their own ([Ruler of the 7th in the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)); with a later time it slips towards the 5th, where partnership keeps its romance ([Ruler of the 7th in the 5th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)). Minutes matter at both edges ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Nothing here makes you anyone's helper by fate. It says you learn who suits you by working beside them, and that for you, love is something done.
