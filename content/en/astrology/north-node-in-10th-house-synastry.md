With their North Node in your 10th house, the other person's direction of growth sits at the top of your chart: your work, your standing, the role you play in public. Their South Node sits at the bottom, in your 4th, where they feel at home before they've taken their coat off.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) is your public direction: the work you're known for, the responsibility you carry, the reputation you're building, the authority you earn or push against. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) describes the less familiar skill a person is building. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), the skill is stepping out into the world, and your public life is where they watch it done.

For you, they may take a real interest in your career: how you got the job, how you handle a difficult client, how you put yourself forward. Their questions can make your ambitions feel legitimate. Sooner or later, they may also ask you to help them take a step of their own.

For them, a role beyond the family, with responsibility and a name attached, is the stretch, and your example lowers the step. Of the four angular houses, the 10th is the one in plainest view, and [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), its natural sign, belongs to Saturn: structure, and patience with slow results. When their North Node sits on your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), their South Node sits on your IC, and their whole line stands upright in your chart. If about nine years separate your birthdays, their North Node also lies close to your own South Node: the public life they're heading for is the ground you started from.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Your home is another matter: their [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) falls in [the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), and there they need no practice at all. They make themselves comfortable quickly, fit into family life, cook, settle. When the public step looms, they may retreat into domestic comfort, and you may find you're the one urging them out of the door.

Seen from their side, your 10th is a ladder with you already halfway up it. They may measure their progress against yours, or ask you to be their mentor and then bristle at the advice.

This overlay doesn't predict success, promotion or a joint business, and it doesn't make you responsible for their career. It describes where they practise taking responsibility: near you, in plain view.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Support the step without taking it for them. In a couple, celebrate their public wins as much as your evenings in; between friends, introduce them to people, then step back; between colleagues, give them visible work and the credit for it; between adult relatives, recognise their standing outside the family, not only their place in it.

Reverse the roles, your North Node in their 10th house, and you'd be the one learning from their public life. [Their North Node in your 9th house](/astrology/north-node-in-9th-house-synastry/) sits a house earlier, among beliefs; [their North Node in your 11th house](/astrology/north-node-in-11th-house-synastry/) a house later, among friends.

In Placidus, the house system I use, your Midheaven is the cusp of your 10th: the point where the zodiac crossed the meridian when you were born, not the point straight overhead, and only your birth time places it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Their node needs only their birth date, given how slowly it crosses a sign, while their time draws the houses where your own node and planets land. With their node a degree or two from your Midheaven, the mean node and the true node may disagree about whether they're learning from your beliefs or from your career; I look at both. Someone's ambitions aren't mine to read without them, so this waits until you both want it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
