Nobody alive today was born with Neptune in Taurus. The last births with it came in 1889 and the next begin in 2038, so this page describes a placement that no living chart holds: a dream of beauty you can touch.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), fixed earth ruled by Venus, is the sign of the senses: what can be touched, tasted, grown and kept. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) in Taurus looks for transcendence there, inside the material world rather than beyond it: the sacred in bread and soil, beauty in a well-made object, a piece of music that slows the heart. Its compassion takes a physical form, care for land, for bodies, for anything that grows slowly. The sign is fixed, which gives the dream patience: it will wait a season, or many, for something to ripen.

Neptune has no dignity in Taurus. [Venus](/astrology/venus/), the sign's ruler, is the planet that carries this dream, and where Venus sits shows what a Taurus Neptune finds beautiful enough to devote itself to. Coming straight after Aries, Taurus also slows Neptune down. Where the Aries dream wants a first step, the Taurus dream wants to stay: to plant, to keep, to make one place beautiful and look after it for years.

Neptune was last in Taurus from June 1874 to March 1889, crossing back and forth with Aries at the start and with Gemini at the end, and it returns from May 2038 to May 2052. None of the births from 1930 to 2009 have it. In the order of the generations, it comes between [Neptune in Aries](/astrology/neptune-in-aries/), the sign being born now, and [Neptune in Gemini](/astrology/neptune-in-gemini/), which follows from 2051. For the people born with it from 2038, the house will be what makes it personal, in their own time. What follows is how I read the sign in principle.

My [notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) checked Neptune like every placement. It wasn't one of the three that held far more often, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith, and overall about a third held.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, Neptune in Taurus describes a devotion that can be felt: shared meals, a hand held, a home made beautiful together, loyalty that needs no speeches. Tenderness here is physical and patient, and since Venus rules the sign, love and beauty sit close together: someone can be loved for the way they make an ordinary evening feel. The risk it carries is idealising comfort, taking a settled life as proof that the bond is good, or staying inside a pleasant routine long after the feeling has quietly moved somewhere else.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, the placement points to callings where beauty and the senses meet care: gardening and farming, cooking, crafts, instrument-making, music, the arts of material and texture, any work that makes the physical world kinder. Its gift is the patience to let something grow. Its trap is a dream of the perfect, simple life that never quite arrives, while the real work waits.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Every Neptune idealises something, and in Taurus the object is security: the field, the house, the steady routine, the things that stay. Possessions can come to stand for feelings, so that losing an object feels like losing a love. And a fixed sign's dream can harden into a reluctance to change, even when the ground itself has moved.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tending something real is the remedy this sign carries within it: a garden, a craft, a meal cooked slowly for someone. Beauty that is used, not hoarded, keeps the dream alive. And from 2038, Neptune in Taurus will be a transit for everyone alive then, crossing part of every chart. I'll read it the way I read all transits: as weather, not a forecast.
