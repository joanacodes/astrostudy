Two water signs meet in this pair, and the more porous of them holds the Moon. Your Cancer Sun protects a circle of its own; your Pisces Moon can hardly draw a circle at all, and feels for whoever is in front of it. When you were born, the Moon was a few days past full and shrinking.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and mutable water, Pisces four signs before Cancer: a trine by sign, water answering water. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) shelters, remembers and keeps its people near; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) settles through compassion, music, rest and imagination, and finds hard edges painful. Between them there's little friction and a great deal of feeling. The missing piece is a boundary: a clear point at which looking after others stops and looking after yourself begins.

Your Moon's sign has a traditional and a modern ruler, and I read both. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the traditional ruler, is exalted in your Sun's sign, Cancer, which I take as compassion your sense of purpose can lean on. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern ruler, discovered in 1846, shows where this Moon reaches for something larger than itself, and where it can lose its footing. And since Cancer belongs to the Moon, whatever moves your Moon moves your purpose too.

Past full and heading for the last quarter, your Moon rose late in the evening and lingered into the morning; it was 91° to 149° short of catching the Sun again. The eight-phase count gives most of these births to the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) and the earliest to the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/); near 240° the water trine turns into an exact waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). In the disseminating phase I see what has been learned looking for someone to give it to, and with two water signs, what gets handed on is usually comfort. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) describes both phases.

The 1930–2009 count gives one birth in about 137 to this pair. Pisces, the twelfth sign, keeps the Moon about two and a half days, with Aquarius before it and Aries after, and if it changed sign on the day of your birth, only the birth time can show whether the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-aquarius/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-aries/) is the page you need ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign and the houses need that time too; with this much water, I look to them for where the pair finds its shore.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with great tenderness and few conditions. The Cancer Sun keeps a partner sheltered and near; the Pisces Moon wants to merge, to understand, to forgive. Partners may feel more deeply understood than ever before. The risk is losing yourself in someone else's needs, or rescuing people who would rather be met as equals. Keeping a little of your life entirely your own, a friend, a practice, an hour, makes the closeness easier to sustain.

## At work {#at-work}

Caring and imaginative work suits this pair: nursing, midwifery, music, arts therapy, chaplaincy, charity work, the care of the very young or the very old, anything that asks for patience with people at their most vulnerable. Your Sun brings the steadiness of someone who stays, your Moon the gift of sensing what isn't said. Harsh, competitive workplaces exhaust both lights; you do your best work where kindness isn't mistaken for weakness.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

No hard edge stands between these two lights, so saying no can be difficult. The Cancer Sun takes on responsibility; the Pisces Moon takes on feelings; together they can absorb a family's sorrows or a workplace's tensions until there's little room left for your own. Escape can then look tempting, into sleep, screens or daydreams. What helps is a boundary kept on purpose, gently: a time, a place, a word, *not tonight*, that protects the care you give by protecting you.
