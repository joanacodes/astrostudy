Slow to change your mind, quick to change the subject: that's often how this pair looks from outside. The Sun stands in steady Taurus, the Moon one sign on in quick Gemini, and the birth belongs to the first week of a lunar month.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Taurus is fixed earth, Gemini mutable air, and they touch only at their border. The [Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/) takes its time and chooses its loyalties carefully; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) needs words before a feeling makes sense, and stays settled on variety, conversation and news. One light wants depth in one place, the other a little of everything, and both needs are honest. Neither light holds a dignity in its sign.

Both your rulers are planets that stay close to the Sun. [Venus](/astrology/venus/), ruler of Taurus, never gets more than about 47° from it, and [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of Gemini, never more than about 28°. With a Taurus Sun, Mercury sits in Aries, Taurus or Gemini, and when it's in Gemini it keeps your Moon company. Venus shows what you value, Mercury how a feeling becomes a sentence, and when the two get on, this pair can talk its way into steadiness.

A new moon had come and gone no more than five days earlier: the Moon stood 1° to 60° on from the Sun. Most of these births, seven in eight, fall in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase, while the young Moon still sets soon after the Sun; the rest come once the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) has begun. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows where that line falls.

Count everyone born from 1930 to 2009, and about 1 in 144 has this pair. Gemini is a short stop for the Moon, two and a half days or so, and births in the hours around its arrival in Gemini, or its departure for Cancer, need the time to choose between this page, the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-taurus/) and the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-cancer/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also gives the rising sign; with it the chart gets its first three lines, and a Gemini Moon will want to hear the rest.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You need a partner who is both reliable and good company. The Taurus Sun shows love by staying and providing; the Gemini Moon needs to talk, to laugh, to hear what happened in each other's day. When the bond is steady but quiet, you may feel restless without knowing why; when it's lively but unreliable, you may feel unsafe. Name which of the two is missing, plainly, rather than waiting for someone to guess.

## At work {#at-work}

You can make a steady living out of words and ideas, or bring curiosity to work that others find routine. The Sun wants something durable to show for its effort; the Moon wants variety and people to talk to. A stable role with plenty of exchange in it, teaching, selling, advising, editing, writing about what you know, tends to keep both lights content. Endless meetings with nothing to show at the end wear down the Taurus Sun, and months of the same task in silence bore the Gemini Moon.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The pair can pull in opposite directions with very little fuss on the surface. The Taurus Sun digs in; the Gemini Moon gets restless and looks for distraction. You may stay in a situation for its security while your mind has already left, or keep talking about changes you never quite make. What helps is feeding the Moon small, regular changes, a new route home, a new book, a different café, so the Sun can keep its larger loyalties without feeling caged by them.
