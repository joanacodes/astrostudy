Turn the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-taurus/) around and you get this pair: the steady sign now holds your sense of purpose, and the quick one your feelings. That pairing has both lights exalted; this one has neither, and I read it as a long patience with a quick spark inside it.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Here the Moon is 1° to 59° short of catching the Sun, which puts the birth in the last five days of a lunar month, while the old crescent thins towards the new moon. Seven of these births in eight are [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) births and the rest belong to the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/); [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) tells you which. Your Moon is still in the sign the Sun had already left behind, so your feelings carry the starting energy of Aries into a life organised around Taurus's patience.

Earth and fire, fixed and cardinal: neighbours that share no quality at all. The [Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/) builds a self around what it values and keeps; the [Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/) has to act on a feeling to be done with it. That makes for an unusual rhythm: a life that rarely changes course and a mood that may change several times a day, often without the one disturbing the other.

Each light answers to a different ruler, [Venus](/astrology/venus/) for Taurus and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) for Aries, and each ruler has its detriment in the other's sign: Venus is out of place in Aries, Mars in Taurus. I read that as two currencies that don't convert easily, comfort and urgency, pleasure and impulse, not as a fault in you. Venus is always close to a Taurus Sun, no further than Pisces on one side or Cancer on the other, and its sign often shows how the pair makes peace.

The pair belongs to about 1 person in 142. A birth on the day the Moon entered Aries from Pisces, or left it for Taurus, needs the time, since the Moon spends only about two and a half days in a sign: some belong to the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-pisces/), others to the double Taurus of the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-taurus/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign, from the same time, adds the third stroke to the chart's first sketch.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You tend to be loyal for years and cross for minutes. The Taurus Sun commits slowly and stays; the Aries Moon reacts at once, says the sharp thing and has usually forgiven before the other person has finished being hurt. A partner who knows that the flare passes and the loyalty stays will understand you well. You can help by saying, after a spark, *that's done, we're fine*, because from outside nobody can see which of the two lights is speaking.

## At work {#at-work}

You can be steady and fast in the same week: patient with the long build, quick in a crisis. The Sun wants work that accumulates into something solid; the Moon wants a jolt of urgency now and then, a problem to tackle, a deadline to beat. A role with a stable core and the odd sprint suits the pair. Endless urgency wears out the Taurus Sun, and endless routine leaves the Aries Moon fidgeting.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The two speeds can catch each other out. A quick Aries reaction can shake the calm the Taurus Sun has spent years building, and the Sun may then dig in to defend the very thing the Moon just rattled, so that irritation flares and then hardens into a stance. Let the Moon move the body first, a fast walk, an hour of physical work, before the Sun decides anything. A feeling acted on in a minute need not become a position held for a year.
