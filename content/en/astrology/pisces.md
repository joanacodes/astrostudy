Pisces is the twelfth and last sign of the zodiac, a mutable water sign: two fish swimming in opposite directions, tied together by a cord. The Sun leaves it at the March equinox, when the zodiac starts again, and I read it as the sign where edges soften — between you and other people, between what's real and what's imagined.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Pisces somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it asks *what is everyone feeling here, and what lies beyond it?* It's a [water sign](/astrology/water-signs/), so it works through feeling and intuition, and a mutable one, so it adapts, flows and lets go (the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/) explain the difference). [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), just before it, thinks about the many; Pisces feels for them. [Aries](/astrology/aries/), next, begins the whole cycle again with a single, separate self.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/):** born between about 19 February and 20 March. You grow through imagination and compassion. You understand people without being told, and part of your path is learning where you end and they begin.
- **[Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/):** you feel what others feel, sometimes before they do. You need quiet, music, water or time alone to come back to yourself.
- **[Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/):** you meet the world softly and adapt to the room, and people find you gentle and a little hard to pin down. With two rulers, there are two planets to look at as your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/): Jupiter and Neptune. A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. So I read Pisces as one voice in a chart, never the whole of it.

## Its rulers: Neptune and Jupiter {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, and where that planet sits in your chart shows where the sign's energy goes. Pisces has two. For most of astrology's history it was ruled by [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), which also rules Sagittarius: in Sagittarius, Jupiter's faith goes out into the world; in Pisces, it turns into compassion. After [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) was discovered in 1846, modern astrologers came to give Pisces to it, and read it as the planet of dreams, longing and dissolving edges. Traditional astrologers still use Jupiter alone; many modern ones read Neptune first and keep Jupiter as co-ruler ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/) explains the split). Neptune's sign is shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so its house is what makes it yours; Jupiter moves faster and speaks more personally.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is [exalted](/astrology/exaltation/) in Pisces: love at its most generous and forgiving. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is both in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), opposite its home in Virgo, and in [fall](/astrology/fall/), opposite its exaltation in Virgo — the only planet weakened twice in the same sign. That doesn't make a dull mind. It makes one that thinks in images and feelings rather than lists, and has to work at putting what it senses into words.

## Planets in Pisces {#planets-in-the-sign}

Every planet passes through Pisces, from the Moon in about two and a half days to Neptune, which stays about fourteen years. In Pisces, each one softens:

- **[Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/):** a self that grows through imagination, empathy and faith, and has to learn its own outline.
- **[Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/):** feelings that soak up the room; you need retreat to recover, and give comfort without being asked.
- **[Mercury in Pisces](/astrology/mercury-in-pisces/):** Mercury in detriment and fall: thinking in pictures, music and intuition; the work is to put words to what you sense.
- **[Venus in Pisces](/astrology/venus-in-pisces/):** Venus exalted: love as devotion and compassion; forgiving, romantic, and in need of boundaries.
- **[Mars in Pisces](/astrology/mars-in-pisces/):** drive that flows around obstacles rather than through them; you act best for others or for a dream.
- **[Jupiter in Pisces](/astrology/jupiter-in-pisces/):** Jupiter at home in the tradition: generosity, faith and compassion on a large scale.
- **[Saturn in Pisces](/astrology/saturn-in-pisces/):** shared by everyone born over about two and a half years: boundaries learned around compassion, and structure given to dreams.
- **[Uranus in Pisces](/astrology/uranus-in-pisces/):** shared by everyone born over about seven years: sudden insight, and new forms of art and belief. Its house shows where.
- **[Neptune in Pisces](/astrology/neptune-in-pisces/):** Neptune at home in modern astrology: the edges between people, art and belief grow thin. Everyone born with it is still a child, so I'll read it when they're old enough to ask.
- **[Pluto in Pisces](/astrology/pluto-in-pisces/):** no one alive today was born with it. Pluto's last passage through Pisces ended long before any of us arrived, and its next is still years away.

## Pisces on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Pisces somewhere in its twelve houses, usually on the cusp of one of them. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Pisces opens a house, that's where your boundaries are softest and your imagination strongest, and Neptune and Jupiter, wherever they sit, carry the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/pisces-rising/):** Pisces rising — a soft, receptive presence that takes on the colour of the room.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/pisces-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** a fluid relationship with money, generous and sometimes vague; you value what can't be bought.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/pisces-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** imaginative speech, learning through images and music, and an intuitive bond with the people around you.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a refuge from the world; a sensitive family atmosphere you absorbed early.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/pisces-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romance idealised; creativity in music, film, dance or poetry, and play as a way to escape.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work that helps or imagines; routines that need gentle structure, and colleagues whose moods you pick up.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Pisces on the Descendant, which means Virgo rising: you're drawn to gentle, dreamy partners — sometimes ones who need saving — and the work is to see the relationship as it is.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/):** intimacy as merging; shared money with blurred edges; deep empathy in other people's hardest moments.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/):** a spiritual or poetic faith; you learn by intuition, and are drawn to the sea and to places that seem sacred.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career in the arts, care, healing or spirituality, and a public image that's hard to define.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/pisces-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends as kindred spirits; groups with an ideal, and compassion for causes bigger than yourself.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/pisces-on-12th-house-cusp/):** Pisces in its natural house: solitude, imagination and the unseen come naturally, and retreat restores you.

## Pisces in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Pisces placements bring empathy, devotion and imagination, and a way of merging that can blur where one person ends and the other begins. Its opposite sign, [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), is the pull: Virgo sorts and sets limits, Pisces merges and forgives, and together they make care with clear edges. Sun signs alone won't tell you how two people will meet — [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind there is — so I read the two whole charts side by side.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Pisces are pushovers.”** Pisces' softness is empathy, not weakness. Boundaries are something it learns, and the rest of the chart — Mars and Saturn above all — says how firmly.
- **“Pisces is the oldest soul because it's the last sign.”** The order of the signs follows the seasons, starting at the March equinox. Pisces comes last because it closes the cycle, not because the people born under it are older souls.
- **“Pisces just want to escape.”** Pisces needs retreat to recover from feeling so much. Retreat isn't escape: it's how the sign comes back to itself.
- **“Born on 20 or 21 March, you're half Aries.”** At the minute you were born the Sun was in one sign; near a change, your birth time settles which ([born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/)).
