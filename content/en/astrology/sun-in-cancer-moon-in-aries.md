Your purpose is to shelter people; your feelings move like a reflex. You look after your own with great tenderness and defend them with startling speed. You arrived late in the lunar month, near the half moon that rises at midnight.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and cardinal fire, with Aries three signs before Cancer, make a square by sign: two starters with opposite instincts, one for shelter and one for action. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) builds an identity around belonging, memory and care; the [Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/) meets a feeling by doing something about it, straight away. One light starts things in order to protect, the other in order to react, and when someone you love is threatened the two act as one, before you've had time to think.

The rulers knot the pair together. Your Sun's sign belongs to the Moon, and your Moon is in Aries, so the body that steers your purpose is a fiery, impatient one. That Moon answers in turn to [Mars](/astrology/mars/), which the old books consider at its weakest in Cancer, where your Sun is. I read the loop as drive that has to learn tenderness, and tenderness that has to accept a little drive. Tradition is otherwise silent on both lights here: no dignity, no debility.

By the time you were born, the lunar month was nineteen and a half to twenty-four and a half days old, and the Moon trailed the Sun by 62° to 120°. In the eight-phase count, the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) and the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) take these births half each; at 90° behind, the square of signs is exactly a waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). I read the last quarter as friction resolved inwardly, by changing your own priorities, which for this pair often means choosing which fights are really yours. Turn the pair round and you get the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-cancer/), its waxing mirror. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows where these phases sit.

About 1 person in 138 has this pair. The Moon spends some two and a half days in Aries, and if you were born on a crossing day, from Pisces or into Taurus, the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-pisces/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-taurus/) could equally be yours; the time settles it ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With the time come the rising sign and the houses, and without them I can't tell you where in your life this protectiveness does its work.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You are loyal, warm and fiercely on your partner's side. The Cancer Sun remembers what matters to the people it loves and builds a home around them; the Aries Moon says what it feels, sometimes sharply, and is usually over it within the hour. What can confuse a partner is the speed: a flash of temper, then tenderness, then an expectation that the matter is closed. Naming the feeling under the flash, *I was frightened for you*, *I felt left out*, helps the other person catch up.

## At work {#at-work}

You do well where care needs courage: emergency and rescue work, advocacy, running a small business that supports a family, leading a team you would defend in any meeting. For the Sun, work has to shelter someone; for the Moon, it has to offer pace, challenge and the chance to act. Slow bureaucracy frustrates the Moon, and cold, competitive cultures leave the Sun homesick. You thrive where loyalty is mutual and quick decisions are trusted.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The sharpness comes out at home. The Aries Moon reacts fast and the Cancer Sun is most exposed among the people it loves, so the quickest words can land on exactly the people you most want to keep safe. Afterwards the Sun may brood while the Moon has already moved on. What helps is a short, physical pause before the reply, a walk, a breath, a glass of water, and a habit of coming back to repair things the same day, so that the flare stays small and the home stays safe.
