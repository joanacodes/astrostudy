Your purpose is to shelter people; your feelings move like a reflex. With the Sun in Cancer and the Moon in Aries, you look after your own with great tenderness and defend them with startling speed. The birth came around the last quarter, under a Moon that rises near midnight.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and cardinal fire, the Moon three signs back from the Sun: a square by sign, two signs that both take the initiative and disagree about how. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) builds an identity around belonging, memory and care; the [Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/) meets a feeling by doing something about it, straight away. One light starts things in order to protect, the other in order to react, and when someone you love is threatened the two act as one, before you've had time to think.

The rulers knot the pair together. Cancer is ruled by the Moon, so your Moon is also the ruler of your Sun, and it stands in Aries: your sense of direction takes its cues from a fast, fiery Moon. That Moon answers in turn to [Mars](/astrology/mars/), which tradition places in its fall in Cancer, the sign of your Sun. I read the loop as drive that has to learn tenderness, and tenderness that has to accept a little drive. Tradition is otherwise silent on both lights here: no dignity, no debility.

The Moon was 62° to 120° behind the Sun, from about nineteen and a half to twenty-four and a half days after a new moon. Half these births are [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) births and half [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) ones, and close to 90° behind the square of signs becomes an exact waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). I read the last quarter as friction resolved inwardly, by changing your own priorities, which for this pair often means choosing which fights are really yours. Turn the pair round and you get the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-cancer/), its waxing mirror. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows where these phases sit.

About 1 person in 138 has this pair. The Moon stays in Aries for some two and a half days; if it moved in from Pisces or out into Taurus on your birthday, the time decides between this page, the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-pisces/) and the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-taurus/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign comes with that time, and so do the houses; without them, I can't tell you where in your life this protectiveness does its work.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You are loyal, warm and fiercely on your partner's side. The Cancer Sun remembers what matters to the people it loves and builds a home around them; the Aries Moon says what it feels, sometimes sharply, and is usually over it within the hour. What can confuse a partner is the speed: a flash of temper, then tenderness, then an expectation that the matter is closed. Naming the feeling under the flash, *I was frightened for you*, *I felt left out*, helps the other person catch up.

## At work {#at-work}

You do well where care needs courage: emergency and rescue work, advocacy, running a small business that supports a family, leading a team you would defend in any meeting. The Sun wants the work to protect someone; the Moon wants pace, challenge and the chance to act. Slow bureaucracy frustrates the Moon, and cold, competitive cultures leave the Sun homesick. You thrive where loyalty is mutual and quick decisions are trusted.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The sharpness comes out at home. The Aries Moon reacts fast and the Cancer Sun is most exposed among the people it loves, so the quickest words can land on exactly the people you most want to keep safe. Afterwards the Sun may brood while the Moon has already moved on. What helps is a short, physical pause before the reply, a walk, a breath, a glass of water, and a habit of coming back to repair things the same day, so that the flare stays small and the home stays safe.
