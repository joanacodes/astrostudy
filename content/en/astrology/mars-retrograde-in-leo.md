Leo has had two of Mars’s retrogrades since 2009, and the next begins on 10 January 2027. Between them, the loops of 2009–10, 2024–25 and 2027 cross all of Leo but about a degree and a quarter, so almost any point of a chart in Leo is passed over backwards in these years.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Leo](/astrology/leo/) is fixed fire, ruled by the Sun: Mars there acts with heart and likes the effort to show. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when Earth passes the red planet and Mars seems from here to stop and reverse for eleven weeks or so, that wish to shine turns round on itself. What slows is the performance: the project you led so that everyone could see, the stand you took out of pride, the generosity that quietly expected thanks. Anger in Leo is often wounded pride, and these weeks can show where it was hurt. Desire asks whether it wants to be admired or to be met.

The 2009–10 loop turned at 19°41′ Leo on 20 December 2009 and came to rest at 0°17′ Leo on 10 March 2010, with Mars facing the Sun, then in Aquarius, in late January. The most recent began at 6°10′ Leo on 6 December 2024 and ended at 17°00′ Cancer ([Mars retrograde in Cancer](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-cancer/)) on 24 February 2025. The next starts at 10°25′ Virgo ([Mars retrograde in Virgo](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-virgo/)) on 10 January 2027 and turns direct at 20°55′ Leo on 1 April: 81 days, among the longest in the dates I use.

A natal Mars retrograde in Leo is part of who you are rather than a season, and I read it separately ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house Leo occupies depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), which is why I won’t read a chart without one. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2027 loop begins in the house Virgo holds and the 2024–25 one ended in Cancer’s, so a cusp near either end of Leo can split the season in two.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/leo-rising/):** Leo rising: a warm, commanding entrance; the effort you put into looking strong, eased.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/leo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** pride in what you have made your own; the drive to prove your worth, told apart from the worth itself.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** speaking with flair; a dramatic remark to a sibling or neighbour, owned and mended.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home where you hold centre stage; a family role you play loudly, looked at quietly.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** Leo’s natural house: play, romance and creative work, which tradition also gave to children; I read it in an adult’s life and never read a child’s chart ([Children’s charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)): a piece made for applause, returned to for its own sake.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work done with pride; the job you do brilliantly but resent doing alone, shared out.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Leo on the Descendant (Aquarius rising): a partner who shines; a rivalry over who takes the light, talked through.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/leo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** desire and trust; a wish to be adored up close, spoken of plainly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs proclaimed with pride; a cause you championed on stage, reconsidered off it.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Leo on the Midheaven: ambition under a spotlight; a bid for recognition at work, rethought before the next act.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/leo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends who cheer you on; the group you lead, asked what it actually needs from you.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/leo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** pride kept out of sight; old hurts to your dignity, tended in private.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Leo’s fire is warm before it is fierce, so I find these weeks go better when you create with no audience in mind: draw, sing, cook for one person, play. The Sun rules Leo, and the house of your natal Sun shows where your sense of self takes the review; the house of your natal Mars, where your drive sets out from. Before the 2027 loop, Mars first crosses the degrees it will later retrace, the stretch astrologers call [the shadow period](/astrology/shadow-period/), a quiet time to notice what you are pushing for.

None of this decides whether a performance lands or a rivalry ends. It describes a season in which the boldest of the fire signs asks who its courage is for.
