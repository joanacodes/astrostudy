Twice in every 18.6 years, the North Node comes to stand a third of the circle away from your rising degree. For about a month, the direction you are still practising and the way you enter a room keep step.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) sets the angle you come in at: the stance taken before you've thought, the opening line, the reflex of a first meeting. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) isn't a body but the point where two paths cross, and it marks the capacity your chart has used least, the one practice builds. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets them cooperate without merging, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the newer capacity can be tried from your usual way in, with no change of manner needed first.

In practice it tends to look like a first move made differently, and without fuss: introducing yourself by what you're learning rather than by what you already know, or starting a conversation you'd normally wait to be invited into. The node's square to your Ascendant asks your manner to adjust ([Transiting North Node square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-ascendant/)), its sextile asks you to take an opening ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-ascendant/)); the trine asks nothing. That is its gift and its catch, since a month in which nothing pushes can pass unused.

The other end of the axis joins in. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) stands 60° from your Ascendant and 120° from your Descendant, so the two nodes and the two ends of your horizon draw the figure astrologers call a [mystic rectangle](/astrology/mystic-rectangle/): two oppositions tied together by trines and sextiles. Your oldest ways with other people come easily in these weeks, and familiar strengths can hold the door open for the newer skill, as long as they don't go through it instead.

In Placidus, the house system I use, one trine usually comes while the node is crossing your 9th or 8th house ([North Node transiting the 9th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-9th-house/)), the other while it crosses your 5th or 4th ([North Node transiting the 5th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-5th-house/)). The house shows where to look for the ease.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My window opens 1½° before exact and closes 1° after. Half of all passes are through it within 34 days, and nineteen in twenty touch the exact trine just once. A tenth take 25 days or less; a tenth take more than 100, and those are the ones an eclipse season catches, because the true node barely moves while the Sun is close to it. Three exact hits, spread over some 100 days, come in about one pass in twenty-five. Whatever eclipse that season brings falls close to one node or the other, a long way from your Ascendant and Descendant, and I count an eclipse only within about three degrees of a point or of the degree opposite ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

By 80, everyone has had this trine eight or nine times. The first of a round comes about six years after the node crosses your rising degree ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)), the second about three years after it reaches your Descendant ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-ascendant/)). You won't find it in the forecast I build readings from: there the node appears only when one end of its axis sits on a point of your chart. I bring it up when someone asks about those particular weeks, as a description of their ease.

All of it hangs on your birth time. At London, four minutes move the Ascendant by less than a degree with Libra rising and by more than two with Aries rising, and the node needs about nineteen days, at its average pace, to cover a degree: the same four minutes shift this month by two weeks or by six. Without the time there is no month to name, and no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Try the new approach where you're already welcome: a group that knows you, a place where a fumble costs nothing. A trine rewards practice in friendly conditions.

Notice your first move in a new situation, the joke, the question, the silence, and once a week this month make a different one on purpose.

Let an old strength make the introduction. Use what people already trust in you to get into the room, then let the newer skill do the talking.

Write down what came easily. Ease is quickly forgotten, and the list is what you'll practise from once the month is over.
