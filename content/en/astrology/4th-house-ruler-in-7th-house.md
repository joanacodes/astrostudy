Two names on the door: that's often how home looks here. Belonging is something you find, and build, with another person, since the ruler of your roots sits in the house of partners.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) begins at the IC and covers home, family and the private self; [the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) begins at the Descendant and covers partners: a spouse or long-term partner, a close collaborator, anyone met as an equal. Here the first is ruled from the second ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), so the bottom of the chart answers to the western horizon.

So home is something you make with someone. Living with a partner, or wanting to, can matter more than the house itself; a partner may become the person who feels most like family, and their people may become yours. Some find their sense of belonging only once they share a life; others bring family into a working partnership, a business run with a spouse, a home that doubles as an office for two. The challenge is placing the whole of home in one relationship, so that when it wobbles, the ground does too.

Both houses begin at angles, the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) at the bottom of the chart and the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) on the western horizon, so the ruler of one angle stands at the next, and the link carries weight. They lie a quarter of the wheel apart, a square in the old count: home and partnership can pull at each other, the private need against the shared plan, and much of the work is letting each have its say.

Partners often show us what we can't yet see in ourselves, and with the ruler of home in their house, a partner can also mirror the family: someone whose ways feel familiar from the start, for better and for worse. As an adult you get to notice which patterns you brought with you, and which you'd rather not repeat.

With Taurus or Libra on the IC, Venus, the 7th's natural ruler, rules home from the partner's house: a home built on courtesy, shared taste and the art of living together. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn IC from the 7th, takes a partnership on as a commitment to build something that lasts. Under a Pisces IC, home has two rulers, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): with Jupiter in the 7th, a partner may be the one who widens your idea of home, while Neptune softens it from another house.

None of this says whether or when you'll marry, or whom; I read it as a way of belonging, not an event ([How I read](/how-i-read/)).

Next door, the same ruler tells other stories: in the 6th, home runs on routine and care ([Ruler of the 4th in the 6th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)); in the 8th, it holds what the family shares and passes on ([Ruler of the 4th in the 8th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). The 7th puts it in a partner's hands, and placing the Descendant to the degree takes the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
