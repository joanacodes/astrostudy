Talk, study, neighbours, brothers and sisters: the house of the everyday mind is where the ruler of your partnerships has settled. Who you pair with, and how, tends to be decided in words.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Every one-to-one bond, from a long marriage to a business partnership to the adversary across a negotiation, belongs to the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/). The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is the world within reach: how you think and speak, what you learn, your siblings, cousins and neighbours, the errands and local comings and goings. With its ruler in the 3rd, the 7th's affairs are carried on in that nearby, talkative world ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

I read it first as a matter of language. Partnership lives or dies, for you, on whether you can talk: you want someone who answers, argues back, sends you the article you'll both enjoy. People often enter your life by the ordinary routes, a course, a shared commute, a neighbour, a sibling's friend, and the relationship keeps some of that ease. Negotiation suits you, since the 3rd likes a good exchange of views; so does any partnership built on writing, teaching or passing on information. The risk is that everything gets discussed and nothing gets felt, or that a partner becomes the person you tell things to and stops being the one you live them with.

Siblings can figure in it as well. A brother or sister may introduce you to people, work beside you, or be the first person you learned to share a room and a life with. I read that from your side only: a sibling's own life belongs to their chart.

Old horary lists put letters and paperwork in the 3rd, contracts included ([horary astrology](/astrology/horary-astrology/)), and the 7th is the house of agreements, so this ruler often shows in the written side of a bond: terms spelled out, messages kept, the habit of putting things in writing. That isn't a forecast of a dispute; I never read a chart for how an agreement would fare in court ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)).

Counted round the wheel, the 3rd and the 7th are a trine apart, and in the natural zodiac they belong to Gemini and Libra, two air signs: an easy line between talking and relating. The Moon has its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) in the 3rd, the house tradition called the Goddess. With Cancer on the Descendant and Capricorn rising, the Moon rules your 7th from there and is at ease: you bond through daily contact, small news, the call at the end of the day.

Other rulers change the tone. Mars, ruling an Aries Descendant with Libra rising, makes conversation a debate you both enjoy; Saturn, ruling a Capricorn one, prefers few words that hold. [Pisces on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/) splits the job between Jupiter and Neptune, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 3rd gives you a partner to learn with, while Neptune, wherever it stands, adds what you hope a partner will understand without being told.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 3rd ends at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the chart's lowest point, and every cusp of it comes from the birth time. A little later, and the ruler might sit in the 2nd, where partnership turns to money and values ([Ruler of the 7th in the 2nd house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)); born earlier, it wouldn't yet have passed that low point, and would sit in the 4th, where it turns towards home ([Ruler of the 7th in the 4th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/)). Only the recorded time can say which ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

So listen to how you talk with the people closest to you. With this ruler, that's where a partnership is made, and where it's mended.
