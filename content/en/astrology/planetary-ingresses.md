Sooner or later every planet leaves one sign for the next: the Moon every two and a half days, Pluto a few times a century. That crossing is an ingress. From 2026 to 2030, leaving the Moon aside, the sky holds 249 of them.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

An ingress is the moment a planet reaches 0° of a sign ([0° of a sign](/astrology/0-of-a-sign/)). How often it comes depends on the planet's speed. In the site's list for the five years from 2026 to 2030:

- **Mercury:** 74.
- **Venus:** 62.
- **The Sun:** 60, twelve a year, four of them the equinoxes and solstices ([solstices and equinoxes](/astrology/solstices-and-equinoxes/)).
- **Mars:** 37.
- **Jupiter:** 5.
- **Saturn, Chiron and the North Node:** 3 each.
- **Uranus and Neptune:** 1 each.
- **Pluto:** none, since it stays in Aquarius until 2043.

Not every crossing goes forwards. Fourteen of the 249 happen backwards, while a planet is retrograde: seven of Mercury's ([Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/)), two of Mars's, one of Venus's, one of Chiron's, and all three of the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/)'s, because the Node moves backwards through the signs.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) shows what a backwards ingress does. It entered Taurus on 19 June 2026, slipped back into Aries on 18 September 2026 and returns to Taurus on 14 April 2027. A first entry is often a preview: a slow planet usually crosses a border more than once before it stays.

The slow planets' ingresses in these years, in the dates this site keeps:

- **[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/),** about once a year, each a month or so later than the last: Leo on 30 June 2026, Virgo on 26 July 2027, Libra on 24 August 2028, Scorpio on 24 September 2029, Sagittarius on 22 October 2030.
- **[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/),** every two years or so: Aries on 14 February 2026, Taurus on 13 April 2028, Gemini on 1 June 2030.
- **Neptune and Uranus,** each settling into a new sign after a first crossing in 2025: Aries on 26 January 2026 ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)), Gemini on 26 April 2026 ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)).
- **The North Node,** backwards: Aquarius on 27 July 2026, Capricorn on 27 March 2028, Sagittarius on 23 September 2029.

When a slow planet changes sign, births on either side of the date carry different signs for it. Over the decades, those dates draw the boundaries of [generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/).

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In the system I built for my readings, a planet is a function, a sign a manner and a house a context. An ingress leaves the function as it was and changes the manner: Saturn still builds structure in Taurus, but by asking how something can last rather than how to begin it. It also moves the planet's transits on to the house of your chart that holds the new sign, which needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and towards any natal planet in that sign's first degrees, the first it will meet.

I time readings with the slow planets, Jupiter to Pluto, Chiron and the North Node, so the ingresses of the Sun, Mercury and Venus, which come and go within weeks, rarely enter a reading. Mundane astrology, which judges the affairs of nations, counts ingresses among the phenomena it reads, with eclipses, lunations, great conjunctions and stations. I read personal charts, and no ingress tells me what will happen ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“An ingress is an event.”** It is a planet crossing a line of the zodiac. What changes is a manner and a stretch of your chart, not your fate.
- **“A first entry into a sign is final.”** Slow planets often cross a border two or three times before they stay.
- **“Every ingress sets the tone for the month.”** The fast planets' ingresses pass within weeks. The slow planets' set a background for years, and those are the ones I follow.
- **“Everyone feels an ingress the same way.”** The sign is shared; the house it falls in and the natal planets it meets are yours.
