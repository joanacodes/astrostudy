Hope can work as a bandage. When Chiron squares your Jupiter, a belief, a plan or a cheerful certainty that grew around an old sore spot meets the limit it was covering, and the question becomes how big that hope can honestly be.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the reach of a chart: growth, learning, faith in where things are going. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the old difficulty that persists and the way you've learned to function around it. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates them through friction that asks both to adjust, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) expansion and limitation keep catching on each other.

The friction often shows as a plan that has grown too large for the life it has to fit: a course of study, a move, a project, a promise to yourself that everything will be fine once this one thing works. Or a belief that has been doing a lot of work, that the hurt happened for a reason, that you should be over it by now, stops quite convincing you. Jupiter likes a meaning; Chiron knows that not every difficulty has to become wisdom. The square asks for a hope you can live inside: smaller where it was inflated, still there where it matters.

Nothing here predicts a loss, of money, faith or opportunity, and I never read Jupiter's transits for fortune. If disappointment turns into despair, that's for a doctor or a therapist rather than a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

With [Jupiter in the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-7th-house/), where you hope for a great deal from partners, Chiron is usually crossing your 4th ([Chiron transiting the 4th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-4th-house/)), and those hopes meet an old ache about home and belonging. Your 7th and 4th begin at the Descendant and the IC, both fixed by the time of birth; without it I leave the square unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Jupiter square Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-square-chiron/) at birth, the gap between hope and limit is an old companion, and the transit asks you to measure it again.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A plan that needs resizing usually gets most of a year. With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the square to Jupiter typically lasts about ten months, Chiron crossing it three times in nearly two passes out of three. About one pass in three is a quick single crossing of some three weeks, and about one in twenty a slow five-crossing season of some twenty-one months, time enough to rebuild a hope from the ground.

Because Jupiter changes sign about once a year, the first square comes at ages that differ widely. In the site's count it came at about 10½ in the middle, usually before adulthood, and as late as about 37: at about 6 for someone born at the start of 1945, 12 for 1985, 20 for 1955, nearly 28 for 1965 and about 35 for 2005. Most people meet three by 80, a few two, a few four.

The first square of a round follows [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Chiron sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-jupiter/); the second comes after [transiting Chiron opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-jupiter/) and the second [transiting Chiron trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-jupiter/). Coming up: Chiron in Taurus, June 2026 to May 2034, squares Jupiter in Leo and Aquarius; Chiron in Gemini, July 2033 to April 2039, Jupiter in Virgo and Pisces.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Take one plan and draw it at its true size, with the limit included.

Let one question stay open. Not every hurt needs a moral to be lived with.

Ask advice from someone who has carried the same difficulty for longer than you.

Keep the hope, drop the promise. A hope can bend; a promise to yourself tends to break.
