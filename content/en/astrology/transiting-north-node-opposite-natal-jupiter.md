About nine years after the North Node crosses your Jupiter, it reaches the degree opposite, and the South Node takes its place on your Jupiter. For a month or so, the way you have always grown, believed and made sense of things sits at the familiar end of the axis, with the less practised direction facing it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is where a chart widens its understanding: learning, principles, the search for meaning, the sense of what a life could grow into. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a capacity built by practising it, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), exactly opposite, one you already have. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two things through polarity and awareness, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your Jupiter stands at the consolidated end while the end of practice looks at it from across the chart.

In plain words, your habitual way of growing comes up for a look. It may be a belief you settled into long ago, a teacher you still quote, the reflex of reaching for the big picture whenever the detail gets uncomfortable, or a generosity you offer before anyone has asked. None of it is wrong, and nothing asks you to give it up. The season shows where Jupiter has become automatic. The other end often speaks through people: a view you don't share, a question your usual books don't answer.

The houses say where. With Jupiter in your 9th house, the South Node crosses your 9th while the North Node crosses your 3rd ([North Node transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-3rd-house/)), and the big answers you trust meet months of asking, listening and checking small facts. Houses come from the birth time, which is why I don't read this transit, or any other, without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Jupiter's reputation for luck makes some people hope for a windfall here, or fear losing one. I don't read any transit for money ([Can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)), and this one describes a review of how you grow, not a turn of fortune.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

I count a transit from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and the true node usually crosses that window in about a month, 32 days in the middle case. If an eclipse season falls in those weeks, the Sun's alignment with the nodes holds the node almost still and the pass runs to three months or more; about one pass in 25 crosses the exact degree three times, over some three and a half months. The eclipses of such a season fall close to the axis through your Jupiter, and one within about 3° of it is read as a contact of its own ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

It comes once a round, so by 80 everyone has met it four or five times. The first depends on where the node stood when you were born: for someone born on 1 January 1965 it came at 11.1, for 1 January 1975 at 4.4, and for births from 1930 to 2009 at any age from a few weeks to about 18½. With [Jupiter opposite North Node](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-north-node/) in your chart, your Jupiter already sits on your natal South Node, so the nodes' return to their birth places brings this transit with it.

Nine years or so before it, the North Node itself crossed your Jupiter ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-jupiter/)). Between the two, on the way out and again on the way back, come [Transiting North Node sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-jupiter/), [Transiting North Node square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-jupiter/) and [Transiting North Node trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-jupiter/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one belief you live by and haven't questioned in years. Put it to someone who sees things differently, and hear the whole answer before you reply.

Look across the chart. The sign and house opposite your Jupiter show the direction being practised; give it an hour a week, as a beginner would.

Keep what Jupiter has taught you. Wisdom that has worked is the South Node's to offer, and the North Node asks you to carry it somewhere new rather than set it down.
