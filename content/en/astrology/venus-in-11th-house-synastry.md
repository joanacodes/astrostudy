Their Venus in your 11th house is friendship with a glow. The other person likes your friends, fits easily into your circle and shares your hopes, and whatever else you are to each other, a real friendship tends to run underneath.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

Friends you choose, groups and networks, the causes you join and the future you hope for belong to [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/). [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is affinity and appreciation, the sense of being among your own kind. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), it brings their warmth into your circle.

You may find that they get on with your friends at once, turn up to the group outing and make it better, and take your hopes seriously. Plans for the future feel lighter when they share them. In a couple, this overlay often means you're friends as well as partners, which is worth more than it sounds.

They feel welcome in your circle and enjoy the people around you. Your ideals appeal to them, and being part of your community gives them a sense of belonging. The 11th is succedent; tradition named it the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy there. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), its natural sign, had Saturn for its ruler in the old scheme and has Uranus in the modern one, and I keep both in view: loyalty and independence together.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Affection spread across a group can be thin on its own. You may find you see them mostly in company, or that they're closer to your friends than to you; group harmony can also become a reason not to raise a personal difficulty.

They may feel they're one of many, liked but never singled out, or that you keep them in the friend role when they'd like more, or less. Shared ideals can also paper over a difference in what each of you actually wants.

This overlay doesn't decide whether a friendship turns into romance or a romance settles into friendship. The 11th shows where affection meets your community and your hopes, not what the bond will become.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Protect some time for just the two of you, away from the group. Talk about what you each hope for, and check that the hopes are really shared. With a partner, keep friends in common and also a life that's only yours. With a friend, this is the easy, warm kind of friendship that lasts. At work, a colleague who makes the team better, in networks that are a pleasure to belong to. Among adult relatives, the cousin who is also a friend.

With your Venus in their 11th house instead, their friends and their hopes are what you warm to. The neighbouring overlays shift the focus: [their Venus in your 10th house](/astrology/venus-in-10th-house-synastry/) admires your public life, and [their Venus in your 12th house](/astrology/venus-in-12th-house-synastry/) loves you quietly, out of sight.

Venus turns retrograde about every eighteen months and can then stay in one sign for months; even so, it moves only a little in a day, and their birth date places it. Your 11th opens and closes where your birth time puts it, as every house does in Placidus, the house system I use ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Friendship doesn't make someone's chart fair game: both people agree, or I don't read the pair ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#the-question)).
