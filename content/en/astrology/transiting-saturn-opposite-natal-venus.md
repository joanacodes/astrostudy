Halfway round its cycle, about fifteen years after crossing your Venus, Saturn stands directly opposite it. What you love and what you owe face each other across the chart, and more often than not the conversation happens through other people.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs value and closeness: what you're drawn to, what you give, whether the exchange feels fair. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) carries duty, rules and time. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, two ends of one axis that need each other. Polarity isn't contradiction, and this transit rarely asks you to choose between love and responsibility; it asks you to see both clearly and find where they balance.

In a relationship it often brings the terms into view: who adapts to whom, whose timetable wins, what was promised and what was only assumed. With friends or family it can show as an obligation taken on out of affection that now needs a clearer shape. It can also be a season when someone more serious than you, an older relative, a demanding partner, a formal setting, asks what you really value. None of this is a break-up foretold. I don't read [transits](/astrology/transits/) for separations or reunions: the opposition shows a seesaw, and the people on it decide.

Across the axis, two houses talk. With Venus in your 4th and Saturn crossing your 10th ([Saturn transiting the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-10th-house/)), the home and the people you love sit across from what work or a public role demands. Your birth time draws that axis, and I don't read the transit without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Saturn crosses Aries until April 2028, so the people meeting this opposition now have Venus in Libra: Venus in one of its own signs, faced by Saturn in the sign tradition calls its fall.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

When Saturn's retrograde loop falls on the point, as it does in a little over half of its transits, the opposition comes in three passes over about nine and a half months: forward, back, forward again. A single pass can be over in three weeks, and most of these transits last about nine months with my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after.

The opposition is the far point of the round that [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-venus/) opens. On the way out come [transiting Saturn sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-venus/), [transiting Saturn square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-venus/) and [transiting Saturn trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-venus/); on the way back, the same three in reverse. Once a round makes two or three oppositions by 80, and everyone meets at least two.

In a birth chart the same axis is [Venus opposite Saturn](/astrology/venus-opposite-saturn/), carried for life rather than for a season. Born with Venus beside Saturn instead, you meet this transit when Saturn faces its own birth place, in your mid-teens and again around 44 ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say what you need, plainly and early. The opposition puts the other person's needs in full view; yours deserve the same light.

Separate duty from love. Some obligations are acts of affection and some have outlived it: name which is which.

Take turns. On a seesaw, the fair arrangement is often alternation rather than a permanent compromise.
