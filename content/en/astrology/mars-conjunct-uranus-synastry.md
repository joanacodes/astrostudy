You mention, half joking, that the whole system could be done differently; by the weekend they've started doing it. In Mars conjunct Uranus in synastry, the urge to change and the drive to act share a single stretch of the zodiac, one person bringing each.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the part of a person that acts: it starts, pushes, competes and defends what it's after. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and reforms: innovation, experiment, autonomy, the wish to change what no longer fits. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) lets two functions fire together. In [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), one person brings the push and the other the new idea, and the process runs between them.

Seven years of births share a sign of Uranus; Mars, which changes sign every six or seven weeks, is what picks out a pair. One person's Mars on the other's Uranus in Capricorn shows the flavour: effort with a long aim lands on a wish to rebuild structures from inside. Work processes, family arrangements, the way a club is run: things get redesigned.

If the Uranus is yours, your ideas stop being ideas. Someone acts on them, quickly, and you may be delighted, or startled at how fast your "what if" became a plan. If the Mars is yours, your energy finds a new direction, and you may feel more original in their company than you do elsewhere.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

At this distance, nobody can quite say whose change it is. The Mars person can run with the Uranus person's idea before it's ready, or in a direction they never meant; the Uranus person can find their freedom turned into someone else's project. Speed is the other snag: change done fast can skip what still works, and Uranus's blind spot, underestimating the value of continuity, meets Mars's readiness without a pause.

From the Mars side, you may start on an idea only to hear it's been improved since breakfast. From the Uranus side, you may feel you can't think aloud without someone starting to build.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Separate thinking aloud from deciding. If the Uranus is yours, say which ideas are for acting on and which are for turning over; if the Mars is yours, ask which kind this is before you start. Agree what stays the same while something changes. Between friends, that could be a new venture with the old friendship left as it is; in a family, a long-overdue change to how holidays are shared, made with everyone; at work, a reform tried in one team before the whole office; in a couple, one change at a time.

About 1 pair of people in 15 has this conjunction, either way round, at up to 6° from exact. Because Uranus moves so slowly, the hour of birth has almost no say in whether the conjunction is there; it has every say in the houses, which place the change in a particular part of each life, and no synastry of mine begins until both times are in hand ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Sun instead of Mars, [Sun conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) touches who someone is rather than what they do.

One person can carry this meeting alone, as [Mars conjunct Uranus](/astrology/mars-conjunct-uranus/): push and inventiveness in a single chart. Between two charts:

- **[Mars sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-uranus-synastry/):** the new idea and the effort cooperate at a short distance, when someone reaches for them.
- **[Mars square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-uranus-synastry/):** push and change keep interrupting each other, and the pair learns to finish what it starts.
- **[Mars trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-uranus-synastry/):** action and invention in one element, at ease rather than at full speed.
- **[Mars opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-uranus-synastry/):** drive and freedom facing each other across a seesaw.
