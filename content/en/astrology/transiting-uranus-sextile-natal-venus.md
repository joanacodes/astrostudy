Uranus sextiles your Venus on the way in, fourteen years or so before crossing it, and again on the way out, fourteen years after. Each time, for about twenty months, pleasure and novelty stop competing, and something new turns out to be enjoyable rather than unsettling.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how you value and relate: attraction, preference, reciprocity, the pleasure you take in people and things. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) adds originality and room to experiment. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, links two functions through an opportunity that has to be met halfway, so here the new arrives as an invitation rather than a disturbance, and accepting it is up to you.

As with the natal [Venus sextile Uranus](/astrology/venus-sextile-uranus/), the opening is social as often as romantic: a friend who introduces you to a new crowd or a new style, a group that becomes a source of pleasure, a collaboration that's simply fun. In a couple, this can be a good season for novelty without strain: a new shared interest, a trip unlike the usual, time apart that makes the time together better.

Nothing is delivered. A sextile rewards the call you make and the invitation you accept, and passes quietly if you do neither. If there's a risk, it's spreading yourself thin: new friends and pleasures are easy to collect under a sextile from Uranus, and the season is kinder to a few chosen ones than to a crowd.

With Venus in your 2nd and Uranus crossing your 4th ([Uranus transiting the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-4th-house/)), the things you own and love get a new setting: a home rearranged to look like who you are now, beauty brought in rather than bought in. Both houses are set by the hour of your birth, which is why I need it before I read this sextile ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

About twenty months is the usual length within my orbs, which run from 1½° before exact to 1° after; some passes are over in eleven. Uranus makes three exact contacts in more than nine passes out of ten, so an invitation turned down at the first can come round again.

One sextile comes in the run-up to [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-venus/) and one in its wake. Further from the conjunction lie [transiting Uranus square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-venus/), about twenty years from it, [transiting Uranus trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-venus/), about 28, and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-venus/), at the far end of the round. If you reach 80, you'll have had one of these sextiles at the very least, and most likely both: of all the Uranus [transits](/astrology/transits/) to Venus, they're among the easiest to use.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Accept the invitation. The sextile's openings tend to arrive as small offers: a dinner, a class, an introduction.

Bring one new thing into an old bond. A shared experiment keeps freshness inside a relationship instead of sending you to look for it elsewhere.

Make something beautiful. Venus is aesthetic judgement as much as affection, and this transit favours your own hands on it: a garden, a room, a piece of music.
