Chiron sextile Lilith opens a working line between the way you adapt and the part of you that won't be tamed. Used, it tends to give you a plain, unapologetic way of asking for what a lasting difficulty needs.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/), the centaur between Saturn and Uranus, describes adaptation: building a skill, a routine or a tool around a limitation that persists, so that the limitation needn't win or vanish. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the averaged far point of the Moon's orbit, is read by many astrologers as instinct, autonomy, authenticity and the parts of a person that were pushed aside; schools differ on its weight, so I keep it symbolic. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through cooperation and opportunity: each can switch the other on, if you let it.

In plain words, your adaptations and your refusals are on speaking terms. The instinct that guards your boundaries can help you ask, directly, for the accommodation you need; the work-arounds you've built can leave room for the part of you that won't fit the usual shape. Saying what you need, without apology and without fuss, is the skill this sextile offers.

It only works if you use it. A sextile is an invitation, not a guarantee: left alone, you may keep adapting quietly and hiding the rest, as if the two had never been introduced.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A sextile joins signs two apart, of elements that sit well together. With Chiron in Pisces and Lilith in Taurus, for example, adapting through imagination and compassion meets an instinct for what is solid and your own, and each steadies the other. Chiron's sign is shared by everyone born over a few years and Lilith's changes every nine months or so, so the sextile comes in two windows of a few months in each round of roughly a decade.

The houses name the two areas that can help each other, and they need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron in the 3rd with Lilith in the 5th might let a way of speaking you had to adapt make room for play that refuses to be polite.

About 1 person in 17 is born with Chiron and Lilith within 6° of a sextile, counted with the mean Lilith. Check which Lilith your software shows, since the true one can sit far from the mean and change the aspect completely ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

Alongside the sextile, the pair's other angles:

- **[Chiron conjunct Lilith](/astrology/chiron-conjunct-lilith/):** the two in one place, adapting and refusing as a single gesture.
- **[Chiron square Lilith](/astrology/chiron-square-lilith/):** the same pair in friction, each accommodation testing your autonomy.
- **[Chiron trine Lilith](/astrology/chiron-trine-lilith/):** a wider, easier flow, with less effort needed to start it.
- **[Chiron opposite Lilith](/astrology/chiron-opposite-lilith/):** the two facing each other, often through the people around you.

Between two charts, [Chiron sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/chiron-sextile-lilith-synastry/) is a gentle contact: one person's way of adapting meets the other's untamed side with curiosity rather than pressure. It's at its best when both say plainly what they need, and I read it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), Lilith sextiles your natal Chiron twice in each nine-year round, for a couple of weeks, and if you were born with the sextile, every Lilith return lays the same opening over your chart again.

The slower visit is Chiron's. Around 50, the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/) brings Chiron back to its birth place and so back into sextile with your Lilith, a season that can last from months to a couple of years.

Weather, not a forecast: these are good moments to ask for what you need, and to notice afterwards whether you did.
