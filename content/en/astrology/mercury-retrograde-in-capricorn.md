Mercury turns back in Capricorn around the turn of the year, between early December and early February, so its weeks often overlap the moment people make plans for the twelve months ahead. In the sign of the long climb, that’s apt: the plan itself is what comes up for review.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) is cardinal earth, ruled by Saturn, and gives Mercury a structured, practical way of thinking: plans in steps, words that mean what they say. When [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) falls here, the planet seems from Earth to halt and back down the slope, and the structure is reread: the schedule, the commitments, the five-year plan, the job description you wrote for yourself. Mercury holds no dignity in Capricorn; rather than tearing anything down, I read these weeks as checking the foundations before the next storey goes on.

From 2000 to 2040, seventeen retrogrades touch Capricorn, and six calendar years, 2003, 2009, 2016, 2022, 2029 and 2036, have two: one in January, another in December that starts within about a third of a degree of where January’s had ended. The most recent ran from 13 December 2023 to 2 January 2024, from 8°29′ Capricorn back to 22°10′ Sagittarius ([Mercury retrograde in Sagittarius](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-sagittarius/)). The next comes from 7 to 27 January 2029, starting at 3°38′ Aquarius ([Mercury retrograde in Aquarius](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-aquarius/)) and ending at 17°32′ Capricorn; then, from 22 December 2029 to 11 January 2030, Mercury stays wholly in Capricorn, from 17°42′ back to 1°26′.

Mercury retrograde in Capricorn at birth is a lasting part of a chart rather than a season, a mind that checks its plans inside before committing to them ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Only [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) shows which house it crosses: the houses turn with the hours, so without the time I can’t place them, and I don’t read a chart that way. In Placidus, the house system I use, a retrograde of about 16° may span two houses; the degrees of the stations decide.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/capricorn-rising/):** Capricorn rising: your reserved, capable manner; the responsibilities you’ve taken on in public, counted again.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** security built slowly; the long-term plan for what’s yours, checked line by line.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** careful words and methodical learning; a duty to a brother, sister or neighbour, talked through.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as something you build and maintain; the household’s plans for the year, set again.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasure you have to give yourself permission for; a creative discipline taken up again.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a strong work ethic; the standards you hold your days to, adjusted so that they leave you room too.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn on the Descendant, with Cancer rising: steady, serious partnership; the commitments you’ve built together, reviewed together.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-8th-house-cusp/):** caution with what’s shared; slow trust, and the terms of an arrangement, reread.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs that have to work in practice; a long course of study or a tradition you follow, reconsidered.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn in its natural house: ambition on a long timetable; the career plan reread before the next step.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a few chosen friends; the group you organise and its long-term goals, revised.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a strength others don’t see; long-held private plans, quietly redrawn.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Treat these weeks as an audit rather than a demolition: check each part of the plan and keep what holds; nothing in the sky says when you may act on it. Saturn rules Capricorn, and the house of your natal Saturn shows where the long view is drawn; I read Saturn as a timetable, not a wall.

A plan for the year written during these weeks has simply been through two drafts, which in Capricorn’s eyes is no bad thing.
