The full moon in Cancer is the Moon shining in its own sign, opposite a Sun in Capricorn, and it is the first full moon after the December solstice. It sets home, family and what you need to feel safe face to face with work, duty and the long term.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) happens when the Moon faces the Sun across the sky, lit in full. In [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) it is at home: Cancer is the sign the Moon rules, so its needs speak clearly here, for shelter, closeness, the people you call yours. The Sun sits at the same degree of [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), where the month's direction is set by work, responsibility and what you're building. The axis runs from the private to the public, and this full moon tends to show what one has been costing the other: the evenings that work took, or the ambition shelved to keep everyone comfortable. Neither end wins by being given up. Your natal [Moon](/astrology/moon/) and your [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the rulers of the two signs, show where the sides meet in your chart.

This is still a marker of a few days. I give it weight when one end of the axis touches a natal planet or angle, within about a degree, and only as the moment something already active, by transit, progression or solar arc, comes to a head. It forecasts nothing.

Last time, on 3 January 2026, it fell at 13°02′ Cancer. The next falls on 24 December 2026 at 01:28 UT, at 2°14′, which in the Americas is still 23 December. 2027 has none: the following one waits until 12 January 2028, at 21°27′, a partial [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/). Then comes 31 December 2028, at 10°33′, a total lunar eclipse, so 2028 has two full moons in Cancer and both are eclipses. The last, on 19 January 2030, falls at 29°37′.

Those eclipses belong to a series on the Cancer–Capricorn axis ([eclipse in Cancer](/astrology/eclipse-in-cancer/)). For an eclipse my orb is wider, about 3°, and it can touch either end; the natal point it meets, with its house, is what I read, as emphasis rather than omen. Two weeks after the total one, on 14 January 2029, the [new moon in Capricorn](/astrology/new-moon-in-capricorn/) is a partial solar eclipse; in other years too, the two fall within the same solar month. A month earlier is the [full moon in Gemini](/astrology/full-moon-in-gemini/); a month later, the [full moon in Leo](/astrology/full-moon-in-leo/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Your chart's Cancer house takes the Moon and its Capricorn house the Sun. Both hang on your birth hour; [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) has the detail. Placidus, which I use, makes the cusps of the 4th and 10th the IC and the Midheaven, so with Cancer on either, this full moon lines up with your chart's own vertical axis: home at the bottom, public life at the top.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Cancer rising: your feelings and your need to be looked after show more than usual, while a partner wants commitment.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** the money that makes you feel safe comes to mind, while shared finances in the 8th ask for structure.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** a talk with a sibling or a neighbour that's really about belonging, while a long study or plan asks for discipline.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** Cancer's own house, at the IC: home and family fill the month just as your career asks the most of you.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** the people you love and what you make for them, set against a group or a role in an organisation.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** the daily care you give and need comes to a head, while something carried privately asks to be set down.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Cancer on the Descendant: a partner's need for closeness surfaces while you're intent on your own goals.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** trust and emotional ties in what you share, set against your own resources and their limits.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a longing for somewhere that feels like home, abroad or in a belief, while daily duties keep you close.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Cancer on the Midheaven: a public role that cares for people comes into view, while home asks you to take charge.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** friends who feel like family, set against a creative project of your own that demands discipline.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** old feelings surface in quiet moments, while the working day asks for structure and stamina.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Start with the degrees: a planet or angle within one of each position above, at the Cancer end or the Capricorn end, or about three degrees either side for the two eclipses of 2028. Ask what is already active there.

Then look at your Moon and your Saturn: the Moon shows how you look after yourself, Saturn how you carry your responsibilities, and this full moon asks them to make room for each other. If you can, give each end of the axis something real this month: an evening that belongs to home, and one clear step at work.
