Capricorn on the 2nd house cusp makes money a matter of construction. What you have becomes something you build, and self-worth tends to grow with what you've learned to do.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Income, belongings, savings and the sense of what you're worth all sit in your [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) on its cusp treats all of it as a structure. I read it as the pension started before anyone told you to, quality bought once rather than cheapness bought twice, and a skill kept up to date because it's the security nobody can take away. You tend to feel worth what you can do, which makes competence precious and idleness uneasy. A budget, for you, is a relief rather than a restriction: it tells you what's safe. The harder side is holding on: spending on pleasure can feel like a lapse, and enough can be hard to recognise when you have it.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules Capricorn, so it rules this house: its house is where money is earned and kept, its sign how. The pages on Saturn as ruler of the 2nd go into each case: the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/) house. In my notebook, Saturn was one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), so its house is the part of this cusp I lean on most.

Capricorn opens the 2nd less often than an even share, about one birth in 13 at London and one in 12 at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. It climbs the eastern sky quickly at northern latitudes, so the cusps just under the horizon don't hold it for long. [Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/) goes with it in close to 9 London charts out of 10, Scorpio in the rest, and Jupiter then rules your chart: since Capricorn is the sign of Jupiter's fall, an open-handed manner tends to sit in front of a careful purse. The 2nd is very wide here, about 42° to 47° at London, too wide for Capricorn to reach a second cusp, and close to half the London charts hold Aquarius whole inside it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Saturn then holds the house through both its signs, sharing Aquarius with Uranus, and money gets a second, more inventive voice. Otherwise Aquarius opens the 3rd, and Saturn runs your everyday dealings as well.

Some time after your birth, the Ascendant itself moved into Capricorn ([Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/)); some time before it, the sign stood on the 3rd cusp ([Capricorn on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-3rd-house-cusp/)). The minute of birth decides which chart is yours, and I don't read one without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

None of this puts a figure on your income. The 2nd shows how you earn, save and value, not the sums, and I don't read a chart to pick a stock or a fund ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). What the cusp offers is a description of the way you build, and a reminder that a structure is for living in.
