Your Moon had just crossed into Pisces, the sign your Sun would reach next, while the Sun itself was still in Aquarius, early in a new lunar month. Both signs are wide in their own way, one through thought and one through feeling. I'd describe the pair as a mind that cares about everyone and a heart that feels with everyone, sharing the same week of the month.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fixed air and mutable water have nothing structural in common, and side by side they make a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign, a quiet adjustment between neighbours. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) thinks in systems and futures, about how people in general could live better; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) recovers through imagination and compassion, through music, solitude and the sense of being part of something larger than one life. The width is the same on both sides; what differs is that the Sun reaches it by reasoning and the Moon by feeling.

This is a pair of four rulers, and for each sign I read both. Aquarius answers to [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), Pisces to [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/). The traditional pair, Saturn and Jupiter, are the two slowest planets the old astrologers knew, one for structure and one for growth. The modern pair were discovered much later, Uranus in 1781 and Neptune in 1846, and they travel so slowly that a whole generation shares their signs. By sign and house, Saturn and Jupiter tend to say most about you in particular. Neither light has a dignity: the Sun is in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), and Pisces gives the Moon none.

Your birth came in the first five days after a new moon, with the Moon 0° to 59° past the Sun. The [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase accounts for most of these births, about seven in eight, and the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) for the remainder; in the earliest hours of the range the lights still form a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/), one on each side of the Aquarius–Pisces border. As I read it, a new moon birth begins the month with purpose and need side by side, and here there's a twist: the needs have already crossed into the next sign. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) describes the first two phases.

Of the births counted, about 1 in 148 has an Aquarius Sun beside a Pisces Moon. The Moon covers Pisces in roughly two and a half days, arriving from Aquarius and leaving for Aries, so on a crossing day the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-aquarius/) and the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-aries/) stay possible until the time is known ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The Sun leaves Aquarius itself around 19 February, so a birth near that date needs the time twice, once for the Moon and once for the Sun.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You care widely and love tenderly, and the two can surprise each other. The Pisces Moon softens, forgives and senses what a partner needs; the Aquarius Sun wants a friend, an equal, room for both of you to be yourselves. You may give a great deal without asking for much back, then step away to think when things get too close. A partner who is kind and also says plainly what they need takes the guesswork away for both lights.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that joins ideas to compassion suits you: humanitarian or charity work, technology with a human purpose, the arts with a social conscience, care organisations, music, research into how people live. The Sun designs the better system; the Moon keeps the people inside it in view. Cut-throat workplaces wear the Moon out, and work without a larger purpose bores the Sun.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Both signs are wide, and a wide heart and a wide mind can lose the edges of a day. You may take in a room's mood and then reason about it from a distance, without ever saying how you felt. Or you give to a cause until nothing is left for yourself. What helps is something with edges: a routine, a craft, a few fixed hours that belong to you, and the habit of naming one personal want before taking on anyone else's.
