Of all the ways Jupiter meets your Venus, the sextile is the most modest: two signs apart, twice a round, a few days at a time. It opens small ways into what you enjoy and leaves the walking to you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs and chooses: what attracts you, what you prefer, how you give and receive. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens what it touches. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) links them through opportunity and cooperation, and like every sextile it has to be taken up: an invitation accepted, a class booked, a friendship given an evening.

The openings are usually pleasant and specific: someone you like suggests a plan, a creative project finds a willing partner, a place or a piece of music turns into a lasting pleasure. Preference is the part of Venus this [transit](/astrology/transits/) sharpens most, and it's worth noticing what you choose when choosing is easy.

Tradition lines this angle up with the planets' own signs. Venus rules Taurus and Libra, Jupiter Sagittarius and, in the old scheme, Pisces, and each of Venus's signs lies a sextile from one of Jupiter's: Taurus from Pisces, the sign of Venus's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), and Libra from Sagittarius.

Where the opening appears depends on the house Jupiter is crossing. From your 5th, which tradition called Good Fortune and gave to Venus's joy ([Jupiter transiting the 5th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-5th-house/)), it often comes through play, creative work and things done for love of them. If your chart has [Venus sextile Jupiter](/astrology/venus-sextile-jupiter/), every Jupiter return repeats that angle in the sky.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Inside my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile usually lasts about eleven days. Now and then, about three times in ten, Jupiter stations near the point and the sextile becomes a season: three exact hits over about eight months.

The houses that give it a subject, your Venus's and the one Jupiter is crossing, come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and I don't read a chart without it.

Thirteen or fourteen of these come by 80 in the count for births from 1930 to 2009: small, regular chances to say yes to what you enjoy. Around them, the round holds the other meetings of the same two planets: [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-venus/), where the warmth begins, [transiting Jupiter square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-venus/), which tests the same pleasures against what they cost, [transiting Jupiter trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-venus/), which lets them flow, and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-venus/), which sets them beside someone else's.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Accept the invitation you'd normally turn down out of habit.

Make something, or take a first lesson in an art you've admired from the outside. A sextile from Jupiter suits beginnings that are small enough to enjoy.

Let pleasure stay modest. This angle offers a little more room for what you love, and a little is easy to keep after the days have passed.
