Venus conjunct Algol is a meeting of opposites in the old books: Venus, which the tradition called a benefic, at home in Taurus, on the degree of the star it counted among the unluckiest. Births between late March and early July can have it, and about 1 person in 212 does.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In a chart, [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how you weigh things: what you find worth having, whom you are drawn to, what feels like a fair exchange, what pleases you enough to keep. [Algol](/astrology/algol/) brings the Gorgon. The tradition named it after a ghoul's head and counted it among the unluckiest stars, and William Lilly treated any planet within five degrees of it as afflicted.

Medieval magic saw something else in it. The fifteen Behenian stars were each paired with a gem and a plant for making talismans, and Algol, of all stars, received the diamond, with black hellebore. For a contact with Venus, the planet of what you value, I find that detail worth keeping: the star with the worst name in the list was matched with its hardest stone.

The sign is what I'd actually read. Algol stood at 25°11′ Taurus in 1930 and stands at 26°31′ in 2026, so this is always a [Venus in Taurus](/astrology/venus-in-taurus/), one of the two signs Venus rules. Taurus slows Venus down and steadies it: taste formed through the senses, affection shown in practical care, and choices made slowly and then kept.

So the tradition sets one of its two benefics at home ([benefics and malefics](/astrology/benefics-and-malefics/)) and lays its unluckiest star on top. An old astrologer would weigh one against the other. I notice that the two labels cancel out, and read what is left, which is Venus in its own sign, in a house and with aspects that only the full chart shows.

Because Venus is never far from the Sun, the contact belongs to births from the end of March to the first days of July, while the Sun is between about 10° Aries and 11° Cancer; for 1930 to 2009 the earliest date was 30 March and the latest 3 July. About 1 person in 212 born in those years has it, a little under the average for a one-degree window, since Venus went straight over this degree once a year and never doubled back.

Nothing here forecasts how love, money or anything else will go. Venus on Algol describes a way of valuing, in a Taurus key, with an old fear written over it, and fears written into old books are not events waiting to happen.

Of the five bodies that reach Algol's degree, Venus and the [Sun conjunct Algol](/astrology/sun-conjunct-algol/) arrive in the same season, though between 1930 and 2009 never on the same day; the [Moon conjunct Algol](/astrology/moon-conjunct-algol/) comes every month, [Mars conjunct Algol](/astrology/mars-conjunct-algol/) every second year or so, and [Saturn conjunct Algol](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-algol/) only three times in those eighty years.

Stars stay out of the charts I read ([How I read](/how-i-read/)): Venus in Taurus, its house and its aspects are what I'd look at. Why a star counts only by conjunction, and here within a single degree, is explained under [conjunctions to fixed stars](/astrology/conjunctions-to-fixed-stars/).
