Some twenty years before Uranus reaches your Mercury, and some twenty years after, the two stand at right angles. Each time, for about twenty months, the way you think and the way you're asked to think stop agreeing, and the argument tends to improve your methods.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) handles information: what you notice, how you reason, how you put it into words. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) tests alternatives and reforms systems. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, makes two functions rub until something adapts, and with Mercury the friction is intellectual: the method, the vocabulary or the explanation you rely on stops being enough, and something better has to be worked out under pressure.

Impatience is usually the first sign. Meetings feel slow, instructions feel out of date, and your own habits of mind can annoy you as much as anyone's. Ideas arrive mid-sentence; replies go out quicker and blunter than you meant. You may change tools, systems or a whole way of working, sometimes by choice and sometimes because the old one stops being an option.

The useful side is real, and people born with [Mercury square Uranus](/astrology/mercury-square-uranus/) often know it best. Squares make a function adapt, and here what adapts is your thinking: a problem that sat unsolved for years can give way once you stop approaching it the old way.

With Mercury in your 3rd and Uranus crossing your 6th ([Uranus transiting the 6th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-6th-house/)), new methods at work press on the way you talk and think every day: a new system to learn, new hours, new people to explain things to. Both houses come from the time you were born, and without that time this square stays unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, which open at 1½° before exact and close at 1° after, the square lasts about twenty months; the quickest passes take about eleven. Uranus crosses the exact square three times in more than nine passes out of ten, so a quick first solution usually gets a second look before the square is over.

The two squares sit about twenty years either side of [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-mercury/). Between the first and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-mercury/) lies the first [transiting Uranus trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-mercury/); between the conjunction and each square comes a [transiting Uranus sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-mercury/). In a life of 80 years the usual count of squares is two, and never fewer than one.

Until 2033 this [transit](/astrology/transits/) comes from Gemini, one of Mercury's own signs, to Mercurys in Virgo, the other one, and in Pisces, where tradition puts Mercury in its fall.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sleep on the sharp reply. The square speeds up your thinking more than your judgement, and the version you send the next morning is usually the better one.

Use one channel at a time. Under this transit attention splits easily, and closing the extra windows does more than any new tool.

Let the old method finish while the new one is tested. Switching everything at once is the square's temptation; running the two side by side for a while is its remedy.
