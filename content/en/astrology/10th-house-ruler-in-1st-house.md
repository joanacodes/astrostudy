Your work doesn't wait for you at a desk. With the ruler of your 10th house in the 1st, vocation and standing travel with you, in your face, your bearing and the way you walk into a room.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In Placidus, the house system I use, the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) begins at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), so the planet that rules the sign on your Midheaven rules the 10th as well ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)): your vocation, your standing, the reputation you build and what you'd like to be known for. That planet sits here in the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), which holds your body, your manner and the first impression you make.

I read it as a vocation carried in person. Colleagues tend to meet you before they meet the job, and the job ends up shaped like you: the chef whose kitchen runs on her temperament, the consultant hired for a manner as much as a method. Many people with this placement make their own way, alone or at the head of something, because a role that doesn't fit them fits badly. Ambition is personal here. The difficulty is the other side of that closeness. When your standing feels like a feature of your face, criticism of the work lands on the person, and a quiet season at work can feel like disappearing. You are more than the role, even when the role wears your features.

This ruler ties two angles together, the Midheaven of your public life and the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) of your self, and sometimes it does so twice. With Sagittarius on the Midheaven and Pisces rising, Jupiter rules the 10th and, as Pisces' traditional ruler beside Neptune, the chart as well ([chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/)); a Jupiter in the 1st then makes your direction and your self one planet's business ([Jupiter in the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-in-1st-house/)). Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo Midheaven from here, makes you known for how you speak. The Sun, ruling a Leo Midheaven, puts your purpose on show.

At your birth this ruler hadn't yet cleared the eastern horizon; it was waiting to rise, so the clock decides a great deal here ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born a few minutes later, you may find it already risen into the 12th, where the work goes on behind the scenes ([Ruler of the 10th in the 12th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)). Born a few minutes earlier, it may still sit in the 2nd, where work is bound up with earning and with what you value ([Ruler of the 10th in the 2nd house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)).

Scorpio on the Midheaven brings Mars and Pluto into play together ([Scorpio on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Mars in the 1st sits in the house the natural zodiac gives to Aries, its own sign: drive you can see, and a working life that starts things without waiting for permission. Pluto shares its sign with a whole generation, so only its house makes it personal, and from wherever it sits it adds a slower story of power and change in your public life.
