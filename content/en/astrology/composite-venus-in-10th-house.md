Some pairs are known for what they make together in public: the restaurant run by two friends, the duo that plays the same summer festival every year, the couple who chair the residents' association with unfailing tact. With composite Venus in the 10th house, the relationship's values show in its public role.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The composite [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) opens [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), and unusually that cusp doesn't depend on the method: both usual ways of building the houses of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) use the midpoint of your two natal Midheavens. Where the house ends is another matter, since the 11th cusp is either a midpoint of two natal cusps or computed for the latitude where you live. Both birth times are needed all the same ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): a [Venus](/astrology/venus/) just past the Midheaven is in the 10th by either method, while one near the next cusp may not be.

This is an angular house, Capricorn's by nature, and it covers public role, work, responsibility and reputation. Venus here gives the bond a gracious public face: people tend to think well of the two of you together, and the relationship enjoys working where it can be seen, in a joint business, a shared cause, on a stage. Its taste may become known as well, the pair whose events, designs or judgement people trust, and colleagues with this placement often find clients asking for the two of them by name. One person's charm at work is what [Venus in the 10th house](/astrology/venus-in-10th-house/) gives in a birth chart; [their Venus in your 10th house](/astrology/venus-in-10th-house-synastry/) lends grace to your ambitions; here the public grace belongs to the pair.

Nine pairs in ten find the composite Sun near this Venus, less than 47° away, as in any sky. Pairs whose birthdays sit far apart can break that, and in about 1 pair in 22 the composite Sun lies close to the opposite point, down by the IC in the 4th house ([composite Sun in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-4th-house/)). The method, not the bond, puts them at the two ends of the meridian.

The public role can crowd out the private one. A bond admired for its grace can start accepting invitations for the bond's sake and spend its best manners on people outside it. Recognition isn't fame and achievement isn't status, and no chart predicts success, a promotion or the fate of a reputation. It helps to decide together which parts of your shared life are public and which aren't, and to protect the second as firmly as you build the first.

[Composite Venus in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-9th-house/) looks to the far horizon; [composite Venus in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-11th-house/), the friends and groups around you. The composite is a technique my reading system doesn't draw ([How I read](/how-i-read/)), and its users weigh it against the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and against each person's natal chart. A chart about a pair's public face still belongs to two private people: both should agree to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
