When Chiron trines your Mercury, explaining becomes the easy part. For about ten months, usually, what you learned the long way round, about a subject, a difficulty or a way of thinking, comes out in words other people can use.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) interprets and passes on: it turns experience into language and language into understanding. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a difficulty stayed, and where you gained the kind of knowledge only experience gives. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets the two coordinate, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the old difficulty stops catching in your throat. You can describe it without apologising for it. You can listen to someone in the middle of it and know which question to ask. You can write down what helped, in steps someone else could follow, without the old tightness creeping into the sentences.

Reasoning isn't certainty, and the trine doesn't make you right, only clearer. Like every trine it starts nothing on its own: the season can pass with nothing written and nobody taught, and its ease goes as quietly as it came.

Your houses, set by the birth time, show who needs your words this time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With Mercury in the 6th, one of these trines comes while Chiron crosses your 10th ([Chiron transiting the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-10th-house/)): speaking in public, training others or explaining your methods at work comes more easily than it has. Born with [Mercury trine Chiron](/astrology/mercury-trine-chiron/), you have a gift for kind explanation already; the transit gives it a stage for a while.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Using the orbs I time transits with, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine usually lasts about ten months, and nearly two passes in three cross the exact degree three times. The other shapes, a three-week single crossing in about a third of passes and a five-crossing pass of some twenty-one months in about one in twenty, depend on where Chiron is on its stretched orbit when it gets there. In Virgo and Libra, which it crosses in under two years each, single crossings are common; in Aries and Pisces, where it stays about eight, the looping kind.

Chiron's trines to your Sun ([transiting Chiron trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-sun/)) are never far away in time, since Mercury keeps close to the Sun; when the two fall together, Chiron's teaching side tends to show at its clearest. By 80, most people have met three of these trines, a few two and a few four. The sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-mercury/)) asks you to go looking for the words, the square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-mercury/)) makes you fight for them, and both come as often as this trine; the conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-mercury/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-mercury/)) come once or twice.

From 2027 to 2033, crossing Taurus, Chiron trines Mercury in Capricorn and in Virgo, Mercury's own sign and, in tradition, its exaltation as well.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down how you got through the hard part, as instructions for someone who is where you were.

Say yes to explaining: a talk, an hour of mentoring, a reply to the question nobody else answers.

Learn the next thing while learning is easy. A subject that used to frighten you may be within reach now.
