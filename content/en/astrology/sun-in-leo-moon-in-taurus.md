Your Sun is in the sign it rules and your Moon in the sign where tradition exalts it, so both lights stand on strong ground, and they meet at a right angle. Born near the last quarter, you have two steady forces that each want to keep something, and they don't always agree on what.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Leo is fixed fire, Taurus fixed earth, and the Moon sits three signs behind the Sun: a square by sign between two signs of the same mode. Both are built to last. The [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) keeps faith with what it has made and wants it seen; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) settles through the body, routine and familiar comforts, and would like them left alone. A stage and a garden, then, and the square asks you to give each its hours.

Few pairs have both lights so well placed. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo, its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/); Taurus is the Moon's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), the sign where Ptolemy says it is honoured. Neither light is weak here, and neither is in a hurry to give way. [Venus](/astrology/venus/), ruler of Taurus, is the planet I read next, because what you enjoy and value is very often what calms you: its sign and house say which comforts work for you, and where you go to find them.

Your Moon was 61° to 120° behind the Sun, which astronomers count as 240° to 299° ahead, nineteen and a half to twenty-four and a half days after the new moon. Half of these births fall in the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) and half in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/); near 90° behind the Sun the square is exact as well as by sign, a waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). On the day of the quarter itself, the hour decides which side of it you fall, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains why a square met from behind tends to be settled by revising your own priorities first.

Roughly one person in 137 is born with this pair. On a day when the Moon came in from Aries or went on into Gemini, the birth time chooses between this page and the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-aries/) or the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-gemini/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also fixes the rising sign and the houses, where your Sun and Moon actually do their work; without it there are two strong lights and nowhere to put them.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love for the long haul, and you show it in two registers. The Leo Sun celebrates: the speech at the party, the gift chosen with care, a partner's name said with pride. The Taurus Moon simply stays: the hand on the back, the Sunday walk, the meal ready on a hard day. What unsettles you is change sprung without warning, or a partner who stops noticing what you give. Because both signs hold their ground, a disagreement can harden into a standoff, and the first to soften is often the one doing both of you a kindness.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that builds something durable and lets you sign it suits this pair: a business of your own, a restored house, a body of work, a reputation earned slowly. The Sun wants creative authority; the Moon wants a steady pace and results you can touch. Constant reorganisation wears the Moon down, and effort nobody notices dims the Sun. Given both, a rhythm you set and credit you can see, you tend to stay for years and do the job handsomely.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two fixed signs at a square can dig in. When pride and the need for security disagree, a choice that should take an afternoon can take a season: the job you've outgrown kept because it's safe, an old grievance kept because letting it go feels like losing face. What helps is the last quarter's own habit, review and release. Ask what you're holding out of pride and what out of comfort, then let one thing go at a time, at a pace the Taurus Moon can trust.
