The Saturn return gets the fame, but it is one step of a longer walk. On the way round, Saturn squares the place it held at your birth, opposes it, then squares it again, about seven years apart. Those are the Saturn squares and opposition, and they come back with every round.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) takes about 29½ years to go round the zodiac, so every seven years or so it reaches another quarter of its cycle. Counted for births from 1930 to 2009, the first exact hit of each step came at these ages, in every one of the 160 birth dates computed:

- **The first square, waxing:** between 5.8 and 8.2.
- **The opposition:** between 12.9 and 16.2.
- **The second square, waning:** between 20.9 and 22.7.
- **The return:** between 28.4 and 29.9 ([Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/)).

Then the sequence starts again, about 29½ years on: a square in the mid-thirties, an opposition in the mid-forties, a square in the early fifties, the second return around 58 to 60. The ranges are wide because Saturn's speed changes along its orbit and its retrograde loops fall differently for each birth. Born on 1 January 1975, you met the first square at 6.8, 7.4 and 7.5, the opposition at 15, the second square at 22.4, 22.8 and 23.1, and the return at 29.5.

The first two steps come in childhood and adolescence. This site doesn't read children's charts ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)); I name them because the cycle makes no sense without them, and adults sometimes recognise them looking back.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Each step is a transit of Saturn to its own birth degree ([transits](/astrology/transits/)), so each lasts what a Saturn transit lasts: with the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, usually about nine months, often with three exact passes, and as little as three weeks when Saturn crosses once.

The two kinds of step relate Saturn to itself in different ways. A square, 90°, works through friction that pushes for adjustment: the structures you are building rub against the ones you set out with ([transiting Saturn square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-saturn/)). An opposition, 180°, works through polarity: from the far side of the circle, what you have built is seen from outside, often against other people's expectations ([transiting Saturn opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-saturn/)). The square is friction, never failure; the opposition is a seesaw, never a war.

I read the two halves of the cycle differently as well. From the return to the opposition, Saturn's cycle is building: commitments taken on, structures put up. From the opposition to the next return, it is consolidating and pruning, and the waning square, about seven years before each return, often reads as the first sign of the review the return will ask for.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I read each step through your natal Saturn: its sign, the manner of the lesson; its house, the area of life concerned, which needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)); and its aspects, which the transit repeats from a new angle. Saturn is the placement I trust most. In my notebook it held 75 to 90% of the time, with Chiron and Lilith ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), so its checkpoints get careful attention and a gentle reading: they ask what is solid and what has outlived its use, and they never guarantee a loss.

The adult steps keep company with other cycles. The waning square of the early twenties comes close to the Uranus square, which fell between 18.4 and 22.9 in the same count. In the mid-forties, Saturn's second opposition can fall near the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/). Close together or not, I read each transit on its own terms, then together.

None of it is fate. A Saturn checkpoint describes a season of structure and responsibility, read alongside everything else that is moving. Weather, not a forecast.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Saturn's squares bring bad years.”** They bring friction that asks for adjustment. How the season goes depends on you and on the rest of the chart.
- **“Only the Saturn return matters.”** It's the main milestone of a cycle that has a checkpoint about every seven years.
- **“The opposition means conflict with other people.”** It means seeing your own structures from the other side. Other people's expectations come into view; enmity doesn't follow.
- **“Everyone the same age gets the same Saturn transit.”** The ages are close; the house and aspects of your Saturn are yours alone.
