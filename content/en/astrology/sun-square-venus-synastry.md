Within one chart, Venus can't square the Sun: it never strays more than about 47° away. Between two people the square is common, and it gives appreciation an edge. One person likes the other, but their taste and their sense of what's fair never quite line up with who the other is.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the identity someone keeps choosing, and the self-expression that goes with it. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) judges value: preference, attraction, fairness, an eye for what's beautiful and what's worth the effort. A [square](/astrology/square/) sets two functions at right angles, so each keeps pushing the other to adapt, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) like this one, the pushing happens between two people.

Cardinal, fixed or mutable, the two signs are usually of one kind. A Capricorn Venus and an Aries Sun: a taste for the solid and the earned, meeting someone who would rather leap first. A Gemini Venus and a Pisces Sun: a liking for wit and variety, meeting someone who lives by feel.

The friction has its uses. Each person's taste gets tested by someone they care about, and comes out more their own. The Sun person learns which of their choices they'd defend; the Venus person learns that liking someone doesn't require agreeing with them. Difference also keeps interest alive: there's always something in the other that doesn't quite fit, and so is worth looking at.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

If yours is the Venus, you may be drawn to the person and uneasy about their choices at the same time. Your sense of what's fair or pleasant seems to get overruled by their direction, and your preferences can be treated as fussiness. As I read the square, the friction signals no failure and the challenge no punishment, though on a hard week it can feel like both.

If it's your Sun, you can feel liked with conditions: appreciated, but with a suggestion attached about how you dress, spend, choose or present yourself. It's easy to hear every preference of theirs as a judgement on you.

From this square, the rest of the family:

- **[Sun conjunct Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-venus-synastry/):** taste sitting right on identity; appreciation without conditions, and approval to watch.
- **[Sun sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-venus-synastry/):** a sign closer; pleasures offered instead of preferences pressed.
- **[Sun trine Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-venus-synastry/):** four signs apart instead of three, in a shared element; liking that never asks the other to change.
- **[Sun opposite Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-venus-synastry/):** across the zodiac; the angle becomes a balance to keep rather than a corner to turn.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Name preferences as preferences: "I'd like" lands differently from "you should". Give separate tastes separate spaces, a room of one's own, an evening each, a playlist each, and negotiate only what you share. At work, agree on the criteria before judging a design or a proposal, so taste doesn't pose as a standard. In a couple or a family, keep a rough tally of whose choice won last time; fairness is one of Venus's own concerns, and seeing it kept eases a lot.

It's frequent: about 1 pair of people in 7, one way round or the other, within 6.8°. Each Venus stays close to its own Sun, never more than about 47° away, which as a rule puts the two birthdays about three months apart, though anywhere from five or six weeks to nearly five months, whatever the years.

In a day, the Sun and Venus cover little ground, so the hour seldom decides the square; it decides the houses, which part of each life the tastes clash over, and a synastry without both times is one I don't do ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
