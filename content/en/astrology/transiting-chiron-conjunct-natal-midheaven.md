The Midheaven is the point where the zodiac crossed the meridian at the minute and place of your birth, and nothing else in a chart can stand in for it, so this transit is read only from a recorded birth time. When Chiron reaches it, an old sore spot climbs to the most visible point of your chart, the one that stands for your direction in the world.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where your capacities become visible: a role, a responsibility, the contribution you are known for. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) turns a limit that stays into experience you can pass on. Fused by a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), the two work as one: in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) what you have learned from an old sore spot asks for a place in your public direction, rather than being kept out of it.

The Midheaven's blind spots are useful to know here: confusing visibility with value, and a public role with your whole identity. With Chiron on the Midheaven, both get tested. A job title can stop feeling like the point; the less visible things you do well, the quiet help, the problem colleagues bring you because you once had it yourself, start to look like contributions in their own right. For some people the step is to say in public what they have kept private about a difficulty, because it now informs how they work. For others it is to stop hiding a limit behind overwork.

Nothing here promotes you, ends a job or picks a profession for you; the Midheaven describes direction, and what you do with your work stays your decision ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Midheaven is the cusp of the 10th, so this crossing ends Chiron's climb through your 9th, the house of belief and study ([Chiron transiting the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-9th-house/)), and opens its stay in your 10th ([Chiron transiting the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-10th-house/)). The IC, at the bottom of the chart, is opposed at the same time, so your private foundations feel the change too.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the crossing usually takes about ten months, and in nearly two passes out of three Chiron goes over the top of your chart three times: into the 10th, back into the 9th as it turns retrograde, and into the 10th again, as if what you believe and what you do in public had to be reconciled more than once. A third of crossings are single, done in about three weeks; one in twenty, five crossings long, takes about twenty-one months.

By 80 there have usually been two, half a century apart, though a late first one leaves room for only one. Your Midheaven's degree is fixed by the minute and the place of birth: two people born the same day in the same town, an hour apart, have Midheavens about fifteen degrees apart, which while Chiron is as slow as it is in Taurus amounts to years ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Without a recorded time, I don't read the chart at all.

From the top of the chart, Chiron's round goes on through [transiting Chiron sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-midheaven/) and [transiting Chiron square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-midheaven/) to [transiting Chiron trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-midheaven/), and halfway round it reaches your IC ([transiting Chiron opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-midheaven/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write a one-line answer to "what would I like to be known for?", and check whether it leaves room for what you learned the hard way.

Offer that experience once in your public life: the advice, the talk, the mentoring you would have wanted yourself.

Look after the base. A routine at home that holds is what lets the public step be taken.
