The Sun crosses into Libra at the September equinox, when day and night are nearly equal, and the first new moon after it is always a new moon in Libra. It opens a month in the sign of the scales, where a beginning is rarely made alone.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

At a [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/), the Moon draws level with the Sun and turns its dark side towards us; a new round of phases starts from that meeting. In [Libra](/astrology/libra/), a cardinal air sign, the round starts by turning towards someone. Libra asks *is this fair?*, so the month it opens tends to begin with an agreement, a conversation that resets the terms, or a partnership seen afresh.

There's an old wrinkle here. Tradition puts the Sun in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Libra, opposite its exaltation in Aries, and at this new moon the Moon joins it there. I don't read it as a weak start: it's a self that finds its shape through other people, so the month leans towards *we* before *I*. Libra is ruled by [Venus](/astrology/venus/): where Venus sits in your chart shows how you weigh what's fair, and that's where a Libra theme goes once it has begun.

I keep the scales honest about the new moon itself. It's one of the lightest weights in a chart: it counts when it falls on a natal point, a degree or so either side, and then only as a trigger for something already set in motion by a transit, a progression, a solar arc or an eclipse. Otherwise it's a month's weather in one house, never a forecast.

The dates, in Universal Time:

- **Most recent:** 10 October 2026, at 17°21′ Libra.
- **Next:** 30 September 2027, at 02:36 UT, at 6°43′.
- **Then:** 18 October 2028 at 25°16′, 7 October 2029 at 14°48′, 27 September 2030 at 4°21′.

None is an eclipse. Each follows the [new moon in Virgo](/astrology/new-moon-in-virgo/) by a month and comes a month before the [new moon in Scorpio](/astrology/new-moon-in-scorpio/). In the same weeks, the Sun still in Libra, comes the [full moon in Aries](/astrology/full-moon-in-aries/): in 2027, the new moon on 30 September and the full moon on 15 October.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house this new moon opens is set by your birth time, because the houses turn with the hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). I use Placidus houses, and there it's usually the house with Libra on its cusp. Sometimes Libra sits wholly inside a house with no cusp of its own, an [intercepted sign](/astrology/intercepted-signs/), and then the new moon falls in that house.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Libra rising: you begin again in the way you meet people, with your own wishes allowed on the scales beside theirs.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** money and fairness: a price that's fair to you too, an agreement about who pays for what.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** a new tone in everyday talk: the conversation that clears the air with a sibling or a neighbour.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** home as a shared arrangement: who does what, a room made beautiful, a family quarrel heard from both sides.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** pleasure in company: a date, a duet, art enjoyed with someone, flirting that's fun because it's mutual.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** working days rebalanced: a fairer share of tasks between colleagues, a routine that alternates effort and rest.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Libra's own house: a partnership begins or begins again, its terms set on an equal footing.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** what you share, weighed in the open: joint money discussed, intimacy that asks as much as it gives.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a belief tested in good conversation, a course in law or the arts, a journey made as a pair.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** a new start in your public role as the one who brings people together: mediator, partner, go-between.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** a circle where you keep the peace, or an alliance formed around a cause you share.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a private reckoning with what you give way on to keep the peace, and the first quiet steps towards saying so.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Start with your own chart's scales. Is anything within 1° of 6°43′ Libra in 2027, 25°16′ in 2028, 14°48′ in 2029 or 4°21′ in 2030? If so, ask what is already at work at that point; the new moon only brings it forward. If not, read it as a light month in one house.

Then find Venus, and the house the new moon falls in, and ask one question there: what would make this fairer, to the other person and to me? Libra prefers a conversation to a resolution, so start with one. If the answer keeps leaving you out of the balance, that's worth noticing too.
