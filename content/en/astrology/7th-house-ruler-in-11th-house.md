The ruler of your 7th house has joined the crowd: it sits in the 11th, the house of friends, groups and hopes. Here love and friendship run into each other, and the people you pair with usually arrive with mutual friends attached.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

One person faces you in the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), in love, in work or in a quarrel; many surround you in the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/): friends, networks, associations, and the causes and plans you share with them. Here the one is met among the many ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Your partnerships keep a social life of their own. You may meet partners through friends, a club, a campaign, an online community, and a relationship tends to sit inside a wider web rather than apart from it. What you want from a partner is often what you want from a friend: shared hopes, honesty, room to breathe. Business partners can come from the same circle, and alliances, between people or between groups, are something you're good at making. The risk is a line between friend and partner blurred in a way that confuses someone, or a couple so absorbed in its group that it forgets to be two.

Rivals follow the same pattern. The 7th's open adversary tends to come from your own circles here, a faction in a club, a competitor you'll meet again at the next gathering, so fair dealing is a practical necessity as well as a principle. And the 11th is the house of hopes: a partnership with this ruler often carries a shared plan, a cause, a picture of the future you're both working towards. When that picture changes, it's the bond that needs talking through, not only the plans.

Counted round the wheel, the 11th is four houses from the 7th, a trine, and in the natural zodiac both are air houses, Libra's and Aquarius's ([the natural zodiac](/astrology/natural-zodiac/)): relating and belonging come easily to each other. Tradition named the 11th the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) there. When Gemini rises, Sagittarius sets and Jupiter rules your 7th from the house where it was said to be glad: generous partners, and partnerships that grow by including people.

With Virgo rising, [Pisces on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/) makes Jupiter share that rule with Neptune, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 11th turns love into friendship and friendship into alliance, while Neptune's house adds the ideal of a love that serves something larger. Saturn, when Capricorn sets, prefers a few loyal friendships to a crowd, and the partner who comes out of them tends to stay.

Its cusps, in Placidus, the house system I use, come from the birth time like every other. Move the time one way and the ruler lands in the 10th, where partnership meets your work ([Ruler of the 7th in the 10th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)); move it the other and it falls in the 12th, where devotion works quietly ([Ruler of the 7th in the 12th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)). A noted time, not a guessed one, makes the difference ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

No chart says your next partner is already among your friends. This one says that friendship is where you learn what you want from a partner, and where you tend to look for it.
