Libra asks how two people can be fair to each other. Uranus asks whether the old answer still holds. Between those two questions sits a generation that renegotiated what a fair partnership looks like, and, one by one, people who need a bond of equals with room to breathe.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the planet of reform and autonomy; [Libra](/astrology/libra/), cardinal air ruled by [Venus](/astrology/venus/), is the sign of the other person, of balance, agreements and taste. Here Uranus works on how people pair up, decide together and settle their differences. Cardinal air starts conversations, so this placement tends to open new ways of relating rather than wait to be offered them. Air thinks before it acts: this Uranus usually reforms by discussing, drafting and proposing, and its changes arrive as new agreements rather than slammed doors. No dignity applies, since modern tropical astrology gives Uranus only Aquarius; Venus's place in your chart shows the tact and the taste this Uranus brings.

Uranus was in Libra from September 1968 to September 1975, and next returns from December 2051 to August 2059; about 1 person in 12 born from 1930 to 2009 has it. Uranus moved back and forth with Virgo from September 1968 to June 1969, and with Scorpio from November 1974 to September 1975 ([Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/uranus-in-virgo/), [Uranus in Scorpio](/astrology/uranus-in-scorpio/)): for those months, the chart says which. Seven years of births share the sign. The house is where, in your own life, you ask for fairness on new terms.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Relationships are this placement's laboratory. You may want a partner who is also a friend and an equal, a bond whose rules you agree together rather than inherit, and the freedom to keep your own circle. An unfair arrangement bothers you far more than an unusual one. The risk is wanting closeness and independence so evenly that you never settle on either; saying which one you need this week helps. When it comes to comparing two charts, I do it only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## At work {#at-work}

At work, Uranus in Libra reforms how people cooperate: mediation, negotiation, law, design, human resources, partnerships, anything where fairer rules can be drawn up. It sees the arrangement that would suit everyone better, and knows how to make it look attractive. It does best as a partner or a go-between, and least well in strict hierarchies where nobody may question the deal. Teams often find in it the colleague who proposes a better way of sharing the work before resentment sets in.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard part is indecision dressed as open-mindedness, or fairness turned into a cause that leaves no room for the person in front of you. You may keep every option open so as never to be bound, or change a relationship's rules faster than the other person can keep up. Politeness can also hide a wish for change until it comes out all at once. Autonomy is not isolation: a bond can be equal and committed at the same time.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write agreements down and review them together; it suits a sign that loves a fair deal. Let Venus in your chart show you what harmony looks like for you, and say what you want before you weigh what the other person wants. Ask yourself, now and then, what you would choose if nobody else's opinion counted. The [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) came for this generation while Uranus crossed Aries, from May 2010 to March 2019: a call to act on your own behalf, put to people used to weighing the other side first. Checked like every placement in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Uranus wasn't one of the three that held far more often; about a third held overall.
