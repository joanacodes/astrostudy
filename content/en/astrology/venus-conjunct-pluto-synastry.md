There are people after whom you never quite want the same things again. Venus conjunct Pluto in synastry can describe one of them: one person's Venus falls on the other's Pluto, within 6°, and what the first values meets the second's need to know what lies underneath. Each side deserves its own reading: being taken this deep is not the same as doing the taking.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs what's worth having: attraction, preference, the give and take that makes a bond feel fair. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) renews from the root: it clears what has become obsolete and turns what remains into strength. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) binds two functions into one movement, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) like this, one person supplies each half.

If the Venus is yours, this person takes what you value seriously enough to ask what's underneath it. Tastes you'd borrowed from others fall away; the ones that are really yours get stronger. A friendship, a style, a way of spending your evenings can come out of this bond renewed, closer to who you are.

If the Pluto is yours, the parts of you that you usually guard are met with appreciation rather than caution. Your intensity is enjoyed, your honesty valued, your capacity to start again admired. Being welcomed for your depth can make you less defended than usual, and more generous with what you know.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

At 0°, value and intensity merge, and it can become hard to keep anything light. If the Venus is yours, your pleasures may start to feel examined, every preference asked to justify itself, until you're not sure which tastes you kept and which you were talked out of. If the Pluto is yours, it's easy to mistake intensity for truth, or privacy for depth, and to guard what you value in this person as if holding on tightly made it safer. Control and safety are not the same thing, and nobody is being dominated simply because the exchange feels uneven for a while. Transformation isn't destruction; it only needs both people's consent.

The pair's other angles leave more air between value and depth:

- **[Venus sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-pluto-synastry/):** depth offered on request, taken up when both are ready.
- **[Venus square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-pluto-synastry/):** the terms of the exchange, and who sets them, rubbing at right angles.
- **[Venus trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-pluto-synastry/):** loyalty that deepens without much effort.
- **[Venus opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** lightness and depth carried by two people facing each other.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep a few things light on purpose: a pleasure enjoyed without discussion, a film watched just for fun. The Pluto person can ask before digging into something the other loves; the Venus person can say when a question is welcome and when it isn't. Be clear about what's private, which is different from what's secret. A mentor and a protégé, two friends who talk late into the night, an adult child and a parent renewing their relationship, or a couple: all do better with that line drawn kindly.

About 1 pair of people in 16 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 6°. Pluto spends twelve to thirty-one years in a sign and about 248 years going round the zodiac, so a whole generation shares the Pluto person's Pluto sign, and people born a year or so either side share nearly its degree. What turns that backdrop into this pair's conjunction is the other's Venus, which crosses a sign in a few weeks. Birth hours barely shift either planet, yet they settle the houses, the part of each life this depth reaches, so I need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In a single chart, [Venus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/venus-conjunct-pluto/) describes someone changed by what they love. Between two charts the change is shared, and I take it up only when both people ask ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
