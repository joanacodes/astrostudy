Once in its 84-year round, Uranus crosses the degree of your Mercury, usually three times over about twenty months. The way you take things in and put them into words keeps being interrupted, in the useful sense: new information cuts in, and you let it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) processes what you notice and turns it into thoughts, words and decisions. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) brings experiment, originality and breakthrough, the quick arrival of a new solution. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes two functions act as one, so for these months thinking and the urge to innovate share a single channel: your ideas come with change built in.

In practice that can look like a field that clicks into place, a skill or a piece of technology you take to at once, or a conversation that rearranges how you see a whole subject. Opinions shift, sometimes in public. You may find you explain things differently, more bluntly or more inventively, and that some people need a moment to follow.

Curiosity isn't the same as distraction, yet a mind under this conjunction can run ahead of the hours it has. The ideas that last are usually the ones you test.

Mercury in the 3rd, the house of the everyday mind, puts this conjunction inside a longer stretch of change there ([Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/)): daily talk, neighbours and short journeys show the new thinking first. That house comes from your birth time, and without the time I don't read the transit ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Unlike [Mercury conjunct Uranus](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-uranus/) in a birth chart, which describes how a mind is built, the transit is thinking under new conditions, and what stays afterwards is whatever you finished.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured with my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus sits on Mercury for about twenty months, long enough for Mercury itself to turn retrograde five or six times in the sky. More than nine passes in ten bring three exact hits; the briefest passes still last about eleven months.

Mercury always stays within about 28° of the Sun, so this crossing falls within about six or seven years of [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-sun/), before or after, when both come within a life.

This conjunction is where the count begins. The far point, [transiting Uranus opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-mercury/), comes about 42 years later; [transiting Uranus sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-mercury/), [transiting Uranus square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-mercury/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-mercury/) each come twice, once on the way out to it and once on the way back round. About 1 person in 33 born between 1930 and 2009 is still waiting for this [transit](/astrology/transits/) at 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write it down. Ideas under this conjunction come fast and leave fast; a notebook turns a bright month into work you can use.

Test before you broadcast. A new idea isn't automatically a better one: try the new method on one real problem before you announce it to everyone.

Learn the new tool properly. The fluency you pick up now, in a language, a program or a craft, outlasts the excitement that brought you to it.
