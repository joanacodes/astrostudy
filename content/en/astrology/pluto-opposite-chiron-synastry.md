Faced with something that won't go away, one of you asks "can we get rid of it?" and the other asks "how do we live with it?" Pluto opposite Chiron in synastry puts those two questions in two different people: one person's Pluto faces the other's Chiron across the zodiac.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is deep renewal: the structures that have stopped serving are cleared, something regenerates, and a strength more truly one's own takes their place. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is adaptation: around a limit that persists, the method, the pace or the tool changes, so that life goes on without waiting for the limit to leave. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions at either end of one axis, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) each answer gets a person to carry it.

What it gives the pair is perspective. The Pluto person sees, in the other, a patience with lasting things they rarely allow themselves; the Chiron person sees, in the other, a willingness to let something end that they may have stopped believing in. Between them, the pair holds both answers, which is more than most people hold alone.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

If the Chiron is yours, this person can seem to see straight to the place you've worked so hard to manage, and to doubt the arrangement. Some days that feels like being understood at depth; others, like being asked to dismantle something that holds your life together.

From the Pluto side, the other's accommodations can look like a door they keep closing. You see what could be released; they see what it would cost. Push, and they hold still; hold still, and nothing changes.

Nothing hostile is going on; it's a seesaw between two sound instincts. It tips into blame only when each forgets that the other's question is also a good one.

The same two, at other angles:

- **[Pluto conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-conjunct-chiron-synastry/):** depth lands on the tender place itself, with no room between.
- **[Pluto sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-sextile-chiron-synastry/):** a quiet offer to rework one old way of coping.
- **[Pluto square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-square-chiron-synastry/):** the two answers colliding instead of facing each other.
- **[Pluto trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/pluto-trine-chiron-synastry/):** renewal and adaptation flowing together without strain.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take turns with the questions. Pick one difficulty and let the Pluto person ask "what could go?" while the Chiron person answers; then swap, and let the Chiron person ask "what would make this easier to live with?" Couples of the same generation, siblings, and friends from the same school years can do this well, often about the same kinds of things.

This is very much a 1960s contact. A birth in mid-1965, Pluto in Virgo, shares it with people born from February 1963 to May 1964, whose Chiron was then in Pisces; their own Chiron, also in Pisces, faces the Pluto of anyone born from July 1967 to November 1969. Many people in those windows carry the same opposition inside their own charts, since it ran on and off through most of the decade ([Pluto opposite Chiron](/astrology/pluto-opposite-chiron/)). A contact this widely shared never counts among the synastry contacts I tally, which touch the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven; its houses show where a generation's two answers meet in a pair.

Whether two people have it doesn't depend on the hour; where it falls in each life, in the houses, does, so both times are needed ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Because it reaches a place someone has spent years managing, I read it only with both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
