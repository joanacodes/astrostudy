Cancer on the 7th house cusp is the sign of home on the Descendant, across the chart from a Capricorn Ascendant. A partnership, here, is somewhere to live as much as someone to be with.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Whoever you meet as an equal, in love, in work or across a negotiating table, you meet through the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), and its sign is what you look for and recognise in them ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) there, it is care: people who remember what matters to you, keep their word and make a place feel lived in. You tend to look after the people you pair with and to want looking after in turn, and a bond feels real once it has its own habits, the shared meal, the call at the end of the day, the jokes that took years to grow. In business, clients and associates often become a kind of family, for better and for worse. A quarrel with someone close can feel like a crack in the house itself, which is why you tend to mend things rather than walk away. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) describes what you seek and recognise in another person, never who you'll meet or when; and care that goes both ways is a partnership, not dependence.

The Ascendant is always the opposite degree, so with this Descendant [Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/) comes every time, and Saturn rules your chart while the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules your partnerships. The reserve people meet first often hides how much you want a home with someone, and the people who draw you tend to carry the tenderness you keep for later. Trust is built before it's given here, so partnerships tend to begin slowly, sometimes out of years of friendship. The Moon's house shows where your partnerships grow: the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

A Cancer Descendant is the rarest of Cancer's cusps: Cancer sets as quickly as Capricorn rises, in under two hours at London, so only about 1 birth in 13 has it, at New York as at London, in Placidus. Because Capricorn and the signs after it rise fast in the north, the 1st and 7th houses come out wide: at London, between about 47° and 59°, far too wide for Cancer to reach the 8th cusp as well. In about 2 London charts in 3, and 2 in 5 at New York, the whole of Leo lies inside your 7th as well ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), so both lights, the Moon through the cusp and the Sun through Leo, speak for your partnerships, and pride enters the bond too: you want to be proud of it, not only safe in it. In more than 1 London chart in 4, Cancer opens the 6th too, and colleagues and partners share the Moon ([Cancer on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/), [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); [Cancer on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/) goes with Sagittarius or Scorpio rising instead.

A Capricorn Ascendant rises in under two hours, so a birth time that's half an hour out can change this whole axis, and the time has to be settled before the reading starts ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
