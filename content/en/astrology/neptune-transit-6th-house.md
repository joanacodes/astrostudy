Neptune in your 6th house brings the planet of ideals into the most practical corner of the chart: the working day, the routines that hold it up, the care you give and the care you take. The stay is nearly thirteen years in the middle, and in a wide 6th it can run to twenty-six.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) serves an ideal: it wants what you do to mean something, and it dissolves whatever doesn't. The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is daily work, routine, craft and care, the tasks rather than the title, and the habits that keep a life running. With Neptune here, routines that mean nothing to you tend to slip away, sometimes before you've found new ones. Work can lean towards service, care or craft done as a kind of devotion; you may want your days to help someone, or to make something beautiful, more than to tick boxes. Compassion at work grows, and so can the risk of giving more than you have.

The 6th's sign in the natural order is Virgo, which lies opposite Pisces, the sign modern astrology gives to Neptune: detail meets dissolution here, and the work of these years is finding where they overlap. With Jupiter in your 6th from birth, the urge to help can outgrow the hours in a day.

The old books filed illness under the 6th and named it Bad Fortune. I read no illness in a transit, and no chart can diagnose anything ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)): tiredness, symptoms and worries about your body are for a doctor. What a [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this brings out is how your chart already works and cares, with more imagination and fewer hard edges.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune's stays in the 6th can be the longest of any house. In Placidus charts drawn for London the middle is nearly thirteen years, but the long end reaches twenty-six, because a 6th at that latitude can come out very wide; the short end is about eight and a half. New York's houses vary less, and its figures show it: nearly fourteen years in the middle, just under twenty-two at the long end.

Neptune's first touch of your 6th cusp starts the clock, its slow retrograde returns don't stop it, and reaching your Descendant ends it. That point, opposite your Ascendant, and the cusp where the stay begins both come from your birth time, and a chart without it gets no reading from me ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Neptune arrives from your 5th, after years of imagination in play and love ([Neptune transiting the 5th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-5th-house/)), and crosses your Descendant into the 7th, where partnership takes its turn ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)).

Right now Neptune is working through 6th cusps in Aries: it holds that sign from January 2026 to March 2039, after a first touch in March 2025. Cusps in Pisces were reached between April 2011 and January 2026, and cusps in Taurus start to be reached in May 2038.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give your days a gentle frame rather than a rigid one: a start, a stop, a few fixed points. Neptune in the 6th dissolves hard routines, but it doesn't do well with none.

Decide where your help ends. Saying how many hours, which tasks and for whom keeps compassion from turning into depletion.

Bring meaning into one ordinary task each day: do it slowly, or beautifully, or for someone. Under Neptune the 6th turns routine into ritual when you let it.
