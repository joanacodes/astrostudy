Some people change course well: they see the better way, take it, and keep their footing. Sun sextile Uranus describes that. With two signs between them, in elements that usually suit each other, change arrives as an offer rather than a shock.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the self you're shaping and where it's heading. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees: it experiments, reforms and makes room. Through a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), the two can cooperate, and change becomes something your purpose can call on.

You tend to welcome new tools, new methods and new people, and to use them in the service of what you're already building. When a situation goes stale, you can usually find the door out without slamming it. Your independence reads as an asset other people can work with, not a wall. This is also where Uranus's sense of systems shows: you often see how a small change in one place would improve the whole, and you can make it without a fight.

The sextile won't do it for you, though. An opportunity for change still has to be taken, and a sextile left unused can turn into a vague restlessness with nowhere to go. It works best, as I see it, for people who experiment on purpose: one change at a time, chosen, with an eye on where it leads.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Elements that complement each other are the rule here. A Sun in Aries with Uranus in Aquarius, its modern sign, brings initiative to new ideas; a Sun in Taurus with Uranus in Cancer changes things gently, starting at home. In the houses, the sextile connects the area where you're becoming yourself with the one where you need room, so freedom in one tends to feed growth in the other.

Within 8°, this sextile marks about 1 person in 11. Because Uranus moves on only a few degrees a year, the weeks that bring it hardly shift from one year to the next, and the Sun alone forms and dissolves the aspect ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)).

Around the sextile, the pair's other aspects:

- **[Sun conjunct Uranus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus/):** two signs closer, in one place; there, difference is simply who you are, not an option beside you.
- **[Sun square Uranus](/astrology/sun-square-uranus/):** a sign further round; the urge for change stops offering and starts pushing.
- **[Sun trine Uranus](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus/):** two signs further, in one element; originality runs with your purpose without needing to be invited.
- **[Sun opposite Uranus](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus/):** across the chart, with Uranus retrograde; change comes through other people and asks for balance.

Between two people, [Sun sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus-synastry/) is one person's fresh perspective helping the other's direction along: the friend who proposes the unexpected route, and turns out to be right. Taking that route stays a choice, and so does being read: I look at this contact only with both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-sun/) comes at most twice in a lifetime, since Uranus needs about 84 years to go round, and each visit stretches over a year or more, Uranus usually passing the point three times. Born with the sextile, you'll also meet Uranus crossing your Sun itself, early in life or late, depending on which side of the Sun it sat.

That transit suits the changes you choose: a new skill, a new circle, a different way of working. Weather, not a forecast: room to move, if you take it.
