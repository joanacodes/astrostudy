At family weddings they're polite through the service and talk about it all the way home: not unkindly, just from somewhere else. Composite Lilith in the 9th house describes that elsewhere: beliefs the relationship has reached on its own, and a refusal to take its convictions from anyone.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Faith, philosophy, study and long journeys have their place in [the 9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), a cadent house with Sagittarius as its natural sign. Its old name is the God, and it is where the Sun has its joy. Applied to [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it becomes the relationship's outlook: what it believes, and how far it reaches. Where [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) falls here, the part of a chart I read as untamed and hidden, those beliefs are the pair's own: a faith, a doubt or a politics worked out together, an appetite for study or for distant places that others find excessive, and a refusal of whatever creed each family assumed the two of you would carry on.

Neither of you needs to be a rebel in matters of belief for the bond to have its own heresy. Two people who kept their families' faith, or their families' scepticism, can find that together they think something else, and say so mostly to each other. What the bond hides is how far its views have travelled from where both of you started.

My rule for a birth chart, no birth time, no reading, doubles here: a composite house needs two times, and a method on top. The birth dates alone give composite Lilith its sign; the 9th house needs both birth times, and a choice between the midpoints of your charts' cusps and houses built from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live. A Lilith near the 9th's edges may be in the 8th or the 10th under another choice ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Set beside its relatives, this placement stands apart: [Lilith in the 9th house](/astrology/lilith-in-9th-house/) is one person's conviction or doubt at odds with their upbringing, and [their Lilith in your 9th house](/astrology/lilith-in-9th-house-synastry/) carries someone's refusal of handed-down creeds into your beliefs, while the composite's 9th is a creed the bond writes for itself. [Composite Lilith in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-8th-house/) guards its intimacy instead; [composite Lilith in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-10th-house/) carries its refusal into public life.

Its difficulty is a quiet sense of superiority. A pair that has thought its own way to its beliefs can come to look down on people who didn't, relatives included, or can live in ideas and travel plans instead of the life in front of it. It helps to hold your shared convictions as questions you keep asking, and to let the people you love believe what they believe.

Composites are not part of what I do in a reading; this is the technique as its users read it, next to both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it foretells no journey, no conversion and no break with family. It concerns two people's deepest convictions, so it is for pairs who both agree to look ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
