About every twelve years, Jupiter reaches the highest point of your birth chart, the degree that crossed the meridian above your birthplace at the minute you were born. Whatever you're building in public, and whatever you'd like to be known for, gets a wider stage for a while.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands, gathers meaning and turns it into principles; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is where your capacities become visible, through a role, a responsibility, a contribution. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes them work as one, so for a while growth shows where other people can see it. Like all [transits](/astrology/transits/), this one brings out what your chart already says about your direction; it doesn't hand out a career, a reputation or success.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Midheaven is the cusp of the 10th, so this crossing begins [Jupiter transiting the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-10th-house/), after a year in the 9th spent on study, travel or belief ([Jupiter transiting the 9th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-9th-house/)). That picture belongs to quadrant houses. In Whole Sign houses the Midheaven can sit in the 9th or the 11th, and the crossing doesn't open the 10th at all ([which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)).

A planet close to the Midheaven is among the most visible in a chart ([angular planets](/astrology/angular-planets/)), and Jupiter borrows some of that visibility while it crosses yours. What you do tends to be noticed, and a direction you had only half named becomes easier to say out loud.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, seven crossings in ten take about eleven days. The other three are loops during [Jupiter retrograde](/astrology/jupiter-retrograde/), bringing Jupiter over your Midheaven three times in about eight months.

The Midheaven needs your birthplace as much as your time. It moves about a degree for every four minutes of clock time and for every degree of longitude, so two people born at the same instant in London and in Paris have Midheavens more than two degrees apart: nearly the width of my whole orb. Get either wrong and the eleven days slide, which is why I won't read without both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has had this crossing six or seven times. Between two of them, Jupiter goes all the way round your chart: [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-midheaven/) and [transiting Jupiter square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-midheaven/) within three years, [transiting Jupiter trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-midheaven/) at about four, and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-midheaven/) at about six, on your IC, before the same angles return on the way back up.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put your name to something. Jupiter at the top of the chart favours work that is visibly yours: a project you lead, a piece you sign, a role you accept in your own words.

Update the way you describe what you do. The Midheaven grows into a direction over years, and this crossing is a good moment to say where you've got to, in a sentence you'd be glad to hear repeated.

Measure the season by contribution rather than status. Visibility isn't value; if, by the end of it, more people can see what your work gives them, the crossing has done its part.
