The kitchen took two years to plan and one long weekend to fit, and nobody has wanted to change it since. Composite Mars in Taurus describes that kind of drive in a relationship: slow to build, fond of what it can touch, and very hard to turn once it has chosen a direction.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

I leave composites out of my own readings, so take this page as a description of the method. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) puts each planet halfway between the two places it held at your births, and its [Mars](/astrology/mars/) shows how the relationship spends its energy. Here that midpoint lies in [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), the fixed earth sign Venus rules, which tradition counts a [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) for Mars: across the zodiac sits Scorpio, one of Mars's own signs. A detriment describes an awkward fit, not a weaker bond, and here the drive works through patience instead of speed. It belongs to about 1 pair in 13.

Such a pair acts by accumulating. It would rather improve one thing for years than start five, and what it makes together, a home, a shared skill, a habit of long Sunday walks, tends to be made to last. Competition between you looks less like a race than an endurance test: who keeps at it after the novelty has gone. Desire is unhurried and physical, and time, touch and a good meal say more in this bond than grand gestures. Someone born with [Mars in Taurus](/astrology/mars-in-taurus/) pushes steadily on their own; in the composite, steadiness is a property of the pair, and two restless people can be surprised to find that together they dig in.

Disagreements start late and last. A row can be put off for weeks, and once it begins neither of you may move an inch; silence and quiet resistance replace raised voices. Persistence isn't stubbornness, but in a standoff the two feel alike from inside. What helps is a way to change course without anyone losing face: agree in advance that either of you can say *let's look at this again in a month*, and keep to it. Small conversations held often suit this Mars better than the big reckoning it tends to save up for.

A long sulk is friction; fear is something else. If either of you is ever frightened of the other, no chart has an answer for that: in the UK, Samaritans listen on 116 123 at any hour, and 999 is there for an emergency.

On one side, [composite Mars in Aries](/astrology/composite-mars-in-aries/) would have started already; on the other, [composite Mars in Gemini](/astrology/composite-mars-in-gemini/) would still be weighing the options aloud. Taurus waits until it's sure, then builds.

Astrologers who use composites would look next at where the composite [Venus](/astrology/venus/) falls, since Venus rules Taurus and shows what all this effort is for, and at the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), because a composite is one view among several and never a verdict: it can't say how long you'll last, or what you'll own. It also belongs only to couples who have both asked for it ([Synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
