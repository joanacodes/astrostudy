Jupiter's year in your 5th house makes room for whatever you do for no reason except that you love doing it. Tradition called the 5th Good Fortune; with Jupiter in it, I read the house as permission.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands and generates: it opens possibilities and helps them grow into something. The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is creativity, play, pleasure and romance, the part of life that doesn't have to justify itself, and Venus has its joy there. While Jupiter is there, making things, playing, flirting and performing tend to come with more appetite. A creative project can take up more room, a hobby can turn into a craft, and you may find out what you actually enjoy, as opposed to what you're good at.

The old books would have called it a doubly kind season, since the house is Good Fortune and Jupiter the greater [benefic](/astrology/benefics-and-malefics/). I keep the kindness and leave the promise. Joy isn't excess, and the year asks you to choose your pleasures rather than collect them.

Each planet already in your 5th gets a few weeks of extra light as Jupiter passes; a natal Sun there, the house's natural ruler, can make that pass feel like being seen at last.

In the tradition, children were a 5th house matter as well. A transit can't tell anyone whether or when they'll have a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/): what I read in your 5th is your own pleasure and creativity, as an adult. This [transit](/astrology/transits/), like any other, adds nothing to your chart: it brings out the pleasure and creativity already written there.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 5th keeps Jupiter about as long as the average house does: in Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, usually about a year, most often from about 11 months to nearly 2 years. At New York's latitude it's about a year too, a little under the all-house middle there, with the long end at about 22 months.

Where your 5th begins and how wide it is both come from your birth time and birthplace; without the time there's no placing the house at all, and so no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Jupiter arrives from your 4th, where the year was about home ([Jupiter transiting the 4th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-4th-house/)), and leaves for your 6th, where the work comes back ([Jupiter transiting the 6th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-6th-house/)).

Jupiter is in Leo, the sign that gives the 5th its character, from June 2026 to July 2027, and again from June 2038; with Leo on the cusp of your 5th, the stay is either under way or about to begin. For the other signs, count in order: Virgo until August 2028, Libra until September 2029, Scorpio until October 2030, Sagittarius until November 2031, Capricorn until November 2032, Aquarius from April 2032 until December 2033, Pisces from April 2033 until April 2034, Aries until April 2035, Taurus until May 2036, Gemini until May 2037 and Cancer until June 2038. Then add the cusp's degree: the stay begins when Jupiter reaches it.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make something you don't need to be good at. Jupiter in the 5th is about the making, and the year is generous with beginnings if you let yourself be a beginner.

Say yes to the class, the stage, the dance floor, the afternoon off: the 5th grows by being lived, not by being planned.

Watch for the moment pleasure turns into proving something. The point of this house is that it needs no audience, even when it has one. And if romance is part of the year, let it be light enough to enjoy; what it becomes is up to the people in it, not to the sky.
