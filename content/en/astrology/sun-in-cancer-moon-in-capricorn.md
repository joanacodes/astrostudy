Cancer, where your Sun stands, is ruled by the Moon; your Moon stands opposite, in the sign where tradition finds it least at home. Cancer and Capricorn are the two ends of the Moon's own axis, and you were born with a light at each end, close to a full moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and cardinal earth, six signs apart: an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) by sign, across the axis of the two solstices, since the Sun enters Cancer at the June solstice and Capricorn at the December one. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) lives for the people it calls its own and keeps their history close; the [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) settles when responsibilities are clear, kept and seen through. One light looks after people through closeness, the other through provision: the hug and the pension, the shared meal and the reliable routine. Both are care, and you need both.

The Moon's axis is what sets this pair apart. The Moon rules Cancer, its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), and Capricorn, the opposite sign, is its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/). So the light that governs your Sun's sign is itself placed where it has to work hardest, and your feelings live in a sign that asks them to wait, carry and endure. I read that as comfort learned the long way round: the need to be looked after turned into the habit of looking after. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), Capricorn's ruler, answers for your Moon; where Saturn sits, by sign and house, shows where holding things together steadies you.

The Moon stood 151° to 209° from the Sun, round or nearly round, rising around sunset; the birth fell close to the full moon, never more than about two and a half days away from it. The [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) edges out the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) in the eight-phase count, with slightly more of these births. Close to 180° the lights make an exact [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/), and with the Sun in Cancer that full moon can only be a [full moon in Capricorn](/astrology/full-moon-in-capricorn/). The gibbous phase I read as fine-tuning, the full as purpose and need meeting face to face; here the meeting is between the one who needs care and the one who gives it, and both are you. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) separates the two.

About one person in every 142 has a Cancer Sun with a Capricorn Moon. The Moon's passage through Capricorn lasts some two and a half days. If it entered or left the sign on your birth date, only the birth time can rule out the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-sagittarius/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-aquarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also gives the houses, which show which two parts of your life face each other across the chart.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love by protecting and by providing. The Cancer Sun wants closeness, shared history and a home that feels like a refuge; the Capricorn Moon shows love through reliability: turning up, keeping promises, building something that lasts. Partners can count on you entirely, though they may not always see the tenderness under the competence. Looking after someone can come easier than admitting you need looking after. Letting a partner carry something for you, and saying that it helps, is part of the closeness the Sun wants.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that protects people through structure suits this pair: a family business, social policy, healthcare management, housing, heritage and archives, any role that keeps something going for the next generation. Care is the motive, responsibility the method. You may become the steady presence of the team, the one who remembers both the birthdays and the deadlines. Workplaces without loyalty, or without order, wear you down; one that offers both can keep you for decades.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With the Moon in its detriment, feelings can start to look like a weakness to be managed. The Cancer Sun feels everything; the Capricorn Moon postpones it until after the work, and the work rarely ends. You may carry family responsibilities early and long, and forget that you're allowed to need something too. What helps, odd as it sounds, is giving feelings a regular place: an evening, a person, a notebook where needing comfort is part of the routine rather than a failure of it.
