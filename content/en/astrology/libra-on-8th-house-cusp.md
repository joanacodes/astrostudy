Libra on the 8th house cusp brings the scales into the house of what passes between people: trust, intimacy, joint money, debts. Libra keeps a running account here, and rests only when both columns match.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) follows the house of partners and holds what a bond creates, risks and owes ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Libra](/astrology/libra/) on its cusp, trust tends to grow in matched steps: you open up about as far as the other person does, and notice when the exchange tilts. A debt, of money or of gratitude, sits uneasily with you until it's squared. Joint accounts, shared bills and loans between friends go best when everyone can see the terms. In a crisis you tend to look for the fair way through, the one that leaves nobody carrying more than their share. Counted from the 7th, this house is your partner's 2nd ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)), and with Libra on it, what the other person brings is something you'd rather weigh openly than guess at.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your 8th, and the house it occupies shows where sharing, owing and trusting come up in your life: the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). This cusp gives Venus a second house in every chart, at London as at New York: Taurus opens the 3rd, so what you share and how you talk about it answer to one planet. The money talk, the conversation before signing, the message that settles things: the 3rd's everyday words do much of the 8th's work for you. Mars, through Aries on your 2nd, rules what you earn and own. What's yours you go out and get; what's shared you agree.

With Placidus houses, the system I use, Libra lands on the 8th cusp in about 1 London birth in 20, and 1 in 17 at New York. The 8th sits just above the western horizon, where Libra moves fast: slow to rise in the north, it is quick to set. The rest of these charts is settled to a surprising degree. Sagittarius is on the Midheaven in every one of them, so Jupiter rules your 10th. At London, Aquarius rises in about 14 charts in 15, and Pisces then lies wholly inside your 1st house and Virgo inside your 7th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). With this cusp, Libra never opens a second house; across all London births, it opens none at all in about 1 in 6.

The old books filed death and legacies under the 8th. I read neither as something to come ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); what this cusp shows is how you share, owe and trust, here and now.

Born a little earlier, Libra could have stood higher, on the 9th ([Libra on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-9th-house-cusp/)); a little later, it could have been setting ([Libra on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/)), with Aries rising in place of Aquarius or Pisces. Which of the three is yours is a matter of minutes, so the recorded time comes before any reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
