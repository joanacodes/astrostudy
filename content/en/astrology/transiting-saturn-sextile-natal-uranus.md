Change made in steps, and steps that stick: that's what the gentlest meeting of Aquarius's two rulers in your chart has to offer. Saturn makes this sextile to your Uranus twice in each round of about 29½ years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is where you need freedom, originality and room to test alternatives. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) supplies patience, method and the long view. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through opportunity and cooperation, and like every opportunity it waits for you: nothing under a sextile changes by itself.

What tends to open is a practical route for reform. A change you've wanted can be made gradually rather than all at once; an experiment gets a trial period and a way to measure it; an unconventional arrangement at work or at home acquires rules that let it last. Reform isn't destruction, and this transit is good at proving it: the old structure doesn't have to fall for the new one to be built beside it.

Uranus's familiar error is assuming that disruption produces progress by itself; Saturn's is believing that control guarantees safety. The sextile lets each soften the other's error without a fight.

Many people your age share your Uranus sign, so it's the house that makes this sextile yours. With Uranus in the 9th, Saturn makes the sextile from your 7th ([Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/)) or your 11th, and a partner, an agreement or a group gives a new way of thinking its first practical form. The houses that carry this sextile come from your birth time, and so does any reading of it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A trial period with a proper review at the end fits inside this sextile, which usually lasts about nine months, counted from 1½° before the exact sextile to 1° after it. A little over half of Saturn's [transits](/astrology/transits/) stretch to about nine and a half months with three exact passes; without a loop, three weeks can see it come and go.

Its first visit depends on the angle between Saturn and Uranus at your birth, which changes slowly from year to year. For someone born on 1 January 1945, 1975 or 1995 it came before the age of three; for 1 January 1935, 1965 or 1985, between about 7 and 10; for 1 January 1955 and 2005, at 16.3 and 13. Nobody born from 1930 to 2009 waited beyond about 20 for the first, half had it by 7, and by 80 everyone has met five or six.

The generations born with [Saturn sextile Uranus](/astrology/saturn-sextile-uranus/), in the mid-1930s, the late 1940s, around 1980 and the mid-1990s, carry this cooperation from birth; everyone else meets it as this transit, twice a round. The first sextile of a round follows [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-uranus/) by about five years and comes two or three years before [transiting Saturn square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-uranus/); [transiting Saturn trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-uranus/) follow.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change one thing in steps. Pick a reform and give it a timetable of small stages.

Run the experiment properly: a trial period, a way to measure it, a date to decide.

Build beside, not instead. Under this sextile, a new structure can grow next to the old one until the old one is no longer needed.
