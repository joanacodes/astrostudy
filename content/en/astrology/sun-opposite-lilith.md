Sun opposite Lilith hangs a mirror across your chart: the Sun on one side, the Moon's far point on the other. It describes the part of you that wouldn't obey showing up first in other people, fascinating or unsettling you, until you recognise it as yours.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your sense of self: the identity and direction you live from. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is read by astrologers in many different ways; I read it as the part of you that learned to stay out of sight: instinct, desire, anger, refusal, the power you didn't feel allowed to use. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them as a polarity, in which each end lights up the other.

In plain words, you may find the “too much” in others before you find it in yourself: the friend who says exactly what they think, the partner who refuses to be managed, the colleague who wants things openly and says so. They may fascinate you or annoy you, often both. The opposition doesn't ask you to become them, or to push them away. It asks you to notice what you're seeing, because it's usually a part of you asking for a place. I don't read Lilith as something to fix, and I don't read this opposition as a pull towards danger. Polarity isn't contradiction: you can be steady and still let that part speak.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show in close relationships, and in whom you're drawn to or wary of. The sign pair gives the mirror its subject. A Sun in Taurus opposite Lilith in Scorpio wants calm and keeps meeting deep, private desires in others; a Sun in Leo opposite Lilith in Aquarius wants to be seen, and keeps meeting people who refuse to belong to anyone. With the Sun in the 1st and Lilith in the 7th, partnership is where the refused part appears, sometimes as an attraction to the forbidden or the unequal, and the work is not shrinking in a couple.

About 1 person in 22 is born within 8° of it, on a day when the Sun was lined up with the near end of the Moon's orbit, across from the far point that mean Lilith marks; the alignment comes round every 13½ months or so. The closer to exact, the brighter the mirror.

The pair's other aspects keep the refused part nearer to you:

- **[Sun conjunct Lilith](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith/):** at your centre instead of across the room.
- **[Sun sextile Lilith](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith/):** available without anyone else having to carry it for you.
- **[Sun square Lilith](/astrology/sun-square-lilith/):** the friction comes from the side, in your own choices, rather than through others.
- **[Sun trine Lilith](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith/):** identity and instinct in step, with no mirror needed.

Between two charts, [Sun opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith-synastry/) sets one person's centre across from the other's refused part: a strong pull, often unsettling. Contacts like this bring out what each person usually keeps hidden, so I read them only when both have asked ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

Mean Lilith comes back to its birth place roughly every nine years, and halfway through each round it reaches the point opposite, where your Sun stands. So in adult life it crosses your Sun at about 22, 31, 40 and so on, each time for a couple of weeks. And each year, for a day or two, the Sun in the sky stands across from your natal Lilith. Weather, not a forecast: moments to ask whether what you see in others is something you'd like back, never events.
