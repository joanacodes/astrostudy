There's a kind of joy that is always asked to explain itself. With Lilith in the 5th house, it's often yours. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point of the Moon's orbit farthest from Earth, was in [the 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) when you were born: the house of play, pleasure, romance and the things you make for love.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The 5th house is succedent and its natural sign is Leo. The old astrologers called it Good Fortune and gave Venus, the planet of pleasure, its joy there. It covers what you do because you want to: creating, playing, flirting, falling in love, taking a risk for the fun of it. With Lilith here, the hidden part is pleasure itself — the game you were told you'd outgrown, the hobby that wasn't a proper use of time, the way you enjoyed things more openly than the people around you liked.

This Lilith refuses to enjoy things in the approved way. It won't make its art agreeable, its fun respectable or its love conventional just to be allowed them. The boundary here protects play: free time that stays free, work made for its own sake, a love that's chosen rather than arranged.

Tradition also gave the 5th children. I don't read them there: no chart can say whether or when anyone will have a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I never read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/). What I read in the 5th is your own joy, as an adult.

You'll need your birth time for this one, as for every house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)); I read houses in Placidus. The house before, [Lilith in the 4th house](/astrology/lilith-in-4th-house/), keeps its hidden part at home; the house after, [Lilith in the 6th house](/astrology/lilith-in-6th-house/), in the daily routine. In the 5th, it hides in the hours that were meant to be free.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Romance is where this Lilith shows most. It loves on its own terms, and may enjoy courtship, flirting and the dizzy early stage of love more openly than it was taught was proper. It refuses to be courted by a script, and it cools when love turns into a performance put on for other people's approval.

What it needs from a partner is someone who can play: who laughs, takes risks, makes things, and doesn't treat your delight as something to grow out of.

## At work {#at-work}

You may do your best work where something of yours is on show: a design, a performance, a piece of writing, a project you'd have done for nothing. The hidden part refuses to sand down its originality to make it more acceptable, and it suffers in jobs where nothing is ever made for the joy of it. Hobbies and work can swap places here; what you do on Sundays may be the truest thing you do.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Pleasure can come with a bill. If joy was judged, you may enjoy something and then pay for it in guilt, or keep it for later until later never comes. Another version turns play into a show, so that even fun has to impress. None of it makes you frivolous. It describes a joy that was never allowed to be simply joy.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make something for nobody: no audience, no purpose, no grade. Keep one regular hour that's only for play, and protect it as you would a meeting. Let yourself be bad at something you love.

In my notebook, Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time, one of three placements that did ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), so I take what it asks for seriously. In the 5th house, what it usually asks for is simple: permission.
