Neptune's trine to Mercury is the easiest of their contacts, and the one that most often goes unused. For about two and a half years, listening, reading and explaining all take on more feeling, for very little effort.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings images, empathy and a feel for the unspoken; [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) interprets and exchanges. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, keeps both functions intact while they work together, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) doesn't blur your thinking: it gives it range. You may listen better, hear what someone means beneath their words, find metaphors that make a hard idea simple, or take to learning by ear. Reading between the lines gets easier without the lines themselves going missing.

Ease isn't inevitability. The trine helps whatever you're already doing with words, so its gift is largest if you're doing something: writing, teaching, translating, telling a story. A deadline helps: Neptune's ease is generous with time, and work without an end date can float. Your own chart, not your birthday, gives the years, since Mercury can sit in the sign before or after your Sun's. Born with Mercury retrograde, you meet the trine the same way: what counts is Mercury's degree, not its direction at birth.

With your Mercury in the 11th and Neptune passing through your 7th ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)), conversations with a partner can widen into a shared ideal; with Mercury in the 12th and Neptune in the 4th, quiet reflection and a sense of belonging feed each other. Both houses need your birth time, and so does every chart I read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine runs for about two and a half years, or about twenty-two months when the loops are tight. The exact trine comes three times in a typical pass; five, in about one pass in four, mean Neptune's loops are overlapping on your Mercury's degree.

Mercury moves quickly, so people born only weeks apart can have it in different signs and meet this trine decades apart, or one of them not at all. By 80, one person in six born between 1930 and 2009 still hasn't had this trine; most have met it once, a few twice. The square ([transiting Neptune square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-mercury/)) comes about fourteen years before or after it, on the conjunction's side, and the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-mercury/)) about twenty-seven years on the other; the sextile ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-mercury/)) and the conjunction ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-mercury/)) lie further back along the same road. [Mercury trine Neptune](/astrology/mercury-trine-neptune/) at birth makes this fluency a habit; under the transit, it's a guest for a couple of years.

Before 2039 this trine reaches Mercury in Leo or Sagittarius, and since Mercury stays close to the Sun, the Sun in those charts is somewhere from Cancer to Virgo or from Scorpio to Capricorn ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Use it on something with words in it: a letter, a talk, a story, a course you teach.

Listen more than usual. The trine's gift often arrives as something you hear.

Keep the ideas that come easily, in a notebook you'll reread. Ease makes them seem slight, and some of them aren't.
