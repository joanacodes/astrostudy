Halfway round its circuit from your Mercury, about 42 years after crossing it, Uranus stands on the far side of the zodiac. For about twenty months the new ideas in your life tend to come from across the table, and your thinking improves by answering them.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) gathers information and turns it into reasons and words. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and reforms, and an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, makes the two answer each other from across the chart. Here your way of reasoning meets ideas that don't come from you, and the contrast makes both clearer.

It often arrives through people. A colleague questions a method you've used for years; a partner or a friend takes up a way of thinking you find strange; someone younger shows you a tool that makes your expertise look slow. You may argue more, and more usefully. The opposition rewards curiosity about the other side, because what you take from it is rarely what you set out to defend.

It runs both ways, too. You may be the one bringing the unsettling idea into a team or a family that thinks one way, and find that the hard part is putting it in words they can hear.

Analysis is the help here, not the obstacle: weigh the new idea on its merits rather than its novelty. Uranus can make difference feel like accuracy, and someone else's certainty is not proof. Readers born with [Mercury opposite Uranus](/astrology/mercury-opposite-uranus/) will know the shape of it: a mind that sharpens against whoever sits across the table.

With Mercury in your 9th and Uranus crossing your 3rd ([Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/)), everyday talk, local news and the people next door challenge beliefs you studied for. Without the hour you were born, that axis can't be drawn, so neither can this reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The opposition holds your Mercury for about twenty months between 1½° before exact and 1° after; eleven months is enough for the briefest passes. In more than nine passes in ten it crosses the exact point three times, which leaves room to change your mind, and to change it back.

This is the far end of Uranus's round with your Mercury, about 42 years after [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-mercury/). Before it come the first [transiting Uranus sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-mercury/), [transiting Uranus square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-mercury/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-mercury/); after it, the same three again in reverse order, the trine first. Leaving aside about 1 person in 33 born between 1930 and 2009, everyone gets to this [transit](/astrology/transits/) before 80, and gets to it once.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Listen to the idea that annoys you. Under this opposition, the irritating suggestion is often the useful one, or the one that shows you why your own holds up.

Ask two questions before you answer. The opposition goes better as an interview than as a debate.

Put agreements in writing. When views are moving on both sides, a short note of what was decided saves a great deal of goodwill.
