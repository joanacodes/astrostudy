Most people have one thing they've learned to work around. Venus conjunct Chiron in synastry puts another person's way of valuing in exactly that place: one person's Venus sits within 6° of the other's Chiron, and the workaround, the careful planning, the particular humour, the way of saying no, tends to be seen and liked rather than politely ignored.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) admires and enjoys: it holds what a person finds lovely, whom they're drawn to, what a fair exchange looks like to them. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts: it finds a way of functioning around a sensitivity or limit that stays, and turns the workaround into skill. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) welds the two into one gesture, and in the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) each half of the gesture comes from a different person.

If the Venus is yours, you find yourself appreciating the very things this person built around a difficulty. What others treat as a detour, you find admirable, sometimes lovely. Their way of adapting may change your own taste too: fewer things for show, more things that work for real people.

If the Chiron is yours, the arrangements you've made around a tender spot are noticed and enjoyed. That can feel unexpectedly easy. You may become less guarded about what you can't do, and more generous with what you've learned the long way.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

At 0°, appreciation and repair can run together. If the Venus is yours, liking someone's adaptations can slide into a project of making things better for them; but adapting doesn't require a cure, and needing some support doesn't make anyone dependent. If the Chiron is yours, being valued around your tender spot can put it at the centre of the bond, until it seems you're appreciated for the limitation rather than for yourself. The tender place is part of the context, not the whole person, on either side.

The other angles hold the same two functions further apart:

- **[Venus sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-chiron-synastry/):** know-how and appreciation traded once somebody asks.
- **[Venus square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-chiron-synastry/):** one person's tastes pressing, unknowingly, where the other has to adjust.
- **[Venus trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-chiron-synastry/):** room made so smoothly that the effort goes unseen.
- **[Venus opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-chiron-synastry/):** ease at one end, hard-won skill at the other.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask before helping, and let the Chiron person say what kind of support, if any, they actually want. Let appreciation range over the whole person, not only the adapted part: talk about films, plans, nonsense. A mentor and an apprentice, two friends, colleagues where one admires the other's way round a problem, adult siblings or a couple can all keep that balance.

About 1 pair of people in 15 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 6°. Chiron's orbit is uneven: it lingers about eight years in some signs and passes through others in under two, so the cohort sharing the Chiron person's Chiron sign can be short or long. Venus, a few weeks in each sign, decides whether this particular person's taste lands on it. Whatever the hour, the two bodies sit in almost the same places that day; the hour sets the houses, where in each life the adapting and the appreciating happen ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Of everything my notebook checked, the trio of Saturn, Lilith and Chiron held 75 to 90% of the time; that's why the Chiron side of this conjunction is where I start, and where I go most gently. Those were single charts; between two people I have no count to offer ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#chiron)). In one chart, [Venus conjunct Chiron](/astrology/venus-conjunct-chiron/) shapes how a person relates around their own tender place. Shared between two, it touches something that is only theirs to show, and it waits for both of them to want the reading ([a chart is personal](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
