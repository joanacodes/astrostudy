Sun square Lilith sets the two a quarter of the zodiac apart, so the part of you that wouldn't obey meets your sense of self from the side. It often describes an old habit of toning yourself down, an equally old urge to stop, and the long work of choosing between them on purpose.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is identity and self-expression: the coherent self you aim to be, and to show. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is read in many different ways; I read it as the part of you that would not obey: instinct, appetite, anger, autonomy, what was rejected or kept out of view. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through constructive friction. The two keep catching on each other, and the catching is what asks you to grow.

In plain words, there's a version of you that you learned was acceptable, and a part that doesn't fit inside it. You may go quiet when you'd like to speak, then say far more than you meant; behave beautifully for months, then refuse something flatly. Neither swing is a failure. The square asks for a third way: letting the refused part speak on purpose, in a form you choose, before it has to break out. I don't read Lilith as something to fix, and I don't read this square as a flaw in your character. Worked with, it gives you a very clear sense of your own boundaries.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as a firm sense of what you will and won't do, and as moments when an old rule about how to behave meets a newer instinct. The signs involved usually share a modality, which keeps the rub regular: with Lilith in Capricorn, a Sun in Aries or Libra keeps meeting the question of whose rules count, and whether it may make its own. Houses name the two areas of life involved: with the Sun in the 7th and Lilith in the 10th, the person you are with a partner and the person you are in public don't always agree about how much to show.

The square, within 8°, turns up in about 1 person in 11. In each 13½-month round, the Sun squares Lilith twice, about three and a half months either side of their meeting. Lilith is one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). That figure is for Lilith's sign and house, not for this square, so I treat the square as a question to ask in a reading rather than an answer.

The pair's other aspects ease or sharpen the same tug:

- **[Sun conjunct Lilith](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith/):** the refused part at the centre, harder to hide and less of a tug-of-war.
- **[Sun sextile Lilith](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith/):** the same instinct at a friendly angle, ready when you call on it.
- **[Sun trine Lilith](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith/):** your own terms flow without a fight, and refusals come calmly.
- **[Sun opposite Lilith](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith/):** the friction moves across the chart and is met in other people.

Between two charts, [Sun square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-lilith-synastry/) rubs one person's sense of self against the other's refused part: attraction mixed with friction, and a contact where honesty helps more than tact, whichever side you're on.

## In transits {#in-transits}

When mean Lilith squares your Sun, the moment is short: at nine months or so per sign, the square arrives and passes within weeks. Twice a year, for a day or two, the Sun in the sky makes the same square to your natal Lilith. Weather, not a forecast: a brief stretch when the old question of how much of yourself to show comes back, with no answer fixed in advance.
