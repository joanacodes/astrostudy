Pluto opposite North Node puts the planet of deep renewal on the South Node, the familiar end of the nodal axis. Letting go and starting over may be something you already know how to do; the growth across the chart asks for something you know less well.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the function of transformation and regeneration: clearing what is obsolete, renewing at depth, and growing a more authentic agency. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a complementary capacity you build by practice; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), always opposite, the established capacities that come with little effort. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates the two through polarity, and with Pluto on the South Node, renewal sits on the familiar side of the line.

In plain words, transformation is an old skill for you. You may know how to end things, begin again and find strength in the process, and other people may lean on you when everything is in pieces. The North Node, opposite, asks you to practise a capacity that doesn't come as easily, and its sign says which. The pull is to reach for the familiar tool, deep change, even when a situation asks for the other one.

Established capacity isn't automatically limiting, and the South Node is no worse than the North. The opposition asks for balance, not rejection: keep the renewal you're good at, and give the less familiar capacity the patient practice it needs.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Pluto and the South Node usually share a sign, with the North Node in the sign opposite. With Pluto and the South Node in Scorpio, intensity and starting over are the old reflex, and the North Node in Taurus asks for something that may feel harder: calm, steady, simple pleasures. The node moves backwards through a sign in about 18 months, so this opposition belongs to a few months of births, about once in each round.

The houses name the two ends, given a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Pluto on the South Node in the 4th, for instance, could make deep change at home familiar ground, with growth asked for in the public life of the 10th.

About 1 person in 31 born between 1930 and 2009 has Pluto within 6° of an opposition to the North Node, counted with the true node. When the orb is close to 6°, I check the mean node as well, a degree or two away at most, before telling you this opposition is there ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

The pair's other aspects, by contrast:

- **[Pluto conjunct North Node](/astrology/pluto-conjunct-north-node/):** the reverse placement, with renewal as the new skill rather than the old one.
- **[Pluto sextile North Node](/astrology/pluto-sextile-north-node/):** renewal bridging the two ends, easy with the familiar.
- **[Pluto square North Node](/astrology/pluto-square-north-node/):** renewal at right angles to both ends, belonging to neither.
- **[Pluto trine North Node](/astrology/pluto-trine-north-node/):** renewal flowing towards the new, with a small opening back.

Between two charts, [Pluto opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/pluto-opposite-north-node-synastry/) sets one person's Pluto on the other's South Node: something deep and strangely familiar, which can steady the other person or keep them circling old ground. I read it from both sides, and slowly.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-pluto/) comes once in each 18.6-year round, and it is also the transiting South Node passing over your Pluto, for a month or two, with that season's eclipses falling close by.

Born with the opposition, you meet it again at each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/), at about 18½, 37 and 56. The [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), at about 9, 28 and 46, brings the North Node itself over your Pluto: for a month or two, the renewal you know so well lines up with the direction you're growing in.

Weather, not a forecast: these seasons set the old skill and the new one side by side. Which one you reach for stays yours.
