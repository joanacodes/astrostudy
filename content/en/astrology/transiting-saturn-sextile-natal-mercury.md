Twice in each round of about 29½ years, Saturn stands 60° from your Mercury and offers your mind something it doesn't always ask for: a method. It's the gentlest of Saturn's angles to the way you think, and the most practical.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) brings structure, patience and the habit of finishing; [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) handles learning, language, reasoning and exchange. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) connects them through opportunity, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) tends to open a door to disciplined thinking rather than push you through it: a syllabus that suits you, a teacher worth listening to, a deadline that helps more than it hurts.

It favours what grows by practice: a language, an instrument, a professional skill, a body of writing that adds up over months. Conversations with people who know more than you tend to be useful now, and so does feedback you'd normally avoid. Ideas you've carried loosely for years can be given an outline.

Of the two sextiles in a round, the first, about five years after Saturn crossed your Mercury, is the better one for taking a method in: a vocabulary, a technique, a discipline of attention. The second, about five years before the next crossing, suits giving it back out, by teaching, explaining or writing up what you know.

Crossing your 1st, for instance, Saturn offers its method through your own discipline, a routine you keep for nobody but yourself ([Saturn transiting the 1st house](/astrology/saturn-transit-1st-house/)). Born with [Mercury sextile Saturn](/astrology/mercury-sextile-saturn/), you've had this cooperation since birth, and the transit invites you to use it on something new.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile usually lasts about as long as a course of study, around nine months, and a little over half the time it comes as a loop of three exact passes over about nine and a half ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)). At full speed it passes in about three weeks.

In nine months, [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) usually comes round two or three times, for about three weeks each. I read those weeks as built-in pauses for revision inside a season of building, not as setbacks: rereading, correcting, checking before the next step. Mercury itself moves up to about two degrees a day, so even the hour of birth can nudge its degree, and the house it sits in needs the full birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Five or six of these sextiles come by 80 for people born between 1930 and 2009. Measured from [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-mercury/), they fall at about five years and about twenty-five: the first leads into [transiting Saturn square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-mercury/), the second follows the round's [transiting Saturn opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-mercury/) and its later [transiting Saturn trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-mercury/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sign up for the structured version of what you want to learn: the course with assessments, the class that meets every week, the mentor who reads your drafts.

Write a little every day, or study at the same hour. Saturn's sextile pays interest on regularity, and small sessions add up faster than occasional marathons.

Ask someone more experienced to look at your work, and listen to the answer. This sextile makes good criticism easier to hear, and easier to use.
