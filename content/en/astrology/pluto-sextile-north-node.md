Pluto sextile North Node lets the planet of renewal lend a hand to your direction of growth while staying on good terms with where you started. It's a bridge more than a push, and it matters only when you walk across it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the deep-renewal function of a chart: it clears out structures past their use, regenerates, and turns what it integrates into authentic strength. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity you develop by practice, less familiar than what came easily, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), exactly opposite, the consolidated habits you began with. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) to one node is a trine to the other, so Pluto here relates to both: an opportunity towards the new, and an easy flow with the familiar.

In plain words, the times you let something go tend to open a door onto your growth, and you can usually make the change without turning against your past. That's an unusual shape for Pluto, whose renewals are often pictured as breaks. Here they work more like bridges: the old strength helps you cross, and the crossing takes you somewhere new.

An opportunity still needs taking. The sextile won't push you towards the North Node; it only makes the way easier when you choose it. The risk is that the easy trine to the South Node keeps you comfortable, renewing what you already know instead of what you're learning.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A sextile joins signs two apart, so Pluto sits two signs from the North Node and four from the South Node. With Pluto in Libra and the North Node in Sagittarius, renewal in your relationships can open the way to a broader view of your own, while staying friendly with the South Node in Gemini and its quick, familiar curiosity. The node does the moving, backwards through a sign in about 18 months, so the sextile belongs to two windows of a few months in each round of the nodes.

The houses place the bridge, given a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Pluto in the 8th with the North Node in the 10th, for instance, might let what you learn about trust and change open doors in your working life, with the South Node in the 4th as familiar ground at home.

About 1 person in 17 born between 1930 and 2009 has Pluto within 6° of a sextile to the North Node, counted with the true node. Read with the mean node instead, the angle shifts by a degree or two at most; I look at both, and say so when they disagree.

How the pair's other angles compare:

- **[Pluto conjunct North Node](/astrology/pluto-conjunct-north-node/):** renewal on the growth point itself, a craft to practise.
- **[Pluto square North Node](/astrology/pluto-square-north-node/):** the same planet across the path, at odds with both ends.
- **[Pluto trine North Node](/astrology/pluto-trine-north-node/):** the mirror of this sextile, an easy flow towards growth and an opening back to the familiar.
- **[Pluto opposite North Node](/astrology/pluto-opposite-north-node/):** Pluto at the starting point, where renewal is already second nature.

Between two charts, [Pluto sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/pluto-sextile-north-node-synastry/) is a quieter contact: one person's capacity for renewal offers the other a way forward, if both take it up. I read it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-pluto/) comes twice in each 18.6-year round of the nodes, for a month or two each time, and that season's eclipses fall a sextile or a trine away from your Pluto.

Born with the sextile, you meet it again exactly at each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/), at about 18½, 37 and 56. The [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/) halfway between swaps the roles: the transiting North Node then trines your Pluto while the South Node sextiles it, a good moment to notice which end you've been leaning on.

Weather, not a forecast: these seasons show when the bridge is easier to see. Crossing it stays a choice.
