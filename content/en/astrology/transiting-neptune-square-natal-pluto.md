Neptune reaches the square to your natal Pluto in adolescence, between about 10½ and 20, for everyone born from 1930 to 2009. It is the first of the Neptune–Pluto transits an adult might remember, and the only way to read it is looking back.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) in your chart is where you hold on hardest and where you learn, slowly, to let go and rebuild. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings ideals into focus and softens the edges round them. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates the two through friction, so as a [transit](/astrology/transits/) this one sets an ideal against an attachment: what you longed for at that age, and what you weren't ready to give up.

I don't read it as the story of a particular adolescence. Looking back, some people recognise in those years the first time a longing and a loss arrived together: a cause, a faith or a friendship taken up with everything, a childhood certainty let go. The transit didn't produce them, and many teenage years hold nothing a chart could name. What lasts is the axis it drew, between the house of your natal Pluto and the house Neptune was crossing about three houses on, and placing those needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The natal [Neptune square Pluto](/astrology/neptune-square-pluto/) is absent from every chart of these years, so the friction it would describe comes only this way, once, before twenty. If you are reading for a teenager, it is under way now for those born with Pluto in Capricorn, from 2008, but I don't read children's charts, teenagers' included ([Children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). A young person who is struggling needs a doctor, a school counsellor or a therapist ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune spends about two and a half years within my orbs of this square, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, under two in the quickest passes; it touches the exact degree three times as a rule, five times in roughly a quarter of passes ([Neptune retrograde](/astrology/neptune-retrograde/)). Everyone meets it once, and no one twice.

The age follows how far Neptune stood ahead of Pluto when you were born: about 44° in the early 1930s, more than 60° through the 1960s, 51° to 55° in the 2000s. The site's count puts the start of the orb at 18 for someone born at the start of 1935, 14.9 for 1945, 12.9 for 1955, 12 for 1965, 12.2 for 1975, 14.1 for 1985, 15.3 for 1995 and 16.2 for 2005, between 10.5 and 20.3 in all. Born at the start of 1965, you met it exact at 13.0, 13.5 and 13.8.

It sits between [transiting Neptune sextile natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-pluto/), in infancy, and the trine of the late twenties and early thirties, [transiting Neptune trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-pluto/); [transiting Neptune opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-pluto/) follows in the fifties, and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-pluto/) never comes. Nor is it the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/), Neptune 90° from its own birth place at about 41.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look back with some tenderness. If you can place those years, ask what you idealised then and what you were letting go, and give the younger you credit for carrying both.

Read the axis rather than the memories. The houses of your natal Pluto and of Neptune at that age show what the friction was about in your chart, and they stay relevant long after the transit.

If you are the parent of a teenager now, keep the chart closed and the conversation open.
