Out of sight, in the house of rest and the inner life, Taurus on the 12th house cusp builds what it builds everywhere: something that lasts. Here that means a private life kept up for years, a retreat you return to, and care given quietly over a long time.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Time apart takes its manner from the sign on the [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/) cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) turns it into a practice. The journal kept since you were twenty, the instrument played when nobody's listening, the meditation, prayer or walk that happens every day whether or not you feel like it: the inner life grows by repetition here, and it steadies everything else. Retreat tends to mean return, the same bench, the same small house, the same hour of the morning. Care given out of sight lasts too: the visit made every week for years, the help no one hears about. What the 12th asks of Taurus is release. Holding on comes naturally to this sign, to memories, to habits, to the way things were, and letting go, gently and without hurry, is the work this house sets.

Pisces, the 12th's natural sign, is where tradition exalted [Venus](/astrology/venus/), and Venus rules your 12th: the planet of what you value has an old affinity with this house, and compassion, devotion and private beauty come easily to it here. Libra, Venus's other sign, always lies in your 5th with this cusp, on its cusp or wholly inside, so your retreats and your pleasures answer to one planet, and the pleasures that restore you most are often the private ones. When Cancer rises, in about 6 London charts in 10, Gemini has no cusp and sits wholly inside your 12th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), so Mercury shares your inner life with Venus: the notebook, the letter never sent, the long walk spent thinking. Venus's house says what your time apart restores you for; its twelve possibilities run from the [1st](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) to the [12th](/astrology/12th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Taurus opens the 12th as often as it opens the 2nd: about 1 London birth in 17 and 1 New York birth in 15, Placidus being the system I use. It rises in a little over an hour at London, so it's seldom on the cusps beside the Ascendant. The Taurus degrees on your 12th cusp were rising an hour or two before you were born, the moment of [Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/), and an hour or two after it they reached the 11th, the moment of [Taurus on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-11th-house-cusp/); in these charts Taurus never holds two cusps at once. Opposite, Scorpio opens the 6th, so your days can be intense and exacting while the private hours stay steady.

Tradition filed confinement and hidden enemies under this house. Neither is something I read in a chart, and an inner life that keeps to itself is not a symptom ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)). The 12th cusp turns with the Ascendant, so it's set by the minute of birth, and I don't read the chart until I have it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
