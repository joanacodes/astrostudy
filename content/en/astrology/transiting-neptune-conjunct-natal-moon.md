The Moon is the most personal point Neptune can reach, and the hardest to date. For about two and a half years, the edges of what you feel soften: you take in more, need differently, and find that your usual comforts don't quite answer.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) loosens edges that no longer serve and makes room for compassion and imagination. Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feeling and need: what soothes you, what you attach to, what makes you feel safe. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) blends the two, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) works on the inside: you may pick up the mood of a room before anyone speaks, feel more for strangers, dream more vividly, and find what you need harder to put into words.

Neptune's core need is contact without losing the line between yourself and others, and that's the work here: knowing whose feeling you're carrying. The natal [Moon conjunct Neptune](/astrology/moon-conjunct-neptune/) describes this porousness as a lifelong trait; as a transit, it comes for a few years and then thins. If the tenderness turns into a low mood that settles in, or a worry that won't let go, see a doctor or a therapist: a transit describes a climate, never an illness ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

Your Moon's degree is the part of this transit that most needs the birth time. In a single day the Moon covers about 13°, so without the time its degree could be anywhere within that span, and Neptune's arrival anywhere within about six years. The house matters too: with your Moon in the 4th, it's home and family where the softening is felt first ([Neptune transiting the 4th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-4th-house/)). I give no reading without your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the conjunction to your Moon lasts about two years and seven months, or a little under two at its shortest. The exact degree is crossed three times in most passes and five times in about one in four.

Neptune needs to start less than half the zodiac behind your Moon to reach it by 80, and half of people born between 1930 and 2009 were born that way; nobody meets it twice. Neptune's other contacts with your Moon differ in tone: the sextile ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-moon/)) and the trine ([transiting Neptune trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-moon/)) are gentler visits to your feelings, the square ([transiting Neptune square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-moon/)) tests the comforts you rely on, and the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-moon/)) brings Neptune's pull through the people you care for.

Neptune's stay in Aries, from 2026 to 2039, brings it to Moons in Aries, the early degrees first ([Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep the small routines that steady you: meals, a walk, an hour of quiet. Under Neptune they do more than they seem to.

Ask, when a feeling arrives, whether it's yours. Naming where it came from is half of looking after it.

Give longing a form: music, water, a sketchbook, a prayer if you pray. Neptune on the Moon asks for somewhere to put what you feel.
