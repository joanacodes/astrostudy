Some people find out what they think by saying it out loud, to someone who answers back. That's air's way, and Gemini, Libra and Aquarius are its three signs, each keeping the conversation going differently.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Air comes third in each run of four signs round the zodiac, so [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), [Libra](/astrology/libra/) and [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) sit a [trine](/astrology/trine/) apart. Air's way is thought and exchange: it lives through ideas, conversation and comparison, links people and notions together, and likes to see a thing from more than one side. Each air sign follows one of the [earth signs](/astrology/earth-signs/), Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn: once something has been built, air starts asking what it means and who it's for. It's also the only element with no animal among its symbols: twins, a pair of scales and a water-bearer.

The three handle air differently ([the three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/)):

- **Gemini**, mutable, circulates. The Sun is there from about 21 May to 20 June, at the end of spring: questions, messages, the next idea.
- **Libra**, cardinal, opens. About 23 September to 22 October, from the September equinox: the first move towards another person, and the search for what's fair.
- **Aquarius**, fixed, holds. About 20 January to 18 February, in midwinter: ideas kept for the long term, and loyalty to the group.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Air's natural houses are the [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house/) and [11th](/astrology/11th-house/), and all three are about other people: the close circle you grew up alongside, the person facing you, the friends and groups you choose. Wherever air falls in your own chart, it tends to bring someone else into that part of life.

Its rulers are mixed. Mercury rules Gemini and Venus rules Libra, as they always have; Aquarius is the one air sign with two rulers, Uranus in modern tropical astrology and Saturn in the tradition, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)).

Saturn has a surprising amount to do with air. Besides ruling Aquarius in the tradition, it's exalted in Libra, the only exaltation in an air sign, and in Dorotheus's scheme it rules the whole triplicity by day, with Mercury by night and Jupiter as partner ([triplicity rulers](/astrology/triplicity-rulers/)). The Sun has its fall in Libra. For an element people call light, air leans a good deal on the planet of structure.

Each air sign then hands over to a water sign, Gemini to Cancer, Libra to Scorpio, Aquarius to Pisces: thinking about a feeling becomes feeling it ([water signs](/astrology/water-signs/)).

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

Planets in air signs show where you need words: where you want to understand before you commit, and where talking a thing through is how you come to feel it. Several planets in air often go with a quick, social, curious temperament. None doesn't mean no ideas; it can mean thought comes after experience or feeling rather than before ([element balance and missing elements](/astrology/element-balance-and-missing-elements/)).

Because air's houses face other people, air often comes up when a reading turns to relationships, and the most direct way to look at two people is [synastry](/synastry/), comparing their charts. I only do that when both people have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)). Air would approve: a conversation works best when everyone in it knows it's happening.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Air signs are cold.”** Air often reaches its feelings through words, which can look cool from outside. That isn't an absence of warmth.
- **“Aquarius is a water sign.”** It's an air sign, the fixed one. The water-bearer is only the picture.
- **“Air signs can't commit.”** Libra is the sign of partnership and Aquarius the fixed air sign, loyal to its people and its ideas. Gemini changes its mind often and keeps its friends.
- **“Air signs are all talk.”** Talk is how air starts, not all it does: Libra acts by turning towards someone, and Aquarius builds groups that last.
