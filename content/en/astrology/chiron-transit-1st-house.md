Chiron moves unevenly, so this stay can be brief or long, but its subject stays the same: the old hurt of being yourself in plain sight, and what you've learned from carrying it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the wounded healer: the hurt you keep circling and, often, the place where you understand others best. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is the self you lead with: your manner, your presence, the way you begin things and how people first meet you. With Chiron passing through, an old discomfort about being seen can come back to mind: a sense of not being quite right as you are, a habit of apologising for taking up space, a way of hiding in plain sight. Coming back is not the same as reopening. This stay can let you look at that hurt as an adult, with more patience than it was first met with, and start to wear your own face more easily.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 1st begins at the Ascendant, so the stay starts with Chiron on your rising degree ([transiting Chiron conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)); a natal Mars in the 1st, met later, is asked where its force has been covering a sore spot.

Like any [transit](/astrology/transits/), this one stirs what is already in your chart, and it forecasts nothing about your body. In my notebook, natal Chiron was one of the three placements that held far more often, 75 to 90% of the time ([The three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)); that was the birth chart, not transits, but it is why I give Chiron real time in a reading.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Chiron's stays vary more than most, because its orbit is stretched: some signs pass in under two years, others take about eight. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, Chiron spends about 4 years in a house across all twelve, most often between about 2 and 9. The 1st is shorter in the middle, about 2 years, but its long end runs to about 13, because the 1st at that latitude can be wide and Chiron lingers in some signs. At New York's latitude the middle is about 3 years, with most stays between about 2 and 12.

Your birth time sets your Ascendant and the size of your 1st; without it I can't say when this begins or how long it lasts, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows Chiron's time in your 12th ([Chiron transiting the 12th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-12th-house/)) and leads into its time in your 2nd ([Chiron transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-2nd-house/)).

Chiron has been in Aries since April 2018 and leaves it for good in April 2027, after a first step into Taurus in June 2026. With Aries rising, the crossing of your Ascendant is under way or already behind you, depending on your degree. Taurus rising has it between June 2026 and May 2034; Gemini rising, between July 2033 and April 2039; Cancer rising, from July 2038, and Chiron is still in Cancer when my count ends in December 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice the moments you shrink, and try taking up a little more room in one of them. Chiron in the 1st responds to small, repeated acts of self-acceptance.

Speak to yourself the way you'd speak to a friend with the same sore spot. The gentleness you already offer others is the skill this transit asks you to turn inward.

If the old hurt feels heavy, find someone you trust to talk it through with. Chiron's lesson tends to arrive through being met, not through managing alone.
