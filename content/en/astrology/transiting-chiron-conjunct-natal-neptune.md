Between September 1993 and February 2011, Chiron crossed every sign from Virgo to Aquarius, and those are the signs where Neptune stood for everyone born from 1930 to 2009. So most adults reading this have already met Chiron on their Neptune: for most of them the first time, for many born in the 1930s and early 1940s the second.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and dissolves edges: it longs for something larger, feels with other people and finds meaning in images, music, faith. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is a vulnerability that stays and the adapting that lets you live with it. Neptune permeates boundaries, Chiron adapts to vulnerability, and a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes the two work as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your tenderest place and your highest hopes share a degree.

What surfaces is often an old longing attached to the hurt: to be understood without having to explain, to lose yourself in music, faith or another person until the sore spot stops mattering. The conjunction tends to show both the beauty and the cost of that longing. Neptune's blind spots are mistaking resonance for truth and imagination for evidence; Chiron's, assuming every difficulty has to become wisdom. Held together, they can produce something gentler than either: compassion that still sees clearly, art made from a sore spot, a faith that asks nothing of the wound.

Neptune is no omen of deception, confusion or addiction, and this transit predicts none of them. If the longing turns into a fog you can't find your way out of, please see a doctor or a therapist ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

Neptune's sign is shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so its degree is close for people born a few years apart, and this conjunction reaches them together. That makes it a generation's transit; in one chart, what matters is the house and the personal planets Neptune touches. With [Neptune in the 8th house](/astrology/neptune-in-8th-house/), the conjunction comes during Chiron's stay there ([Chiron transiting the 8th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-8th-house/)): closeness, trust and what you share with someone, and an old hurt from having given too much of yourself. Without your birth time I can't tell which house holds your Neptune, and I don't read the conjunction without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you were born with [Neptune conjunct Chiron](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-chiron/), the two already share a degree in your chart, and the transit returns to it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Longings keep no timetable, but this conjunction does: about ten months in the usual case, counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, with three crossings of your Neptune in nearly two passes out of three. About one pass in three is a single crossing of some three weeks, and about one in twenty a long stretch of five crossings and some twenty-one months.

In the site's count the first came at about 16 in the middle, anywhere from infancy to about 50: before 1 for someone born at the start of 1945, around 2 for 2005, 9 for 1935 and 1995, 17 for 1985, 25 for 1975, 33 for 1965 and 42 for 1955. By 80 most people meet it twice and everyone once; for most adults now, the next one is about fifty years after the first, around the middle of the century. From this conjunction the round runs on to [transiting Chiron sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-neptune/), [transiting Chiron square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-neptune/), [transiting Chiron trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-neptune/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down the ideal you were holding, then the version of it you could live with.

Make something from the longing: a song, a page, a garden, anything that holds it.

Check one assumption about someone close with a plain question rather than intuition.

When a feeling arrives that seems to belong to someone else, ask whose it is before you act on it.
