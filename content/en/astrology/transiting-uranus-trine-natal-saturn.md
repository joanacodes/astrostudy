Of all the meetings between the planet of structure and the planet of change, this is the one where they help each other. Twice in most lives Uranus reaches 120° from your natal Saturn, and for about twenty months a reform can be built to last.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) organises durable frameworks: commitments, rules, the slow mastery that comes from staying with something. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) innovates and reforms. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates two functions through flow while each keeps its identity, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) lets Saturn stay careful while Uranus gets inventive.

Changes under this trine tend to arrive with their foundations already poured. A new way of working you've been testing becomes the standard; a team, a family or an institution you belong to updates its rules with your help; your own discipline finds a form that suits who you are now. From outside it can look unremarkable, a quiet victory for good sense rather than a revolution.

The risk is that nothing forces the issue. Saturn is content when things work, and a trine makes things work, so the chance to modernise what you're responsible for can slide by. I'd use it on purpose: it's the easiest season you'll get to rewrite a rule while everyone, you included, can still see why it mattered.

With Saturn in your 9th and Uranus crossing your 1st ([Uranus transiting the 1st house](/astrology/uranus-transit-1st-house/)), convictions you built slowly find a fresher, more personal way to show. The 1st and the 9th, like every house, come from the minute of your birth; without that minute, I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine gives a reform about twenty months to be planned and built, eleven at the shortest, with three exact hits in more than nine passes out of ten; the middle one comes while Uranus is moving backwards ([Uranus retrograde](/astrology/uranus-retrograde/)).

Uranus is in Gemini until May 2033, trining every natal Saturn in Libra or Aquarius on its way. With Saturn in Libra, as for most births from September 1980 to August 1983 and from 1950 to 1953, or in Aquarius, as for births from January 1962 to December 1964 and from February 1991 to January 1994, one of your trines falls between 2025 and 2033.

In the site's count, births just after one of the runs of [Saturn trine Uranus](/astrology/saturn-trine-uranus/), 1955–58, 1971–74 and 2002–04, met the transit within their first years: at about 3 for someone born at the start of 1975, about 4 for the start of 2005. Someone born at the start of 1955 waited until about 52. That birth reaches 80 with a single trine, the second waiting until about 82; most births meet two, and none fewer than one.

From the conjunction, [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-saturn/), the first trine lies about twenty-eight years on, after [transiting Uranus sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-saturn/) and [transiting Uranus square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-saturn/); the second trine follows [transiting Uranus opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-saturn/) by about fourteen years.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose the structure that most needs an update and give it the season. A trine works best with a project attached.

Bring experience into the change. Saturn's trines favour reform with a mentor's patience behind it, or reform that makes you the mentor.

Write the new rule down. What you formalise under this transit is easier to keep once the easy months are over.
