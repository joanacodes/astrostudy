Here home answers to itself. The planet ruling the sign on your IC stands inside the house it rules, and roots, family and private life don't have to send their story anywhere else.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) begins at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart, and covers home, family, belonging and the private self. Most charts send its ruler off elsewhere, so that home leans on some other part of life ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). In yours it stays in, and the house is both the subject and the setting.

I read it as roots that go a long way down. Home has two layers for you: the one you came from, which you may know in great detail, and the one you make, which you take seriously. Your private life often carries more weight than your public one, and the people who know you at home see a fuller person than colleagues ever do. Rituals, a family table, a house kept for years, a place you go back to: that's where you recharge. The risk is the same depth turned inward, a life so centred on home that the outside world has to knock twice.

With Cancer on the IC, the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), natural ruler of this house, sits in it twice over, by rulership and by nature: needs and home are hard to separate, and a move can take longer to settle into than you expect.

An angular house gives its planets weight, and the 4th is the one angle that faces away from the world ([angularity](/astrology/angularity/)). A ruler here is strong in private: it shapes your choices from underneath, and it can surprise people who only know your public side. Across the chart sits the 10th, your public direction, and with home's ruler staying in, a career tends to make sense to you only when it leaves room for the life at home.

Saturn, ruling a Capricorn or Aquarius IC from the 4th, often describes a home put together carefully, with rules that make sense to you and walls that last; Venus, ruling a Taurus or Libra IC, a home where comfort and calm are worth protecting.

Scorpio on the IC hands the 4th to Mars and Pluto, Aquarius to Saturn and Uranus, Pisces to Jupiter and Neptune, and I read both of the pair ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Often only one of them is at home: then one thread of the family story stays in the house while the other is lived out somewhere else in the chart.

A 4th-house ruler at home says nothing about what anyone in your family is like. It says how large home looms in your own life, and the planet's sign and aspects say what that home asks of you.

Near the IC, a slightly later birth would carry the ruler across into the 3rd, where home is kept up by talk and neighbours ([Ruler of the 4th in the 3rd house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)); near the far edge, a slightly earlier one would leave it in the 5th, where home is for play ([Ruler of the 4th in the 5th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)). Both edges move with the birth time, so I check the record before reading anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
