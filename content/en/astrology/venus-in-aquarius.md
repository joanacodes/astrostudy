Venus in Aquarius is often a friend first, and sometimes a friend for so long that love has to introduce itself. The planet of what you value sits here in fixed air, among ideas, causes and equals. I read it as affection that wants you free, and a loyalty that keeps its course for decades, even when it looks detached.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) evaluates what you value and how you relate. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, gives it an independent, principled and friendly manner. You tend to value equality, honesty and the freedom to be yourself: the friend who lets you be odd, the cause you've stood by for years, the group where nobody has to pretend. Fixed signs keep their course, and here that means loyalty to people and ideals over a long stretch of life.

Venus has no dignity in Aquarius. In modern tropical astrology the sign is ruled by [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/); in the tradition, by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/). I read both. Uranus, by sign and house, shows where this Venus needs room to differ. Saturn shows the structure and the principles that keep its loyalty steady. Freedom and fidelity, in this sign, are meant to work as a pair.

Venus usually crosses Aquarius in about 25 days, and when it turns retrograde there it can stay up to 122, which is part of why about 1 person in 11 born from 1930 to 2009 has it. Your Sun is in Aquarius or within two signs, from Sagittarius to Aries.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, this Venus starts with friendship: shared ideas, a common cause, long talks about how the world could be. You tend to be drawn to people who are original, independent and kind without being clingy, and you show affection by respecting their freedom as much as your own. Each neighbour gives in its own currency. [Venus in Capricorn](/astrology/venus-in-capricorn/) gives time and reliability, [Venus in Pisces](/astrology/venus-in-pisces/) gives compassion without limits, and Venus in Aquarius gives room: the rare kind of love that wants you more yourself, not less. Reciprocity means equality, with neither person owning the other. What you may find hard is the moment a friendship turns intimate and asks for vulnerability rather than ideas.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, an Aquarius Venus values fairness, teamwork and room to try new things. You're often good in groups, networks and communities: organising, campaigning, building tools, working where technology and people meet. You may count colleagues as friends, and you tend to treat everyone alike, from the newest arrival to the director. Hierarchy for its own sake, and rules nobody can explain, wear this Venus down.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Distance can stand in for safety. You may explain a feeling instead of having it, or keep a partner at the same friendly distance you keep everyone. Principles can outrank the person in front of you, so that being right about fairness matters more than being kind in the moment. And because neediness makes you uneasy, your own needs may go unspoken until they arrive all at once.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let one person be special, not just one among many. Say a feeling without a theory attached: *I missed you* needs no explanation. Ask for closeness as directly as you ask for space. And notice that your loyalty is already a kind of intimacy; the friend you've stood by for twenty years knows you better than you think.

Venus belongs with the placements that held about a third of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). An Aquarius Venus tends to like that kind of evidence, a rough count rather than a promise, and I'd ask it to keep the same independence with this page: agree where your life agrees, and nowhere else.
