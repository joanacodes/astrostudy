Some pairs are the reason a group of friends exists at all: they host the dinners, start the group chat and introduce everyone to everyone. With composite Mercury in the 11th house, a relationship does its thinking in company.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) gives a relationship a [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) of its own, the midpoint of your two natal Mercurys, and the house it lands in shows where that mind spends its time. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is friendship, groups, networks, causes and shared hopes. A succedent house whose natural sign is Aquarius, it went by the name of Good Spirit in the old texts, and Jupiter has its joy there.

With the bond's mind here, conversation is often plural. The pair thinks best with friends round the table, belongs to a club or an association, keeps a group chat going and passes news through its circle. It enjoys connecting people, and plans for the future (next summer, the community garden, the project a few friends are starting) come up easily. Its humour is often a whole group's in-joke rather than a private one. Couples with this placement often feel like best friends as well, and friends with it can form the centre of a much larger group. The natal [Mercury in the 11th house](/astrology/mercury-in-11th-house/) is one person thinking aloud among friends; [their Mercury in your 11th house](/astrology/mercury-in-11th-house-synastry/), one person whose talk ties another into a network. In the composite, the circle gathers around the pair, and the pair keeps it in good repair: birthdays remembered, the friend who moved abroad still called, introductions followed up.

Composite Mercury seldom wanders more than about 28° from the composite Sun, so the Sun is usually in the 11th or a neighbouring house. About one pair in 26 sees the composite Sun pushed to the far side, around the 5th: the group facing the private pleasures of two, a symmetry made by arithmetic rather than meaning.

Even the place matters here. By the midpoint method, the 11th's cusp blends cusps calculated for your two birthplaces; rebuilt from the composite Midheaven, it is calculated for the latitude where you live now. Both need both birth times, and a Mercury near the cusp can change house between them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The risk is the private conversation dissolving into the group one. A bond that thinks in company can end up forming its views by consensus, telling the group before telling each other, or filling every free evening with other people. Sometimes a friend becomes the third voice in every decision. Community isn't conformity. Keep some subjects for the two of you, and one evening a week with nobody else invited.

[Composite Mercury in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-10th-house/) speaks in public; [composite Mercury in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-12th-house/) thinks in private. This technique isn't one I work with, so read this as a description: astrologers who use it read the composite with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and only once both people have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
