Pluto opposite your Mercury deepens your thinking through other people's words. For two to three years the planet of transformation faces the part of you that reasons and speaks, and a debate, a negotiation or someone who sees things very differently presses on what you think.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) brings motives and power patterns into the open. Your [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) exchanges, reasons and interprets. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates them through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the pressure on your thinking tends to arrive from across the table: someone's strong conviction, a negotiation with real stakes, a conversation in which you find out how much power words carry. The opposition shows you your own reasoning by contrast, the way a second voice shows you the shape of the first.

The most intense voice in a room is not always the one telling the truth, and that includes your own on the days you feel certain. Intensity measures how much something matters to someone, not whether it is right. Polarity isn't contradiction, either: under an opposition two views can each hold part of what is true, and the way through is often to put them together rather than to pick one.

Words can also be used to dominate, and some people do. That is never fate, and no transit asks you to endure it; I don't read Pluto as permission for anyone's control ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). [Mercury opposite Pluto](/astrology/mercury-opposite-pluto/) in a birth chart is read as a lifetime's lesson in the power of words; the transit gives the same lesson to anyone it reaches, in a few years.

With Mercury in the 4th and Pluto crossing the 10th ([Pluto transiting the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-10th-house/)), debates at work or in public press on what you think and say at home. Whether your Mercury sits low in the 4th and Pluto high in the 10th is something only the birth time can tell ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Using my orbs, 1½° on the approach and 1° on the way out, the opposition lasts about two years and eight months, and about twenty-two months in the shortest passes. In about three passes out of five Pluto crosses the exact point three times; in the rest, five times, over about three years and seven months.

This is the rarest of Pluto's five contacts with Mercury: nearly three people in five born between 1930 and 2009 never meet it by 80, a few more than miss the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-mercury/)). The square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-mercury/)), the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-mercury/)) and the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-mercury/)) each reach three people in four or more.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it opposes Mercurys in Leo. Mercury stays close to the Sun, so these belong to birthdays from about late June to late September.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Listen to the end before you answer. Under an opposition, the reply you prepare while the other person is still talking is usually your weakest.

Separate the argument from the person. You can reject an idea without rejecting whoever holds it, and keep a friend whose view you don't share.

Write agreements down when the stakes are real. Memory bends under intensity, everyone's included.
