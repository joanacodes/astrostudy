The Descendant is the other end of the line that starts at your Ascendant. The Ascendant is the point of the zodiac that was rising in the east when you were born; the Descendant is the point that was setting in the west, and it's where astrologers look for the people you meet face to face.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

At the minute and place of your birth, the horizon cut the zodiac in two points. The eastern one is your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/); the western one, exactly opposite, is the Descendant. Together they make the [horizon axis](/astrology/horizon-axis/). On most chart wheels the Descendant is the line on the right, at three o'clock, and in Placidus and the other quadrant systems it is the cusp, the starting line, of the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/): partners in love and in work, close friends, rivals, and what the old books called open enemies. Everyone you deal with one to one.

I read the Descendant as what you go looking for in other people, often the qualities you don't lead with yourself. Your Ascendant says *here I am*; your Descendant answers *and this is what I need from you*. It's often called your other half: not a half you lack, but one you tend to meet in company.

- **Its sign** is always the one opposite your rising sign, and shows the qualities you're drawn to in others.
- **Its ruler**, the ruler of the 7th house, shows where relationships tend to find you, and what they get tied up with.
- **Planets close to it** are [angular planets](/astrology/angular-planets/): strong, and often met in other people before you recognise them as yours.

It works with [Venus](/astrology/venus/), which rules Libra, the 7th sign, and describes what you love and how; with the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), what you need to feel safe with someone; and with the other two angles, the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) and the [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/).

Because the Descendant is always exactly opposite the Ascendant, every aspect to one is an aspect to the other. A planet in [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) with the Descendant is in [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) to the Ascendant: what it describes tends to reach you through partners first. A [square](/astrology/square/) to one is a square to both; a [trine](/astrology/trine/) to the Descendant is a sextile to the Ascendant. And if you were born around sunset, the Sun itself is close to your Descendant: you often come into your own in company.

## The Descendant through the signs {#through-the-signs}

The Descendant is always the sign opposite your rising sign, so it changes sign when the Ascendant does, about every two hours on average, and only your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) can settle it. What you look for in the people closest to you:

- **[Descendant in Aries](/astrology/aries-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Libra rising, you're drawn to people who decide fast and say what they want.
- **[Descendant in Taurus](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Scorpio rising, you look for steadiness, loyalty and simple comfort.
- **[Descendant in Gemini](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Sagittarius rising, you need conversation, and a partner who keeps you curious.
- **[Descendant in Cancer](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Capricorn rising, you look for warmth and care, someone to make a home with.
- **[Descendant in Leo](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Aquarius rising, you're drawn to warm, expressive people who aren't afraid to be seen.
- **[Descendant in Virgo](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Pisces rising, you look for reliability and care in the small things.
- **[Descendant in Libra](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Aries rising, you want a partnership of equals: fair, graceful, a team.
- **[Descendant in Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Taurus rising, you're drawn to depth; closeness is all or nothing.
- **[Descendant in Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Gemini rising, you look for people with a big view: travellers, teachers, believers.
- **[Descendant in Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Cancer rising, you look for solid, responsible people, and commitment taken seriously.
- **[Descendant in Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Leo rising, you need friendship and freedom inside love.
- **[Descendant in Pisces](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Virgo rising, you're drawn to gentleness and imagination, and to a love that keeps a touch of the ideal.

## Its ruler in the houses {#in-the-houses}

The planet that rules the sign on your Descendant is the ruler of your 7th house ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)): Venus if it's Taurus or Libra, Mars if it's Aries, Saturn if it's Capricorn, and so on; Scorpio, Aquarius and Pisces have a modern and a traditional ruler, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Where it sits shows where relationships tend to find you, and what they get tied up with:

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** partners seek you out, and relationships shape who you become.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** love and security go together; shared values matter as much as feelings.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** partners met close to home, through studies, neighbours or siblings; talk is the glue.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** you want a partner to build a home with; partners become family.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** love has to stay playful; partners met through pleasure or creative work.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** partners met through work or daily routine; love shown in practical help.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** relationships are a story of their own; commitment comes naturally.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** bonds that change you, built on deep trust and on what you share.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** partners from elsewhere: another country, culture or belief; love as a journey.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** partners met through work, or a relationship lived in public.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** partners who arrive through friendship, often within a group.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** relationships you keep private, or that grow quietly, away from view.

## The Descendant in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), the Descendant shows where another person lands in the part of your chart that's about partners. [Their Venus in your 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house-synastry/) often feels like affection that fits; [their Sun in your 7th house](/astrology/sun-in-7th-house-synastry/), like someone you can build something with, or someone who takes up all the light. When your Descendant falls on their Ascendant, you meet at the horizon: what one of you leads with, the other is looking for. It describes a pull, not a promise, and I read it in both directions. The angles need both birth times, so the other person has to know about the reading: [here's why I insist](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## Transits to the Descendant {#transits}

The angles have no return: what moves is the sky. A slow planet crossing your Descendant is, by definition, opposite your Ascendant, so when [Saturn is opposite your natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-ascendant/), it is on your Descendant. That happens about every 29½ years, before Saturn moves on through your 7th house ([Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/)), and people often describe it as relationships asking to be taken seriously: commitments made, or looked at again. [Jupiter opposite the Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-ascendant/), about every 12 years, tends to feel like an opening towards others, followed by [Jupiter transiting the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-7th-house/). Uranus, Neptune and Pluto reach the Descendant once in a lifetime at most.

A transit is weather, not a forecast: it describes the climate of a period in your relationships, never a wedding or a break-up written in advance. And it is only as precise as your birth time.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

My notebook doesn't report the Descendant on its own, so I won't give it a figure. The angle it reports is the rising sign, which held about a third of the time: 30 to 40%, like most of [what I checked](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). The three that stood out were [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), at 75 to 90%. So I read your Descendant, and I tell you which parts of a reading rest on it, so you can weigh them for yourself. It's a notebook, not a study, and it shapes [how I read](/how-i-read/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Your Descendant sign is your soulmate's sign.”** It describes qualities you're drawn to, not a partner's Sun sign. The person you love doesn't have to match it.
- **“An empty 7th house means you'll be alone.”** An [empty house](/astrology/empty-houses/) isn't an empty life: the sign on your Descendant and its ruler still describe your relationships.
- **“The Descendant is only about romance.”** It covers anyone you meet one to one: partners in love or in work, close friends, rivals.
- **“The Descendant is everything you're not.”** It's the other end of the same line, read with the Ascendant, never against it.
