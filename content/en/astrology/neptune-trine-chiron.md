Neptune trine Chiron is the easiest of the angles between these two, and the most common: about one person in nine born between 1930 and 2009 carries it, most of them in the 1960s. It lets compassion and the place that stays tender cooperate without much fuss.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) widens the heart and the imagination: it identifies with others, reaches for a larger meaning, and softens what doesn't need to be hard. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the place where something stays uneven, and the know-how that grows there: the adjusted method, the patience, the way of helping others without pretending your way is the only one. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets the two flow into each other, each strengthening the other without losing itself.

In plain words, kindness and the tender place get on. You may find it natural to treat your own limits gently, and other people's the same way; imagination helps you adapt rather than deny, and what you learned around your sore spot turns easily into understanding. The risk is only that ease can look like nothing at all. A trine works best when it's used, and this one can go unused simply because it never complains.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Among births from 1930 to 2009, about 1 in 9 has Neptune within 6° of a trine to Chiron, the most common of the pair's five aspects in my count. Most were born between 1960 and 1972: in part of each year from May 1960, without a break from February 1963 to May 1967, then in part of each year again until February 1972. A last, short group was born from September 1994 to September 1995.

The difference in length is Chiron's doing. In the 1960s it was moving slowly, in the stretch of its orbit where it lingers longest, and the trine held for over four years at a time; in the mid-1990s it was moving fast, and the same angle came and went within a year.

So many people born in the 1960s share the trine that I read it through the houses. A trine usually links houses four apart, in signs of the same element: Chiron in the 1st with Neptune in the 5th, for example, might soften a sensitivity about how you come across through play, art or romance. Without a birth time, the houses stay unknown ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects, and when they came:

- **[Neptune conjunct Chiron](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-chiron/):** the two in one place, for people born in 1944–45 or 2008–09.
- **[Neptune sextile Chiron](/astrology/neptune-sextile-chiron/):** a smaller opening of the same kind, taken up by choice.
- **[Neptune square Chiron](/astrology/neptune-square-chiron/):** ideal and limit grinding instead of flowing, born 1933–38, 1953–56 or 1996–97.
- **[Neptune opposite Chiron](/astrology/neptune-opposite-chiron/):** the two facing each other, born between 1988 and 1991.

Between two charts, [Neptune trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-chiron-synastry/) is one of the kinder contacts: one person's compassion finding the other's tender place without pressing on it. Even so, each person feels it differently, and I look at it from each chart.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-neptune/) brings this flow for a while, when Chiron reaches 120° from your Neptune, which it does twice in each round of about 50 years. Born with the trine in the 1960s, you find it again at the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/) around 50, and because Chiron moves at the same pace in the same part of its orbit, it is slow again then, so the return lingers.

Weather, not a forecast: a season when being gentle with an old limit comes more easily. It doesn't say what you'll do with the ease.
