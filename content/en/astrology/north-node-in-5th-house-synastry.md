Play is a skill, and for the other person here it's one still being learned. Their North Node falls in your 5th house, among your pleasures, your creative projects and your way of falling for things, and their South Node falls opposite, in your 11th, among your friends and causes.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is the part of your life that needs no reason: what you make for the joy of it, games, romance, risks taken for fun. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a capacity someone builds on purpose and does badly at first. This [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) asks them to practise joy, and your pleasures are where they do it.

For you, they may turn up to your choir, ask to see your sketchbook, or try your sport and laugh at how bad they are. Their delight in what you enjoy can make you take your own play more seriously, or rather, take it less seriously and more often.

For them, being seen as themselves and making something that is theirs is the unfamiliar end, and beside you it feels permitted. The 5th is succedent; its natural sign, [Leo](/astrology/leo/), is the Sun's, and tradition called the house Good Fortune and gave Venus its joy here. Across the chart, the 11th was the Good Spirit, Jupiter's joy, so both ends of their line fall in houses tradition named as favourable.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Your friends and groups, where their [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) falls in [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), are ground they cross without thinking. They may fit into your circle within a week, and on the evening you hoped would be just the two of you, they invite everyone. Belonging to the group is habit; being singled out, by you or by their own creativity, is the stretch.

From their side, your 5th can feel like a stage they're not sure they're allowed on. They may hang back, turn it into a joke, or hide in organising, the comfortable role the 11th gives them.

Tradition gave the 5th children. Neither end of this overlay says anything about whether either of you will have any ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/): what it describes is play and pleasure between two adults.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make room for fun that isn't a group activity. In a couple, plan dates that are only yours and let them choose some; between friends, make something together, badly, and enjoy it; between colleagues, the creative side of a project may be where they surprise you; between adult relatives, a shared hobby can mean more than another family gathering.

Put the node in your chart and the house in theirs, and you'd be the one learning to play among their pleasures: your North Node in their 5th house. A house earlier, [their North Node in your 4th house](/astrology/north-node-in-4th-house-synastry/) learns belonging at your table; a house later, [their North Node in your 6th house](/astrology/north-node-in-6th-house-synastry/) learns routine from your working week.

Their node is shared by everyone born in the same year and a half or so, which is one reason I never treat it as a verdict on their capacity for joy; their birth date places it, and your houses make it this pair's. Those houses need your birth time: in Placidus, the house system I use, a few minutes either way move the cusps of your 5th ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If their node sits close to the cusp your 5th shares with your 4th, the mean node and the true node can split between your home and your playground; I look at both, and wait for both birth times before reading the pair. Flirting and play belong to the people doing them, so I read this only when both of you have asked for it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
