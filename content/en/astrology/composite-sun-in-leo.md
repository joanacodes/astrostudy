Leo is the Sun's own sign, so a composite Sun in Leo is a relationship's centre at home. It describes a bond organised around what the two of you make together, and around the warmth you bring to making it.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Each planet in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is the midpoint of two natal positions, and the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) there is read as the bond's identity, vitality and purpose. In [Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire, those come together as expression: the relationship knows itself through what it creates and how it shows it. About 1 pair in 12 has this composite Sun, and the two people in it can have their own Suns in any signs at all.

Such a bond tends to make things wholeheartedly: a band, a business with both your names on it, a house where friends stay late at long dinners, a way of celebrating birthdays that nobody else would think of. Its vitality is warmth, and it comes alive when the two of you are generous together. Its purpose is to bring something into being, and it shares that with the 5th house, the natural house of Leo ([composite Sun in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-5th-house/)). What it wants in return is to be known for what it actually makes. Two shy people can have it too: the warmth may show only to a few, at a kitchen table rather than on a stage, and still be the bond's centre.

Tradition calls Leo the Sun's [domicile](/astrology/domicile/). For any other composite Sun, you'd look to the planet that rules its sign to see where the bond's energy goes; here the ruler is the composite Sun itself, so the relationship's centre answers to nothing outside it. Set beside the [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) of a birth chart, the difference is whose warmth it is: here it belongs to the bond, and the bond can look, from outside, more sure of itself than either of you feels inside it.

The difficulty is the season with nothing to show. A bond that knows itself by what it creates can doubt itself in a hard year, when the project stalls and nobody is hosting anything. Generosity can also slide into performance, the relationship arranged for others to admire. Visibility isn't attention-seeking, though, and this warmth doesn't need applause to be real. When the big thing stalls, making something small together helps: a meal, a song, an afternoon's walk with a camera. Next to it, the [composite Sun in Cancer](/astrology/composite-sun-in-cancer/) keeps its light indoors, and the [composite Sun in Virgo](/astrology/composite-sun-in-virgo/) turns it on the work in hand.

In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), which checked birth charts, the Sun sign held about a third of the time. A composite is another kind of chart, I don't draw them in my readings, and there's no figure of mine behind this page: it's the technique as astrologers describe it, read beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and never a verdict on how long a bond will last or whether it will thrive. A relationship that wants to be seen clearly starts with both people seeing what's done with their charts ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
