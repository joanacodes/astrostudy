Mars conjunct Lilith fuses your drive with your untamed side, the part of you that never agreed to be domesticated. Whatever you go after, you tend to go after it on your own terms, and being told to wait for permission rarely works on you for long.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the function that moves: it initiates, competes, makes the effort and holds a line. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) isn't a body at all but the Moon's apogee, the far point of its orbit, taken in its mean position. Astrologers read it as instinctive autonomy, what was pushed aside or never tamed, and your relationship with your own power; traditions disagree about it a great deal, so I offer my reading as symbolism. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), the two act as one unit while each keeps its nature.

In plain words, this is drive with nothing borrowed in it. You act from instinct, set your own pace and resist being managed, and the effort and the refusal come from the same place. It can show as courage that doesn't wait to be authorised, a very clear no, or a want you were once told to keep quiet about. Assertion isn't aggression: the conjunction is about the right to act as yourself, not about overriding anyone.

If you were asked, growing up, to be less forceful, this may be why the request grated: it went against the grain. As an adult, the work is choosing where that force goes rather than apologising for having it. In my notebook, Lilith belonged to the small group that kept proving right, with Saturn and Chiron, at 75 to 90%; that's why it gets real time in my readings ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Mean Lilith creeps forward through a sign in about nine months, and Mars changes sign every six or seven weeks, so the meeting usually falls in one sign, which sets its style: direct and quick in Aries, slow to start and hard to stop in Taurus, strategic and persistent in Scorpio. The house shows where you refuse to be steered: the 2nd, around money and what you value; the 6th, in daily work; the 10th, in your public life. A few hours either way hardly move Mars or Lilith, so the conjunction rarely hangs on your birth time, while its house always does ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

About 1 person in 34 is born with Mars within 6° of mean Lilith. Close to exact, the drive and the refusal are almost impossible to tell apart; at 5° or 6°, the untamed side shows when something matters enough. This page counts mean Lilith, the averaged point. True Lilith can sit a long way from it, so a conjunction with one may be missing with the other ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

The other four aspects of the pair, a line each:

- **[Mars sextile Lilith](/astrology/mars-sextile-lilith/):** an opening; you act on instinct when you decide to, and choose the moment.
- **[Mars square Lilith](/astrology/mars-square-lilith/):** instinct and effort rub, and you learn to hold a boundary under pressure.
- **[Mars trine Lilith](/astrology/mars-trine-lilith/):** acting on your own terms comes easily, without a fight.
- **[Mars opposite Lilith](/astrology/mars-opposite-lilith/):** the untamed side tends to turn up in other people first, and you learn to own it.

Between two charts, [Mars conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) puts one person's drive on the other's untamed point: electric, sometimes unsettling, and workable only when both people have a say.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), the meeting comes back in two ways. Mars in the sky passes over your natal Lilith roughly every two years and stays close for a matter of days: a short push to act on something you've been holding back. Mean Lilith, for its part, returns to its birth place about every nine years, and because your Mars sits beside it, each Lilith return brings you back to this conjunction: a few weeks when acting on your own terms is in the air again.

Neither crossing tells you what you'll do with it. I read them as weather, not a forecast, and the choice of where the force goes stays yours.
