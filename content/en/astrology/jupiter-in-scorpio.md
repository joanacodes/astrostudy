There are two ways to widen a world: add to it, or go down into it. Jupiter in Scorpio goes down, under the surface of a person, a subject or a situation, until the thing finally makes sense.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the function that seeks meaning and helps experience add up. [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), fixed water, is intense, private, loyal and unwilling to stay on the surface. So here meaning comes from depth. You understand something once you've seen what drives it, your beliefs are the ones that have been through difficulty and held, and your principles centre on truth, loyalty and what people owe each other. Wisdom, with this Jupiter, often grows from what you've lived rather than what you've read.

Scorpio has two rulers ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and I read both. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), its ruler in modern tropical astrology, shows where this Jupiter seeks transformation; [Mars](/astrology/mars/), its traditional ruler, shows the drive and courage it uses to get there. Scorpio holds none of Jupiter's own dignities, so those two rulers carry much of the story.

Jupiter's recent stays in Scorpio ran from November 1981 to December 1982, November 1993 to December 1994, October 2005 to November 2006 and October 2017 to November 2018. The count for births from 1930 to 2009 is about 1 person in 11. The late 1950s and the turn of the 1970s were untidy: from January 1958 to October 1959, and from December 1969 to September 1971, the retrograde months spilled over into [Jupiter in Libra](/astrology/jupiter-in-libra/) territory at one end and [Jupiter in Sagittarius](/astrology/jupiter-in-sagittarius/) at the other, so a chart is needed to know which you have. The sign belongs to a whole birth year; what makes it yours is the house, which shows where you go deep.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Intimacy that is real is where you grow in love: secrets kept, fears admitted, a trust that has been tested. Generosity shows up when it counts most, in a crisis, at three in the morning, when someone else would have left. You may ask a lot of a bond, and give a lot back. The risk is treating trust as all or nothing, testing a partner to see whether they'll stay, or keeping parts of yourself hidden while asking for the whole of them. The bond grows when depth is allowed to be ordinary too.

## At work {#at-work}

You grow at work wherever something needs uncovering or rebuilding: research, investigation, psychology, crisis work, turnarounds, anything that handles other people's confidences or resources. You see what's going on underneath, and people sense they can trust you with it. Openings often arrive in the difficult moments others avoid. One trap: seeing meaning only in crises, as though calm work couldn't count.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Depth can become the only thing that counts. Light conversation may feel pointless and simple explanations suspect, and beliefs about hidden motives can harden into certainty. Growth can seem to need a crisis, so that calm stretches feel like stagnation. And because you know how much can lie under the surface, you may control what others know of you, which leaves the people closest to you guessing.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let some things be as simple as they look. Share one lesson you learned the hard way with someone who could use it; that turns depth into generosity. Let trust be partial while it builds, rather than total or nothing. Keep a practice that takes you below the surface on purpose, writing, study, research, so that intensity has somewhere to go besides your relationships. Both rulers matter here: Pluto for what wants transforming, Mars for the will to do it.

I don't take a placement on trust, Scorpio-style or otherwise: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) every line had to earn its tick or its cross. Jupiter wasn't among the three that held far more often, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith, and overall about a third of what I checked held. So I read a Scorpio Jupiter with the same healthy suspicion it would bring to me.
