In the year or so before Jupiter reaches your Ascendant, it crosses your 12th house, the quiet end of the chart. Its growth turns inward: rest, reflection, compassion, and the closing of chapters that are finished.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens understanding and gathers experience into meaning; the [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/) is the inner life: solitude and rest, retreat and reflection, compassion, what you let go of and what you carry without saying. Meaning, in this year, tends to come quietly: in time alone, in care given or received without fuss, in study or practice done for its own sake, and in understanding something about yourself that only shows in private.

The 12th has a family tie to Jupiter. In the natural order it belongs to Pisces, and Pisces has two rulers, Neptune, the modern one, and Jupiter, the traditional one; I read both, and with Jupiter here, the older ruler is crossing the house of its own old sign.

Whatever your 12th holds from birth is met in private. A natal Saturn there, in the house where tradition gave it its joy, can make the pass feel like an old fear loosening its grip.

Tradition was hard on this house: confinement, hidden enemies, hospitals. A transit predicts none of those, and a chart can't diagnose anything ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)): what I read here is the inner life, not a symptom. Like all [transits](/astrology/transits/), this one only brings forward what your chart already holds.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 12th is one of the four houses, with the 1st, the 6th and the 7th, that can be widest or narrowest at London's latitude, yet Jupiter's stays in it come out right at the average: about a year in the middle, most between about 11 months and nearly 2 years in Placidus charts drawn there, and up to about 22 months for a birth in New York.

The stay ends when Jupiter reaches your Ascendant, the degree that begins your 1st, and that degree comes from your birth time. My rule there is short: there's always a birth time, or no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Jupiter arrives in the 12th after a year of friends and shared hopes in your 11th ([Jupiter transiting the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-11th-house/)), and leaves it by crossing your Ascendant into your 1st ([Jupiter transiting the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-1st-house/)).

To place your window, take the sign on your 12th cusp. Leo gives way to Virgo in July 2027, Virgo to Libra in August 2028, Libra to Scorpio in September 2029, Scorpio to Sagittarius in October 2030 and Sagittarius to Capricorn in November 2031. Capricorn and Aquarius trade places through 2032, from April to November; Aquarius and Pisces do the same from April to December 2033, and Pisces, the 12th's natural sign, holds Jupiter until April 2034. Then Aries gives way to Taurus in April 2035, Taurus to Gemini in May 2036, Gemini to Cancer in May 2037 and Cancer to Leo again in June 2038.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make time alone a habit rather than an emergency. Jupiter in the 12th grows what you tend in private, and it needs the hours to do it.

Finish what's finished. The 12th is the house of closure, and the cleaner the endings here, the freer the start when Jupiter reaches your Ascendant.

Give quietly: care given without an audience is this house's kind of growth. And if what comes up in private feels too heavy to carry alone, talk to someone qualified; a transit is weather, and some weather is better met with help.
