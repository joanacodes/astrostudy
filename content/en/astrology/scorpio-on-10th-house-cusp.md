Scorpio on the 10th house cusp makes the sign of depth the one the world sees at the top of your chart. The standing it points you towards rests on knowing a thing all the way down.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In Placidus, the house system I use, the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) begins at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), and what I read there is the part you play in public: your responsibilities, your standing, what you hope your work will leave ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) there, authority tends to come from knowing the material thoroughly rather than from a title. You may be known for one thing done completely, and trusted with what others would rather not handle, which you carry without broadcasting it. Visibility matters less to you than what your work changes, and you may do your best where results count for more than credit. Colleagues often come to you when something has gone wrong, because you don't flinch from looking at it. Standing, for you, is built slowly and, if it has to be, rebuilt: you may reinvent your public role more than once. It points to a way of carrying responsibility, never to a particular profession.

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) rule your 10th, and I read both. Their houses show where your ambitions are fed, the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/), and when they fall in different houses, your public life draws on two sources at once, which I read as two threads of one ambition. Aries opens your 3rd in about 3 London charts in 4 with this cusp, and 4 in 5 at New York, so Mars runs both your public role and the everyday words, lessons and errands that feed it. Below, Taurus is always on the IC ([Taurus on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/)): the intensity goes into your public life, and home is where Venus lets you rest from it. At London, Capricorn rises in about 5 charts in 6, so Saturn, the 10th's natural ruler, rules your chart.

The sign on the Midheaven is set by the clock, not by latitude, so the figure is the same at London and New York: about 1 birth in 12, with Scorpio taking roughly two hours to pass the meridian. At about 18° to 22° wide at London, your 10th is smaller than a sign, and Scorpio spills over onto a neighbouring cusp in nearly half these charts ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)): the 11th in about 3 in 10, the 9th in about 1 in 6. Scorpio never hides inside the 10th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)); it goes without a cusp in about 1 London birth in 7 and 1 New York birth in 11, and then lies lower in the chart.

Born earlier, you could have had Scorpio on your 11th cusp ([Scorpio on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-11th-house-cusp/)); later, on the 9th ([Scorpio on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/)), with Libra or Sagittarius at the top of the chart instead. At the top of a chart a few minutes count, and I read the Midheaven only from a recorded time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
