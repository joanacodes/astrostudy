Joint accounts, debts, inheritances, trust, the closeness that changes you: these are 8th-house matters. With Virgo on the 8th house cusp, all of it gets read carefully, the small print first.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) brings to the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)) the wish to understand before letting go. I read it as trust built in small, reliable steps: someone keeps their word over little things, and only then are the big ones handed over. When money is held in common, you're usually the one keeping the joint budget, reading the contract and tracking what's owed, so nobody is caught out. In intimacy, care shows as attention: you notice what someone needs and quietly provide it, and you hope for the same precision back. Feelings that can't be analysed are the hard part, since the 8th asks for a surrender Virgo would rather research first. Worry about debt or dependence tends to be handled with lists, which helps, until the list becomes the worry.

Through its house, [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) shows the part of life where joint accounts are kept and trust is earned: as ruler of the 8th, in the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). At London nearly every chart with this cusp has [Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/), so Saturn rules the chart, and the whole of Leo lies inside the 7th with no cusp of its own ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The Sun then rules no house by its cusp, and the warmth of the chart gathers in the house of partners, while the house of what partners share opens in careful Virgo. Every one of these London charts has Gemini, Mercury's other sign, on the 5th cusp, so intimacy and pleasure answer to the same planet. In about 2 London charts in 5, the 8th also holds the whole of Libra, and Venus has a say in what's shared.

Placidus cusps, the ones I use, put Virgo on the 8th for about 1 London birth in 20 and 1 New York birth in 17: exactly as often as on the 6th, the cusp that mirrors this one below the western horizon. Both sit near the setting point, where Virgo moves quickly, about 72 minutes a day on this cusp at London. Here Virgo keeps to this one cusp, never the 7th or the 9th as well, while about 1 London chart in 6 gives it no cusp at all.

Seventy-odd minutes give a rounded time plenty of room to mislead. The minute decides between this page and its neighbours, [Virgo on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-9th-house-cusp/) for an earlier birth and [Virgo on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-7th-house-cusp/) for a later one, which is why I want it written down before I read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Death and inheritance were the 8th's business in older astrology, and neither can be read from a chart ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). What I read here is trust, intimacy and the care you take with what's held in common; with Virgo, that care is considerable.
