With Taurus rising, you were born while [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) was crossing the eastern horizon, and your first move is often to wait. Before you join in, you take the measure of the place, the people and the ground under your feet; once you've joined, you tend to stay.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

A fixed earth sign on the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) gives a steady entrance. Earth trusts what it can check, fixity holds a course, and together they make a manner that settles first and acts second. In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant is the cusp of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/). Taurus is the natural sign of the 2nd, the house of what you own and value, so with Taurus rising the question of worth comes to the front door: is this solid, and is it worth staying for?

It's a quicker riser than its calm suggests. At London Taurus clears the horizon in about 69 minutes, at Paris in 74 and at New York in 86: a little more than an hour, where an average sign takes two. Roughly one London birth in 21 has it, more than [Aries rising](/astrology/aries-rising/) before it, fewer than most. The catch is the hour itself. A time written to the nearest hour may be half an hour out, and at London half an hour is nearly half of Taurus: enough to turn it into Aries rising or [Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/). So I want the minute before I name it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). South of the equator the timing turns round: signs that rise fast in the northern hemisphere rise slowly in the southern, Taurus included.

## First impressions {#first-impressions}

People often find you calm, and many find that reassuring before they could say why. You tend to listen before you speak and decide before you move, and once you've moved, you rarely take it back. People notice, too, that you don't rush to fill a silence.

The misreading is slowness taken for reluctance, or for a closed mind. What looks like reluctance is usually checking: Taurus rising wants to know the ground will hold before it puts its weight down. Rush it and you meet the fixed sign's resistance; give it a little time and you meet its loyalty.

A caution before you build on any of this: in [my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the rising sign is among the placements everyone reads first, and it held about a third of the time. A steady first impression is a tendency, and the rest of your chart decides how much of it is you.

## Its ruler {#its-ruler}

Taurus belongs to [Venus](/astrology/venus/), so Venus is your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/). I read Venus as the planet of value and attraction: what you prize, what you're drawn to, how you weigh one thing against another. As chart ruler, its sign shows how you choose, and its house the part of life your choices keep leading you back to. Venus never strays more than about 47° from the Sun, so yours sits in your Sun sign or within two signs of it: the season you were born in shapes the ruler of the whole chart.

In Taurus, Venus is at home, and the ruler doubles the Ascendant's steadiness. In Pisces, its exaltation, what you value widens into generosity; in Virgo, its fall, care shows in useful things more than in declarations. In Scorpio, the sign on your Descendant, Venus is in detriment. Your chart ruler then sits in the sign you look for in others, and what you value most may come to you first through someone else's world. A Venus in detriment chooses differently, not worse.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Trust comes slowly with Taurus rising, and then it's built to last. You want to know where you stand, and you give the same certainty back. The harder part is change inside a bond: a new routine or a sudden plan can feel like the ground moving, even when the news is good. Saying so out loud, rather than digging in, saves a great many quiet stand-offs.

Opposite, your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is in Scorpio: the people who draw you in tend to be intense and private, and they want all of you or nothing ([Scorpio on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/)).

Your calm and their depth can make a lasting pair, and Venus, wherever it sits, shows what you're hoping that pair will build.
