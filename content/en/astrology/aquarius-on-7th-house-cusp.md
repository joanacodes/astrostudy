With Aquarius on the 7th house cusp, your Descendant faces a Leo Ascendant. You bring the warmth into a room; the people you choose to pair with bring a cooler curiosity, and they treat you as an equal rather than an audience.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) describes the person across the table ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and with [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) there you're drawn to people with minds and lives of their own: the partner who has opinions, causes and friends you didn't bring, and who isn't dazzled by your entrance. A bond works for you when it feels like an alliance between two free people. Shared plans, honest talk and separate evenings keep it alive; jealousy and scorekeeping tend to end it. Some couples with this cusp arrange their lives in ways others find unusual, and that's their business. In the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) the same holds for business partners and collaborators: you do best with people who argue from principle and keep their word. Open rivals, the 7th's old enemies, tend to be people who disagree with you about how things should be run, rarely about who gets the credit.

Two planets speak for your partnerships, and I read both. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) shows where you meet people unexpectedly and where a relationship asks for room or for change; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) shows where commitment is built and tested over years. In more than 8 charts in 10 with this cusp the 6th opens in Capricorn, which gives Saturn a hand in your working days too: a partner may also be a collaborator, and a bond often runs on shared tasks as much as on feeling. [Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/) comes with every chart that has this Descendant, so the Sun rules your chart while Uranus and Saturn rule your partners: you bring the warmth, and the people you choose bring the long view. The house each ruler occupies shows where you tend to meet partners and what you build with them, as ruler of the 7th in the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Aquarius sits on the Descendant for about 1 London birth in 8 and 1 New York birth in 9, in Placidus, the house system I use, more often than on any other cusp. Seen from London, the stretch of zodiac setting in the west moves slowly through Aquarius, a little under three hours for the whole sign, so the 7th comes out narrow in these charts, about 16° to 20° wide, and in about 2 London charts in 5 Aquarius opens the 8th as well ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). The same two planets then rule your partners and what you share with them, trust and money in common among it ([Aquarius on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-8th-house-cusp/)). Aries or Taurus holds the Midheaven. Charts with no Aquarian cusp at all exist too, about 1 in 7 at London ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and they read partnership through other signs.

Nothing on this cusp says whether you'll marry, whom, or for how long: it describes what you look for, and what partnership asks of you. The Descendant moves with the Ascendant, so it needs your birth time. A little later in the day and Aquarius would have been on your 6th cusp instead ([Aquarius on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-6th-house-cusp/)), and a chart guessed from a date alone can't tell the two apart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
