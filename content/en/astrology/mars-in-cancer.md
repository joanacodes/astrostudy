Some drives are aimed at a goal. Mars in Cancer is aimed at a perimeter: the people, rooms and memories it has decided to keep safe. Inside that line it can seem mild, even hesitant. Cross it, and you meet one of the most tenacious placements Mars has.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is initiative and effort, and the part of you that defends a boundary. [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) is cardinal water, ruled by the Moon: like every cardinal sign it starts things, but what it starts is shelter, care, a place to belong. In Cancer, Mars puts its energy into protecting. It acts for the people it counts as its own, often more readily than for itself, and it moves according to how safe things feel.

Tradition calls Cancer Mars's [fall](/astrology/fall/), the sign opposite its exaltation in Capricorn: where Capricorn aims effort at a summit, Cancer wraps it round a centre. I read the fall as indirectness rather than weakness. This Mars rarely charges a problem head on. It circles, waits and defends, and it becomes formidable when someone it loves is under threat.

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/), Cancer's ruler, says a great deal about when this Mars feels safe enough to act, so I read its sign and house alongside. The Moon changes sign every two and a half days or so, which means that on many birth dates its sign depends on the hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)).

Mars lingers here: about 47 days on a usual pass, and up to 228, about seven and a half months, when it turns retrograde in the sign. Because those stays run long, Cancer is one of the more common Mars signs, held by about 1 person in 10 of those born 1930–2009. You share the sign with people born a few weeks either side of you; the house shows which perimeter is yours to guard. [Mars in Gemini](/astrology/mars-in-gemini/), before it, fights with words; [Mars in Leo](/astrology/mars-in-leo/), after it, steps into the open. In between, Cancer keeps its strength for what it holds.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Close to you, this Mars shows love by guarding it. You notice when someone you care for is being treated badly and act before they've asked, and your loyalty can be fierce. Your own wants are harder to put into words. Rather than say *I'm angry* or *I need this*, you may go quiet, withdraw for a while, or let the feeling leak out sideways, in a tone, a door closed a little too hard, a remark about something else entirely. Partners who ask gently, and who make it safe to say the direct thing, find in you a very steady ally.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, Mars in Cancer does its best when it has something to protect: a team, a client, a project it has nursed from the start. It can be a far more tenacious advocate than it looks. Work that involves care, food, homes, families or keeping people safe draws on this drive naturally. Cold competition, where colleagues are rivals and nobody looks out for anyone, tends to drain it. You'll usually work harder for people you trust than for a target on a spreadsheet.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Indirectness. Anger that isn't named tends to surface somewhere else, at the wrong moment or towards the wrong person. You may keep a careful record of hurts long after the other person has forgotten them, and defend your circle so hard that criticism of anyone in it feels like an attack on you. Acting from hurt, rather than from what you actually want, is the pattern to watch.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the feeling early, even clumsily: *I'm upset, and I'll explain later* is enough. Give yourself the protection you give others, including a no when you need one. Effort that ends in comfort suits this Mars: cooking, building, gardening, putting a room in order. And when you catch yourself fighting someone else's battle, ask what your own battle is. It's often the one you've been putting off.
