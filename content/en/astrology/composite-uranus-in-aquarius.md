Aquarius is Uranus's own sign in modern tropical astrology, so this is the composite Uranus most at home where it stands. The relationship is built the way Aquarius builds things: around a shared idea of the future, with room for two independent people and a circle of friends around them.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the relationship's independent streak. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, is ruled by Uranus itself in modern tropical astrology and by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in the tradition, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). So composite Uranus here is in its own sign, and composite Saturn matters too: Uranus says what the pair wants to change, Saturn what keeps the change workable.

As the relationship's own placement, this makes friendship the core of the bond. The pair tends to live inside a network, friends, causes, communities, and to treat each person's independence as a shared value rather than a threat. Its plans face forward: what the two of you want the world, or your corner of it, to look like in twenty years. Fixed air keeps those ideals steady, and a bond like this can hold to a principle for decades. [Uranus in Aquarius](/astrology/uranus-in-aquarius/) marks a generation of networks and ideas ahead of their time; in the composite, the network has a couple at its centre.

Being at home doesn't make it commoner: about 1 pair in 12 of the random pairs in my count, born 1930–2009, have it, as with most signs. Couples of the same age inherit it from April 1995 to December 2003, when Uranus crossed Aquarius: they are in their twenties or just past thirty, among the youngest adult couples with any composite Uranus sign. With an age gap of under four decades, the younger of the two was born after about 1995. Someone born in mid-1991, with Uranus near 13° Capricorn, and someone born in mid-2006, with Uranus near 15° Pisces, 35 and 20 today, find their composite Uranus near 14° Aquarius, at home in a sign neither of them was born with.

Autonomy is not isolation, and that is the trap. A relationship this comfortable with distance can be warmer to the group than between its own two people, and an even, objective tone can hide a need for closeness. What helps is time that belongs to the two of you alone, kept as firmly as any meeting, and Saturn's reminder that a commitment is one of the freedoms you chose.

Shared by a whole age group, the sign speaks for an era; the house, drawn from both birth times, speaks for you: [composite Uranus in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-11th-house/), Aquarius's natural house, doubles the emphasis on friends and causes. [Composite Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-capricorn/) reforms structures; [composite Uranus in Pisces](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-pisces/) changes by imagining. I explain composites without drawing them; their users read this beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), as a description and never a forecast, and never without both people's say-so ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
