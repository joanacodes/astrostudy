In Lilith trine North Node in synastry, one person's Black Moon Lilith stands about four signs, within 2.55°, from the other's North Node, and two signs from their South Node. The untamed side of one seems to wave the other on. For the node person, growing and being themselves start to look like the same thing.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's mean apogee, stands for an instinct for self-rule, for authenticity and for whatever was pushed out of view, in readings that vary enough for me to keep it symbolic. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) names what someone is learning to do through steady practice, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite, what they do without trying. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) keeps two functions in easy step while each stays itself, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one brings a sextile along: the Lilith that trines one node sextiles the other.

If the Lilith is yours, your independence reads to the other as encouragement. The refusals and appetites you learned to hide elsewhere seem to help this person rather than alarm them, and there's relief in having your edges welcomed.

Carrying the North Node, you find that the other's frankness makes your unfamiliar direction feel more like you. With them around, becoming more capable and becoming more yourself line up, and the light sextile to your South Node keeps your familiar strengths within reach.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The risk is taking ease for readiness. Because the trine makes the other's instinct feel right, the node person may treat every impulse it inspires as growth, or adopt the Lilith person as a compass instead of practising. The Lilith person, for their part, may find their private wildness has become someone's inspiration, which is flattering and a little exposing.

- **[Lilith conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the instinct dropped onto the path itself, with no distance to work across.
- **[Lilith sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** the reverse proportions, at home with the old ground and only a narrow opening to the new.
- **[Lilith square North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-square-north-node-synastry/):** the instinct pressing across the axis, claimed by neither end.
- **[Lilith opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** the instinct resting on the other's familiar end.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Test the ease with feedback. Whoever holds the North Node can try one thing the trine makes feel natural, then look honestly at how it went; whoever holds the Lilith can keep their own pace rather than performing boldness for someone else's benefit. A couple, two friends, an adult child and a parent, or colleagues can all use the encouragement without handing over the steering.

Mean Lilith and the true node set the dates, and the mean node is compared. Someone born in mid-1965 has the nodes across Gemini, the northern end, and Sagittarius, the southern; anyone born from June to August 1983 has Lilith in Aquarius, trine that North Node and sextile that South Node. Lilith comes back to Aquarius every nine years or so, so births from August to October 1974 and from April to June 1992 repeat it, and between them come Libra windows, when Lilith trines the same North Node from the other side.

However warm it feels, it belongs to birth months, and I leave it off the contacts I tally in a synastry; each of those needs the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Its houses tell the story: one era's independence lending another era's growth a little nerve. Those houses take both birth times, since the hour barely nudges Lilith or the node ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Lilith trine North Node](/astrology/lilith-trine-north-node/) in a single chart lines up a person's instinct with their own growth. Between two charts, the encouragement comes from another person, who should agree to the reading as much as you do ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
