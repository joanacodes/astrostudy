Pisces on the 4th house cusp puts your IC, the ground under the chart, in the last sign of the zodiac. Home is less a fixed address than a feeling you return to, and its walls tend to be soft: moods, music and other people's needs pass easily through them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) as where you come from and what you need in private before you can face anything else ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) there, you pick up the mood of a house the moment you walk in, and you rest best somewhere quiet enough to drift: a dim room, music on, water within reach. Family history may come in fragments, a story half told, a move nobody explained, more than one place that counted as home, and belonging rests on feeling more than on documents. Your door tends to open to whoever needs a bed for the night. What the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) shows me is home as you felt it and feel it now, not a portrait of anyone in your family. The lesson it asks for is a gentle one: a home also needs a door you can close.

Behind your front door, Neptune and Jupiter share the say, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows where home turns into a refuge, or into a longing for one; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where it grows, the house that fills with guests, the family that widens to take people in. Jupiter also rules your money in about 7 London charts in 10, through Sagittarius on the 2nd, and what you earn tends to flow towards home. The house holding each one is where your idea of home keeps drifting, as ruler of the 4th in the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Pisces reaches the IC in about 1 birth in 13, at London and New York alike, because the sign on the meridian depends on the time and not on how far north you are, and it crosses the meridian in about an hour and fifty minutes, a little faster than most signs. The 4th begins exactly there in Placidus, the house system I use. Virgo always holds the Midheaven above ([Virgo on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/)), so the meridian runs from Virgo at the top to Pisces at the base: in public, precision and usefulness, with Mercury in charge of your career; in private, refuge and imagination. In about 5 London charts in 6 the Ascendant is in Scorpio, and Mars and Pluto rule the chart; at New York, Scorpio and Sagittarius rise about equally often. Aquarius usually opens the 3rd just before, and in about 1 London chart in 3 lies whole inside that house instead ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Charts where Pisces opens nothing at all are common too, about 1 in 6 at London and 1 in 8 at New York.

Your birth time is what sets Pisces down at the IC rather than on the cusps around it, the 3rd had you been born later ([Pisces on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-3rd-house-cusp/)), the 5th had you been born earlier ([Pisces on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-5th-house-cusp/)). Without it the chart has no base for me to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
