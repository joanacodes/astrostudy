Once in its 84-year round, Uranus climbs to the top of your birth chart. For about twenty months, the part of you that takes part in the wider world, your work, your role, what you'd like to be known for, is where the wish to differ shows first.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reforms, and reform isn't demolition: it keeps what a structure is for and changes how it works. The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is the point where what you can do meets the wider world: role, responsibility, contribution. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into one process, so for these months the push to reform and your public direction work as one. Some people redesign the role they already hold; some bring a skill they kept private into view; some find the title they carry no longer describes what they do.

The Midheaven isn't your job. It covers any way you take part in the world beyond your own walls, paid or not, which is why a cause, a craft or a public stand can carry this [transit](/astrology/transits/) as much as a career. I read the climate around your public life from it, never a post gained or lost ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 10th house begins at the Midheaven, so Uranus's years in it begin with this crossing ([Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/)). The same Uranus stands opposite your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the base everything else rests on: a change of direction asks something of your foundations too.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within the orbs I use, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus stays on your Midheaven for about twenty months, sometimes only eleven, with three exact hits in all but about one pass in fourteen.

Most people born between 1930 and 2009 meet it once before 80; about 1 in 33 don't meet it at all. In the 84-year round this crossing opens, Uranus sextiles your Midheaven about fourteen years in ([transiting Uranus sextile natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-midheaven/)), squares it at about twenty-one ([transiting Uranus square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-midheaven/)), trines it at about twenty-eight ([transiting Uranus trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-midheaven/)) and reaches your IC at about forty-two ([transiting Uranus opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-midheaven/)), then makes the same contacts in reverse on the way back.

A time remembered as “around three” can easily be half an hour out, and half an hour moves your Midheaven by about seven degrees: nearly two years of Uranus. It's why I ask for the time before anything else ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Midheavens in Gemini are crossed while Uranus is in that sign, to May 2033, and Cancer ones from 2032 to 2039 ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Describe the work you'd want in a sentence before you change anything. Uranus at the top of the chart is easier to steer with a destination in mind.

Change from inside before you change from outside: a new method, a project within the role, a skill you've kept private, put to use where people can see it.

Check what your foundations can carry. Uranus faces your IC at the same time, and a new direction needs a home life that can hold it.
