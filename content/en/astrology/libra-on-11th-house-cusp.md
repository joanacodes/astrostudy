With Libra on the 11th house cusp, the sign of the pair runs your wider circle. Even among many people you tend to relate one at a time, and the alliances you keep are the ones that run both ways.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is where a chart gathers the people you choose to stand with and the aims you share with them: allies, networks, the circle that outlasts a job or a move ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Libra](/astrology/libra/) relates in pairs, and on this cusp your circle often turns out to be a set of one-to-one friendships rather than a crowd: you know each friend on their own terms, and may introduce them to one another only later. Reciprocity matters, and a friendship that only ever runs one way tends to fade with you. In a collective, your gift is often the alliance, the arrangement between two groups that need each other. The future you'd sign up for tends to be a fairer one, whether that means a cause about equal treatment or simply a club where the rules apply to everyone.

Follow [Venus](/astrology/venus/), ruler of your 11th, to its house and you find where those chosen ties lead: the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In about 3 London charts in 4 with this cusp, Scorpio rises and Taurus sits on your Descendant, so Venus rules your 7th as well: the equals you choose in company and the one you face across the table answer to the same planet, and I read the two houses together. In more than half, Taurus opens the 6th too, and the people you work beside join the list. Aries, opposite on your 5th, gives Mars your pleasures and romances.

Libra climbs the eastern sky slowly at these latitudes and lingers on the cusps above the Ascendant, so the 11th is one of its commoner cusps: about 1 London birth in 11 and 1 New York birth in 12 have it, in Placidus, the house system I use. With Scorpio rising, Mars and Pluto rule your chart, and I read both; otherwise Sagittarius rises and Jupiter does. Narrow here, about 20° to 24° at London, the 11th often lets Libra run on to the next cusp or back to the previous one ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)): the 12th in about 1 London chart in 4, the Midheaven in about 1 in 11. Venus then rules your retreat or your public life alongside your circle. Libra never hides inside the 11th: when it misses every cusp, in about 1 London birth in 6 and 1 New York birth in 8, it lies in one of the houses from the 3rd to the 9th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Born earlier, you could have had Libra on your 12th cusp, just above the horizon ([Libra on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-12th-house-cusp/)); later, on the Midheaven itself ([Libra on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/)). With a house this narrow, a few minutes change which cusps Libra holds and how many, which is why I work only from a recorded birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
