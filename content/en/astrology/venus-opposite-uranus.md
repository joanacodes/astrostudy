Venus opposite Uranus stretches a line between the wish to be close and the wish to be free, and hands you both ends. Its main request is that you stop choosing between them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) draws you towards what you value and whom you're fond of: it weighs, attracts and keeps exchanges fair. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) pulls towards independence and renewal: it differentiates, frees and reforms. In an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), each sits at one pole of the same axis, and neither is meant to win.

In plain words, togetherness and independence face each other across your chart. You may long for closeness and then feel hemmed in by it, or prize your freedom and then miss the warmth you held off. Often one end turns up in someone else: a partner who wants more space than you do, or who brings change into your life while you hold the steady end; sometimes it's the reverse, and you're the one who keeps the door open. The aim isn't a compromise in which nobody gets enough, but a bond, or a life, built to hold both: real closeness with real room.

I read all this as a tendency in how you love, never a prediction about who will stay or leave ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Venus and Uranus face each other from signs of the same modality. With Venus in Libra, one of its own signs, facing Uranus in Aries, a wish for harmony and partnership faces an urge to strike out alone; with Venus in Taurus and Uranus opposite in Scorpio, a love of comfort faces a pull towards change that goes deep. The houses make it concrete: Venus in the 2nd with Uranus in the 8th might set a love of steady comforts against an intimacy that keeps reinventing itself; Venus in the 5th with Uranus in the 11th, romance and friendship asking to be told apart.

About 1 person in 32 is born with Venus and Uranus within 6° of an opposition. Tight, it's a theme in every close bond; wide, it comes back mainly when a relationship reaches a crossroads.

The pair's other aspects, seen from this one:

- **[Venus conjunct Uranus](/astrology/venus-conjunct-uranus/):** the two side by side; the spark and the need for room in a single gesture.
- **[Venus sextile Uranus](/astrology/venus-sextile-uranus/):** a friendly angle; new things find their way into what you love through friends and ideas.
- **[Venus square Uranus](/astrology/venus-square-uranus/):** a right angle; attachment and change rubbing until something reforms.
- **[Venus trine Uranus](/astrology/venus-trine-uranus/):** a smooth angle in one element; freedom and affection without a quarrel.

Between two charts, [Venus opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-uranus-synastry/) pairs one person's affection with the other's independence, from either side of the table: electric, and asking for a lot of honesty about space. One person usually feels the pull and the other the push, so I read each chart in turn.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), [Transiting Uranus opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-venus/) marks the halfway point of the cycle, about 42 years after Uranus meets your Venus, and lasts about a year too. If you were born with it, the other half of the picture arrives around 42 instead: at your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), Uranus in the sky comes to stand on your Venus, as if the other end of the line had crossed the chart to visit.

Weather, not a forecast: a season for renegotiating closeness and room, never the forecast of an ending.
