The 10th house sits at the top of a birth chart: the part of your life lived in full view. It begins at the Midheaven, and it describes your work, your standing and your reputation — what people know you for, and what you'd like them to.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 10th house as your public direction. Work is part of it, but not the whole: it's the role you play in the world, the reputation you build, the authority you earn or push against, and what you'd like to have done. Tradition gave it honour, rank and the actions others see.

It describes a direction, not a job title. Two people with the same 10th house can work in completely different trades and still be climbing the same kind of hill.

- **An angular house.** It's one of the four houses that begin at the angles, and the 10th is the most public of them: what sits here tends to be seen. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its angle.** Its cusp is the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), or MC: the point where the zodiac crossed the meridian when you were born, which isn't the point straight overhead. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, the Midheaven is where the 10th house begins; in whole sign houses, it can fall in another house ([which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)).
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/): patience, structure, the long climb.
- **Its opposite.** The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), home and roots: the 4th is what goes on behind your own front door, the 10th what the world sees of you.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), just before it, is where you work out what you believe; the 10th is where you act on it in public. The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), just after it, is the people and causes you build with.

The Midheaven goes round the whole zodiac in a day, like the Ascendant, so the 10th house needs your birth time to be placed ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 10th house {#planets}

A planet in the 10th house is on show. It tends to describe what you're known for, or what you're working towards in public, sometimes before you'd choose to be seen.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), against about a third for most placements. With Saturn, [Chiron in the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-in-10th-house/) or [Lilith in the 10th house](/astrology/lilith-in-10th-house/) here, a reading of mine would take its time over how you want to be seen, and what being seen has cost you.

- **[Sun in the 10th house](/astrology/sun-in-10th-house/):** your purpose shows in public, in your work and standing; you want to be recognised for what you do.
- **[Moon in the 10th house](/astrology/moon-in-10th-house/):** you want work that feels like care, or care that becomes your work; your public life follows your moods.
- **[Mercury in the 10th house](/astrology/mercury-in-10th-house/):** known for your words and ideas; a reputation for being clear.
- **[Venus in the 10th house](/astrology/venus-in-10th-house/):** a gift for being liked in public; work with people, art or beauty.
- **[Mars in the 10th house](/astrology/mars-in-10th-house/):** ambition with drive; you learn to fight for your place without fighting everyone.
- **[Jupiter in the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-10th-house/):** you aim high, and others often see a leader or a teacher in you; the lesson is to choose the hill worth climbing.
- **[Saturn in the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-in-10th-house/):** in the house of its own sign: ambition on a long timetable; authority earned rather than given.
- **[Uranus in the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-in-10th-house/):** a path that doesn't follow the usual route; sudden changes of direction, and a need for independence at work.
- **[Neptune in the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-in-10th-house/):** a vocation led by imagination or compassion; a public image others project onto.
- **[Pluto in the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-in-10th-house/):** a strong drive for influence, and a long lesson about power — yours and other people's.

No planet here? An [empty 10th house](/astrology/empty-houses/) isn't an empty career, or a life without ambition. It's read through the sign on the Midheaven and the place of the 10th's ruler, the next two sections.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, the sign on the cusp of the 10th house is your Midheaven sign. It describes the style of your public life: how you'd like to be seen, and how you go about being taken seriously.

- **[Aries on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/):** you want to lead and start things; a public life built on initiative and nerve.
- **[Taurus on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a reputation for steadiness; work that builds something lasting, at your own pace.
- **[Gemini on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/):** known for your words and versatility; often more than one role at a time.
- **[Cancer on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** you want work that looks after people; a public role that feels like family.
- **[Leo on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** you want to be seen and to shine; work where creativity or leadership can show.
- **[Virgo on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** known for competence and care; work that's useful, precise and done well.
- **[Libra on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a reputation for fairness and tact; work with people, art or balance.
- **[Scorpio on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a private person with a strong public presence; work that goes where others won't.
- **[Sagittarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** you want your work to mean something; teaching, travel, ideas, wide horizons.
- **[Capricorn on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/):** the house's natural sign: ambition with patience, and authority earned over time.
- **[Aquarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** known for doing things differently; work for a group, a cause or the future.
- **[Pisces on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a path that feels more like a calling than a ladder.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 10th house is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp, and where it sits shows what your public life draws its energy from: the part of life that feeds your work, or that your work keeps coming back to. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the cusp, there's a modern and a traditional ruler to read ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** your work is part of who you are; you often make your own way.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** work tied to earning and to what you value; security built through what you do.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** work with words, teaching or your local world; a career that moves and talks.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** work and home overlap: working from home, a family business, a career that builds a base.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** work through creation, performance or play; a vocation that has to bring joy.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** a vocation built through daily craft and service.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** work tied to partnership: working with a partner, or with clients one to one.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** work with other people's resources, crises or research; a career with depth.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** work in teaching, travel, publishing or belief; a career tied to ideas or other countries.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** the ruler at home: a strong sense of direction, and a public life you steer yourself.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** work through networks, groups and causes; a career built with and for others.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** work behind the scenes, in care or in institutions; a vocation that's quiet or private.

## Transits through the 10th house {#transits}

The Moon passes through your 10th house every month, the Sun once a year, Mercury, Venus and Mars for a few weeks at a time: a meeting, a deadline, a week when you're more visible. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-10th-house/):** about a year when ambitions feel within reach and you may be more visible than usual.
- **[Saturn transiting the 10th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-10th-house/):** about two and a half years of responsibility at work, and a clear look at what you've built.
- **[Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/):** around seven years of wanting to change direction, with room for work that doesn't fit the usual mould.
- **[Neptune transiting the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-10th-house/):** around fourteen years when your vocation asks for more meaning, and your public image is harder to pin down.
- **[Pluto transiting the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-10th-house/):** a long, deep change in your relationship with power, ambition and the role you play.
- **[Chiron transiting the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-10th-house/):** an old wound around success, failure or being seen comes up to be tended; its pace is uneven, from under two years to about eight in a sign.
- **[North Node transiting the 10th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-10th-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards public life, while the South Node crosses your 4th.

I read these as weather, not a forecast: they describe the season you're in at work, not what will happen to your career.
