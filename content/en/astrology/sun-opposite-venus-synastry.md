Sun opposite Venus is a contact no birth chart contains: Venus never gets more than about 47° from the Sun. Between two people it's ordinary, and particular. One person's Venus faces the other's Sun across the zodiac, so what one of you values sits directly opposite who the other is.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) carries what a person wants recognised in themselves: identity, purpose, self-expression. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs things: value, reciprocity, attraction, the balance of give and take. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) holds two functions in balance across the zodiac, each defining the other, and of all the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) between these two, it's the one that most plainly needs two people to exist.

Tradition gives Venus two signs, Taurus and Libra, and Libra's emblem is the scales. An opposition is a pair of scales too, so here the seesaw is an exchange: what one person gives, what the other receives, and whether it evens out.

Opposite signs share a mode. An Aries Venus facing a Libra Sun: a liking for the direct and the bold, facing someone whose identity rests on tact and balance. A Pisces Venus facing a Virgo Sun: a taste for the dreamy and the boundless, facing someone who finds themselves in precision and use.

If it's your Sun, you're appreciated by someone whose taste runs opposite to your nature. They value in you what they don't have, and that can be magnetic, like being admired from across a room. If yours is the Venus, you're drawn to someone who embodies what your preferences don't usually choose. Liking them stretches your values, and your sense of what's attractive quietly widens.

What flows is attraction across a difference, and attraction is not romance: two colleagues with opposite strengths, friends who open opposite worlds to each other, siblings who have always balanced each other: the contact belongs to all of them.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

This seesaw, never an enmity, tips on fairness. One person may always give and the other always receive; one person's taste may always decide. The attraction to difference can also turn into a wish to see it gone: the Venus person nudging the Sun person towards their own preferences, the Sun person feeling admired and corrected at once. Any tension comes from how the exchange is handled, not from the angle itself.

At narrower angles, the meeting changes:

- **[Sun conjunct Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-venus-synastry/):** birthdays about a month apart; appreciation lands on the self instead of facing it.
- **[Sun sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-venus-synastry/):** pleasures offered from two signs away, with no scales to keep level.
- **[Sun square Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-venus-synastry/):** a quarter of the circle; preferences pressing at an angle rather than weighing across.
- **[Sun trine Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-venus-synastry/):** a third of the circle, mostly in one element; liking that needs no balancing.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take turns, visibly: the film, the holiday, the colour of the walls, the next project. Keep the exchange in view; the scales stay level when both people can see them. Say what you admire in the other's difference instead of trying to correct it. At work, two people with opposite strengths make a strong pairing once they agree who leads on what; in a couple or a family, alternating who plans and who enjoys keeps either role from setting.

This one is common: about 1 pair of people in 12, counting both directions, within 8°. The birthdays then tend to sit about five months apart, between about four and six, whatever the years, since each Venus keeps company with its own Sun.

The birth hour barely shifts either body, so it seldom makes or breaks the opposition; it places it in the houses, the two areas of life the balancing concerns, and both times come before any reading I give ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). And because it weighs what each person gives, I read it only at the request of both ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
