Scorpio on the 5th house cusp gives the house of play to the sign that never does anything by halves. The pleasures you keep are the ones you can lose yourself in, and come back from changed.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is where a chart keeps what you do for the joy of it: play, flirtation, creative work, the risks you take for fun ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) on its cusp, a hobby rarely stays a hobby, and you'd rather master a few pleasures than sample many. Creative work tends to be transformation: a piece rewritten until it rings true, a material others threw away turned into something new. A game is fun when it asks something of you, and strategy can be a pleasure in itself. Attraction tends to matter when it changes something in the people involved. Tradition put the joy of Venus in this house and its detriment in Scorpio, and I read the pairing as pleasure that is less light than absorbing.

Where your joys lead you depends on two planets, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), and I read both: the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In about 3 London charts in 4 with this cusp, and 4 in 5 at New York, Aries is on your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), so Mars rules both what you do for pleasure and what you do in public: your creative drive and your ambitions tend to run on the same engine. Venus gets your wider circle, through Taurus on the 11th.

The 5th is wide in these charts, about 40° to 45° at London, and in about 2 in 5 it holds the whole of Sagittarius after Scorpio ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), so Jupiter adds its wider horizons to your pleasures. In Placidus, the house system I use, about 1 London birth in 14 has Scorpio on the 5th, and 1 New York birth in 13: a slow riser in the north, the sign spends less time on the cusps that lead down to the Descendant than on those around the Ascendant. At London, Leo rises a little more often than Cancer with this cusp, so the Sun or the Moon rules the chart; at New York, Cancer rises in about 7 charts in 10. And in about 1 London birth in 7, 1 in 11 at New York, Scorpio misses every cusp; it then lies most often in the 6th, and next most often in the 5th.

People sometimes ask whether a Scorpio 5th says something about children, since tradition put them in this house. In my reading it doesn't: a chart can't tell anyone whether or when a child will come ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and this site never reads [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/). What it describes is the intensity you bring to your own pleasures as an adult.

The cusps on either side matter here: the IC, where Scorpio could have been a little later ([Scorpio on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/)), and the 6th, where it might have been a little earlier ([Scorpio on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/)). The width of your 5th, and whether Sagittarius fits inside it, change with the minute too, so I need the recorded time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
