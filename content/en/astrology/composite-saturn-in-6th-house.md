The dog walked at seven every morning, two lunches packed side by side, a small shop opened together and run six days a week. Composite Saturn in the 6th house puts a relationship's commitment into the working day.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Ordinary days are the 6th house's subject. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [the 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the relationship's daily round: who does what, how the work gets shared, the routines that keep two lives running. It's a cadent, humble house, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) suits it better than one might expect. The pair commits through ordinary days. Jobs get divided and done, a shared project or small business may be run side by side for years, and looking after each other is part of the routine rather than a special occasion: the early night before the other's hard day, the coffee made without asking.

Craft belongs here too: a skill refined over years, a trade the two of you share, the slow mastery that comes of doing one thing well, again and again. That kind of patience is Saturn's own, and in this house it has daily material to work on.

Tradition called this house Bad Fortune and gave Mars its joy here; its natural sign is Virgo. It also gave the 6th illness, which no chart predicts and I never read for ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); in a composite, the 6th describes how two people organise life and work, nothing more. Where the house falls depends on both birth times and on the method, cusp midpoints or houses rebuilt from the composite Midheaven where you live, so a Saturn near its edges may sit in the 5th or the 7th by another reckoning ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In someone's own chart, [Saturn in the 6th house](/astrology/saturn-in-6th-house/) is a strong sense of duty about work, which needs rest as much as rigour; [their Saturn in your 6th house](/astrology/saturn-in-6th-house-synastry/) is one person's standards meeting the other's daily habits. The composite version describes the routines the two of you invent together, which can be steadier, or stricter, than anything either of you would keep alone.

Laid side by side, the neighbours make a week: [composite Saturn in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-5th-house/) is the bond's weekend, [composite Saturn in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-7th-house/) its appointments with the wider world, and the 6th the working days in between.

The hard part is a relationship that turns into a to-do list. Work can crowd out tenderness, the division of labour can become unequal and stay that way, and rest starts to look like slacking. It helps to keep the shared tasks visible, so that nobody's work disappears; to take days off together that have no tasks in them at all; and to thank each other for the dull jobs, which in this bond are a form of love.

Composites have no place in my own readings; this is the technique as its practitioners describe it, always read beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/).
