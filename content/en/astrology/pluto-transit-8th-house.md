Some houses are crossed lightly. The 8th is not one of them: it is where people keep what they share with very few, and Pluto, more than any other planet, belongs to that territory.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is depth, power and change, the part of life that happens below the waterline. The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is what you merge with other people: intimacy and sexuality, trust, joint money and debts, what you inherit in every sense, and the crises that change you. It is the natural house of Scorpio, whose modern ruler is Pluto and whose traditional ruler is Mars; I read both, and either way the 8th's themes sit close to Pluto's own. Over the years of this stay, closeness tends to ask for more honesty: who you let in, what you hide, what you are afraid to depend on. Shared arrangements, a joint account, a family estate, a debt, an unspoken deal about who owes whom, may come up for review. And experiences that once left you guarded can be looked at again, with more understanding than you had then.

A natal Moon or Venus in the 8th meets Pluto on the way, and either one is asked whether it can be close without keeping a part back.

Tradition named the 8th the house of death, and this transit is often written about in that shadow. I don't read death in a chart, ever ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and Pluto here forecasts no loss, of people or of money. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this one describes how you share and how much you trust, and gives you many years to change both.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the 8th, like the 7th, holds Pluto longer in the middle than any other house: about 24 years, compared with about 20 for all twelve, and most stays fall between about 10 and 36. At New York's latitude the middle is about 23 years, most often between about 12 and 34.

Both cusps of your 8th come from your birth time, the one that starts the stay and the one that ends it; without the time I can't say when Pluto arrives, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Pluto comes here after its long work on your partnerships ([Pluto transiting the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-7th-house/)) and moves on to the house of belief, study and distant places ([Pluto transiting the 9th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-9th-house/)).

In the sky, the sign that matters from now on is Aquarius. Pluto entered it in March 2023, stopped crossing back into Capricorn in November 2024, and is still there in the last month of my count, December 2039. If your 8th cusp is in Aquarius, this is your stay, now or soon, and the cusp's degree tells you the year. If it is in Capricorn, the crossing came between January 2008 and November 2024. If it is in Pisces, it falls after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let trust be built slowly and on evidence. Pluto in the 8th asks you neither to hand over everything nor to hold everything back, but to know what you are sharing and with whom.

Put shared arrangements in writing where you can. Clarity about joint money, debts and promises takes the charge out of them, and that is the kind of work the 8th rewards.

If old experiences come back to mind, give them time and good company. This house holds what changed you, and you don't have to revisit it alone.
