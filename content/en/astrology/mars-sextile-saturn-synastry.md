A training plan only works if someone sticks to it, and a person with energy to burn rarely has one. Mars sextile Saturn in synastry puts one person's Mars about 60° from the other's Saturn, and lets energy and method find each other, quietly, whenever one of them asks.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the drive to begin and keep going: initiative, effort, competition, assertion. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the urge to build things that hold: responsibility, discipline, limits, long-term order. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers two functions a cooperation that responds when called on, and only then. As with any of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the opportunity is held between two people, and either can open it.

One person's Mars in Scorpio with the other's Saturn in Virgo, water and earth: a determined, private kind of effort meets a careful method. Mars rules Scorpio by tradition and Pluto by the modern reckoning, and I read both; here the old ruler is at home, and it digs in. The Mars person settles into a long job; the Saturn person knows the order to do it in. A thesis, a long renovation, the restoration of an old boat: these are the sort of projects where this sextile shows.

If the Saturn is yours, you get to see your method used by someone who'll actually carry it out, which can be oddly moving. If the Mars is yours, you get structure without feeling caged: a schedule you didn't have to invent and don't resent keeping.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The offer in this sextile waits to be taken, and it waits politely. Advice that would save the Mars person a month sits unspoken in the other's head; energy that would rescue the Saturn person's stalled plan goes into something else. Nothing goes wrong, which is exactly why the help goes unused.

There's a subtler snag: the arrangement can harden into one-way help. If the Saturn person always supplies the method and the Mars person always the effort, the Saturn person may never get to act on their own plans, and the Mars person may never learn to plan. Easy roles are still roles.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask, and offer. If the Mars is yours, bring the other in at the planning stage rather than when you're stuck; if the Saturn is yours, offer one piece of structure and then let it go. Swap now and then: let the Saturn person start something and the Mars person schedule it. Between friends, this might be a weekly run kept up for years; in a family, the relative who organises a move and the one who carries the boxes; at work, a colleague who sets the milestones and one who meets them.

With 3.6° as the orb, about 1 pair of people in 13 has this sextile, one way round or the other. Mars covers about half a degree a day, so it passes through an orb this narrow in a couple of weeks; a few hours hardly move it, but they decide the houses, which is where I start ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

One chart can carry it as [Mars sextile Saturn](/astrology/mars-sextile-saturn/), a person's own drive and discipline on easy terms. The pair's other angles between two people:

- **[Mars conjunct Saturn in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-saturn-synastry/):** drive and structure on the same point, closer and tighter.
- **[Mars square Saturn in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-saturn-synastry/):** the offer turns into friction over timing, which teaches it.
- **[Mars trine Saturn in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-saturn-synastry/):** the same cooperation as a default, in one element.
- **[Mars opposite Saturn in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-saturn-synastry/):** drive and structure facing each other, balanced in turns.
