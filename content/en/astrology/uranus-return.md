Of the outer planets, Uranus is the only one whose return fits inside a lifetime: Neptune needs about 165 years to come round, Pluto about 248. Uranus needs about 84, and the Uranus return is the moment it reaches the degree it held when you were born.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) goes round the Sun in about 84 years, and so round the zodiac. Its return is transiting Uranus conjunct its own natal degree: the end of one full cycle and, for anyone still counting, the start of the next. Three turning points come before it. The first square fell between 18.4 and 22.9 in the site's count for births from 1930 to 2009 ([transiting Uranus square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-uranus/)), the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) between 38.9 and 44.5, and the second square comes in the sixties.

The opposition's age moves with the birth year because Uranus doesn't travel at an even pace round its orbit. A full lap evens that out: wherever the lap begins, it takes the same time, so the return comes at about 84 for everyone.

Uranus spends about seven years in each sign, so everyone born in the same seven or so years shares a Uranus sign and meets the return in the same stretch of sky. In the dates this site keeps, Uranus crossed Gemini from August 1941 to June 1949 and came back to it in July 2025, for a stay that runs to May 2033: people born in the 1940s with Uranus in Gemini are meeting their returns now ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)). Those born with Uranus in Cancer, between 1948 and 1956, meet theirs between 2032 and 2040.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

A Uranus transit to one degree stays in orb about twenty months with the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and the yearly retrograde loop usually brings three exact passes ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). The return works like any transit of a planet over its own place: the moving Uranus repeats, on its birth degree, everything natal Uranus does in the chart, its sign, its house and the aspects it makes to your other planets. If Uranus squared your Moon when you were born, the returning Uranus squares your Moon again.

Other long cycles tend to gather around it. Jupiter's seventh return comes between about 82 and 84 ([Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/)), so the two often fall within a year or two of each other. Set side by side, the cycles show the scale of this one: Venus repeats its pattern with the Sun about every eight years, the round behind the [Venus star point](/astrology/venus-star-point/); Jupiter comes back every twelve; Uranus needs eighty-four.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In my readings Uranus stands for difference: the part of you that won't fit a pattern just because the pattern is there, and the freedom to reorganise what has stopped working. Its return brings that function back to the place your chart first gave it. I read it as a late review of how that freedom has been used: where you went your own way, where you didn't, and what still feels yours to change. The system I built for my readings also names a blind spot that suits this season well: underestimating the value of continuity. At 84, what has lasted deserves as much attention as what could still change.

Natal Uranus's house says where in your life that review sits, and the house needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). [My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) checked natal Uranus with every other placement, and it wasn't among the three that held far more often, so I read its return as one theme among several, beside the [solar return](/astrology/solar-return/) of the same year and the other transits. It is weather, not a forecast.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Few people ever have a Uranus return, so it doesn't matter.”** Everyone who lives to about 84 meets it, and its earlier turns, the squares and the opposition, come to everyone much sooner.
- **“The Uranus return brings sudden upheaval.”** In the system I read with, Uranus isn't chaos or rebellion. The return asks what still needs room, and the answer may well be very little.
- **“It says how long you have left.”** No transit can say how long anyone will live, and I never read one that way ([Can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)).
- **“Everyone's return is the same, since everyone is 84.”** The age is shared; the sign, house and aspects of your Uranus are yours.
