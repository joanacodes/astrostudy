Moon sextile Lilith sets the Moon about 60° from the far point of its own orbit, a friendly distance. Your need for comfort and the part of you that won't be tamed are on speaking terms: when you let the second into your private life, it tends to steady you rather than shake you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is where comfort comes from: attachment, felt security, nurturing, whatever soothes you. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), a point astrologers interpret in more than one way, stands in my readings for the instincts you keep for yourself: autonomy, refusal, the things you were taught to tuck away. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperation, each ready to help the other while staying itself.

In practical terms, the parts of you that don't fit polite expectations can become a source of comfort rather than a secret. A blunt word, an unconventional way of resting, a need you'd normally keep to yourself: when you allow them, you tend to feel more at home in your own life. The sextile doesn't insist. Like every sextile, it waits to be used, and its help grows each time you stop editing yourself for the sake of ease.

Its risk is simply leaving the door shut. An opening this gentle can go years unused, especially if you learned early that some parts of you were better hidden.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

At home and in close relationships, this sextile tends to let a less tamed side of you be seen without drama. It usually joins fire with air or earth with water: with Lilith in a fire sign and the Moon in an air sign, your boldness finds words; with Lilith in an earth sign and the Moon in a water sign, your instincts find practical, bodily forms of comfort.

The sextile turns up in about 1 person in 11, within 8°. At this angle the Moon is still on the far side of its orbit, measured from Lilith's average position. Mean Lilith moves slowly enough that its sign is there without a birth time; the Moon's, on the day it changes sign, isn't, and the houses never are ([What changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The Moon's other meetings with Lilith:

- **[Moon conjunct Lilith](/astrology/moon-conjunct-lilith/):** comfort and autonomy fuse, and you feel safe only when nothing has to be hidden.
- **[Moon square Lilith](/astrology/moon-square-lilith/):** belonging and authenticity pull against each other, and you learn to keep both.
- **[Moon trine Lilith](/astrology/moon-trine-lilith/):** instinct and comfort move together, with less effort still.
- **[Moon opposite Lilith](/astrology/moon-opposite-lilith/):** the untamed part is met in others, across the chart, before it's claimed.

A sextile between one person's Moon and another's Lilith, [Moon sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/moon-sextile-lilith-synastry/), tends to make honesty easier between them: each can show the other a less polished side and be met kindly.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Its sextile to your natal Moon, made by transiting mean Lilith, lasts only a couple of weeks, since Lilith takes about nine months over each sign: a short window in which saying what you need, plainly, comes easier. If you were born with the sextile, every Lilith return, about every nine years, recreates it. And twice a month, for a few hours, the Moon itself sextiles your natal Lilith.

These, too, are weather, not a forecast: small openings, with no outcome attached.
