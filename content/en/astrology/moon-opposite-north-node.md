Moon opposite North Node is the Moon on the South Node: at your birth it was crossing the Sun's path heading south, at the end of the nodal axis astrologers read as familiar ground. The North Node sits across the chart, where growth is asked for.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) holds what you feel and need: attachment, care, habit, feeling secure. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the capacity you're still developing, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) the one you already have, practised early and used without thinking. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates the Moon and the North Node through polarity: each keeps its identity, and awareness grows from holding both.

In plain words, emotional know-how is where you start. You may have known early how to read a room, comfort someone, keep a home or a friendship steady; feelings are well-trodden ground for you. The North Node, across the chart, asks for something less practised, and the opposition sets the two like the ends of a balance. When the new feels awkward, familiar comfort is right there, and it's tempting to go home.

I don't read that as something to cure. Growth on the North Node doesn't ask you to give up what the Moon knows; it asks you to carry it across. People with this opposition often grow best once they stop treating their feelings as the thing to get past, and start using them as the tools they are.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Moon's sign shows the kind of comfort that comes easily, and the North Node's sign, opposite, the quality it balances: a Moon in Cancer with the North Node in Capricorn knows how to care, and is learning to take up a place in the world beyond the family. In the houses, the Moon's is the area you go back to under pressure.

About 1 person in 23 is born with the Moon within 8° of this opposition. Like the conjunction, it's a once-a-month event, and the Moon, at about 13° a day, holds it for little more than a day, so a few hours of birth time decide the orb. The mean and true node differ by a degree or two, and I check both. In a reading, I start from what this Moon already does well, then look across the chart ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

How the pair's other aspects compare:

- **[Moon conjunct North Node](/astrology/moon-conjunct-north-node/):** the Moon at the other end of the axis, where needs feel new rather than well known.
- **[Moon sextile North Node](/astrology/moon-sextile-north-node/):** a trine to the South Node instead of a conjunction; the familiar supports you without holding you in place.
- **[Moon square North Node](/astrology/moon-square-north-node/):** the Moon equidistant from both ends, asked to come along both ways rather than settle at one.
- **[Moon trine North Node](/astrology/moon-trine-north-node/):** a sextile to the South Node; the familiar opens a door, and the ease leans forward.

Between two charts, [Moon opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/moon-opposite-north-node-synastry/) lays one person's Moon on the other's South Node: a comfort that feels deeply familiar, sometimes so familiar that neither of you moves. I look at it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-moon/) puts the transiting South Node on your Moon for a month or two, about every 18.6 years. With this opposition at birth, your [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) (about 18½, 37 and 56) repeats it; halfway between, near 9, 28 and 46, it's the transiting North Node that crosses your Moon itself. The first tends to show what has become habit, the second asks your needs to step forward. Each is weather, not a forecast.
