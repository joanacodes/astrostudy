In the middle of Aquarius, the two ways of ruling the decans agree on Mercury, and nowhere else in the zodiac do they agree on it. The planet of thought and talk gets a double hold on the sign of ideas.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

The [Chaldean order](/astrology/chaldean-order/) passes from Venus ([Aquarius, first decan](/astrology/aquarius-first-decan/)) to Mercury, so the middle face of [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) is Mercury's ([faces](/astrology/faces/)). The modern decans, counting round the air signs, reach Gemini for this middle third, and Gemini belongs to Mercury. A [Mercury in Aquarius](/astrology/mercury-in-aquarius/) between 10° and 20° is therefore in its own face and its own modern decan at once. The face after it belongs to the Moon ([Aquarius, third decan](/astrology/aquarius-third-decan/)).

The sign keeps its two rulers, Saturn in the tradition and Uranus in modern tropical astrology, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). The decan only adds the planet of thought and exchange to a sign already given to ideas. Gemini, the sign the modern scheme borrows here, is where Mercury's curiosity stays close to home; in Aquarius the same curiosity turns towards systems, networks and groups. Mercury was among the placements that held about a third of the time in my notebook ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)), so I treat its double rule here as a refinement, not a headline.

## Its image {#its-image}

Wallis Budge's Egyptian name for these degrees, Kher-khept-sert, closes the run of three names built on sert that began at the end of Capricorn ([Capricorn, third decan](/astrology/capricorn-third-decan/)). The Greek-Egyptian lists have Xu; the Hermetic texts, Sosomo in Greek and Luxois in Latin. [Firmicus Maternus](/astrology/firmicus-maternus/) gave Cratero or Astiro, and the second name was still Astiro, letter for letter, in Joseph Scaliger's list twelve centuries later: of the three sert decans, it is the only one whose name reached Scaliger unchanged. Vucula is Athanasius Kircher's name for it, in 1652.

No picture comes down with any of these names that I can vouch for. What survives is the chain itself: one Egyptian decan, written down under different names by the lists that copied it, still holding its place among the thirty-six.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun is here from about 30 January to 8 February, the middle of the [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) season. I read Mercury's double rule as an Aquarius that thinks out loud: ideas for the group, tested in conversation, and changed when a better argument arrives. Mercury itself, placed here, is in its own face and its own decan, and I read it as a mind at ease with abstraction: the system behind the detail, the argument behind the argument.

[Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/) in its middle decan takes about 23 minutes to rise in London and 24 in Paris, so the Ascendant spends less than half an hour here, and a birth time rounded to the nearest half-hour can't be trusted to place it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Uranus in Aquarius](/astrology/uranus-in-aquarius/) crossed these degrees between early 1998 and the start of 2001, and [Neptune in Aquarius](/astrology/neptune-in-aquarius/) between 2002 and 2007. Pluto reaches them in 2029 and will stay until about 2037.
