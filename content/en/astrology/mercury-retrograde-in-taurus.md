Mercury’s retrogrades in Taurus are the most regular in the dates I use: each lasts just under 24 days, give or take a few hours, and all of them fall between early April and early June. They turn back through the sign that likes a question settled, and reopen a few.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) is fixed earth, ruled by Venus, and Mercury there thinks deliberately and keeps what it concludes. When [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) comes to this sign, the planet seems from Earth to halt and slip back through it, and that thoroughness turns on its own conclusions: the opinion you’ve held for years, the arrangement everyone treats as decided, the words repeated so often that nobody hears them any more. Mercury holds no dignity in Taurus, and I read its retrograde there as a slow re-reading rather than an upheaval, at the pace of a sign that wants things to last.

Thirteen retrogrades touch Taurus from 2000 to 2040, six of them wholly within it. From 21 April to 15 May 2023, Mercury went from 15°37′ back to 5°50′ Taurus; from 1 to 25 May 2029, it goes from 26°40′ back to 17°33′. A year after that the pattern shifts: from 13 April to 6 May 2030, it stations at 7°47′ Taurus and backs into Aries ([Mercury retrograde in Aries](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-aries/)), to 27°26′. In 2009 and 2022 it came the other way, stationing in Gemini ([Mercury retrograde in Gemini](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-gemini/)) and turning direct in the second half of Taurus, and it will again in 2035.

For anyone born during one of these weeks, Mercury retrograde in Taurus is part of the birth chart for life, a mind that checks its foundations before it speaks; [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/) is where I write about that rhythm.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

These weeks cover only about 9° to 11° of the zodiac, so in Placidus, the house system I use, they often stay inside the one house Taurus occupies. Which house that is, [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) decides: the cusps shift from minute to minute, and without the time I can’t draw them, which is why I don’t read a chart without it.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/taurus-rising/):** Taurus rising: your calm, steady way of meeting people; something you’ve always said about yourself, heard again with fresh ears.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/taurus-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** Taurus in its natural house: what you value; a long-held view of what’s worth keeping, examined again.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/taurus-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** your deliberate way of talking and learning; a lesson you thought you knew by heart, gone over once more.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a fixed point; household arrangements nobody has questioned in years come back into the conversation.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/taurus-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasures and things made by hand; an unfinished piece of work asks for your hands again.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/):** routines that have run for years; a method you never question, gently questioned.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Taurus on the Descendant, with Scorpio rising: a steady partnership; an understanding long taken as read, talked through again.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/taurus-on-8th-house-cusp/):** trust built slowly and what is shared; an old agreement about what belongs to whom, revisited.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/taurus-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs grounded in experience; a conviction you once tested for yourself, put to the test again.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career built steadily; the long plan reread before its next stage.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/taurus-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a few long friendships; plans made years ago with friends, ready for a second look.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/taurus-on-12th-house-cusp/):** quiet habits of thought; the private reasons you hold on to things, looked at in your own time.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the review the time Taurus likes: one subject at a time, looked at properly, rather than everything reopened at once. Venus rules Taurus, so the house Venus occupies in your birth chart shows where the matters under review touch what you value most.

If something long settled comes up again, that isn’t proof it was wrong; Taurus trusts a conclusion that has survived a second look. Kept or changed, a view you’ve examined twice stands on firmer ground than one you never questioned.
