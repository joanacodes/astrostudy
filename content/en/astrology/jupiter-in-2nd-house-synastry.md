Their Jupiter in your 2nd house brings another person's generosity to what you own, earn and value. Around them, your sense of what is enough, and of what you're worth, tends to widen.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) enlarges: it adds meaning, room and trust to whatever it touches. [The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) covers your resources and values: what you earn and keep, the things you care for, the talents you rely on and the self-worth underneath them. Through this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their generosity meets the ground of your security.

With them, you may value yourself more generously. They notice what you're good at and name its worth, encourage you to spend time on a skill, or share what they have without making you feel small. Your relationship with possessions can loosen too: less clutching, and more confidence in the way you look after what's yours. Time and attention count among the things they give, not only objects.

For them, your values can be a place where their generosity feels well used. They may enjoy giving to you, sharing what they know about managing things, or simply admiring the care you take of what belongs to you. The 2nd is succedent, a house where things gather and last, and its natural sign, [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), belongs to Venus and loves comfort and steadiness: their breadth meets your sense of what is solid.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Gifts can carry weight. Their generosity may leave you feeling indebted, or their ideas about what you should do with your resources, bigger, bolder, more, may sit badly with your own sense of enough. Different values about spending and saving can surface fast, and so can the question of who owes whom.

From their side, your caution can feel like doubt, of their ideas or of their good faith. They may also feel that their giving goes unreturned, or that you hold on to things they'd happily let go.

No chart says whether money will come, to either of you, which is why I never look for it ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). Their Jupiter here describes generosity and values meeting, not wealth: resources and worth are not the same thing as a balance.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Say what is a gift and what is a loan. Generosity stays light when both of you know which is which, and when you're free to say "thank you, but no". Gratitude is easier to carry when it can be returned in kind: a meal, a skill, an hour. In a couple, talk about what "enough" means to each of you before planning big purchases; between friends, keep presents in proportion so that neither feels in debt; between colleagues, share resources and know-how with clear credit; between adult relatives, family generosity is easier when expectations are spoken.

In reverse, your Jupiter sits in their 2nd house, and your generosity widens what they value; the same care about gifts and loans applies. [Their Jupiter in your 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-in-1st-house-synastry/) widens how you show up; [their Jupiter in your 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-in-3rd-house-synastry/) fills your everyday talk with ideas.

Their birth year and month, far more than the hour, place their Jupiter, which spends about a year in each sign. Your own birth time is what draws the line where your 2nd begins in Placidus, the house system I use; only a Jupiter lying on that cusp would need their time as well, and the rest of the synastry needs it anyway ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). What two people give each other is theirs to talk about, and so is this reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
