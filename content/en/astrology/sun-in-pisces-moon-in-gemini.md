Pisces thinks in images and Gemini in words, and your lights sit a quarter of the zodiac apart, Pisces holding the Sun and Gemini the Moon. You were born when the evening Moon was close to half lit, a week or so into the lunar month. The pair reads to me as an imagination that wants to be talked over.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and mutable air, three signs apart: a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign, but between two adaptable signs, so the friction tends to bend rather than lock. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) gathers itself around what it feels for and imagines; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) settles by sorting a feeling into words and trying it out on someone else. You may sense the whole mood of a situation at once and then need an hour of conversation to find out what you actually think.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), ruler of Gemini, keeps company with the Sun, never more than about 28° from it, so with your Sun in Pisces it stands in Aquarius, Pisces or Aries, and in Pisces tradition gives it both its detriment and its fall. The planet your Moon relies on for words may be working where words come hardest, and I read that as a Moon that reaches for a sentence and sometimes finds an image instead: both count. Pisces has two rulers, and I read both, [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) for where your imagination runs freest and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) for where it finds its meaning; Jupiter, for its part, is in its detriment in Gemini. Neither light has a dignity in its sign.

The Moon was 60° to 119° ahead of the Sun, five to almost ten days after the new moon, and these births divide evenly between the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) and the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/): your hour of birth puts you on one side or the other. Near 90° there is a waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/), friction that pushes outward, into doing, and between two mutable signs it tends to show as many small adjustments rather than one big clash. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) marks where the crescent ends and the first quarter begins.

In the count, about 1 person in 143 has a Pisces Sun with a Gemini Moon, among the more frequent of the Pisces pairs. The Moon reaches Gemini from Taurus and leaves for Cancer about two and a half days later; on either of those days the hour decides between this page, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-taurus/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-cancer/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)), just as it decides the phase. It also gives the house of Mercury, which tells me where your feelings go looking for words.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Conversation and tenderness both matter to you, and you need a partner who offers both. The Gemini Moon wants to talk things through, trade news, ask and be asked; the Pisces Sun wants to be understood without having to explain everything. You may say a great deal and still feel unheard, or understand a partner perfectly and struggle to say so. Tell them which kind of closeness you need tonight, words or quiet; the right one changes.

## At work {#at-work}

Writing, translation, teaching, counselling, journalism, storytelling in any form: work that carries feeling into words suits you. The Pisces Sun brings empathy and imagination; the Gemini Moon brings curiosity and the gift of explaining. Both signs are mutable, so variety helps and rigid routine wears on you, though a little structure keeps the many threads from tangling.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two mutable signs at right angles can scatter: too many moods, too many options, too many half-finished conversations, and a feeling that changes shape before you can name it. The square asks you to act, and action helps here: write it down, choose one thing, finish it. A notebook kept by the bed, for the images and the words alike, can do more than it sounds.
