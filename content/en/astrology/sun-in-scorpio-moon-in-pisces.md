Your Sun in Scorpio feels one thing all the way down; your Moon in Pisces feels everything in the room. Both lights are in water, a trine apart, and at your birth the Moon was gibbous to the eye and still growing, with a full moon a few days off.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Scorpio and Pisces are both water signs, one fixed, one mutable, and four signs apart they [trine](/astrology/trine/) each other in a shared element. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) gathers its feeling into a few bonds and keeps it there for years; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) is porous: it takes the weather of a room into itself, imagines its way into other lives, and needs quiet to sort out what is its own. What the Sun concentrates, the Moon spreads: you may love a handful of people with great intensity and still be moved by strangers in the news.

In modern astrology your lights answer to the two slowest planets, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) for the Sun and [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) for the Moon; in the older books, to [Mars](/astrology/mars/) and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/). All four count in my reading. Mars shows what you do with a feeling, and Pluto where feeling transforms you; Jupiter shows where your feeling finds meaning and room to grow, and Neptune where it reaches beyond you, into art, care or the feeling of belonging to something wider. By the old rules the Moon is neither strengthened nor weakened in Pisces, but [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is exalted there, so its sign and house say something about how this Moon gives.

By your birth the Moon had travelled 91° to 149° beyond the Sun, 7½ to 12 days into the lunar month: past the half and still filling. Rudhyar's [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) phase holds the bulk of these births, roughly seven in eight, and the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) the last few; around 120° the trine of signs turns into a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). The full moon followed between three days and a week later, usually a [full moon in Taurus](/astrology/full-moon-in-taurus/) opposite your Sun: depending on the year, the [Hunter's moon](/astrology/hunters-moon/), the [Beaver moon](/astrology/beaver-moon/), or both at once. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) has more on the first quarter phase.

Roughly 1 person in 146 has the lights in these two water signs. A birth on a day the Moon was changing sign might give an Aquarius or an Aries Moon instead, and then your page is the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-aquarius/) or the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-aries/); the birth time decides which ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Your rising sign and houses hang on that time as well: they show the parts of your life where all this feeling has to go.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with your whole self, and you may sense a partner's mood almost before they do. The Scorpio Sun needs a bond it can trust completely; the Pisces Moon needs tenderness, imagination and room to withdraw when the world gets loud. Someone gentle and steady suits both lights. What helps most is telling which feelings are yours and which you have picked up: in a shared mood, you may not know at first.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that asks for depth and compassion together suits you: therapy, medicine, nursing, music, film, the study of the mind, rescue and care, any work with people at their most vulnerable. The Scorpio Sun can stay with what is hard; the Pisces Moon brings imagination and an instinct for what someone needs. You may need quiet afterwards more than most, and a firm line at the end of each working day.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two water signs can take in more than they can hold. The Scorpio Sun takes a feeling all the way down and keeps it; the Pisces Moon takes in the feelings around it, and together they can leave you carrying moods long after the moment has passed. Give the feeling somewhere to go: a page, music, water itself, a friend who listens without needing you to be fine. And keep a few hours each week that belong to nobody but you.
