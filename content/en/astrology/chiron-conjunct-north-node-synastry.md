Chiron conjunct North Node in synastry sets one person's Chiron down on the other's North Node, within 3°, so that it also faces their South Node across the chart. What one of you worked out the hard way sits exactly where the other is trying something new. It's one of the few contacts where a difficulty can double as a map.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the adaptive part of a chart: around a limitation that won't go, a person changes the method, the pace or the tool, and keeps taking part. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity that has to be built by repetition rather than found ready-made, and its partner, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), sits opposite with whatever already comes easily. Under a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), two functions run as one process, and as one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), that process is shared out between two lives.

If the Chiron is yours, the methods you built around a difficulty sit right on the direction the other person is learning. Without trying, you become a working example of how to pace something, ask for help or keep going when the usual route is closed.

Carrying the North Node, you may find the other person showing you, in how they cope, part of the capacity your own chart asks you to practise. You pick up their tools before you notice. Because their Chiron also opposes your South Node, their way can feel foreign to everything you find easy, which is exactly why it teaches.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

At this distance, growth and struggle can blur. The node person may admire the other's coping so much that taking on a difficulty starts to look like progress, but unfamiliarity isn't a virtue, and nobody's growth requires borrowing someone else's pain. The Chiron person, for their part, may feel their limitation has quietly become someone else's syllabus.

- **[Chiron sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** a useful tip offered from two signs away, easy on the other's familiar ground.
- **[Chiron square North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-square-north-node-synastry/):** the difficulty set across the other's path instead of along it.
- **[Chiron trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-trine-north-node-synastry/):** experience flowing towards the other's growth without sitting on top of it.
- **[Chiron opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/chiron-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** the Chiron landing on old, familiar ground rather than on the new direction.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Offer, don't prescribe. If the Chiron is yours, share how you manage when asked, and let the other keep only what fits their life; if the North Node is yours, take what helps and say what doesn't, without guilt. Between a mentor and a younger colleague this can be generous; between a parent and an adult child, or friends, it works best when both remember whose difficulty it is.

The dates come from the true node. A Chiron in Gemini, for someone born in mid-1985, has the Gemini North Node of February and March 1984 births right on it. The node circles backwards in about 18.6 years, so that same Chiron also meets the North Node of people born from April to September 1965 and from August 2002 to January 2003: a parent's era, a child's era. The other way round, the Taurus Chiron of people born in spells of 1980 to 1982 rests on the 1985 North Node. I look at the mean node too, which strays from the true one by a degree or two at most, and say when the two disagree at the edge of the orb.

With so many births sharing it, this conjunction stays out of the contacts I tally in a synastry, a tally kept for the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant and the Midheaven. Its houses are where I look: one generation's way of coping lying across another's way forward. They need both birth times, even though neither point shifts much in a day ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

With [Chiron conjunct North Node](/astrology/chiron-conjunct-north-node/) inside one chart, a person grows out of their own work-arounds. Here the work-around belongs to one person and the growth to the other, so I look at it only with both on board ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
