A new moon gives the darkest nights of the lunar month, and Scorpio is the sign that goes looking under the surface. When the two meet, once a year between late October and late November, a month begins out of sight: in what you trust, what you share, and what you're ready to stop pretending about.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

The [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) puts the Moon at the Sun's degree, lost in its glare, and the cycle of phases starts over. In [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), a fixed water sign, that start is slow and serious. Scorpio holds on to what matters and wants the truth rather than the surface, and its month often opens on a conversation about trust, a debt or a promise looked at squarely, or a feeling you've carried long enough to know whether to keep it.

Tradition puts the Moon in its fall in Scorpio. I read that as feeling that runs deep and stays private, not as damage: the month may begin quietly, with more going on inside than shows. Scorpio has two rulers, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) in modern tropical astrology and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) in the tradition, and I read both. Pluto's house in your chart shows where you're asked to transform something; Mars's, where you find the nerve to begin.

None of this happens on cue. A new moon earns a place in a reading only when it falls within about 1° of a planet or angle of yours while something slower is already at work there (a transit or progression, a solar arc, an eclipse). Then it can bring that into focus for a few days. On its own, it forecasts nothing.

The next falls on 9 November 2026 at 07:02 UT, at 16°53′ Scorpio. After it come 29 October 2027 at 5°54′, 16 November 2028 at 24°43′, 6 November 2029 at 14°02′ and 26 October 2030 at 3°27′. None of the five is an eclipse.

Two weeks before the 2026 one, on 26 October, the [full moon in Taurus](/astrology/full-moon-in-taurus/) also had the Sun in Scorpio; in 2027 the order flips, and the full moon follows on 14 November. The [new moon in Libra](/astrology/new-moon-in-libra/) comes a month earlier, the [new moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/new-moon-in-sagittarius/) a month later.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Your birth time decides the house, and a few minutes can move a cusp by a degree ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With Placidus houses, which I use, the new moon falls in the house that contains its degree: most often the one with Scorpio on its cusp, though if that cusp is at 20° Scorpio, the 16°53′ new moon of 2026 still belongs to the house before.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Scorpio rising: a more deliberate start in how you meet the world, letting people see a little of what you usually keep back.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** what you own and what owns you: a new way of holding on to money or things, and what you're ready to release.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** the conversation you've avoided, finally begun; questions asked to reach the truth rather than keep the peace.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** family history: something old brought into the open, a clear-out that goes deeper than the cupboards.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** love and creativity with nothing held back: a passion committed to fully, a work made from what you've lived through.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** work that asks for focus: one project taken on in depth, a routine stripped down to what really sustains you.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** a partnership begins again at a deeper level: trust renegotiated, loyalty spelled out, power shared more evenly.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** Scorpio's own house: a fresh start in what you share and owe, money and intimacy alike, nothing swept under the rug.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a belief questioned all the way down; study of the side of things others avoid, from psychology to history.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** a public role built on resolve: the person who handles what others can't, the job taken on in a crisis.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** friendships tested for depth: a smaller, truer circle, or a group bound by a cause you feel strongly about.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a private reckoning: what you've kept to yourself, looked at in quiet, with a professional's help if that's the right door.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Look for the degree.** Does anything of yours sit within a degree of one of those five positions? If not, the month starts quietly in one house, and that's all.
- **Read both rulers.** The houses of Pluto and Mars show where a Scorpio beginning gathers its force.
- **One honest thing.** Scorpio prefers one deep change to several shallow ones.
- **No talk of endings.** Older books tie Scorpio and its house to death. I don't read death in a chart, ever ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)): if this new moon asks you to end anything, it's a pretence.
