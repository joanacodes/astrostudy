Pisces gives your Sun its imagination; Taurus, where tradition exalts the Moon, gives your feelings their footing. The month was only days old when you arrived, its crescent still slim in the dusk. The pair asks a question I find lovely: how can a dream be made to last?

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and fixed earth, two signs apart, make a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) by sign, water and earth supporting each other. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) takes its shape from what it is devoted to and what it imagines; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) settles once something is steady enough to rely on: a routine, a place, a person who stays. Your imagination ranges widely, and your needs keep coming back to what can be held and kept.

In Ptolemy's scheme Taurus is the Moon's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), and I read feelings that settle well and hold steady there, not a better heart, while the Pisces Sun holds no dignity at all. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) holds two titles in this chart, ruler of your Moon's sign and, in the same tradition, exalted in your Sun's; where it sits by sign and house shows what you value and where you go to feel safe. As for Pisces, I read both of its rulers: [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), in modern tropical astrology, marking where you are most open to others and to images, and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), in the tradition, marking where that openness finds a purpose.

The Moon was 31° to 89° ahead of the Sun, two and a half to seven days into the lunar month. Seven in eight of these births are [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) births, the rest [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) ones, and around 60° there is a waxing [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/) in the chart, an opening that pays off when you use it. I read the crescent as needs beginning to make their own way, and a Taurus Moon makes its way slowly and surely. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the boundary between the two phases.

On the day the Moon crosses into Taurus, or leaves it for Gemini, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-aries/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-gemini/) are just as possible, and only the hour can settle it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)); across the years counted, about 1 person in 145 lands on this page. The hour also sets the houses, and so the part of life where the dream is given its form.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with devotion and with steadiness, a combination partners tend to treasure. The Pisces Sun feels its way into another person's world; the Taurus Moon shows love by staying, by turning up, by keeping the small rituals of a shared life going. The risk is staying past the point where staying helps, out of compassion on one side and loyalty on the other. A partner who meets your tenderness with reliability gives both lights what they need.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that turns imagination into something durable suits you: crafts, music practised over years, gardening, design, cooking, care work with a steady rhythm, building something by hand. The Sun brings the vision; the Moon wants it made properly and kept. Constant upheaval unsettles the Moon, and work with no beauty or meaning in it leaves the Sun flat.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Dreams and durability don't always agree. The Pisces Sun can fall for an ideal that the Taurus Moon then has to live with, or the Moon can hold on to something long after the Sun has quietly moved on. Disappointment hits hard when a beautiful plan meets slow reality. What helps is giving a dream a small, solid first form, a weekly practice or a sketch made real, and letting time do what Taurus does best: make it last.
