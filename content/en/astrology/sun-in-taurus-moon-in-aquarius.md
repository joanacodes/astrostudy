Both of these signs are fixed, and each is loyal to something different: the Taurus Sun to what it has, the Aquarius Moon to what it thinks is right. You were born around the last quarter moon, when the Moon comes up near midnight, and I read the pair as a keeper and a reformer sharing one roof.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fixed earth meets fixed air, three signs back from the Sun: a square by sign. The two share staying power and little else. The [Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/) builds a self on continuity, keeping what works and improving it slowly; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) steadies itself by standing back, thinking a feeling through and testing it against a principle, and it needs room to differ. Where the two dig in together, you can be very hard to move, but for two separate reasons, and it helps to know which one is talking.

Your Moon answers to two planets, and I read both. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), its ruler in the old books, gives this Moon its constancy, the reason a friendship or a conviction can last decades; [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), given to Aquarius by modern astrologers, marks where it has to break a habit in order to breathe. Your Sun answers to [Venus](/astrology/venus/), which wants a pleasant, settled life, and how Venus gets on with those two shapes the pair's ordinary days. Neither light holds a dignity in its sign.

At this birth the Sun had a lead of 61° to 120° over the Moon, nineteen and a half to twenty-four and a half days after a new moon: a Moon close to half lit, up from about midnight. Almost exactly half of these births are [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) births, and the other half belong to the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/). Near 90° behind, the square by sign becomes a waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/), which I read as friction settled by revising your own priorities first. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows how the two phases differ.

The count gives this pair to about 1 person in 138. The Moon's stay in Aquarius lasts roughly two and a half days, between Capricorn and Pisces; if it came or went on your birth date, the hour picks between this page, the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-capricorn/) and the [Sun in Taurus, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-taurus-moon-in-pisces/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The hour also sets the rising sign, and with it the houses where these two fixed lights actually live; I won't read a chart until it's known.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

A good partner for you is steady and free-thinking at once, and you offer both in return. The Taurus Sun is loyal through presence: the same table, the same arms, year after year. The Aquarius Moon wants friendship inside love, room for its own opinions, and a partner who won't take a need for space as a verdict. A bond can feel completely secure to you while looking a little cool from outside. When you dig in, tell the other person which loyalty is speaking: the one that wants the familiar, or the one that has decided something matters on principle.

## At work {#at-work}

You can hold a long course and still see where the system ought to change. The Sun gives you patience, a sense of value and the habit of finishing; the Moon gives you independence of mind and a lasting loyalty to colleagues you respect. You may be the person who quietly improves how a place works without disrupting it, or who declines, politely and for years, to do something you think is wrong. Constant upheaval tires the Taurus Sun, and rules nobody can explain wear down the Aquarius Moon.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Square two fixed signs and a disagreement can become a stand-off with yourself. The Taurus Sun resists change because change is costly; the Aquarius Moon resists pressure because pressure feels like being told what to feel. Under strain you can stay in a situation and leave it in spirit at the same time: present in body, absent in feeling. What helps is separating the two loyalties on paper, what you keep for comfort and what you keep on principle, then changing one small thing at the Sun's pace, for reasons the Moon can stand behind.
