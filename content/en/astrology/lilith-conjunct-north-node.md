Lilith conjunct North Node brings together two points of the same orbit: the Moon's farthest point from Earth and the place where its path crosses the Sun's, heading north. In a chart, the direction you grow in runs straight through the part of you that wouldn't be tamed.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's mean apogee, is read by many astrologers as instinctive autonomy, authenticity and what was rejected or left unintegrated; how much it means varies between traditions, and I read it as a symbol. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a less familiar capacity you build through practice and feedback, always paired with the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) and the strengths it holds. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one, each keeping its identity.

In plain words, your growth doesn't ask you to become more acceptable; it asks you to bring back what was left out. The capacity your chart points to includes your instinct, your boundaries, a frankness you once learned to hide, and practising it can feel exposed at first.

Development isn't destiny, and unfamiliarity isn't a virtue in itself. Lilith on the North Node doesn't make defiance your path. It asks that the untamed part take part, a little at a time, with feedback, while the familiar strengths of the South Node stay yours.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The two usually share a sign, and the North Node's sign describes the kind of growth; with Lilith there, the growth includes that sign's particular way of refusing. Mean Lilith moves forward through a sign in about nine months and the nodes move backwards, a sign in about 18 months, so the two close on each other quickly: they meet about every six years, and each conjunction covers a couple of months of births.

The house shows where the growth asks for practice, given a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In the 10th, it might be a public role that grows by letting your frankness show; in the 4th, a home life where the part of you that didn't fit is finally given room.

About 1 person in 28 is born with the two within 6°, counted with the mean Lilith and the true node. Both choices matter: the true Lilith can sit far from the mean ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)), and the mean node can move the orb by a degree or two.

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Lilith sextile North Node](/astrology/lilith-sextile-north-node/):** the untamed part offering openings to growth, with an easy trine to the familiar end.
- **[Lilith square North Node](/astrology/lilith-square-north-node/):** the instinct across the path, fitting neither the old way nor the new.
- **[Lilith trine North Node](/astrology/lilith-trine-north-node/):** the instinct flowing into growth, with a sextile back to the South Node.
- **[Lilith opposite North Node](/astrology/lilith-opposite-north-node/):** Lilith on the South Node, where refusing is old ground.

Between two charts, [Lilith conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) sets one person's untamed point on the other's direction of growth: magnetic, sometimes unsettling, and best explored only when both people want to ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), born with the conjunction, your returns line up. Each Lilith return, about every nine years, brings Lilith back over your North Node for a few weeks, and each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) brings the node back over your Lilith; the second Lilith return, around 18, falls close to the first nodal return, at about 18½.

Every six years or so, Lilith and the North Node also meet in the sky, wherever that falls among your houses. I read it as an echo of your birth angle rather than a transit to it.

Weather, not a forecast: these seasons tend to ask how much of your instinct your growth has room for. The answer is yours to give.
