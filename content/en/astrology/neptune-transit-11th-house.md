Neptune in your 11th house carries ideals into the crowd: the friends you choose, the groups you belong to, and the future you hope to share with them. As a rule it stays about fourteen years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines a shared good: it feels for others and longs for a world more connected than this one. The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is friendship, community, networks and the hopes you pursue with others. With Neptune crossing it, belonging can take on a dreamlike quality. Friends may become companions in an ideal; causes, volunteering or creative circles may draw you; you may long for a community that feels like a calling rather than a club. It's also easier, for a while, to see groups and friends as you hope they are, so part of the work is letting them be people. Neptune is also the planet of collective meaning, and the 11th is where your private hopes join other people's: the sense of being part of something larger can be one of the gifts of these years.

Aquarius, the 11th's sign in the natural order, has Saturn as its old ruler and Uranus as its new one, and I read them side by side ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)); the tradition named the house the Good Spirit. With Venus in your 11th from birth, friendship can turn especially tender and generous, and it helps to let friends be ordinary too.

A [transit](/astrology/transits/) doesn't choose your companions or decide who drifts away. What Neptune brings out is how your chart already belongs and hopes, with more compassion, softer edges and, now and then, the need to look again.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 11th keeps Neptune about fourteen years in the middle for a London birth, in Placidus, a hair above the all-house figure there, with most stays between nearly nine years and about twenty-one. For a New York birth its figures match that latitude's all-house ones exactly: nearly fifteen in the middle, nearly eleven to nearly nineteen.

Its stay opens with the first crossing of your 11th cusp and closes on the last day before your 12th, and nothing in between, retrograde returns included, restarts the count. The degree of that cusp is fixed at your birth, to the minute, and I need that minute before I'll read anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Before it, Neptune spends its years in your 10th, asking what your work is for ([Neptune transiting the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-10th-house/)); after it come its years in your 12th, the house of the inner life and of Neptune's own sign ([Neptune transiting the 12th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-12th-house/)).

In the sky the order is Pisces, Aries, Taurus. Pisces had Neptune from April 2011 to January 2026, Aries has it from January 2026 to March 2039 after a first visit in 2025, and Taurus receives it from May 2038: whichever of those signs is on your 11th cusp tells you which years open your stay.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give your time to one cause you believe in, at a pace you can keep. Neptune in the 11th draws you towards shared ideals, and steady help outlasts a burst of devotion.

Let friends be people. Admiration is a lovely part of friendship; knowing someone's ordinary days is what makes it last.

When a group starts to feel like the answer to everything, ask a friend outside it what they see. A shared dream is strongest when its members can still disagree.
