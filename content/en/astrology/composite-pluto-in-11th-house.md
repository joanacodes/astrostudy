A relationship can keep to itself or gather people around it, and one with a composite Pluto in the 11th house gathers them. Its friendships, groups and causes, and the hopes the two of you hold for the years ahead, are where it changes most.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) holds the relationship's wider circle: the friends it shares, the groups and networks it belongs to, the causes it signs up for, and its plans for the future. That circle is where [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) changes the bond. Astrologers read a pair whose friendships go through deep turns: the old crowd that drifts away after a move, the friend who becomes family, the association the two of you join and end up reshaping, the shared hope, a community garden, a cooperative, a campaign, that changes you both while you work for it.

By the doctrine of [planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/), Jupiter is happiest in this house, which the old astrologers called the Good Spirit; the hopes it holds, in a composite, are the pair's own. They're not one person's handful of deep friendships, the theme of [Pluto in the 11th house](/astrology/pluto-in-11th-house/), nor one person rearranging another's social world, as with [their Pluto in your 11th house](/astrology/pluto-in-11th-house-synastry/). Two people who kept separate friends for years can find that, together, they gather a crowd around them, and that the crowd changes them in return. Often the relationship becomes a meeting point in its own right: the kitchen where friends end up, the pair others call in a crisis. That gift has a cost worth counting, because a circle can ask more of a relationship than it has to give.

Loyalty tests are the danger. A bond this invested in its circle can start sorting friends into those who are with the pair and those who aren't, and cutting the second group out; or it can treat each friend's closeness to one of you as a threat to the other. Pruning isn't the same as growing. It helps to let each of you keep a friend of your own, and to let a friendship fade without turning it into a verdict.

If one of you is being cut off from friends, or kept from seeing them unless the other approves, that is isolation, not a placement, and help comes before any chart: 999 in a UK emergency, and Shout by text on 85258 at any hour, with more on my Disclaimers page ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)).

Composites are drawn for friendships too, and the rules don't relax for them: both exact birth times, without which the houses can't be found, and a stated method, because the midpoints of your two charts' cusps and houses cast from the composite Midheaven where you live can put Pluto either side of the 11th cusp ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Friends who want a composite of their friendship need both yeses, not just the keener one's ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

It sits between a house of full public view ([composite Pluto in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-10th-house/)) and one where nothing is seen ([composite Pluto in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-12th-house/)), and borrows from each: the 11th is public, but among friends.
