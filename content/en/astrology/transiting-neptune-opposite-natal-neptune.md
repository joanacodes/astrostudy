Halfway round its orbit from your birth, Neptune faces the place it started from. The half-circle takes a little over eighty years, so this is the transit of the early eighties, and the last step of the Neptune cycle a life can hold.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Eighty years on, natal [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is less a promise than a record: what you longed for, what you gave, what you forgave. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) puts two ends of one function in view at once. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), Neptune in the sky stands across from where it began, so the dream you have carried all your life can be seen whole, from the outside, and set beside the dreams that came after it.

I read it as a season of perspective. What your generation hoped for, and what you made of those hopes, meets the ideals of the people around you, many of them much younger. Compassion has the most room here: for the person you were in your twenties and at forty, and for those now living their own versions. Neptune's blind spot, confusing compassion with unlimited availability, still applies, and generosity keeps its limits.

Neptune's fog is an image, not a diagnosis. Memory or confusion, at this age or any other, belongs with a doctor, and no transit can foretell it ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). The house the opposition falls in says where the perspective is felt, and that needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If it's a parent's chart you're looking at, ask them first ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Neptune spends about two and a half years facing its birth place, twenty-two months in the briefest passes; three exact hits are usual, and one pass in four or so has five. Neptune needs a little over 80 years for the half-circle: about 82 for births of the 1930s to the 1950s, about 81 for the 1960s and 1970s, and just under 80 from the late 1980s on. Born at the start of 1950, you would meet it exact at 82.4, 82.7 and 83.2.

The site's count follows births from 1930 to 2009 to the age of 80, and about 7 people in 10 haven't met it by then; the earliest, born in the 1990s and 2000s, enter the orb at 79 or 80, the very youngest at 78.8. No one meets it twice. Before it, a life has already held three steps of the cycle: the sextile at 25 to 27 ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-neptune/)), the square at about 40 ([transiting Neptune square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-neptune/), the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/)), and the trine at 52 to 55 ([transiting Neptune trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-neptune/)).

From now until March 2039, with Neptune in Aries, it is the turn of people born with [Neptune in Libra](/astrology/neptune-in-libra/), from 1942 to 1957, in their early eighties. The generation born under the Uranus–Neptune conjunction, from 1990 to 1996, will find [transiting Neptune opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-uranus/) arriving within a few years of this one.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Tell the story of your ideals from the beginning: what you hoped for in your twenties, what loosened around forty, what came easily in your fifties. The opposition shows the whole arc, and saying it out loud is a way of keeping it.

Hand part of the dream on. An opposition works through other people, and at this age that can mean passing a cause, a craft or a family story to someone who will carry it further.

Keep the days concrete: a walk at the same hour, a call made every week. Neptune widens the view, and small fixed points make it a pleasure to look at.
