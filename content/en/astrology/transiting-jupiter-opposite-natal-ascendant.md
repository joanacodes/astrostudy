Six years or so after it crossed your rising degree, Jupiter reaches the opposite point: the degree that was setting in the west when you were born. From there it faces your Ascendant across the whole chart, and what you learn tends to come through someone else.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens, teaches and looks for meaning; the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is how you orient yourself and set out. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets the two at either end of one line, the horizon, so the growth this [transit](/astrology/transits/) describes stands on the other side of you: in a partner, a client, a collaborator, an adviser, even a rival who makes you think. Polarity isn't conflict. You see your own way of starting things more clearly because someone else does it differently.

A planet opposite your Ascendant stands on your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the cusp of the 7th house in Placidus, the system I use, so this crossing opens [Jupiter transiting the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-7th-house/). The year that follows gives the part of life you share with others more room. It doesn't promise a partner, a wedding or a contract, and it doesn't end anything either: what other people decide belongs to them.

Born around sunset, you have the Sun near your Descendant, so this transit and [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-sun/) come close together: a season when who you are and who you're with both get more room.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Jupiter usually needs about eleven days to pass your Descendant; that's seven crossings in ten. In the other three, a retrograde loop takes it back and forth over the point, three exact hits in about eight months ([Jupiter retrograde](/astrology/jupiter-retrograde/)).

The date is only as good as the time on your birth record. A chart drawn for noon because the time is missing has a Descendant too, but it's a placeholder, and so would be any date I gave you for this transit. That's why I wait for the real time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have met it six or seven times, halfway through each of Jupiter's rounds with their rising degree. [Transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) lies about six years behind it and six ahead. On the way here, Jupiter made [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-ascendant/), [transiting Jupiter square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-ascendant/); it makes them again, in reverse order, on the way back.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let someone teach you something this year, and choose them for what they know rather than for how comfortable they are. The opposition grows you through difference.

If a partnership is already in place, give it something to grow into together: a shared project, a trip, a plan with both your names on it. Jupiter on the Descendant favours what two people build over what one asks of the other.

Offer as much as you hope to receive. Jupiter on the Descendant tends to bring generous people closer, and generosity keeps best when it runs both ways.

Watch what you admire in other people. What you see across the table is often a quality of your own, waiting to be used: the far end of your Ascendant is still your chart.
