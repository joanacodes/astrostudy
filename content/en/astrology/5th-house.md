The 5th house is the house of what you do for joy: creativity, pleasure, play and romance. Tradition gave it children too; I read it in an adult's own life, as the part of that life which doesn't need a reason.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 5th house as the part of life you don't have to justify. It covers what you make for the pleasure of making it, the games and hobbies you lose track of time in, the risks you take for the fun of it, flirting and falling in love, and the way you show yourself when nobody's grading you. It's where you find out what you enjoy, as opposed to what you're good at.

Tradition gave the 5th children, and some astrologers still look there for them. I don't. No chart can tell anyone whether or when they'll have a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/): a chart is read for the person it belongs to, once they're an adult and happy for it to be read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#your-child)). What I read in the 5th is your own creativity and pleasure.

- **A succedent house.** It follows an angle, and succedent houses are where things settle and grow: the 5th is what grows once you feel at home. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Leo](/astrology/leo/) and the [Sun](/astrology/sun/): warmth, self-expression, being seen.
- **Good Fortune.** Tradition gave it that name and said [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rejoices here ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): the planet of pleasure, at home in the house of play.
- **Its opposite.** The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), friends and groups, which tradition called Good Spirit, where Jupiter has its joy: the 5th is what you enjoy for yourself and with the people you love, the 11th what you share with many.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), just before it, is home; the 5th is what you do once you feel safe there. The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/), just after it, brings the work back: routines, effort, the care of the body.

In synastry, another person's planets in your 5th house tend to feel like fun and attraction — [their Venus in your 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house-synastry/), for instance ([house overlays](/astrology/house-overlays/)). And like every house, the 5th is placed by your birth time ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 5th house {#planets}

A planet in the 5th house shows in how you play, create and love — and in what makes you feel most like yourself.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), against about a third for most placements. If Saturn, [Chiron in the 5th house](/astrology/chiron-in-5th-house/) or [Lilith in the 5th house](/astrology/lilith-in-5th-house/) sits here, joy is where I'd spend the most time in a reading — and usually the permission to have it.

- **[Sun in the 5th house](/astrology/sun-in-5th-house/):** creation, play and romance light you up; you shine when you make something your own.
- **[Moon in the 5th house](/astrology/moon-in-5th-house/):** you need play to feel safe, and your feelings come out in what you make.
- **[Mercury in the 5th house](/astrology/mercury-in-5th-house/):** playful with words: games, puzzles, writing for pleasure, flirting by message.
- **[Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house/):** in its joy: pleasure comes easily, and so do romance and art.
- **[Mars in the 5th house](/astrology/mars-in-5th-house/):** competitive play, sport and passion; you go after who and what you want.
- **[Jupiter in the 5th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-5th-house/):** big enthusiasms and generous fun; you bring everyone along.
- **[Saturn in the 5th house](/astrology/saturn-in-5th-house/):** joy and play you had to give yourself permission for.
- **[Uranus in the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-in-5th-house/):** unusual hobbies and loves; you need freedom in pleasure.
- **[Neptune in the 5th house](/astrology/neptune-in-5th-house/):** romantic and imaginative; art, music, and falling for what could be.
- **[Pluto in the 5th house](/astrology/pluto-in-5th-house/):** creativity and love that take everything you've got.

No planet here? An [empty 5th house](/astrology/empty-houses/) isn't a life without fun or love. It's read through the sign on its cusp, the place of its ruler, and Venus and the Sun.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the cusp of the 5th house gives your pleasures their style: how you flirt, play and create, and what fun feels like to you.

- **[Aries on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-5th-house-cusp/):** spontaneous fun, and a taste for the chase.
- **[Taurus on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-5th-house-cusp/):** slow pleasures: food, music, touch, things made by hand.
- **[Gemini on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/):** flirting through words, games and variety; a light touch.
- **[Cancer on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/):** tender romance, and pleasures close to home.
- **[Leo on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** the house's natural sign: big-hearted fun, and a love of the stage.
- **[Virgo on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** crafts and skills; love shown through care and attention.
- **[Libra on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romance, art and beauty, best enjoyed with someone.
- **[Scorpio on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-5th-house-cusp/):** intense attractions and private pleasures.
- **[Sagittarius on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** adventure and laughter; fun found far from home.
- **[Capricorn on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasures you work at; hobbies that become crafts.
- **[Aquarius on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** unusual hobbies, and friendship first in love.
- **[Pisces on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-5th-house-cusp/):** dreamy romance, music, films and imagination.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 5th house is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp. Where it sits shows where your creativity and pleasure go to play out: the part of life your joy flows into. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the cusp, there's a modern and a traditional ruler to read ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** creativity is part of who you are; you show yourself through what you make.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** what you create can earn; pleasure in good things.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** writing, talk and games; fun close to home.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** creating at home; pleasure in home life.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** joy for its own sake; creativity that feeds itself.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** creativity in daily work; a craft practised every day.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** romance that wants to become partnership; fun shared with a partner.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** intense loves; pleasure tied to depth and trust.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** fun through travel and study; romance far from home.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** creativity as a career; a public stage.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** fun with friends; creative groups.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** creativity done in private; a love life kept to yourself.

## Transits through the 5th house {#transits}

The Moon crosses your 5th house every month, the Sun once a year, Mercury, Venus and Mars for a few weeks: short spells when play and pleasure come more easily to mind. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 5th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-5th-house/):** about a year of more fun, more creativity and more appetite for life.
- **[Saturn transiting the 5th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-5th-house/):** about two and a half years of taking a craft seriously — or of learning to make time for joy.
- **[Uranus transiting the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-5th-house/):** around seven years of new pleasures, and a taste for the unexpected in love.
- **[Neptune transiting the 5th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-5th-house/):** around fourteen years of romance and imagination, and of idealising easily.
- **[Pluto transiting the 5th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-5th-house/):** a long, slow change in how you create and how you love.
- **[Chiron transiting the 5th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-5th-house/):** an old hurt around play or being seen comes up, with the chance to tend it.
- **[North Node transiting the 5th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-5th-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards creating, playing and loving.

I read these as weather, not a forecast: none of them says whether you'll fall in love or have a child, only what pleasure and creativity may feel like for a while.
