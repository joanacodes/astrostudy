Saturn holds a life up; Neptune lets things go. When Neptune reaches your natal Saturn, once at most and for about two and a half years, the structures you rely on meet slow water, and the question becomes which of them still have a reason to stand.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) establishes structure: responsibility, boundaries, discipline, the long work of building something that lasts. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves the boundaries that aren't needed and brings compassion and imagination in their place. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into one process, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) building and dissolving happen in the same place: a structure softens even as you hold it.

It often shows as a duty that loses its meaning, a routine that no longer protects anything, or an ambition that stops pulling. It can also show as structure gaining a heart: responsibility taken on out of compassion, a discipline put at the service of a dream. Saturn fears chaos and trusts control to keep it away; Neptune underestimates boundaries. Between the two lies what growth looks like for Saturn as I read it: stewardship rather than control, keeping what serves and letting the rest dissolve.

If the ground under you seems to soften for weeks on end, or low spirits settle in, please see a doctor or a therapist; a chart can give the season a name, never a treatment ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

Natal Saturn rang true unusually often in my notebook, 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), which is why I take such care with it in a birth chart. That count was about Saturn's sign and house at birth, and I don't carry it over to any transit.

With Saturn in your 10th, Neptune crosses it during its years there ([Neptune transiting the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-10th-house/)): the role you carry in public, and the responsibilities attached to your name, ask what they're for. Your 10th, like every house, is cast from the moment you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Saturn conjunct Neptune](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-neptune/) belongs to people born in 1952–53 and 1988–90, when the two planets met in the sky; for them this transit came in childhood or not at all, depending on which planet stood ahead.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In the time Saturn in the sky takes to cross one sign, about two years and seven months, Neptune stays within my orbs of your natal Saturn, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, touching the exact degree three times, or five in one pass out of four; even the shortest passes take twenty-two months.

It comes once at most, and just over half of the people born between 1930 and 2009 meet it before 80. Saturn and Neptune meet in the sky about every thirty-six years: in 1952–53, in 1989, and on 20 February 2026 at the very start of Aries ([the Saturn–Neptune conjunction of 2026](/astrology/saturn-neptune-conjunction-of-2026/)). Born a few years after one of those meetings, with Saturn ahead, you meet the transit young: at about 9 for someone born at the start of 1955, about 19 for 1995. Born in the years before one, as at the start of 1945, 1975 or 1985, you don't meet it before 80.

Neptune's passage through Aries, from 2025 to 2039, brings the conjunction to nearly everyone born with Saturn in Aries, the sign of Saturn's fall in the tradition: births of 1967 to 1969 meet it at about 58 to 69, and births of 1996 to 1999 at about 28 to 40.

For that 1995 birth, [transiting Neptune sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-saturn/) follows at about 46, [transiting Neptune square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-saturn/) at about 60 and [transiting Neptune trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-saturn/) at about 73½; [transiting Neptune opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-saturn/) lies out of reach.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List the structures you rely on, and next to each, what it's for. Keep the ones whose reasons you can still name.

Let compassion into your responsibilities without letting it dissolve them: a duty done with heart still needs doing.
