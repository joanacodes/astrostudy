Fresh from Leo, the Sun keeps a foothold in the first decan of Virgo, 0° to 10° of the sign: it holds the face of these degrees, which it crosses in the last week of August, while Mercury has the sign and the decan.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

Mars closed Leo, and the next planet in the Chaldean order is the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), so in Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) the ruler of the previous sign opens [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/). The modern list keeps the decan with [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), Virgo's ruler, which is exalted in Virgo as well.

Mercury, then, speaks twice, by sign and by decan: sorting, checking and improving, the mind that notices what doesn't fit. The Sun's face adds a centre to that work, the wish that what is refined should also be recognisably yours. So your Mercury is my first stop for a planet here, and the Sun's sign and house the second.

A [Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) in the first ten degrees has its own face there, one of the Sun's five, and the only one that follows straight on from the Sun's own sign. Mercury's exaltation degree lies in the next decan ([Virgo, second decan](/astrology/virgo-second-decan/)).

## Its image {#its-image}

Budge's next pair of names starts here: Themat-ḥert now, Themat-khert in the decan after. The Greco-Egyptian name is a single syllable, Tom, and the Greek Hermetic one is Athoum. Thumis, or Thinnis, is Firmicus Maternus's name for it and Thumi Joseph Scaliger's; in the *Testament of Solomon* the name is Katrax, or Atrax.

Themis, goddess of right order, is Cosmas of Maiuma's figure for these degrees; Athanasius Kircher put Isis here, the figure Cosmas had placed in the middle of Leo. Themis at the threshold of the sign of discernment is a pleasing match; I take it as a picture, not an argument ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The decan holds the Sun's first ten days in Virgo, from about 23 August to 2 September. At the near edge, births late on 22 August or early on the 23rd may still have a Leo Sun ([Leo, third decan](/astrology/leo-third-decan/)), and at the far edge 2 September changes hands from year to year.

[Regulus](/astrology/regulus/), the star of the Lion's heart, has stood in these degrees since about 2012, at 0°11′ Virgo in 2026: charts for recent births place it here, older ones at the end of Leo.

Pluto is here for births from June 1958 to November 1961, and for some from late 1956 and up to mid-1963 ([Pluto in Virgo](/astrology/pluto-in-virgo/)); Neptune came first, for births from 1929 to 1932, and Uranus spent August 1962 to December 1963 here. Saturn's years in the decan were 1948–49, 1977–79 and 2007–08, and Jupiter is back from 26 July to 10 September 2027.

Two eclipses touch the decan: the partial eclipse of the Moon on 28 August 2026, with the Sun at 4°51′ Virgo, and the penumbral one of 20 February 2027, with the Moon at 1°58′ Virgo ([Eclipse in Virgo](/astrology/eclipse-in-virgo/)).

Mercury turns retrograde three or four times a year, for about three weeks each time, nine to twelve weeks a year in all, so the planet that rules this decan twice is retrograde in many birth charts; I read that as a mind that works by going back over things, never as a fault ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).
