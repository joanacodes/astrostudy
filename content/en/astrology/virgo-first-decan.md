The first decan of Virgo is 0° to 10° of the sign, the start of mutable earth, which the Sun crosses in the last week of August. Mercury rules it by sign and by element; the Sun, which has just left its own sign, holds the face.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

The Chaldean order runs on from Mars, which closed Leo, to the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), so Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) give the opening of [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) to the planet that ruled the sign before. The modern decans keep these degrees with Virgo's own ruler, [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), which is exalted in Virgo as well.

Mercury, then, speaks twice, by sign and by decan: sorting, checking and improving, the mind that notices what doesn't fit. The Sun's face adds a centre to that work, the wish that what is refined should also be recognisably yours. With a planet here, I read your Mercury first, then the Sun's sign and house.

[Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/) between 0° and 10° stands in its own face, one of the Sun's five, and the only one that follows straight on from the Sun's own sign. Mercury's exaltation degree comes ten degrees later ([Virgo, second decan](/astrology/virgo-second-decan/)).

## Its image {#its-image}

Budge's list opens another pair here, Themat-ḥert, followed by Themat-khert in the next decan. The Greco-Egyptian lists write simply Tom, the Greek Hermetic list Athoum; Firmicus Maternus has Thumis, or Thinnis, and Joseph Scaliger Thumi. The *Testament of Solomon* calls it Katrax, or Atrax.

Cosmas of Maiuma gave these degrees to Themis, the goddess of right order, and Athanasius Kircher to Isis, the figure Cosmas had placed in the middle of Leo. Themis at the threshold of the sign of discernment is a pleasing match; I take it as a picture, not an argument ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun stands here for births from about 23 August to 2 September. In 2026 it entered Virgo on 23 August at 02:18 UT and crossed 10° on 2 September at 10:53 UT; in 2027, at 08:13 and 16:41 UT, so the 2nd changes hands from year to year, and births late on 22 August or early on the 23rd may still have a Leo Sun ([Leo, third decan](/astrology/leo-third-decan/)).

[Regulus](/astrology/regulus/), the star of the Lion's heart, has stood in these degrees since about 2012, at 0°11′ Virgo in 2026: charts for recent births place it here, older ones at the end of Leo.

Pluto held this decan for births from June 1958 to November 1961, with visits from late 1956 and until mid-1963 ([Pluto in Virgo](/astrology/pluto-in-virgo/)); Neptune before it, for births from 1929 to 1932; Uranus from August 1962 to December 1963. Saturn passed in 1948–49, 1977–79 and 2007–08, and Jupiter returns from 26 July to 10 September 2027.

Two eclipses touch the decan: the partial eclipse of the Moon on 28 August 2026, with the Sun at 4°51′ Virgo, and the penumbral one of 20 February 2027, with the Moon at 1°58′ Virgo ([Eclipse in Virgo](/astrology/eclipse-in-virgo/)).

Mercury turns retrograde three or four times a year, for about three weeks each time, nine to twelve weeks a year in all, so the planet that rules this decan twice is retrograde in many birth charts; I read that as a mind that works by going back over things, never as a fault ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).
