Here the Sun stands in its exaltation and the Moon in the Sun's own sign, so both lights are on the Sun's ground. You were born a week or more into the lunar month, under a Moon already more than half lit, and I read the pair as warmth that wants to begin things and to see them land.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aries and Leo are both fire, four signs apart: a trine by sign, where the elements agree. The rhythm differs. Aries is cardinal and comes in bursts; Leo is fixed and keeps a flame going once it's lit. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) wants to be first; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) needs its warmth returned by the people who matter. Put simply, you throw your whole heart into starting things, and you feel safe once someone has noticed it was your whole heart.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules your Sun's sign. Your Moon's sign belongs to the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) itself, so this Moon reports to the very light that, in your chart, is exalted. What soothes you is bound up with what you're becoming: recognition for the thing you began matters more than recognition in general. The Moon has no dignity of its own in Leo; its standing comes through the Sun.

In the sky, every birth with this pair falls after the first quarter and before the full moon: the Moon was 91° to 149° ahead of the Sun, seven and a half to about twelve days after the new moon. In the eight-phase count, seven in eight of these births are [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) births and the rest [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) ones, and around 120° the chart has a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). The sky's names and the astrologers' don't line up here, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) untangles them.

About 1 person in 142 has the pair. The Moon reaches a new sign about every 2½ days; if it left Cancer or entered Virgo on your birthday, the time says whether you have this pair, the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-cancer/) or the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-virgo/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also gives the rising sign, and a chart's first impression needs all three, two lights and a horizon, before it means much.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love, for this pair, is something you do with energy and want acknowledged. The Aries Sun makes the first move; the Leo Moon turns it into an occasion, and stays loyal long after the first flush. Indifference wounds you more than a quarrel does. You can recover from a row; a partner who stops noticing what you bring can leave both lights burning lower. Saying plainly what you'd like to hear spares you both a season of guessing.

## At work {#at-work}

Enthusiasm is your gift at work, and it spreads. You bring energy to the start of things and like your name attached to them, and teams often rally because the warmth is real. The difficult moments come when a project you launched is taken over without a word of thanks. Roles where initiative is visible and credit is specific tend to suit you: leading from the front, presenting, building something people will see.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Pride runs on both sides. An exalted Sun needs little permission to act, and a Leo Moon needs to know its warmth has landed; when it hasn't, you may redouble the effort or go quiet and wait to be missed. With fire in both lights and nothing else between them, rest doesn't come easily from the pair itself. What helps is keeping the two needs apart: the Sun's need to begin, which you can meet alone, and the Moon's need to be seen, which takes one or two people, asked directly.
