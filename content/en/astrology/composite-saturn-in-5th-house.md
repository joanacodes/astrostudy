The Friday-night date kept for twenty years, the choir you joined together and never left, the board game whose house rules have been written down. Composite Saturn in the 5th house takes a relationship's pleasures seriously, and keeps them going.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is where a chart keeps its pleasures: play, romance, creativity, the things done for the joy of doing them. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it's what the relationship does for fun, and how it goes on courting itself. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) turns pleasure into a commitment. The pair keeps its romance going on purpose, sticks with a shared hobby or creative project for years, and treats a promise to go dancing as seriously as a promise to pay the rent. The courtship itself may have been slow, taken one careful step at a time.

Tradition called the 5th Good Fortune and gave Venus its joy here; it's a succedent house, with Leo as its natural sign. Tradition also gave the house children, and that is a reading I never make: no chart, composite or natal, says whether or when children will come ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)). In an adult relationship, the 5th is what the pair creates and enjoys, whatever form that takes. The house itself depends on both birth times and on the method, since the midpoints of your two charts' cusps and houses rebuilt from the composite Midheaven where you live can disagree about where the 5th begins ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Where one person has [Saturn in the 5th house](/astrology/saturn-in-5th-house/), joy is often something they had to give themselves permission for; with [their Saturn in your 5th house](/astrology/saturn-in-5th-house-synastry/), one person's caution meets the other's playfulness. The composite moves the question of permission inside the bond. The two of you may be easy-going apart and oddly solemn about fun together, which is the composite describing the relationship rather than either of you.

On either side sit two of Saturn's more natural stations in a relationship: the household of [composite Saturn in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-4th-house/) and the daily work of [composite Saturn in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-6th-house/). Between duty and duty, the 5th is the bond's playtime, and this Saturn has to learn to leave it as play.

The difficulty is pleasure that turns into performance: the anniversary that has to be perfect, the hobby that has to improve, the creative project that becomes a second job. Romance kept up out of duty alone can lose the lightness it was meant to protect. It helps to guard time for play as firmly as any other commitment and then leave that time unplanned; to have at least one shared pastime neither of you is any good at; and to say, now and then, what each of you enjoys now, which may not be what you enjoyed ten years ago.

I don't work with composites myself. This is the technique's reading, which its practitioners set beside both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/).
