Some relationships are a soft place for other people to land: a friend after a break-up, a neighbour who needed company, a stranger at the station given directions and then a lift. In a relationship with composite Jupiter in Pisces, compassion and imagination are how the bond grows.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Set at the midpoint of your natal Jupiters, the [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) shows how the relationship widens: its faith, its understanding, its sense of what matters. [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), mutable water, colours it with empathy, imagination and acceptance. It is the sign tradition gave Jupiter before [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) was discovered in 1846, and the one modern tropical astrology gives to Neptune; I read both. In traditional terms, then, composite Jupiter is in its own sign here ([domicile](/astrology/domicile/)), and composite Neptune adds the modern ruler's note: where the bond dreams, and where it blurs.

Only about 1 pair in 15 has it, among two million random pairs born between 1930 and 2009, which puts Pisces level with Aries at the bottom of the twelve. Two people born during the same stay of Jupiter in Pisces share it: from late March 1962 to early April 1963, from March 1974 to March 1975, from late February 1986 to early March 1987, or from February 1998 to February 1999.

Here compassion is something the pair produces together. Two brisk, practical people can find that together they slow down for whoever is struggling, forgive more easily than either would alone, and share a quiet faith, in a practice, in music, in the sea, that neither talks about much elsewhere. Understanding by feeling, which [Jupiter in Pisces](/astrology/jupiter-in-pisces/) gives one person, is shared in a composite, and the bond's beliefs tend to concern acceptance and connection rather than rules. Its generosity is often anonymous: the volunteering never mentioned, the slight quietly forgiven, the spare room kept for whoever needs it. People often leave this pair's company calmer than they arrived, without quite knowing why.

The risk is kindness without edges. A bond that grows by giving can take in more than it can hold, rescue others at its own expense, or let plans stay so vague that nothing is ever decided. Compassion isn't passivity, and empathy doesn't mean giving up your limits. What helps is deciding together what the two of you can offer and what you can't, and giving the bond's dreams a date and a first step, while checking now and then that each of you is being looked after too.

The circle closes here: after Pisces comes Aries again, where [composite Jupiter in Aries](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-aries/) grows through first times, while [composite Jupiter in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-aquarius/), just before, grows through friends and causes. Opposite, [composite Jupiter in Virgo](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-virgo/), Jupiter's detriment, refines what Pisces would rather accept. Composites aren't part of my readings; the technique reads a Pisces Jupiter alongside both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and a Jupiter at home in a midpoint promises no happiness, rescue or future. Pisces blurs boundaries easily, but this one has to hold: a composite is drawn only once both people have agreed to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
