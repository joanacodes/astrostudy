Some pairs are liked on sight. A host who meets them once asks them back; a shopkeeper remembers them a year later. With composite Venus in the 1st house, the relationship's warmth is the first thing about it that anyone meets.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The 1st house of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) starts at the composite [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), and that point is where the methods differ most. One takes the midpoint of your two natal Ascendants; the other finds the composite Midheaven first and computes an Ascendant from it for the place you call home, which in practice means its latitude. Either way, both birth times go in ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and with [Venus](/astrology/venus/) a few degrees from the Ascendant, one method can place it in the 1st and the other in the 12th, which tells a different story ([composite Venus in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-12th-house/)).

In [the 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), angular and naturally Aries's, Venus puts the bond's taste and warmth on the surface. The pair tends to arrive well: a shared style that others notice, a way of greeting people that makes them feel included, an ease that softens a tense room. People who are awkward on their own can be charming together, and often are. Tradition gave this house to Mercury as its joy, and its natural sign, Aries, is Venus's detriment, a reminder that this charm works best when it's frank rather than merely smooth. In one person's chart, [Venus in the 1st house](/astrology/venus-in-1st-house/) describes someone whose grace is visible at once; in synastry, [their Venus in your 1st house](/astrology/venus-in-1st-house-synastry/) is about finding the other person's presence lovely. Here the grace belongs to the two of you together.

Composite Venus usually keeps the composite Sun nearby, as it would in any birth chart: the two lie within 47° of each other in about 9 pairs out of 10, often in the same house or the next. The rest are a quirk of the method. When the two of you were born in very different seasons, the midpoint of your Suns and that of your Venuses can be taken on opposite sides of the circle, which leaves about 1 pair in 22 with the composite Sun near the point facing this Venus, in or close to the 7th house ([composite Sun in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-7th-house/)). That split comes from the arithmetic, not from you.

The difficulty is that a likeable pair can find it hard to look like anything else. Keeping things pleasant in company can become a habit that follows you home, so a disagreement never quite gets aired because the two of you are so good at smoothing. Appearance isn't superficiality, and charm can carry honesty as easily as it covers for its absence. It helps to let a friend or two see you disagree kindly, and to count a frank word between you as part of the bond's grace.

On its other side, [composite Venus in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-2nd-house/) puts the bond's taste into what you own and keep. My reading software draws no composite, and those who do draw them read the 1st house of one together with the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and each person's own chart. A bond this easy to warm to should be easy to ask, too: both people know about the chart before anyone draws it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
