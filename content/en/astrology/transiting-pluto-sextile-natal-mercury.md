Pluto sextile your Mercury is an invitation, and it waits for an answer. For two to three years the planet of depth stands 60° from the part of you that learns and explains, and study, research and honest conversation come more easily, for anyone who goes looking for them.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) seeks access to what is hidden: records, motives, the question under the question. Your [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) learns, analyses and exchanges. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through opportunity and cooperation, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the means of going deeper are within reach: a course that finally fits, an archive that opens, someone who knows the answer and is willing to tell you. A sextile only offers. It rewards whoever signs up, asks, or sits down to read. As with every transit, Pluto here activates what your chart already holds, a mind able to dig; it creates no event and promises no result.

Clearing out an old opinion isn't the same as building a better one. The sextile gives you time for both: let go of the view that no longer holds, then do the slower work of finding out what you think instead. Curiosity isn't distraction, either, and it serves you best here when it follows one thread a long way rather than twenty a little.

Each of Pluto's contacts with Mercury asks something different of your mind: the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-mercury/)) to think from the root, the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-mercury/)) to rethink under pressure, the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-mercury/)) to hear the other side, the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-mercury/)) to let insight come. The sextile asks you to go and look. In a birth chart, [Mercury sextile Pluto](/astrology/mercury-sextile-pluto/) is this opening made permanent; under the transit, the same tool is on loan.

With Mercury in the 7th and Pluto crossing the 9th ([Pluto transiting the 9th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-9th-house/)), talks with a partner or a close collaborator can open into a deeper study of what you believe. Placing Mercury in the 7th, or in any house, takes the birth time, and a chart without one is a chart I don't read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If Mercury was retrograde when you were born, the deeper thinking may start inward, in notes and private reading, before it reaches conversation ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the span my timing uses, the sextile lasts about two years and eight months, and about twenty-two months in the shortest passes. Three exact hits are the rule; about two passes in five bring five.

About four people in five born between 1930 and 2009 meet one of Mercury's two Pluto sextiles by 80, and a few meet both, one on each side of the circle.

In the years Pluto spends in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), the Mercurys it sextiles are in Aries and Sagittarius.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Enrol in something with a syllabus: a course, a reading group, an apprenticeship. This sextile works best with a structure to fill.

Pick the one question you have always wanted answered, and follow it all the way to the sources.

Raise the subject you have been avoiding, at a quiet moment rather than in the middle of a disagreement. Depth is easier to reach when nobody needs to win.
