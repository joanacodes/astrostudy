There is such a thing as too much drive, and this is the transit that shows it. Twice a round, Jupiter squares your Mars, and the wish to do more, bigger and faster runs into how much one person can push in a week.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is effort, courage and the will to act; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) multiplies whatever it meets. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, sets them against each other at a hard angle, so the friction here runs between ambition and capacity: Jupiter adds fronts, and Mars tries to fight on all of them.

In practice that means a project that keeps expanding, three commitments where one would do, impatience with anything that feels small, a habit of pushing harder when easing off would work better. Courage isn't recklessness, and assertion isn't aggression; the square tests both lines. It can also show as impatience with other people's pace, worth noticing before it turns into a habit. Its gift, if you take it, is a better sense of where your effort counts.

Tradition gave these two mirror-image dignities. Jupiter has its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Cancer, where Mars has its fall; Mars is exalted in Capricorn, where Jupiter falls. What strengthens one weakens the other, and a square is where that difference rubs.

People sometimes come to me worried about this one, because squares to Mars have a name for accidents and rows. It's weather, not a forecast, and neither is written in it ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)): Mars stands for the capacity to act, not for anger, and Jupiter only enlarges it.

Jupiter's house at the time is where the push tends to overreach. From your 10th ([Jupiter transiting the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-10th-house/)), it's usually ambition at work, a bigger role taken on before the old one is finished. That house and your Mars's own both come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), so without it I leave the [transit](/astrology/transits/) unread. If [Mars square Jupiter](/astrology/mars-square-jupiter/) is in your chart, every Jupiter return brings this pressure round again.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The square stays inside my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, for about eleven days as a rule. When a retrograde loop of Jupiter lands on it, as in about three cases out of ten, it is exact three times over about eight months.

Thirteen or fourteen of these squares come by 80 for everyone born between 1930 and 2009, which is a good deal of practice at pacing. Of the two in each round, the earlier tests the momentum that [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-mars/) started; the later, after [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-mars/), trims the round's ambitions before the next crossing. In between, [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-mars/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-mars/) let effort and purpose cooperate without the strain.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose the fight worth having and drop two that aren't. The square adds fronts; your job is to close some.

Rest before you're forced to. Mars runs on willingness, and willingness runs out when it's spent on everything at once.

Hand something over. A square from Jupiter makes it feel as if only you can do it, which is rarely true.

When you feel the urge to push harder, try pushing less, for longer.
