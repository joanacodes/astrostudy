A cupboard you finally clear out, a family story that finally gets told: their Pluto in your 4th house brings another person's depth down to the roots of your chart, where home, family and your most private self are kept.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) works at the foundations: it reaches what is buried and makes room for renewal. [The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is your base: the place you live, the family you come from, what you have inherited, and the self you are behind a closed door. Through this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their depth reaches your foundations.

With them, home can become a place of real change. You may clear out, move or rebuild, or simply see your family history differently; patterns you inherited come into view and can be chosen or set down. Feelings about home run deep in their company.

Your private world draws them. They may sense your family's undercurrents at once, feel a belonging with you that goes very deep, or want a strong hand in how your home is run. The 4th is angular, the house beneath your feet, with [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), keeper of what fed us, as its natural sign. On your [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), their Pluto conjoins that lowest angle itself, and the overlay is felt in your sense of where you come from.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Home can turn into territory. Whose rules hold, whose things stay, who decides about family matters: questions of power can surface just where you most want rest. Inherited patterns may resurface before they're understood, and the intensity can make home feel less like a refuge for a while.

From their side, being kept out of your inner rooms can hurt, and they may push to belong faster than you can let them in.

The 4th describes home as you live it, not who your parents are, and their Pluto here predicts nothing about anyone's family. It describes depth and change at your foundations.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Decide together who has a say in what, and keep one corner of home that is unquestionably yours. Let family history come up at the pace it can be heard. In a couple, sharing a home goes better with rules made together; between friends, staying with you is a privilege that keeps its limits; between colleagues, home stays off the agenda unless you choose otherwise; between adult relatives, someone who stirs the family's roots can help it heal, if every voice counts.

From where they stand, it's your Pluto in their 4th house, your depth at their roots. [Their Pluto in your 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-in-3rd-house-synastry/) goes deep in conversation; [their Pluto in your 5th house](/astrology/pluto-in-5th-house-synastry/), in pleasure and creative work.

Ideas of family and home shift between generations, and Pluto's long stay in each sign marks those shifts. With only a few years between your births, Pluto stands at nearly the same degree in both charts, and should your own be in your 4th ([Pluto in the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-in-4th-house/)), this overlay repeats something your generation carries; with a generation between you, two ideas of what a family owes its members meet at your table. Pluto's degree comes from their birth date, and their houses from their birth time, without which I don't read the pair. Your IC, the cusp of your 4th in Placidus, the house system I use, depends on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)). Home and family are private, so this overlay waits for both people's agreement before it is read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
