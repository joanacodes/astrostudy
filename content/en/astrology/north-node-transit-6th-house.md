The North Node reaches your 6th house by crossing your Descendant, and for the next seventeen months or so it works through the house of ordinary days while the South Node works through your 12th. Routine on one side, retreat on the other: the axis asks how your days are actually spent.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the direction that gets easier with use, and the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is daily work, routine, craft and the care you give your body. The growth on offer is very practical: a timetable you keep, a task finished rather than imagined, a skill sharpened by doing it every day, help given in useful form.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in your 12th describes the familiar move of these months: drifting, waiting to feel ready, slipping away when the day gets loud. Rest and imagination are real needs, and nobody's asking you to drop them. The stretch is to give them edges.

Mars, the planet of effort, had its joy in this house, which the old books named Bad Fortune. If you were born with Mars in the 6th, the weeks the node spends on it can turn restlessness into steady work.

Books once read illness in the 6th. I don't. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) can't diagnose anything ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and if your health worries you, a doctor is the person to see. What this one brings out is what your chart already says about work and habit.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 6th is one of the four houses whose size swings most at London's latitude, and the node's stays swing with it. In Placidus charts drawn there, the true node spends about 17 months in the 6th in the middle, and most stays run from about 11 months to about 2 years and 10 months. At New York's latitude the middle is the same and the long end about 2 years and 5 months.

A wobble of the true node back over the cusp within 120 days stays part of one stay in that count. I look at the mean node too, which is never more than a degree or two away ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

In Placidus, the house system I use, the stay opens at your Descendant, and as the North Node crosses it, the South Node crosses your Ascendant ([transiting North Node opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-ascendant/)). Both angles come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node arrives from your 7th ([North Node transiting the 7th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-7th-house/)) and goes on to your 5th ([North Node transiting the 5th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-5th-house/)).

Your Descendant is in the sign opposite your rising sign, and the node crosses it while in that sign. With Leo rising, the Aquarius Descendant is crossed between July 2026 and March 2028. Cancer rising follows (March 2028 to September 2029), then Gemini rising (to March 2031), Taurus rising (to December 2032), Aries rising (to June 2034), Pisces rising, whose Descendant is in Virgo, the 6th's natural sign (to November 2035), Aquarius rising (to May 2037) and Capricorn rising (to February 2039). With Virgo rising, the crossing came between January 2025 and July 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Build one routine around a time of day rather than a mood, and keep it for a month before judging it.

Finish small things. The North Node in the 6th grows through tasks completed, not tasks planned.

Schedule the quiet the South Node needs. An hour of real solitude on the calendar does more for these months than a week of vague withdrawal.
