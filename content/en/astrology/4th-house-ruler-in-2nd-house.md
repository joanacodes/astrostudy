With the ruler of the 4th in the 2nd house, a home is something you build, pay for and keep. The planet that rules your roots sits in the house of money and belongings, so belonging and security tend to arrive together.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, family and the ground you stand on, and the old books add property to it: land and houses. When the planet ruling the IC's sign stands in [the 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), that ground gets measured in what you earn, own and value, and in the worth you feel underneath.

So home tends to have a price and a value, and you know both. You may feel settled only once there's money put by, a roof that's yours or a tenancy you can count on, and the things in your home often carry meaning beyond their use: furniture from a grandparent, books moved from flat to flat, a recipe in a family hand. Family can be bound up with resources in adult life too: a loan, a shared purchase, a business, help given or received. The pinch comes when security becomes the only measure of home, or when what you own starts to stand in for where you belong.

Horary astrology draws the line neatly: movable possessions belong to the 2nd, immovable ones, houses, gardens, land, to the 4th ([horary astrology](/astrology/horary-astrology/)). With this ruler the two kinds meet. What's fixed in your life is looked after by the house of what you can carry, and a home can be both your refuge and your main asset.

The 2nd also holds values. What your family prized, thrift or generosity, saving or sharing, owning or renting, tends to shape what you think things are worth, and part of adult life is keeping the values that serve you and pricing the rest again.

Venus, ruling a Libra IC from Virgo in the 2nd, is in the sign of its [fall](/astrology/fall/), and may count the cost of comfort carefully before allowing itself any; ruling a Taurus IC, Venus in the 2nd works in the house of its own natural sign, and home is where good things gather. An Aquarius IC hands the 4th to Saturn and Uranus, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 2nd tends to pay a home off patiently and keep a reserve, while Uranus, from another house, keeps the right to up sticks.

None of this forecasts wealth or property. The 2nd shows how you handle what you have, not how much will come ([Can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

Between its neighbours, the 2nd is a middle way: from the 1st, the ruler of the IC would make home part of who you are ([Ruler of the 4th in the 1st house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/)); from the 3rd, it would tie home to siblings, neighbours and the local streets ([Ruler of the 4th in the 3rd house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)). Telling the three apart is a matter of the birth time, to the minute ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
