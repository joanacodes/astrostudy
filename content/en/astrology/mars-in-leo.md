In Aries, Mars is a spark. In Leo it's a hearth: a fire that's meant to last, to warm the people gathered round it, and to be seen from across the room. Mars in Leo puts its heart, and often its name, on what it does.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is drive and courage: how you go after what you want and stand up for it. [Leo](/astrology/leo/), the Sun's sign, brings fixed fire: warmth, pride, loyalty, a wish to be seen. Mars in Leo acts from the heart, and wants its effort to mean something personal. You put yourself fully into what you love, and would rather do one thing wholeheartedly than ten things on half power.

Tradition gives Mars neither honour nor difficulty in Leo, so what's left to read is the manner. Fixed fire burns steadily rather than in bursts: once this Mars has chosen a cause or a person, it stays loyal through a great deal.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo, so this Mars works for the Sun's purposes, and the sign and house of your Sun show what your courage is really in the service of. When the two point the same way, your drive has a clear centre. When they don't, you may feel you're fighting hard for something that isn't quite yours.

No sign holds Mars longer on an ordinary pass: about 49 days, as in Virgo, and up to 234 when it turns retrograde in Leo. Those long stays are why Leo comes out as the most common Mars sign in the count, with about 1 person in 10 of the births of 1930 to 2009. Anyone born in the same month or so has it too; the house tells me where your hearth is lit. [Mars in Cancer](/astrology/mars-in-cancer/), before it, protects from the inside; [Mars in Virgo](/astrology/mars-in-virgo/), after it, puts the effort into getting things right.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, Mars in Leo is generous and demonstrative. You court openly, with gestures, and you like the person you love to be proud of having been chosen. Loyalty runs deep, and so does the need to be admired by the one whose admiration counts. Hurt pride is the sticking point: a public slight, or a joke at your expense, can stop you in your tracks, and backing down can feel like losing face. Comparing your Mars with a partner's chart can make a lovely conversation, as long as you have it together: I only read a synastry when both people have said yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Mars wants to sign what it makes. Leading, performing, teaching, creating, building a project that's recognisably yours: that's where your energy is most generous. You can carry a team on enthusiasm alone, and you work best when the effort is seen and the goal is something you believe in. Being invisible drains you faster than hard work ever does, and so does a role where the credit always goes to someone else.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Pride that can't step back. Once you've taken a public position, changing your mind can feel impossible, even when you know you should. You may need applause to keep going, hear disagreement as a personal slight, or take on too much in order to look strong. And because the fire is fixed, a quarrel born of wounded pride can smoulder for a long time.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Do a few things with no audience at all, for the pleasure of doing them. Let someone else lead now and then, and notice that your warmth doesn't go out. Learn the graceful climb-down: *I was wrong about that*, said with good humour, is a very Leo kind of courage. Play, perform, make things; creative and physical outlets keep this fire even. And when your pride is hurt, say so in words, before it hardens into a stand-off.
