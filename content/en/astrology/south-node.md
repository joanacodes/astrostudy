The South Node is the North Node's other end: the point where the Moon's path crosses the [ecliptic](/astrology/ecliptic/) going south, always exactly opposite. Where the North Node points to what you're growing towards, the South Node describes where you start from.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

I read the South Node as familiar ground: what came easily or early, the skills you never had to learn, the habits you fall back on when you're tired or scared. That's a strength, not a fault. The trouble only starts when the familiar becomes the only option, and the other end of the line — the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/), exactly opposite — goes unexplored.

Some astrologers, in [evolutionary astrology](/astrology/evolutionary-astrology/) especially, read the South Node as memories of past lives, or as [karma](/astrology/karma/) carried in. I can't check that, so I don't build a reading on it. Read in plain terms, it already says a great deal about this life.

- **Its sign** shows the style that comes naturally: quick, steady, caring, detached.
- **Its house** shows the area of life you know by heart, sometimes too well.
- **Its aspects** show which planets belong to that familiar ground: talents that came ready-made, or habits that are hard to put down.

The two nodes make one line, the [nodal axis](/astrology/nodal-axis/): find one and you've found the other, at the same degree of the opposite sign. And because the nodes belong to the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), the South Node sits well beside it: two pictures of what feels like home.

A planet on your South Node is, by definition, opposite your North Node, so its pages are the North Node's. With the [Moon opposite the North Node](/astrology/moon-opposite-north-node/), the Moon sits on the South Node: old emotional habits feel like safety, even once you've outgrown them. [Saturn opposite the North Node](/astrology/saturn-opposite-north-node/) often describes responsibility taken on early, and kept out of habit. [Venus opposite the North Node](/astrology/venus-opposite-north-node/) can describe charm or comfort that comes so easily it's never questioned. And a planet square the nodes, like [Mars square the North Node](/astrology/mars-square-north-node/), sits across the path, halfway between the two ends: a drive that wants a say in which way you go.

## The South Node through the signs {#through-the-signs}

The South Node spends about 18 months in each sign, moving backwards, so it's shared by everyone born in the same year and a half or so; the house makes it personal. It has no pages of its own: each sign is read with the North Node across from it.

- **South Node in Aries**, with the [North Node in Libra](/astrology/north-node-in-libra/): independence came early; you know how to act alone, and sharing the decision is the stretch.
- **South Node in Taurus**, with the [North Node in Scorpio](/astrology/north-node-in-scorpio/): steadiness and comfort are home ground; letting things change asks more of you.
- **South Node in Gemini**, with the [North Node in Sagittarius](/astrology/north-node-in-sagittarius/): words, facts and quick answers come easily; finding what you believe takes longer.
- **South Node in Cancer**, with the [North Node in Capricorn](/astrology/north-node-in-capricorn/): caring and protecting come first; a role of your own in the wider world is the reach.
- **South Node in Leo**, with the [North Node in Aquarius](/astrology/north-node-in-aquarius/): you know how to shine and lead; serving something larger than you is new.
- **South Node in Virgo**, with the [North Node in Pisces](/astrology/north-node-in-pisces/): fixing, sorting and worrying well; trusting what you can't control is the stretch.
- **South Node in Libra**, with the [North Node in Aries](/astrology/north-node-in-aries/): keeping the peace and reading other people; knowing what you want first takes practice.
- **South Node in Scorpio**, with the [North Node in Taurus](/astrology/north-node-in-taurus/): intensity and crisis feel familiar; simple, steady peace is the reach.
- **South Node in Sagittarius**, with the [North Node in Gemini](/astrology/north-node-in-gemini/): big answers and firm beliefs come easily; asking and listening are new.
- **South Node in Capricorn**, with the [North Node in Cancer](/astrology/north-node-in-cancer/): duty and control are the default; letting yourself be looked after is the stretch.
- **South Node in Aquarius**, with the [North Node in Leo](/astrology/north-node-in-leo/): detachment and the group come easily; being seen as yourself takes courage.
- **South Node in Pisces**, with the [North Node in Virgo](/astrology/north-node-in-virgo/): dreaming and absorbing come easily; grounded, daily form is the reach.

## The South Node in the houses {#in-the-houses}

The South Node's house is the part of life you know by heart; the house across from it, where the North Node sits, is the one asking for attention.

1. **South Node in the 1st**, with the [North Node in the 7th](/astrology/north-node-in-7th-house/): self-reliance is old ground; partnership is the new one.
2. **South Node in the 2nd**, with the [North Node in the 8th](/astrology/north-node-in-8th-house/): your own resources and comforts; learning to share and to trust.
3. **South Node in the 3rd**, with the [North Node in the 9th](/astrology/north-node-in-9th-house/): information, errands and the close circle; the bigger picture is the stretch.
4. **South Node in the 4th**, with the [North Node in the 10th](/astrology/north-node-in-10th-house/): home and family are familiar; public life asks for you.
5. **South Node in the 5th**, with the [North Node in the 11th](/astrology/north-node-in-11th-house/): the spotlight and your own creations; friendship and shared aims are new.
6. **South Node in the 6th**, with the [North Node in the 12th](/astrology/north-node-in-12th-house/): busy usefulness and routine; rest and letting go are the reach.
7. **South Node in the 7th**, with the [North Node in the 1st](/astrology/north-node-in-1st-house/): other people's needs came first; your own initiative is the new ground.
8. **South Node in the 8th**, with the [North Node in the 2nd](/astrology/north-node-in-2nd-house/): intensity and what others hold; building values of your own.
9. **South Node in the 9th**, with the [North Node in the 3rd](/astrology/north-node-in-3rd-house/): beliefs and far horizons; the everyday, and asking questions.
10. **South Node in the 10th**, with the [North Node in the 4th](/astrology/north-node-in-4th-house/): work and standing come naturally; home and an inner life ask to be built.
11. **South Node in the 11th**, with the [North Node in the 5th](/astrology/north-node-in-5th-house/): groups and causes; personal joy, play and romance.
12. **South Node in the 12th**, with the [North Node in the 6th](/astrology/north-node-in-6th-house/): retreat and imagination; daily routine and useful work.

## The South Node in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), a planet on someone's South Node tends to feel familiar at once, as if you'd known each other for years. Some astrologers read that as a tie from a past life; I read it as ease, which is lovely, and can also become a rut. When [your Moon falls opposite their North Node](/astrology/moon-opposite-north-node-synastry/) — on their South Node — they may find you comforting in a way that asks nothing new of them, and you may slip easily into an old role with them. I read these contacts in both directions, and always with the rest of the two charts; [the nodes in synastry](/astrology/nodes-in-synastry/) goes further.

## Transits and the nodal return {#nodal-return}

The transiting South Node is always opposite the transiting North Node, so while one crosses a house of your chart, the other crosses the house across from it: with the [North Node transiting the 1st house](/astrology/north-node-transit-1st-house/), the South Node moves through your 7th. When the South Node crosses one of your planets — the [transiting North Node opposite your natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-sun/) is the South Node on your Sun — astrologers read a time of release: finishing, clearing out, putting down what has served its time. Eclipses fall near both nodes, so the South Node has its share of them too ([why eclipses follow the nodes](/astrology/why-eclipses-follow-the-nodes/)).

The two nodes come back to their birth places together, about every 18.6 years: the [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/), at about 18½, 37 and 56. In between, at about 9, 28 and 46, the transiting South Node sits on your natal North Node: the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/). It's weather, not a forecast: a season of taking stock, never a sentence.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

The nodes aren't named in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). Overall, about a third of what I checked held — 30 to 40% — and the angle it does report, the rising sign, held about a third of the time too. The three that stood out were [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) and [Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), at 75 to 90% ([how I read the three](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)). I have no figure for the South Node, so I don't give it one. It's a notebook, not a study: I offer the South Node as a description to hold up against your own life, and I say so when part of a reading leans on it.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The South Node is bad.”** It's what you're already good at. The trouble is only staying there when life asks for more.
- **“The South Node proves you've lived before.”** Some astrologers read it as past lives. A chart can't prove that, and a reading doesn't need it.
- **“You have to leave your South Node behind.”** Growth adds the North Node to what you have; it doesn't ask you to throw the rest away.
- **“Planets on the South Node are wasted.”** They're often talents that came ready-made; the question is what you do with them now.
