A house renovated one room at a time over fifteen years, a parent who comes to live with you, a Sunday table that has seated the same family for decades. Composite Saturn in the 4th house builds a relationship from the foundations up.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The 4th house of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is the relationship's base: where it lives, whom it counts as family, what it inherits and what it hands on. [The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is angular, opening at the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart, and what sits there is the ground the rest stands on. With [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) on that ground, the pair lays its foundations deliberately. Home is something it builds, slowly and solidly, and family duties are taken on without fuss: the parent who needs care, the relatives' traditions, the house that has to be kept up.

Composite houses need both birth times, but this one has an unusual anchor. Whether the cusps are averaged or rebuilt from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, that Midheaven is always the midpoint of your two Midheavens, and the IC sits directly across from it: the 4th starts in the same place under either method. Its far edge, the cusp of the 5th, is where the methods part company, and a Saturn near that line may change house with the method ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Saturn is in detriment in Cancer, the 4th house's natural sign, though a house doesn't inherit its sign's verdicts; still, the theme carries over: structure asked to serve tenderness. In one birth chart, [Saturn in the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-in-4th-house/) often means responsibility met early at home, and a home built later on one's own terms; [their Saturn in your 4th house](/astrology/saturn-in-4th-house-synastry/) is one person bringing their sense of duty into the other's private world. A composite 4th-house Saturn belongs to the household the two of you make together, which may look nothing like either home you came from.

Its neighbours are the doorstep and the garden. [Composite Saturn in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-3rd-house/) keeps the bond's everyday comings and goings, and [composite Saturn in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-5th-house/) asks it to make room for play. The 4th is the house itself, and the work done inside it.

The hard part is home as obligation. A pair that takes family duties seriously can find its own life squeezed between other people's needs, and a house that's always being improved can feel like a job that never ends. Old family rules can creep in unexamined, too. What helps is choosing which traditions to keep rather than keeping them all; sharing caring duties with the wider family where you can; and protecting a part of home life that belongs to the two of you alone. None of this predicts a move, a purchase or a family event.

This page describes a technique I don't use myself. Those who do use it read the composite 4th with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) beside it.
