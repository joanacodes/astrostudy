Both your lights stand in Capricorn and answer to Saturn, and you were born within a couple of days of the new moon in that sign, the first after the December solstice. Purpose and need speak with one voice here. I read the pair as one long project, with the same patient hands for work and for feeling.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Same sign, same manner: both lights are cardinal earth, so what you are building and what keeps you steady ask for the same things, a plan, a responsibility, a sense that effort adds up. The [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) organises a life in load-bearing stages; the [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) finds its footing in being relied on: the keys held for the whole family, the plan B already drafted, the forms filed early. There is little inner argument about what matters. There is also little inner voice saying *enough for today*.

Tradition puts the Moon in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Capricorn, opposite Cancer, its own sign: needs tend to wait until the work is done, which tells me about timing, not about how much you feel. The Sun has no dignity here. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules both lights, so its sign, house and aspects carry the whole first sketch of the chart. That matters more than usual, because Saturn has a standing of its own in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/): it was one of three placements, with Chiron and Lilith, that held 75 to 90% of the time, against about a third overall. With both lights resting on it, Saturn is where I spend most of a reading.

You were born near the [new moon in Capricorn](/astrology/new-moon-in-capricorn/), within about two and a half days of it: the Moon was between 28° behind the Sun and 30° ahead. Half these births fall in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), before the conjunction, and half in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase after it, and near 0° the chart has a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). To me a birth this close to the new moon means beginnings come naturally and endings are harder to see; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the difference between the days just before and just after.

About 1 person in 153 has both lights in Capricorn. If the Moon arrived from Sagittarius or left for Aquarius on your birthday, the time chooses between this page, the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-sagittarius/) and the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-aquarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With the lights in one sign, the rising sign does much of the colouring, and it needs the same time, as does Saturn's house.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love through commitment and constancy. Promises are kept, plans are shared, and a partner can lean on you through the hardest years. What's harder is the softer register: saying you're tired, asking for comfort, letting a relationship be somewhere you play rather than one more thing you manage. Someone who brings lightness into the routine, and makes rest feel allowed, does more for this pair than any grand gesture.

## At work {#at-work}

Work is where this pair feels most at home, and where it is easiest to lose yourself. You take on responsibility early, stay the course, and build things that outlast the people who started them: an organisation, a practice, a body of work. Recognition matters less than knowing that something holds. The risk is a self measured entirely by output. The [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/), about every 29½ years, is a natural time to look at that balance again, and with Saturn ruling both your lights, it is worth meeting on your own terms.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

A single earth sign gives a strong spine and few soft places. When the Sun and Moon agree that comfort comes after the work, nobody inside argues for rest, and needing anything can feel like failing. Feelings may be postponed so long that they arrive as tiredness instead. What helps is putting care on the schedule as seriously as work: a weekly hour that belongs to nothing useful, a person with whom nothing has to be finished, and the plain fact that rest kept for later rarely arrives.
