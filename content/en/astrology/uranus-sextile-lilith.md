Uranus sextile Lilith links the planet of difference to the point that marks what you learned to hide, two signs apart. The link works like a side door: your originality can let the untamed part of you out into the open, on your terms, whenever you choose to open it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, differentiation and liberation: new ideas, new methods, a life arranged your own way. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is, as I read it, instinctive autonomy, the part of you once found too much or too strange, and the boundaries you keep around your own power; other astrologers weigh it differently. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates the two through opportunity and cooperation, a link that comes alive when it's engaged.

In plain words, originality offers the hidden part a respectable channel. What you once kept back can come out through an unusual project, a group of people who don't flinch at it, or a way of working that leaves room for instinct. In return, the untamed part gives your originality its edge: it keeps your difference from becoming a pose. The sextile doesn't push. Opportunity requires engagement, and the potential here is real only when you pick a moment and let that part of you show.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 15 born between 1930 and 2009 has Uranus within 6° of a sextile to mean Lilith, counted with mean Lilith, the one I use ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). Lilith is the faster of the two, a sign in about nine months against Uranus's seven years, and it passes the two sextile points about twice a decade, a few months each time: a personal aspect, not a generation's.

The two usually sit in elements that get on. With Uranus in Scorpio and Lilith in Capricorn, water and earth, a private independence can give an unapologetic ambition its outlet; with Uranus in Gemini and Lilith in Leo, air and fire, a restless mind can give a hidden boldness its stage. Houses add the setting: Uranus in the 11th sextile Lilith in the 1st, say, could find friends and groups that let the untamed part show in the way you meet the world. For that you need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Lilith](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-lilith/):** difference and the untamed part in a single act.
- **[Uranus square Lilith](/astrology/uranus-square-lilith/):** the same two parts in friction rather than cooperation.
- **[Uranus trine Lilith](/astrology/uranus-trine-lilith/):** a wider, easier flow between them that needs no decision.
- **[Uranus opposite Lilith](/astrology/uranus-opposite-lilith/):** the two face to face across the chart.

[Uranus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-lilith-synastry/) lets one person's originality give the other's untamed side some air. I read it both ways, since offering that air and receiving it are different experiences.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Transiting Lilith sextiles natal Uranus twice in each round of about 8 years and 10 months, for a couple of weeks each time; transiting Uranus sextiles natal Lilith twice in its 84-year round, for months, often in three passes.

Born with the sextile, you meet a quiet echo halfway between Lilith's returns: transiting Lilith then stands opposite its birth place, which puts it in a trine to your natal Uranus for a few weeks. Weather, not a forecast: brief, friendly conditions for letting a hidden part of you speak, if you want to.
