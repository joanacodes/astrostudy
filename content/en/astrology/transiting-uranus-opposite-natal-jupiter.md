Once in its round of about 84 years, Uranus reaches the point opposite your Jupiter. For about twenty months, the outlook you live by meets another one face to face, and you get to see both.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates: it frees what has grown too narrow and tries what hasn't been tried. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is your frame of meaning, the beliefs and principles you grow by. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates two functions through polarity, each showing up clearly against the other, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your way of making sense of life stands beside one that works differently.

Often the other view arrives in a person: a partner who believes otherwise, a colleague from another culture, a teacher who contradicts the one you trusted, a group whose values look freer than yours. Sometimes you're the one holding the new idea, and the people around you defend the older picture. Either way, a belief you never had to explain now has to stand next to its alternative.

I read it as a widening by contrast. Polarity isn't contradiction, and the opposition doesn't ask you to convert or to win; it asks what each view sees that the other misses. The risk is swinging from a long-held faith to its opposite, wholesale, because the new one happens to be in front of you.

With Jupiter in your 9th and Uranus crossing your 3rd ([Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/)), your big picture meets the news of the street: what neighbours, a sibling, a short course or a daily commute keep telling you. That axis can only be drawn from your birth time, and I won't read a chart that lacks one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Twenty months is long enough to hear another outlook out: with my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, that is how long the opposition lasts, eleven at the briefest, and in more than nine passes in ten it is exact three times. Nobody meets it twice by 80, and about 1 person in 33 born between 1930 and 2009 misses it altogether. Otherwise the age is anyone's: in the site's count, about 13 for someone born at the start of 1965, about 74 for someone born at the start of 1975.

Two natal pictures fix it. Born with [Jupiter conjunct Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus/), as happens for a few months about every fourteen years, you meet it with your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/): someone born at the start of 1955 met both at about 39. Born with [Jupiter opposite Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-uranus/), you had it in your first year or so, and Uranus won't stand there again until about 84; people born just after the two planets were exactly opposite are the ones who reach 80 without it.

Around it, [transiting Uranus trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-jupiter/) comes about fourteen years before and after, and [transiting Uranus square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-jupiter/) about twenty-one; [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Uranus sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-jupiter/) belong to the other half of the circle.

## What helps {#what-helps}

State the other side's view in its best form, out loud or on paper, before you answer it. Jupiter's understanding grows most when it has to make room for a fair opponent.

Change your mind in instalments. A belief traded wholesale under an opposition tends to swing back; one revised point by point usually stays revised.

Notice where you've been preaching. Jupiter can turn an outlook into a sermon, and other people's reactions in these months show you where yours has hardened.
