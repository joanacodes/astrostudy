A year of work abroad, a relationship begun between two languages, a conversation about what matters that is still going ten years on. Composite Uranus in Sagittarius describes a bond that changes by widening its horizon, and in random pairs it is the commonest composite Uranus sign of all.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), is the relationship's appetite for freedom. [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire ruled by [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), aims that freedom at meaning: beliefs, study, travel, the larger picture. Sagittarius is neither Uranus's own sign nor its detriment, so the ruler matters: composite Jupiter shows how the pair grows and what it comes to trust once the questions have been asked.

Read as the relationship's own, it describes a bond that keeps rethinking what it believes. Its worldview is built from both people's and belongs to neither: a faith left or found together, a politics argued into shape, a philosophy of how to live that changes as you travel. Freedom here is freedom of movement and of thought, and the bond often stretches across distance, cultures or languages without losing its thread. Two people who rarely leave home can find that, together, they keep booking the far-off trip; two people of different faiths, or none, can build a third way of seeing without either giving theirs up. In a birth chart, [Uranus in Sagittarius](/astrology/uranus-in-sagittarius/) gives a generation the wish to think and move freely; in the composite, the exploring is done together, and each discovery belongs to the pair.

No sign turns up more often for composite Uranus in the two million random pairs of the count, born 1930–2009: about 1 pair in 11, against 1 in 12 or 13 for the rest, a slim margin. Couples of the same age get it from their birth years, February 1981 to December 1988, and are in their late thirties and early forties today. A gap moves it: a person born in 1977 and a person born in 1992, with Uranus at about 8° Scorpio and 17° Capricorn in June of those years, share a composite Uranus at about 13° Sagittarius.

The trap is the next horizon. A relationship that grows by widening can keep moving the goalposts, until freedom starts to mean having no responsibilities, and principles get argued while the person in front of you waits. Another form of the same trap is the sermon: a pair sure it has found the better way can start preaching it to everyone else. What helps is to bring each discovery home: one practice, place or promise that stays put while the ideas travel.

Its sign is a cohort's, so the house carries the reading, and it needs both birth times: [composite Uranus in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-9th-house/), the natural house of Sagittarius, makes study, travel and belief the pair's own ground. [Composite Uranus in Scorpio](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-scorpio/) changes at depth; [composite Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-capricorn/), through structures. None of my readings include a composite: the technique, as its users practise it, reads this placement beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), promises nothing about where the relationship goes, and needs both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
