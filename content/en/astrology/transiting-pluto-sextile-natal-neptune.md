Pluto sextile your natal Neptune is the Pluto transit almost everyone meets, and many meet it twice: once in the cradle, carried by the long sextile between Neptune and Pluto in the sky of their birth years, and again in their fifties, from Neptune's other side.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and connects: it holds your ideals, your compassion, your sense of a meaning larger than yourself. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) renews by taking things to the root. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, offers your ideals a means rather than an order, and the means has to be taken up. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), an ideal can find a practical, lasting shape: a creative project gets its discipline, a spiritual interest becomes a practice, a wish to help others becomes work you can sustain.

The sextile's gift is that it asks for no sacrifice. Neptune's boundaries matter here all the same: compassion isn't self-erasure, and transcendence isn't escape. The opening is for the dream that can be lived, not the one that asks you to disappear into it.

The sextile adults live comes after Pluto has crossed your natal Neptune in your twenties ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-neptune/)). By then the ideals tested in those years have had decades to settle, and this transit offers a way to put the survivors to use. Born with [Neptune sextile Pluto](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto/), as most people born between 1943 and 1993 were, you began life close to it.

With Neptune in your 6th, the sextile comes from around your 8th ([Pluto transiting the 8th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-8th-house/)) or your 4th: what you share with others, or what you rebuild at home, gives your ideal of service a practical form. An approximate birth time can move Neptune from one house to the next ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each sextile lasts about two years and eight months, from the moment Pluto comes within 1½° of exact to the moment it is 1° past, the orbs I use, and about twenty-two months at the shortest. Most passes bring three exact hits; about two in five bring five.

Almost everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets it by 80, and about half meet it twice. For many births from the late 1940s to the late 1980s, the first came within a year or two: at 0.6, for example, for a birth on 1 January 1955. The second comes after Pluto has passed natal Neptune: at about 50 to 57 for births up to the mid-1980s, and later for later births, up to 73.3 for 1 January 2005. Born in the 1930s or most of the 1940s, you meet only that later one, at 53.9 for 1 January 1935.

From Aquarius, until early 2044, Pluto gives the later sextile to the generation born from 1970 to 1984 with Neptune in Sagittarius, in their early to late fifties.

Only the conjunction reaches everyone as surely, in the twenties. Beyond the sextile, the wider the angle, the rarer the transit: about 1 person in 3 never meets the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-neptune/)), about 5 in 6 never meet the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-neptune/)), and no one meets the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-neptune/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give one ideal a timetable: the hours, the place, the first piece of work.

Share it with other people. A sextile works through cooperation, and an ideal held in common is harder to lose in a fog.

Check the dream against the facts now and then. Neptune's blind spot is confusing imagination with evidence, and a practical dream survives the check.
