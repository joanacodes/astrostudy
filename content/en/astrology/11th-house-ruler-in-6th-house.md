Some of the best friendships begin on a Tuesday, at the same time every week. Your people, with the 11th's planet in the 6th, are the ones you work, train and turn up beside, and friendship grows out of the ordinary week rather than the special occasion.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) runs the working week: the tasks you do, the habits that keep you going, the people at the next desk, the care you give your body. The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is where you belong by choice: allies, associations, the aims you share with others. With the 11th's ruler sitting in the 6th, that belonging is built in the daily round ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Colleagues tend to become friends, and the friends who last are often the ones you've worked beside: the shift you covered together, the project that ran late, the rota you share at the food bank. Groups suit you when they have a job to do, a union branch, a volunteer team, a running club with a training plan, and you're often the member who keeps the practical side going. Your hopes advance the same way, step by step and on a schedule. The pull to watch is towards friendships that exist only at work, so that a change of job empties the diary.

The old astrologers had little good to say of the 6th. It was Bad Fortune, a house of toil and sickness, and in horary it's one of three houses, with the 8th and the 12th, where a planet counts as hindered. Yet the 6th was where Mars rejoiced, the planet that likes a task ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With Aries on your 11th cusp, the planet of your friendships is Mars at its ease ([Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/)), and your closest bonds are forged in shared work, the harder the better. I read the old verdict as a description of labour, not of luck.

Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 11th from here, runs the group's logistics: the spreadsheet, the reminders, the lift-share. Scorpio on the cusp brings Pluto in beside Mars, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Mars in the 6th puts its effort into the team, while Pluto, from its own house, deepens a few working friendships into something that changes you.

William Lilly gave the 6th small animals, and the friends you meet walking the dog belong to this placement as much as any colleague. The body's care belongs here too, and with the 11th's ruler in the 6th it's often done in company: a swimming club, a yoga class, a walking group. That's about habits and company, not health: illness isn't something any chart can show ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

The 6th ends at the Descendant, where planets set, so a ruler close to that edge had only just gone down when you were born. With a slightly earlier birth it would still be above the horizon in the 7th, and your circle would meet you one to one, as partners ([Ruler of the 11th in the 7th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)). Deeper in the house, by the 6th cusp, a later birth would have carried it into the 5th, where your circle plays rather than works ([Ruler of the 11th in the 5th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)). Only the exact time settles it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
