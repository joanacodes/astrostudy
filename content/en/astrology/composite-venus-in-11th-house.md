A standing table on Friday nights, a circle of friends with a name nobody outside it understands, a pair who are always the ones organising the reunion: with composite Venus in the 11th house, a relationship takes its pleasure in company.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Succedent, naturally Aquarius's, [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) covers friendships, groups, networks, alliances and the hopes people pursue together; tradition remembered it as Good Spirit, the house where Jupiter rejoiced ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [Venus](/astrology/venus/) can be placed there only with both birth times, and its cusps vary with the method: either the midpoint of two natal cusps or a cusp computed around the composite Midheaven for your home latitude ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Venus here makes friendship the bond's favourite setting. The relationship enjoys a circle: introducing people, hosting, belonging to a choir, a club or a cause, building something larger than the two of you. Friends tend to become shared, many such bonds began in a group, and the pair is often the one that keeps a group together, remembering birthdays and organising the meal. Its pleasures also look ahead: hopes the two of you share, a plan for the neighbourhood, a project friends are invited into. Couples, colleagues and relatives with this placement all run their bond partly as a friendship with a guest list. [Venus in the 11th house](/astrology/venus-in-11th-house/) describes, in one birth chart, a person whose friends are a large part of their happiness; [their Venus in your 11th house](/astrology/venus-in-11th-house-synastry/) warms your circle of friends; the composite's circle is one the two of you gather together.

The composite Sun is a near neighbour of this Venus, under 47° away, for about 9 pairs in 10. Pairs born months apart are the exception: their two midpoints can fall on opposite sides, and in about 1 pair in 22 the two face each other, with the composite Sun around the 5th house of play ([composite Sun in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-5th-house/)). That's the way midpoints behave, not a sign of anything between you.

Company can crowd out intimacy. A bond whose pleasures are found among friends may rarely be alone together, and its affection, spread round a whole circle, can feel thin to the two people at its centre. Belonging needn't mean conforming, and friendship is no popularity contest, but a circle needs a door that can close. It helps to keep some evenings for two, and to make sure each of you also has friends who are only yours. Shared hopes can crowd out private ones as well, so ask now and then what each of you wants that the group doesn't.

[Composite Venus in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-10th-house/) is seen in public life; [composite Venus in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-12th-house/) holds its tenderness out of sight. I explain composites without using them, and their users never separate them from the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) or from the two natal charts. However wide the circle, only the two people in this bond can agree to its chart ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
