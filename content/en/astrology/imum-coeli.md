The IC sits at the very bottom of a birth chart, opposite the Midheaven. Its Latin name, *Imum Coeli*, means the bottom of the sky, and it's where astrologers look for roots: home, family, the ground you grew from.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

At the minute and place of your birth, the zodiac crossed the meridian, the line running north to south through the sky, in two points: above the horizon, the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/); below it, exactly opposite, the IC. Together they make the [meridian axis](/astrology/meridian-axis/), the vertical line of the chart, as the Ascendant and Descendant make the [horizon axis](/astrology/horizon-axis/). The IC lies below the horizon, on the far side of the Earth, though not straight underfoot. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems it is the cusp, the starting line, of the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), which tradition gave to home, family, land and roots.

I read the IC as the ground you stand on: where you come from, what home feels like, what you need in private before you can go out and do anything else. It's the most private of the four angles, and the one the other three rest on.

- **Its sign** describes what home and belonging feel like, and what you need in order to rest.
- **Its ruler**, the ruler of the 4th house, shows where the story of your roots plays out in the rest of your life.
- **Planets close to it** are [angular planets](/astrology/angular-planets/): out of sight at the bottom of the chart, but strong, and often felt most in private life.

It works with the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), which rules Cancer, the 4th sign, and describes what you need to feel safe. As for the [Sun](/astrology/sun/): born around the middle of the night, by the sun rather than the clock, you have the Sun close to your IC.

Because the IC and the Midheaven are always exactly opposite, an aspect to one is an aspect to the other. A planet in [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) with the IC is in [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) to the Midheaven: what it describes lives at home first, and reaches the world from there. A [square](/astrology/square/) to the IC is a square to the Midheaven too, the old pull between home and work; a [trine](/astrology/trine/) to it is a sextile to the Midheaven. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) close to the IC often describes responsibility that came early at home.

## The IC through the signs {#through-the-signs}

The IC is always the sign opposite your Midheaven, and changes sign with it, about every two hours, so only your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) can settle it. What home tends to feel like, and what you need there:

- **[IC in Aries](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a lively home, where you need room to act on your own terms.
- **[IC in Taurus](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as comfort and stability; roots in simple, physical things.
- **[IC in Gemini](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home full of talk, books, comings and goings.
- **[IC in Cancer](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a strong pull towards family and belonging; home is where you recharge.
- **[IC in Leo](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a warm, generous home, and pride in where you come from.
- **[IC in Virgo](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an orderly home, where care is shown in practical ways.
- **[IC in Libra](/astrology/libra-on-4th-house-cusp/):** you need peace and beauty at home; tension there costs you more than elsewhere.
- **[IC in Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/):** deep, private roots; at home, trust is everything.
- **[IC in Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** roots in more than one place; home as a base for exploring.
- **[IC in Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/):** responsibility early at home, and a home built to last.
- **[IC in Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** an unusual home, or a chosen family; space to be yourself.
- **[IC in Pisces](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a refuge; roots that are hard to pin down.

## Its ruler in the houses {#in-the-houses}

The planet that rules the sign on your IC is the ruler of your 4th house ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)): the Moon for a Cancer IC, Venus for Taurus or Libra, Saturn for Capricorn, and so on; Scorpio, Aquarius and Pisces have a modern and a traditional ruler ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Where it sits shows where the story of home and family plays out in the rest of your life:

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** you carry home with you; your family shaped who you are, visibly.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** home means security; you build it with what you earn and value.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** siblings and neighbours matter; a close-knit home, kept alive by talk.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** deep roots; home and family are a world of their own.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** home as a place of play, warmth and creativity.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** home and daily work overlap: duties at home, or working from it.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** home is made with a partner; partners become family.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** what the family passes down — its stories, habits and unfinished business — weighs on you more than on most.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** home far from where you started, or roots in another culture or belief.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** family and career intertwine, through a family business or a family name.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** friends become family; home as a meeting place.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** a private, retreat-like home; family stories that stay unspoken.

## The IC in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), the IC shows who feels like home. When someone's Moon falls on your IC or in your 4th house ([their Moon in your 4th house](/astrology/moon-in-4th-house-synastry/)), being together can feel familiar from the start, sometimes so familiar that it stirs up old family patterns. [Their Venus in your 4th house](/astrology/venus-in-4th-house-synastry/) tends to bring tenderness into the home you share. These contacts matter for couples who live together, and for adult members of the same family. I read them in both directions and, as always, only [with both people's agreement](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/).

## Transits to the IC {#transits}

The angles have no return: what moves is the sky. A slow planet crossing your IC is, by definition, opposite your Midheaven, so when [Saturn is opposite your natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-midheaven/), it is on your IC. That happens about every 29½ years, before Saturn moves on through your 4th house ([Saturn transiting the 4th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-4th-house/)), and people often describe it as a time of foundations: home, family and where you belong, all asking for attention at once. [Jupiter opposite the Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-midheaven/), about every 12 years, tends to bring more room to breathe at home; [Pluto opposite the Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-midheaven/), which many people never meet, a slow rebuilding of roots.

A transit is weather, not a forecast: it describes the climate at home, never a move or a family event written in advance. And it is only as precise as your birth time.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

My notebook doesn't report the IC on its own, so I won't give it a figure. The angle it reports is the rising sign, which held about a third of the time: 30 to 40%, like most of [what I checked](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). The three that stood out were [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), at 75 to 90%. I read the IC carefully, because home is tender ground, and I tell you which parts of a reading rest on it. It's a notebook, not a study, and it shapes [how I read](/how-i-read/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The IC is your mother.”** Or your father: astrologers have never agreed which parent goes with the 4th house and which with the 10th. I read the IC as home as you lived it, without the labels; a parent has a chart of their own.
- **“It's at the bottom of the chart, so it matters less.”** It's one of the four angles, and planets close to it are among the strongest in a chart; they just work out of sight.
- **“A difficult IC means an unhappy childhood.”** It describes what home asked of you, not a verdict on it, and the home you make later is part of the reading too.
