Jupiter in Leo grows in the open. Understanding comes through what you make, what you celebrate and whom you champion, and beliefs tend to be held with the whole heart rather than kept in reserve.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is your capacity for growth and for meaning. [Leo](/astrology/leo/), fixed fire, is warm, proud, loyal and creative, and likes the light. With Jupiter here, expression becomes the road to understanding. You often find out what you think by performing it, writing it, building it or saying it to a room, and your principles have a lot to do with dignity, loyalty and courage of heart. You believe in people generously, and they often rise to it.

As for dignity, Leo is neutral ground for Jupiter: not one of its signs, not its exaltation, and not a place of detriment or fall. That puts the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), Leo's ruler, at the centre. Its sign, house and aspects show where this Jupiter wants to shine, and what you need to be recognised for.

The Leo years include August 1990 to September 1991, August 2002 to August 2003 and July 2014 to August 2015, and across births from 1930 to 2009 it turns up in about 1 person in 11. Between September 1966 and June 1968, Jupiter shuttled between Leo and both its neighbours, [Jupiter in Cancer](/astrology/jupiter-in-cancer/) and [Jupiter in Virgo](/astrology/jupiter-in-virgo/); in 1955–56 and 1978–79, between Leo and one of them. Births in those months need a chart to tell. A sign shared by a whole year becomes yours through its house: the part of life where you most want to make something.

I checked Jupiter in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) along with everything else, and the spotlight went elsewhere: to Saturn, Chiron and Lilith, the three that held far more often. Of all I checked, about a third held. So for all the warmth of a Leo Jupiter, I weigh it with the rest of your chart rather than giving it centre stage.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Your warmth in love is wholehearted: praise, attention, a sense of occasion. You may be the one who plans the celebration, remembers what someone is proud of and tells them so, believes in their talent out loud. You grow with a partner who lets you shine and shines back, and who can also see you on an ordinary Tuesday. The lesson is receiving: pride can make it hard to accept care, or to admit a need that doesn't look grand.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, you grow by making things and by encouraging others to make theirs: creative and performing work, teaching with flair, coaching, events, the kind of leadership that lifts a team rather than standing over it. Openings often find you because people remember you. Scale is what to keep an eye on: projects that grow bigger rather than better, or a need for applause that decides which work you take on.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

When growth depends on being seen, the quiet stretches of learning feel like failure. Beliefs held with the whole heart can become part of who you are, so that a challenge to an idea lands as a challenge to you. Generosity can be given with an audience in mind. And the wish to make everything special leaves little room for the plain, unglamorous practice that most growing actually takes.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make something purely for the pleasure of it, and show it to nobody for a while. Let someone else take the stage, and enjoy their moment as if it were yours. Ask one person whose judgement you trust for honest feedback, and thank them for it. The Sun in your chart shows where your sense of self burns brightest, and so where this Jupiter will want its light.

Jupiter is back in Leo from June 2026 to July 2027, which makes this a [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) year for everyone born with it, at about 24, 36, 48, 60 and beyond. I read it as weather, not a forecast: a year when the question of what you want to create, and for whom, comes round again.
