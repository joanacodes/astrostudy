Some people sense the part of you that you keep out of sight, and find it beautiful rather than alarming. In synastry, Neptune conjunct Lilith is one picture of that: one person's Neptune sits on the other's Black Moon Lilith, and imagination gathers exactly where the other learned to hide.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and feels its way into other lives: compassion, a feel for symbols, edges softened where they were never needed. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) marks where the Moon swings farthest from Earth, and every school weighs it differently; to me it stands for the instinct that refused to be tamed, whatever a person shelved in order to be liked, and the power they quietly keep. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) works two functions as one without erasing either; in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), though, the work is split: one person imagines, the other carries what is imagined.

If the Lilith is yours, this person seems to know what you never said aloud: the temper you tidied away, the appetite, the flat refusal. They aren't shocked; they're moved. Some days this is the relief of being met at last. Other days you feel cast as their muse, your hidden side admired from a distance instead of lived as yours.

On the Neptune side, the other person's untamed streak pulls at your imagination like a tide. You sense more than they show, and you start to picture who they would be if they let it out. Your gift is compassion without judgement; your blind spot is Neptune's everywhere, taking a strong feeling about someone for knowledge of them.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Two things this close blur. The Neptune person can fall for the picture: the other's wildness as a beautiful myth, edited of everything inconvenient. The Lilith person can start playing the part, showing only the version that keeps the fascination alive, or hiding it again the moment it gets romanticised. Neither is deceit; admiration has simply arrived before the details, and it eases once they're let in.

The other distances between these two:

- **[Neptune sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** imagination held out as an invitation, which the other can take or leave.
- **[Neptune square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-lilith-synastry/):** the picture keeps missing the person, and both of them notice.
- **[Neptune trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-lilith-synastry/):** the same warmth with more room between the two, and no blur.
- **[Neptune opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** one person's longing and the other's refusal at either end of a line.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask, and let the answer redraw the picture. If the Neptune is yours, check what you sense with the person before treating it as fact, and let them stay more ordinary than your idea of them. If the Lilith is yours, say which parts you're glad to have seen and which stay yours alone. Creative partners often put this conjunction to work; a couple can make a home where nothing needs tidying away; a parent and an adult child, a late and gentle recognition.

It belongs to birth dates. Someone born in mid-1955 has their Neptune, in Libra, under the Lilith of anyone born between March and June 1954, and again nine years on, under that of people born from February to April 1963. The other way round, their Lilith, in Sagittarius, lies under the Neptune of anyone born from November 1974 to December 1977. With whole spells of births sharing it, it stays out of the count I make in a synastry, which takes only contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. What I do read is its houses, where two eras touch more than two temperaments.

Neither point moves fast enough for the hour to decide whether the conjunction exists; where it falls in each life takes both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). These months use mean Lilith; true Lilith may sit somewhere else entirely ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

Inside a single chart, [Neptune conjunct Lilith](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-lilith/) is a person's own wildness behind their own veil. Here the veil is offered by someone else, which is why I read this only when both people want the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
