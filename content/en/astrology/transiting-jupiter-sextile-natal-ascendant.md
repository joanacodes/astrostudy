Twice in each twelve-year round, Jupiter stands 60° from your rising degree: once from below the horizon, about two years after crossing it, and once from above, about two years before crossing it again. Each time, a way in opens at the point where you meet the world.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens what you understand and finds meaning in experience; the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is where contact begins, the angle from which you approach people and places. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) links them through cooperation: Jupiter offers, and you decide whether to go in. It's the [transit](/astrology/transits/) of introductions: a setting that suits you, someone who opens a door, a first meeting that goes better than you expected. It asks for a step, not a leap.

The same Jupiter makes a trine, 120°, to your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the other end of the horizon line, so the opening often comes with other people already on your side: a partner, a colleague, a new acquaintance who makes your way in easier.

In Placidus, the first sextile of a round usually finds Jupiter in your 2nd or 3rd house, among your skills and everyday exchanges, and the opening tends to be close to home: a course, a neighbourhood, a skill someone needs. The second usually comes from your 10th or 11th, so the invitation arrives through work, friends or a group ([Jupiter transiting the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-11th-house/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

An introduction doesn't take long, and most of the time neither does this sextile: within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, seven in ten are over in about eleven days. The other three meet [Jupiter retrograde](/astrology/jupiter-retrograde/) on the way and stretch to about eight months, with three exact hits.

Your rising sign sets the year, because Jupiter makes this sextile from the sign two before it and from the sign two after, spending about a year in each. It's also why the birth time matters so much here: the rising sign changes about every two hours, so two people born on the same day can meet these sextiles a year apart. A reading waits until the time is known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have had thirteen or fourteen of these openings, about half with Jupiter below their horizon and half above it. Two years or so after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) comes the round's first sextile, and [transiting Jupiter square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-ascendant/) are close behind it. The second comes about ten years in, well after [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-ascendant/), as Jupiter heads back towards your rising degree.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Accept the introduction, then follow it up. A sextile opens the door a little way, and the first message after the meeting is what keeps it open.

Go where people who share your interests gather, and arrive as yourself rather than as the version you think the room wants. Your Ascendant is the angle people meet first, and this transit rewards it for being honest.

Change one thing about how you start: a different hour, a different place, a different first sentence. Small changes at the threshold are what this sextile is for; the large ones belong to the conjunction.
