A sextile to your Ascendant is the quietest of Saturn's angles to your rising degree. It comes twice in each round, once on the way down from the crossing and once on the way back, and it offers your manner something it may lack: steadiness, on request.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) structures, commits and endures; the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is the way you orient yourself at the start of anything, before you've had time to think. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity. In this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the structures around you can support your way of meeting the world, if you lean on them: an invitation to a role that suits how you come across, a teacher who helps you speak in public, a regular group where your manner gets practice. Nothing is forced, and nothing happens unless you take the offer.

The two sextiles of a round arrive from different parts of the chart. The first, about five years after Saturn crosses your Ascendant, usually comes with Saturn around your 3rd house ([Saturn transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-3rd-house/)): words, learning, the people close by. The second, about five years before the next crossing, comes with Saturn around your 11th ([Saturn transiting the 11th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-11th-house/)): friendships, groups, shared plans. In Placidus, with its unequal houses, "around" is the honest word; your own chart says which house. Because the two sit either side of Saturn's crossing of your Ascendant, they frame it: the second often reads as preparation for that arrival, the first as putting what it taught you to use.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Taking my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, Saturn spends about nine months in this sextile. A loop of three exact hits, about nine and a half months long, is a little more common than a single hit, which can be over in three weeks.

The count by 80 is five for most people and six for a few. The dates hang on your rising degree, and a quarter of an hour's doubt about your birth time moves them by three or four months, since the Ascendant covers a degree in about four minutes and Saturn takes about a month over one. No reading of mine starts without that time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Gemini and Aquarius rising have a sextile under way now, since Saturn is in Aries until April 2028: for Gemini rising it comes from the side of the 11th house, for Aquarius rising from the 3rd. Between the two sextiles of a round lie the other angles: [transiting Saturn square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-ascendant/) near your IC and again near your Midheaven, [transiting Saturn trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-ascendant/) around your 5th and 9th, and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-ascendant/) on your Descendant; [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) opens and closes the round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to one offer that fits the way you'd like to come across: the talk, the role, the introduction. The sextile works through invitations.

Practise one habit of presence on purpose: arriving on time, speaking first, listening longer. Saturn lends it staying power.

Ask someone you trust how you come across, and keep what's useful. A sextile is a good season for honest feedback, gently given.
