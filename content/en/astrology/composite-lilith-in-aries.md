Some bonds are under way before anyone has approved them, sometimes before the two people have quite admitted it to themselves. Composite Lilith in Aries gives a relationship an untamed streak of its own: an impulse that won't wait for permission, and a habit of keeping its first wants out of sight.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the far point of the Moon's orbit, is the part of a chart I read as what was hidden or refused taming. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) moves every body to the midpoint of its two natal places, so composite Lilith is that part for the relationship itself: what the couple, or the friendship, keeps out of view, and what it won't let anyone domesticate. In [Aries](/astrology/aries/), cardinal fire ruled by [Mars](/astrology/mars/), it lives in the first move. Such a bond may have begun faster than family or friends thought wise, decide things before consulting anyone, or bristle when outsiders try to set its pace.

None of this has to be true of either person. Two careful people can make a relationship that won't wait, and each may be startled by how fast they act together. What it tends to hide is its own wanting: a competitive edge between the two, a restlessness it doesn't admit, a no to the outside world said before either partner has thought it through. From inside, it can feel as though the relationship has an appetite neither of you quite owns.

Every sign is about equally likely for composite Lilith: about 1 pair in 12 had it in Aries, in a count of two million pairs of birth dates from 1930 to 2009. Two people born during the same Aries stay of mean Lilith, from September 1984 to June 1985, say, or July 1993 to April 1994, share it, and so does their composite. So can two people born nearly nine years apart. Mean Lilith goes round the zodiac in about 8 years and 10 months: someone born in March 1976 and someone born in January 1985 both have it near 15° Aries, and the midpoint sits there with them. The sign needs both birth dates; the times matter only when the midpoint lies within a few hundredths of a degree of the next sign.

In one birth chart, [Lilith in Aries](/astrology/lilith-in-aries/) is a person's held-back first impulse. In the composite, the impulse belongs to the bond, and each of you may meet it as the other's impatience. The neighbouring signs move the refusal elsewhere: [composite Lilith in Pisces](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-pisces/) keeps a dreamier inner life out of view, while [composite Lilith in Taurus](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-taurus/) digs in where this one charges.

What makes it hard is that a relationship like this can shut doors quickly: on a friend who criticised it, on a plan that bored it, on a disagreement it would rather not have. It helps to say the pair's wants aloud while they are small (*I'd like us to start this*), and to let either of you slow the other down without that counting as disloyalty to the bond.

I don't draw composites in my readings; this is the technique as the astrologers who use it read it, always beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It predicts no quarrel, no parting and no wedding. And since Lilith is what a pair keeps to itself, looking it up is for two adults who both want to know ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
