Venus opposite North Node puts Venus on the South Node, the familiar end of the axis, facing the direction you're growing in. Relating, valuing and making things pleasant are well-practised skills here; the growth asks you to carry them across the chart rather than leave them behind.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a chart that values and relates: attraction, preference, harmony, and the fairness of an exchange. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) holds consolidated, familiar capacity; the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) opposite, a complementary one you develop by practice. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates Venus to the growing end through polarity, and since Venus sits on the South Node, your way of valuing is part of what you start from.

In plain words, you probably know how to be liked, how to keep the peace, how to make a place or an evening pleasant: those come easily. The North Node's capacity, across the chart, may feel less comfortable, a different kind of strength that your Venus skills don't automatically supply. Development doesn't erase what you can already do, and it isn't a judgement on the familiar. The opposition asks for integration: using what you value as a resource while you practise the less familiar thing, and noticing when ease in relating has become a way of not growing.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Venus usually shares the South Node's sign here. With the South Node in Libra, Venus in its own sign there makes a natural diplomat, growing towards the North Node in Aries: deciding for yourself, first. With the South Node in Pisces, Venus exalted there knows compassion and imagination well, and grows towards the North Node in Virgo: discernment, practical care and limits. Without a birth time you would know the signs but not where in your life this axis runs ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

About 1 person in 30 is born with Venus within 6° of an opposition to the North Node, which is the same as a conjunction with the South Node, counted with the true node.

Moving Venus away from the South Node gives the other aspects:

- **[Venus conjunct North Node](/astrology/venus-conjunct-north-node/):** the axis turned the other way, with Venus on the growing end, where relating itself is the new skill.
- **[Venus sextile North Node](/astrology/venus-sextile-north-node/):** Venus a step from the new direction, and trine to the familiar.
- **[Venus square North Node](/astrology/venus-square-north-node/):** Venus between the two ends, pulled on by both.
- **[Venus trine North Node](/astrology/venus-trine-north-node/):** your taste already pointing the way you're growing, with a friendly sextile back to the start.

Between two charts, [Venus opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-north-node-synastry/) is one person's Venus landing on the other's South Node, and it tends to feel like a familiar sweetness, like coming home. I read it from both sides, and ask what the familiarity helps each person grow towards.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-venus/) happens once per nodal cycle, when the South Node in the sky reaches your Venus and the North Node faces it. It lasts a month or two, and the eclipses of that year fall near your Venus and opposite it; astrologers often treat it as a time to sort which habits of relating you'll keep. If this opposition is yours, the two nodal cycles take turns with your Venus: around 9, 28 and 46 the North Node passes over it, and around 18½, 37 and 56 the South Node does.

About once a year, Venus opposes your natal North Node and crosses your natal South Node at the same moment: a day or two that feels familiar.

Weather, not a forecast: a season for sorting, never for giving anything up by force.
