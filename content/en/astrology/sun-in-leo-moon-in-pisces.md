A clear outline surrounds your Leo Sun; your Pisces Moon has hardly any outline at all, and takes in whatever mood is in the room. The full moon had only just passed at your birth, and I read the pair as a bright stage with the sea behind it.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fixed fire and mutable water have nothing in common, and with the Moon five signs behind the Sun they make a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign: two manners that never quite line up, and keep asking for adjustment. The [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) organises itself around a coherent, creative centre that wants to be recognised; the [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/) settles through gentleness, imagination and regular time alone, where the day's moods can drain away. The Sun gives its warmth outward and the Moon soaks things in, and both, in their way, are generous.

I read both of the rulers Pisces has been given: [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern one, is where this Moon's softness and imagination feel most at home, and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the ruler in the older books, is where that softness learns what it's for. Your Sun, in its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), answers to itself, so the centre of the chart stays firm even when the Moon's shoreline shifts. When Neptune and Jupiter are well placed, that openness has somewhere to go, and the Sun gives it a stage.

By the day you arrived, the full moon was up to five days behind you: the lunar month was 15 to 19½ days old and the Moon 181° to 239° along its round from the Sun, still round to the eye, or nearly. About seven of these births in eight belong to the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase, which astrologers who follow Rudhyar stretch over the four days or so after the opposition, and the rest to the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/). Near the start of the range the lights may still hold a [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/), and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) follows the month on from there.

One person in 144 or so has this pair. If the Moon was crossing from Aquarius or into Aries on your birthday, the hour chooses between this page, the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-aquarius/) and the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-aries/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). For a Moon as porous as this one, the houses say where in life you soak things up, and they need that hour as much as the rising sign does.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you're romantic, warm and imaginative, and you give without keeping score. The Leo Sun loves out loud and likes its devotion returned in kind; the Pisces Moon reads a partner's unspoken wishes and can take on their moods as if they were its own. The risk is falling for the version of someone you imagined, or staying on to rescue them. A partner who is kind, steady and clear about what they feel lets both lights rest.

## At work {#at-work}

Creative and caring work suit you, often both at once: performance, music, design, teaching, any role where imagination meets an audience or a person in need. The Sun brings presence and the courage to be seen, the Moon empathy and a feel for what can't be put into words. Noisy, cut-throat workplaces drain the Moon faster than the Sun notices, so keep an hour each day that belongs to nobody but you.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The Sun wants to be seen shining, and the Pisces Moon may be flooded with everyone else's feelings at the very moment it's on stage. You can perform being fine long after you've stopped being fine, and give away more than you have, because giving feels like love. With a quincunx, neither light has a ready way to warn the other. What helps is a ritual of coming back to yourself after every bright day, and boundaries kept as gently as you keep your promises.
