Twice in each twelve-year cycle, Jupiter comes round to a trine with its own birth degree, usually from a sign of the same element. For a few days, or a few months when it loops back, the way you grow is simply in season, and the only risk is not noticing.

## What changes {#what-changes}

A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates two functions through flow while each keeps its own character. Here both are [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/): the one in the sky, widening and looking for meaning now, and the one in your chart, which describes how you have always grown. With the two in the same element, the climate outside suits the habit inside. Born with [Jupiter in Cancer](/astrology/jupiter-in-cancer/), its exaltation, you meet the trines with Jupiter in Scorpio and in Pisces, all three water signs: growth through care and belonging, in a sky that speaks the same language.

The two trines of a cycle come after different events. The first, about four years after a [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/), follows the first square: the plan that was adjusted a year earlier finds its footing and starts to move. The second, about four years before the next return, follows the opposition at the halfway mark, and suits gathering in what the round has produced: a skill now easy, a belief now tested, something you can hand on.

Ease is not inevitability, and flow is not passivity. A trine doesn't push, so nothing changes unless you lean on it; plenty of people only see it in hindsight, as the months when something came together. Your birth time decides the house your Jupiter sits in, and so the part of life where things come together ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Held to my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, a trine that Jupiter crosses without turning lasts about eleven days; when it turns nearby, as in about three [transits](/astrology/transits/) out of ten, three exact hits spread over about eight months.

Fourteen by 80: in the site’s count, nobody born from 1930 to 2009 had more or fewer. Somebody born on 1 January 1975 met the first one at about 3.4, somebody born on 1 January 2005 at about 4.2; across all the births counted, between 2.9 and 4.3. After that, they keep to the cycle, one on the way out from each return and one on the way back.

Each trine sits between two harder moments: [transiting Jupiter square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-jupiter/) a year or so before the first trine and after the second, and [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-jupiter/) two years either side, at the cycle's halfway point. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-jupiter/), the other soft angle, is a smaller version of the same ease, closer to the return.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put the ease into something that will outlast it. A practice set up now, a skill taken one level further, a piece of work finished rather than kept in progress: the trine makes all three cheaper than usual.

Teach what came easily. What your natal Jupiter knows best is often what you notice least, and explaining it to someone else is how you find out how much you have learned since the last return.

If nothing seems to be happening, that may be the point: a stretch without friction is a good moment to rest a conviction instead of defending it, and to see whether it still holds up when nobody is pushing.
