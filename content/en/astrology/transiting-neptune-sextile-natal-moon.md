A sextile from Neptune to your Moon is easy to miss and easy to use. For about two and a half years, the way you look after your feelings has a chance to become gentler, and the chance waits for you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings imagination and compassion, and loosens boundaries that have outlived their use. Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) keeps you steady: needs, attachments, the routines you lean on. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates the two through opportunity, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) doesn't remake your emotional life; it opens a door in it. A habit of comfort that has worn thin can be swapped for one that actually soothes, kindness towards yourself gets easier, and a creative pastime can take over some of the work worry used to do. Nurturing isn't self-sacrifice, and the opening often starts there: in letting some of the care you give others come back towards you.

Habits are where the Moon lives, and a sextile is the right size for changing one: not a new life, just a new evening routine, a different way of winding down, a kinder sentence said to yourself. If [Moon sextile Neptune](/astrology/moon-sextile-neptune/) is in your birth chart, this opening will feel familiar; the transit widens it for a while. Responsiveness isn't passivity either: the sextile helps you answer what you feel, rather than wait for it to pass.

Every two and a half days or so the Moon enters a new sign, so if you were born on such a day, only the birth time says which sign it was in, and so whether this sextile belongs to these years. The house needs the time too: with your Moon in the 6th and Neptune moving through your 4th ([Neptune transiting the 4th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-4th-house/)), the care you put into your days and the comfort you find at home can support each other ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile lasts about two and a half years, or not quite two in the quickest passes. Neptune usually touches the exact degree three times, and in about one pass in four, five. It isn't a dramatic transit, and its work is easiest to see afterwards, in what you no longer reach for.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have had it once, a few twice, and about 1 in 6 never. On Neptune's round it sits between the conjunction ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-moon/)) and the square ([transiting Neptune square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-moon/)); the trine ([transiting Neptune trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-moon/)) and the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-moon/)) lie further on.

Moons in Gemini and Aquarius are the ones this sextile reaches during Neptune's years in Aries, to 2039, the earlier degrees first ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one gentler habit and give it a regular place: a walk by water, music before bed, a page of writing.

Let someone look after you a little. A sextile to the Moon makes receiving care easier than usual.

Notice which old comfort you reach for out of habit, and try the kinder one instead.
