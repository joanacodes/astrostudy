Neptune opposite your Venus sets what you value at one end of the chart and what you idealise at the other, and for about two and a half years the far end often has a face. About half of all people never meet this transit; those who do, meet it once.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is your sense of worth and attraction: what you choose, and what you offer in return. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the longing for something beyond the ordinary, and the compassion that blurs the line between your feelings and someone else's. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, each making the other visible, and under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) that polarity is usually lived through other people.

A partner, a friend or a stranger may seem to embody an ideal you'd stopped looking for, or need your care so much that your own preferences go quiet. You may catch yourself admiring in someone else a gentleness you haven't allowed yourself. The opposition asks a question Venus is built to answer: what do I value here, and what am I hoping for? Once the two are told apart, the hope can stay without having to be the person.

It names no one and ends nothing. I read it as the pull between your own values and other people's dreams while it lasts, never as a forecast of a meeting, a deception or a parting.

In Placidus, the house system I use, opposite houses face each other exactly, so with Venus in your 4th this transit comes while Neptune crosses your 10th ([Neptune transiting the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-10th-house/)): the wish for work or a public life with meaning pulls on what you love at home. Both ends of that axis are drawn from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Venus opposite Neptune](/astrology/venus-opposite-neptune/), you carry this pull all your life; the transit lends it, for a season, to charts that don't have it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The opposition holds for about two years and seven months within my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, and rarely less than twenty-two: time enough for a relationship to go through every season with this question in it. Along the way Neptune faces your Venus exactly three times, or five in about one pass in four.

Almost everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets either this opposition or [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-venus/) before 80, and nobody meets both: the two points lie half a circle apart, and Neptune covers just under half a circle in eighty years. That splits people roughly in two, and about half never meet the opposition.

From Venus in Libra, its own sign, the opposition falls in Aries, where Neptune is from 2026 to 2039; those years bring it to everyone born with Venus in Libra.

Halfway between the conjunction and the opposition lie the squares, [transiting Neptune square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-venus/), about forty-one years from either. [Transiting Neptune trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-venus/) comes about twenty-seven years before or after this opposition, and [transiting Neptune sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-venus/) about fifty-five.

## What helps {#what-helps}

When someone looks like your ideal, write down exactly what you admire. Some of it is theirs; some of it is a part of you waiting to be lived, and that part is yours to keep.

Keep an eye on reciprocity. Care offered freely is lovely, and it stays lovely when it can also be returned.

Ask the people close to you what they hope for, and tell them what you value. Two clear sentences do more than a long silence full of guesses.
