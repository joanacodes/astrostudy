Aries on the 11th house cusp puts a starter among your people. Community, friendship, networks, the future you'd like to build with others: in this house they gather around whoever makes the first move, and that's often you.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

How you belong to a group takes the colour of the sign on the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and with [Aries](/astrology/aries/) belonging is something you do. The friendships that last tend to start with a shared task: a team, a campaign, a club that meets to make something rather than talk about it. You'd rather have allies than admirers, and a group that never gets round to acting loses you, however warm it is. Causes interest you when there's something to do this week, not only something to believe. In a network you're often the one who sets the date, opens the thread or says the plan out loud, and you keep your independence inside the collective; your real friends are the ones who can disagree with you and still turn up on Saturday. Aquarius, the 11th's natural sign, cares for the whole; Aries in its place cares for the whole by giving it a direction.

Tradition called the 11th the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its joy there. With this cusp, the help you find among your own people is usually help to get something started. Cancer rises with it in every London chart and about two New York charts in three, the rest having Gemini, so the person others meet first is often careful and protective, and the push shows once you're among friends. Opposite, Libra opens your 5th: with a group you push, while with a lover or a hobby you lean more towards give and take.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules your 11th. Scorpio, Mars's other sign by tradition, often has no cusp at all in these charts: at London it lies wholly inside the 5th more often than not, and Mars then rules your 11th and nothing else by its cusp, so friendship and shared projects become its main business. Otherwise Scorpio opens the 6th, Mars and Pluto share it, and your allies and your daily work are tied together; I read both rulers. Your alliances tend to form around the part of life where Mars stands, and each placement has its own page: the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Between the Midheaven and the Ascendant, Aries thins out. At London, 1 birth in 13 has it on the Midheaven ([Aries on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/)), 1 in 16 on this cusp (1 in 15 at New York), 1 in 20 on the 12th ([Aries on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-12th-house-cusp/)) and 1 in 27 rising, counting Placidus cusps, the system I use. It never takes a second cusp in these charts. In others it takes none, about 1 London birth in 6, and the 11th is one of the houses where an intercepted Aries most often lies ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). All of it turns with the clock: there's always a birth time, or no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
