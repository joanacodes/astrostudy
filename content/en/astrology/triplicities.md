A triplicity is a triangle drawn inside the zodiac: three signs of one element, each 120° from the next. It's the tradition's name for the element groups, and it carries a layer the modern word doesn't, the planets that rule each triangle by day and by night.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Draw lines between the three fire signs, [Aries](/astrology/aries/), [Leo](/astrology/leo/) and [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), and you get an equilateral triangle. The other elements give three more, each turned one sign further round:

- **Fire:** Aries, Leo, Sagittarius.
- **Earth:** Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn.
- **Air:** Gemini, Libra, Aquarius.
- **Water:** Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces.

Ptolemy called them the triangles, and *triplicity* is the old word for the same groups. The signs of a triplicity share an element, so they share a temperament ([The four elements](/astrology/four-elements/)), and they share a [polarity](/astrology/polarity/): the fire and air triplicities are active, the earth and water ones receptive. What they never share is a modality. Each triangle holds one cardinal sign, one fixed and one mutable, which belong to the other way of grouping the signs, by fours ([Quadruplicities](/astrology/quadruplicities/)).

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Because the signs of a triplicity sit 120° apart, planets in them tend to make [trines](/astrology/trine/), and three planets, one in each sign of a triangle, make a [grand trine](/astrology/grand-trine/). That's the modern use, and it's about ease: the system I built for my readings describes the trine as a flow between functions that reinforce each other, never as luck or automatic success.

The tradition adds rulers. In the scheme handed down by Dorotheus of Sidon, each triplicity has three: one for charts cast by day, one for charts cast by night, and a third that takes part in both.

- **Fire:** the Sun by day, Jupiter by night, Saturn taking part.
- **Earth:** Venus by day, the Moon by night, Mars taking part.
- **Air:** Saturn by day, Mercury by night, Jupiter taking part.
- **Water:** Venus by day, Mars by night, the Moon taking part.

Whether a chart counts as day or night is a matter of sect: a day chart has the Sun above the horizon at birth ([Sect: day and night charts](/astrology/sect-day-and-night-charts/)). A planet in a sign of a triplicity it rules was said to have dignity by triplicity, one of the five old measures of how much at home a planet is, alongside domicile, exaltation, terms and faces ([The table of dignities](/astrology/table-of-dignities/)). Not every author gives the same table, and the differences have a page of their own ([Triplicity rulers](/astrology/triplicity-rulers/)).

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

The first thing a triplicity shows me in a chart is where things flow. Planets spread round one triangle support each other without being asked, and that ease shapes a life: the talent that never needed practice, the subject that always came naturally. It's worth noticing and worth questioning, because ease isn't growth. A grand trine can describe a gift so comfortable that it never gets worked on, and a square or opposition elsewhere in the chart is often what puts it to use.

The second is a temperament with a direction. A chart whose planets gather in the water triangle reads the world through feeling, as the element says, but the triplicity adds where the support comes from, and, for astrologers who work with the rulers, which planet presides over it by day or by night. Both need the full chart: the houses, which come from [the birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), decide which areas of life the triangle connects.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Triplicities and elements are different things.”** They're the same groups of three signs. The element names the temperament; the triplicity is the traditional name, with its rulers.
- **“The fire triplicity is ruled by Mars.”** Mars rules Aries, one sign of it. The rulers of the fire triplicity, in Dorotheus's scheme, are the Sun, Jupiter and Saturn.
- **“A grand trine means a charmed life.”** It means ease between three functions. Ease is a resource, not a guarantee, and it can let a talent sit idle.
- **“Every book gives the same triplicity rulers.”** They don't. Tables differ from one author to another, so it's worth knowing whose you're reading.
