Of the Uranus transits to your Sun, the sextile is the one people most often remember as a decision rather than an event. Nothing knocks at the door: for a year or two, it simply becomes possible to change something on your own terms.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your sense of self and direction, what you're trying to become; [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) experiments, frees and reforms. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) change arrives as an option your purpose can use: a method you've wanted to try, people who think differently, a way of organising your life that gives you more room without asking you to start again. Opportunity needs engagement. Left alone, the sextile is a pleasant restlessness; taken up, it can turn your direction by a few useful degrees. Because it asks rather than insists, it's also the Uranus transit most easily wasted: twenty months can pass in curiosity alone if no single experiment gets a start date.

The house your Sun sits in shows what you'd be changing, and the house Uranus is crossing, usually two houses away, where the idea comes from. With your Sun in the 1st, Uranus may be moving through your 3rd, and conversation, study or a sibling's example supplies the spark ([Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/)). Which houses those are depends on the minute you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Sun sextile Uranus](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus/), you know this kind of change as a habit; the transit gives you a season of it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

For about twenty months Uranus holds the sextile, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use. Nine passes in ten, and more, are loops with three exact hits, and the shortest still take about eleven months.

Most people have it twice by 80, and everyone at least once. The two sextiles fall either about 28 years apart, either side of Uranus's crossing of your Sun ([transiting Uranus conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-sun/)), or about 56 years apart, depending on where Uranus stood at your birth. In the second case, [transiting Uranus square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-sun/), [transiting Uranus trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-sun/) and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-sun/) lie in between.

Uranus is in Gemini until 2033, after a first visit in 2025, and Gemini is sextile to Aries and Leo: born roughly 21 March to 19 April, or 23 July to 22 August, you have this sextile in these years, earlier for birthdays early in your sign. Pisces and Cancer Suns had theirs while Uranus crossed Taurus, from 2018 to 2026; Taurus and Virgo Suns come next, from 2032.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick one change you've been circling and give it a trial with a start date. With a sextile, starting is most of the effort; what follows tends to come more easily.

Look for people already doing what you're curious about. Uranus's openings often arrive through someone a step ahead.

Keep what works while you try the new. This transit favours adjustments that leave your foundations standing.
