Your colleagues tend to become your allies, and your allies your colleagues. With the ruler of your 10th house in the 11th, the work you're known for grows out of friendships, groups and causes you share with other people.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Through the sign on your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), which in Placidus, the house system I use, opens the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), the 10th has a ruler, and that planet carries your vocation, standing and reputation into its own house ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). It lives in the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), tradition's Good Spirit: the friends you choose, groups and networks, the people who help you without having to, and the future you'd like to build with them.

Your vocation keeps good company. Careers here tend to run on networks: the job found through a friend, the business built with a circle of peers, the professional association you end up chairing. Many people with this placement work for a cause, a union, a charity, a movement, a community, and are known for what they've built with others more than for what they've done alone. Horary astrology also gave the 11th wishes and hopes, so the work tends to point at a future, not just a promotion. The risk lies in the overlap. When friends are colleagues and colleagues are friends, a falling-out at work costs twice, and loyalty can keep you in a group you’ve outgrown.

The 11th follows the 10th the way a team follows a decision: what you start in public, the 11th carries on with other people. Many people with this placement find that their most lasting work is the network itself, the group still meeting long after the project that brought it together.

Saturn makes a particular link here. With Capricorn on the Midheaven and Aquarius on the 11th cusp, Saturn rules both houses by tradition, those being its two signs, and a Saturn in the 11th sits in a house it rules: career and community answer to one patient planet ([Saturn](/astrology/saturn/)). Venus, ruling a Taurus or Libra Midheaven from here, makes work out of friendship and shared taste. The Moon, ruling a Cancer Midheaven, gives colleagues something of a household’s warmth.

Like everything between the eastern horizon and the Midheaven, this ruler was still rising through the sky at your birth, and the minute decides how far it had got ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Had it got further, it would sit in the 10th, its own house, at the helm of your public life ([Ruler of the 10th in the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)). Not so far, and it would be in the 12th, where your work happens out of sight ([Ruler of the 10th in the 12th house](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)).

An Aquarius Midheaven brings Uranus in beside Saturn ([Aquarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Uranus in the 11th is at home by the natural zodiac’s reckoning, since Aquarius, its sign, belongs to that house: the reformer, the inventor of new ways of working together ([Uranus in the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-in-11th-house/)). Whichever of the two sits here, your public life is tied to the groups that share your future.
