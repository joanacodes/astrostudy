The Pink moon is the name almanacs give April's full moon, and it comes from a flower, not from the colour of the Moon or from astrology. In a chart, it is a full moon in Libra or in Scorpio, and that's how I read it.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Between the [Worm moon](/astrology/worm-moon/) of March and the [Flower moon](/astrology/flower-moon/) of May, April's [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) rises opposite a Sun in Aries or early Taurus. Its dates over the next few years, in Universal Time:

- **2026:** 2 April, 02:12 UT, at 12°21′ Libra
- **2027:** 20 April, 22:27 UT, at 0°37′ Scorpio
- **2028:** 9 April, 10:27 UT, at 20°06′ Libra
- **2029:** 28 April, 10:37 UT, at 8°26′ Scorpio
- **2030:** 18 April, 03:20 UT, at 28°09′ Libra

In April, Paris runs two hours ahead of UT and New York four hours behind: the 2026 Pink moon was still the evening of 1 April across North America.

The Sun spends most of April in Aries and moves into Taurus around the 20th, so the Pink moon falls in Libra when it comes early and in Scorpio when it comes late. The 2027 one shows how fine the line can be: at 0°37′ Scorpio, it has crossed out of Libra by just over half a degree, because the Sun opposite has only just entered Taurus.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

The pink is a plant's. NASA's full moon guide, following the Maine Farmer's Almanac, which began printing names for the full moon of each month in the 1930s, explains that the name comes from moss pink, also called creeping phlox or *Phlox subulata*. It's a low-growing wildflower of eastern North America, and one of the earliest to spread across the ground in spring.

The almanac gives April other names, all about the season rather than the sky: the Sprouting Grass Moon, the Egg Moon, and the Fish Moon, for the shad that swim upstream to spawn at this time of year. None of them says anything about the Moon's colour either.

As for the colour you actually see, it's the same as every month. A full moon close to the horizon can look yellow, orange or rosy, because its light crosses more of the atmosphere on the way to you, and it whitens as it climbs.

The name goes with the month: the full moon that falls in April takes it, whatever its sign. Because the Moon's cycle is about 29½ days, April's full moon can fall on any day of the month, from the 2nd in 2026 to the 28th in 2029.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

A North American wildflower tells me nothing about your chart, so let me say it plainly: the Pink moon is a folk and almanac name, and astrology doesn't read names. I read the full moon, which in April is either the [full moon in Libra](/astrology/full-moon-in-libra/), the Moon in Libra and the Sun in Aries, or the [full moon in Scorpio](/astrology/full-moon-in-scorpio/), the Moon in Scorpio and the Sun in Taurus. Those are different axes of the zodiac, and they speak to different parts of a chart.

What makes a full moon personal is contact. I count it only when it lands within one degree of something in your chart, at the Moon's end of the line or the Sun's: the 2027 Pink moon, for example, on a planet at 0° Scorpio or 0° Taurus. Even then, it's a marker inside something a transit or progression has already set going, a short focus rather than a forecast.

And for the house it falls in, which is where it would touch your life, I need the time you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Libra and Scorpio can sit in any house, depending on that time.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The Pink moon is pink.”** It's named after a flower. The Moon keeps its usual colours: warm near the horizon, white high in the sky.
- **“The Pink moon is a full moon for love.”** Pink suggests romance, but the name belongs to a wildflower. Astrology reads its sign, Libra or Scorpio, and the house it falls in.
- **“It's always the first full moon of spring.”** Not when March's full moon comes after the equinox: in 2027 and 2029 the Worm moon gets there first.
