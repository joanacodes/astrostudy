A Pisces Sun takes the world in through feeling and imagination; a Capricorn Moon feels safe once there is a plan and someone responsible for it, usually you. You were born in the last week of the lunar month, around the last quarter or just after it, with the Moon shrinking in the morning sky. Here I see compassion that wants something solid to stand on.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and cardinal earth, Capricorn two signs behind Pisces: a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) by sign, and two elements that suit each other, earth giving water a shape and water softening earth. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) forms a self from devotion and imagination; the [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) steadies itself by taking charge, and lets a feeling in once the situation is secure. You may sit with someone's distress with great tenderness, then quietly make the phone calls that sort it out.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules Capricorn, so it speaks for your Moon, and its sign and house show where you go to feel in control again. Saturn is one of [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#saturn) 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook, so I give it my closest attention here, and I read it as a timetable, not a wall. For Pisces I read both rulers, [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) from the tradition and [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) from modern tropical astrology. Jupiter has its fall in Capricorn, which leaves the older ruler of your Sun's sign uneasy in your Moon's: I read generosity that learns to count its cost, not generosity held back. Tradition also puts the Moon in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) here, opposite its home in Cancer, and I read feelings that look after themselves by looking after things; your Sun, in Pisces, has neither dignity nor debility.

The next new moon came two and a half to about seven days after your birth, with the Moon trailing the Sun by 31° to 89°. About seven of these births in eight fall in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) phase, the rest in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), and the exact waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/), with the Moon 60° behind, lies inside the range: an alliance for completing things and clearing them away. The last quarter phase turns inward to decide what stays, and a Capricorn Moon decides by asking what will hold. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows where the last quarter hands over to the balsamic phase.

Roughly 1 person in 145 has a Pisces Sun with a Capricorn Moon. On a birthday when the Moon moved from Sagittarius into Capricorn, or on into Aquarius, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-sagittarius/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-aquarius/) stay just as possible until the hour is found ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). That hour also puts Saturn in one of your houses, and the rising sign and the houses are where a chart stops being general and becomes yours, so it is the first thing I ask for.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love by turning up: reliably, practically, for years. The Pisces Sun brings tenderness and forgiveness; the Capricorn Moon shows care through responsibility, a problem solved, a promise kept. The risk is managing a partner's feelings, and your own, instead of sharing them, and postponing rest until everything is in order, which it never quite is. Let someone see you tired; for a Capricorn Moon that is a real act of trust.

## At work {#at-work}

Work where compassion needs organising suits you: running a care service or a charity, social work, nursing management, teaching music, conservation, planning places where people heal or gather. The Sun gives the work its meaning, the Moon its structure and its stamina. You may end up in charge without asking to be; keep some room in the job for the imagination that started it all.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Every feeling the Pisces Sun takes in can become a task for the Capricorn Moon. Someone's sadness turns into your project, a family worry into your responsibility, and the list grows until your own needs are filed under "later". What helps is treating rest as one of the obligations: put it in the diary and keep it like any other appointment. Born so near the end of the lunar month, this pair does best finishing things and letting them go, not taking on more.
