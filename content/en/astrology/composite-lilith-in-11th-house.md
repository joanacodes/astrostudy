Invited everywhere as a pair and never quite at home in any of the groups that invite them: some couples, and some friendships, live like that for years, and rather like it. Composite Lilith in the 11th house describes that place among other people, and the hopes for the future that go with it.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Friends, clubs, colleagues met for a cause, the people you plan a future with, and the future itself: all of that falls to [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), a succedent house with Aquarius as its natural sign. Tradition named it the Good Spirit, and Jupiter has its joy there. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) reads it as the relationship's circle, and the future the pair hopes for. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) there, the point I read as hidden and untamed, keeps the pair from slotting into a crowd the way people expect. It may move between groups without joining any, gather a few friends as unconventional as itself, and keep its real plans for the world, the cause it cares about, the life it wants in ten years, out of ordinary conversation. What it won't do is buy its place in a circle with silence.

The misfit streak can be the bond's alone. Two sociable people, each at ease in a crowd, can find that as a pair they don't quite fit any of their old circles, and each may wonder whether the other is the one making things awkward. Neither is: the refusal belongs to the relationship.

Of all the houses, this is one where the second method shows its dependence on place. Houses built from the composite Midheaven are worked out for the latitude where the pair lives, and the cusps between the angles, the 11th's among them, shift if you move north or south; the first method, from the midpoints of your two charts' cusps, doesn't change with an address. Both need both birth times, and a Lilith near the 11th's edges is read with that in mind ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Natal [Lilith in the 11th house](/astrology/lilith-in-11th-house/) describes a self kept back to stay included, and [their Lilith in your 11th house](/astrology/lilith-in-11th-house-synastry/) someone who never fitted a crowd entering your circles. The composite version is the pair's own place among others. On the public side of it, [composite Lilith in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-10th-house/) does its untamed work in plain view; on the private side, [composite Lilith in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-12th-house/) withdraws it out of sight altogether.

The difficulty is a pair that grows lonely together, or proud of not belonging. It helps to say one shared hope aloud to friends who might help with it, and to look after the few people who see the bond as it is, rather than the many who would prefer it different.

I explain composites here without drawing them; their users read this Lilith next to both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it predicts no falling-out and no loss of friends. A chart of how two people stand among others should only be drawn when both of them agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
