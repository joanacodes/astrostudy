Cancer on the 9th house cusp opens the far horizon of the chart in the sign of home. Study, travel and belief tend to start from somewhere you belong, and to come back to it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is where you look for meaning, in miles or in thought, and the sign on its cusp is the way you set out ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) sets out carrying home with it. You tend to learn best from a teacher you trust, and to stay loyal to the school, the tradition or the book that first opened a subject for you. A belief is something you belong to as much as something you think: a community, a language, a way of marking the year. Travel often means going towards someone, or towards the place a family came from, and the journeys that stay with you are the ones where a stranger's kitchen felt like your own. You may study your own roots: history, a grandparent's language, the songs of a region. The hard part is that a belief held like family can make questioning it feel like disloyalty; you can protect what you believe without fencing it in.

Tradition exalts Jupiter, the 9th's own planet by nature, in Cancer, so the house of faith and far horizons opens here in the sign where its planet is most honoured ([Jupiter in Cancer](/astrology/jupiter-in-cancer/)). The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules the house, and its house shows where your search for meaning plays out: the [1st](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). [Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/) comes with this cusp in nearly every chart at London and New York, so Pluto and Mars rule the chart, and I read both; Leo is usually on the Midheaven, in about 7 London charts in 10, and the Sun rules your public life. Opposite, Capricorn opens the 3rd, so your everyday thinking runs on Saturn's care and structure while your beliefs run on feeling.

About 1 birth in 12 has Cancer on the 9th house cusp, at London as at New York, counting Placidus cusps, the system I use: about two hours of each day, the even share. The 9th is wide here, close to 40° at London, so Cancer never opens the 8th or the 10th as well, and in about 1 London chart in 3 the whole of Leo lies inside the 9th too ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The Sun, which tradition gave its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) in this house, the God, then has a say in it. [Cancer on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/) and [Cancer on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/) come mostly with Sagittarius rising for the one and Libra for the other, and describe other charts.

A Scorpio Ascendant and a Cancer 9th both hang on the minute of birth, and I wait for it before I say anything about your chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
