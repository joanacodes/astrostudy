Cancer on the 9th house cusp opens the far horizon of the chart in the sign of home. Study, travel and belief tend to start from somewhere you belong, and to come back to it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

You look for meaning in the [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), in miles or in thought, and its cusp sign is how you set out ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) sets out carrying home with it. You tend to learn best from a teacher you trust, and to stay loyal to the school, the tradition or the book that first opened a subject for you. A belief is something you belong to as much as something you think: a community, a language, a way of marking the year. Travel often means going towards someone, or towards the place a family came from, and the trips that stay with you are the ones where a stranger's kitchen felt like your own. Pilgrimage, in the old sense of travelling towards something that matters, describes many of your travels better than tourism does. You may study your own roots: history, a grandparent's language, the songs of a region. The hard part is that a belief held like family can make questioning it feel like disloyalty; you can protect what you believe without fencing it in.

Tradition exalts Jupiter, the 9th's own planet by nature, in Cancer, so the house of faith and far horizons opens here in the sign where its planet is most honoured ([Jupiter in Cancer](/astrology/jupiter-in-cancer/)). The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules the house, and its own house is the ground where belief becomes practice: the [1st](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). With this cusp, nearly every chart at London and New York has Scorpio on the Ascendant ([Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/)), which brings in Pluto and Mars as chart rulers; I read both, and together they describe someone who wants to know a belief from the inside before taking it on. Leo is usually on the Midheaven, in about 7 London charts in 10, and the Sun rules your public life. Opposite, Capricorn opens the 3rd, so your everyday thinking runs on Saturn's care and structure while your beliefs run on feeling.

On the 9th, Cancer gets its even share of the day, about two hours, which in Placidus is about 1 birth in 12 at either latitude. Close to 40° wide at London, the 9th leaves Cancer on one cusp only, but often has room for a second sign: in about 1 London chart in 3, all of Leo is inside it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and the Sun, which tradition gave its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) in this house, the God, then has a say in it. A Scorpio Ascendant sits between the Sagittarius of [Cancer on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/) and the Libra of [Cancer on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/), so neither page is yours.

A Scorpio Ascendant and a Cancer 9th both hang on the minute of birth, and I wait for it before I say anything about your chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
