At the full moon in Leo the two lights trade places: the Moon shines in Leo, the Sun's own sign, while the Sun sits opposite in Aquarius. It comes once a year, in the depth of the northern winter, and asks how much of you is on show, and how much belongs to the group.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

Every [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) is a face-off, the Moon opposite the Sun and lit in full. Here the Moon is in [Leo](/astrology/leo/), where feelings want warmth, recognition and room to play, and the Sun is at the same degree of [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), steering the month towards friends, groups and causes larger than one person. Tradition puts the Sun in its detriment in Aquarius, the sign opposite its home.

The Leo–Aquarius axis runs between the heart and the crowd: your need to be seen and loved as yourself, and your wish to belong to something fair and shared. This full moon often shows where one has been starving the other, the self kept small for the group's sake, or the group neglected while you shone. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo; Aquarius has two rulers, Uranus and Saturn, and I read both.

It's a short marker, all the same. Only when the Moon's degree or the Sun's lands on a planet or angle of yours, a degree either way, does it count, and then as the point where something a transit or a progression has started becomes visible. On its own, it predicts nothing.

The most recent shone on 1 February 2026, at 13°04′ Leo. The next comes on 22 January 2027 at 12:17 UT, at 2°14′, then 10 February 2028 at 21°24′, 30 January 2029 at 10°38′ and 18 February 2030 at 29°38′. These years bring several eclipses to the Leo–Aquarius axis, but none at a full moon in Leo.

It shares its month with the [new moon in Aquarius](/astrology/new-moon-in-aquarius/), an annular solar eclipse in 2026, 2027 and 2028: on 6 February 2027, two weeks after this full moon. The [full moon in Cancer](/astrology/full-moon-in-cancer/) precedes it by a month, the [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/) follows.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The Moon lands in the house holding its Leo degree, the Sun across the chart in your Aquarius house. Your birth time sets both, and I won't place them without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)); my houses are Placidus. There, Leo on the 5th cusp puts this full moon on its own ground, since the 5th is Leo's natural house and the 11th Aquarius's.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Leo rising: you want to be seen, warmly and as yourself, while a partner asks for space and equality.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** spending on what makes you proud or happy, set against shared money that has to work for everyone.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** you speak with your heart in it, while the Sun in the 9th turns your mind to ideas and causes.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** home as a place to be loved and celebrated, while your public role asks for teamwork and new ideas.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** Leo's own house: a creative project, a romance, a pleasure that's yours alone, set against your friends' plans.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** pride in your daily work comes to a head, while something quieter asks you to step back from the stage.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Leo on the Descendant: a partner wants attention and warmth, while you guard your independence.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** generosity in shared money or intimacy, set against your own values and the freedom to keep them.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a belief you're proud of, or a journey that feels like a celebration, while daily talk stays cool and curious.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Leo on the Midheaven: recognition at work comes into view, while home asks for a fairer share of your time.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** friends who cheer you on, a group you could lead, set against a love or a project of your own.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a private longing to be appreciated, while the working day asks you to serve the team.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Find the degree at both ends.** Anything of yours, planet or angle, sitting within a degree of the positions above, in Leo or at the same degree of Aquarius: that is the only place this full moon really lands.
- **Give each side its due.** Something this month that's yours to be proud of, and something you do with or for other people.
- **Read the three rulers together.** The Sun's house shows where you need to shine; the houses of Uranus and Saturn, where you need to belong and on what terms.
