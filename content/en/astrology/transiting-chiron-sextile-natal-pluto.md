People born in 1945 and people born in 1995 had this sextile before their first birthday. Both groups arrived close to one of Chiron's perihelions, in 1945 and 1996, when it is nearest the Sun and moves fastest, so it reached the angle almost at once; for most other births the wait runs to years, sometimes decades.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) goes to the root: it clears out what has stopped working, gives back agency where it was lost, and renews. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is practical about hurt: it looks for what still works around a limit. With a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) between them the relation is one of opportunity: in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) depth becomes available without being forced on you.

The opening is often modest and very concrete. A book that finally names what happened. A friend who went through the same thing and is willing to talk. A group, a course, a therapist with a free slot. Or simply the energy, for once, to sort out a corner of life you have kept closed because touching it hurt. Pluto's blind spot is underestimating gradual change, and the sextile is all gradual: a coping habit retired one step at a time, a decision about the sore spot taken back from whoever made it for you.

Depth has an address in your chart, the house of your natal Pluto; the offer arrives from the house Chiron is crossing, about two houses away. With [Pluto in the 11th house](/astrology/pluto-in-11th-house/), a sextile from Chiron in your 1st can let a group see the part of you that you usually keep back. Both houses are fixed by the minute of birth; when it is missing, the sextile has no address and the chart goes unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A planet sextile your Pluto on Chiron's side receives Chiron's conjunction in the same months, and one trine your Pluto from the far side receives its opposition. If you were born with [Pluto sextile Chiron](/astrology/pluto-sextile-chiron/), in 1930–34, 1945–46 or 1994–95, the opening is one your chart already knows.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A loop gives the opening three visits: Chiron reaches the sextile, slips back across it and returns, about ten and a half months from first to last. Nearly two passes in three are built that way. About a third are a single visit of some three weeks, and one in twenty stretches to five visits over about twenty-one months. These figures use the orbs I time everything with, 1½° before exact and 1° after.

The first sextile comes in the first weeks of life for some and at nearly 40 for others: about 10 for people born in 1935, under 1 for 1945, about 32½ for 1955, about 25 for 1965, about 17 for 1975, about 9 for 1985, under 1 again for 1995 and about 4 for 2005. Over a life to 80 that makes three for most people, and two or four for the others. In the next decade Chiron in Gemini, from 2033, sextiles the Leo Pluto of people born between 1937 and 1958, and Chiron in Cancer, from 2038, the Virgo Pluto of people born between 1956 and 1972.

The first sextile of a round follows [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-pluto/) and leads to [transiting Chiron square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-pluto/). The second comes on the way back from [transiting Chiron opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-pluto/), after the second [transiting Chiron trine natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-pluto/) and the second square.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose the depth: one conversation, one page of writing, one session with someone trained to listen. A sextile is satisfied by a step, and you set its size.

Take back one small decision about the sore spot that you had handed to someone else.

Pass something on, a book, a name, an hour, to a person a few steps behind you on the same road.
