Most of the eclipses that fall in Taurus are full moons: between 2000 and 2040, eight of its thirteen are lunar, with the Moon in Taurus and the Sun across the sky in Scorpio. Taurus likes things to last, and an eclipse there asks what, exactly, you mean to keep.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) is fixed earth, ruled by Venus: continuity, care for what you have, the steady pace that lets things last. Its eclipses sit on the Taurus–Scorpio axis, what is yours and what is shared, and they alternate with Scorpio’s as the Moon’s nodes move. A [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) here, like the partial one of 28 October 2023 at 5°03′ Taurus, puts the full Moon in Taurus and the Sun in Scorpio; a [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/), like the annular one due on 9 May 2032 at 19°28′, puts both lights together in Taurus.

None of that matters to a chart unless it lands on something. The AGS rule I work with counts an eclipse only within about three degrees of a natal planet or angle, at either end of its axis, read through that point and its house, as emphasis rather than omen. The next Taurus eclipse, on 30 October 2031 at 6°47′, is penumbral, and a penumbral lunar eclipse weighs less under that rule. Natal nodes near these degrees count too, and for them I check both the mean and the true position.

From 2000 to 2040, in my list:

- **2002–2004:** 20 November 2002, penumbral lunar, 27°39′ · 9 November 2003, total lunar, 16°15′ · 28 October 2004, total lunar, 5°00′
- **2013–2014:** 10 May 2013, annular solar, 19°31′ · 29 April 2014, partial solar, 8°51′
- **2021–2023:** 19 November 2021, partial lunar, 27°17′ · 30 April 2022, partial solar, 10°28′ · 8 November 2022, total lunar, 15°59′ · 28 October 2023, partial lunar, 5°03′
- **2031–2032:** 30 October 2031, penumbral lunar, 6°47′ · 9 May 2032, annular solar, 19°28′
- **2040:** 11 May 2040, partial solar, 21°04′ · 18 November 2040, total lunar, 27°01′

The pattern keeps a nineteen-year beat: 28 October 2004 at 5°00′, then 28 October 2023 at 5°03′; 19 November 2021 at 27°17′, then 18 November 2040 at 27°01′. The neighbouring signs have their own eclipse pages ([Eclipse in Aries](/astrology/eclipse-in-aries/), [Eclipse in Gemini](/astrology/eclipse-in-gemini/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Taurus can sit on any house, and only [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) says which; that is why I won’t read a chart without one. Each line, in Placidus, the house system I use, describes an eclipse in Taurus meeting a natal point in that house; the Scorpio end of the axis reaches the house opposite.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/taurus-rising/):** Taurus rising: the steadiness people see in you; what you want to keep about the way you live.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/taurus-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** Taurus on its natural house: what you own and look after, weighed for what it’s worth to you.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/taurus-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** patient words and familiar routes; a slow conversation that settles something.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as a base that lasts; the rooms, the garden, the family rhythm, tended.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/taurus-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasures that take time; a craft, a romance or a pastime you’ve stuck with.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/):** routines and steady work; the habits that keep your days running. Tradition named this house Bad Fortune and tied it to illness; I keep to the shape of your days and never read a diagnosis there ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).
7. **[7th house](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Taurus on the Descendant, with Scorpio rising: a partner who steadies you; loyalty, and what each of you contributes.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/taurus-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what you hold jointly; agreements about keeping and letting go.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/taurus-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs held for the long run; a teaching or a place you keep returning to.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/taurus-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a career built slowly; your name for reliability.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/taurus-on-11th-house-cusp/):** long friendships; the groups that last, and what you bring to them.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/taurus-on-12th-house-cusp/):** quiet attachments; what you hold onto in private, looked at calmly.
