A Sagittarius Sun is made for setting out; a Taurus Moon is made for keeping what's worth keeping. You were born as the Moon swelled towards full, a few days short of it. I read the pair as a self that keeps leaving and needs that keep things going, learning to travel with roots.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Five signs separate the two lights, so they share neither element nor modality: mutable fire for the Sun, fixed earth for the Moon, a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, the link that asks for recalibration rather than offering ease or friction. The [Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/) grows by widening its view, and the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) feels safe when something lasts: the same bakery for fifteen years, a garden built up a season at a time, a friendship looked after every week. One part of you is planning a year abroad while another is still repotting the plants.

The Moon is in its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/): Ptolemy gives Taurus as the sign where it is honoured, and I read that as needs that are clear and hard to shake, a fixed point the travelling Sun can come back to. The Sun has no dignity in Sagittarius. The rulers repeat the pattern: [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), for your Sun, widens and looks for meaning; [Venus](/astrology/venus/), for your Moon, chooses what is worth keeping. Venus's sign and house show what steadies you, Jupiter's where you go looking for more.

The full moon was a few days off. Your Moon had moved 120° to 180° ahead of the Sun, ten to almost fifteen days after the new moon, so about seven births in eight sit in the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) as eight phases count it, and the earliest few in the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/). At the very start of the range the lights can make a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/); near 150°, about twelve days in, the quincunx by sign is exact. I read a gibbous birth as a time for fine-tuning before the full light, apt for a pair whose daily work is adjustment, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) places it in the month.

About 1 person in 150 has this pair. When the Moon crossed from Aries into Taurus, or from Taurus into Gemini, on your birthday, only the hour can choose between this page, the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-aries/) and the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-gemini/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). That hour also gives the rising sign and the houses, where the leaving and the keeping actually happen.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you bring loyalty and a taste for adventure, which is rarer than it sounds. The Taurus Moon attaches slowly and for good, and shows care by turning up, unchanged, week after week; the Sagittarius Sun wants a partner who'll say yes to the unknown city. Trouble starts when one light decides for the other: the trip booked before the Moon has caught up, or the Moon needing a week to absorb a plan the Sun announced in a minute. A partner who can be both your fixed address and your fellow traveller suits you, and so does saying which of the two you need this month.

## At work {#at-work}

You can carry a big idea and build something durable from it, which is this pair's particular gift. The Sun supplies the vision, the teaching, the long view; the Moon steadies it with patience, upkeep and a sense of what will last. Work that reaches far from a solid base suits you: a school, a publisher, a farm that hosts visitors, a practice with roots and a wide reach. Constant upheaval tires the Moon; a job that never changes bores the Sun.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The two lights keep different calendars. The Sun's year is full of departures; the Moon needs rhythm and repetition to stay steady, so a life that keeps moving can leave you unmoored without knowing why, and a life that stays put can feel airless. Neither life is the wrong one. What helps is continuity you can carry: the morning routine that comes with you, the person you call every Sunday from wherever you are, a home you can leave and come back to. Give the Moon its rhythm and the Sun can go further.
