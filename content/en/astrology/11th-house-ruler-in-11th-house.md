Most house rulers leave home to do their work. This one stays: the planet that rules your 11th house sits in the 11th, among the people and causes it speaks for, and the whole business of belonging is conducted on its own ground.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) gathers the people you pick and the plans you make with them: friends, allies, communities, the causes that give an evening its purpose. Usually a house's ruler carries its matters elsewhere and ties them to another part of life ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). Here the thread loops back on itself. Your friendships aren't a means to anything; they're the point.

People with this placement often keep friendship near the centre of life, the way others keep a career or a family. A circle can last for decades, a group can be the place you feel most like yourself, and plans tend to be made in the plural. You may be the one who keeps a society going, organises the reunion, notices who hasn't been heard from. The risk is the one any self-contained house carries: the group can become the whole world, and hopes shared with everyone may never become your own. The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) opposite, private joy and love, is worth leaving room for.

In [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, the 11th holds what rose between two and four of its own daytime hours before you were born, so a ruler here stood high in the east, still short of the meridian. Whether it sits in the sign on the cusp or in the next sign along matters: in the cusp's sign it's in its own sign too ([domicile](/astrology/domicile/)), at home twice over.

Jupiter has the strongest claim on this house. With Sagittarius on the cusp, or Pisces through its traditional ruler, Jupiter rules your 11th and sits in its joy there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), the old planet of generosity in the house tradition called the Good Spirit ([Jupiter in the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house/)): kindness runs both ways in your circle, and help tends to come from the people you've helped. Aquarius on the cusp is the modern match, the sign the natural zodiac pairs with the 11th, though the old astrologers made no such link, since Saturn, Aquarius's traditional ruler, wasn't thought at ease in Jupiter's house. I read both of its rulers ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 11th keeps a few friendships for life and takes its duties to a group seriously, while Uranus, wherever it is, wants the circle to stay open to change.

Both cusps framing the house hang on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A ruler sitting just inside the 11th cusp needed only a little more time to cross into the 10th, where your circle and your career meet ([Ruler of the 11th in the 10th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)); one near the 12th cusp, with a slightly earlier birth, would have kept to the quiet of the 12th, where your circle keeps a lower profile ([Ruler of the 11th in the 12th house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)).
