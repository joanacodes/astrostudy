Moon conjunct Fomalhaut needs a clock as much as a calendar. The Moon passes the degree of the Southern Fish's star, early in Pisces, once a month, and the few hours it spends within reach of it can fall on any date of the year.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) as regulation: what you need in order to feel safe, the habits that settle you, the people and places you lean on when things shake. [Fomalhaut](/astrology/fomalhaut/) arrives with a picture and a title rather than a planetary nature. The title is the royal one astrology books give it, on thin evidence. The picture is Greek: a great fish swallowing the water that Aquarius pours out of his jar. Set beside the Moon, it becomes an image of taking in whatever flows your way, and a reading that used stars would lean on it. I keep it as an image and nothing more, since stars were never among the placements I tested.

What does describe you is the sign. For births from 1930 to 2009 the window ran from about 2° to 5° Pisces, so the Moon on Fomalhaut is always a [Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/), in the sign's first degrees, where needs tend to be met through imagination and fellow feeling.

The Moon moves about 13° a day and crosses the two-degree window in three to four hours. On a day when it reaches the star, a few hours make the difference between having the contact and missing it. It is the plainest case I know for my rule, no birth time, no reading: without the hour, nobody can say whether you have this contact at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The same hour places the Moon in a house, and in Placidus, the house system I use, a few minutes can move it across a cusp.

In the sky, the Moon never comes close to this star. Its path tilts about five degrees either side of the Sun's, while Fomalhaut sits 21° south, so on its monthly pass the Moon goes by somewhere between 16° and 26° north of it. The Moon can slide in front of some bright stars; Fomalhaut is out of its reach.

The phase ties some passes to a season. A full Moon on Fomalhaut needs the Sun opposite, in the first degrees of Virgo, so it belongs to a [full moon in Pisces](/astrology/full-moon-in-pisces/) falling in the last days of August.

About 1 person in 184 born between 1930 and 2009 has the Moon within a degree of the star, as near the average for a one-degree window as makes no difference. Of the five contacts this star receives, the Moon's is the only one any birthday can carry: the [Sun conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/sun-conjunct-fomalhaut/) is tied to late February, [Venus conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/venus-conjunct-fomalhaut/) to winter and early spring, [Mars conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/mars-conjunct-fomalhaut/) to particular years, and [Saturn conjunct Fomalhaut](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-fomalhaut/) to a few seasons in three decades.

Stars have no place in the system I built for my readings, so Fomalhaut stays on this page and out of them; the Moon's sign, house and aspects are what I'd read. The rule that astrologers who do use stars follow is in [conjunctions to fixed stars](/astrology/conjunctions-to-fixed-stars/).
