Venus has charge of both lights here, and tradition treats them very differently: your Moon is at its exaltation in Taurus, your Sun in its fall in Libra. I read two kinds of good taste in one person, one that wants things to be fair and one that wants them to last.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Libra is cardinal air and Taurus fixed earth: no shared element, no shared mode, and five signs between them, a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, which I read as a constant small adjustment between parts that don't come naturally to each other. The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) will reopen any question that fairness asks it to; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) settles when the question stays closed, the routine kept, the furniture where it was. You can renegotiate the terms of almost anything and still need your evenings to stay the same.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules both signs, so a single planet answers for your sense of direction and for what steadies you, and its sign and house are the first place I'd look. Ptolemy gives Taurus as the Moon's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), the sign where it is most at ease, while Libra is the Sun's fall. I read that contrast plainly: your needs know what they want, and your sense of self takes shape more slowly, by comparing and weighing. When a decision drags on, the Taurus Moon is often what brings it to rest.

Your birth came after a full moon, by up to five days: the Moon was 180° to 240° ahead of the Sun, in a lunar month about 15 to 20 days old. In the eight-phase count the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase lasts until the Moon is 225° ahead, so it takes nearly all these births; only the latest, about one in eight, reach the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/). Around 210°, in the middle of the range, the lights make a quincunx by degree as well. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains where one phase ends and the next begins.

Roughly 1 person in 145 is born with Venus ruling both lights in this way. The Moon crosses Taurus in two and a half days or so, and on the day it came in from Aries, or went on into Gemini, only the hour can tell this page from the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-aries/) and the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-gemini/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same hour sets the rising sign and the houses, and with Venus answering for both lights, its house is the one I'd most want to see. Like every house, it needs the time.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You bring both grace and constancy to love. The Libra Sun wants things fair and pleasant between you; the Taurus Moon wants them reliable, physical and unhurried. Affection often comes from you as care you can touch: a meal, a well-kept home, an evening kept free. The friction arrives when a partner wants to talk something through again. The Sun is willing; the Moon would rather the matter stayed settled. Saying so, kindly, is fairer than agreeing to a discussion you've already left.

## At work {#at-work}

Work where taste and patience both count suits you: design, craft, gardens, buildings, anything that has to please and also last. The Libra Sun senses what everyone will accept; the Taurus Moon wants a steady place and a pace it can keep. Negotiations often go more calmly with you in the room, and colleagues trust your sense of what is well made. Constant reorganisation costs you more than you show, so protect the routines that keep the Moon fed.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two Venus signs without a common element can disagree about what *good* means: fair, or lasting. You may keep the peace by giving way on a principle and then carry the Moon's quiet unease for months, or hold on to a habit long after fairness says it should be shared. The repair is slow and practical: decide by the Libra Sun's measure, one question at a time, then give the Taurus Moon a week to live with the answer before you call it final.
