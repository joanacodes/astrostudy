Some pairs could teach a course in negotiation: they know how to make a proposal, hear an objection and land on terms both sides can live with. Composite Mercury in the 7th house describes a relationship that does its thinking as a partnership.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), the relationship reasons and speaks through its [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), the midpoint of your two natal Mercurys. [The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is the house of partnership, agreements, contracts and the people one meets face to face. It is angular, opening at the composite [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), with Libra as its natural sign, and it faces the 1st, where Mercury has its joy.

In a composite, the 7th describes how the relationship meets others as a unit and how it settles its own terms. With Mercury here, both happen in conversation. The pair negotiates well together, with a landlord, a client, a builder, another couple choosing a holiday. It talks openly about its own arrangements too: who does what, what each of you expects, what you agreed last spring and whether it still holds. The natal page on [Mercury in the 7th house](/astrology/mercury-in-7th-house/) describes a mind that thinks in dialogue; in synastry, [their Mercury in your 7th house](/astrology/mercury-in-7th-house-synastry/) seats one person's voice across the table from the other. Here the relationship itself sits on one side of the table, and the world on the other.

Composite Mercury normally travels within about 28° of the composite Sun, so the Sun is often in the 7th too, or beside it. In about 1 pair in 26 the composite Sun ends up near the opposite point, around the 1st: the bond's purpose turned towards its own presence while its mind faces outward, a pairing the arithmetic of midpoints produces rather than chooses.

Tradition counts the angular houses as the strongest, so a Mercury close to the composite Descendant would be read as prominent. Where that angle falls depends on both birth times, and the two ways of drawing composite houses, from the midpoints of your two charts' cusps or from the composite Midheaven at the place where you live, can place it a few degrees apart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What to watch is the negotiation that never ends. A bond that talks so readily about its own terms can reopen them every week, until the relationship feels like a contract under permanent review. The talk about *us* can also crowd out talk about anything else. It helps to agree that some things are settled, and to spend more evenings discussing the world than the relationship.

None of this foretells a marriage, a lasting partnership or the outcome of any agreement: a composite describes the manner of a bond, not its future. [Composite Mercury in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-6th-house/) organises the daily work, and [composite Mercury in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-8th-house/) talks about what is shared and owed. There's no composite in the readings I do; astrologers who use one read it next to both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and the first agreement it needs is the one between the two of you ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
