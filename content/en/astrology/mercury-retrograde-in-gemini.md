When Mercury turns back in Gemini, it turns back in a sign it rules, so the business that slows is Mercury’s own: gathering, comparing, connecting. It happens between early May and early July, and covers barely 9° each time, the narrowest stretch of any sign in the dates I use.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) is mutable air, and Mercury is at home there: quick, curious, following several threads at once. During [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/), when the planet only seems from Earth to reverse, those threads are gone over rather than added to: the conversations left halfway, the messages saved for later, the subjects you meant to look into, the people you kept meaning to call back. Its own sign doesn’t make the retrograde stronger or weaker as I read it; it makes it more purely about Mercury.

Between 2000 and 2040, twelve retrogrades back through Gemini, two years running about every six or seven years: 2001 and 2002, 2008 and 2009, 2014 and 2015, 2021 and 2022, 2027 and 2028, 2034 and 2035. The most recent, from 10 May to 3 June 2022, stationed at 4°51′ Gemini and turned direct at 26°05′ Taurus ([Mercury retrograde in Taurus](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-taurus/)). The next starts at 6°21′ Cancer ([Mercury retrograde in Cancer](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-cancer/)) on 10 June 2027 and ends at 27°28′ Gemini on 4 July; then, from 21 May to 14 June 2028, Mercury stays wholly in Gemini, between 16°18′ and 7°45′. In 2040 it stops at 0°31′ Cancer, half a degree short of the sign.

Born with Mercury retrograde in Gemini, you carry the planet backing through its own sign in your birth chart for life, a rhythm I describe in [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Gemini opens in your chart depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and with it everything this page says about your life rather than the sky; without the time I don’t draw the houses or read the chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, a retrograde of 8½° to 9° usually stays inside one house, so for most charts one line below is enough.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/gemini-rising/):** Gemini rising: your quick, questioning way of meeting people; a name, a face or an old conversation comes back to mind.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/gemini-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** the ideas and skills you value; a side project or second string you’d put aside, looked at again.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** Gemini in its natural house: messages, reading, short trips, brothers and sisters; the busiest place for this retrograde, full of replies to go back over.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a talkative home; a family conversation that went round in circles, picked up where it stopped.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/):** wit, games and small creative projects; a piece of writing dropped halfway, back in view.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/gemini-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a working day of many tasks; the jobs left open on the desk, finished before new ones join them.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Gemini on the Descendant, with Sagittarius rising: a partner you think out loud with; your long conversation picks up an old thread.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/gemini-on-8th-house-cusp/):** talking through trust and what’s shared; a delicate subject, discussed again in clearer words.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/):** study and the comparing of beliefs; a course, a language or a book you started, reopened.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a working life built on communication; your pitch, your profile or your public words, revised.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends from many circles; old contacts and old group conversations, back in the thread.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/gemini-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a busy private mind; notes, journals and thoughts you never shared, sorted in quiet.

## What helps {#what-helps}

With Mercury retrograde in its own sign, I find these weeks more useful for closing what’s open than for opening more: finishing the reading, answering the old message, filing the notes. The house your natal Mercury occupies shows where Gemini’s curiosity usually goes in your chart, and where these weeks tend to take the review.

If a misunderstanding comes up, a plain sentence untangles more than a clever one. And if a new subject catches your eye in June, note it down: it will keep, and so will your curiosity.
