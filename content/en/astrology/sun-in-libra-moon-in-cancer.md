All day the Libra Sun weighs, adjusts and negotiates, and the Cancer Moon needs one place where nothing is up for negotiation. I read this pair as a fair-minded self with a strong, sheltering heart, born in the waning half of the month, around the last quarter.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Both signs are cardinal and quick to begin, but they begin in different places: the [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) at the table where people have to agree, the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/) at home, among the few it shelters. Air and water, three signs apart counting back from the Sun: a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign, two lights without a common element that rub along by friction. What you aim for is fairness between people, every view given its weight; what you need is a few people and a place that are simply yours, with no weighing required.

The Moon is in its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/): Cancer belongs to it, so for your needs there is no other ruler to consult, only the Moon's own house and aspects. Your Sun, in its [fall](/astrology/fall/), answers to [Venus](/astrology/venus/). I find the contrast useful: the needs are sure of what they want, while the sense of self forms through consultation. When you can't tell what you think, ask what you need. The Cancer Moon usually knows.

With the Moon 241° to 300° ahead of the Sun, the month was waning, about 20 to 24½ days old. These births are split down the middle between the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) and the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/), and at 270° the chart has a waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). The last quarter, as I read it, asks you to review your own priorities before anyone else's; for this pair, that often means deciding again how much of you the world gets and how much stays at home. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) tells you which half is yours.

This square of signs belongs to about 1 person in 143. If the Moon crossed from Gemini into Cancer, or from Cancer into Leo, on the day you were born, your page could as easily be the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-gemini/) or the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-leo/), and only the time of birth settles it ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign and the houses depend on that time as well, and for a Moon this attached to home, its house shows which home it means: a family, a building, a circle of friends, a body of work.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you're attentive and fair, quick to see the other person's side and make room for it. What the Cancer Moon needs in return is shelter: a home where you can stop being reasonable for an evening, be tired, be sad, be fed. A partner who makes that space without asking you to justify it reaches you more deeply than any compliment. Say what you need in plain words, and early. The Sun asks for things diplomatically; the Moon needs them asked for simply.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that brings people together and looks after them suits you: hospitality, human resources, mediation, care work, running a team that feels like a household. You're often the one who makes newcomers welcome and keeps the peace. In public the Libra Sun handles friction well; in private the Cancer Moon carries it home. Keep a clear door between the two, so the Moon doesn't take every meeting into the kitchen.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Being reasonable all day can use up the energy the Moon needs for feeling. You may smooth over a slight at work and then be undone by something small at home, or keep a hurt so private that even a partner doesn't know it's there. The square asks for honesty in both directions: let the Libra Sun say, politely, when something isn't fair to you, and let the Cancer Moon have its tears without needing a fair reason for them.
