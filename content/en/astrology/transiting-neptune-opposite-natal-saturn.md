When Neptune stands opposite your Saturn, your responsibilities and other people's longings face each other across the chart. Fewer than half of all people ever meet it, and those who do, meet it once, for about two and a half years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is what you hold together: commitments, standards, the patience to carry a responsibility to the end. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the pull of compassion and of an ideal larger than any rule. Facing each other, as an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) places them, the two ask to be balanced rather than ranked, and under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the balance is often worked out with other people.

Someone else's dream may ask you to carry it, or someone's need may call for more care than your rules allow. You may find others blurring the lines you keep, or find yourself holding the structure while the people around you chase a vision. Saturn finds it hard to delegate and easy to confuse responsibility with worth; Neptune finds it easy to give everything. The opposition asks you to see both pulls clearly: to help without carrying it all, and to keep your standards without closing your heart.

It foretells no loss, no failure and no parting; it describes a polarity to live with for a while.

With Saturn in your 1st, the opposition comes from your 7th, the house facing it ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)): a partner's ideals or needs press on the self-reliance you've built. Your 1st and 7th are fixed by your rising degree, which needs a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Saturn opposite Neptune](/astrology/saturn-opposite-neptune/), in 1935–37, 1970–72 or 2005–07, you met this transit in your first years or not at all, depending on which side of exact the two planets stood.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A responsibility taken on as this opposition begins has time to show what it costs and what it gives: within my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, the transit runs about two years and seven months, rarely under twenty-two. Three exact contacts mark most passes, five about one pass in four.

It comes to a little under half of the people born between 1930 and 2009 before 80, and to no one twice: at about 15 for someone born at the start of 1975, 41 for 1945 and 63½ for 1985, and not before 80 for the starts of 1935, 1955, 1965, 1995 or 2005.

Tradition exalts Saturn in Libra, and from 2026 to 2039 Neptune in Aries stands opposite that sign: people born with Saturn in Libra, between late 1980 and 1982, meet this transit at about 44 to 55.

For someone born at the start of 1945, [transiting Neptune square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-saturn/) came in the first weeks, [transiting Neptune trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-saturn/) at about 14 and this opposition at about 41; [transiting Neptune sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-saturn/) and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-saturn/) never came before 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Decide what is yours to carry. Write down the responsibilities you hold for others, and mark the ones you chose.

Help in ways that don't depend on you alone: share the load, bring others in, let some structures be held by more than one person.

Keep one standard you won't bend, and let the rest be kind. Under this opposition, a single firm line protects more than a wall of rules.
