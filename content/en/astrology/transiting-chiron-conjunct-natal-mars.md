In the *Iliad*, Achilles, the greatest of the Greek warriors at Troy, also knows how to treat a wound, because Chiron taught him, and he passes the skill on. When Chiron reaches your Mars, the fighter and the healer in you meet on one degree: the way you push, and what pushing has cost.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is agency: beginning, asserting, making the effort, defending a boundary. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) describes a sensitivity that stays and the practical skill of living alongside it. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes two functions work as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your way of acting and your way of coping become hard to tell apart.

That is often when an old method shows its seams. Some people learned early that strength meant never stopping, and carry on long after a rest was due. Others learned that speaking up got them into trouble, so their force goes quiet just when it's needed. Some compete hardest exactly where they doubt themselves most. None of this is a failing; it's how a person adapts. What the conjunction asks is to notice what the old method costs you, and to choose a better one: pacing instead of bursts, asking for a hand, saying no without a long defence.

Mars is not anger, and Chiron is not injury. Nothing here forecasts an accident, an illness or a fight, and I never read the body from a chart ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

With [Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/), the conjunction comes during Chiron's stay in that house ([Chiron transiting the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-6th-house/)), so it is the daily round of work, and how hard you drive yourself through it, that comes up for review. Tradition gave the 6th to illness; I read it as work and routine, in an adult life. Your house is set by your birth time, and I don't read the conjunction without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Mars conjunct Chiron](/astrology/mars-conjunct-chiron/), you'll know this fusion from the inside, and the transit is a long, slow look at it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The orbs I time it with are 1½° before exact and 1° after. Within them, the conjunction usually lasts about ten months, because in nearly two passes out of three Chiron's retrograde loop brings it over your Mars three times. About one pass in three is a single crossing of some three weeks. About one in twenty is long: five crossings over roughly twenty-one months. The spread comes from Chiron's lopsided orbit, which takes it through Virgo and Libra in about two years each and keeps it eight or nine years in Aries and Pisces.

The conjunction opens Chiron's round over your Mars, once in about 50 years, so by 80 most people have met it twice and everyone at least once. [Transiting Chiron sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-mars/) and [transiting Chiron square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-mars/) come next, then [transiting Chiron trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-mars/) and, at the halfway point, [transiting Chiron opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-mars/).

Mars changes sign every six or seven weeks, so its sign says little about your birth year. Chiron is in Aries until April 2027, in Taurus from June 2026 to May 2034 and in Gemini from July 2033 to April 2039: a Mars in one of those signs meets the conjunction within those years.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Stop one task before you're spent, on purpose, and see what happens to the rest of the day.

Ask for help with something you have always done alone.

Say no once without a long explanation. A boundary doesn't need a defence to be valid.

Learn one new way of doing a hard thing, whether it's a tool, a technique or a different pace.
