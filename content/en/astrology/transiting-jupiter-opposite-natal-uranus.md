Once in every Jupiter lap, the planet of growth arrives on the far side of your chart from your natal Uranus. The part of you that insists on its own way finds itself facing someone else's big plan, and both have something to learn from the meeting.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is your difference: the need to choose for yourself, to question, to keep room to change. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is growth and the beliefs that carry it. The [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) puts them at two ends of an axis, so each makes the other visible. Very often Jupiter's end is held by somebody else: a partner with an ambitious plan, a group that wants you on board, a teacher or friend whose certainty is generous and large.

What follows is a negotiation. Sometimes the shared plan asks for less independence than you're willing to give; sometimes meeting another person's conviction shows you exactly where you differ, and why it matters. Opposition isn't conflict: the useful outcome is a version of the larger aim with room in it for your own way.

Uranus's house sets the scene, and Jupiter is then crossing the house opposite. With Uranus in your 3rd, where you think and talk in your own fashion, the opposition comes during [Jupiter transiting the 9th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-9th-house/): other people's big beliefs meet your independent mind. That reading needs a birth time to place either house ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Jupiter opposite Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-uranus/), you meet this transit near each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/).

It isn't the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), transiting Uranus opposite its own birth place at about 42. That one comes once; this one is Jupiter's, every twelve years or so.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A single pass, start to finish, takes Jupiter about eleven days within my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after. If a station falls close by, which is the case about three times in ten, the opposition comes back twice more and stays about eight months. Over a life to 80, that adds up to six or seven for every birth from 1930 to 2009.

The first came at very different ages: at about 1.2 for a birth on 1 January 1975, not until about 11 for 1 January 1935.

Think of the [transit](/astrology/transits/) round in two halves. On the way out from [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-uranus/), Jupiter passes [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-uranus/), [transiting Jupiter square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-uranus/) before it reaches this opposition; on the way back it meets them again in reverse.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Negotiate room inside the shared plan before deciding whether to leave it. Say which parts you can commit to and which you need to keep your own.

Let the other person's certainty sharpen your difference. Put into words exactly where you see it otherwise; it's usually clearer to you after that conversation than before.

And leave the irreversible decisions until the opposition has passed. Most of these transits are over in days, and what felt like a choice between two lives often turns out to be an arrangement.
