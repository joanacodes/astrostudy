In a double Cancer, the Moon answers for everything: it rules the sign your Sun is in, and it is home in its own sign as well. What you live for and what you need to feel safe are made of the same material, belonging, memory, care, and the new moon of your birth was only a day or two away.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

One sign, one element, one mode: cardinal water twice over. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) finds its purpose among the people and places it belongs to; the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/), settled in its own sign, holds on to memories, senses moods and moves first to protect. No translation is needed between purpose and need. When the home is safe and the people you love are well, you tend to feel both purposeful and calm; when something threatens them, both lights react at once.

Cancer is the Moon's [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), and here that home is also the sign of your Sun, so a single body governs both lights. Only a double Leo shares this shape, with the Sun in charge instead: here the Moon's house, its aspects and its condition speak for your whole centre, and I read them first. The Sun has no dignity in Cancer and needs none; it borrows its strength from the Moon. Other planets, a fiery Mars, an airy Mercury, a rising sign in another element, are what bring outside weather into this very inward pair.

Your Moon had either just caught up with the Sun or was about to: under 30° separated them, so the birth falls a couple of days either side of a new moon, usually a [new moon in Cancer](/astrology/new-moon-in-cancer/), in the weeks after the June solstice when the Sun is in Cancer. Births before the conjunction belong to the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), births after it to the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase, in equal numbers, and close to exact the chart has a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says which side of the new moon you're on.

The double Cancer turns up in about 1 birth in 142. With barely two and a half days in each sign, the Moon makes crossing days common: born as it arrived from Gemini or left for Leo, you might have the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-gemini/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-leo/), and only the time can judge ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). From that time I'd also take the rising sign and the houses, and in a chart this centred on the Moon, the Moon's house is the first thing I'd want to know.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You give yourself completely to the people you love and remember everything that matters to them. Home is where the relationship lives: meals, rituals, the shared history you protect. You need the same steadiness back, and you tend to feel a partner's moods as if they were weather in your own house. Saying what you need out loud, instead of hoping it's noticed, keeps the bond from becoming a place where you look after everyone but yourself.

## At work {#at-work}

Work suits you best when it feels like looking after something: a team, a family firm, a community, a collection, a client you've known for years. Your Sun needs the work to protect something; your Moon needs it to feel safe and familiar. You can be remarkably tenacious for a cause you love. Cold, purely transactional places drain both lights at once, so choose work where loyalty is returned.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in the Moon's sign, there's no outside voice inside the pair. Your mood can become your sense of who you are for the day, and a hurt at home can shake everything else. You may withdraw into your shell when you most need company, or hold on to people and the past for safety. What helps is one thing that belongs to you alone, a craft, a project, a walk, a friend of a very different temperament, and the habit of letting a little air into the house.
