In a double Cancer, the Moon answers for everything: it rules the sign your Sun is in, and it is home in its own sign as well. What you live for and what you need to feel safe are made of the same material, belonging, memory, care, and you were born within a couple of days of a new moon.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

One sign, one element, one mode: cardinal water twice over. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) builds an identity around the people and places it belongs to; the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/), in its own sign, feels deeply, remembers everything and moves first to protect. No translation is needed between purpose and need. When the home is safe and the people you love are well, you tend to feel both purposeful and calm; when something threatens them, both lights react at once.

Tradition gives the Moon its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/) in Cancer, and here it also rules the sign of your Sun, so a single body governs both lights. Only a double Leo shares this shape, with the Sun in charge instead: here the Moon's house, its aspects and its condition speak for your whole centre, and I read them first. The Sun has no dignity in Cancer and needs none; it borrows its strength from the Moon. Other planets, a fiery Mars, an airy Mercury, a rising sign in another element, are what bring outside weather into this very inward pair.

The two lights stood within about 29° of each other, the Moon either just behind the Sun or just ahead, so the birth fell within some two and a half days of a new moon, usually a [new moon in Cancer](/astrology/new-moon-in-cancer/). The eight phases divide these births equally between the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) that ends one cycle and the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase that begins the next, and near the exact meeting the chart has a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says which side of the new moon you're on.

About 1 person in 142 is a double Cancer in this sense. The Moon is in Cancer for only some two and a half days, so a birth on the day it came in from Gemini or went on into Leo might belong to the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-gemini/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-leo/) instead ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The time also gives the rising sign and the houses, and in a chart this centred on the Moon, the Moon's house is the first thing I'd want to know.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You give yourself completely to the people you love and remember everything that matters to them. Home is where the relationship lives: meals, rituals, the shared history you protect. You need the same steadiness back, and you tend to feel a partner's moods as if they were weather in your own house. Saying what you need out loud, instead of hoping it's noticed, keeps the bond from becoming a place where you look after everyone but yourself.

## At work {#at-work}

You do your best work where it feels like looking after something: a team, a family firm, a community, a collection, a client you've known for years. The Sun wants the work to protect; the Moon wants it to feel safe and familiar. You can be remarkably tenacious for a cause you love. Cold, purely transactional places drain both lights at once, so choose work where loyalty is returned.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in the Moon's sign, there's no outside voice inside the pair. Your mood can become your sense of who you are for the day, and a hurt at home can shake everything else. You may withdraw into your shell when you most need company, or hold on to people and the past for safety. What helps is one thing that belongs to you alone, a craft, a project, a walk, a friend of a very different temperament, and the habit of letting a little air into the house.
