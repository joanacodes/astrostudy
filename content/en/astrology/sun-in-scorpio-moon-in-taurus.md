You were born close to a full moon, usually the one that falls in Taurus each year while the Sun is in Scorpio. Across that axis your Sun goes looking for what lies underneath, and your Moon holds on to what is solid and familiar.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Scorpio and Taurus face each other across the zodiac, fixed water and fixed earth: two signs that hold on, each in its own way. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) holds on to the truth of things and often aims to change something at the root; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) holds on to continuity, settling through routine, the senses and the familiar. You may transform everything at work and still need the same chair, the same walk, the same supper at home.

Tradition puts the Moon's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Taurus and its fall in Scorpio, so your needs stand on the Moon's best ground while your Sun works on its hardest. I read that as feelings that steady quickly, in the service of a purpose that goes where feelings are hardest to bear. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your Moon's sign. Your Sun's sign has two rulers and I read both: [Mars](/astrology/mars/), Scorpio's ruler in the tradition, for where you persist, and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), its modern ruler, for what you are willing to see end. Venus is in detriment in Scorpio and Mars in Taurus, so their houses show where comfort and drive have to make allowances for each other.

With 151° to 210° between them, the lights were close to opposition, and a full moon, usually a [full moon in Taurus](/astrology/full-moon-in-taurus/), came at most two and a half days before or after the birth. Births before it belong to the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) and births after it to the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase, in equal numbers, and the nearer the birth to the full, the closer the chart comes to a [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/). The almanacs call the November full moon the [Beaver moon](/astrology/beaver-moon/), and in years when it is the first full moon after the Harvest moon it is also the [Hunter's moon](/astrology/hunters-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sorts out the before and the after.

Roughly 1 person in 149 shares this pair. If the Moon entered Taurus from Aries, or moved on into Gemini, on the day you were born, you may belong with the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-aries/) or the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-gemini/) instead, and the time decides ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The houses hang on it too, and with the lights opposite, the two houses they occupy show which parts of your life this pair holds in tension.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with loyalty and depth: the Taurus Moon shows it through presence, touch and constancy, the Scorpio Sun through an attention that sees the whole of a person. You need a partner who is reliable and also honest about what lies underneath. Two fixed signs keep their grievances as faithfully as their loves, so a small hurt named early costs far less than a large one discovered late.

## At work {#at-work}

Patience and penetration make you strong in work that has to last and has to be right: research, restoration, building, conservation, crafts that take years to master, any role where you are trusted with something others value. The Scorpio Sun digs; the Taurus Moon keeps steady while it does. You may outlast most people on a long project. Constant change wears the Moon down, and work that stays on the surface leaves the Sun unsatisfied.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two fixed signs facing each other can make change slow and then sudden: the Taurus Moon holds on to the familiar, the Scorpio Sun knows that something has to end, and for a long time nothing moves. Then everything does at once. Let change come in stages you can live with: decide what must end, keep what still nourishes you, and give the Taurus Moon a new routine before you take the old one away.
