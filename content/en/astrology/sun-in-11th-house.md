The Sun was well up in the morning sky when you were born, in the house tradition called the Good Spirit. I read the Sun in the 11th house as a self that takes shape among chosen people: friends, allies, and the future you're working towards together.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is friends, groups and hopes: the people you build a future with, and the future you'd like to build. Succedent and tied to Aquarius, it was the Good Spirit of Hellenistic astrology, where Jupiter had its joy. Aquarius is the sign of the Sun's detriment, but a house isn't a sign, and I don't read the Sun as weakened here; the echo is a self that learns to be fully itself as one among many. In the 11th, the [Sun](/astrology/sun/)'s sense of who you are and where you're going grows in company and in hope: who you are shows in what you're building with others.

In Placidus, the house system I use, an 11th-house Sun was well above the eastern horizon in the middle of the morning, by the Sun rather than the clock. A few minutes can move a cusp, so this, like every house, needs your exact [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/). The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/), facing it, is the joy you make for yourself; the 11th is the joy you share.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love for the [Sun in the 10th house](/astrology/sun-in-10th-house/) often comes with a calling, and for the [Sun in the 12th house](/astrology/sun-in-12th-house/) with a need for privacy; for an 11th-house Sun it usually comes with friends attached. Partners tend to start as friends, or become your closest one, and you may feel nearest to each other when you're working on something bigger than the two of you. The relationship itself can slide into being one more project run by committee. The lesson is to keep time that belongs only to the two of you, with no cause and no guest list.

## At work {#at-work}

Teams, networks, associations, community work, campaigns, any project that exists only because people chose to join it: this Sun works best among peers. You may be the one who connects people, gets a group moving, or keeps a shared hope alive when others lose heart. Recognition means the respect of the people you work alongside more than any title. Purpose fades in work that seems to be for no one in particular. If you work alone, a Sun here tends to build a community around the work anyway: a circle of clients, a network of peers, readers who slowly turn into a group.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Groups can swallow a self like this one. You may find it easier to say what the team needs than what you want, or end up organising everyone's hopes except your own, the friend everyone calls and nobody thinks to ask. Belonging can also become conditional, so that disagreeing with your circle feels like losing your place in it. And if friendships carry the whole of your sense of self, a falling-out can shake far more than one relationship.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name one hope that is only yours, and give it time alongside the shared ones. Choose your groups on purpose, and leave the ones that ask you to be smaller. Keep a few friends who know you apart from any cause. Accept help when it's offered, since the Good Spirit was also the house of benefactors. And notice how your Sun's sign shapes the company you keep: a Leo Sun in the 11th wants its friends close, an Aquarius Sun wants its principles shared, and both are looking for the same thing, a place among others where they can stay themselves.
