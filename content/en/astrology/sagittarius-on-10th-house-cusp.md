With Sagittarius on the 10th house cusp, the highest point of the chart is in the sign of the far horizon. What you're known for tends to be what you believe, and your working life follows a question more often than a ladder.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In Placidus, the house system I use, your [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) starts at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), and it holds your public direction: the work people know you for, your standing, the authority you earn ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) there makes it a vocation as much as a career. I read it as the person colleagues come to for the why, a reputation for frankness and generosity, and authority that persuades rather than commands. In public you tend to come across as the one with the wider view, even in jobs that have nothing to do with travel or teaching. Working lives with this Midheaven often change direction when a conviction changes, and that's the cusp doing its job, not a failure. The 10th's natural sign is Capricorn, the sign of Jupiter's fall, so this house of slow, earned standing is run by the planet tradition thought least at ease in the house's own sign: the long climb is something you choose deliberately, and public promises are best kept small enough to keep.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules this Midheaven. Its sign shows the spirit of your public life, its house where that life tends to centre: the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Below, at the IC, Gemini always opens the 4th ([Gemini on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/)): the private end of the line answers to Mercury, the public end to Jupiter. Pisces, Jupiter's other sign, almost always touches your 1st house at London. It rises in about 2 charts in 5, and in most of the rest it lies wholly inside the 1st behind an Aquarius Ascendant ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Either way Jupiter, with Neptune, has a say in how you come across as well as in what you're known for. When Aquarius rises, the commoner case at London, Saturn and Uranus rule the chart, and a reserved, independent manner fronts a public life lit by conviction.

In about 1 birth in 11, in both cities, Sagittarius holds the Midheaven: the sign at the top of the chart is set by the hour, whatever the latitude. As the last sign before the December solstice, it keeps the meridian a few minutes longer than the two-hour average. At London the 10th is narrow in these charts, barely 18°, so Sagittarius often opens a second house: the 11th in about 2 charts in 5, linking your work to the people you build it with ([Sagittarius on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-11th-house-cusp/)), or the 9th in about a third, linking it to what you believe and study ([Sagittarius on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-9th-house-cusp/); [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)).

Working life is the subject people most often bring to a reading, and it's where an hour's rounding does the most damage here: the Midheaven needs barely more than two hours to cross Sagittarius. So I ask for the minute, and without it I leave the chart alone ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
