Uranus square Pluto is first of all the aspect of births in the early 1930s, when Uranus was in Aries and Pluto in Cancer, a quarter of the circle apart. The urge to change things and the urge to transform them from the root met at right angles, and that friction belongs to a generation before it belongs to anyone.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, liberation and reform: it wants out of whatever confines, and it wants a better system. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) enables transformation, regeneration and the release of what is obsolete: a slower pressure that works from inside and doesn't stop at the surface. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through constructive friction, the kind that forces each to adapt without either losing its nature.

The two disagree about pace and depth. Uranus wants change now and in the open; Pluto wants change that goes all the way down, whatever it takes. Lived, this can feel like an impatience with anything half-done, and a habit of pushing against structures that confine, sometimes to find that the push has started a deeper renewal than you meant. The square refines both: quick change learns depth, and deep renewal learns to accept new forms. Reform isn't destruction, transformation isn't either, and friction isn't failure.

Aries and Cancer are both cardinal signs. As the shared theme of the 1930s generation, I read the square as friction between striking out alone and a deep renewal of home and belonging.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 17 born between 1930 and 2009 has this square within 6°. It ran from April to September 1930, from February 1931 to May 1934, from August 1934 to March 1935 and from October 1935 to February 1936, then came back into orb from June to September 2009, a few months before the count ends. Births of 2009 are still under 18, and I don't read children's charts ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).

As a generation's aspect, it turns personal through the houses ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). Uranus in the 1st square Pluto in the 4th, say, could set a need to meet the world on your own terms against a slow, deep reshaping of home life. Contacts with your Sun, Moon or angles count too. All of that needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Uranus is the faster planet, about 84 years round the zodiac against Pluto's 248: after this square, it went on to catch Pluto in the conjunction of the 1960s.

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/):** born from 1963 to 1968; the friction resolved into a single, fused urge.
- **[Uranus sextile Pluto](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto/):** born in the 1940s or the 1990s; the two cooperate when the opening is taken.
- **[Uranus trine Pluto](/astrology/uranus-trine-pluto/):** none of the births from 1930 to 2009.
- **[Uranus opposite Pluto](/astrology/uranus-opposite-pluto/):** none of them either.

[Uranus square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-pluto-synastry/) puts one person's need for change across the other's depth. It can be stirring or wearing, often both, and I read it from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-pluto/) comes twice in Uranus's 84-year round and lasts months, usually with more than one pass. [Transiting Pluto square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-uranus/) is slower and rarer: Pluto takes 12 to 31 years over a sign, and reaches the square only for some birth years.

For the generation of the 1930s, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) at about 42 was also a square from transiting Uranus to natal Pluto: the same friction, replayed at midlife. Weather, not a forecast: transits like these describe pressure for change, never what the change will be.
