In the years of this transit your birthday sits as far from an eclipse season as it can: the two seasons fall about three months before it and three months after. The North Node stands 90° from your Sun, the South Node 90° on the other side, and for about a month your sense of self stands where the familiar and the new cross.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the part of you that holds a purpose and keeps a centre. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) are one line ([nodal axis](/astrology/nodal-axis/)): a capacity still being built at one end, an established one at the other. The [square](/astrology/square/) between them is friction meant to produce adjustment, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) it comes from both ends at once, each at a right angle to your sense of self.

It rarely feels like a choice between old and new. More often both make a claim on who you are: an established role that still needs you, a newer direction that won't wait, and your sense of purpose caught between them. The axis has one rule worth keeping in mind, to develop the new capacity without throwing away the useful old one, and the North Node's blind spot is its mirror image, mistaking a rejection of the familiar for growth. The square tends to be resolved by bringing yourself along: deciding what part of you each end can have.

You feel it most in your Sun's house, while the axis runs through the houses on either side. With the [Sun in the 7th house](/astrology/sun-in-7th-house/), the nodes are usually crossing your 4th and 10th, so home and public life both make demands on the way you partner. Those houses depend on your birth time, which the Sun's degree does not; without it I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Sun square North Node](/astrology/sun-square-north-node/), you know this geometry from the inside.

With the node in Aquarius until March 2028, the square falls to Taurus Suns, born about 20 April to 20 May, and to Scorpio Suns, born about 23 October to 21 November; from March 2028, with the node in Capricorn, to Aries and Libra Suns.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured from 1½° before exact to 1° after, a square usually lasts about a month, crossed once in nineteen passes out of twenty, and the birthday works on it the other way round from the conjunction. If the exact square falls near your birthday, the Sun in the sky is square to the node, when the true node moves fastest, and the pass can be as short as three and a half weeks. If it falls in one of the two eclipse seasons, about three months either side of your birthday, the node nearly halts: one pass in ten stretches past three and a half months, and one in twenty-five crosses three times. The eclipses of those seasons fall 90° from your Sun, and I don't count them on your chart ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

The two squares of a round fall a quarter and three quarters of the way through it, about four and a half and fourteen years after the North Node crosses your Sun ([transiting North Node conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-sun/)), so by 80 most people have met nine and nobody fewer than eight. The first comes after [transiting North Node sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-sun/); between the two lie [transiting North Node trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-sun/), [transiting North Node opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-sun/) and the second trine.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what the old role still needs from you and what the new direction asks, and give each a share of your week.

Refuse the false choice. You rarely have to drop who you were to become who you are becoming.

Keep the month's decisions small and reversible. A square from the nodes rewards adjustment more than verdicts.
