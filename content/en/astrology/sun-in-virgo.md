Plenty of people can tell when something is wrong. A Virgo Sun tends to know exactly where, and why, and builds a sense of self out of that precision. Discernment is its organising principle: you know who you are by the distinctions you're able to make.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) describes identity, vitality and direction, the centre the rest of the chart is organised around. [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), mutable earth, gives that centre an analytical, practical manner. Purpose here tends to look like method: you feel most yourself when you're doing something carefully, with a process you trust, and when the result could bear inspection. A standard isn't a burden to this Sun so much as a way of saying *this is what I care about*.

For the Sun itself, Virgo is neither home nor exile; it holds no dignity there. Its ruler is another matter. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Virgo and is exalted in it too, and since Mercury is never more than about 28° from the Sun, yours sits in Leo, Virgo or Libra. When it shares the Sun's sign, the ruler of this Sun is at its most exact, and part of my reading is checking that the analysing mind isn't doing all the talking for the self.

The Sun reaches Virgo around 23 August, as the [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) ends its month, and leaves at the September equinox, about 22 September, where the [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) begins. It stays 30 or 31 days, long enough to be the Sun sign of about 1 person in 12; born on a changeover day, your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) decides.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

The question under many Virgo Suns' relationships is a quiet one: *would I still be wanted if I stopped being useful?* You may show love by paying exact attention, the right book, the problem solved before it was mentioned, and feel safest when you have something to offer. What this Sun needs to learn is being cared for on a day when you have nothing to give. A partner who appreciates you precisely, for the thing you actually did and for who you are when you're doing nothing, helps far more than general praise.

## At work {#at-work}

A Virgo Sun is often the one who notices the step everyone else skips. Work that rewards accuracy suits it: editing, analysis, quality, research, any system that needs keeping honest. Purpose grows when your method is trusted and shrinks when you're asked to cut corners you can see. Recognition matters, though you may find it awkward to claim. The kind that lands is specific, *that change you made saved us a week*, and it's worth asking for in those terms.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The self, here, can become a draft that never gets finished. If you know yourself by what could be better, you may never quite let yourself be done, and the inner editor that serves your work so well can turn its red pen on you. Rest is hard to justify when rest isn't useful, so vitality wears down without anyone noticing, you least of all. None of this is coldness. It's a self that is very exact, still learning that it's allowed to be enough.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Set the standard before you start, and stop when you reach it. Finish things and let them go out slightly imperfect, then watch what happens: usually, nothing bad. Keep one craft that is purely for pleasure and never improve it on purpose. Ask for precise appreciation instead of waiting to be noticed. And on the days the editor won't stop, give it a job outside your own head.

A Virgo Sun tends to want the error bars, so here are mine. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign held about a third of the time, and so did Mercury; Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90%. It's a notebook, not a study, and I'd rather say so than claim a precision I don't have.
