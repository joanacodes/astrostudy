Each year there's a fortnight when Saturn stands opposite the Sun: retrograde, up all night, as close and bright as it gets that year. Sun opposite Saturn is that sky, brought into a chart: who you are at one end, the weight of structure and authority facing it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Here the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is self-expression: who you are, shown, and the direction you choose. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), facing it here, is structure: rules, responsibility, limits, the long view, and the authority that goes with them. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) holds the two as a polarity, each end showing the other more clearly, neither erasing the other.

I often find that Saturn arrives first from outside: a strict teacher, an exacting boss, an institution, a partner who carries the rules for both of you. You learn who you are partly by pushing against them, and partly by realising that some of those rules were yours all along. Self-belief here is tested against authority, sometimes against your own inner authority, and it's earned by standing your ground.

Over time, the work is to take the Saturn end back: to choose your structures rather than only resist or obey other people's. When that happens, I see this aspect turn into a steady, fair authority of your own, one that knows both sides of the table.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Opposite signs of one modality hold the two. A Sun in Aries with Saturn in Libra puts initiative across from fairness, with Saturn in its traditional exaltation; a Sun in Cancer with Saturn in Capricorn puts care across from duty, with Saturn in its own sign. Opposite houses often hold them as well: with the 10th and 4th, public duty across from private life.

About 1 person in 25 has this opposition, within 8°: the rarest of the pair's five aspects, though only a little rarer than the conjunction, because Saturn's backward drift barely speeds the Sun up. Saturn's yearly retrograde, about four and a half months long, is centred on the opposition, so everyone born with it has Saturn retrograde ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)). I read that as rules you rewrite for yourself before you live by them ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

Seen from across the chart, the rest of the family:

- **[Sun conjunct Saturn](/astrology/sun-conjunct-saturn/):** about six months away in the year's cycle, Saturn hidden behind the Sun; the structure is inside you rather than across from you.
- **[Sun sextile Saturn](/astrology/sun-sextile-saturn/):** a quiet offer of steadiness, with none of the face-off.
- **[Sun square Saturn](/astrology/sun-square-saturn/):** the demands come from the side, through your own commitments, more than from across the table.
- **[Sun trine Saturn](/astrology/sun-trine-saturn/):** one element instead of one axis; structure and purpose on the same side rather than facing each other.

Between two charts, [Sun opposite Saturn in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-saturn-synastry/) sets one person's sense of structure across from the other's sense of self: steadying or constraining, often both. Which one each person feels depends on the side of the table they sit on, so I need both of them, and their agreement, before I read it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

Saturn reaches the far side of your Sun roughly fifteen years after each crossing ([transiting Saturn opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-sun/)). At roughly two and a half years per sign, the opposition stays in orb for months, often in three passes.

I read it as a season of weighing what you owe others against what you owe yourself. Weather, not a forecast: the scales are in the sky, and the choices stay yours.
