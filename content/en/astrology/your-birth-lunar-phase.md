On the night you were born, the Moon had a shape: a thread of light after sunset, a half disc, a full face, a last sliver before dawn, or nothing at all. That shape is your birth lunar phase, and your chart holds it in one number, the distance between your Sun and your Moon.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Every month the Moon laps the Sun. At the new moon the two stand together; the Moon then pulls ahead by about 12° a day, reaches the far side of the sky at the full moon, about 180° on, and closes the gap again until the next new moon, about 29½ days after the last. Your birth phase is the point of that lap you were born at.

Astronomers mark four moments in it: new moon, first quarter, full moon and last quarter. Astrologers mostly count eight stretches, following Dane Rudhyar's *The Lunation Cycle* (1967): each covers 45° of the Moon's lead on the Sun, about three and three-quarter days. Here they are in order, with what the sky showed:

- **[New moon](/astrology/new-moon/), 0° to 45°:** born from the new moon to three or four days after it; the Moon unseen, then a thin thread in the west after sunset.
- **[Waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/), 45° to 90°:** a growing crescent in the evening sky.
- **[First quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/), 90° to 135°:** from the half moon on, high in the sky at sunset.
- **[Waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/), 135° to 180°:** more than half lit, rising in the afternoon, nearly round.
- **[Full moon](/astrology/full-moon/), 180° to 225°:** from the full moon on, up most of the night.
- **[Waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/), 225° to 270°:** shrinking, rising later each evening.
- **[Last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/), 270° to 315°:** the half moon of the morning, rising around midnight.
- **[Balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), 315° to 360°:** the last crescent before dawn, then the dark of the moon.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

To find yours, take the Sun's and the Moon's positions from your chart and count from the Sun to the Moon in the order of the signs. Say your Sun is at 10° Aries and your Moon at 25° Cancer. From 10° Aries to 10° Cancer is three signs, 90°; on to 25° Cancer is 15° more. The Moon was 105° ahead of the Sun: the first quarter phase.

The count always runs forwards, from the Sun to the Moon. With the Sun at 10° Aries and the Moon at 25° Capricorn, the Moon is 285° ahead, not 75° behind: a last quarter birth, in the waning half of the month.

Your Sun and Moon signs already give a rough answer. With the Moon in the Sun's own sign, you were born within about two and a half days of a new moon, either side of it; with the Moon in the opposite sign, near a full moon. The degrees settle the rest, and near the edge of a phase so does the hour: the Moon gains about half a degree an hour on the Sun, so a few hours can move a birth from one phase into the next, and on some days change the Moon's sign as well ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)).

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I read the phase as the relationship between two functions: the Sun, which organises identity and purpose, and the Moon, which looks after needs and feeling. The aspect between them says how they meet; the phase adds which way they're moving. The first and last quarter phases both open with a square, yet in the first the Moon is pulling away from the Sun and in the last it is coming back. I read the waxing half, new to full, as needs reaching out towards a purpose, and the waning half, full to new, as experience being sorted and handed on. Each phase has its own page.

Your birth phase also starts a slower clock. In secondary progressions the Moon and the Sun repeat the lap in about 29½ years, and the phase you were born in is where that cycle begins ([progressed lunar phases](/astrology/progressed-lunar-phases/)).

I hold the phase lightly, as a theme rather than a foundation. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. A phase is made of those two.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Your Moon sign tells you your phase.”** The phase depends on where the Moon stood from the Sun. Any Moon sign can come with any phase, depending on the Sun's sign.
- **“A full-moon birth makes you dramatic.”** The phase describes how needs and purpose meet: at the full moon, face to face. It doesn't hand anyone a temperament.
- **“Your birth phase comes back on every birthday.”** Each birthday falls in a different phase. The Moon's phases return to the same dates about every 19 years, the Metonic cycle, so it's at 19, 38, 57 and 76 that a birthday's Moon comes close to your birth phase.
- **“First quarter means the same to astronomers and astrologers.”** For astronomers it's a moment, the Moon 90° ahead of the Sun. In Rudhyar's eight phases it names the 45° stretch that begins there.
