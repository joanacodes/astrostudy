When Capricorn is on your 7th house cusp, the sea-goat stands on the western horizon, opposite a Cancer Ascendant. You lead with care, and your partners tend to bring the frame.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In Placidus, the house system I use, the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is where the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) begins: partners, close collaborators, the people you deal with on equal terms, and the open rivals tradition added ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) there, I read a pull towards people who are established or competent, older in years or in manner, people who know what they're doing and keep their word. A partnership tends to grow in stages, the way a career does, with roles, plans and a shared idea of where it's going, and it often improves with age. Contracts get read before they're signed, at work and at home, and rivalries are fought on merit. The risk is choosing reliability over warmth, or ending up as the one who holds every frame, when the point of a partner is that the weight can be shared.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules this Descendant. Its house shows the part of life through which partners arrive and what you build together, its sign the terms: the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Tradition gave Saturn its exaltation in Libra, the 7th's natural sign, so the planet of commitment held a place of honour in the house of partners long before it came to rule yours. When partnership is the question, Saturn's house is the first thing I look at: it's a placement my notebook found reliable, holding 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)).

Opposite, the Ascendant is in Cancer in every one of these charts ([Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/)), which gives the Moon your chart and Saturn your partnerships: care on your side of the table, structure on theirs. You tend to look after people; the partner you trust is the one who looks after things. Of all twelve cusps, the Descendant is where Capricorn is found most often, in roughly one birth in 10 in either city. Cancer takes some 151 minutes to rise at London, one of the longest climbs in the north, and for all that time Capricorn holds the western horizon. The 7th is narrow in these charts, barely 16° at London, and in nearly half of them the 8th cusp falls in Capricorn too: the vow and the joint account both answer to Saturn ([Capricorn on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-8th-house-cusp/); [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)).

Marriage, its timing, its length: none of that is written on a cusp, and I don't read one for it. What this one describes is what you value in the person facing you, and what you're willing to build with them. Earlier in the day the sign would have stood on the 8th cusp, later on the 6th ([Capricorn on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-6th-house-cusp/)), a matter of minutes near the horizon, which is why I want the time on the certificate rather than the one remembered ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
