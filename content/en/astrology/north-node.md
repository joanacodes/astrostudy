The North Node isn't a planet, or anything a telescope could find. It's one of the two places where the Moon's path crosses the [ecliptic](/astrology/ecliptic/), the Sun's path through our sky — the one where the Moon heads north — and astrologers read it as the direction a life grows in: the way forward that doesn't come naturally yet.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

I read the North Node as a direction, not a destination. It points to qualities that feel slightly foreign at first — a little clumsy, a little exposed — and that get easier, and more rewarding, the more you use them. It never works alone: the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) sits exactly opposite and describes where you start from, what came easily or early. Together they make one line, the [nodal axis](/astrology/nodal-axis/), and I read both ends together.

- **Its sign** shows the kind of growth: bolder, calmer, more curious, more tender.
- **Its house** shows the area of life where that growth is asked for: love, work, home, friendship.
- **Its aspects** show which planets join the journey, and which pull the other way.

The nodes belong to the [Moon](/astrology/moon/): they mark where its path meets the [Sun](/astrology/sun/)'s, and eclipses happen near them. So the North Node is worth reading beside the Moon — what you need to feel safe, and where you're asked to grow beyond it. Its sign changes only every 18 months or so, so it's shared by everyone born around the same time; its house needs a [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and that's where it becomes yours.

A planet on the North Node joins the direction. With the [Sun conjunct the North Node](/astrology/sun-conjunct-north-node/), who you are and where you're growing pull the same way. The [Moon conjunct the North Node](/astrology/moon-conjunct-north-node/) puts your feelings on the path itself: what you need and where you're going tend to meet. [Saturn conjunct the North Node](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-north-node/) often describes growth that comes slowly and is built to last. And a planet square the nodes, like [Venus square the North Node](/astrology/venus-square-north-node/), sits across the path, halfway between the two ends: a pleasure or a bond that asks to be included rather than given up.

## The North Node through the signs {#through-the-signs}

The North Node spends about 18 months in each sign, moving backwards, so everyone born within the same year and a half or so shares it; the house and the aspects make it personal. In plain words:

- **[North Node in Aries](/astrology/north-node-in-aries/):** learning to act on your own say-so, after years of keeping the peace.
- **[North Node in Taurus](/astrology/north-node-in-taurus/):** growing towards calm, steady, simple pleasures, away from one crisis after another.
- **[North Node in Gemini](/astrology/north-node-in-gemini/):** asking, listening and staying curious instead of already knowing the answer.
- **[North Node in Cancer](/astrology/north-node-in-cancer/):** letting feelings, home and care count as much as achievement.
- **[North Node in Leo](/astrology/north-node-in-leo/):** stepping out of the group to be seen, and to create something that is yours.
- **[North Node in Virgo](/astrology/north-node-in-virgo/):** turning good intentions into practical, daily, useful habits.
- **[North Node in Libra](/astrology/north-node-in-libra/):** learning partnership and give-and-take, after getting used to going it alone.
- **[North Node in Scorpio](/astrology/north-node-in-scorpio/):** letting go of comfort to share deeply and let things change.
- **[North Node in Sagittarius](/astrology/north-node-in-sagittarius/):** trusting a bigger view and a belief of your own over endless detail.
- **[North Node in Capricorn](/astrology/north-node-in-capricorn/):** taking up responsibility and a place in the world beyond the family.
- **[North Node in Aquarius](/astrology/north-node-in-aquarius/):** offering your gifts to a group or a cause, without needing the spotlight.
- **[North Node in Pisces](/astrology/north-node-in-pisces/):** easing off control and trusting what can't be planned or perfected.

## The North Node in the houses {#in-the-houses}

Its house shows where in life the growth is asked for; the South Node sits in the house opposite, on familiar ground.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/north-node-in-1st-house/):** putting yourself forward, after a life tuned to other people.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/north-node-in-2nd-house/):** building resources and values of your own, rather than leaning on shared ones.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/north-node-in-3rd-house/):** the near and everyday: conversations, neighbours, siblings, learning by asking.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/north-node-in-4th-house/):** home, family and an inner life, after a strong pull towards public achievement.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/north-node-in-5th-house/):** joy, play, romance and creations of your own, not only the group's plans.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/north-node-in-6th-house/):** daily routines and useful work that bring a drifting life down to earth.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/north-node-in-7th-house/):** partnership and cooperation: letting someone else in on the decisions.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/north-node-in-8th-house/):** trust, intimacy and sharing, beyond doing everything yourself.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/north-node-in-9th-house/):** travel, study and a philosophy of your own, beyond facts and small talk.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/north-node-in-10th-house/):** a public role and responsibility out in the world, beyond the comfort of home.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/north-node-in-11th-house/):** friends, groups and shared aims, beyond being the centre of attention.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/north-node-in-12th-house/):** rest, solitude and compassion, after a habit of staying busy.

## The North Node in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), the nodes show where two people stretch each other, or settle into the familiar. When one person's planet sits on the other's North Node, the one whose node it is often feels encouraged — sometimes pushed — towards exactly what they find hardest, in a way that can be exciting or tiring. [Venus conjunct the North Node](/astrology/venus-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) tends to feel like affection that makes the next step easier; when [their North Node falls in your 7th house](/astrology/north-node-in-7th-house-synastry/), the relationship itself can feel like part of where they're heading. Some astrologers call these contacts fated. I'd rather say they feel significant, and leave the meaning to the two of you. I read them in both directions, because who stretches whom isn't always mutual; [the nodes in synastry](/astrology/nodes-in-synastry/) goes further.

## Transits and the nodal return {#nodal-return}

The nodes move backwards through your chart, about 18 months in each sign, and they bring the eclipses with them: an eclipse happens when a new or full moon falls near the nodes, so for a year and a half or so the eclipses land mostly in the same pair of signs, and the same pair of your houses ([why eclipses follow the nodes](/astrology/why-eclipses-follow-the-nodes/)). When the North Node crosses one of your planets — the [transiting North Node conjunct your natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-sun/), say — that part of you tends to come forward; as it moves through a house, like the [North Node transiting the 7th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-7th-house/), that area of life asks for attention.

About every 18.6 years the nodes come back to where they were when you were born: the [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/), at about 18½, 37 and 56. Halfway between, at about 9, 28 and 46, they lie the other way round, the North Node over your natal South Node: the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/). Astrologers read both as checkpoints, when the question of direction comes back. It's weather, not a forecast: a season to notice, never an event to wait for.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

My notebook doesn't name the nodes. When I [went through charts like an invoice](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) — Sun, Moon, rising, each planet in its sign and house — about a third of what I checked held: 30 to 40%. The angle it reports is the rising sign, which held about a third of the time too. The three that stood out were [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), at 75 to 90%. So I have no figure for the North Node, and I won't invent one. I read it as a direction worth trying on, never a verdict, and I tell you when part of a reading leans on it.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Your North Node is your destiny.”** It's a direction many astrologers read as worth growing towards. Nothing in a chart decides for you, and nobody fails their North Node.
- **“The North Node is good and the South Node is bad.”** They're two ends of one line. What came easily is still yours; growth adds to it rather than replacing it.
- **“The North Node is a planet.”** It's a crossing point between two paths in the sky. There's nothing there to see.
- **“A retrograde North Node means something special.”** The mean node always moves backwards; that's simply how the nodes travel ([retrograde, explained](/astrology/retrograde-explained/)).
