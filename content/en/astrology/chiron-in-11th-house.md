Friends, groups, causes and hopes: in the 11th house a chart looks for its people and its future. Fitting in is usually where this Chiron feels it — the party you watched from the edge, the team you never quite joined — and the adapting tends to build friendships and communities with the door left open.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Jupiter has its joy in [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), which the old astrologers called the Good Spirit; a succedent house tied to Aquarius, it holds friends, groups and hopes. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) here adapts around a sensitivity about belonging to a group: being left out, being the odd note in a crowd, hopes that once felt naive. The adapting builds your own circles — friendships chosen with care, communities around a shared cause, networks that welcome people who arrive late or different. Many people with this placement become the one who notices who's missing and goes to find them. Tradition also gave the 11th its benefactors, and this Chiron often has to learn to accept their help as readily as it gives its own.

Where exactly Chiron falls in the 11th depends on the birth time ([why I wait for the time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#why-i-wait-for-the-time)); in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, the house follows straight on from the Midheaven, so an early Chiron here sits close to the top of the chart. Public life lies on one side, in [Chiron in the 10th house](/astrology/chiron-in-10th-house/); the private quiet of [Chiron in the 12th house](/astrology/chiron-in-12th-house/) on the other.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Love often starts as friendship with this placement, and the closeness it understands best is the kind between equals who chose each other. You may hold back in groups even with a partner beside you, or lean on one person for all the belonging you missed elsewhere. A partner who shares your circle without swallowing it, and has friends of their own, takes the pressure off. The relationship tends to thrive when it is also a team with a project. Big gatherings may tire you more than they tire your partner; agreeing on a time to leave, in advance, can help.

## At work {#at-work}

Teams, networks, charities, unions, online communities, anything collective suits it, especially roles that make a group more welcoming. You may be the one who sets up the support group, the alumni network, the group chat that keeps everyone connected. Your sense of who is being left out is a professional strength. It works best when you're inside the circle too, not only holding the door. Leading a group can suit you well, especially the kind of leading that shares the credit.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Holding back is the habit. On a hard day this Chiron may assume it won't be welcome and stay away, join groups only as the helper, or give up on a hope before anyone can disappoint it. Belonging can feel conditional, earned by being useful. And hopes may be kept small on purpose, so that nobody, you included, can be let down. Belonging online can stand in for the real thing, convenient and a little lonely.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Go to the thing, and stay for the second half. Let one friend help you, rather than always being the one who helps. Join a group for the activity rather than for the people; belonging often follows. Write down a hope you'd be embarrassed to say aloud, and keep it somewhere safe. Host something small: a dinner, a walk, a club of three.

My notebook is the reason I give an 11th-house Chiron time: of everything I checked, Chiron, Saturn and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time, against about a third for most of the rest ([The invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/#what-held)).
