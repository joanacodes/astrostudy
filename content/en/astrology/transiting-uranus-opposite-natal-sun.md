Once in its 84-year round, Uranus reaches the point straight across from your Sun. For about twenty months the change is less one you make than one you meet, in someone else or in the world around you, and then have to answer.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) breaks patterns open so that something more distinct can grow; your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your identity and the direction you organise your life around. In an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, two functions stand at either end of one axis, and each is seen most clearly from the other end. Under this transit your direction is seen against change that seems to come from outside. It can look like a partner who needs more room, a workplace being reorganised, a friend whose life suddenly runs on different rules.

The reverse is just as common: you meet in another person the freedom you never claimed for yourself, and admire it, or resent it, before you see that it's a question about you. Neither reaction is wrong. The opposition asks for a balance rather than a winner: room for the other person's change, and a direction that doesn't depend on everyone around you staying still. I don't read it for a separation, a dismissal or any other ending; what people do in these months is theirs ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

The axis decides where. With your Sun in the 1st and Uranus crossing the 7th ([Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/)), your sense of self meets a partner's or a collaborator's need for room, one to one. That axis is drawn from the minute you were born: no birth time, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within the orbs I use for timing, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition lasts about twenty months, sometimes as little as eleven. More than nine times in ten, Uranus crosses the exact point three times, the middle crossing made while it's retrograde.

Of all the steps Uranus takes with your Sun, this is the farthest: about 42 years after [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-sun/), roughly fourteen years after the first [transiting Uranus trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-sun/) and fourteen before the second. [Transiting Uranus square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-sun/) brackets it about twenty years either side, and [transiting Uranus sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-sun/) lies further off, near the conjunction. Among people born between 1930 and 2009, about 1 in 50 reaches 80 without meeting this [transit](/astrology/transits/) at all.

Two kinds of birth have a fixed timetable. Born with [Sun opposite Uranus](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus/), you met it at birth, and Uranus won't stand there again until about 84. Born with the two together ([Sun conjunct Uranus](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus/)), you meet it at your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), between about 39 and 44½.

Uranus crosses Gemini until May 2033, facing Sagittarius. If your birthday falls between about 22 November and 21 December, your opposition comes some time in those years.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask whose change it is. Some of what moves around you is yours to answer; some is only yours to allow.

Let the other person be a mirror rather than a map. Their freedom shows you something about your own, and it doesn't tell you where to go.

Negotiate before you dig in. Your direction comes through this opposition in better shape when it bends a little than when you try to hold everyone else still around it.
