Taurus on the 8th house cusp turns the house of what's shared into the house of what's kept. Trust, intimacy, joint money and the debts people carry between them begin here in the sign of continuity, and they grow the way Taurus grows things: by accumulation.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

What's held in common, once a bond is real, takes its manner from the sign on the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) holds by looking after. Trust builds up for you through kept promises, one after another, until it's solid enough to lean on; intimacy deepens by continuity more than by revelation. Someone who has been there for ten years knows a part of you that no one new could reach in a month. Joint arrangements, a shared account, a home bought together, a loan, tend to be kept in good order, and you'd rather maintain what's shared than renegotiate it every year. When a crisis comes, your instinct is to keep daily life going, the meals made, the bills paid, and that steadiness is often what others lean on. Opposite, the 2nd opens in Scorpio, which turns the zodiac's natural order round: you can be guarded about what's yours alone, and patient, even generous, with what's joint.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your 8th, and Libra, its other sign, rises with this cusp in about 9 London charts in 10, and more at New York. So the planet that speaks for you, your chart ruler, also speaks for what you share: the way you meet the world and the way you live intimacy come from the same place, and sometimes, when Libra holds the 12th cusp too, so does your private life. Scorpio, the 8th's natural sign, is a sign of Venus's detriment; with Taurus on your cusp, the house opens in one of Venus's own signs instead, and Venus deals with what's shared from a position of strength. Follow Venus to its house and you find where the shared things are looked after: the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/) house.

The figures for this cusp, in the Placidus houses I use, are about 1 birth in 9 at London and 1 in 10 at New York. When Scorpio rises instead of Libra, about 1 London chart in 10, Taurus spills back onto the Descendant, and partnership and what partnership holds share a ruler ([Taurus on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-7th-house-cusp/); [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). It never holds the 9th cusp as well, so [Taurus on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-9th-house-cusp/) never shares a chart with this one. And a chart can have Taurus on no cusp at all, the reverse of a duplicated sign ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Inheritance, which tradition put in this house alongside death, is part of what you look after, money, a house, a box of letters, and Taurus looks after it well. Death isn't something any chart can foretell, and I don't read for it ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). The cusp, like every cusp, comes from your birth time, so the time comes first ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
