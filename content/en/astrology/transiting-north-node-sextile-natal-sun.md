Some years, the two eclipse seasons sit lopsidedly around your birthday, one about two months away, the other about four months off on the other side. That is the mark of this transit: the North Node 60° from your Sun, the South Node 120° from it, and a month or so of opening.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the part of a chart that holds you together: identity, purpose, the way you put yourself into the world. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a less practised capacity, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite it to the one you can already rely on. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates through opportunity, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) both ends of the axis are on good terms with your Sun at once: the North Node by sextile, the South Node by trine.

It tends to bring a chance to try something new in a way that still sounds like you: a course in a subject you have circled for years, an offer to lead a small project, a fresh way of describing what you do. Because the South Node trines your Sun, skills you already have can get you started. That helps, and it also makes it easy to slip back into the familiar version without trying the new one. A sextile only works once you take it up.

With the node in Aquarius until March 2028, the sextile is the turn of Aries Suns, born about 21 March to 19 April, and of Sagittarius Suns, born about 22 November to 21 December. For both, the South Node in Leo shares the Sun's element, so the old strengths are the same kind of fire as the self, while the Aquarius end asks for something cooler and more collective. From March 2028, with the node in Capricorn, it moves on to Pisces and Scorpio Suns.

Where the opening appears depends on your Sun's house. With a [Sun in the 3rd house](/astrology/sun-in-3rd-house/), a conversation or a class tends to be the door. That house, unlike your Sun's degree, hangs on the clock time of birth, which I ask for before I read anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). [Sun sextile North Node](/astrology/sun-sextile-north-node/) in a birth chart is this same geometry fixed at the moment of birth; the transit lends it to everyone for a month.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My forecast passes over this sextile: it counts only the two angles that bring a node onto your Sun, the conjunction and the opposition. Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, most sextiles take about a month, and nineteen in twenty cross once. Eclipse seasons decide the exceptions ([eclipse seasons](/astrology/eclipse-seasons/)). While the node sextiles your Sun, the Sun reaches the nodes about two months from your birthday on one side and about four on the other, and if the exact sextile falls in one of those seasons the true node slows almost to a halt: one pass in ten runs past three and a half months, and one in twenty-five crosses three times. A quick pass takes three and a half weeks.

The sextile comes twice in each round of about 18.6 years, eight or nine times by 80: about three years after the North Node crosses your Sun ([transiting North Node conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-sun/)), and again about fifteen and a half years after it, on the way back. In the long stretch between them the node squares your Sun ([transiting North Node square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-sun/)), trines it ([transiting North Node trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-sun/)) and, halfway round, brings the South Node onto it ([transiting North Node opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-sun/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to one offer in the month, and choose it because it sounds like you, not because it is the hardest.

Use an old skill as the way in. The familiar can carry you to the new, as long as it doesn't do all the work.

Keep the step small enough to finish within the month.
