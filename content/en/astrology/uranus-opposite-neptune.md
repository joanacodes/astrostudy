Uranus opposite Neptune is the one aspect of this pair that nobody born between 1930 and 2009 carries: in those eighty years, the two planets never stood within 6° of opposite each other. The aspect still has a meaning, and it turns up in two places, between two charts and in transits.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables differentiation, liberation and the reform of systems: the part of a chart that steps apart in order to change something. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination, compassion and the softening of boundaries that have stopped serving: the part that reaches towards a larger whole. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) holds the two in polarity: across the chart, each makes the other visible, and the work is integration, not a contest.

For this pair, the polarity runs between breaking away and merging, between the experiment and the dream. Freedom shows up most clearly against a longing to belong; an ideal shows up most clearly against someone's insistence on doing things differently. Integrated, the two let a vision guide the reform and a reform give the vision a shape. Polarity isn't contradiction, and it isn't a quarrel inside a person.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The count found no birth with it. Across those eighty years Uranus, the faster planet, was closing in on Neptune: the trine of 1936 to 1946, the square of the 1950s and the sextile of the 1960s led up to the meeting of 1990 to 1996, after which Uranus began to pull ahead. The opposition is the far point of that cycle, so it lies before the count on one side and long after it on the other.

If a chart for someone born in those years shows Uranus opposite Neptune, check the birth date first, then the orb the software allows. What a chart can have instead is a third body linking the two, opposite one and beside the other; I read that as two separate aspects, each with its own page.

The pair's other aspects, with the births that have them:

- **[Uranus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune/):** January 1990 to March 1996, and August to December 1996.
- **[Uranus sextile Neptune](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune/):** on and off from October 1963 to June 1970.
- **[Uranus square Neptune](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune/):** on and off from July 1951 to May 1958.
- **[Uranus trine Neptune](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune/):** on and off from June 1936 to March 1946.

Between two charts, [Uranus opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune-synastry/) does happen, mostly between people born many years apart: one person's Uranus facing the other's Neptune. It's one of the ways two generations meet, the one who wants to change things and the one who carries a picture of how they could be. As with any synastry, I read it only with both people's agreement ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

The natal aspect is missing, but its transits reach many people. [Transiting Uranus opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-neptune/) comes once in Uranus's 84-year round, at an age set by where the two planets sit in your chart; at about seven years a sign, it lasts months, often in three passes. For people born with the conjunction of the 1990s, it coincides with the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 42. [Transiting Neptune opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-uranus/) moves far more slowly and reaches only some birth years, at ages that vary widely.

Weather, not a forecast: a stretch when breaking away and belonging pull from opposite sides, and ask to be brought together.
