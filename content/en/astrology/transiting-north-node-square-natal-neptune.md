A square from the nodes doesn't land on your Neptune; it sets the whole axis across it. For about a month, twice a round, a longing you carry stands in the road between the way you have always lived and the way you are learning to.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) holds a chart's ideals: what you long for, what moves you, where you feel part of something larger. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the capacity you are practising, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) the one you have consolidated. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates through friction that demands adjustment, and since the nodes are exactly opposite, this [transit](/astrology/transits/) squares your Neptune from both ends.

It often shows as an ideal that fits neither side. The old routine has no room for it; the new plan treats it as a distraction. Or the ideal itself has been asked to carry too much: a person expected to rescue, a job expected to give a whole life meaning. Neptune's blind spots, taking imagination for evidence or compassion for unlimited availability, come into view here, and the square's work is to give the longing a place in your plans at its true size. If what arrives is a fog or a low mood that doesn't lift, the right door is a doctor or a therapist ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

A generation meets it in the same months. From July 2026 to March 2028, with the North Node in Aquarius and the South Node in Leo, both square the Neptune of everyone born with [Neptune in Scorpio](/astrology/neptune-in-scorpio/), from December 1955 to November 1970. People born a year apart have Neptune about two degrees apart, and the node takes some six weeks to cover two degrees, so friends born a year or two apart meet the square within a few months of each other. Which houses it stirs depends on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the true node needs about a month. An eclipse season in those weeks makes it dawdle, the Sun being close to the nodes, and the square stretches to three months or more; about one pass in 25 crosses the exact degree three times. My forecast passes over this square, as it does every contact in which no end of the axis meets the point, so I read it beside the slower transits of the moment rather than on its own.

The squares come twice a round, nine years and a few months apart; by 80, eight or nine. The first came at 8.2 for someone born on 1 January 1955 and at 2.7 for 1 January 1960. Each falls between [Transiting North Node sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-neptune/) and [Transiting North Node trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-neptune/), halfway between [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-neptune/) and [Transiting North Node opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-neptune/). [Neptune square North Node](/astrology/neptune-square-north-node/) carries the same right angle from birth.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write the ideal down in one sentence, then write what it would take, in hours and in money, to give it a real place.

Take back one expectation you placed on a person or a job. It may be lighter for both of you.

Keep one quiet practice that feeds Neptune without asking it to save anything: music, water, a walk with no destination.
