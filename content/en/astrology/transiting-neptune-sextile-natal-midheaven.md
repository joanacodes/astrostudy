Two sextiles reach every Midheaven, one from around your 8th house on Neptune's way up to the top of the chart, one from around your 12th after it. Most people meet one, some both and about 1 in 6 neither, and each is an opening for imagination in the role you play in the world.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) is the point of public actualisation: what you do that others see, and what it contributes. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings compassion, ideals and imagination. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, opens a door between the two that you have to walk through, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) an ideal finds an easier way into your work or your public role: a project with a purpose, a gentler kind of authority, a skill put at the service of something you believe in.

On Neptune's climb to the top of the chart, the first sextile is cast from your 8th house, where commitments and trust run deep, and it tends to open through a collaboration ([Neptune transiting the 8th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-8th-house/)). The second, after the crossing, is cast from your 12th and opens through work no one watches: care, solitude, retreat ([Neptune transiting the 12th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-12th-house/)). That second side answers the Midheaven's blind spot, underestimating the contributions nobody sees.

Nothing is handed over. A sextile describes a climate that helps if you act, and a role changed by an ideal is changed by you, not by the sky.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each pass runs about two and a half years in my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and about twenty-two months at its shortest, with three exact hits as a rule; roughly one pass in four brings five.

The hour of birth sorts people here. Born within an hour or two of Neptune rising, with Neptune close to your Ascendant, you are among the 1 in 6, in the count for births from 1930 to 2009, who never meet either sextile: Neptune has just passed one, and the other is too far round. Born around Neptune's setting, near your Descendant, you have time for both, and for the conjunction with your Midheaven in between ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)). Most people, born at other hours, meet one.

Both sextiles hang on the minute of birth. A Midheaven worked backwards from the events of your life would make this transit circular, the time fitted to the very years it is then read against, and so I don't work from a rectified chart ([Rectification](/astrology/rectification/), [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Each sextile sits between the conjunction and one of the squares ([transiting Neptune square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-midheaven/)); the trines ([transiting Neptune trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-midheaven/)) and the opposition ([transiting Neptune opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-midheaven/)) belong to the lower half of the round. In these years of Neptune in Aries, which end in March 2039, the sextile lands on Midheavens in Gemini, cast from the 8th-house side, and on those in Aquarius, cast from the 12th ([Gemini on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/), [Aquarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Bring one ideal into your work in a form someone else can see: a project, a standard, a way of treating the people you work with.

Value the unseen part. If the opening comes from the 12th-house side, the work behind the scenes is the work, and it doesn't need an audience to count.

Say yes to a collaboration that shares your values. From the 8th-house side, the opening usually needs another person's trust as well as your own.
