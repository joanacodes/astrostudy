A square from Uranus to your natal Neptune is a season when an ideal meets the urge to change it, and neither simply gives way. Most people meet it twice, for about twenty months each time, at ages that slide by about five years with every decade of birth.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) holds your ideals, your imagination and your capacity for compassion. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) wants what is true brought into the open and tries something new in its place. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates two functions through constructive friction, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) sets the longing for something larger against the impatience to see it differently.

The friction often starts with disappointment: a cause, a teacher, a belief or a picture of how life would be that no longer matches what you see. Uranus wants it named and replaced; Neptune wants the longing kept, even when its object has changed. With both pulling at once, it can feel like restlessness in a fog.

The trap is swapping one illusion for a newer one, or escaping into novelty because the old dream hurts. The square's work is to keep the longing and change what it's attached to, so that what you hope for survives being looked at clearly. If what you feel in these months is heavier than restlessness, that's a matter for a doctor or a therapist rather than a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

With Neptune in your 9th and Uranus crossing your 12th ([Uranus transiting the 12th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-12th-house/)), beliefs about the wider world loosen in private, and insight tends to arrive when you're alone. Only your birth time can draw that pair of houses, and I don't read charts that lack it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

An ideal under strain gets about twenty months of this square within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, eleven at the shortest, and in more than nine passes out of ten Uranus is exact three times.

For births from the mid-1960s on, the first square comes about five years younger with every decade of birth: in the site's count, at about 35 for someone born at the start of 1965, about 30 for 1975, about 25 for 1985, about 21 for 1995 and about 16 for 2005. The second follows some forty years later, so the latest of those births meet it in their fifties and the earliest only near 80.

Born between 1951 and 1958 with [Uranus square Neptune](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune/), you had the first square at birth or soon after, and the second came at about 40, in the mid-1990s, close to your [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/), transiting Neptune squaring its own place. Someone born at the start of 1965 meets the second at about 79, and a few births meet only one before 80, though every one of them meets at least that.

The two squares sit about twenty-one years either side of [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-neptune/) and of [transiting Uranus opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-neptune/); [transiting Uranus sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Uranus trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-neptune/) lie about seven years from each square.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the ideal that disappointed you, and what it was protecting. The longing underneath usually still deserves a home.

Test the new vision before you move into it. What replaces an old dream can arrive fast under this square; give it a season of ordinary days first.

Keep your routines plain. Regular sleep, meals and walks are the ground Neptune needs while Uranus stirs the air.
