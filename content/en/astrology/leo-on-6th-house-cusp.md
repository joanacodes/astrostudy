The 6th house is the ordinary day: the work you do, the habits that keep you going, the people beside you while you do it. Leo on the 6th house cusp asks that the ordinary day be something you can be proud of.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on a cusp shapes how its house is lived ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Leo](/astrology/leo/) brings to the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) a need to put your heart into the daily round. I read it as work done with pride whatever the task: the report finished properly although nobody will praise it, the shop counter run as if it were a small stage. You do your best when what you do is noticed, and a word of appreciation can matter to you as much as the pay. In a team you're often the one who brings the warmth, remembers the birthdays and stands up for a colleague treated unfairly. Routines last when they give you some pleasure and lapse when they feel like penance, so the habits that look after you work best when they're also things you enjoy. The harder side is pride: tasks that feel beneath you, or working too hard to earn the recognition that ought to come anyway.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules your 6th house, and its place shows what your working days are spent on: as ruler of the 6th, in the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). The 6th is wide in these charts, from about 38° to more than 57° at London, and in more than half of them it holds the whole of Virgo, the house's natural sign, with no cusp of its own ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Mercury, Virgo's ruler and the 6th's natural one, then has a say alongside the Sun: the Sun brings the heart to the work, Mercury the method, and I read both before saying how a working day suits you.

About 1 birth in 17 has Leo on the 6th house cusp at London, and 1 in 15 at New York, counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use. The 6th cusp sits just below the western horizon, and in the north Leo goes down far faster than it comes up: it holds this cusp for less than an hour and a half a day at London. The further from the equator, the wider that gap between rising and setting, which is why New York, further south, sees Leo here more often. Aries or Pisces rises in these charts, and Leo hardly ever opens a second house: the 5th in about 1 London chart in 30, the 7th never. Across all births, Leo opens no house at all in about 1 in 7 at London.

Less than an hour and a half is a narrow window. A time noted to the nearest hour may belong to this page or to the ones on either side, [Leo on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/) and [Leo on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/), and only the minute settles it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Tradition called the 6th Bad Fortune and looked there for illness. I read it as the care you give your body from day to day, never as a forecast of what will go wrong with it ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). With Leo on the cusp, the care that lasts is the kind you enjoy.
