Venus’s summer retrograde, from late July to early September every eight years, has settled into Leo: since 2023 it begins and ends there. Leo loves out loud, and these weeks turn the volume down long enough to hear what the love is saying.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Leo](/astrology/leo/) is fixed fire, ruled by the Sun, the sign of expression and of warmth that wants to be seen. Venus there loves generously and visibly, and enjoys admiring as much as being admired. When [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) returns to Leo, the planet seems from Earth to halt and back through the sign for about 43 days, and the display turns inward: the gestures you make to show affection, the creative work you’re proud of, the delight of being noticed and how much of it you need.

The five summer retrogrades in my list, from 2000 to 2040, all end in Leo and come round every eight years about 2° lower. The first two began just across the border ([Venus retrograde in Virgo](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-virgo/)): from 2°57′ Virgo back to 16°35′ Leo in 2007, from 0°46′ Virgo back to 14°23′ Leo in 2015. The last three stay wholly in Leo: from 23 July to 4 September 2023, 28°36′ to 12°12′, the most recent; from 20 July to 1 September 2031, 26°25′ to 10°01′, the next; and from 18 July to 30 August 2039, 24°14′ to 7°50′. The series moves slowly towards Cancer, which has none in my list ([Venus retrograde in Cancer](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-cancer/)).

Venus retrograde in Leo at birth is another matter, a warmth that shows itself once it’s sure, and I read it on its own terms ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Leo occupies is fixed by [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), since the houses come from the hour and the place; without the time I can’t draw them, so I don’t read the chart. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 2007 and 2015 retrogrades could begin in the house where Virgo sits and end in Leo’s; the later ones keep to the Leo house unless a cusp falls within their 16½°.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/leo-rising/):** Leo rising: a warm, open presence; the way you shine on first meeting, and whether it’s still how you want to be seen.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/leo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** generosity with what you have; the gifts you give, and what they say about what you value.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** warm, expressive words; the praise you give freely, and the praise you’d like to hear.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home you’re proud of; the gatherings you host, and the warmth that makes them worth the effort.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** Leo in its natural house, and Venus’s joy: romance, play and creative work; what you love making, and for whom.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** pride in daily work; the job done well that nobody thanked you for, valued by you first.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Leo on the Descendant, with Aquarius rising: a partner who shines; admiration given and returned, looked at kindly.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/leo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** loyalty and intimacy; what you share wholeheartedly, and what pride keeps back.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** big-hearted beliefs and journeys; a place or a teaching you loved, revisited.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** being seen at work; recognition, and what you’d do even without the applause.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/leo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a generous circle; the friends who cheer you on, and the ones you cheer.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/leo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** hidden warmth; affection you show only when nobody is watching.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Your natal Sun, Leo’s ruler, shows by its house where you most want your warmth to be noticed. These weeks suit showing affection in smaller ways than usual and seeing what still lands: a note rather than a speech, one person rather than a crowd.

Nothing in the sky decides how a relationship goes. Leo’s retrograde asks only that the warmth comes from the heart rather than from habit.
