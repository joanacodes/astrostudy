If [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) was rising when you were born, you probably settle into a new place by finding something to do in it. It's a modest, observant way in, and a reliable one.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

On your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), Virgo's mutable earth is practical and always adjusting. Call it a working welcome: you get to know a place by how it runs, and people by what they need, and you join in once you've found where you could be of use. With the Ascendant as the cusp of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), as it is in Placidus, the house system I use, the sign of the 6th, daily work and craft, opens the house of the self, and you tend to introduce yourself through what you can do.

Virgo and Libra mirror each other across the September equinox point, and they rise at the same pace: about 170 minutes at London, 165 at Paris and 151 at New York. Nearly three hours at London makes Virgo the rising sign of about one London birth in eight. For the sign, then, a time rounded to the hour is usually enough, since half an hour covers less than a fifth of Virgo at London. But Virgo would be the first to point out that usually isn't always. Near the first or last degrees, a round hour can belong to [Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/) or [Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/), and the record has the last word ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you were born in the southern hemisphere, turn these figures round: signs that rise fast in the northern hemisphere rise slowly in the southern, and Virgo's long rise becomes a shorter one.

## First impressions {#first-impressions}

What newcomers notice first is your attention: the thing you picked up that others missed, the practical question, the quiet offer to help. You can seem reserved, even modest to a fault, because you'd rather be useful than be the subject. They often trust you with a task before they know much else about you.

It's easy to take your attention for judgement. Analysis isn't criticism: when a Virgo Ascendant notices what could work better, it's usually a sign of interest, a way of caring about the thing in front of it.

Precision cuts both ways, so here is mine: in [my testing notes](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the rising sign is among the placements everyone reads first, and it held about a third of the time. I read your Virgo Ascendant as a likely first impression, then check it against the rest of you.

## Its ruler {#its-ruler}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Virgo, and as your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/) it has its strongest sign on your Ascendant and its weakest on your Descendant. In Virgo it is both at home and exalted; in Pisces, opposite, it is in detriment and fall. Wherever your Mercury actually sits, by sign and house, shows where your attention goes to work. It usually stays two to three weeks in a sign, longer when it turns retrograde.

With Mercury in Virgo too, a mind that sorts, checks and refines meets an Ascendant that does the same. In Gemini, its other home, the chart ruler is quicker and lighter than your Ascendant suggests. In Pisces, on your Descendant, the chart ruler that wants exact words sits in the sign of what can't be measured, and it can describe a mind that learns a second way of knowing through the people closest to it. Neither end is a flaw; they're the two ends of one axis, and you have both.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Virgo rising loves in verbs: checking, fixing, helping, making the days run better for both of you. The risk is helping in place of saying, or improving when the other person only wanted to be heard. Saying the feeling out loud, even imperfectly, counts for more than another problem solved.

Pisces holds your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), and gentle, imaginative people draw you, the kind who forgive easily and let things blur where you would sharpen them ([Pisces on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/)).

The partner who lets you stop fixing for an evening may be giving you the rarest thing of all: permission to be imperfect in company.
