A relationship can run like clockwork and still feel, to the two people in it, as if it never quite measures up. That nagging *not quite good enough* is the territory of composite Chiron in Virgo, along with the patient, practical craft a pair develops in reply.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Practice, not perfection, is what this relationship learns. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/), in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) of two people, marks the limit the bond keeps returning to and the know-how it builds around it; [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), mutable earth ruled by [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), turns that limit into a matter of standards. Is the relationship working properly, is it useful, is it doing enough? The pair may audit itself constantly, correct each other's ways of doing things, or feel the bond has to justify its place by what it gets done.

Two relaxed people can find their relationship oddly exacting, as if it kept a checklist neither of them wrote. Craft, rather than cure, is how it copes: routines tried and adjusted until they suit both, tasks shared out by what each does well, a practice of fixing one small thing at a time instead of the whole arrangement at once. Friends swamped by the practical side of life find them useful company, since they've learned how to break it into steps.

Few pairs have it: about 1 in 24, in two million pairs of birth dates drawn from 1930 to 2009, which makes Virgo the rarest composite Chiron sign after Libra. Chiron spent barely two years in Virgo on each visit, from July 1943 to July 1945 and from September 1993 to September 1995, so only people born within those spans share it as a generation; everyone else needs a gap. Eight years can be enough: a June 1990 birth puts Chiron at about 16° Cancer, a June 1998 birth at about 13° Scorpio, and the pair's composite Chiron at about 14° Virgo. Other pairs arrive here from opposite sides of the circle: with Chiron at about 16° Sagittarius for a July 1950 birth and about 17° Gemini for a January 1987 one, almost exactly opposite, the nearer midpoint is 16° Virgo on some days and 16° Pisces on others.

[Chiron in Virgo](/astrology/chiron-in-virgo/) in a birth chart concerns the standard one person holds themselves to. In the composite the standard belongs to the bond, which is why a remark about the washing-up can land like a verdict on the whole relationship.

A relationship can turn into a project to improve. Critique takes the place of affection, busy usefulness the place of time together. The cure is unglamorous: name what already works, out loud and often, and agree that some things are good enough as they are. Beside it, [composite Chiron in Leo](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-leo/) worries about being noticed and [composite Chiron in Libra](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-libra/) about being equal; Virgo worries about being right.

This is the composite technique as its users describe it: I don't cast composites, and those who do read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), never as a prediction of anyone's health, work or future together. My own checking was of birth charts, placement by placement, and never of composites ([my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)). And the first item on any checklist is consent: no chart of two people should be drawn until both have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
