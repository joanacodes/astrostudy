A relationship can run like clockwork and still feel, to the two people in it, as if it never quite measures up. Composite Chiron in Virgo makes *good enough* the bond's tender spot, and describes the patient, practical craft the pair develops in answer.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Method is where this relationship does its adapting. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/), in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) of two people, marks the limit the bond keeps returning to and the know-how it builds around it; [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), mutable earth ruled by [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), turns that limit into a matter of standards. Is the relationship working properly, is it useful, is it doing enough? The pair may audit itself constantly, correct each other's ways of doing things, or feel the bond has to justify its place by what it gets done.

Neither partner has to be a perfectionist for that to appear. Two relaxed people can find their relationship oddly exacting, as if it kept a checklist neither of them wrote. The adapting is craft rather than cure: routines tried and adjusted until they suit both, tasks shared out by what each does well, a practice of fixing one small thing at a time instead of the whole arrangement at once. Pairs like this are often a real help to friends swamped by the practical side of life, because they've learned how to break it into steps.

Few pairs have it: about 1 in 24, in two million pairs of birth dates drawn from 1930 to 2009, which makes Virgo the rarest composite Chiron sign after Libra. Chiron spent barely two years in Virgo on each visit, from July 1943 to July 1945 and from September 1993 to September 1995, so only people born within those spans share it as a generation; everyone else needs a gap. Eight years apart, born in June 1990 and June 1998, two people have Chirons at about 16° Cancer and 13° Scorpio, and the composite's sits at about 14° Virgo. Other pairs arrive here from opposite sides of the circle. Born in July 1950 and January 1987, two people have Chirons at about 16° Sagittarius and 17° Gemini, almost exactly opposite, and depending on the days the nearer midpoint falls at about 16° Virgo or at 16° Pisces.

[Chiron in Virgo](/astrology/chiron-in-virgo/) in a birth chart concerns the standard one person holds themselves to. In the composite the standard belongs to the bond, and each of you may feel it as the other's criticism, or as your own failure to meet it.

The risk is a relationship that turns into a project to improve. Critique can take the place of affection, and busy usefulness the place of time together. It helps to name what already works, out loud and often, and to agree that some things are good enough as they are. Beside it, [composite Chiron in Leo](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-leo/) worries about being noticed and [composite Chiron in Libra](/astrology/composite-chiron-in-libra/) about being equal; Virgo worries about being right.

This is the composite technique as its users describe it: I don't cast composites, and those who do read this Chiron with both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), never as a prediction of anyone's health, work or future together. My own checking was of birth charts, placement by placement, and never of composites ([my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)). And a chart of two people waits until both of them agree to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
