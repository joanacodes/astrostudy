The Hunter's moon is defined by another full moon: it's the first one after the Harvest moon. That makes it an October full moon most years and a November one in some, and like the Harvest moon it is an old English name, not an astrological term. In a chart, it's a full moon in Aries or Taurus.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Start from the [Harvest moon](/astrology/harvest-moon/), the full moon nearest the September equinox. The next [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/), about 29½ days later, is the Hunter's moon. Since the Harvest moon falls between about 8 September and 8 October, the Hunter's moon falls between about 7 October and 7 November. Its dates, in Universal Time:

- **2026:** 26 October, 04:12 UT, at 2°46′ Taurus
- **2027:** 15 October, 13:47 UT, at 21°59′ Aries
- **2028:** 2 November, 09:17 UT, at 10°29′ Taurus
- **2029:** 22 October, 09:28 UT, at 29°16′ Aries
- **2030:** 11 October, 10:47 UT, at 18°10′ Aries

The 2028 one shows the rule at work. That year's Harvest moon is the full moon of 3 October, so the Hunter's moon slides to 2 November, and the November full moon carries two names at once: Hunter's moon by the rule, [Beaver moon](/astrology/beaver-moon/) by the month.

The signs follow from the same geometry. The Harvest moon has the Moon near the first degree of Aries; one lunation later, the Sun has moved on about a sign, so the Hunter's moon lands in the second half of Aries or the first half of Taurus, with the Sun opposite in Libra or Scorpio. The 2029 one, at 29°16′ Aries, sits just short of the line.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Like the Harvest moon, this name is English and older than the almanacs' list. NASA's full moon guide gives 1710 as the year of its first record in the Oxford English Dictionary, four years after the Harvest moon's, and states the rule: the full moon after the Harvest Moon.

The almanacs explain the name by the season. The leaves have fallen and the deer have fattened, and with the fields reaped under the Harvest moon, game was easier to see: hunting was the work of the month. Old as it is, the name isn't one of the Maine Farmer's Almanac's “Indian” ones. When the almanac printed those from the 1930s, its October names were, as NASA's October guide lists them, the Travel Moon, the Dying Grass Moon, and the Sanguine or Blood Moon.

So the Hunter's moon follows the Harvest moon, not the calendar. A year in which the Harvest moon comes late has a late Hunter's moon too.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

The Hunter's moon is a folk name, not astrology, and I don't read a hunt into it. What I read is the full moon itself, which by this rule is either the [full moon in Aries](/astrology/full-moon-in-aries/), with the Sun in Libra, or the [full moon in Taurus](/astrology/full-moon-in-taurus/), with the Sun in Scorpio. In the Taurus years, 2026 and 2028, the Moon is in the sign tradition gives as its exaltation, a sign where its need for steadiness and comfort is read as being at ease.

Whatever the sign, a full moon only reaches your chart through contact: within one degree of a planet or angle, on the Moon's side of the axis or the Sun's. A Hunter's moon at 21°59′ Aries, as in 2027, would touch a planet near 22° Aries or 22° Libra. I read it as a moment that brings into focus what a transit or progression is already working on there, never as a forecast standing on its own.

To know which house it lights up, I need the time you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The Hunter's moon is always in October.”** When the Harvest moon falls in October, the Hunter's moon moves into November, as on 2 November 2028.
- **“It's a Native American name.”** It's English, recorded since 1710. The almanac's “Indian” names for October were the Travel, Dying Grass and Blood Moon.
- **“The Hunter's moon is a blood moon.”** The almanac does list a Blood Moon for October, but no Hunter's moon from 2026 to 2030 is a lunar eclipse.
- **“It's the moon for going after what you want.”** The name is about deer and reaped fields. Astrologically, it's a full moon in Aries or Taurus, read through your chart.
