When Jupiter moves on from your 1st house into your 2nd, its growth turns from who you are to what you have and what you value. For about a year the question is less *who am I becoming?* than *what can I count on, and what is it worth to me?*

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens understanding and looks for meaning. The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds resources, values and self-worth: what you earn and keep, the talents you could live from, the things you'd protect if you had to choose. With Jupiter crossing it, I read a year when your sense of your own worth tends to stretch. A skill you'd taken for granted can start to look like something to build on, and values you'd only half said out loud get clearer, until you want your time, your work and your spending to agree with them.

Planets you were born with in the 2nd are the first Jupiter meets there, and each meeting widens that planet's way of valuing for a few weeks: with your Moon in the 2nd, what makes you feel secure; with your Mars there, what you're willing to work for.

This is also the stay where Jupiter's reputation gets in the way. People hope it will bring money, and some books promise as much. I don't read money in a chart, coming or going, and a [transit](/astrology/transits/) can't tell anyone what they'll earn ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). It brings out what your chart already holds about security and worth. What grows is your relationship with resources, never a figure on a statement.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Charts drawn at London's latitude, in Placidus, give Jupiter about a year in an average house, most often between about 11 months and nearly 2 years. The 2nd runs a little over a year in the middle and stops a little sooner at the long end, at about 22 months, and New York's latitude gives the 2nd exactly the same figures. Your own stay depends on the width of your 2nd house, which comes from your birth time and the latitude you were born at. Without the time, nobody can say where your 2nd begins, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The year before belongs to your 1st house ([Jupiter transiting the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-1st-house/)), the year after to your 3rd ([Jupiter transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-3rd-house/)).

To find your window, look up the sign and degree on the cusp of your 2nd house: Jupiter enters the house when it reaches that degree. It spends from June 2026 to July 2027 in Leo, then reaches Virgo in July 2027, Libra in August 2028, Scorpio in September 2029 and Sagittarius in October 2030, about a year apiece. Capricorn follows from November 2031 to November 2032, overlapping with Aquarius, which Jupiter enters in April 2032, leaves again, and keeps until December 2033. Pisces comes in April 2033, Aries in April 2034, and then Taurus, the 2nd house's own sign in the natural order, from April 2035 to May 2036; Gemini runs to May 2037 and Cancer to June 2038.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you value, then look at where your money and your hours actually go. This stay tends to make the gap between the two easier to see, and closing it is most of the growth on offer.

Price a skill honestly. If something you do easily is worth something to other people, this is a year to find out what, and to stop giving it away out of habit, if that's what you've been doing.

Be generous where you mean it. Jupiter widens whatever it touches, and in the 2nd that can include spending as freely as you earn: a budget isn't the opposite of growth, it's what lets growth stay. Measure the year by what you feel able to count on rather than by any number, because that's the part of the 2nd house a transit can actually change.
