When Jupiter moves on from your 1st house into your 2nd, its growth turns from who you are to what you have and what you value. For about a year the question is less *who am I becoming?* than *what can I count on, and what is it worth to me?*

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens understanding and looks for meaning. The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds resources, values and self-worth: what you earn and keep, the talents you could live from, the things you'd protect if you had to choose. As Jupiter crosses it, your sense of your own worth tends to stretch. A skill you'd taken for granted can start to look like something to build on, and values you'd only half said out loud get clearer, until you want your time, your work and your spending to agree with them.

Whatever planets sit in your 2nd from birth, Jupiter widens each one's way of valuing for a few weeks as it passes: with your Moon there, what makes you feel secure; with your Mars there, what you're willing to work for.

This is also the stay where Jupiter's reputation gets in the way. People hope it will bring money, and some books promise as much. I don't read money in a chart, coming or going, and a [transit](/astrology/transits/) can't tell anyone what they'll earn ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). It brings out what your chart already holds about security and worth. What grows is your relationship with resources, never a figure on a statement.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

A little over a year is the usual length of a Jupiter stay in the 2nd, in Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, and most fall between about 11 and 22 months. Across all twelve houses the middle is about a year and the long end nearly 2, so the 2nd is a shade longer at the centre and a shade shorter at the edge; New York's latitude gives it exactly the same figures. Your own stay depends on the width of your 2nd house, which comes from your birth time and the latitude you were born at. Without the time, nobody can say where your 2nd begins, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

It picks up from a year spent on who you are ([Jupiter transiting the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-1st-house/)) and leads into one of talk and learning ([Jupiter transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-3rd-house/)).

To find your window, look up the sign and degree on the cusp of your 2nd house: Jupiter enters the house when it reaches that degree. It spends from June 2026 to July 2027 in Leo, then reaches Virgo in July 2027, Libra in August 2028, Scorpio in September 2029 and Sagittarius in October 2030, about a year apiece. Capricorn follows from November 2031 to November 2032, overlapping with Aquarius, which Jupiter enters in April 2032, leaves again, and keeps until December 2033. Pisces comes in April 2033 and Aries in April 2034. Taurus, the sign the natural zodiac pairs with the 2nd house, has Jupiter from April 2035 to May 2036, and the cycle closes with Gemini, to May 2037, and Cancer, to June 2038.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you value, then look at where your money and your hours actually go. This stay tends to make the gap between the two easier to see, and closing it is most of the growth on offer.

Price a skill honestly. If something you do easily is worth something to other people, this is a year to find out what, and to stop giving it away out of habit, if that's what you've been doing.

Be generous where you mean it. Jupiter widens whatever it touches, and in the 2nd that can include spending as freely as you earn: a budget isn't the opposite of growth, it's what lets growth stay. Measure the year by what you feel able to count on rather than by any number, because that's the part of the 2nd house a transit can actually change.
