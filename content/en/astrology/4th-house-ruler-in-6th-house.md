Home keeps a timetable. Belonging is built, and kept, by the small jobs of each day, the very business of the house its ruler works from.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is where you rest from the working day, yet its ruler ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)) works two houses on, in [the 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/): daily work, routines, habits, colleagues and the care you give your body.

I read it as home that runs on routine. You probably feel most at home when the days have a shape: the same breakfast, the shopping on a certain day, the jobs done in order. Many people with it work from home, run a household with real method, or bring the family's habits into the workplace, the colleague who keeps biscuits in the drawer and remembers everyone's birthday. Care at home tends to be practical, shown by fixing, cooking and organising rather than said aloud. When the balance tips, home turns into one more workplace, where the jobs never end.

The 6th also holds the animals people care for, and with home's ruler there, a dog to walk or a cat to feed can be part of what makes a place yours: a routine with a heartbeat.

With Aries on the IC, the ruler is Mars, and the 6th is Mars's joy in the old scheme ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): energy for chores, repairs and improvements, and the satisfaction of a thing fixed. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo IC from the 6th, keeps the household's lists, rotas and paperwork in order, and may be the one everyone calls when something at home stops working.

In the old books the 6th was the house of illness, and a ruler of home there could stand for sickness in the family, or a home turned into a place of nursing. No chart can predict that for anyone, and I don't read health at all ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). What I read is the way you look after home and the people in it, day by day, and whether you keep some of that care for yourself.

Horary astrologers also count the 6th as the 3rd house from the 4th, and read it for a parent's brothers and sisters ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)). I use that sparingly, and only to see how aunts and uncles figure in your life, never to read theirs.

With Aquarius on the IC, the 4th answers to Saturn and Uranus, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Saturn in the 6th can make the household a well-run system, while Uranus, elsewhere, keeps changing the rules.

The 6th sits between two other ways of being at home: in the 5th, the ruler of the IC would make home a place of play ([Ruler of the 4th in the 5th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/)); in the 7th, a home built with a partner ([Ruler of the 4th in the 7th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)). I need the birth time to know which side of those lines it falls ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
