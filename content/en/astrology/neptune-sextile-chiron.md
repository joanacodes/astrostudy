Neptune sextile Chiron leaves a door ajar between your imagination and the place in you that stays tender. Nobody is pushed through it. People born in a few short spells carry it, and for them the question is what they do with the opening.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings a chart's soft focus: the capacity to imagine, to sympathise, to sense meaning in symbols, and to let an outline blur where it doesn't need to be hard. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the uneven place you keep coming back to, together with what you build around it: workarounds, a different rhythm, and knowledge you can pass on without turning it into a rule. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity: imagination is offered as a tool for the tender place, and the tender place as a source of compassion.

In plain words, you have access to gentler ways of living with what is hard for you: a creative practice, a spiritual one, the right image at the right moment. You may also find that what you learned around your own sore spot makes you good company for other people's. None of it happens by itself. A sextile is an invitation, and this one needs you to pick up the brush, the instrument or the conversation.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Counting within 6°, about 1 person in 21 born from 1930 to 2009 has this sextile. They were born in five spells: August to December 1939, May 1940 to June 1941, February to May 1949, November 1949 to December 1950, and November 1998 to December 1999. Each falls a few years either side of one of the pair's meetings: the spells of 1939–41 lead up to the conjunction of 1944–45, those of 1949–50 follow it, and the 1998–99 spell leads up to the conjunction of 2008–09. Chiron, the faster of the two, is doing the moving, which is why each spell is short.

Everyone born in a spell shares the sextile, so its sign says more about the year than about you. The two houses involved, usually two apart, are what make it personal: Chiron in the 9th with Neptune in the 11th might put the tender spot in matters of belief or study, and the compassion into friendships and groups. For that, the hour of birth matters ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Close to exact, the opening is easy to feel; at 5° or 6°, it's a quieter offer.

How the other Neptune–Chiron angles compare:

- **[Neptune conjunct Chiron](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-chiron/):** the meeting these spells surround, compassion and the tender spot as one.
- **[Neptune square Chiron](/astrology/neptune-square-chiron/):** dream and limit pulling across each other, and learning from the pull.
- **[Neptune trine Chiron](/astrology/neptune-trine-chiron/):** the wider, easier angle, and the most common of the five in the count.
- **[Neptune opposite Chiron](/astrology/neptune-opposite-chiron/):** the two at full stretch, often met through other people.

Between two charts, [Neptune sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-chiron-synastry/) can be a gentle contact: one person's imagination offering the other a kinder way to see a sore spot. Like every contact with someone's Chiron, I read it only with both people's agreement ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-neptune/) is the opening arriving from the sky: Chiron, in its own round, reaching 60° from your Neptune. Each of Chiron's rounds, about 50 years, brings it twice, once on either side of your Neptune, and how long it stays depends on where Chiron is on its stretched orbit: a few weeks in the signs it crosses fast, many months where it slows down.

Born with the sextile, your [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, brings the old angle back along with Chiron itself.

Weather, not a forecast: a season when imagination is easy to bring to an old tender place, if you choose to.
