In Mercury conjunct Venus in synastry, only a few degrees separate one person's Mercury from the other's Venus, so the way one of them thinks and talks lands right on what the other values. Being admired for your mind and admiring someone else's are different pleasures, and this contact hands one to each person.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) covers the way a person takes in, sorts and hands on information: their questions, their explanations, their favourite words. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how they weigh value: what draws them, what they'd choose, what seems a fair exchange, what moves them as beautiful. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) turns the two into one gesture, and between two people that gesture is a conversation.

If the Mercury is yours, the other likes how you think. Your explanations, your turns of phrase, the odd fact you bring home all land as something worth having. With your Mercury and their Venus both in Gemini, the quick, playful way you talk is exactly what they enjoy, and they tell you so.

If the Venus is yours, your tastes get put into words. The other notices what you love and can say why it's good, which feels less like flattery than like being understood. Things you only knew by instinct, they help you name.

What flows is talk that's a pleasure for its own sake: colleagues who draft well together, friends who swap books and argue happily about them, a couple who can always find something to say over dinner.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Thinking and liking blur when they meet this closely. The Mercury person may drift towards saying what pleases, because it's received so warmly; the Venus person may like an idea partly because of who said it. Agreement comes easily, and some of it is pleasure rather than conviction. Peace and agreement aren't the same thing, but at this distance they're easy to confuse, and a real disagreement can get smoothed away instead of talked through.

The other aspects of this pair keep mind and taste further apart:

- **[Mercury sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-venus-synastry/):** birthdays about two months apart, and talk that's pleasant once someone starts it.
- **[Mercury square Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-venus-synastry/):** possible only between two people; reasons and tastes rub, and tact gets tested.
- **[Mercury trine Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-venus-synastry/):** possible only between two people; two styles that suit each other at 120°.
- **[Mercury opposite Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-venus-synastry/):** possible only between two people; words on one side of the table, values on the other.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make room for disagreement. Say "I don't agree, and I still enjoy this" often enough that it stops sounding like a risk. Separate the two questions this contact runs together: "do you like it?" and "is it right?" Between coworkers who edit each other's work, that means asking for the honest version, not the kind one; between partners or friends, it means letting a difference of opinion stand without rushing to repair it.

About 1 pair of people in 16 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 6°. Mercury and Venus are both tied closely to the Sun, which makes this one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) a calendar almost predicts: the birthdays tend to sit about a month apart, whatever the years, and rarely more than ten weeks. Mercury spends two or three weeks in a sign and Venus three or four, so the hour of birth seldom makes or breaks the contact itself; the houses, though, depend on it, so both times are required before I look at any synastry ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one person's chart, [Mercury conjunct Venus](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-venus/) is the same meeting, where thinking and valuing share one voice.
