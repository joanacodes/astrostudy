Pisces on the Descendant, the 7th house cusp, always comes with Virgo rising. The precise, attentive manner you show the world looks for its counterpart in someone softer, a person who forgives easily and doesn't keep count.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is the sign of the person across the table ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and with [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) there you're drawn to gentleness, imagination and a kind of understanding that needs no explaining: the partner who reads your mood from the doorway, the friend who makes art, the colleague who forgives. Love can come with a touch of rescue, you saving them or them saving you from your own exactness, and the work is to keep two outlines visible inside one bond. In the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) the same pattern runs through business: partnerships made on trust and a handshake, where a written agreement protects the goodwill rather than insulting it. Open rivals are hard to pin down with this cusp; conflicts tend to drift and dissolve instead of being settled, so naming the problem is half the cure.

I read both rulers of your Descendant, Neptune and Jupiter, and they ask different things of a partner. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows where you idealise and merge, and where a bond feels like a shared dream; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where partnership widens your world and where generosity holds it together. Sagittarius sits on the IC in about 3 London charts in 4 and more than 4 New York charts in 5, so Jupiter rules your home as well as your partners, and sharing a life often means making a home together. Look at their houses for the setting in which partners tend to find you, as ruler of the 7th in the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

The Descendant is where Pisces turns up most: about 1 London birth in 8 and 1 New York birth in 9, in Placidus, the house system I use. [Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/) comes with every one of these charts, so Mercury rules the chart, and usually the Midheaven too, through Gemini. Mercury is in detriment and fall in Pisces: the careful self that rises in the east faces, in the west, the sign where its own logic counts for least, and partners often supply what the logic leaves out. The 7th is narrow here, about 20° to 24° at London, and in about 3 London charts in 10 Pisces opens the 8th as well ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), tying partners to what you share with them. The other side of the coin: about 1 London chart in 6 gives Pisces no cusp at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

No cusp says whether you'll marry, or whom: this one describes what you look for in a partner and what you learn there. At London, Virgo takes nearly three hours to rise, and Pisces holds the Descendant all that time; near either end of it, minutes decide. A little later and Pisces would be on the 6th cusp ([Pisces on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/)), a little earlier on the 8th ([Pisces on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-8th-house-cusp/)), and I need the birth time to tell ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
