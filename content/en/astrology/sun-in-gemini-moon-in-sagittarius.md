Your Sun gathers questions and your Moon goes looking for answers big enough to live by. Gemini and Sagittarius face each other across the zodiac, and you were born when the Moon stood across the sky from the Sun, full or nearly so.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Opposite signs share a mode, and here it is mutable on both sides: two lights that adapt, move and change their minds when the facts change. Gemini is air and Sagittarius fire, elements that suit each other. The [Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/) knows the world up close, through talk, news, short trips and the people next door; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) settles when it can see far, through a journey, a belief or a question large enough to live with. One collects the details, and the other wants the story they add up to.

The rulers mirror each other. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) governs Gemini and is in detriment in Sagittarius; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) governs Sagittarius and is in detriment in Gemini. I read that as two ways of knowing, the precise and the panoramic, which need each other, and never as a flaw. The lights themselves hold no dignity in either sign, so the conversation between Mercury and Jupiter, by sign, house and aspect, tells me how this axis works for you.

Between the twelfth day of the lunar month and the seventeenth, the Moon stood 151° to 210° on from the Sun, so the full moon lay no more than two and a half days off, before or after. The eight-phase count splits these births evenly between the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) and the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/); the closer the gap came to 180°, the stronger the [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/) in your chart. The full moon nearest your birth very probably fell in Sagittarius, as every full moon does while the Sun is in Gemini: a [full moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/full-moon-in-sagittarius/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says which side of it you're on.

The count finds this axis in about 1 person in 141. The Moon visits Sagittarius for about two and a half days; if your birthday saw it arrive from Scorpio or leave for Capricorn, you might belong with the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-scorpio/) or the [Sun in Gemini, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-gemini-moon-in-capricorn/): the time decides ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The time also sets the rising sign, and with an opposition the houses matter twice, since they show which two areas of life the axis runs between.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You tend to learn about yourself through the people you love, and you love people who widen your world. The Gemini Sun wants conversation and variety; the Sagittarius Moon wants honesty, humour and freedom to roam. Partners often feel they've gained a travelling companion and a debating partner at once. The risk is restlessness inside the bond, when a new idea or a new place looks more inviting than the person in front of you. Plans made together, a trip, a course, a project, keep the horizon inside the relationship.

## At work {#at-work}

Teaching, publishing, translation, travel, journalism, anything that moves between the local and the global suits the pair. The Sun handles the details and the people; the Moon supplies the vision and the reason. Narrow, repetitive work frustrates both, and so does a job that never asks you to explain anything. You may do your best work as a bridge between experts and everyone else.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights mutable and facing each other, it's easy to keep moving and hard to land. You may collect courses, plans and destinations faster than you can use them, and rethink the big picture each time a new fact arrives. Feelings can be outrun rather than felt. What helps is a few fixed points, one place, one practice, one long project, and the habit of finishing a thought before setting off on the next journey.
