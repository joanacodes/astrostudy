The loud rebel and the quiet refuser often find each other, and each is a little startled by what they see. Uranus opposite Lilith in synastry puts one person's Uranus about 180° from the other's Black Moon Lilith, within 4°, so open difference and guarded refusal sit across the zodiac from each other.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates in public: it breaks with what is expected, reforms systems and makes its independence visible. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is the Moon's apogee read as a symbol, and astrologers stress different things in it; for me it stands for autonomy kept close, a boundary, and the instincts a person once learned to keep quiet. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions up as poles to be integrated; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it gives each end of the axis to a different person.

From the Uranus end, the other person's refusals can look like courage you haven't needed, or like a secret you can't read. You may want them to say out loud what they keep in, and feel shut out when they don't.

From the Lilith end, the other's open difference can look reckless, or enviable. They do in public what you do only inside, and their freedom can make your guardedness feel like a choice worth questioning, or worth defending.

Each sees, in the other, what their own way of standing apart leaves out: the Uranus person, depth and privacy; the Lilith person, room and visibility.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Roles harden easily. One becomes "the free one", the other "the one who won't", and each starts living only their end: the Uranus person never keeping anything back, the Lilith person never showing anything. Projection follows: each blames the other for the half of the axis they don't express. A seesaw is a polarity, not a quarrel, and it moves again as soon as both put weight on both ends.

- **[Uranus conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** the two ends folded into one, with nowhere to stand apart.
- **[Uranus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** an opening between them that waits for the guarded one to use it.
- **[Uranus square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-lilith-synastry/):** pressure at right angles, settled by terms rather than roles.
- **[Uranus trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-lilith-synastry/):** freedom and refusal flowing together, so neither has to be performed.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Swap ends deliberately. The Uranus person can keep one thing private on purpose and notice what that protects; the Lilith person can show one thing openly and notice what it frees. A couple might divide which battles each fights in public; colleagues can let the quiet dissenter speak first.

Lilith's cycle spreads it widely. For a birth in mid-1945, with Uranus in Gemini, the opposition forms with anyone born from February to May 1973, from December 1981 to March 1982, or from November 1990 to January 1991, when mean Lilith stood opposite in Sagittarius. The Uranus in Aries of anyone born, on and off, from May 1932 to March 1935 faces that 1945 Lilith in Libra. Nobody picks those dates, and with windows coming round every nine years the contact turns up between people of very different ages. That is why it sits outside the contacts I count, all of which involve the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Through the houses, I read it as two eras' ways of standing apart, facing each other.

I date it with mean Lilith; on a chart set to true Lilith, which can lie tens of degrees away, the opposition may shift or vanish ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). The hour changes neither body enough to matter, only the houses the axis crosses, which is why I ask for both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Uranus opposite Lilith](/astrology/uranus-opposite-lilith/) in one chart sets a person's urge to differ across from their own untamed side, often met through others. Between two charts the "others" are one particular person, and both get a say in whether it's looked at ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
