Some help arrives only when it's asked for, and then arrives well. In Mars sextile Neptune in synastry, one person's Mars and the other's Neptune lie about 60° apart, a gap that invites cooperation and leaves the first move to someone.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is how a person starts, pushes and sees a thing through. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is how they imagine it first: ideals, compassion, a sense of what a situation could become. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) joins them by opportunity: they cooperate once someone takes the opening. Among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one lets practical help reach someone else's ideals without either person giving anything up.

With the Mars on your side, the other person's ideas give your energy good things to do, and few of them are urgent, so you choose when to step in. Their feel for what people need can soften how you push, too: around them, you may assert things more kindly. With the Neptune on your side, your imagination gets a willing pair of hands. A hope you'd mentioned in passing, a garden for the street, a better way to run the family holidays, comes back to you as a first step already taken.

Usually fire meets air, or earth meets water, so the two signs get along. Mars in Scorpio with Neptune in Capricorn is a quiet, persistent drive meeting an ideal of things built to last; Mars in Leo with Neptune in Libra, a warm, open push meeting a dream of fairness.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The opening works only if someone walks through it. The Neptune person tends to hint, describing a picture rather than asking for a hand; the Mars person tends to wait for a clear request before starting. Both can be patient for years, and a good idea stays a pleasant conversation.

Seen from the Mars side, you may not realise how welcome your initiative would be, and hold back out of respect. Seen from the Neptune side, help can seem to come only when you ask in a way that feels awkward, so you stop asking. The ease is real; it is simply quiet, and an opportunity left alone stays one.

How the pair's other angles change the arrangement:

- **[Mars conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** two signs closer, the drive sits right on the dream, and the two can blur.
- **[Mars square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-neptune-synastry/):** a sign further on, cooperation turns into a friction that sharpens both.
- **[Mars trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-neptune-synastry/):** two signs further, the same help flows without anyone having to ask.
- **[Mars opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** the dream and the effort face each other and settle into roles.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Turn hints into invitations, and invitations into a date in the diary. The Neptune person can say "would you help me with this?" in plain words; the Mars person can offer to take the first step, and mean a small one. Shared projects suit it: volunteering together, building something for the household, a pitch at work where one person has the idea and the other the stamina, a reunion one sibling dreams up and another organises.

Within 3.6°, the narrow orb a sextile takes from Mars's 6°, about 1 pair of people in 13 has it, one way round or the other. It is the Mars person's birthday, set against the other's birth year, that makes it: Neptune's side would look much the same in anyone born within a year or two of the Neptune person, whereas a Mars a few weeks later is already in the next sign. With so small an orb, a pair near its edge is where the hour can count, since Mars covers well under a degree a day; the houses always need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one chart, [Mars sextile Neptune](/astrology/mars-sextile-neptune/) is a door a person can open for themselves. Here one of you holds the handle, and the other knows what's in the room.
