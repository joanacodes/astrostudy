Capricorn is the tenth sign of the zodiac, a cardinal earth sign, and its old symbol is a sea-goat: a goat with a fish's tail. The Sun enters it at the December solstice, the turning point of the year, and I read it as the sign of the long climb — patient, practical and built to last.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Every chart has Capricorn somewhere, whether or not a planet sits in it. Wherever it falls, it asks *what will this be worth in ten years?* It's an [earth sign](/astrology/earth-signs/), so it works with what's real and measurable, and a cardinal one, so it takes the lead — usually by taking responsibility (the [three modalities](/astrology/three-modalities/) explain what that means). [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), just before it, finds the vision; Capricorn builds the structure that makes it real. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), next, will ask who the structure is for.

Three placements bring most people here:

- **[Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/):** born between about 22 December and 19 January. You grow by earning your place, slowly. You'd rather be respected than admired, and you often take on responsibility before anyone asks you to.
- **[Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/):** you feel safe when things are under control and you know what's expected of you. It often describes someone who grew up early. The Moon is in detriment here, which in practice tends to mean care shown through doing rather than saying — and a need to let yourself be looked after.
- **[Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/):** you meet the world with reserve and quiet competence, and people trust you with things. Saturn becomes your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/), so where it sits matters a great deal. A rising sign needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): it changes about every two hours.

None of these tells the whole story. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and the rising sign each held about a third of the time, and the Moon, checked like every other placement, wasn't one of the three that held far more often. Capricorn's ruler was one of those three: Saturn held 75 to 90% of the time, so in a chart with a lot of Capricorn, Saturn is where I slow down.

## Its ruler: Saturn {#ruler}

A sign's ruler is the planet that's at home in it, and where that planet sits in your chart shows where the sign's energy goes. Capricorn is ruled by [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), which tradition also gave to Aquarius: in Aquarius, Saturn builds systems for the group; in Capricorn, structures you can stand on — a career, a reputation, a craft. If Capricorn is on your 2nd house cusp and Saturn sits in your 10th, say, money comes through slow, serious work. I read Saturn as a timetable, not a wall, and it's one of the [three placements I read most closely](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/).

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is [exalted](/astrology/exaltation/) in Capricorn: energy at its best when it's disciplined and aimed. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) here, opposite its home in Cancer, and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is in [fall](/astrology/fall/), opposite its exaltation in Cancer: feelings and generosity both have to fit inside limits. Neither is a flaw. They describe a warmth that shows in what you do, and a faith that wants proof before it believes.

## Planets in Capricorn {#planets-in-the-sign}

Every planet passes through Capricorn, from the Moon in about two and a half days to Saturn, which comes home about every twenty-nine and a half years and stays about two and a half. In Capricorn, each one gets serious:

- **[Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/):** a self built through effort and responsibility, which tends to grow lighter with time.
- **[Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/):** the Moon in detriment: feelings handled with self-control; safety comes from structure, and asking for help is the lesson.
- **[Mercury in Capricorn](/astrology/mercury-in-capricorn/):** practical, structured thinking; you plan in steps, say less than others and mean what you say.
- **[Venus in Capricorn](/astrology/venus-in-capricorn/):** love shown through commitment and reliability; slow to open, and loyal for a long time once you do.
- **[Mars in Capricorn](/astrology/mars-in-capricorn/):** Mars exalted: disciplined, strategic drive; ambition with patience, and energy spent where it counts.
- **[Jupiter in Capricorn](/astrology/jupiter-in-capricorn/):** Jupiter in fall: growth through limits and hard work; generosity that's measured, and optimism that has to be earned.
- **[Saturn in Capricorn](/astrology/saturn-in-capricorn/):** Saturn at home, shared by everyone born over about two and a half years: responsibility, structure and mastery. Its house shows where you build.
- **[Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/uranus-in-capricorn/):** shared by everyone born over about seven years: impatience with old structures and a talent for redesigning them. Its house shows where.
- **[Neptune in Capricorn](/astrology/neptune-in-capricorn/):** shared by everyone born over about fourteen years: dreams that want a concrete shape, and ideals about work and institutions. Its house makes it personal.
- **[Pluto in Capricorn](/astrology/pluto-in-capricorn/):** shared by everyone born over many years: deep change in power, institutions and the structures people rely on. Its house says where it lands.

## Capricorn on a house cusp {#on-a-house-cusp}

Every chart has Capricorn somewhere in its twelve houses, usually on the cusp of one of them. In Placidus and the other quadrant systems, a sign can open two houses, or none at all when it is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) inside one. Wherever Capricorn opens a house, that's where you take things seriously, start slowly and build to last, and Saturn, wherever it sits, carries the story on. Which house it is depends on your birth time.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/capricorn-rising/):** Capricorn rising — a reserved, capable presence; often serious early on, and lighter with the years.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money built slowly and kept; security comes from structure, savings and skills you've earned.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** careful words and methodical learning; sometimes responsibility for brothers, sisters or neighbours early on.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home as something you build and maintain; family duty taken seriously, sometimes from a young age.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasure you have to give yourself permission for; creativity that becomes a discipline, and romance taken seriously.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a strong work ethic and routines that hold; the lesson is to set limits as well as standards.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn on the Descendant, which means Cancer rising: you look for steady, serious partners, and commitment is a structure you build together.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-8th-house-cusp/):** shared money and intimacy handled with caution; trust is given slowly, and kept.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs that have to work in practice; higher study as a long climb, and respect for tradition.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn in its natural house: ambition on a long timetable, and authority earned rather than given.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-11th-house-cusp/):** few friends, chosen with care; you often end up organising the group, and your hopes are long-term plans.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a private seriousness; fears of failing worked through quietly, and a strength others don't see.

## Capricorn in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Capricorn placements bring commitment, reliability and a sense of building something together, along with a reserve that a partner can read as coldness. Its opposite sign, [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), is the pull: Cancer cares by nurturing, Capricorn by providing, and between them they hold both the home and the world outside it. A pair of Sun signs won't tell you whether two people will last — [Sun-sign compatibility](/astrology/sun-sign-compatibility-and-why-it-isnt-enough/) is the least reliable kind there is — so I look at both whole charts, and at Saturn's contacts in particular.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Capricorns are cold.”** Capricorn shows care through what it does rather than what it says. Reserve isn't an absence of feeling, and the Moon and Venus say far more about warmth than a Sun sign does.
- **“Capricorns only care about work.”** Capricorn cares about building something that lasts: a career, yes, but also a family, a craft, a friendship of thirty years.
- **“Capricorn is a mountain goat.”** Its old symbol is the sea-goat, half goat and half fish: a creature of the depths as well as the heights.
- **“Born on 19 or 20 January, you're half Aquarius.”** At the minute you were born the Sun was in one sign; near a change, your birth time settles which ([born on the cusp](/astrology/born-on-the-cusp/)).
