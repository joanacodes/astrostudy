Some hurts are about what happened; the ones Chiron finds in the 2nd house are about what you believe you're worth. For a few years, sometimes longer, that belief comes up to be looked at.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) finds the hurt you keep circling, and the understanding it has given you. The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is what you have and what you value: earnings, belongings, the talents you could live from, and your sense of your own worth underneath them. With Chiron passing through, an old sore spot about value can come back into view: a family story about money, an early lesson that you had to earn your keep, a reflex of asking for less than you need. These years tend to show where that reflex came from. Once you can see it, you can start to price your time, your work and your needs more fairly, and notice how much of your wish for security was really a wish to feel allowed to have anything at all.

A natal Venus in the 2nd, met along the way, is asked whether it can enjoy what it has without guilt.

No figure is forecast here. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) touches your relationship with resources, not your bank balance, and I don't read finances in a chart ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). Chiron describes a tender subject being understood, slowly, and that is all it describes.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Because Chiron's orbit is stretched, its time in a house can be short or very long. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the figure across all twelve houses is about 4 years, most often between about 2 and 9. The 2nd is a little shorter in the middle, nearly 3 years, and its long end reaches about 12. At New York's latitude the 2nd keeps Chiron about 3 years in the middle, most often between about 2 and 11.

How long yours lasts depends on the width of your 2nd house and the signs on it, both set by your birth time; without it I can't say when the stay starts, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron reaches the 2nd after crossing your 1st ([Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/)), and moves on to the house of words and daily exchanges ([Chiron transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-3rd-house/)).

If the sign on your 2nd cusp is Aries, the stay may be under way: Chiron is in Aries from April 2018 until April 2027. With Taurus on the cusp, it falls between June 2026, when Chiron first enters Taurus, and May 2034. Gemini follows, from July 2033 to April 2039, and then Cancer, from July 2038, which still holds Chiron at the end of my count in December 2039. The degree on your cusp gives the year.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you charge, or would charge, for your time, and ask whose voice set that number. Chiron in the 2nd rewards noticing where an old belief is doing the pricing.

Keep one thing you own simply because you love it. This stay is as much about permission as it is about security. And when someone values your work highly, let the compliment land before you argue with it.

Talk about money with someone you trust, plainly. Shame grows in silence, and this transit gives you time to make the subject easier.
