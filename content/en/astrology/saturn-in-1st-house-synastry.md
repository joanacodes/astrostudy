A person whose Saturn lands in your 1st house tends to change your posture before anything else. Their sense of time and consequence lands on the way you walk in, speak first and start things, and you may notice, a little to your surprise, that you weigh your gestures more carefully with them than with anyone.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) establishes structure in a chart: boundaries, responsibility, the discipline that lets something last. [The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is the self you lead with: your body, your manner, the first move you make. When one person's Saturn sits in the other's 1st, the [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) sets their feel for what holds, and what doesn't, right where you meet the world.

What you may feel first is ballast. Your first reactions slow by half a second; you finish the sentence you'd have abandoned; you start fewer things around them and see more of them through. You may feel older in their company, in the good sense: taken seriously, as someone whose word counts.

For them, your way of arriving can look like something to protect, or to steady. Your spontaneity calls up their sense of responsibility, and they may find themselves advising, waiting, checking the ground before you step on it. Angular houses show whatever lands in them, and this one shows it on your person; its natural sign, [Aries](/astrology/aries/), is where tradition puts Saturn's fall, as if patience and the first move had never been easy company. If their Saturn sits right at the start of the house, close to your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), it also conjoins the point your chart rises from, and I give that contact as much weight as the house.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Steadiness can tip into self-consciousness. You may start editing yourself before you speak, holding yourself as if for an inspection nobody announced, or waiting for their nod before you begin. Their silence, which may only mean they're thinking, can read as a verdict on your person.

They, meanwhile, can take on a job nobody gave them. Feeling answerable for how you come across, they can mistake your quickness for carelessness and their own caution for care; and being cast as the sensible one, every single time, gets heavy.

Neither of you is failing a test. Saturn describes time, limits and what gets built with them, and in your 1st it asks for a pace you can both live with, never for anyone to shrink.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Say what kind of attention you want. A comment on how you come across is a gift when you've asked for it and a weight when you haven't, so agree which is which. In a couple, let first reactions stand without review; between friends, enjoy the one person who makes you feel grown-up; between colleagues, this can be the senior who shapes your professional manner, at their best when they let you find your own; between adult relatives, notice whether an old judgement of how you present yourself still sets the tone.

Swap the charts and this page describes your Saturn in their 1st house: you become the weight on their manner, and the one to watch for advice nobody requested. On either side, [their Saturn in your 12th house](/astrology/saturn-in-12th-house-synastry/) works on fears you keep to yourself, and [their Saturn in your 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-in-2nd-house-synastry/) on what you count on.

Saturn covers so little ground in a day that their birthday fixes theirs to the degree, and their birth year very nearly gives its sign; your own time does the rest. Your Ascendant moves on a degree for every four minutes of your birth, and in Placidus, the house system I use, a Saturn close to that line sits in your 12th or your 1st according to a few minutes of your birth, which is why I wait for both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born within a couple of years of each other, you may share Saturn's sign: theirs then lies near your own, and if yours is in your 1st as well ([Saturn in the 1st house](/astrology/saturn-in-1st-house/)), the overlay meets a lesson you already carry. And because it touches how someone sees you, it's read only with their agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
