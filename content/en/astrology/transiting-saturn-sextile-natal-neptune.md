A sextile is the smallest door Saturn opens onto your Neptune: no pressure, just a chance to give something you care about a regular place in your week. It comes twice in each round of Saturn, and it's easy to walk straight past.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) gives shape, timing and limits; [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the part of your chart that imagines, empathises and longs for something larger than one life. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity: nothing is forced, but for the months of this [transit](/astrology/transits/) structure and imagination can cooperate. The openings tend to be small and practical: a weekly class in an art you've neglected, a volunteering slot with set hours, a teacher for a contemplative practice, a deadline that finally gets a piece of writing finished.

Opportunity needs engagement. A sextile left alone is pleasant and leaves nothing behind; taken up, it can set a habit that lasts long after Saturn has moved on. It also helps with one of Neptune's harder lessons, that compassion isn't unlimited availability: help offered with a clear beginning and end is often the form this transit suits best.

The house of your Neptune shows where the opening appears: in your 9th, study or faith; in your 6th, a more meaningful way of doing daily work. Which house that is depends on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile usually lasts about nine months. A little over half the time it comes as a retrograde loop with three exact hits, about nine and a half months in all; when Saturn touches the degree only once, the opening can close within three weeks.

The two sextiles of a round fall about ten years apart, either side of Saturn's crossing of your Neptune ([transiting Saturn conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-neptune/)), and nearly twenty years pass before the next pair. Most people meet five by 80, some six. Neptune shifts so slowly that a year group keeps one calendar: the first came at about 11 for people born in 2005, about 15 for 1965, and within the first months for 1995, a year whose births carry [Saturn sextile Neptune](/astrology/saturn-sextile-neptune/) in the chart itself. Saturn's crossing of Aries, until April 2028, sextiles every degree of Aquarius: with Neptune in Aquarius, as for most people born between 1998 and 2012 ([generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/)), it's your turn now, at about 21 if you were born in 2005.

A sextile is usually two or three years from a square ([transiting Saturn square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-neptune/)); [transiting Saturn trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-neptune/) lie further round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Book the class, the retreat, the lesson: something with a date. Under a sextile, the first concrete step is most of the work.

Offer help in a form you can sustain, two hours on Thursdays rather than whenever you're asked. Neptune's generosity lasts longer with Saturn's edges.

Finish one thing you began in a dreamier season: the song, the sketchbook, the long letter. Completion is the quiet gift of this transit.
