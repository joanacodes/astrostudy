The North Node steps into your 12th house over your rising degree, and for around sixteen months it stays in the quiet last house of the chart while the South Node crosses your 6th. Busy usefulness on one side, rest on the other: these months lean towards the rest.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to the less practised way, and the [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/) is what happens out of sight: solitude, rest, quiet work, care given or received without fuss, the inner life. Practising rest can feel strange, yet that's the direction: time alone that isn't an escape, a retreat taken on purpose, an hour of doing nothing that you don't have to earn.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in your 6th describes the familiar reflex: staying busy, fixing, organising, measuring the day by what got done. That competence remains yours. The stretch is to let it stop being the only way you feel all right.

Saturn, of all planets, found its joy in this house, which tradition called the Bad Spirit. Pisces, its natural sign, has two rulers, Jupiter, the traditional one, and Neptune, the modern one, and I read both. A natal planet in your 12th that sits close to your Ascendant is met early in the stay, for a month or so.

The old books also put confinement and hidden enemies here. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) predicts neither, and a chart can't diagnose anything ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)): I read the inner life, not a symptom. If what comes up in the quiet feels too heavy, a qualified person is the one to talk to.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 12th gives the node one of its shortest middles and its longest tail. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the true node usually stays about 16 months, against a year and a half for the average house, while most stays run from about 11 months to about 2 years and 10 months. At New York's latitude the middle rises to about 17 months and the long end falls to about 2 years and 4 months, a month short of the 1st, 6th and 7th there.

The true node wobbles, so a step back over the cusp that is undone within 120 days doesn't count as leaving. I look at both nodes, mean and true ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)): near your Ascendant, the degree between them can matter.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the stay begins as the North Node crosses your Ascendant and the South Node your Descendant ([transiting North Node conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)). That degree is set by your birth time, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node comes from your 1st ([North Node transiting the 1st house](/astrology/north-node-transit-1st-house/)) and goes on to your 11th ([North Node transiting the 11th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-11th-house/)).

The crossing happens while the node is in your rising sign. For Pisces rising, the 12th's natural sign, it came between January 2025 and July 2026, so the stay is running now. Aquarius rising begins it between July 2026 and March 2028, Capricorn rising between March 2028 and September 2029, and each rising sign further back waits about eighteen months more: Sagittarius until March 2031, Scorpio until December 2032, Libra until June 2034, Virgo until November 2035, Leo until May 2037 and Cancer until February 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put solitude in the diary, in small doses, and keep the appointment.

Let one task stay unfinished on purpose. The South Node in the 6th will survive it.

Notice what surfaces when you're quiet, and write it down. The 12th's work happens out of sight; it still deserves a record.
