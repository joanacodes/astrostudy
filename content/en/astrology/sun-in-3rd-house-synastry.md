Their Sun in your 3rd house brings another person's sense of purpose into the busiest, most ordinary part of your chart: the talking, texting, reading and running about of a normal week. Conversations with them tend to have a pulse, and you may notice your own mind moving faster for it.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is the mind in daily use: thinking, talking, writing and learning, the people within reach, the short trips of an ordinary week. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is a person's sense of who they're becoming, and the warmth they bring to it. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their purpose moves into your conversations.

You may find you learn from them without anyone setting out to teach. They mention a book, and you read it; they ask what you think, and you discover you have an opinion. Their presence gives your everyday mind a direction, a reason to finish the sentence. The 3rd is cadent, one of the houses of adjusting and passing things on, and [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) belongs to it by nature: curiosity, quick exchange, two voices. Tradition called it the Goddess.

For them, your way of talking things through can be where their purpose finds its words. Telling you about a plan, they hear it properly for the first time, and your questions sharpen what they want. The bond can take on a sibling's ease: familiar, quick, part of the furniture of the week.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Talk can tilt. If their purpose fills your conversations, you may end up as the audience to their plans, asking the questions while they do the answering, and notice later that your own news never came up. Your thinking can also start borrowing their terms, until it's hard to tell what you think from what they've said.

From their side, the lightness of the 3rd can sting. Their big direction arrives in the house of errands and messages, and your quick, many-sided interest may leave them feeling like one topic among several, or questioned when they wanted to be heard.

Late in your 3rd, near the bottom of the chart, their Sun also approaches your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/): talks with them may then reach down into your sense of home and of where you come from.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take turns, literally: one evening for their plans, one for your day. Write down the ideas your talks produce, so that they belong to both of you. Between siblings, cousins or neighbours, the people this house traditionally covers, the overlay often settles into a friendly daily rhythm; between colleagues, it suits the person you think aloud with, provided they also ask what you think.

If it's your Sun in their 3rd house, swap the roles: you're the one bringing direction to their ordinary talk, and the one who should check that they get a word in. Next door, [their Sun in your 2nd house](/astrology/sun-in-2nd-house-synastry/) shines on what you value, and [their Sun in your 4th house](/astrology/sun-in-4th-house-synastry/) on your home and roots.

Placing their Sun here takes your birth time: in Placidus, the house system I use, the lines between your houses move with the minute you were born, and the far edge of the 3rd is your IC itself. Their own time matters only if their Sun sits within a degree of one of those lines; I ask for it all the same, since a synastry reads two whole charts ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A synastry between siblings is still a reading of two people, and both should know about it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
