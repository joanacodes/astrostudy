In Leo, the new moon happens in the Sun's own sign, fixed fire at the height of the northern summer. Warmth, creativity and the wish to be seen are its themes, and these years it is anything but ordinary: the 2026 one was a total eclipse of the Sun, and so is the next, in 2027.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) sets the Sun and Moon off together from one sign, direction and need pointing the same way. In [Leo](/astrology/leo/), that sign is the Sun's domicile, where tradition says it works most fully as itself: identity, vitality and self-expression in their home ground. Leo is fire and fixed. It warms, sustains and stays; it wants to make something that is recognisably its own, and to be seen making it. A new moon here tends to stir the wish to show up, create, and give generously.

Its dates, in Universal Time, with the degree of each:

- **2026:** 12 August, 17:37 UT, at 20°02′ Leo, a total solar eclipse
- **2027:** 2 August, 10:05 UT, at 9°55′ Leo, a total solar eclipse
- **2028:** 20 August, 10:44 UT, at 27°56′ Leo
- **2029:** 10 August, 01:56 UT, at 17°45′ Leo
- **2030:** 30 July, 11:11 UT, at 7°21′ Leo

The eclipses come from the nodes. From July 2026 to March 2028 the Moon's South Node is in Leo, and a new moon close to a node passes across the Sun's face; both of these do so completely. After 2027, the next solar eclipses in Leo don't come until 2036 ([eclipse in Leo](/astrology/eclipse-in-leo/), [solar eclipses](/astrology/solar-eclipses/)).

I keep the ordinary years in proportion. In the rule I follow, a new moon in Leo counts within one degree of a planet or angle in your chart, and only as a trigger inside a period a transit or progression has already opened. The eclipse of 2 August 2027 reaches further, within about three degrees of a natal point, at either end of its axis: a planet near 10° Leo or 10° Aquarius. Even then, what I read is that natal point and its house, given extra weight for a while, never an omen.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Leo's place in your chart depends on the hour you were born, not the month, so your Sun sign won't tell you where it is: that takes [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and I use Placidus houses. Where Leo opens a house, its new moon generally falls inside it.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/leo-rising/):** Leo rising: a warmer, bolder presence; letting more of yourself show.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/leo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money and values; earning or spending in a way you can be proud of, valuing your own gifts.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** words, siblings, the neighbourhood; speaking up with more confidence, a talk you give.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home and family; hospitality, a home that looks like you, a family celebration.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/):** Leo's natural house: play, romance, creative work; a project begun for joy, pleasure taken seriously.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/):** working days; pride in a job well done, warmth brought into routine.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/leo-on-7th-house-cusp/):** partners, with Aquarius rising; a generous, expressive partner, or being properly seen by someone close.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/leo-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what is held in common, money and trust alike; how much of yourself you're ready to share.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** learning, faith and travel; teaching what you love, a journey made for delight.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/leo-on-10th-house-cusp/):** career and reputation; stepping into view, leading by example.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/leo-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friendships and causes; hosting, gathering people round a shared enthusiasm.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/leo-on-12th-house-cusp/):** retreat; creative work done for no audience, rest that restores your warmth.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Before reading anything into it, find its degree in your chart and the house it falls in. Then check for contact: a planet or angle within one degree, or, for the eclipse of 2027, within about three. With no contact, think of it as a little extra light on that house for the month, and nothing to dread.

When there is contact, the new moon is only the spotlight; the slower movement working on that point is the story. Leo's own manner is the help here: make something that is yours, and let it be seen. The Sun rules Leo, so the house of your natal Sun shows where that urge to shine tends to live.

No new moon decides anything for you, and a total eclipse is a sight to remember, not a sentence. A month after the inward [new moon in Cancer](/astrology/new-moon-in-cancer/), this one turns outward; a month later still, the [new moon in Virgo](/astrology/new-moon-in-virgo/) sets things in order.
