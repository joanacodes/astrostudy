If the elements describe a sign's temperament, the modalities describe its pace: whether it starts things, holds them, or adapts them as it goes. Three words, cardinal, fixed and mutable, and four signs to each.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Every sign has one of three modalities, also called qualities or modes:

- **Cardinal:** [Aries](/astrology/aries/), [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), [Libra](/astrology/libra/) and [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/). They start things. Aries charges in, Cancer takes the first step to look after someone, Libra turns towards someone else, Capricorn takes responsibility.
- **Fixed:** [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), [Leo](/astrology/leo/), [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) and [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/). They hold. Once committed, they keep going: Taurus with what it has built, Leo with its warmth, Scorpio with its loyalties, Aquarius with its ideas.
- **Mutable:** [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) and [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/). They adapt. They adjust, refine, move on and let go, which makes them the bridge from one state to the next.

The names come from the year. In the tropical zodiac, the Sun enters the four cardinal signs at the equinoxes and solstices, so each one opens a season; *cardinal* comes from the Latin *cardo*, a hinge, and the year turns on these four. The fixed signs follow, when a season is at its steadiest, and the mutable signs close each season and lead into the next ([tropical zodiac](/astrology/tropical-zodiac/)).

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Round the zodiac, the modalities take turns: cardinal, fixed, mutable, four times over. Aries is cardinal, Taurus fixed, Gemini mutable, Cancer cardinal again. Since [the four elements](/astrology/four-elements/) come round in fours and the modalities in threes, every sign gets a pairing of its own: cardinal fire for Aries, fixed earth for Taurus, mutable air for Gemini, and so on, with no two signs alike.

The same rhythm explains the hard aspects. Signs of one modality sit three or six signs apart, so planets in them make [squares](/astrology/square/) and [oppositions](/astrology/opposition/). Two cardinal planets in square both want to start something, in different directions; two fixed planets in opposition both hold their ground. That's why a shared modality can rub: the signs keep the same pace and want different things with it. The four signs of one modality form a cross, the subject of [Quadruplicities](/astrology/quadruplicities/).

Modality isn't the same thing as [polarity](/astrology/polarity/), the alternation between active and receptive signs. Polarity changes from one sign to the next; a modality comes round every third sign.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

A modality tells me how a planet moves, not what it does. In the system I built for my readings, the planet is the function and the sign its style, and the modality is part of that style. Mars initiates action wherever it is, but in a cardinal sign it starts, in a fixed sign it persists, and in a mutable sign it adjusts its aim as it goes. One function, three paces.

Across a whole chart, the balance can be telling ([Modality balance](/astrology/modality-balance/)). Many planets in cardinal signs often describe someone who starts more than they finish; many in fixed signs, someone who sees things through, sometimes long after they could have stopped; many in mutable signs, someone who adjusts easily and can lose the thread. None of these is a flaw. They're rhythms, and knowing yours helps you plan around it. The Ascendant changes sign about every two hours, so its modality, like the signs on the house cusps, needs [the birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Fixed signs are stubborn.”** They persist, which is also called loyalty. Whether it shows as staying power or as digging in depends on the rest of the chart.
- **“Mutable signs are unreliable.”** They adapt. Every life needs someone who can change course, and that's a skill.
- **“Cardinal signs are bossy.”** They start things. Starting can mean leading, and it can just as well mean being the first to apologise.
- **“My modality is my Sun sign's.”** Every planet has one. Your Sun's modality is one voice among ten.
