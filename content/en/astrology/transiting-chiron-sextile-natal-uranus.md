At its farthest from the Sun, Chiron reaches just past the closest point of Uranus's orbit, and never as far as Uranus's average distance. In a chart, the sextile is their most frequent meeting: most people have four by 80.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) experiments: it questions the inherited way of doing things and tests another. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is an old limitation that stays and the inventive adapting it has taught you. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates two functions through opportunity that needs engagement, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) curiosity and the old difficulty can work on the same problem.

The opening is often technical or social. A gadget, an app, a piece of equipment, a way of arranging a room or a working day that makes the difficulty easier to handle. An online group of people with the same difference, where methods get swapped and nobody needs an explanation. A small change to a routine that turns out to free more than expected. The sextile favours experiments sized for a week rather than a revolution, which suits Chiron: adapting works best when it keeps what already helps.

Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, so this sextile sweeps through a generation at once, and in the site's count people born at the start of 1945, 1955 or 1965 met their first in their mid-twenties. For one person, the shared timing matters less than where it lands: the house of your Uranus, the house Chiron is crossing, and any personal planet that Uranus aspects in your chart.

With [Uranus in the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-in-10th-house/), Chiron is often crossing your 12th ([Chiron transiting the 12th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-12th-house/)): the experiment may be in how you rest, retreat or recover from a working life that keeps changing. Tradition gave the 12th to confinement; I read it as rest and the unseen part of an adult life. Your 10th begins at the Midheaven and your 12th ends at the Ascendant, so both hang on the birth time; without it I don't read the sextile ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Uranus sextile Chiron](/astrology/uranus-sextile-chiron/), you already have this opening between the two; the transit gives it a date.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Small experiments suit a transit that can come and go quickly: about one sextile to Uranus in three is a single crossing of some three weeks. The usual pattern is three crossings, in nearly two passes out of three, over about ten and a half months as Chiron loops back; about one in twenty brings five crossings and some twenty-one months. I count it from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and the typical length is about ten months.

In the site's count the first came at about 20 in the middle and always before about 28: at about 2½ for someone born at the start of 1935, 4 for 1995, 11 for 1985, 19 for 1975, 22 for 2005, 25 for 1945 and 1965, 27 for 1955. Most people meet four by 80 and nobody fewer than three. The two sextiles of each round sit either side of the circle: one between [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Chiron square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-uranus/), the other after [transiting Chiron opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-uranus/), [transiting Chiron trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-uranus/) and the second square.

While Chiron crosses Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, it sextiles Uranus in Pisces, the natal Uranus of most people born from March 2003 to March 2011, and Uranus in Cancer, that of most people born from August 1948 to June 1956.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Run one small experiment with the difficulty this month, and keep notes.

Try the tool you've dismissed as not for people like you.

Join a group where your difference is ordinary, and swap one method.

Keep the old arrangement running alongside the new one until you know which works better.
