Mars square Chiron sets your drive at an angle to the place where you've had to adapt the most. When you push, you tend to meet that place; when you meet it, the way you act has to change. Over time, those changes add up to a skill.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the push: initiative, assertion, effort, the will to compete and to hold a line. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts around a persistent limit: it alters method, pace, tool or expectation so that you can keep taking part, and builds capacity through repeated, adjusted practice. A [square](/astrology/square/) makes them rub, usefully: neither replaces the other, and each keeps prompting the other to adjust.

In plain words, effort and sensitivity keep interrupting each other. You may push hard to prove you can, then find you need a different approach; or hold back from a challenge that touches the sensitive place, then go at it on your own terms. None of this is failure. The square's learning task is practical: to treat a limit as information, to say plainly where it is, and to adjust the plan rather than abandon it.

The sensitive place is real, but it isn't the whole of you, and it needn't be permanent. Many people with this square become unusually good at helping others push in a way that lasts.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Cardinal, fixed or mutable, the signs of the square colour the friction: in cardinal signs, it tends to show in how you start things; in fixed signs, in how you keep going; in mutable signs, in how often you change approach. The houses show where: Mars in the 10th square Chiron in the 7th, say, might set career ambition against a sensitivity in partnership, until each teaches the other.

About 1 person in 13 has this square within 6°, more than have the pair's conjunction or sextile.

Mars brings the square in and out over a few weeks of births, twice in each two-year cycle with Chiron, while Chiron spends from under two to about eight years in a sign. Chiron is the placement I read most carefully, because it held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook and because naming a sensitive place has to be offered, never imposed ([The three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#chiron)).

Set beside the other Mars–Chiron aspects:

- **[Mars conjunct Chiron](/astrology/mars-conjunct-chiron/):** acting and adapting in one movement, with no angle for them to rub across.
- **[Mars sextile Chiron](/astrology/mars-sextile-chiron/):** the know-how of adapting on friendly terms with effort, there when you reach for it.
- **[Mars trine Chiron](/astrology/mars-trine-chiron/):** doing and adjusting flow together, where the square makes them negotiate.
- **[Mars opposite Chiron](/astrology/mars-opposite-chiron/):** the sensitive place seems to sit across from you, met in others before it's owned.

Between two charts, [Mars square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-chiron-synastry/) can mean one person's push keeps catching the other's sensitive place. It needs gentleness and plain words, and I read it from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Chiron squares your natal Mars twice in its round of about 50 years: [Transiting Chiron square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-mars/). Because Chiron's speed changes so much along its orbit, the transit may pass in a few months or stretch beyond a year, in several passes: a season when the way you push needs adjusting. Born with the square, you meet it again around 50, at the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), as Chiron comes back to its birth place.

Mars in the sky squares your natal Chiron twice every two years, for a few days each time: short reminders to pace yourself.

No forecast here, only weather: a season for better methods.
