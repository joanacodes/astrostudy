Some seasons ask for more room than a life has spare. When Jupiter squares your Moon, it's your feelings and your home that want it: the urge to widen runs into the habits that keep you steady.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) enlarges; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feeling and keeps you feeling safe, largely through habit: the routines, places and people that steady you. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates them through friction that makes things adapt, so Jupiter's appetite pushes against the shape your needs have settled into.

That can feel like restlessness at home, a sense that your space or your routines have grown too small, or a pull to take on other people's needs on top of your own. Comfort can tip into excess, generosity into overcommitment. None of it is a warning. The square asks you to change a habit rather than add another, and many people come out of it with a home life that fits them better. It's also when your old ways of comforting yourself show most clearly, the ones you reach for when a week gets full; seeing them is half the adjustment.

Where Jupiter is crossing at the time sets the source of the pressure. From your 10th house ([Jupiter transiting the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-10th-house/)), work that is growing can press on the hours you need at home; your Moon's own house says which need feels the squeeze. Born with [Moon square Jupiter](/astrology/moon-square-jupiter/), you'll find each Jupiter return re-forms your square from the same side.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each square lasts about eleven days within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, or about eight months when Jupiter's retrograde loop falls on it, with three exact hits, which happens about three times in ten.

Your birth time decides more here than the houses. A chart drawn for noon, as some are when the time is unknown, can put the Moon six or seven degrees out, which moves this square by two or three months on average, into the wrong season. That's why I don't read this [transit](/astrology/transits/) without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Each square sits midway between a crossing and an opposition. The first comes three years after [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-moon/) and three before [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-moon/), when feelings that widened at the crossing ask for room in an actual week; the second comes on the way back, when comforts you've outgrown can go. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-moon/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-moon/) are the softer versions, offering room instead of demanding it. By 80, everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has felt their Moon squeezed this way thirteen or fourteen times.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make space before you take on more. This square tends to offer extra: more people to care for, more to host, more to feel responsible for. The useful move is usually to clear something first.

Give the restlessness somewhere to go: a room rearranged, a weekend planned for yourself, a class that feeds you rather than one more duty.

Check what you're promising. A Jupiter square makes generosity easy to offer and hard to keep up, and kindness you can sustain is worth more than kindness you can't. If you're the one everyone leans on, say which weeks you can't be.
