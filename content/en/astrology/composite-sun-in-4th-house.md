A composite Sun in the 4th house puts the relationship's centre at its foundations: home, roots, the private life the two of you share. It describes a bond that knows itself best where nobody else is watching.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) begins at the composite [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), opposite the composite Midheaven. One way of drawing the houses then builds the rest for the place where the two of you live, so if you move to a city at another latitude, the cusps between the angles shift with you by that method, though the IC stays put. The other way takes the midpoints of your two charts' cusps and never asks where you live. Both start from both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and a [Sun](/astrology/sun/) near a cusp can change house between them. The IC itself, like the Midheaven opposite, is a cusp the two agree on: in both, it's the halfway point between your two natal ICs.

The 4th is the house of the base a life stands on, and here the life is the bond's. Such a relationship is organised around a shared private life: the kitchen table, the rituals nobody else knows about, the place you both mean when you say *home*. It recovers its strength there, and its purpose often involves laying foundations, for each other, for a household, sometimes for a wider family of friends. It also tends to make its own traditions, borrowing a little from each of your families and keeping what suits the two of you.

Angular like the 1st, 7th and 10th, the 4th takes Cancer as its natural sign; none of the seven classical planets has its joy here. Origins are what the [Sun in the 4th house](/astrology/sun-in-4th-house/) describes in a single chart; feeling at home with someone is what [their Sun in your 4th house](/astrology/sun-in-4th-house-synastry/) describes between two. A composite 4th belongs to neither family of origin. It's the home the two of you make, which may be a house, a flat shared for a year, or simply a way of being together that both of you recognise as somewhere to return to.

A private centre can be hard for others to see, and sometimes for the two of you: when life outside gets busy, the bond's foundations go unvisited, and it can feel as if the relationship has gone quiet. Time at home that isn't spent catching up on chores helps, a meal with the phones left in another room, and so does letting a few people in. In the round of houses, the [composite Sun in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-3rd-house/) lives in daily talk before it, and the [composite Sun in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-5th-house/) in play after it.

Composites aren't charts I draw in my readings; their users read them with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and a 4th-house Sun predicts no move, no marriage and no children. A bond built around a private home should begin privately and fairly, with both people knowing their birth data is being used ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
