The day Uranus reaches your Descendant, the planet of freedom turns to face the people you meet one to one. For about seven years, partnership is where change asks for room: in love, in work, in any bond between equals.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) liberates and differentiates: it wants each part of a system free enough to be itself. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is partnership, cooperation, contracts and commitment, the bonds where two people meet on level ground. With Uranus here, the shape of a close bond tends to come up for review. You may want more independence inside a relationship, an arrangement that fits two real lives rather than a template, or partners and collaborators who wake you up rather than settle you down. Agreements made long ago can ask to be renegotiated on fairer terms.

Placidus, the house system I use, puts the 7th's cusp at the Descendant, so the stay opens with Uranus opposite your rising degree ([transiting Uranus opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-ascendant/)): what you want for yourself and what you want with someone else get looked at side by side. With Mercury in your 7th from birth, the change often starts in conversation, new terms said aloud before they're lived.

I never read this transit for a break-up, and it doesn't decide one. Uranus here describes a climate of change in partnership; what two people make of it is theirs. The same goes for tradition's contracts and open rivals: no [transit](/astrology/transits/) tells me how a dispute or a case will end. It brings out what your chart already says about the way you relate.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn for a London birth, Uranus usually spends about seven years in the 7th, and most stays run from nearly five years to about twelve. That's a year longer in the middle than the 1st across the chart, although the two houses are always the same size, one of the quirks of the count. New York's latitude shifts both ends: nearly eight years in the middle, about ten at the long end.

Every figure here runs from the first day Uranus touches your Descendant to the last day it spends in the 7th, retrograde slips back into the 6th included. Your Descendant is set by the same minute of birth that sets your Ascendant, and without that minute there is nothing to time and nothing I'll read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The 6th, where Uranus spent the previous years changing your daily round, lies behind it ([Uranus transiting the 6th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-6th-house/)); the 8th, where what's shared and owed comes up for change, lies ahead ([Uranus transiting the 8th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-8th-house/)).

Sagittarius rising means a Gemini Descendant, and Uranus is in Gemini from July 2025 to May 2033, so the crossing comes somewhere in those years. Capricorn rising gives a Cancer Descendant, reached between August 2032 and August 2039; Aquarius rising, a Leo one, from August 2039. With Scorpio rising, the Descendant is in Taurus, crossed between May 2018 and April 2026, and the stay may well be running now.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say what freedom would look like before you take it. A partner can make room for a need they can see; a sudden absence only teaches them to brace.

Tell distance from clarity. Uranus can make stepping back feel like seeing straight, and sometimes it is, but the view from closer up counts too.

Redesign together. The arrangements that come through this transit well are usually the ones both people had a hand in, with room written into them on both sides.
