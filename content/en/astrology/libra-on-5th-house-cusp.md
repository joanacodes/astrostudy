Libra on the 5th house cusp opens the house of play in the sign that wants every game evenly matched. Flirting, pleasure and the things you make for the joy of it tend to come alive when someone else is in on them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is where a chart keeps what you do for its own sake, and [Libra](/astrology/libra/) on its cusp makes that pleasure a two-way thing ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). A match is fun when either side could win; a dance needs a partner; a flirtation goes flat when only one person is trying. You may enjoy being courted as much as courting, and notice quickly when the attention runs one way. What you make for pleasure often turns on proportion: arranging, matching, finding the point where a colour, a phrase or a melody balances. Collaboration can bring out your best creative work, and so can a piece made with one particular person in mind.

Tradition placed the joy of Venus in this house, and with Libra on the cusp, [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules it as well ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): the planet of pleasure keeps the house of pleasure. Its own house shows where your enjoyment goes looking: the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Taurus, Venus's other sign, opens the 12th in about 2 London charts in 5 with this cusp, and nearly 2 in 3 at New York, so Venus governs both your pleasures and your time alone; in the rest it opens no house, and Venus rules the 5th by itself. Opposite, Aries gives your 11th to Mars.

In about 3 London charts in 5 with Libra on this cusp, the 5th also holds the whole of Scorpio behind it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The house is wide here, about 45° to 48° at London, so one part of life keeps two manners: Libra's lightness at the door, Scorpio's intensity further in, with Pluto and Mars joining Venus among its rulers. I read both of Scorpio's rulers when that happens. At New York the house is narrower, about 40°, and Scorpio lies inside it in about 3 charts in 8. Libra itself goes without a cusp in about 1 London birth in 6 and 1 New York birth in 8, and the 5th is one of the three houses where it then most often lies, with the 6th and the 7th.

In Placidus, the house system I use, Libra opens the 5th in about 1 London birth in 16 and 1 New York birth in 15. Libra rises slowly in the north and spends a long time near the Ascendant; the cusps further round, towards the Descendant, see less of it, and the further north, the stronger that effect. Every London chart with this cusp has Cancer rising, which makes the Moon your chart ruler; at New York it's Cancer in about 2 charts in 3, otherwise Gemini.

Older books also looked to the 5th for children. With Libra here, what I read instead is how you share your pleasures with the adults in your life: no chart can say whether or when anyone will have a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/).

A birth an hour or two later could have brought Libra down to your IC ([Libra on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-4th-house-cusp/)); earlier, it could have sat on the 6th ([Libra on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-6th-house-cusp/)), with every house between resized. That turns on the minute, so I work only from a recorded time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
