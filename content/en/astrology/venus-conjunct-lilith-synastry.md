Some people like you precisely for what you were told to tone down. Venus conjunct Lilith in synastry is one way that happens: one person's Venus sits on the other's Black Moon Lilith, and the first person's taste goes straight to the part of the second that learned to stay out of sight.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how a person weighs what's worth having: whom and what they're drawn to, what they prefer, what they find beautiful, and what makes an exchange feel fair. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) marks where the Moon swings farthest from Earth, a calculated point that astrologers read in more than one way; for me it's the side of someone that refused to be tamed, and was put away for it. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) runs two functions as a single process. In [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), each function stays with the person who carries it, so this one meeting is lived from two places at once.

If the Lilith is yours, the other person seems to like exactly what you learned to apologise for. Take one person's Venus in Leo on the other's Lilith in Leo: a taste for warmth, colour and a little show lands on someone who was told, early, to stop showing off. Around them you may laugh louder, dress as you please, and stop checking the room first.

If the Venus is yours, you're drawn to something in this person that others seem to miss, or mind. It doesn't feel like tolerance; it feels like taste. And what you give them is rare: the hidden part treated as worth having, rather than as a problem to manage.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A conjunction blurs, and here the blur runs between liking something and owning it. The Venus person can start to curate the other's wildness: praising it in the right company, explaining it to friends, quietly preferring the version that suits their own eye. The Lilith person can start to perform it, because that's the part that gets liked, and forget it was never meant as a show.

There's a quieter snag too. Venus looks for reciprocity, and appreciation hopes for something back; Lilith keeps something back on principle. When the Venus person waits for a return the other never promised, liking can turn into a bill, and the Lilith person into the one who won't pay it.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

The hidden part belongs to the person who hid it. If the Venus is yours, ask before you make their bluntness, their appetite or their strangeness part of your story about them; if the Lilith is yours, you can enjoy being liked there and still keep some of it to yourself. Between friends, that's the friend who loves your laugh and doesn't need it at every party; between an adult child and a parent, finally being liked for what used to be corrected; between colleagues, a manager who values your refusal to sugar-coat without making it your job.

With mean Lilith and an orb of 6°, the count comes to about 1 pair of people in 15, one way round or the other. Ask which Lilith a chart uses before anything else: the true, osculating point wobbles far from the mean one, by tens of degrees at times, so this conjunction can be exact by one reckoning and absent by the other ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). The hours matter for the houses, where each life feels the meeting, and that's why I need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). What it touches is private by nature, so I read it with both people or not at all ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

In one chart, [Venus conjunct Lilith](/astrology/venus-conjunct-lilith/) is a single person's taste and refusal in the same place. Between two, the angle opens and the meeting changes: [Venus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-lilith-synastry/) coaxes the hidden side out in play, [Venus square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-lilith-synastry/) asks it to be smoother and hears no, [Venus trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-lilith-synastry/) lets it be without comment, and [Venus opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-lilith-synastry/) puts taste and refusal at the two ends of a seesaw.
