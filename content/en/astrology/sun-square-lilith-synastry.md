In Sun square Lilith in synastry, one person's Sun stands at right angles to the other's Black Moon Lilith. Without either of them trying, one person's way of being presses on exactly what the other refuses, and the refusal answers back.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is how a person holds together and shows up: their identity, their purpose, the light they give off without meaning to. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), read in many different ways by astrologers, is for me the part that won't be shaped: instinct, autonomy, appetite, anger, the limits a person sets. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction that asks for adjustment, and of all the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) it's the one that most often teaches two people their limits by having them bump into each other.

What flows here is honesty earned the hard way. The Lilith person gets practice at saying no early and plainly, instead of late and loudly. The Sun person learns that who they are doesn't depend on the other's agreement. Once it's worked with, few contacts make two people this clear with each other.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

If the Sun is yours, you can feel resisted for no reason you can name: a plan, a habit, a way of taking up space that sits fine with everyone else meets a flat refusal from this one person. It can feel personal. It usually isn't: your Sun simply lands on the spot where they stopped agreeing to be shaped, long before they met you.

On the Lilith side, the other person can seem to expect you to fit their picture without ever saying so. Their direction feels like a rule, and something in you refuses on principle, sometimes more fiercely than the moment deserves. You may go along for weeks and then dig in over something small.

The signs set the rhythm. A square usually joins two signs of one modality: between two fixed signs, a Sun in Leo against a Lilith in Taurus or Scorpio, a stand-off can last; between two cardinal ones, sparks come quickly and pass quickly. It's friction, not failure. Nor is it [Sun square Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-chiron-synastry/), where an old sore spot gets touched: what gets touched here is an old refusal, and a refusal can be talked to.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Name the trigger once things are calm. If the Sun is yours, ask instead of assuming, and treat a no as information rather than rejection. If the Lilith is yours, say your no early, while it's still small, and say what you would say yes to. Between co-workers, agree in advance who sets which rules; a couple, a friendship, or a parent and an adult child can do the same with the unwritten ones. It's tempting to look up a difficult person's chart to explain the friction, but I only read two charts when both people have agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Counted within 6.8°, this square turns up in about 1 pair of people in 7, one way round or the other. Like any synastry I read, it needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): less to find the square, which a few hours rarely move, than to find where it lands, through the houses each body falls in, in both charts.

The same square inside one chart is [Sun square Lilith](/astrology/sun-square-lilith/), a single person's two sides. Between two people, the pair's other angles change the temperature: [Sun conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) exposes, [Sun opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith-synastry/) mirrors, and the soft [Sun sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith-synastry/) and [Sun trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith-synastry/) let the refusal relax.
