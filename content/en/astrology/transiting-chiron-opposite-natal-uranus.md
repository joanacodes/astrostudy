This opposition splits generations sharply. Someone born at the start of 1975 or 1985 met it as a baby; someone born at the start of 1955 or 1965 waited until about 49. Whenever it comes, your need to be free and an old sore spot face each other across the chart, usually in the shape of other people.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) wants autonomy: room to change, to differ, to arrange a life on your own terms. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is a tenderness that stays and the know-how of living with it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) freedom and care stand at the two ends of one line and other people tend to hold one of the ends.

Sometimes the other person's vulnerability asks you to stay close just when you need space: a partner, a friend, a parent whose sore spot is touched by your independence. Sometimes it's the reverse, and someone else's need for freedom lands on your old hurt about being left. Uranus's risk is treating other people's slower pace as resistance; Chiron's, mistaking the helping you do for the help you need. Autonomy is not isolation, and the opposition asks for a kind of freedom that stays in contact: room inside a bond, change at a pace the people around you can follow.

Uranus moves so slowly that your whole age group shares it, so this opposition belongs to a generation's timetable, with the sharp split by birth year to show for it. For your chart, though, the date is the least of it. The houses and the personal planets your Uranus touches decide whether the pull is felt at home, at work or with a partner.

With [Uranus in the 6th house](/astrology/uranus-in-6th-house/), where you need to work in your own way, Chiron is crossing your 12th ([Chiron transiting the 12th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-12th-house/)), the house of retreat and of what stays out of sight: independence at work faces a hurt that's hard to put into words. The cusps of your 6th and 12th come from your birth time, and I don't read the opposition without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you were born with [Uranus opposite Chiron](/astrology/uranus-opposite-chiron/), as many people were between 1951 and 1990, Chiron stood near this point when you arrived, which is why the transit came so early or comes so late.

Nothing here forecasts a separation or a sudden departure ([disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Negotiating room with the people close to you usually gets about ten months here. Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Chiron crosses the point opposite your Uranus three times in nearly two passes out of three; in roughly a third of passes it crosses once, in some three weeks, and in about one in twenty five times, over some twenty-one months.

In the site's count the first came at about 10½ in the middle, but the middle hides the split: within the first year or two for someone born at the start of 1975 or 1985, about 4 for 1945, 12 for 1935, 39 for 2005, 46½ for 1995, and 48 or 49 for 1965 and 1955. There is one opposition in each round, so most people meet two by 80 and everyone one. It sits at the far side of the round from [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-uranus/), with [transiting Chiron sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-uranus/), [transiting Chiron square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Chiron trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-uranus/) on either side.

While Chiron crosses Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, it opposes Uranus in Scorpio, the Uranus of most people born from November 1974 to November 1981.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ask the people close to you what your freedom costs them, and listen to the answer.

Negotiate room instead of taking it: an evening, a project, a trip, agreed in advance.

When someone else needs space, notice the old hurt it touches before you react.

Stay in touch while you change. Distance with contact is very different from disappearing.
