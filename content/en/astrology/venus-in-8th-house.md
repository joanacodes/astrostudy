Venus in the 8th house values what is shared behind closed doors: trust, confidences, the closeness that changes both people. The planet of attraction and reciprocity sits in the house of intimacy and of what two people come to owe each other. I read it as love that wants depth, and a generosity that needs clear agreements to stay sweet.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) follows the house of partners and holds what a bond creates: intimacy, sexual and otherwise, trust, joint money and debts, and the closeness that leaves you changed. It is succedent, and its natural sign is Scorpio, where Venus is in detriment. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) here wants more than company. It values depth, loyalty and the kind of trust that takes years, and it can find real pleasure, and real meaning, in what two people build in private.

The tradition that named this house also handed it death and inheritance. Neither is something I read in a chart: no one's death is written there ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and I make no forecasts about legacies, loans or debts. In an adult's life, Venus in the 8th describes how you share, your body, your trust, your resources, and the terms on which you let someone close.

When you were born, Venus had passed the top of the sky and was sinking towards the west; with the Sun nearby, that usually means an afternoon birth. [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), which I read in, sets the 8th house from the exact time, and an hour can make the difference: a later birth sinks Venus into [Venus in the 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house/), an earlier one raises it into [Venus in the 9th house](/astrology/venus-in-9th-house/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, an 8th-house Venus is drawn to intensity and honesty. You'd rather have one deep bond than many light ones, and you may be slow to let someone in, then wholly committed once you have. Physical closeness and emotional trust tend to go together for you. Money and affection can get tangled here, a shared account, a loan, a gift with strings attached, so being clear about what is whose protects the tenderness. What wounds this Venus is a broken confidence; what heals it is time, and proof.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Venus is often at ease with what others find uncomfortable: other people's money, secrets, crises, endings. Finance, insurance, estates, research, therapy, mediation in hard situations, restoration, any work that asks for discretion and nerve, can suit it. You may be the colleague people confide in. The pleasure here is depth: work that matters, done with people you trust.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Intimacy can tip into fusion. You may give too much of yourself, or ask too much of a partner, and feel lost when a bond loosens. Jealousy and suspicion can surface where trust was broken before. And when money and love mix, resentment can grow quietly over who gave what.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Talk about money and trust plainly, early, and more than once. Let intimacy grow at a pace that leaves you room to breathe. Keep some things that are yours alone, a little privacy, an income, a friend, so that closeness stays a choice rather than a dependence. And if a past betrayal still shapes how you love, it may help to talk it through with someone trained to listen.

Venus held about a third of the time in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), and in the 8th, where people often arrive wanting answers about money or loss, that matters. I read this Venus as a way of sharing and trusting, which is what it can honestly describe.
