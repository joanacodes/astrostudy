A square from Chiron doesn't sit on your Sun; it leans on it from the side. For about ten months, usually, an old sore spot presses on the direction you've set, the way a stone in a shoe changes how you walk without stopping you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is purpose and agency: acting from a centre, and putting yourself into the world. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a limitation stayed and you adapted, often by compensating: doing more, preparing harder, hiding the part you find difficult. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates the two through friction that asks for an adjustment. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the compensation is what tends to show its price. The overpreparing that kept anyone from seeing your doubt, the extra workload taken on to prove you belong, the cheerfulness that covered a sore subject: each can start to cost more than it gives.

The square doesn't ask the sore spot to disappear. Chiron's way is to change how you function around a limitation that stays, and this transit pushes for a better method: a pace you can keep, a question you're allowed to ask, a goal pursued by a different route. Feeling the old ache again isn't going backwards. It's information that the old method has had its day.

Your houses say where the friction comes from, and they need your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Sun in the 10th, one of Chiron's squares arrives while it crosses your 1st ([Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/)): how you come across, and an old discomfort about being looked at, rub against the direction you've built in public. With [Sun square Chiron](/astrology/sun-square-chiron/) in your chart, this sideways pressure on your ambitions is familiar; the transit turns it up for a season, and one of its squares comes back with your Chiron return, around 50.

A square can make a hard year feel harder. If you're struggling to get through the days, please talk to a doctor or someone you trust; a chart is the wrong place to carry that alone ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/); help lines are in my [disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, nearly two squares in three bring Chiron over the exact degree three times. Those passes are rarely shorter than nine months and can stretch to nearly two years when Chiron is at its slowest, about ten and a half months in the middle. About one square in three is a single crossing of some three weeks, and about one in twenty crosses five times, over twenty-one months or so.

Every point has two squares, on opposite sides of the circle, and Chiron's round of about fifty years crosses each once, so most people meet three by 80, some two and some four. The gentler contacts, the sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-sun/)) and the trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-sun/)), come as often as this square; the conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-sun/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-sun/)), which put the sore spot on your Sun or straight across from it, come once or twice.

At the end of 2026, with Chiron in the last degrees of Aries, the square falls on birthdays around 16 to 19 January and 18 to 22 July. Through Taurus, from 2027 to 2033, it moves on to Aquarius and Leo Suns, four or five more days of birthdays each year; Virgo and Pisces Suns follow once Chiron reaches Gemini.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List the workarounds you use to look capable, and drop the most expensive one for a week.

Change the method, not the goal. Ask what you're trying to reach, and look for a second road to it.

Tell one person where it's hard. A sore spot costs less once you're not also spending effort to hide it.
