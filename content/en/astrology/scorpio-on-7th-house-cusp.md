Scorpio on the 7th house cusp gives your Descendant, and with it the house of partners, to the sign of transformation. The bonds that matter to you tend to be the ones that change both people in them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) sits on the western horizon, and the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) that begins there takes in partners, close associates and declared rivals ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) there, you tend to be drawn to people with an inner life you can't read at a glance, and a partnership feels real once it has changed something in both of you. Agreements hold when you know what each side really wants from them: you read the terms beneath the terms. A rival earns your full attention. Trust, for you, is built by what someone does when things get difficult more than by what they promise. With Taurus rising you meet the world at a steady pace, and across the chart you look for someone who goes deeper; often you become the ground they come back to. That is the bond you recognise, not a forecast of who or when.

Scorpio's two rulers, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), both answer for your partnerships, and I read both: their houses show where your bonds take you, the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). At London, Aries, Mars's other sign, is left without a cusp in nearly every chart with this one, so the 7th is the only house either ruler governs by its cusp, while Libra, the 7th's own sign, lies wholly inside your 6th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The sign of partnership turns up among your colleagues and routines, and your Descendant gets Scorpio's depth instead. At New York, Aries has no cusp in more than half these charts and opens the 12th in the rest.

About 1 birth in 21 has Scorpio on the Descendant at London, and 1 in 17 at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. Scorpio can only set while Taurus rises, and Taurus is one of the quick risers in the north, about 69 minutes at London and 86 at New York ([Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/)). Venus then rules your chart, and Mars and Pluto your partners: the rulers of the two signs that face each other across your horizon. The 7th itself is fairly narrow, about 23° to 34° at London, and there Capricorn is always on the Midheaven. Across all London births, about 1 in 7 have no Scorpio cusp at all; at New York, 1 in 11.

Scorpio takes a little over an hour to set at London, so a time rounded to the hour can land on either side of it: on the 8th cusp ([Scorpio on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/)) if the true time was earlier, on the 6th ([Scorpio on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-6th-house-cusp/)) if it was later. I read from the recorded minute ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and a partner's chart is theirs to bring, with their own time and their own consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
