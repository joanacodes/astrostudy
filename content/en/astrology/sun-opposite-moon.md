On the night you were born, the Moon was full: it stood across the sky from the Sun, rising as the Sun set, its whole face lit. That is Sun opposite Moon, and in your chart it puts the two lights at opposite ends of one axis, each showing the other up.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is where you organise yourself around a purpose: identity, vitality, the self you're trying to express. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is where you register and regulate: feelings, attachments, the needs that keep you steady. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, which isn't contradiction: each keeps its identity, and each makes the other easier to see.

That's the side of this aspect I come back to most: awareness. At a [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) the Moon returns the Sun's light in full, and a birth there, as I read it, gives a clear view of both ends. You usually know what you want and what you need in some detail, precisely because they don't sit in the same place. What takes longer is letting both have a say at the same time.

In plain words, it can feel like a seesaw. For a while you lean into the goal, the project, the person you're becoming; then the needs you put aside ask to be heard, and you lean the other way. Sometimes one end shows up in someone close: a partner who seems all feeling when you feel all purpose, or the reverse. None of that is conflict, as I read it. It's a balance you learn to hold, and once you can, few people understand both sides of a choice as well as you do.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The two lights fall in opposite signs, which share a modality and lean in complementary directions: a Sun in Scorpio with the Moon in Taurus, depth across from steadiness; a Sun in Aquarius with the Moon in Leo, the group across from the need to be seen. They often fall in opposite houses as well, and that axis is where the balancing happens: the 3rd and 9th, everyday learning against far horizons; the 6th and 12th, the working week against time alone; the 5th and 11th, what you create against the friends you share it with.

Within 8°, the orb I count with, about 1 person in 24 has this opposition: one full moon a month makes it exactly as rare as the conjunction. The Moon is the one travelling, about 13° a day, and the birth time tells which side of the exact full moon your birth fell on: the Moon either still growing towards full, or already beginning to wane ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)). When a full moon falls near the Moon's nodes, it is a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/), and some births fall on one.

Across the month, the pair makes its other aspects:

- **[Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/):** the new moon, half a month from yours; where your lights face each other, that birth has them side by side.
- **[Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/):** a crescent, close to the new moon; a low-key alliance rather than a polarity.
- **[Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/):** the half moon; the two cross paths at an angle, inside the same days, instead of facing each other across the chart.
- **[Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/):** the gibbous moon, a few days before or after yours; nearly the same brightness, without the pull in two directions.

Between two charts, [Sun opposite Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon-synastry/) places one person's Sun across from the other's Moon: two people who each hold the end the other leans away from. I read it from each side, and only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

Each month, for a few hours, the Moon opposes your natal Sun; once a year, over a couple of days, the Sun opposes your natal Moon ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). The full moons matter more to me. In a year when one falls within a day or two of your birthday, the sky lines up on nearly the same axis as your chart: your birth sky, roughly repeated. I'd take it as a time for checking the balance, nothing more. Weather, not a forecast: a full moon brings a mood to the surface, never an event.
