Of all the pairs of Sun and Moon signs, only this one sets the Sun in its detriment and the Moon in its exaltation. Your sense of self is at work in Aquarius, a sign that asks it to stand among others, and your feelings rest on Taurus, the ground the old books liked best for the Moon. You were born near the half moon of the evening sky.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aquarius and Taurus are both fixed, air and earth, three signs apart: a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign, two steady signs holding on to different things. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) holds to an idea of how people could live, work or organise themselves better, and keeps hold of it for years; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) holds to what already works, and is calmest when a day has a shape it recognises. You can argue for change at a meeting and go home to the same route, the same supper, the same few people, and see no contradiction, because there isn't one: the reform is for the world, and the routine is for you.

Ptolemy gives Taurus as the Moon's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), and Aquarius, facing Leo, is the Sun's [detriment](/astrology/detriment/). I read the first as feelings that settle easily and hold steady, the second as a self more at home saying we than I, and neither as a ranking of you. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Taurus and shows what your Moon values and keeps. Aquarius has two rulers, and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern one, for the habit your Sun most wants to break, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the older one, for the structure it is willing to maintain. Of the three, Saturn and Venus speak your Moon's language of keeping; Uranus is the one that asks it to move.

The Moon was 61° to 119° ahead of the Sun, five to almost ten days into the lunar month, and these births split evenly: half in the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/), half in the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) phase, so the hour can decide which is yours. Close to 90° the lights form a waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/) between two fixed signs, friction that asks you to act rather than wait for it to pass. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains where one phase ends and the next begins.

About 1 person in 145 has an exalted Moon under a Sun in its detriment like this. The Moon arrives in Taurus from Aries and leaves for Gemini about two and a half days later, so a birthday on which it changed sign can belong to the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-aries/) or the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-gemini/) instead, and only the birth time settles it ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It settles the houses too, which show where in your life the reform happens and where the routine is kept.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You're loyal, physically present and slow to change your mind about the people you love. The Taurus Moon shows care by turning up, keeping the rituals going and staying; the Aquarius Sun wants a partner who is also a friend, with room for each of you to think differently. A partner may be puzzled that someone so open to new ideas needs home to stay so constant. Say it plainly: the ideas can change daily, the bond isn't up for revision.

## At work {#at-work}

You're good at making a new idea last. The Aquarius Sun sees what could be improved in a system, a service or a community; the Taurus Moon wants it built properly and kept running, with the resources it needs. Work that asks for both suits you: infrastructure, cooperatives, long research projects, design meant to be used for decades. Restructuring for its own sake wears the Moon down, even when the Sun approves of the principle behind it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two fixed signs at right angles can dig in. When a change you argued for arrives at your own door, the Moon may need far longer to settle into it than you expected, and when someone challenges your principles, the Sun can hold its ground past the point of listening. What helps is pace: change one thing at a time, keep one routine untouched while you experiment elsewhere, and count the Moon's need for continuity as a need rather than a failing.
