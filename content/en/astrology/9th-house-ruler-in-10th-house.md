What you believe, what you've studied and where you've travelled tend to become what you're known for. The ruler of your 9th house has climbed into the 10th, the most public stretch of the chart.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Study, travel, philosophy and faith are the business of the [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), and its ruler carries them into whatever house it occupies ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) begins at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) in Placidus, the house system I use, and holds your public direction: your vocation, your standing, your reputation, the role you play in the world.

Convictions here have a job to do. Ideas don't stay private here: they shape the work you choose and the way people see you. It often fits vocations built on knowledge or belief, teaching and lecturing, publishing and journalism, law, the church, travel, international work, but it can just as well describe a business run on principles, or an expert known for one subject. What it describes is direction, never success: it doesn't say whether you'll be promoted, published or well paid. The thing to watch is a reputation that outgrows the search. Once you're known for an opinion, changing your mind in public gets expensive, and the 9th needs room to keep learning.

The planet shapes the public voice. Jupiter, ruling a Sagittarius 9th, may climb into Capricorn inside the 10th, the sign of its fall: big ideas asked to prove their use in public, and often better for it ([Jupiter in the 10th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-10th-house/)). Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 9th, makes the writer, the broadcaster, the one people quote. The Moon, ruling a Cancer 9th, tends towards public work that looks after people far from home.

Sometimes one sign covers both cusps. Placidus houses vary in width, and when the 9th is narrower than a sign, the same sign opens the 9th and the 10th ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). The ruler of your 9th is then the ruler of your 10th too, and its place in the 10th puts it in a house of its own: belief and vocation answer to one planet, which tends to make them hard to tell apart.

A planet in the 10th is in the last third of its climb towards the Midheaven, and the Midheaven moves about a degree every four minutes, so a ruler close to it can cross with a small change of time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A short delay would carry it over the top of the chart into the 9th, its own house, where the search stays personal ([Ruler of the 9th in the 9th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)); an earlier time would leave it lower, in the 11th, among friends and shared causes ([Ruler of the 9th in the 11th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/)).

Mars, one of Scorpio’s two rulers, is the planet to look for here when Scorpio holds the 9th cusp ([Scorpio on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/)); Pluto is the other, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Mars in the 10th carries its convictions into public life with energy: the campaigner, the outspoken professional, the one who argues the case. Pluto, from its own house, shows how deeply those convictions can change you, whatever title you end up with.
