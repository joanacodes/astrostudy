The 8th house holds what a bond shares and what it owes, and Saturn's years there are an audit of both: money held in common, debts and favours, and the trust that makes intimacy possible.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) checks foundations and builds what can be relied on; the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is shared resources, intimacy, trust and the obligations that come with letting someone in. While Saturn is there, the shared side of life tends to come into the open. Joint money wants a clearer structure, debts and favours ask to be counted, and you may find you're slower to trust, or clearer about whom you trust and why.

One of Saturn's old habits is to mistake control for safety, and the 8th is where that habit meets its limit: closeness can't be audited into being. The work of these years is to build trust that holds, on both sides, and to let go of the kind of control that was only standing in for it.

With your Moon in the 8th, the pass can make closeness feel more guarded for a while, and teach what a safe intimacy needs.

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance. I don't read either in a [transit](/astrology/transits/), for anyone ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and Saturn here predicts no loss of money or of people. It brings out what your chart already holds about sharing, and gives it a structure.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude keep Saturn in the 8th a little under three years, about 2 years and 10 months in the middle, mostly from about 23 months to just under 4 years. At New York's latitude the range narrows to between 2 years and about 3 years and 8 months, around a middle of just under 3.

Without your birth time I couldn't tell you where your 8th begins, and I won't read a chart on a guess ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Saturn arrives here from your 7th, where the bond itself was examined ([Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/)), and moves on to your 9th, where belief gets its turn ([Saturn transiting the 9th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-9th-house/)).

Is the sign on your 8th cusp Aries? Then it's now: February 2026 to April 2028, after a first visit in 2025. Taurus? April 2028 to May 2030. Gemini? June 2030 to July 2032. Cancer? July 2032 to May 2035. Leo? August 2034 to July 2037. Virgo? October 2036 to September 2039. Libra? From September 2039. Pisces had its turn from March 2023 to February 2026, and Scorpio, the 8th's own sign, waits with Sagittarius, Capricorn and Aquarius until after 2039. The degree on the cusp gives you the month.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look at shared money and obligations together, on paper, with the people they concern: who owes what, who carries what, what was agreed and what was only assumed.

Trust slowly, and let others take their time with you. Saturn in the 8th doesn't hurry intimacy, and the trust built at its pace tends to be the kind that lasts.

Notice where you've been holding on to control because it felt safer than trusting. Loosening that grip, a little at a time, is the quiet achievement of these years. And keep a note of what you've settled: by the time Saturn leaves your 8th, that list is the structure it came to build.
