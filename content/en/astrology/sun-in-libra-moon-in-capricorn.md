Saturn ties this pair together: it is exalted in Libra, where your Sun is, and rules Capricorn, where your Moon is. Two cardinal signs a square apart, born around the first quarter, and I read them as a self that negotiates and needs that organise, with Saturn holding the timetable for both.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Libra is cardinal air, Capricorn cardinal earth, three signs on, which makes a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign. Each likes to begin things, and each likes structure of a kind: the [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) a fair one, with rules everyone can accept; the [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) a dependable one, with a plan and someone responsible for it, often you. You may be the person who brokers the agreement and then quietly sees it through.

Neither light is at ease by the old rules: the Sun has its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Libra and the Moon its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Capricorn, on the far side of the zodiac from Cancer. I don't read either as a verdict. The fall describes a self that works through consultation; the detriment, a Moon that shows its care through reliability rather than softness. Your Sun's sign belongs to [Venus](/astrology/venus/); [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules your Moon's and is exalted in your Sun's, so its sign, house and aspects matter twice. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), where every placement was checked one by one, Saturn was one of [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), 75 to 90% of the time, while all my ticks and crosses together came to about a third; with Saturn behind both your lights, that is where I'd begin.

Between the fifth and tenth day of the lunar month, the Moon had drawn 61° to 119° ahead of the Sun, a crescent thickening towards the half, or just past it. These births are split almost exactly in two: the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) takes the earlier ones, the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) the later, and an exact waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/) sits at 90°. The first quarter turns friction into action, as I read it, and a Capricorn Moon is very good at action. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) puts the two phases side by side.

About 1 person in 144 has the lights in this square, with Saturn behind both. Two neighbouring pages, the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-sagittarius/) and the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-aquarius/), wait on the time for anyone born on a day the Moon crossed into or out of Capricorn ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With Saturn this central, its house is the one I'd want first, and like the rising sign it can't be found without the time.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Your love tends to arrive as reliability and fair dealing: the plan made, the promise kept, the work shared evenly. Saying how you feel comes harder. The Libra Sun keeps things pleasant, the Capricorn Moon puts its own needs off until later, and a partner may not know how tired you are until the crisis has passed. Let someone look after you for no reason, and don't repay them the same week.

## At work {#at-work}

You can be very effective where fairness needs structure: management, law, policy, administration, project work with many parties. The Libra Sun finds terms people accept; the Capricorn Moon builds the plan that holds them to it. You may take on responsibility early and keep it long. Watch the hours: a square between two cardinal signs can always find one more thing to start.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two debilities and a square can make you hard on yourself: never quite fair enough by the Sun's measure, never quite secure enough by the Moon's. Comfort may start to feel like something to be earned. Give Saturn a better job than judging you: let it keep the calendar instead, with working hours that end, one day a week nobody can claim, and a clear point at which a task is finished and you stop.
