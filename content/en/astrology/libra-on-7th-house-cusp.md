Libra on the 7th house cusp sets the sign of partnership on your Descendant, the western point of the chart, where its own house begins. Across the chart Aries is rising, so your horizon falls exactly where the natural zodiac puts it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is the frame through which you meet the person across the table, and the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) it opens holds partners, close collaborators and open rivals ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Libra](/astrology/libra/) is the 7th's own sign, so here the house speaks in its native manner: reciprocity. What you look for is an exchange that stays even over time, with someone who stops to weigh what you both want. Agreements matter to you as much as feelings: terms either of you could have written, a give and take that neither has to police. Even a rival earns your respect by playing fair. All of this describes the bond you recognise, not a person you'll meet or a date when you will.

With Aries rising, Mars rules your chart and [Venus](/astrology/venus/) your partnerships, each from the house of its own sign. I read that as a self that moves first, and partnerships in which you learn to wait: what you bring is momentum, what you look for is the patience to settle things between two. Venus's house shows where your partnerships are lived: the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In about 7 London charts in 10 with this cusp, and 7 in 8 at New York, Taurus opens your 2nd house too, so Venus rules the same two houses it rules in the natural zodiac: partnership and your own resources. In the rest, more than 1 London chart in 4, the 7th holds the whole of Scorpio behind Libra ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and Mars, your chart ruler, has a say in your partnerships through it, with Pluto beside it.

This is the rarest of Libra's cusps: about 1 birth in 27 at London and 1 in 20 at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. Libra can only set while Aries rises, and in the north Aries rises fast, in about 52 minutes at London and 71 at New York ([Aries rising](/astrology/aries-rising/)), so the Descendant is in Libra for less than an hour of each London day. The 7th runs wide in these charts, about 34° to 48° at London, and every London chart with this cusp has an intercepted pair somewhere. In every one of them, at London as at New York, Capricorn is on the Midheaven. Libra misses every cusp in about 1 London birth in 6 (1 in 8 at New York), and when it does, the 7th is among the houses that most often hold it.

A time rounded to the hour can miss a window this short. A little earlier and Libra would still be above the horizon, on your 8th cusp ([Libra on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-8th-house-cusp/)); a little later, below it, on the 6th ([Libra on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-6th-house-cusp/)). A recorded minute is the only way to tell these charts apart, and I won't guess it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The same goes for anyone you'd like to compare your chart with: a synastry needs their time, and their consent, as much as yours ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
