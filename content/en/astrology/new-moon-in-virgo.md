Once a year, while the Sun crosses Virgo, the Moon catches up with it there and vanishes from the night sky. That meeting is the new moon in Virgo: a lunar month starting in the sign of craft and sorting, and a fresh page in the house where Virgo sits in your chart.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) is the moment the Moon passes the Sun: the two stand at the same degree, and the 29½-day round of phases begins again. In [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), a mutable earth sign, that beginning has a practical grain. Virgo asks how something could work better, so the month it opens favours repair over reinvention: the method you adjust, the skill you finally learn properly, the small disorder you stop stepping around. In the northern hemisphere it comes at harvest time, often as a new working or school year starts. Virgo's ruler is [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), so I also look at where Mercury sits in your chart: that's where a Virgo theme goes once it has begun.

A new moon is a short monthly marker. It counts in a chart only within about 1° of a natal planet or angle, and even then I read it as a trigger: a few days when something already active, by transit, progression, solar arc or eclipse, comes into focus. On its own, it's weather, not a forecast.

The most recent fell on 11 September 2026, at 18°26′ Virgo. The next is on 31 August 2027 at 17:41 UT, at 8°06′, then 18 September 2028 at 26°21′, 8 September 2029 at 16°04′ and 28 August 2030 at 5°41′, none of them an eclipse. Twelve lunar months are about eleven days shorter than a year, so each lands about ten degrees earlier than the last, until a year of thirteen sends it back late in the sign, as in 2028.

It comes a month after the [new moon in Leo](/astrology/new-moon-in-leo/) and a month before the [new moon in Libra](/astrology/new-moon-in-libra/). Two weeks before or after it, with the Sun still in Virgo, comes the [full moon in Pisces](/astrology/full-moon-in-pisces/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Where it lands in your life depends on the house that holds its degree, and only your birth time places the houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). In Placidus, the house system I use, that's usually the house with Virgo on its cusp, or the one before if the cusp sits later in Virgo than the new moon: at 8°06′, the 2027 new moon stays in your 5th house if your 6th begins at 20° Virgo.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Virgo rising: a fresh start in how you meet the world, built from details, like the competence you stop hiding behind modesty.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** the month opens on your money's small print: subscriptions, leaks, the price of a skill you could charge for if you trusted it.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** a new start in everyday words and errands: clearer messages, a short course, a system for the to-do list that finally works.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** home gets Virgo's attention: the repair, the cupboard, the routine that makes it easier to live in rather than more impressive.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** pleasure through practice: a craft or an instrument you want to do properly, the getting better enjoyed as much as the result.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** Virgo's own house: a new way of organising your working days, small adjustments rather than resolutions, rest planned in.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** a partnership starts again through what you do for each other: a fair division of tasks, help asked for and given.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** a clear look at what you share: joint accounts sorted, a loan read line by line, trust built on reliability.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** study with a method: a language learned grammar first, a journey planned to the last connection, beliefs checked against the facts.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** your public role renewed through skill: expertise made visible, work done so well that it becomes your reputation.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** in a group, you bring the useful thing, the plan or the spreadsheet, and friendships grow out of shared work.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a quiet start out of sight: rest taken seriously, worries written down so they stop circling at night.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Find the degree.** Is a planet or angle of yours within 1° of one of the positions above? If not, this is a quiet start in one house, and that's enough.
- **If something is there, look around it.** Ask what's already moving at that point, a slow transit, a progression, an eclipse: the new moon only turns the light on.
- **One thing, done well.** Virgo's way: one improvement for the month, not ten.
- **Leave health to the people trained for it.** Older books tie Virgo to health, but a new moon can't tell you anything about your body ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

When a reading leans on something this light, I say so: that's part of [how I read](/how-i-read/).
