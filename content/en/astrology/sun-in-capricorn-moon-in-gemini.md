Saturn rules your Sun and Mercury your Moon: time on one side, words on the other. You were born under a swelling Moon a few days before the full. Think of the pair as a builder who needs to talk the building through.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Gemini lies five signs on from Capricorn, so the lights share neither element nor modality: cardinal earth, mutable air, a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by sign, the link of constant readjustment. The [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) commits to a course and keeps to it; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) gets its footing back by turning a feeling over in words: the call to a friend on the way home, a page in a notebook, the joke that names what everyone was thinking. One part of you wants the plan signed off; the other wants to discuss it a little more.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules your Sun's sign and [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) your Moon's, and neither has a dignity in the other's sign; the lights have none here either. Saturn's sign and house show where you build slowly and carry responsibility; Mercury's show how your feelings find their words, and Mercury, never more than about 28° from the Sun, sits in Sagittarius, Capricorn or Aquarius in your chart. An aspect between Saturn and Mercury often says whether your planning and your talking work as one team or two.

Your birth came in the days before a full moon. The Moon was 122° to 180° ahead of the Sun, ten to almost fifteen days after the new moon, bright and rising in the afternoon or at dusk. The [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) takes about seven of these births in eight as eight phases count it, the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) the others, and close to 150° the quincunx by sign is exact. I read the gibbous days as a time for refining something almost finished, and a Gemini Moon refines by talking it over. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) places your birth among the eight.

About 1 person in 149 has this pair. When the Moon reached Gemini from Taurus, or left it for Cancer, on your birthday, your birth time is what chooses between this page, the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-taurus/) and the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-cancer/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign, from the same minute, completes the first sketch; the houses, also from it, show where the plan and the conversation take place.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You show love with reliability and with conversation. The Capricorn Sun keeps its promises and carries its share; the Gemini Moon needs a partner who talks, listens, sends the small news of the day and argues back with good humour. Dry wit often carries the tender things. The risk is that the Sun's reserve and the Moon's lightness together keep the deepest feelings out of the conversation altogether. A partner who asks the second question, gently, reaches you.

## At work {#at-work}

Organisations run on information, and this pair can be the person who keeps it flowing and makes it count: communications, editing, teaching, planning, turning policy into plain words. The Sun gives structure and staying power; the Moon gives flexibility, contacts and a quick read of a room. Long stretches of the same solitary task are harder for the Moon, and constant chatter with no outcome tries the Sun. A role that mixes long projects with daily exchange fits both.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The quincunx asks for adjustment, and here it shows as restlessness inside a schedule: the Moon wants variety, the Sun consistency, and you may judge your own need for change as frivolous. Or the opposite: a head full of conversations and no time to finish anything. Worry can arrive in words, at night. What helps is building the Moon's needs into the plan, a weekly call, a notebook, one new subject a season, so that curiosity serves the long work instead of competing with it.
