A trine between the Sun and Venus can only happen across two charts, because Venus is never more than about 47° from the Sun in anyone's sky. There, one person's Venus sits about 120° from the other's Sun, usually in the same element, and appreciation needs no prompting: one of you simply enjoys the way the other is.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) gives a life its centre and its warmth: identity, vitality, self-expression. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the capacity to appreciate: pleasure, affinity, an eye for what's good. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets two functions run together, each reinforcing the other, and as one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it places the warmth in one person and the appreciation in the other.

The two signs usually share an element. A Sagittarius Venus with a Leo Sun: a taste for generosity and adventure, meeting someone who lives warmly and in the open. A Capricorn Venus with a Virgo Sun: respect for work done properly, meeting someone who does it that way.

If it's your Sun, you feel liked without trying, and your direction gets warm, uncomplicated support: the other seems to enjoy watching you be yourself, and says so. If yours is the Venus, appreciating them takes no work at all. Their way of being fits your taste, generosity comes easily, and time with them feels well spent.

So what flows is goodwill: praise that's meant, help offered before it's needed, shared pleasure in the same kinds of things. A friendship, a family tie, a working partnership or a couple can all run on it for years.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Two things can shelter behind this ease. The first is honesty: when two people approve of each other this readily, criticism feels like a breach, and the feedback each needs can go unsaid. Venus is discernment as much as pleasure, the ability to tell good from not quite, and appreciation that never uses it loses some of its worth.

The second is the direction of the flow. Goodwill tends to travel from the Venus person to the Sun person, and over years it can be received like weather rather than as a gift; the Venus person may give and give, and notice late how little comes back.

The other contacts of the pair, by comparison:

- **[Sun conjunct Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-venus-synastry/):** one person's Venus on the other's Sun; warmer still, and harder to tell from approval.
- **[Sun sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-venus-synastry/):** two signs closer; pleasures offered, waiting to be taken up.
- **[Sun square Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-venus-synastry/):** three signs apart instead of four; liking that asks the other to change.
- **[Sun opposite Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-venus-synastry/):** two signs further; appreciation across a gap, kept in balance by taking turns.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Thank out loud, and specifically: "I noticed what you did, and it mattered" turns weather back into a gift. Return the appreciation in the other's own currency, whatever they value most, rather than in yours. Ask, now and then, for one honest criticism, and take it well when it comes. At work, make sure the person who quietly backs everyone else's ideas gets credit for their own; in a family or a friendship, keep an eye on who always remembers the birthdays.

The trine joins about 1 pair of people in 7, whichever of the two has the Venus, within 6.8°. Their birthdays usually fall about four months apart, from roughly ten weeks to nearly six months, whatever the years, since each Venus stays near its own Sun.

A birth hour earlier or later moves neither body much, so the trine rarely depends on it; the houses do, and they show where in each life the goodwill is felt. I won't read this contact, or anything else in a synastry, without both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
