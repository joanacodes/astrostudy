Change and ideals rarely keep the same timetable, and Uranus square Neptune in synastry sets that difference at right angles between two people. One person's Uranus sits about 90° from the other's Neptune, within 3.4°, and what one generation wants to change keeps catching on what another holds as an ideal.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reworks systems: it differentiates, frees, and wants arrangements that fit how things are now. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) holds the larger picture: compassion, imagination, a sense of what things could mean. A [square](/astrology/square/) makes their meeting a working friction, and among [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one sets one person's reforms across the other's ideals.

In the count's examples the signs share a modality. A Virgo Uranus with a Sagittarius Neptune, both mutable, sets improvements made through method and detail against ideals of meaning, belief and far horizons.

What the friction makes possible is ideals that survive testing, and reforms with a conscience. The Neptune person learns which hopes can take a practical form; the Uranus person, which changes serve something beyond themselves.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

If the Uranus is yours, your reforms keep landing on someone's sacred ground. Their ideals can look to you like fog over a problem that needs fixing, and it's easy to push past them.

If the Neptune is yours, your ideals keep being tested by someone's experiments. Their changes can feel like disenchantment, the world made smaller, and it's easy to withdraw into the vision. A square is friction, never failure: each era's instinct is useful, and each needs the other's.

- **[Uranus conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** the two fused on one degree, change laid straight on the ideal.
- **[Uranus sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** a sign closer, where they trade help instead of friction.
- **[Uranus trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune-synastry/):** a sign further, where new ideas and ideals agree.
- **[Uranus opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** the whole zodiac between them, a seesaw across decades.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Test the ideal and honour the reform, one piece at a time. The Neptune person can say which part of the vision is its heart; the Uranus person, which part of the change is need rather than novelty. Try changes as pilots and keep what survives both tests. A parent and an adult child arguing about how a family should live, colleagues a decade apart rewriting a mission, friends across ages: each can turn the square into reforms people believe in.

A Virgo Uranus from mid-1965 squares the Sagittarius Neptune of anyone born between October 1974 and January 1977; a Capricorn Neptune from mid-1995 is squared by the Libra Uranus of anyone born between September 1973 and October 1974, often a parent's age. Shared by everyone born in those years, it stays outside my synastry count, which only takes contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Through the houses, I read the friction between two eras' instincts about change and ideals, more than between two people.

The hour of birth moves neither planet, only the houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Neptune in the Uranus person's 4th house, the friction plays out over home and family: what a home should be, and how it should change ([Their Neptune in your 4th house](/astrology/neptune-in-4th-house-synastry/)).

For births of 1951 to 1958, on and off, the friction sits inside a single chart as [Uranus square Neptune](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune/). Between two charts it belongs to two people of different eras, and both need to want it read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
