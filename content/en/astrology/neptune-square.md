Neptune never comes home within a lifetime: its orbit takes about 165 years. The first great step it takes from its birth place is a quarter of the way round, the Neptune square, and for everyone born between 1930 and 2009 it has come, or will come, around forty.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) moves so slowly that a quarter of its orbit takes about forty years. The Neptune square is transiting Neptune 90° on from the degree it held at your birth, a [square](/astrology/square/) to its own natal place. In the site's count for births from 1930 to 2009, the first exact square fell between 39.4 and 42.1 in all 160 birth dates computed.

Neptune's orbit is close to a circle, so the age moves less than for Uranus or Pluto, under three years across eighty birth years, and it has crept earlier. Births on 1 January of every fifth year show it:

- **1930 to 1955:** about 42. Born in 1950, you met it at 42.1, 42.6 and 42.9.
- **1960 to 1975:** about 41. Born in 1970, at 41.2, 41.6 and 42.1.
- **1980 to 1995:** about 40. Born in 1990, at 40.3, 40.8 and 41.2.
- **2000 and 2005:** about 39½. Born in 2005, at 39.5, 39.8 and 40.3.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

With the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, a Neptune transit usually stays in orb about two and a half years ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). Most people meet the square in three exact passes; when one of Neptune's stations falls on the degree, there are five, as for births on 1 January 1960, 1965, 1980 and 2000. Born in 1980, you met it at 40.4, 40.5, 41.2, 41.8 and 42.

Neptune spends about fourteen years in a sign, so a whole cohort shares the sky of its square. People born between January 1984 and November 1998, with [Neptune in Capricorn](/astrology/neptune-in-capricorn/), meet theirs while Neptune crosses Aries, from 2026 to 2039 ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)): born on 1 January 1990, at 40.3, 40.8 and 41.2, in 2030 and 2031. What makes the square yours is the house your natal Neptune occupies, which needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and the aspects it makes to your personal planets.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In the system I built for my readings, Neptune enables imagination, compassion and the sense of meaning behind things, and loosens boundaries that are no longer needed. Its core need is openness to feeling and image without losing the ability to tell yourself from others, and fact from interpretation. At the square I look at both halves of that: which aims and boundaries were necessary and which were only habit, and where it matters to keep a clear line. Friction from Neptune softens rather than breaks. It doesn't mean confusion or deception by decree.

It shares its years with other transits. The [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) came between 38.9 and 44.5 in the same count, and for births from the mid-1950s to the late 1970s the [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/) came at 37 to 40, close by. I read each through its own planet, then together. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Neptune was checked like every placement and wasn't one of the three that held far more often, so I say so when a conclusion rests on it.

If a foggy stretch at this age starts to weigh on you more than a season should, the right door is a doctor or a therapist, not a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The Neptune square brings confusion.”** It softens edges. What blurs is often what had stopped meaning much.
- **“It always happens at 41.”** It came between 39.4 and 42.1, earlier for later generations.
- **“Neptune will come back to your birth degree.”** Its orbit takes about 165 years. No life sees its return.
- **“It's a midlife spiritual crisis.”** No transit is a crisis by itself. This one asks what your aims were for, and leaves the answer to you.
