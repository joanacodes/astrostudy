Pluto entered Aquarius in March 2023 and stays until January 2044, so everyone born with it since then is still a small child. On this page I describe a generation's placement as astrologers read it, never a child's chart: the planet of deep change in the sign of groups, networks and shared ideals.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms by clearing out what no longer serves and strengthening what is real. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, thinks in terms of the group: friendships, communities, networks, the ideals people hold in common, and the independence of each person within them. In Aquarius, Pluto's depth goes into collective life. Astrologers read this generation's Pluto as transformation through groups and shared ideas, through how people organise, belong and decide together. Being fixed, it holds its convictions steadily once it has them.

Aquarius has two rulers, and I read both. Modern tropical astrology gives it to [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the planet of innovation and liberation; tradition gives it to [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the planet of structure. Uranus was discovered in 1781, in the middle of Pluto's previous stay in Aquarius, which ran from April 1777 to December 1798. In a chart with this Pluto, Uranus shows where change wants to break through, and Saturn where it has to be built to last. Pluto itself has no dignity here; its modern domicile is Scorpio.

The present stay runs from March 2023 to January 2044. Until November 2024 Pluto crossed back and forth with [Pluto in Capricorn](/astrology/pluto-in-capricorn/), and from March 2043 it will do the same with [Pluto in Pisces](/astrology/pluto-in-pisces/), so births at either edge need the chart to say which sign is theirs. Nobody born from 1930 to 2009 has Pluto in Aquarius. As with every Pluto sign, the house is what makes it personal, and the house needs a [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/). For adults, Pluto in Aquarius is a transit instead: weather passing through the sky, not a placement in their chart, and never a forecast.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Read in an adult's chart, as it would be for anyone born from 1777 to 1798, this Pluto puts friendship at the root of love. Bonds are built on shared ideals and on respect for each other's independence, and closeness grows out of long talks about how the world could be. The tender point is distance: keeping feelings at arm's length, or finding a cause easier to love than a person. Relationships thrive on space and honesty together. The people born with it now are children, and [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/) are not something I read: this describes the placement, not anyone's child.

## At work {#at-work}

In an adult's working life, this Pluto is drawn to collective projects: networks, science, reform, organisations built around a shared purpose. It can transform how a group works, so that each member has a real voice, and it has little patience for authority that hasn't earned its place. Its gift is seeing a system as a whole; its work is remembering the people inside it.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard side of the placement is ideals held so firmly that people get lost behind them. Pluto in Aquarius can turn a cause into an identity and a group into a test of loyalty, until doubt feels like betrayal. Detachment can become a way of controlling closeness. And a group can end up carrying the power struggles that each person in it avoids.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Ideals tested on real people, one at a time. Groups where disagreement is safe. Friendship that leaves room for feeling as well as for thinking. And for the adults reading this, a more useful question than any about a child's chart: which house of your own chart Pluto is crossing during its years in Aquarius, and what it asks you to renew there, slowly, as weather rather than as fate.
