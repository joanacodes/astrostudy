With Pisces on the 6th house cusp, your working days belong to a sign that works by feel. You tend to work best where the job is to help or to create, and your routine runs more on rhythm and ritual than on the clock.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the daily round: the tasks, the habits, the colleagues, the way you look after yourself from one week to the next ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). Its natural sign is Virgo, and with [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), Virgo's opposite, on the cusp, the house of method opens in the sign of flow. You may work in waves, absorbed for hours and then adrift, and keep going on small rituals, a walk before work, the same mug, a playlist, rather than on a rigid timetable. At work you notice what someone needs before they ask, and you can end up doing their share too: learning where your job stops is part of this cusp. You may be drawn to work that cares, comforts or imagines, in any trade, and an office with no kindness in it drains you. Looking after yourself goes best gently, and rest, quiet and enough sleep count for more than rules.

Your working days answer to a ruler that blurs and a ruler that widens, Neptune and Jupiter, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows where routines dissolve, where inspiration takes the place of planning and where you give yourself away; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where work grows meaningful and generous, and where you take on too much in good faith. Sagittarius opens the 3rd in about 3 London charts in 5 and 3 New York charts in 4, so Jupiter rules your words and errands as well as your workdays, and teaching, writing or explaining often find their way into the job. Their houses show who and what your daily effort ends up caring for, as ruler of the 6th in the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

About 1 birth in 10 at London, and the same at New York, has Pisces on the 6th cusp in Placidus, the house system I use. In the north, Pisces is quick to rise in the east and slow to set in the west, so the cusps around the Descendant spend a long time in it. In about 4 charts in 5 the sign rising is Libra ([Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/)), so Venus, the planet exalted in Pisces, rules your chart, and the Moon usually rules your Midheaven through Cancer. In the rest, Virgo rises and Pisces opens the Descendant as well as the 6th ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), and the same two planets then govern your work and your partners. Just before it, the 5th nearly always opens in Aquarius. In about 1 London chart in 6, by contrast, Pisces lies wholly inside a house and opens none ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

The 6th's tradition includes illness, and I leave that to doctors: no chart can predict a health problem ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and what I read here is how you work and how kindly you treat yourself. As the day went on, Pisces would have moved on to the 5th cusp ([Pisces on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-5th-house-cusp/)); earlier, your Descendant would still hold it ([Pisces on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-7th-house-cusp/)). The exact time decides, and the chart waits for it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
