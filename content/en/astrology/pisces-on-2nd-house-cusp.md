With Pisces on the 2nd house cusp, the house of what you own and what you're worth opens in the last sign of the zodiac. What you value tends to be what can't be priced, and money passes through your hands more like a tide than a store.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is what you can count on, and [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) on its cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)) makes the counting the hard part. You may earn through care, art, music, healing or any work that asks for imagination, and find it easier to give your skill away than to name a fee for it. Money tends to follow need: a friend's rent, a cause, a present that was too generous. The things you keep are the ones with a story, a letter, a stone from a beach, a coat that belonged to someone, and their worth to you has nothing to do with the price. Your sense of worth can be fluid too, rising and falling with how much you feel you've helped. Clear accounts are the kindness this cusp most needs from you: a budget written down, a fee agreed before the work begins.

Neptune and Jupiter between them govern this 2nd, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern ruler, shows where money blurs, where it slips away unnoticed or where you'd rather not look at the figures; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the traditional one, shows where you trust there'll be enough, and where generosity comes back to you. Sagittarius, Jupiter's other sign, opens your 11th in every one of these charts, so the planet that rules your resources also rules your friends and allies, and money and friendship tend to mix. Find each ruler's house and you find the tide's direction, as ruler of the 2nd in the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Few charts have this cusp: about 1 London birth in 20, and 1 New York birth in 17, counted with the Placidus houses I use. With Aries, Pisces is the quickest sign to rise in the north, about 52 minutes at London, so the cusps beside the Ascendant hardly stop in it. At London, almost every chart with Pisces on the 2nd has [Capricorn rising](/astrology/capricorn-rising/) and the whole of Aquarius tucked inside the 1st house ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)); at New York, Capricorn rises in just over half of them and Aquarius in the rest. With Capricorn rising, Saturn rules the chart, a careful, structured manner in front of a fluid hand with money, and Saturn is the planet I lean on, since in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held 75 to 90% of the time. The 2nd is wide here, about 39° to 46° at London, and in about 2 London charts in 5 it holds all of Aries too, which adds Mars and a quicker way of earning. And about 1 London chart in 6, 1 New York chart in 8, has no Pisces cusp at all.

No chart can say what you'll earn, and I don't read one for figures ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)); this cusp describes how money and value move through your life. [Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/) would have taken a later birth the same day, and [Pisces on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-3rd-house-cusp/) an earlier one. With a sign that rises this fast, minutes matter, and I need the birth time before I say anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
