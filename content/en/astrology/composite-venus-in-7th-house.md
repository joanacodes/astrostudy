Some pairs are at their best across a table from someone else: two friends negotiating a lease, a couple who host other couples, business partners who can settle a dispute between clients. With composite Venus in the 7th house, partnership itself is what the relationship values, and it tends to be good at it.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The composite [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) faces the composite Ascendant and opens [the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), so in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) this house inherits the Ascendant's uncertainty. One method takes the midpoint of the two natal Descendants; the other derives it from the composite Midheaven at the latitude of the pair's home. Both need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and near the Descendant they can put [Venus](/astrology/venus/) on either side of it, in the 6th or the 7th.

Libra is the natural sign of this angular house, so Venus stands on its own ground here: partnership, agreements, negotiation, the allies a pair makes. The bond values fairness and enjoys cooperation, and it often shows its affection by being a good team for others: hosting, mediating, making deals that leave everyone feeling well treated. Neither of you need be a natural negotiator for the pair to be formidable across a table. The natal [Venus in the 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house/) is one person's wish for grace in a partner; [their Venus in your 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house-synastry/), the way one person can seem made for partnership with the other; in the composite, partnership is what the two of you prize.

As in every sky, composite Venus and the composite Sun stay within 47° of each other for about 9 pairs in 10. For pairs born months apart the midpoints can part company, and in roughly 1 pair in 22 the composite Sun lands on the far side of the chart, close to the composite Ascendant, at the start of the 1st house ([composite Sun in the 1st house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-1st-house/)). However that looks on paper, the method made it.

The risk is being better partners to the world than to each other. A bond that is gracious with clients, friends and other couples can be curt at home, saving its fairness for outsiders. Partnering needn't mean depending, and balance isn't the same as pleasing everyone, outside the bond or inside it. It helps to bring the same courtesy home and to make agreements between the two of you as carefully as with anyone else. And no chart can tell whether a partnership will become a marriage; that belongs to the two people, not to the 7th house.

[Composite Venus in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-6th-house/) takes pleasure in the work; [composite Venus in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-8th-house/) values the trust underneath it. Composites stay outside my own readings; this is how the technique is read by those who use it, next to the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), where each person's planets also fall in the other's houses, the 7th included ([house overlays](/astrology/house-overlays/)). A bond that values fair agreements will recognise this one: the chart is drawn only with both people's consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
