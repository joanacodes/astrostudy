The degree that was rising at your birth is the threshold of your chart, and this transit puts the planet of depth on it. Only your birth time can place that degree, so without it this transit has no date, which is one reason I don't read a chart without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is your orientation: the stance you take before you've had time to think, the way you start a day, a job, a conversation. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms and empowers, bringing up what lies underneath. At the [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), Pluto and your rising degree become one process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the way you begin is reworked from below. A manner adopted to be acceptable can start to feel like a costume; a habit of guarding yourself on arrival can ease, or harden for a while before it eases. Other people may notice before you do that you arrive with more weight than you used to.

The Ascendant's blind spot is confusing a first response with a permanent identity, and Pluto's is confusing intensity with truth: a stronger manner can feel like the real you simply because it is stronger. What changes is your way in, not who you are. No transit to the Ascendant forecasts an illness, an accident or a change in your looks ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

Pluto arrives here from your 12th house, where it has been working out of sight for years ([Pluto transiting the 12th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-12th-house/)), and crosses into your 1st, where it stays a decade or more ([Pluto transiting the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-transit-1st-house/)); in Placidus, the house system I use, the rising degree is the line between them. Born with Pluto just above that line, late in your 12th, you met this crossing in your first years. Born with Pluto just inside your 1st ([Pluto in the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-in-1st-house/)), it had already passed and won't return within a life.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Pluto needs about two years and eight months to get across your rising degree, measured from 1½° before exact to 1° after, my orbs, and twenty-two months when it moves quickly. In about three passes out of five it goes over the degree three times, and five times in the rest.

More than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet it by 80, and nobody meets it twice. Pluto covers well under half the zodiac in a lifetime, and your rising degree may not lie in that stretch: it depends on how far Pluto stood behind your Ascendant at birth, counted forward through the signs.

Here the birth time decides everything. On average the rising degree moves about 15° an hour, and while Pluto crosses Aquarius it covers about a degree and a half a year, so an hour's error in the time moves this transit by about a decade, or out of your life altogether. Until early 2044 Pluto crosses [Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/): its early degrees in the mid-2020s, the middle ones in the 2030s, the last around 2042.

Most people meet the square instead ([Transiting Pluto square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-ascendant/)), about 7 in 8 of them, and about 3 in 4 the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-ascendant/)) or the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-ascendant/)). The opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-ascendant/)), Pluto on your Descendant, is as rare as this crossing.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Find your birth time before you look for dates. A certificate, a hospital record or a parent's diary is worth more than any estimate ([how to find your birth time](/astrology/how-to-find-your-birth-time/)).

Pay attention to what you lead with in new company, and keep only what still sounds like you.

Let the change come a pass at a time. Pluto crosses this degree more than once, and a new way of meeting the world settles best in stages.
