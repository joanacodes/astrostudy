Twice in the Uranus cycle, in the late twenties and again in the mid-fifties, Uranus stands 120° from its birth place. These are the cycle's easy stretches: about twenty months each in which the way you differ can be lived rather than defended.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in your chart is how you differ and where you need room; in the sky it's the same function, moving on. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates two functions through flow, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) lets the freedom you were born with work with your life instead of against it.

At about 27, the first trine usually falls between the trial of the first square and the weight of the [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/). Things you tried at 20 out of restlessness can settle into a form of your own: a way of working, a circle of friends, a way of living that fits. Nothing has to be torn down to make room for it.

In the mid-fifties, the second trine comes after the opposition has been lived through. By then independence is easier to use than to win, and the trine favours putting experience to work in a freer form: teaching, advising, starting the project you postponed. The risk, as with any trine, is coasting, an ease you notice only once it's gone.

With your natal Uranus in the 10th, the first trine reaches it from your 2nd ([Uranus transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-2nd-house/)): your own way of working finds support in what you value and know how to do. Placing your Uranus in the 10th takes the birth time, and so does every reading I give ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, 1½° before exact to 1° after, each trine lasts about twenty months, eleven at the shortest, with three exact hits in more than nine passes out of ten: in the late twenties, a stretch long enough to give a way of living its first lasting shape.

From one birth year to another, the age of the first trine varies from about 24½ to 30 in the site's count: at 25 for someone born at the start of 1945, at 29.4 for the start of 1995. For births from the mid-1970s on, it shares its years with the first Saturn return, which falls between 28.4 and 29.9; for earlier births it came a few years before. The second trine, in the planets' positions, falls between about 52 and 58, well before 80 for everyone.

Air signs trine one another, so Uranus's stay in Gemini, until 2033, reaches natal Uranus in Aquarius and in Libra alike ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)). People born from April 1995 to December 2003 are meeting their first trine at about 28 to 30; people born from September 1968 to September 1975, their second, at about 56 or 57.

[Transiting Uranus square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-uranus/) stands about seven years before the first trine and seven after the second, with [transiting Uranus opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-uranus/), the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) at about 42, between the two. [Transiting Uranus sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-uranus/) comes at about 13 and about 70, and the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/) closes the cycle at about 84.

## What helps {#what-helps}

At 27, build the thing that came out of the restless years. The first trine favours giving a shape to what the first square stirred up.

Around the Saturn return, let the trine loosen the deadline. Saturn asks what you'll commit to; Uranus in trine reminds you that a commitment can still be of your own design.

In the mid-fifties, hand something on. Your way of doing things is easier to share now than to defend, and the second trine rewards whoever teaches it.
