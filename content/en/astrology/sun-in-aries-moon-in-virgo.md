One light wants to start; the other needs things to be right. In this pair, the first draft and the careful edit live in the same person, and you were born under a Moon a few days short of full.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aries is cardinal fire, Virgo mutable earth: no element in common, no modality, and five signs between them, a quincunx by sign. The old books gave signs this far apart no aspect at all, and I read the quincunx as recalibration, two parts that keep adjusting because neither comes naturally to the other. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) moves first and sorts things out later; the [Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/) calms down by sorting them out first.

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) rules your Sun's sign and [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) your Moon's, so effort and analysis each have a vote. Virgo is the one sign where a planet is both at home and exalted, and the planet is Mercury, which gives this Moon a precise, attentive way of looking after itself and others. The Sun keeps its exaltation in Aries; the Moon has no dignity in Virgo. The pair can look like a contradiction from outside. From inside, it's usually a sequence: act, then check.

The Moon had run 121° to 179° ahead of the Sun, ten to almost fifteen days after a new moon. Of every eight such births, seven fall in the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/), the last phase before full, and one in the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/). Within a degree or two of 150°, the lights also make a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/) by degree, not just by sign. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows how the eight phases are drawn.

This is the pair of about 1 person in 146. A sign holds the Moon for barely two and a half days. Born on the day it moved from Leo into Virgo, or from Virgo into Libra, you have the time to thank for your page: this one, the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-leo/) or the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-libra/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With the rising sign, which comes from that time too, the two lights give a first rough plan of a chart, and Virgo, of all signs, knows a rough plan isn't the finished thing.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You show love by noticing what needs doing and doing it, quickly. The Aries Sun acts on affection; the Virgo Moon makes the act exact: the spare key cut, the repaired bike, the form sent before anyone asked. Where it goes wrong is the commentary. A suggestion meant as care can come out with Aries bluntness and land as criticism. Saying *I'm offering this because I care* before the fix changes how it's heard, and letting a partner do something their own way, imperfectly, is its own kind of gift.

## At work {#at-work}

You can launch a thing and also make it work, which few people manage alone. The Sun likes the beginning; the Moon is happiest once the process is clean and the errors are gone. Under pressure the two compete, speed against accuracy, and you may end up doing both badly in the rush to do both at once. Your best work tends to come in two passes, a fast first one for the Aries Sun and a careful second one for the Virgo Moon, with a night's sleep between them.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The inner editor works faster than you'd like. The Aries Sun wants to act now; the Virgo Moon has already found three things wrong with the plan and a fourth with you. That can stall a start, or spoil one you were proud of. Treat the critic as a colleague with a timetable rather than a voice with a veto: let the first version go out rough, then give the Moon its hour to improve it. And at the end of the week, list what went well before you list what didn't.
