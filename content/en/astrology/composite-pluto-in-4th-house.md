What happens behind a couple's front door is rarely what the world sees. A composite Pluto in the 4th house puts the relationship's deepest changes there: in the home it makes, the families it comes from, and the private life it keeps.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is the floor of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/): home, family, roots, and the emotional foundation everything else rests on. It begins at the composite [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart. Put [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) here and astrologers read a bond that transforms from the base up. Homes get rebuilt, sometimes literally: a ruin restored over years, a flat emptied after a loss and made new, a move that cuts old ties and puts down new ones. The two family stories that meet in this house get reworked too, what each of you inherited about money, anger, love or silence, until the pair has a way of doing things that is its own.

The relationship's roots aren't either person's. [Pluto in the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-in-4th-house/) in one birth chart describes one person's deep roots and a past with weight; [their Pluto in your 4th house](/astrology/pluto-in-4th-house-synastry/), one person reaching right down into the other's sense of home. The composite's 4th belongs to the pair, which is why two people who left home lightly can find that, together, home becomes the most serious thing in their lives. Much of what this placement does goes on out of sight: the bond may look calm from outside while it quietly rebuilds its foundations.

The trap is carrying old survival habits into the new home as if they were who you are. Each of you learnt ways of getting through in your own family, keeping the peace, keeping score, keeping quiet, and a 4th-house Pluto can set those habits in stone under the shared roof. They were adaptations, not identities. It helps to name which family rule you're following when a row starts, and to decide together whether your home still needs it.

Sometimes home is where one person is afraid. If that is true for either of you, no reading of a chart is the answer: in a UK emergency ring 999, and for a voice at any hour, Samaritans on 116 123; my Disclaimers page lists the rest ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)).

Composite houses need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and this one has a twist. The 4th begins at the composite IC, opposite the composite Midheaven, which is the midpoint of your two Midheavens, so both ways of drawing the houses agree on where it starts. Where it ends, at the 5th cusp, can differ: in the method that casts the houses from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, moving a long way north or south shifts that cusp, so a house about home can change shape when the home moves. Family records often hold the birth times you'll need; even so, the pair's chart is drawn only when both of you say yes, never by a parent or a sibling on your behalf ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

[Composite Pluto in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-3rd-house/) works through the street and the conversation; [composite Pluto in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-5th-house/), through play and what the pair creates. In the 4th, it works under the floorboards.
