The planet that rules your Descendant, the western end of your horizon, sits across the chart in your 1st house. The business of other people is handed to the house of you: partnership becomes part of your manner, and you bring the other person into every first meeting.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) holds the people you meet one to one: a partner in love or in work, a close collaborator, the rival who faces you openly. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is you at the point of entry: your body, your manner, how you begin. A house's ruler takes the house's matters wherever it stands ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and this one crosses the horizon to bring them over to your side.

I read it as partnership worn on the outside. You tend to arrive with the other person already in mind: you ask, adjust, make room, and people often feel met within minutes. What you look for in a partner doesn't stay in them, either. Over the years it turns up in your own manner, so the people you pair with leave marks anyone can see. Rivals count too: you may open with the arguments you expect from the other side, which makes for fair negotiating and, now and then, for arguing against yourself. What this asks is a line between taking on a partner's ways and losing your own. Leaning towards people isn't leaning on them, and a manner borrowed from someone you love is still yours to shape.

There's a pattern in the signs. Your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is always the sign opposite your rising sign, so when its ruler stands in your rising sign, it's in the sign opposite its own home, in [detriment](/astrology/detriment/): Saturn in Cancer with Cancer rising and Capricorn setting, Jupiter in Gemini with Gemini rising and Sagittarius setting. The other person's planet works in your manner rather than its own. I read that as a partner's way of doing things that you keep translating, never as a fault. If your 1st runs on into the next sign and the ruler stands there, the detriment drops away.

The planet sets the tone, and its sign the manner. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo Descendant from here, is in its joy, the house where tradition said it rejoices ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): you talk your way into closeness, and conversation is how you choose people. The Moon, ruling a Cancer Descendant from a Capricorn 1st, gives a reserved manner that softens the moment someone needs looking after.

Taurus rising puts [Scorpio on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/) and gives your partnerships two rulers, Mars and Pluto; I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)), and here Mars stands in a Taurus 1st, in detriment as well, putting a partner's heat into a steady bearing. Pluto, which shares its sign with a whole generation, shows through its own house where the intensity of your bonds is lived.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 1st lies just under the eastern horizon: the planet that rules the setting degree was itself waiting to rise. That can turn on minutes. Born a little later, it would already have risen into the 12th, where love keeps part of its life out of view ([Ruler of the 7th in the 12th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)); a little earlier, it could sit lower, in the 2nd, where partnership and what you value run together ([Ruler of the 7th in the 2nd house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/)). The sign on the Descendant moves with the time as well, and can hand the 7th to another planet, which is why I don't read a chart without the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A chart can't name your partner or date a wedding, and I don't ask this one to. What it shows is closer to home: the people you choose, and the ones you argue with, become part of the person others meet.
