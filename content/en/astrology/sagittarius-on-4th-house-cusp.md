Sagittarius on the 4th house cusp places the sign of the open road at the very bottom of the chart, the IC, where the house of home begins. The ground you stand on is somewhere you leave from, and come back to with stories.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) is the root of the chart, and in Placidus, the house system I use, it opens the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/): home, family, belonging and the private self ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) there tends to make home a base camp rather than a fortress. I read it as a kitchen where people argue about ideas, a house that takes in guests from elsewhere, a sense of belonging carried in a language, a faith or a way of seeing as much as in a postcode. The private self underneath is often restless and hopeful, and needs room even indoors: a window with a view, a door you can go out of. What the cusp says about family is how it felt from the inside, never who your parents are; their charts are their own. When I read this IC, I ask where you feel most at home, and the answer is often a place you chose rather than the one you started from.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules this IC, so the house it occupies shows where home is made and remade, and its sign shows how: the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In these charts Jupiter has a second post. Virgo always rises with a Sagittarius IC at London and New York, so Pisces, Jupiter's other sign, always sets on the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/): home and partnership answer to one planet, with Neptune sharing the 7th, and I read both of Pisces's rulers. The other two angles belong to Mercury, through Virgo in front and Gemini at the top ([Gemini on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/)). All four angles sit in mutable signs, the signs that adapt and move on, and a chart built on that cross tends to handle change better than standing still.

About 1 birth in 11 has Sagittarius on the IC, and London and New York agree, because the sign at the bottom of the chart depends on the time of day alone, not on latitude. Sagittarius leads up to the December solstice, where the ecliptic runs almost parallel to the equator, so the meridian takes a little longer than average to pass through it: about two hours and eight minutes of every day. The 4th house is wide in these charts, some 36° to 38° at London, so Sagittarius never opens the 3rd or the 5th as well, and in about 1 London chart in 5 Capricorn lies wholly inside the 4th, an [intercepted sign](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) that brings Saturn's sense of duty indoors.

Born later that day, you'd have had Sagittarius on the 3rd cusp ([Sagittarius on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/)); born earlier, on the 5th ([Sagittarius on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-5th-house-cusp/)). The IC turns with the rest of the wheel, close to a degree every four minutes, so I want the minute of birth before saying anything about your roots, and without a birth time I don't read the chart at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
