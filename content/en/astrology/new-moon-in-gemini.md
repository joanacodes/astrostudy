The new moon in Gemini is the year's new moon in mutable air, at the end of the northern spring, when the Sun and Moon meet in the sign ruled by Mercury. I read it as a marker for curiosity, talk and learning, and in 2029 and 2030 it carries more weight than usual, because it's a solar eclipse.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

At a [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/), the Sun's purpose and the Moon's needs set out together from one sign. In [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/), the sign is air and mutable: quick, sociable, interested in everything, happiest with two options rather than one. Mercury rules it, the planet that processes information and carries it between people, so this new moon tends to stir questions, messages and plans that are still being talked through. Neither the Sun nor the Moon has any traditional dignity in Gemini; they simply take on its manner.

The dates, in Universal Time:

- **2026:** 15 June, 02:54 UT, at 24°03′ Gemini
- **2027:** 4 June, 19:40 UT, at 13°58′ Gemini
- **2028:** 24 May, 08:16 UT, at 3°41′ Gemini
- **2029:** 12 June, 03:51 UT, at 21°29′ Gemini, a partial solar eclipse
- **2030:** 1 June, 06:22 UT, at 10°50′ Gemini, an annular solar eclipse

The last two are eclipses because the Moon's nodes are moving from Cancer and Capricorn into Gemini and Sagittarius in those years, and a new moon near a node passes in front of the Sun. The run continues one more year: on 21 May 2031 the new moon at 0°04′ Gemini is an annular eclipse too ([eclipse in Gemini](/astrology/eclipse-in-gemini/), [solar eclipses](/astrology/solar-eclipses/)).

That changes how far they reach. An ordinary new moon in Gemini counts, in the rule I follow, only within a degree of a planet or angle in your chart, as a trigger inside something a transit or progression is already doing. The eclipses of 2029 and 2030 count within about three degrees, at either end of their axis: a planet near 21° Gemini or 21° Sagittarius for the first, near 11° Gemini or Sagittarius for the second. Even then I read them through the point they touch and its house, as emphasis, never as an omen.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Where Gemini opens a house, its new moon usually falls there, unless Gemini straddles two and the degree puts it in the other. In the eclipse years, 2029 and 2030, the house opposite, where Sagittarius sits, is touched too. All of it rests on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) and Placidus houses.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/gemini-rising/):** Gemini rising: a lighter, more curious way of meeting people; a new way of introducing yourself.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/gemini-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** income and what you value; comparing options, an idea about earning in more than one way.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** Gemini's natural house: messages, writing, short trips, siblings; a fresh round of conversations.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/gemini-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home and family; talk around the table, a busier household, books moved in.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/gemini-on-5th-house-cusp/):** play, romance, creative work; flirting with words, games, writing for pleasure.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/gemini-on-6th-house-cusp/):** daily work; juggling tasks, a new tool, a change in how things get passed along.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/):** partners, with Sagittarius rising; a lively exchange with someone close, two people comparing notes.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/gemini-on-8th-house-cusp/):** shared money and trust; the paperwork of joint matters, a frank question finally asked.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs, study, distant places; reading widely, questioning a view, a journey with many stops.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/gemini-on-10th-house-cusp/):** career and public voice; a role that involves talking, a contact that matters.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/gemini-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends and groups; new acquaintances, ideas traded in company.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/gemini-on-12th-house-cusp/):** retreat; a private notebook, quiet reading, thinking things through alone.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Start with where it lands. Find the degree in your chart, the house it falls in, and any natal planet or angle close by: within a degree for an ordinary year, within about three for the eclipses of 2029 and 2030. If nothing is that close, read it as the month's weather in that house, a breeze of interest rather than a storm.

If something is, look for the slower movement already at work there, and let the new moon simply show it to you. Gemini's manner is a good tool in itself: ask the question, write it down, try two versions and keep the one that works. Mercury's house in your chart tells you where that thinking tends to happen.

Don't let it rush you. A new moon in Gemini is no reason to sign, send or decide anything by a date, and an eclipse is no warning. Before it comes the [new moon in Taurus](/astrology/new-moon-in-taurus/), slower and steadier; after it, the [new moon in Cancer](/astrology/new-moon-in-cancer/), which turns towards home.
