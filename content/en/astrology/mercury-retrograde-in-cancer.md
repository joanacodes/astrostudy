Every one of Mercury’s retrogrades in Cancer lasts a little over 24 days, longer on average than in any other sign, and all of them fall between early June and early August. In the sign that remembers how things were said, they send the mind back through memory.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) is cardinal water, ruled by the Moon, and Mercury there thinks by feeling its way, keeping the tone of a conversation as well as its words. When [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/) reaches it, the planet seems from Earth to stop and slide back through the sign, and memory comes forward: family stories retold, an old exchange replayed for how it sounded, a message from someone close read again. What slows is the protective side of thinking, deciding whom to keep close; Mercury has no dignity in Cancer, and I read these weeks as tender rather than troubled.

In the dates I use, thirteen retrogrades touch Cancer from 2000 to 2040, mostly two years running. The most recent was this year, from 29 June to 23 July 2026, wholly inside the sign, from 26°15′ back to 16°19′. The next follows soon after: from 10 June to 4 July 2027, beginning at 6°21′ Cancer and ending at 27°28′ Gemini ([Mercury retrograde in Gemini](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-gemini/)). In 2032 it comes in from Leo ([Mercury retrograde in Leo](/astrology/mercury-retrograde-in-leo/)), from 7°32′ Leo to 26°49′ Cancer, between 10 July and 3 August.

Mercury retrograde in Cancer at birth is something else, a mind that keeps feeling and memory close for life; I write about that in [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house Cancer occupies, and so the part of your life these weeks fall in, comes from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): a few hours move every cusp, and I don’t read a chart without it. I draw the houses in Placidus; with Cancer on a cusp, a retrograde of 9° to 11° usually stays in that house, and its two stations settle the rest.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** Cancer rising: the careful way you meet people; you may notice tone more than usual, yours and other people’s.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/cancer-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what makes you feel safe to own; keepsakes, and the reasons you keep them.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** talk with the people close by, brothers, sisters, neighbours; how something was said, more than what.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** Cancer in its natural house: home, family and memory; the house you grew up in and its stories come back into conversation.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/):** tender romance and creative work drawn from memory; a love letter, a poem or an old song you return to.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/):** a workplace that feels like a family; the care you put into daily work, and the colleague you meant to thank.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Cancer on the Descendant, with Capricorn rising: the partner who feels like home; what you need from each other, said again more gently.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/):** deep bonds and what you hold in common; matters of family trust, talked over at a slower pace.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs rooted in where you come from; the pull of a familiar place, or curiosity about your family’s past.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a public life built on care; the way you look after people at work, reconsidered.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends who became family; old friends come back to mind.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/cancer-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private feelings and memory; things you have never told anyone, turned over quietly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Memory is the material here, so let it be useful: write down what comes back, the story or the sentence, before deciding what it means. The Moon rules Cancer, and the house of your natal Moon shows where those feelings tend to settle.

If a family conversation turns to the past, listening often does more than correcting the record. A retrograde in Cancer only looks like going backwards: the past it brings up is material for now, not a place to stay.
