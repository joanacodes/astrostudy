Some people make honesty feel like a gift rather than a test. Venus sextile Pluto in synastry can be one reason: about 60° lie between one person's Venus and the other's Pluto, and what the first holds dear can be looked at, kept or let go with the second's help, whenever either of them asks.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) sorts what a person cares for from what they can do without: preference, pleasure, the balance of an exchange. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) gets to the bottom of things: it lets go of what's finished and makes what's left stronger. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) puts two functions within reach of each other, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the reaching is something two people have to choose.

If the Venus is yours, you can count on this person to show you which attachments still matter. Ask, and you'll get an honest answer about the job you keep for comfort or the hobby you've outgrown, given with care rather than judgement. What survives the question tends to be loved more freely afterwards.

If the Pluto is yours, your honesty is welcome here, and even enjoyed. The Venus person brings warmth to the hard conversations and appreciation to whatever you've rebuilt, so depth stops feeling like something you carry alone. You may find yourself lighter in this company than in most.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A sextile offers rather than insists, so depth can stay optional for years. The Pluto person may hold insights back for fear of intruding, or keep their own motives private, which isn't the same thing as depth. The Venus person may keep things pleasant, choosing harmony over the conversation that would renew something. Then the bond stays nice, and a little shallower than either wanted. Opportunity, as the sextile keeps reminding, needs someone to take it.

The other contacts of the pair don't wait to be asked: [Venus conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-pluto-synastry/) makes everything matter at once; [Venus square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-pluto-synastry/) turns the question of what to keep into friction; [Venus trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-pluto-synastry/) lets loyalty deepen by itself; and [Venus opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-pluto-synastry/) splits lightness and depth between two people.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Ask the real question once in a while, and agree beforehand that it can be asked. "Is this still working for you?", about a shared flat, a standing arrangement or a joint project, gives the sextile something to do. The Pluto person can offer insight as a question rather than a verdict; the Venus person can say what they'd keep no matter what. Friends after a rough patch, two colleagues reviewing work they've built together, an aunt and a grown nephew clearing out a family attic, or a couple can all use the same opening.

In about 1 pair of people in 13, one person's Venus sextiles the other's Pluto within 3.6°, in one direction or the other. Pluto's place belongs to the Pluto person's generation: it shifts so slowly that people born near them in age bring nearly the same one. The sextile is made on the Venus side. Venus usually passes any given degree within days, though in a year when it turns retrograde nearby it can hover for weeks, so this contact comes down to when the Venus person was born. Over a single day the angle hardly changes, so the hour is no threat to it; what the hour places is the houses, and with them the areas where the honest talks happen, which is why both times come first ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Venus sextile Pluto](/astrology/venus-sextile-pluto/) in one chart is someone who can renew what they love when they choose to. Between two, the choosing involves both people, and so does the honesty.
