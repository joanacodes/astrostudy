With the Moon in the 7th house, home is often a person. You feel most like yourself with someone close beside you, and you come to know your own feelings partly by seeing them reflected in another face.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is about bonds between equals, face to face: partners, spouses, close collaborators, and the open rivals tradition also put here. It is angular, its cusp is the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the setting degree opposite the Ascendant, and Libra is its natural sign. With the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) here, the chart's sense of safety is bound up with closeness to one other person. You regulate partly through company: a shared meal after a hard day, a voice on the phone, someone who knows how you take bad news.

Moonset was near when you were born: the Moon hung just above the western horizon. In Placidus, the house system I use, the line between the 6th and the 7th is the Descendant itself, so a Moon a little under it is a 6th-house Moon, while whole sign houses would simply give the 7th to the sign opposite your rising sign. A few minutes of birth time can move the Moon across that line ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)).

Attachment isn't dependency, and I don't read this Moon as needing someone in order to function. It describes where your feelings come most alive and where you look for comfort: in a bond with one person. It doesn't say who that person will be, or when they'll arrive. It isn't the same thing as [their Moon in your 7th house](/astrology/moon-in-7th-house-synastry/) either, which is about another person's Moon landing in your chart.

The [Moon in the 6th house](/astrology/moon-in-6th-house/), just before, steadies itself through the routine of the day; the [Moon in the 8th house](/astrology/moon-in-8th-house/), just after, needs a deeper and more private trust.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

This is where the placement lives. You tend to give a great deal of care in partnership, noticing a partner's moods, adapting to their rhythms, making a home of the two of you. You may also look for someone to watch over you, or someone to watch over, and feel unsettled after a long time single. The partners you choose often show you something about your own needs: the qualities that draw you are frequently the ones you're learning to give yourself. The work is keeping your own feelings in view while you're tuned so closely to someone else's.

## At work {#at-work}

You tend to work best in pairs: a trusted colleague, a business partner, a client you get to know well. One-to-one roles suit this Moon, counselling, consulting, representing others, any work built on a relationship of trust. You read people quickly and often know how a negotiation is going before anyone says so. Working entirely alone can feel oddly cold, and a falling-out with a close colleague can affect you as much as a personal one.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Your own needs can blur with the other person's. You may feel what a partner feels so readily that you lose track of what you feel, or adjust so much to keep things smooth that resentment builds unseen. Choosing partners who need looking after can feel like love and turn into exhaustion. And the end of a relationship, or even a cold spell in one, can shake your sense of safety more than it would shake someone whose Moon sits elsewhere.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep a relationship with yourself alongside the one with your partner: time alone, your own friends, your own plans. Before you respond to someone else's mood, check your own. Let care flow both ways, and notice who looks after you, not only whom you look after. And when you're between relationships, use the time to build the inner company this Moon looks for in others; it's there to be found.

In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), this house of the Moon was checked against real lives like every other placement, while the far better results belonged to Saturn, Chiron and Lilith; overall, the proportion that held was about a third. I read a 7th-house Moon, accordingly, as a tendency in how you love, never a forecast of whom you'll love.
