Mars in the 3rd house fights in sentences. Its arena is the conversation, the message thread, the meeting, the kitchen table, and its sharpest tool is the quick reply that lands before anyone else has found their words.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is assertion and the drive to act. [The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is the everyday mind and the world close at hand: how you think, talk, write and learn; the people nearby, brothers and sisters, cousins, neighbours; and the short trips of an ordinary week. Mars here puts its energy into communication and local life. You think fast, decide quickly, say what you mean and enjoy an exchange with some edge to it. Learning is active for you: you learn by arguing, trying and doing.

It's a cadent house, one of the houses of adjusting and processing, and its natural sign is Gemini. Tradition called it the Goddess and gave the Moon its joy here, so this Mars works in the Moon's house of everyday movement: errands, visits, the comings and goings that fill a week. Across the chart lies the 9th, the house of the far horizon; with Mars in the 3rd, the battles are local and the arguments are about today rather than about ultimate truths.

When you were born, Mars had passed the lowest point of its daily circle and begun its climb towards the eastern horizon. [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, divides the time each degree takes to travel from one angle to the next into three equal parts; the 3rd is the first of the three between the IC and the Ascendant, so where it starts and ends depends on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). [Mars in the 2nd house](/astrology/mars-in-2nd-house/) builds up what it can keep; [Mars in the 4th house](/astrology/mars-in-4th-house/) defends a home.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

With the people closest to you, words are where the energy shows. You may flirt by teasing, show affection by debating, and settle disagreements by talking them through, fast. Siblings, or the people you grew up beside, can bring early rivalry or fierce loyalty, sometimes both; I read that as a pattern in how you stand up for yourself among equals, never as a forecast about any one relationship. A partner who enjoys conversation, and doesn't take a sharp sentence for a declaration of war, suits this Mars.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, this Mars is quick and verbal: writing, journalism, teaching, sales, negotiation, transport, anything with deadlines, phones and constant movement. You're good at the fast reply, the first draft, the summary nobody else had time to write. Long stretches without contact, or tasks that never change, sap your energy. You do your best work in short loops: ask, answer, adjust, ask again.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Speaking before thinking, then regretting the edge on a remark. Arguing to win rather than to understand. Impatience with people who learn or talk more slowly than you do. And a restless mind that can turn small irritations into running battles: in the car, in the queue, in the group chat.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write it down before you send it, and read it once as the person receiving it. Put the urge to debate where it belongs: a class, a club, a page of your own. Walk, cycle, or run the errands on foot, because this Mars thinks better moving. Listen to the end of a sentence before you answer it. Give the mind a daily drill, a language, a puzzle, an instrument, so the energy has a task of its own. And use the speed for good: the quick, clear word at the right moment is one of this placement's real gifts.
