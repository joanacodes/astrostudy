Pisces on the 10th house cusp sets the highest point of your chart, the Midheaven, in a sign that dislikes fixed outlines. The direction you take in the world tends to follow meaning more than rank, and people often see in you what they hope to find: a listener, a helper, an artist, a guide.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your public life starts at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the cusp of the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) in Placidus, the house system I use ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) there, ambition tends to attach to a purpose rather than a position. You may change direction more than once, and find that no job title quite describes what you do; you're often at your best where the work is larger than the job, in care, art, teaching, faith or service of some kind. Others project onto a Pisces Midheaven, and you may be seen as kinder, wiser or vaguer than you are. The care this cusp asks for is to name what you do plainly, take credit when it's due and decide how much of other people's hopes you'll carry.

Your public direction belongs to Neptune and Jupiter, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows where your vocation takes on vision, and where it can blur into doing whatever is asked; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where you grow into authority, through teaching, generosity or a wider view. Jupiter often runs your working days too: the 6th opens in Sagittarius in about 3 London charts in 4 and half the New York ones, so the job you do each day and the work you're known for share a ruler. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), neither Jupiter nor Neptune was one of the three placements that held far more often, while overall about a third held, so I read this Midheaven as one voice among several. Look at their houses for where your vocation draws its meaning, as ruler of the 10th in the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Latitude plays no part in which sign culminates, so a Pisces Midheaven comes with about 1 birth in 13 at London and New York alike, and Pisces crosses the meridian in a little under two hours. [Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/) comes with every London chart that has it and about 4 in 5 at New York, so the Moon rules the chart: a caring, receptive manner in front of a public life that's hard to define. Virgo always holds the IC below ([Virgo on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-4th-house-cusp/)), putting Mercury in charge of home. The 10th is wide here, about 33° to 39° at London, and in about 1 London chart in 4 the whole of Aries lies inside it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), adding Mars's push to a gentle reputation. Pisces misses the cusps entirely in about 1 London chart in 6 and 1 New York chart in 8.

I can't tell anyone which job to take or whether recognition will come; what this cusp gives is the kind of purpose that keeps you going. Pisces would have sat on the 11th cusp for an earlier birth that day ([Pisces on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-11th-house-cusp/)) and on the 9th for a later one ([Pisces on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/)), so the clock decides, not a guessed hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
