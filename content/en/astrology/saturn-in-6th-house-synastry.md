Someone's Saturn in your 6th house tends to show up in your diary before it shows up anywhere else. Lists get written, alarms get set, a habit you've promised yourself for years finally starts on a Monday, and the question of who keeps the schedule is never far away.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) keeps things on time and in order in a chart: method, limits, effort sustained past the point of novelty. [The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is where the week's work gets done: tasks and habits, the colleagues beside you, the everyday care you give your body. This [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) is scaffolding, put up around your days by someone else's Saturn.

With them, routines that never stuck can start to hold. You may find a working rhythm, a filing system, a weekly plan that survives contact with Wednesday, and some of their steadiness may rub off on the way you look after yourself: sleep kept, meals eaten, the walk actually taken.

For them, your daily round can look like a system they could help improve, and they may quietly take responsibility for it. Being useful matters to them here; your thanks, or its absence, registers. Cadent houses tune and maintain, and the 6th does it for the working week; [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/), careful and practical, is its natural sign, and tradition called it Bad Fortune and gave Mars its joy there. Should their Saturn lie at the very end of the house, it borders your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/): duty and partnership then share a fence, and I read the contact with that angle as well.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A planner can turn into a supervisor. You may feel your habits under review, your rest treated as slack, your own way of working corrected into theirs. Days together can grow so organised that nothing unplanned fits, and you may find yourself doing chores to their standard instead of yours.

On their side, the risk is carrying the whole timetable. Delegating may not come easily to them, and once they've built the structure they can feel taken for granted, or anxious that everything will slide if they let go of it.

Old texts filed illness under the 6th, and a Saturn there is easily turned into a warning. Health stays outside every reading I give, single or shared ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)): this overlay is about how you work and keep your days, and your health belongs with a doctor.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Build the routine together, and leave slack in it on purpose. Decide which tasks are shared, which are yours, and which can stay undone. In a couple, divide the household work in writing and revisit it each season; between friends, a shared habit, an early swim, a language exchange, a Saturday run, can last for years; between colleagues, this is often an exacting manager or teammate, at their best with clear expectations and room for your method; between adult relatives, it may be the one who organises everyone's errands, and needs telling when to stop.

In the other chart the picture turns over: your Saturn falls in their 6th house, you keep the clock of their days, and you're the one who must remember to rest. Just before it comes [their Saturn in your 5th house](/astrology/saturn-in-5th-house-synastry/), about play; just after, [their Saturn in your 7th house](/astrology/saturn-in-7th-house-synastry/), about commitment.

Your Descendant, where the 6th ends, moves as fast as your Ascendant, so in Placidus, the house system I use, a few minutes of your birth can carry that cusp past their Saturn. Their own time barely moves Saturn at all, though I ask for it along with yours ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Colleagues of one generation, a year or two apart, often have Saturn in the same sign; with your own Saturn in your 6th too ([Saturn in the 6th house](/astrology/saturn-in-6th-house/)), you may both have a work ethic that forgets to stop. Working together doesn't make a reading consensual; their agreement does ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
