The full moon in Aquarius comes at the height of the northern summer, with the Sun at home in Leo and the Moon opposite, in the sign of friends and groups. In August 2027 it is a penumbral lunar eclipse, two weeks after a total eclipse of the Sun in Leo.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A full Moon faces the Sun across the sky; this time it sits in [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, while the Sun crosses [Leo](/astrology/leo/), the sign it rules. The Moon brings Aquarius's needs up: room to be yourself among others, friends who don't crowd you, a cause bigger than you. The Sun in Leo keeps the month warm and personal: what you create, whom you love, how you want to be seen. It's the [full moon in Leo](/astrology/full-moon-in-leo/) turned round: there the lights swap places, here the Sun is at home and the Moon stands among the many.

What tends to show is where belonging and self-expression have pulled apart: a group that has asked you to dim, or a self that has forgotten its friends. Of Aquarius's two rulers, [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), I read both; the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo.

As ever, it's a marker of a few days. I count this [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) when the degree of either light coincides, within about 1°, with a planet or angle of your chart, and read it then as the high point of something a transit or progression has already begun. It isn't a forecast.

The most recent was on 29 July 2026, at 6°30′ Aquarius. The next, on 17 August 2027 at 07:29 UT, at 24°12′, is a penumbral [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/): the Moon only brushes the Earth's outer shadow, and it carries less weight in a chart than a total or partial one, though I still allow it about 3°. Then come 5 August 2028 at 13°26′, 25 July 2029 at 2°54′ and 13 August 2030 at 20°45′, all ordinary.

That eclipse follows the total solar eclipse of 2 August 2027, at the [new moon in Leo](/astrology/new-moon-in-leo/), so that summer both lights are eclipsed in turn on the same axis ([eclipse in Aquarius](/astrology/eclipse-in-aquarius/)). Even so, the natal point it touches, with its house, is all I read in it, and never as an omen. That new moon belongs to the same solar month every year. A month before, the Moon is full in Capricorn ([full moon in Capricorn](/astrology/full-moon-in-capricorn/)); a month after, in Pisces ([full moon in Pisces](/astrology/full-moon-in-pisces/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which two houses it lights is the birth time's business ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and I place houses in Placidus. Your Aquarius house takes the Moon; opposite, the house Leo opens takes the Sun.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Aquarius rising: your independence shows, cool and friendly, while a partner wants warmth and attention.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** money handled your own way, while shared resources ask for generosity.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** ideas traded freely with neighbours or siblings, while a belief you hold dear asks for passion.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** a home that runs on freedom, a shared house, an unusual arrangement, while your career wants you centre stage.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** a love with room to breathe, play shared with friends, while a group asks you to shine.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** a change in how work is done, new tools, a team effort, while a private wish to be appreciated stirs.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Aquarius on the Descendant: a partner who needs space, or is more friend than romance, while you want to be seen.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** shared money given to a cause, intimacy with independence, while your own values ask for pride and warmth.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** new ideas, science, a view of the world brought up to date, while everyday talk stays warm and personal.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Aquarius on the Midheaven: a public role as innovator or reformer, while home asks for warmth and loyalty.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** Aquarius's own house: friendships and groups at a peak, while a love or a creative project wants your heart.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a quiet need to step away from the crowd, while the working day asks you to take pride in your craft.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Both ends, and the eclipse.** A degree around each ordinary position above, in Aquarius or at the same degree of Leo; about three for the penumbral eclipse of 2027.
- **Belong without disappearing.** Aquarius asks who your people are, Leo how you want to be seen among them. This full moon goes well when one doesn't cancel the other.
- **Read the Sun, Uranus and Saturn together:** where you shine, where you change things, and where you build something that lasts.
