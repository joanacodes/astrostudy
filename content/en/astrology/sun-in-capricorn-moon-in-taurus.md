Capricorn asks how a thing gets built; Taurus asks how it lasts. With the Sun in Capricorn and the Moon in Taurus, both earth, you were born just after the half moon of the month's first week. I read the pair as a builder whose needs look after what has been built.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Taurus is the fourth sign on from Capricorn: a trine by sign between cardinal and fixed earth. The [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) sets out a structure and climbs it a stage at a time; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) feels safe when what it loves is kept up: the house maintained, the routines of each season repeated, the friendship tended for decades. One starts the building, the other keeps it standing, and both measure their days by what endures.

The Moon is in its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Taurus, which I read as needs that are clear, constant and hard to shake, a heavy anchor for a cardinal Sun. The Sun has no dignity in Capricorn. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), your Sun's ruler, and [Venus](/astrology/venus/), your Moon's, have none in each other's sign either: Saturn shows where you take on responsibility, Venus what you value and what restores you. When the two are in aspect in the chart, duty and pleasure tend to share one calendar rather than fight over it.

Your birth came just past the first quarter. The Moon had moved 91° to 148° ahead of the Sun, seven and a half to twelve days after the new moon, more than half lit, growing and up in the evening sky. The [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) of the eight phases takes about seven of these births in eight, the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) the rest, and around 120° the trine by sign is exact as a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). A first quarter birth, in my reading, is a time for committing to what has begun, which suits two signs that would rather finish than start over. Read more about first quarter births in [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/).

About 1 person in 148 has this pair. If the Moon left Aries or reached Gemini on your birthday, the hour you were born settles it between this page, the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-aries/) and the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-gemini/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same hour gives the rising sign and the houses of Saturn and Venus, which say where this building and keeping happen.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love in deeds that last: the home made, the promise kept, the anniversary remembered for thirty years. The Taurus Moon attaches slowly and wholly; the Capricorn Sun commits with a plan. Partners find you dependable and calm, sometimes to the point of not noticing how much you carry. What can go missing is lightness and surprise. A partner who brings some spontaneity, and whom you let pull you off the schedule now and then, keeps the steadiness from hardening.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that grows in value as it ages suits this pair: a craft mastered over years, land or buildings looked after, a business with loyal clients, an institution that has to outlast its founders. The Sun supplies the long plan and the authority; the Moon supplies patience, upkeep and a fine sense of what is worth keeping. Constant reorganisation wears you down. You do best where you can see this year's work still standing in ten years' time.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The trine is easy, and ease can set like concrete. Both signs prefer the known road, so a routine can outlive its purpose and a role can be kept long after it stopped fitting. Rest and pleasure, which the Venus-ruled Moon needs, can be postponed by a Sun that thinks they have to be earned first. What helps is scheduling change in small doses, a new route, a new skill, a short trip, and letting the Moon's need for pleasure count as a duty in its own right.
