About once in twelve years, Jupiter lands on your birth Uranus. The planet that widens things meets the part of you that wants room to live differently, and ideas, plans and restlessness all tend to arrive at once.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part of a chart that differentiates: it questions inherited ways, tries alternatives, reforms what has stopped working and wants the freedom to do so. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) makes room for whatever it meets and wants to know what it is for. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one, so for a while growth means change, and change feels like growth.

People often describe these weeks as a sudden clarity: the idea that had been circling lands, the experiment you'd postponed starts, the routine that had stopped fitting is suddenly impossible to keep. Jupiter also enlarges Uranus's blind spots. Novelty can pass for innovation, and disruption for progress, so the useful question is what still works before anything is changed.

Jupiter–Uranus contacts have a reputation for sudden luck and windfalls. I don't read them, or any [transit](/astrology/transits/), for money ([Can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). What they describe is pace and appetite for change.

Your Uranus sits within a few degrees of the Uranus of everyone born in the same year as you, so Jupiter reaches all of you within the same few months. What makes it yours is the house, and that comes from the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If Jupiter and Uranus sat together at your birth ([Jupiter conjunct Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus/)), each [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/) arrives with this transit close behind or just ahead.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Uranus likes speed, and a straight pass of Jupiter is the briefest of the transits I follow for timing: with orbs from 1½° before exact to 1° after, about eleven days. Roughly three times in ten Jupiter is near a station, crosses three times, and keeps the conjunction going for some eight months. Over eighty years, that makes six or seven conjunctions for anyone born between 1930 and 2009.

The first could come at any age from a few weeks to twelve, depending on where Jupiter stood that day: at about 3.6 for a birth on 1 January 1965, about 10.2 for 1 January 1985. Each later one comes about twelve years after the last.

From here Jupiter carries on round your Uranus. [Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-uranus/) and [transiting Jupiter trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-uranus/) give the new idea easy openings; [transiting Jupiter square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-uranus/) tests whether it survives routine; [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-uranus/), six years on, sets it against other people's plans. [Transiting Uranus conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) is the reverse, Uranus reaching your Jupiter: far slower, and far rarer, since Uranus takes about 84 years to go round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Try the new thing small before you try it big: one project run a different way, one month of the new schedule, a prototype rather than a resignation.

Keep one constant while everything else moves. Change lands better when something familiar holds still beside it.

And tell the people it touches. Uranus tends to read other people's slower pace as resistance; they usually just need more time than these eleven days to catch up.
