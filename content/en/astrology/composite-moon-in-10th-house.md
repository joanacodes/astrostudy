Some relationships have a public life of their own: a business run together, a reputation as the couple who host, a shared cause that other people associate with both names. A composite Moon in the 10th house places the bond's emotional needs where everyone can see them.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The relationship's [Moon](/astrology/moon/), halfway between your two natal Moons in the [composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), describes how it feels and what it needs; [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), angular and at the top of the chart, beginning at the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), is its public role: work, standing, responsibility, the reputation it builds over time. Capricorn is its natural sign. A bond with its Moon here feels secure when it has a place in the world and is seen to do well in it: a joint project respected, a role in a community, care that reaches beyond the two of you.

This is the relationship's public face, not either career. [Their Moon in your 10th house](/astrology/moon-in-10th-house-synastry/) would describe how someone touches your ambitions, and a [Moon in the 10th house](/astrology/moon-in-10th-house/) in one birth chart a person whose feelings show in public life. The composite's 10th is the role the pair plays together, and it often matters more than either of you expected: two private people can find their relationship known, asked for advice, or carrying a responsibility they took on side by side. Business partners, and couples who work together, may recognise it at once. Care here often travels outward: the pair looks after a team, a neighbourhood, a family business or a cause, and feels most like itself when that care is needed.

The difficulty is living for the audience. A relationship that feels safe when it is seen to be doing well can hide its troubles to protect its name, or let the joint project crowd out the private bond that started it. Achievement isn't status, and recognition isn't fame. The bond needs a private life as well as a public one, and permission to be ordinary when no one is watching.

Of all composite house placements, a Moon just past the Midheaven is the one the two methods of drawing houses are most likely to agree on. Whether the houses come from the midpoints of the two charts' cusps or are cast from the composite Midheaven for your home, the 10th begins at the midpoint of your two Midheavens; the methods part only later, at the cusp of the 11th. Each Midheaven depends on its own birth time, so the composite one needs both ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)).

The 10th sits between the [composite Moon in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-moon-in-9th-house/), at home on a wide horizon, and the [composite Moon in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-moon-in-11th-house/), at home among friends. Composites stay out of my own readings, so what you have here is the method as others practise it, alongside the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and each person's chart. A 10th-house composite Moon describes a need to matter in the world, not success, status or a career to come.
