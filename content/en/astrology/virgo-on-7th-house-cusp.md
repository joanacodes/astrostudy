Opposite the rising sign, where the chart turns towards other people, lies the Descendant, the cusp of the 7th house. Virgo on the 7th house cusp puts the sign of care and competence there: the person you pair up with tends to be someone who notices.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) shows what you look for, and often find, in the person across the table ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) there, it's competence and care: someone reliable, practical, attentive to the details of your life, who fixes the thing before you've finished describing it. I read the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) as a mirror too, and this one often reflects the precision you don't credit yourself with; beside a partner who sorts and checks, you discover you can do it as well. Love shows in what gets done for each other, the lift, the form, the soup when you're unwell, more than in declarations. Agreements, at home or at work, are best spelled out on both sides. The difficulty is criticism: from a partner who sees every flaw, or your own when the relationship doesn't run as it should. None of this names a person, or a date.

Every Virgo Descendant comes with [Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/): Neptune and Jupiter rule your chart, and I read both, while [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your partnerships. You meet the world through Pisces, open, imaginative, easily moved, and look across the chart for someone who brings edges and order. Mercury, ruler of your partners, has its detriment and its fall in Pisces, your rising sign: the exact, practical mind you look for in another is the one your Ascendant is least at home with, which is partly why you look for it. Sagittarius holds the Midheaven in every one of these charts, so Jupiter, one of your chart's two rulers, rules your public life as well. The house Mercury occupies shows where partnerships take shape, whether, as ruler of the 7th, it stands in the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). At London the 7th is wide in these charts, from about 48° to 58°, and in 9 charts in 10 it holds the whole of Libra too ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)): Venus, the 7th's natural ruler, adds courtesy and a wish for harmony to Virgo's practical care. Gemini, Mercury's other sign, opens your 4th in every London chart with this cusp, and often your 3rd as well, so the planet of your partnerships also rules your home.

No cusp holds Virgo less often than the Descendant. Drawn in Placidus, my house system, it turns up in roughly one London chart in 27 and one New York chart in 20. Pisces, opposite, is one of the two quickest signs to rise in the north, and Virgo sets as fast as Pisces rises: about 52 minutes a day at London, against nearly three hours to rise. From here Virgo never opens the 6th or the 8th as well, and in about 1 London birth in 6 it has no cusp anywhere.

Fifty-two minutes is less than the margin of a birth time noted without its minutes. [Virgo on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-8th-house-cusp/) describes a birth a little earlier, [Virgo on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-6th-house-cusp/) one a little later, and only the minute settles which is yours; without it I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
