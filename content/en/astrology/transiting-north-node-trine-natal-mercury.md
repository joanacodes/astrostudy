Some transits of the node feel like effort; the trine to Mercury tends to feel like understanding. For a month or so the node is 120° from your Mercury, most often from a sign of the same element, and the South Node sextiles it: new ideas and old knowledge are both on your side.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is your way of learning, linking and saying: it notices, analyses and turns what it finds into language. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to what you have practised least, and a [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets two functions coordinate easily while each keeps its own nature. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), ideas that point towards growth make sense quickly, and saying them aloud costs less than usual.

The catch is the one every trine carries: ease is not the same as doing. A subject can click in a week and still need the month of practice that turns understanding into skill. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), sextile your Mercury in the same weeks, offers the old shortcuts, which are useful as long as they don't replace the new route.

With your [Mercury in the 1st house](/astrology/mercury-in-1st-house/), where tradition gave Mercury its joy, the trine tends to show in how you speak for yourself: putting an idea forward as yours, explaining who you are without rehearsing it. Whether Mercury is in your 1st depends on your Ascendant, which needs the exact birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with Mercury retrograde, you may notice the trine first as thinking that falls into place privately, well before it reaches words; that inward rhythm is the subject of [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, 1½° on the way in and 1° on the way out, the transit usually takes about a month. Its length depends less on Mercury than on the Sun: the true node runs backwards fastest when the Sun is a quarter of the zodiac from the nodes, and nearly stops when the Sun reaches them in an eclipse season. A tenth of these trines are over in three and a half weeks; at the other end, a tenth last three and a half months or more. Three exact contacts are rare, about one pass in twenty-five. Neither Mercury nor the Sun decides whether it appears in my forecast: the node goes in only by conjunction or opposition, so this trine never does.

The trines fall a third of the zodiac from your Mercury on either side, so the node meets one on its way out to the opposition ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-mercury/)) and the other on its way back towards the conjunction ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-mercury/)), with [Transiting North Node sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-mercury/) and [Transiting North Node square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-mercury/) nearer the conjunction on both sides. Most people have nine of these trines by 80, nobody fewer than eight. If you were born with [Mercury trine North Node](/astrology/mercury-trine-north-node/), your nodal return is itself one of them.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Learn the hard part now. If a subject has always looked forbidding, this is the month when it is most likely to open.

Teach someone what you've just understood. Explaining it is what fixes it.

Turn a good idea into a routine within the month: a page a day, a lesson a week.
