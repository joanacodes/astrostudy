Some relationships are held together by the ordinary: who makes the coffee, which day is for shopping, the shared calendar on the fridge, the way you divide the week's work without discussing it. A composite Moon in the 6th house finds the bond's comfort in those routines.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The [composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) places its [Moon](/astrology/moon/) halfway between your two natal Moons, and [the 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is where this one does its living: daily work, routines, service, the upkeep of a shared life and the care of the body. The house is cadent, with Virgo as its natural sign; tradition called it Bad Fortune and gave Mars its joy there. With the Moon here, the relationship feels safe when its days run well, and it shows care through small, useful acts: the lunch packed, the errand done, the appointment remembered.

These are the relationship's own routines, not either person's habits. A [Moon in the 6th house](/astrology/moon-in-6th-house/) of one birth chart describes how one person steadies through routine; [their Moon in your 6th house](/astrology/moon-in-6th-house-synastry/), how someone settles into your working day. The composite 6th is the daily rhythm two people build together, which is why this Moon is so often recognisable between colleagues, business partners or housemates: a working pair at its most relaxed when the tasks are shared out and the week has a shape.

Its difficulty is that a bond made of routines can turn into a bond of chores. The relationship may talk only about logistics, show love only by doing things, or keep score of who did what. Service isn't servitude, and discipline isn't perfectionism. It helps to leave room in the week for something with no use at all, and to say thank you for the small acts this Moon tends to take for granted.

Tradition also linked the 6th with illness. A composite doesn't describe anyone's health, and no chart predicts illness ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); what this Moon describes is how a relationship looks after its everyday life, the shared habits of rest and work included.

The size of this house depends on how it is drawn. With the midpoints of the two charts' cusps, each composite house spans the average of the same house in your two charts, so a wide 6th in one Placidus chart and a narrow one in the other give a middling composite 6th. Built from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, the house takes whatever size that place gives it. Either way, both birth times decide where it starts and ends ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)).

On one side, the [composite Moon in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-moon-in-5th-house/) refuels through play; on the other, the [composite Moon in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-moon-in-7th-house/) through the way the pair meets other people. Composites aren't a chart I draw; this is the technique as astrologers use it, beside the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and the two natal charts, and it describes the rhythm of a shared life, not how that life will go.
