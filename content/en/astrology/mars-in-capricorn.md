Mars in Capricorn climbs. It doesn't sprint up the mountain or argue about the route: it picks a path, sets a pace it can keep for years, and arrives, often after the people who started faster have turned back.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is effort, initiative and the will to win. [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), Saturn's cardinal earth sign, stands for structure, ambition and the long term. Tradition calls this Mars's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), the sign where its effort is said to be at its best, because here it is disciplined and aimed. I read it as drive with a plan. You tend to ration your energy, spend it where it counts, and keep going when others give up, less from stubbornness than because you worked out long ago that the goal was worth it. Its fall, Cancer, sits opposite: there Mars wraps its effort round a centre, here it points it at a summit.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), Capricorn's ruler, is the planet I look at next, because this Mars works on Saturn's terms: responsibility, timing, the long game. Saturn is also among [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) most often in my notebook, 75 to 90% of the time; Mars itself, checked like every other placement, was not. So in a chart with Mars in Capricorn I spend real time on where Saturn sits and what it asks.

Mars passes through Capricorn fairly quickly, in about 40 days as a rule, though a retrograde there can stretch the stay to 195. In the 1930–2009 count, it comes to about 1 person in 14. A whole season of births shares the sign; the house shows which mountain is yours. [Mars in Sagittarius](/astrology/mars-in-sagittarius/), before it, runs on enthusiasm; [Mars in Aquarius](/astrology/mars-in-aquarius/), after it, works for the group and the future.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, Mars in Capricorn is steady and serious. You show desire through reliability: turning up, keeping promises, building something together that will last. You may be slow to show what you feel, and private about it, but your commitment, once given, is solid. Respect matters as much as attraction: you're drawn to people who are good at something and take it seriously. Partners sometimes wish for more spontaneity, or for less time at work. The lesson is that a relationship isn't one more project to manage well; it needs play too, and a few evenings with nothing planned.

## At work {#at-work}

Work is where this Mars feels most at home. Management, strategy, building an organisation, any long project with real responsibility suits it. You're good at planning backwards from a goal, patient with slow results, and inclined to lead by example rather than by noise. Authority tends to come with time, often because people notice you're the one who finishes things. What you dislike is wasted effort: disorganisation, empty meetings, plans that change for no reason.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Everything can turn into work. Feelings get filed under *later*, rest has to be earned, and your worth starts to feel like the sum of what you've achieved. You can be hard on yourself and, by extension, on anyone who doesn't share your discipline. And with both the brake and the accelerator under such careful control, you may hold back so long that the moment passes.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Schedule play as firmly as work, and keep the appointment. Let a few goals be purely for joy, with nothing to prove. Delegate one task you'd normally keep, and let it be done differently. Treat feelings as useful information rather than as interruptions. Choose exercise with a long arc, a training plan, a hill walk, a slow build of strength, so your body gets the same patient effort as your work. And mark the milestones when you reach them: this Mars climbs better when it stops now and then to look at the view.
