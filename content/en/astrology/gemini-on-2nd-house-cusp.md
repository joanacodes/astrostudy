With Gemini on the 2nd house cusp, the sign of exchange opens the house of what you own and what you feel you're worth. A resource, here, is as likely to be a skill, a phone number or something you read last week as a sum in the bank.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read the sign on a cusp as the way its house does business ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) does the business of the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) by trading. Worth grows when it circulates: what you learn can be sold, taught or swapped, and a good contact can feel as solid as savings. You compare before you pay and enjoy finding out what a thing is really worth. Income tends to come through more than one door, a main job and a sideline, a skill you use and another you teach. Self-worth rests on your mind more than on your belongings: you feel secure when you understand how things work, your own accounts included. Taurus, the 2nd's natural sign, keeps and accumulates; Gemini prefers movement, and money left untouched for years can start to feel like money doing nothing.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), Gemini's ruler, rules your 2nd house with this cusp. Find the house it occupies and you find the part of life your money runs through, whether that's the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/); its sign shows how you think about money. Mercury rules a second sign, Virgo, and with this cusp Virgo usually opens the 6th: in about 2 London charts in 3, and half of those drawn for New York. Your earnings and your working day then answer to one planet, so I read the two together. In most of the remaining London charts Virgo lies wholly inside a house, an [intercepted sign](/astrology/intercepted-signs/); at New York it often opens the 5th instead.

Counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use, over a whole day of births, Gemini opens the 2nd for about 1 birth in 13 at London's latitude and 1 in 12 at New York's: a little under two hours of each day. Gemini rises quickly north of the equator, the cusps just below the horizon cross it at almost the same pace, and the further north you go, the faster that is. [Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/) usually comes with it, about 3 times in 5 at London and nearly 3 in 4 at New York, so Venus, the 2nd house's own planet by nature, rules your chart while Mercury handles your resources. In about 1 London chart in 4, Gemini rises as well ([Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/)), and one planet then governs both your manner and your money ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). In about 1 in 3 it runs on into the next cusp, and [Gemini on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/) describes your chart too. And about 1 London birth in 8 has Gemini on no cusp at all.

Which of these is yours depends on the minute you were born: a little earlier puts Gemini on the 3rd cusp, a little later on the Ascendant, so I settle the time before I read anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). What this cusp won't give you is a figure. I read the 2nd for habits and needs, never for amounts, and a chart can't tell you which shares to buy ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).
