Everyday words, the street you know, the people you grew up beside: with Taurus on the 3rd house cusp, the house of the near and the daily opens in the sign that makes things last.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) on the [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) cusp sets the pace of your everyday mind ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and the pace is one of building. You tend to learn by practice and repetition, coming back to a subject until it's yours, and what you learn that way stays learned. Words are chosen to last: you'd rather say a thing once and stand by it than take it back later. The local matters to you more than to most: the same café, the same route to work, neighbours you've known for years, messages kept up with the same few people. Short journeys follow the same pattern: you'd rather know a few walks well than keep collecting new ones, and the commute you've done a thousand times is where your best thinking gets done. What helps is a little variety on purpose, one new route a month, so the familiar ones don't go stale. Where Gemini, the sign that belongs to the 3rd by nature, would sample a little of everything, Taurus goes deep into less. If you grew up with brothers, sisters or cousins, the 3rd describes your experience of those ties rather than the people themselves, and with Taurus it's often a long one: ties kept up over decades, or a distance that takes time to close.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your 3rd, and I read it as the planet of valuing and of what you're drawn to: here it turns everyday talk into something you take pleasure in, a well-made sentence, a book reread, a conversation that goes back and forth. Libra, Venus's other sign, usually opens your 8th with this cusp, so Venus rules that house too, and the hard conversations about money held in common, debts or trust often fall to you, because you can have them without turning them into a contest. Where your everyday mind likes to settle depends on the house Venus is in, from the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/) through the [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) to the [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

About 1 birth in 14 at London's latitude has this cusp, and 1 in 13 at New York's, in Placidus, the house system I use: a little commoner than on the 2nd ([Taurus on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-2nd-house-cusp/)), which lies closer to the Ascendant. Aquarius rises with it in about two charts in three, so the first impression is often cool and independent, and the steadiness shows once people learn how you think. In about 1 London chart in 5, Taurus runs on to open the IC as well ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); Venus then rules both your everyday mind and your home, and [Taurus on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/) is yours too. A sign can also lie wholly inside a house with no cusp of its own ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and Taurus does so in about 1 London birth in 7, though in only 1 New York birth in 11.

The cusp is worked out from the birth time and place, so it can't be read without them, and that's why I ask for the time before anything else ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
