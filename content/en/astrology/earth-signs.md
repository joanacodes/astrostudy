A promise means little to earth until it has seen the plans, handled the material and watched the thing work. Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn each carry that wish for proof in their own way.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Earth's signs, [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) and [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), come every fourth sign round the zodiac, a [trine](/astrology/trine/) apart. Earth's way is practical and patient: it works with what's real, looks after the body and the details, and builds towards what lasts. Where fire wants to start, earth wants to know whether the thing will hold.

Each of the three takes a different [modality](/astrology/three-modalities/):

- **Taurus**, fixed, keeps. The Sun is there from about 20 April to 20 May: settled ground, and what's worth having and holding on to.
- **Virgo**, mutable, refines. About 23 August to 22 September, as the harvest comes in: sorting, mending, making useful.
- **Capricorn**, cardinal, builds. About 22 December to 19 January, from the December solstice: structure, responsibility, the long view.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Earth holds more exaltations than any other element. Of the seven traditional ones, three fall in earth signs: the Moon in Taurus, Mercury in Virgo and Mars in Capricorn ([exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/)). I read them as feeling steadied, thought sharpened and drive made patient. Two planets have their fall here as well, Venus in Virgo and Jupiter in Capricorn: love asked to be practical, growth asked to be earned.

The element's rulers are Venus, Mercury and Saturn, and no earth sign gained a modern ruler. Its triplicity rulers, in Dorotheus's scheme, are Venus by day, the Moon by night and Mars as their partner, the very three Ptolemy counts as night planets; earth, like water, sits in the feminine, receptive half of the zodiac ([triplicity rulers](/astrology/triplicity-rulers/)).

In the houses, earth's natural places are the [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house/) and [10th](/astrology/10th-house/): what you have and value, the work of an ordinary day, and what you build in public. Together they make up the practical side of a life, the part people tend to see before the rest.

In the zodiac's order, every earth sign comes between a fire sign and an air sign: Aries, Taurus, Gemini; Leo, Virgo, Libra; Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius. What the [fire signs](/astrology/fire-signs/) set going, earth gives a body, before the [air signs](/astrology/air-signs/) turn it into ideas and connections.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

Planets in earth signs show where you go slowly and get there. Several of them, or an earth Sun, Moon or rising sign, often go with a steady, practical temperament other people lean on. No planets in earth isn't a lack of common sense: it can mean structure is something you reach for on purpose, through habits and other people, rather than ground you stand on ([element balance and missing elements](/astrology/element-balance-and-missing-elements/)).

Capricorn belongs to Saturn, and Saturn, with Chiron and Lilith, is what held 75 to 90% of the time when I tested charts in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/). So wherever Saturn sits, by sign and house, gets the closest reading I give. And because earth's houses touch money and work, I say plainly that I read how you handle both, never what you'll earn ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Earth signs are boring.”** Earth is steady, which isn't the same as dull. Pleasure, craft and the body belong to it as much as duty does.
- **“Earth signs are materialistic.”** Earth values what's real. Wanting things to be solid, useful or beautiful isn't greed, and Venus, ruler of Taurus, is about what you value, not what you buy.
- **“Earth signs don't feel much.”** They often show feeling through what they do: turning up, fixing, staying. The Moon is exalted in Taurus.
- **“Earth signs resist change.”** Capricorn is cardinal and starts things; Virgo is mutable and adjusts all the time. Only Taurus is fixed, and even Taurus changes, once it's sure.
