Venus conjunct Uranus sets your taste right on top of your urge to change things. The sign is your generation's, since Uranus lingers some seven years in each; the conjunction is yours, because Venus only passes Uranus for a short spell each year.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) evaluates and attracts: what you find beautiful, whom you're drawn to, what feels fair between people. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees: it questions habit, tries the untried, and loosens what has grown too tight. Fused by a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), they act together while each stays itself, so what you like and what you want to change arrive in the same moment.

In plain words, your taste tends to run ahead of the crowd, or simply off to one side of it. You may be drawn to people who surprise you, to friendships that ignore the usual categories, to a kind of beauty nobody else has noticed yet. Attraction can arrive all at once, like a switch. And you need room: love that leaves space for each person to stay themselves feels like love; love that shuts the doors feels like a cage, however kind. Autonomy isn't isolation, though, and the conjunction's real work is learning to stay close while staying free.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Because Uranus moves so slowly, the conjunction falls in your generation's Uranus sign, and the house tells you where it lives: in the 7th, partnerships that work best with room in them; in the 2nd, values that ignore the usual price list; in the 5th, romance and creativity that like to experiment. Uranus is the modern ruler of Aquarius, and there the conjunction speaks most plainly, with friendship and freedom as forms of love; in Libra or Taurus, Venus's signs, the reform tends to reach the shape of relationships themselves.

About 1 person in 29 is born with Venus and Uranus within 6° of a conjunction. The closer the meeting, the more the need for freedom shows in love itself; at 5° or 6°, it's more a taste for the unusual. Uranus is retrograde for about five months a year, which means many people with this conjunction have Uranus retrograde as well; if you were, the urge to change tends to work quietly on your own values before it shows ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

How the pair's other angles compare:

- **[Venus sextile Uranus](/astrology/venus-sextile-uranus/):** openings for the new in how you love, through friends, groups and ideas, taken when you choose.
- **[Venus square Uranus](/astrology/venus-square-uranus/):** closeness and freedom at right angles; relationships that learn to make room.
- **[Venus trine Uranus](/astrology/venus-trine-uranus/):** originality and affection in one element; change in love that rarely feels like upheaval.
- **[Venus opposite Uranus](/astrology/venus-opposite-uranus/):** togetherness at one end, room at the other; a balance often negotiated with a partner.

Between two charts, [Venus conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) drops one person's Uranus onto the other's Venus, the contact astrologers describe as a spark: sudden attraction, and both people needing space. Its current rarely runs at the same strength in both directions, so I read each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), [Transiting Uranus conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-venus/) comes once in Uranus's round of about 84 years, at an age set by how far your Venus sits from your Uranus, and lasts about a year in all, usually in three passes. Born with the conjunction, you meet its echoes instead: around 21, Uranus squares its own birth place and your Venus in the same stretch, and around 42, at the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), it opposes both.

Weather, not a forecast: seasons when love and taste ask for more air, never an instruction to leave anything or anyone.
