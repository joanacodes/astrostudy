The sextile is the quietest of the major aspects: two planets a sixth of the circle apart, close enough to talk, far enough apart to stay themselves. It describes help that's there for the taking, and more than any other aspect, it waits for you to take it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

A sextile is an angle of 60°: two signs apart. Count two signs on from Aries and you reach Gemini; count two back and you reach Aquarius. All the way round the zodiac, that pairs fire with air and earth with water, elements that get on without being alike: air feeds fire, water makes earth fertile. The two signs also share a [polarity](/astrology/polarity/), both active or both receptive, which is part of why the contact feels friendly.

I read a sextile as an open door. It doesn't push like a square, or flow on its own like a trine; it offers. Two planets in sextile can help each other, the mind steadying the heart, the drive serving the patience, but usually only once you notice the link and use it. That makes it the easiest help in a chart to overlook.

Astrologers allow a few degrees either side of exact, more with the Sun and Moon (the page on [orbs](/astrology/orbs/) has the detail), and many read an aspect that is still forming differently from one already past exact: [applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/). Near the end of a sign, a sextile can fall between signs one or three apart rather than two, and then the elements no longer match so neatly: an [out-of-sign aspect](/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/).

Among the five major aspects, the sextile is the smallest angle of Ptolemy's four, with the [square](/astrology/square/), the [trine](/astrology/trine/) and the [opposition](/astrology/opposition/); the [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) is a meeting rather than one of his four. With the trine, it's one of the two [soft aspects](/astrology/hard-and-soft-aspects/), and the lighter of the two: the trine joins signs of the same element, the sextile signs whose elements complement each other. The square, at 90°, is the next step round, and the mood changes completely.

## Between which planets {#between-which-planets}

The Sun never makes a sextile with Mercury or Venus: Mercury never strays more than 28° from it, Venus never more than 47°. The two of them can make one with each other, but only from either side of the Sun: [Mercury sextile Venus](/astrology/mercury-sextile-venus/). These are the sextiles people look up most:

- **[Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/):** born a few days from a new moon; what you want and what you need talk easily.
- **[Venus sextile Mars](/astrology/venus-sextile-mars/):** affection and desire cooperate; a warmth that knows how to take the first step.
- **[Moon sextile Saturn](/astrology/moon-sextile-saturn/):** feelings with a steady frame; you can comfort yourself without shutting down.
- **[Sun sextile Jupiter](/astrology/sun-sextile-jupiter/):** confidence that finds its openings; an optimism you can actually use.
- **[Mercury sextile Saturn](/astrology/mercury-sextile-saturn/):** a practical, patient mind; ideas taken all the way to the end.
- **[Moon sextile Venus](/astrology/moon-sextile-venus/):** affection that comes naturally; a gift for making people feel at home.
- **[Venus sextile Saturn](/astrology/venus-sextile-saturn/):** love that grows steadily; loyalty without the heaviness.
- **[Sun sextile Pluto](/astrology/sun-sextile-pluto/):** a quiet strength; the ability to start again when something ends.
- **[Moon sextile Chiron](/astrology/moon-sextile-chiron/):** an old hurt you can talk about; often, a gentle way with other people's.

[My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) counted placements, each planet in its sign and house, so I have no figure of my own for sextiles. I'd still underline the Saturn lines: Saturn was one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time, and a sextile is where its patience is easiest to put to use.

## The sextile in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), a sextile between two charts is a friendly contact rather than a spark. I don't read it as what draws two people together; I read it as what makes the everyday easier: conversations that flow, plans that fit, help offered without fuss. [One person's Sun sextile the other's Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon-synastry/) tends to feel like support given and received without effort; [Venus sextile Mars](/astrology/venus-sextile-mars-synastry/) adds warmth and an easy kind of attraction. Between two charts, any planets can meet at any angle, so your Sun can sextile someone's Venus, which no single chart allows: that's [Sun sextile Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-venus-synastry/). As always, I read it in both directions, and next to everything else: a sextile softens a relationship; it doesn't settle anything on its own.

## The sextile in transits {#in-transits}

A sextile [transit](/astrology/transits/) is a window. A planet in today's sky reaches 60° from one of yours and, for a while, something in that part of life becomes easier to do. Nothing happens on its own, which is why sextile transits are easy to miss; but if you were waiting for the right moment to ask, start or mend, this is the weather for it. [Transiting Saturn sextile your natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-sun/) reads as a good season for steady effort; [Jupiter sextile your natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-venus/), for warmth and pleasure taken without guilt; [Uranus sextile your natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-sun/), for a change you choose rather than one forced on you. The fast planets pass in hours or days; the slow ones can stay in orb for months, and cross the same degree up to three times as they turn retrograde. Weather, not a forecast: a sextile shows you what's easier, not what will happen.
