Mercury is the smallest of the planets, and from Earth it never seems to wander far from the Sun. It carries the name of the Roman messenger god, and astrologers read it as the messenger in you: the way you notice, think, and say what you think.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

I read Mercury as the way your mind moves, not as a measure of how clever you are. It answers *how do you think?* rather than *how much?* — the speed of it, the order of it, what you notice first and how you put it into words.

- **Its sign** shows the style of your thinking: quick, careful, blunt, dreamy.
- **Its house** shows the subjects your mind keeps returning to.
- **Its aspects** show whether thoughts turn into words easily, and what colours them on the way.

Mercury stays close to the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), never more than about 28° away, so it sits in your Sun sign or in the sign just before or after it: purpose and mind are read together. With the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), it shows how easily you can name what you feel; with [Venus](/astrology/venus/), how gently you say it. It rules [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) and [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) — the curious mind and the careful one — and Virgo is also its exaltation, which makes Mercury the only traditional planet exalted in a sign it rules. It is least at ease in [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) and [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), where thinking runs on big pictures and intuition more than on detail: less tidy, not less wise.

Its aspects fill in the detail. Because Mercury stays so close to the Sun, the only major aspect the two can make is the conjunction — [Sun conjunct Mercury](/astrology/sun-conjunct-mercury/): mind and purpose in the same place. A [Moon square Mercury](/astrology/moon-square-mercury/) often describes head and heart arguing before they agree. A [Mercury square Saturn](/astrology/mercury-square-saturn/) tends to choose its words carefully, sometimes for fear of getting them wrong. And Mercury and Venus never stray far apart either, so between them only the conjunction and the sextile are possible; [Mercury conjunct Venus](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-venus/) gives a gift for saying things kindly.

## Mercury through the signs {#through-the-signs}

Mercury usually spends two to three weeks in each sign, longer when it turns retrograde, so it's shared by people born within a few weeks of each other; its house and its aspects make it yours. In plain words:

- **[Mercury in Aries](/astrology/mercury-in-aries/):** you think fast, speak first and decide on the spot.
- **[Mercury in Taurus](/astrology/mercury-in-taurus/):** you think slowly and thoroughly, and change your mind only for a good reason.
- **[Mercury in Gemini](/astrology/mercury-in-gemini/):** a quick, curious mind that loves to connect ideas and people; Mercury's own sign.
- **[Mercury in Cancer](/astrology/mercury-in-cancer/):** you think through memory and feeling, and remember how things were said.
- **[Mercury in Leo](/astrology/mercury-in-leo/):** you speak with warmth and conviction, and enjoy a story well told.
- **[Mercury in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-in-virgo/):** a precise, practical mind, at home with detail; its own sign and its exaltation.
- **[Mercury in Libra](/astrology/mercury-in-libra/):** you weigh both sides and look for the fair, tactful word.
- **[Mercury in Scorpio](/astrology/mercury-in-scorpio/):** you look for what's under the surface, and keep your own counsel.
- **[Mercury in Sagittarius](/astrology/mercury-in-sagittarius/):** you think in big pictures and speak frankly, sometimes before checking the details.
- **[Mercury in Capricorn](/astrology/mercury-in-capricorn/):** you think in plans and steps, and say what's useful.
- **[Mercury in Aquarius](/astrology/mercury-in-aquarius/):** an independent mind that enjoys the idea nobody has had yet.
- **[Mercury in Pisces](/astrology/mercury-in-pisces/):** you think in images and impressions, and understand more than you can explain.

## Mercury in the houses {#in-the-houses}

Its house shows what your mind keeps busy with. Tradition gave Mercury its joy in the 1st house, where a lively mind is the first thing people meet.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/mercury-in-1st-house/):** Mercury's joy: people notice your curiosity and your questions first.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/mercury-in-2nd-house/):** you think practically about money, skills and what things are worth.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/mercury-in-3rd-house/):** talk, writing, short journeys and siblings keep your mind busy.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/mercury-in-4th-house/):** home is a place of conversation; you think best in private.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/mercury-in-5th-house/):** a playful mind: games, stories, flirting with words.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/mercury-in-6th-house/):** your mind likes a job to do: lists, methods, fixing what isn't working.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/mercury-in-7th-house/):** you think best in dialogue, and need someone close you can really talk to.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/mercury-in-8th-house/):** a mind drawn to research, secrets and the questions people avoid.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/mercury-in-9th-house/):** study, languages, travel and big questions feed you.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/mercury-in-10th-house/):** your voice and your ideas are part of your working life.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/mercury-in-11th-house/):** you think out loud with friends, networks and groups.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/mercury-in-12th-house/):** a quiet, inward mind; you understand more than you say.

## Mercury in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), Mercury shows how two people talk: whether conversation flows or knots, and how you find your way back to it after a disagreement. When their Mercury falls in your 7th house — [Their Mercury in your 7th house](/astrology/mercury-in-7th-house-synastry/) — talking tends to sit at the heart of the bond. One person's Mercury on the other's Venus — [Mercury conjunct Venus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-venus-synastry/) — often brings kind words and easy compliments; on the other's Mars, debates that spark or sting. I read Mercury contacts in both directions: the one who explains and the one who listens can swap places.

## Transits and Mercury retrograde {#mercury-retrograde}

Mercury moves quickly, so its transits are short: a day or two of thinking, talking or writing about whatever it touches in your chart. Three or four times a year, for about three weeks, it seems to move backwards from where we stand on Earth. That's [Mercury retrograde](/astrology/mercury-retrograde/), and the [Mercury retrograde dates](/sky/mercury-retrograde-dates/) have their own page. Astrologers read those weeks as a time for reviewing, re-reading and going back over plans — a description of the weather, not a forecast.

Being born during one is common, and in a birth chart it often describes a mind that works inward first; I wrote about that rhythm in [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/). When a slow planet crosses your Mercury — [Transiting Saturn conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-mercury/), say — the season is longer, and it tends to ask you to think more slowly and say less, but better.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

Mercury is one of the placements everyone reads first, and in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) it held about a third of the time — 30 to 40%, with the Sun sign, the rising sign and Venus. That's close to what a flattering, vague horoscope scores. It isn't nothing, and I still read Mercury in every chart. But [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) held 75 to 90% of the time, so that's where I spend the most time, and when part of a reading rests on Mercury, I tell you, so you can weigh it for yourself. It's a notebook, not a study: a count by hand, mostly on people I knew.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Mercury retrograde breaks everything.”** No planet turns back; Mercury only seems to, from Earth, three or four times a year. A transit describes weather, not events, and whether those weeks really go wrong more often is a question with its own page: [Does Mercury retrograde break things?](/astrology/does-mercury-retrograde-break-things/)
- **“Mercury shows how intelligent you are.”** It shows how you think, not how much. A slow, careful Mercury and a quick one can reach the same answer by different roads.
- **“Your Mercury is always in your Sun sign.”** Often, not always: it can be in the sign just before or just after. It's worth checking, because it changes how your mind and your purpose work together.
- **“Mercury in Pisces can't think straight.”** Tradition calls Pisces both its detriment and its fall. It describes a mind that works through images and intuition rather than lists — another kind of clarity.
