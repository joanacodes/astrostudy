Nearly everyone meets this square once, and at almost any age: in the first months of life for some, past 70 for others. For about two and a half years, the beliefs that give your life its shape and the longings that reach beyond any shape rub against each other.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is how you make sense of things: the principles you live by, the beliefs that give experience a meaning, the confidence to grow. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves fixed forms and reaches for an ideal. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates two functions through friction that asks you to adapt, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your sense of meaning is asked to stretch without coming apart.

Certainties can soften. A teaching, a philosophy or a cause that seemed to explain everything may start to look partial; a plan may grow faster than the ground under it; you may be tempted to put your faith in whatever promises most. Neptune's blind spot is taking a symbol for a prediction, and Jupiter's is taking a belief for the whole truth, so this is the transit when I'm most careful with any system that promises answers, astrology included. The friction is constructive: what remains after it is usually a smaller set of beliefs you can actually stand on.

I don't read it for money lost, ventures failed or anything else a transit can't foretell.

With Jupiter in your 10th, the square often comes while Neptune crosses your 1st or your 7th: the way you see yourself ([Neptune transiting the 1st house](/astrology/neptune-transit-1st-house/)), or the way you see a partner, goes soft just as your public aims ask for confidence. Those houses can only be drawn from a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Jupiter square Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-square-neptune/), as at the start of 1955, you had this transit in your first months, the natal aspect itself slowly loosening; it doesn't come back before 80.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune keeps the square for about two years and seven months within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, and twenty-two months in the quickest passes, touching the exact point three times as a rule and five in about one pass in four: long enough to change your mind about something that matters, and to be sure you have.

Almost everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets it before 80, at an age their chart decides: in the first months for the start of 1955, at about 1 for 1975, 18 for 1995, 37 for 1945, 39 for 1965, 49 for 1985, 67½ for 2005 and 70 for 1935.

Someone born at the start of 1965 meets every one of Neptune's contacts with Jupiter before 80, which is rare: [transiting Neptune opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-jupiter/) in the first months, [transiting Neptune trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-jupiter/) at about 26, this square at about 39, [transiting Neptune sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-jupiter/) at about 53 and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) at nearly 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you believe, then what you know. The gap between the two lists is what this square is working on.

Let a plan prove its footing before it grows: one stage at a time, each paid for and finished before the next.

Keep the faith and lose the certainty. You can go on believing in something you love while admitting you could be wrong about it.
