Pluto feels at home in the 8th house, and a composite with it there puts the relationship's centre of gravity below the surface: in trust, intimacy, money held in common, and the crises the two of you come through together.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) holds what the pair merges and what it owes: intimacy, sexual and emotional; the joint account and the mortgage; promises, secrets and debts; the hard times that test a bond. Its natural sign is Scorpio, the modern home of [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), so planet and house speak the same language. Astrologers read a bond that transforms by going through things together and coming out changed: a loss grieved side by side, a debt cleared over years, a betrayal faced and, sometimes, repaired, a confession that ends one version of the relationship and begins a truer one.

Natally, [Pluto in the 8th house](/astrology/pluto-in-8th-house/) is how one person handles closeness and crisis; across two charts, [their Pluto in your 8th house](/astrology/pluto-in-8th-house-synastry/) is one person's depth stirring another's sense of trust. The composite's 8th belongs to both and to neither: two private, guarded people may tell each other what they have never told anyone, and the secrets they hold in common can bind them more than anything they own.

The trap is mistaking secrecy for depth. Discretion can harden into proof: the relationship comes to feel that its intimacy is measured by what it hides, from friends, from family, even from parts of each other, and to treat every closed door as a sign of seriousness. Privacy keeps a relationship safe; secrecy keeps it small. The antidote is some deliberate openness, finances both can see, a friend who knows you as a couple, and the willingness to let a crisis end once it's over.

The 8th was once the house of death and legacies. No placement foretells anyone's death or what they'll leave behind, and I don't read charts for either ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); in a composite, the 8th is the trust two people build and what they hold together. If intimacy is ever demanded rather than offered, or money or secrets are used as leverage, depth has nothing to do with it and the chart can't help: 999 for the police or an ambulance, Shout answers texts at any hour on 85258, and my Disclaimers page has the other lines ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)).

Asking someone for their exact birth time is a small act of trust, and this house needs two of them: without both times there is no 8th, and with them its cusp still depends on whether it's taken from the midpoints of your two charts' cusps or cast from the composite Midheaven for where you live ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Draw it only when both of you want to look this deep: the 8th is the last place to enter uninvited ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Its neighbours move outwards, [composite Pluto in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-7th-house/) into the partnership's terms and [composite Pluto in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-9th-house/) towards a wider horizon; in the 8th, the movement is downwards.
