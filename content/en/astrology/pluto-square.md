Of all the slow planets' turns in a life, the Pluto square is the one with no fixed age. Depending on the year you were born, it came, or will come, anywhere from about 35½ to nearly 60.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) takes about 248 years to go round the Sun, and its orbit is so stretched that it spends anywhere from 12 to 31 years in a sign. The Pluto square is transiting Pluto 90° on from the degree it held at your birth, a [square](/astrology/square/) to its own natal place. Because the quarter circle can run through fast signs or slow ones, the time it takes changes from one generation to the next more than for any other life transit. In the site's count for births from 1930 to 2009, the first exact square fell between 35.5 and 59.3 in all 160 birth dates computed.

Births on 1 January of every fifth year show the swing:

- **1930 to 1950:** from about 49 down to about 41. Born in 1930, you met it at 48.9; in 1940, at 44.8; in 1950, at 40.9.
- **1955 to 1980:** the youngest, between about 37 and 39. Born in 1965 or 1970, at 37; those born in July of the mid-1960s, at 35.5.
- **1985 to 2005:** later and later. Born in 1985, at 41.1; in 1990, at 44.2; in 2000, at 51.3; in 2005, at 55.4.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

With the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, a Pluto transit usually stays in orb two to three years, and sometimes nearly four ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). The square most often comes in three exact passes; when a station falls on the degree there are five, as for births on 1 January 1975, 1995, 2000 and 2005.

Your natal Pluto sign is shared with everyone born over twelve to thirty-one years ([generational placements by birth year](/astrology/generational-placements-by-birth-year/)), and so is the sky of the square. Most people born between November 1983 and November 1995, with [Pluto in Scorpio](/astrology/pluto-in-scorpio/), meet theirs while Pluto crosses Aquarius, until 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)): born on 1 January 1985, at 41.1, 41.6 and 42, in 2026 and the first days of 2027; born in 1990, in 2034. Those born with Pluto in Libra, from 1971 to 1984, met theirs while Pluto crossed Capricorn. The house your natal Pluto occupies, which needs the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and its aspects to your personal planets are what make the square yours.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In the system I built for my readings, Pluto's function is transformation: regeneration, the clearing out of what has become unsustainable, and growth in real agency. Its core need is access to hidden motives, attachments and patterns of power, enough to reorganise what no longer works. At the square, I look at what you are holding on to and whether it still sustains you. The same system lists Pluto's blind spots, confusing intensity with truth and control with safety, and warns against underestimating gradual change: many Pluto squares work slowly and quietly.

It never means death, violence or catastrophe ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). Where it falls among the other life transits depends on the birth year. For births from the mid-1950s to the late 1970s, it came at 37 to 40, just before the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) and the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/); for births of the 1990s and 2000s, it comes in the late forties or the fifties, years after both.

In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Pluto was checked like every placement and wasn't one of the three that held far more often, so I hold its transits as one strand of a reading and tell you when a conclusion leans on it.

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The Pluto square comes at about 40 for everyone.”** For births from 1930 to 2009 it came between 35.5 and 59.3.
- **“It's a death transit.”** Pluto describes transformation. No transit predicts a death.
- **“It destroys what you've built.”** It reworks what has become unsustainable, and often slowly. Transformation isn't destruction.
- **“It's over in a few weeks.”** It usually stays in orb two to three years, sometimes nearly four.
