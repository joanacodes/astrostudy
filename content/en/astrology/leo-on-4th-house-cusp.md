At the bottom of every chart lies the IC, the point where the 4th house begins: home, roots, the private self. Leo on the 4th house cusp lights a fire there, the kind people gather round.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) describes the ground under your feet ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Leo](/astrology/leo/) makes it a hearth. I read the [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) here as a home you want to be proud of and to fill: the table that seats more people than live there, the corner where you make things, the house friends remember for its welcome. Family loyalty runs strong, and not only towards the family you came from: whatever you build in order to belong, you defend. The private self under the public one is playful and likes to be appreciated, more than colleagues may ever see. At home, being taken for granted is what hurts: the meal nobody remarked on, the effort that went unseen. Leo is a fixed sign, so roots, once put down, tend to hold. You may move often, but you carry your way of making a home from place to place, and rebuild it quickly.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules your 4th house, and its position shows what home is for and where you draw on it: as ruler of the 4th, in the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). If your Sun is in Leo as well, you were born in the hours around local midnight, with the Sun near the IC, and the ruler of your home sits at its own root. Opposite, Aquarius is always on the Midheaven ([Aquarius on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-10th-house-cusp/)), so your public life answers to Uranus and Saturn, Aquarius' two rulers, and I read both. The axis runs from one warm centre to the wider group: at home you want to be the heart of things, while out in the world you're often more at ease as one of many, or as the one who changes how things are done.

About 1 birth in 12 has a Leo IC, at London as at New York, counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use. The IC is the point opposite the Midheaven, and the sign there is set by the turning of the meridian, which takes no account of latitude: that's why the two cities agree, and why Leo, on this cusp for about two hours a day, sits right on the average. At London, Gemini rises with it in about 5 charts in 6, making Mercury your chart ruler. Leo opens the 3rd as well in nearly 1 London chart in 3, and the 5th in about 1 in 6 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)); and in some charts it opens nothing at all, an [intercepted sign](/astrology/intercepted-signs/), in about 1 London birth in 7.

Two hours is not long. Half an hour's error moves the IC about a quarter of the way across Leo, and near either end of the sign that's enough to leave Leo on the 3rd cusp instead ([Leo on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/)) or on the 5th ([Leo on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-5th-house-cusp/)). I won't guess which, and I don't read a chart without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
