Cancer on the 11th house cusp opens the house of friendship, community and the future you build with others in the sign that looks after its own. The people you choose tend to become people you care for, and a group becomes a place to belong.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the cusp of the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) describes how you belong to something larger than a couple: a circle of friends, a team, a cause ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) takes part by looking after. You tend to keep friends for decades, know what's happening in their lives, and turn up with soup when it's needed. Old friends matter: a friendship kept from school or a first job can mean more than any new circle, however lively. In any group, you are often the one who notices who's missing and makes room for the person who has just arrived. Your hopes for the future tend to centre on the people you love: a home for them, a safe place, a community that holds. Community isn't conformity, and belonging to a group doesn't mean losing your own voice in it; with Cancer here, it does mean caring what happens to everyone in the room. Tradition called the 11th the Good Spirit and gave Jupiter its [joy](/astrology/planetary-joys/) there, and it exalted Jupiter in Cancer: on this cusp the two old ideas of generosity meet.

[Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/) comes with this cusp in every chart at London and New York, so Mercury rules your chart while the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules your friendships, and in about 3 London charts in 4 Gemini is on the Midheaven, which puts both your manner and your public life in Mercury's hands. What people meet first is precise and helpful; what friends find is a much softer care. Mercury notices what needs doing; the Moon notices who needs looking after. The Moon's house shows where your friendships and shared hopes take root: the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Opposite, Capricorn opens the 5th, so your pleasures and loves take a slower, more serious shape under Saturn, while friendship runs on feeling.

About 1 birth in 11 has Cancer on the 11th house cusp, at London as at New York, counting Placidus cusps, the system I use: a little over two hours of each day. The 11th is slightly wider than a sign in these charts, about 31° to 34° at London, so Cancer never opens the 10th or the 12th as well: [Cancer on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/) comes with Libra rising, [Cancer on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-12th-house-cusp/) with Leo. When Taurus holds the Midheaven instead of Gemini, Gemini is left without a cusp, wholly inside the 10th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Leo, on the other hand, rarely fits inside the 11th, so the Moon usually speaks for your friendships alone.

The Ascendant, the Midheaven and this cusp all move with the birth time, and a Virgo Ascendant late in the sign can tip into Libra within minutes; I read none of them without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
