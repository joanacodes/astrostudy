Older astrologers expected good things to arrive through the 11th: patrons, gains, a wish granted. Put the planet that rules it in the 2nd, the house of money, belongings and self-worth, and your friendships and your resources lean on each other, in ways both practical and quiet.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) holds your means: the money coming in, the things you own and look after, the abilities that pay, and the private sense of what you're worth. The [11th](/astrology/11th-house/) covers the people you're allied with, the associations and networks you belong to, the benefactors, and the plans you hatch together. The ruler of the sign on the 11th cusp carries that side of life to wherever it stands ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and here it brings it to your purse and your values.

In practice, networks and earning often share a thread. Work comes through someone you know, a recommendation turns into a client, a group you belong to pays its way. You may be the one who holds the kitty, keeps the club's accounts or sets up the shared fund. What you value takes its colour from your company too: the things you save for are often things your circle cares about, and your sense of your own worth can rise and fall with your sense of belonging. On a good day that's generosity that knows its limits. On a harder one, it's measuring friends by what they bring in, or paying for your place in a group.

Tradition gave the 11th more of this than modern tropical astrology does. It was the Good Spirit, a house of hopes fulfilled in a very worldly way, of abundance and benefactors, and Jupiter, the ancient planet of money, rejoiced there ([Jupiter in the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house/)). Modern astrologers kept the friends and the wishes and mostly dropped the gains. With the house's ruler in the house of money, that older side comes back into view. I read it as resources that come and go through people, never as a sum: no chart can say what a network will pay ([Can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

Counted by sign, the 2nd stands a square from the 11th, the aspect tradition gave friction and effort. I read it as a real pull between what you give the group and what you keep for yourself, between the shared plan and your own budget. It rarely settles for good: you settle it again with each cause that asks for a contribution, and the settling is where your values become clear.

The planet sets the terms. Venus, ruling a Taurus or Libra 11th, tends to make friendship and taste one subject: you value what your friends value, and you give well. Jupiter, ruling a Sagittarius 11th, can be generous to a fault with your circle. Where Pisces opens the house, I follow two planets, Jupiter and Neptune, and read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 2nd ties your means to your ideals, while Neptune, wherever it stands, asks you to see clearly what a cause costs.

Both cusps come from your birth time, in [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/) as in any quadrant system ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A ruler close to the 2nd cusp would have crossed into the 1st with a slightly later birth, and your circle would shape your manner rather than your means ([Ruler of the 11th in the 1st house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/)). One close to the 3rd cusp needed only a slightly earlier birth to sit among conversations, classes and neighbours ([Ruler of the 11th in the 3rd house](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)).
