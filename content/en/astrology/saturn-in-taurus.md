Saturn in Taurus works the way a hedge grows: you never catch it moving, and one year there it is, thick enough to shelter a whole garden. The slowest planet you can see, in the sign that least likes to be hurried, builds a life that takes time to grow and a great deal to knock down.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), fixed earth and Venus's sign, things are measured by what they're worth and how long they last. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the part of a chart that structures and plays the long game, settles into that pace easily, and its lessons come through the senses and through what you can hold: security, the worth of your time and skills, and the right to enjoy what you've earned.

Tradition gives Saturn no special dignity in Taurus: it isn't Saturn's home, its exaltation, its detriment or its fall. Earth suits its practical side, so the work isn't overcoming the sign. It's not letting caution and a fixed sign's love of sameness add up to standing still.

Saturn was in Taurus from July 1939 to May 1942, April 1969 to February 1972 and June 1998 to April 2001 — about 1 person in 12 among the births of 1930 to 2009. Two people born in the spring of 1970 share it; what separates them is the house it occupies and the place of their [Venus](/astrology/venus/), the planet that rules Taurus and shows what this Saturn is guarding. It follows [Saturn in Aries](/astrology/saturn-in-aries/), where the lesson is starting on your own authority, and comes before [Saturn in Gemini](/astrology/saturn-in-gemini/), where it moves into words: Taurus is the middle step, where what was started has to be kept.

In my [invoice method](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), where every placement earned a tick or a cross, Saturn was one of only three that kept their ticks much more often than everything else: fitting, for a planet in the sign that keeps things.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, this Saturn shows itself through constancy rather than declarations: turning up, remembering, keeping a promise for years. It can be slow to commit and slower to leave, sometimes staying long after a relationship has stopped feeding you. Touch and comfort matter a great deal, yet receiving them may feel like something you have to earn first. The lesson, with Venus ruling the sign, is that pleasure shared is not a debt.

## At work {#at-work}

At work you're the one who builds for the long haul. Materials, land, food, objects, anything that rewards a steady hand and patience suits this Saturn, and so does any job where reliability compounds over years. You may stay somewhere for its security long after it has taught you what it can. Every so often, it's worth asking whether stability is still a choice or has become a habit.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Holding on. To things, routines, a role, a way of doing it that once kept you safe. A fear of not having enough can make it hard to spend on yourself, to rest without guilt, or to let go of what no longer fits. Fixed earth can turn Saturn's caution into a quiet immovability that looks like calm from outside. And self-worth can end up measured in what you produce, own or are able to show.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Decide what *enough* looks like before you start counting. Put one pleasure a week in the diary, and keep that appointment as faithfully as any other. Change one routine on purpose, to show your body that change can be safe. Saturn comes back to Taurus from April 2028 to May 2030: a first [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/) if you were born with it between 1998 and 2001, a second if you were born between 1969 and 1972. It's a good stretch for looking at what you've built and asking whether you still want to live in it.
