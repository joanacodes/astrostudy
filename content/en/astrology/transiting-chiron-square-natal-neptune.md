In Ovid's *Fasti*, an arrow from Hercules' quiver falls on Chiron's foot, and the healer's own herbs do nothing for the wound. When Chiron squares your Neptune, a similar discovery tends to come round: the hope that an old hurt will simply melt away meets a limit that stays.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves boundaries and longs for what lies beyond them. At its best it is compassion and imagination; at its most hopeful, a wish to be lifted out of a difficulty altogether. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) works the other way, adapting to a limit without needing it to vanish. A [square](/astrology/square/) sets the two at a right angle, in friction that asks for an adjustment, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) what usually needs adjusting is a hope.

The hope comes in many shapes: the practitioner who was going to fix it, the relationship that was meant to make it stop hurting, the belief that enough goodness would earn a cure. Under the square the shape shows its limits, and that can feel like disappointment. It can also show where you have rescued others rather than yourself, or given more than you had. What tends to come out of it is a plainer kind of care: help that helps, limits said aloud, a practice kept because it works rather than because it promises.

If the disappointment turns into a low that doesn't lift, or the fog thickens, that is for a doctor or a therapist rather than a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

Your Neptune's house shows what you idealise; Chiron's, about three houses on, where the sore spot gets pressed. With [Neptune in the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-in-7th-house/) and Chiron crossing your 10th, a hope invested in a partner can meet an old ache about being seen at work. Both houses hang on the birth time: without it, I can't say where this square presses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A natal Mercury–Neptune conjunction puts Mercury in this square too, and a planet square your Neptune at birth receives Chiron itself, by conjunction or opposition. If your chart holds [Neptune square Chiron](/astrology/neptune-square-chiron/), this friction is an old acquaintance.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Chiron's speed sets the length, and the Capricorn-Neptune generation, born between 1984 and 1998, shows the range: their first square came in the mid-1990s, while Chiron hurried through Libra, and their second while it lingered in Aries, between 2018 and 2027. Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, most squares last about ten months and cross the exact angle three times. The quickest, single crossings, take about three weeks; the slowest, with five exact hits, about twenty-one months.

Nobody waits past about 36 for the first: about half a year for people born in 1935, about 7 for 1945, about 36 for 1955, about 28 for 1965, about 19 for 1975, about 11 for 1985, under 2 for 1995 and about 24½ for 2005. Three of them by 80 is typical, four for some, two for a few. Next in line are those born between 1998 and 2012, whose first square comes from Chiron in Taurus, between 2026 and 2034.

Each square sits between [transiting Chiron sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Chiron trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-neptune/); [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-neptune/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-neptune/) mark the two ends of the round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what you hoped the cure would be, and next to it what has actually helped, however small. The second list is the one to build on.

Say one limit out loud: an hour you won't give, a role you won't play. A no said early spares a resentment later.

Keep one source of meaning that has nothing to do with the sore spot, a piece of music, a walk, a place. Neptune needs somewhere to go that isn't a rescue.
