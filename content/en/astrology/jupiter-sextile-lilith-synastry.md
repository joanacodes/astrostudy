Nothing in Jupiter sextile Lilith in synastry pulls or presses: one person's Jupiter and the other's Black Moon Lilith sit about 60° apart, a sextile with only 3° of leeway. One person's wider world simply keeps leaving a door ajar, and the other's hidden side can walk through it, or not.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the part of a person that grows by understanding: it studies, travels, believes, makes room. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is a point, the Moon's farthest reach from Earth, and astrologers read it in many ways; I read it as what someone learned to keep back, their instinct, their refusals, their sense of where their limits really are. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates two functions through opportunity, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) that means one person offers and the other decides.

If the Jupiter is yours, your interests become somewhere the other person loosens. The class you suggested, the friends you introduced, the city you took them to: those are where their blunter, freer self tends to show. You may enjoy being the one who opens doors. You can't walk through them for anyone.

If the Lilith is yours, this person offers occasions rather than verdicts. You can try out a little more of yourself in their company, in a setting they brought you to, and retreat if it doesn't suit. The invitation never asks you to explain why you kept that part out of sight.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

What a sextile hides is how easy it is to leave unused. The offer is so gentle that both people can mistake politeness for interest, or caution for indifference: the Jupiter person keeps suggesting, the Lilith person keeps saying "maybe", and years go by with the door ajar and nobody through it. At 3°, the orb is narrow, so the contact is easy to overlook beside louder ones.

Other angles give these two points other characters. [Jupiter conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) drops the distance and blurs whose freedom it is; [Jupiter square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-lilith-synastry/) turns the offer into a test of principles; [Jupiter trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-trine-lilith-synastry/) makes the welcome feel natural, with no asking needed; [Jupiter opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-lilith-synastry/) sets belief and refusal at either end, like a mirror.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make the invitations concrete and keep them light: name the place and the day, and make clear that no is a perfectly good answer. If the Lilith is yours, accept the ones that interest you, on your own terms, and say so when one doesn't. Friends can take a course together; a couple can travel somewhere neither has been; adult siblings can find a hobby that has no family history attached.

Dates decide who has it. Take someone born in mid-1965: people born from April to June 1967 have their Lilith 60° from that Jupiter, and so do people born in April or May 1970; anyone born outside such brief windows doesn't. A whole season of births carries the same Jupiter, and another the same Lilith, so I leave this sextile out when I tally what two charts share: my tally takes only contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. What I do with it is look at the houses, and read it as the gap between two moments in time rather than between two characters.

Those houses need both birth times, since Jupiter and Lilith move too slowly for the hour to matter to the aspect itself ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). My months use mean Lilith; true Lilith can wander far enough to cancel a sextile this narrow ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

Inside a single chart, [Jupiter sextile Lilith](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-lilith/) lets a person's own growth open a way out for what they hid. Between two, the opening belongs to one person and the hidden part to the other, which is why I read it only when both have asked ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
