Your lights sit side by side in the zodiac, the Moon in Aquarius one sign behind the Sun in Pisces, and each of those signs has an old ruler and a new one. You were born in the last days of the lunar month, when the Moon is a thin crescent before sunrise or can't be seen at all. I see a heart that cares about everyone and understands itself best from a few steps away.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and fixed air, neighbours with no element, mode or polarity in common: a [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) by sign, a quiet link made of small adjustments. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) shapes a self through compassion, imagination and a sense of being joined to everything; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) needs a little distance to understand what it feels, and finds comfort in friendship, freedom and ideals shared with others. Your Sun closes the gap between you and other people; your Moon needs some of that gap back before it can name a feeling.

Both signs gained a modern ruler after a discovery: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), found by William Herschel in 1781, for Aquarius, and [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), found in 1846, for Pisces. The tradition gave them to [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), and I read both rulers of each sign: Uranus and Saturn for where your Moon goes to breathe, to change things and to keep its loyalties, Neptune and Jupiter for where your Sun opens up and finds its meaning. Four rulers make a crowded chart, and their houses decide which voices carry. Both lights stand in signs where they hold no dignity, guests in houses ruled by others.

Your Moon trailed the Sun by 2° to 59°, so the next new moon, usually the [new moon in Pisces](/astrology/new-moon-in-pisces/), was a few hours to about five days away. About seven of these births in eight are [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/) births; the earliest still belong to the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) phase. To me the balsamic phase clears the ground for the next cycle, and an Aquarius Moon clears it with the future already in view. At the far end of the range, a Sun in the first degrees of Pisces can even be conjunct a Moon in the last degrees of Aquarius ([out-of-sign aspects](/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/)), and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) says more about being born as a cycle closes.

The count gives about 1 person in 148. Aquarius holds the Moon for roughly two and a half days, so a birthday on which it came in from Capricorn, or crossed into Pisces, leaves three pages open, this one, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-capricorn/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-pisces/), until the hour is known ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With the lights this close, the hour may also put them in one house or in two, which changes where in your life the pair does its work; that is why I don't read a chart without it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Friendship is the ground of love for you, with compassion running through it. The Pisces Sun gives itself warmly; the Aquarius Moon needs a partner who is also a friend, who respects your independence and doesn't mistake your need for space for coldness. The risk is caring for the whole world while the person beside you has to share you with it. Tell them when the distance you need has nothing to do with them; it usually doesn't.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that helps many people at once suits you: humanitarian or community projects, research with a human purpose, technology built for the people who use it, campaigns, music or art that gathers a crowd. The Sun brings compassion and imagination, the Moon a gift for systems and the long view. You tend to do your best in a team with a cause, provided you can step away to think.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Taking everything in and stepping back from it can happen in the wrong order. You may absorb a room's distress and then explain it, calmly and well, without letting yourself feel your share, or feel guilty for needing distance from people you love. Distance is how this Moon looks after itself; it doesn't betray the Sun's compassion. What helps is choosing it on purpose, a walk alone after a crowded day, before it turns into withdrawal.
