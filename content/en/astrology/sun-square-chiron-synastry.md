A joke that lands on the one subject the other can't laugh about: Sun square Chiron between two charts often shows up like that. One person's Chiron sits about 90° from the other's Sun, and ease keeps brushing against a sore spot from the side.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity and self-expression, the way a person shows up and takes their place. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the capacity to adapt around a lasting limitation: to compensate, find other methods and pass on what was learned. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction that promotes adaptation, and because this is one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the rub runs between two lives.

What the friction can build is a map of tender ground. The Chiron person, adapting again in the other's presence, often finds new methods and a firmer grip on skills they already had. The Sun person learns tact in one very specific area, and an attention to other people's limits that serves them well beyond this relationship.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

With the Sun on your side, something you do without thinking, speaking up, taking the stage, being good at one particular thing, touches a place where the other had to work around a limit. You may feel accused of what you never intended, and start editing yourself until some of your spontaneity is gone.

With Chiron on your side, the other's ease can rub. Your effort may feel invisible next to their talent, comparisons arrive whether anyone makes them or not, and you may react before you've worked out why it hurt.

Neither of you is doing anything wrong. A square describes friction, not failure, and the sting is the sign of two different histories meeting, not of ill will.

Chiron's sign is shared by an age group whose span varies: sometimes under two years, sometimes about eight. Whether the square forms depends on the Sun person's birthday, and that is what makes it personal.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Map the tender ground early. The Chiron person can name the area, specifically and without a long story ("teasing about this doesn't work for me"); the Sun person can ask before advising there, and stop apologising for their ease everywhere else. A couple can agree on a few subjects that are off limits for jokes; friends can laugh about everything else; siblings can retire an old comparison; colleagues can give feedback in the sore area privately rather than in a meeting.

About 1 pair of people in 7 has this square, one way round or the other, within 6.8°. I won't look at it unless both people have asked for the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)), and never without both birth times: a few hours barely move the Sun, but the houses decide where the sore spot sits in each life, money, home or public life, and so where the friction is felt ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The other angles: [Sun conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-chiron-synastry/) places the ease right on the sore spot, [Sun opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-chiron-synastry/) sets the two face to face, and [Sun sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-chiron-synastry/) and [Sun trine Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-chiron-synastry/) let experience and ease work together without rubbing. In one chart, [Sun square Chiron](/astrology/sun-square-chiron/) keeps a person working around their own limit; between two, the limit is someone else's, and you keep bumping into it.
