You said Thursday; they heard "sometime soon". They said they were fine; you heard a fact. In Mercury square Neptune in synastry, one chart's Mercury and the other chart's Neptune meet at a right angle, and between clear words and soft impressions, meaning can drift. I read it gently, from both sides, because neither of you is being careless with the other.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how a person sorts, names and says what they mean. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is how they imagine and feel their way into things: compassion, inspiration, the meaning behind the words, an ease with ambiguity. A [square](/astrology/square/) makes two functions rub, and the rubbing is how they adapt; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one rubs two people's ways of talking together. Within one chart, [Mercury square Neptune](/astrology/mercury-square-neptune/) sets a person's precision at odds with their own imagination; here precision and imagination each have their own speaker.

Worked with, the friction produces something rare: imagination that checks itself, and precision that stays kind. The Mercury person learns to ask about the feeling behind a statement; the Neptune person learns to give the practical answer when it's needed. Decisions become more careful, because both have been caught out by assuming. When one person's Mercury in Gemini squares the other's Neptune in Pisces, both planets are at home, Gemini being Mercury's own sign and Pisces the one modern tropical astrology gives Neptune: the clearest talker and the deepest dreamer, at right angles. Getting understood becomes a two-step habit: the Mercury person says it plainly, the Neptune person says what it feels like, and only then do you both know what was meant.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

For the Mercury person, the other can seem hard to pin down: plans stay open, answers come as feelings, and you may find yourself asking the same question twice. It's easy to grow impatient, or to suspect evasion where there is only a different way of speaking.

For the Neptune person, the other can seem literal to the point of coldness: your mood is met with a solution, your hope with a list of objections. It's easy to stop sharing what you sense, or to hear criticism where there is only a request for clarity.

Neither of you is deceiving the other. A square is friction, and this one lives in the gap between what's said and what's meant. Astrologers have read Neptune only since it was found in 1846, and it moves so slowly, about fourteen years in a sign, that the Neptune side of this square belongs to a generation; the Mercury side is personal, and makes the square a matter between two people.

At the pair's other angles:

- **[Mercury conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-neptune-synastry/):** words and imagination fused, so meaning travels unspoken instead of drifting.
- **[Mercury sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** an easy opening, where imagination joins the conversation only when invited.
- **[Mercury trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-neptune-synastry/):** a smooth flow in one element; feelings find their words, and words keep their feeling.
- **[Mercury opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** the literal and the imagined face to face, a balance to keep rather than a gap to cross.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Give each kind of conversation its own time. Let some talks be for hopes and impressions, with no conclusions required. When something has to be agreed, the Mercury person can state it in one plain sentence and the Neptune person can say whether that matches what they feel; dates and money go in writing. At work, the square can pair a designer with an engineer to good effect; between friends, it's the person who teaches you that facts can be gentle; in a couple or a family, a reason to ask "is that a wish, or a plan?"

About 1 pair of people in 9 has Mercury and Neptune square, one way round or the other, within 5.1°. The hours show where the drift happens: Neptune falling in the Mercury person's 7th house, [their Neptune in your 7th house](/astrology/neptune-in-7th-house-synastry/), puts it at the heart of the partnership, and that needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
