Uranus sextile Neptune belongs to births of the mid- and late 1960s, with Uranus mostly in Virgo and Neptune in Scorpio, two signs apart. It describes an opening between invention and imagination that a whole generation shares, and that your houses turn into something of your own.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation and differentiation: new methods, independent thinking, the reform of systems that have stopped working. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination, compassion and a sense of meanings beyond the literal. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperation: each can switch the other on, but only when something, or someone, engages.

So the vision has access to new tools, and the new tools have access to a vision. An ideal of care might find a better method; a technical skill might be put at the service of something felt rather than measured. Of all the aspects these two planets make, the sextile insists least. It waits to be used, and its potential is not yet a result.

Earth and water work well together, so with Uranus in Virgo and Neptune in Scorpio this generation's openings tend to be practical and felt rather than loud. Many people born with the sextile between 1963 and 1968 also carry [Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/): the same charts hold an intense fusion of change and renewal beside this gentler door.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 17 born between 1930 and 2009 has this sextile within 6°: those born in October and November 1963, from August 1964 to February 1965, from June 1965 to August 1969, and from March to June 1970. Nobody else born in those eighty years has it. The closer your birth to an exact pass, the more readily the door opens; at 5° or 6° it needs a push.

Like every aspect of these two planets, it describes a generation more than a person ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). What's yours is where it lands. Uranus in the 3rd sextile Neptune in the 5th could show as new ways of speaking that open up play and imagination; Uranus in the 10th with Neptune in the 12th, as work that changes how things are done in service of something quieter. Placing those houses takes your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Uranus is the faster of the two, about 84 years round the zodiac against Neptune's 165.

The pair's other aspects, each with its own birth years:

- **[Uranus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune/):** born from 1990 to 1996; the two fused into a single process.
- **[Uranus square Neptune](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune/):** born from 1951 to 1958; reform and ideals in friction until a workable form is found.
- **[Uranus trine Neptune](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune/):** born from 1936 to 1946; the two flow together without being asked.
- **[Uranus opposite Neptune](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune/):** no birth between 1930 and 2009.

In [Uranus sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune-synastry/), one person's appetite for the new offers a way into the other's imagination, and the other way round. I read both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-neptune/) comes twice in Uranus's 84-year round; at about seven years a sign, it lasts some months, often in three passes. [Transiting Neptune sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-uranus/) is slower, since Neptune spends about 14 years in a sign, and comes at most twice in a lifetime.

Born with the sextile, you find that your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 42, is also a trine from transiting Uranus to your natal Neptune: the midlife unsettling of old arrangements arrives with imagination on its side. Weather, not a forecast: an easier climate for rethinking, with nothing promised.
