The second decan of Gemini runs from 10° to 20° of the sign, the first ten days of June for the Sun. Mars has its face and Venus rules it by element, so the planets of wanting and of valuing share it between them, under Mercury.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) follow Jupiter with [Mars](/astrology/mars/), so the middle of [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) goes to the planet of action in the sign of exchange. By element, the modern decans give the middle of an air sign to the ruler of the next air sign, Libra, and that is [Venus](/astrology/venus/).

Mars and Venus, one in each list: the pair that tradition sets against each other, Mars ruling Aries and Scorpio, Venus the signs opposite them, Libra and Taurus. I take Mars, here, as the part of a planet that asserts, starts and argues its point, and Venus as the part that weighs, chooses and looks for agreement. Mercury, as ruler of the sign, comes first; then I look at your Mars and your Venus, and at whether they make an aspect to each other, which would tie the two lists together in your chart.

[Mars in Gemini](/astrology/mars-in-gemini/) between 10° and 20° stands in its own face: energy that goes into words, debate and many projects at once, with the small dignity of the face behind it. Ten degrees on, the face passes to the Sun and the element to Aquarius's two rulers ([Gemini, third decan](/astrology/gemini-third-decan/)).

## Its image {#its-image}

In Budge's list the decan is Remen-kher-Saḥ, and its name answers the last decan of Taurus, Remen-ḥeru-an-Saḥ: the same first word, a different ending, Saḥ in both ([Taurus, third decan](/astrology/taurus-third-decan/)). The Greco-Egyptian lists write Uaret, Firmicus Maternus Ver or Asuae, and the *Testament of Solomon* Belbel.

The later lists reach for figures from very different worlds. Cosmas of Maiuma gave these degrees to Kybele, the great mother goddess; Athanasius Kircher, in 1652, to the Cyclops. I keep both as history: a mother goddess and a one-eyed giant say more about the people who made the lists than about a planet at 15° Gemini ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Births from about 1 to 10 June have the Sun here ([Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/)). The Sun crossed 10° Gemini on 31 May 2026 at 10:31 UT and 20° on 10 June at 21:14 UT; in 2027 it reaches 20° only in the first hours of 11 June.

Two bright stars sit in the decan. [Aldebaran](/astrology/aldebaran/), the red eye of the Bull, crossed 10° Gemini around 2015 and stands at 10°09′ in 2026, so older charts place it in the first decan ([Gemini, first decan](/astrology/gemini-first-decan/)). [Rigel](/astrology/rigel/), β Orionis, sits at 17°11′, in a decan whose Egyptian name already carried Saḥ, the god of Orion's stars.

Uranus crossed these degrees for births from April 1945 to July 1946, with shorter stays in the second half of 1944 and early in 1947, and comes back from 2028 to 2031. Saturn passed in 1942–44, 1972–73 and 2001–02.

The sky will mark the decan again soon. The annular eclipse of the Sun on 1 June 2030 falls at 10°50′ Gemini, and the penumbral eclipse of the Moon on 9 December 2030 at 17°47′ ([Eclipse in Gemini](/astrology/eclipse-in-gemini/)). In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Venus and Mercury, this decan's modern ruler and the sign's ruler, were among the placements everyone reads first, and they held about a third of the time; so I read the decan as a shade of the sign and keep the weight on the planets themselves.
