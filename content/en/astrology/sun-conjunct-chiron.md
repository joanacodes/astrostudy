Sun conjunct Chiron puts the two side by side, so the most tender part of a chart sits right beside its centre. It describes a sense of self that grew around a sore spot, and that tends, over the years, to turn what it learned there into a way of being useful.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is your identity and vitality: who you are, and the self you want to be recognised for. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) shows a tender place: something that didn't work the way you wanted, or wasn't welcome, and that you learned to work around. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one while each keeps its identity, so the sore spot isn't off to one side of your life. It's near the middle of it.

In plain words, what you're proudest of and where you're most easily hurt can be the same subject. Praise for it might sting as well as please; someone touching it can feel like being seen right through. That closeness is also the conjunction's strength. Because you've lived around that place, you tend to know its shape exactly, and you may be the person others come to when they struggle with the same thing. The work, as I read it, is letting the limitation and the skill sit together without reducing you to either: you're not the wound, and you're not only the one who handles it well.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as a sense of self built partly from adaptation: a way of shining that took practice, or a gift for others that came out of your own difficulty. Chiron's sign is shared by everyone born over anything from under two years to about eight, and the Sun beside it makes that sign's subject personal: confidence in Aries, belonging in Cancer. The house shows where it lives. In the 10th, a public direction can end up serving people who struggle as you did; in the 6th, daily work is where adapting becomes the skill.

About 1 person in 21 is born with Chiron within 8° of the Sun. Chiron's pace changes a great deal along its stretched orbit, so the date the Sun passes it moves on by a few days a year in some decades and by a couple of weeks in others. The closer the two, the closer the tender place sits to your sense of self.

In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign held about a third of the time, and Chiron 75 to 90%. The notebook counted signs and houses, not aspects, so it proves nothing about this conjunction; but it's why, when the two meet, I start from Chiron ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#chiron)).

Each of the pair's other aspects keeps the tender place at another distance:

- **[Sun sextile Chiron](/astrology/sun-sextile-chiron/):** at a friendly distance; what you learned there is on offer to your purpose.
- **[Sun square Chiron](/astrology/sun-square-chiron/):** the sore spot presses on how you shine, from the side, and adapting becomes daily practice.
- **[Sun trine Chiron](/astrology/sun-trine-chiron/):** the tender place and your self-expression get along, and care for others comes easily.
- **[Sun opposite Chiron](/astrology/sun-opposite-chiron/):** met across the room, often through people you help or who touch it.

When one person's Sun falls on the other's Chiron, [Sun conjunct Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-chiron-synastry/), there's recognition, and the power to soothe a tender place or to sting it. It asks for gentleness on both sides, and I read it in both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-sun/) comes at an age that differs from one generation to the next, because Chiron's orbit is so uneven, and how long it stays near your Sun depends on how fast Chiron is moving in that stretch of its orbit.

Born with the conjunction, you meet it again at the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, when Chiron comes back to its birth place and so to your Sun. Weather, not a forecast: a Chiron transit describes a season when an old tender place asks for attention, never an illness or an event.
