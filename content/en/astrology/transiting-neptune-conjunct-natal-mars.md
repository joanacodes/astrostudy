When Neptune reaches your Mars, the part of you that pushes, defends and gets things done meets the part that dreams and lets go. It comes once at most, for about two and a half years, and a little over half of people never meet it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is directed effort: initiative, courage, the will to start something and see it through, and the sense of where your limits are. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) loosens the boundaries that aren't needed and puts imagination and compassion in their place. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) the two act as one, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) effort and inspiration stop working separately: you act most readily for something you believe in, or for someone else.

Drive can lose its edge for goals that no longer mean anything, and gain a surprising strength for those that do. Competing for its own sake may feel pointless, while working for a cause, an art or a person feels almost effortless. Anger tends to come out softer, or not at all, which can be a relief or leave you unsure where you stand. The question running through these years is which of your limits protect something real. Mars can still say no while Neptune widens what it says yes to.

Tradition counted Mars among the [malefics](/astrology/benefics-and-malefics/), planets of heat and strife. I read it as the part of you that acts and protects, and nothing in this conjunction foretells a fight, a failure or a loss ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

With Mars in your 1st, Neptune crosses it during its years in that house ([Neptune transiting the 1st house](/astrology/neptune-transit-1st-house/)): the way you start things and stand up for yourself takes on a gentler, less predictable outline, and other people may find you harder to read. The house of your Mars comes from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The natal pairing, [Mars conjunct Neptune](/astrology/mars-conjunct-neptune/), is drive led by imagination from the start; the transit brings that blend, for a season, to a chart that may run on quite different lines.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, Neptune spends about two years and seven months on your Mars and seldom less than twenty-two months. Three times in most passes, and five in about one in four, it crosses the exact degree, so the theme returns in waves rather than all at once.

A little over half of the people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet it before 80, since Neptune covers less than half the zodiac in that time; those who do meet it once.

Mars rules Aries, and Neptune is in Aries from 2026 to 2039, so those years bring the conjunction to everyone born with Mars in its own sign ([Mars in Aries](/astrology/mars-in-aries/)): people born within a few weeks or months of each other, about every two years, since Mars takes about that long to go round the zodiac.

Each of Neptune's other contacts with your Mars has its place on the same circle. [Transiting Neptune sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-mars/), [transiting Neptune square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-mars/) and [transiting Neptune trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-mars/) lie on both sides of the conjunction, at 60°, 90° and 120°, and [transiting Neptune opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-mars/) faces it from the far side, half a circle and some eighty years of Neptune's travel away.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose the cause before the effort. Under this conjunction energy follows meaning, so the clearer you are about what you're working for, the more you have to give it.

Keep one firm no. Pick the limit that protects your time, your body or your word, and hold it even when compassion argues for an exception.

Put the dream to work in small, physical ways: rehearse, train, build, practise. Effort you can see keeps Neptune's inspiration from floating off.
