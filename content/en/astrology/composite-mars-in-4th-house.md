Most of this couple's energy never leaves the house: the wall knocked through, the garden dug over, the family gathered for Sunday lunch, and the row about whose turn it is to cook. Composite Mars in the 4th house turns a relationship's drive inwards, towards home, roots and private life.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) holds home, family, roots and the private life behind the front door. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) its cusp is the composite [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the point opposite the composite Midheaven, and [Mars](/astrology/mars/) there sends the relationship's drive into its foundations. The house is angular, so this Mars is strongly felt, and Cancer, its natural sign, holds Mars's fall in tradition; a fall belongs to the sign, but the picture of energy working through care carries over.

Such a pair builds its nest with its own hands. It renovates, moves furniture at midnight, digs the garden, takes on the landlord, and turns a house into a place that is unmistakably its own. It guards its privacy and the people it shelters, and family, the one you come from as much as the household you share, absorbs a good deal of its energy. Desire is domestic and private, strongest at home. A person with [Mars in the 4th house](/astrology/mars-in-4th-house/) puts energy into home; [their Mars in your 4th house](/astrology/mars-in-4th-house-synastry/) tends to liven up, or unsettle, your home life; the composite gives the household itself a drive.

Disagreements happen at home and are often about home: territory, noise, who decides how the place is run, how much room each family gets. Tempers can run high precisely because this is where the bond feels safe enough to let them. Old family patterns, the way your parents fought, can replay here. What helps is giving each of you a corner that is yours, splitting projects into clear roles, and agreeing on house rules you both chose.

A lively home is not the same as a frightening one. If either of you ever dreads the sound of the key in the door, put the chart down and get help: in the UK, call 999 if you're in danger; Samaritans answer free on 116 123.

The 4th is unusual among composite houses: both ways of drawing them agree on where it begins. Whether the cusps are averaged from your two charts or constructed from the composite Midheaven for the town you live in, that Midheaven sits halfway between your two Midheavens, and the Imum Coeli faces it. The point still moves with both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and the far edge of the house, the 5th cusp, can differ between the methods.

I describe composites without drawing them; those who use them weigh this Mars against your own charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and read no move, marriage or children into it. [Composite Mars in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-3rd-house/) roams the neighbourhood; [composite Mars in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-mars-in-5th-house/) heads out for fun. The 4th keeps its energy indoors, and a chart that looks this far inside a home belongs only to couples who have each agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
