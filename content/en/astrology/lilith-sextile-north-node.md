Lilith sextile North Node gives your untamed side a working role in your growth. The instinct you once kept quiet can point out which new directions are really yours, and the opening is there whenever you choose to use it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the mean apogee of the Moon, is commonly read as the instinct for self-rule, the authenticity you guard and the parts of you that were pushed aside; traditions differ, and I hold it as a symbol. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity you develop by trying, testing and practising; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, the established ground you began on. Because 60° from one node is 120° from the other, the [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) leaves Lilith easy with the familiar while offering an opportunity towards the new.

In plain words, your instinct can scout for your growth. It tends to know which unfamiliar paths are genuinely yours and which are only someone else's idea of improvement, and following it can make practising the new capacity feel less like a performance.

An opportunity still needs taking. Left alone, the easy trine to the South Node can keep your instinct where it has always been, useful and out of sight; the sextile is the invitation to bring it forward.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A sextile places Lilith two signs from the North Node and four from the South Node, so Lilith usually shares its element with the South Node: your instinct speaks the language of your familiar ground while pointing towards the new. With the North Node in a fire sign and the South Node in air, for instance, an air-sign Lilith can lend a cool, independent eye to growth that asks for more warmth and daring. Lilith moves forward a sign in about nine months and the nodes back a sign in about 18, so the sextile forms twice in each six-year cycle of the pair, for a couple of months of births each time.

The houses show where the opening sits, given a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Lilith in the 3rd with the North Node in the 5th, for example, might let a frank tongue open room for play and creations of your own, while staying on good terms with the friendships of the 11th.

About 1 person in 15 has this sextile within 6°, counted with the mean Lilith and the true node; I check it against the mean node too ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)).

The pair at its four other angles:

- **[Lilith conjunct North Node](/astrology/lilith-conjunct-north-node/):** instinct and growth at one point, the untamed part leading.
- **[Lilith square North Node](/astrology/lilith-square-north-node/):** the instinct at odds with both ends, asking to be included.
- **[Lilith trine North Node](/astrology/lilith-trine-north-node/):** the reverse balance, a flow towards growth and an opening back.
- **[Lilith opposite North Node](/astrology/lilith-opposite-north-node/):** refusal as old ground, facing a new direction.

Between two charts, [Lilith sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/lilith-sextile-north-node-synastry/) is a lighter contact: one person's frankness can encourage the other's growth without pushing. I read it from each side.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), born with the sextile, each Lilith return, about every nine years, brings back the same opening for a few weeks, and each [nodal return](/astrology/nodal-return/) does the same from the node's side. At the [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), the transiting North Node trines your Lilith instead.

In the sky, the two form a sextile twice in every six years or so, an echo of your birth angle rather than a transit to it.

Weather, not a forecast: these are seasons when your instinct's view of your growth is easier to hear.
