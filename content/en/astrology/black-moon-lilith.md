The [Moon](/astrology/moon/)'s orbit isn't a circle: it's an ellipse, and once a month the Moon passes its farthest point from Earth. That point slowly turns around the zodiac, and astrologers call it Black Moon Lilith, after the figure of folklore who would not obey. It's read as what in you would not obey either.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Lilith points to the part of someone they learned to hide: too much, too loud, too strange, too hungry. In a reading, it's usually where people go quiet, and then say *how did you know*.

I don't read Lilith as something to fix. I read it as something that was taken off the table early, and might be worth putting back. Its sign shows the style of that refusal; its house, where in life it shows; its aspects, how much it colours everything else.

## Which Lilith? {#which-lilith}

Four things are most often called Lilith in astrology, which is why charts disagree:

- **Mean Black Moon Lilith:** the smoothed average of the Moon's apogee. The one I use.
- **True (osculating) Lilith:** the Moon's actual apogee at that moment; it wobbles widely.
- **Dark Moon Lilith:** a hypothetical second moon that was never confirmed.
- **Asteroid 1181 Lilith:** a real asteroid, read separately.

To read the right one, first [check which Lilith your chart shows](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/).

## Lilith in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), when one person's Lilith touches the other's planets, the pull is strong and often unsettling: fascination, taboo, a sense of being seen in a way that's both thrilling and exposing. These contacts ask for honesty, because they bring out what each person usually keeps hidden.

## Transits and the Lilith return {#lilith-return}

Mean Lilith moves through a sign in about nine months and comes back to its birth position roughly every nine years. Those returns often coincide with an old refusal surfacing again — a moment to ask whether the part you hid still needs hiding.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

When I checked charts placement by placement — mine, then my family's, friends', and strangers' who told me nothing first — Lilith, [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) and [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) held 75 to 90% of the time, while most placements held about a third. It's a notebook, not a study: a count by hand, mostly on people I knew. But it's why Lilith always gets its own part in my readings. ## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Lilith is only about sex.”** Desire is part of it; so is anger, autonomy and every kind of refusal.
- **“A strong Lilith makes you a femme fatale.”** It makes you less willing to be tamed. That isn't the same thing.
- **“Lilith is a planet.”** It's a point in the Moon's orbit, not an object.
