Venus trine Lilith lets what you love and the part of you that won't be tamed get along. Being fully yourself in a relationship, tastes, appetites and limits included, tends to feel natural rather than daring.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a chart that values and connects: attraction, preference, aesthetic judgement, and fairness between people. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is the Moon's apogee, a point rather than a body, and traditions read it in different ways; for me it describes autonomy, instinct, and what got pushed aside so that you'd fit. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates them through facilitative flow, so that one makes room for the other without either giving way.

In plain words, the people you're drawn to tend to like you as you are, and you tend to choose them partly for that. You can enjoy what you enjoy without apology, wear what you like, keep your own hours in a shared life, and still be good company. The trine doesn't make you provocative; it makes your autonomy easy to live with, for you and for others.

Ease isn't inevitability, and flow isn't passivity. It's possible to coast on this trine and never notice the people around you who can't be themselves as easily. Used on purpose, it lets you be the friend or partner in whose company others stop hiding too.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

A trine usually links signs of one element. With Venus in Taurus, its own sign, trine Lilith in Virgo or Capricorn, the ease is earthy: a frank enjoyment of good food, comfort and craft. With Venus in Libra trine Lilith in Gemini or Aquarius, it's social: fairness and freedom side by side in your friendships. Mean Lilith spends about nine months in each sign, so its element belongs to everyone born in those months; the houses place the trine in your life. Venus in the 11th trine Lilith in the 3rd might show as friendships where you can say anything, and a way of talking that others find refreshingly direct.

About 1 person in 15 is born with Venus within 6° of a trine to mean Lilith, much as for the sextile and the square. Venus is the faster by far, and forms the trine for a week or two at a time.

The pair's other angles:

- **[Venus conjunct Lilith](/astrology/venus-conjunct-lilith/):** fused rather than flowing; the untamed and the beloved share a single degree.
- **[Venus sextile Lilith](/astrology/venus-sextile-lilith/):** a cousin of this ease that waits to be invited, linking complementary elements.
- **[Venus square Lilith](/astrology/venus-square-lilith/):** the same two at odds, so that pleasing and being yourself have to negotiate.
- **[Venus opposite Lilith](/astrology/venus-opposite-lilith/):** across the chart instead of alongside; autonomy you first meet in other people.

Between two charts, [Venus trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-lilith-synastry/) often feels like relief: one person's affection and the other's untamed side get on from the start, and both can drop the act. I look at it only when both people have asked me to ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

When transiting mean Lilith trines your natal Venus, about twice in each nine-year round, the contact lasts a couple of weeks: a small window in which choosing what you actually like comes easily. With the trine in your chart, each Lilith return, about every nine years, rebuilds the exact angle you were born with.

Venus, much quicker, adds a day or two of the same ease whenever it trines your natal Lilith, a couple of times a year.

Weather, not a forecast: a clear spell for being yourself, short and easy to miss.
