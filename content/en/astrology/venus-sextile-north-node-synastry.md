Some people know both versions of you, the one you're leaving behind and the one you're trying out, and like both. Venus sextile North Node in synastry is often how that looks: one person's Venus is about 60° from the other's North Node, and since the nodes face each other, about 120° from their South Node too, an easy trine to the familiar end.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is a person's way of valuing and relating: attraction, taste, pleasure, fair give and take. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a complementary capacity someone is developing by practice, and its opposite, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), to the capacities they've already consolidated; together they make the [nodal axis](/astrology/nodal-axis/). With a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), two functions meet through opportunity, so the gift here is an opening that has to be used. In [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it's an opening one person offers and the other takes, or doesn't.

Take a North Node in Sagittarius, growing towards a broad view and a conviction of one's own, with the South Node in Gemini, at home among facts, errands and quick talk. The other person's Venus in Aquarius sits sextile to the first and trine to the second. They love an idea with some size to it, so they cheer whenever the Node person states a belief; they also enjoy the Gemini wit, so nobody has to give that up to be liked.

On the node side, this feels like comfort plus a nudge: you can be your familiar self with them, and they happen to like the new things you try. On the Venus side, you like the person as they are and as they're becoming, and your affection doesn't depend on which of the two turns up.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A sextile and a trine pull with different strength. The trine to the South Node is the easy half, so a pair can spend most of its time on the Node person's familiar ground, enjoying what already works, while the opening towards the North Node waits. Comfort isn't the problem; staying there by default is.

The opening is also small. The Venus person's encouragement can be so light that it's easy to ignore, and the Venus person may not notice they're offering anything. Neither of you is failing anyone. A sextile asks for engagement, and this one asks it gently.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Name the opening once in a while. If the node is yours, tell the other which new thing you're trying, so their liking can find it; if the Venus is yours, ask about it, and enjoy it out loud. Between friends, that might be the one who joins the evening class you'd never go to alone; between an adult child and a parent, a parent who praises the new convictions without missing the old banter; between coworkers, a colleague who backs your first strong opinion in a meeting.

Counted by the true node, with an orb of 3.6°, the sextile turns up in about 1 pair of people in 13, one way round or the other. With an orb that narrow, the degree or two between mean and true node can decide whether you have it at all, so I look at both, and a reading names the answer each one gives. The nodes aren't in my notebook at all, so no figure of mine stands behind this contact, only the count of how often it occurs ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)). The houses each body falls in, in the other's chart, need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In one chart, [Venus sextile North Node](/astrology/venus-sextile-north-node/) is a person's own taste linking where they started and where they're going. The rest of the pair, between two charts:

- **[Venus conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** Venus right on the growing edge, where the example is impossible to miss.
- **[Venus square North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-north-node-synastry/):** Venus off to the side of both ends, asking the growth to make room for it.
- **[Venus trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-north-node-synastry/):** Venus trades places, trine to the new end and sextile to the old.
- **[Venus opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-north-node-synastry/):** Venus on the familiar end itself, the comfort without the nudge.
