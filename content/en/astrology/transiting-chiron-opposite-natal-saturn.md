Responsibility looks different from the other side of the chart. When Chiron opposes your Saturn, the duties you shoulder and the old difficulty you'd rather nobody saw stand face to face, and the question is no longer whether you can carry it all but whether you should.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) carries: commitments, standards, the people and structures that rely on you. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the place where a limitation stays and support makes the difference between coping and managing well. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions face to face, usually through other people, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your load and your need for help meet in the room.

The people involved are often the obvious ones. Someone who depends on you needs more than you can give without help; someone with authority over you sets a demand that runs straight into your sore spot; a colleague or partner offers to take part of the load and you notice how hard it is to say yes. Chiron's blind spot is to confuse needing support with being incompetent, and Saturn's to find delegating difficult; facing each other, they can correct each other. Sharing a load is part of competence, and the opposition gives you a season to practise it.

Born within a year or two of you, your friends meet this opposition at about the same age, because Saturn's slow pace keeps a generation's Saturn in one sign. For your own chart I look past the shared date: the houses involved, the personal planets your Saturn touches, and what else is moving, which is where the opposition becomes particular to you.

With [Saturn in the 5th house](/astrology/saturn-in-5th-house/), where play and creation can feel like obligations, Chiron is crossing your 11th ([Chiron transiting the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-11th-house/)): a group or a circle of friends can take some of the weight, if you let it. Those houses come from your birth time; without it I can't place the opposition and I don't read it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Saturn opposite Chiron](/astrology/saturn-opposite-chiron/) in your own chart, holding competence and limitation side by side is familiar work, and the transit brings it out into your relationships.

No hardship, loss or collapse is forecast here ([disclaimers](/disclaimers/#help)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Learning to share a load is rarely quick, and the opposition usually lasts about ten months within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, with Chiron crossing the point opposite your Saturn three times in nearly two passes out of three. About one pass in three is a single crossing of some three weeks; about one in twenty a slow five-crossing stretch of around twenty-one months.

In the site's count the first opposition came at about 27½ in the middle, from infancy to about 50: at 7 or 8 for someone born at the start of 1935 or 1945, about 25 for 1955, 28 or 29 for 1965 and 1975, and between 47 and 50 for 1985, 1995 and 2005. Those late ones belong to a stretch of births, many between 1985 and 2007, when Chiron already stood opposite Saturn or had just passed that point, so it had to go nearly all the way round to get back. Since each round holds one, two by 80 is usual, and nobody has none. Before it come [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-saturn/), [transiting Chiron sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-saturn/), the first [transiting Chiron square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-saturn/) and the first [transiting Chiron trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-saturn/).

While Chiron crosses Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, it opposes Saturn in Scorpio, as for most births from October 1953 to October 1956 and from November 1982 to November 1985.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name one load you carry alone, and ask one person to share it.

Agree who carries what, in words, with the people who depend on you.

Let someone see the effort, not just the result.

Accept help in the form it's offered, even if it isn't the form you'd have chosen.
