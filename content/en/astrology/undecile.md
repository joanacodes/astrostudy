The undecile is the eleventh part of the circle: two planets about 32°44′ apart, a little more than a sign, a breath past the semi-sextile. I read it as the aspect of difference, of two planets that together make a pattern of their own.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Divide 360 by eleven and you get 32.73°, or 32°44′: one sign and less than three degrees. So an undecile almost always joins neighbouring signs, the same pairs as a semi-sextile; only a planet in roughly the last three degrees of its sign reaches two signs on.

To me it describes difference that emerges as two planets work together: an unusual pattern, a variation that's recognisably yours. It's adaptive, a difference you can integrate rather than a fracture. It doesn't make anyone exceptional, it isn't a mark of superiority, and it isn't instability. Defended against, it can feel like being split by your own differences; worked with, it becomes a stable, inventive way of combining things.

Its orb is the main question it raises. The [semi-sextile](/astrology/semi-sextile/) is only 2°44′ away, so with a degree or two either side the two would overlap. I give the undecile about a degree (see [orbs](/astrology/orbs/)), and look at it only when the angle is clearly nearer 32°44′ than 30°. The [decile](/astrology/decile/), at 36°, lies 3°16′ further on.

In [harmonic charts](/astrology/harmonic-charts/), the eleventh harmonic is the undecile's own: multiplying every position by eleven folds each undecile onto a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/). The undecile is barely more than half a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), and three of them, 98°11′, land 8°11′ past the [square](/astrology/square/). Its multiples never meet the [trine](/astrology/trine/) or the [opposition](/astrology/opposition/): four stop at 130°55′, between the trine and the sesquiquadrate, and five at 163°38′, 16°22′ short of the opposition.

## Between which planets {#between-which-planets}

There are no undecile pages pair by pair. I read it by speed, as everything else, and with extra care for the orb.

Between two personal planets, the unusual pattern shows in daily habits: a way of combining feeling and thought, say, that other people find unexpected and that works for you. Paired with Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune or Pluto, a personal planet develops its difference over years, finding its own variation on a larger theme. When both planets are slow, the undecile is a generation's, and I look to the houses for where its difference reaches you.

Its neighbour among the minor aspects is the [tredecile](/astrology/tredecile/), at 108°, read as adaptive reorganisation: where the tredecile rearranges patterns, the undecile produces new ones.

## The undecile in synastry {#in-synastry}

In synastry, an undecile can describe two people who bring out each other's unusual side: together they do things in a way neither would alone, and others may find it odd. That isn't a sign of a special destiny, and it isn't instability either. It's one for the end of a reading: I bring it up in [synastry readings](/synastry/) only when it's tight and touches a personal planet, and I look at it from both sides.

## The undecile in transits {#in-transits}

An undecile from today's sky is gone almost as soon as it forms; only from the slow planets does it last weeks. If it shows at all, it's as a moment when doing things differently feels natural. Weather, not a forecast: an undecile transit describes an opening for difference, never what you'll make of it.
