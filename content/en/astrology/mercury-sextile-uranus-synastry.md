Some people are worth ringing when you're stuck. Mercury sextile Uranus in synastry links a Mercury in one chart to a Uranus about 60° away in the other, and a question from the first tends to get an answer from an angle nobody else took. It doesn't happen uninvited, which is part of why it's so easy to live with.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how a person thinks things through, asks and explains. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part that looks for the other way: experiment, autonomy, reform of systems that have stopped working. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) is an angle of opportunity, two functions cooperating as soon as one invites the other, and like every one of the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it splits them between two people.

What comes easily is problem-solving. The Mercury person lays out how something has always been done; the Uranus person asks what would happen if you didn't, and the two of you find a third way that works. In conversation, you can be ordinary together and switch to inventing when it's needed. Decisions benefit from that switch: the plan you agree on is often better than either of you would have made alone, and it still makes sense to other people.

If yours is the Mercury, this is the person who gives your thinking a fresh route without making you feel slow. If yours is the Uranus, it's the person who hears your unusual idea out, asks a good question, and helps you say it so that it lands.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

This opening only works when someone walks through it. The Uranus person, who values independence, may not offer ideas unless asked; the Mercury person may not ask, out of habit or pride. Or the two of you enjoy the brainstorm so much that nothing is ever tested: a hundred good ideas, none of them tried. Innovation isn't the same as having a new idea; it's the idea put to work.

Uranus lingers about seven years in a sign, so the Uranus side of this sextile is a generation's signature. The Mercury side, which moves through a sign in a few weeks, is the part that belongs to one person.

On its own in one chart, [Mercury sextile Uranus](/astrology/mercury-sextile-uranus/) gives a person an inventive reflex of their own. Here the reflex lives in someone else, and the skill is knowing when to call on it. At the pair's other angles:

- **[Mercury conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-uranus-synastry/):** the two on one degree; invention arrives whether or not anyone asked.
- **[Mercury square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-uranus-synastry/):** a right angle; the new idea interrupts instead of waiting for a cue.
- **[Mercury trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-uranus-synastry/):** a wider, easier flow in one element, where the odd idea sounds natural at once.
- **[Mercury opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-uranus-synastry/):** the two face to face; the new idea always comes from across the table.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Bring real problems. The sextile works best on something with stakes: a process at work, a move, a stuck family arrangement. The Mercury person can set out the facts, the Uranus person can propose the change, and both can agree how to test it before committing. Colleagues often make a good pair for it; friends, a source of second opinions; a couple or relatives, a way out of the same old argument.

About 1 pair of people in 13 has a Mercury–Uranus sextile, one way round or the other, within 3.6°. The house each body occupies in its own chart, where the Mercury person does most of their thinking and where the Uranus person needs the most room, comes from the birth times, which is why I ask for both before anything else ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
