The 6th's ruler in the 7th puts a person across the desk. Your daily work happens one to one: with a client, a customer, a business partner, or a spouse you work beside.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

In the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), the house of the person you meet as an equal, partners in love and in business alike, the ruler of your [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) turns daily work into a relationship ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)). The tasks get done for someone or with someone, and how that someone feels about it matters to you.

I read it as service with a face. You may freelance for a few long-standing clients, coach, advise or represent people, run a business with a partner, or simply work best in a pair. The 7th is also where horary puts competitors and opponents, and a well-matched rival can sharpen your work more than any manager. In a couple, the daily round becomes shared ground: chores, plans, the rhythm of two working lives. The risk is a relationship that turns into a to-do list, or a client who quietly eats the hours you meant for yourself.

The 7th follows straight on from the 6th, and the old geometry gives adjacent houses no aspect ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)): the ruler can't see the back room it came from, so the work moves out of the office and into the meeting. The 7th is angular as well, opening at the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), and what you do for others is seen by them.

The planet sets the tone of the collaboration. With Taurus or Libra on the 6th cusp, Venus rules the house, and in the 7th it brings grace to dealings with clients and a keen eye for fairness in shared work. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 6th, works through negotiation: emails, agreements, contracts read twice. Saturn, ruling a Capricorn 6th, makes commitments slowly and keeps them.

Generosity towards the people you serve marks Jupiter in the 7th, sometimes to the point of giving your time away. Jupiter rules your 6th from there if Pisces is on the cusp ([Pisces on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-6th-house-cusp/)), sharing the house with Neptune ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Both are read: Neptune, wherever it sits, adds the wish that the work serve something larger than the two of you.

If the ruler sits just above the Descendant, a slightly later birth would have seen it set into the 6th, its own house ([Ruler of the 6th in the 6th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)). If it sits higher, near the 8th cusp, a slightly earlier one would put it in the 8th, among other people's money and crises ([Ruler of the 6th in the 8th house](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). The minute of birth draws that line ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Caring for a partner can become part of your daily work, and a chart has nothing to say about anyone's health, theirs or yours ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). If a working partnership starts to wear you down, a doctor or a therapist will help more than a reading ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)). And a partner's chart is theirs to share; I read two charts together only when both people agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
