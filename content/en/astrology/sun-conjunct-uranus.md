Each year, for a few weeks, the Sun passes Uranus, and a few days later each time. If you were born then, Sun conjunct Uranus is yours: the self you're becoming and the urge to differ, experiment and renew, fused into one.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises a self around a centre and a direction. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the function that differentiates and frees: it innovates, reforms what has grown too tight, and insists on autonomy. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two into one active unit, though each keeps its own job.

In daily life, being yourself tends to mean doing things your own way. You notice what could be done differently, you bristle at structures that exist only because they always have, and you would rather reinvent a role than fit into it. None of that is rebellion for its own sake. Innovation is not chaos, and reform is not destruction: this is a self that grows by differing.

The learning, as I read it, lies in Uranus's own boundaries: autonomy isn't isolation, and differentiation isn't alienation. You can be entirely your own person and still belong. I often see that the more connected you stay, the more original you can afford to be.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Everyone born within the same seven years or so has Uranus in the same sign, so on its own that sign says more about a generation than about you ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). The conjunction narrows that down: the Sun passes Uranus for only a few weeks a year, a few days later each year, so it marks people born in one stretch of one year. What makes it most personal is the house: the 10th, work you reinvent rather than inherit; the 7th, partnerships that need room; the 1st, a first impression that surprises people. Without the birth time, I can tell you that you have it, but not where it lives ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The count is about 1 person in 21, within 8°. The Sun does all the work of the meeting, since Uranus is barely moving; a birth before exact has the conjunction still forming ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)).

The year then brings the pair's other aspects, one by one:

- **[Sun sextile Uranus](/astrology/sun-sextile-uranus/):** two signs apart; change turns from a trait into an option you take up.
- **[Sun square Uranus](/astrology/sun-square-uranus/):** a quarter of the circle on; the need for freedom pushes against your direction, and reform has to be earned.
- **[Sun trine Uranus](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus/):** four signs apart, in one element; originality flows alongside your purpose rather than fused with it.
- **[Sun opposite Uranus](/astrology/sun-opposite-uranus/):** across the chart, Uranus retrograde; the urge for change faces you, often in other people.

Between two charts, [Sun conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) sets one person's appetite for change on top of the other's identity. It can feel electric or unsettling, and it rarely leaves either person quite as they were.

## In transits {#in-transits}

A full round of the zodiac takes Uranus about 84 years, so [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-sun/) comes at most once in most lifetimes. It lasts a year or more, usually in three passes, since Uranus spends about seven years in each sign. Born with the conjunction, you meet it differently: Uranus leaves your Sun in your first years, stands opposite it at about 42, together with your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), and returns to it at about 84.

Weather, not a forecast: a long season in which your need for room grows louder, never a prediction of upheaval.
