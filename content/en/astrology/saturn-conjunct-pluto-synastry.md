What one person maintains, their rules, roles and responsibilities, sits in Saturn conjunct Pluto in synastry exactly where the other needs to change things at the root, Saturn in one chart lying on Pluto in the other. With two slow planets, that meeting is as much between two eras as between two people.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) maintains: structure, authority, boundaries, the long work of keeping something standing. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms: it clears what has become obsolete, regenerates, and turns hidden material into strength. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one lets one person hold the structure while the other carries the renewal, on the same degree.

If the Pluto is yours, this person's structures sit right where you need to dig. They can give your transformation a frame strong enough to hold it, a method, a timetable, a promise to stay. Or they can feel like a wall poured exactly where you needed a door.

If the Saturn is yours, the other person's intensity lands on what you're responsible for. You may feel their need for deep change as pressure on everything you maintain, and then discover that some of what you built can be rebuilt stronger, with them.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A conjunction blurs who holds the power. The Saturn person can try to manage the other's transformation, setting its terms and pace; the Pluto person can try to transform the other's structures from inside, by force of will. Each can mistake control for safety. Neither authority nor intensity is the point here: the conjunction asks for an honest account of who decides what, and why.

- **[Saturn sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/saturn-sextile-pluto-synastry/):** deep change offered in workable pieces.
- **[Saturn square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/saturn-square-pluto-synastry/):** a structure under steady pressure to renew.
- **[Saturn trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/saturn-trine-pluto-synastry/):** renewal that settles into lasting form.
- **[Saturn opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/saturn-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** keeping and ending at two ends of one line.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Divide the decisions before the change starts: who holds which ones, and when they'll be reviewed. Make the deep change in stages, each with a point where either person can say stop. Business partners restructuring a company, an adult child taking over a parent's role in the family, a couple rebuilding the way they share money: each can use this conjunction to transform something without anyone being steamrollered.

In the count, Saturn in Scorpio for a birth in mid-1985 meets the Pluto in Scorpio of every birth between October 1990 and December 1993; Pluto in Virgo for a birth in mid-1965 meets the Saturn of every birth from October 1978 to March 1979, or June to September 1979. Contacts shared by such stretches of births don't enter my count of a synastry: the count takes only contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. I read this one through the houses, as one era's authority meeting another era's depth, more than one person meeting another.

For the houses, I need both birth times; the conjunction would be there at any hour ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The figure people ask me about, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith holding 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook, belongs to single charts; a conjunction between two charts was never part of that count ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)).

In a single chart, [Saturn conjunct Pluto](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-pluto/) is one person rebuilding from the foundations. Between two, the builder and the one who digs are different people, and both must agree to the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
