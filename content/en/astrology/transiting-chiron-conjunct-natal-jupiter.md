In the myth, the centaur who could heal everyone but himself gives up his immortality, and it is Zeus, the god the Romans called Jupiter, who sets him among the stars as the constellation Centaurus. When transiting Chiron reaches your Jupiter, an old hurt meets the part of you that looks for the larger picture, and the meaning you've given that hurt is looked at again.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands and makes sense: it learns, believes, hopes and fits experience into a frame. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) describes a sore spot that stays and the adapting you've done around it. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) makes the two work as one, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your search for meaning and your old difficulty are in the same place.

Often what comes up is the story. Years ago you settled on an explanation for the hurt, a reason it happened, a lesson it was supposed to teach, a belief about what you can and can't expect, and the conjunction tests whether that story still helps. Some people find their faith, their politics or their philosophy shifting around the sore spot. Others meet a teacher, a book or a course that reframes it, or notice that they've become the person others come to for perspective on exactly that difficulty. Wisdom is not certainty; the conjunction tends to loosen a conclusion that had hardened.

It doesn't bring luck, money or a windfall, whatever Jupiter's reputation, and I never read it that way.

With [Jupiter in the 9th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-9th-house/), the conjunction comes during Chiron's stay there ([Chiron transiting the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-9th-house/)), so study, belief and the wider world carry the review. The house comes from your birth time, and I read none of this without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If you were born with [Jupiter conjunct Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-chiron/), the two already work as one in your chart, and the transit gives that pairing a long season of attention.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Reviewing a belief rarely takes three weeks, though about one pass in three is that short: a single crossing of your Jupiter. Nearly two in three bring three crossings as Chiron loops back, about ten months within my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after, and about one in twenty spreads five crossings over some twenty-one months, long enough to study the question properly.

Jupiter moves about a sign a year, so the age at the first conjunction depends on your birth year as much as your birthday, and it spreads widely. In the site's count of births from 1930 to 2009, it came anywhere from the first months of life to about 50, at 24 in the middle: within months for someone born at the start of 1945, at about 19 for the start of 1985, about 36 for 1955, 38 for 1975 and 42 for 2005. By 80 most people have met it twice and everyone once. It opens the round that continues with [transiting Chiron sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Chiron square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Chiron trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-jupiter/).

The next ones are easy to place once you know Jupiter's sign. With Jupiter in Aries, it falls between April 2018 and April 2027 and may be behind you; in Taurus, while Chiron crosses that sign between June 2026 and May 2034; in Gemini, between July 2033 and April 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down the story you tell about your old hurt, then ask whether you'd still choose it.

Read, study or listen to someone who has thought hard about the same difficulty.

Offer your perspective where it's asked for. Others may need the frame you've built.

Hold one belief a little more loosely, and see what comes in.
