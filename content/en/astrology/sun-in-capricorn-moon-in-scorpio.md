Both signs know about endurance: Capricorn builds to last, Scorpio feels to the bottom and holds on. Your Moon sits two signs behind your Sun, and you were born in the last week of a lunar month, a little after the last quarter. I read the pair as stamina with depth beneath it, slow to trust and slow to quit.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Scorpio lies two signs before Capricorn: a sextile by sign, fixed water beside cardinal earth, the element that gives water a bed. The [Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/) takes responsibility and keeps its eyes on the long term; the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) steadies itself through bonds and truths that time has tested: the friend who stayed through the worst year, the confidence kept for decades, the problem examined until its root shows. A sextile offers cooperation, and these two take it: the Sun gives depth a structure, and the Moon gives the work a reason.

Tradition puts the Moon in its [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Scorpio, the sign facing its exaltation, and I read the fall as comfort that has to be earned through trust, never as a smaller heart. Scorpio has two rulers and I read both: [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the modern one, for where this Moon renews itself, and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the traditional one, which has its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/) in Capricorn, your Sun's sign. So the Moon's older ruler is honoured on the Sun's ground, and effort and depth help each other. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), your Sun's ruler, has no dignity in Scorpio.

You were born in the last week of the month. The Moon had moved 271° to 329° ahead of the Sun, 31° to 89° behind it, twenty-two to twenty-seven days after the new moon, visible before dawn as a waning half moon thinning towards a crescent. Seven births in eight fall in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) as eight phases count it, the rest in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), and near 300° the sextile by sign becomes an exact waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). I read this part of the month as a clearing-out before the next beginning, and a Scorpio Moon clears out thoroughly. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains it.

About 1 person in 150 has this pair. If the Moon left Libra or reached Sagittarius on the day you were born, the time decides between this page, the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-libra/) and the [Sun in Capricorn, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn-moon-in-sagittarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign, from the same time, finishes the first sketch, and the houses show where Saturn and Scorpio's two rulers do their work.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You commit deeply and for a long time. The Scorpio Moon opens slowly, after watching how a small confidence is handled; the Capricorn Sun commits with a plan and keeps its promises. Once given, your loyalty can hold through years that would break weaker bonds. The risk is control by caution: testing a partner quietly instead of asking, or carrying a hurt in silence because saying it feels like weakness. A partner who is consistent, honest about difficult things and patient with your pace earns everything.

## At work {#at-work}

Strategy, research, investigation, crisis management, any role where you must see what is really going on and then stay the course: the Sun brings discipline and ambition, the Moon insight into motives and stamina under pressure. You may be the person trusted with the difficult file. Office politics bore you and shallow targets don't move you; long, serious projects do.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Both signs can carry a great deal without showing it, which is a strength until it isn't. Feelings can be stored for years under responsibility, then surface all at once. Trust offered slowly can become trust withheld, and self-reliance can turn into isolation. What helps is letting the depth out somewhere safe before it builds up: one person who can hear anything, a long walk, a journal, and the reminder that asking for help is also a form of strength.
