The 2nd house is the house of what you have and what you value: money, belongings, the resources you can count on, and the sense of your own worth underneath them. It describes your relationship with money, not your bank balance.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 2nd house as the answer to *what can I count on?* It covers what you earn and how you earn it, what you buy and what you keep, the skills you could live from, and the things you'd protect if you had to choose. Underneath the money sits self-worth: how much you feel allowed to have, and what you think you're worth.

- **A succedent house.** It follows an angle, and succedent houses are where things settle and build up: the 2nd gathers what the 1st set in motion. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) and [Venus](/astrology/venus/): comfort, pleasure in good things, a steady pace.
- **Its opposite.** The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/): the 2nd is what's yours, the 8th what you share or owe — a partner's money, joint accounts, debts, trust.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), just before it, is the self that arrives; the 2nd is what that self needs in order to stay. The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/), just after it, takes what you have out into words, errands and your close circle.

What the 2nd house won't do is tell you whether you'll be rich, when to buy, or where to put your savings. No chart can, and I don't read one for that ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). It shows how you handle money, not how much of it there will be. And like every house, it's placed by your birth time: without it, I can read your planets in their signs, but not which of them speak to your 2nd house ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 2nd house {#planets}

A planet in the 2nd house colours how you earn, spend and value — and how safe money makes you feel.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith are the placements I look at longest. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) they held 75 to 90% of the time, against about a third for most placements, so if Saturn, [Chiron in the 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-in-2nd-house/) or [Lilith in the 2nd house](/astrology/lilith-in-2nd-house/) is in your chart, your sense of worth is where a reading of mine would slow down.

- **[Sun in the 2nd house](/astrology/sun-in-2nd-house/):** you grow through what you earn, keep and value; confidence and security are tied together.
- **[Moon in the 2nd house](/astrology/moon-in-2nd-house/):** feeling safe and having enough go together; familiar things are a comfort.
- **[Mercury in the 2nd house](/astrology/mercury-in-2nd-house/):** you think a lot about value, and your words, ideas or trade are worth something.
- **[Venus in the 2nd house](/astrology/venus-in-2nd-house/):** in the house of its own sign: a taste for quality and comfort, and pleasure and money walking together.
- **[Mars in the 2nd house](/astrology/mars-in-2nd-house/):** you go out and earn, spend as fast as you make, and fight for what you're worth.
- **[Jupiter in the 2nd house](/astrology/jupiter-in-2nd-house/):** a sense that there'll be enough, and a generous hand — sometimes too generous.
- **[Saturn in the 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-in-2nd-house/):** money and self-worth learned the long way; security built brick by brick.
- **[Uranus in the 2nd house](/astrology/uranus-in-2nd-house/):** unusual ways of earning, and freedom valued over a safe income.
- **[Neptune in the 2nd house](/astrology/neptune-in-2nd-house/):** money that's hard to hold on to or keep track of; values set by ideals more than prices.
- **[Pluto in the 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-in-2nd-house/):** all or nothing about money and possessions; what you value can change from the root.

No planet in your 2nd house? That's common, and it doesn't mean money will be missing from your life. An [empty 2nd house](/astrology/empty-houses/) is read through the sign on its cusp and the place of its ruler, the next two sections.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the cusp of the 2nd house is your style with money and things: how you earn, how you spend, and what feels like security. It describes habits and needs, never amounts.

- **[Aries on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** you go out and get it; quick to earn, quick to spend.
- **[Taurus on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** steady earning, a taste for quality and a strong need to feel secure.
- **[Gemini on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** often more than one source of income; money through words and contacts.
- **[Cancer on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money as safety for you and yours; a saver's instinct.
- **[Leo on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** generous spending, and a wish for what you own to say who you are.
- **[Virgo on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** careful with money; you earn through skill and useful work.
- **[Libra on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money through people and partnerships; spending on beauty and balance.
- **[Scorpio on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** private about money, and slow to let anyone near what you value most.
- **[Sagittarius on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** optimistic about money; you spend on travel, learning and experience.
- **[Capricorn on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** long-term thinking; security built patiently, with a plan.
- **[Aquarius on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** values of your own; money as a means to freedom or to a cause.
- **[Pisces on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** a loose hold on money, a giving hand, and values beyond the material.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 2nd house is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp. Where it sits shows where your money and your sense of worth come from, and where they tend to go: the part of life that feeds your security, or the one that spends it. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the cusp, there's a modern and a traditional ruler, and I read both ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** you earn through your own effort and presence; worth and identity sit close together.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** the story stays at home; what you have tends to build on itself.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** money through words, teaching, trade or local ties.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** money tied to home, family or property.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** earning through what you create; spending on pleasure.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** income from daily work and skills put to use.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** money through partners, clients and agreements.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** your resources mixed with other people's: shared money, debts, trust.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** earning through teaching, travel, publishing or far-off places.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** your career and public role as the main source.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** money through networks, friends and groups.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** money handled quietly, behind the scenes; generosity no one sees.

## Transits through the 2nd house {#transits}

The Moon crosses your 2nd house every month and the Sun once a year, while Mercury, Venus and Mars pass through in a few weeks: brief spells when money and what you value are on your mind. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-2nd-house/):** about a year of wanting more room and more comfort — and of spending as easily as you earn.
- **[Saturn transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-2nd-house/):** about two and a half years of budgets, limits and building security slowly.
- **[Uranus transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-2nd-house/):** around seven years of changing how you earn or what you value, sometimes abruptly.
- **[Neptune transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-2nd-house/):** around fourteen years when money is harder to keep track of and values grow less material: a time for clear accounts.
- **[Pluto transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-2nd-house/):** a long, deep change in what you hold on to, and why.
- **[Chiron transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-2nd-house/):** an old sore spot about your worth asks to be looked at, kindly this time.
- **[North Node transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-2nd-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards your own resources and your own values.

I read these as weather, not a forecast: none of them says what will happen to your money, only what money and worth may feel like for a while.
