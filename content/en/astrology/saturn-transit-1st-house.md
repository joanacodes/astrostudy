Some transits pass; this one settles in. For about three years after Saturn crosses your Ascendant, the planet of time and structure works on the self you lead with, asking it to take a clearer shape.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) builds structure, sets boundaries and works on the long term; the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is identity and presence, the self you lead with and the way you begin. Put the two together and you get a season of definition. You may take yourself more seriously, drop a role that never fitted, make commitments to yourself and keep them, and find that people start treating you as someone they can rely on. It can feel heavier than usual, as if every step needed a reason: that's Saturn asking you to choose what you stand for.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 1st begins at the Ascendant, so the stay opens with Saturn on your rising degree ([transiting Saturn conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)), often the stretch that feels most like a turning point. A natal Sun or Mars in the 1st meets Saturn after that, and the meeting tends to ask that planet for patience: drive given a plan, presence given a backbone.

Tradition called Saturn a malefic, and plenty of books still describe this transit as a burden. I read it as a timetable, not a wall. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) brings out what your chart already holds, and this one gives your sense of self the structure it was missing, slowly. It says nothing about your body or your health.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Across all twelve houses, Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude give Saturn about three years a house, most often between about 2 and 4. The 1st is shorter in the middle, about 2 years and 8 months, and much longer at the far end: some stays run to about 5 years, the longest of any house there, because at that latitude the width of the 1st swings a long way with the time of birth. At New York's latitude the same house keeps Saturn about 2 years and 10 months in the middle, and the long end comes in at about 4 years.

Your Ascendant sets the start and the size of your 1st sets the end, and both come from the minute you were born; without the time I can't tell you when this begins, so I don't read the chart at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows Saturn's years in your 12th, a long review behind closed doors ([Saturn transiting the 12th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-12th-house/)), and leads into its years in your 2nd, where the structure turns to resources ([Saturn transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-2nd-house/)).

If your rising sign is Aries, the crossing is under way: Saturn is in Aries from February 2026 to April 2028, after a first visit in 2025, and your degree gives the month. For the signs after it: Taurus rising, April 2028 to May 2030; Gemini rising, June 2030 to July 2032; Cancer rising, July 2032 to May 2035; Leo rising, August 2034 to July 2037; Virgo rising, October 2036 to September 2039; Libra rising, from September 2039. With Pisces rising, the crossing came between March 2023 and February 2026, and with Scorpio to Aquarius rising it comes after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one commitment to yourself and keep it for the whole stay: a practice, a boundary, a way of working. Saturn in the 1st responds to consistency far more than to intensity.

Let your outline get simpler. This transit tends to strip away the parts of a persona you no longer need, and letting them go costs less than defending them.

Expect a slower pace, and treat it as information rather than failure. The 1st house is learning, during these years, what you're really prepared to stand behind, and that is worth the time it takes.
