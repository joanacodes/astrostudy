The Uranus opposition is this transit's other name: Uranus halfway round its 84-year orbit, facing the place it held when you were born. Everyone meets it once, at about 42, for about twenty months.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Natal [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the way you were born to differ, the part of you that needs room and resists the mould. In the sky, the same function now stands at its farthest point from that start. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates two functions through polarity, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) sets your need for freedom against the life it has actually produced, and lets you see both at once.

The question I bring to it is less what you want to escape than which of your freedoms are still yours. Some of the independence you claimed at 20 has hardened into habits that look like freedom and work like walls; some of what you postponed is asking for room. The opposition shows the difference, often through someone else: a person living the life you didn't choose, admired or resented.

It isn't a crisis by decree. Uranus's blind spot, assuming disruption brings progress by itself, is easiest to fall into here, and the useful work runs the other way: name what still works before changing what doesn't. I read it through your Uranus's house and aspects, and beside whatever else is moving in the same years ([Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/)).

With your natal Uranus in the 4th, the opposition comes from your 10th ([Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/)): the independence you need at home faces the shape of your public life. Only your birth time can place that axis, and a chart without one gets no reading from me ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

At about 42, for everyone, the opposition lasts about twenty months within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, eleven at the shortest, and in more than nine cases out of ten it is exact three times. Someone born at the start of 1945 came within my orb of it at 38.1, in the site's count, and someone born at the start of 1985 does so at 43.6; across all births from 1930 to 2009, from about 38 to 44½. The first exact hit falls between about 39 and 44½. Nobody misses it by 80, and nobody meets it twice.

Because it reaches the whole degree, it also opposes whatever sat beside your Uranus at birth. Born in 1963–68, with Uranus conjunct Pluto, you meet [transiting Uranus opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-pluto/) in the same years: at 42.2 for someone born at the start of 1965, against 41.3 for the Uranus opposition itself. Born in 1990–96, with Uranus conjunct Neptune, you get [transiting Uranus opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-neptune/) with it; in the late 1980s, with Saturn beside Uranus, [transiting Uranus opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-saturn/); and with Jupiter conjunct Uranus at birth, [transiting Uranus opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-jupiter/).

Before it come [transiting Uranus sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-uranus/) at about 13, [transiting Uranus square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-uranus/) around 20 and [transiting Uranus trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-uranus/) in the late twenties; after it, the second trine, square and sextile, and last the [Uranus return](/astrology/uranus-return/) at about 84.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List what you've built that still fits before you list what doesn't. The opposition makes the second list loud; the first is the one that steadies the season.

Look closely at the person whose life you envy or dismiss. Under this opposition they often stand for a freedom of your own, which you can claim without copying their life.

Make one change you can live inside for a year. Twenty months is long enough to test it, and a tested change outlasts a dramatic one.
