Venus sextile Lilith keeps a friendly door open between what you value and the part of you that won't be domesticated. You can bring your own taste, your directness and your boundaries into a bond whenever you choose, and the sextile rewards you for choosing.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a chart that chooses and relates: attraction, preference, beauty as you judge it, and the balance of give and take. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the far point of the Moon's orbit, is read in several ways; I read it as the instinct for autonomy and the material you once set aside to fit in. Joined by a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), the two cooperate through opportunity: neither replaces the other, and both need you to engage.

In plain words, you have access to an unapologetic streak in love and taste, and it tends to make relationships better rather than more fraught. You can say what you want with some humour, refuse what doesn't suit you without slamming a door, and enjoy what others think you shouldn't, without needing an audience. It tends to make you a good ally for other people's oddities, too: you like them for the very things they hide. A sextile is an opportunity, though, and opportunity requires engagement: left unused, the untamed part stays tidied away, and you may find yourself pleasing people more than you'd like.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The sextile tends to pair a fire sign with an air sign, or earth with water. With Lilith in an air sign and Venus in fire, autonomy comes out as frankness and play; with Lilith in water and Venus in earth, as a steady, private loyalty to what you actually enjoy. Lilith changes sign about every nine months, so its element is shared by everyone born in those months; Venus's sign and the houses make the sextile yours. Venus in the 2nd sextile Lilith in the 4th might show in a home furnished entirely to your own taste, whatever the visitors think.

About 1 person in 15 is born with Venus within 6° of a sextile to mean Lilith, about as many as have the square or the trine. Lilith was one of the three placements that held far more often than the rest in my notebook, so when this sextile appears I ask where in your life you let that streak out, and where you don't ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)).

The pair's other angles:

- **[Venus conjunct Lilith](/astrology/venus-conjunct-lilith/):** taste and the untamed self in one point; what you like is non-negotiable.
- **[Venus square Lilith](/astrology/venus-square-lilith/):** pleasing and refusing at an angle; authenticity learned the hard, useful way.
- **[Venus trine Lilith](/astrology/venus-trine-lilith/):** the same freedom with less effort, in one element, and often taken for granted.
- **[Venus opposite Lilith](/astrology/venus-opposite-lilith/):** the refusal to be tamed met across the chart, often in a partner's behaviour first.

Between two charts, [Venus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-lilith-synastry/) is a playful contact: one person's affection invites the other's wilder streak out, and both can enjoy it, if both want to.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Mean Lilith sextiles your natal Venus twice in each round of about nine years, for two weeks or so each time: a brief opening to say what you want, or to stop doing something only to please. Venus touches the same angle from its own side about twice a year, sextiling your natal Lilith for a day or two: small permissions, easily missed.

Weather, not a forecast: a fortnight's permission, nothing more binding than that.
