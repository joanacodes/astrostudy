Venus conjunct Neptune is the meeting of what you love and what you dream of, in the same few degrees of the zodiac. It tends to show as tenderness, a feeling for beauty that's hard to put into words, and a habit of seeing people at their best.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) values: it notices what draws you, weighs what's worth keeping and looks after the balance of a bond. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and dissolves: compassion, longing, the sense of something larger than the everyday. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses them into one feeling without erasing either, so valuing and imagining tend to happen together.

In plain words, what you love is never only what's in front of you; it's also what it could become, or what it reminds you of. Music, a face, a landscape can move you more than you can explain. Your affection tends to be generous and forgiving, and your compassion reaches people others overlook. The same gift can blur edges: you may fall for a hope before the person catches up with it, or give more than the bond gives back. Idealising isn't delusion, though, and compassion isn't self-erasure. The conjunction's work is to keep both in view: the person and the picture, the gift and its limits.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In Pisces, Venus's exaltation and Neptune's modern sign, the conjunction is at its most fluent: love as compassion, beauty as something felt. In Taurus or Libra, the signs Venus rules, the imagination serves taste, art and the shape of relationships. Neptune's sign belongs to everyone born across some fourteen years; its meeting with Venus belongs to a few days within each of them, which is what makes it personal. The house shows where the longing lives: in the 5th, romance and creative work; in the 12th, a private tenderness and a pull towards quiet.

About 1 person in 30 is born with Venus and Neptune within 6° of a conjunction. The tighter it is, the more the line between loving and imagining blurs, beautifully and sometimes confusingly; wider, it's a tenderness that colours the rest of the chart. [My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) allowed only a tick or a cross, which is a useful discipline with Neptune: I read this conjunction gently, and check what it describes against your real life rather than the hoped-for one.

Venus and Neptune at their other angles:

- **[Venus sextile Neptune](/astrology/venus-sextile-neptune/):** imagination at the service of what you love, through openings you choose: art, care, a shared dream.
- **[Venus square Neptune](/astrology/venus-square-neptune/):** the dream and the daily out of step; love that learns clear sight and keeps its warmth.
- **[Venus trine Neptune](/astrology/venus-trine-neptune/):** tenderness and imagination flowing in one element; a gift for seeing the best in people, with the occasional need to check it's there.
- **[Venus opposite Neptune](/astrology/venus-opposite-neptune/):** the real at one end, the ideal at the other; a balance often learned through a partner who seems to hold the dream.

Between two charts, [Venus conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-neptune-synastry/) is a contact of soft focus and deep tenderness, in which each person can find the other a little dreamlike. I read it slowly, from each chart.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), [Transiting Neptune conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-venus/) arrives at most once in a lifetime, since Neptune takes about 165 years to go round, and stays a year or two, coming and going as Neptune turns retrograde: a season when love and beauty feel larger, and edges softer. If the conjunction is yours, a related season comes around 41 instead, at the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/), when Neptune, a quarter of the way round, squares your Venus along with its own starting point.

Weather, not a forecast: a long, gentle season that softens things, never one that decides them.
