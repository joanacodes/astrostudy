Neptune's sextile to Uranus is a generation's appointment: everyone born in the same year meets it at about the same age, and about a third of the people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet it at all. For about two and a half years, invention and imagination can open doors for each other.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) experiments, reforms and frees: it wants to try another arrangement and see whether it works. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines, empathises and reaches for something shared. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers cooperation and leaves the step to you, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the experiment can borrow a vision and the vision a method, if you put them to work.

It can show as technology put at the service of art or care, a new kind of community found online or nearby, a reform you start because you can picture the people it would help. Uranus's blind spot is taking novelty for innovation, Neptune's is taking a shared feeling for proof, and a sextile is gentle enough to let you test both. Nothing here is forced; an idea that's never tried simply stays an idea.

With Uranus in your 9th, the sextile often comes while Neptune crosses your 11th or your 7th: a network, a cause or a group of friends ([Neptune transiting the 11th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-11th-house/)), or a partner's ideals, give your convictions a new outlet. None of those houses can be placed without your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Uranus sextile Neptune](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune/), in the late 1960s, you met this transit in early childhood, while the natal aspect itself was still within reach.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune holds this sextile, within my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after, for about two years and seven months and no less than twenty-two, reaching exactness three times in most passes and five in about a quarter.

It comes once at most for anyone born between 1930 and 2009, and the age moves with the birth year, as it does for the [generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/): about 9 for someone born at the start of 1975, 19 for 1985, 27 for 1995 and 34 for 2005. Births from the early 1940s to the mid-1960s fall in a long gap, and for them the sextile doesn't come before 80, which is why about 1 person in 3 never meets it; for the start of 1935 it came at about 74.

Someone born at the start of 1985 meets this sextile at about 19, [transiting Neptune square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-uranus/) at about 32 and [transiting Neptune trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-uranus/) at about 46, with [transiting Neptune opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-uranus/) much later; [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-uranus/) never comes for them.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Try one experiment with a dream in it: a tool, a format, a way of working you had only imagined.

Find the people who share the vision and test it together. Under this sextile, a group can turn an inspiration into something that works.

Keep what works and let the rest go. An opening between invention and imagination is generous with ideas; the sorting is up to you.
