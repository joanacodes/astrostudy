Uranus opposite Chiron is by far the commonest aspect this pair made in the count: about 1 birth in 4 between 1930 and 2009, spread on and off over nearly forty years. The urge for freedom and a place that stays tender face each other across the chart, and a very large share of adults carry that polarity.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables liberation, autonomy and the reform of whatever leaves no room to differ. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks a tender place, a limit that keeps coming back, and the skill of adapting around it, which often becomes something you can teach. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity: each is seen most clearly from the other's side, and the task is integration.

So freedom and vulnerability tend to show each other up. When you claim room to be yourself, the tender place answers; when the tender place speaks, the wish to break free gets louder. Other people often carry one end for a while: a friend whose independence lands right on your sore spot, a partner who needs space just when you need care. They aren't opponents: they show you the other end of your own axis. Over time the opposition tends to teach a particular balance: an independence that includes your limits, and limits that don't cancel your freedom. Autonomy, here, learns that it doesn't have to mean isolation.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 4 born between 1930 and 2009 has this opposition within 6°: on and off from February 1951 to May 1990, and without a break from August 1968 to November 1971. Outside that stretch, it came into orb for a few months of nearly every year and left again, so the month matters as much as the year. Someone born in March 1960 doesn't have it; someone born in October 1960 does.

It lasted so long because Uranus and Chiron moved at about the same average pace for four decades. Each crossed half the zodiac in that time, and the opposition passed through all six pairs of opposite signs: from Uranus in Cancer with Chiron in Capricorn in the early 1950s, through Leo and Aquarius, Virgo and Pisces, Libra and Aries, Scorpio and Taurus, Sagittarius and Gemini, to Uranus in Capricorn with Chiron in Cancer at the end. Shared so widely, it is personal only through the houses: Uranus in the 1st opposite Chiron in the 7th, for example, could set a strong need to meet the world your own way against a tender place in partnership. Those houses rest on a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The pair's other aspects:

- **[Uranus conjunct Chiron](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-chiron/):** no birth of the count.
- **[Uranus sextile Chiron](/astrology/uranus-sextile-chiron/):** 1938 to 1941, and 1999 to 2001.
- **[Uranus square Chiron](/astrology/uranus-square-chiron/):** 1942 to 1944, and 1996 to 1998.
- **[Uranus trine Chiron](/astrology/uranus-trine-chiron/):** 1945 to 1946, and 1994 to 1996, just before and after this long opposition.

[Uranus opposite Chiron in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-chiron-synastry/) places one person's need for freedom across from the other's tender place. Each sees something of themselves in the other, so I read it both ways, and gently.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-uranus/) lasts from months to a couple of years, depending on Chiron's pace in the sign. For people born with the opposition, it comes back at the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, since Chiron's return to its birth place re-forms the angle with natal Uranus; a few years earlier, their Uranus opposition, around 42, had laid transiting Uranus over natal Chiron.

Weather, not a forecast: seasons when freedom and the tender place ask, once more, to be held in the same life.
