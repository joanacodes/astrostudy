A sextile from Pluto to the top of your chart is an opening, not a push. For two to three years there's a way to take a deeper, more effective part in the world, and it stays open as long as you're willing to walk through it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) empowers: it helps agency that is really yours grow, and lets go of what was only habit. The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) frames how your capacities become visible: role, responsibility, contribution. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates the two through opportunity, and opportunity needs engagement. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the opening tends to be specific. A subject you've studied privately for years becomes useful to other people. An organisation you belong to has one thing you could change, and someone asks you to. A mentor, a collaborator or a training turns up just when you're ready to go further. Nothing happens if you wait for it to happen.

The same Pluto trines your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the base under your public life, so home and the people closest to you tend to support the step rather than resist it.

Pluto makes two sextiles to your Midheaven in its cycle, and where each comes from changes its flavour. In Placidus, the house system I use, the one before Pluto reaches your Midheaven usually comes from your 8th or 9th house: the opening arrives through what you share with others, or through study, travel and belief ([Pluto transiting the 9th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-9th-house/)). The one after the crossing usually comes from your 11th or 12th: through a group, a network or a cause ([Pluto transiting the 11th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-11th-house/)), or through work done out of sight.

Two people born on the same day a couple of hours apart can have Midheavens a whole sign apart, one inside this sextile and the other nowhere near it. Only the birth time settles which you are, and I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Using my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the sextile lasts about two years and eight months, and few passes are shorter than twenty-two months. In about three passes out of five Pluto crosses the exact degree three times, for a little over two and a half years in all; in the rest it crosses five times, and the stay runs to about three years and seven months.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have met one of the two sextiles, and nearly one in four have met neither: they sit a third of the circle apart, and Pluto covers less than half of it in a lifetime. The square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-midheaven/)) reaches more people, about seven in eight; the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-midheaven/)) as many as this sextile; the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-midheaven/)) fewer than half.

Pluto is in Aquarius until January 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), sextiling Midheavens in Aries and Sagittarius, the early degrees in the late 2020s and the last around 2040. For an Aries Midheaven it's the sextile on the way up, with the crossing itself decades off. For Sagittarius it's the sextile that follows the crossing, which Pluto made between 1995 and 2008.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to something specific: one project, one course, one introduction. A sextile rewards a concrete step more than a general intention.

Let what you know in depth be of use in public. Write it up, teach it, offer it where it's missing.

Ask for the backing at home that the trine to your IC makes available. People close to you can carry part of a new step, if you tell them what it is.
