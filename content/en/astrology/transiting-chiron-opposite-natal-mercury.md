Opposite your Mercury, Chiron arrives in other people's words: the colleague who finishes your sentences, the examiner, the friend who asks exactly the question you dread. Once or twice in a life, an old sensitivity about being understood is handed back to you from across the table.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is exchange: speaking, listening and making sense of what passes between you and someone else. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks an old tender place in how you're understood, and what living with it has taught you. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, sets your way of thinking face to face with another, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the old subject tends to come through a counterpart. A partner's question can sound like the old correction. A review, a meeting or a teacher's comment can land on the place where you once felt slow or unheard. Or you find yourself across from someone who struggles to say things the way you once did, and you know exactly what would help them.

Communication isn't constant talking, and this opposition often asks for less of it. Listening to the whole of what was said, before answering what you feared you heard, is usually the skill it trains. Out of it tends to come a steadier way of speaking, one that doesn't need the other person's agreement to feel complete.

Whose words carry the subject depends on your houses, and so on the minute you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Partners, clients and close collaborators carry it when your Mercury is in the 1st, because Chiron then opposes it from your 7th ([Chiron transiting the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-7th-house/)). If your chart holds [Mercury opposite Chiron](/astrology/mercury-opposite-chiron/), others' words have long been where this sensitivity shows; the transit repeats the pattern for a season.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The opposition to your Mercury usually holds for about ten months within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, with three exact crossings in nearly two passes out of three; about one pass in three crosses only once, in some three weeks. Now and then, about once in twenty passes, two of Chiron's yearly loops overlap on the point opposite your Mercury, and it is crossed five times over some twenty-one months.

It comes once in each of Chiron's rounds of about fifty years: twice by 80 for most people, and at least once for everyone. The conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-mercury/)) puts the same sensitivity in your own words rather than someone else's, and is as rare; the sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-mercury/)), square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-mercury/)) and trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-mercury/)) come about three times each.

From 2027 to 2033, as Chiron crosses Taurus, it opposes Mercury in Scorpio, a Mercury that belongs to someone born between about late September and mid-December. At the end of 2026 it faces Mercury in the last degrees of Libra.

## What helps {#what-helps}

When a remark stings, ask what was actually said before you answer what you heard.

Listen through one whole conversation without preparing your reply.

Put a disagreement in writing once, calmly, and read it back as if someone else had written it.
