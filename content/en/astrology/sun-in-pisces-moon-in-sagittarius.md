A Pisces Sun gives itself to something larger than itself; a Sagittarius Moon needs a road that leads somewhere. Both signs belong to Jupiter in the old scheme, and you were born around a last quarter moon, the half moon that rises near midnight and is still up in the morning. I think of this pair as a pilgrim: devotion that has to travel.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and mutable fire, with Sagittarius three signs behind Pisces: a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign, between two signs that adapt, so the friction keeps shifting instead of settling into a deadlock. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) finds itself through compassion, imagination and whatever it devotes itself to; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) steadies itself with space, honesty and something to look forward to, and a feeling makes sense to it once it fits a larger picture. One light wants to stay with a feeling until it dissolves, the other to keep moving until it means something, and you may do both before lunch.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules Sagittarius outright and Pisces in the tradition, so a single planet speaks for both your lights: its sign and house tell me where you look for meaning and where you go to feel free again. Until Neptune was found in 1846, Pisces was Jupiter's alone. Modern tropical astrology gives it to [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), and I read both, Neptune for where your imagination and your sense of the sacred are most alive, Jupiter for where they grow into a faith or a philosophy. No dignity comes to either light from its sign; what joins them is the planet that rules both.

Your Moon was 61° to 119° behind the Sun, five to almost ten days before the next new moon. These births divide evenly: the earlier half in the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) phase, the later half in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/), and the exact waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/), at 90°, sits between them. I read the last quarter as a time for sorting out what to keep, and a Sagittarius Moon sorts beliefs: the ones that still carry you, and the ones you only inherited. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) gives the line between the two phases, and only your birth hour can put you on one side of it.

About 1 person in 142 is born with this pair. The Moon reaches Sagittarius from Scorpio and leaves for Capricorn some two and a half days later; on either of those days the hour decides between this page, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-scorpio/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-capricorn/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also gives Jupiter its house, the first thing I'd want to know about this chart; without the hour there is no house to read, so I wait for it.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love generously and with a great deal of hope. The Pisces Sun idealises gently and forgives; the Sagittarius Moon needs candour, humour and room to roam, even inside a close bond. Trouble starts when devotion and the need for air pull in opposite directions: you give everything, then feel hemmed in by what you gave. A partner who shares a horizon with you, a cause, a place you both want to see, a question you never tire of, lets you be devoted and free at once.

## At work {#at-work}

Work with a purpose beyond the payslip, and some movement in it, suits you: teaching, chaplaincy, aid work abroad, publishing, translation, outdoor education, music on the road. The Sun brings empathy and imagination, the Moon enthusiasm, frankness and a feel for the bigger picture. Routine with no meaning in it drains both lights, and a little structure from outside helps two mutable signs finish what they start.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two mutable lights at right angles, both answering to Jupiter, can say yes to too much: another cause, another plan, another person to help, while the last one is still open. The square asks for choices, and the last quarter for clearing out. What helps is a regular reckoning, a monthly walk spent deciding what you still believe in and what you can let go, and promises kept smaller than your hopes.
