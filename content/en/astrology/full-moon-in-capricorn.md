The full moon in Capricorn is the first full moon after the June solstice: the Sun has just entered Cancer, and the Moon opposite rides low through the short northern nights. From 2027 to 2029 it is a lunar eclipse three years running, a little deeper each time.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

A [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) sets the Moon against the Sun across the whole sky. Here the Moon is in [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), cardinal earth, and the Sun at the matching point of [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/). Capricorn is the Moon's detriment in tradition, the sign opposite its own: feelings go into duty, needs wait until the work is done, and at this full moon that habit tends to show. The Sun in Cancer turns the month towards home, family and the people you look after.

It's the [full moon in Cancer](/astrology/full-moon-in-cancer/) seen from the public side: the Moon carries the career, the standing, the long-term aim, and the Sun the private life that supports it, or pays for it. [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules Capricorn and the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules Cancer, so look at your Saturn and your natal Moon together: one carries your duties, the other your needs.

Even an eclipse is weather, and an ordinary full moon lighter still. It counts in a reading when a natal planet or angle lies within a degree of either end of the axis, and then as the high point of a period already under way by transit or progression. It forecasts nothing.

The most recent fell on 29 June 2026, at 8°15′ Capricorn. Then come three [lunar eclipses](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) in a row:

- **18 July 2027**, 15:45 UT, at 25°49′: penumbral, the lightest kind;
- **6 July 2028**, at 15°11′: partial;
- **26 June 2029**, at 4°50′: total.

After them, the full moon of 15 July 2030, at 22°41′, is an ordinary one. The series belongs to the Cancer–Capricorn eclipses of these years ([eclipse in Capricorn](/astrology/eclipse-in-capricorn/)), which also bring a total solar eclipse in Cancer on 22 July 2028. For an eclipse my orb is about 3°, at either end of the axis, and the natal point it reaches is what I read, with its house: emphasis, not omen.

Its partner in the same solar month is the [new moon in Cancer](/astrology/new-moon-in-cancer/): on 4 July 2027, two weeks before the penumbral eclipse. The Moon is full in Sagittarius a month earlier ([full moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/full-moon-in-sagittarius/)) and in Aquarius a month later ([full moon in Aquarius](/astrology/full-moon-in-aquarius/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Both houses need the exact moment you were born, which is why I keep pointing to [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/). Then, Placidus being my system, read the house with Capricorn on its cusp for the Moon, and the one opposite for the Sun. The 10th and 4th are this axis's natural houses, so wherever it falls for you, it carries a little of their question: what is public, and what is private?

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Capricorn rising: your composure and sense of duty on show, while a partner needs care and reassurance.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** your own earnings and long-term security come to a head, while shared resources ask for a softer touch.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** serious talks, a deadline on paper, while a faraway place or belief tugs at the heart.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** duties at home peak, a repair, a family role, while your public life asks for warmth.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** pleasure that has to be earned, a creative project with a deadline, against friends who just want you to come along.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** the working routine at full stretch, while something private asks to be cared for.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Capricorn on the Descendant: a partner's expectations or commitments, while your own feelings ask to be looked after.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** joint money and obligations ask for a clear structure, while your own sense of security needs tending.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** a long course of study or a professional qualification, while family and neighbours want your attention.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Capricorn's own house: your career and public role at a peak, while home and family pull you back.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** a responsibility in a group or organisation, set against a joy of your own, a love or a project, that needs time.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** what you carry alone comes to the surface, while the working day asks you to look after yourself as well as others.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Both ends, and the eclipse orb.** A degree around each ordinary full moon above, in Capricorn or at the same degree of Cancer; about three for the eclipses of 2027 to 2029, the penumbral one counting least.
- **One duty set down, one need met.** The Capricorn side asks what you're carrying; the Cancer side, what you need. Your Saturn and your Moon, by house, show where each belongs.
