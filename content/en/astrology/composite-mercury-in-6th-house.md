Some pairs are at their best with a list between them: the move to plan, the shed to clear, the shift pattern to untangle. Composite Mercury in the 6th house describes a relationship whose mind likes a job to do.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) builds the relationship's [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) from the midpoint of your two natal Mercurys; its house is where that thinking gets used. [The 6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the daily round: work, routines, chores, craft, the small maintenance that keeps a life running. Cadent, it was Bad Fortune in tradition and the house of Mars's joy, but its natural sign is Virgo, one of Mercury's own, and a practical mind is at home here.

With the bond's thinking in the 6th, talk is most fluent when there's something to sort out. The week gets planned together, jobs are divided by who does them best, and problems are fixed as they come: the leaking tap, the clash in the calendar, the form nobody understands. This suits colleagues and business partners as much as couples, and housemates especially. A person born with [Mercury in the 6th house](/astrology/mercury-in-6th-house/) organises their own days; with [their Mercury in your 6th house](/astrology/mercury-in-6th-house-synastry/), one person helps organise another's. Here the organising is joint, and it often works better than either of you would manage alone.

Tradition also gave the 6th illness. I don't read it that way, in a composite or anywhere: no chart can predict a health problem ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)). Between adults, the house describes everyday care, the habits a pair keeps, such as the evening walk or the shared cooking, and that is as far as it goes.

Composite Mercury usually stays within about 28° of the composite Sun, so the Sun often shares this house or the one beside it. In about 1 pair in 26 the composite Sun lies opposite, around the 12th, the house of retreat: work facing rest, and nothing more meaningful than arithmetic.

The 6th ends where the 7th begins, at the composite Descendant, so a Mercury late in this house sits close to an angle. Both birth times decide where that angle falls, and the two methods (midpoints of your natal cusps, or houses rebuilt from the composite Midheaven at the place where you live) may put it a few degrees apart, enough to move Mercury across the line ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-the-time-decides)).

The trap is the relationship that only talks shop. When every conversation is a to-do list, the bond can start to feel like a workplace, and help can tip into correcting how the other one loads the dishwasher. Service isn't servitude, and method isn't criticism. A fixed weekly slot for planning, which frees the other evenings from logistics, helps; so does thanking each other for the ordinary jobs.

[Composite Mercury in the 5th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-5th-house/) plays; [composite Mercury in the 7th house](/astrology/composite-mercury-in-7th-house/) negotiates. I don't cast composites myself; where they are used, they're read beside both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). Between colleagues, a composite needs what any chart of two people needs: both of them knowing and agreeing ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#what-i-ask)).
