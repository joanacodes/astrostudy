Some pairs can spend an hour on the phone about nothing and still send three messages after hanging up. A 3rd-house composite Venus puts the relationship's pleasure in everyday exchange: talk, short trips, the local café, the crossword finished between you over breakfast.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) once went by the name of the Goddess, and the Moon, not Venus, had its joy in it ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)); it is cadent, with Gemini as its natural sign. It covers talk, learning, siblings, neighbours and the streets you walk every day. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) it exists only once both birth times are known, and even then its cusps depend on the method, midpoints of the two charts' cusps or houses rebuilt from the composite Midheaven, so a [Venus](/astrology/venus/) near either edge can slip into the 2nd or the 4th ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Venus here makes the everyday the bond's favourite place. It enjoys exchange for its own sake: a running joke kept up for years, a walk round the block after dinner, a language learned side by side, a weekend in the next town rather than a long-haul flight. Siblings and neighbours are often part of its pleasures, and the relationship may well have begun next door or in a classroom. One person's [Venus in the 3rd house](/astrology/venus-in-3rd-house/) is a gift for warm words; [their Venus in your 3rd house](/astrology/venus-in-3rd-house-synastry/) sweetens your daily talk; the composite makes the talk itself what the two of you share.

In a real sky the gap between Venus and the Sun tops out at about 47°, and nine composites in ten honour that, the Sun keeping to this house or a near one. The tenth can surprise. Between pairs born in opposite seasons, the two midpoints may land on either side of the circle. For about 1 pair in 22, the composite Sun then sits almost exactly across from this Venus, out in the 9th house of far horizons ([composite Sun in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-9th-house/)). That's an effect of the calculation, not a tug between near and far in the two of you.

A bond this fond of easy talk can confuse being in touch with being close. The messages keep flowing while the conversation that matters waits for a better moment, and pleasantries cover a worry neither of you has named. Communication isn't the same as chatter, and curiosity isn't restlessness, but the difference has to be made on purpose. It helps to keep one unhurried conversation a week for whatever is hardest, face to face, with the phones in another room.

Its neighbours put Venus elsewhere: [composite Venus in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-2nd-house/) in what you keep, [composite Venus in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-venus-in-4th-house/) in the home itself. I set the composite out as a technique rather than a reading of mine, and those who use it pair it with the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and with both people's own charts. Since talk is this bond's favourite thing, the first conversation should be about whether both of you want the chart drawn at all ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
