The 10th house is the relationship in public: what it does in the world, what it aims at, what it's known for. Composite Uranus there gives the pair a reputation for doing things its own way.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The 10th begins at the composite [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), and here the two ways of drawing [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) agree: both take the midpoint of your two natal Midheavens. They part only at the far edge, the 11th cusp. The Midheaven is where birth times matter most, though. It moves about fifteen degrees for each hour of birth time, on average, so an hour's error in one person's time shifts the composite Midheaven by about seven or eight degrees, which can be enough to move Uranus between the 9th and [the 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/). *No birth time, no reading* applies twice over ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Angular, with Capricorn as its natural sign, the 10th holds career, public role, reputation and the aims a life works towards. With [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) at the top of the chart, the relationship's public face is unconventional. The pair may run a joint venture in a way its field hasn't seen, change careers together, take turns leading, or be known in its community as the couple who tried something first. Its shared aims tend to change direction rather than climb a ladder, and both people need room to pursue their own work alongside the pair's. Two people with steady, conventional careers can find that, as a couple, others see them as pioneers.

Read in one birth chart, Uranus here is [Uranus in the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-in-10th-house/), a working life off the usual path; read across two, it is [their Uranus in your 10th house](/astrology/uranus-in-10th-house-synastry/), someone who unsettles your sense of direction. In the composite, the unusual path is the pair's. Couples in their thirties tend to have the matching sign, [composite Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-capricorn/); the 10th house waits only on two known birth times.

The trap is a public role that changes faster than the pair can sustain, or an image of boldness that the private life no longer matches. What helps is to agree what the two of you want to be known for, and what stays private, and to let each person's own work keep its place beside the shared one. Over the years, what the pair leaves behind tends to be less a status than an example: a way of working or living together that others borrow. A public venture also tends to go better when both people can say, in a sentence, what it is for.

[Composite Uranus in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-9th-house/) changes what the pair believes; [composite Uranus in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-11th-house/), the company it keeps. Nothing here predicts success, money or status. I describe the composite as a technique, not as something I draw for anyone: it is read beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and only for two people who both want it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
