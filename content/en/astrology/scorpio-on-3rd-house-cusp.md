Scorpio on the 3rd house cusp hands the ordinary traffic of your days, messages, lessons, errands, to the sign that wants to know what lies underneath. Even small talk tends to turn, with you, into a question about how things really work.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) covers how you think, talk and learn in an ordinary week, and the people close at hand ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) on its cusp, you tend to go to the source: the original rather than the summary, the person who was there rather than the rumour. You'll stay with a subject until you see how it works underneath, and even an errand can turn into a small investigation. A conversation feels worth having when something has shifted by the end of it. When you write, you tend to cut rather than pad, and short trips often have a purpose beyond the trip: the archive, the old street, the person you need to see face to face. Tradition put the Moon's joy in this house and the Moon's fall in Scorpio, and I read the pairing as everyday talk that carries more feeling than it shows. With brothers, sisters, cousins or neighbours, what you've lived may have been intense, with ruptures and repairs; that is your side of those ties, not a verdict on the people in them.

Your words and lessons lead wherever [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), Scorpio's two rulers, are placed, and I read both: the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). With this cusp Mars always has a second house: Aries opens your 8th in every chart at London and New York, so the talk of an ordinary day and the conversations that change a relationship answer to the same planet. When Libra rises, in about 1 London chart in 5, Aries opens the 7th as well as the 8th. Taurus, opposite, opens the 9th and gives Venus your beliefs and far journeys.

Scorpio's long rise, nearly three hours at London, keeps it a good while on the cusps below the eastern horizon, and the 3rd is one of its common ones: about 1 birth in 11, at London as at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. Virgo rises in about 4 London charts in 5 with this cusp, which hands your chart to Mercury, the planet that rules the 3rd in the natural zodiac. In the rest, Libra rises and the 3rd, about 35° wide at London, holds the whole of Sagittarius after Scorpio, which brings Jupiter into your everyday thinking ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Scorpio never opens the 2nd or the 4th as well in these charts, and across all London births it has no cusp at all in about 1 in 7, against 1 in 11 at New York.

Scorpio holds your 3rd cusp for a little over two hours of the London day; born before that, you could have had it on the IC ([Scorpio on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-4th-house-cusp/)), and after, on the 2nd ([Scorpio on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-2nd-house-cusp/)). A rounded time can blur all three, so I wait for the recorded one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
