A love affair can become a world for two, with its own jokes, its own rituals and hardly a friend in common. Composite North Node in the 11th house props that door open and lets other people in.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

None of my readings uses a composite; I describe the technique for those who meet it elsewhere. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is a chart of midpoints read as the relationship itself, and its [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows where the bond is growing, while its [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, shows where it already shines. With the South Node in [the 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/), the bond shines in romance, play and creativity, two people absorbed in each other's company. Its North Node is placed in [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), the succedent house that Aquarius matches by nature; Greek astrologers named it Good Spirit and made it Jupiter's joy. Friends live here, with groups, shared hopes and the future you build with other people.

The circle closes between the two of you. Sociable as each of you may be, the bond itself can keep everyone at arm's length, as if friends might break the spell. The 11th-house direction says friendship strengthens it: a circle you share, a club or a cause you join together, a dinner table with more than two chairs, hopes for the future that include more people than yourselves.

In one chart, [North Node in the 11th house](/astrology/north-node-in-11th-house/) turns a person from the centre of attention towards friends and shared aims; [their North Node in your 11th house](/astrology/north-node-in-11th-house-synastry/) draws someone into your friendships to grow. The composite node asks the pair to belong somewhere together, which can feel like a loss of intimacy at first and usually turns out to protect it.

The 5th was also, in tradition, the house of children. No chart predicts a pregnancy or a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)); here the 5th is simply the pleasure two adults take in each other.

The house itself needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and its cusp depends on more choices than the angles do. Every quadrant house system, Placidus included, agrees on the four angles and differs on the cusps between them, and the 11th's is one of those; the method, midpoints of your two natal 11th cusps or houses rebuilt from the composite Midheaven for the place you live, adds a second choice. A node near either edge may belong to [composite North Node in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-10th-house/) or [composite North Node in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-12th-house/), and the mean and true node can decide it too: I look at both.

Dilution is the hazard here: a relationship so busy with its circle that it forgets to be two people who like each other, or that hides in a group to avoid a private conversation. The 5th-house end keeps the spark, since time alone together, purely for fun, is what makes a couple worth inviting.

Checked against both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), as it always should be, this node predicts nothing about the future it points to. Friends don't read each other's charts behind their backs, and a pair's chart is no different: both people agree first ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
