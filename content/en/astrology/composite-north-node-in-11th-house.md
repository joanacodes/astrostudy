Some love affairs are conducted as a private world of two: their own jokes, their own rituals, and hardly a friend in common. Composite North Node in the 11th house asks a relationship like that to open its doors and let other people in.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

None of my readings uses a composite; I describe the technique for those who meet it elsewhere. [The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is a chart of midpoints read as the relationship itself, and its [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows where the bond is growing, while its [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), opposite, shows where it already shines. With the South Node in [the 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/), the bond shines in romance, play and creativity, two people absorbed in each other's company. The North Node sits in [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/), succedent, naturally Aquarius's, called Good Spirit by the Greek astrologers and given to Jupiter as its joy: friends, groups, shared hopes, the future you build with other people.

The closed circle belongs to the relationship. Two sociable people can still build a bond that keeps everyone at arm's length, as if friends might break the spell. The 11th-house direction says friendship strengthens it: a circle you share, a club or a cause you join together, a dinner table with more than two chairs, hopes for the future that include more people than yourselves.

One person's [North Node in the 11th house](/astrology/north-node-in-11th-house/) is about friends and shared aims beyond being the centre of attention; [their North Node in your 11th house](/astrology/north-node-in-11th-house-synastry/) ties someone's growth to your friendships. The composite node asks the pair to belong somewhere together, which can feel like a loss of intimacy at first and usually turns out to protect it.

Tradition also gave the 5th house to children. No chart predicts a pregnancy or a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)); here the 5th is simply the pleasure two adults take in each other.

The house itself needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and its cusp depends on more choices than the angles do. Every quadrant house system, Placidus included, agrees on the four angles and differs on the cusps between them, and the 11th's is one of those; the method, midpoints of your two natal 11th cusps or houses rebuilt from the composite Midheaven for the place you live, adds a second choice. A node near either edge may belong to [composite North Node in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-10th-house/) or [composite North Node in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-north-node-in-12th-house/), and the mean and true node can decide it too: I look at both.

The 11th-house trap is dilution: a relationship so busy with its circle that it forgets to be two people who like each other, or that hides in a group to avoid a private conversation. The 5th-house end keeps the spark, since time alone together, purely for fun, is what makes a couple worth inviting.

Practitioners read this node beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it predicts nothing about the future it points to. Friends don't read each other's charts behind their backs, and a pair's chart is no different: both people agree first ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
