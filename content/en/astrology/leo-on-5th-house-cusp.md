The 5th is the house of what you do for joy, and in the natural order of the zodiac its sign is Leo. With Leo on the 5th house cusp, the house speaks its own language, and speaks it out loud.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Every house has a natural sign, the one that falls in its place when the zodiac is laid round the wheel from Aries, and for the [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) it's [Leo](/astrology/leo/). When the cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)) and the natural sign agree, the house's matters tend to come without translation. Here that means pleasure taken wholeheartedly: the thing you make because making it delights you, the evening you throw yourself into, the stage, large or small, where you're glad to be seen. I read it as a creativity that wants an audience, even an audience of one, and a way of loving that shows: you court, you celebrate, you remember the anniversary and hope yours is remembered too. Games are played to win, with style, and losing gracefully is part of the performance. The shadow is a joy that comes to depend on applause, so that a project stalls the moment nobody is watching. Keep one thing you do for the pleasure of it alone, and the house stays yours.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the 5th's natural ruler and here its real one, so the Sun's house shows what your delight is spent on, and where it lights things up: as ruler of the 5th, in the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In about 2 London charts in 3, this cusp comes with [Taurus rising](/astrology/taurus-rising/), and Venus rules the chart. Tradition called the 5th Good Fortune and gave Venus its joy there, so in these charts the planet that rules you rejoices in the house the Sun rules, and I give pleasure more room in the reading than usual.

Spread a day's births over the clock and draw their houses in Placidus, as I do, and Leo opens the 5th for about 1 in 14 at London, 1 in 13 at New York. That's rarer than the average of 1 in 12, because the 5th lies on the western side of the chart, below the horizon, and Leo, so slow to rise in the north, crosses the western cusps quickly: about an hour and three-quarters a day on this one at London. It seldom opens a second house from here, the 4th in about 1 London chart in 5 and the 6th almost never, and Leo itself is [intercepted](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) in about 1 London birth in 7. The 5th runs wide in these charts, up to about 42° at London, wide enough to hold the whole of Virgo in about 1 London chart in 4: Mercury then has a say in your pleasures too, and craft gets into the play.

Your neighbours in time are [Leo on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-4th-house-cusp/) and [Leo on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-6th-house-cusp/), births a little after yours and a little before it. The recorded time is what tells them apart, and why I don't read a chart without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Tradition also looked to the 5th for children. I read it in an adult's own life, never for whether or when a child will come ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/). With Leo here, what I read is your own delight.
