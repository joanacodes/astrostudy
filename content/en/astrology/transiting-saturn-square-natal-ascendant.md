Saturn squares your rising degree twice in each round, once from the bottom of your chart and once from the top. Each time, the way you habitually step into things meets a demand it wasn't built for, and something in your manner has to adjust.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) brings structure, duty and the long view; the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is your orientation, the first stance you take before thinking. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates them through friction, and in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the friction is between your usual approach and what your circumstances now require. The quick starter is asked for follow-through, the cautious one for a decision, and the person who always pleases for a clear position.

The two squares come from opposite ends of the chart's vertical. The first, about seven years after Saturn crosses your Ascendant, falls near your IC, where home and private foundations ask your manner to change, close in time to [transiting Saturn opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-midheaven/). The second, about seven years before the next crossing, falls near your Midheaven, where public responsibilities press on it, close to [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-midheaven/). Close, but rarely the same: the Midheaven is seldom exactly 90° from the Ascendant, so a square and the crossing of an angle can come weeks apart or more than a year apart.

Friction isn't failure. What a square to the Ascendant usually leaves behind is a more deliberate manner, one that fits the life you have now.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

I time it with orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after, and within them the square usually lasts about nine months. A little over half the time Saturn's loop brings three exact hits, over about nine and a half months; with a single hit, three weeks is possible. Most people meet five squares to their Ascendant by 80, some six.

Your rising degree sets the dates, and a ten-minute error in your birth time moves them by more than two months: the Ascendant covers a degree in about four minutes, Saturn in about a month. That, and the houses Saturn crosses, are why I start no reading without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Saturn is in Aries until April 2028: with Capricorn rising, that's the square near your IC; with Cancer rising, the one near your Midheaven. Between the two squares lie [transiting Saturn trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-ascendant/), around your 5th and 9th houses, and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-ascendant/), on your Descendant; [transiting Saturn sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-ascendant/) comes before the first and after the second, and [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) marks the start and end of the round.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the habit of approach that no longer fits, and try its opposite once a week: speak where you'd wait, wait where you'd speak.

Make your private and public arrangements match. Under this square, a home routine that supports your outward role, or the reverse, does more than willpower.

Give the adjustment time. A square to the Ascendant changes a manner, and manners change by practice, not by decision.
