The scales are Libra's symbol, and Lilith in Libra is the hand that was kept off them. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point where the Moon's orbit swings farthest from Earth, was in [Libra](/astrology/libra/) when you were born, and it marks the half of the bargain you were taught not to claim — and the anger that comes when the scales are rigged.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Libra is cardinal air: it weighs, compares and starts things between people, looking for a balance both sides can live with. With Lilith in it, the part that went underground is usually your own weight. Maybe you kept the peace at home, or learned that disagreeing made people leave, so you became expert at everyone's side but yours. Underneath stayed a very exact sense of fairness, and a very particular anger when something isn't fair.

The refusal is measured and precise. It doesn't storm out; it names the imbalance, often calmly, sometimes after a long time. You'll meet someone halfway, and the hidden part knows to the inch where halfway is. When an arrangement only ever bends one way, this Lilith refuses it, politely and completely.

Mean Lilith enters Libra by crossing the point where the Sun stands at the September equinox, and stays about nine months. It was there from January to October 1936, November 1944 to August 1945, September 1953 to June 1954, July 1962 to April 1963, May 1971 to February 1972, April to December 1980, February to November 1989, December 1997 to September 1998 and October 2006 to July 2007. Those are mean Lilith's months, and a true Lilith can land in a different sign ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). You share the sign with everyone born in the same stretch; its house, which only [the birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#what-it-doesnt) can give, makes it yours.

Fairness matters in my readings too: I tell people which parts of a reading stand on placements that held less often in my tests. Lilith isn't one of those. It's one of [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), 75 to 90% of the time.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

This is where Lilith in Libra shows most. You may be very good at partnership on paper — attentive, considerate, quick to see the other point of view — while keeping your own needs off the agenda. The hidden part keeps the score anyway. If you're always the one adjusting, it records every adjustment, and one day the account is presented in full.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/), Libra's ruler, shows by sign and house what you're weighing when you weigh a relationship. And between [Lilith in Virgo](/astrology/lilith-in-virgo/), which notices what's wrong, and [Lilith in Scorpio](/astrology/lilith-in-scorpio/), which wants the truth underneath, Lilith in Libra asks a simpler, harder question: is this fair to both of us?

## At work {#at-work}

At work this is the colleague who points out that the rota is uneven, that the credit went to the wrong person, that the contract favours one side. You can be a superb negotiator once you allow yourself a side. The hidden part refuses deals that look balanced and aren't, and resents being cast as the diplomat who smooths over what should be fixed.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Keeping the peace on the surface while keeping score underneath is exhausting, and it turns small unfairnesses into large reckonings. The other version is a refusal to compromise at all, because compromise once cost too much. Neither is the balance this Lilith is actually after.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put your own weight on the scales early, while the amounts are small: *I'd rather we didn't*, *that doesn't work for me*, *my turn.* A disagreement said kindly is still kind, and a fairness that includes you is the only kind worth keeping.

Let other people meet you halfway, and notice when they do; not every arrangement is rigged, and some are just new. When the old anger rises at something unfair, ask whose it is. Sometimes it belongs to this situation. Sometimes it's every time nobody counted your side.
