Every couple has a way of holding what it owns; with a composite Pluto in the 2nd house, that way of holding is where the relationship changes most. Its deepest turns come through money, possessions and values, and through the question of what makes the pair feel safe.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) holds the relationship's resources: shared money, what the two of you own, the skills you pool, the values you live by together, and the sense of security all that brings. Those are the things [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) reworks here. Astrologers read a bond that rebuilds its finances from the ground up at turning points, after a redundancy, a business started together, a move that means starting again with very little, and that comes out of each rebuilding with clearer values than before. What the pair decides it can do without often tells you more about it than what it buys.

The composite belongs to the relationship, and this one can surprise both of you: two people who are relaxed about money on their own can find that, together, money is where everything important gets decided. Compare a single chart, where [Pluto in the 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-in-2nd-house/) is one person's deep need for security, or a synastry, where [their Pluto in your 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-in-2nd-house-synastry/) presses on another's resources and self-worth: here, the need belongs to the pair.

Where this goes wrong is in confusing control with safety. A relationship that feels secure only when it knows where every penny is can drift into one person holding the purse strings and the other asking permission, or into keeping possessions, savings and old arrangements long after they've stopped serving anyone. What helps is openness both ways: an account both of you can see, a sum each can spend without explaining it, and a yearly look at what the pair values now rather than ten years ago.

A 2nd-house Pluto describes how the pair holds what it has, never how much it will have. No chart predicts money, debt or a windfall, and I never read one for that ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)). If money is being used to control one of you, an allowance doled out, accounts kept out of sight, debts run up in your name, that isn't something to interpret but something to get help with, and the lines on my Disclaimers page, Samaritans on 116 123 among them, are a place to start ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)).

Like a joint account, this house needs both signatures: without both birth times the composite has no 2nd house at all, and with them its cusp still depends on whether it's taken as the midpoint of your two 2nd-house cusps or cast from the composite Midheaven for the place you live ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Birth data isn't joint property, though: each of you hands over your own, knowing what it's for ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Before it, [composite Pluto in the 1st house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-1st-house/) remakes how the pair appears; after it, [composite Pluto in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-3rd-house/) remakes how it talks. In the 2nd, the change goes through the bank statement and the short list of things you'd never sell.
