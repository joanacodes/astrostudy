Some pairs are known as a pair: at work, in their community, sometimes further afield. A composite Pluto in the 10th house puts the relationship's deepest changes at the top of its chart, in what it takes on in the world and how it's seen doing it.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) begins at the composite [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the highest point of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), and covers the relationship's public life: shared work or a joint enterprise, the role it plays in a family, a firm or a town, its reputation, and its ambitions for what it will leave behind. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) makes that public role the arena of transformation. Astrologers read a bond that others see as a force, and that changes most through what it takes on: a family business rebuilt after a crisis, a cause the pair leads together, a joint career that keeps reinventing itself, a standing in the community that grows, gets tested and is earned again.

The power here is the pair's, not either person's. Where [Pluto in the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-in-10th-house/) describes one person with a public life of weight, and [their Pluto in your 10th house](/astrology/pluto-in-10th-house-synastry/) one person's depth pressing on the other's ambitions, the composite's 10th gives that weight to the relationship: two people with no wish for authority on their own can find that, together, they end up running things.

The trap is authority turned on others. A pair that gains standing together can start using it: the joint business run as a private kingdom, colleagues managed through the couple's alliance, a public image kept so polished that no private trouble can ever be admitted. Authority isn't domination, and a reputation can survive honesty. It helps to keep one area of life where neither of you is in charge, and a few friends who knew you before anyone else did.

If one partner's career is being controlled or sabotaged by the other, earnings taken, work forbidden, a job made impossible, that's not a 10th-house theme but a reason to get help: 999 in a UK emergency, Samaritans on 116 123, with more numbers on my Disclaimers page ([when to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/)). None of this promises success, fame or failure, either; a composite shows how a pair carries its public role, never what the world will make of it.

The 10th needs both birth times, and its starting point moves quickly ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Each natal Midheaven travels about a degree every four minutes on average, so a quarter of an hour's error in either time shifts the composite Midheaven by about two degrees, enough to carry a Pluto that sits close to it between the 9th and the 10th. The rest of the house, up to the 11th cusp, depends on the method as well. Work is also where people are most tempted to chart someone without asking: I never read anyone for hiring, firing or promotion, and a composite of two colleagues waits for both of them to agree ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

[Composite Pluto in the 9th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-9th-house/) looks to the far horizon; [composite Pluto in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-pluto-in-11th-house/), to friends, groups and causes. In the 10th, the pair stands in full view.
