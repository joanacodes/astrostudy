Most relationships inherit a script for what a partnership should look like. With composite Uranus in the 7th house, this one writes its own, and keeps the pen.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The 7th begins at the composite [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), opposite the Ascendant, and that is exactly where the two ways of drawing [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) part company: the midpoint of your two natal Descendants by one method, the point opposite an Ascendant rebuilt for the place where you live by the other. A few degrees either side of that line, Uranus can change between the 6th and the 7th with the method. Both methods need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), one more reason to know them before anyone reads this house.

In [the 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), an angular house whose natural sign is Libra, sit the partnership itself, its agreements, its allies, and the open rivals it meets as a pair. With [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) there, the relationship is its own experiment. It may live apart, keep separate names and friends, divide roles in ways neither family recognises, or renew its commitment in terms that change every few years. Other couples tend to notice that this one doesn't follow their pattern. Freedom isn't the opposite of commitment here; it is one of its clauses. The pair meets other people in its own way too: collaborations struck up quickly, alliances that shift, a way of standing together in a disagreement that surprises the other side. Two people who expected a traditional partnership can find that what they built together is nothing of the kind, and that it suits them better for being theirs.

The natal version, [Uranus in the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-in-7th-house/), is one person's need for room inside partnership; the synastry version, [their Uranus in your 7th house](/astrology/uranus-in-7th-house-synastry/), is the jolt one partner brings to the other's idea of it. In the composite, the need for room is the partnership's own, and both feel it. Libra, this house's sign, gives [composite Uranus in Libra](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-libra/) to couples now in their fifties; the 7th house can come to a couple of any age.

The trap is a commitment that always sounds provisional. If freedom is mentioned more often than loyalty, one of you may start to hear every renegotiation as a step towards the door. What helps is to state the commitment and the freedom in the same breath, so that neither has to be guessed at, and to treat a change in the terms as the partnership working, not failing. A regular conversation about what each of you needs more of, and less of, keeps the renegotiating out in the open instead of hanging in the air.

Nothing here predicts a wedding, a separation or a divorce. Next door, the change goes elsewhere: into daily routine with [composite Uranus in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-6th-house/), into the depths of trust with [composite Uranus in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-uranus-in-8th-house/). My readings compare two birth charts in a [synastry](/astrology/synastry/); the composite is a technique I describe without drawing, read alongside them, and a relationship chart is made only from the data of two people who both agreed ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
