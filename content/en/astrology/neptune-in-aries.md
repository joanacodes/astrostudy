Neptune crossed into Aries in March 2025, went back and forth over the border until January 2026, and stays until March 2039. Aries is the first sign of the zodiac, so this opens a new round of Neptune's 165-year journey. The last round began in 1861.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Aries](/astrology/aries/), cardinal fire ruled by Mars, starts things: it acts first, asks later, and trusts the body's courage. In Aries, the imagination of [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) wants to do more than dream. Its compassion steps in instead of looking on, and its ideals take the shape of a first move: a cause to start, a rescue to attempt, a world to begin again. Neptune has no dignity here. [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the ruler of Aries, is the planet to read beside it, because Mars shows how a dream like this gets moving.

Aries is also where the zodiac starts again after Pisces, so Neptune arrives here straight from its own sign. The imagination that had no edges in Pisces now meets the sign of the single, separate self, and the dream has to find a body: one person's nerve, one first step.

Neptune was last in Aries from April 1861 to April 1875, so nobody alive today was born under it before now. The people born with it are the babies of 2025 and 2026, and everyone still to come until 2039. The border months are shared: until January 2026 with [Neptune in Pisces](/astrology/neptune-in-pisces/), and from May 2038 with [Neptune in Taurus](/astrology/neptune-in-taurus/).

I don't read [children’s charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/). So I describe Neptune in Aries as a generation's sign, the dream in the air of these years, and leave each person's house, the part that makes it theirs, for the day they're old enough to ask.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

As a generation's placement, Neptune in Aries describes an ideal of love as courage: the brave declaration, the first step taken for someone else, a closeness that proves itself in action. It's a dream of love as a rescue, or as two people setting out together. I won't say how any child born now will love. The sign only describes the ideal it carries, and that ideal has a tender side, protection, and a hasty one, falling for the thrill of falling.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, the dream of Neptune in Aries is the pioneer with a cause: starting something new because it ought to exist, acting on compassion rather than discussing it, leading by going first. When the people born with it are adults, their own charts will show where that dream meets a working life, and the house and sign of Mars will matter there as much as Neptune's.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Every ideal has a shadow, and this one's is haste. Inspiration and impulse can look alike in Aries, and a dream of fresh starts can make it hard to stay with what has already begun. Courage, idealised, can turn into the belief that only bold acts count. And for adults watching this generation arrive, there's a hard part too: generational signs invite prophecy, and a sign is not a forecast for anyone's future.

My [notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) was a slower kind of courage, one placement at a time. Neptune's sign and house were checked like every other placement; Neptune wasn't one of the three that held far more often, Saturn, Chiron and Lilith, and overall about a third held.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Patience, mostly, which Aries finds hard and Neptune finds easier. Let the sign stay a description of a sky rather than a plan for a person. And if you're an adult and curious, look at your own chart instead: Neptune is crossing it now, and the house it moves through until 2039 is where, in these years, an ideal may be asking you to act.
