Sooner or later the teacher turns out to be human. Under this opposition, the people you look to for meaning, or who look to you for it, show their own sore spots, and your beliefs about how a life should go are asked to make room for one that went differently.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) holds your principles: the beliefs that give a life its frame, the advice you'd give anyone, your trust in growth. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is a limitation that persists and the patient skill of living alongside it. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, usually met through other people, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your convictions and somebody's old difficulty face each other.

It can go either way round. A mentor, a teacher or a guide you admired shows a wound they never mentioned, and you have to fit their humanity into the respect you have for them. Or someone who looks up to you brings a hurt that your usual answers can't tidy away, and the honest response is to listen rather than explain. Jupiter's risk is mistaking a belief for a certainty; Chiron's, mistaking expertise for a complete cure. The opposition corrects both: your frame grows wide enough to include what can't be fixed.

Nothing here forecasts a loss of faith, money or standing, and no transit sets the course of a belief.

With [Jupiter in the 2nd house](/astrology/jupiter-in-2nd-house/), where you keep what you value and rely on, Chiron is crossing your 8th ([Chiron transiting the 8th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-8th-house/)): your trust in what you can count on meets an old wariness about closeness and sharing. Both houses come from your birth time; without it I don't read the opposition ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If your chart holds [Jupiter opposite Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-chiron/), balancing the wide view against the tender spot is old work for you, and the transit moves it out into the people around you.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My orbs run from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and within them the opposition usually lasts about ten months. In nearly two passes out of three Chiron crosses it three times, once going backwards; about one in three is a single crossing of some three weeks; about one in twenty is a five-crossing stretch of around twenty-one months.

The first opposition comes at ages spread over half a century, because Jupiter's place at birth changes from one year to the next. In the site's count it came at about 24 in the middle, from the first months of life to about 50: near 6 for someone born at the start of 1985, about 17 for 2005, 20 for 1975, 21 for 1945, 33 for 1965, 40 for 1995, 45 for 1935 and 49 for 1955. There is one in each round of about 50 years, so by 80 most people have met it twice and everyone once.

It stands at the far side of the round: [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Chiron sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-jupiter/), the first [transiting Chiron square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-jupiter/) and the first [transiting Chiron trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-jupiter/) come before it, the same aspects in reverse after. Ahead: Chiron in Aries opposes Jupiter in Libra until April 2027; in Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, Jupiter in Scorpio; in Gemini, from July 2033 to April 2039, Jupiter in Sagittarius.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Listen to someone's whole story before offering a single principle.

Ask a person you admire what they got wrong, and what it taught them.

Let one belief be questioned by somebody's experience, and notice whether it bends or breaks.

Share the role of the wise one. Being the person with answers can keep you from asking your own questions.
