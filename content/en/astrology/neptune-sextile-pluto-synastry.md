Two friends born a couple of years apart can share an outlook so completely that it feels personal, when much of it is the air of their years. Neptune sextile Pluto in synastry sets one person's Neptune about 60° from the other's Pluto, within 2.4°, and of all the Neptune–Pluto contacts it is the one people of nearly the same age most often share.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is the function of ideals and compassion, of symbolic meaning and of boundaries that can soften. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the function of renewal: it eliminates what no longer works and regenerates what remains. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates two functions through opportunity, an opening that becomes something only when used; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one lets one person's vision and the other's capacity for deep change help each other.

The help runs both ways. The Neptune person offers the other's drive a direction worth the effort, a reason beyond power; the Pluto person offers the ideals a way to become real, with the patience to clear the ground first.

With the Neptune on your side, you may find the other willing to do the hard, structural part of what you can only imagine.

With the Pluto on your side, the other's hopes give your appetite for change a gentler purpose, and some company.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

What's common can pass for chemistry. Near-contemporaries share this contact so widely that "we see the world the same way" may describe two people's years more than the two people. The opening also stays unused unless someone acts: a shared vision of change that nobody starts is still only a mood.

- **[Neptune conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-pluto-synastry/):** an elder's ideal resting directly on a younger person's depth.
- **[Neptune square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-square-pluto-synastry/):** a right angle between ideal and renewal, with the Pluto usually the elder's.
- **[Neptune trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-pluto-synastry/):** an easy current between ideal and depth, across about a generation.
- **[Neptune opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** the two face each other between people born more than fifty years apart.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Turn the shared outlook into a shared project. Friends who agree on what should change can choose one thing to change; colleagues can pair one person's vision with the other's persistence; a couple can test their common ideals against a real decision; adult siblings can notice where their generation's views end and their own begin.

Nothing on the site moves more slowly than a Neptune–Pluto contact. Someone born in mid-1975 has this one with anyone born, on and off, from January 1972 to August 1977, each pair holding a Neptune in Sagittarius and a Pluto in Libra, one on each side. It can also link people more than fifty years apart, as with someone born in mid-1935 and anyone born, on and off, from January 1987 to October 1989. With so much of a generation sharing it, I leave it out of my synastry count, where every contact touches the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Through the houses, I read it as a difference between two eras, which for near-contemporaries is hardly a difference at all.

The hour can't touch a sextile between two bodies this slow; it decides where the sextile lives, with the Neptune falling in the Pluto person's 3rd house, say, among words and ideas, and that needs both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Nearly everyone born between 1943 and 1993 carries [Neptune sextile Pluto](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto/) in their own chart, which is why it repeats so often between two. Whether to read it is still a question for both people ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
