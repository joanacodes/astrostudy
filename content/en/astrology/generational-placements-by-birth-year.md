The Moon changes sign every two and a half days, the Sun every month. Uranus, Neptune and Pluto take years, so everyone born across those years shares their signs. Here they are by birth month, from 1930 to 2009, with what I do and don't read into them.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

The slower a planet, the more people share its sign. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) spends about a year in each, [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) about two and a half. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) needs about seven, [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) about fourteen, and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), on its stretched orbit, anything from twelve to thirty-one. Those three outer planets are the ones astrologers call generational: their sign is a date stamp, shared by a whole cohort.

The periods below are the months this site computes. At each edge, a retrograde takes the planet back and forth across the border, so neighbouring periods overlap; for a birth in the overlap, the chart says which sign.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

**Uranus,** by birth month:

- **Aries:** until March 1935.
- **Taurus:** June 1934 to May 1942.
- **Gemini:** August 1941 to June 1949.
- **Cancer:** August 1948 to June 1956.
- **Leo:** August 1955 to August 1962.
- **Virgo:** November 1961 to June 1969.
- **Libra:** September 1968 to September 1975.
- **Scorpio:** November 1974 to November 1981.
- **Sagittarius:** February 1981 to December 1988.
- **Capricorn:** February 1988 to January 1996.
- **Aquarius:** April 1995 to December 2003.
- **Pisces:** March 2003 to March 2011.

**Neptune:**

- **Virgo:** until August 1943.
- **Libra:** October 1942 to August 1957.
- **Scorpio:** December 1955 to November 1970.
- **Sagittarius:** January 1970 to November 1984.
- **Capricorn:** January 1984 to November 1998.
- **Aquarius:** January 1998 to February 2012.

**Pluto:**

- **Cancer:** until June 1939.
- **Leo:** October 1937 to June 1958.
- **Virgo:** October 1956 to July 1972.
- **Libra:** October 1971 to August 1984.
- **Scorpio:** November 1983 to November 1995.
- **Sagittarius:** January 1995 to November 2008.
- **Capricorn:** January 2008 to November 2024.

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is uneven too. In the site's samples, taken on 1 January and 1 July of each year, it was in Pisces and then Aries for births from 1960–61 to 1976–77, and crossed Virgo and Libra in the mid-1940s and again in the mid-1990s.

A shared sign also means shared timing. A cohort meets the slow planets' cycles at about the same age, and that age shifts from one cohort to the next: Uranus opposes its birth place anywhere from about 39 to 44½, depending on the birth year ([Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/)). Since 2023 all three outer planets have changed sign, Pluto into Aquarius, Neptune into Aries and Uranus into Gemini ([Uranus in Gemini, 2026–2033](/astrology/uranus-in-gemini-2026-2033/)); [planetary ingresses](/astrology/planetary-ingresses/) explains how such changes of sign work.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

A generational sign tells me which sky you were born under, and that sky is shared, so on its own it says little about you. What makes Uranus, Neptune or Pluto yours is first the house it falls in, which needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), then its contacts with your personal planets and angles: Pluto square your Moon, Neptune on your Ascendant. Those change from one birthday to the next, and they are where I start.

In the system I built for my readings, a planet is a function and a sign a manner. Innovation, imagination and transformation are the same functions in everyone; the sign shows the manner a cohort's charts give them. I don't describe what a generation is like, and I don't read children's charts ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“My generation's Pluto sign describes my character.”** It dates your birth to a span of years shared with everyone born then. The house it falls in and what it touches describe you.
- **“Everyone born in the same year has the same outer planets.”** Not at the edges: a planet can cross a border, go back and cross again, so births a few months apart can differ.
- **“Generational placements don't matter in a personal chart.”** They do once they touch a personal planet or an angle, and their house always says where they work.
- **“The ages of life transits are the same for every generation.”** They shift with the birth year: in the births computed, the [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/) came anywhere from 35.5 to 59.3.
