The IC sits at the foot of the chart, where the 4th house begins: home, family, the private self. Virgo on the 4th house cusp makes that foundation a well-kept one, held together by care that's mostly practical.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/) is the chart's root ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and with [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) on it the root is tended daily. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) I see here is a home that works: things have their place, repairs get done, the cupboard is restocked before it runs out. Care is shown in kind more than in words, the meal ready, the dripping tap fixed, the paperwork sorted for a parent who finds it hard. You may have learned early that being useful was how you belonged, and part of you still unwinds by tidying, cooking or mending. The private self is quieter and more anxious than your public face suggests, and it settles when the day is in order. The risk is a home that turns into a second job, where you never sit down because something always needs doing.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your 4th house, and the house it occupies shows which part of your life home serves and feeds: look for it, as ruler of the 4th, in the [1st](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). At London, every chart with a Virgo IC has [Cancer rising](/astrology/cancer-rising/): the Moon, the 4th house's natural ruler, rules the whole chart, while Mercury rules the house itself. Home sits close to the centre of how you meet the world, and it's run on Mercury's lines. Gemini, Mercury's other sign, opens the 12th in about 3 London charts in 4, so the planet that rules your home also rules your time alone: home is often where you retreat, and where unseen work gets done.

A Virgo IC is a little rarer than average, about 1 birth in 13, the same at London as at New York with Placidus cusps, the kind I use. Virgo lies just before the point where the Sun's path crosses the equator in September, and near that crossing the path runs at a slant to the equator, so Virgo's 30° cover less of the turning sky than most signs' do, and cross the IC in about 1 hour 51 minutes a day; the time of day alone sets the IC's sign, so latitude changes nothing. Opposite, Pisces holds the Midheaven in every case, and Neptune and Jupiter, its two rulers, govern your public life; I read both ([Pisces on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-10th-house-cusp/)). At London the 4th spans 33° to 39° in these charts, and about once in four Libra fits inside it whole, giving Venus a say at home ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Virgo itself goes without any cusp in about 1 London birth in 6.

Under two hours a day leaves little room for a rounded time. Half an hour either way can be enough to hand this cusp to a neighbouring sign, leaving Virgo on the 3rd cusp ([Virgo on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-3rd-house-cusp/)) after a later birth or on the 5th ([Virgo on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-5th-house-cusp/)) after an earlier one. I don't guess between them; without the time there's no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
