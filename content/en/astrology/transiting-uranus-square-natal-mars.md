A right angle between Uranus and your Mars comes round twice in 84 years. Each time, for some twenty months, the way you push and the urge to break out of the usual channel keep catching on each other.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is effort, assertion and the drive to get things done. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and wants autonomy. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, holds two functions in friction until something adapts, and with Mars the friction is physical as well as mental: you want to move, and the usual routes feel blocked or too slow.

It often shows as impatience with limits: a rule that suddenly seems absurd, a boss or a system you can't work around, a plan that stalls while you're ready to go. Energy comes in surges, and competition sharpens along with the wish to win by a method of your own. Some people change how they work or train, some take on a challenge they'd have avoided before, and some simply notice how much of their effort went into waiting.

If you've read that this square brings accidents, put that down: I don't read transits for injuries ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and haste is something you can choose against. Assertion isn't aggression, and courage isn't recklessness; the square asks you to tell them apart.

With Mars in your 7th and Uranus crossing your 4th ([Uranus transiting the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-4th-house/)), changes at home press on the way you stand your ground with the people closest to you: who decides, who moves, who gives way. The 7th and the 4th are drawn from your birth time; without it the square has no setting, and I don't read it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If [Mars square Uranus](/astrology/mars-square-uranus/) is in your birth chart, the friction will feel familiar, and so may the remedy you've already learned for it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Uranus needs about twenty months to get through this square, from 1½° before exact to 1° after; about one pass in ten takes eleven months or less. Three exact hits are the norm, in more than nine passes out of ten, so the pressure tends to come in three waves rather than one.

The squares are the two hard turns of Uranus's round with your Mars, one on each side of [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-mars/). [Transiting Uranus sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-mars/) comes just before the first square and [transiting Uranus trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-mars/) just after it, while [transiting Uranus opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-mars/) falls halfway between the two squares. Most lives that reach 80 see both squares and none sees neither, which puts this [transit](/astrology/transits/) in nearly everyone's diary at least once.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose your battles. Under this square everything can look worth fighting, so pick the change that matters most and give it your energy.

Build pauses into your days. Deliberate breaks keep a surge of energy from turning into haste.

Use frustration as information. What irritates you most in these months usually marks the constraint that's ready to go.
