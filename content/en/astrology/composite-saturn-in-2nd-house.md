The sofa bought together twenty years ago and still in the sitting room, the savings jar with its rules, the long conversation about what's worth spending on. With composite Saturn in the 2nd house, security is made slowly, and kept.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) is what a person has and values; in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it's what the relationship has and values: shared possessions, the means the pair lives on, and the sense, built over time, of what is worth keeping. With [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) here, stewardship becomes a commitment. The pair budgets, repairs, saves up for things rather than borrowing for them, and tends to keep what it buys for a long time. Agreeing on values may take years, but once agreed, they hold.

Values count here as much as things. Time, attention and skills are resources too, and a pair with this Saturn tends to treat them the same way: budgeted, protected, spent on what lasts. Across the chart, the 8th house holds what a relationship shares with others or owes them; the 2nd is what it holds in its own name, which is why a Saturn here concerns the decisions the two of you make between yourselves more than any outside obligation ([composite Saturn in the 8th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-8th-house/)).

It's a succedent house, Taurus is its natural sign, and no planet has its joy here; what puts Saturn in it is arithmetic on both birth times. The 2nd's cusp can be the midpoint of your two natal 2nd-house cusps, or it can be rebuilt from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live, and a Saturn close to either edge may change house with the method, or with ten minutes on either clock ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Neither of you needs Saturn in your own 2nd house for the bond to have it. [Saturn in the 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-in-2nd-house/) describes one person learning self-worth the long way, and [their Saturn in your 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-in-2nd-house-synastry/) one person's caution meeting the other's resources. The composite version is a habit the pair forms together, which is how two easy spenders can become, as a household, careful stewards.

On the wheel, it sits between [composite Saturn in the 1st house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-1st-house/), where the bond's seriousness is on show, and [composite Saturn in the 3rd house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-3rd-house/), where it goes into words. Here it goes into a ledger, and into the things a pair chooses to own.

The danger is caution turning into scarcity. Every purchase becomes a negotiation, one person keeps the accounts and with them a quiet power, and possessions start to stand in for proof that the relationship is secure. It helps to write down what the two of you actually value and to spend something on it regularly, pleasure included; to share the bookkeeping, or at least the knowledge of it; and to remember that a composite describes how a bond holds things, never how much it will have.

Composite charts are outside my practice. This is the technique as it's practised, beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and money is one subject I never read a chart for, composite or not.
