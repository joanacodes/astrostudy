Sagittarius wants a philosophy to live by; Aquarius wants a world that works better for everyone. Your Sun and Moon carry one each, and you were born under a young crescent Moon, a few evenings after the new moon. The result, as I read it, is a far-sighted self whose needs are met in company of minds.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aquarius lies two signs on from Sagittarius: a sextile by sign, fixed air beside mutable fire, the element that fans a flame. The [Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/) builds its sense of self around a conviction it keeps testing; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) steadies itself in good company of minds: the friend it can call after a year's silence and pick up mid-sentence, the cause it has signed up to, the evening spent working out how something could be done better. The sextile offers cooperation, and here it is easy to take up: your beliefs and your loyalties tend to point the same way.

Aquarius has two rulers, and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern one, for where this Moon needs freedom to try things its own way, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional one, for the commitments that let it stay close without feeling fenced in. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules your Sun's sign. Jupiter has no dignity in Aquarius, nor Saturn in Sagittarius, and the lights have none either. Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, so its sign belongs to a generation; its house, which needs your birth time, is what makes the Moon's need for freedom personal.

This is a crescent birth. Your Moon had moved 30° to 89° ahead of the Sun, two and a half to seven days after the new moon, and it set in the evening a few hours after the Sun. The [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) takes about seven of these births in eight, the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase the rest, and near 60° the lights form a waxing [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/), the sextile by sign made exact. A crescent birth, to me, is need gathering the nerve to go its own way; with an Aquarius Moon it goes towards other people and new ideas. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains the crescent.

About 1 person in 146 has this pair. When the Moon left Capricorn or reached Pisces on your birthday, the time tells this page from the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-capricorn/) and the [Sun in Sagittarius, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius-moon-in-pisces/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The same time gives the rising sign, which completes the first sketch these two lights begin, and the houses of Uranus and Saturn.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Friendship comes first for this pair, and often stays at the heart of love. You want a partner who is also a fellow thinker, with views of their own, a life of their own and an appetite for the same big questions. You give loyalty and honesty in good measure. What can go missing is small, personal attention: the bad day noticed, the hand held, the feeling said rather than explained. Partners who need closeness in that form may feel you are with them in principle more than in person. Show up in person too.

## At work {#at-work}

Ideas with a social purpose suit you: education, research, technology, campaigning, international work, anything that asks how things could work better for many people. The Sun brings vision and the willingness to say what others only think; the Moon brings networks, a sense of fairness and steadiness when a group gets heated. Micromanagement wears this pair down. A role with a mission you believe in, colleagues you respect and freedom to experiment gets the best from both lights.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Both lights are at home with the large view, which can leave the near one unattended. A belief and a principle can both sit some way above the feeling underneath, so you may argue well for a cause without noticing that you are hurt. Other people's need for closeness can read as pressure. What helps is translating the big into the small: one friend checked on this week, one feeling named before it turns into an opinion, one evening without a project.
