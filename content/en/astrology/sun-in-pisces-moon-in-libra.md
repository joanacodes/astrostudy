A Pisces Sun accepts people as they come; a Libra Moon is at ease when what passes between two people is even. The Moon had only just turned from full when you were born. Here I see a wide compassion that still needs someone who gives back.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Pisces is mutable water and Libra cardinal air, and five signs separate them: by sign a [quincunx](/astrology/quincunx/), an angle with no shared element, mode or polarity to lean on. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) grows around what moves it and what it imagines, and takes people as they come; the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) feels secure when there is balance between two people, courtesy, beauty and a fair share of attention each way. You may forgive a friend almost anything and still feel quietly hurt if the invitations only ever go one way.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra, yet in your chart it can't stand there: it keeps within about 47° of your Sun, which puts it somewhere between Capricorn and Taurus. If it shares your Sun's sign it is in its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), and then the ruler of your Moon works from inside Pisces, lending your need for harmony the Sun's tenderness. With Pisces I read both rulers: [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the older ruler, for the generosity your Sun grows into, and [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), the modern one, for its longing to be at one with someone. The Sun and Moon themselves hold no dignity here.

Your Moon had travelled 181° to 239° from the Sun, so a full moon had come a few hours to almost five days before you were born, most often a [full moon in Virgo](/astrology/full-moon-in-virgo/). The great majority of these births fall in the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase, and about one in eight has crossed into the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/); the waning quincunx, exact at 210°, lies inside the range. I read the full moon phase as the moment when what began at the new moon is seen clearly, often through another person, which suits a Libra Moon. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) marks the line at 225°.

Roughly one birth in 149 brings this pair. Because the Moon crosses Libra in about two and a half days, if you were born the day it came in from Virgo or moved on to Scorpio, your page is one of three, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-virgo/), this one or the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-scorpio/), and nothing but the hour can settle it, which is why [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon) singles out the Moon. The hour also sets the houses, and with a Libra Moon I want to know which house holds Venus, since that is where your Moon looks for its balance.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love tenderly and with real courtesy, and partnership itself draws you. The Pisces Sun gives and forgives; the Libra Moon notices whether the giving comes back, and is unsettled when it doesn't. The risk is accepting less than you need and then carrying the imbalance in silence. Name what would make things even: a fair relationship is one where both people can ask.

## At work {#at-work}

Mediation, counselling, design, music, the arts, diplomacy: work where kindness meets taste or fairness suits you. Your Sun lends empathy and imagination, your Moon grace, tact and an eye for proportion. You tend to do your best alongside others, in a team or a pair, where the effort is shared and the credit is too.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

This quincunx means many small adjustments between accepting everything and needing things to be fair. You may let an imbalance run for months because you understand why the other person can't do more, then feel the whole weight of it at once. Just after a full moon, as I read it, the task is to see the relationship clearly and say what you see. Being fair to yourself belongs to fairness too.
