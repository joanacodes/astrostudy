Their Sun in your 8th house is an overlay of depth. Another person's sense of purpose arrives in the part of your life you open only once trust is there: intimacy, what you share and what you owe, and the kind of closeness that changes people.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) begins where a bond gets serious: intimacy, sexual and otherwise, trust and what it costs to give it, money and decisions held in common, debts in both senses of the word. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the source of a person's aliveness, their sense of being someone with a direction. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their Sun here shines into rooms you don't show to many.

You may find that things go deep quickly with them. You tell them what you'd normally keep, ask for help you'd normally manage alone, or make a joint decision sooner than you meant to. Their presence seems to give you permission to be known, and the bond often leaves you changed, the way a long conversation at three in the morning can.

For them, your capacity for closeness can draw out more of who they are. Around you they show what they usually guard, and their sense of purpose gains weight from being trusted. Like every succedent house, the 8th is where things settle and build up, here whatever a bond creates, and its counterpart among the signs is [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/): loyalty, privacy, feeling that runs deep.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Depth asks for trust, and trust has its own pace. If their Sun moves faster than you're ready to open, you may feel exposed, or seen more clearly than you'd chosen; if you open quickly, you may later wonder who holds what. Questions of what's shared, whether money, time or confidences, tend to surface early with this overlay, and they're easier asked than assumed.

From their side, the difficulty is a house that keeps its doors closed. Their light wants to be welcomed, and your privacy can feel like being kept outside; or, once inside, they may feel your gaze reach further into them than they expected.

Tradition gave the 8th death and inheritance. I read neither, for anyone, in one chart or in two ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)): another person's Sun here describes trust, intimacy and what the two of you hold together, not a loss.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Go at the pace of whoever needs more time, and say so out loud: "not yet" is part of trust, not a refusal of it. In a couple, intimacy and joint money both go better when what's shared and what stays private are named early; between friends, agree which confidences can travel; between colleagues, keep shared budgets and sensitive matters on paper, with clear roles.

From their chart, the overlay becomes your Sun in their 8th house; the questions of pace and privacy simply change hands. [Their Sun in your 7th house](/astrology/sun-in-7th-house-synastry/) is the partnership that comes before; [their Sun in your 9th house](/astrology/sun-in-9th-house-synastry/) is where shared experience goes looking for meaning.

Where your 8th begins and ends depends on your birth time, and in Placidus, the house system I use, it can be a wide house or a narrow one. Their Sun travels about a degree a day, so its house is a question for your time, except at the 8th's edges, where theirs joins in ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). What sits in this house is personal, which is why it stays out of any reading the other person hasn't agreed to ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
