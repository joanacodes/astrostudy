Sixty degrees from your Mercury, Chiron opens a door that's easy to walk past: a class you'd written off, a way of explaining that finally fits, a reader for the page you never showed anyone. About three times in a life, for weeks or months, your words have a way round an old difficulty.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how you learn, interpret and exchange: curiosity, language, the meaning you make of things. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a difficulty stayed and you built other capacities around it, and it has a mentor's side: knowledge passed on from experience. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, puts the two within reach of each other, and something has to be reached for. This [transit](/astrology/transits/)'s openings are usually practical. A subject you were once steered away from, numbers, a language, public speaking, turns out to be learnable by another route. You meet someone who learned the way you do and can show you how. What you know from the long way round becomes material: a talk, a workshop, a piece of writing.

Curiosity isn't distraction, and the sextile tends to reward following it. Nothing arrives by itself, though. The door stays open for months, and it's as easy to admire it as to go through.

Your houses decide where this opening sits, one more thing the birth time settles ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With Mercury in the 11th, one of these sextiles comes while Chiron crosses your 1st ([Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/)): speaking as yourself, in your own voice and in front of others, gets easier to try. If [Mercury sextile Chiron](/astrology/mercury-sextile-chiron/) is in your chart, you already have a knack for putting hard experience into useful words; the transit is a season for doing it in public.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured from 1½° before exact to 1° after, my orbs, the sextile to Mercury takes about ten and a half months when Chiron loops over it three times, as it does in nearly two passes out of three. Roughly one pass in three is a single crossing, gone in about three weeks; one in twenty loops twice and crosses five times, over some twenty-one months.

Your Mercury is always within a sign or so of your Sun, so Chiron's sextiles to the two arrive close together ([transiting Chiron sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-sun/)), and now and then overlap. A lifetime to 80 usually holds three of these sextiles, occasionally two or four. The trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-mercury/)) makes the same words flow without effort, the square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-mercury/)) makes them hard-won, and both come as often; the conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-mercury/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-mercury/)) come once or twice.

From 2027 to 2033, crossing Taurus, Chiron sextiles Mercury in Cancer and in Pisces, the sign where tradition places Mercury in both detriment and fall. That colours the tone, towards images and feeling rather than lists, not the size of the opening.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sign up for the course, class or workshop you ruled out years ago.

Find someone who learned the way you do, and ask how they went about it.

Write one page about what you know from the hard way, and show it to one reader.
