Their table seats eight, and half the guests met each other through them. Composite Jupiter in Aquarius describes a relationship that grows by connecting people and ideas, and believes in what a group can do that two people can't.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

How far a relationship's circle reaches is one of the things [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) describes in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), along with what the bond learns and believes in. [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, steers it towards experiment, fairness and the future. Tradition neither strengthens nor weakens Jupiter in Aquarius. The sign has two rulers, [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in modern tropical astrology and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in the tradition, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): composite Saturn shows how the bond gives its ideals a frame, composite Uranus how far it is willing to change the frame.

The count drops from Capricorn to Aquarius: about 1 pair in 14 has composite Jupiter here, against 1 in 12 for Capricorn, among two million random pairs born from 1930 to 2009. Pairs of the same age share it when both were born while Jupiter crossed Aquarius, as from late February 1973 to early March 1974, from February 1985 to February 1986, or from late January 1997 to early February 1998. Across the zodiac, [composite Jupiter in Leo](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-leo/) is generous in the spotlight; in Aquarius, the generosity is spread across a network, and often unsigned.

It is the couple, more than either person, that reaches out. Two people who each keep to a small circle can find that, together, they run the residents' association, host the reading group, and introduce the friend looking for work to the friend who's hiring. Someone with [Jupiter in Aquarius](/astrology/jupiter-in-aquarius/) holds principles that concern the many; in a composite, those principles are the pair's, and they often take the form of experiments in how to live: an unusual arrangement of work and home, a shared house, a cause the two of you organise your year around. Its generosity often travels as access rather than objects: a contact passed on, a room lent to a friend of a friend, a skill taught to whoever turns up.

The risk is that the cause outgrows the couple. A bond that widens through groups can give its best ideas to everyone else and keep its own needs for last, or plan the future so well that the present goes unattended. Objectivity isn't coldness, and a shared vision needs a shared evening now and then. What helps is putting the relationship itself on the agenda: a night a week that belongs to no committee, and an ideal or two kept just between you.

Capricorn comes before it, and [composite Jupiter in Capricorn](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-capricorn/) grows by building; Pisces comes after, and [composite Jupiter in Pisces](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-pisces/) grows by compassion and imagination. I describe composites here as a technique and leave them out of my own readings. Those who draw them read this Jupiter alongside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and no midpoint predicts what a pair will achieve or how long it will last. No cause justifies drawing one in secret: it needs the consent of both people ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
