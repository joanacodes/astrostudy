Some changes are easiest to make from inside a structure, by the person who keeps it running. When Uranus in the sky reaches 60° from your natal Saturn, twice in most lives, you get about twenty months in which a rule, a routine or a commitment can be updated by proposal rather than by protest.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the function of structure and long-term responsibility: what you've committed to, the discipline that keeps it going, the limits you've learned. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is reform and experiment. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates the two through cooperation and opportunity, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) lets change come in through the front door of something you've built.

The opening tends to be modest and specific: a better method for work you've done the same way for years, a new agreement at work or at home about who carries what, a tool that saves an old commitment rather than replacing it. Saturn makes it last and Uranus makes it new, and the people around you may accept it easily, because it comes from someone they already rely on.

The sextile's limit is that it waits. Opportunity needs engagement, and Saturn can let the moment pass because the old way still works well enough. The question worth asking isn't whether the system is broken, but whether it could be better.

With Saturn in your 3rd and Uranus crossing your 5th ([Uranus transiting the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-5th-house/)), a discipline you trained hard for, a language, an instrument, a craft, can find a freer and more playful use. Those houses come from the clock time of your birth, which is why I won't read a chart that lacks it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

In a birth chart the same angle is [Saturn sextile Uranus](/astrology/saturn-sextile-uranus/): there, a lifelong readiness to modernise what you're responsible for; in transit, a season of it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My orbs run from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and within them Uranus's sextile to Saturn lasts about twenty months, eleven when the pass is short. Its three exact contacts, in more than nine passes out of ten, suit the way reform by proposal works: an idea put forward, adjusted, then adopted.

The ages scatter: in the site's count, about 9 for someone born at the start of 1985, about 46 for someone born at the start of 1975. The second can follow soon or very late, at about 37 for someone born at the start of 1955 but not before 80 for the start of 1935, so a few people meet only one; most meet two. Born with [Saturn conjunct Uranus](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-uranus/), in the early 1940s or the late 1980s, you meet them in step with Uranus's own sextiles to its birth place, at about 13 and about 70 ([transiting Uranus sextile natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-uranus/)).

On the circle, the two sextiles stand about fourteen years either side of [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-saturn/). Going on from the first, [transiting Uranus square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-saturn/) comes about seven years later and [transiting Uranus trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-saturn/) about seven more, while [transiting Uranus opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-saturn/) marks the halfway point.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put the change in writing. Saturn respects a plan, and under a sextile a clear proposal is often all it takes.

Replace one piece, not the foundations. Reform under this transit means updating a part of the structure that has fallen behind, while the rest goes on carrying its load.

Teach the new method once it holds. Saturn's mastery grows by being passed on, and a change you can explain to someone else is one that tends to last.
