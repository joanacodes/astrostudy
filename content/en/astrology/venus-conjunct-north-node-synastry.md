Venus never strays more than about 47° from the Sun, so if your Venus sits on someone's North Node, your birthday falls within about two months of the date the Sun crosses that point each year. Venus conjunct North Node in synastry puts what one person enjoys, and does without trying, right on the capacity the other is learning.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is how a person values and relates: who and what attracts them, what they prefer, their pleasures, their sense of a fair exchange. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a capacity someone is still building, less familiar and less automatic, that grows through practice and feedback; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), exactly opposite, holds what they've long done well. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions into one process, and in [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) each person carries one of them.

Carrying the node, you meet someone whose ordinary manner is your homework. Say your North Node is in Libra, where the work is sharing decisions after a long habit of making them alone, and the other's Venus is in Libra too, in its own sign: their easy way of asking what you'd like, of splitting a choice, of making room, is exactly what you're practising. Around them it looks possible, and it looks pleasant.

Carrying the Venus, you may notice that what you take for granted gets watched, asked about, tried out. You aren't teaching on purpose. Your affection simply arrives together with the capacity they want, and it can be touching to see your own habits through someone else's wish for them.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A conjunction blurs, and the blur here runs between liking someone and growing. The Node person can start to credit the other with their progress, or practise only in the other's company, so the new capacity stays on loan. The Venus person can end up feeling responsible for someone else's development without having signed up, and feel it as weight when the other stumbles.

Then there's the South Node, opposite your Venus. In the Libra example it sits in Aries, on the familiar ground of deciding alone, and around you that old independence can look less appealing to the other. That can feel like progress to them and, some days, like losing a part of themselves they were proud of. Nothing needs losing: the familiar strength stays, and the new one is added to it.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let the growth stay with the person whose node it is. If the North Node is yours, practise the new thing in other places as well, and thank the other for the example without making them the reason; if the Venus is yours, enjoy what you share and leave the pace to them. Between partners, that might be one person learning to consult the other on plans without being marked on it; between friends, picking up a pleasure from someone who has it naturally; at work, a colleague whose easy way with people shows a quieter one what's possible.

Out of every 15 pairs of people, about one has this conjunction, either way round, within 6° of the true node. The true and mean nodes differ by a degree or two at most; I check both, and they only disagree about this conjunction when Venus is near the edge of the orb ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The houses, and so where in each life this plays out, need both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Nodal contacts are sometimes sold as destiny. I read this one as encouragement, and with both people present ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

In one chart, [Venus conjunct North Node](/astrology/venus-conjunct-north-node/) puts a person's own way of relating on their growing edge. Between two, Venus can sit elsewhere around the other's axis: [Venus sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-north-node-synastry/) a step from the new and in trine with the old, [Venus square North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-north-node-synastry/) at right angles to both ends, [Venus trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-north-node-synastry/) flowing towards the growth, and [Venus opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-north-node-synastry/) right on the familiar end.
