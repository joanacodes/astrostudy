Uranus conjunct Neptune is the sky of the early and mid-1990s: the two outermost planets side by side, mostly in Capricorn. Nearly everyone born between 1990 and 1996 has it, so it describes a generation first, and you second, through your houses.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables innovation, differentiation and change in whole systems: it tries the untried and frees what has grown too tight. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) enables imagination, compassion and symbolic perception: it dissolves boundaries that no longer need to be there, and senses meanings larger than one life. Brought together by a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), the two work as a single unit, though neither loses its identity.

Fused, they give imagination a working edge. The vision doesn't stay a dream and the invention doesn't stay a gadget; each wants the other. I read it as an urge to imagine new systems, and to build systems that leave room for what can't be measured. Ideals and experiments arrive together, sometimes faster than you can tell which is which.

The meeting fell in [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), the sign of structures meant to last, so the shared theme is the reimagining of frameworks such as institutions and careers. For births of 1996, and of a few months in 1995, Uranus had crossed into Aquarius while Neptune stayed in Capricorn: the same meeting, with Uranus a step ahead.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

About 1 person in 13 born between 1930 and 2009 has this conjunction within 6°, and every one of them was born between January 1990 and March 1996, or between August and December 1996. No one born outside those months, in the eighty years counted, has it. It was exact three times, all in 1993, and the closer your birth to that year, the tighter the fusion. The two planets meet only about every 170 years: this is the one meeting anyone alive was born under.

Shared by a generation, it becomes personal through the houses ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)). Its house shows where invention and imagination keep meeting in your life: in the 7th, partnerships that ask to be imagined differently; in the 10th, work that changes how a field sees itself. A contact with your Sun, Moon or an angle turns the volume up. All of this needs a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Uranus and Neptune take decades to change angle, so each of their other aspects belongs to a span of birth years:

- **[Uranus sextile Neptune](/astrology/uranus-sextile-neptune/):** born on and off from 1963 to 1970; vision and invention open doors for each other when someone walks through.
- **[Uranus square Neptune](/astrology/uranus-square-neptune/):** born on and off from 1951 to 1958; ideals and reform rub until a workable form appears.
- **[Uranus trine Neptune](/astrology/uranus-trine-neptune/):** born on and off from 1936 to 1946; new ideas and wide ideals run in the same direction.
- **[Uranus opposite Neptune](/astrology/uranus-opposite-neptune/):** nobody born between 1930 and 2009 has it.

[Uranus conjunct Neptune in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-neptune-synastry/) is almost a given between two people born in the early 1990s, since both charts hold the pair in Capricorn. I give it weight when it lands on a personal planet or an angle of the other chart, and read it in both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

For people born outside these years, [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-neptune/) comes at an age set by the distance between the two in their chart. Uranus crosses a sign in about seven years, so the transit lasts months, often in three passes. [Transiting Neptune conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-uranus/) is slower: Neptune spends about 14 years in a sign and can stay near the point for a year or more.

Born with the conjunction, you meet both planets' turning points in the same stretch of mid-life. Around 41, transiting Neptune squares its own birth place, the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/), and so squares your Uranus too; around 42, transiting Uranus opposes its own place, the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), and so opposes your Neptune as well. Weather, not a forecast: a season that asks which parts of the vision are still yours.
