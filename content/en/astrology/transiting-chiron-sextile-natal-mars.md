A sextile is an offer, and Chiron's offer to Mars is a different way of doing something that has always been hard for you. Nothing pushes you to take it. About three times in a life, the door stands open for a few weeks or most of a year, and closes again whether or not you went through.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) starts things: it gathers your effort, points it and keeps it going. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the old sore spot and the skill that grows from working with it rather than against it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, links two functions through cooperation, and the cooperation needs someone to begin. During this [transit](/astrology/transits/), effort and adaptation are on speaking terms.

The opening is usually practical. A course turns up that teaches a hard thing in a way that suits you; a person who has lived with the same difficulty shows you how they manage; a tool makes the task you've always struggled with half as heavy. Some people go back to a sport, a craft or a role they abandoned after an early failure and find it works now, with a different method. Others stand up for someone else in exactly the way nobody stood up for them. In each case, Mars supplies the move and Chiron the know-how.

What the sextile doesn't do is decide for you. Opportunity needs engagement, and the angle guarantees no outcome.

With [Mars in the 3rd house](/astrology/mars-in-3rd-house/), where you speak up, argue and learn, Chiron is usually crossing your 5th ([Chiron transiting the 5th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-5th-house/)): a game, a creative practice or a hobby gives your voice a way back in. Both houses are measured from your Ascendant, which moves about a degree every four minutes, so I need your birth time before I read this sextile ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Mars sextile Chiron](/astrology/mars-sextile-chiron/) at birth, you have this cooperation in your chart already, and the transit is a season of practising it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

On my count, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, nearly two sextiles out of three bring Chiron over the exact angle three times, the second time going backwards, and spread over about ten and a half months; that is the usual shape, and the typical length overall is about ten months. About one in three is a single crossing of some three weeks, the kind of opening you hear about afterwards. About one in twenty crosses five times and stays some twenty-one months.

Each of Chiron's rounds, about 50 years long, has two sextiles to your Mars: one after [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-mars/), on the way to [transiting Chiron square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-mars/), and one near the end, after the second square, as Chiron comes back towards your Mars. Most people meet three by 80, some two and some four, and everyone at least two. Between them lie [transiting Chiron trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-mars/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-mars/).

For the next few years: Chiron in Aries, until April 2027, sextiles Mars in Gemini and Aquarius; Chiron in Taurus, June 2026 to May 2034, Mars in Cancer and Pisces; Chiron in Gemini, July 2033 to April 2039, Mars in Leo and Aries.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Pick one thing you've always found hard and look for a different way to do it, not a harder way.

Ask someone who manages the same difficulty how they do it, and listen to the details.

Say yes to one offer of help or training that you'd normally turn down.

Begin small and soon. A sextile rewards the first step more than the grand plan.
