With Capricorn on the 5th house cusp, play has a long horizon. The fun you choose tends to be the kind that takes years to get good at, and pays you back for decades.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is where you play, make things, flirt and fall in love, for no reason but the pleasure of it ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) on its cusp gives that pleasure a shape. I read it as the instrument practised for twenty years, the garden planned by the season, the race trained for over a winter, the craft you'd happily spend a lifetime improving. Play often comes after the work is done, as a reward, and the risk is that it never quite starts: Capricorn brings a critic into the one house where nobody is meant to be marking. In romance, it describes a slow beginning, courtship with intent, and affection shown through reliability; a love affair tends to feel real to you once it has lasted.

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) rules this 5th house, and Saturn's house shows where you find joy, or have to make room for it: the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Of everything I tested in my notebook, Saturn was among the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), so I take its house seriously when I look at what you do for fun. Aquarius, the other sign Saturn rules, shared with Uranus, opens your 6th in nearly every one of these charts: pleasure and daily work come under one ruler, and a hobby can become a job, or a job the thing you'd do for fun.

About one birth in 11, at London as at New York, has Capricorn here, a little above an even share, in Placidus, the house system I use: on the chart's western side, below the horizon, it passes as slowly as Cancer climbs the opposite cusps. [Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/) comes with it every time in both cities, which makes Mercury your chart ruler: a precise, modest manner, and pleasures that are often skills. The old astrologers placed Venus's joy in the 5th, the house they called Good Fortune; here the old house of Venus's delight opens in Saturn's sign, and pleasure and patience have to learn to share it. The 5th is about 31° to 34° wide at London, so Capricorn opens one cusp only, and in about a quarter of the London charts Sagittarius and Gemini are intercepted across the IC and the Midheaven ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

The 5th was also the house of children in the old books. I don't look in a chart for a pregnancy, now or later ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I never read a child's chart ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)); the cusp speaks of an adult's pleasures, and of how freely they're allowed.

An hour or two later, Capricorn would have reached the IC ([Capricorn on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/)); earlier, it sat on the 6th ([Capricorn on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-6th-house-cusp/)). The minute decides between them, and I read nothing until I have it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
