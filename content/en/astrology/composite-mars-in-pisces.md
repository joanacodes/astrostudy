Their weekends are mostly spent on other people: the friend who is moving house, the shelter that needs volunteers, the neighbour who is unwell. That is composite Mars in Pisces: a relationship whose drive moves most easily for someone else, wants with imagination, and lets quarrels blur rather than break.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), [Mars](/astrology/mars/) sits where your two natal Marses average out, and stands for the bond's capacity to act and to assert itself. [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/), mutable water, closes the zodiac; the tradition gives it to [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), modern tropical astrology to [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/), and I read both whenever they matter in a chart. Neither gives Mars a dignity, and the sign softens the drive into compassion, intuition and adaptability. About 1 pair in 15 shares it, one of the lower figures, since Mars spends only about 39 days in Pisces.

Such a pair acts on behalf of others more readily than for itself. It volunteers, takes in a friend in a crisis, plays music for people who need it, and its best effort often goes into something that will never be credited to it. Competition barely interests it; a game played too seriously can spoil the evening. Desire is romantic and imaginative, a matter of atmosphere and of feeling understood without having to explain. Shared music, a film or a sea swim can do more for this bond's closeness than any planned evening out. [Mars in Pisces](/astrology/mars-in-pisces/), in one person's chart, turns drive towards others; the composite turns the two of you towards others together, sometimes at your own expense.

Disagreements are hard to pin down. Anger dissolves, goes quiet or comes out as hurt, and the pair can avoid a conflict for so long that it finally overflows over something small. Each of you may guess what the other wants instead of asking, and give way to keep the peace. Being kind isn't being passive, and feeling for someone doesn't mean having no edges. What helps is saying plainly what you each want, even when it feels unkind, and naming irritation while it's still mild.

A bond that gives way easily may not notice when giving way has turned into fear. If that ever happens, it is not a chart question: Samaritans can be reached free on 116 123 across the UK and Ireland; in an emergency, dial 999.

I don't draw composites, but here is how the method reads this one. Astrologers would look at the composite Jupiter and Neptune, and at the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), where [Mars square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-neptune-synastry/) sets the drive of one against the ideals, or the haze, of the other. Nothing in it predicts how long you'll stay together, and a chart that reads two people this intimately is drawn only with both their consent ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

Just before it, [composite Mars in Aquarius](/astrology/composite-mars-in-aquarius/) acts from an idea; just after, where the zodiac starts again, [composite Mars in Aries](/astrology/composite-mars-in-aries/) acts first and asks later. Pisces acts from feeling, and its task is to say so out loud.
