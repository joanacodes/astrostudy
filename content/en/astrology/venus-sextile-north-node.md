Venus sextile North Node puts what you value a friendly step from your direction of growth, and in an easy trine with the South Node, the familiar ground behind it. Relating well is a resource you can carry forward, and it helps most when you use it on purpose.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a chart that values and relates: taste, preference, attraction, and fair exchange. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks a less familiar, complementary capacity that grows with repeated practice; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite holds the established skills you already rely on. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates Venus to the growing end through opportunity, and the same Venus makes a trine to the familiar end, so it links the two.

In plain words, what you value can serve as a bridge. The people you like, the things you find beautiful, the way you keep an exchange fair: these can carry the comfort of the familiar into the new without dragging you back. A friendship might open a door to less familiar work, or your taste might give you a way into a world you'd otherwise find daunting. Opportunity requires engagement, though. The sextile offers the bridge; you still have to walk across, and the first crossings may feel slow.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

With the North Node in Leo and Venus in Libra or Gemini, the sextile is to the growing end and the trine to the South Node in Aquarius: your gift for fairness or for conversation can help you step forward and be seen. With the North Node in Capricorn and Venus in Pisces or Scorpio, the trine goes back to the South Node in Cancer: care and depth that know home well, put to the service of a longer aim. The nodes' sign belongs to everyone born within about a year and a half; the houses, which need a birth time, make the sextile personal.

About 1 person in 15 is born with Venus within 6° of a sextile to the North Node, by the true node. In a reading I name it as a bridge we can look at together, then check it against your life ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

The pair's other angles:

- **[Venus conjunct North Node](/astrology/venus-conjunct-north-node/):** Venus right on the growing end, so relating itself is the new skill.
- **[Venus square North Node](/astrology/venus-square-north-node/):** Venus crosswise to the path; growth that has to include what you value.
- **[Venus trine North Node](/astrology/venus-trine-north-node/):** a smoother, quieter link to the same direction, with a sextile back to the familiar.
- **[Venus opposite North Node](/astrology/venus-opposite-north-node/):** Venus on the South Node, where valuing is the resource you grow from.

Between two charts, [Venus sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-north-node-synastry/) tends to be encouraging: one person's affection gives the other a gentle push in the direction they're growing. The one who encourages and the one encouraged feel it differently, so I read both directions.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-venus/) arrives twice in each 18.6-year round of the nodes, for a month or so each time: a light, useful season for relationships that help you grow. At each [nodal reversal](/astrology/nodal-reversal/), around 9, 28 and 46, the transiting North Node reaches your natal South Node, and so trines your Venus: for a few months the two ends of the axis trade places.

Venus brushes past the same sextile to your natal North Node about twice a year, briefly: good days for a step you've been rehearsing.

Weather, not a forecast: a nudge in a direction, never a timetable.
