When the node trines your Neptune, the soft part of your chart and the part that is still learning move in the same current. It happens twice a round, for a month or so each time, and it rarely makes noise: kindness and imagination simply make the next step easier.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where a chart feels its way: imagination, empathy, the sense of what can't be measured. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the less familiar capacity you build by doing. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates two functions through flow, each supporting the other without losing its shape, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the new direction borrows Neptune's gentleness and Neptune finds a use.

What tends to show is help that doesn't feel like effort. A creative habit that feeds the work you are learning. A kindness that opens a door. A sense of what someone needs, which this month turns out to be accurate and welcome. The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), at the other end of the axis, sextiles your Neptune, so old refuges stay available without pulling you back. Like every trine, it can drift by unused: ease isn't inevitability, and Neptune's version of ease is the most likely of all to stay a pleasant mood.

A generation has a double season in these months. For everyone born from August 1943 to December 1955, [Neptune in Libra](/astrology/neptune-in-libra/) and Pluto in Leo were both in the chart, so while the node crosses Aquarius, from July 2026 to March 2028, the North Node trines their Neptune and the South Node crosses their Pluto ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-pluto/)). The gentle current and the deep review come in the same twenty months, a few weeks or months apart. The houses involved, and so the parts of each life, depend on the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, the true node usually needs about a month. An eclipse season can slow it until the trine lasts three months or more, the node idling while the Sun is near; about one pass in 25 makes three exact hits. Its eclipses fall a trine or a sextile from your Neptune. Because the node counts in my forecast only when one of its ends sits on a point, this trine isn't listed there; I take it as a soft accent beside whatever else the months hold.

It comes twice a round: eight or nine times by 80. The first came at 2.2 for someone born on 1 January 1945 and at 9.7 for 1 January 1955. The two trines sit about three years either side of [Transiting North Node opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-neptune/), with [Transiting North Node square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-neptune/), [Transiting North Node sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-neptune/) and [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-neptune/) on the far side of each. [Neptune trine North Node](/astrology/neptune-trine-north-node/) holds the same angle from birth.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Put the easy gift to work: if drawing, music or listening comes naturally this month, use it for the thing you are learning.

Act on one kind impulse the same day you have it. Under a trine, the first instinct is often the right size.

Write down what moves you this month. Neptune's currents pass, and a note keeps the direction they pointed.
