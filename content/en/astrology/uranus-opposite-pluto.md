Of the five major aspects Uranus and Pluto can make, the opposition is the one no birth between 1930 and 2009 carries. In those eighty years the two planets closed in on each other, met, and began drawing apart again, without ever standing even as far apart as a trine.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) enables liberation, innovation and reform: the push to get out of what confines and try a new system. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) enables transformation and regeneration: the slower pressure to let what's obsolete end and to rebuild from inside. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets two functions face to face in polarity, so that each becomes visible through the other and the work becomes balance.

Here the polarity would run between sudden change and deep change. One end wants out, now; the other wants the roots dealt with, whatever the pace. Across a chart, the two would tend to meet through other people: the person who keeps overturning things, the person who quietly transforms everything they touch, and the recognition that both are you. An opposition isn't enmity, and tension here would be a matter of circumstance, not of nature. Integrated, each end would check the other: the urge for change asking whether a transformation is really needed, the depth asking whether a change goes far enough.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

No birth in the eighty years has it. The count's periods for this pair tell the story instead: a square in the early 1930s, with Uranus about 90° behind Pluto; the meeting in Virgo from 1963 to 1968; the sextile of the 1990s as Uranus moved ahead; and in 2009, a square once more. The opposition is the far point of that cycle, before the count on one side and long after it on the other.

So if you were born in those years, the pair's story in your chart is told by one of the other aspects, or by none:

- **[Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/):** August 1963 to August 1968, on and off.
- **[Uranus sextile Pluto](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto/):** 1941 to 1947, and 1991 to 2001.
- **[Uranus square Pluto](/astrology/uranus-square-pluto/):** 1930 to 1936, and June to September 2009.
- **[Uranus trine Pluto](/astrology/uranus-trine-pluto/):** missing from the count, like the opposition.

Between two charts, [Uranus opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-pluto-synastry/) can happen for people born decades apart: Uranus in Aries, from a birth of the early 1930s, faces Pluto in Libra, from a birth of the 1970s or early 1980s. One person's urge for change meets the other's depth across the chart, and as with any synastry I read it only when both have agreed ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus opposite natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-pluto/) comes once in Uranus's 84-year round and lasts months, often in three passes. [Transiting Pluto opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-uranus/) reaches only some generations, because Pluto takes 12 to 31 years to cross a sign. Where in your life either one lands is a matter of houses: the house the moving planet crosses, and the house of the natal planet it faces, both of which need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The generation born with the square of the 1930s met both. Pluto's years in Libra put it opposite their natal Uranus in Aries, and in the first half of the 1990s Uranus, crossing Capricorn, opposed their natal Pluto in Cancer. Weather, not a forecast: long seasons in which change and renewal pull from opposite ends of a life, and ask to be brought into balance.
