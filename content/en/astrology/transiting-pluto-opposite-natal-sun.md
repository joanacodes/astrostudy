When Pluto reaches the point opposite your Sun, the planet of depth stands across the chart from the part of you that knows who you are. For two to three years, that sense of self tends to meet intensity from outside: a person, a bond, a situation where something real is at stake.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) works by transformation: what has become unsustainable comes up so that it can be reorganised. Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises your identity, your vitality and your sense of purpose. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates them through polarity, so they meet face to face rather than from inside, and what Pluto asks of you often wears someone else's face first. A partner's will, an employer's demands, a friend who sees through you, an institution with its own agenda: under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), any of these can loom larger than usual, and each puts the same question to you, about how much of your strength is really yours.

The trap is to read every strong person as a rival. Not every uneven exchange is someone trying to dominate you, and Pluto's season is better spent on the differences: where you give way out of habit, where you hold on out of pride, where the other person simply has a point. Agency isn't domination, on either side of the axis. What tends to come out of it is a self that is less borrowed, with a purpose you'd keep even if nobody were watching.

Born with [Sun opposite Pluto](/astrology/sun-opposite-pluto/), you met this transit in your first years or not at all, depending on which side of exact Pluto stood. For everyone else it is the far end of a pairing their chart doesn't hold, which is partly why it can feel so unfamiliar.

The houses say where it lands. With your Sun in the 1st and Pluto crossing the 7th ([Pluto transiting the 7th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-7th-house/)), the meeting happens in your closest one-to-one bonds; with the Sun in the 10th and Pluto in the 4th, family and private life press on the role you've built in public. Placing them takes your birth time, and without it I don't read the chart at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Nor does a tiring season foretell an illness: no transit does ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition holds for about two years and eight months, and about twenty-two months in the quickest passes. Pluto backs up for about five months of every year, so it never crosses the point just once: three exact hits in about three passes out of five, five in the rest, and those longer passes last about three years and seven months.

More than half of people born between 1930 and 2009 never meet it by 80, because in a lifetime Pluto gets through only about 110° to 170° of the zodiac, depending on the generation: less than half the circle. Its other contacts with the Sun come more often. About seven people in eight meet the square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-sun/)), about three in four the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-sun/)) or the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-sun/)), while the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-sun/)) is about as rare as this one.

Pluto has been in Aquarius since 2023, for good since November 2024, and stays until early 2044. In those years it opposes Suns in Leo: birthdays around 23 July first, and those around 22 August last, in the early 2040s.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sort the pressure. When someone else's will presses on you, ask which part is theirs to carry and which part is a question you've been avoiding on your own account.

Keep one commitment that is entirely yours: a project, a practice, a promise to yourself that doesn't wait on anyone's approval. It gives the opposition something to push against that isn't a person.

Give big decisions about other people time. Pluto crosses this point more than once, and what feels urgent at the first exact hit often reads differently by the last.
