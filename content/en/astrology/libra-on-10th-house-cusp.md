Libra on the 10th house cusp gives your Midheaven, the highest point of the chart, to the sign of the scales. The standing it points you towards is that of the person both sides trust to make an arrangement work.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) opens the [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) in Placidus, the house system I use, and I read it as the direction you take in public: the role you grow into, the responsibilities you carry, what you'd like your work to have done ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Libra](/astrology/libra/) there, authority tends to consult. You'd rather lead by bringing people to an agreement than by giving orders, and a role shared with a counterpart can suit you better than one held alone. When a decision is yours to make, you tend to make it after hearing every side, and to explain it so that the people who lost the argument can still live with it. Your reputation is likely to rest on how you treat people as much as on what you produce. Whatever the trade, you may well become the person others come to when an arrangement has to work for everyone in it. It points to a way of standing in the world, never to one particular job.

Your public life draws on the house that holds [Venus](/astrology/venus/), ruler of your 10th: the [1st](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/10th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In these charts Venus never rules the Midheaven alone. Taurus, its other sign, opens your 5th, your 6th or both: in about 9 London charts in 10 Venus also rules your pleasures, and in nearly half it rules your pleasures and your working day as well, three houses answering to one planet. Below, Aries is always on the IC, so under a public manner of agreement runs a private drive, ruled by Mars ([Aries on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/)).

Latitude makes no difference to the sign on the Midheaven; only the hour does. About 1 birth in 13 has it in Libra, at London as at New York, and Libra crosses the meridian in about an hour and 50 minutes, which makes it, with Aries, Virgo and Pisces, one of the quickest signs there. The rising sign does depend on latitude. At London every chart with this Midheaven has Sagittarius rising, so Jupiter rules the chart; at New York Sagittarius rises in about 4 in 5, Capricorn in the rest. The 10th is narrow here, about 22° to 27° at London, and in about 1 London chart in 10 Libra opens the 11th as well. In about 1 London birth in 6, 1 in 8 at New York, Libra has no cusp at all; it is never the 10th that holds it then. At London an intercepted pair always comes with it, and the example charts on the pages about [intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) and [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/), both drawn for London with Sagittarius rising, have a Libra Midheaven.

Libra holds the Midheaven for less than two hours a day; born earlier, you could have had it on the 11th cusp ([Libra on the 11th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-11th-house-cusp/)), and later, past the meridian, on the 9th ([Libra on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-9th-house-cusp/)). The Midheaven moves about a degree every four minutes, and I don't read a chart without the time that fixes it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
