Neptune's sextile to Saturn is the builder's contact: it asks little and offers a great deal to anyone willing to put in the hours. For about two and a half years imagination and structure can work together, provided you give them something to work on.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) gives things form: responsibility, discipline, the patience to build over years. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings ideals, compassion and a sense of what could be. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) puts an opportunity between two functions and leaves the taking to you, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) a dream can borrow Saturn's method, and a duty Neptune's reason, whenever you set them to work together.

It suits the slow making of something meaningful: a creative practice given fixed hours, a commitment to care organised so that it can last, a vague ideal turned into a plan with steps. A responsibility you carry out of habit may find a purpose; a hope you'd filed as unrealistic may turn out to need only a timetable. A sextile offers rather than imposes, and with Saturn the offer is patient, so nothing is lost by starting small.

With Saturn in your 2nd, the sextile often comes while Neptune crosses your 4th or your 12th: a softer sense of home ([Neptune transiting the 4th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-4th-house/)), or time spent quietly, gives new meaning to the way you secure your life. Both houses are reckoned from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Saturn sextile Neptune](/astrology/saturn-sextile-neptune/) in a birth chart came to four runs of births between 1946 and 1997; as a transit, the same opening reaches your own Saturn at an age your chart decides.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

About two years and seven months, twenty-two at the least: that is how long the sextile lasts within my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after, long enough to lay a foundation and live on it for a while. In most passes Neptune makes the exact sextile three times, in about a quarter five times.

Saturn moves about 12° a year and Neptune about 2°, so for people born a year apart the gap between the two differs by some 10°, about four and a half years of Neptune's travel. The ages in the site's count spread accordingly: about 10 for someone born at the start of 1985, 19 for 1965, 37 for 1955, 43½ for 2005, 46 for 1935 and 1995, 69½ for 1975, and not before 80 for 1945. Some meet it twice, and about 1 person in 7 born between 1930 and 2009 not at all.

Someone born at the start of 1985 meets this sextile at about 10, [transiting Neptune square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-saturn/) at about 23, [transiting Neptune trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-saturn/) at about 37 and [transiting Neptune opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-saturn/) at about 63½. [Transiting Neptune conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-saturn/) doesn't reach them before 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give an ideal a schedule. Decide when, how often and for how long, and let the hours do what enthusiasm alone can't.

Find the reason behind one duty. If you can name what it serves, it gets lighter; if you can't, this is a good season to change it.

Start smaller than you think you should. Saturn rewards foundations, and this transit gives you time to build on them.
