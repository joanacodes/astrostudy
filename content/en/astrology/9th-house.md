The 9th house is the far horizon of a chart. Where the 3rd covers the next street and the next message, the 9th covers the long journey, the language you had to learn, and the idea that changed your mind.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 9th house as the house of meaning: what you believe, how you came to believe it, and how far you'll go — in miles or in thought — to understand more. Travel abroad, other cultures, university and long study, philosophy, faith, teaching and publishing all live here. So does the moment you stop repeating what you were taught and start thinking it through.

- **A cadent house.** It follows a succedent one, and cadent houses are read as places of adjusting, learning and passing things on: the 9th takes what the 8th went through and looks for what it means. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/): the seeker, and the planet of growth.
- **The God.** Tradition gave it that name and said the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rejoices here ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): the light of the chart, at home in the house of faith and far horizons.
- **Its opposite.** The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/), which tradition called the Goddess, where the Moon has its joy. The 3rd is the near — short trips, first lessons, the neighbourhood — and the 9th the far: long journeys, higher study, big questions.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/), just before it, is what you've been through with other people; the 9th is where you go looking for what it meant. The [10th house](/astrology/10th-house/), just after it, is where what you believe turns into what you do in public.

Like every house, the 9th is placed by your birth time: without it, there's no telling which of your planets sit here ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 9th house {#planets}

A planet in the 9th house tends to go looking: for places, ideas, teachers, a reason.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith are where I slow down: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) they held 75 to 90% of the time, against about a third for most placements. With Saturn, [Chiron in the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-in-9th-house/) or [Lilith in the 9th house](/astrology/lilith-in-9th-house/) here, a reading of mine would ask what you were taught to believe, and what you had to find for yourself.

- **[Sun in the 9th house](/astrology/sun-in-9th-house/):** in its joy: travel, study and belief stretch you, and you often end up teaching what you've found.
- **[Moon in the 9th house](/astrology/moon-in-9th-house/):** at home in the unfamiliar; you may feel most yourself far from where you started.
- **[Mercury in the 9th house](/astrology/mercury-in-9th-house/):** a mind that wants the big picture; languages, writing and debate.
- **[Venus in the 9th house](/astrology/venus-in-9th-house/):** you love what's new to you; beauty in ideas, travel and art, and people who widen your world.
- **[Mars in the 9th house](/astrology/mars-in-9th-house/):** you fight for what you believe; adventurous and outspoken, with the task of arguing without converting.
- **[Jupiter in the 9th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-9th-house/):** in the house of its own sign: a generous faith, a hunger for travel and learning, and a tendency to preach.
- **[Saturn in the 9th house](/astrology/saturn-in-9th-house/):** convictions earned after doubting the ones you were handed.
- **[Uranus in the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-in-9th-house/):** beliefs that break with what you were taught; sudden changes of mind.
- **[Neptune in the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-in-9th-house/):** a spiritual hunger and a wide, mystical faith — and a need to check your sources.
- **[Pluto in the 9th house](/astrology/pluto-in-9th-house/):** beliefs that transform you; a drive to get to the bottom of things, and little patience for easy answers.

No planet here? An [empty 9th house](/astrology/empty-houses/) doesn't mean a life without travel or belief. It's read through the sign on its cusp and the place of its ruler, the next two sections.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the cusp of the 9th house is the style of your search: how you learn, how you travel, how you come to believe something.

- **[Aries on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-9th-house-cusp/):** you learn by doing and believe with fire; you'd rather set off than plan the trip.
- **[Taurus on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-9th-house-cusp/):** you trust what you can test and touch; slow, practical learning.
- **[Gemini on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/):** curiosity in every direction, and every belief questioned at least once.
- **[Cancer on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs rooted in family and tradition; you travel to feel at home somewhere new.
- **[Leo on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** you believe wholeheartedly and love to teach; learning should bring joy.
- **[Virgo on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-9th-house-cusp/):** you study carefully and test what you're told; travel with a plan.
- **[Libra on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-9th-house-cusp/):** you learn best in dialogue; drawn to art, culture and ideas that weigh both sides.
- **[Scorpio on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/):** drawn to what lies underneath; study that goes deep, and beliefs that change you.
- **[Sagittarius on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** the house's natural sign: a born seeker, with a taste for far places and big questions.
- **[Capricorn on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs built slowly and tested against real life; study with a goal.
- **[Aquarius on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** independent thinking, and beliefs of your own, often ahead of their time.
- **[Pisces on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-9th-house-cusp/):** a wide, mystical faith; you learn through imagination, and some journeys feel like pilgrimages.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 9th house is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp, and where it sits shows where your search for meaning plays out: the part of life your beliefs are built from, or put to work in. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the cusp, there's a modern and a traditional ruler to read ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** your beliefs show in who you are; a natural traveller, student or teacher.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** what you know becomes how you earn; values shaped by belief.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** everyday learning and big questions mix; writing, teaching, talk that goes far.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** beliefs rooted in family; a home abroad, or a home full of books.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** learning as joy; travel for pleasure, and creative work that carries a meaning.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** beliefs that show in your daily work; work in teaching, travel or study.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** partners from elsewhere, or who share your search; meaning found through another person.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** beliefs tested in depth; drawn to psychology and to the big questions about change.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** the ruler at home: travel, study and belief are a central, self-led theme.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** a vocation in ideas — teaching, publishing, travel — or a public role tied to what you believe.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** friends from abroad, or groups gathered around ideas and causes.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** a private, inward faith; meaning found in solitude.

## Transits through the 9th house {#transits}

The Moon passes through your 9th house every month, the Sun once a year, Mercury, Venus and Mars for a few weeks at a time: a book, a trip, a conversation that stays with you. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 9th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-9th-house/):** about a year of wanting to learn, travel or teach, when the world can feel larger.
- **[Saturn transiting the 9th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-9th-house/):** about two and a half years when beliefs are put to the test, and serious study or a long project takes shape.
- **[Uranus transiting the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-9th-house/):** around seven years of sudden changes of mind, and of breaking with old certainties.
- **[Neptune transiting the 9th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-9th-house/):** around fourteen years of spiritual longing and wide horizons — and of checking the map twice.
- **[Pluto transiting the 9th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-9th-house/):** a long, deep change in what you believe, and why.
- **[Chiron transiting the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-9th-house/):** an old wound around faith or schooling comes up to be tended; its pace is uneven, from under two years to about eight in a sign.
- **[North Node transiting the 9th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-9th-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards the bigger picture, while the South Node crosses your 3rd.

I read these as weather, not a forecast: they describe how your search may feel for a while, not where it will take you.
