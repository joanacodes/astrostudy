A thought isn't finished until it's been shared with the group. Your mind works best in company, its ruler placed among friends and networks.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is the everyday mind and the people close by; [the 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) is friends, the groups and communities you belong to, and the future you'd like to build with them. Because the planet ruling the 3rd's cusp stands in the 11th ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), the talk and learning of the one are spent in the circles of the other.

I read it as a mind that thinks in networks. You probably learn a great deal from friends: clubs, forums, study groups, associations, the friend who always sends the article. Passing things on is part of how you think, so you introduce people, forward the link, start the thread and keep the conversation going. Plans and hopes tend to take shape in talk, and the future you'd like to build often has a lot of discussion in it. The pitfall is losing your own view inside the group's, or keeping so many conversations alive that none of them goes deep.

The 11th also holds hopes, the future you're working towards with other people. With this ruler, plans tend to become real once they've been said aloud to a friend, and a group's encouragement can carry an idea a long way; its silence can stall one just as easily. Learning often happens the same way: an evening class with the same faces each week, a reading group, a language exchange, a team that teaches itself as it goes.

Jupiter, tradition said, rejoices in the 11th, the house it called the Good Spirit ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With Sagittarius or Pisces on your 3rd house cusp, Jupiter rules the 3rd and rejoices here: a generous talker in a wide circle, the friend who teaches without meaning to. Counted round the wheel, the 11th also sees the 3rd by trine, so the ruler keeps its own house in view, and the people close by and the wider circle feed each other easily.

Siblings and friends tend to blur here. A brother, sister or cousin may become one of your closest friends as adults, or a group of friends may come to feel like the siblings you chose; neighbours can turn into a community. The chart describes the warmth or the distance you feel, never what anyone owes you.

Saturn and Uranus divide an Aquarius 3rd between them, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) in the 11th, the house that answers to Aquarius in the natural zodiac, can make you the one with the odd, ahead-of-its-time idea in every group, while Saturn, wherever it sits, asks that ideas be tested before they're shared. Mercury, ruling Gemini or Virgo on the cusp, simply keeps the group chat busy.

Compared with its neighbours, the 10th, where your words build a public name ([Ruler of the 3rd in the 10th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)), and the 12th, where they stay private ([Ruler of the 3rd in the 12th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)), the 11th spreads them sideways, among equals. Only the birth time can say where the lines between the three fall ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
