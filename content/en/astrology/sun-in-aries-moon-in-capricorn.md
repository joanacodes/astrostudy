Two cardinal signs, two ways of taking charge: the Aries Sun leads by impulse, the Capricorn Moon by plan. You were born in the last third of the lunar month, around the last quarter, and I read the pair as drive and self-command learning to share the controls.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aries is cardinal fire and Capricorn cardinal earth. The Moon sits three signs behind the Sun, a square by sign: both start things, and they disagree about how. The [Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/) wants to begin now and find out on the way; the [Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/) feels safe once there's a structure, a role and a plan it can rely on. Under pressure you tend to act fast and then organise the aftermath with great competence.

The rulers cross over. [Mars](/astrology/mars/), which rules your Sun's sign, is exalted in Capricorn, your Moon's sign; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), which rules your Moon's sign, has its fall in Aries, your Sun's. Drive is honoured on the Moon's ground, and patience struggles on the Sun's. Tradition also puts the Moon in detriment here, opposite Cancer, while the Sun is exalted: a self that acts freely, and needs that would rather manage a feeling than display it. Saturn is the planet I trust most in any chart, one of [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook, so with this Moon I read Saturn's sign and house early.

Measured the way astronomers measure a phase, the Moon was 242° to 299° ahead of the Sun; measured the short way, 61° to 118° behind it, about twenty to twenty-four and a half days after a new moon. Half [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/), half [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/): that is how these births divide, and a Moon close to 90° behind the Sun makes the square exact, a waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains what changes when the square comes from behind.

Around 1 person in 139 has the pair. The Moon spends roughly 2½ days in Capricorn; on the day it came from Sagittarius or went on to Aquarius, the birth time chooses among this page, the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-sagittarius/) and the [Sun in Aries, Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aries-moon-in-aquarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign comes from the same time, and the three together draw the first lines of a chart, nothing more.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you're a protector with a plan. The Aries Sun defends the people it loves on the spot; the Capricorn Moon builds them something to stand on, a home, a budget, a future. Affection may come out as practical arrangements, and a partner who wants words may have to learn your dialect. The tender part is letting yourself be cared for before you feel you've earned it: with this pair, comfort can seem like something to schedule once the work is done.

## At work {#at-work}

This is a pair built for responsibility. The Sun brings initiative and the Moon brings stamina and structure, so you're often given charge of things early and rise to it. Mars, exalted in your Moon's sign, suits ambitious, well-organised effort: the plan carried out, not just announced. The cost is that work can become the place where every feeling goes to be managed. Keep one ambition that is purely yours, owed to nobody.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Impatience meets self-control, and the two can wrestle inside you. The Aries Sun wants to act on a feeling now; the Capricorn Moon decides it can wait until everything is handled, which may be never. The result can be a temper that flares at small things while the large ones stay locked away. What helps is giving the Sun a fast outlet and the Moon a slow, regular one: a run when you're furious, and one evening a week when you report on yourself as carefully as you report on everything else.
