Uranus trines your Sun twice in its 84-year round, and the two visits are among the quietest it makes. For about twenty months each time, the part of you that wants to differ and the part that knows where it's going stop arguing.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) brings originality, experiment and the freedom to rearrange. Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity and direction: who you're becoming, and the effort you put behind it. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates two functions through flow while each keeps its own nature, so for these months change and selfhood stop competing. You can alter something central, your work, your look, the story you tell about yourself, without the sense of betrayal that usually comes with it.

It often shows as permission. A side interest becomes the main thing; an idea you'd filed under “one day” turns out to be possible this year; people who knew you one way accept you another with less fuss than you feared. The help is real but quiet. A trine doesn't come with an event attached, and plenty of people spend this transit as they spent the year before, only to see later how open the road had been.

Where it helps depends on two houses. With your Sun in the 5th and Uranus crossing your 9th ([Uranus transiting the 9th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-9th-house/)), a subject you study or a journey off the usual route feeds what you create for the joy of it. Placing those houses needs the hour you were born, and I don't give this reading without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The natal [Sun trine Uranus](/astrology/sun-trine-uranus/) is a talent carried for life. This [transit](/astrology/transits/) lends a similar ease for a season, whatever angle the two planets made at your birth.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, the orbs I use for timing, a trine from Uranus usually lasts about twenty months, and seldom less than eleven. In more than nine passes out of ten Uranus touches the exact degree three times as its yearly retrograde loop carries it back and forward. In the rest it touches it once, turning round within orb before it can cross again, and still stays about twenty months.

In the round Uranus makes with your Sun, the two trines sit about 28 years before and 28 years after [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-sun/). Between them, about fourteen years from each, comes [transiting Uranus opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-sun/); nearer the conjunction come [transiting Uranus sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-sun/) and [transiting Uranus square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-sun/). By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have met both trines, and none has met fewer than one.

Uranus stays in Gemini until May 2033, so the Suns it trines in these years are in the other two air signs: Libra, with birthdays from about 23 September to 22 October, and Aquarius, from about 20 January to 18 February.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Start the thing you keep describing. Under a trine the cost of beginning is low, and the trine won't begin it for you.

Let people see the change. The Sun is also how you're recognised, and this is a good season to show the work, the look or the direction you've been trying out in private.

Learn the skill your next step will need. Twenty easy months are the cheapest time to acquire what a harder season may later ask of you.
