Bold change and slow renewal don't often help each other, but they can. Uranus sextile Pluto in synastry sets one person's Uranus about 60° from the other's Pluto, within 2.4°: two generations' ways of changing things, close enough to cooperate if someone starts.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) tries the new: it differentiates, frees, and reorganises whole systems. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) renews from below: it clears out the obsolete and rebuilds strength at the core. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity and cooperation, and in the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) the cooperation is between two people, often of different ages.

If the Uranus is yours, you bring the experiment, and this person shows you how deep a change has to go to last. Your ideas gain staying power around them.

If the Pluto is yours, this person offers a practical route for the change you have been working on underneath. Their readiness to try things can make your renewal visible sooner.

A Virgo Uranus with a Scorpio Pluto: reform through method and detail, beside a renewal that wants to reach the core.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The opening can stay closed. The Uranus person may find the other's depth heavy and move on to the next idea; the Pluto person may find the other's changes shallow and keep their own counsel. Or the cooperation happens only on the surface, a quick fix where a deeper change was possible. A sextile offers the help; someone still has to reach for it.

- **[Uranus conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto-synastry/):** the two welded on one degree, change straight onto depth.
- **[Uranus square Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-square-pluto-synastry/):** a sign further, change and depth pushing against each other.
- **[Uranus trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-trine-pluto-synastry/):** two signs further, the two urges flowing together.
- **[Uranus opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/uranus-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** across the zodiac, breaking free at one end, renewing at the other.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Choose one thing to change together, and divide the work by speed. The Uranus person can run the trial; the Pluto person can say what must change underneath for the trial to last. A parent and an adult child reworking a family business, colleagues years apart redesigning a process, friends of different ages starting something: each can make the sextile a working partnership.

The count shows how far apart such pairs can be: a mid-1965 birth, Uranus in Virgo, with anyone born between October 1987 and December 1988, Pluto in Scorpio, some twenty-three years on; a mid-1945 birth, Pluto in Leo, with anyone born between April and August 1971, Uranus in Libra, twenty-six years on. Each span shares the contact as a whole, so I leave it outside my synastry count, which holds only contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Read through the houses, it is an opening between two eras' ways of changing things, more than between two people.

Neither planet moves with the birth hour; the houses do ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Uranus in the Pluto person's 3rd house, the cooperation runs through conversation, learning and everyday ideas ([Their Uranus in your 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-in-3rd-house-synastry/)).

Inside a single chart, the same opening belongs to people born in 1941–47 or 1991–2001 ([Uranus sextile Pluto](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto/)). Across two charts it needs two willing people, for the work and for the reading ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
