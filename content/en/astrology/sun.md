The Sun is the one placement nearly everyone knows: ask someone their sign and they'll give you the sign the Sun was in on the day they were born. In a chart it's the centre of gravity, the part of you that wants to grow into something.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

I read the Sun as direction rather than personality. It answers *what are you trying to become?* more than *what are you like?* — and that's why two people with the same Sun sign can seem nothing alike: they're heading the same way from very different places.

- **Its sign** shows the style of that effort: bold, patient, curious, protective.
- **Its house** shows where in life you're most visibly trying: work, love, home, ideas.
- **Its aspects** show how much help it gets from the rest of the chart, and where it meets resistance.

The Sun works with the [Moon](/astrology/moon/), which describes what you need to feel safe, and with the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), which describes how you meet the world. Read together, the three give a first sketch; the rest of the chart fills it in.

Its aspects are where it gets personal. Born with the [Sun conjunct the Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/), at a new moon, what you want and what you need pull the same way; with the [Sun opposite the Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/), at a full moon, they face each other and ask to be balanced. A [Sun square Saturn](/astrology/sun-square-saturn/) often describes someone who had to earn their confidence slowly. And Mercury never strays more than 28° from the Sun, so the only major aspect the two can make is the [conjunction](/astrology/sun-conjunct-mercury/).

## The Sun through the signs {#through-the-signs}

The Sun spends about a month in each sign, so your Sun sign comes from your birth date — within a day of a change, from your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) too. In plain words:

- **[Sun in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-aries/):** you come alive by starting things.
- **[Sun in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-taurus/):** you grow by building something that lasts.
- **[Sun in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-gemini/):** you shine by connecting ideas and people.
- **[Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/):** you grow by caring for what is yours.
- **[Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/):** you shine by creating and being seen doing it.
- **[Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/):** you grow by making things work better.
- **[Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/):** you shine through balance and fairness with others.
- **[Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/):** you grow by going deep, and staying.
- **[Sun in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-sagittarius/):** you come alive by searching for meaning.
- **[Sun in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-capricorn/):** you grow by earning your place, slowly.
- **[Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/):** you shine by thinking differently, for others.
- **[Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/):** you grow through imagination and compassion.

## The Sun in the houses {#in-the-houses}

1. **[1st house](/astrology/sun-in-1st-house/):** you lead with yourself; people notice you first.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/sun-in-2nd-house/):** you grow through what you earn, keep and value.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/sun-in-3rd-house/):** words, learning and your close circle are your stage.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/sun-in-4th-house/):** home and family are where you find your purpose.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/sun-in-5th-house/):** creation, play and romance light you up.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/sun-in-6th-house/):** you shine through useful, well-made daily work.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/sun-in-7th-house/):** you find yourself through partners and close relationships.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/sun-in-8th-house/):** you grow through intimacy, trust and shared resources.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/sun-in-9th-house/):** travel, study and belief stretch you.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/sun-in-10th-house/):** your purpose shows in public, in your work and standing.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/sun-in-11th-house/):** you shine in groups, friendships and shared causes.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/sun-in-12th-house/):** you grow in private, behind the scenes, often late.

## The Sun in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), the Sun shows where one person lights the other up. The contact astrologers have written about longest is [one person's Sun on the other's Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon-synastry/) — purpose meeting need — which tends to feel like recognition. When the Sun touches the other person's Venus, there is warmth and admiration; when it meets their [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the bond can feel steadying or heavy, depending on how each of you takes structure. I always read the Sun contacts in both directions: who lights up whom isn't always mutual.

## Transits and the solar return {#solar-return}

The Sun crosses each house of your chart in about a month, so its transits are short: a day or two of emphasis when it meets one of your planets. Once a year, on or within a day of your birthday, it comes back to the exact degree it held when you were born. The chart drawn for that moment, the [solar return](/astrology/solar-return/), is read as the tone of the year ahead — a description of the weather, not a forecast of events.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

The Sun sign is the placement everyone reads first. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), it held about a third of the time — 30 to 40%, with the rising sign, Venus and Mercury — which is close to what a flattering, vague horoscope scores. That's not nothing, and I still read it in every chart. But [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#why-not-the-sun) held 75 to 90% of the time, so that's where I spend the most time, and I tell you which parts of a reading rest on less. It's a notebook, not a study, and it shapes [how I read](/how-i-read/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Your Sun sign is your chart.”** It's one placement out of more than a dozen. Two people born on the same day can have different Moons, risings and houses.
- **“Born on the cusp, you're a bit of both.”** At the minute you were born the Sun was in one sign; near a change, your birth time settles which.
- **“Your sign is wrong because the stars have moved.”** Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, tied to the seasons, not the constellations; Ophiuchus is a constellation, not a missing sign.
- **“Horoscopes describe Sun signs.”** A horoscope written for a twelfth of humanity can only be general, which is exactly why it fits most people a little.
