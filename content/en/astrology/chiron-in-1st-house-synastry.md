Some people notice how you hold yourself before anything else, and seem to know which part of it costs you effort. With their Chiron in your 1st house, the other person's old hurt, and the skill they built around it, sits right where you meet the world.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) adapts. It marks a sensitivity someone has lived with for a long time and the know-how they built around it, which is often what they end up teaching. [The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is your manner, your body and your first moves: the self that walks in before anyone knows you. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), what they learned the hard way stands next to the way you show up.

For you, this can bring an unusual ease. If you've always felt slightly wrong in your own skin, too tall, too quiet, too quick to blush, they tend to notice and not mind, because they once had to make peace with something of their own. Their remarks on how you come across are usually precise and kind, and you may find yourself standing a little straighter.

For them, your way of arriving can touch something tender. The nerve with which you begin things, or the awkwardness you haven't bothered to hide, may recall what they had to work around, and they often respond by protecting you, as if they could spare you what the adapting cost them. The 1st is angular, so what lands here shows; its natural sign is [Aries](/astrology/aries/), the first move of the zodiac. When their Chiron lies within 3° of your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), the orb I give Chiron in synastry, it also touches the degree your chart rises from, and I read that contact along with the house.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Care about how you come across can turn into coaching. You may hear about your posture, your tone, the way you introduce yourself, and even when they're right it can feel as if your manner were a problem to fix. Looked at through their old sensitivity, small things about you can start to seem larger than they are.

From their side, standing next to you can be exposing. You see them exactly where they've worked hardest to look at ease, and a light joke about how someone looks or sounds can land on a place you didn't know was there. They may also wonder whether you like them, or only the skill they've built.

Neither of you put the hurt there. Their Chiron describes something they carried long before you met; your 1st house is only where it shows between you.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Let advice about how you present yourself be something you ask for, not something that simply arrives. Tell them when their ease helped: people who adapted around a sore spot rarely hear that the adapting did someone else good. In a couple, talk about how each of you likes to be seen; between friends, enjoy turning up unpolished; between colleagues, let them prepare you for a first meeting if you like, then walk in your own way; between adult relatives, old comparisons about looks are best retired.

Turned the other way, the same page reads as your Chiron in their 1st house: then your sore spot is the one their manner brushes, and you are the one tempted to coach. On either side of this house, [their Chiron in your 12th house](/astrology/chiron-in-12th-house-synastry/) meets what you keep out of view, and [their Chiron in your 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-in-2nd-house-synastry/) what you value and own.

Their Chiron moves so little in a day that their birth date places it. The line it might straddle is yours: four minutes of your birth time shift your rising degree by about one, and in Placidus, the house system I use, a Chiron close to that line falls in your 12th or your 1st according to those minutes.
