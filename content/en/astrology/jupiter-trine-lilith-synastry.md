One person's outlook, in Jupiter trine Lilith in synastry, seems to have been built with room for the other's wilder side. Jupiter in one chart sits about 120° from Black Moon Lilith in the other, usually in signs of one element, and what was once hidden doesn't have to argue its way in.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is where a person finds meaning and gives it room: principles, perspective, a generous idea of what a life can hold. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), a calculated point at the Moon's greatest distance from Earth, is read in many ways; I read it as the instinct and autonomy someone set aside to be acceptable, and their own feel for their limits. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets two functions support each other while each stays itself, and in the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) that support travels from one person to the other.

If the Lilith is yours, you may notice you stop editing yourself around this person. The bluntness, the hunger, the refusal to be convenient: none of it seems to need explaining. It's a rare kind of rest.

If the Jupiter is yours, the other person's untamed side fits your sense of how life should be, with no strain. You may be the first person who treats it as ordinary. Your faith in people, whatever form it takes, gets a living example.

One element usually holds both. One person's Jupiter in Sagittarius with the other's Lilith in Leo, both fire: a roomy, adventurous outlook beside someone who learned to hide how much they wanted to be seen. Around this Jupiter, being seen stops feeling like too much.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Ease hides what never gets tested. The welcome can stay inside the relationship, a freedom that exists in one kitchen or one friendship and nowhere else. And it tends to run one way: the Jupiter person gives room, the Lilith person takes it, and nobody asks what the Jupiter person keeps back. Ease isn't inevitability; a trine left alone becomes a comfortable habit.

- **[Jupiter conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-lilith-synastry/):** closer and more charged, with the welcome turning into a cause.
- **[Jupiter sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-lilith-synastry/):** half the distance, and the welcome comes as an offer to accept.
- **[Jupiter square Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-square-lilith-synastry/):** a sign nearer, and the beliefs push back instead of making room.
- **[Jupiter opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-lilith-synastry/):** two signs further, where principle and refusal stand face to face.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Take the permission outside. If the Lilith is yours, let a little of what you can be here show somewhere it costs something: at work, with family, in your own plans. If the Jupiter is yours, turn the question round and ask yourself what you don't let out. A mentor and a younger colleague can use it to make a workplace roomier; friends who travel together, to try out who they are away from home; a couple, to keep a few freedoms that belong to each of them alone.

If you were born in mid-1995, you have it with people born from July to October 1996, whose Lilith trines your Jupiter, and with people born from April to August 1997, whose Jupiter trines your Lilith. Whole runs of births share each side, so I don't add this trine to the contacts I count between two charts, which always include the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. What it can tell me comes through the houses, and even there I read it as a meeting of two periods more than of two people.

Both birth times decide those houses; the trine itself doesn't depend on the hour, since Jupiter and Lilith hardly move in a day ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The dates assume mean Lilith; true Lilith, which wanders tens of degrees, may not agree ([Which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)).

When one chart holds [Jupiter trine Lilith](/astrology/jupiter-trine-lilith/), the welcome is inward. Here it's given by one person to another, and touches what that other usually keeps private, so I read it only when both ask ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
