On one side of Saturn opposite Uranus in synastry sits one person's Saturn, with its wish for things to hold; about 180° away sits the other's Uranus, with its wish for room. Each person tends to see the other as the one doing the pulling.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) secures: it commits, sets limits, keeps promises over the long haul. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) liberates: it questions, experiments, reorganises a system to let air in. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates two functions through polarity, each clearer against the other, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) this one hands each end to a person born in a different era.

If the Saturn is yours, the other person can look like everything you've held back from: a readiness to walk away, to start again, to refuse what's expected. You may admire that and fear it in the same breath.

If the Uranus is yours, the other person can look like the anchor you never asked for: dependable, cautious, ready with the reasons something should stay as it is. You may resent the drag and still notice, later, that their caution saved something you'd have lost.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An opposition is a seesaw, never enmity, and the trouble with a seesaw is that every push on one end lifts the other. The more the Uranus person reaches for freedom, the more the Saturn person tightens the rules; the tighter the rules, the stronger the urge to get out. Left alone, each ends up playing a more extreme version of their part than either really believes in.

Other angles change the shape of the tension. [Saturn conjunct Uranus in synastry](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-uranus-synastry/) puts keeping and leaving on one spot; [Saturn sextile Uranus in synastry](/astrology/saturn-sextile-uranus-synastry/) offers a partnership instead of a pull; [Saturn square Uranus in synastry](/astrology/saturn-square-uranus-synastry/) makes the habits cross; [Saturn trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/saturn-trine-uranus-synastry/) lets them fit.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Name the pull out loud, then trade something. The Saturn person can sponsor one freedom the other wants; the Uranus person can take on one commitment the other values. Agree what is fixed and what is open, and revisit it on a date rather than in a row. Co-founders can split the roles on purpose; partners can write down which parts of their life are settled; an adult child and a parent can stop arguing about the whole and bargain over the parts.

Someone born in mid-1975 has it with anyone born between November 1992 and March 1994, whose Uranus faces that 1975 Saturn; someone born in mid-1955 has it with anyone born during 1961, whose Saturn faces the 1955 Uranus, so it isn't always the elder who holds the Saturn end. Whole cohorts share each side, so the opposition is never part of my count of a synastry, which keeps to contacts with the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven. Through the houses I read it as two eras balancing each other, more than two people.

The houses need both birth times, though no hour moves either planet far enough to change the opposition ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Saturn was one of the three placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook, but only in single charts ([the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)); this contact has no figure of its own.

Inside one chart, [Saturn opposite Uranus](/astrology/saturn-opposite-uranus/) is a balance one person learns to strike alone. Between two, it's struck together, and only read when both people want it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
