Sagittarius has no Venus retrograde of its own between 2000 and 2040. The autumn retrogrades turn back in Scorpio and the winter ones turn direct in Capricorn, and for forty years the sign between them is left to Venus moving forwards.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) is mutable fire, ruled by Jupiter: the sign of exploring, learning and looking for meaning. Venus there values freedom, honesty and a shared horizon, and loves what widens its world. If [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) came to Sagittarius, the planet would seem from Earth to halt and back through the sign for about six weeks, and the review would be about room: how much freedom you want inside love, the places and ideas you fell for, the promise of adventure and whether it was kept.

In my list it doesn’t come. Of Venus’s five retrograde places between 2000 and 2040, the autumn one starts in Scorpio, at 15°36′ in 2002 and lower each time, moving away from Sagittarius ([Venus retrograde in Scorpio](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-scorpio/)). The winter one ends in Capricorn, turning direct at 16°01′ Capricorn in February 2006 and about 2½° lower every eight years, down to 6°08′ in January 2038 ([Venus retrograde in Capricorn](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-capricorn/)). That one drifts towards Sagittarius, but it is still six degrees short when my list ends.

Nobody born between 2000 and 2040 has Venus retrograde in Sagittarius in their chart. Where I do meet it, in a birth from outside those years, I read it as a love of freedom that takes its time to say what it wants ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Sagittarius’s place among your houses is drawn from the minute and the place of your birth, so it needs [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and I never read a chart without it. The lines below, drawn in Placidus, my house system, are what one would slow if it came.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/):** Sagittarius rising: a frank, adventurous manner; the freedom you show the world, and what it gives you.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** spending on experience; what you value more than possessions, valued again.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** candid words and big ideas; a frank remark about love, revisited.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home that serves as a base for adventure; how much room the people under it give each other.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romance as adventure; an affair of the heart or a pastime chosen for its excitement, valued for what it taught you.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-6th-house-cusp/):** work with meaning; daily tasks weighed for whether they still feel worthwhile.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius on the Descendant, with Gemini rising: a partner who widens your world; freedom and closeness, kept in honest proportion.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-8th-house-cusp/):** shared beliefs and shared means; how much of your freedom you trust to someone else.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-9th-house-cusp/):** Sagittarius in its natural house: travel, study and faith; a journey or a teacher you loved, remembered.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a vocation with a vision; the ideals that first drew you to your work.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends from far away; the circle that shares your questions.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/sagittarius-on-12th-house-cusp/):** a quiet search for meaning; what you believe in private, looked at gently.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Jupiter rules Sagittarius, and the house of your natal Jupiter shows where your sense of adventure and meaning lives. With no retrograde due in this sign before 2041, look where the autumn and winter retrogrades do fall: in many charts, the houses on either side of your Sagittarius house.

What Sagittarius’s Venus would review is the size of the room love leaves you. That question doesn’t need a retrograde to be worth asking.
