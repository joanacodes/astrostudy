Neptune spends about fourteen years in a sign, so the degree of your birth Neptune belongs to a whole generation, and the North Node crosses it for all of them during its year and a half in that sign. For each person, it is a month in which imagination and the direction of growth stand on the same degree.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is where a chart imagines and empathises: ideals, compassion, the sense of something larger, the ease with which boundaries soften. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the less practised capacity you build by repetition. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses them, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) growth comes through a softer skill: making something, caring for someone, letting a plan stay open, trusting a meaning you can't prove.

It needs a form to hold it. Neptune's core need is open contact without losing the line between you and others, between an image and a fact, and its blind spots are taking resonance for truth and compassion for unlimited availability. A conjunction with the node brings both the gift and the blind spots forward. Across the chart the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) sits opposite your Neptune, on the practical, familiar ground you know well, which is worth keeping as an anchor. If the month brings fog rather than inspiration, a low mood that doesn't lift, that is for someone trained to help, not for a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

The generation comes first. Those born with [Neptune in Capricorn](/astrology/neptune-in-capricorn/), from January 1984 to November 1998, met this transit between 2009 and 2011, while the node last crossed Capricorn, and meet it again from March 2028 to September 2029, the youngest first. The person comes next: with Neptune in your 4th, it falls during the node's stay there ([North Node transiting the 4th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-4th-house/)), and the inner life or the home asks for room. The house needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the true node usually crosses your Neptune in about a month. In an eclipse season, while the Sun is near the nodes and the node hardly moves, it can take three months or more, and about one pass in 25 makes three exact hits. The year or so around this transit, and around the opposition, are when an eclipse can land on your Neptune.

The first came at 7.3 for someone born on 1 January 1985 and at 1.8 for 1 January 1990; after that, every 18.6 years, four or five times by 80. With [Neptune conjunct North Node](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-north-node/) in your chart, every nodal return brings it back.

About three years later the node sextiles your Neptune ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-neptune/)), then squares it ([Transiting North Node square natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-neptune/)) and trines it ([Transiting North Node trine natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-neptune/)); after nine years or so the South Node reaches it ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Neptune](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-neptune/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the softer skill a container: a weekly class, a notebook, an hour of practice at the same time each week.

Check impressions against facts. Write down what you sense about a person or a plan, then what you know, and keep both.

Offer compassion with a limit you name in advance. Care that has an edge can go on for longer.
