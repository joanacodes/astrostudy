Of the node's contacts with Uranus, the sextile is the smallest and the easiest to miss. For a month or so the direction your chart grows in stands two signs from your Uranus, and an unusual idea, your own or someone else's, gets an easy hearing.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the part of a chart that needs room to do things differently: to experiment, to reform, to stand apart. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to what you are learning by doing it again and again. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity that has to be taken up, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the new direction and your appetite for change can cooperate, provided you give them something to do.

What tends to appear is a modest invitation: a tool that would change the way you work, a course run by people who think differently, a chance to try an arrangement of your life for a few weeks. At the far end of the axis the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) trines your Uranus, so the independence you already have makes the step easier. A sextile between these two is rarely dramatic; it is a door left ajar, and Uranus needs no pushing to look through it.

The generations show the rhythm. While the node crosses Aquarius, from July 2026 to March 2028, it sextiles the Uranus of everyone born with [Uranus in Sagittarius](/astrology/uranus-in-sagittarius/), from February 1981 to December 1988, each at their own degree. For them the sextile is a forerunner: the node reaches their Uranus itself about three years later, while it crosses Sagittarius from September 2029 to March 2031. Which part of each life opens depends on the house, and the house on the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Measured in my orbs, 1½° before exact and 1° after, a sextile from the node to Uranus is a matter of a month or so. In an eclipse season, when the Sun reaches the nodes and the node slows to a crawl, the sextile can last three months or more, and about one pass in 25 brings three exact hits; that season's eclipses fall two signs or four signs from your Uranus and leave it untouched. This isn't one of my forecast's seasons: there the node counts only when an end of the axis lands on a point.

With two sextiles a round, your Uranus meets eight or nine of them by 80. The node's first exact sextile to Uranus reached someone born on 1 January 1985 at 5.2, and someone born on 1 January 1965 at 11.2. One sextile leads into [Transiting North Node conjunct natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-uranus/); the other follows it, and leads on to [Transiting North Node square natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-uranus/), [Transiting North Node trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-uranus/) and, halfway round, [Transiting North Node opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-uranus/). With [Uranus sextile North Node](/astrology/uranus-sextile-north-node/) in your chart, the nodal return brings this angle back.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Try the new method on one task, for the length of the month, and keep notes on what changes.

Go where people do things differently from you: a workshop, a forum, a neighbour's way of organising a week.

Make the experiment reversible. A sextile rewards trying more than committing, and you can decide later what to keep.
