Most house rulers send their house's business off to another part of the chart. This one stays home: the planet that rules your 7th house stands inside it, and partnership is lived as partnership, not first through work, money or family.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Whatever happens one to one belongs to the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/): partners in love and business, close collaborators, the adversary who faces you openly, and the agreements that bind you to any of them. Its ruler inside it adds its own planet's voice to the sign on the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), and the house speaks twice ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Relationships, with this ruler, need no translation. You tend to know where you stand with people, and to want them to know where they stand with you. Partnerships take up a large part of your life, often by choice: you commit, renegotiate and recommit with your eyes open. Rivals tend to be out in the open as well, which can make conflict sharper and fairer at once. If anything needs watching, it's proportion: a life arranged round one bond can neglect the others, and a self that lives mostly in company needs some hours of its own.

Often the ruler is in its own sign too. Venus on a Libra Descendant, with Aries rising, is at home twice over, in its own sign ([domicile](/astrology/domicile/)) and in the house Libra stands for in the natural zodiac: grace, and a strong sense of what's fair ([Venus in the 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house/)). Saturn in Capricorn on a Capricorn Descendant, with Cancer rising, makes commitment slow, formal and lasting; Mars in Aries, with Libra rising, likes a partner who pushes back. Further into the house, in the next sign, the ruler takes that sign's manner instead.

Scorpio, Aquarius and Pisces on the Descendant give the 7th a pair of rulers, often with only one of them inside it, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). With [Aquarius on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/), Uranus here asks for freedom inside commitment, while Saturn, from wherever it stands, shows what you're willing to build to keep it.

Contracts belong to the 7th, and so do disputes. A ruler at home here doesn't predict a wedding, a separation or a lawsuit, and I don't read a chart for any of them ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)). It describes someone who takes agreements seriously, and who would rather say what's wrong than let it fester.

The 7th is angular, and its ruler here was above the western horizon when you were born, in the last stretch before setting. Had you been born later, it would have set, into the 6th, where partnership runs through the working day ([Ruler of the 7th in the 6th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/)); with an earlier time, it would stand higher, in the 8th, where bonds turn to what you share and owe ([Ruler of the 7th in the 8th house](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)). In Placidus, the house system I use, the Descendant that opens this house comes straight from the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What's left is a clear invitation: meet people as they are, and let them meet you the same way.
