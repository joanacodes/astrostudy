Gemini on the 8th house cusp brings the sign of questions to the house of what two people share and owe each other: trust, intimacy, money in common. Here depth is reached the way Gemini reaches anything, by asking.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read the sign on the cusp of the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) as the way you let someone in ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) lets them in through conversation. You tend to trust what you understand: the terms of a joint account read through to the end, the history behind a quarrel, the question nobody else dared to ask. Intimacy grows with words as well as touch, and a partner who can't talk about desire, money or fear may find you slow to open up. In a crisis you look for information first: what happened, what the choices are, who knows more. That curiosity often reaches past your own life into what lies underneath in general, psychology, research, the stories families don't tell. None of this makes you less loyal. Talking a secret through with the person it belongs to is not the same as passing it on.

Scorpio always rises with this cusp at the latitudes of London and New York ([Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/)), so Pluto and Mars, the 8th's own rulers by nature, rule your whole chart, and I read both. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is left with the 8th itself, and its house is where questions of trust and sharing come up: the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Mercury's other sign, Virgo, is on your Midheaven in just over half the London charts and opens the 11th in most of the others, so what you share is tied either to your public role or to the groups you belong to; at New York the 11th is the more common of the two. Across the chart, the 2nd cusp is always in Sagittarius: your own money answers to Jupiter and tends to be spent with an open hand, while money held with others answers to Mercury and needs its terms talked through.

Gemini lingers on the cusps of the western side, and this one holds it for nearly two and a half hours a day: in Placidus, the system I draw charts in, that is about 1 birth in 10, the same at London and New York. The 8th is wide when Gemini opens it, about 35° to 42° at London, roomy enough in about 3 London charts in 10 for all of Cancer as well, and the Moon then joins Mercury in speaking for it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Your Ascendant is Scorpio, so the pages next door, [Gemini on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/) with its Sagittarius rising and [Gemini on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/) with its Libra, describe other people's charts.

The old books put death in this house. A chart can't tell anyone when they or someone else will die, and I never look for it ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); with Gemini on the cusp, what I read is how you trust and what you share. And without the birth time there is no 8th house to read at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
