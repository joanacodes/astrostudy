Gemini on the 8th house cusp brings the sign of questions to the house where a bond gets serious: trust, intimacy, money in common and the debts people carry between them. Here depth is reached the way Gemini reaches anything, by asking.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

I read the sign on the cusp of the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) as the way you let someone in ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) lets them in through conversation. You tend to trust what you understand: the terms of a joint account read through to the end, the history behind a quarrel, the question nobody else dared to ask. Intimacy grows with words as well as touch, and a partner who can't talk about desire, money or fear may find you slow to open up. In a crisis you look for information first: what happened, what the choices are, who knows more. That curiosity often reaches past your own life into what lies underneath in general, psychology, research, the stories families don't tell. None of this makes you less loyal. Talking a secret through with the person it belongs to is not the same as passing it on.

Scorpio always rises with this cusp at the latitudes of London and New York ([Scorpio rising](/astrology/scorpio-rising/)), so Pluto and Mars, the 8th's own rulers by nature, rule your whole chart, and I read both. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is left with the 8th itself, and its house is where questions of trust and sharing come up: the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Mercury's other sign, Virgo, is on your Midheaven in just over half the London charts and opens the 11th in most of the others, so what you share is tied either to your public role or to the groups you belong to; at New York the 11th is the more common of the two.

Counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use, about 1 birth in 10 has Gemini on the 8th, at London as at New York. Gemini lingers on the cusps of the western side, nearly two and a half hours of each day on this one, and the 8th is wide when it opens it: about 35° to 42° at London. Gemini never opens the 7th or the 9th as well in these charts. In about 3 London charts in 10, the whole of Cancer lies inside the 8th too, and the Moon joins Mercury in speaking for it ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The neighbours are separate cases: [Gemini on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-7th-house-cusp/) comes with Sagittarius rising, [Gemini on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-9th-house-cusp/) with Libra.

The old books put death in this house. A chart can't tell anyone when they or someone else will die, and I never look for it ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); with Gemini on the cusp, what I read is how you trust and what you share. And without the birth time there is no 8th house to read at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
