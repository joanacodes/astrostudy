The 8th house is where a chart goes deep, and yours has sent its ruler there, among intimacy, trust and what you share with other people or owe them. The self you lead with keeps being drawn into the bonds that change you, and into what it costs to let someone close.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The ruler of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/), shows where your life tends to gather ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and here that's the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/), busy with the living: closeness, sexual and otherwise; trust; joint money, loans and debts; anything held in common with another person. Counted from the 7th, it's your partner's 2nd house, which is why it carries both what a bond creates and what it binds you to ([derived houses](/astrology/derived-houses/)).

Depth makes this self, and remakes it. Small talk tells people little about you; what you've been through with others tells them a great deal. You tend to know yourself through the relationships that ask something real of you, and through the moments when an arrangement, a trust or a shared life has to be renegotiated. Change is part of your story rather than an interruption of it: you come out of hard passages knowing more about yourself, and often more like yourself. Boundaries are the thing to watch. Depth isn't obsession, and closeness doesn't mean handing over the key to every room.

The old books read this house for death and for legacies. I don't read death in anyone's chart, and a chart ruler here says nothing about how long you'll live or what you'll inherit ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)). Nor does it forecast gains or losses. It describes how you share, and how you hold the trust that sharing needs.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 8th lies high in the western half of the sky: a ruler there had passed the Midheaven and was coming down towards the western horizon when you were born. The old books also listed the 8th, with the 6th and the 12th, among the houses where a planet's circumstances hold it back. I take that as a ruler doing its work where few people see it, in private and between two people.

With Scorpio rising, the chart has two rulers, Mars and Pluto, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)); they're also the traditional and modern rulers of Scorpio, the 8th house's own sign in the natural zodiac, so either of them in the 8th works on familiar ground. With Aries rising, Mars may rule your 8th as well as your 1st, if Scorpio sits on that cusp; then one planet ties the self and its closest bonds into a single thread. Venus, ruling Taurus or Libra rising from here, looks for beauty and fairness in what's shared.

Only the birth time places the cusps on either side of the 8th ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)): lower down, in the 7th, your ruler would sit with the partnership itself, the person across the table ([Ruler of the 1st in the 7th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)); higher, past the 9th cusp, it would carry what you've lived through out towards meaning, study and belief ([Ruler of the 1st in the 9th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)).
