Both lights stand in the sign of the scales, so purpose and need are weighed on the same balance, and the counterweight has to come from somewhere else: another person, another part of the chart. You were born within about two and a half days of the new moon in Libra.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

The [Sun in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-libra/) and the [Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/) share one manner, cardinal air. The Sun finds its identity in fairness and proportion; the Moon steadies itself through harmony with the people nearby, an even exchange, a room that looks right. When things are balanced around you, you feel both purposeful and calm. When they aren't, both lights feel it at once, and the urge to set things right can arrive before any thought of what you want.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules Libra, so a single planet speaks for both lights, and its sign, house and aspects are where I start. Tradition counts Libra as the Sun's [fall](/astrology/fall/) and gives the Moon no dignity there. I read that plainly: a self that finds its shape in relationship rather than alone, and needs that settle through fairness rather than retreat. Whatever in your chart lies outside air, the rising sign first, is what gives this pair its counterweight.

At most 30° lay between your Sun and Moon, on one side or the other, so you arrived within about two and a half days of a [new moon in Libra](/astrology/new-moon-in-libra/), the one that falls while the Sun is in Libra, in the weeks after the September equinox. Half of these births come before it, under the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/), and half after it, in the [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) phase; close to exact, the chart holds a [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/). Near the conjunction a few hours decide the side, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) tells the two phases apart.

Two Libra lights belong to about 1 person in 146. A double Libra can only be confirmed with the time on the days the Moon entered Libra from Virgo or left it for Scorpio; on those days the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-virgo/) or the [Sun in Libra, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-libra-moon-in-scorpio/) may be yours instead ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The time also gives the rising sign, and for a chart this centred on one sign, the rising sign and Venus's house are the first things I'd want to know.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

Partnership is part of both your purpose and your comfort, which can make you a devoted, considerate partner who works hard at the relationship's balance. The danger is losing your own weight on the scales: agreeing early, adjusting quickly, and finding out what you felt only later. A partner who asks what you want, and waits for the answer, does you a great kindness. So does a friendship or two that runs to a different rhythm from your love life.

## At work {#at-work}

You tend to shine wherever fairness and appearance both matter: mediation, design, diplomacy, curating, client work, any role where the result must be just and also look right. You read a room quickly and can usually find terms everyone will accept. What drains you is open hostility, and work that asks you to take sides for reasons you don't respect. Keep one area where the decision is entirely yours.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in Libra, it's easy to know what everyone else needs before you know what you need, and to call it peace when it is only postponement. A disagreement kept quiet for months may then arrive all at once. Make your own view the first one you hear: write it down before a conversation, say it before you ask for theirs, and let a disagreement stand a while without fixing it. Balance can include you.
