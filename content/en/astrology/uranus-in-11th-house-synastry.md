Your address book changes when someone's Uranus lands in your 11th house. New friends, groups you'd never have joined, a cause that suddenly matters, a plan for the future that didn't exist last year: through them, your circle and your hopes start to rearrange themselves.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) builds new networks and imagines other ways for a group to work: it is the planet of reform and shared vision as much as of personal freedom. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds your friends and allies, the communities and teams you belong to, and the future you'd like to help make. This [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) puts their Uranus in a house it knows well.

For you, it can widen the world. They may introduce you to people unlike your usual crowd, draw you into a campaign, a club, an online community, or simply make your hopes for the future braver. Friendship with them tends to feel free: you can be yourself without having to fit in.

For them, your circle can feel like home. Your friends, your groups, your projects give their originality an audience and a purpose, and they may feel more themselves among your people than anywhere. Here Uranus lands in the house of its own sign: [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), the 11th's natural sign, belongs to Uranus in modern tropical astrology and to Saturn in the tradition, and I read both. The house is succedent; its old name, the Good Spirit, comes with Jupiter’s joy.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

New friends can unsettle old ones. You may feel pulled between the people you've known for years and the crowd they bring, or find that their causes begin to set your calendar. Groups with them can change shape fast, and loyalty can start to feel optional.

Mistaking distance for objectivity, and your long friendships for conformity, is the risk on their side; your circle took years to build.

None of this says who will stay in your life; it describes how belonging and change meet in it.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep old friends and try new groups, and let each know about the other. Choose which causes are yours, not only theirs. In a couple, let each of you keep a few friends of your own, and talk before giving your evenings to a new group; between friends, this may be the one who connects everyone, best enjoyed without becoming the centre of your social life; between colleagues, networks, unions and professional bodies can gain from their ideas; between adult relatives, a shared cause can bring generations together.

Trade places and it's your Uranus in their 11th house: you're the one reshaping their circle, and the one to respect old friendships you don't share. [Their Uranus in your 10th house](/astrology/uranus-in-10th-house-synastry/) shakes your career; [their Uranus in your 12th house](/astrology/uranus-in-12th-house-synastry/), your inner life.

The 11th's cusp is set by your birth time and place, and in Placidus, the house system I use, a few minutes can move their Uranus in or out of the house; their date places Uranus, which barely moves in a day, and I still want both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, so people close in age share it, and the sign itself tells me about an era rather than about the two of you: their Uranus probably sits near your own, and if yours is in your 11th too ([Uranus in the 11th house](/astrology/uranus-in-11th-house/)), the wish to belong without fitting in may be your generation's signature as much as theirs. Even between friends, two charts are read only with both people knowing and agreeing ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
