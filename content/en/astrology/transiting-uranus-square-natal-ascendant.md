About twenty-one years after Uranus crosses your Ascendant, and twenty-one years before it comes back to it, Uranus stands at right angles to your rising degree. For about twenty months, the way you open a conversation, take your place in a room or start a piece of work fits a little less well each month, and asks to be brought up to date.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reforms: it questions an arrangement as a whole and tries other versions of it. The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is the frame you start from, the posture you take before you've decided anything. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates two functions through friction that pushes both to adapt. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), your habitual way in rubs against a wish to arrive differently: the polite opening you always use, the part you fall into in a new group, the speed at which you commit, or a growing sense that people keep meeting a version of you that's out of date.

The same Uranus squares your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), so the friction reaches the people across from you as well. Part of it is plain timing: you change your manner faster than others change their picture of you, and their slower pace isn't opposition.

Where Uranus stands shapes the pressure. Below the horizon, as a rule in your 3rd or 4th house in Placidus, the house system I use, it tends to come from private life: home, family, the place you live ([Uranus transiting the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-4th-house/)). Above it, usually from your 9th or 10th, it comes from a belief or a public role that wants a different entrance ([Uranus transiting the 10th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-10th-house/)). Those houses, like your rising degree, are drawn from your birth time, and without one I don't read the chart at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus keeps the square for about twenty months; the quickest passes take about eleven. It nearly always comes in three exact hits, the middle one while Uranus is retrograde, so the friction arrives in waves rather than all at once.

By 80, most people born between 1930 and 2009 have had both squares, about forty-two years apart, and everyone has had at least one. Between them lies [transiting Uranus opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-ascendant/) or [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-ascendant/), depending on which square comes first. [Transiting Uranus sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-ascendant/) falls about seven years from a square on the conjunction's side, and [transiting Uranus trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-ascendant/) about seven years from it on the opposition's.

With Uranus in Gemini until May 2033, it's the turn of [Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/), squared from below some twenty years after Uranus crossed it between 2003 and 2011, and of [Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/), squared from above, long before a crossing that comes well after 2039. In Cancer, from 2032 to 2039, Uranus squares Aries and Libra rising in the same two ways.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change one habit of approach at a time. A square from Uranus can make your whole manner feel wrong at once, when usually only one part of it has gone stale.

Tell the people closest to you what you're trying. Your Descendant is squared too, and a change explained is easier to meet than a change guessed at.

Practise the new entrance where little is at stake: a new group, an unfamiliar place, a class. A square asks for practice more than for a grand gesture.
