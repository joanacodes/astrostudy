Some people put their drive into the world and come home to rest. With Mars in the 4th house it's often the other way round: home is the project, the base to defend, the place where the real effort goes.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is effort, initiative and the will to protect what's yours. [The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, roots and family: where you come from, what you build in order to belong, and the private self underneath. With Mars here, drive is rooted. You put energy into making and keeping a home, renovating, moving, mending things with your own hands, and into defending the people and the ground you call yours. Much of this Mars's work is private; the people who see it most are the ones you live with. Some people with this Mars move house often; others stay put and rebuild the same rooms for decades. Either way, the energy keeps circling back to the base.

The 4th is an angular house, and its cusp is the [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), the lowest point of the chart. When you were born, Mars was below the horizon, approaching the lowest point of its daily circle. In the Placidus houses I read, the 4th begins at the IC, and like every angle it moves fast enough that only an exact birth time can place it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Its natural sign is Cancer, the sign of Mars's fall, and the echo is apt: in both, a drive that likes to act directly turns protective instead. The 10th house sits opposite, so what you build at the base often feeds what you do in the world. One house earlier, [Mars in the 3rd house](/astrology/mars-in-3rd-house/) argues in sentences; one house on, [Mars in the 5th house](/astrology/mars-in-5th-house/) plays to win.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

At home, this Mars is protective and practical, and it likes to have a say. You may be the one who decides, organises and defends the household, and you show love by building a safe place for the people in it. Sharing the work of the home fairly matters more to you than grand gestures. The difficulty is that home can also be where frustration lands: tension swallowed at work may come out at the dinner table. Tradition linked the 4th with parents and origins. I don't forecast family events, but I do ask how arguments were handled in the home you grew up in, because this Mars often learned its first lessons in assertion there.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, roots matter. You may thrive working from home, or in property, the building trades, the design of spaces, a family business, or anything that creates security for others. Your ambition often has a private motive: to give yourself, or the people you love, a solid base. Work that keeps you constantly on the move, with no ground of your own, tends to wear this Mars down.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Restlessness at home: rearranging, renovating, moving, never quite settling. Tension that builds in private and comes out with the people closest to you. Old family battles replayed in new relationships. And a drive so bound up with security that taking risks in the outside world feels harder than it should.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the energy a physical home project: a garden, a workshop, a room being slowly made better. Keep a space that's entirely yours, even a corner, where nobody else's rules apply. Leave the day's battles at the door; a short walk before going in can be enough. Talk about how anger worked in your family with someone you trust. And let home be a place where you rest, not only one you defend.
