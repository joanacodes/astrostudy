There are people whose attention goes straight past your manner to what lies underneath. With their Pluto in your 1st house, another person's depth meets the self you lead with, and the way you present yourself can begin to change from the root.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is depth and renewal in a chart: the power to let what no longer fits come to an end, and to rebuild on something truer. [The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) holds your appearance and bearing, your temperament, the impulse with which you start anything. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their depth meets the way you appear.

With them, a persona you have outgrown may start to fall away. You might change your style, your posture or the way you introduce yourself, and feel more present for it: harder to talk over, closer to who you are.

For them, your presence can be magnetic. They sense what is under your surface and want to know it, and your directness may make them face something in themselves. Angular houses don't hide what they hold, and the 1st least of all. Its natural sign, [Aries](/astrology/aries/), belongs to Mars, the planet tradition also gave to Scorpio before Pluto was found, so an old thread runs between this house and Pluto's sign. Right on your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), their Pluto conjoins the rising degree, and the overlay is at its most intense.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Intense attention can feel like scrutiny. You may feel exposed on days you'd rather be ordinary, or sense a question of power in the air: who decides how you come across. You might resist a change you'd welcome from anyone else, or rush a reinvention to meet a gaze.

From their side, frustration can build when you keep your surface on, and the temptation is to push for a depth you haven't offered.

None of this predicts control. Pluto describes depth and change; empowerment is not domination, and being seen clearly is not the same as being laid bare.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Change at your own pace, and say what you'd like to keep. Tell them when their attention feels like warmth and when it feels like a spotlight. In a couple, let reinvention be something you choose, not something you're steered into; between friends, the one who sees through your mask can be the one you let take it off; between colleagues, an intense gaze on your manner deserves plain boundaries; between adult relatives, it may be the person who no longer accepts the old family version of you.

Reverse the charts and your Pluto falls in their 1st house, your depth meeting their manner. [Their Pluto in your 12th house](/astrology/pluto-in-12th-house-synastry/) reaches what you keep private; [their Pluto in your 2nd house](/astrology/pluto-in-2nd-house-synastry/), what you own and value.

Pluto takes 12 to 31 years to cross a sign, depending on where it is in its stretched orbit, so their whole age group shares it, and if you're close in age their Pluto sits near yours. If your own Pluto is in your 1st ([Pluto in the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-in-1st-house/)), this intensity is partly your generation's; with a wide age gap, two eras' ways of remaking the self meet in your face and manner. Their birth date settles Pluto's degree, and their birth time their own houses, which I read in turn, so I wait for both. Your birth time sets your Ascendant, and in Placidus, the house system I use, it decides whether their Pluto stands just inside your 1st or just behind it, in your 12th ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A gaze this searching should only ever fall on someone who has agreed to it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
