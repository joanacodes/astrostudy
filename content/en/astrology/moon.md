The Moon is the fastest body in a birth chart: it crosses a sign in about two and a half days and goes round the whole zodiac in under a month. Astrologers have long read it as the part of you that reacts before it thinks — what you need, what soothes you, what you reach for when the day has been long.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

I read the Moon as need rather than character. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) answers *what are you trying to become?*; the Moon answers *what do you need to feel safe on the way?* It's the self that shows at home, when you're tired or hurt, before you've had time to choose a face.

- **Its sign** shows the style of those needs: room, routine, company, quiet.
- **Its house** shows where in life you go looking for comfort and belonging.
- **Its aspects** show how easily those needs are met — and which other parts of you they argue with.

The Moon works with the Sun, which gives the direction, and with the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), which describes how you meet the world: together they make the first sketch of a chart. It rules [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), and tradition says it is strongest in [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), where comfort is solid, and least at ease in [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) and [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), where feelings are guarded more closely. Less at ease, never less able to love.

Its aspects are where it gets personal. If you were born at a new moon — [Sun conjunct Moon](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon/) — what you want and what you need pull the same way; at a full moon — [Sun opposite Moon](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon/) — they face each other and ask to be balanced. A [Moon square Saturn](/astrology/moon-square-saturn/) often describes someone who learned early to look after their own feelings, and still finds it hard to ask. A [Moon conjunct Venus](/astrology/moon-conjunct-venus/) needs warmth and beauty close by, and tends to give both easily. And the Moon's contacts with [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) show how easily a feeling finds its words.

## The Moon through the signs {#through-the-signs}

The Moon spends about two and a half days in each sign, so people born the same week can have different Moons — and on the day it changes sign, your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon) decides which one is yours. In plain words:

- **[Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/):** feelings arrive fast and burn off fast; you need room to act on them.
- **[Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/):** you feel safe with routine, good food and things you can touch.
- **[Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/):** you understand a feeling once you've talked it through.
- **[Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/):** you need a home base, and people to look after.
- **[Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/):** you need warmth, loyalty and to be seen by the people you love.
- **[Moon in Virgo](/astrology/moon-in-virgo/):** you calm down by being useful and putting things in order.
- **[Moon in Libra](/astrology/moon-in-libra/):** you feel steadier with someone beside you; open conflict unsettles you.
- **[Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/):** you feel deeply and privately; trust comes slowly, then completely.
- **[Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/):** you need space, movement and something to look forward to.
- **[Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/moon-in-capricorn/):** you cope by taking charge, and let feelings out once things are safe.
- **[Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/):** you need a little distance to understand what you feel.
- **[Moon in Pisces](/astrology/moon-in-pisces/):** you soak up the moods around you, and need quiet to find your own again.

## The Moon in the houses {#in-the-houses}

Its house shows where you go for comfort and belonging, and only a birth time can place it. Tradition gave the Moon its joy in the 3rd house, once called the house of the Goddess.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/moon-in-1st-house/):** your moods show on your face; people read you before you speak.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/moon-in-2nd-house/):** you feel safe when you know what you have and what you can count on.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/moon-in-3rd-house/):** the Moon's joy: you settle by talking, writing and staying close to your everyday circle.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/moon-in-4th-house/):** home and family sit at the centre of your emotional life.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/moon-in-5th-house/):** play, creating and falling in love are how you refill.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/moon-in-6th-house/):** routines steady you; your mood shows in the way you work.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/moon-in-7th-house/):** you look for safety in close partnership, and feel things through the other person.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/moon-in-8th-house/):** strong feelings kept private; you open up only where trust runs deep.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/moon-in-9th-house/):** ideas, travel and beliefs are a refuge; distance soothes you.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/moon-in-10th-house/):** your feelings show in public life; often work that looks after people.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/moon-in-11th-house/):** friends and groups are where you feel you belong.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/moon-in-12th-house/):** feelings run quiet and deep; you need time to yourself to know what they are.

## The Moon in synastry {#in-synastry}

In [synastry](/synastry/), the Moon shows whether two people feel safe with each other. When one person's Moon meets the other's Sun — the contact of [Sun conjunct Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon-synastry/) — it often feels like being understood without having to explain. When their Moon falls in your 7th house — [Their Moon in your 7th house](/astrology/moon-in-7th-house-synastry/) — their feelings walk straight into your idea of partnership, and being together can feel like home. Moon contacts with Saturn ask for patience; with Mars, they can feel exciting or raw. I read every Moon contact in both directions: who soothes whom isn't always mutual.

## Transits and the lunar return {#lunar-return}

The Moon goes round your whole chart in about 27⅓ days, so its transits are the shortest of all: a few hours of emphasis when it crosses one of your planets, two days or so in each house — [the Moon through the houses](/astrology/moon-through-the-houses/). Once a month it comes back to the exact degree it held when you were born. The chart drawn for that moment, the [lunar return](/astrology/lunar-return/), is read as the tone of the month ahead.

The slow planets keep a longer clock. When Saturn crosses your Moon — [Transiting Saturn conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-moon/) — astrologers read a season in which feelings ask to be taken seriously. All of it describes the weather, not a forecast: a mood, never an event.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

The Moon was on my list like every placement: its sign and its house, a tick or a cross, in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) — my own chart first, then family, friends and strangers who agreed to it. It wasn't one of the three that held far more often; those were [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), which held 75 to 90% of the time. Overall, about a third of what I checked held: 30 to 40%. So I read the Moon in every chart, but I don't build a reading on it alone, and when part of one rests on it, I tell you. If you don't know your birth time and the Moon changed sign that day, I say so too, and read both. It's a notebook, not a study, and it shapes [how I read](/how-i-read/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“The Moon is a planet.”** It's Earth's satellite. Astrology counts it, with the Sun, as one of the two [luminaries](/astrology/luminaries/) — the lights — and reads it alongside the planets.
- **“You need a birth time to know your Moon sign.”** Only on the day the Moon changes sign. On most days it stays in one sign from midnight to midnight, and the date is enough.
- **“A Moon in Scorpio or Capricorn is a bad Moon.”** Tradition calls them its hardest signs, and they often describe feelings kept close, or held back until it's safe. That's a way of feeling, not a lack of it.
- **“Your Moon sign is the real you; your Sun sign is a mask.”** Both are you. The Moon describes what you need, the Sun where you're heading, and the Ascendant comes closest to a front door.
