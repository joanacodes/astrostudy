Once in its round, the planet of change stands directly across from your natal Saturn. For about twenty months, the order you keep and the change going on around you face each other across your chart, and each shows you what the other is for.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) holds a life together with structure, duty and patience. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees, testing whatever has been taken for granted. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), 180°, relates two functions through polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) your structures meet their counterweight, usually in plain view.

The change often seems to come from outside: an employer reorganising, a partner who wants more room, an institution rewriting its rules, a younger person who does things differently. Or you're the one asking for room, and the person across from you holds the rules. Either way, arrangements that were private habits become something to explain, defend or renegotiate in front of someone else.

I read it as a balance rather than a contest. Saturn shows what deserves to last, Uranus what has outlived its use, and each is clearer with the other in view. Nothing in it decides whether a partnership, a job or a home continues, and I don't read transits for that ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

With Saturn in your 1st and Uranus crossing your 7th ([Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/)), the standards you hold yourself to meet a partner's or a collaborator's need for change, one to one. Without your birth time there's no 1st or 7th house to speak of, and so no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

It isn't Saturn's own opposition, Saturn facing its birth degree at about 44, which belongs to another cycle ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)), though for some birth years the two fall close together.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition lasts about twenty months, eleven at the shortest, with three exact hits in more than nine passes out of ten. It comes once at most by 80, and about 1 person in 25 born between 1930 and 2009 never meets it.

Two natal pictures date it. Born with [Saturn opposite Uranus](/astrology/saturn-opposite-uranus/), in 1964–67 or 2008–09, you started life at or near that point, so the transit came in your first years or won't come until your eighties: in the site's count, someone born at the start of 1965 reaches 80 without it. Born in the late 1980s with [Saturn conjunct Uranus](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-uranus/), you meet it with your [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 43½.

Uranus in Gemini, until 2033, faces every natal Saturn in Sagittarius, the Saturn of births from November 1985 to November 1988. For them the opposition comes in their late thirties or early forties, a few years before their own Uranus opposition or with it. Elsewhere the ages scatter: about 17 for someone born at the start of 1975, about 72 for the start of 2005.

The opposition is the halfway mark of a round that starts at [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-saturn/). [Transiting Uranus trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-saturn/) stands about fourteen years either side of it and [transiting Uranus square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-saturn/) about twenty-one, with [transiting Uranus sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-saturn/) nearer the conjunction.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Separate the structure from the person defending it. Under this opposition a rule often arrives wearing someone's face, and it can be renegotiated while the relationship stays as it is.

Keep a short list of what you won't change. Saturn's side of the axis is easier to hold when it's specific, and everything off the list becomes open to discussion.

Set a review date instead of a verdict. An arrangement both sides agree to try for six months is easier to accept than one meant to be final, and twenty months leaves time for more than one try.
