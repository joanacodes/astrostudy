What in you needs applause answers to what in you would rather not be watched. Your Moon is in Leo, the Sun’s own sign, while your Sun sits in Scorpio: warmth that wants to be seen, governed by a self that lives below the surface.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Scorpio is fixed water and Leo fixed fire, three signs back: a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) keeps its centre private, organised around what is essential and true; the [Moon in Leo](/astrology/moon-in-leo/) needs warmth that comes back, to be noticed, and warmly, by the people it has chosen. Both are loyal and both hold on, which makes for great constancy and, at times, a stand-off inside you between hiding and being seen.

Your Moon's sign belongs to the [Sun](/astrology/sun/), so the Moon follows wherever your Sun leads, and your Sun's sign answers to two planets I read for every Scorpio: [Mars](/astrology/mars/), its ruler in the tradition, and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), its modern one. Follow the chain and both your lights end up in their hands. Mars's placement shows how you act for what you love; Pluto's shows where what you love changes you. Tradition grants no dignity to the Moon in Leo, or to the Sun in Scorpio.

Counted forward from the Sun, the Moon lay 241° to 299° along the zodiac, about 20 to 24½ days into the lunar month, waning towards its last quarter or just past it. The [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) takes half of these births and the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) the other half, and the square of signs tightens into an exact waning [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/) at 270°. With the square waning, the push tends to go inward: you are more likely to rework your own position than to demand that others move, and with a Leo Moon the position in question is usually your pride.

About 1 person in 145 carries this square. Born on a day the Moon changed sign around Leo, you could just as well have the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-cancer/) or the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-virgo/); the time tells ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). That same moment fixes the rising sign and the houses; a Leo Moon's house shows where it looks for its chosen few.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you are loyal, generous and private all at once. The Leo Moon needs to be chosen, warmly and openly; the Scorpio Sun needs to trust before it shows anything. You may long for a partner's admiration and still keep the parts of yourself that would earn it out of sight. Showing a little more, sooner, to someone who has proved trustworthy lets the Moon get the warmth it needs without the Sun feeling exposed.

## At work {#at-work}

You can bring both intensity and warmth to work: leading a team through something difficult, creative work with real depth, any role where your name stands behind something serious. The Leo Moon wants recognition; the Scorpio Sun wants the work to matter. You may prefer influence to the spotlight, and feel quietly hurt when the influence goes unthanked. Ask for the credit you would give anyone else.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Pride and privacy together can let a small hurt harden into a long silence. When appreciation doesn't come, the Leo Moon feels slighted and the Scorpio Sun may withdraw to watch who notices, and nobody does, because nobody knows. Break the stand-off early: say what you need to hear, to the person you need to hear it from, and let them see that it matters.
