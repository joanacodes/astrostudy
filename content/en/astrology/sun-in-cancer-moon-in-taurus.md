Here the ruler of your Sun's sign is the Moon itself, and your Moon stands where tradition honours it most, in Taurus. A self built around shelter rests on needs that are calm, physical and slow to change, and I read the pair as care made solid. A waning crescent, visible before sunrise, was the Moon of your birth.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Cardinal water and fixed earth, with Taurus two signs before Cancer: a sextile by sign, two elements that hold each other, the earth giving the water a shape. The [Sun in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-cancer/) knows itself through belonging, memory and the people it looks after; the [Moon in Taurus](/astrology/moon-in-taurus/) finds its calm in the body and in constancy: meals, gardens, touch, the same evening ritual for years. Cancer takes the first step to shelter people; Taurus keeps the shelter standing. Between them, a household can run for decades.

The rulers make this pair distinctive. Cancer's only ruler is the Moon, so your Moon governs your Sun's sign, and it does so from its [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), the sign where the old books say it works with the greatest ease. That gives the emotional side of your chart a steady authority: when your feelings are settled, your sense of direction follows. [Venus](/astrology/venus/), ruler of Taurus, then shows what comforts this Moon most, and since Venus is never more than about 47° from the Sun, it may even share Taurus with your Moon when the Sun is early in Cancer.

The birth falls in the last week of the lunar month, with the Moon 31° to 88° short of the Sun and rising in the early hours. Most of these births fall in the [last quarter moon](/astrology/last-quarter-moon/) phase of the eight-phase count, and a late few in the [balsamic moon](/astrology/balsamic-moon/); near 60° behind, the Sun and Moon are in a waning [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/). The last quarter is a phase of reviewing and letting go, as I read it, which a Taurus Moon learns slowly and, once it has, well. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sets out the phases.

About one birth in 140 has this pair. Taurus holds the Moon for roughly two and a half days, between Aries and Gemini, so a birth on either crossing could just as easily be the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Aries](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-aries/) or the [Sun in Cancer, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-cancer-moon-in-gemini/), and only the time says which ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The rising sign needs the same time, and it often explains why one person with this pair seems so much more restless than another.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with warmth you can feel: the meal kept hot, the blanket, the arm round a shoulder, the anniversary nobody else remembered. The Cancer Sun protects; the Taurus Moon steadies. The people close to you rarely doubt that they're safe with you. Change inside the bond is the harder part: a move, a new routine, a partner's new ambitions. Give yourself time to get used to things, and say what you need in order to adjust, rather than quietly digging in.

## At work {#at-work}

Food, hospitality, gardening, property, crafts, care work, a family business: your best work joins looking after people to making something that lasts. The Sun needs the work to shelter someone; the Moon wants a steady pace, a familiar place and results you can touch. Constant restructuring wears both lights down. A stable team that values loyalty and a job done well brings out your patience and warmth.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Comfort can turn into a fortress. With the Moon exalted and in charge of your Sun's sign, the familiar has great pull, and keeping everything just as it is can come to feel like keeping people safe. Old belongings, old habits and old grievances may stay long past their use. What helps is gentle, regular change on your own terms: one new thing each season, a clear-out shared with someone you trust, and the reminder that a home is safest when its doors still open.
