Halfway through its round of about 29½ years, Saturn reaches the point across the zodiac from your Sun. Where the crossing asked who you are, the opposition asks who you're with: what you've promised other people, and what you've kept for yourself.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) brings structure, duty and the long view; the [Sun](/astrology/sun/) brings identity and purpose. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets them face to face, so this [transit](/astrology/transits/) tends to show your direction from outside: through a partner's needs, an employer's rules, a commitment that looks different from the other side of the table. Polarity isn't defeat. You see what you've built more clearly because someone or something stands opposite it.

It often works as a review halfway through a round. What you committed to at the last crossing, fifteen years or so ago, has taken root or it hasn't, and this is when other people notice too. Some arrangements need renegotiating; some responsibilities turn out to belong to someone else. None of it predicts a break-up or an ending ([how I read](/how-i-read/)): it describes a season of weighing.

Your Sun's house says what is being weighed. With the Sun in your 1st, the opposition comes while Saturn crosses your 7th, and the questions run through partnership and the promises you've made to others ([Saturn transiting the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-7th-house/)). Houses need your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Sun opposite Saturn](/astrology/sun-opposite-saturn/) in your chart, you've known this tension since birth, and the transit comes back with each Saturn return.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the opposition usually holds for about nine months. A little over half the time it comes as a loop, with three exact passes in about nine and a half months; when Saturn passes once at full speed, three weeks can be enough.

Your birthday tells you which kind to expect. Saturn turns retrograde in the months around its opposition to the Sun in the sky, and while it stands opposite your natal Sun, the Sun reaches that opposition around your birthday. So a loop's middle pass falls within about two months of your birthday, with Saturn rising at sunset and up all night, while a single quick pass lands five or six months away from it ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)). In 2026, Saturn was retrograde over early Aries from late July to mid-December, and the early-October birthdays it opposed sat in the middle of that stretch.

By 80, people born between 1930 and 2009 have had this opposition two or three times, each about fifteen years after [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-sun/). On the way to it, Saturn passes [transiting Saturn sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-sun/), [transiting Saturn square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-sun/) and [transiting Saturn trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-sun/); it meets the same three again, in reverse, on the way back.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List your commitments to other people and mark the ones you'd choose again. The opposition is a good season for renegotiating terms openly, before resentment does it for you.

Let someone else carry their share. Saturn opposite the Sun often shows where you've taken on responsibility that belongs across the table.

Keep one thing that's only yours: an hour, a practice, a project. Balance is the point of this transit, and you are one of its two ends.
