In Moon sextile Mars in synastry, one person's Moon and the other's Mars sit 60° apart. Needs on one side and drive on the other make a natural team: one notices what matters, the other gets moving, and the work goes better for both as soon as one of them gets started.

## What flows {#what-flows}

A person's [Moon](/astrology/moon/) senses what's needed: the mood of a room, what would comfort, what matters to them. Their [Mars](/astrology/mars/) acts: initiative, effort, the will to get on with it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) puts them on working terms through shared opportunity, and of the pair's [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), it's the one most suited to doing things side by side.

When the Moon is yours, this person turns your needs into next steps. Mention that you're dreading something and they offer to come along; say you miss the sea and a trip appears on the calendar.

When Mars is yours, the other person gives your energy a reason. Their feelings tell you what's worth doing, and your effort lands where it helps.

There's often a friendly competitiveness too: who finishes first, who walks faster, who finds the better bargain. Competition isn't hostility, and here it's mostly play; kept light, it energises both, as long as the Moon person can opt out without losing face.

As a rule the two are two signs apart: fire with air, earth with water. A Moon in Pisces with Mars in Capricorn gets its feelings a practical shape; a Moon in Libra with Mars in Sagittarius or Leo gets a push towards what it would otherwise only weigh up.

Between co-workers, it's the pairing that notices a problem and fixes it before anyone else has finished complaining.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

The ease can turn feelings into tasks. When doing things together is this natural, the pair may go for a walk, clean the kitchen or start a project instead of saying what's wrong. Activity helps a mood; it doesn't replace being heard. And like any sextile, this one waits: if neither person suggests anything, nothing much happens.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Suggest something. If Mars is yours, offer one concrete thing to do together; if the Moon is yours, name what you'd like help with instead of hoping it's noticed. Once in a while, swap the plan for a conversation: sit down and talk about the feeling itself before you do anything about it. Friends and couples find this sextile in shared projects; adult relatives, in the jobs that get done together.

With an orb of 4.8°, this sextile turns up between about 1 pair of people in 10, in one direction or the other; with so tight an orb, the Moon person's birth time is the whole question: a morning birth and an evening one can fall on either side of the contact, while Mars, which needs six weeks or more for each sign, hardly shifts. Both birth times come first in any synastry I do, and they also set the houses ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Lived by one person, the angle is [Moon sextile Mars](/astrology/moon-sextile-mars/). Between two charts, [Moon trine Mars in synastry](/astrology/moon-trine-mars-synastry/) offers the same teamwork without needing a first move; [Moon conjunct Mars in synastry](/astrology/moon-conjunct-mars-synastry/) closes the gap between feeling and action altogether; [Moon square Mars in synastry](/astrology/moon-square-mars-synastry/) and [Moon opposite Mars in synastry](/astrology/moon-opposite-mars-synastry/) make the two keep different paces.
