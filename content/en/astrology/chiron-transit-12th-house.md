Some hurts never found their words. They live at the back of the mind, in dreams, in a heaviness that arrives when the house goes quiet, and those are the ones Chiron finds in the 12th, the house that sits just above your Ascendant.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the wounded healer: an old sore spot, and the compassion that grows from living beside it. The [12th house](/astrology/12th-house/) is the part of life that happens out of sight: solitude, rest and retreat, dreams, quiet work, the care you give or receive without witnesses, and what you carry without saying. Tradition named it the Bad Spirit and let [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the planet of solitude, rejoice there. For the length of this stay, a hurt that never quite had a name can come up in private: a grief nobody marked, a loneliness from long ago, a sense of being responsible for something that was never yours. These years ask you to meet it without an audience, and to give yourself the kind of unseen care you may have given others. Natal [Chiron in the 12th house](/astrology/chiron-in-12th-house/) is read as a quiet compassion others feel without knowing why, and this stay can teach some of it to anyone. Not every hurt has to become wisdom, either; some only need to be acknowledged.

If you were born with Saturn in the 12th, Chiron's visit to it asks what it has been guarding alone.

The old books gave the 12th confinement, hospitals and hidden enemies. I don't read any of them in a chart, and nothing here is a diagnosis ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)): if something heavy surfaces, the right door may be a doctor's or a therapist's. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) shows a climate, not a symptom.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 12th has the widest range of Chiron's houses. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude its stays centre on nearly 3 years, but run from about 1 to about 14. At that latitude a 12th house can be anything from 16° to 61° wide: the widest fall around Pisces, Aries and Taurus, the very signs Chiron takes eight or nine years to cross, and the narrowest around Scorpio and Sagittarius, which it crosses fast. New York's latitude narrows the spread to about 2 to 13 years, around a middle of about 3.

The cusp where this stay begins and the Ascendant where it ends both come from your birth time; without it neither date exists, and neither does a reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). It is the last stretch before Chiron rises over your Ascendant ([transiting Chiron conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)) into the house of the self ([Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/)); before it comes the house of friends and shared hopes ([Chiron transiting the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-11th-house/)).

Chiron has been in Aries since April 2018, so a 12th cusp in Aries may have been holding it for years already, and at London's latitude such a 12th is often a wide one. After Aries come Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, and Gemini, from July 2033 to April 2039, then Cancer, from July 2038 to past the end of my count in December 2039. The degree on your cusp gives the year the stay begins; your rising degree, the year it ends.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep a regular hour alone that isn't for anything. The 12th answers to unhurried time.

Write down dreams or late-night thoughts without trying to interpret them at once; patterns show over months.

Give one kindness nobody will know about, and accept one: let someone look after you without having to explain why you need it.

As Chiron nears your rising degree, at the end of the stay, notice what is ready to come out into the open, and what can stay private a while longer.
