Both lights burn in fire here, one tending a hearth and the other carrying a torch down the road. Your Moon had passed its first quarter and was growing, high in the evening sky, and I read the pair as a generous heart that needs a horizon in order to stay generous.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Leo and Sagittarius are both [fire signs](/astrology/fire-signs/), and with four signs between them they make a trine by sign: the two lights agree about warmth, candour and the worth of a big gesture. Their modes differ. The [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) is fixed, loyal to what it has made and the people it has chosen; the [Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/moon-in-sagittarius/) is mutable, and settles once it can see room ahead, a plan, a journey, a question worth chasing. Your sense of self wants a stage to come home to, while your comfort wants the road out of town.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo and is at home there, in its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/); [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules Sagittarius, so this Moon looks to Jupiter for meaning and room to grow. Jupiter's sign and house show where your feelings find their larger story: in study, travel, teaching, belief, or long talks about how to live. With no dignity for the Moon in Sagittarius and a Sun that rules itself, Jupiter is the one other planet this pair leans on first.

Your Moon had pulled 91° to 149° ahead of the Sun by then and was more than half lit, the lunar month a week and a half old, give or take (7½ to 12 days). Close to nine births in ten fall in Rudhyar's [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) phase and the rest in the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/); around 120° the trine becomes an aspect as well as a sign relation, a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). That an astronomer would call most of these Moons gibbous while astrologers file them under the first quarter is one of the small confusions [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) clears up.

Of every 137 people born, roughly one has this pair. On the day the Moon came in from Scorpio or crossed into Capricorn, the time of birth decides whether you read this page, the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-scorpio/) or the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-capricorn/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also puts the rising sign on the horizon, and a pair this fond of horizons deserves to know which one it was born with.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love generously, with humour and a sense of adventure, and you're honest even when tact would be easier. The Leo Sun wants to be chosen and kept; the Sagittarius Moon needs room to roam, on the map or in the mind. A partner who travels with you, laughs easily and trusts you out of their sight suits both lights. The risk is that the Moon's need for freedom reads, to a partner, as a lack of the devotion your Sun in fact feels deeply. Say both out loud.

## At work {#at-work}

You can sell a vision and mean every word. The Leo Sun brings conviction and presence; the Sagittarius Moon brings enthusiasm, range and a feel for the larger picture. Teaching, publishing, leading projects that open something new, any work that inspires other people suits the pair. Detail and routine are where you flag: the follow-up email, the long middle of a project. Team up with someone who loves finishing, and give them the credit they're due.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Fire on fire can overreach. The Leo Sun promises wholeheartedly and the Sagittarius Moon promises the future, so between them you may say yes to more than any week can hold, then feel your pride on the line when it can't. Restlessness can also look like boredom to the people who rely on your loyalty. What helps is one horizon at a time, chosen on purpose, and a plain practice that keeps you earthed: a garden, a long walk, a list with dates on it.
