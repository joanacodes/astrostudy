Each traditional planet has a sign it rules and a house it enjoys. The first is its domicile; the second is its joy, a house where the old astrologers said it was glad to be.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

The joys run through the houses like this:

- **Mercury in the 1st,** the house of the self and the body ([Mercury in the 1st house](/astrology/mercury-in-1st-house/)).
- **The Moon in the 3rd,** the Goddess: daily life, talk and the people close by ([Moon in the 3rd house](/astrology/moon-in-3rd-house/)).
- **Venus in the 5th,** Good Fortune: pleasure, play and what you make ([Venus in the 5th house](/astrology/venus-in-5th-house/)).
- **Mars in the 6th,** Bad Fortune: work, effort and the daily round ([Mars in the 6th house](/astrology/mars-in-6th-house/)).
- **The Sun in the 9th,** the God: study, travel and belief ([Sun in the 9th house](/astrology/sun-in-9th-house/)).
- **Jupiter in the 11th,** Good Spirit: friends, groups and hopes ([Jupiter in the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house/)).
- **Saturn in the 12th,** Bad Spirit: solitude, retreat and what happens out of sight ([Saturn in the 12th house](/astrology/saturn-in-12th-house/)).

The other five houses, the 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th and 10th, have no joy. And a joy isn't a [domicile](/astrology/domicile/) or an exaltation. Those are signs, where a planet's nature is at home or honoured; a joy is a house, a part of life where its work suits it.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Set the joys round the wheel and a design appears. They come in facing pairs: the two lights on the axis of the 3rd and the 9th, the Goddess and the God; the two benefics on the 5th and the 11th, Good Fortune and Good Spirit; the two malefics on the 6th and the 12th, Bad Fortune and Bad Spirit. Mercury, which belongs to no pair, stands alone in the 1st.

The design follows [sect](/astrology/sect-day-and-night-charts/) as well. The three day planets, the Sun, Jupiter and Saturn, rejoice in the 9th, 11th and 12th, all above the horizon; the three night planets, the Moon, Venus and Mars, in the 3rd, 5th and 6th, all below it. Mercury, which can join either sect, has its joy in the one house that begins on the horizon itself. Each sect's benefic and malefic sit side by side in their own half of the chart, the same pairs that sect sets apart in every chart ([Benefic and malefic of the sect](/astrology/benefic-and-malefic-of-the-sect/)).

A joy was never a measure of strength. Four of the seven, the 3rd, 6th, 9th and 12th, are cadent houses, the kind tradition ranked weakest ([Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/)). Tradition had other ways of describing a planet at ease, among them [hayz](/astrology/hayz/), for a planet whose sect, sign and side of the horizon all agree.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I read a planet in its joy as a planet at home in that part of life: the work it does and the setting it does it in suit each other. It's ease, not rank, and it doesn't cancel the planet's sign, its aspects or anything else in the chart. Saturn in the 12th still asks for patience and structure; it simply asks for them in a part of life that suits it, the quiet work done alone.

The joys need the houses, and the houses need the birth time: a few minutes can carry a planet across a cusp, into its joy or out of it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). I read them in Placidus, the house system I use. In Whole Sign houses a planet can sit a house away, and so in or out of its joy, which is worth knowing before you compare two charts ([Which house system should I use?](/astrology/which-house-system-should-i-use/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“A planet in its joy is exalted.”** Exaltation is a sign; a joy is a house. Mars, for instance, is exalted in Capricorn and rejoices in the 6th.
- **“Bad Fortune and Bad Spirit are bad houses.”** They're tradition's names for the houses of toil and of what lies out of sight. A planet there isn't condemned to either.
- **“Saturn in its joy means you're meant to be alone.”** The 12th is the house of solitude and retreat; Saturn there describes how you use time alone, not a life without people.
- **“Only the benefics have joys.”** All seven traditional planets have one, the malefics included: Mars in the 6th, Saturn in the 12th.
