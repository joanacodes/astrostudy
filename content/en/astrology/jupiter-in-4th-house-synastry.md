Their Jupiter in your 4th house brings another person's generosity home: to the place you live, the family you belong to and the private life behind your door. With them around, home can feel roomier, warmer and more like somewhere you're glad to come back to.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) grows things: belief, understanding, the sense that there is room enough. [The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) holds your foundations: the home you make and the one you came from, belonging and the self you keep for those closest. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their Jupiter here widens your foundations.

With them, home may open up. They're the guest who becomes family, the partner who makes a flat feel like a house, the friend whose kitchen table becomes yours too. Old ideas about where you come from may soften as well: they can help you look at your roots with more generosity than you managed alone.

To them, your private world may feel like belonging found. They may love being welcomed into your home and your household's rituals, and their generosity there feels natural. Angular and private at once, the 4th has [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) for its natural sign, the sign of Jupiter's exaltation in tradition, where its generosity was thought to work best: their Jupiter arrives in the house that suits it most warmly. Close to your [Imum Coeli](/astrology/imum-coeli/), it also touches the lowest point of your chart, the one that holds your foundations, and that contact is added to the reading.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

An open door can let in more than you meant. Their generosity may fill your home with people, plans and opinions until there's no quiet left, or their ideas about family, how close, how often, how many, may not be yours. Being looked after can tip into having your home run for you.

From their side, a closed door can feel like a closed heart. If you need your home to stay small and private, they may take it personally, or feel like a visitor where they hoped to belong.

Their Jupiter here promises nothing about houses, property or family events; it describes how belonging and generosity meet in your private life, between two adults.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Decide together how open the door is. Hospitality is a gift when the person whose home it is chooses it. In a couple who share a home, agree how often it fills with people and how often it's yours alone; between friends, they may be the one with a standing invitation, and it's fine to set the days; between adult relatives, family gatherings go better when no one is pressed into belonging on someone else's terms; between colleagues, a welcome at home is a generous step, best taken when it suits you both.

Their own chart holds the mirror: your Jupiter in their 4th house, your generosity filling their home. [Their Jupiter in your 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-in-3rd-house-synastry/) brings ideas to your daily talk; [their Jupiter in your 5th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-5th-house-synastry/) carries the same warmth into play and delight.

Your Imum Coeli, opposite your Midheaven and, in Placidus, the house system I use, the cusp of your 4th, is set by your birth time: a few minutes move it by a degree. Jupiter's daily step is tiny, about a twelfth of a degree, so the date they were born places it; their time would matter only if it sat on your IC, and I still ask for both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Home is where people are most themselves, which is why this reading needs their consent as much as yours ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
