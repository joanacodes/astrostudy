The new moon in Aries comes once a year, when the Moon catches up with the Sun in the first sign of the zodiac. It's the first new moon after the spring equinox, and I read it as a short marker for beginnings, in whichever part of your chart Aries occupies.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

At every [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/), the Moon passes between the Earth and the Sun and joins it in the same sign. The Sun is the part of a chart that organises identity, direction and purpose; the Moon regulates needs, feeling and the sense of being safe. At a new moon the two start from the same point, and in [Aries](/astrology/aries/) that point is fire and cardinal: quick to act, direct, at its best at the start of things. Mars rules the sign, and tradition gives the Sun its exaltation there, so this is a new moon at ease with initiative.

Its dates, in Universal Time:

- **2026:** 17 April, 11:52 UT, at 27°29′ Aries
- **2027:** 6 April, 23:51 UT, at 16°57′ Aries
- **2028:** 26 March, 04:31 UT, at 6°03′ Aries
- **2029:** 13 April, 21:40 UT, at 24°14′ Aries
- **2030:** 2 April, 22:03 UT, at 13°12′ Aries

The degrees fall by about eleven from one year to the next, because twelve lunar months are about eleven days shorter than a year. When the slide would carry the new moon back into Pisces, as after 2028, the following lunation takes its place, late in Aries again. None of these is an eclipse: the most recent solar eclipse in Aries was on 29 March 2025, and the next comes on 30 March 2033 ([eclipse in Aries](/astrology/eclipse-in-aries/)).

How much it means for you depends on contact. In the rule I follow for lunations, a new moon counts within one degree of a natal planet or angle, and as a trigger inside something already active there, a transit or a progression, never as a forecast of its own. The 2027 new moon, at 16°57′ Aries, would count only for a chart with something within a degree of it.

## House by house {#house-by-house}

With Aries on the cusp of a house, the new moon in Aries usually falls in that house; the exact degree decides when Aries straddles two. Finding them takes [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), in Placidus, the house system I use.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aries-rising/):** Aries rising: the new moon falls on you, on how you meet people and set off; a fresh start you make in person.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aries-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you earn and own; the urge to start a plan for money or worth on your own terms.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aries-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** talk, errands, siblings, the neighbourhood; a conversation you open first, or a subject you start learning.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/):** home and family; a start under your own roof, a repair or a change you've been putting off.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aries-on-5th-house-cusp/):** play, romance, creative work; a project begun for the fun of it, a first move made openly.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aries-on-6th-house-cusp/):** your working days and routines; a new habit started briskly rather than planned to death.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aries-on-7th-house-cusp/):** partners and close others, with Libra rising; someone else may take the initiative, or you start something as a pair.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aries-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what is shared, owed and trusted; the frank first word on a joint matter.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aries-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs, study, distant places; a trip, a course or an idea taken up on impulse.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/):** work and public role; an initiative you put your name to.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aries-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends, groups, hopes; a shared project you get moving.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aries-on-12th-house-cusp/):** rest and what happens out of sight; a private beginning, started before anyone else knows.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Find its degree in your chart before anything else: which house it falls in, and whether a planet or angle sits within a degree of it. If nothing does, the new moon in Aries is the month's weather in that house, a small push to begin, and nothing to brace for.

If something does, look past the new moon to what is already working on that point, because a lunation only brings it into focus. Then let Aries' own manner help: start small and soon, rather than waiting for a perfect plan. Mars, the sign's ruler, shows by its house where the push tends to go.

And leave room to change your mind. A new moon never makes a decision for you: it isn't a deadline, a green light or a warning. Two weeks later comes a full moon in Libra; six months later, a full moon falls in Aries. The neighbouring new moons, in [Pisces](/astrology/new-moon-in-pisces/) before it and [Taurus](/astrology/new-moon-in-taurus/) after, describe quieter starts.
