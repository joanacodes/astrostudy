They have a game no one else knows the rules of, a running joke twenty years old, a way of celebrating that would embarrass their friends. Composite Lilith in the 5th house puts a relationship's untamed side in its pleasures: the joy it takes on its own terms and doesn't explain.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is the house of what is done for joy: play, pleasure, romance, creative work, the self that shows when nobody is grading it. It is succedent, Leo is its natural sign, and tradition called it Good Fortune and gave Venus its joy there. In [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/), it describes how the relationship enjoys itself. With [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the point I read as hidden and untamed, that enjoyment refuses to be polite. The pair's fun may be odd, private, a little irreverent; its romance may follow no script the families would recognise; the things it makes together, music, a garden, a story, a business on the side, may be for its own eyes only.

Neither of you needs to be playful elsewhere for this to show. Serious people can share a silliness nobody else sees, and each may be surprised, years in, by how much of the relationship's life happens in that private room. What it hides is how much it enjoys itself; what it refuses is to justify its pleasures to anyone.

Here the difference between a composite sign and a composite house matters. The two birth dates fix composite Lilith's degree to within a few minutes of arc, because mean Lilith moves so slowly. The house is another matter: it needs both birth times, and a choice between the midpoints of your two charts' cusps and houses built from the composite Midheaven where you live. A composite can have a well-defined Lilith sign and no reliable house at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Tradition also gave the 5th house children. A composite never predicts them, any more than a birth chart does ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and the site reads only adults' charts ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).

On one person, [Lilith in the 5th house](/astrology/lilith-in-5th-house/) is a joy that was once made to justify itself; between two charts, [their Lilith in your 5th house](/astrology/lilith-in-5th-house-synastry/) turns someone's unruly delight loose in yours. The composite's 5th is the delight the pair shares. Before it, [composite Lilith in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-4th-house/) guards its home; after it, [composite Lilith in the 6th house](/astrology/composite-lilith-in-6th-house/) keeps its untamed side in the daily round.

Its difficulty is a pleasure so private it can't be shared, or one that exists mostly as a protest. It helps to let some of the play be seen by friends who will enjoy it, and now and then to finish one of the things you make together and show it.

Composites are a technique I explain rather than use in my readings; their users read this Lilith beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). Its pleasures belong to two adults, and so does the decision to look at them ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
