Sagittarius on the 3rd house cusp puts the sign of far horizons in charge of the nearest things: the street, the next message, the walk to the shops. Everyday talk tends to drift from what happened to what it means.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is everyday thinking in motion, messages, errands, lessons, the people next door, and the sign at its threshold sets the pace ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) there, it moves in leaps. You tend to answer the question behind the question, turn an errand into a detour and a train journey into a seminar, and remember the story long after the figures have gone. In this chart the 3rd and the 9th trade their natural signs: Gemini, the 3rd's own sign, opens your 9th, while the sign of long journeys and big questions opens the house of short ones. [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), the planet this house belongs to by nature, is in detriment in Sagittarius. To me that describes a mind that prefers the principle to the footnote, not a weaker one: checking dates and spellings is simply the part you do deliberately.

Where the 3rd touches siblings, cousins and neighbours, the cusp describes your side of the tie, not who they are: often a bond kept up through talk and argument about large things, sometimes across a distance. Early schooling may have left you with a taste for learning that's free to roam, and little patience for copying out what you were told.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules this 3rd house. Its sign colours how you speak and study, and its house is the one your conversations keep coming back to: the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Pisces, the sign Jupiter shares with Neptune, opens your 5th or your 6th in every one of these charts, now and then both, so the way you talk and learn shares a ruler with your pleasures or with your working days.

Sagittarius sits on the 3rd cusp in about 1 birth in 11, and the two cities agree, in Placidus, the house system I use. That's a little more than an even share: Sagittarius rises slowly in the north, and the cusps beneath the eastern horizon spend longer in it than in most signs. Every one of these charts has [Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/), at both latitudes, so Venus rules your chart: people meet a tactful manner first and Sagittarius's frank opinions once the talk warms up. These charts give the 3rd some 36° to 39° at London, and about a quarter of them hold all of Capricorn inside it, an [intercepted sign](/astrology/intercepted-signs/) that brings a patient, methodical streak to the way you learn.

A later birth the same day would have lifted Sagittarius to the 2nd cusp ([Sagittarius on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-2nd-house-cusp/)); an earlier one would have left it lower, on the 4th ([Sagittarius on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/)). Telling these apart takes the birth time to the minute, and a reading waits until I have it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With it, this cusp often explains why your best thinking happens halfway through a conversation, or on the way somewhere.
