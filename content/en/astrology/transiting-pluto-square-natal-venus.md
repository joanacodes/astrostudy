Pluto square your Venus presses what you value to become more honest. For two to three years a 90° angle sets the planet of depth against your tastes, your affections and your sense of a fair exchange, and arrangements that worked only because nobody looked closely start to chafe.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) reorganises what has become unsustainable, beginning with what was kept out of sight. Your [Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs value and reciprocity: what you like, what you give, what you expect back. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates them through friction that forces adaptation, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the terms of your attachments come up for review. The bargain in a friendship that has quietly become one-sided. A pleasure that has turned into a habit. A taste you keep because it is expected of you.

Reciprocity is the word I come back to with this square. A bond doesn't have to be even every week, but over two or three years the giving and the receiving should roughly balance, and Pluto shows you plainly where they don't.

Holding on tighter can feel like keeping a bond safe. Control isn't safety, though, and it rarely keeps what it holds; what tends to last under this square is what you can name honestly and still choose. No transit forecasts a break-up, and none makes control acceptable. If a relationship has begun to feel controlling or frightening, the sky is not the reason and waiting is not the answer: talk to someone you trust, or to someone trained to help ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

Each of the two squares sits halfway between the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-venus/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-venus/)), with a sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-venus/)) 30° to one side and a trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-venus/)) 30° to the other. In a birth chart, [Venus square Pluto](/astrology/venus-square-pluto/) turns this friction into a lifelong lesson in honest attachment.

With Venus in the 7th and Pluto crossing the 10th ([Pluto transiting the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-10th-house/)), what you value in partnership rubs against the demands of your work and your public role. Those houses are drawn from the minute of birth; a chart without its time is one I leave unread ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, which run from 1½° before exact to 1° after, Pluto takes about two years and eight months to get through this square: about twenty-two months in the quickest passes, nearly four years in the slowest. Most bring three exact hits; about two in five bring five.

About seven people in eight born between 1930 and 2009 meet one of Venus's two Pluto squares by 80, which makes it the most widely shared of the five contacts; about one in eight never does.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it squares Venuses in Taurus and Scorpio.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name what you have been tolerating, on paper first, where nobody else has to read it.

Say no, once, to something you agreed to only to keep the peace, and notice what the bond does with your honesty.

Ask what you would keep if nobody minded either way. That list is your Venus without the pressure.
