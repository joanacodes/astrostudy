Once a year the Moon goes dark in its own sign: the new moon in Cancer, the Sun and Moon together in cardinal water as the northern summer begins. It turns attention towards home, care and belonging, and in 2028 and 2029 it doubles as a solar eclipse.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

Each month the Moon catches up with the Sun, and for a moment the two stand at the same degree: a [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/), purpose and need starting out together. In [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), that degree lies in the Moon's domicile: the sign it rules, where tradition says it works most like itself. Cancer is water and cardinal. It starts things through feeling and care rather than argument, and it protects what it starts. A new moon here tends to turn attention to who and what makes you feel at home.

In Universal Time, from 2026 to 2030:

- **2026:** 14 July, 09:44 UT, at 21°59′ Cancer
- **2027:** 4 July, 03:02 UT, at 11°57′ Cancer
- **2028:** 22 June, 18:28 UT, at 1°51′ Cancer, and 22 July, 03:02 UT, at 29°51′ Cancer, a total solar eclipse
- **2029:** 11 July, 15:51 UT, at 19°38′ Cancer, a partial solar eclipse
- **2030:** 30 June, 21:35 UT, at 9°09′ Cancer

2028 has two. The Sun takes about 31 days to cross Cancer, longer than a lunar month, and that year's first Cancer new moon comes about two days after the Sun enters the sign, leaving room for a second one in its very last degree. That second one, and the new moon of July 2029, are solar eclipses, because the Moon's South Node is in Cancer in those years ([eclipse in Cancer](/astrology/eclipse-in-cancer/)).

The eclipses widen the reach. In an ordinary year, I give a new moon in Cancer a single degree either side of a natal planet or angle, and only when a transit or progression is already at work on that point. The eclipse of 22 July 2028 reaches about three degrees, to anything near 29° Cancer or 29° Capricorn; the partial one of 11 July 2029, anything near 19° or 20° of either sign. Read through that point and its house, each is emphasis; neither is an omen ([solar eclipses](/astrology/solar-eclipses/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Cancer may open one of your houses or reach across two, and that matters more than usual in 2028, when its two new moons sit at the start and the very end of the sign. [Your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/) settles it; I draw the houses in Placidus.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** Cancer rising: a gentler way of meeting the world; tending to how you feel before how you look.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/cancer-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money and belongings as a source of safety; things kept for the memories in them.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** siblings, neighbours, everyday words; checking in on people, writing to someone you miss.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** Cancer's natural house: home, family, roots; a fresh start under your roof.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/):** joy, love affairs, creative work; work drawn from memory, affection shown in small acts.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/):** working days; looking after the people you work with, making a workspace feel like yours.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** partners, with Capricorn rising; being looked after, or doing the looking after, in a close bond.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what you share and owe; feelings tied up in family money or shared belongings.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/):** faraway places and big questions; a journey towards your roots, learning where you come from.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** your calling and public face; a role that protects or cares for others, a reputation built on trust.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/):** the people you gather with; friends as chosen family, a group that feels like home.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/cancer-on-12th-house-cusp/):** rest and retreat; quiet time with memories, care you give yourself out of sight.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look for its degree in your chart first: the house, then any natal planet or angle within a degree, or within about three for the eclipses of 2028 and 2029. If there's nothing that close, take it as a mood passing through that house: a pull towards home and comfort, with nothing to fear in it.

If something is that close, find what a slower movement is already doing to the point, and let the new moon light it up. Cancer's way helps: see to what you need to feel safe first, then build a base you can come back to. The Moon rules Cancer, so the house of your natal Moon shows where those needs are felt most.

It sets no deadline and makes no decision for you, and an eclipse in Cancer is no omen for a family or a home. Its neighbours sound different notes: the [new moon in Gemini](/astrology/new-moon-in-gemini/) a month earlier is all questions, and the [new moon in Leo](/astrology/new-moon-in-leo/) a month later steps out into the light.
