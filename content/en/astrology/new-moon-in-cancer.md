The new moon in Cancer is the year's new moon in the Moon's own sign, cardinal water, at the start of the northern summer. I read it as a marker for home, care and belonging, and in 2028 and 2029 it carries the extra weight of a solar eclipse.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

Every [new moon](/astrology/new-moon/) brings the Sun and Moon together in one sign, so the Sun's sense of purpose and the Moon's needs start from the same place. In [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/), that place is the Moon's domicile: the sign it rules, where tradition says it works most like itself. Cancer is water and cardinal. It starts things through feeling and care rather than argument, and it protects what it starts. A new moon here tends to turn attention to who and what makes you feel at home.

Its dates, in Universal Time:

- **2026:** 14 July, 09:44 UT, at 21°59′ Cancer
- **2027:** 4 July, 03:02 UT, at 11°57′ Cancer
- **2028:** 22 June, 18:28 UT, at 1°51′ Cancer, and 22 July, 03:02 UT, at 29°51′ Cancer, a total solar eclipse
- **2029:** 11 July, 15:51 UT, at 19°38′ Cancer, a partial solar eclipse
- **2030:** 30 June, 21:35 UT, at 9°09′ Cancer

2028 has two. The Sun takes about 31 days to cross Cancer, longer than a lunar month, and that year's first Cancer new moon comes about two days after the Sun enters the sign, leaving room for a second one in its very last degree. That second one, and the new moon of July 2029, are solar eclipses, because the Moon's South Node is in Cancer in those years ([eclipse in Cancer](/astrology/eclipse-in-cancer/)).

The eclipses widen the reach. An ordinary new moon in Cancer counts, in the rule I follow, within one degree of a natal planet or angle, and only as a trigger inside something a transit or progression is already doing. The eclipses of 22 July 2028 and 11 July 2029 count within about three degrees, at either end of their axis: near 29° Cancer or Capricorn for the first, near 19° or 20° for the second. I read them through the point they reach and its house, as emphasis, never as an omen ([solar eclipses](/astrology/solar-eclipses/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Where Cancer opens a house, the new moon in Cancer usually falls inside it; if Cancer stretches over two houses, its own degree decides. The houses need [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and I draw them in Placidus.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** Cancer rising: a gentler way of meeting the world; tending to how you feel before how you look.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/cancer-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** money and belongings as a source of safety; things kept for the memories in them.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** siblings, neighbours, everyday words; checking in on people, writing to someone you miss.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** Cancer's natural house: home, family, roots; a fresh start under your roof.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasure, romance, making things; work drawn from memory, affection shown in small acts.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/):** working days; looking after the people you work with, making a workspace feel like yours.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** partners, with Capricorn rising; being looked after, or doing the looking after, in a close bond.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what you share and owe; feelings tied up in family money or shared belongings.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs, study, travel; a journey towards your roots, learning where you come from.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** work and public role; a role that protects or cares for others, a reputation built on trust.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends and groups; friends as chosen family, a group that feels like home.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/cancer-on-12th-house-cusp/):** rest and retreat; quiet time with memories, care you give yourself out of sight.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Look for its degree in your chart first: the house, then any natal planet or angle within a degree, or within about three for the eclipses of 2028 and 2029. If there's nothing that close, it's the month's weather in that house, a pull towards home and comfort with nothing to fear in it.

If something is that close, find what a slower movement is already doing to the point, and let the new moon light it up. Cancer's way helps: see to what you need to feel safe first, then build a base you can come back to. The Moon rules Cancer, so the house of your natal Moon shows where those needs are felt most.

It sets no deadline and makes no decision for you; an eclipse in Cancer is no omen for a family or a home. Before it comes the [new moon in Gemini](/astrology/new-moon-in-gemini/), all questions; after it, the [new moon in Leo](/astrology/new-moon-in-leo/), which steps out into the light.
