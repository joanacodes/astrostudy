Some contacts between two charts make no noise at all. In Sun sextile Moon, one person's Sun stands about 60° from the other's Moon, usually two signs away, and what passes between them is everyday help: the plan that happens to suit both, the mood that lifts a little when the other arrives.

## What flows {#what-flows}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is a person's centre, the identity and direction they live from. The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) keeps them steady: it registers needs, forms attachments, settles into habits. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) joins two functions through cooperation that has to be taken up, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) it's one of the quietest: two people, each holding one of the lights, with a door left open between them.

Two signs apart usually means fire with air, or earth with water: temperaments that suit each other without being alike. One person's Sun in Aries, the other's Moon in Aquarius: the Sun person's independence is just the company an Aquarius Moon needs, close without crowding. A Sun in Virgo beside a Moon in Cancer: practical care, offered to a Moon that feels safe when someone looks after the details.

If it's your Sun, your plans tend to find a friendly reception. You mention an idea, and the other person's response is practical and warm; their moods rarely argue with your direction, so you can be yourself without managing them. If it's your Moon, the other's way of being gives your days a little lift. Their enthusiasm suits your needs, and asking them for what would help feels easy, because they seem glad to be asked.

For flatmates, that's chores shared without a rota; for a couple or two friends, trips that work for both; for colleagues, one who sets the course and one who notices how everyone is doing, each glad of the other.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A sextile doesn't push, so its gifts can sit unused. Nothing forces either of you to say what you need, and so neither may: the Moon person adjusts quietly, the Sun person assumes all is well, and the help stays on offer instead of in use. Pleasant company can stay pleasant and stop there.

There's a smaller risk too. Because the Sun person's plans usually suit the Moon person, the one time they don't can surprise both. A sextile is a fit between two different people, not a promise that their needs will always agree. The pair's other angles change the terms:

- **[Sun conjunct Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-moon-synastry/):** one person's nature right on the other's needs; closer, and quicker to blur.
- **[Sun square Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-square-moon-synastry/):** a sign further round, and the trade becomes a negotiation over the same hours.
- **[Sun trine Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-trine-moon-synastry/):** two signs further, in one element; the help arrives before anyone asks.
- **[Sun opposite Moon in synastry](/astrology/sun-opposite-moon-synastry/):** the lights face each other across the zodiac, each showing the other what it leaves out.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Use it on purpose. Say the small things: "I'd love a quiet evening", "I'm starting something and I'd like your eye on it". The Moon person can name a need before it turns into a mood; the Sun person can invite rather than announce. Between a parent and an adult child, or in an old friendship, a regular call or a shared walk is often enough to keep the door open.

Within 4.8°, and counting it either way round, about 1 pair of people in 10 has this sextile. That's the narrowest orb I use for the Sun and Moon, and with a body as quick as the Moon, it makes the birth time decisive: the Moon crosses the whole window in less than a day. I wait for both birth times before reading any synastry, and here a few hours can decide whether the contact exists at all ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Within one chart, the same angle is [Sun sextile Moon](/astrology/sun-sextile-moon/), a birth under a crescent moon. Between two people, the two lights come from two different birthdays, and the cooperation needs both of them to take it up.
