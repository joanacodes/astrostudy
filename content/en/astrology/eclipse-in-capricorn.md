On 26 June 2029 the Moon will be eclipsed at 4°49′ Capricorn, nineteen years to the day after an eclipse at 4°50′: one minute of arc apart. Capricorn builds over the long run, and its eclipses come back with much the same patience.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/) is cardinal earth, ruled by Saturn: structure, responsibility, the work of years. Its eclipses fall on the Capricorn–Cancer axis, the public world and the private one, duty and care, alternating with Cancer’s as the Moon’s nodes move. A [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) in Capricorn joins the Sun and Moon in the sign, as on 26 December 2038 at 4°19′, the only total one in my list; a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) sets the full Moon in Capricorn against the Sun in Cancer, as on 26 June 2029.

In a chart I go by the AGS rule: an eclipse counts within about three degrees of a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of its axis, and I read it through that point and its house. Five of Capricorn’s twenty are penumbral, the most recent, on 5 July 2020, and the next, on 18 July 2027, among them, and those weigh less. I look at the mean and the true node alike. Never an omen; a stretch of emphasis.

Twenty from 2000 to 2040:

- **2000–2002:** 16 July 2000, total lunar, 24°19′ · 25 December 2000, partial solar, 4°14′ · 5 July 2001, partial lunar, 13°34′ · 24 June 2002, penumbral lunar, 3°02′
- **2009–2011:** 7 July 2009, penumbral lunar, 15°32′ · 15 January 2010, annular solar, 25°01′ · 26 June 2010, partial lunar, 4°50′ · 4 January 2011, partial solar, 13°38′
- **2019–2020:** 6 January 2019, partial solar, 15°25′ · 16 July 2019, partial lunar, 24°00′ · 26 December 2019, annular solar, 4°07′ · 5 July 2020, penumbral lunar, 13°29′
- **2027–2029:** 18 July 2027, penumbral lunar, 25°57′ · 6 July 2028, partial lunar, 15°15′ · 14 January 2029, partial solar, 24°49′ · 26 June 2029, total lunar, 4°49′
- **2037–2038:** 16 January 2037, partial solar, 26°36′ · 5 January 2038, annular solar, 15°18′ · 16 July 2038, penumbral lunar, 23°56′ · 26 December 2038, total solar, 4°19′

The pattern moves in nineteen-year steps: 5 July 2001 at 13°34′ and 5 July 2020 at 13°29′; 25 December 2000, 26 December 2019 and 26 December 2038, all near 4° Capricorn. Its neighbours have pages too ([Eclipse in Sagittarius](/astrology/eclipse-in-sagittarius/), [Eclipse in Aquarius](/astrology/eclipse-in-aquarius/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house a Capricorn eclipse falls in is fixed by [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); every cusp depends on it, which is why I don’t read without it. These lines are in Placidus, my house system; each applies when a Capricorn eclipse meets a natal point in that house, with the Cancer degrees opposite reaching the house across.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/capricorn-rising/):** Capricorn rising: a composed, capable bearing; the responsibility you show the world.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you’ve built up; the security that comes from effort.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/capricorn-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** measured words; a plan talked through step by step.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-4th-house-cusp/):** duty at home; the structure a family rests on.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-5th-house-cusp/):** pleasures that are earned; a craft or a romance you take seriously.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-6th-house-cusp/):** discipline in daily work; routines that hold over years.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn on the Descendant, with Cancer rising: a committed partnership; who carries what.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-8th-house-cusp/):** long-term commitments shared; obligations held jointly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-9th-house-cusp/):** traditions and teachers; a belief tested by time.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Capricorn in its natural house: career, standing, public responsibility; the long climb, reviewed.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-11th-house-cusp/):** dependable friendships; your role in a group that lasts.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/capricorn-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private burdens and quiet resilience. In the old scheme this was the house of confinement; I read time apart and inner work in it, and questions of mental health belong with qualified people ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).
