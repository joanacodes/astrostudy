A hand that has learned to hold on is where the North Node in Scorpio starts, and opening it is the work. Growth with the [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) in [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) goes down rather than up: into trust, intimacy and the kind of change you can't plan.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Fixed water runs deep: Scorpio is loyal, intense and alert to what lies under the surface. Opposite it, the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) describes your home ground: steadiness, endurance, an easy relationship with the material world, a strong preference for what is known and solid. That steadiness is a real gift. The Scorpio end asks you to use it as a base rather than a wall: to share what you'd rather keep, to look at what you'd rather not, and to let a situation that has run its course actually end.

Scorpio has two rulers, the modern and the traditional, and I read both. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), the modern one, moves so slowly that its sign belongs to a generation, so I look at its house: the part of life where the deepest changes ask to happen. [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the traditional ruler, shows by sign and house where you find the nerve for them.

The true node was in Scorpio from March 1938 to September 1939, October 1956 to June 1958, July 1975 to January 1977, February 1994 to August 1995 and August 2012 to February 2014, and comes back from March 2031 to December 2032. In births from 1930 to 2009, it turns up for about 1 person in 13. It arrived from Sagittarius and moved on to Libra; born close to one of those changes, you may want [North Node in Sagittarius](/astrology/north-node-in-sagittarius/) or [North Node in Libra](/astrology/north-node-in-libra/) as well. That edge is also where the mean node and the true node can disagree by a sign, though never by more than a degree or two; I look at both, and you'll hear from me when they differ. The node's sign is shared by everyone born in its eighteen months; its house, which depends on [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), is the private part.

Some astrologers describe this node in the language of karma. I read it in this life: a habit of holding on, and a capacity for depth that grows with use.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

The Taurus end brings loyalty, comfort and a steady presence to love, and may keep the deepest feelings politely out of reach. The Scorpio end asks for the conversation you've been postponing: what you're afraid of, what you want, what you've never said. Intimacy here means being known, not only being reliable.

It also means sharing: decisions, money, power, the right to change the shape of things. Trust grows when both people risk something.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, the Taurus end is often the steady one who keeps things running and resists change until it's unavoidable. The North Node points to work that goes deeper: research, crisis work, joint ventures, anything that handles what's shared or hidden. It also asks you to notice when a role has quietly died, and to leave before the habit of staying decides for you.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Holding on is the old comfort. A job, a home, a relationship, a belief: Taurus can keep any of them long after it stopped feeding you, because the known feels safer than the unknown, even while the known is shrinking.

When change finally comes, it can feel like losing control. That fear is understandable. It's also the very thing this node practises: discovering that you survive letting go, and often come out with more than you had.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Practise small endings. Give away what you don't use, drop a habit that has gone stale, say the thing that changes the conversation. Spend time with people who can hear something difficult without flinching: a friend, a partner, someone whose job is listening.

One honest note about evidence: in my [testing notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the angle on record is the rising sign, which held roughly one time in three, and the ones that stood out were Saturn, Chiron and Lilith.

And keep the Taurus end close. Your steadiness is what makes depth bearable.
