Pisces begins under Saturn, the planet of form, in the sign the tradition gave to Jupiter and the modern scheme to Neptune. Three bright stars and an old name for the world's encircling river share its first ten degrees.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

After the Moon's face at the end of Aquarius ([Aquarius, third decan](/astrology/aquarius-third-decan/)), the [Chaldean order](/astrology/chaldean-order/) starts again from the top, and the first face of [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) goes to Saturn ([faces](/astrology/faces/)). Leo's three faces come round again here in the same order: Saturn, Jupiter ([Pisces, second decan](/astrology/pisces-second-decan/)) and Mars. They are the only two signs whose three faces all go to the planets above the Sun in the old ordering of the heavens. [Saturn in Pisces](/astrology/saturn-in-pisces/) gains its own face in these degrees.

The modern decans give the opening ten degrees to Pisces's own rulers, Jupiter in the tradition and Neptune in modern tropical astrology. I read both, and between them and Saturn the decan holds a real tension: two planets that widen and soften, and one that gives shape. I read it as compassion with a frame, care that knows where it stops.

## Its image {#its-image}

Cosmas of Maiuma gives these degrees to Okeanos, the Titan who was, in Greek myth, the great river encircling the whole world: a fitting name for the first ten degrees of the last water sign. Wallis Budge's Egyptian name is Baba, written Biu in the Greek-Egyptian lists; [Firmicus Maternus](/astrology/firmicus-maternus/) has Acha or Atapiac, and Athanasius Kircher, in 1652, Rephan. The Hermetic lists know the decan as Tetimo in Greek and Fambais in Latin.

Three bright stars share the decan. [Fomalhaut](/astrology/fomalhaut/), the mouth of the Southern Fish, is at 4°13′ Pisces; [Sadalmelik](/astrology/sadalmelik/), the water-bearer's right shoulder, at 3°42′, in the sign of Pisces since precession carried it over the line around 1760; and [Deneb Adige](/astrology/deneb-adige/), the tail of the Swan, at 5°41′. The Sun reaches Fomalhaut's degree about 21 or 22 February.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun is in these degrees from about 19 to 28 February, so a [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) born in the second half of February belongs here. Under Saturn's face and the sign's two rulers, I read the shade as feeling that keeps its shape: imagination with a discipline, kindness with an edge where it needs one. Saturn itself, in its own face here, I read as responsibility taken on through care: the person who makes the practical arrangements so that others are free to feel.

On the Ascendant, these first ten degrees of Pisces last only about 18 minutes in London and 20 in Paris ([Pisces rising](/astrology/pisces-rising/)), among the quickest ten degrees of the zodiac at those latitudes. A birth time out by ten minutes can move the Ascendant out of it, which is why a time known only to the nearest hour isn't enough here ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Uranus crossed these degrees from 2003 to 2006 ([Uranus in Pisces](/astrology/uranus-in-pisces/)), and Neptune, at home in Pisces in the modern scheme, from 2011 to 2017 ([Neptune in Pisces](/astrology/neptune-in-pisces/)).
