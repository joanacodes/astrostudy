They're the pair who end up running the quiz, hosting the street party and painting a mural on the garden wall. Composite Mars in Leo is that warm, visible drive in a relationship's chart: the bond acts with heart, wants to be admired, and argues about respect.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The composite places [Mars](/astrology/mars/) halfway between your two natal Marses, as the relationship's way of putting effort and courage behind what it wants ([the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/)). [Leo](/astrology/leo/), the Sun's sign, is fixed fire, and Mars has no dignity there; the sign lends the drive warmth, stamina and a liking for being seen. This is the most common composite Mars of all, about 1 pair in 10, a shade ahead of Virgo. Mars lingers longest in Leo and Virgo, about 49 days a visit, and averaging two positions pulls the midpoints a little further towards where Mars spends most time, so composites land here slightly more often than birth charts do.

Such a pair acts generously and in full view. It throws the party, leads the fundraiser, builds the treehouse with a turret, and it works best when its effort has an audience, even an audience of two. Competition between you can be lively: who tells the better story, who wins the game, who is praised more at dinner. Aimed outwards, the same pride makes a formidable team. Desire is demonstrative and playful, and this bond likes being chosen out loud. A single person with [Mars in Leo](/astrology/mars-in-leo/) acts from a bold heart; the composite makes that boldness common property.

Composites aren't drawn in my readings; I set out the method so that you know what it claims. Its users look at the composite [Sun](/astrology/sun/), Leo's ruler, for what all this pride serves, and at the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), where [Sun conjunct Mars in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-mars-synastry/), the drive of one falling on the identity of the other, can feel like encouragement or like rivalry. No composite tells you whether to stay, marry or part, and none should be drawn unless both of you have said yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

[Composite Mars in Cancer](/astrology/composite-mars-in-cancer/) defends its people quietly, from home; [composite Mars in Virgo](/astrology/composite-mars-in-virgo/) puts its effort into getting the details right. Leo wants the effort to shine.

Arguments turn on respect. The question under most of them is *do you see me, and do you value what I did?* Pride can make them theatrical, and it can make the ending hard, because neither of you wants to be first to give way. The warmth usually wins, and a row here tends to finish in a large, sincere reconciliation.

Drama is friction; intimidation is not. If a quarrel ever leaves one of you feeling unsafe, set the chart aside, because no placement explains or excuses that: 999 is the number in a UK emergency, and in the US or Canada, 988 takes calls and texts, day and night.

What helps is generosity in both directions: praising the effort before correcting it, and treating an apology as a gift rather than a defeat. Visibility isn't attention-seeking, but the relationship should check now and then that both of you are getting the light.
