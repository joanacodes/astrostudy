Home lies a long way from the doorstep. With its ruler in the house of far journeys and belief, belonging may be found abroad, in a language, or in a way of seeing the world.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[The 4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home, family and roots. Its ruler ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)) has travelled up into [the 9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), just past the top of the sky: long journeys, other countries and cultures, higher study, faith and philosophy.

I read it as roots that travel. Some people with this ruler make a home far from where they started, or carry family from elsewhere: two languages at the table, a heritage that crosses borders. For others the distance is in the mind, and home is a belief, a tradition, a library, a university town, a teacher's way of thinking. Either way, belonging tends to come with a horizon. The difficulty can be feeling slightly foreign wherever you are, or measuring every home against a far-off ideal.

Distance changes what home is made of. Far from where you began, it may become a set of things you carry, a recipe, a song, a way of keeping a festival; going back, you may find you've become the one from elsewhere. Many people with this ruler end up with more than one home, and the work is to let both be real.

Tradition would add that the ruler of home can't see home from here: the 9th and the 4th are five signs apart, and signs that far apart make no aspect ([aversion](/astrology/aversion/)). I read that as the distance itself. Home is something you go looking for rather than something that stays in view, and finding it is part of the journey.

The 9th also holds belief, so the faith, politics or philosophy of the household you grew up in tends to be a live question in adult life: kept, questioned, translated or replaced. That's your relationship with those beliefs; I don't read anyone else's.

With Leo on the IC, the Sun rules the 4th and, in the 9th, stands in its joy ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): you were born in the first hours after the Sun had crossed the meridian, with the ruler of home high in the sky. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), ruling a Sagittarius or Pisces IC from the 9th, the house of its own natural sign, makes the pull outwards generous and hopeful.

Under a Pisces IC, Jupiter and Neptune share the 4th, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): Jupiter in the 9th may carry you abroad, while Neptune, from its own house, keeps home something half-remembered and half-imagined.

Its neighbours put home elsewhere: in the 8th, among what the family shares and passes on ([Ruler of the 4th in the 8th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/)); in the 10th, in a career or a family name ([Ruler of the 4th in the 10th house](/astrology/4th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)). Between them, the 9th sets it on the horizon, and only a birth time accurate to the minute can place it there ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
