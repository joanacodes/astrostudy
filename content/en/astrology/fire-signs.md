Fire is the element of the first move. Aries, Leo and Sagittarius all have it, and wherever they fall in a chart, that's where you'd rather act on a conviction than wait for permission.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

The fire signs are [Aries](/astrology/aries/), [Leo](/astrology/leo/) and [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), one of the four groups of three that the [four elements](/astrology/four-elements/) make of the zodiac. They sit a [trine](/astrology/trine/) apart, 120°, every fourth sign, so planets in different fire signs tend to understand each other without much effort. Fire's way is warmth, initiative and conviction: it runs on enthusiasm, wants to see a result, and trusts its own spark more than other people's caution.

Each of the three has a different [modality](/astrology/three-modalities/), and so a different way with the flame:

- **Aries**, cardinal, lights it. The Sun is there from about 21 March to 19 April, at the start of the northern spring: the urge to begin.
- **Leo**, fixed, keeps it burning. About 23 July to 22 August, in high summer: warmth that wants to be seen, and to last.
- **Sagittarius**, mutable, carries it further. About 22 November to 21 December, as autumn turns: a fire that goes looking for meaning and a wider horizon.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

The three fire signs kept their rulers when the outer planets were given signs of their own: no fire sign has a modern ruler ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). That keeps fire's story easy to follow through a chart: Mars for Aries, the Sun for Leo, Jupiter for Sagittarius.

Tradition gave the element its own rulers too, a separate layer from the signs': in Dorotheus's scheme, the triplicity of fire is ruled by the Sun by day, Jupiter by night and Saturn as their partner ([triplicity rulers](/astrology/triplicity-rulers/)). All three are what Ptolemy calls day planets, and fire, like air, belongs to the masculine, active half of the zodiac ([polarity](/astrology/polarity/)). The Sun is exalted in Aries, and Saturn has its fall there.

In the houses, fire's natural places are the [1st](/astrology/1st-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house/) and [9th](/astrology/9th-house/): the self you lead with, what you do for joy, and what you believe. Wherever the fire signs fall in your own chart, they lend those houses some of the same heat.

Each fire sign hands over to an earth sign, Aries to Taurus, Leo to Virgo, Sagittarius to Capricorn, and the [earth signs](/astrology/earth-signs/) turn what fire started into something that holds.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I count the planets in each element, and the balance tells me something before the details do ([element balance and missing elements](/astrology/element-balance-and-missing-elements/)). Several planets in fire, especially the Sun, the Moon or the rising sign, often go with a quick, generous, impatient way of living. No planets in fire doesn't mean no courage: Mars, the Sun and Jupiter are still in your chart, and the fire signs still open some of your houses.

I also weigh the element against everything else. A fire Sun is how most people meet their element, and in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) the Sun sign held about a third of the time, so "I'm a fire sign" is a beginning, not a description. Where the fire actually goes in your life is a matter of houses, and the houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Fire signs are aggressive.”** Fire is direct, which isn't the same thing. Assertion isn't aggression, and how anyone handles anger depends on far more than an element.
- **“Fire signs are always confident.”** Fire likes to act, and acting looks like confidence from outside. A good deal of fire is doubt that moves anyway.
- **“Fire and water signs can't get along.”** They're never a trine or a sextile apart, but two people are two whole charts, not two elements.
- **“You need a fire Sun to have fire.”** Any planet in a fire sign brings it: a Moon in Aries or a Mars in Leo is fire too, and the rising sign counts as much as the Sun.
