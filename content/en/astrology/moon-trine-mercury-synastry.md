Moon trine Mercury in synastry puts 120° between one person's Moon and the other's Mercury, most often in two signs of the same element. Moods and words keep the same pace here: these are two people who find each other easy to talk to, about feelings most of all.

## What flows {#what-flows}

A person's [Moon](/astrology/moon/) is where their emotional memory and habits live, and how they look after themselves and others. Their [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) learns, links and exchanges: language, questions, the patterns they notice. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) is flow and mutual support, and among [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) between these two, this is where the conversation simply flows.

For the Moon person, being heard takes no effort. You can say how you feel in plain words and be understood; the other person remembers the small things that matter to you and brings them up at the right time. Even a complaint tends to stay a conversation: you can tell this person they've upset you and come out of it closer.

For the Mercury person, your words are welcome. The other's feelings give your thinking something warm to work with, and you may find yourself more fluent about feelings with them than with anyone else.

The element sets the tone. In fire, a Moon in Sagittarius with Mercury in Aries or Leo, feelings become stories told with gusto; in earth, a Moon in Taurus with Mercury in Virgo or Capricorn, comfort comes as practical, well-chosen words: the right advice, the reminder, the plan.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Easy conversation can stand in for other things. A feeling gets discussed instead of felt; a need gets talked about instead of met; a disagreement is smoothed over because the chat was going so well. The Mercury person can drift into being the pair's permanent interpreter, the one who always finds the words, while the Moon person stops looking for their own. And when talking is this easy, silence can start to feel like a problem.

Flow isn't the same as depth, and a quiet evening isn't a failure of the trine.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Spend the ease on the conversation you've both been avoiding: it will never be easier. Let some things be felt without being discussed, and check, now and then, that a need you talked about was actually met. Co-workers with this trine often have the one-to-one that always runs over time; adult relatives, the phone call that can go anywhere. Friends and couples can make room for quiet as well as talk.

In my count it comes up in roughly 1 pair of people in 7 at an orb of 6.8°, whichever person brings the Moon. The Moon is what makes the birth time decisive: at about 13° a day, it would pass right through this trine's orb between one morning and the next, so its owner's birth time can decide whether the contact exists. Mercury's position, by comparison, hardly changes in a day. Both birth times are needed, for the houses too ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)); I don't read a synastry with either missing.

Inside one chart, this is [Moon trine Mercury](/astrology/moon-trine-mercury/). Between two, [Moon sextile Mercury in synastry](/astrology/moon-sextile-mercury-synastry/) offers the same ease but waits for a first move; [Moon conjunct Mercury in synastry](/astrology/moon-conjunct-mercury-synastry/) puts the talk right on the feeling; [Moon square Mercury in synastry](/astrology/moon-square-mercury-synastry/) and [Moon opposite Mercury in synastry](/astrology/moon-opposite-mercury-synastry/) make the same conversation work for its ease.
