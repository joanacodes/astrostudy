Twelve years or so after its last visit, Jupiter is back on the eastern edge of your chart, on the degree that was coming up over the horizon as you were born. The way you come through a door, open a conversation or take on something new is where this transit does its work.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands understanding and looks for what experience means; the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is your orientation, the stance you take before you've had time to think. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses the two, so for a while growth shows first in how you approach things: more willing to begin, quicker to say yes, readier to be seen trying. Like every [transit](/astrology/transits/), it gives room to what your chart already holds. A reserved rising sign becomes a more generous version of itself, not a different sign.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the Ascendant is the cusp of the 1st house, so this crossing opens [Jupiter transiting the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-1st-house/). It follows a quieter year behind the horizon, [Jupiter transiting the 12th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-12th-house/), and the contrast is often what people notice: something that grew in private starts to show in your manner.

Born around sunrise, you have your Sun close to your Ascendant, so this crossing and [transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-sun/) come close together: two beginnings in one season, one about who you are, the other about how you show it.

People sometimes ask whether Jupiter here puts weight on, because the old books read the body from the Ascendant. A chart can't see your body or forecast its changes, and I never read it for that ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, seven crossings in ten are a single pass of about eleven days. The other three fall in [Jupiter retrograde](/astrology/jupiter-retrograde/): Jupiter crosses your rising degree, backs over it and crosses again, three exact hits in about eight months.

Eleven days is a narrow window, and it is only as good as your birth time. The Ascendant moves about a degree every four minutes on average, so a time ten minutes out shifts it by the whole width of my orb, and you'd be watching the wrong eleven days. No birth time, no reading: here, the minute decides the dates ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

By 80, everyone born between 1930 and 2009 has had this crossing six or seven times. Each one opens a twelve-year round of angles to the same degree. Look next for [transiting Jupiter sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-sextile-natal-ascendant/), about two years on; [transiting Jupiter square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-square-natal-ascendant/) follows within a year or so, then [transiting Jupiter trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-ascendant/). Halfway round, at about six years, Jupiter reaches your Descendant: [transiting Jupiter opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-opposite-natal-ascendant/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Walk into something new: a place, a group, a subject where nobody knows you yet. The Ascendant is about entry, and Jupiter makes entry easier.

Notice the first move you make in unfamiliar rooms, and widen it. If you usually wait and watch, speak first once; if you usually charge in, open with a question. The transit gives your manner room to grow, not a replacement.

Keep the promises you make on the doorstep. With Jupiter here you can agree to more than you meant to, simply because agreeing feels like you right now, so leave a day between the yes and the commitment.
