A circle of friends, a team, a cause: with the planet of your rising sign in the 11th house, that's where the self you lead with finds its place, among the people you choose and the future you're building with them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

As ruler of the [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/), your [chart ruler](/astrology/chart-ruler/) has taken the self of the Ascendant into the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), the house of belonging and of what lies ahead: the friends you choose rather than inherit, the teams, networks and communities you join, the people who help without having to, and the plans you'd like to see through.

Your people are where you find yourself. You tend to know who you are by who you stand with: the group that understands you, the cause that gives your effort a direction, the friendships that outlast jobs and addresses. You're often the one who connects people, or who keeps a circle together after others drift. Hope matters here too, and you're shaped as much by what you're working towards as by where you come from. Keeping your own voice is the work. Belonging isn't conformity, and the right group makes room for the parts of you that don't fit it.

Jupiter, the planet of generosity, was said to rejoice in the 11th, the house tradition named the Good Spirit and filled with friends, hopes and benefactors ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). With Sagittarius rising, or with Pisces rising through its traditional ruler, a Jupiter in the 11th rules your chart from that happy place. Counted by sign, the 11th also sits a sextile from the rising sign, an aspect tradition read as harmony and opportunity.

With Aquarius rising, your two rulers, Saturn and Uranus, belong to the very sign the [natural zodiac](/astrology/natural-zodiac/) pairs with the 11th, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Saturn here builds groups that last and takes its duties to them seriously; Uranus wants the circle to change something, or at least to let you be odd in it. The Moon, ruling Cancer rising from this house, makes friends into family and family into friends. Mercury, ruling Gemini or Virgo rising from the 11th, keeps the group talking: the newsletter, the group chat, the meeting you end up chairing.

In Placidus, the house system I use, a ruler in the 11th had risen a few hours before your birth and was climbing the eastern sky towards the Midheaven. Had it climbed further, into the 10th, your life would gather around your public role and the work you're known for ([Ruler of the 1st in the 10th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-10th-house/)); a little lower, in the 12th, around solitude and what happens out of sight ([Ruler of the 1st in the 12th house](/astrology/1st-house-ruler-in-12th-house/)). How high it had climbed is something only the birth time can tell ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Across the chart sits the 5th, private pleasure and love. With your ruler in the 11th, the joys that last are usually the ones a group can share, and even your most personal projects tend to find their way to an audience of friends.
