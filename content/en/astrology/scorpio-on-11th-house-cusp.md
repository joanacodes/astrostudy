Scorpio on the 11th house cusp brings the sign of depth into the part of the chart that looks ahead with other people. The alliances you keep are usually the ones that have been tested.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Allies, networks, causes, the circle you'd call on in a bad week: the [11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) holds the people you stand with and the future you build together ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) on its cusp, alliances tend to be forged through something hard, and the people who stayed when things went wrong become your people. You tend to know a network from the inside, who really holds it up and who doesn't, and the friendships that last are the ones that survive both of you changing. The causes that draw you aim to change something at the root rather than decorate it, and a shared plan earns your commitment once it has survived a hard look for weak points. In a team, you may be the one others ask for an honest reading of where things really stand.

Your alliances follow [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), the rulers of your 11th, into whichever houses they occupy, and I read both: the [1st](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/11th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). In about 3 London charts in 4 with this cusp, and 4 in 5 at New York, Aries is on your IC, so Mars governs your roots as well as your circle, and the two tend to run on the same engine. Tradition gave the 11th the joy of Jupiter ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)), and in about 9 London charts in 10 with this cusp Sagittarius rises, which makes Jupiter your chart ruler: the planet said to rejoice in the house of allies rules you, while Scorpio sets the tone of the house itself. Your pleasures, through Taurus on the 5th, belong to Venus.

Scorpio takes so long to clear the eastern horizon in the north that the cusps above it see a great deal of the sign: about 1 birth in 11 has it on the 11th, at London as at New York, in Placidus, the house system I use. Only about 17° to 20° wide at London, this 11th rarely holds Scorpio alone: in about 2 London charts in 3 the sign takes a second cusp ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), the 12th in about 2 in 5, the Midheaven in about 1 in 4. The rulers of your 11th then have a second house to run, the 12th or the 10th ([Scorpio on the 12th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-12th-house-cusp/)). An intercepted Scorpio, which turns up in about 1 London birth in 7 and 1 New York birth in 11, sits below the horizon or just past the Descendant, far from this cusp ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

An hour or two either side and the picture changes: earlier, Scorpio could have held the 12th cusp alone; later, the top of the chart ([Scorpio on the 10th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-10th-house-cusp/)). The narrowness of this house makes the minute count twice over, so I read from the recorded time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
