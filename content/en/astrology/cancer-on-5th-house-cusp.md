With Cancer on the 5th house cusp, the house of what you do for joy opens in the sign of home. Fun, romance and creativity tend to start once you feel safe, and often among people you already love.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the cusp of the [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is the way you play ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) plays close to home. Long dinners with friends, games round the kitchen table, a film you've seen ten times with the people who know the lines: pleasure here is shared and familiar more than new. Creative work tends to draw on memory, family stories, old photographs, the songs of a particular summer, and to be shown first to the few people you trust. In love you open slowly, test the water, and show affection by looking after someone: the meal cooked, the coat fetched, the date of the first meeting remembered. A love that feels like coming home suits you better than a dramatic one. The 5th's natural sign is Leo, which follows Cancer in the zodiac, so here the Moon's sign stands at the door of the Sun's house: warmth that needs to feel safe before it shines.

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules your 5th, and its house is where your play and your loves tend to lead: the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). What rises with this cusp varies: Pisces in about 2 London charts in 5 and more than half at New York, then Aries, then Aquarius. The chart ruler may therefore be Jupiter with Neptune, or Mars, or Saturn with Uranus, and for Pisces and Aquarius I read both rulers. In nearly 2 London charts in 5 Cancer opens the 4th as well, and home and pleasure answer to the Moon together; in about 1 in 3 it opens the 6th, and play and daily work share a ruler ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). [Cancer on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/) and [Cancer on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/) are the pages for those charts.

Counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use, about 1 birth in 12 has Cancer on the 5th house cusp, at London as at New York: about two hours of each day, the even share, because once past the IC the cusps begin to cross Cancer faster than the Ascendant does. In about 1 London birth in 8, Cancer opens no house at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). The time of birth decides which of these charts is yours, and I read none of them without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Tradition also gave the 5th children, and Cancer, the sign of nurture, makes the question come up often. I read your own play, creativity and loves here, as an adult, and never whether there will be children ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)); the charts I read are adults' charts only ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).
