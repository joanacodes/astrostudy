Cancer on the 5th house cusp keeps pleasure close to home. Fun, romance and creativity tend to start once you feel safe, and often among people you already love.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Play, in the [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/), takes its style from the cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), and [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) plays among the people it trusts. Long dinners with friends, games round the kitchen table, a film you've seen ten times with the people who know the lines: pleasure here is shared and familiar more than new. Creative work tends to draw on memory, family stories, old photographs, the songs of a particular summer, and to be shown first to the few people you trust. Hosting is a pleasure in itself: a table laid for others can be as creative as anything you make alone. In love you open slowly, test the water, and show affection by looking after someone: the meal cooked, the coat fetched, the date of the first meeting remembered. A love that feels like coming home suits you better than a dramatic one. The 5th's natural sign is Leo, which follows Cancer in the zodiac, so here the Moon's sign stands at the door of the Sun's house: warmth that needs to feel safe before it shines.

Your play and your loves tend to lead towards the house the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) occupies as ruler of the 5th: the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). What rises with this cusp varies: Pisces in about 2 London charts in 5 and more than half at New York, then Aries, then Aquarius. The chart ruler may therefore be Jupiter with Neptune, or Mars, or Saturn with Uranus, and for Pisces and Aquarius I read both rulers. And because the Moon changes sign every two and a half days, two people born in the same week with this cusp can have their pleasures ruled by Moons in very different signs. Home and pleasure answer to the Moon together in nearly 2 London charts in 5, where Cancer opens the 4th as well; play and daily work share it in about 1 in 3, where Cancer also opens the 6th ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). [Cancer on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/) and [Cancer on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/) cover those charts from the other side.

In Placidus, the house system I use, Cancer is on the 5th house cusp for about 1 birth in 12, in London and New York alike: two hours or so of each day. Cancer is slow on the Ascendant, but by the 5th, past the IC, the cusps move through it faster. Cancer can also miss every cusp: it does in about 1 London birth in 8 ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Only the time of birth can say which of these charts is yours, so it comes before any reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Tradition also gave the 5th children, and Cancer, the sign of nurture, makes the question come up often. I read your own play, creativity and loves here, as an adult, and never whether there will be children ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)); the charts I read are adults' charts only ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).
