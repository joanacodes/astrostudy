Mercury opposite North Node is Mercury sitting on the South Node. Your way of thinking lives at the familiar end of the nodal axis, and the direction you grow in lies straight across the chart: a fluent mind, asked to reach towards something it didn't grow up speaking.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is the function that takes in, sorts and passes on: the way you notice, reason and talk. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a less familiar capacity, built by practice, and the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), exactly opposite, is what came easily or early. In an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), two functions become the two ends of one line, to be balanced rather than chosen between. Here, your mind stands with the familiar and looks across at the new.

In plain words, thinking is something you can do without trying. Explanations, opinions, the right word at the right time: they came early, and they're where you go when you're tired. That's a strength, and nothing in this opposition asks you to give it up. What it asks is that you notice when the fluent answer is standing in for the less familiar response the North Node describes. You may meet that other end in other people first: someone who trusts a hunch where you'd want the facts, and seems to have what your quick answers leave out.

Growth here doesn't silence the familiar mind; it puts it to work learning the other thing, with both ends in view.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, expect a ready mind that keeps returning to the same ground; the signs say which ground. With Mercury in Gemini on the South Node and the North Node in Sagittarius, for instance, quick facts and many conversations come easily, and the growth is towards a view of your own. The houses add where: Mercury on the South Node in the 3rd keeps you close to the near and everyday, while the 9th, across the chart, holds the wider horizon.

About 1 person in 23 is born with Mercury within 6° of an opposition to the North Node. And an astronomical twist: Mercury never strays more than about 28° from the Sun, so with Mercury on the South Node, the Sun was within about 34° of the nodes as well. You were born in or near an [eclipse season](/astrology/eclipse-seasons/), the weeks when new and full moons fall close enough to the nodes for eclipses.

Your birth date decides the aspect, since Mercury does nearly all the moving; near 6°, it can depend on the node you use, mean or true ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), and I look at both. [My notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) checked Mercury like every placement: it held about a third of the time, while Saturn, Chiron and Lilith held far more often. So I hold this opposition lightly, and say so when a reading leans on it.

Mercury's other aspects to the nodes, seen from here:

- **[Mercury conjunct North Node](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-north-node/):** the reverse of this page; Mercury on the growing end, with the familiar one behind it.
- **[Mercury sextile North Node](/astrology/mercury-sextile-north-node/):** a helpful angle from which the mind can nudge growth along, when you use it.
- **[Mercury square North Node](/astrology/mercury-square-north-node/):** Mercury halfway between the ends, square to both; a go-between whose friction keeps it learning.
- **[Mercury trine North Node](/astrology/mercury-trine-north-node/):** an easy road from thought to growth, with the familiar end within friendly reach.

Between two charts, [Mercury opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-north-node-synastry/) puts one person's way of thinking on the other's South Node: talk that feels familiar from the first evening, comfortable, and worth watching in case it keeps you both in place, a risk each person may judge differently.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), Mercury in the sky crosses your natal South Node, and so opposes your North Node, at least once a year, for roughly a day: a day when the old way of seeing things comes back easily. The slow version is [Transiting North Node opposite natal Mercury](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-mercury/), the South Node in the sky passing over your Mercury, once in each round of about 18.6 years, for some weeks. Born with the opposition, you meet it at your [nodal returns](/astrology/nodal-return/), at about 18½, 37 and 56; at the [nodal reversals](/astrology/nodal-reversal/) between them, at about 28 and 46, the North Node in the sky crosses your Mercury instead.

Weather, not a forecast: seasons when a familiar mind is asked to look across the chart, never events.
