Sagittarius on the 5th house cusp hands the house of play to the sign that wants to know what lies beyond the next hill. Pleasure here tends to have a question in it, and romance a horizon.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) covers what you do for the joy of it: games, hobbies, the things you make, flirting and falling in love ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) on its cusp makes discovery part of the fun. I read it as the holiday planned around a question, the evening class that becomes a passion, the hobby that grows into a small expertise, the friend who becomes a lover over a long argument about something that matters. Creativity tends to tell a story or carry a conviction. And the 5th's taste for risk meets Sagittarius's faith that things will work out: generous gambles on a plan, rarely cautious ones, so this is the house where you learn which risks are worth the story.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules this 5th house, and its sign and house show where your joy is found and how it shows itself: the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Pisces, Jupiter's other sign, which it shares with Neptune, falls on your 8th cusp in nearly 9 of these charts in 10, and on the 9th in the rest: what you love doing and what you share most closely answer to the same planet, and I read Neptune there too.

About 1 birth in 12 has Sagittarius on the 5th, at London as at New York, counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use. That's an even share. Sagittarius is slow to rise in the north and quick to set, and the 5th cusp, between the bottom of the chart and the western horizon, lands halfway between the two. [Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/) comes with it in about 19 London charts in 20, Virgo in the rest, so the Sun, the 5th house's natural ruler, usually rules your whole chart as well, and play and self-expression sit close to the centre of it. In more than half of these charts Aries opens the 9th as well, and the three houses whose natural signs are the fire signs, the 1st, 5th and 9th, all open in fire, each in another's sign. In about 1 London chart in 6, Capricorn lies wholly inside the 5th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), and Saturn then asks that some of the fun be practised until it becomes a craft.

Tradition gave the 5th children. I don't read them in a chart: no chart can say whether or when anyone will have a child ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I don't read [children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/). In an adult's chart, this cusp describes your own pleasures, and how much room you give them.

A later birth that day would have put Sagittarius on the 4th cusp ([Sagittarius on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-4th-house-cusp/)), an earlier one on the 6th ([Sagittarius on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-6th-house-cusp/)). Only the birth time tells which house Sagittarius opens, and I don't guess: no birth time, no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
