For about two and a half years, effort and imagination keep the same pace. Work done for others, for an art or for an ideal comes with more stamina than usual, and the risk, as with every trine, is that so much ease goes unused.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is the will to act and the stamina to keep going: initiative, courage, the energy you put into what you want. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) adds compassion, intuition and a feel for what lies beyond the immediate goal. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates two functions through flow while each stays what it is, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) Mars keeps its edge and Neptune gives it a reason, without the confusion the harder contacts can bring.

It often shows as stamina for things that matter to you: long rehearsals, a garden or a building taking shape, care for someone that you give without resentment. Acting on a hunch feels more natural than usual, and disagreements can be settled with less force. Some people find this is when they take up a cause, or put real effort into a creative project they had only dreamed about. Flow isn't passivity, though: a trine eases the movement you choose, and if you choose none, the years pass pleasantly and leave little behind.

With Mars in your 11th, the trine often comes while Neptune crosses your 3rd or your 7th: words, learning and the people nearby, or a partner's ideals, feed the work you do with friends and for causes ([Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/)). Which houses are yours is fixed by your time of birth, to the minute or near it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

People born with [Mars trine Neptune](/astrology/mars-trine-neptune/) work this way by temperament; for everyone else the transit is a chance to try it for a few years.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counting from 1½° before exact to 1° after, my orbs, a pass of the trine runs to about two years and seven months, never much under twenty-two. Mars in the sky turns retrograde at least once in that time ([Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/)), so its own rhythm of pushing and pausing runs on top of Neptune's slow one: three exact contacts in most passes, five in about one in four.

Nearly everyone born between 1930 and 2009 meets four of Neptune's contacts with their Mars by 80, counting repeats, and which four depends on where Mars stood at birth. For most people they are this trine, [transiting Neptune square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-mars/), [transiting Neptune sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-mars/), and either [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-mars/) or [transiting Neptune opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-mars/). For some, the trine comes twice, about fifty-five years apart, with the opposition halfway between; about 1 person in 7 has no trine at all before 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Give the ease a project. Pick one piece of work that needs both stamina and imagination, and let these years carry it.

Offer your strength where it's needed. Under this trine, helping costs you less than usual, and the help tends to be well aimed.

Keep a little friction in the plan: a deadline, a teacher, a weekly session. A flowing transit gives more when something asks you to show up.
