Libra has the fewest eclipses of the twelve signs in my list: eleven between 2000 and 2040. Libra weighs one thing against another, and its eclipses fall on the axis where it meets Aries, you and the other person.

## What it stirs {#what-it-stirs}

[Libra](/astrology/libra/) is cardinal air, ruled by Venus: balance, fairness, the agreements people make with each other. Its eclipses fall on the Libra–Aries axis and take turns with those of Aries as the Moon’s nodes move. On 14 October 2023 the Sun and Moon met in Libra for an annular [solar eclipse](/astrology/solar-eclipses/) at 21°07′, almost on the degree of the partial one of 14 October 2004, at 21°06′; on 14 April 2033 a [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/) will put the full Moon in Libra and the Sun in Aries.

In a chart I apply the AGS rule: about three degrees from a natal planet, angle or node, at either end of the axis, read through that point and its house, with penumbral lunar eclipses, three of Libra’s eleven, counting for less. Natal nodes I read both ways, mean and true. The emphasis is symbolic, and an eclipse is never an omen.

Eleven from 2000 to 2040:

- **2004–2005:** 14 October 2004, partial solar, 21°06′ · 3 October 2005, annular solar, 10°18′
- **2014–2016:** 15 April 2014, total lunar, 25°17′ · 4 April 2015, total lunar, 14°21′ · 23 March 2016, penumbral lunar, 3°10′
- **2023–2024:** 14 October 2023, annular solar, 21°07′ · 25 March 2024, penumbral lunar, 5°13′ · 2 October 2024, annular solar, 10°03′
- **2033–2034:** 14 April 2033, total lunar, 25°06′ · 23 September 2033, partial solar, 0°51′ · 3 April 2034, penumbral lunar, 13°59′

The most recent, the annular eclipse of 2 October 2024 at 10°03′, came nineteen years after the one of 3 October 2005 at 10°18′; the next is the total lunar eclipse of 14 April 2033. Before and after Libra come Virgo and Scorpio, each with its own list ([Eclipse in Virgo](/astrology/eclipse-in-virgo/), [Eclipse in Scorpio](/astrology/eclipse-in-scorpio/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

To know which house Libra occupies, I need [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/): the houses are calculated from it, and I don’t read a chart that lacks one. These lines use Placidus, the house system I work with; each applies when a Libra eclipse meets a natal point in that house, and the Aries degrees opposite reach the house across.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/libra-rising/):** Libra rising: your gracious, diplomatic manner; how much of yourself shows while you keep the peace.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/libra-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what you count as a fair exchange; the value you place on what you have and what you give.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/libra-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** tactful words; a conversation that restores a balance.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/libra-on-4th-house-cusp/):** harmony at home; the arrangements that make a household work.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/libra-on-5th-house-cusp/):** romance, art and shared pleasure; beauty you make or enjoy with someone.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/libra-on-6th-house-cusp/):** fairness at work; how tasks and credit are shared out.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Libra in its natural house, on the Descendant with Aries rising: partnerships and agreements. Contracts belong to this house in the tradition; I never forecast a court case or its verdict ([can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/)).
8. **[8th house](/astrology/libra-on-8th-house-cusp/):** balance in what’s shared; give and take in trust and joint means.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/libra-on-9th-house-cusp/):** ideals of justice; a belief about fairness, put to the test.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a public role built on tact; the way you mediate at work.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/libra-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friendships among equals; your part in keeping a group fair.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/libra-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private compromises; what you have given up quietly for the sake of peace.
