Venus conjunct Pluto sets what you value in the same few degrees as the part of you that renews things from the root. What you love tends to matter all the way down, and it rarely leaves you unchanged.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the part of a chart that evaluates: attraction, preference, the sense of what's worth having, and the fairness of an exchange. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) is the part that transforms: it removes what has become obsolete, regenerates what remains and makes it stronger. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) they work as a single unit without either dissolving into the other: whatever you come to value, you also want to see renewed.

In plain words, you don't like things lightly. A friendship, a piece of music, a place or a person can become part of you, and when something stops being true for you, you may let it go as completely as you took it in. From outside that looks like intensity, and it is. But transformation isn't destruction, and empowerment isn't domination: the strength of this conjunction is that what you value can change you, not that you must hold on to it. Lived well, it gives bonds that can go through change and come out renewed, and a taste remade by experience rather than by fashion.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Pluto's sign is shared by everyone born over twelve to thirty-one years, so what makes this meeting personal is the week or two each year when Venus passes it, and the house where that happens. The sign still colours it. In Libra, Venus's own sign, the depth goes into partnership and fairness; in Scorpio, ruled by Pluto and traditionally by Mars, and the sign of Venus's detriment, into loyalty and honesty about what you want; in Virgo, the sign of Venus's fall, into care that reworks and improves. In the 7th house the renewal tends to come through partners; in the 2nd, through what you hold dear and how you measure your own worth.

About 1 person in 30 is born with Venus and Pluto within 6° of a conjunction. Venus is far the faster, so it does the meeting, and in a year when it turns retrograde close to Pluto it can cross the same stretch three times in a few months. In my notebook Venus held about a third of the time, like the other placements everyone reads first, and Pluto wasn't among the three that held far more often, so I offer this conjunction as something to test against your life ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)).

Venus and Pluto at their other angles:

- **[Venus sextile Pluto](/astrology/venus-sextile-pluto/):** depth as an offer; you can renew what you value when you choose to reach for it.
- **[Venus square Pluto](/astrology/venus-square-pluto/):** liking and transforming at cross-purposes, each pushing the other to grow.
- **[Venus trine Pluto](/astrology/venus-trine-pluto/):** attachments that deepen and renew with little drama, usually in one element.
- **[Venus opposite Pluto](/astrology/venus-opposite-pluto/):** valuing and renewing on opposite sides, held in balance mostly through the people you're close to.

Between two charts, [Venus conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-pluto-synastry/) is one of the contacts people remember: one person's way of valuing meets the other's depth at close range. It can feel magnetic, and the two rarely feel it the same way, so I read it from both sides.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-venus/) comes once in a lifetime at most. Pluto takes 12 to 31 years to cross a sign and is retrograde about five months a year, so it stays within a degree of your Venus for about two years, often in three passes. Born with the conjunction, you meet it early or not at all: with your Venus a few degrees ahead of Pluto, Pluto reached it in your first years; behind, it had already gone by. For everyone else it's a long season in which what you value tends to be looked at more deeply.

The short version is Venus's yearly pass over your natal Pluto: a day or two in which feelings run deeper than the occasion.

Weather, not a forecast: Pluto's season shows how deep the weather goes, never what it will bring.
