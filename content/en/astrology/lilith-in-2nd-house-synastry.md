Wanting things out loud is harder than it sounds: a fair price, a good coat, money that's yours alone. Their Lilith in your 2nd house puts the part of them that refused to be tamed among your possessions and your sense of worth.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) marks what someone was taught to want less of, and wanted anyway: an appetite, a boundary, a plain no. [The 2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) covers what you earn and keep, what you buy and protect, the skills you could live from and, underneath, how much you feel allowed to have. As a [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their unapologetic wanting meets your values.

With them, you may stop apologising for what you like. They tend to ask the blunt question, who told you that wanting it was greedy?, and a rule you inherited about money or comfort may lose its grip. Some people with this overlay finally raise a rate, buy the thing they'd admired for years, or stop chasing what they were only told to want.

For them, the way you handle having can provoke or release. Your carefulness may stir their own refusal to be owned by anything; your pleasure in good things may give their appetite permission. The 2nd follows the first angle and holds on to what arrives; [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), its natural sign, enjoys the solid and the sensual without needing a reason.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Values can clash in plain view. They may tease what you treasure, borrow without asking, or treat your savings as timidity, and you may feel your whole sense of worth is being called tame. Spending together can turn into a contest of appetites.

From their side, your rules about money and possessions may feel like a cage they've already escaped once. They can bristle at being asked to account for a purchase, or feel judged when you count.

An overlay says nothing about what anyone will earn, owe or lose; I never read money that way. It shows how two people's wants meet.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Say what's yours, and what you're happy to share; it spares both of you the guessing. Let their questions about your values be invitations rather than verdicts. A couple can keep a pot each and one together; friends can agree how lending works before it happens; colleagues can let the frank one help negotiate a fee; adult relatives can look again at a family's old attitudes to money, and keep only those still worth keeping.

Seen from their chair, this is your Lilith in their 2nd house, and your appetite or your indifference meets what they keep. Beside it, [their Lilith in your 1st house](/astrology/lilith-in-1st-house-synastry/) touches your presence, and [their Lilith in your 3rd house](/astrology/lilith-in-3rd-house-synastry/) your words.

Mean Lilith, the one I read, moves a little over 3° a month, so anyone born within a few months of them puts it in the same part of your chart; what makes it theirs is everything else they bring. Your birth time decides which house of yours that part is, in Placidus, the house system I use, and I wait for both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If one chart shows true Lilith, the house can change completely, so compare like with like ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). Money and desire are private matters, read only with both people's yes ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
