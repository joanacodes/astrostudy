Ask someone with this placement about a crisis and you may find they've been through several, quietly, and come out steadier. The ruler of your 8th house stands inside the 8th itself, so trust, intimacy, what's shared and what changes you are dealt with on their own ground.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) covers trust, intimacy, money held jointly, debts, and the changes that follow when people let each other close. With its ruler inside it, these questions stay where they began ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

Depth is familiar ground for you. You're unlikely to be frightened by intensity, your own or anyone else's, and you may be the person others call when something has gone badly wrong. Privacy matters a great deal: you share fully with very few, and what you're trusted with stays where it was put. Crises tend to be worked through rather than avoided, and you often come out of them knowing more about yourself. The other side of that self-containment is the care it needs: letting people help you too, and letting closeness be easy sometimes, not only deep. Intimacy follows the same rule: slow to begin, and complete once it does.

If the ruler was retrograde when you were born, as Jupiter is for about four months of every year, its work turns further inward: old agreements revisited, old griefs returned to until they're finished ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

The planet shows how. Mars in Scorpio, ruling a Scorpio 8th from inside it, stands in its own sign as well as its own house ([domicile](/astrology/domicile/)): stamina in a crisis, fierce loyalty. Jupiter in Sagittarius on a Sagittarius 8th brings generosity with what's shared and a faith that hard times pass; Venus in Libra on a Libra 8th, a strong sense of what's fair between two people. With [Scorpio on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-8th-house-cusp/), Pluto shares the rule, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)): [Pluto in the 8th house](/astrology/pluto-in-8th-house/) is on the ground of its own Scorpio in the natural zodiac, and adds a talent for starting again.

The 8th is succedent, one of the houses that gather and keep what the angles start ([angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/)), and a ruler at home in one holds on to what it collects: experience, confidences, resilience.

Tradition's 8th, death and inheritance, would be doubled here, and I don't follow it. No chart says how long anyone will live ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)) or what anyone will leave ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)); in an adult's life, this is a ruler at home with change.

A small change in the birth time could put the ruler in the 7th, where a partner tests your trust ([Ruler of the 8th in the 7th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/)), or in the 9th, where what you've lived turns into belief ([Ruler of the 8th in the 9th house](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/)). In Placidus, the house system I use, those cusps come from the time you were born, not just the day ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

What this describes is someone comfortable at the deep end, and a reminder that the deep end is better shared.
