Pluto sextile your Jupiter opens a long door into deeper understanding. For two to three years the planet of depth stands 60° from your Jupiter, and a course of study, a teacher, a tradition or a question can take your beliefs further than they have gone before, if you walk through.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) wants access to what lies underneath, the resources and motives most people never look at. Your [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) learns, synthesises and makes room for growth. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates them through opportunity and cooperation, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) understanding can deepen by choice: the course you always meant to take, a mentor who knows more than you, a body of work you finally go into properly.

A teaching doesn't have to be hidden or esoteric to be deep. Under this sextile the plainest ideas, followed far enough, open furthest; the depth is in the following, not in any secrecy. Opportunity isn't guaranteed success, either. The sextile helps the work along; it doesn't do it for you. It activates what your Jupiter already describes rather than bringing anything new, and what grows depends on what you do with the opening.

[Jupiter sextile Pluto](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-pluto/) in a birth chart is shared by a whole year group. The transit is personal by contrast: it comes when Pluto reaches 60° from your own Jupiter.

With Jupiter in the 3rd and Pluto crossing the 1st ([Pluto transiting the 1st house](/astrology/pluto-transit-1st-house/)), a changing sense of who you are opens new ways of learning, and of speaking about what you believe. Both houses are counted from the Ascendant, which the birth time sets ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

My timing counts the sextile from 1½° before exact to 1° after. On that count it lasts about two years and eight months, about twenty-two months at the quickest, with three exact hits, or five in about two passes out of five.

When it comes follows your birth date, through the angle Jupiter and Pluto made when you were born. Someone born on 1 January 1995 is meeting it now, at 31. For 1 January 1985 it came at about 6, for 1 January 1955 at about 14, for 1 January 1935 at about 30, for 1 January 1975 at about 39 and for 1 January 1945 at about 49; births on 1 January 1965 and 2005 don't meet it by 80. Across everyone born from 1930 to 2009 who meets it, the first pass came within orb at anything from a few weeks old to 80, at about 30 in the middle, and nearly four in five meet one of the two sextiles by 80, some of them both.

While Pluto is in Aquarius, until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)), it sextiles Jupiters in Aries and Sagittarius: among others, births from December 1982 to January 1984, December 1994 to January 1996, March 1987 to March 1988 and February 1999 to February 2000.

## What helps {#what-helps}

If a square ([Transiting Pluto square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-jupiter/)) or the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-jupiter/)) has already tested your beliefs, the sextile is the time to rebuild them by choice. If the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-jupiter/)) or the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-jupiter/)) is still to come, it is a good time to prepare the ground.

Find the teacher, the course or the apprenticeship, and commit to it for longer than feels comfortable.
