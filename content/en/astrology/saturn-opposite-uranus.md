Saturn opposite Uranus puts what holds and what breaks free at the two ends of one line. Twice in the eighty years of the count, that line ran between Pisces and Virgo, and the people born on it tend to know both parts: the one who keeps the rules, and the one who rewrites them.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) holds the structure of a chart: the rules, boundaries and commitments that let something last. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) is the urge to change: it differentiates, frees and reforms, and has little patience with a rule nobody can explain. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) sets them up as a polarity, each keeping its identity and each making the other visible: you notice your need for stability most clearly when change is pressing, and your need for freedom when a structure closes in.

Lived, it can feel like a seesaw. A stretch of order and loyalty is followed by a sudden need for air; a burst of independence by a pull back towards something solid. Often the two halves are met first in other people, a strict figure and a free spirit, an institution and someone who wants out of it, with you standing between them, or playing one part and leaving the other to someone else.

Polarity isn't contradiction. Over the years this opposition tends to teach a rare skill: changing a structure while it's still in use, keeping what works and freeing what doesn't, without either side having to lose.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

It's the rarest Saturn–Uranus aspect in the count. About 1 person in 35 born between 1930 and 2009 has it within 6°, and all of them were born in one of two windows: on and off from March 1964 to February 1967, and from September 2008 to October 2009. In the first, Saturn was in Pisces, after a few weeks at the end of Aquarius, and Uranus in Virgo; in the second, Saturn was in Virgo and Uranus in Pisces. The same axis, with the parts swapped.

Because so many people share it, I read it as a generation's aspect first and make it personal through the houses. An opposition usually links two opposite houses: across the 4th and 10th it can be home against career, your family's way against your own; across the 1st and 7th, your independence against your commitments. Touching your Sun, Moon or an angle, it speaks louder, and all of it needs a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Many people born in 1965 and 1966 also have Saturn opposite Pluto, since Uranus was meeting Pluto in Virgo then ([Uranus conjunct Pluto](/astrology/uranus-conjunct-pluto/)): for them, structure faces change and renewal at once. Saturn is the faster of the pair, and each year its retrograde made the angle come and go.

Set beside the other Saturn–Uranus aspects:

- **[Saturn conjunct Uranus](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-uranus/):** the early 1940s or the late 1980s; the two start from one point, and the work is telling them apart.
- **[Saturn sextile Uranus](/astrology/saturn-sextile-uranus/):** 1934–36, 1947–49, 1978–81 or 1995–97; cooperation on offer, if you act on it.
- **[Saturn square Uranus](/astrology/saturn-square-uranus/):** the early 1930s, 1950–53, the mid-1970s or 1999–2001; friction at right angles rather than a face-off.
- **[Saturn trine Uranus](/astrology/saturn-trine-uranus/):** 1955–58, 1971–74 or 2002–04; the same two functions in easy step.

Between two people, [Saturn opposite Uranus in synastry](/astrology/saturn-opposite-uranus-synastry/) often feels like a tug between security and freedom, each person seeing the other as the one who pulls. I read it both ways round, because who plays which part can change.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Saturn opposite natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-uranus/) arrives once per Saturn cycle of 29½ years and can last several months. [Transiting Uranus opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-saturn/) comes about once in a lifetime, since Uranus takes some 84 years to go round, and lingers for about a year.

If you were born under the opposition, both are written into your calendar. Saturn's own opposition, at about 14–15 and again around 44 ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)), brings Saturn onto your natal Uranus; the [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/), at about 42, brings Uranus onto your natal Saturn. Your early to mid-forties therefore hold two passes over the same axis, a couple of years apart. Treat them as weather, not a forecast: a season for re-balancing structure and freedom, with nothing decided in advance.
