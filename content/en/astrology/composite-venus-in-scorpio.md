Some pairs skip the small talk. At two in the morning they're still working out what a friend really meant, what each of them is afraid of, what neither would tell anyone else. Composite Venus in Scorpio values that depth more than any amount of pleasantness.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Composite [Venus](/astrology/venus/) is the relationship's Venus in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/): the point halfway between the two natal ones, read for what the bond values, enjoys and means to keep. [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), fixed water, is its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/), facing Taurus, one of Venus's own signs: Taurus values what is easy to keep, Scorpio what has been tested. Its rulership is split: modern astrologers give the sign to [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), tradition to [Mars](/astrology/mars/), and in birth charts I read both. Composite Pluto and composite Mars are midpoints like everything else, and since two people close in age share their generation's Pluto, it's usually composite Mars that has more to say about this particular pair.

Lightness elsewhere doesn't prevent any of this. People known among their friends for jokes and easy company can find that, alone together, they go straight to what matters. One person's all-or-nothing loyalty is what [Venus in Scorpio](/astrology/venus-in-scorpio/) describes in a birth chart; the composite's loyalty is a pact between two, and it shows in what you hold in common: confidences kept, resources pooled, a promise made at a bad moment and honoured. It turns up in about 1 pair in 12. At birth Scorpio is one of the commonest Venus signs, about 1 person in 10, because Venus can spend months there when it turns retrograde; the composite thins that out.

Depth has its shadow. A bond that values what has been tested can start testing: holding back to see who reaches out first, reading a silence as a verdict, treating ease as suspicious. Intensity isn't aggression and depth isn't secrecy, yet the two of you may need to say so to each other. It helps to name a need plainly instead of waiting to see whether the other guesses it, and to let some pleasures stay light: a film, a walk, a meal with nothing to process.

As for the composite Sun, it usually stays close, as it does to Venus in any birth chart: within 47° for about 9 pairs in 10, which for a Scorpio Venus means a composite Sun from Virgo round to Capricorn. The exceptions can only come from pairs whose birthdays are about three months or more apart, counted the short way round the year. Then the two midpoints can be taken on opposite sides, which in about 1 pair in 22 leaves the composite Sun near Taurus, across from this Venus. A Scorpio Venus may want to find a hidden meaning there, but it's the method, not a secret about you.

Between [composite Venus in Libra](/astrology/composite-venus-in-libra/), which values what is fair, and [composite Venus in Sagittarius](/astrology/composite-venus-in-sagittarius/), which values what is far, Scorpio values what is true. I don't use composites myself; this is the technique as those who do describe it, read with the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/) and both natal charts. For a bond that prizes trust this much, it matters that both people know the chart exists ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
