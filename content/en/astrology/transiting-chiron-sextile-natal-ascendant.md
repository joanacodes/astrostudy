A sextile to the Ascendant can only be dated from a birth time, because it is measured from the degree that was rising when you were born. Once dated, it tends to be a light season: chances to come into a room as yourself, sore spot and all, without having to explain it first.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) orients you at the start of things: posture, manner, first response. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) marks an old sore spot, and the know-how you have built around it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity, so in this [transit](/astrology/transits/) new ways of arriving are offered, and taking them is up to you.

Two sextiles come in each round, from different parts of the chart. The earlier, before Chiron reaches your rising degree, usually finds it in your 10th or 11th house: a group, a circle of friends or a public setting where you can practise meeting people without the old defence ([Chiron transiting the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-11th-house/)). The later, once the crossing is behind you, usually finds it in your 2nd or 3rd: everyday conversations, neighbours, a class, the plain words for what you used to keep quiet ([Chiron transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-3rd-house/)).

The sextile to your Ascendant is also a trine to your Descendant, the point opposite, so the people closest to you tend to make the opening easier rather than harder: a partner who backs the new manner, a friend who stopped needing the old joke long ago.

Every date here hangs on your rising degree. A birth time rounded to the half hour can leave the Ascendant several degrees out, and while Chiron is slow, as it is in Aries and Taurus, that can move this sextile by a year or so. No time, no rising degree, and no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, a typical pass lasts about ten months, during which Chiron touches the exact angle three times; nearly two passes in three are like that. About one in three is a quick, single touch of some three weeks, and one in twenty draws the season out to about twenty-one months with five touches.

Most people meet three of these sextiles by 80, some four, and nobody fewer than two. Chiron in Aries, until April 2027, sextiles Gemini rising from the 11th-house side and Aquarius rising from the 3rd; Chiron in Taurus, from 2026 to 2034, does the same for Cancer and Pisces rising; Chiron in Gemini, from 2033, for Leo and Aries rising. Within those years, your own rising degree fixes the months.

The earlier sextile leads into [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-ascendant/); the later one leads on towards [transiting Chiron square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-ascendant/), then [transiting Chiron trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-ascendant/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-ascendant/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say yes to one setting where nobody knows your old story, and arrive there the way you would like to be met.

Practise the plain sentence about your sore spot, the one you would say if it didn't need defending.

Let your closest people help. This sextile has them on its side.
