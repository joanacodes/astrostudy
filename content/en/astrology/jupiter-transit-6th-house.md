When Jupiter crosses your 6th house, its growth goes to work: the tasks of an ordinary day, the habits that hold the day together, the people you work alongside. It's the least glamorous house Jupiter visits, and the year can be one of the most useful.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) expands, learns and looks for meaning; the [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is daily work, routine, craft and the care you give your days. Put together, I read a year when ordinary work grows: more of it, often, and more willingness to do it, with better methods and better habits within reach. Something you do every day can turn into a craft you're proud of.

There's a friction in it worth naming. Tradition put Jupiter in detriment in Virgo, the 6th house's sign in the natural order, and the year can feel like that: a large outlook asked to work at the scale of a to-do list. I read the friction kindly. Meaning found in small, repeated tasks is still meaning, and the 6th is where tradition said Mars, the planet of effort, rejoices.

If Mars sits in your 6th from birth, the weeks Jupiter spends on it tend to make effort easier to keep up; with your Moon there, it's the routines that make you feel looked after.

Tradition also gave this house illness, and called it Bad Fortune. I don't read health in a transit, either way: no chart can predict an illness or a recovery ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and anything about your body belongs with a doctor. As with every [transit](/astrology/transits/), Jupiter here brings out what your chart already holds about work and care, nothing more.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

At London's latitude the 6th and the 7th have the widest spread of all: in Placidus charts drawn there, a Jupiter stay in the 6th lasts about a year in the middle, but most fall anywhere from about 10 months to just over 2 years, because a 6th house that far north can be very narrow or very wide. At New York's latitude the same house runs from about 11 months to nearly 2 years.

Which of those is yours depends on your birth time, and that's the reason I wait for the time before I read anything ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The stay follows a year of play in your 5th ([Jupiter transiting the 5th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-5th-house/)) and ends at your Descendant, where a year about partnership begins ([Jupiter transiting the 7th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-7th-house/)).

Take the signs two by two. Leo and Virgo hold Jupiter from June 2026 to August 2028, handing over in July 2027, so Virgo, the 6th's natural sign, is the very next one. Libra and Scorpio follow from August 2028 to October 2030, with the change in September 2029; Sagittarius and Capricorn from October 2030 to November 2032, changing in November 2031. Aquarius has Jupiter from April 2032 to December 2033 and Pisces from April 2033 to April 2034, the two overlapping as Jupiter goes back and forth. Then come Aries and Taurus, April 2034 to May 2036, with the change in April 2035, and Gemini and Cancer, May 2036 to June 2038, changing in May 2037. The degree on the cusp of your 6th tells you the month.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Build one habit at a time. A year of Jupiter in the 6th makes it tempting to overhaul everything at once, and the habits that last are usually the ones that started small.

Say no to the task that isn't yours. More work tends to arrive in this year, and willingness is a gift until it turns into carrying everyone else's load.

Learn a method, a tool or a craft that makes your days easier. And treat rest as part of the routine rather than a reward at the end of it: the 6th is the house of maintenance, and that includes yours.
