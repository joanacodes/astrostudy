Chiron on your Sun puts an old sore spot in the middle of the picture, on the degree that holds your sense of who you are. Once or twice in a life, for a few weeks or the better part of two years, being yourself and the place you learned to work around become one subject.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises identity and purpose: the self you put into the world and want to be recognised for. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a limitation stayed and you adapted around it, by compensating, practising, finding another way in. In a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), Chiron and your Sun share a degree and act as one, and under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the sore spot no longer sits off to the side. Questions you thought were settled come back with your name on them: whether your way of shining is welcome, whether you're allowed to want recognition, whether the thing you struggled with disqualifies you from the thing you'd like to do.

It also brings the skill into view. What you know because of that difficulty, how to start again, how to explain the hard part, how to sit with someone who feels the way you once did, can turn out to be part of what you're for. Two traps lie on either side. One is letting the sore spot become your whole story. The other is believing that because you help others with it so well, it must be finished for you. Neither is needed: the conjunction asks you to carry both, the tenderness and the competence, as yours.

Where it lands depends on the house your Sun is in, which needs your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Sun in the 5th, Chiron crosses it during its stay in that house ([Chiron transiting the 5th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-5th-house/)): play and creative work, and the old memory of being laughed at for them, come up together. Born with [Sun conjunct Chiron](/astrology/sun-conjunct-chiron/), you met this transit in your first months, and your [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, brings it back once your sense of purpose has had half a century to grow around the sore spot.

Nothing here concerns your health. The Sun is vitality in a chart, but a transit can't see the body, and I never read it for that ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the typical pass lasts about ten months. Nearly two passes in three have three exact hits as Chiron loops back over your Sun, over about ten and a half months; about one in three is a single crossing of some three weeks; about one in twenty crosses five times, over twenty-one months or so. Chiron's stretched orbit gives it all three.

Which one you get depends on your birthday. Chiron is slow in Taurus, where it spends 2027 to 2033 gaining only four or five degrees a year, so it loops back over every degree of that sign: Taurus birthdays get the long, three-crossing kind. By 80, most people have met the conjunction twice and everyone at least once; Chiron takes about fifty years to come round, so a first meeting before your thirties leaves room for a second. Its other contacts come more often: the sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-sun/)), the square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-sun/)) and the trine ([Transiting Chiron trine natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-sun/)) about three times each, the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-sun/)) once or twice, like this one.

At the end of 2026, Chiron is back on the last degrees of Aries, the Suns of birthdays around 16 to 20 April. From 2027 it reaches four or five more days of birthdays each year, late April first and mid-May by 2032, before Gemini birthdays take their turn from 2033.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say plainly what you're good at because of the sore spot, without apologising for how you learned it.

Spend time where you're recognised for all of you, tender place included. One such room is enough.

Keep one project that has nothing to do with the old difficulty, so it stays a chapter of your story and not the title.
