Uranus in your 6th house brings the planet of change to the most ordinary part of life: how a working day runs, which habits hold it up, and what you'd change about both if nobody was watching. In the middle, the stay lasts about seven years.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reforms systems: it looks at the way something is done and asks whether another arrangement would work better. The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the daily round, work as tasks rather than titles, routines, craft, the colleagues beside you and the care you give your days. Under Uranus, routines that once held you up can start to feel like rails. You may change the hours you keep, the tools you use, the way a team is organised, or the kind of daily work you're willing to do at all. In these years, the routines that last tend to be the ones you invent.

Mars, the planet of effort, was said to rejoice in the 6th. Uranus brings effort a question, why this way?, and the answer can give you back hours every week. With Venus in your 6th from birth, the change can reach the pleasure you take in your work, or the people you share it with.

Illness was the 6th's in the old books, and I don't read it in a transit: no chart can predict a health problem ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)), and any change to how you look after your body is a conversation for a doctor. What a [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this stirs is what your chart already holds about how you work and keep your days going.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

For a London birth, Uranus's stays in the 6th centre on about seven years in Placidus, but at that latitude a 6th can come out very narrow or very wide, so most stays run anywhere from nearly five years to nearly thirteen, one of the longest reaches of any house there, behind only the 12th and the 1st. Charts cast for New York turn the figures round: there the middle is longer, nearly eight years, and the long end shorter, about ten and a half.

Those figures count Uranus's whole visit, from the first crossing of the cusp to the last day in the house, retrograde returns included. Which of them is closest to yours depends on how wide your 6th is, and that, like the cusp's degree, comes from your birth time; without it I won't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Its years in your 5th, changing what you enjoy, come first ([Uranus transiting the 5th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-5th-house/)); the stay closes when Uranus reaches your Descendant and turns to partnership ([Uranus transiting the 7th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-7th-house/)).

Virgo, the sign that gives the 6th its eye for detail, isn't on Uranus's path until after 2039. Closer to now: with your 6th cusp in Gemini, Uranus crosses it between July 2025 and May 2033; in Cancer, between August 2032 and August 2039; in Leo, from August 2039. A Taurus cusp was crossed between May 2018 and April 2026, so for some the stay is already well under way.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change the method before you change anything bigger. Uranus in the 6th often wants a new way of working more than a new place to work, and the first is far easier to test.

Find out what the old routine was quietly doing for you. Some habits look like rails and turn out to be handrails: keep those, and redesign the rest.

Leave room for an irregular day. A week with one unplanned afternoon can hold more change than a timetable rewritten from scratch.
