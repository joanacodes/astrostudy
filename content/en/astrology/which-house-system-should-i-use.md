Two websites, one birth time, and your Venus is in the 4th house on one and the 5th on the other. Usually nothing is broken: the two are drawing the houses by different rules. That rule is the house system, and there are several, each with its reasons.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

Every system starts from the same two points: the [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), the degree rising in the east at your birth, and the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), where the zodiac crosses the meridian. They differ in how they cut the rest of the sky into twelve. There are three broad families:

- **[Whole Sign houses](/astrology/whole-sign-houses/).** The rising sign is the whole 1st house, the next sign the 2nd, and so on: twelve houses of exactly one sign each. Robert Hand argued, in a short book published in 2000, that it is the oldest house system of all; not everyone reads the ancient sources the same way.
- **[Equal houses](/astrology/equal-houses/).** Twelve houses of 30° each, counted from the degree of the Ascendant. The Midheaven floats, and can fall in the 9th or the 11th.
- **Quadrant systems.** The four angles are the cusps of the 1st, 4th, 7th and 10th houses, and each quarter of the chart between them is divided in three. They differ in how: [Porphyry](/astrology/porphyry/) cuts each quarter into three equal stretches of the zodiac; [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/) divides it by time, the time each degree takes to travel from the horizon to the meridian; [Koch](/astrology/koch/) also divides by time, from the Midheaven's own degree; [Regiomontanus](/astrology/regiomontanus/) and [Campanus](/astrology/campanus/) divide a circle of the sky into twelve equal parts, the celestial equator for one and the circle through the east point, the zenith and the west point for the other, and project them onto the zodiac.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

The differences grow with distance from the equator, because the zodiac rises at a steeper or gentler slant the further north or south you are. In quadrant systems, houses can become very unequal, and a sign can open two houses or none ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)). Beyond the polar circles, where some degrees of the zodiac never rise or set, Placidus and Koch can't be calculated at all.

What changes between systems is only the houses. Every planet keeps its sign, its degree and its aspects; the Ascendant and the Midheaven keep their degrees. What moves is the house a planet falls in, and so the part of life it's read as speaking to, most often for planets near a cusp. Whole Sign can shift a planet a whole house away from where a quadrant system puts it; between two quadrant systems the differences are usually smaller.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

I use Placidus. Every house on this site, in the pages on planets in houses, on the houses themselves and on transits through them, is written in it, and in Placidus the angles are always the cusps of the angular houses ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). The other systems aren't mistakes: each is a reasoned answer to the same question of how to divide the sky. What matters most is to keep to one, to read about your chart in the system it was drawn in, and to compare like with like.

So if a chart from another site puts your planets in different houses, check which system it uses before deciding which description fits. If it's set to Placidus and still disagrees, check the birth time. Every system needs one, Whole Sign included, because without the time there's no rising sign to start from ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Whole Sign is the ancient system, so the others are wrong.”** Age isn't accuracy, and the history is still argued over. Each system is a reasoned answer to the same question.
- **“Placidus is broken because it fails near the poles.”** It can't be calculated beyond the polar circles, a real limit there; everywhere else it works as designed.
- **“Another house system will change your signs.”** Your planets' signs, degrees and aspects are the same in every system. Only the houses move.
- **“Whole Sign and equal houses don't need a birth time.”** They need it as much as any other: the rising sign is where the count begins.
