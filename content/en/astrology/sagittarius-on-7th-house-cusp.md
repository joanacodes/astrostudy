Sagittarius sets in the west while Gemini rises in the east, and a birth in that window puts the archer on your Descendant. Across the table, you tend to find the one holding the bigger map.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) opens the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) in Placidus, the house system I use, and its sign describes what you look for, and keep meeting, in the person across the table ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/) there, it's often someone who arrives with a different country, faith, discipline or sense of humour, and who makes your own life look larger from where they stand. A bond grows while you're still learning from each other, and cools when it settles into a routine nobody chose. Frankness counts as respect here, and room to move as trust; a partner's growth, even when it takes them somewhere new, tends to feel like good news rather than a threat. At work, the same goes for partnerships and close collaborations: you do well beside someone with a vision, while your quick Gemini side handles the detail. The old books add open enemies to this house; with Sagittarius on its cusp, the arguments you have in the open tend to be about principles, with opponents you can respect.

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules this Descendant. The house it sits in is where partners tend to come into your life, through study, travel, work or friends, and its sign describes the bond you build: the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). [Gemini rising](/astrology/gemini-rising/) comes with every Sagittarius Descendant, so Mercury rules your chart. By tradition's account Mercury is ill at ease in Sagittarius and Jupiter in Gemini, and I read that as two people who fill each other's gaps rather than mirror each other. Pisces, Jupiter's second sign and Neptune's too, lies in the 11th in every London chart, on its cusp or wholly inside: friends and partners come under Jupiter together.

Sagittarius is on the Descendant in about 1 birth in 13, at both latitudes, fewer than an even share. It sets as fast as Gemini rises on the other side, in under two hours at London, so the window is short. With only about 16° to 23° between the 7th and 8th cusps at London, Sagittarius reaches the 8th cusp as well in close to 3 charts in 10 ([Sagittarius on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-8th-house-cusp/); [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), and Jupiter then speaks for the bond and for what it shares, joint money and trust included. Every London chart with this Descendant also has an intercepted pair, most often Taurus inside the 12th and Scorpio inside the 6th ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Whom you marry, if anyone, and how long a bond lasts are not written on a cusp; I read what draws you, and leave the outcome to the two people living it. Move the birth an hour or two and the picture shifts: earlier, Sagittarius would sit on the 8th cusp; later, on the 6th ([Sagittarius on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-6th-house-cusp/)). The Descendant needs the birth time as much as the Ascendant does, and I won't read either without it ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
