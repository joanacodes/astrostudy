What you do for joy, with Taurus on the 5th house cusp, tends to get better the longer you keep doing it. Play, romance, creative work: here they deepen with time rather than wear out.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [5th house](/astrology/5th-house/) is the part of life that asks for no reason, and its cusp sign is how you enjoy it ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) enjoys by keeping at it. The hobby you took up at fifteen may still be yours at fifty; the creative project you return to over years ends up better than anything you could have rushed. Pleasure builds by repetition here: the Saturday ritual, the trip you make every year, the favourite place you never tire of. In love, the second and the tenth meetings tend to matter more than the first: attraction grows by staying, and you show it by turning up. Creative work is a long game for you, the tenth version of a song, the garden in its fifth summer, and what helps is showing it to someone before it's perfect, so that it gets finished. Leo, the 5th's natural sign, is fixed like Taurus, so the house's own nature and this sign agree: these are joys you commit to rather than sample. Across the chart, the 11th opens in Scorpio, and the contrast is worth knowing: your own pleasures are loyal and plain, while the groups you join tend to run deep.

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your 5th, and tradition put the joy of Venus in this very house: the planet of pleasure and preference rules the house where it was said to be happiest. With this cusp, Libra usually sits on your Midheaven as well, so Venus also rules your public direction, and the line between what you love and what you do for a living is often thin. Each house Venus might occupy turns your pleasures towards a different part of life: the [1st](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/5th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Taurus opens the 5th in about 1 birth in 11, London and New York alike, counting Placidus cusps, the system I use, and Sagittarius usually rises with it. It often opens a second house too, in about 2 London charts out of 3 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)): most often the 6th, so that pleasure and daily work share a ruler and a job can become something you love ([Taurus on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/)), and otherwise the 4th, which ties your pleasures to home ([Taurus on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-4th-house-cusp/)). Either way, Venus speaks for two neighbouring parts of your life at once. In other charts Taurus has no cusp at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), about 1 London birth in 7.

Children were the 5th's other business in the old books. What I read here is your own pleasure and your own loves, never whether or when a child will come ([can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)), and I leave children's charts unread ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)). The cusp itself depends on your birth time, and without it there's no reading ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
