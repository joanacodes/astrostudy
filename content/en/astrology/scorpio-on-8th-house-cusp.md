Scorpio on the 8th house cusp puts the sign in the house that is naturally its own. The 8th speaks its native language here: trust tested, debts honoured, and change lived through rather than avoided.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) holds what passes between people once a bond is made: intimacy, trust, shared money, debts, and the changes that sharing brings ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/) is its natural sign, so nothing here needs translating. Closeness, for you, includes the hard truths, and an intimacy that avoids them doesn't feel like intimacy. What's owed between people, money or loyalty, you treat as binding, and you expect the same in return. When something breaks in a shared life, you tend to stay and work through what lies underneath rather than patch the surface, and you come out of those passages changed. An interest in what drives people, yourself included, often comes with the cusp.

The 8th's natural rulers are Scorpio's, [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) and [Mars](/astrology/mars/), and with this cusp they rule your own 8th as well. I read both, and wherever they sit is where the 8th's business of trust, sharing and change gets done: the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). When Aries rises, in about 4 London charts in 9 and 2 in 3 at New York, Mars is also your chart ruler: the way you meet the world and the way you share and trust answer to the same planet. When Pisces rises instead, Aries lies wholly inside your 1st ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), Jupiter and Neptune rule the chart, and Mars has its say in your 1st through the sign inside it. Opposite, Taurus opens your 2nd: Venus keeps what's yours, Mars and Pluto what's shared, the axis as the natural zodiac draws it.

About 1 London birth in 17 has Scorpio on the 8th cusp, and 1 New York birth in 15; like every figure here, that counts Placidus cusps, the house system I use. In the north, Scorpio crosses the western half of the sky faster than the eastern one, and the 8th, just above the Descendant, is among its rarer cusps. The house is narrow in these charts, about 21° to 29° at London, yet Scorpio almost never reaches the 9th cusp as well. When it misses every cusp, in about 1 London birth in 7 and 1 New York birth in 11, it is never in the 8th: it lies somewhere from the 2nd to the 7th.

Tradition made the 8th the house of death and of legacies, and Scorpio's reputation adds weight to both. Neither is a forecast in my reading ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)): this cusp describes how you live through change, share and trust.

With Aries or Pisces rising, the minute matters twice. It decides which of the two rises, and so who rules your chart, and whether Scorpio is on the 8th at all, rather than setting on the 7th ([Scorpio on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-7th-house-cusp/)) or still up on the 9th ([Scorpio on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/)). For both, the recorded time is what counts ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
