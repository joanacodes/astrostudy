Of all Neptune's contacts with Jupiter, the trine is the one where two expansive planets help each other without blurring each other. For about two and a half years faith, study and kindness flow with imagination; about 1 person in 7 never meets it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) grows understanding and builds the beliefs and principles you live by. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings vision, compassion and a sense of the sacred, in whatever form that takes for you. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) relates two functions through flow while preserving what each is, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) imagination widens your sense of meaning and Jupiter keeps it coherent.

This is often a good season for study and for teaching, for travel that means something, for art or a spiritual practice that has been waiting for time. Generosity comes without anxiety, and it may be easier than usual to hold a belief and stay open to someone else's. The risk is the trine's own: ease taken for granted. A widening that nobody uses simply passes, and for Jupiter an opportunity is something you develop.

With Jupiter in your 11th, the trine often comes while Neptune crosses your 7th or your 3rd: a partner's faith or vision ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)), or what you read and hear close to home, feeds the ideals you share with friends. Which house is your 11th, and whether Jupiter sits in it, is a matter of your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Jupiter trine Neptune](/astrology/jupiter-trine-neptune/), as at the start of 2005, you had this transit in your first months; for everyone else it comes later, from the same circle.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune keeps the trine within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, for about two years and seven months, twenty-two at the shortest. In most passes the trine is exact three times and in about one in four five times, so there's time to start something and see it through.

From 2026 to 2039, with Neptune in Aries, the trine reaches people born with Jupiter in Sagittarius, Jupiter's own sign: those born in 1983 meet it between about 41 and 52, those born in 1995 between about 30 and 36. About 6 people in 7 born between 1930 and 2009 meet it at some point before 80, some of them twice; for births at the start of 1935 or 1975 it doesn't come.

For someone born at the start of 1995 the sequence runs: [transiting Neptune sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-jupiter/) at about 4, [transiting Neptune square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-jupiter/) at about 18, this trine at about 31 and [transiting Neptune opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-jupiter/) at about 58½. For them, [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) lies beyond 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Start the course, the book or the practice you've been saving for later. The trine makes the first year easy, and the first year is the one that usually stops people.

Share what you believe without needing agreement. This transit makes it easier to hold your own faith and stay curious about someone else's.

Put your generosity on a schedule. A regular gift of time or help gives an easy season something to show for itself.
