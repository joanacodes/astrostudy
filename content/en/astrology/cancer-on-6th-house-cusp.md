With Cancer on the 6th house cusp, the working day is something you look after like a household: its rhythms, its people and the small comforts that keep it going. Work, routines and the care of your body all run here on familiarity and feeling.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [6th house](/astrology/6th-house/) is the shape of a working day, and the sign on its cusp is how that day needs to feel ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) there, the atmosphere matters as much as the tasks. You tend to notice when a colleague is having a bad week, keep the biscuit tin full, remember the birthdays nobody else does, and become the person newcomers are sent to. Work that looks after people, places, animals or things often suits you, though no cusp chooses a trade. Routines work best when they comfort: the same mug, the same walk, a meal at roughly the same hour. The Moon, which rules this cusp, is the planet of habit, and your habits tend to follow how you feel, so a routine that bends with your mood lasts longer than one that fights it. Modern astrologers file pets under the 6th, and with Cancer here they are family rather than a chore.

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rules your 6th, and its house shows what your daily work serves: the [1st](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/6th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). [Aquarius rising](/astrology/aquarius-rising/) comes with this cusp in about 3 London charts in 5 and nearly 3 in 4 at New York, so Uranus and Saturn rule the chart, and I read both: an independent, sometimes cool first impression, with a working life full of care behind it. Capricorn rises in about 1 London chart in 4, and Pisces in the rest. In about 1 London chart in 3, Cancer opens the 5th as well, and play and work answer to the Moon together; in more than 1 in 4 it opens the 7th, and the Moon speaks for colleagues and partners alike ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). [Cancer on the 5th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/) and [Cancer on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/) describe those charts too.

Counting Placidus cusps, the house system I use, about 1 birth in 13 has Cancer on the 6th at London and 1 in 12 at New York: a little under two hours of each day. Here Cancer moves at nearly the pace Capricorn rises on the other side of the chart, which is quick in the north, so this is one of Cancer's rarer cusps. In about 1 London chart in 8, the whole of Leo lies inside the 6th as well, and the Sun adds its warmth to your working days ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Illness belonged to the 6th in the old books, which named it Bad Fortune. No chart can diagnose or predict a health problem, and questions about your health are for a doctor ([can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)); I read the 6th for how you work and how you look after yourself, and neither can be placed without the birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
