At a first dinner with friends, people assume the two of you have been together for decades. Composite Saturn in the 1st house gives a relationship a serious face: steady, reserved, older than its years.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

The 1st house of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) is the bond's own presence: how it appears, how it starts things, what people notice first when they meet the two of you together. [The 1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is angular, beginning at the Ascendant, so whatever sits there is visible. With [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) in it, what shows first is structure. The pair seems settled, careful, a little formal, and it tends to be taken seriously from the start. It may also take its time to show itself: friends can know you as two people long before they know you as a pair.

This house depends on both birth times more than most, because it begins at the composite [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/). One way of drawing it takes the midpoint of your two Ascendants; another works the Ascendant out from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live. An error of ten minutes in one birth time moves that person's Ascendant by a couple of degrees, and the midpoint of the two by about half as much, which is enough to push a Saturn near the line into the 12th house or out of it. Without both times the composite has no Ascendant, so no 1st house at all, and I hold single charts to the same rule ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The sign of composite Saturn is shared by most people your age; its house is not. Two people born in the same week share Saturn's sign, but the composite's houses turn on the hours, so this placement belongs to your pair in a way the sign never can. Aries is the 1st house's natural sign, and tradition puts Saturn's fall there. A sign's dignities don't pass to its house, but the echo is fair: this bond is asked to show itself, and Saturn would rather wait.

One person with [Saturn in the 1st house](/astrology/saturn-in-1st-house/) often seemed serious early and grew into their own ease later; in a synastry, [their Saturn in your 1st house](/astrology/saturn-in-1st-house-synastry/) is one person steadying, or watching, the way the other shows up. In the composite, nobody is watching anybody. The gravity belongs to the bond, and you both carry it when you walk into a room together.

The neighbours divide the work differently: [composite Saturn in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-12th-house/) keeps the bond's structure out of sight, and [composite Saturn in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-saturn-in-2nd-house/) puts it into what the pair owns and values; in the 1st, it's on show. The risk is a front that never comes down. A pair that always appears composed can find it hard to be playful in public, or to let anyone see it struggle, and newcomers may read its reserve as disapproval. It helps to let a few people see the relationship unpolished, to start things together before they're perfect, and to enjoy the respect this placement tends to earn rather than guarding it.

Composite charts stay out of my own readings; this is the technique in the hands of those who use it, always set beside both birth charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), and it predicts nothing about the two of you.
