Chiron opposite Lilith sets the way you adapt around a lasting difficulty across the chart from the part of you that refuses to be tamed. One end often feels like you and the other like somebody else's business, until the opposition shows you that both are yours.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the function of adaptation: around a limitation that lasts, it compensates, accommodates and builds new skill, so that you keep taking part. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the averaged far point of the Moon's orbit, is read by many astrologers as autonomy, instinct, boundaries and the parts of a person kept out of view, though traditions weigh it differently and I read it as a symbol. Across an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), the link is polarity: each end shows the other up.

In plain words, accommodating and refusing live at opposite ends of your chart. You might be the one who always adapts in one area of life and the one who never bends in another, or you may find people who ask you to fit in on one side and people whose defiance fascinates you on the other. The opposition makes the pattern visible, and seeing it is how you start to choose.

Polarity isn't contradiction. What this one builds is judgement: knowing, situation by situation, when an accommodation is wise and when a refusal is, and being able to do either without losing yourself.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

An opposition joins opposite signs. With Chiron in Aries and Lilith in Libra, the adaptation concerns acting on your own and the refusal concerns being agreeable on demand; with Chiron in Pisces and Lilith in Virgo, adapting through imagination faces an instinct that refuses to be tidied into someone else's order. Lilith reaches the point opposite Chiron once in each round of about a decade, for a few months.

The houses name both ends, given a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron in the 4th with Lilith in the 10th, for instance, might describe someone carefully adapted at home and unbending in public.

About 1 person in 39 has Chiron and Lilith within 6° of an opposition, counted with the mean Lilith, the rarest of the pair's five aspects. Both ends held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook as placements, by sign and house, which is why I read this opposition slowly ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#gently)); the aspect itself was never counted.

Measured against the opposition, the pair's other aspects:

- **[Chiron conjunct Lilith](/astrology/chiron-conjunct-lilith/):** the two ends folded together, adapting and refusing in one move.
- **[Chiron sextile Lilith](/astrology/chiron-sextile-lilith/):** an easy working relationship, if you ask for it.
- **[Chiron square Lilith](/astrology/chiron-square-lilith/):** friction inside you rather than a polarity met through others.
- **[Chiron trine Lilith](/astrology/chiron-trine-lilith/):** a natural flow between the two, with no balance to strike.

Between two charts, [Chiron opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/chiron-opposite-lilith-synastry/) sets one person's tender, adapted place across from the other's untamed point. It can feel like being seen whole or like being pushed, depending on the care taken, and I read it only with both people's consent ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), born with the opposition, Chiron's own clock matters most. When Chiron reaches the point opposite its birth place, at an age that varies a great deal from one generation to the next, it is also passing over your Lilith; and the [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, sets it opposite Lilith once more.

Lilith's visits are quicker: twice in each nine-year round it lines up with one end of the axis or the other, for a few weeks.

Weather, not a forecast: these seasons bring both ends into view at once. Where you find the balance is up to you.
