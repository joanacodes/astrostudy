Some exchanges cost more than they should: the compliment you hand straight back, the favour you return twice over, the taste you keep quiet about in company. When Chiron squares your Venus, that old arithmetic of being valued comes up for checking, and most people go through it three times.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs worth: what attracts you, what you find beautiful, and whether an exchange gives back as much as it takes. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the place where a sensitivity stays and you learn to take part around it, by compensating, adjusting and practising. A [square](/astrology/square/) sets the two at 90°, in friction that asks for an adjustment rather than a winner.

Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the sore spot tends to show in the ordinary trade between people. You notice that you pay in advance for affection: the extra effort, the present that's slightly too generous, the yes given before anyone has asked. Or the wish to be found attractive, admired or chosen meets an old memory of being passed over. Compensating has a cost that is easy to underestimate, and on Venus the cost is often your own pleasure. The square doesn't ask you to stop caring how you're valued. It asks for a better bargain, one in which you may receive before you've earned it.

Where it lands depends on your houses. With [Venus in the 11th house](/astrology/venus-in-11th-house/), Chiron is usually crossing your 2nd ([Chiron transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-2nd-house/)): what you're worth to your friends meets what you believe you're worth at all. Placing either needs the minutes of your birth, and without them I don't read the square ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Venus square Chiron](/astrology/venus-square-chiron/), you meet one of these squares at your [Chiron return](/astrology/chiron-return/), around 50, since Chiron back on its own degree is at right angles to your Venus again.

Nothing here foretells a break-up, a loss or a lonely stretch. If the friction turns into a low that won't lift, it belongs with a doctor or a therapist rather than a chart ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, this square usually lasts about ten months. In nearly two passes out of three, Chiron's yearly retrograde loop carries it over the exact square three times: I read the first crossing as raising the question, the backward one as reopening it, and the third as settling how you'll answer. About one pass in three is a single crossing of some three weeks, and about one in twenty loops five times over roughly twenty-one months. Chiron's orbit is stretched enough to give it all three shapes.

Most people meet this square three times by 80, some twice and some four times, and nobody misses it, because every point has two squares in each of Chiron's rounds of about 50 years. The first comes after [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-venus/) and [transiting Chiron sextile natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-venus/), before [transiting Chiron trine natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-venus/); the second follows [transiting Chiron opposite natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-venus/) and the second trine.

In the years ahead, Chiron in Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, squares Venus in Leo and Aquarius; Chiron in Gemini, from July 2033 to April 2039, squares Venus in Virgo and Pisces. The degree of your Venus narrows it to a season.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name one exchange that feels uneven, and say once, plainly, what you'd need from it.

Let a compliment land without returning it at once. Receiving is the skill this square is training.

Notice when you pay for affection in advance, and try waiting to be asked.

Keep one pleasure that needs nobody's approval, and enjoy it on purpose.
