The 3rd house is the house of the everyday: talk, errands, short journeys, the people close by. With the composite Sun there, that ordinary traffic becomes the relationship's centre, and the bond knows itself in the daily exchange.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) places its houses from both birth times, and they matter more than people expect. An hour's error in one of them moves that person's Midheaven by about 15° and the composite Midheaven by about half that, carrying the cusps along, so a [Sun](/astrology/sun/) near the edge of [the 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) can slip into the 2nd or the 4th ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The cusps can also be drawn two ways, as midpoints of your natal cusps or rebuilt from the composite Midheaven at the place where you live, and the two needn't agree.

Exchange at close range is the 3rd house's subject. A relationship like this lives in the running conversation: the call on the way home, the book passed back and forth, the local walk where most things get decided. It's revived by learning something together, a language, a craft, a new route through town, and its purpose often has to do with connecting people nearby. Neighbours, colleagues down the corridor and siblings tend to know this pair well. Even apart, it keeps the thread going: a message about something small, sent because the other one will want to know.

Tradition named the 3rd the Goddess and gave the Moon its joy there ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)); it's a cadent house, and Gemini is its natural sign. It was also the house of siblings, and since a composite can be drawn for any two adults, a pair of siblings with this placement has its centre in the very house tradition gave to brothers and sisters, though it describes the bond as the two of you live it now, not a family's history. A single chart's [Sun in the 3rd house](/astrology/sun-in-3rd-house/) describes a person who grows through talking and learning, and the synastry overlay, [their Sun in your 3rd house](/astrology/sun-in-3rd-house-synastry/), one person brightening the other's thinking. Here, the thinking happens between you.

The everyday can swallow the conversation. Logistics, who's collecting what and when, can replace real talk, and a bond organised around the near and familiar can start to feel small. A conversation with no errands in it helps, and so does a trip a little further than usual. On either side, the [composite Sun in the 2nd house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-2nd-house/) gathers a bond around what it values and the [composite Sun in the 4th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-4th-house/) around its roots; the 3rd is the road between the two.

This is a technique I describe rather than practise, and its users read it with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/). It says how a bond talks to itself, never how long it will last or where it's going. Asking each other first is in the spirit of this house: a composite uses both people's birth data, and both should know ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
