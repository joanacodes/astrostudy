A sextile from the node to Mars is an opening you have to walk through yourself. For about a month the node is 60° from your Mars, the South Node trine it from the far side, and a first step in a less familiar direction costs less effort than usual.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is how you begin and push: motivation, initiative, the effort that turns intention into action. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is a capacity that grows by repetition, and a [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) offers cooperation between functions on one condition, that you respond. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), effort aimed at something new meets less resistance, and nothing moves until you do.

The openings are practical: a training you could start, a role that asks for some initiative, a skill learned by doing rather than reading, a moment to speak up that would usually pass. With the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) trine your Mars, your established ways of getting things done are on hand, so the new effort can lean on the old competence.

With your [Mars in the 3rd house](/astrology/mars-in-3rd-house/), the house of everyday words, short journeys, brothers and sisters, the opening may be as simple as saying what you think, sooner, or learning something hands-on close to home. That house, like all of them, is drawn from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

A transit to Mars also reaches the houses Mars rules: the one with Aries on its cusp, and the one with Scorpio, whose two rulers I read together, Mars from the tradition and Pluto from modern tropical astrology. So this sextile can open a door in either of those houses as well.

Mars in Virgo or Capricorn, for example, gets its next sextile in the node's Scorpio years, March 2031 to December 2032, with the exact month set by the degree of your Mars. That month won't appear in my forecast, which leaves out every aspect of the node but the conjunction and the opposition.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Usually the sextile holds for about a month, counting 1½° before exact and 1° after. The true node moves at the Sun's bidding, fastest when the Sun is 90° from the nodes and almost still when the Sun lines up with them, so the quickest passes are done in three and a half weeks and the slowest, in an eclipse season, take three and a half months or more. Roughly one pass in twenty-five hits exact three times.

Two sextiles fall in each round, one either side of the conjunction ([Transiting North Node conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-mars/)), so by 80 the count is nine for most people and never below eight; [Transiting North Node square natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-mars/), [Transiting North Node trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-mars/) and [Transiting North Node opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-mars/) fill the rest of the round. If you were born with [Mars sextile North Node](/astrology/mars-sextile-north-node/), add your nodal returns, in your late teens, your late thirties and your mid-fifties, to the list.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sign up for something you'd learn by doing: a class, a sport, a workshop. This sextile favours hands over plans.

Take one small initiative a week that you would normally leave to someone else.

Use what you already do well as the way in. A new effort is easier when it starts from a strength.
