Sun sextile Chiron joins the two a sixth of the zodiac apart, close enough to cooperate. It describes experience of a tender place that your sense of purpose can draw on, and that tends to become practical help, for you and for others, once you let it.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is purpose and self-expression: what you want to do with your life, and how you put yourself into it. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where something didn't work smoothly and you learned to adapt: a different method, a different pace, a tool that made things possible. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) makes the link one of opportunity: friendly, and active when you use it.

In plain words, the thing that once held you back has taught you something you can use. You may know how to explain what others find hard because it was hard for you, or how to adjust a task so that someone else can take part. Sharing what you learned works best when you remember that other people's situations differ from yours: what helped you is a method, not a law. And like every sextile, this one waits. The opportunity is real, but it asks for your engagement. Chiron is also the placement I name most carefully, and only where it's welcome ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as practical, unfussy kindness, and as competence in an area that once cost you effort. Two signs apart, the elements usually complement each other. With Chiron in Aries, a Sun in Gemini or Aquarius can put words and ideas to work around the subject of confidence; with Chiron in Pisces, a Sun in Taurus or Capricorn brings steadiness to a tender place that's hard to name. The houses show which two areas of life help each other: the Sun's is where you want to shine, Chiron's where you learned to adapt.

Within 8°, about 1 person in 11 is born with the Sun sextile Chiron. The closer to 60°, the more readily the experience is there to use, and the more naturally it reaches other people. Chiron was found only in 1977, so a chart drawn before then, or by software that leaves it out, won't show this sextile at all.

The pair's other aspects hold the same tender place closer or further off:

- **[Sun conjunct Chiron](/astrology/sun-conjunct-chiron/):** the tender place sits at the centre of who you are rather than at a helpful distance.
- **[Sun square Chiron](/astrology/sun-square-chiron/):** the same experience as friction; the sore spot gets in the way, and adapting is daily work.
- **[Sun trine Chiron](/astrology/sun-trine-chiron/):** a stronger soft link than this one; care for others and self-expression flow together without being asked.
- **[Sun opposite Chiron](/astrology/sun-opposite-chiron/):** the tender place is met through other people first, across the chart.

Between two charts, [Sun sextile Chiron in synastry](/astrology/sun-sextile-chiron-synastry/) lets one person's purpose meet the other's tender place gently: encouragement that helps without prying, in either direction.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Chiron sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-sun/) is an opening to use what you've learned, or to find a new way round an old limit. When it comes, and how long it lasts, depend on where Chiron is in its uneven orbit: it crosses some signs in under two years and others in about eight. For a day or so, twice a year, the Sun in the sky echoes the sextile to your natal Chiron. Weather, not a forecast: an opening, never a promise.
