When Chiron trines your Sun, what you learned the long way round starts to feel like an ordinary part of who you are. For about ten months, usually, it's easy to talk about, to use and to hand on, and just as easy to let the season pass without doing any of it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) is the centre you act and create from: identity, purpose, self-expression. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is a limitation that stayed and the practical wisdom it grew; it has a teacher's side, experience handed on to someone who needs it. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets that wisdom and your self-expression run together, each keeping its own character. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), the teacher tends to come forward. You find you can say "this is what happened to me, and this is what I do now" without the old catch in your voice. A colleague asks how you manage, and the answer is useful. A creative project draws on the very thing you used to keep out of it.

Ease isn't inevitability, and flow isn't passivity: the trine helps what you start and starts nothing itself. Nor does every sore spot have to become a lesson for others. Some of what this transit offers is simply rest, a stretch when your sense of self and your old difficulty stop pulling against each other.

Where the ease shows is a matter of houses, which the birth time places ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With the Sun in the 1st, one of these trines comes while Chiron crosses your 9th ([Chiron transiting the 9th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-9th-house/)): study, teaching, writing or a journey becomes the place where what you've lived is turned into something others can use. [Sun trine Chiron](/astrology/sun-trine-chiron/) in a birth chart makes this ease part of how a person shines for life; the transit lends it to everyone else for a season.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Most of Chiron's trines to your Sun last about ten months within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, because nearly two in three cross the exact degree three times. About one in three is a single crossing, normally some three weeks; now and then a single-crossing pass lasts much longer, close to ten months, when Chiron turns round inside the orb without touching the exact degree again. About one pass in twenty crosses five times, over twenty-one months or so.

Three trines by 80 is the usual count, two or four for some people: the trine points sit a third of the zodiac either side of your Sun, and Chiron reaches each once a round. The square ([Transiting Chiron square natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-sun/)) presses where this trine eases, and the sextile ([Transiting Chiron sextile natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-sun/)) asks for a first step where this one offers a current; both come as often, while the conjunction ([Transiting Chiron conjunct natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-sun/)) and the opposition ([Transiting Chiron opposite natal Sun](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-sun/)) come once or twice.

At the end of 2026, from the last degrees of Aries, the trine falls on birthdays around 18 to 22 August and 17 to 21 December. Through Taurus, from 2027 to 2033, it reaches the Suns of the other two earth signs, Virgo and Capricorn, four or five days of birthdays further each year.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Teach one thing you learned the hard way, to one person, in plain words.

Make something from it that can outlast the transit: a page of writing, a talk, a method someone else can follow.

Let part of the ease be rest. The trine doesn't require you to turn your history into anyone's lesson.
