Their Sun in your 10th house carries another person's purpose up to the most visible part of your chart: the work you do, the role you play and the reputation you're building. It can be the overlay of the mentor, the sponsor or the colleague whose belief in you changes what you aim for.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[The 10th house](/astrology/10th-house/) sits at the top of the chart: your public direction, the role you take on in the world, the responsibilities you accept and what you'd like to have done. The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) gives a person a direction and the vitality to follow it; this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) points that direction at your public life.

For you, it can feel like being taken seriously. They notice what you're capable of in the world and say so; they ask about your plans as if those plans mattered; and you may find yourself aiming higher, or more precisely, because someone with a clear purpose of their own believes yours is worth pursuing. Of the four angular houses it is the most public, and its natural sign, [Capricorn](/astrology/capricorn/), stands for slow, deliberate building.

For them, your role in the world can look like a version of their own aims. They may feel proud of what you do, invest their energy in your path, or simply like being seen beside you. When their Sun is just past your [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/), the degree of the zodiac on the meridian at the hour of your birth, it meets the reference point of your public life, and I treat that contact as part of the same reading.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Being believed in is a gift until it becomes a plan someone else has for you. Their sense of direction can start steering yours, so that you find yourself building the career they would want, or judging your work by their standards rather than your own. Visibility can be a sore point too: who gets the credit, whose name goes first, who is introduced as whose partner.

From their side, your public life can feel like a rival. They may sense that your role takes the light they hoped to share, or that they meet you mostly in your professional capacity, polished and busy, and miss the person behind it.

The overlay says nothing about success or standing, for either of you. It describes where your paths meet in public, and what each of you tends to feel there.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep your aims in your own words. Write down what you want from your work before you ask what they think, and tell them what kind of help you'd like: encouragement, advice, an introduction, or simply room. In a couple, talk about how two working lives fit around each other; between colleagues, agree on credit before the project, not after; between friends, cheer each other's work without competing for it; between adult relatives, notice which expectations about careers are yours and which are inherited.

Seen from the other chart, this overlay is your Sun at the top of theirs, in their 10th house, where your belief in them can lift their aims, or steer them. Before it, [their Sun in your 9th house](/astrology/sun-in-9th-house-synastry/) is about belief and horizons; after it, [their Sun in your 11th house](/astrology/sun-in-11th-house-synastry/) is about friends and shared hopes.

The sky turns by about a degree every four minutes, so a birth time out by half an hour can move your Midheaven, and the start of your 10th with it, by several degrees. In Placidus, the house system I use, that can decide whether a Sun near the top of your chart falls in the 9th or the 10th. Their Sun crawls by comparison, a degree a day, and their own time matters only at the 10th's edges, though their own houses, for the other half of the reading, need it too ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If they manage your work or share it, the reading waits for their agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
