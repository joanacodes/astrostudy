People ask it in different ways: is this a good year to buy, will my Jupiter return bring money, should I wait for Mercury to turn direct before I sell. Underneath, it's one question, whether the sky can tell you what your money will do, and it deserves a plain answer.

## The short answer {#the-short-answer}

No. Astrology can't pick an investment, time a market or tell you how much you'll have. Investments and financial decisions are on [my list of what I won't read](/how-i-read/), with health, death, pregnancy and ongoing legal proceedings, and for the same reason: each is a decision with real consequences, and a chart doesn't hold the information it turns on.

What a chart can describe is your side of the money: how you tend to earn, spend, save and share, and what makes you feel secure. I read the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) as your relationship with money, never a figure in an account, and the [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/), opposite it, as what you share or owe: joint accounts, loans, debts, trust. I read [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) as growth and meaning; luck and wealth aren't part of it, wherever it sits. And like every house, the 2nd and the 8th are placed by your birth time, which is one reason I don't read a chart without one ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Why it matters {#why-it-matters}

An investment turns on things outside you: a company's accounts, interest rates, fees, what other people decide next week, and how much you could afford to lose. None of that is in a birth chart. A chart describes a person, not a market. The planets' movement over your chart, the [transits](/astrology/transits/), I read as weather, not a forecast: they can mark a season when money is on your mind, never what the money will do.

My own testing points the same way. When I [checked my chart like an invoice](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), I was testing description: whether a placement described a person in a way that fitted no one else. About a third held overall, 30 to 40%, and Saturn, Lilith and Chiron held 75 to 90% of the time. None of it told anyone when to buy or sell, because none of it was about markets.

A forecast also changes the person who receives it. If the sky said the time was right and the investment fails, the planets get the blame; if it succeeds, they get the credit. Either way you haven't practised deciding, which is the skill money asks of you most. The same thinking is behind [Can astrology predict a court case?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-a-court-case/): when an outcome depends on other people, on rules and on facts no chart can see, a forecast adds nothing but false confidence.

Some astrologers do sell market timing and lucky dates. Reading prices in the sky has a history of its own, told in [Financial astrology, in history](/astrology/financial-astrology-in-history/), but a history isn't a track record. Whoever pairs a promise of returns with a deadline, astrologer or not, is someone to be wary of.

## What to do instead {#what-to-do-instead}

Use each tool for its own job. A chart can help you see how you behave with money; the decision belongs to you, ideally after talking it through with someone qualified to advise on it.

- **Know your patterns.** Do you spend for comfort, save out of fear, leave the post unopened when money is tight, lend when you can't afford to? A chart can give those habits a name, and naming a habit is the first step to changing it.
- **Ask a qualified, independent adviser.** Ask how they're paid, what they would earn from what they recommend, and who regulates them, then check that with the regulator yourself rather than through a link they send you.
- **Give yourself time.** Pressure to act today is a warning sign with money, as it is with astrology. An offer that can't wait for you to think it over is one to leave.
- **Read the season, not the outcome.** If you'd like astrology to have a part, let a transit through your 2nd house be the prompt to look at your accounts, your habits and what security means to you, never the signal to buy.

And if money worries are keeping you awake, the worry deserves help as much as the money does: [When to see a professional instead](/astrology/when-to-see-a-professional-instead/) says where to find it.
