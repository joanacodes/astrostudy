Almost everyone meets this square once. For about two and a half years, the part of you that pushes towards a goal and the part that dissolves goals into something larger pull at right angles, and effort can feel like walking in fog.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) asserts, competes, defends and carries things out. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) loosens boundaries and asks what all the effort is for. The [square](/astrology/square/) between them is a constructive friction, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) neither cancels the other: drive keeps going, but it has to adapt to a sense that the old aims are no longer quite yours.

Motivation can come and go. Plans built on willpower alone stall, competition loses its taste, and a goal you'd chased for years may suddenly look beside the point. You may find yourself fighting someone else's battle out of sympathy, or unable to say where your limits are until they've been crossed. Neptune has a familiar blind spot here: letting go of an aim can feel like helplessness, and it isn't. The friction is asking for aims that are smaller, truer and closer to hand.

If what drops is your energy rather than your motivation, or low spirits stay for weeks, see a doctor or a therapist; I don't read health in a chart ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/), [astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

With Mars in your 7th, the square often arrives while Neptune crosses your 10th or your 4th: your public aims, or your sense of home, go soft at the edges while partnerships and rivalries still ask you to stand your ground ([Neptune transiting the 10th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-10th-house/)). The 7th, the 10th and the 4th are cusps drawn from the minute you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Born with [Mars square Neptune](/astrology/mars-square-neptune/), you know this friction from the inside; the transit gives everyone a stretch of it, with a beginning and an end.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

About two years and seven months, rarely less than twenty-two, is how long Neptune holds the square with my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after. That's a long time to run on low certainty, so it helps to know that the exact square comes three times in most passes, and five in about one in four, rather than pressing evenly throughout.

From 2026 to 2039 Neptune is in Aries, and from there it squares Mars in Cancer and Mars in Capricorn: the sign of Mars's fall and the sign of its exaltation, in the tradition. The same transit, in the same years, meets the planet where the old books thought it weakest and where they thought it strongest ([Mars in Cancer](/astrology/mars-in-cancer/), [Mars in Capricorn](/astrology/mars-in-capricorn/)). It comes once to almost everyone born between 1930 and 2009, and twice to very few.

If the square comes early in your life, the next contact is [transiting Neptune trine natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-mars/) or [transiting Neptune sextile natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-mars/), about fourteen years on; [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-mars/) or [transiting Neptune opposite natal Mars](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-mars/) lies about forty-one years from it, on one side or the other.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Shorten the horizon. When the big goal blurs, pick the next useful thing you can finish this week, and finish it.

Say where your limits are before they're tested. Under this square, a boundary named in advance holds better than one defended in the moment.

Rest without calling it failure. Effort comes back in waves with this transit, and the pauses between them are part of the work.
