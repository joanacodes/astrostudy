Pisces on the 3rd house cusp hands the everyday mind to the sign of images and feeling. Talk, messages, errands and early lessons all take on a softer, more intuitive manner, and you tend to hear what people mean before you've worked out what they said.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/) is the near world: what you say and hear, read and write, the people you grew up beside and the streets you walk every week ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). Its natural sign is Gemini and its natural planet Mercury, and [Pisces](/astrology/pisces/) is the one sign where Mercury is both in detriment and in fall. Your thinking runs on pictures, tones and moods, and the words follow after. You may remember how something was said long after the words are gone, learn a song or a language by ear before you could explain its rules, and find that a conversation drifts somewhere unexpected and true. Brothers and sisters, where there are any, can share a wordless understanding with you, and the neighbourhood is an atmosphere as much as an address. What needs care is the detail: the reply you meant to send, the date you didn't write down, the message read too fast.

Your everyday mind has a dreamer and a teacher for rulers, Neptune and Jupiter, and I read both. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) shows where your thoughts drift and imagine, where intuition does the work of argument; [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) shows where you learn generously and widely, and where a lesson becomes a belief. At London every chart with this cusp has [Sagittarius rising](/astrology/sagittarius-rising/), and at New York two in three: Jupiter then rules your whole chart as well as your talk, often your 12th too, so the way you come across and the way you think are of a piece, broad, warm and a little unfocused. Their houses show where your mind wanders to and what it brings back, as ruler of the 3rd in the [1st](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

Pisces lands on the 3rd cusp in about 1 London birth in 16 and 1 New York birth in 15 under Placidus, the house system I use. Pisces rises fast in the north, and the cusps below the eastern horizon share in that speed, a little less so this far from the Ascendant. Libra holds the Midheaven in about 9 charts in 10. The sign before Pisces, Aquarius, is often left without a cusp, tucked whole inside the 2nd house in about 3 London charts in 5 ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)), so your 2nd opens in Capricorn and holds Aquarius as well. Pisces can be left out in the same way, with no cusp at all, in about 1 London birth in 6.

Two hours or so either way and Pisces would sit on a neighbouring cusp, the 2nd if you'd been born later, the IC if earlier ([Pisces on the 2nd house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-2nd-house-cusp/); [Pisces on the 4th house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-4th-house-cusp/)), which is why the birth time comes before anything else I say ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
