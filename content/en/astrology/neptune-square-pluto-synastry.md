One of them wants to keep the dream; the other wants to know what is rotten underneath it. Neptune square Pluto in synastry sets one person's Neptune about 90° from the other's Pluto, within 3.4°, and in most pairs my data holds, the Pluto is the elder's.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) carries ideals and empathy, the capacity to sense meaning and to let rigid lines dissolve. [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) carries the drive to transform: to face what is hidden, end what is obsolete and rebuild from what is sound. A [square](/astrology/square/) puts two functions at odds in a way that forces work; among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), this one sets an ideal against a clearing-out, held by two people of different ages.

The two signs usually belong to one modality. With Pluto in Virgo for a birth of the mid-1960s and Neptune in Sagittarius for births of the late 1970s, both mutable, a drive to fix how things work meets an ideal of meaning and wider horizons.

What the friction can do is honest work on both sides: ideals that survive a hard look, and renewal that keeps its compassion.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

If the Neptune is yours, the other's push to strip things to the core can feel like a threat to what you believe in, or an invitation to disillusion. You may soften the facts to protect the hope, or drift away from the argument altogether.

If the Pluto is yours, the other's ideals can look like avoidance, a mist over something that needs doing. You may press harder, and mistake their gentleness for weakness. This is friction between two ways of caring, and neither person has failed the other.

- **[Neptune conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-conjunct-pluto-synastry/):** dream and depth on one degree, with the Neptune always the elder's.
- **[Neptune sextile Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-sextile-pluto-synastry/):** an opening between ideal and renewal, most often between near-contemporaries.
- **[Neptune trine Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-trine-pluto-synastry/):** ideal and depth flowing together across about a generation.
- **[Neptune opposite Pluto in synastry](/astrology/neptune-opposite-pluto-synastry/):** the two at opposite ends of the zodiac, between people more than fifty years apart.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Name what each of you is protecting. The Neptune person usually guards a hope, the Pluto person a truth, and both can be honoured. Between an adult child and a parent, it may be a family story one wants to keep and the other to examine; between colleagues, a mission statement and an audit; between partners, a dream for the future and the work it will take.

Neptune with Pluto makes the slowest contact on the site, so who has it is written in the birth years. Someone born in mid-1965, with Pluto in Virgo, has it with anyone born, on and off, from January 1975 to November 1978, whose Neptune in Sagittarius squares it; the same birth's Neptune in Scorpio squares the Pluto of anyone born, on and off, from September 1947 to May 1953. In most of my examples the Pluto belongs to the elder, by about ten to twenty-four years; the rest set the Neptune of someone more than sixty years older against a young adult's Pluto. Shared by years of births on each side, it stays out of the contacts I count, which always include the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, the Ascendant or the Midheaven; through the houses, I read it as two eras disagreeing about what is worth saving.

Neither body cares about the hour, but the houses do: whether the Pluto falls in the Neptune person's 9th house, where their beliefs live, or their 10th, where their work is seen, needs both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

No birth I counted has [Neptune square Pluto](/astrology/neptune-square-pluto/) inside one chart. Between two, it asks a hope and a hard truth to share a table, and I set that table only for two people who both want it ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
