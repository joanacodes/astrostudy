Sometimes you finish each other's sentences, and sometimes you finish them differently from how they were meant. Mercury conjunct Neptune in synastry places one person's Mercury on the other's Neptune, and words between you travel through imagination as much as through meaning. I read it from both chairs, and with care, because it is one of the more intimate contacts two people can share.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) is how a person names things, reasons and passes on what they know. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) is how they imagine, empathise and sense the meaning behind the facts: inspiration, compassion, a feel for symbols, an ease with what can't be pinned down. With a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/), two functions bind into one active unit, each still recognisable, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) few bind a voice so closely to someone else's imagination.

In conversation, much goes unsaid and is understood anyway. The Mercury person describes their day and the Neptune person hears what it felt like; the Neptune person speaks in images and the Mercury person finds they know exactly what was meant. Decisions can be made on a shared sense of rightness rather than a list of reasons. Explaining yourselves feels almost unnecessary, which is the pleasure of it and, as you'll see, the catch.

On the Mercury side, you may feel listened to as never before: your words seem to arrive with all their meaning intact, and sometimes with more than you put in. On the Neptune side, you may feel your intuitions finally have a spokesperson, someone who can say the thing you sensed but couldn't phrase.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A conjunction blurs, and here it blurs what was said with what was heard. Each of you can hear a little of your own hope in the other's words. The Mercury person makes a passing remark and the Neptune person treasures a meaning that wasn't intended; the Neptune person leaves something open and the Mercury person fills it in with their own version. Practical details slip: dates, money, who agreed to ring whom. Idealisation isn't delusion, but it does need a reality check now and then.

Neptune spends about fourteen years in each sign, so the Neptune person's Neptune sits close to that of their whole age group. It's the Mercury resting on it that turns a generation's placement into a conversation between two people. As a natal aspect, [Mercury conjunct Neptune](/astrology/mercury-conjunct-neptune/) describes one person thinking in pictures; here one of you supplies the pictures and the other the words.

Mercury and Neptune at their four other angles:

- **[Mercury sextile Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mercury-sextile-neptune-synastry/):** an opening; imagination enters the conversation when one of you invites it.
- **[Mercury square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mercury-square-neptune-synastry/):** precise words and impressions at right angles, where the gap between said and meant becomes the subject.
- **[Mercury trine Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mercury-trine-neptune-synastry/):** an easy current in one element; the imagination is shared without the merger.
- **[Mercury opposite Neptune in synastry](/astrology/mercury-opposite-neptune-synastry/):** the literal and the imagined on two sides of the table, each with its own champion.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Keep two languages. Let some conversations wander, dreamy and half-worded, and keep others plain: dates, money and promises, said simply and written down. After a decision, each of you can say back in one sentence what you think was agreed. In a couple, that protects a very tender way of talking; between friends, it keeps the one who always knows what you meant from guessing wrong; between colleagues, it lets a creative partnership also meet its deadlines; between relatives, it puts into words what has always gone without saying.

About 1 pair of people in 14 has this conjunction, one way round or the other, within 6°. The date settles most of it; the times settle where it lives. Falling in the Mercury person's 3rd house, the house of everyday talk, it is read with [their Neptune in your 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-in-3rd-house-synastry/), and that needs both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A contact this close to someone's inner life is also one I read only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
