Venus square Neptune sets your sense of what's worth having against your longing for something more. It tends to make love and beauty feel immense, and then asks you to meet the people and things you love as they actually are.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs and chooses: attraction, taste, the fair exchange that keeps a bond honest. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) dissolves and idealises: compassion, imagination, the longing for something beyond the everyday. Under a [square](/astrology/square/), the planet that discerns and the planet that dissolves keep testing each other, and both come out wiser.

In plain words, the picture and the person don't always match. You may fall for potential, see someone as they could be and wait for them to become it, or give more than you get back because the giving itself feels like love. Disappointment, when it comes, is usually the ideal meeting the real, not proof that you chose badly. The square's gift arrives as you adjust: tenderness that sees clearly, compassion that keeps a boundary, an imagination that can still dream after it's been surprised. Idealising isn't delusion, and nothing here describes being deceived.

In readings I go slowly with this square and ask about the real people first ([how I read](/how-i-read/)). And if longing or disappointment ever weighs more than a chart can hold, that's for a therapist rather than a reading ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Usually the two planets share a modality, three signs apart. With Venus in Gemini and Neptune in Virgo or Pisces, three mutable signs, curiosity and dreaming trade places, and you may love several possibilities at once; with Venus in Leo and Neptune in Scorpio, both fixed, a warm wish to be seen meets a longing that goes deep and stays. Neptune's sign belongs to a generation, so the house shows where the square is felt: Venus in the 2nd square Neptune in the 11th might let a love of beauty and a dream shared with friends pull in different directions.

About 1 person in 15 is born with Venus and Neptune within 6° of a square. Exact or nearly, the gap between ideal and real is a lifelong teacher; at the edge of the orb, it shows mainly when you fall for something.

The pair's other meetings:

- **[Venus conjunct Neptune](/astrology/venus-conjunct-neptune/):** the two in one sign; loving and imagining fused, beautiful and hard to tell apart.
- **[Venus sextile Neptune](/astrology/venus-sextile-neptune/):** imagination and taste cooperating, once you give the dream a form.
- **[Venus trine Neptune](/astrology/venus-trine-neptune/):** the two in one element; an easy kindness that lets love see generously.
- **[Venus opposite Neptune](/astrology/venus-opposite-neptune/):** the everyday at one end, the dream at the other; balance comes from welcoming both.

Between two charts, [Venus square Neptune in synastry](/astrology/venus-square-neptune-synastry/) can feel enchanting and confusing in turn, as one person's taste meets the other's dream at an angle. It's worth asking, from both sides, who is being seen and who is being imagined, so I read both charts.

## In transits {#in-transits}

In [transits](/astrology/transits/), [Transiting Neptune square natal Venus](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-venus/) comes at most once in most lifetimes and stays for a year or two: a stretch when what you love, and what you find beautiful, can blur before it clears. For anyone born with this square, the [Neptune square](/astrology/neptune-square/) around 41 comes with a twist: Neptune in the sky then crosses your Venus or opposes it, depending on which side of Neptune your Venus sits.

Weather, not a forecast: a soft-focus season that clears in its own time.
