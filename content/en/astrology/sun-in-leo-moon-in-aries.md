Both of the Sun's places of honour are filled in this chart: its own sign holds your Sun, and the sign of its exaltation holds your Moon. The full moon had come and gone a few days before your birth, the light already thinning, and I read the pair as a steady flame with a quick spark underneath it.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Leo and Aries are both fire, and counted from the Sun, the Moon stands four signs back: a trine by sign, the elements in agreement. Their tempos differ. Leo is fixed and tends a fire once it's lit; Aries is cardinal and lights new ones. The [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) wants to make something whole and stand behind it, while the [Moon in Aries](/astrology/moon-in-aries/) settles a feeling by acting on it, fast. Day to day, your needs supply the spark and your sense of self keeps it burning, which makes for a courage that outlasts the first burst.

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) rules Leo, so your Sun answers to no other planet: tradition calls this its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/). Aries gives the Moon no dignity, yet it is the Sun's [exaltation](/astrology/exaltation/), so even your Moon stands on ground that honours the Sun. Its ruler is [Mars](/astrology/mars/), and Mars's sign and house show where your feelings go once they're up: into a sport, a cause, a deadline.

Measured forward from the Sun, the Moon had travelled 211° to 269°, leaving it 91° to 149° short of the Sun again, between day 17 and day 22 of the lunar month. Close to seven in eight of these births belong to the [waning gibbous (disseminating) moon](/astrology/waning-gibbous-disseminating-moon/) in the eight-phase count and the rest to the [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) phase; where the Moon trailed by about 120°, the lights also make a waning [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). I read a disseminating birth as knowing that wants to be shared, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) sets it in the whole cycle.

One person in 137 or so has this pair. Since the Moon moves on roughly every two and a half days, a birthday on which it was leaving Pisces or crossing into Taurus has two candidates, and only the time chooses between this page and the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-pisces/) or the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-taurus/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). That same time gives the rising sign, and with the two lights it draws a chart's first outline, which is why I wait for it before I read anything.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love you lead with warmth and follow with action. The Leo Sun wants to be someone's particular person; the Aries Moon needs things said straight and settled now. A flare passes quickly for the Moon, while a bruise to the Sun's pride can take longer to cool, so a quarrel may be over for one part of you and not for the other. Asking *it's done for me, is it done for you?* closes that gap. Partners tend to feel defended and celebrated; what you need back is candour, and to be noticed for what you began.

## At work {#at-work}

You can start a project and also stay with it, which is rarer than it sounds. The Aries Moon is at its best in the first rush, a launch, a rescue, a deadline met against the odds; the Leo Sun gives what you begin a name and a standard, and doesn't drop it once the novelty fades. Waiting for permission wears on both lights, and so does watching someone else take the credit for your initiative.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

There's heat on both sides and little in the pair to cool it. A slight lands on the Aries Moon as an instant flash and on the Leo Sun as a wound to pride, and together they can answer more sharply than the moment deserves. Rest is the other gap, because neither sign asks for it. What helps is a pause between the flash and the reply, a walk round the block or a night's sleep, and the habit of saying plainly what hurt rather than proving that nothing did.
