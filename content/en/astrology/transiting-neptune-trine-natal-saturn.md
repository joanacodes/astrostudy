The trine is the gentlest way Neptune and Saturn can meet in your chart. For about two and a half years patience and imagination pull in the same direction, and something you care about can be given a form that lasts.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) wants durable order: work done well, commitments kept, foundations that survive time. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) wants meaning beyond the measurable: compassion, imagination, a sense of the whole. In a [trine](/astrology/trine/) two functions support each other without losing themselves, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) structure can serve an ideal, and the ideal can accept structure, without either feeling compromised.

It often shows as a craft learned slowly, a vocation that starts to make sense, care for someone organised so that it can go on, a spiritual or artistic practice with regular hours. Duties can feel lighter because their purpose is clearer, and dreams more reachable because they finally have steps. Saturn's fear of wasted effort eases, since the effort now has a reason. Ease isn't inevitability: the trine makes the building easier, and the building is still yours to do.

With Saturn in your 10th, the trine often comes while Neptune crosses your 2nd or your 6th: what you value and how you use what you have ([Neptune transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-2nd-house/)), or the shape of your daily work, gives your public responsibilities a gentler purpose. Each of these houses depends on the time you were born ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

[Saturn trine Neptune](/astrology/saturn-trine-neptune/) marks runs of births between 1930 and 2002, for whom this cooperation is a lifelong habit; the transit offers a season of it to anyone.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine holds for about two years and seven months and at least twenty-two, long enough for a new habit to become part of who you are. Most passes bring the trine to exactness three times; about one in four brings it there five times.

Before 80 it comes to about 6 people in 7 born between 1930 and 2009, and to some of them twice. The site's count puts it at about 14 for someone born at the start of 1945, 17 for 2005, 19 for 1935, 37 for 1985, 42 for 1975, 64 for 1955 and 73½ for 1995. For a start-of-1965 birth it doesn't come before 80, though [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-saturn/) does, at about 46.

Someone born at the start of 1975 meets [transiting Neptune opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-opposite-natal-saturn/) at about 15, this trine at about 42, [transiting Neptune square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-saturn/) at about 56 and [transiting Neptune sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-saturn/) at about 69½; the conjunction doesn't come for them before 80.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one thing worth building slowly and give it regular hours. The trine rewards consistency more than intensity.

Let a duty serve something you love. Under this transit, the work you owe and the work you care about can often be the same work.

Write down what you'd like to have built by the end of the transit, and look at the list each season.
