Once in about twelve years, Jupiter reaches the degree that was rising when you were born, and for about a year it crosses your 1st house. The growth it stands for starts with you: your manner, your presence, the way you begin things and the way people first meet you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is the part of a chart that widens what you understand and helps experience add up to something. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is identity and presence: the self you lead with, before anyone knows the rest. Together they describe a year of room. You may want to try a broader version of yourself, change the way you present it, or start something with your own name on it, and the 1st house is where people watch that happen.

The stay opens at an angle. In Placidus, the house system I use, the 1st house begins at the Ascendant, so its first weeks are Jupiter crossing your rising degree ([transiting Jupiter conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)), the stretch people tend to feel most, as a wish to begin. Any planet you were born with in the 1st comes next, each one a shorter accent inside the year.

None of this arrives from outside. [Transits](/astrology/transits/) bring out what a chart already holds, and this one gives your own way of being more space; it doesn't hand you luck or a confidence you didn't have. Tradition called Jupiter a benefic, and I read the word as an open door, never a promise about what's behind it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In charts drawn at London's latitude, in Placidus, Jupiter usually stays about a year in a house, with most stays between about 11 months and nearly 2 years. The 1st matches that in the middle and runs a touch longer at the far end, up to about 2 years: that far north, the sign rising can make a 1st house very wide or very narrow. At New York's latitude, where the houses come out more even, the middle is a little over a year and the long end about 22 months.

Your Ascendant decides when the stay starts, and the size of your 1st house when it ends. Both come from your birth time and birthplace, which is why I don't read a chart without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Before reaching your Ascendant, Jupiter spent a year or so in the quiet house behind it ([Jupiter transiting the 12th house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-12th-house/)); after the 1st it moves on to what you have and value ([Jupiter transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-2nd-house/)).

Your rising sign gives you the window. Jupiter is in Leo from June 2026 to July 2027, so a Leo Ascendant gets its crossing now or in the months ahead. Virgo rising has it between July 2027 and August 2028, Libra rising between August 2028 and September 2029, Scorpio rising between September 2029 and October 2030, Sagittarius rising between October 2030 and November 2031. For Capricorn rising it falls between November 2031 and November 2032, and for Aquarius rising between April 2032 and December 2033, since Jupiter goes back and forth between those two signs in 2032. Pisces rising follows (April 2033 to April 2034), then Aries rising (April 2034 to April 2035), Taurus rising (April 2035 to May 2036), Gemini rising (May 2036 to May 2037) and Cancer rising (May 2037 to June 2038). Jupiter left Cancer in June 2026, so a Cancer Ascendant has already been crossed, and Jupiter may still be inside that 1st house.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Start one thing you'd like to still be doing when Jupiter comes back to your Ascendant, twelve years or so from now.

Let people see the version of you that's growing, unfinished as it is: the 1st house is public by nature, and with Jupiter here the trying matters more than the arriving.

Watch what you say yes to. Jupiter widens whatever it touches, and in the house of the self that can mean taking on a bigger role than you want to carry. Ask what you're growing into, and whether it's yours. Born with [Jupiter in the 1st house](/astrology/jupiter-in-1st-house/)? Then this stay brings Jupiter back over its own place, your [Jupiter return](/astrology/jupiter-return/), and the year is a good one for looking at what that openness has given you so far.
