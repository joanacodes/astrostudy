The 12th house holds the inner life: retreat, reflection, solitude, the things done without an audience. A composite Sun there gives the relationship a private centre, and the bond's most important life goes on where others rarely look.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

[The 12th house](/astrology/12th-house/) of [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) ends at the composite [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/), one of the most time-sensitive points there is: in each natal chart the Ascendant moves about a degree every four minutes on average. A few minutes' error in either birth time moves the composite one too ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)), and it can be found in two ways, as the midpoint of your two Ascendants or worked out from the composite Midheaven for the place where you live. A [Sun](/astrology/sun/) just above it may belong to the 12th or, by the other method or a slightly different time, to the [composite Sun in the 1st house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-1st-house/).

The inner life is the 12th house's realm. A relationship like this tends to be a refuge: the place where each of you can be quiet, unguarded, unproductive, and two people who find rest hard alone can find it easy together. It may centre on a shared practice, meditation, prayer, music played only for each other, or on compassion offered without fuss, the care of someone who needs it, work done behind the scenes. It's revived by retreat, and its purpose often has to do with letting go of what neither of you needs to carry.

Saturn, by tradition, rejoiced in the 12th, the cadent house of Pisces that tradition called the Bad Spirit, a label that says nothing about your bond ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). Alone in a birth chart, the [Sun in the 12th house](/astrology/sun-in-12th-house/) speaks of one person's need for retreat; across two charts, [their Sun in your 12th house](/astrology/sun-in-12th-house-synastry/) speaks of someone who reaches the other's inner life. Shared between two, the inner life can become the quiet heart of an otherwise busy, sociable pair.

Tradition also gave the 12th confinement, and I never read it that way: a composite Sun here predicts no illness, hospital or confinement for either of you ([astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)). The difficulty is visibility. A bond lived mostly in private can go unacknowledged by family and friends, and it can withdraw further than is good for it. Privacy isn't secrecy, though; letting one or two people see the relationship as it is, and naming between you what usually goes unsaid, keeps the quiet from turning into distance.

Before it, the [composite Sun in the 11th house](/astrology/composite-sun-in-11th-house/) gathers a bond around friends and shared hopes. Composites sit outside my own work, and those who use them read them with both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/), never as a forecast of what will become of a relationship. A bond that guards its privacy would want its birth data guarded too: a composite is drawn only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
