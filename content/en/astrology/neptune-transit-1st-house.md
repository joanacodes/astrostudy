Neptune usually needs well over a decade to cross the house that begins at your Ascendant, and what it does there is soften the outline: how sure you are of who you are, and how clearly other people think they see you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) imagines and dissolves: it loosens boundaries that are no longer needed and lets in what can't quite be named. The [1st house](/astrology/1st-house/) is identity, presence and the body you meet the world in. Over these years a self-definition that once felt fixed tends to blur at the edges. You may be less certain of who you are and more open to who you could be; your manner can turn gentler, more receptive, harder to pin down; people may see in you what they hope to see. The same openness can make you kinder, more intuitive and more inventive about how you show up.

The stay opens on your rising degree itself, which Placidus, my house system, uses as the 1st's cusp ([transiting Neptune conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-ascendant/)): often the stretch when your sense of yourself feels most like mist. Planets born in your 1st meet Neptune one at a time after that; with Mars there, drive can soften into something more willing to follow an inspiration than a plan.

This isn't a loss of self, and nobody is fooling you. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) works with what the chart already holds: Neptune here lends the person your chart describes more imagination about who they might become, and asks for a little more care in saying it plainly. Anything about your body belongs with a doctor, never with a transit.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Neptune's stays in the 1st are among the longest figures in a chart: nearly thirteen years in the middle, in Placidus charts drawn for London, and anywhere from nearly nine years to nearly twenty-six for most of them, against a middle of nearly fourteen for all houses there. The spread is that wide because the hour of birth decides how much of the zodiac the 1st takes up, and at London's latitude that can be a little or a great deal. A New York birth puts the middle at nearly fourteen, with most stays between nearly ten and nearly twenty-two.

Counted from the first touch of your Ascendant to Neptune's last day in the 1st, with its slow retrograde returns over the cusp inside the same stay, every one of these years rests on your birth time: the Ascendant moves on with every few minutes of the clock, so without the time there's nothing to date and no reading from me ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Neptune comes to the 1st from your 12th, where its long stay was spent on the inner life ([Neptune transiting the 12th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-12th-house/)), and moves on, many years later, to your 2nd and what you value ([Neptune transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-2nd-house/)).

Aries rising, this is your era. Neptune first entered Aries in March 2025, slipped back into Pisces, and has been in Aries since January 2026; it stays until March 2039, so an Aries Ascendant is crossed somewhere in those years, the early degrees first. A Pisces Ascendant was crossed between April 2011 and January 2026, and the stay in that 1st may not be over yet. Taurus rising starts to be reached from May 2038. For any other rising sign, the crossing came before 2011 or comes after 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say who you are in plain words now and then, even when the answer has changed. Neptune in the 1st makes it easy to let other people's pictures of you stand in for your own.

Use the softness. Years when your outline blurs suit trying a more creative way of being seen, through an art, a practice or a gentler manner, without having to defend it as final.

Agree to things with a clear head and, where it matters, in writing. Being more open is a gift; being easy to persuade is a different one, and not always a gift.
