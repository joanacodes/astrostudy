Virgo on the 2nd house cusp hands the house of earnings and possessions to the sign of craft. What you have tends to come from what you can do, and you like to know exactly how much of it there is.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) brings to the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/) the habits of a good workshop, and [house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/) explains why the sign on a cusp counts for so much: tools kept in order, materials not wasted, prices known. I read it as worth measured by competence. You feel most secure when you're good at something people need, and money earned through a skill you've practised means more to you than a windfall. You tend to compare before buying, to repair before replacing, and to spend freely only on what works: a good knife, a reliable bike, the course that teaches you something useful. The 2nd's natural sign, Taurus, is earth like Virgo, so the house is at ease here; Taurus enjoys what it owns, while Virgo checks it. The shadow is undervaluing yourself. With this cusp, people often price their work below its worth, or worry about money beyond what the figures say.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules your 2nd house, here from its own sign and its exaltation, and its position shows where your resources are earned and spent: as ruler of the 2nd, in the [1st](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/2nd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/). Two London charts in three with this cusp have [Leo rising](/astrology/leo-rising/), so the Sun rules the chart, and Mercury, never more than about 28° from the Sun, always stands close to it: what you earn stays near the centre of who you are. In the rest, Virgo rises too, and Mercury rules the 1st and the 2nd together. Its other sign, Gemini, opens the 11th in about 7 London charts in 10, so your earnings and the circles you move in often answer to the same planet.

Placidus, the house system I read with, gives Virgo the 2nd cusp in about 1 birth in 10, at London as at New York. Like Leo before it, Virgo is one of the four slowest signs to rise in the north, and the cusps either side of the Ascendant hold it a long time each day, some 150 minutes on this one at London. Virgo opens the 3rd as well in about 1 London chart in 5 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)), and on other days it opens no house at all: it's intercepted in about 1 London birth in 6 and 1 New York birth in 8 ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

A time remembered as "about nine in the morning" isn't enough here. [Virgo rising](/astrology/virgo-rising/) and [Virgo on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-3rd-house-cusp/) are each a short step away, one later, one earlier, and only the recorded minute says which chart is yours ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

And the cusp sets no figure. I read the 2nd for how you earn, keep and value, and leave amounts and investments well alone ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).
