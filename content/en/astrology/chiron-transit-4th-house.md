Some hurts are older than memory and live in the house you grew up in. When Chiron reaches the IC and enters your 4th house, those are the ones it finds.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is the wounded healer: the hurt you keep coming back to, and the care you learn because of it. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/) is home and roots: the family you came from, the place you live, the private self, and your sense of where you belong. With Chiron passing through, an old ache about belonging can come back to mind: a home that didn't feel safe or didn't feel yours, the sense of being the odd one out in your family, a worry you carried for someone older long before it was yours to carry. These years let you look at that story as an adult. Often the person who knows this hurt well becomes the one who makes a home feel welcoming for others, and this stay can show you how much of that skill you already have.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the 4th begins at the IC, so the stay opens with Chiron on that point; a natal Moon in the 4th, met later, is asked what you need in order to feel at home in yourself.

Chiron here predicts nothing about your parents' health or lives, and nothing about where you will live. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) touches what your own chart describes, which in the 4th means your relationship with your origins, looked at with more tenderness than it may have had.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 4th is Chiron's most typical house. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the middle is about 4 years and most stays fall between about 2 and 9, almost exactly the figures for all twelve houses together; New York's latitude gives the same. Chiron's stretched orbit still means some signs pass quickly and others slowly, so your own stay could sit at either end of that range.

Your IC opens the stay and the cusp of your 5th closes it, and both come from your birth time; without it I can't say when this begins, and I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Chiron arrives from the house of words and learning ([Chiron transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-3rd-house/)) and goes on to the house of play, pleasure and creation ([Chiron transiting the 5th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-5th-house/)).

With your IC in Aries, the stay falls before April 2027, Chiron having been in Aries since April 2018. With it in Taurus, between June 2026 and May 2034; in Gemini, between July 2033 and April 2039; in Cancer, from July 2038, with Chiron still there at the end of my count in December 2039. The exact degree gives the year.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make one place, however small, that feels like yours. Chiron in the 4th responds to a home you choose, not only the one you were given.

Write down the family rules you grew up with, and mark the ones you still want. Seeing them on paper makes the choice gentler.

Visit, in memory or in person, a place where you once felt at ease, and notice what made it so.

If memories of home are painful, you don't have to sort them alone. Someone you trust, or someone trained to listen, can help you hold them while you understand them.
