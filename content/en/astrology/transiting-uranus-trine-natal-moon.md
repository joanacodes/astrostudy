When Uranus trines your Moon, comfort and change, which usually pull against each other, pull the same way for a while. It happens twice in most lives, each time for about twenty months, and it's easy to miss because nothing about it hurts.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates feeling and attachment, and it adapts: that's part of its work, alongside keeping you safe. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) differentiates and frees. With a [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, two functions cooperate without either losing its character, so the Moon's adaptability gets a long run of easy practice: change arrives, and you settle into it faster than you expected.

Mostly it feels like relief. A routine you'd outgrown slips away without guilt; a household finds a better rhythm; a friendship turns closer, or looser, without anyone being hurt. People often feel more like themselves at home during this trine, because the way they live has quietly caught up with who they are.

The ease reaches beyond home. The Moon is also how you respond when plans change, so under this trine, news you'd normally brace against tends to land softly, and a change of plan is just a change of plan.

Because nothing pushes, the trine can pass unused. It helps whatever you start, and starts nothing.

With the Moon in your 8th and Uranus crossing your 12th ([Uranus transiting the 12th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-12th-house/)), time alone gives closeness more air: insight comes in quiet hours, and trust is easier to give when you have room of your own. The Moon's degree hangs on the hour too: get the birth time two hours wrong and this trine moves by about three months, so I don't read it without the time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

If you were born with [Moon trine Uranus](/astrology/moon-trine-uranus/), this transit is your own temperament reflected back by the sky, for a season.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Allow about twenty months for the trine, counting from 1½° before exact to 1° after; a fast pass, about one in ten, can be through in eleven. In more than nine passes out of ten the trine has three exact peaks rather than one, as Uranus loops back over the point.

The two trines sit at the wide points of Uranus's round with your Moon. After [transiting Uranus conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-conjunct-natal-moon/), [transiting Uranus sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-moon/) and [transiting Uranus square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-moon/) come first; the first trine follows at about 28 years, [transiting Uranus opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-moon/) at about 42, and the second trine at about 56. Two trines by 80 is the usual count for births from 1930 to 2009, one the lowest, which makes this [transit](/astrology/transits/) a fair-weather season most people get twice.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Make the change at home you keep postponing. Under this trine, what you rearrange tends to stay rearranged, and the people around you adjust with you.

Let someone new become familiar. With Uranus trine the Moon, it's easier to feel at ease with people and places that weren't yours before.

Write down what got easier. Trine seasons are forgotten quickly, and knowing what helped you settle is worth keeping for the harder ones.
