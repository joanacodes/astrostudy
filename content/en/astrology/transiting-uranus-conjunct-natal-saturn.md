Aquarius has two rulers, Saturn for the tradition and Uranus for modern tropical astrology, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). When Uranus in the sky reaches your natal Saturn, once in a life at most, the two sit on one degree of your chart for about twenty months, and the rules you live by get rewritten from the inside.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) establishes structure: responsibility, boundaries, the long work that holds a life together. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) reforms structures by testing alternatives to them. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) co-activates two functions into one process, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) stability and change stop taking turns and start working as one.

What changes is usually a rule rather than a whole life: a duty you took on because it was expected, a routine that once protected you from chaos and now only protects itself, a way of being responsible that was never quite yours. The transit asks you to rebuild it on your own terms, so the structure stays and the reason for it changes.

Saturn fears chaos and wasted effort; Uranus can change a system before it understands what still works. Together they can produce either a sudden clear-out or a tighter grip on the old order. The work I'd point to lies between the two: keep the walls that bear weight, and move the ones that only divide rooms.

With Saturn in your 4th, Uranus crosses it there ([Uranus transiting the 4th house](/astrology/uranus-transit-4th-house/)): the way a household runs, who carries which duty at home, the shape of family obligation are the structures that get redrawn. Placing that house takes your birth time, as every house does, and without it I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Natal Saturn was one of the placements that held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/)), which is why I slow down when I reach it. That figure belongs to the birth chart: it says how often Saturn's sign and house rang true, not how a transit to Saturn will go.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within my orbs, 1½° before exact to 1° after, Uranus stays on your Saturn for about twenty months, eleven at the shortest: more than twice the nine months or so that Saturn's own transits usually take. In more than nine passes out of ten it crosses the exact degree three times.

The sky sets the date: Saturn and Uranus met in 1942 and 1988, and meet again in 2032. Born soon after one of those meetings, with Saturn a little ahead of Uranus, you meet the transit early, at about 5 for someone born at the start of 1945; born soon before, Uranus has nearly its whole round to travel, about 79 years for someone born at the start of 1985, and more than 80 for births a year or two nearer a meeting: those make up most of the 1 person in 25, among births from 1930 to 2009, who never meet it. Nobody meets it twice. Born with [Saturn conjunct Uranus](/astrology/saturn-conjunct-uranus/), you had it at birth or soon after, unless your Saturn lay just behind your Uranus.

With Saturn in Gemini, as for most people born between August 2000 and June 2003, it falls while Uranus crosses Gemini, in your mid to late twenties. For births in the first half of 2003 it shares the months of 2031–32 with the first [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/), when Saturn too comes back to that degree.

Round the rest of the circle come [transiting Uranus sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-sextile-natal-saturn/), on average about fourteen years after the conjunction, [transiting Uranus square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-square-natal-saturn/) at about twenty-one, [transiting Uranus trine natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-saturn/) at about twenty-eight and [transiting Uranus opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-uranus-opposite-natal-saturn/) at about forty-two.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List the rules you follow and ask who wrote each one. The ones you'd choose again can stay; the others are what this transit is for.

Reform from inside before you think of leaving. Uranus on Saturn favours change made by someone who knows how the thing works, and in Saturn's house, that's you.

Give the new arrangement time to bear weight. A structure needs a season to prove itself, and twenty months is long enough to see which changes hold.
