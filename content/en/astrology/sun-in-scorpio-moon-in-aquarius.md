Your Sun in Scorpio looks into things; your Moon in Aquarius looks across them. One wants to reach the bottom of a single truth, the other to see how the whole system fits together, and you were born with the Moon close to half lit, about a week into the lunar month.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Aquarius lies three signs on from Scorpio: the two make a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign, fixed water against fixed air, and neither gives ground easily. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) is drawn to what is hidden, essential and true, and wants change that reaches the root; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) is at ease when it belongs to a circle of equals, can see the larger pattern and has a cause that points forward. You may go further into a problem than anyone else, then want the answer to help everyone.

Each light has two rulers, and I read all four. For the Sun, [Mars](/astrology/mars/), from the older books, shows how you act on what you find, and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), its modern ruler, what you are ready to see end. For the Moon, [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional ruler of Aquarius, shows the structures your needs lean on, and [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern one, where they want the freedom to change them. The two modern rulers are both planets of change, Pluto from beneath and Uranus from outside, and with both behind your lights you may find it hard to leave a broken arrangement as it is. My [notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) splits the Moon's two rulers. Saturn sits with Chiron and Lilith among [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/), in 75 to 90% of my checks against about a third for the notebook as a whole; Uranus, checked like any other placement, stayed outside that small group.

The new moon lay five to ten days or so behind, and the Moon was 61° to 119° past the Sun: a fattening crescent, or a half moon standing high at sunset. The births split evenly, the earlier half in the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/), the later in the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/), with an exact waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/) at the 90° seam between them. A first quarter birth, as I read it, turns tension into action in the world; under an Aquarius Moon that action often serves a cause. More on the two phases is on [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/).

Two fixed signs at a square, four rulers between them: about 1 person in 146. On the day the Moon leaves Capricorn for Aquarius, or Aquarius for Pisces, a birth could belong here, to the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-capricorn/) or to the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-pisces/); only the hour of birth can tell ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). Where Saturn and Uranus fall in your houses waits on the same hour, and shows where in your life this Moon asks for room.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You want a bond with no false bottom, and the person in it to be your friend as well. The Scorpio Sun asks for complete honesty and a trust earned slowly; the Aquarius Moon needs equality, conversation and a life of its own beside the shared one. You may be intensely loyal and still need a door that opens outward. A partner who doesn't mistake your need for room for a lack of feeling lets both lights settle.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that digs deep for the sake of many suits you: research, investigation, science, data, psychology, public health, the repair of systems that no longer serve the people in them, any field where hidden causes matter to large numbers of people. The Scorpio Sun finds out what is really going on; the Aquarius Moon wants the finding to alter how things are run. A team of equals brings out your best; a place where information is kept for a few wears you down.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Both lights are in fixed signs, and at a square they can hold their positions against each other for a long time. Intensity and distance may take turns: you go very deep with someone, then step back into ideas, causes or friendships when it gets too close, and the people near you feel the temperature change without knowing why. Name it when it happens. "I need a day to think" is kinder than a silence, and it leaves you a way back.
