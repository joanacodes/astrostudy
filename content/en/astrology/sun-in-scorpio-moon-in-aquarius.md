Your Sun in Scorpio looks into things; your Moon in Aquarius looks across them. One wants to reach the bottom of a single truth, the other to see how the whole system fits together, and you were born with the Moon close to half lit, about a week into the lunar month.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Scorpio is fixed water and Aquarius fixed air, three signs on: a [square](/astrology/square/) by sign, with the steadiness of a fixed sign at each end. The [Sun in Scorpio](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio/) builds its identity around what is hidden, essential and true, and wants change that goes all the way down; the [Moon in Aquarius](/astrology/moon-in-aquarius/) is at ease when it belongs to a circle of equals, can see the larger pattern and has a cause that points forward. You may go further into a problem than anyone else, then want the answer to help everyone.

Each light has two rulers, and I read all four. For the Sun, [Mars](/astrology/mars/), from the older books, shows how you act on what you find, and [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), its modern ruler, what you are ready to see end. For the Moon, [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional ruler of Aquarius, shows the structures your needs lean on, and [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern one, where they want the freedom to change them. The two modern rulers are both planets of change, Pluto from beneath and Uranus from outside, and with both behind your lights you may find it hard to leave a broken arrangement as it is. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), Saturn held 75 to 90% of the time, alongside Chiron and Lilith, when everything I checked came to about a third; Uranus I checked like any other placement, and it was not one of [the three that held](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/).

At your birth the Moon stood 61° to 119° ahead of the Sun, five to ten days or so into the lunar month: a thick crescent, or a half moon high in the sky at sunset. Half these births belong to the [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) and half to the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/), and where the two phases meet, at 90°, sits an exact waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/). I read the first quarter as friction that pushes outward; with an Aquarius Moon, it often pushes towards a cause. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) tells the two phases apart.

Two fixed signs at a square, four rulers between them: about 1 person in 146. On the day the Moon leaves Capricorn for Aquarius, or Aquarius for Pisces, the time of birth decides whether you read this page, the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Capricorn](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-capricorn/) or the [Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-scorpio-moon-in-pisces/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). That hour also places Saturn and Uranus in your houses, and so shows where in your life this Moon asks for room.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You want a bond that goes all the way down, with a partner who is also a friend. The Scorpio Sun asks for complete honesty and a trust earned slowly; the Aquarius Moon needs equality, conversation and a life of its own beside the shared one. You may be intensely loyal and still need a door that opens outward. A partner who doesn't mistake your need for room for a lack of feeling, and never asks you to choose between depth and freedom, lets both lights settle.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that digs deep for the sake of many suits you: research, investigation, science, data, psychology, public health, the reform of systems that have stopped working, any field where hidden causes matter to large numbers of people. The Scorpio Sun finds out what is really going on; the Aquarius Moon wants the finding to change how things are done. You may do your best work in a team of equals, and your least happy where information is kept for a few.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Both lights are in fixed signs, and at a square they can hold their positions against each other for a long time. Intensity and distance may take turns: you go very deep with someone, then step back into ideas, causes or friendships when it gets too close, and the people near you feel the temperature change without knowing why. Name it when it happens. "I need a day to think" is kinder than a silence, and it leaves you a way back.
