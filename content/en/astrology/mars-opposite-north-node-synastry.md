One person's drive lands on the other's home ground. In Mars opposite North Node in synastry, the Mars of one person faces the other's North Node from 180° away, sitting right on their South Node: the place where they're already practised, quick and sure of themselves.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Mars](/astrology/mars/) is how a person gets going and keeps going: first moves, effort, competition, a finish. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) shows where someone is still finding their feet; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/), exactly opposite, where they found them long ago, a resource rather than a fault. Across an [opposition](/astrology/opposition/), Mars faces the growing edge and sits on the home ground, and among the [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) that makes this the contact of shared competence.

On the Node side, being around this person brings out what you already do well. Your established skills get energy and use; you feel capable, quick, recognised. On the Mars side, you meet someone whose practised strengths match your drive, and working or playing together feels efficient and satisfying from the start.

With the South Node in Capricorn, for example, and the Mars on it, the familiar move is to take charge and get things done; the North Node in Cancer, across from the Mars, asks the Node person to give care and home the weight they give achievement, the very thing a busy, capable pair can skip.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Comfort can quietly replace growth. A pair this good at the familiar can keep returning to it, and the Node person's less practised direction, across from where the Mars lands, gets little room. The Mars person may also find that their energy pulls the other back into old roles just as they're trying to leave them.

This isn't a verdict on the South Node: established capacity is not a weakness, and nothing has to be given up. The work is noticing when familiarity is chosen out of habit rather than because it's the best tool.

Measured from the familiar end, the other contacts:

- **[Mars conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-conjunct-north-node-synastry/):** the same drive on the opposite end, pushing towards growth.
- **[Mars sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-sextile-north-node-synastry/):** an opening forward, with an easy road behind.
- **[Mars square North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-square-north-node-synastry/):** the drive at the crossing, between the two ends.
- **[Mars trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/mars-trine-north-node-synastry/):** company on the way to growth, friendly to old strengths.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Enjoy the shared competence, and add one deliberate step towards the less familiar. The Mars person can back the Node person's new aim openly, even when it's slower and less satisfying than what you do well together. Business partners can use the efficiency and still budget time for the unfamiliar; old friends can keep their rituals and add one new one; in a family, siblings can stop casting each other in the roles they had as children.

By the true node, about 1 pair of people in 15 has this opposition, one way round or the other, within 6°. I read the mean node as well; it shadows the true one closely, so the two disagree only for a Mars at the very limit ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)). The South Node's degree, like the North's, belongs to everyone born within a few months of the Node person, the axis backing through a sign in about 18 months; the Mars, set by a birthday, puts these two in particular on the same ground. The houses, which tell where that ground lies, need both times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). There's more on contacts like this in [The nodes in synastry](/astrology/nodes-in-synastry/).

A chart with [Mars opposite North Node](/astrology/mars-opposite-north-node/) in it already knows effort as an old friend. Between two charts, someone else's drive meets that practised ground, and can help point it forward.
