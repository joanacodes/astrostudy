Your Sun thinks in networks, your Moon in conversations. Both lights are air signs, Gemini four on from Aquarius, and the Moon you were born under was a half moon or a little fuller, high in the sky at dusk. The pair, to me, is a mind that keeps connecting people and ideas, and a heart that settles in an exchange.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fixed air and mutable air: a [trine](/astrology/trine/) by sign, the easy relation between signs of one element. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) organises its identity around a system of ideas and the people linked through it, and stays loyal to both; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) settles through exchange, a message sent, a question answered, a comparison that shows how one thing differs from another. The Sun is steady and the Moon quick: you may hold the same convictions for twenty years and change the subject five times in an evening.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Gemini, and it is tied to the Sun, never more than about 28° away: with your Sun in Aquarius it can only be in Capricorn, Aquarius or Pisces. Its sign says a good deal about how this Moon finds words: carefully and in order in Capricorn, in the Sun's own idiom in Aquarius, through images and impressions in Pisces, where tradition gives Mercury its detriment and fall. On your Sun's side I read both rulers: the field where your ideas break new ground belongs to [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern ruler of Aquarius, and the frameworks you give them to [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), its traditional one. Gemini gives the Moon no dignity; Aquarius is the Sun's [detriment](/astrology/detriment/).

The Moon had a lead of 91° to 148° over the Sun: you were born in the second week of the lunar month, seven and a half to twelve days after the new moon. All but the latest of these births, about one in eight, fall in the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) phase, and those latest are [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) births. Around 120° comes a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/), an angle that flows inside a phase that asks you to act, and [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) marks where that phase starts and stops.

The count gives this air trine to about 1 person in 148. On the days the Moon crosses into Gemini from Taurus, or out of it into Cancer, a birth could just as well be the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-taurus/) or the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-cancer/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)); a sign takes the Moon only about two and a half days. The hour is what sets the rising sign and the houses, which show where these exchanges happen: among friends, at work, at home. Mercury and the Sun sign are among the placements everyone reads first, and in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) that group held about a third of the time: a start, then, and I wait for the rest of the chart.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You need someone you can say anything to, who lets you think out loud. The Aquarius Sun wants a friend in a partner, with independence on both sides; the Gemini Moon wants the daily traffic of small news, jokes and questions that tells it the bond is alive. Harder, perhaps, is the wordless kind of closeness, and saying the simplest things: *I missed you*, *I'm scared*. A partner who understands that conversation is your way of holding hands will rarely feel shut out.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that moves information between people suits you: teaching, journalism, research networks, software, community organising, any role where you translate between groups who don't speak each other's language. The Aquarius Sun sees how the whole system could work; the Gemini Moon enjoys the many small exchanges it takes to get there. Isolation wears you down faster than workload, and so does a job where questions are unwelcome.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

With both lights in air, a feeling can stay in the head. You can explain what upset you, put it in context, even make it funny, and still not have let yourself feel it. The fixed Sun can also make your opinions harder to move than your moods, which surprises people who take your quickness for flexibility. What helps is something that isn't words: cooking, swimming, a long walk without your phone, anything that lets a feeling arrive before you've described it.
