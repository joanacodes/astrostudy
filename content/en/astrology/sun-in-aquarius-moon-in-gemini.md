Your Sun thinks in networks, your Moon in conversations. Both lights are in air, four signs apart, and the Moon at your birth was a half moon or a little fuller, high in the sky at dusk. I read the pair as a mind that keeps connecting people and ideas, and a heart that settles in an exchange.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Fixed air and mutable air make a [trine](/astrology/trine/) by sign, the easy relation between signs of one element. The [Sun in Aquarius](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius/) organises its identity around a system of ideas and the people linked through it, and stays loyal to both; the [Moon in Gemini](/astrology/moon-in-gemini/) settles through exchange, a message sent, a question answered, a comparison that shows how one thing differs from another. The Sun is steady and the Moon quick: you may hold the same convictions for twenty years and change the subject five times in an evening.

[Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) rules Gemini, and it never strays more than about 28° from the Sun, so with your Sun in Aquarius it can only be in Capricorn, Aquarius or Pisces. Its sign says a good deal about how this Moon finds words: carefully and in order in Capricorn, in the Sun's own idiom in Aquarius, through images and impressions in Pisces, where tradition gives Mercury its detriment and fall. Aquarius has two rulers, and I read both: [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/), the modern one, for the field where your ideas break new ground, and [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), the traditional one, for the frameworks you give them. The Moon has no dignity in Gemini; the Sun is in its [detriment](/astrology/detriment/) in Aquarius.

When you were born the Moon was 91° to 148° ahead of the Sun, seven and a half to twelve days into the month. Seven of these births in eight fall in the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) phase and the last few in the [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/); around 120° the lights are in a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/), the same easy angle by degree as by sign. A trine inside a first quarter phase is a mixed message I like: the angle flows, and the phase still asks you to act on what you think. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) shows where each phase begins.

The count gives this air trine to about 1 person in 148. Since the Moon covers a sign in about two and a half days, a birth on the day it moved from Taurus into Gemini, or from Gemini into Cancer, could just as well be the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Taurus](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-taurus/) or the [Sun in Aquarius, Moon in Cancer](/astrology/sun-in-aquarius-moon-in-cancer/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). The hour also fixes the rising sign and the houses, which show where these exchanges happen: among friends, at work, at home. In [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), the Sun sign and Mercury, two of the placements everyone reads first, held about a third of the time, so I treat them as a start and wait for the rest of the chart.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You need a partner you can talk to about anything, and who lets you think out loud. The Aquarius Sun wants a friend in a partner, with independence on both sides; the Gemini Moon wants the daily traffic of small news, jokes and questions that tells it the bond is alive. Harder, perhaps, is the wordless kind of closeness, and saying the simplest things: *I missed you*, *I'm scared*. A partner who understands that conversation is your way of holding hands will rarely feel shut out.

## At work {#at-work}

Work that moves information between people suits you: teaching, journalism, research networks, software, community organising, any role where you translate between groups who don't speak each other's language. The Aquarius Sun sees how the whole system could work; the Gemini Moon enjoys the many small exchanges it takes to get there. Isolation wears you down faster than workload, and so does a job where questions are unwelcome.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two air signs can keep a feeling in the head. You can explain what upset you, put it in context, even make it funny, and still not have let yourself feel it. The fixed Sun can also make your opinions harder to move than your moods, which surprises people who take your quickness for flexibility. What helps is something that isn't words: cooking, swimming, a long walk without your phone, anything that lets a feeling arrive before you've described it.
