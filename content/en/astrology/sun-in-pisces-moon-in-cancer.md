Your Moon sits four signs on from your Sun, in Cancer, the only sign it rules, and both lights are in water. You were born with the Moon just beyond its first quarter, high at nightfall. I read the pair as compassion that reaches widely and a heart that knows how to make a home for it.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Mutable water and cardinal water: a [trine](/astrology/trine/) by sign, an easy flow between two signs of one element. The [Sun in Pisces](/astrology/sun-in-pisces/) shapes a self around compassion, imagination and whatever it gives itself to; the [Moon in Cancer](/astrology/moon-in-cancer/) feels safe once it has gathered its people under one roof and made sure they're fed. One light reaches out to the whole world's sorrows, the other brings a few people home. You may cry at the news and then cook for six.

In Cancer, its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/), the Moon is its own ruler, so its house and aspects tell its story directly. For your Sun I weigh both rulers of Pisces, and I read both: [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) for where you are most open to images and to other people, and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/), the traditional ruler, which tradition also exalts in Cancer, so the older ruler of your Sun's sign finds its place of honour in your Moon's. I read that as generosity with somewhere to live: your wish to help has a home base. The Sun itself has no dignity in Pisces.

The Moon was 90° to 148° ahead of the Sun, seven and a half to twelve days after the new moon. The [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) phase holds most of these births, about seven in eight, and the latest are [waxing gibbous moon](/astrology/waxing-gibbous-moon/) births; around 120° the lights form a waxing [Sun trine Moon](/astrology/sun-trine-moon/). The phase asks for action and the trine makes it easy, and with two water signs the action tends to take the form of care. [Your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) separates the two phases.

Some 1 person in 146 is born with the Moon at home under a Pisces Sun. The Moon passes from Gemini into Cancer and on to Leo in two and a half days or so, and on a day of crossing the time chooses among three pages: this one, the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Gemini](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-gemini/) and the [Sun in Pisces, Moon in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-pisces-moon-in-leo/) ([the Moon, the one to check](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). With the Moon ruling its own sign, its house is where I'd look first, and that house needs the hour.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love protectively and imaginatively, sensing what someone needs and then quietly providing it. The Cancer Moon builds a nest and remembers every birthday; the Pisces Sun forgives easily and sees the best in people. The risk is that everyone is looked after except you, and that you stay loyal to someone long after the care has stopped flowing both ways. Let one person look after you, and notice how it feels.

## At work {#at-work}

Care is your natural field: nursing, teaching, social work, hospitality, therapy, any work where people need to feel safe before anything else can happen. The arts suit you too, especially when they hold memory or tenderness. The Sun brings empathy and imagination; the Moon brings protectiveness and a long memory for what people need. Harsh, impersonal workplaces wear down both lights at once.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Two water signs can take in more than they can hold. Other people's moods, old memories and new worries can fill an evening until there's no room left for your own. The first quarter's answer, as I read it, is to do something with what you feel: write it, cook it, walk it off, turn it into help with a limit. And keep a shelter for yourself as carefully as you keep one for everyone else.
