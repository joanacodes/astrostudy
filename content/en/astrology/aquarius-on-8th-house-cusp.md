Aquarius on the 8th house cusp brings fixed air into the house of intimacy, trust and everything held in common. You share most easily on clear and equal terms, and closeness works best for you when it leaves both people their freedom.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is where a bond deepens into what's shared: trust, desire, joint money, debts, the things people owe each other. With [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) on its cusp ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)), you tend to want those arrangements spelled out and fair. A joint account with separate pots beside it, a loan between friends written down, a frank talk about who pays for what: none of this feels cold to you, it feels like respect. Intimacy grows through honesty and friendship, and it stalls when someone tries to own you or reads your need for space as a lack of love. When feelings run high around you, you're often the one who can still think, and people lean on that. What you've agreed to, you stick to; but once an arrangement stops fitting, you may rewrite it from top to bottom.

Everything you hold in common falls under Uranus and Saturn, and I read both. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) shows where shared life brings sudden turns, the unexpected bill as much as the unexpected closeness; [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) shows where trust is earned slowly and where you take on obligations you then keep. In every one of these charts Saturn also rules your Descendant: Cancer rises in about half the London charts and 2 in 3 at New York, putting Capricorn on the 7th cusp, and Leo rises in the rest, with Aquarius opening the 7th as well as the 8th. Either way, the planet that governs what you share also governs the people you share it with. Follow each ruler to its house to see where the 8th's questions of trust and debt come up in your life, as ruler of the 8th in the [1st](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/8th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

In Placidus, the house system I use, Aquarius opens the 8th for about 1 birth in 9 at London and 1 in 10 at New York, and the house is narrow here, about 18° to 23° at London. That narrowness is why the sign so often spills onto a neighbouring cusp: onto the 7th in nearly half the London charts, onto the 9th in about 1 in 3 ([duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). Pisces follows on the 9th cusp, or on the Midheaven itself in about 2 London charts in 5. Elsewhere in the wheel, nearly 9 London charts in 10 with this cusp have a pair of signs with no cusp of their own; and in about 1 London chart in 7 it's Aquarius that has none ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Death and inheritance were the 8th's business in the old books. I don't read death in any chart ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); I read the 8th in an adult's life, where it's busy with trust and sharing. Your birth time places the cusp: a little later, Aquarius would sit on your Descendant ([Aquarius on the 7th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-7th-house-cusp/)), and a little earlier it would head the 9th ([Aquarius on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-9th-house-cusp/)). Without the time, I can't say which of the three is yours ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
