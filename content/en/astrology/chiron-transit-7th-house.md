The 7th house is where you meet people as equals, and where you learned, early or late, what closeness can cost. When Chiron crosses your Descendant, the sore spots of partnership come into the room with the person across the table.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is named after a healer who could not heal his own wound, and I read it as a hurt that doesn't fully close, with the understanding that grows around it. The [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) is the person facing you, a partner in love or in work, a close collaborator, an open rival, and what you look for in them. While Chiron is here, an old relationship wound can come back into view: being left, being the one who always adapted, being valued for what you gave rather than for who you were, choosing people who needed rescuing. Often it arrives through someone close who presses on the sore spot without meaning to, and the question becomes less whose fault it is than what the hurt has trained you to expect. Chiron doesn't promise to erase that training; it asks you to work with it, so that you can stand opposite someone without bracing. Many people come out of this stay able to listen to a partner as few can, which is how [Chiron in the 7th house](/astrology/chiron-in-7th-house/) is read at birth.

In [Placidus](/astrology/placidus/), the house system I use, the 7th begins at your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), so the stay opens with Chiron on the angle of partnership; a natal Venus in the 7th, reached later, is asked whether it keeps the peace at its own expense.

A [transit](/astrology/transits/) like this predicts no meeting, no wedding and no break-up. I read it as weather, not a forecast: the climate of your closest bonds for as long as Chiron stays.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 7th keeps Chiron longer than any other house. In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude the middle is about 5 years, with most stays between about 3 and 8, while across all twelve houses the middle is about 4 and the spread 2 to 9. Your 7th is exactly as wide as your 1st, yet a stay in the 1st can be as short as a year or so: at that latitude the 7th is widest when it falls around Leo, Virgo and Libra, where Chiron moves fastest, and narrowest around Capricorn and Aquarius, which take it four to six years to cross. New York's latitude tells the same story, about 5 years in the middle and 3 to 8 for most stays.

The same minutes that give your rising sign give your Descendant, and with it the start of this stay; without them there is no chart for me to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The house of daily work hands Chiron on to this one ([Chiron transiting the 6th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-6th-house/)), and this one hands it on to the house of trust and what is shared ([Chiron transiting the 8th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-8th-house/)).

Because your Descendant always sits opposite your rising sign, the coming years are easy to place. Libra rising puts it in Aries, where Chiron has been since April 2018 and stays until April 2027; Scorpio rising, in Taurus, which Chiron enters in June 2026 and leaves in May 2034; Sagittarius rising, in Gemini, from July 2033 to April 2039; Capricorn rising, in Cancer, from July 2038, where my count leaves Chiron in December 2039. The degree of your Descendant narrows it to a year.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Notice what you brace for with the people closest to you, and ask whether it is happening now or happened long ago.

Say one need plainly to a partner, a friend or a colleague, and let them meet it. Being met is the practice of these years.

Keep an eye on who does the adapting. If it is always you, say so, kindly, once, and see what changes.

If an old relationship pattern feels too heavy to untangle alone, a counsellor is a good companion for this stay.
