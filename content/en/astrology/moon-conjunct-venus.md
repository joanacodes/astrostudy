Moon conjunct Venus is the thin crescent beside the evening or morning star, carried into a birth chart. Venus never strays far from the Sun, which leaves the Moon only the days around a new moon to reach it. It describes needs and pleasures that arrive together: what comforts you is also what you love.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) describes how you tend your needs and attachments, the felt sense of being safe. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) weighs value: what attracts you, what you prefer, how you keep an exchange fair and pleasant. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) binds the two into one process without erasing either, so needing and enjoying happen in the same breath.

In plain words, you feel safe through what pleases you: a meal shared, a room arranged just so, a friend's warmth on a bad day. Care and pleasure come in one gesture. You look after people by making things nice for them, and you feel looked after when someone remembers what you like. People tend to feel at ease near you quickly. The work it asks is quiet but real: telling what you like from what you need, since one can stand in for the other, and learning that saying no doesn't spoil the warmth. Keeping the peace and keeping yourself steady aren't always the same thing.

The two usually share a sign. In [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/), where Venus is at home and the Moon exalted, comfort and pleasure are at their most settled; in Cancer, the Moon's sign, affection turns protective; in Libra, Venus's other sign, care shows as fairness and good company.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In practice it often shows as a warmth people sense at once, and a home or a circle that matters more than you admit. The house it falls in shows where that warmth gathers: in the 4th, around the people you live with; in the 10th, in the way you are at work, where kindness becomes part of your reputation.

About 1 person in 22 has the Moon within 8° of Venus. When Venus is an evening star, the meeting comes just after the new moon; when it's a morning star, just before. A day and a bit is all the Moon spends in that window, and the closer the two stand, the less you can tell needing from enjoying. In my notebook, Venus by sign and house was one of the placements everyone reads first, and it held about one time in three ([the invoice method, and what held overall](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)); I hold this aspect lightly for that reason.

Each of the pair's other aspects sets need and affection at a different distance:

- **[Moon sextile Venus](/astrology/moon-sextile-venus/):** two signs apart, affection on friendly terms with need; warmth you offer once someone opens the door.
- **[Moon square Venus](/astrology/moon-square-venus/):** what comforts you and what attracts you pulling different ways, a friction that teaches you to ask.
- **[Moon trine Venus](/astrology/moon-trine-venus/):** need and affection in one element, at a comfortable distance, so tenderness flows without merging.
- **[Moon opposite Venus](/astrology/moon-opposite-venus/):** born near a full moon, with need and affection across the chart from each other, so comfort is often sought in someone else's warmth.

Between two charts, [Moon conjunct Venus in synastry](/astrology/moon-conjunct-venus-synastry/) puts one person's need for comfort right beside the other's affection: often a sweet, homely contact. I read it in both directions, since the one who soothes and the one soothed rarely notice the same things.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Each month, for a few hours, the travelling Moon passes over your natal Venus: a good evening for the people you love. Venus in the sky crosses the Moon in your chart roughly once a year, staying within a degree for two days or so; in a year when Venus turns retrograde nearby, it can pass three times over several weeks.

Weather, not a forecast: these are small, warm days for comfort and company. They don't bring anyone into your life, or take anyone out.
