The North Node comes into your 9th house over the top of your chart, the Midheaven, and spends about nineteen months there while the South Node crosses your 3rd. Between the next street and the far horizon, these months point at the horizon.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) is the direction that asks to be practised. The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) is the far horizon: long journeys, higher study, belief, the search for meaning. With the node here, the practice is synthesis: choosing a question large enough to live with, studying it properly, travelling in body or in mind, and arriving at a view you'd defend.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in your 3rd knows the near ground by heart: facts, messages, errands, the quick answer and the next notification. Your curiosity stays a gift, and so does the ease with which you talk to anyone. The stretch is to let it all add up to something.

In the old scheme the 9th was the God, the house where the Sun rejoiced. A natal Sun in your 9th, crossed by the node for a month or so, can make a conviction feel like yours rather than borrowed; a natal Jupiter there, ruler of Sagittarius, the house's natural sign, tends to widen the question.

Like every [transit](/astrology/transits/), this one brings out what your chart already holds about belief. It doesn't hand you a creed, and I don't read it as a calling you're fated to answer.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

In Placidus charts drawn at London's latitude, the true node usually stays about 19 months in the 9th, a little longer than the year and a half of the average house, and most stays fall between about 11 months and 2 years. New York's latitude gives the same middle, with a range from about 13 months to just under 2 years.

The true node wobbles, so a crossing back over the cusp that's undone within 120 days doesn't split a stay. I check the mean node too ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)), since near a cusp the two can sit on either side of it.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the stay opens at your Midheaven: as the North Node crosses it ([transiting North Node conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-north-node-conjunct-natal-midheaven/)), the South Node crosses your IC. Both need your birth time, without which I don't read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node comes from your 10th ([North Node transiting the 10th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-10th-house/)) and moves on to your 8th ([North Node transiting the 8th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-8th-house/)).

If your Midheaven is in Aquarius, the stay starts somewhere between July 2026 and March 2028. In Capricorn, it starts between March 2028 and September 2029; in Sagittarius, the 9th's natural sign, between September 2029 and March 2031; in Scorpio, by December 2032; in Libra, by June 2034; in Virgo, by November 2035; in Leo, by May 2037; and in Cancer, by February 2039. A Pisces Midheaven was crossed between January 2025 and July 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Choose one big question for these months and give it real time: a course, a long book, a journey with a purpose.

Write down what you believe, then test it against someone who disagrees.

Keep the South Node's quickness for the details. A view of the world is better for being checked against the facts you already know how to find.
