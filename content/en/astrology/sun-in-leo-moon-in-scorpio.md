Daylight suits a Leo Sun; a Scorpio Moon keeps its feelings below the waterline, for the few who have earned them. The pair belongs to the days around the first quarter, the half moon of the evening sky, and I read it as a fierce loyalty with two faces, one in the open and one kept private.

## How they get along {#how-they-get-along}

Leo is fixed fire and Scorpio fixed water, three signs on from the Sun: a square by sign between two signs that both hold on. The [Sun in Leo](/astrology/sun-in-leo/) organises itself around expression and wants what it loves and makes to be seen; the [Moon in Scorpio](/astrology/moon-in-scorpio/) settles through depth and tested trust, and wants what it feels kept safe. Both are loyal to the bone. Where they part company is visibility: what the Sun would show, the Moon would rather guard.

Tradition calls Scorpio the Moon's [fall](/astrology/fall/), the sign opposite its exaltation, and I read the word gently: comfort takes more work to reach here, which says nothing about how deeply you love. Above it, the Sun stands at home in its own sign. For Scorpio I read both rulers. [Mars](/astrology/mars/), given to the sign in the old books, shows how your Moon defends what it loves; [Pluto](/astrology/pluto/), its modern ruler, shows where it lets itself be changed by what it feels. When the two agree, the Moon's depth has a direction.

With your Moon somewhere between 61° and 119° ahead of the Sun and the month 5 to 10 days old, the sky held a crescent thickening towards half. The [waxing crescent](/astrology/waxing-crescent/) and the [first quarter moon](/astrology/first-quarter-moon/) take equal shares of these births, and the hour can decide which is yours. Near 90° the square is exact, a waxing [Sun square Moon](/astrology/sun-square-moon/), friction that tends to push outward into doing; [your birth lunar phase](/astrology/your-birth-lunar-phase/) explains why a first quarter acts where a last quarter reviews.

The pair turns up once in about 137 births. On a day the Moon was leaving Libra or reaching Sagittarius, the hour sorts this page from the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Libra](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-libra/) and the [Sun in Leo, Moon in Sagittarius](/astrology/sun-in-leo-moon-in-sagittarius/) ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/#the-moon)). It also decides the rising sign, and for a pair that shows one face and keeps another, the rising sign is often the part people meet first.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

You love with your whole heart, and you guard it. The Leo Sun is generous and openly proud of the people it loves; the Scorpio Moon lets people in one step at a time and, once it has, holds nothing back, asking only for the truth, unwelcome truths too. A partner who is warm in public and evasive in private unsettles you more than any quarrel. Hurts sink deep with two fixed signs and can be kept a long time, so saying *that hurt* while it's still small keeps loyalty from turning into a silent standoff.

## At work {#at-work}

Staying power is your gift. Once committed, you see things through, and you can carry a project, a team or a cause through a crisis that sends others home. The Sun wants a visible result with your name on it; the Moon needs to know the real story under the official one. Leadership with depth suits you: running something difficult, investigating, restoring, looking after what others entrust to you. Hidden agendas exhaust the Moon, and anonymity starves the Sun.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

Pride and privacy can make you hard to reach just when it matters most. The Leo Sun would rather not be seen hurt, and the Scorpio Moon doesn't like to be seen at all while it's raw, so a wound may vanish behind a confident front and go on working below. Two fixed signs at a square also find it hard to let go, of a grievance, a role, a version of events. What helps is one person who gets the private version, told early, and the habit of asking what you're holding on to, and why.
