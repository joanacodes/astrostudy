When someone's Saturn falls in your 7th house, the relationship tends to feel like a real arrangement early on, whatever kind it is. Promises are kept, or noticed when they aren't, and the word *we* starts to carry weight, sometimes sooner than either of you planned.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) stands for what we commit to and keep, the slow work of making something last. [The 7th house](/astrology/7th-house/) gathers the people you team up with one to one: spouses and long-term partners, business associates, close collaborators, anyone met face to face as an equal. This [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/) gives partnership with them a long horizon.

For you, they may feel like someone you could build with. They turn up when they said they would, they keep agreements, and their loyalty, once given, is not casual. Partnering them can teach you what commitment asks: patience, fairness, staying when it's dull.

For them, partnering you can feel like a responsibility as much as a pleasure. They want to get it right, slowly, and may hold back until they're sure; once sure, they tend to stay. The 7th is angular, so this shows in what you do together, and [Libra](/astrology/libra/), its natural sign, is where tradition exalts Saturn: an old hint that fairness and lasting agreements belong together. Near the start of the house their Saturn meets your [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), the setting point of your chart, and I read that conjunction first.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Seriousness can arrive before warmth. The bond may feel formal, slow or conditional, as if you were on probation, and their caution about commitment can look like doubt about you. In business, they can be the partner whose rules you follow; in love, the one whose timetable you wait for.

From their side, being cast as the serious one is a weight. Your hesitations may look to them like unreliability; they can come to believe that control keeps a partnership safe, or fear wasting years on something that won't hold, and that fear can make them stricter than they mean to be.

No overlay says whether two people marry, stay or part, and this one is no exception: Saturn here describes how commitment feels between you, never its outcome.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Make your agreements explicit, and review them rather than enforce them; Saturn respects what's been said aloud. In a couple, talk about timelines plainly, and let affection be said as well as shown; between business partners, a contract with a review date suits this overlay perfectly; between friends, this is the friend who shows up, every time, and hopes for the same; between adult relatives, it can be the one who takes family duties seriously and needs help sharing them.

Their Saturn also touches your other planets, and where it meets your Venus or your Moon I read it with particular care ([Venus conjunct Saturn in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-saturn-synastry/)): those are the contacts that tip steadiness towards comfort or towards criticism.

Turned the other way, the picture shows your Saturn in their 7th house: you become the partner who asks for time, and the one whose caution needs explaining. [Their Saturn in your 6th house](/astrology/saturn-in-6th-house-synastry/) is about the working day; [their Saturn in your 8th house](/astrology/saturn-in-8th-house-synastry/), about trust and what you share.

Your Descendant, the cusp of the 7th, is fixed by your birth time to the minute, so in Placidus, the house system I use, a few minutes decide whether their Saturn sits just inside your 7th or still in your 6th. Their date fixes Saturn's degree; their time I ask for anyway, as for any synastry ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born close together, you may have both Saturns in one sign, and your own in your 7th as well ([Saturn in the 7th house](/astrology/saturn-in-7th-house/)); fourteen or fifteen years apart, half of Saturn's cycle, the two tend to face each other from opposite signs instead. Synastry usually starts with this house, and reading it supposes that both people wanted it read ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
