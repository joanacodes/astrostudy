One of you would like the evening to be lovely; the other won't pretend it is. Venus square Lilith in synastry sets one person's Venus about 90° from the other's Black Moon Lilith, so a sense of what's pleasing and a refusal to be made pleasing keep meeting at an awkward corner.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) judges and attracts: preference, pleasure, the eye for what fits, and the reciprocity that keeps a bond even. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/), the Moon's farthest point from Earth, means different things in different traditions; in my readings it stands for the autonomy and appetite a person learned to hide, and the boundaries they keep anyway. A [square](/astrology/square/) relates two functions through friction that pushes both to adapt. Spread across two charts, as in all [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/), the friction belongs to neither person alone.

Squares usually join signs of one modality. Picture a Venus in Pisces squaring a Lilith in Gemini, two mutable signs: a soft, forgiving taste meets a sharp tongue its owner learned to hold. The first wants kindness in the room; the second wants the true sentence said.

What the friction makes possible is a rare kind of honesty. The Venus person finds that a pleasant evening and a peaceful one aren't always the same thing, and that a bond can hold a refusal. The Lilith person finds that a no can be heard without becoming a rupture. Pairs who stay with it tend to trust each other's yes, because they've heard each other's no.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Holding the Venus, you may feel your taste is being mocked, or your wish for things to go well treated as fake. You offer something lovely, a plan, a gift, a compliment, and it's met with a shrug or a flat no. It's easy to decide they're being difficult on purpose.

Holding the Lilith, you may feel asked, again, to be easier: quieter, softer, more presentable, nicer to the people at the table. The request may be gentle, and it can still sound like the old one, the one that taught you to hide. Pushing back is tempting, and so is going quiet, which costs more.

Neither of you is wrong about what you want, and the square isn't failure. It rubs most when the Venus person's requests pile up unspoken, and when the Lilith person's refusals arrive without a reason.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Separate the refusal from the relationship. "Is this a no to the plan, or to me?" is a fair question from the Venus side, and "to the plan" is often the true answer. Agree where polish is wanted and where it isn't: a family wedding may want some, a long friendship can do without, and between colleagues a client meeting differs from the team's own room. In a couple, it helps to name one or two places where the Lilith person never has to perform.

Counted with mean Lilith, the one I use, about 1 pair of people in 9 has this square, one way round or the other, within 5.1°. Lilith is one of the three placements, with Saturn and Chiron, that held 75 to 90% of the time in my notebook. That was each person's own Lilith, in a single chart, and it gives no figure for this square; it's only the reason I'd never advise the Lilith person to sand their refusals down ([the three that held: Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#lilith)). The hour seldom decides whether the square exists, but the houses it falls in, where the friction shows, depend on both birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Within a single chart, [Venus square Lilith](/astrology/venus-square-lilith/) is one person caught between pleasing and refusing. Between two, the pair's other angles rub less or differently: [Venus conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-lilith-synastry/) falls straight on the hidden part, [Venus sextile Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-lilith-synastry/) and [Venus trine Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-lilith-synastry/) welcome it, and [Venus opposite Lilith in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-lilith-synastry/) faces it from across the chart.
