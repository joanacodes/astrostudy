Neither the old life nor the new plan has much room for the last-minute trip, the concert, the friend who wants to talk all night. Venus square North Node in synastry puts one person's Venus about 90° from the other's North Node, and so 90° from their South Node as well: what the first person values sits at right angles to the second person's whole line of travel.

## What flows {#what-flows}

[Venus](/astrology/venus/) is what a person values and how they relate: preference, pleasure, the people and things they're drawn to, and an even exchange. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to what someone is learning to do, a capacity still short of habit; the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) opposite, to what they've long relied on. A [square](/astrology/square/) sets two functions at an angle where each keeps catching on the other, and here it catches both ends of the axis at once. Among [synastry aspects](/astrology/synastry-aspects/) with the nodes, it's the one that sides with neither end.

Picture the nodes across Virgo and Pisces: a North Node in Virgo, building practical daily habits, with the South Node in Pisces, long used to letting days drift. The other person's Venus in Sagittarius, square to both, wants a spur-of-the-moment journey and a good argument on the way. That fits neither the new timetable nor the old drift.

Carrying the nodes, you may feel the other's pleasures tugging you sideways, away from your familiar ground and your new direction alike. Carrying the Venus, you may feel your values treated as a side issue in someone else's project: liked, but not quite made room for.

The friction is worth having, because it makes room for growth that includes enjoyment. The Node person learns that a new capacity can have pleasure in it, and isn't only effort; the Venus person learns that what they value can travel alongside someone's change without being its centre.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

A pattern can set in. The Node person schedules growth, or falls back on habit, and either way the Venus person's pleasures come last. The Venus person then asks for them more firmly, and the Node person hears a demand to stop changing. Neither reading is true: one is busy, the other wants company.

It also rubs when the Venus person decides the other's growth is a phase, or the Node person decides the other's pleasures are a test. A square is friction, never failure, and this one is about priorities, not about who matters.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Put pleasure inside the change. If the nodes are yours, ask what of the other's would make the new step more enjoyable, and try it; if the Venus is yours, offer your pleasures as company for the change rather than a break from it. In a couple, the course one of you is taking can end with the meal you both like; between friends, a new routine can keep the old Saturday walk; between colleagues, a demanding project can still have a decent lunch in it.

By the true node and within 5.1°, about 1 pair of people in 9 has this square, one way round or the other; the mean node sits a degree or two away at most, and I look at both. Since the square reaches both nodes, a reading names both: Venus squares the familiar end as much as the new one. Where it lands in each life, through the houses, waits on the two birth times ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Inside a single chart, [Venus square North Node](/astrology/venus-square-north-node/) is a person's own pleasures crossing their own path. Across two charts, Venus sits differently at the pair's other angles: [Venus conjunct North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-north-node-synastry/) on the growing edge, [Venus sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-sextile-north-node-synastry/) a friendly step from it, [Venus trine North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-trine-north-node-synastry/) in easy step with it, and [Venus opposite North Node in synastry](/astrology/venus-opposite-north-node-synastry/) on the familiar ground behind. Because both nodes are involved, the pair can also read [the nodes in synastry](/astrology/nodes-in-synastry/) for the larger picture.
