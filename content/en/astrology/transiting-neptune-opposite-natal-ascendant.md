Neptune opposite your Ascendant is Neptune on your Descendant, the western point of the chart where you meet other people. Half of people reach it once by 80, and for some two and a half years it softens the way they meet a partner, a friend or a client.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) is the Ascendant's partner point: where your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) sets your own stance, it frames the person across from you. [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/) brings compassion, longing and a gift for seeing the best in people. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) works through that polarity, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the space between you and others grows more porous: you sense what someone needs before they say it, you may idealise a partner, a friend or a client, and the line between their wishes and yours takes more care to draw.

Neptune's blind spot here is taking empathy for knowledge of another person; the Descendant's is taking reciprocity for agreement. Neither is a forecast. The transit brings no particular person into your life and takes none away; it describes how you meet whoever is there. I read it as a time to see people kindly and clearly at once.

In Placidus the Descendant is the cusp of the 7th house, so the crossing closes Neptune's stay in your 6th, among the routines of work and care ([Neptune transiting the 6th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-6th-house/)), and opens its long one in your 7th ([Neptune transiting the 7th house](/astrology/neptune-transit-7th-house/)). If you are tempted to look at a partner's chart to see what this means for them, ask them first ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Crossing your Descendant takes Neptune about two and a half years within my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and twenty-two months when the pass is short. Three exact hits are the usual count; about one pass in four has five.

This crossing and [transiting Neptune conjunct natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-conjunct-natal-ascendant/) share the population between them. Neptune's road from its place at your birth reaches one end of the horizon within 80 years, almost never both: the Descendant if your natal Neptune lay below the horizon, anywhere in houses 1 to 6. The closer it sat to the 6th, the sooner; the closer to the Ascendant, the nearer to 80. The Descendant is exactly opposite the rising degree, so it hangs on the minute of birth just as much ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

On the way, Neptune passes the square near the bottom of the chart ([transiting Neptune square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-square-natal-ascendant/)) and the trine around your 5th ([transiting Neptune trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-trine-natal-ascendant/)); the sextiles ([transiting Neptune sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-neptune-sextile-natal-ascendant/)) lie on the other half of the round, either side of the Ascendant. With Neptune in Aries until March 2039, it is [Libra rising](/astrology/libra-rising/) whose Descendant Neptune reaches now, the earliest degrees first.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Keep a plain record beside the hopeful one: what someone has actually done, next to what you imagine for them.

Say what you need out loud. Under Neptune it is easy to give the other person what you sense they want and leave your own wishes unspoken.

Let compassion keep its limits. Helping a partner, a friend or a client is part of this transit; disappearing into their needs isn't.
