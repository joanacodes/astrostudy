Mars turns back through Cancer in the darkest months of the northern year: three times in the dates I use, from November or December to February. It is the retrograde of the protector, the energy you spend defending home and the people you call yours, brought back for a quieter look.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Cancer](/astrology/cancer/) is cardinal water, ruled by the Moon, and Mars is in fall there, opposite Capricorn, its exaltation. Drive in Cancer rarely charges straight ahead; it circles, shields and acts for someone else. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when the planet appears from Earth to halt and reverse for eleven weeks, that sideways energy turns round: the battle you fought on a relative’s behalf, the hurt you answered by withdrawing, the want you never said out loud because it seemed selfish. Anger here often hides inside care, and these weeks can show which is which. Desire turns towards comfort and closeness.

The most recent loop began in Leo ([Mars retrograde in Leo](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-leo/)) at 6°10′ on 6 December 2024, backed into Cancer and turned direct at 17°00′ Cancer on 24 February 2025. The one before, from 15 November 2007 to 30 January 2008, stationed at 12°27′ Cancer and ended in Gemini ([Mars retrograde in Gemini](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-gemini/)) at 24°04′. The next, from 23 November 2039 to 9 February 2040, stays wholly in Cancer, from 21°45′ to 3°01′, with Mars opposite the Sun, then in Capricorn, around the turn of the year. The data also lists the loop of December 2009 to March 2010, but both its stations, at 19°41′ and 0°17′, fall in Leo, so I don’t count it here.

A Mars born retrograde in Cancer is a lifelong placement, a protective drive that gathers inside first, and I read it apart ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Which house Cancer occupies, and so where this winter review settles, comes from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/); I don’t read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, loops of 18° or more that cross from Leo or into Gemini can touch two houses.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/cancer-rising/):** Cancer rising: a guarded, caring first presence; the shell you put up before you act, noticed.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/cancer-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** effort spent keeping your people secure; how much of it is yours to carry, reconsidered.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** the way you speak up for family and neighbours; a protective outburst, recalled and softened.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/cancer-on-4th-house-cusp/):** Cancer’s natural house: home and roots; an old family friction, revisited with more patience than before.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/cancer-on-5th-house-cusp/):** play, romance and creative work, a house tradition also gave to children; read in an adult’s life, never as a forecast of pregnancy ([Can astrology predict pregnancy?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-pregnancy/)): a tender wish you held back, looked at again.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/cancer-on-6th-house-cusp/):** caring for colleagues as much as for tasks; the extra care you give unasked, measured against your own needs.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/cancer-on-7th-house-cusp/):** with Capricorn rising, Cancer sets on the Descendant: a partner whose moods steer the pace; who protects whom, talked over.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/cancer-on-8th-house-cusp/):** closeness, trust and what is shared; a need for safety you pressed in silence, finally named.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/cancer-on-9th-house-cusp/):** beliefs inherited from home; a family conviction you defended, examined as your own.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/cancer-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Cancer on the Midheaven: ambition that looks after people; the effort you put into being needed at work, rethought.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/cancer-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friends who feel like family; a loyalty you fought for, weighed again.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/cancer-on-12th-house-cusp/):** feelings kept behind closed doors; a resentment nursed in private, let up for air.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Cancer protects first, so I find these weeks ask a simpler question than usual: what do you want for yourself? Saying it directly, once, often eases more than another round of caring for others. The Moon rules Cancer, and the house of your natal Moon shows where comfort can steady the review, while your natal Mars shows where the drive itself starts.

None of this foretells a family row or a reunion. It describes a long winter in which the urge to defend learns to rest when nothing is under attack.
