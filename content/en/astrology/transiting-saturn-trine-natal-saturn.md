A third of the way round its cycle from a return, and again a third of the way before the next, Saturn trines its own birth place. It happens six times by 80, more often than any other step of the Saturn cycle, and it's the gentlest of them.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your natal [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) is the slow builder in your chart, the part that carries responsibility and learns structure by doing. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, relates functions through facilitative flow, and here Saturn's function cooperates with itself: the structure you've been building and the one you started from agree, for once. Things you set up begin to run with less effort, and discipline learned the hard way starts to look like competence.

Saturn's familiar weakness is finding it hard to delegate, and the trine is a good season to practise: hand over a task, teach a method, let a structure work without watching it. The opposite risk is coasting. Flow isn't passivity, and a trine can make a routine so comfortable that nobody checks whether it's still needed.

The waxing trines, at about 9½, 39 and 68, come between a square and the opposition, and tend to consolidate what that square made you adjust. The waning ones, at about 19½, 49 and 78, come after the opposition and before the next square, and suit refining and passing things on. The first, around nine, falls in childhood, which this site leaves unread ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)).

With natal Saturn in the 6th, the trine comes from your 2nd ([Saturn transiting the 2nd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-2nd-house/)) or your 10th, so the way you work day to day gets the backing of steadier resources or a clearer professional standard. Saturn's house in your chart, and the two it trines from, all wait on your birth time, as does any reading of them ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Each of these trines lasts about nine months in my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, and because nothing pushes, most of that time passes unnoticed unless you use it. Like a little over half of Saturn's [transits](/astrology/transits/), it often comes as three exact passes over about nine and a half months as Saturn doubles back; a quick single crossing can be over in three weeks.

The first trine came at 8.6 for someone born on 1 January 1965, at 9 for 1 January 1975 and at 10.4 for 1 January 1985; across births every fifth day from 1930 to 2009 it fell between 8.5 and 11.1, with 9.4 in the middle. The adult ones follow at about 19½, 39, 49, 68 and 78, and everyone has had exactly six by 80.

In the cycle ([Saturn squares and opposition](/astrology/saturn-squares-and-opposition/)), the waxing trine follows [transiting Saturn sextile natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-saturn/) and [transiting Saturn square natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-saturn/) and comes about five years before [transiting Saturn opposite natal Saturn](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-saturn/). The waning trine comes about ten years before the [Saturn return](/astrology/saturn-return/).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Consolidate. Turn what works into a routine that would survive your absence.

Delegate one thing. Under this trine, letting go of control costs less than usual.

Check what you carry out of habit. Even an easy season is a checkpoint, and the trine is the cheapest moment to drop what you no longer need.
