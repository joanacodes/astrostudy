Summer's last ten days in the northern hemisphere belong to the third decan of Virgo, 20° to 30° of the sign, and the September equinox closes it. Here the two lists of decan rulers pick a planet Virgo honours and one it doesn't: Mercury, the sign's own ruler, by face, and Venus, in fall in Virgo, by element.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

After Venus the Chaldean order reaches [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), and so Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) close [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) with the sign's own ruler. Mercury is also exalted in Virgo, the one traditional planet with its home and its exaltation in the same sign, so a [Mercury in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-in-virgo/) between 20° and 30° holds domicile, exaltation and face together: five points, four and one, ten in the medieval scoring.

Taurus is the third earth sign, and its ruler, [Venus](/astrology/venus/), takes the decan by element. Venus is in [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Virgo, so the modern list hands the end of the sign to the planet the sign treats least kindly, while the old list hands it to the one it treats best.

Mercury gets the closest reading: how you notice, sort and put things right. Venus, through the modern decan, asks what all that care is for, and what you value enough to perfect. I give her a second look, not the first.

## Its image {#its-image}

Budge calls the decan Usthȧ, and the Greco-Egyptian lists Aposot. Joseph Scaliger's Aphut is a step from Firmicus Maternus's Afut, or Asuth, while the Greek Hermetic Amphatham and the *Testament of Solomon*'s Buldumêch stand further off.

For Cosmas of Maiuma the decan belonged to Hestia, the goddess of the hearth; for Athanasius Kircher, to Cronus, the Titan of Greek myth. Hestia, keeping the fire at the centre of the house, is a quiet picture for the last ten degrees of a sign that likes things in order; I give her no more weight than that ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

September's second and third weeks, from about the 13th to the 22nd, bring the Sun into this decan ([Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/)). In 2026 the equinox, 0° Libra, came at 00:05 UT on 23 September, in the night after the 22nd; when it falls like that, only the time of birth places the Sun in Virgo or in Libra ([Libra, first decan](/astrology/libra-first-decan/), [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Two stars sit in the decan: [Denebola](/astrology/denebola/), β Leonis, the tail of the Lion, at 21°58′ Virgo in 2026, and [Alkaid](/astrology/alkaid/), the last star in the handle of the Plough, at 27°18′, more than 54° north of the Sun's path.

Uranus and Pluto came on here after their meetings in the middle of the sign ([Virgo, second decan](/astrology/virgo-second-decan/)): Uranus for births from September 1966 to September 1968, Pluto from September 1967 to October 1971, so births of 1967–68 often carry both ([Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/uranus-in-virgo/)). Neptune was here earlier, for births from August 1938 to October 1942, and Saturn's dates are 1950–51, 1979–80 and 2008–10. Jupiter arrives on 30 October 2027 and stays until 2 April 2028, returning from late June to August 2028.
