The third decan of Virgo is 20° to 30° of the sign, the last ten days before the September equinox. Mercury rules the sign and holds the face here, and Venus, the planet tradition puts in its fall in Virgo, rules the decan by element.

## Its rulers {#its-rulers}

The Chaldean order follows Venus with [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/), so Lilly's [faces](/astrology/faces/) close [Virgo](/astrology/virgo/) with the sign's own ruler. Mercury is also exalted in Virgo, the one traditional planet with its home and its exaltation in the same sign, so a [Mercury in Virgo](/astrology/mercury-in-virgo/) between 20° and 30° holds domicile, exaltation and face together: five points, four and one, ten in the medieval scoring.

By element, the modern decans give the last ten degrees of an earth sign to the ruler of the third earth sign, Taurus, which is [Venus](/astrology/venus/). Venus is in [fall](/astrology/fall/) in Virgo, so the modern list hands the end of the sign to the planet the sign treats least kindly, while the old list hands it to the one it treats best.

With a planet here, I read your Mercury first and most closely: how you notice, sort and put things right. Venus, through the modern decan, asks what all that care is for, and what you value enough to perfect. I give her a second look, not the first.

## Its image {#its-image}

Budge's name for it is Usthȧ, and the Greco-Egyptian lists write Aposot. Firmicus Maternus has Afut, or Asuth, and Joseph Scaliger Aphut; the Greek Hermetic list gives Amphatham, and the *Testament of Solomon* Buldumêch.

Cosmas of Maiuma put Hestia here, the goddess of the hearth, and Athanasius Kircher Cronus, the Titan of Greek myth. Hestia, keeping the fire at the centre of the house, is a quiet picture for the last ten degrees of a sign that likes things in order; I give her no more weight than that ([the Egyptian decans](/astrology/egyptian-decans/)).

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

The Sun stands here for births from about 13 to 22 September ([Sun in Virgo](/astrology/sun-in-virgo/)). In 2026 it reached 20° Virgo on 12 September at 18:08 UT and the September equinox, 0° Libra, on 23 September at 00:05 UT; in 2027, at 23:58 UT on the 12th and 06:01 UT on the 23rd. When the equinox falls in the night between the 22nd and the 23rd, only the time of birth places the Sun in Virgo or in Libra ([Libra, first decan](/astrology/libra-first-decan/), [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Two stars sit in the decan: [Denebola](/astrology/denebola/), β Leonis, the tail of the Lion, at 21°58′ Virgo in 2026, and [Alkaid](/astrology/alkaid/), the last star in the handle of the Plough, at 27°18′, more than 54° north of the Sun's path.

Uranus and Pluto came on here after their meetings in the middle of the sign ([Virgo, second decan](/astrology/virgo-second-decan/)): Uranus for births from September 1966 to September 1968, Pluto from September 1967 to October 1971, so births of 1967–68 often carry both ([Uranus in Virgo](/astrology/uranus-in-virgo/)). Neptune held these degrees for births from August 1938 to October 1942, and Saturn passed in 1950–51, 1979–80 and 2008–10. Jupiter crosses from 30 October 2027 to 2 April 2028, and again from late June to August 2028.
