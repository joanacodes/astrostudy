Pluto was found in 1930 and reclassed as a dwarf planet in 2006, and astrologers have gone on reading it all the same. In a chart it shows where you go deepest: what you hold on to hardest, what you can't do by halves, and where, in time, you can let go and begin again.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Pluto is the slowest of the [generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/): its sign is shared by everyone born over twelve to thirty-one years, depending on the sign. So its sign tells me about your generation; its house and its aspects tell me about you. I read Pluto slowly and gently, because it points to where people feel most intensely and guard most carefully.

- **Its sign** describes what a generation transforms, and what it can't leave alone.
- **Its house** shows where you go all in, and where questions of trust and control come up.
- **Its aspects** to your personal planets show how close that intensity sits to your everyday self.

Pluto is the modern ruler of [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/); before 1930, Scorpio belonged to [Mars](/astrology/mars/), and traditional astrologers still read it that way. It's the last of the three planets beyond Saturn, after [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) and [Neptune](/astrology/neptune/). Like them, it spends about five months of every year retrograde, so many people are born with [Pluto retrograde](/astrology/pluto-retrograde/). I read it as change worked through inside first — [a rhythm, not a delay](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

Its aspects are where it gets personal. With the [Moon conjunct Pluto](/astrology/moon-conjunct-pluto/), feelings run very deep and are guarded closely. A [Sun square Pluto](/astrology/sun-square-pluto/) often describes someone who had to claim their own strength, sometimes against someone else's. A [Venus opposite Pluto](/astrology/venus-opposite-pluto/) loves all or nothing, and learns that closeness doesn't need control. And a [Mars conjunct Pluto](/astrology/mars-conjunct-pluto/) has a staying power few people can match.

## Pluto through the signs {#through-the-signs}

Pluto's orbit is so stretched that it spends anywhere from twelve to thirty-one years in a sign, so its sign is shared by everyone born over a long stretch: it's the mark of a generation, and the house and the aspects make it yours. In plain words:

- **[Pluto in Aries](/astrology/pluto-in-aries/):** transformation through courage; rebuilding by starting from scratch.
- **[Pluto in Taurus](/astrology/pluto-in-taurus/):** deep change in what we own and value, and a stubborn hold on what lasts.
- **[Pluto in Gemini](/astrology/pluto-in-gemini/):** transformation through ideas, words and the way information travels.
- **[Pluto in Cancer](/astrology/pluto-in-cancer/):** deep change in home, family and belonging; roots fiercely defended.
- **[Pluto in Leo](/astrology/pluto-in-leo/):** intensity around creating and being seen; power through self-expression.
- **[Pluto in Virgo](/astrology/pluto-in-virgo/):** transformation through work, method and service; a drive to fix what's broken.
- **[Pluto in Libra](/astrology/pluto-in-libra/):** deep change in partnership and fairness; relationships remade.
- **[Pluto in Scorpio](/astrology/pluto-in-scorpio/):** Pluto in its modern sign: intensity, intimacy and nothing left unexamined.
- **[Pluto in Sagittarius](/astrology/pluto-in-sagittarius/):** belief transformed; faiths questioned and rebuilt.
- **[Pluto in Capricorn](/astrology/pluto-in-capricorn/):** deep change in structures, institutions and authority.
- **[Pluto in Aquarius](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius/):** transformation through groups, networks and shared ideals.
- **[Pluto in Pisces](/astrology/pluto-in-pisces/):** deep change through imagination and compassion; borders that dissolve.

## Pluto in the houses {#in-the-houses}

The house is where Pluto becomes personal, and it needs your [birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/):

1. **[1st house](/astrology/pluto-in-1st-house/):** an intense presence; you can reinvent yourself completely.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/pluto-in-2nd-house/):** a strong need for security and for what's truly yours; values you'd defend.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/pluto-in-3rd-house/):** words that go straight to the core; a mind that digs until it finds the truth.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/pluto-in-4th-house/):** deep roots and family stories with weight; home is a place you guard.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/pluto-in-5th-house/):** passion in love and in creating; you play for keeps.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/pluto-in-6th-house/):** an intense relationship with work; you can transform any system you're part of.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/pluto-in-7th-house/):** partnerships that change you; closeness where trust and power have to be shared.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/pluto-in-8th-house/):** at home in the house of Scorpio: intimacy and shared bonds run deep, nothing by halves.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/pluto-in-9th-house/):** beliefs tested to the core; a search for truth that changes you.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/pluto-in-10th-house/):** a public role that carries weight; you'd rather change a system than fit into it.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/pluto-in-11th-house/):** fierce loyalty to friends and causes; groups that change you, and that you change.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/pluto-in-12th-house/):** deep inner work; strength found in private.

## Pluto in synastry {#in-synastry}

Pluto contacts are the most intense in [synastry](/synastry/), and the ones I read most carefully. When one person's Pluto touches the other's Venus or Moon — [Venus conjunct Pluto in synastry](/astrology/venus-conjunct-pluto-synastry/), for instance — the pull can be magnetic and hard to explain. [Their Pluto in your 7th house](/astrology/pluto-in-7th-house-synastry/) can make a partnership feel bigger than both of you. The same contacts raise questions of power: who leads, who holds on, who changes whom. I read them in both directions, and only when both people have agreed to the reading: these are tender places to name, which is why I [won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/). And since people close in age share Pluto's sign, it's the houses and the contacts that count.

## Transits and the Pluto square {#pluto-square}

Pluto takes about 248 years to go round the zodiac, so it never comes back to where it was when you were born. What people do meet is the **[Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/)** — [transiting Pluto square natal Pluto](/astrology/transiting-pluto-square-natal-pluto/) — when it has moved a quarter of the way round. Because Pluto's orbit is so uneven, that takes very different lengths of time for different generations, so there's no single age for it: only your own chart can date it. Astrologers read it as a season when what you've been holding on to asks to be looked at honestly.

As it moves through a house, Pluto stays for many years: [Pluto transiting the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-4th-house/) works slowly on home and roots; [Pluto transiting the 10th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-10th-house/), on work and the role you play in public. Its transits are the slowest weather there is — deep, gradual, and never a forecast. A chart can't tell you what will happen, only what kind of season you're in.

## What held in my tests {#what-held}

I checked Pluto like every other placement in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/): its sign and its house, mine first, then my family's, friends' and strangers', with their consent. It wasn't one of the three that held far more often — [Saturn, Chiron and Lilith](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/) held 75 to 90% of the time — and across everything I checked, about a third held. So I read Pluto through its house and its aspects, I hold it lightly, and when part of a reading rests on it, I tell you. It's a notebook, not a study, and it shapes [how I read](/how-i-read/).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Pluto doesn't count any more.”** Astronomers reclassed it as a dwarf planet in 2006. Astrology reads a body by where it sits and what it touches, not by its size or its label, and astrologers still read Pluto.
- **“Pluto means death.”** Pluto is named after the Roman god of the underworld, and astrologers read it as endings and renewal. No placement predicts a death, and I never read a chart for one: [can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)
- **“Pluto makes people controlling.”** It shows where someone feels deeply and fears losing what matters. Control is one answer to that; trust is another, and it can be learned.
