Born with Sun conjunct Venus, you arrived while Venus was crossing from one side of the Sun to the other, between its seasons as morning star and evening star. In your chart, what you value, enjoy and find fair stands right beside your sense of who you are, so your warmth and your judgement start from the centre.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) gives you an identity and a direction. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) evaluates: it judges value, notices what draws you, forms preferences and checks that an exchange is fair. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) puts the two in one place: one working unit, two distinct jobs.

In plain words, your values aren't something you consult; they're part of you. Your taste, in people, in work, in how a room is arranged or a meal is shared, says something true about who you are, and you tend to feel most yourself around what you love. Relationships matter, and not only romantic ones: friendship, collaboration and any fair exchange carry some of your identity. That isn't vanity, and it isn't a promise of beauty. Venus describes how you value, not how you look.

The cost is the mirror image of the gift. When what you value sits this close to who you are, a dismissed preference can feel like a dismissed self, and a relationship can carry more of your identity than it needs to. Harmony matters to you, and harmony isn't the same thing as avoiding friction: the learning I see is keeping your own direction inside the exchanges you care about.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

With at most about 47° between them, your Venus shares your Sun sign or sits one or two signs either side, and no sextile, square, trine or opposition between the two can exist in a single chart. About 1 person in 8 has Venus within 8° of the Sun. The sign they share gives your values the temperament of your identity. The house shows where you put that warmth to work: the 2nd for what you earn and keep, the 7th for partnership, the 11th for friendship and shared causes.

Venus meets the Sun twice in each cycle of about a year and a half, and the two meetings are very different. On the far side of the Sun, Venus travels the same way, only a little faster, and the pair stays together for weeks; that's the meeting most people with this aspect were born at. Between the Sun and the Earth, Venus is in the middle of a [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/), and the two pass each other in days. Born then, with Venus retrograde, I read your values as worked out inside before they're shown ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)). On the far side, Venus is also the faster of the two, so a birth before the exact meeting finds Venus still approaching ([applying and separating aspects](/astrology/applying-and-separating-aspects/)).

Between two charts the pair is free of that 47° limit. [Sun conjunct Venus in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-venus-synastry/) is one person's Sun on the other's Venus: appreciation flowing towards who the other person is. I read it in both directions, because admiring and being admired don't feel alike.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Venus crosses your natal Sun about once a year, for two or three days, or three times when it turns back over that stretch of the zodiac. The Sun's pass over your natal Venus comes once a year and is over in a day or two ([transits](/astrology/transits/)). And in the occasional year when the Sun and Venus meet in the sky close to your birthday, the sky repeats your birth angle right on your own Sun.

I read these as small seasons of appreciation: good days for noticing what you value and saying so. Weather, not a forecast: a warmer mood around the things you love, nothing it can promise.
