Their Venus in your 8th house is attraction with depth to it. What the other person finds lovely reaches the most guarded part of your life: intimacy, trust, desire, and the things two people come to hold in common.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

In [the 8th house](/astrology/8th-house/), a bond becomes serious: closeness of every kind, trust and what it asks, shared money, debts, the changes intimacy brings. [Venus](/astrology/venus/) values, desires and seeks reciprocity. In this [house overlay](/astrology/house-overlays/), their affection doesn't stay at the surface.

You may feel deeply wanted, and safe enough to let someone see more of you than usual. Their affection doesn't skate over your difficult parts; if anything it's drawn to them. In practical matters they may be generous too, sharing what they have and making joint plans feel like a pleasure rather than a risk.

Your depth attracts them. They take pleasure in closeness and in the trust you give, and may value your private self above your public charm. The 8th is succedent. Its natural sign, [Scorpio](/astrology/scorpio/), has two rulers worth naming, Mars the traditional one and Pluto the modern one; I read both, and here both speak of intensity. Tradition also counts Scorpio as Venus's detriment: love that is all or nothing rather than light, never love that is less.

## Where it rubs {#where-it-rubs}

Intensity can outrun trust. Attraction this strong may push for closeness before either of you is ready, and possessiveness can creep in where intimacy should be. Money shared out of affection can also get complicated: generosity given freely is easy to resent later if nobody said what it meant.

They may feel that you hold something back however close you get, or that their generosity has created a debt neither of you mentions. Wanting all of someone is a heavy thing to carry, for the one who wants and for the one who is wanted.

The 8th was the house of death and inheritance for the old astrologers. Their Venus here forecasts neither, and I don't read for them ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)); between two adults, this overlay describes desire, trust and what you choose to share.

## How to meet it {#how-to-meet-it}

Talk about desire and limits plainly, and keep talking: closeness of every kind works best when either of you can say yes, no or not yet and be heard. Put shared money on paper, however much you trust each other. With a partner, intimacy moves at the speed of whoever needs more time. With a close friend, this may be the one who saw you through a crisis. With a business partner, joint finances deserve a clear agreement and a way out. Among adult relatives dividing what a family held in common, kindness and written notes belong together.

From your Venus in their 8th house, the view is reversed: you're drawn to their depths, and keeping their trust is up to you. The pages around it widen the view: [their Venus in your 7th house](/astrology/venus-in-7th-house-synastry/) is the partner facing you, [their Venus in your 9th house](/astrology/venus-in-9th-house-synastry/) the companion of far horizons.

Their Venus keeps within about 47° of their Sun and moves around a degree a day, so a birth date is enough to place it in your chart. Where your 8th begins and ends is another matter: in Placidus, the house system I use, those cusps are worked out from the minute and the place of your birth ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Desire and trust are as private as a chart gets, so I read them only with both people's agreement ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/#a-chart-is-personal)).
