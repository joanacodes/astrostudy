Sun conjunct Lilith puts the Sun right beside Black Moon Lilith, the point that marks where the Moon swings farthest from Earth. It describes a self in which the part you were taught to tone down sits near the centre, and so keeps coming back into view.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Sun](/astrology/sun/) organises your identity: who you are, what you're for, and how you express it. [Black Moon Lilith](/astrology/black-moon-lilith/) is read, very differently by different astrologers, as the part of you that would not obey: instinct, autonomy, what was rejected or kept out of sight, and your relationship to your own power. A [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses them into a single process without erasing either, so the refused part isn't tucked away in a corner of the chart. It sits next to your sense of self.

In plain words, the thing you were once told you were too much of tends to be part of who you are. You may have learned early to dial it down: the loud laugh, the strong opinion, the appetite, the flat refusal. With this conjunction, dialling it down tends to dim you as well, because the two are bound together. I've never read Lilith as something to repair. I read the conjunction as an invitation to put that part back on the table, in a form you choose, with boundaries that are yours.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

In a chart, it tends to show as directness and a strong sense of your own terms, and sometimes as an old habit of hiding exactly what makes you you. The house shows where it appears. In the 3rd, the thing you weren't allowed to say becomes part of how you shine, with a sharp, honest tongue; in the 10th, being seen as “too much” in public sits alongside an authority that doesn't ask permission.

Counting within 8°, about 1 person in 22 is born with the Sun on Lilith. Mean Lilith moves about 40° a year, so the Sun catches up with it only every 13½ months or so, about six and a half weeks later in the calendar each time, and the two stay in orb for some eighteen days. That count uses mean Lilith, the one I use. True Lilith wobbles widely around it, so if your chart shows the true one, the conjunction may or may not be there ([which Lilith is mine?](/astrology/which-lilith-is-mine/)). Lilith is also why every reading I give has a part of its own for what you were told to hide ([Lilith: what you were told to hide](/blog/saturn-chiron-lilith/#lilith)).

Each of the pair's other aspects holds the refused part at another distance:

- **[Sun sextile Lilith](/astrology/sun-sextile-lilith/):** at a friendly distance, there to call on when your purpose needs it.
- **[Sun square Lilith](/astrology/sun-square-lilith/):** it pushes from the side, and the work is choosing when to let it speak.
- **[Sun trine Lilith](/astrology/sun-trine-lilith/):** instinct and identity flow together, so saying no comes without drama.
- **[Sun opposite Lilith](/astrology/sun-opposite-lilith/):** met across the room, in people who seem too much or who refuse you.

[Sun conjunct Lilith in synastry](/astrology/sun-conjunct-lilith-synastry/), one person's Sun on the other's Lilith, tends to bring fascination, and a sense of being seen in a way that's thrilling and exposing at once. It asks for honesty, and I read it both ways.

## In transits {#in-transits}

Mean Lilith moves through a sign in about nine months, so its transits are brief: when it crosses your Sun, it stays within a degree or so for two or three weeks. It comes back to its birth place roughly every nine years, and since your Sun stands there too, each Lilith return also brings it onto your Sun, at about 9, 18, 27, 35 and so on. Once a year, too, the Sun in the sky crosses your natal Lilith for a day or two. Weather, not a forecast: these are moments when an old refusal surfaces and asks whether it still needs hiding, never events.
