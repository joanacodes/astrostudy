Pluto changes sign only a few times a century. It crossed into Aquarius in March 2023, slipped back twice, and settled there on 19 November 2024. It stays until January 2044: about twenty years in which every chart has Pluto moving through the same stretch of the zodiac.

## What it is {#what-it-is}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) takes about 248 years to go round the Sun, and its stretched orbit gives it anywhere from 12 to 31 years in a sign. Its entry into [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/) came in steps, as a slow planet's often does when its yearly retrograde straddles the border. In the dates this site keeps:

- **23 March 2023:** first crossing into Aquarius.
- **11 June 2023:** back into Capricorn.
- **21 January 2024:** into Aquarius again.
- **1 September 2024:** back into Capricorn.
- **19 November 2024:** into Aquarius for good.
- **9 March 2043:** into Pisces, then back into Aquarius on 1 September 2043, and gone for good on 19 January 2044.

Pluto last crossed Aquarius from April 1777 to December 1798. Children born since 2023 mostly carry it in their charts; that natal placement has its own page ([Pluto in Aquarius](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius/)), and I don't read children's charts. For everyone else, it is a transit.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

A transit of Pluto in Aquarius reaches your chart in three ways.

- **Through a house.** Aquarius falls somewhere in every chart, and Pluto now crosses that house, very slowly. Which house it is, and how long the stay lasts, depends on your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)); [Pluto transiting the 4th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-4th-house/) shows what such a stay looks like.
- **Through natal planets in Aquarius.** Pluto meets them one degree at a time: the first degrees of the sign from 2023, the last ones in the early 2040s. With the orbs I use for timing, each meeting usually lasts two to three years.
- **Through aspects.** From Aquarius, Pluto squares planets in Taurus and Scorpio and opposes planets in Leo. Most people born between November 1983 and November 1995, with Pluto in Scorpio, meet their [Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/) in these years. Those born with Pluto in Leo, from October 1937 to June 1958, see transiting Pluto opposite their own, in their mid-eighties.

Aquarius has two rulers, Uranus and, traditionally, Saturn, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). So Pluto in Aquarius has two dispositors whose own movements colour it ([dispositors and the final dispositor](/astrology/dispositors-and-the-final-dispositor/)): Uranus, in Gemini from April 2026 into 2033, and Saturn, in Aries until April 2028, in Taurus until June 2030, then in Gemini.

## In a reading {#in-a-reading}

In the system I built for my readings, Pluto's function is transformation: regeneration, and the clearing out of what has become unsustainable. Aquarius is a manner, not a function: systems thinking, networks, shared aims, the long view. So in the house Pluto crosses, I read a long reworking done in that manner. Whatever that part of your life runs on, its groups, systems and shared projects, gets looked at closely, and what has outlived its use is rebuilt. It is slow work, and it describes a climate, not an event: weather, not a forecast.

These years are crowded. Saturn and Neptune met at 0°45′ Aries in February 2026 ([the Saturn–Neptune conjunction of 2026](/astrology/saturn-neptune-conjunction-of-2026/)), and Neptune stays in Aries until 2039 ([Neptune in Aries, 2026–2039](/astrology/neptune-in-aries-2026-2039/)). I read each slow planet through your houses and natal points one at a time, then together. None of them defines a generation: a sign shared by twenty years of births describes the sky those births came under, never the character of the people born in it ([how I read](/how-i-read/)).

## Common myths {#myths}

- **“Pluto in Aquarius will bring a revolution.”** I don't forecast world events. In a chart, it is a slow reworking of one house and of what it meets there.
- **“It began in March 2023.”** That was the first crossing. Pluto went back twice and settled in Aquarius on 19 November 2024.
- **“It affects everyone the same way.”** The sign is shared; the house it crosses and the planets it meets are yours.
- **“Children born now will all be alike.”** A generation's Pluto sign isn't a personality, and I don't read children's charts.
