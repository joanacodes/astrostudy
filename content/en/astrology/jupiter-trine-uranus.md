Jupiter trine Uranus lets your sense of meaning and your appetite for change pull in the same direction. New ideas tend to widen your outlook rather than shake it, and change, for you, often feels like a form of growth. Like the pair's other aspects, it is shared within a short spell of births.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) grows understanding: it learns, believes, finds the thread that ties experience together. [Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) renews: it innovates, frees, tests alternatives and reorganises systems that have stopped working. A [trine](/astrology/trine/) lets the two reinforce each other, so expanding and renewing feel like one gesture.

In plain words, you're at ease with the new. A change of plan, a new field or an unfamiliar way of thinking can feel exciting rather than threatening, and you often see how it fits into a larger picture. It can show as quick learning, an independent mind that isn't contrary for the sake of it, and a generous openness to people whose lives look nothing like yours.

Flow isn't passivity. Because this comes so easily, it can stay at the level of interest: the open mind that never quite commits to a change. Used deliberately, it becomes a real skill, helping a family, a team or yourself through a transition without losing the thread.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Counting within 6°, it is found in about 1 person in 15 born between 1930 and 2009, in twelve groups of births: September 1931 to August 1932; December 1936 to January 1937 and August to October 1937; October to December 1945 and April to September 1946; March to August 1950 and December 1950 to February 1951; December 1959 to February 1960 and July to November 1960; April to June 1964 and November 1964 to February 1965; February to March 1974; September to November 1977 and May to July 1978; May 1987 to April 1988; October 1991 to February 1992 and July to August 1992; September to October 2000 and May to July 2001; and October 2005 to September 2006.

With so many births sharing each spell, the trine is a cohort's temperament first ([personal, social and generational planets](/astrology/personal-social-and-generational-planets/)); its house makes it yours. Jupiter in the 11th trine Uranus in the 3rd, for example, might show as friendships and groups that keep widening what you think about. Houses need a birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

Beside the trine:

- **[Jupiter conjunct Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-conjunct-uranus/):** the meeting itself, in 1941, 1954–55, 1968–69, 1982–83 or 1997.
- **[Jupiter sextile Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-uranus/):** a narrower opening, for those born in 1943 or 1999, among others.
- **[Jupiter square Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-square-uranus/):** the friction version, for the births of 1944–45 or 2006–07, for example.
- **[Jupiter opposite Uranus](/astrology/jupiter-opposite-uranus/):** the two at opposite ends, for those born in 1933–34, 1975–76 or 1989–90.

With [Jupiter trine Uranus in synastry](/astrology/jupiter-trine-uranus-synastry/), one person's search for meaning and the other's appetite for change fit easily together: good company for travel, study and new ventures.

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting Uranus trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-uranus-trine-natal-jupiter/) lingers through a long season, Uranus usually crossing the exact point three times, and is often read as a stretch in which the new arrives as an opening rather than a jolt. [Transiting Jupiter trine natal Uranus](/astrology/transiting-jupiter-trine-natal-uranus/) is brief, days or weeks each time Jupiter, on its twelve-year round, reaches 120° from your Uranus.

Weather, not a forecast: a favourable wind for change you choose, not a promise that change will come and choose you.
