A sextile to your Midheaven has a second face that's easy to miss: it's also a trine to the IC, the point at the bottom of your chart. So when Saturn opens a door in your public life, it tends to do so with your foundations quietly in agreement.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) organises, commits and builds slowly; the [Midheaven](/astrology/midheaven/) frames how your capacities become visible: the role you play, the responsibility you hold, the contribution others can see. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) relates them through opportunity. During this [transit](/astrology/transits/), public life offers something workable: a responsibility that matches your skills, a course that makes them official, a working arrangement put on a proper footing, a mentor who knows the route. Opportunity needs engagement: the transit opens the door and leaves the walking to you.

The IC side matters. Because the same angle reaches your [IC](/astrology/imum-coeli/) by trine, the step tends to fit your private life rather than strain it: a role that leaves room for home, or a home that can carry a little more work. Visibility isn't value, and this sextile favours the kind of step you'd take even if nobody noticed. Where the trine to your Midheaven tends to firm up what you already do, the sextile usually brings something slightly new: a first step into a role rather than a firmer hold on an old one.

Where your Midheaven falls, and which houses Saturn crosses on its way, both come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Within the orbs I time things by, 1½° before exact and 1° after, a sextile to the Midheaven runs to about nine months. It reaches exact three times a little more often than once, which takes about nine and a half months, and a single exact hit can make it a matter of three weeks. Five of these sextiles by 80 is the common count; a few people have six.

The first comes about five years before Saturn reaches your Midheaven, as it climbs the western side of your chart; the second about five years after, as it heads down towards your Ascendant. Two birth times given by different relatives, half an hour apart, would put these sextiles seven or eight months apart, which is why the recorded time matters.

If your Midheaven is in Gemini or Aquarius, one of these sextiles is happening now: Saturn crosses Aries, two signs from each, until April 2028. [Transiting Saturn conjunct natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-midheaven/) sits between the two sextiles; [transiting Saturn square natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-midheaven/) lies about two and a half years beyond each, [transiting Saturn trine natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-midheaven/) about five, and [transiting Saturn opposite natal Midheaven](/astrology/transiting-saturn-opposite-natal-midheaven/) on the far side of the round, at your IC.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Name the responsibility you'd actually like, and ask for it. Under a sextile, the asking is often the whole difficulty.

Turn a skill you use informally into something recognised: a certificate, a portfolio, a title that matches the work.

Check that the step suits your home life as well as your work. This sextile is at its best when both ends of the axis agree.
