A birthday that turns into a reunion of five circles of friends, a group chat that organises half the street's summer, a cause that started over dinner and now has a committee. It is the 11th house at work: with composite Jupiter there, friends, groups and shared hopes become how the relationship grows.

## What it says about the two of you {#what-it-says}

Of the twelve houses, the 11th is the one tradition gave Jupiter as its joy, the place where it was said to rejoice ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)). Its old name is Good Spirit; it is succedent, and its natural sign is Aquarius. [The 11th house](/astrology/11th-house/) covers friendship, community, networks, collaboration and the hopes people pursue together, and [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) placed here in [the composite chart](/astrology/composite-chart/) makes those the relationship's way of growing.

In practice, the bond widens through other people: the friends you make as a pair, the groups you join or found, the causes you organise around, the future you imagine for more than the two of you. Its generosity is collective: an introduction that changes someone's year, a fundraiser hosted in the garden, an evening a week given to a shared project. Old friends of each of you tend to become friends of both. Neither of you need be sociable alone; as a pair, you may still end up at the centre of a wide circle.

The house is the relationship's own in a second sense. Composite Jupiter can sit in the 11th while each of you has Jupiter somewhere else entirely in your own chart, in the 4th, say, or the 7th, because composite houses are measured from the composite angles, not from either Ascendant. A natal [Jupiter in the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house/), growth through friends and groups, and [their Jupiter in your 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house-synastry/), one person widening the other's circle, needn't be true of either of you for the composite to put Jupiter here. Placing it takes both birth times and one of the two house methods, midpoints of the two charts' cusps or houses built from the composite Midheaven ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

The difficulty is that the circle can crowd out the couple. A bond that grows through friends and causes can fill every weekend with other people, measure its worth by how many it brings together, or put the group's hopes before its own. Friendship isn't popularity, and a shared vision isn't a crowd. What helps is a standing appointment the two of you keep alone, and the courage to leave a group when the bond has outgrown it.

The 10th, before it, puts growth on public show ([composite Jupiter in the 10th house](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-10th-house/)); the 12th, after it, keeps it out of sight ([composite Jupiter in the 12th house](/astrology/composite-jupiter-in-12th-house/)). Composites aren't among the charts I read, and this is how the technique reads Jupiter in its joy, always alongside both natal charts and the [synastry](/astrology/synastry/); a joy in tradition is not a promise of happiness. A chart your friends might one day hear about should be one you both chose to have drawn ([why I won't read a synastry behind someone's back](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).
