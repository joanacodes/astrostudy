Of Pluto's contacts with the Moon, the square is the one most people meet: about seven in eight by the age of 80. For two to three years it sets the planet of renewal at right angles to the habits you use to feel safe, and the habits that no longer work give way.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Pluto](/astrology/pluto/) transforms what has become unsustainable. Your [Moon](/astrology/moon/) regulates your feelings, your needs and the habits that keep you steady, many of them learned long ago. A [square](/astrology/square/), 90°, relates the two through friction that forces adaptation: under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), comforts that worked for years start to cost more than they give. The television that used to end the day, the busyness that kept feelings at bay, the role of the one who never needs anything: each can stop doing its job.

Some of those habits were survival strategies, and they served you when you needed them. What makes the square hard is that they feel like who you are. They aren't; they are what you did to get through. Friction isn't failure, either. The square asks you to adapt, and adapting here means finding ways of feeling safe that fit the person you are now. I read it as weather, not a forecast: a climate around your feelings, never an event waiting to happen.

When the friction turns into weeks of low mood, or your sleep and appetite change, the person to see is a doctor or a therapist, not an astrologer ([Astrology and mental health](/astrology/astrology-and-mental-health/)).

With your Moon in the 2nd and Pluto crossing the 11th ([Pluto transiting the 11th house](/astrology/pluto-transit-11th-house/)), what makes you feel secure rubs against friendships, groups and plans for the future that are changing shape. The houses depend on your birth time, and so does the Moon's degree; without the time I don't read the chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). Born with [Moon square Pluto](/astrology/moon-square-pluto/), you meet a related season when Pluto squares its own birth place ([Pluto square](/astrology/pluto-square/)): Pluto then reaches your Moon by conjunction or by opposition, depending on which side the Moon stood.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

With the orbs I use for timing, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the square runs for about two years and eight months, and the longest passes for nearly four. In about three passes out of five Pluto crosses the exact square three times; in the others, five times, over about three years and seven months.

There are two squares on your Moon's circle, a quarter-turn either side of it. To reach both, Pluto would have to travel half the zodiac, and a lifetime gives it less, but almost every Moon has one of them within reach: about one person in eight born between 1930 and 2009 meets neither by 80. One square comes after the conjunction ([Transiting Pluto conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-conjunct-natal-moon/)) and the sextile ([Transiting Pluto sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-sextile-natal-moon/)), as Pluto heads for the trine ([Transiting Pluto trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-trine-natal-moon/)); the other comes after the opposition ([Transiting Pluto opposite natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-pluto-opposite-natal-moon/)), on the way back round.

The squares of the 2020s, the 2030s and the early 2040s fall on Moons in Taurus and Scorpio, since Pluto stays in Aquarius until early 2044 ([Pluto in Aquarius, 2024–2044](/astrology/pluto-in-aquarius-2024-2044/)); a Moon early in either sign meets its square first.

## What helps {#what-helps}

List the comforts you reach for when things are hard and, next to each, whether it still helps. Keep the ones that do.

Replace before you remove. A habit taken away with nothing in its place tends to come back.

Ask for practical help early, while the friction is still small: a lift, a meal, an hour on the phone.
