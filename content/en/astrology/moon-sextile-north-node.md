Moon sextile North Node puts the Moon two signs or so from the North Node, and four from the South Node. It's the aspect of small openings: your needs and the direction you're growing in can help each other, as soon as you give them the chance.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [Moon](/astrology/moon/) looks after your feelings and needs: comfort, attachment, the habits that keep you steady. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to a capacity you haven't practised much yet, one that widens your range when you do. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/) is an aspect of opportunity: it lets the two cooperate without either absorbing the other, and each can set the other in motion.

In plain words, the things that make you feel at home tend to lead, one small step at a time, towards what you're learning: a friend who makes the unfamiliar feel safe, a routine that leaves room for practice, a mood that makes you curious rather than wary. Nothing pushes you. The sextile opens a door and waits, which is both its kindness and its catch.

What makes this sextile particular is its other half. The nodes are always exactly opposite, so a Moon 60° from the North Node is 120° from the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/): a trine to the familiar end. Your needs flow easily with what you already know how to do. I read the two together: the trine keeps you supplied, the sextile keeps you moving, and growth comes from letting the first feed the second.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

Sextiles usually link signs two apart, in elements that get on, so your Moon tends to share a temperament with the North Node's sign without sitting in it. The Moon's house is where the openings come from, and the North Node's house is where they lead: a Moon in the 3rd sextile the North Node in the 5th might make conversation the way into play and things of your own making.

About 1 person in 11 is born with the Moon within 8° of a sextile to the North Node. The Moon makes it twice a month, once on each side of the node, for about a day each time. On one side the Moon is climbing north of the Sun's path towards its highest point; on the other, it's coming up from the south towards the node. Mean and true node can differ by a degree or two, so near the edge of the orb I check both.

Beside the pair's other aspects:

- **[Moon conjunct North Node](/astrology/moon-conjunct-north-node/):** no gap at all; the Moon sits on the growing edge, more intense and less of a choice.
- **[Moon square North Node](/astrology/moon-square-north-node/):** a quarter turn on, the Moon stands across the path, and the openings turn into pressure.
- **[Moon trine North Node](/astrology/moon-trine-north-node/):** this aspect's mirror image, with the trine pointing forward and the sextile back towards the familiar.
- **[Moon opposite North Node](/astrology/moon-opposite-north-node/):** the Moon sitting right on the South Node, where comfort and the familiar share one place.

Between two charts, [Moon sextile North Node in synastry](/astrology/moon-sextile-north-node-synastry/) sets one person's Moon two signs from the other's North Node: a bond that keeps offering a next step, gently. I read it from both sides, and never without both people’s consent ([synastry and consent](/blog/synastry-behind-their-back/)).

## In transits {#in-transits}

[Transiting North Node sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-moon/) comes round twice per nodal cycle of 18.6 years, once from each side. Moving backwards at about 18 months a sign, the node holds the sextile, within a degree, for a month or two. The Moon in the sky, for its part, sextiles your natal North Node twice a month. I treat both as weather, not a forecast: small invitations, easy to miss, and yours to take or leave.
