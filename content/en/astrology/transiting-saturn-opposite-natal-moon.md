Fifteen years or so after Saturn crosses your Moon, it reaches the far side of it. The needs your Moon describes, rest, comfort, belonging, now face Saturn from across the chart, and the face Saturn wears is often someone else's.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Saturn](/astrology/saturn/) stands for duty, limits and what lasts; the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) for feelings, needs and the sense of being safe. An [opposition](/astrology/opposition/) relates them through polarity, each end showing most clearly against the other. In this [transit](/astrology/transits/), that often means your needs meet other people's expectations, a partner's, an employer's, a family's, or the plain demands of a commitment, and the two have to be weighed in the open.

It can be uncomfortable, because arrangements that worked while nobody said anything now ask to be said. Some people feel more alone with their needs for a while. I read that as a call to name them, not as proof that nobody cares. The opposition doesn't predict a separation or any other ending; it describes a balance that needs resetting.

You may also notice how much of your day goes on adapting to others. The Moon is responsive by nature, and Saturn opposite it shows where responsiveness has turned into always giving way. The work is to keep your care for other people without losing track of your own.

The Moon's house shows which needs are on the scales. With the Moon in the 3rd, the opposition comes while Saturn crosses your 9th, so what you believe or plan far ahead pulls against daily habits and close ties ([Saturn transiting the 9th house](/astrology/saturn-transit-9th-house/)). Those houses, and the Moon's exact degree, come from your birth time ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If [Moon opposite Saturn](/astrology/moon-opposite-saturn/) is in your chart, you've known this tug since birth; the transit gives it new people and a new setting.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Needs and duties usually stay face to face for about nine months, counted with my orbs of 1½° before exact and 1° after. In a little over half the cases, Saturn's retrograde turn ([Saturn retrograde](/astrology/saturn-retrograde/)) makes it a loop: three exact passes in about nine and a half months. At full speed, three weeks.

It comes two or three times by 80 for people born between 1930 and 2009. It falls at the far point of each round, about fifteen years after [transiting Saturn conjunct natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-conjunct-natal-moon/) and fifteen before the next. Its nearest neighbours are the trines: [transiting Saturn trine natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-trine-natal-moon/) comes about five years before and five after, often a steadier stretch to prepare in or recover in. Further off sit [transiting Saturn square natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-square-natal-moon/), about seven years away on either side, and [transiting Saturn sextile natal Moon](/astrology/transiting-saturn-sextile-natal-moon/), about ten.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say what you need in a sentence someone else can act on. Oppositions respond to clarity: “I need two evenings a week to myself” gets further than a general sense of strain.

Ask what the other side needs, too. Half of what feels like a demand is often someone else's need that hasn't been said either.

Keep one comfort that depends on nobody's approval: a walk, a book, a friend outside the situation. It lets you weigh things from steadier ground.
