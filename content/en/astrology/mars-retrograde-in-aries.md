In the forty-one years of dates I work from, Mars turns back in its own sign only once: from 9 September to 14 November 2020. For those nine weeks the planet of drive went back over its home ground, starting, competing and standing up for yourself, in the sign that gives those things their quickest form.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Aries](/astrology/aries/) is cardinal fire, and Mars rules it: here the planet acts first and asks afterwards. During [Mars retrograde](/astrology/mars-retrograde/), when Earth overtakes the red planet and it seems from here to slide backwards against the stars, that speed has nowhere to go but over its own tracks. What slows is the launch: the plan announced before it was ready, the contest you meant to win, the impatience with anyone who moves at another pace. Desire turns inward too, and the question shifts from how fast you can get something to whether you still want it.

The 2020 loop began at 28°08′ Aries on 9 September and ended at 15°14′ on 14 November: 65 days over just under 13°, all of it inside the sign. Around the middle, in October, Mars stood opposite the Sun, then in Libra, at its brightest.

Nothing else between 2000 and 2040 reaches Aries. Its neighbours come close: in 2035 Mars stations at 28°26′ Pisces ([Mars retrograde in Pisces](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-pisces/)) without crossing the line, and the loops of 2005 and 2037 work through Taurus ([Mars retrograde in Taurus](/astrology/mars-retrograde-in-taurus/)). Being born with Mars retrograde in Aries is another matter, a placement of your own rather than a season, and I write about it in [natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The house Aries opens in your chart, and so the part of life where this loop pressed the brake, comes from [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and I don’t read a chart without one. In Placidus, the house system I use, 13° of retrograde can straddle a cusp, so two neighbouring lines may both speak to you.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/aries-rising/):** Aries rising: the way you charge into a room; a first move, slowed enough for you to watch yourself make it.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/aries-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** the drive behind what you build up for yourself; a goal chased at full tilt, checked against what you value.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/aries-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** quick replies and daily errands; a sharp exchange with a sibling or neighbour, replayed until you see your own part in it.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/aries-on-4th-house-cusp/):** a home where you take charge; a project under your roof begun at speed, wanting a second plan.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/aries-on-5th-house-cusp/):** sport, flirting and creative dares; the need to win even at play, seen with some humour.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/aries-on-6th-house-cusp/):** Mars’s traditional joy, also given to illness; I read it as daily work, never a forecast about health ([Can astrology predict health problems?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-health-problems/)): the pace you set yourself, brought down to one you can keep.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/aries-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Aries on the Descendant, with Libra rising: the other person brings the fire, and who leads in the partnership comes up for discussion.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/aries-on-8th-house-cusp/):** desire and what you share with someone close; a need you pressed hard, or never voiced, looked at honestly.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/aries-on-9th-house-cusp/):** a belief you would fight for; a crusade or a journey taken up on impulse, reconsidered.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/aries-on-10th-house-cusp/):** Aries on the Midheaven: ambition that wants to lead; a bid for a role, rethought before the next push.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/aries-on-11th-house-cusp/):** a group you set in motion; the cause you championed for everyone, weighed again with them.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/aries-on-12th-house-cusp/):** anger kept to yourself; the fight you never had, thought through in private.

## What helps {#what-helps}

When drive turns back in its own sign, I like the review to involve the body as well as the head: a long walk or a hard swim spends restlessness that has no target yet. Then go back to what you started and left. Aries begins more than it finishes, and these weeks are a fair time to choose which beginnings still deserve your effort.

The house your natal Mars occupies matters too: it is where your drive lives, and where its review tends to echo. If anger surfaces, it belongs to you and to the situation, not to the sky, and saying it plainly usually costs less than swallowing it or throwing it. None of this decides how anything ends; it describes a season in which the fastest sign asks itself what it was running towards.
