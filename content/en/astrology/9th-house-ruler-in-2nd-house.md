With the ruler of your 9th house in the 2nd, what you know, believe and have seen of the world turns into something you keep, spend and live by: books on the shelf, skills you're paid for, values you won't sell.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/) covers the far side of experience: study, travel, faith, philosophy, teaching and publishing. Its ruler, the planet of the sign on its cusp, takes those matters to the house where it sits ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)), and here that's the [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/): the money you earn and how you earn it, your belongings, the skills you could live from, and underneath them your sense of what you're worth.

Knowledge, here, is treated as something of value, in both senses of the word. The old lists make the link almost literal: horary astrology gave the 9th house books and the 2nd movable possessions, so a library built over the years is the 9th's matter in the 2nd's keeping. Many people with this placement live, partly or wholly, from what they've learned: teaching, translating, guiding travellers, editing, explaining a law or a scripture to others. Others simply spend that way, on courses, tickets and books before furniture. Your values tend to come from your convictions rather than from habit, and earning in a way you don't believe in can cost you more than it pays.

The risk runs the other way: a belief priced too high, or the feeling that you're worth only what you know. Learning isn't a bank balance, and no amount of study buys certainty. What it describes is where meaning and money cross in your life. It says nothing about how much you'll earn, and nothing about where to put it ([can astrology pick investments?](/astrology/can-astrology-pick-investments/)).

Counted by sign, the 2nd is the sixth sign from the 9th, a distance that makes none of Ptolemy's aspects, so nothing in the geometry ties the two houses together: the ruler does the linking alone. Venus, ruling a Taurus or Libra 9th, makes the link easily, since the natural zodiac gives the 2nd to Taurus ([Venus in the 2nd house](/astrology/venus-in-2nd-house/)): beauty in ideas is something you value and pay for gladly. Mercury, ruling a Gemini or Virgo 9th, earns through words and explanations. Jupiter, ruling a Sagittarius 9th, widens what you value more than what you own.

In Placidus, the house system I use, neither cusp is an angle. Each is found by dividing into three the time the sky takes to turn from one angle to the next, so both shift with the minute of birth and the latitude of the place ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). A ruler close to the 2nd cusp would, with a slightly later time, already sit in the 1st, where belief shows in the person ([Ruler of the 9th in the 1st house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/)). Near the other end of the house, a slightly earlier time would leave it in the 3rd, where big ideas meet everyday words ([Ruler of the 9th in the 3rd house](/astrology/9th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/)).

When Scorpio opens your 9th ([Scorpio on the 9th house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-9th-house-cusp/)), both its rulers count, the traditional and the modern, Mars and Pluto, and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). Mars in the 2nd turns conviction into effort: you'll work hard for what you believe in, and want your money to stand for something. Pluto, wherever it sits, tells a slower story about how changing what you believe changes what you're willing to keep.
