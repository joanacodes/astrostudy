This is Uranus in the sign modern tropical astrology gave it: the planet of change in the sign of the group and the future. At home, Uranus does its work with the fewest detours, which makes this generation's wish for a fairer, freer system unusually plain to see.

## In a birth chart {#in-a-birth-chart}

[Uranus](/astrology/uranus/) stands for innovation, autonomy and reform; [Aquarius](/astrology/aquarius/), fixed air, is the sign of networks, friendship, principles and shared hopes. In modern tropical astrology Uranus rules Aquarius, which makes this its [domicile](/astrology/domicile/); the tradition gives the sign to [Saturn](/astrology/saturn/), and I read both ([traditional and modern rulers](/astrology/traditional-and-modern-rulers/)). In practice that means two questions: where Uranus sits by house, which is where this generation's independence becomes yours, and where Saturn sits, which shows the structure that gives that independence a shape. Fixed air holds on to ideas: once this placement has decided what's fair, it isn't easily talked out of it.

The Aquarius generation was born from April 1995 to December 2003, and Uranus won't return to the sign until March 2079, staying to February 2087. About 1 person in 10 born from 1930 to 2009 has it, the largest share of any sign in that count. Capricorn shares its first months, April 1995 to January 1996, and Pisces its last, March to December 2003, while Uranus was crossing back and forth ([Uranus in Capricorn](/astrology/uranus-in-capricorn/), [Uranus in Pisces](/astrology/uranus-in-pisces/)); there, the chart has the last word. Until February 2001, on and off, many were also born with [Uranus sextile Pluto](/astrology/uranus-sextile-pluto/). The sign speaks for the cohort; the house is where the network, the group or the principle turns personal.

## In close relationships {#in-close-relationships}

In love, this Uranus wants friendship first: a partner who is also an ally, who shares your causes, and who doesn't need you to belong to them. Space isn't a sign of cooling; it's how you stay. You may build relationships that look unusual from outside and work very well from inside. What you learn over time is that warmth matters as much as principle, and that a partner is a person, not a project. Shared projects can be a love language here: building something together often says more than any declaration.

## At work {#at-work}

At work, Uranus in Aquarius builds networks and systems: collective projects, research, campaigns, communities, technology, anything where many minds work together on something new. You notice how a whole system could run better and may not wait to be asked. You do best among peers rather than in a strict hierarchy, and with a mission you believe in. Saturn's side of the sign shows here too: the rules you write for a group can be the most useful thing you make.

## The hard part {#the-hard-part}

The hard part is detachment that passes for freedom. With Uranus at home in its sign, you can live so much in ideas and causes that feelings are kept at arm's length, or hold principles so firmly that a friend's different view feels like a betrayal. Autonomy is not isolation, and differentiation is not alienation: you can stand for something without standing alone. Change pursued for the group can also leave your own needs last in the queue.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Let Saturn, the traditional ruler, give your ideas a form and a timetable. Choose one cause and see it through. Keep a few friendships that have nothing to do with any cause at all. The [Uranus opposition](/astrology/uranus-opposition/) comes for this generation while Uranus crosses Leo, from August 2039 to July 2046: the heart and personal expression, facing people who first learned to think in terms of the group. A home sign earns no exemption in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/): I checked Uranus there like every placement, and it was not one of the three that held far more often, while about a third held overall.
