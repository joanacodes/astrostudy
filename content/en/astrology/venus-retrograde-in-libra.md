Venus’s autumn retrograde begins in Scorpio and finishes in Libra, so Libra gets its second half: the weeks when Venus, still moving backwards, comes home to its own sign and turns there. The most recent ended on 14 November 2026.

## What slows down {#what-slows-down}

[Libra](/astrology/libra/) is cardinal air and one of Venus’s two own signs: the scales, fairness, proportion, the give-and-take between two people. When [Venus retrograde](/astrology/venus-retrograde/) backs into Libra, the planet seems from Earth to retreat across its home ground for the last weeks before it turns, and what slows is Venus’s own work: whether an exchange is still mutual, whether harmony is real or merely kept up, whether you’ve been agreeable at the expense of what you value.

In my list, four retrogrades between 2000 and 2040 reach Libra, eight years apart, each starting in Scorpio ([Venus retrograde in Scorpio](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-scorpio/)) and turning direct about 2⅓° deeper into Libra than the one before: 27°39′ on 18 November 2010, 25°14′ on 16 November 2018, 22°51′ on 14 November 2026, 20°27′ on 11 November 2034. The most recent ran from 3 October, starting at 8°29′ Scorpio; the next begins on 30 September 2034 at 6°07′ Scorpio. The list also files the 2002 retrograde here, but both its stations fell in Scorpio, at 15°36′ and 0°03′: by its stations it never came back into Libra. None reaches as far back as Virgo ([Venus retrograde in Virgo](/astrology/venus-retrograde-in-virgo/)).

Venus retrograde in Libra in a birth chart is a different study, fairness weighed inwardly before it’s offered, and I read it as such ([natal retrograde planets](/blog/retrograde-planets/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

Placing Libra in your houses takes [your birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/), and so does every line below; that dependence is why I never read a chart without it. In Placidus, the house system I use, these retrogrades start in the house where Scorpio sits and end in Libra’s, so two neighbouring houses usually share the weeks.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/libra-rising/):** Libra rising: your graceful, accommodating manner; how much of it is choice and how much is keeping the peace.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/libra-on-2nd-house-cusp/):** what feels fair to pay and to own; the value of what you have, set against what you give.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/libra-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** diplomatic words; the point you softened so much that it went unsaid.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/libra-on-4th-house-cusp/):** peace at home; the compromises that keep it, and whether everyone shares them.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/libra-on-5th-house-cusp/):** Venus’s joy: romance and the arts; the pleasures you enjoy as a pair, and those you enjoy on your own.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/libra-on-6th-house-cusp/):** harmony at work; the colleague you always accommodate, and what fairness would look like.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/libra-on-7th-house-cusp/):** Libra on the Descendant, with Aries rising, in its natural [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/): partnership itself; the balance of give and take, looked at with no verdict attached.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/libra-on-8th-house-cusp/):** what’s shared, and whether fairly; joint commitments and trust, weighed again.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/libra-on-9th-house-cusp/):** ideals of justice and beauty; a belief or a place you love for its balance, revisited.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/libra-on-10th-house-cusp/):** a public role built on tact; the agreements you broker for others, and the ones you’d like for yourself.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/libra-on-11th-house-cusp/):** friendships among equals; who gives more in the circle, and whether that suits you both.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/libra-on-12th-house-cusp/):** private compromises; what you gave up quietly to keep things smooth, looked at kindly.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Here the planet turning back is the sign’s own ruler, so look at the house of your natal Venus as well as the Libra house, and notice where the two meet. Fairness includes you.

These weeks, as I read them, suit saying what would make an arrangement feel even, rather than waiting for the scales to settle by themselves. They decide nothing about how a relationship turns out.
