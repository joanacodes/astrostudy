For a year and a half or so the North Node backs through your 8th house, where closeness, trust and what's held in common live, and the South Node backs through your 2nd, where you keep what's yours. These are months of learning to let something be joint, and to let it matter.

## What changes {#what-changes}

The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) points to growth that's awkward before it's natural. The [8th house](/astrology/8th-house/) is intimacy and trust: what you share with others and what you owe them, the closeness that changes you. Here the practice, slow and sometimes uncomfortable, is letting people in: accepting help, telling the truth about what you need, entering an arrangement where means or feelings are held in common.

The [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) in your 2nd knows the opposite habit well: self-sufficiency, keeping your own account, never quite depending on anyone. It's a sturdy skill, and you keep it. The stretch is to stop it becoming a wall.

Scorpio, the 8th's natural sign, has two rulers, Mars, the traditional one, and Pluto, the modern one, and I read both: with either natal in your 8th, questions of control and trust tend to come forward while the node crosses it.

Death and inheritance belonged to this house in the tradition. A [transit](/astrology/transits/) predicts neither ([can astrology predict death?](/astrology/can-astrology-predict-death/)), and I don't read debts, legacies or joint money for their outcome. What this one stirs is what your chart already holds about closeness.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

The 8th shares its figures with the 2nd across from it. In Placidus the two houses are always the same size and the nodes always exactly opposite, so the North Node's stay here and the South Node's stay in your 2nd begin and end on the same days. In charts drawn at London's latitude that usually means about a year and a half, with most stays between about 11 months and 2 years and 5 months; at New York's latitude, between about a year and 2 years, around the same middle.

The figures come from the true node, with a return over the cusp inside 120 days kept in one stay. I look at the mean node alongside it ([mean node and true node](/astrology/mean-node-and-true-node/)); the two rarely part by more than a degree or two.

The cusps of your 8th need your birth time, as every house does, and without the time I don't read a chart ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). The node reaches the 8th from your 9th ([North Node transiting the 9th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-9th-house/)) and passes into your 7th ([North Node transiting the 7th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-7th-house/)).

It leaves your 8th by crossing the cusp, while it's in that cusp's sign: Aquarius between July 2026 and March 2028, Capricorn to September 2029, Sagittarius to March 2031, Scorpio, the 8th's own sign, to December 2032, Libra to June 2034, Virgo to November 2035, Leo to May 2037 and Cancer to February 2039. An 8th cusp in Pisces saw the node leave between January 2025 and July 2026.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Say one true thing about what you need to someone who can hear it.

Make a shared arrangement clear: who holds what, who owes what, written down if that helps you both.

Keep the South Node's steadiness for yourself. Trusting someone doesn't mean giving up your footing; it means standing on it while you reach out.
