Most people meet their first Chiron sextile to Jupiter as children, and the ones that matter to an adult come later. When it arrives, it tends to look like an invitation: a class, a mentor, a group, a chance to put into words what an old difficulty has taught you.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) widens your view: it learns, believes, plans and looks for what something means. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) describes a tender place that lasts and the competence that grows from living with it. A [sextile](/astrology/sextile/), 60°, relates two functions through cooperation, and cooperation needs someone to say yes. Under this [transit](/astrology/transits/), growth and your old sore spot can work on the same project.

The opening is usually an exchange of knowledge. Someone who has lived with the same difficulty offers to show you what they learned; a course appears that explains it in terms you can use; a group forms around it, or you're asked to speak to one. Some people study the very thing that once hurt them, others discover they can teach it. Jupiter's opportunities are not guaranteed success, and Chiron's skills don't remove the difficulty; together, under a sextile, they make the next step easier to take.

With [Jupiter in the 11th house](/astrology/jupiter-in-11th-house/), where hopes are shared with friends and groups, Chiron is often crossing your 1st ([Chiron transiting the 1st house](/astrology/chiron-transit-1st-house/)): a group you belong to helps you show up as you are. Those houses need your birth time, and without it I don't read the sextile ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). If your chart holds [Jupiter sextile Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-sextile-chiron/), the cooperation is already part of you; the transit gives it an occasion.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Counted from 1½° before exact to 1° after, my orbs, a pass usually lasts about ten months. In nearly two passes out of three, Chiron crosses the exact sextile three times on its retrograde loop; about one in three is a single crossing of some three weeks; about one in twenty is a five-crossing stretch of roughly twenty-one months.

Jupiter's sign changes about once a year, so the age at the first sextile depends a great deal on when you were born. In the site's count it came at about 11 in the middle, usually in childhood, and as late as about 40: within weeks of birth for someone born at the start of 1965, at about 4 for 1945, 14 for 1985, 27 for 1955 and 38 for 2005. I don't read children's charts ([children's charts](/astrology/childrens-charts/)), so for an adult it's the later sextiles that count, and there are usually two more. Most people meet three by 80, some two, some four.

Each round of about 50 years has two sextiles: one after [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-jupiter/) and before [transiting Chiron square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-jupiter/), the other after [transiting Chiron opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-jupiter/), [transiting Chiron trine natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-trine-natal-jupiter/) and the second square, as Chiron comes home. Ahead: Chiron in Taurus, from June 2026 to May 2034, sextiles Jupiter in Cancer and Pisces; in Gemini, from July 2033 to April 2039, Jupiter in Leo and Aries.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Sign up for the course, the workshop or the conversation you keep putting off.

Ask someone who has lived with the same difficulty what helped them, and take notes.

Offer what you know where it's wanted: a talk, a letter, an hour with someone at the start of the road.

Join one group that gathers around the thing you used to keep quiet about.
