Generosity comes most easily from a place you know. When Chiron trines your Jupiter, an old sore spot and your sense of what things mean stop arguing, and what you learned from the difficulty becomes something you can hand to someone else without reopening it.

## What changes {#what-changes}

[Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) is where a chart grows: by learning, believing, giving and seeing the larger shape of a life. [Chiron](/astrology/chiron/) is where a limitation stays and a hard-won competence grows around it. A [trine](/astrology/trine/), 120°, lets the two support each other while each keeps its own character, so during this [transit](/astrology/transits/) perspective on the old difficulty tends to come without being forced.

You may notice that you can speak about it calmly for the first time, or that a younger person brings you exactly the question you once had nobody to ask. Some people write the account they wished they'd been given; others take on a mentoring role, formal or not; others go back to study a subject the difficulty had closed off. A belief that once felt like armour can relax into plain understanding. None of this happens because of the trine. It's easier because of it, which is a different thing, and the ease can pass unused if nobody picks it up.

With [Jupiter in the 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-in-3rd-house/), where you learn, talk and write, Chiron is often crossing your 11th ([Chiron transiting the 11th house](/astrology/chiron-transit-11th-house/)): what you have to say finds a group that needs it. The houses holding Jupiter and Chiron can change within a couple of hours of a birth, so I need the time before I read the trine ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). With [Jupiter trine Chiron](/astrology/jupiter-trine-chiron/) at birth, this generosity around the tender place is already yours, and the transit gives it room.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

Perspective arrives on Chiron's timetable rather than yours. With my orbs, from 1½° before exact to 1° after, the trine to your Jupiter usually lasts about ten months, and in nearly two passes out of three Chiron crosses it three times, once moving backwards. About one pass in three is a single three-week crossing, quickly gone; about one in twenty is a long five-crossing stretch of some twenty-one months.

The age at the first trine depends on where Jupiter stood when you were born, and Jupiter moves about a sign a year, so it varies widely. In the site's count it came at about 11½ in the middle, and as late as about 42: at about 3 for someone born at the start of 2005, 5 for 1935, 10 for 1985, 15 for 1975, 24 for 1995 and 30 for 1965. Most people have three of these trines behind them by 80; a few have two, a few four.

The first trine comes between [transiting Chiron square natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-square-natal-jupiter/) and [transiting Chiron opposite natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-opposite-natal-jupiter/); the second after the opposition, before the second square and [transiting Chiron sextile natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-sextile-natal-jupiter/) lead back to [transiting Chiron conjunct natal Jupiter](/astrology/transiting-chiron-conjunct-natal-jupiter/). By sign: Jupiter in Leo or Sagittarius has its trine before April 2027, Jupiter in Virgo or Capricorn between June 2026 and May 2034, Jupiter in Libra or Aquarius between July 2033 and April 2039.

## What helps {#what-helps}

Write down what the old difficulty taught you, plainly, as if for someone starting out.

Answer the question someone brings you, even if it's the one that used to hurt.

Study something for the pleasure of it, especially a subject you once thought wasn't for you.

Give time rather than advice. Company is often the more generous gift.
