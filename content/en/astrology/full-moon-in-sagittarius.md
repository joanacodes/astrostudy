The full moon in Sagittarius is the last full moon before the June solstice: opposite a Sun riding high in Gemini, the Moon stays low over the southern horizon through the short northern nights. It lights up what you believe against what you know, and in 2030 it's a partial lunar eclipse.

## What it brings {#what-it-brings}

The full Moon sits opposite the Sun, lit from rim to rim. This one is in [Sagittarius](/astrology/sagittarius/), mutable fire, with the Sun on the same degree of [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/). The Moon brings Sagittarius's side to a peak: the beliefs that keep you going, the wish to get away, the big question that won't wait. The Sun in Gemini keeps the month busy with facts, messages and the next small thing. Think of the [full moon in Gemini](/astrology/full-moon-in-gemini/) seen from the far end: here the Moon carries meaning and the Sun the details. What tends to show is where conviction has outrun the evidence, or where so much information has piled up that you've lost the thread. [Jupiter](/astrology/jupiter/) rules Sagittarius and [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/) Gemini, and their houses tell you where each side lives in your chart.

It is a short marker for all that. I count this [full moon](/astrology/full-moon/) only when one of its two degrees falls on a natal planet or angle, within about 1°, and then as the moment something set going by a transit or progression comes to a head. There's no forecast in it.

The most recent was on 31 May 2026, at 9°56′ Sagittarius. The next falls on 19 June 2027 at 00:45 UT, at 27°33′, two or three days before the solstice; then 7 June 2028 at 17°02′, 27 May 2029 at 6°45′ and 15 June 2030 at 24°43′, a partial [lunar eclipse](/astrology/lunar-eclipses/).

That eclipse of 2030 follows, by a fortnight, the annular solar eclipse of 1 June in Gemini, in the same run that brings eclipses to this axis in 2029 and 2030 ([eclipse in Sagittarius](/astrology/eclipse-in-sagittarius/)). A partial eclipse gets a 3° orb from me, at the Sagittarius end or the Gemini end, and it's read through that point and its house: emphasis, never an omen.

The [new moon in Gemini](/astrology/new-moon-in-gemini/) shares this full moon's solar month; in 2030 it is that annular eclipse of 1 June. A month earlier, the Moon was full in Scorpio ([full moon in Scorpio](/astrology/full-moon-in-scorpio/)); a month later it will be full in Capricorn ([full moon in Capricorn](/astrology/full-moon-in-capricorn/)).

## House by house {#house-by-house}

The Moon's house is whichever holds its Sagittarius degree, the Sun's the house opposite, and both wait on your birth time, as I explain in [what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/). Placidus is the system I use. In 2028 the full moon sits mid-sign, at 17°02′: if Sagittarius spans two of your houses, check which side of the cusp it falls.

1. **[1st house](/astrology/1st-house/):** Sagittarius rising: a need for freedom and a wider horizon shows, while a partner fills the month with talk and plans.
2. **[2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/):** spending on a trip, a course or a cause, set against the practical details of shared money.
3. **[3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house/):** everyday talk turns philosophical, while the Sun in the 9th fills your study or travel plans with facts.
4. **[4th house](/astrology/4th-house/):** home feels too small for your plans, while work asks for quick, many-sided communication.
5. **[5th house](/astrology/5th-house/):** an adventure taken for joy, a love that widens your world, set against a crowded social diary.
6. **[6th house](/astrology/6th-house/):** restlessness with the daily round comes to a head, while private thoughts in the 12th need sorting.
7. **[7th house](/astrology/7th-house/):** Sagittarius on the Descendant: a partner who wants freedom, or wants to travel, while you're busy with a dozen things.
8. **[8th house](/astrology/8th-house/):** shared money or trust put to the test of a big plan, while you count your own resources carefully.
9. **[9th house](/astrology/9th-house/):** Sagittarius's own house: a journey, a course or a belief at its peak, while daily errands keep calling.
10. **[10th house](/astrology/10th-house/):** Sagittarius on the Midheaven: a public role with a mission comes into view, while home is all comings and goings.
11. **[11th house](/astrology/11th-house/):** a circle bound by ideals, a cause that inspires, set against a light, playful pleasure of your own.
12. **[12th house](/astrology/12th-house/):** a private search for meaning, while the working day is full of small practical tasks.

## What helps {#what-helps}

- **Check both ends.** Allow a degree on each side of the positions above, in Sagittarius or at the same degree of Gemini; about three for the eclipse of 2030.
- **Test one belief against one fact.** Sagittarius brings the conviction, Gemini the evidence, and this full moon goes best when the two talk to each other.
- **Read Jupiter and Mercury by house.** One shows where you look for meaning, the other where you look for information.
