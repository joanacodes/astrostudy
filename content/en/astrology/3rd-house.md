The 3rd house is the house of the everyday mind: how you think, talk, write and learn, and the people and places close at hand. It's the house of short distances — the next street, the next message, the next thing to understand.

## What it covers {#what-it-covers}

I read the 3rd house as the traffic of daily life: messages, conversations, errands, what you read on the way somewhere, and the way your mind moves through an ordinary day. It also covers the people you didn't choose but grew up alongside — brothers and sisters, cousins, neighbours — and the first learning, before anyone asked what you wanted to study. Where it touches family, it describes your experience of those ties, not who the others are.

- **A cadent house.** It follows a succedent one, and cadent houses are read as places of adjusting, learning and passing things on: the 3rd takes what the 2nd gathered and puts it into circulation. [Angular, succedent and cadent houses](/astrology/angular-succedent-and-cadent-houses/) explains the three kinds.
- **Its natural sign and planet.** [Gemini](/astrology/gemini/) and [Mercury](/astrology/mercury/): curiosity, words, movement.
- **The Goddess.** Tradition gave it that name and said the [Moon](/astrology/moon/) rejoices here ([planetary joys](/astrology/planetary-joys/)): the quickest of the lights, at home among daily comings and goings.
- **Its opposite.** The [9th house](/astrology/9th-house/), which tradition called the God, where the Sun has its joy. The 3rd is the near — short trips, first lessons, the neighbourhood — and the 9th the far: long journeys, higher study, big questions.

A house is read with its neighbours. The [2nd house](/astrology/2nd-house/), just before it, is what you have; the 3rd is what you do with it day to day, in words and errands. The [4th house](/astrology/4th-house/), just after it, is home: the place all the 3rd's comings and goings lead back to.

Like every house, the 3rd is placed by your birth time. Without it, I can read your Mercury by sign, but not the house it speaks from ([what an unknown birth time changes](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).

## Planets in the 3rd house {#planets}

A planet in the 3rd house shows in how you talk, learn and get about, and often in how things felt with siblings and neighbours.

Saturn, Chiron and Lilith are the three I read most slowly: in [my notebook](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) they held 75 to 90% of the time, against about a third for most placements. With Saturn, [Chiron in the 3rd house](/astrology/chiron-in-3rd-house/) or [Lilith in the 3rd house](/astrology/lilith-in-3rd-house/) in your chart, being heard is usually where a reading goes.

- **[Sun in the 3rd house](/astrology/sun-in-3rd-house/):** words, learning and your close circle are your stage.
- **[Moon in the 3rd house](/astrology/moon-in-3rd-house/):** in its joy: you feel your way through talk, and the daily round steadies you.
- **[Mercury in the 3rd house](/astrology/mercury-in-3rd-house/):** in the house of its own sign: a quick, busy mind; words come easily and often.
- **[Venus in the 3rd house](/astrology/venus-in-3rd-house/):** a pleasant way with words, and warm ties with the people close at hand.
- **[Mars in the 3rd house](/astrology/mars-in-3rd-house/):** quick decisions and a sharp tongue; you enjoy a good argument.
- **[Jupiter in the 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-in-3rd-house/):** a love of learning and talk; you collect ideas and give them away.
- **[Saturn in the 3rd house](/astrology/saturn-in-3rd-house/):** careful words, and a late confidence in your own ideas.
- **[Uranus in the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-in-3rd-house/):** sudden ideas and an unusual way of thinking; a mind that won't sit still.
- **[Neptune in the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-in-3rd-house/):** you think in images and impressions; details slip, imagination doesn't.
- **[Pluto in the 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-in-3rd-house/):** a probing mind; few words, chosen to land.

No planet here? An [empty 3rd house](/astrology/empty-houses/) doesn't mean a quiet mind or a silent family. Read the sign on its cusp and the place of its ruler, and look at Mercury, which speaks for the 3rd in every chart.

## The sign on its cusp {#sign-on-the-cusp}

The sign on the cusp of the 3rd house gives your everyday mind its style: the pace of your thinking, the tone of your messages, the way you learn best.

- **[Aries on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/aries-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** you say it straight and fast, and think on your feet.
- **[Taurus on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** slow, solid thinking; once you've learned something, you keep it.
- **[Gemini on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/gemini-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** several conversations at once, and a mind that loves to switch.
- **[Cancer on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/cancer-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** you talk about feelings and remember what was said.
- **[Leo on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/leo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** a storyteller's voice; you speak to be heard.
- **[Virgo on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/virgo-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** precise words; you spot the mistake in the message.
- **[Libra on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/libra-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** tactful and fair-minded; you weigh both sides before you speak.
- **[Scorpio on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/scorpio-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** you say less than you know, and ask the question that matters.
- **[Sagittarius on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/sagittarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** big ideas and frank opinions; the details can wait.
- **[Capricorn on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/capricorn-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** measured and practical; you speak when you're sure.
- **[Aquarius on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/aquarius-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** original ideas, discussed at a cool distance.
- **[Pisces on the 3rd house cusp](/astrology/pisces-on-3rd-house-cusp/):** you think in pictures and feelings more than in arguments.

## Its ruler {#ruler}

The ruler of the 3rd house is the planet that rules the sign on its cusp, and where it sits shows what your thinking and talking are put to work for: the part of life your words serve. With Scorpio, Aquarius or Pisces on the cusp, there's a modern and a traditional ruler, and I read both ([house rulers](/astrology/house-rulers/)).

1. **[In the 1st house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-1st-house/):** how you speak is a large part of who you are.
2. **[In the 2nd house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/):** your words and skills can earn; you think a lot about value.
3. **[In the 3rd house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/):** the mind stays close to home: local life, siblings, everyday learning.
4. **[In the 4th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-4th-house/):** conversations at home; family stories shape how you think.
5. **[In the 5th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-5th-house/):** words as play: writing, games, flirting.
6. **[In the 6th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-6th-house/):** a practical mind, put to use every working day.
7. **[In the 7th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-7th-house/):** you think best in dialogue; agreements and negotiation.
8. **[In the 8th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-8th-house/):** research, secrets, the conversations people rarely have.
9. **[In the 9th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-9th-house/):** everyday learning grows into study, travel and big questions.
10. **[In the 10th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-10th-house/):** words in public; work built on communication.
11. **[In the 11th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-11th-house/):** ideas shared with friends, groups and networks.
12. **[In the 12th house](/astrology/3rd-house-ruler-in-12th-house/):** private thoughts, things left unsaid, writing for no one but you.

## Transits through the 3rd house {#transits}

The Moon passes through your 3rd house every month, the Sun once a year, Mercury, Venus and Mars for a few weeks at a time: busy spells of messages, errands and conversations. The slow planets stay for years, roughly as long as they take to cross a sign, more or less with the size of the house:

- **[Jupiter transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/jupiter-transit-3rd-house/):** about a year of wanting to learn, read, talk and get about more.
- **[Saturn transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/saturn-transit-3rd-house/):** about two and a half years of careful study and weighed words.
- **[Uranus transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/uranus-transit-3rd-house/):** around seven years of new ideas and new ways of keeping in touch.
- **[Neptune transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/neptune-transit-3rd-house/):** around fourteen years when imagination grows and details blur: a good time to write, and to read twice what you sign.
- **[Pluto transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/pluto-transit-3rd-house/):** a long change in how you think and in what you're willing to say.
- **[Chiron transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/chiron-transit-3rd-house/):** an old hurt about not being heard comes back, this time to be listened to.
- **[North Node transiting the 3rd house](/astrology/north-node-transit-3rd-house/):** about a year and a half of being pulled towards asking, learning and speaking up.

I read these as weather, not a forecast: they describe how your mind and your days may feel for a while, not what anyone will say or do.
