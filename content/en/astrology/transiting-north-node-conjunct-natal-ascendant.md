Of the points the North Node can cross, the Ascendant is the one that depends entirely on your birth time: it is the degree that was rising in the east at the minute you were born, and without the time there is nothing to read ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)). When the node reaches it, the South Node reaches your Descendant, and for a month or so the line between you and other people is also the line of the nodes.

## What changes {#what-changes}

Your [Ascendant](/astrology/ascendant/) is the orientation you start from: the stance you take on entering a room, your first move in anything new. The [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/), exactly opposite, is the frame through which you meet a counterpart. The [North Node](/astrology/north-node/) marks the capacity your chart is still practising, and a [conjunction](/astrology/conjunction/) fuses two functions, so under this [transit](/astrology/transits/) the new capacity becomes your way in: you begin from the less familiar side.

It can be small and visible. You speak first in a meeting where you usually listen, or introduce yourself by what you are learning rather than by what you know. In the same weeks the [South Node](/astrology/south-node/) crosses your Descendant, the end of your horizon where you meet others, and its familiar skill there is adjusting to them, reading the room, mirroring. That skill stays yours; the month asks you to bring it along rather than lead with it. No meeting and no parting is foretold: what moves is your stance, not anyone's arrival.

In Placidus, the house system I use, the crossing closes the node's stay in your 1st ([North Node transiting the 1st house](/astrology/north-node-transit-1st-house/)) and opens its stay in your 12th ([North Node transiting the 12th house](/astrology/north-node-transit-12th-house/)), while the South Node leaves your 7th for your 6th. If you were born with the North Node close to your rising degree, each nodal return brings this transit with it.

## How long it lasts {#how-long-it-lasts}

From 1½° before exact to 1° after, the true node usually takes about a month over your rising degree. If an eclipse season falls in those weeks, the node nearly halts while the Sun is close to the nodes and the pass lasts three months or more; about one pass in 25 brings three exact hits. The node is in Aquarius from July 2026 to March 2028, moving backwards: with an Ascendant at 6°10′ Aquarius, for instance, the crossing is exact on 7 November 2027, and the annular eclipse of 26 January 2028 falls on that very degree about eleven weeks later ([eclipses on your natal planets](/astrology/eclipses-on-your-natal-planets/)).

Those dates are only as good as the time they come from. A birth time rounded to the nearest quarter of an hour can be up to seven and a half minutes out, which moves the Ascendant by about two degrees on average and this transit by about five weeks.

It comes once a round, four or five times by 80. About three years later the node sextiles your Ascendant ([Transiting North Node sextile natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-sextile-natal-ascendant/)), then squares it ([Transiting North Node square natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-square-natal-ascendant/)) and trines it ([Transiting North Node trine natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-trine-natal-ascendant/)); about nine years on it reaches your Descendant ([Transiting North Node opposite natal Ascendant](/astrology/transiting-north-node-opposite-natal-ascendant/)).

## What helps {#what-helps}

Change your first move. Once a week this month, begin a conversation or a task from the side you usually leave for later.

Notice the moment you adjust to someone before saying a word, and say your word first, once a day.

If your birth time comes from memory rather than a record, look for the record. This month's place in the year depends on it.
