Across from your Scorpio Ascendant, Taurus sits on the 7th house cusp. You meet the world with intensity and reserve, and the people you choose to face, one to one, tend to be the ones who hold their ground.

## What it means {#what-it-means}

The sign on the [Descendant](/astrology/descendant/) describes what you look for, and tend to meet, in the people you face one to one ([house cusps](/astrology/house-cusps/)). With [Taurus](/astrology/taurus/) there, it's the person who builds things to last: the partner who keeps the shared calendar, mends what breaks, and still remembers after twenty years how you take your tea. Scorpio on your Ascendant goes deep and gives little away; Taurus opposite offers ground that holds, and a partnership often gives you the calm you don't easily give yourself. In the [7th house](/astrology/7th-house/), business partners and close collaborators count too, and the pattern holds there: you work best with people who look after what's shared. Tradition put open rivals in this house as well. With Taurus on its cusp, disagreements tend to be about what to keep and what to change, and they're settled by time more often than by a single row.

Every 7th house has Venus as its natural ruler, since Libra is the seventh sign, but your own 7th is ruled by whatever planet rules its cusp. With Taurus there, the two coincide: [Venus](/astrology/venus/) rules your partnerships both by nature and in your chart, so its house and sign weigh more than usual. Libra, Venus's other sign, falls in your 11th or 12th in these charts, so the same planet speaks for your partners and for either your friends or your private life. The house holding Venus is where you tend to find, and keep, your partners: the [1st](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-1st-house/), [2nd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-2nd-house/), [3rd](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-3rd-house/), [4th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-4th-house/), [5th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-5th-house/), [6th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-6th-house/), [7th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-7th-house/), [8th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-8th-house/), [9th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-9th-house/), [10th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-10th-house/), [11th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-11th-house/) or [12th](/astrology/7th-house-ruler-in-12th-house/).

This is the cusp where Taurus turns up most: about 1 birth in 8 at London's latitude and 1 in 9 at New York's, counting Placidus cusps, the system I use. The reason is the slow setting of Taurus. In the time Scorpio needs to rise at London, nearly three hours, Taurus sits on the western horizon, so a large share of births land with it there. Many of these charts have Taurus on the 6th cusp too, about 2 London charts in 5: work and partnership then answer to Venus together, and the people you work with can become the people you share your life with ([Taurus on the 6th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-6th-house-cusp/); [duplicated signs](/astrology/duplicated-signs/)). Taurus on the 8th as well is rare ([Taurus on the 8th house cusp](/astrology/taurus-on-8th-house-cusp/)). In about 1 London birth in 7, Taurus is on no cusp at all ([intercepted signs](/astrology/intercepted-signs/)).

Nothing in this cusp says whom you'll marry or how long a bond will last: it shows what you value in partnership, and the rest belongs to two people. The Descendant moves with the Ascendant, so it takes your birth time to find it, and I won't guess it from a birth date alone ([what changes without a birth time](/blog/unknown-birth-time/)).
