## For reflection and entertainment {#entertainment}

Readings from The Astro Study are offered **for reflection and entertainment**. No outcome is promised, and a reading is not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

That’s the customary sentence, and I think it’s fair. Not because I take astrology lightly — I tested my own chart line by line before reading anyone else’s — but because **nobody knows exactly how it works**, me included. Saying so is a promise not to pretend.

## Adults only {#adults}

Readings are for people aged 18 and over. No readings for anyone under 18, and none about them either — including inside a family synastry reading.

## Where readings are offered {#where}

Readings aren’t offered to people in Pennsylvania (USA), where state law (18 Pa.C.S. § 7104) makes predicting the future for money an offence — including by the movement of the heavenly bodies.

## What I don’t claim {#claims}

- To know **how** astrology works. I know what held in my own tests, and I tell you which parts held less.
- To have a **gift**. There’s nothing in a reading you couldn’t learn yourself, with time.
- That my count is a **scientific study**. It’s a hand count in a notebook, not audited.
- To predict **the future**. A chart describes tendencies and seasons; your choices do the rest.

## A chart is not an excuse {#excuse}

“It’s because I’m a Scorpio.” No. A birth chart describes tendencies, not permissions. It never excuses anyone — you, me, or the person beside you — from being responsible for what they do.

## You stay in charge {#choices}

Act on what you feel and know, not on what was said in a reading. A reading is not an instruction, and you remain responsible for your choices. If you notice you’re consulting charts all the time, take a break: the muscle that decides is yours, and it gets stronger with use.

## Synastry and consent {#consent}

A synastry reading involves another person’s birth details. Both people must be adults. Please share someone else’s details only if they know you’re asking for the reading — and for co-worker readings, only with their agreement. I never use a chart to tell anyone to stay, leave, forgive, or cut someone off, and I never read anyone for hiring, firing or promotion.

## If you’re struggling {#help}

A reading can sit beside care, never in place of it. If you’re finding things hard right now, please talk to someone today:

- **Samaritans** — free, day and night, on **116 123** (UK and Ireland), or [samaritans.org](https://www.samaritans.org).
- **Shout** — free text support, any time: text **SHOUT** to **85258** (UK).
- **988** — call or text, free, day and night (US and Canada).
- **NHS 111** for urgent medical help, and **999** in an emergency.
