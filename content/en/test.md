## Why it’s blind {glyph="☽"}

In 1948 a psychologist, Bertram Forer, gave each of his students a personality profile “written just for them”. They rated it 4.26 out of 5 for accuracy. Every student had the same text, taken mostly from an astrology book. It’s known today as the [Barnum effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnum_effect).

That’s the weak spot of any reading handed to you with your name on it, mine included: it’s hard to tell a good fit from a flattering one. So this test doesn’t hand you yours. It hands you three, and you pick.

## Why I’m doing it {glyph="♄"}

I came to astrology to mock it, then [checked my own chart like an invoice](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/): a tick or a cross beside every placement. But the people I read knew the chart was theirs. This is the blind version, at scale: if the descriptions drawn from a chart are good, people should pick their own more often than one time in three. If they don’t, I’ll publish that too.
